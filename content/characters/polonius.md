*Character* — appears in **1** work(s).

**IT** — Polonio, il ciarliero consigliere del re Claudio in Amleto, padre di Ofelia e Laerte; Amleto lo uccide mentre spia nascosto dietro un arazzo.

**EN** — Polonius, King Claudius's long-winded counsellor in Hamlet, father of Ophelia and Laertes; Hamlet kills him as he spies from behind a tapestry.
