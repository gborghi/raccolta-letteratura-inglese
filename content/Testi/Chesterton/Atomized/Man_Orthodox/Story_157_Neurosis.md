NEUROSIS

According to [a modern critic], it is morbid to confess your sins.
I should say that the morbid thing is not to confess them.
The morbid thing is to conceal your sins and let them eat
your heart out, which is the happy state of most people
in highly civilized communities.

                                            Daily News, January 18th, 1908
