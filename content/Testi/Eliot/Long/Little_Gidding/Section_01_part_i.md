I  
Midwinter [[Spring|spring]] is its own season  
Sempiternal though sodden towards sundown,  
Suspended in [[Time|time]], between pole and tropic.  
When the short day is brightest, with [[Frost|frost]] and [[Fire|fire]],  
The brief sun flames the ice, on pond and ditches,  
In windless cold that is the heart’s heat‚  
Reflecting in a watery [[Mirror|mirror]]  
A glare that is blindness in the early afternoon.  
And glow more intense than blaze of branch, or brazier,  
Stirs the dumb spirit: no [[Wind|wind]], but pentecostal [[Fire|fire]]  
In the dark [[Time|time]] of the year. Between melting and freezing  
The [[Soul|soul]]’s sap quivers. There is no earth smell  
Or smell of living thing. This is the [[Spring|spring]] [[Time|time]]  
But not in [[Time|time]]’s covenant. Now the hedgerow  
Is blanched for an hour with transitory blossom  
Of [[Snow|snow]], a bloom more sudden  
Than that of [[Summer|summer]], neither budding nor fading,  
Not in the scheme of generation.  
Where is the [[Summer|summer]], the unimaginable  
Zero [[Summer|summer]]?  
                         If you came this way,  
Taking the route you would be likely to take  
From the place you would be likely to come from,  
If you came this way in may [[Time|time]], you would find the hedges  
White again, in May, with voluptuary sweetness.  
It would be the same at the end of the [[Journey|journey]],  
If you came at [[Night|night]] like a broken [[King|king]],  
If you came by day not knowing what you came for,  
It would be the same, when you leave the rough [[Road|road]]  
And turn behind the pig-sty to the dull façade  
And the tombstone. And what you thought you came for  
Is only a shell, a husk of meaning  
From which the purpose breaks only when it is fulfilled  
If at all. Either you had no purpose  
Or the purpose is beyond the end you figured  
And is altered in fulfilment. There are other places  
Which also are the world’s end, some at the sea jaws,  
Or over a dark lake, in a [[Desert|desert]] or a [[City|city]] —  
But this is the nearest, in place and [[Time|time]],  
Now and in England.  
                                     If you came this way,  
Taking any route, starting from anywhere,  
At any [[Time|time]] or at any season,  
It would always be the same: you would have to put off  
Sense and notion. You are not here to verify,  
Instruct yourself, or inform curiosity  
Or carry report. You are here to kneel  
Where [[prayer]] has been valid. And [[prayer]] is more  
Than an order of words, the conscious occupation  
Of the praying mind, or the sound of the voice praying.  
And what the dead had no speech for, when living,  
They can tell you, being dead: the communication  
Of the dead is tongued with [[Fire|fire]] beyond the language of the living.  
Here, the intersection of the timeless moment  
Is England and nowhere. Never and always.
