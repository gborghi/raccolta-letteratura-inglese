CHAPTER HEADING


  For we have thought the longer thoughts
          And gone the shorter way.
  And we have danced to devils’ tunes,
          Shivering home to pray;
  To serve one master in the night,
          Another in the day.



  PRINTED AT DIJON
  BY
  MAURICE DARANTIERE
  M. CM. XXIII
