| *(didascalia)* | THEKLA, LADY NEUBRUNN. |
| THEKLA | Now, gentle Neubrunn, shew me the affection<br>Which thou hast ever promised — prove thyself<br>My own true friend and faithful fellow-[[Pilgrim\|pilgrim]].<br>This [[Night\|night]] we must away! |
| NEUBRUNN | Away! and whither? |
| THEKLA | Whither! There is but one place in the world.<br>Thither where he lies buried! To his coffin! |
| NEUBRUNN | What would you do there? |
| THEKLA | What do there?<br>That would'st thou not have asked, hadst thou e'er loved.<br>There, there is all that still remains of him.<br>That single spot is the whole earth to me. |
| NEUBRUNN | That place of [[Death\|death]] —— |
| THEKLA | Is now the only place,<br>Where life yet dwells for me: detain me not!<br>Come and make preparations: let us think<br>Of means to fly from hence. |
| NEUBRUNN | Your father's rage —— |
| THEKLA | That [[Time\|time]] is past ——<br>And now I [[Fear\|fear]] no human being's rage. |
| NEUBRUNN | The sentence of the world! The tongue of calumny! |
| THEKLA | Whom am I seeking? Him who is no more.<br>Am I then hastening to the arms —— O God!<br>I haste but to the [[Grave\|grave]] of the beloved. |
| NEUBRUNN | And we [[Alone\|alone]], two helpless feeble women? |
| THEKLA | We will take weapons: my arms shall protect thee. |
| NEUBRUNN | In the dark [[Night\|night]]-[[Time\|time]]? |
| THEKLA | Darkness will conceal us. |
| NEUBRUNN | This rough tempestuous [[Night\|night]] —— |
| THEKLA | Had he a soft bed<br>Under the hoofs of his war-horses? |
| NEUBRUNN | Heaven!<br>And then the many posts of the enemy! — |
| THEKLA | They are human beings. Misery travels free<br>Through the whole earth. |
| NEUBRUNN | The [[Journey\|journey]]'s weary length — |
| THEKLA | The [[Pilgrim\|pilgrim]], travelling to a distant shrine<br>Of [[Hope\|hope]] and healing, doth not count the leagues. |
| NEUBRUNN | How can we pass the gates? |
| THEKLA | Gold opens them.<br>Go, do but go. |
| NEUBRUNN | Should we be recognized — |
| THEKLA | In a despairing woman, a poor fugitive,<br>Will no one seek the daughter of Duke Friedland. |
| NEUBRUNN | And where procure we horses for our flight? |
| THEKLA | My equerry procures them. Go and fetch him. |
| NEUBRUNN | Dares he, without the knowledge of his lord? |
| THEKLA | He will. Go, only go. Delay no longer. |
| NEUBRUNN | Dear lady! and your mother? |
| THEKLA | Oh! my mother! |
| NEUBRUNN | So much as she has suffered too already;<br>Your tender mother — Ah! how ill prepared<br>For this last anguish! |
| THEKLA | Woe is me! my mother! |
| *(didascalia)* | [Pauses. |
| THEKLA | Go instantly. |
| NEUBRUNN | But think what you are doing! |
| THEKLA | What can be thought, already has been thought. |
| NEUBRUNN | And being there, what purpose you to do? |
| THEKLA | There a divinity will prompt my [[Soul\|soul]]. |
| NEUBRUNN | Your heart, dear lady, is disquieted!<br>And this is not the way that leads to quiet. |
| THEKLA | To a deep quiet, such as he has found.<br>It draws me on, I know not what to name it,<br>Resistless does it draw me to his [[Grave\|grave]].<br>There will my heart be eased, my [[Tears\|tears]] will flow.<br>O hasten, make no further questioning!<br>There is no rest for me till I have left<br>These walls — they fall in on me — A dim power<br>Drives me from hence — Oh [[Mercy\|mercy]]! What a feeling!<br>What pale and hollow forms are those! They fill,<br>They crowd the place! I have no longer room here!<br>[[Mercy]]! Still more! More still! The hideous swarm!<br>They press on me; they chase me from these walls —<br>Those hollow, bodiless forms of living men! |
| NEUBRUNN | You frighten me so, lady, that no longer<br>I dare stay here myself. I go and call<br>Rosenberg instantly. |
| *(didascalia)* | [Exit LADY NEUBRUNN. |
