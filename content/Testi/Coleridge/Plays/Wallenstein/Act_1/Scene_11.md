| COUNTESS TERTSKY | I can endure no<br>longer. No! [Looks around her.<br>Where are they?<br>No one is here. They leave me all [[Alone\|alone]],<br>[[Alone]] in this sore anguish of suspense.<br>And I must wear the outward shew of calmness<br>Before my sister, and shut in within me<br>The pangs and agonies of my crowded bosom.<br>It is not to be borne. — If all should fail;<br>If — if he must go over to the Swedes,<br>An empty-handed fugitive, and not<br>As an ally, a covenanted equal,<br>A proud commander with his army following;<br>If we must wander on from land to land,<br>Like the Count Palatine, of fallen [[Greatness\|greatness]]<br>An ignominious monument — But no!<br>That day I will not see! And could himself<br>Endure to sink so low, I would not bear<br>To see him so low sunken. |
