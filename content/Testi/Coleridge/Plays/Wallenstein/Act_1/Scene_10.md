| Chi parla | Battuta |
|---|---|
| *(didascalia)* | To these enter BUTLER. |
| TERTSKY | O look there! Butler! Here we've still a friend! |
| WALLENSTEIN | Come to my heart, old comrade! Not the sun<br>Looks out upon us more revivingly<br>In the earliest month of [[spring]],<br>Than a friend's countenance in such an hour. |
| BUTLER | My General: I come — |
| WALLENSTEIN | Know'st thou already?<br>That old man has betrayed me to the Emperor.<br>What say'st thou? Thirty years have we together<br>Lived out, and held out, sharing joy and hardship.<br>We have slept in one camp-bed, drunk from one glass,<br>One morsel shared! I leaned myself on him,<br>As now I lean me on thy faithful shoulder.<br>And now in the very moment, when, all [[Love\|love]],<br>All confidence, my bosom beat to his,<br>He sees and takes the advantage, stabs the knife<br>Slowly into my heart. |
| *(didascalia)* | He hides his face on BUTLER'S breast. |
| BUTLER | Forget the false one.<br>What is your present purpose? |
| WALLENSTEIN | [[Well]] remembered!<br>[[Courage]] my [[Soul\|soul]]! I am still rich in friends,<br>Still loved by Destiny; for in the moment,<br>That it unmasks the plotting hypocrite,<br>It sends and proves to me one faithful heart.<br>Of the hypocrite no more! Think not, his loss<br>Was that which struck the pang: O no! his treason<br>Is that which strikes this pang! No more of him!<br>Dear to my heart, and honoured were they both,<br>And the young man — yes — he did truly [[Love\|love]] me,<br>He — he — has not deceived me. But enough,<br>Enough of this — Swift counsel now beseems us.<br>The Courier, whom Count Kinsky sent from Prague<br>I expect him every moment: and whatever<br>He may bring with him, we must take good care<br>To keep it from the mutineers. Quick, then!<br>Dispatch some messenger you can rely on<br>To meet him, and conduct him to me. |
| *(didascalia)* | ILLO is going. |
| BUTLER | My General, whom expect you then? |
| WALLENSTEIN | The Courier<br>Who brings me word of the event at Prague. |
| BUTLER | Hem! |
| WALLENSTEIN | And what now? |
| BUTLER | You do not know it? |
| WALLENSTEIN | [[Well]]? |
| BUTLER | From what that larum in the camp arose? |
| WALLENSTEIN | From what? |
| BUTLER | That Courier. |
| WALLENSTEIN | [[Well]]? |
| BUTLER | Is already here. |
| TERTSKY and ILLO | Already here? |
| WALLENSTEIN | My Courier? |
| BUTLER | For some hours. |
| WALLENSTEIN | And I not know it? |
| BUTLER | The centinels detain him<br>In custody. |
| ILLO | Damnation! |
| BUTLER | And his letter<br>Was broken open, and is circulated<br>Through the whole camp. |
| WALLENSTEIN | You know what it contains? |
| BUTLER | Question me not. |
| TERTSKY | Illo! alas for us. |
| WALLENSTEIN | Hide nothing from me — I can hear the worst.<br>Prague then is lost. It is. Confess it freely. |
| BUTLER | Yes! Prague is lost. And all the several regiments<br>At Budweiss, Tabor, Brannau, Konigingratz,<br>At Brun and Znaym, have forsaken you,<br>And ta'en the oaths of fealty anew<br>To the Emperor. Yourself, with Kinsky, Tertsky,<br>And Illo have been sentenced. |
| *(didascalia)* | TERTSKY and ILLO express alarm and fury. WALLENSTEIN remains firm and collected. |
| WALLENSTEIN | 'Tis decided!<br>'Tis [[Well\|well]]! I have received a sudden cure<br>From all the pangs of [[Doubt\|doubt]]: with steady stream<br>Once more my life-[[Blood\|blood]] flows! My [[Soul\|soul]]'s secure!<br>In the [[Night\|night]] only Friedland's [[Stars\|stars]] can beam.<br>Lingering irresolute, with fitful fears<br>I drew the [[Sword\|sword]]—'twas with an inward strife,<br>While yet the choice was mine. The murderous knife<br>Is lifted for my heart! [[Doubt]] disappears!<br>I fight now for my head and for my life. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit WALLENSTEIN; the others follow him. |
