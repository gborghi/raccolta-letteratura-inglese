| Chi parla | Battuta |
|---|---|
| *(didascalia)* | The COUNTESS, THEKLA. |
| COUNTESS | It does not please me, Princess! that he holds<br>Himself so still, exactly at this [[Time\|time]]. |
| THEKLA | Exactly at this [[Time\|time]]? |
| COUNTESS | He now knows all.<br>'Twere now the moment to declare himself. |
| THEKLA | If I'm to understand you, speak less darkly. |
| COUNTESS | 'Twas for that purpose that I bade her leave us.<br>Thekla, you are no more a [[Child\|child]]. Your heart<br>Is now no more in nonage: for you [[Love\|love]],<br>And boldness dwells with [[Love\|love]] — that you have proved.<br>Your [[Nature\|nature]] moulds itself upon your father's<br>More than your mother's spirit. Therefore may you<br>Hear, what were too much for her fortitude. |
| THEKLA | Enough! no further preface, I entreat you.<br>At once, out with it! Be it what it may,<br>It is not possible that it should torture me<br>More than this introduction. What have you<br>To say to me? Tell me the whole and briefly! |
| COUNTESS | You'll not be frightened — |
| THEKLA | Name it, I entreat you. |
| COUNTESS | It lies within your power to do your father<br>A weighty service — |
| THEKLA | Lies within my power?<br>Max Piccolomini loves you. You can link him<br>Indissolubly to your father. |
| COUNTESS | I?<br>What need of me for that? And is he not<br>Already linked to him? |
| THEKLA | He was. |
| COUNTESS | And wherefore<br>Should he not be so now — not be so always? |
| THEKLA | He cleaves to the Emperor too. |
| COUNTESS | Not more than duty<br>And [[Honour\|honour]] may demand of him. |
| THEKLA | We ask<br>Proofs of his [[Love\|love]], and not proofs of his [[Honour\|honour]].<br>Duty and [[Honour\|honour]]!<br>Those are ambiguous words with many meanings.<br>You should interpret them for him: his [[Love\|love]]<br>Should be the sole definer of his [[Honour\|honour]]. |
| COUNTESS | How? |
| THEKLA | The Emperor or you must he renounce. |
| COUNTESS | He will accompany my father gladly<br>In his retirement. From himself you heard,<br>How much he wished to lay aside the [[Sword\|sword]]. |
| THEKLA | He must not lay the [[Sword\|sword]] aside, we mean;<br>He must unsheath it in your father's cause. |
| COUNTESS | He'll spend with gladness and alacrity<br>His life, his heart's [[Blood\|blood]] in my father's cause,<br>If shame or injury be intended him. |
| THEKLA | You will not understand me. [[Well]], hear then!<br>Your father has fallen off from the Emperor,<br>And is about to join the enemy<br>With the whole soldiery — |
| COUNTESS | Alas, my mother! |
| THEKLA | There needs a great example to draw on<br>The army after him. The Piccolomini<br>Possess the [[Love\|love]] and reverence of the troops;<br>They govern all opinions, and wherever<br>They lead the way, none hesitate to follow.<br>The son secures the father to our interests —<br>You've much in your hands at this moment. |
| COUNTESS | Ah,<br>My miserable mother! what a [[Death\|death]]-stroke<br>Awaits thee! — No! She never will survive it. |
| THEKLA | She will accommodate her [[Soul\|soul]] to that<br>Which is and must be. I do know your mother.<br>The far-off future weights upon her heart<br>With torture of anxiety; but is it<br>Unalterably, actually present,<br>She soon resigns herself, and bears it calmly. |
| COUNTESS | O my fore-boding bosom! Even now,<br>E'en now 'tis here, that icy hand of horror!<br>And my young [[Hope\|hope]] lies shuddering in its grasp;<br>I knew it [[Well\|well]] — no sooner had I entered,<br>A heavy ominous presentiment<br>Revealed to me, that spirits of [[Death\|death]] were hovering<br>Over my happy fortune. But why think I<br>First of myself? My mother! O my mother! |
| THEKLA | Calm yourself! Break not out in vain lamenting!<br>Preserve you for your father the firm friend,<br>And for yourself the [[Lover\|lover]], all will yet<br>Prove good and fortunate. |
| COUNTESS | Prove good? What good?<br>Must we not part? Part ne'er to meet again? |
| THEKLA | He parts not from you! He can not part from you. |
| COUNTESS | Alas for his sore anguish! It will rend<br>His heart asunder. |
| THEKLA | If indeed he loves you,<br>His resolution will be speedily taken. |
| COUNTESS | His resolution will be speedily taken —<br>O do not [[Doubt\|doubt]] of that! A resolution!<br>Does there remain one to be taken? |
| THEKLA | Hush!<br>Collect yourself! I hear your mother coming. |
| COUNTESS | How shall I bear to see her? |
| THEKLA | Collect yourself. |
