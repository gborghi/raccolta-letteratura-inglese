| *(didascalia)* | To these enter WALLENSTEIN, in conversation with the Burgomaster of Egra. |
| WALLENSTEIN | You were at one [[Time\|time]] a free town. I see,<br>Ye bear the half eagle in your [[City\|city]] arms.<br>Why the half eagle only? |
| BURGOMASTER | We were free,<br>But for these last two hundred years has Egra<br>Remained in pledge to the Bohemian [[Crown\|crown]],<br>Therefore we bear the half eagle, the other half<br>Being cancelled till the empire ransom us,<br>If ever that should be. |
| WALLENSTEIN | Ye merit [[Freedom\|freedom]].<br>Only be firm and dauntless. Lend your ears<br>To no designing whispering [[Court\|court]]-minions.<br>What may your imposts be? |
| BURGOMASTER | So heavy that<br>We totter under them. The garrison<br>Lives at our costs. |
| WALLENSTEIN | I will relieve you. Tell me,<br>There are some Protestants among you still? |
| *(didascalia)* | [The Burgomaster hesitates. |
| WALLENSTEIN | Yes, yes; I know it. Many lie concealed<br>Within these walls — Confess now — you yourself —<br>Be not alarmed. I hate the Jesuits.<br>Could my will have determined it, they had<br>Been long ago expelled the empire. Trust me —<br>Mass-book or [[Bible]]—'tis all one to me.<br>Of that the world has had sufficient proof.<br>I built a church for the reformed in Glogan<br>At my own instance. Hark'e, Burgomaster!<br>What is your name? |
| BURGOMASTER | Pachhälbel, may it please you. |
| WALLENSTEIN | Hark'e! ——<br>But let it go no further, what I now<br>Disclose to you in confidence. |
| *(didascalia)* | [Laying his hand on the Burgomaster's shoulder. |
| WALLENSTEIN | The times<br>Draw near to their fulfilment, Burgomaster!<br>The high will fall, the low will be exalted.<br>Hark'e! But keep it to yourself! The end<br>Approaches of the Spanish [[Double\|double]] monarchy —<br>A new arrangement is at hand. You saw<br>The three moons that appeared at once in the Heaven. |
| BURGOMASTER | With [[Wonder\|wonder]] and affright! |
| WALLENSTEIN | Whereof did two<br>Strangely transform themselves to bloody daggers.<br>And only one, the middle [[Moon\|moon]], remained<br>Steady and clear. |
| BURGOMASTER | We applied it to the Turks. |
| WALLENSTEIN | The Turks! That all? — I tell you, that two empires<br>Will set in [[Blood\|blood]], in the East and in the West,<br>And Luth'ranism [[Alone\|alone]] remain. |
| *(didascalia)* | [Observing GORDON and BUTLER. |
| WALLENSTEIN | I'[[Faith\|faith]],<br>'Twas a smart cannonading that we heard<br>This evening, as we journeyed hitherward;<br>'Twas on our left hand. Did you hear it here? |
| GORDON | Distinctly. The [[Wind\|wind]] brought it from the South. |
| BUTLER | It seemed to come from Weiden or from Neustadt. |
| WALLENSTEIN | Tis likely. That's the route the Swedes are taking.<br>How strong is the garrison? |
| GORDON | Not quite two hundred<br>Competent men, the rest are invalids. |
| WALLENSTEIN | Good! And how many in the vale of Jochim? |
| GORDON | Two hundred arquebussiers have I sent thither<br>To fortify the posts against the Swedes. |
| WALLENSTEIN | Good! I commend your foresight. At the works too<br>You have done somewhat? |
| GORDON | Two additional batteries<br>I caused to be run up. They were needless.<br>The Rhinegrave presses hard upon us, General! |
| WALLENSTEIN | You have been watchful in your Emperor's service.<br>I am content with you, Lieutenant-Colonel. |
| *(didascalia)* | [To BUTLER. |
| WALLENSTEIN | Release the outposts in the vale of Jochim<br>With all the stations in the enemy's route. |
| *(didascalia)* | [To GORDON. |
| WALLENSTEIN | Governor, in your faithful hands I leave<br>My wife, my daughter, and my sister. I<br>Shall make no stay here, and wait but the arrival<br>Of letters, to take leave of you, together<br>With all the regiments. |
