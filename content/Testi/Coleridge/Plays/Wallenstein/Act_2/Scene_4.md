| Chi parla | Battuta |
|---|---|
| *(didascalia)* | To these enter BUTLER. |
| BUTLER | General! This is not right! |
| WALLENSTEIN | What is not right? |
| BUTLER | It must needs injure us with all honest men. |
| WALLENSTEIN | But what? |
| BUTLER | It is an open proclamation<br>Of insurrection. |
| WALLENSTEIN | [[Well]], [[Well\|well]] — but what is it? |
| BUTLER | Count Tertsky's regiments tear the Imperial Eagle<br>From off the banners, and instead of it,<br>Have reared aloft thy arms. |
| ANSPESSADE | Right about! March! |
| WALLENSTEIN | Cursed be this counsel, and accursed who gave it! |
| *(didascalia)* | To the Cuirassiers, who are retiring. |
| WALLENSTEIN | Halt, children, halt! There's some mistake in this;<br>Hark! — I will punish it severely. Stop!<br>They do not hear. (To ILLO.) Go after them, assure them,<br>And bring them back to me, cost what it may. |
| *(didascalia)* | ILLO hurries out. |
| WALLENSTEIN | This hurls us headlong. Butler! Butler!<br>You are my evil genius, wherefore must you<br>Announce it in their presence? It was all<br>In a fair way. They were half won, those madmen<br>With their improvident over-readiness —<br>A cruel game is fortune playing with me.<br>The zeal of friends it is that razes me,<br>And not the hate of enemies. |
