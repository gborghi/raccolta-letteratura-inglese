| *(didascalia)* | COUNTESS, DUCHESS, MAX, and THEKLA. |
| COUNTESS | Let them but see him — there is [[Hope\|hope]] still, sister. |
| DUCHESS | [[Hope]]! I have none! |
| MAX | This can I not endure.<br>With most determined [[Soul\|soul]] did I come hither,<br>My purposed action seemed unblameable<br>To my own [[Conscience\|conscience]] — and I must stand here<br>Like one abhorred, a hard inhuman being;<br>Yea, loaded with the curse of all I [[Love\|love]]!<br>Must see all whom I [[Love\|love]] in this sore anguish,<br>Whom I with one word can make happy — O!<br>My heart revolts within me, and two voices<br>Make themselves audible within my bosom.<br>My [[Soul\|soul]]'s benighted; I no longer can<br>Distinguish the right track. O, [[Well\|well]] and truly<br>Didst thou say, father, I relied too much<br>On my own heart. My mind moves to and fro —<br>I know not what to do. |
| COUNTESS | What! you know not?<br>Does not your own heart tell you? O! then I<br>Will tell it you. Your father is a traitor,<br>A frightful traitor to us — he has plotted<br>Against our General's life, has plunged us all<br>In misery — and you're his son! 'Tis yours<br>To make the amends — Make you the son's fidelity<br>Outweigh the father's treason, that the name<br>Of Piccolomini be not a proverb<br>Of infamy, a common form of cursing<br>To the posterity of Wallenstein. |
| MAX | Where is that voice of [[Truth\|truth]] which I dare follow?<br>It speaks no longer in my heart. We all<br>But utter what our passionate wishes dictate:<br>O that an angel would descend from Heaven,<br>And scoop for me the right, the uncorrupted,<br>With a pure hand from the pure Fount of Light. |
| *(didascalia)* | [His eyes glance on THEKLA. |
| MAX | What other angel seek I? To this heart,<br>To this unerring heart, will I submit it,<br>Will ask thy [[Love\|love]], which has the power to bless<br>The happy man [[Alone\|alone]], averted ever<br>From the disquieted and guilty — canst thou<br>Still [[Love\|love]] me, if I stay? Say that thou canst,<br>And I am the Duke's —— |
| COUNTESS | Think, niece —— |
| MAX | Think nothing, Thekla!<br>Speak what thou feelest. |
| COUNTESS | Think upon your father. |
| MAX | I did not question thee, as Friedland's daughter.<br>Thee, the beloved and the unerring god<br>Within thy heart, I question. What's at stake?<br>Not whether diadem of royalty<br>Be to be won or not — that might'st thou think on.<br>Thy friend, and his [[Soul\|soul]]'s quiet, are at stake;<br>The fortune of a thousand gallant men,<br>Who will all follow me; shall I forswear<br>My oath and duty to the Emperor?<br>Say, shall I send into Octavio's camp<br>The parricidal ball? For when the ball<br>Has left its cannon, and is on its flight,<br>It is no longer a dead instrument!<br>It lives, a spirit passes into it,<br>The avenging furies seize possession of it,<br>And with sure malice guide it the worst way. |
| THEKLA | O! Max —— |
| MAX | Nay, not precipitately either, Thekla.<br>I understand thee. To thy noble heart<br>The hardest duty might appear the highest.<br>The human, not the great part, would I act.<br>Ev'n from my [[Childhood\|childhood]] to this present hour,<br>Think what the Duke has done for me, how loved me,<br>And think too, how my father has repaid him.<br>O likewise the free lovely impulses<br>Of hospitality, the pious friend's<br>Faithful attachment, these too are a holy<br>Religion to the heart; and heavily<br>The shudderings of [[Nature\|nature]] do avenge<br>Themselves on the barbarian that insults them.<br>Lay all upon the balance, all — then speak,<br>And let thy heart decide it. |
| THEKLA | O, thy own<br>Hath long ago decided. Follow thou<br>Thy heart's first feeling —— |
| COUNTESS | Oh! ill-fated woman! |
| THEKLA | Is it possible, that that can be the right,<br>The which thy tender heart did not at first<br>Detect and seize with instant impulse? Go,<br>Fulfil thy duty! I should ever [[Love\|love]] thee.<br>Whate'er thou had'st chosen, thou would'st still have acted<br>Nobly and worthy of thee — but repentance<br>Shall ne'er disturb thy [[Soul\|soul]]'s fair peace. |
| MAX | Then I<br>Must leave thee, must part from thee! |
| THEKLA | Being faithful<br>To thine own [[Self\|self]], thou art faithful too to me:<br>If our fates part, our hearts remain united.<br>A bloody hatred will divide for ever<br>The houses Piccolomini and Friedland;<br>But we belong not to our houses — Go!<br>Quick! quick! and separate thy righteous cause<br>From our unholy and unblessed one!<br>The curse of heaven lies upon our head:<br>'Tis dedicate to ruin. Even me<br>My father's [[Guilt\|guilt]] drags with it to perdition.<br>Mourn not for me:<br>My destiny will quickly be decided. |
