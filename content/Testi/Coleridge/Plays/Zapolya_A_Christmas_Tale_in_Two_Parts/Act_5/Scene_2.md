| *(didascalia)* | SCENE. — Chamber in CASIMIR’S [[Castle]]. Confederates discovered. |
| FIRST CONFEDERATE | It cannot but succeed, friends. From this palace<br>E’en to the wood, our messengers are posted<br>With such short interspace, that fast as sound<br>Can travel to us, we shall learn the event! |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter another Confederate. |
| FIRST CONFEDERATE | What tidings from Temeswar? |
| SECOND CONFEDERATE | With one voice<br>Th’ assembled chieftains have deposed the [[Tyrant|tyrant]]:<br>He is proclaimed the public enemy,<br>And the protection of the law withdrawn. |
| FIRST CONFEDERATE | Just doom for him, who governs without law!<br>Is it known on whom the sov’reignty will fall? |
| SECOND CONFEDERATE | Nothing is yet decided: but report<br>Points to Lord Casimir. The grateful [[Memory|memory]]<br>Of his renownéd father —— |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter SAROLTA. |
| *(didascalia)* | Hail to Sarolta! |
| SAROLTA | Confederate friends! I bring to you a joy<br>Worthy your noble cause! Kiuprili lives,<br>And from his obscure [[Exile|exile]], hath returned<br>To bless our country. More and greater tidings<br>Might I disclose; but that a woman’s voice<br>Would mar the wondrous tale. Wait we for him,<br>The partner of the glory — Raab Kiuprili;<br>For he [[Alone|alone]] is worthy to announce it.<br>[Shouts of ‘Kiuprili, Kiuprili,’ and ‘The [[Tyrant]]’s fallen,’ without. Enter KIUPRILI, CASIMIR, RUDOLPH, BATHORY, and Attendants. |
| RAAB KIUPRILI | Spare yet your joy, my friends! A higher waits you:<br>Behold, your [[Queen]]!<br>[Enter ZAPOLYA and ANDREAS royally attired, with GLYCINE. |
| CONFEDERATE | Comes she from heaven to bless us? |
| OTHER CONFEDERATES | It is! it is! |
| ZAPOLYA | Heaven’s work of grace is full!<br>Kiuprili, thou art safe! |
| RAAB KIUPRILI | Royal Zapolya!<br>To the heavenly powers, pay we our duty first;<br>Who not [[Alone|alone]] preserved thee, but for thee<br>And for our country, the one precious branch<br>Of Andreas’ royal [[house]]. O countrymen,<br>Behold your [[King]]! And thank our country’s genius,<br>That the same means which have preserved our sovereign,<br>Have likewise reared him worthier of the throne<br>By virtue than by birth. The undoubted proofs<br>Pledged by his royal mother, and this old man,<br>(Whose name henceforth be dear to all Illyrians)<br>We haste to lay before the assembled council. |
| ALL | Hail, Andreas! Hail, Illyria’s rightful [[King|king]]! |
| ANDREAS | Supported thus, O friends! ‘twere cowardice<br>Unworthy of a royal birth, to shrink<br>From the appointed charge. Yet, while we wait<br>The awful sanction of convened Illyria,<br>In this brief while, O let me feel myself<br>The [[Child|child]], the friend, the debtor! — Heroic mother! —<br>But what can breath add to that sacred name?<br>Kiuprili! gift of [[Providence]], to teach us<br>That loyalty is but the public form<br>Of the sublimest [[Friendship|friendship]], let my youth<br>Climb round thee, as the vine around its elm:<br>Thou my support and I thy faithful fruitage.<br>My heart is full, and these poor words express not,<br>They are but an art to check its over-swelling.<br>Bathory! shrink not from my filial arms!<br>Now, and from henceforth thou shalt not forbid me<br>To call thee father! And dare I forget<br>The powerful intercession of thy virtue,<br>Lady Sarolta? Still acknowledge me<br>Thy faithful soldier! — But what invocation<br>Shall my full [[Soul|soul]] address to thee, Glycine?<br>Thou [[Sword|sword]] that leap’dst forth from a bed of roses:<br>Thou falcon-hearted dove? |
| ZAPOLYA | Hear that from me, son!<br>For ere she lived, her father saved thy life,<br>Thine, and thy fugitive mother’s! |
| CASIMIR | Chef Ragozzi!<br>O shame upon my head! I would have given her<br>To a base slave! |
| ZAPOLYA | Heaven overruled thy purpose,<br>And sent an angel to thy [[house]] to guard her!<br>Thou precious bark! freighted with all our treasures!<br>The sports of tempests, and yet ne’er the victim,<br>How many may claim salvage in thee! Take her, son!<br>A [[Queen|queen]] that brings with her a richer dowry<br>Than [[Orient|orient]] kings can give! |
| SAROLTA | A banquet waits! —<br>On this auspicious day, for some few hours<br>I claim to be your hostess. Scenes so awful<br>With flashing light, force wisdom on us all!<br>E’en women at the distaff hence may see,<br>That bad men may [[Rebel|rebel]], but ne’er be free;<br>May whisper, when the waves of faction foam,<br>None [[Love|love]] their country, but who [[Love|love]] their [[Home|home]]:<br>For [[Freedom|freedom]] can with those [[Alone|alone]] abide,<br>Who wear the golden chain, with honest [[Pride|pride]],<br>Of [[Love|love]] and duty, at their own [[Fire|fire]]-side:<br>While mad [[Ambition|ambition]] ever doth caress<br>Its own sure fate, in its own restlessness! |
