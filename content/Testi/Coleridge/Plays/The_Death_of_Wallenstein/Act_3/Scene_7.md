| Chi parla | Battuta |
|---|---|
| *(didascalia)* | To these enter ILLO and TERTSKY. |
| TERTSKY | Our luck is on the turn. To-morrow come<br>The Swedes — twelve thousand gallant warriors, Illo!<br>Then straightways for Vienna. Cheerily, friend!<br>What! meet such news with such a moody face? |
| ILLO | It lies with us at present to prescribe<br>Laws, and take vengeance on those worthless traitors,<br>Those skulking cowards that deserted us;<br>One has already done his bitter penance<br>The Piccolomini, be his the fate<br>Of all who wish us evil! This flies sure<br>To the old man's heart; he has his whole life long<br>Fretted and toiled to raise his ancient [[house]]<br>From a Count's title to the name of Prince;<br>And now must seek a [[Grave\|grave]] for his only son. |
| BUTLER | 'Twas pity though! A youth of such heroic<br>And gentle temperament! The Duke himself,<br>'Twas easily seen, how near it went to his heart. |
| ILLO | Hark'e, old friend! That is the very point<br>That never pleased me in our General —<br>He ever gave the preference to the Italians.<br>Yea, at this very moment, by my [[Soul\|soul]]!<br>He'd gladly see us all dead ten times over,<br>Could he thereby recall his friend to life. |
| TERTSKY | Hush, hush! Let the dead rest! This evening's business<br>Is, who can fairly drink the other down —<br>Your regiment, Illo! gives the entertainment.<br>Come! we will keep a merry carnival —<br>The [[Night\|night]] for once be day, and mid full glasses<br>Will we expect the Swedish Avantgarde. |
| ILLO | Yes, let us be of good cheer for to-day,<br>For there's hot work before us, friends! This [[Sword\|sword]]<br>Shall have no rest, till it be bathed to the hilt<br>In Austrian [[Blood\|blood]]. |
| GORDON | Shame, shame! what talk is this,<br>My Lord Field Marshal? Wherefore foam you so<br>Against your Emperor? |
| BUTLER | [[Hope]] not too much<br>From this first victory. Bethink you, sirs!<br>How rapidly the [[Wheel\|wheel]] of Fortune turns;<br>The Emperor still is formidably strong. |
| ILLO | The Emperor has soldiers, no commander,<br>For this [[King]] [[Ferdinand]] of Hungary<br>Is but a tyro. Galas? He's no luck,<br>And was of old the ruiner of armies.<br>And then this viper, this Octavio,<br>Is excellent at stabbing in the back,<br>But ne'er meets Friedland in the open field. |
| TERTSKY | Trust me, my friends, it cannot but succeed;<br>Fortune, we know, can ne'er forsake the Duke!<br>And only under Wallenstein can Austria<br>Be conqueror. |
| ILLO | The Duke will soon assemble<br>A mighty army, all come crowding, streaming<br>To banners dedicate by destiny<br>To fame and prosperous fortune. I behold<br>Old times come back again, he will become<br>Once more the mighty Lord which he has been.<br>How will the fools, who've now deserted him,<br>Look then? I can't but laugh to think of them,<br>For lands will he present to all his friends,<br>And like a [[King]] and Emperor reward<br>True services; but we've the nearest claims. |
| *(didascalia)* | To GORDON. |
| ILLO | You will not be forgotten, Governor!<br>He'll take you from this nest and bid you shine<br>In higher station: your fidelity<br>[[Well]] merits it. |
| GORDON | I am content already,<br>And wish to climb no higher; where great height is<br>The fall must needs be great. 'Great height, great depth.' |
| ILLO | Here you have no more business for to-morrow;<br>The Swedes will take possession of the citadel.<br>Come, Tertsky, it is supper-[[Time\|time]]. What think you?<br>Say, shall we have the State illuminated<br>In [[Honour\|honour]] of the Swede? And who refuses<br>To do it is a Spaniard and a traitor. |
| TERTSKY | Nay! Nay! not that, it will not please the Duke — |
| ILLO | What! we are masters here; no [[Soul\|soul]] shall dare<br>Avow himself imperial where we've rule.<br>Gordon! Good [[Night\|night]], and for the last [[Time\|time]], take<br>A fair leave of the place. Send out patroles<br>To make secure, the watch-word may be altered<br>At the stroke of ten; deliver in the keys<br>To the Duke himself, and then you're quit for ever<br>Your wardship of the gates, for on to-morrow<br>The Swedes will take possession of the citadel. |
| TERTSKY | You come though to the [[Castle\|castle]]. |
| BUTLER | At the right [[Time\|time]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt TERTSKY and ILLO. |
