|---|---|
| *(didascalia)* | GORDON and BUTLER. |
| GORDON | (looking after them). Unhappy men! How free from all foreboding!<br>They rush into the outspread net of murder,<br>In the blind drunkenness of victory;<br>I have no pity for their fate. This Illo,<br>This overflowing and [[Fool\|fool]]-hardy [[Villain\|villain]]<br>That would fain bathe himself in his Emperor's [[Blood\|blood]]. |
| BUTLER | Do as he ordered you. Send round patroles.<br>Take measures for the citadel's security;<br>When they are within I close the [[Castle\|castle]] gate<br>That nothing may transpire. |
| GORDON | Oh! haste not so!<br>Nay, stop; first tell me —— |
| BUTLER | You have heard already,<br>To-morrow to the Swedes belongs. This [[Night\|night]]<br>[[Alone]] is ours. They make good expedition.<br>But we will make still greater. Fare you [[Well\|well]]. |
| GORDON | Ah! your looks tell me nothing good. Nay, Butler,<br>I pray you, promise me! |
| BUTLER | The sun has set;<br>A fateful evening doth descend upon us,<br>And brings on their long [[Night\|night]]! Their evil [[Stars\|stars]]<br>Deliver them unarmed into our hands.<br>And from their drunken dream of golden fortunes<br>The dagger at their heart shall rouse them. [[Well]],<br>The Duke was ever a great calculator;<br>His fellow-men were figures on his chess-board,<br>To move and station, as his game required.<br>Other men's [[Honour\|honour]], dignity, good name,<br>Did he shift like pawns, and made no [[Conscience\|conscience]] of it:<br>Still calculating, calculating still;<br>And yet at last his calculation proves<br>Erroneous; the whole game is lost; and lo!<br>His own life will be found among the forfeits. |
| GORDON | O think not of his errors now; remember<br>His [[Greatness\|greatness]], his munificence, think on all<br>The lovely features of his character,<br>On all the noble exploits of his life,<br>And let them, like an angel's arm, unseen<br>Arrest the lifted [[Sword\|sword]]. |
| BUTLER | It is too late.<br>I suffer not myself to feel compassion,<br>Dark thoughts and bloody are my duty now: |
| *(didascalia)* | [Grasping GORDON'S hand. |
| BUTLER | Gordon! 'Tis not my hatred (I pretend not<br>To [[Love\|love]] the Duke, and have no cause to [[Love\|love]] him)<br>Yet 'tis not now my hatred that impels me<br>To be his murderer. 'Tis his evil fate.<br>Hostile concurrences of many events<br>Control and subjugate me to the office.<br>In vain the human being meditates<br>Free action. He is but the wire-worked puppet<br>Of the blind power, which out of his own choice<br>Creates for him a dread necessity.<br>What too would it avail him, if there were<br>A something pleading for him in my heart —<br>Still I must kill him. |
| GORDON | If your heart speak to you,<br>Follow its impulse. 'Tis the voice of God.<br>Think you your fortunes will grow prosperous<br>Bedewed with [[Blood\|blood]] — his [[Blood\|blood]]? Believe it not! |
| BUTLER | You know not. Ask not! Wherefore should it happen,<br>That the Swedes gained the victory, and hasten<br>With such forced marches hitherward? Fain would I<br>Have given him to the Emperor's [[Mercy\|mercy]]. — Gordon!<br>I do not wish his [[Blood\|blood]] — But I must ransom<br>The [[Honour\|honour]] of my word — it lies in pledge —<br>And he must die, or —— |
| *(didascalia)* | [Passionately grasping GORDON'S hand. |
| BUTLER | Listen then, and know!<br>I am dishonoured if the Duke escape us. |
| GORDON | O! to save such a man —— |
| BUTLER | What! |
| GORDON | It is worth<br>A sacrifice. — Come, friend! Be noble-minded!<br>Our own heart, and not other men's opinions,<br>Forms our true [[Honour\|honour]]. |
| BUTLER | He is a great Lord,<br>This Duke — and I am but of mean importance.<br>This is what you would say? Wherein concerns it<br>The world at large, you mean to hint to me,<br>Whether the man of low extraction keeps<br>Or blemishes his [[Honour\|honour]] —<br>So that the man of princely rank be saved.<br>We all do stamp our value on ourselves.<br>The price we challenge for ourselves is given us.<br>There does not live on earth the man so stationed,<br>That I despise myself compared with him.<br>Man is made great or little by his own will;<br>Because I am true to mine, therefore he dies. |
| GORDON | I am endeavouring to move a rock.<br>Thou hadst a mother, yet no human feelings.<br>I cannot hinder you, but may some God<br>Rescue him from you! |
| *(didascalia)* | [Exit GORDON. |
