| Chi parla | Battuta |
|---|---|
| *(didascalia)* | GORDON, BUTLER (at first behind the scenes). |
| BUTLER | Here stand in [[Silence\|silence]]<br>till I give the signal. |
| GORDON | 'Tis he, he has already brought the murderers. |
| BUTLER | The lights are out. All lies in profound [[Sleep\|sleep]]. |
| GORDON | What shall I do, shall I attempt to save him?<br>Shall I call up the [[house]]? Alarm the guards? |
| BUTLER | A light gleams hither<br>from the corridor.<br>It leads directly to the Duke's bedchamber. |
| GORDON | But then I break my oath to the Emperor;<br>If he escape and strengthen the enemy,<br>Do I not hereby call down on my head<br>All the dread consequences? |
| BUTLER | Hark! Who speaks there? |
| GORDON | 'Tis better, I resign it to the hands<br>Of [[Providence\|providence]]. For what am I, that I<br>Should take upon myself so great a deed?<br>I have not murdered him, if he be murdered:<br>But all his rescue were my act and deed;<br>Mine — and whatever be the consequences,<br>I must sustain them. |
| BUTLER | I should know that voice. |
| GORDON | Butler! |
| BUTLER | 'Tis Gordon. What do you want here?<br>Was it so late then, when the Duke dismissed you? |
| GORDON | Your hand bound up and in a scarf? |
| BUTLER | 'Tis wounded.<br>That Illo fought as he was frantic, till<br>At last we threw him on the ground. |
| GORDON | Both dead? |
| BUTLER | Is he in bed? |
| GORDON | Ah, Butler! |
| BUTLER | Is he? speak. |
| GORDON | He shall not perish! Not through you! The Heaven<br>Refuses your arm. See—'tis wounded! — |
| BUTLER | There is no need of my arm. |
| GORDON | The most guilty<br>Have perished, and enough is given to justice. |
| *(didascalia)* | The Groom of the Chamber advances from the gallery with his finger on his mouth, commanding [[Silence\|silence]]. |
| GORDON | He sleeps! O murder not the holy [[Sleep\|sleep]]! |
| BUTLER | No! he shall die awake. *(Is going.)* |
| GORDON | His heart still cleaves<br>To earthly things: he's not prepared to step<br>Into the presence of his God! |
| BUTLER | God's merciful! |
| GORDON | Grant him but this [[Night\|night]]'s respite. |
| BUTLER | The next moment<br>May ruin all. |
| GORDON | One hour! —— |
| BUTLER | Unhold me! What<br>Can that short respite profit him? |
| GORDON | O — [[Time]]<br>Works miracles. In one hour many thousands<br>Of grains of sand run out; and quick as they,<br>Thought follows thought within the human [[Soul\|soul]].<br>Only one hour! Your heart may change its purpose,<br>His heart may change its purpose — some new tidings<br>May come; some fortunate event, decisive,<br>May fall from Heaven and rescue him. O what<br>May not one hour achieve! |
| BUTLER | You but remind me,<br>How precious every minute is! |
| *(didascalia)* | (He stamps on the floor.) |
