|---|---|
| *(didascalia)* | To these enter MACDONALD and DEVEREUX, with the Halberdiers. |
| GORDON | No, [[Monster\|monster]]!<br>First over my dead [[Body\|body]] thou shalt tread.<br>I will not live to see the accursed deed! |
| BUTLER | Weak-hearted dotard! |
| *(didascalia)* | [Trumpets are heard in the distance. |
| DEVEREUX AND MACDONALD | Hark! The Swedish trumpets!<br>The Swedes before the ramparts! Let us hasten! |
| GORDON | O, God of [[Mercy]]! |
| *(didascalia)* | [rushes out] |
| BUTLER | Governor, to your post! |
| *(didascalia)* | [calling after him] |
| GROOM OF THE CHAMBER | Who dares make larum here? Hush!<br>The Duke sleeps. |
| *(didascalia)* | [hurries in] |
| DEVEREUX | Friend, it is [[Time\|time]] now to make larum. |
| GROOM OF THE CHAMBER | Help!<br>Murder! |
| BUTLER | Down with him! |
| *(didascalia)* | Groom of the Chamber run through the [[Body\|body]] by Devereux, falls at the entrance of the gallery. |
| GROOM OF THE CHAMBER | Jesus [[Maria]]! |
| BUTLER | Burst the doors open! |
| *(didascalia)* | They rush over the [[Body\|body]] into the gallery — two doors are heard to crash one after the other — Voices deadened by the distance — Clash of arms — then all at once a profound [[Silence\|silence]]. |
