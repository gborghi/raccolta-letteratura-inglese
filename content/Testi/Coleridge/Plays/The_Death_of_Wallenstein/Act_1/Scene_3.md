| *(didascalia)* | To them enter the DUCHESS. |
| DUCHESS *(to the Countess)* | Who was here, sister? I heard some one talking,<br>And passionately too. |
| COUNTESS | Nay! There was no one. |
| DUCHESS | I am grown so timorous, every trifling noise<br>Scatters my spirits, and announces to me<br>The footstep of some messenger of evil.<br>And can you tell me, sister, what the event is?<br>Will he agree to do the Emperor's pleasure,<br>And send the [[Horse\|horse]]-regiments to the Cardinal?<br>Tell me, has he dismissed Von Questenberg<br>With a favourable answer? |
| COUNTESS | No, he has not. |
| DUCHESS | Alas! then all is lost! I see it coming,<br>The worst that can come! Yes, they will depose him;<br>The accurséd business of the Regenspurg diet<br>Will all be acted o'er again! |
| COUNTESS | No! never!<br>Make your heart easy, sister, as to that. |
| *(didascalia)* | [THEKLA throws herself upon her mother, and enfolds her<br>in her arms, weeping. |
| DUCHESS | Yes, my poor [[Child\|child]]!<br>Thou too hast lost a most affectionate godmother<br>In the Empress. O that stern unbending man!<br>In this unhappy [[Marriage\|marriage]] what have I<br>Not suffered, not endured. For ev'n as if<br>I had been linked on to some [[Wheel\|wheel]] of [[Fire\|fire]]<br>That restless, ceaseless, whirls impetuous onward,<br>I have passed a life of frights and horrors with him,<br>And ever to the brink of some abyss<br>With dizzy headlong violence he whirls me.<br>Nay, do not weep, my [[Child\|child]]! Let not my sufferings<br>Presignify unhappiness to thee,<br>Nor blacken with their shade the fate that waits thee.<br>There lives no second Friedland: thou, my [[Child\|child]],<br>Hast not to [[Fear\|fear]] thy mother's destiny. |
| THEKLA | O let us supplicate him, dearest mother!<br>Quick! quick! here's no abiding-place for us.<br>Here every coming hour broods into life<br>Some new affrightful [[Monster\|monster]]. |
| DUCHESS | Thou wilt share<br>An easier, calmer lot, my [[Child\|child]]! We too,<br>I and thy father, witnessed happy days.<br>Still think I with delight of those first years,<br>When he was making progress with glad effort,<br>When his [[Ambition\|ambition]] was a genial [[Fire\|fire]],<br>Not that consuming flame which now it is.<br>The Emperor loved him, trusted him: and all<br>He undertook could not but be successful.<br>But since that ill-starred day at Regenspurg,<br>Which plunged him headlong from his dignity,<br>A gloomy uncompanionable spirit,<br>Unsteady and suspicious, has possessed him.<br>His quiet mind forsook him, and no longer<br>Did he yield up himself in joy and [[Faith\|faith]]<br>To his old luck, and individual power;<br>But thenceforth turned his heart and best affections<br>All to those cloudy sciences, which never<br>Have yet made happy him who followed them. |
| COUNTESS | You see it, sister! as your eyes permit you.<br>But surely this is not the conversation<br>To pass the [[Time\|time]] in which we are waiting for him.<br>You know he will be soon here. Would you have him<br>Find her in this condition? |
| DUCHESS | Come, my [[Child\|child]]!<br>Come, wipe away thy [[Tears\|tears]], and shew thy father<br>A cheerful countenance. See, the tie-knot here<br>Is off — this hair must not hang so dishevelled.<br>Come, dearest! dry thy [[Tears\|tears]] up. They deform<br>Thy gentle eye — [[Well\|well]] now — what was I saying?<br>Yes, in good [[Truth\|truth]], this Piccolomini<br>Is a most noble and deserving gentleman. |
| COUNTESS | That is he, sister! |
| THEKLA *(to the Countess)* | Aunt, you will excuse me? |
| *(didascalia)* | [Is going. |
| COUNTESS | But whither? See, your father comes. |
| THEKLA | I cannot see him now. |
| COUNTESS | Nay, but bethink you. |
| THEKLA | Believe me, I cannot sustain his presence. |
| COUNTESS | But he will miss you, will ask after you. |
| DUCHESS | What now? Why is she going? |
| COUNTESS | She's not [[Well\|well]]. |
| DUCHESS | What ails then my beloved [[Child\|child]]? |
