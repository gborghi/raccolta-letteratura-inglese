| *(didascalia)* | WALLENSTEIN, COUNT TERTSKY. |
| WALLENSTEIN | Who has been taken? — Who is given up? |
| TERTSKY | The man who knows our [[Secrets\|secrets]], who knows every<br>Negotiation with the Swede and Saxon,<br>Through whose hands all and every thing has passed — |
| WALLENSTEIN | Nay, not Sesina? — Say, No! I entreat<br>thee. |
| TERTSKY | All on his [[Road\|road]] for Regenspurg to the Swede<br>He was plunged down upon by Galas' agent,<br>Who had been long in ambush, lurking for him.<br>There must have been found on him my whole packet<br>To Thur, to Kinsky, to Oxenstirn, to Arnheim:<br>All this is in their hands; they have now an insight<br>Into the whole — our measures, and our motives. |
