| Chi parla | Battuta |
|---|---|
| *(didascalia)* | WALLENSTEIN, TERTSKY, and ILLO (re-enter). |
| ILLO | Is't all right? |
| TERTSKY | Are you compromised? |
| ILLO | This Swede<br>Went smiling from you. Yes! you're compromised. |
| WALLENSTEIN | As yet is nothing settled: and ([[Well\|well]] weighed)<br>I feel myself inclined to leave it so. |
| TERTSKY | How? What is that? |
| WALLENSTEIN | Come on me what will come,<br>The doing evil to avoid an evil<br>Cannot be good! |
| TERTSKY | Nay, but bethink you, Duke? |
| WALLENSTEIN | To live upon the [[Mercy\|mercy]] of these Swedes!<br>Of these proud-hearted Swedes! I could not bear it. |
| ILLO | Goest thou as fugitive, as mendicant?<br>Bringest thou not more to them than thou receivest? |
