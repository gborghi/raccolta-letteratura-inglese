| *(didascalia)* | To these enter the COUNTESS TERTSKY. |
| WALLENSTEIN | Who sent for you? There is no business here<br>For women. |
| COUNTESS | I am come to bid you joy. |
| WALLENSTEIN | Use thy authority, Tertsky, bid her go. |
| COUNTESS | Come I perhaps too early? I [[Hope\|hope]] not. |
| WALLENSTEIN | Set not this tongue upon me, I entreat you.<br>You know it is the weapon that destroys me.<br>I am routed, if a woman but attack me.<br>I cannot traffic in the trade of words<br>With that unreasoning sex. |
| COUNTESS | I had already<br>Given the Bohemians a [[King\|king]]. |
| WALLENSTEIN | They have one,<br>In consequence, no [[Doubt\|doubt]]. |
| COUNTESS | Ha! what new scruple? |
| TERTSKY | The Duke will not. |
| COUNTESS | He will not what he must! |
| ILLO | It lies with you now. Try. For I am silenced,<br>When folks begin to talk to me of [[Conscience\|conscience]],<br>And of fidelity. |
| COUNTESS | How? then, when all<br>Lay in the far-off distance, when the [[Road\|road]]<br>Stretched out before thine eyes interminably,<br>Then hadst thou [[Courage\|courage]] and resolve; and now,<br>Now that the dream is being realized,<br>The purpose ripe, the issue ascertained,<br>Dost thou begin to play the dastard now?<br>Planned merely, 'tis a common felony;<br>Accomplished, an immortal undertaking:<br>And with [[success]] comes pardon hand in hand;<br>For all event is God's arbitrement. |
| *(didascalia)* | Servant (enters). |
| SERVANT | The Colonel Piccolomini. |
| COUNTESS | — Must wait. |
| WALLENSTEIN | I cannot see him now. Another [[Time\|time]]. |
| SERVANT | But for two minutes he entreats an audience.<br>Of the most urgent [[Nature\|nature]] is his business. |
| WALLENSTEIN | Who knows what he may bring us? I will hear him. |
| COUNTESS | Urgent for him, no [[Doubt\|doubt]]; but thou mayest wait. |
| WALLENSTEIN | What is it? |
| COUNTESS | Thou shalt be informed hereafter.<br>First let the Swede and thee be compromised. |
| *(didascalia)* | [Exit Servant. |
| WALLENSTEIN | If there were yet a choice! if yet some milder<br>Way of escape were possible — I still<br>Will choose it, and avoid the last extreme. |
| COUNTESS | Desir'st thou nothing further? Such a way<br>Lies still before thee. Send this Wrangel off.<br>Forget thou thy old hopes, cast far away<br>All thy past life; determine to commence<br>A new one. Virtue hath her heroes too,<br>As [[Well\|well]] as Fame and Fortune. — To Vienna —<br>Hence — to the Emperor — kneel before the throne;<br>Take a full coffer with thee — say aloud,<br>Thou did'st but wish to prove thy fealty;<br>Thy whole intention but to dupe the Swede. |
| ILLO | For that too 'tis too late. They know too much.<br>He would but bear his own head to the block. |
| COUNTESS | I [[Fear\|fear]] not that. They have not evidence<br>To attaint him legally, and they avoid<br>The avowal of an arbitrary power.<br>They'll let the Duke resign without disturbance.<br>I see how all will end. The [[King]] of Hungary<br>Makes his appearance, and 'twill of itself<br>Be understood, that then the Duke retires.<br>There will not want a formal declaration.<br>The [[Young King\|young King]] will administer the oath<br>To the whole army; and so all returns<br>To the old position. On some morrow morning<br>The Duke departs; and now 'tis stir and bustle<br>Within his castles. He will hunt, and build,<br>Superintend his horses' pedigrees;<br>Creates himself a [[Court\|court]], gives golden keys,<br>And introduceth strictest ceremony<br>In fine proportions, and nice etiquette;<br>Keeps open table with high cheer; in brief,<br>Commenceth mighty [[King]] — in miniature.<br>And while he prudently demeans himself,<br>And gives himself no actual importance,<br>He will be let appear whate'er he likes;<br>And who dares [[Doubt\|doubt]], that Friedland will appear<br>A mighty Prince to his last dying hour?<br>[[Well]] now, what then? Duke Friedland is as others,<br>A [[Fire\|fire]]-new Noble, whom the war hath raised<br>To price and currency, a Jonah's Gourd,<br>An over-[[Night\|night]] creation of [[Court\|court]]-favour,<br>Which with an undistinguishable ease<br>Makes Baron or makes Prince. |
| WALLENSTEIN | Take her away.<br>Let in the young Count Piccolomini. |
| COUNTESS | Art thou in earnest? I entreat thee! Canst thou<br>Consent to bear thyself to thy own [[Grave\|grave]],<br>So ignominiously to be dried up?<br>Thy life, that arrogated such a height<br>To end in such a nothing! To be nothing,<br>When one was always nothing, is an evil<br>That asks no stretch of patience, a light evil,<br>But to become a nothing, having been —— |
| WALLENSTEIN | Shew me a way out of this stifling crowd,<br>Ye Powers of Aidance! Shew me such a way<br>As I am capable of going. — I<br>Am no tongue-[[Hero\|hero]], no fine virtue-prattler;<br>I cannot warm by thinking; cannot say<br>To the good luck that turns her back upon me,<br>Magnanimously: 'Go! I need thee not.'<br>Cease I to work, I am annihilated,<br>Dangers nor sacrifices will I shun,<br>If so I may avoid the last extreme;<br>But ere I sink down into nothingness,<br>Leave off so little, who began so great,<br>Ere that the world confuses me with those<br>Poor wretches, whom a day creates and crumbles,<br>This age and after-ages speak my name<br>With hate and dread; and Friedland be redemption<br>For each accurséd deed! |
| COUNTESS | What is there here, then,<br>So against [[Nature\|nature]]? Help me to perceive it!<br>O let not Superstition's nightly goblins<br>Subdue thy clear bright spirit! Art thou bid<br>To murder? — with abhorr'd accurséd poniard,<br>To violate the breasts that nourished thee?<br>That were against our [[Nature\|nature]], that might aptly<br>Make thy flesh shudder, and thy whole heart sicken.<br>Yet not a few, and for a meaner object,<br>Have ventured even this, ay, and performed it.<br>What is there in thy case so black and monstrous?<br>Thou art accused of treason — whether with<br>Or without justice is not now the question —<br>Thou art lost if thou dost not avail thee quickly<br>Of the power which thou possessest — Friedland! Duke!<br>Tell me, where lives that thing so meek and tame,<br>That doth not all his living faculties<br>Put forth in preservation of his life?<br>What deed so daring, which necessity<br>And desperation will not sanctify? |
| WALLENSTEIN | Once was this [[Ferdinand]] so gracious to me:<br>He loved me; he esteemed me; I was placed<br>The nearest to his heart. Full many a [[Time\|time]]<br>We like familiar friends, both at one table,<br>Have banquetted together. He and I —<br>And the young kings themselves held me the bason<br>Wherewith to wash me — and is't come to this? |
| COUNTESS | So faithfully preserv'st thou each small favour,<br>And hast no [[Memory\|memory]] for contumelies?<br>Must I remind thee, how at Regenspurg<br>This man repaid thy faithful services?<br>All ranks and all conditions in the Empire<br>Thou hadst wronged, to make him great, — hadst loaded on thee,<br>On thee, the hate, the curse of the whole world.<br>No friend existed for thee in all [[Germany]],<br>And why? because thou hadst existed only<br>For the Emperor. To the Emperor [[Alone\|alone]]<br>Clung Friedland in that [[Storm\|storm]] which gathered round him<br>At Regenspurg in the Diet — and he dropped thee!<br>He let thee fall! He let thee fall a victim<br>To the Bavarian, to that insolent!<br>Deposed, stript bare of all thy dignity<br>And power, amid the taunting of thy foes,<br>Thou wert let drop into obscurity. —<br>Say not, the restoration of thy [[Honour\|honour]]<br>Hath made atonement for that first injustice.<br>No honest good-will was it that replaced thee,<br>The law of hard necessity replaced thee,<br>Which they had fain opposed, but that they could not. |
| WALLENSTEIN | Not to their good wishes, that is certain,<br>Nor yet to his affection I'm indebted<br>For this high office; and if I abuse it,<br>I shall therein abuse no confidence. |
| COUNTESS | Affection! confidence! — They needed thee.<br>Necessity, impetuous remonstrant!<br>Who not with empty names, or shews of proxy,<br>Is served, who'll have the thing and not the symbol,<br>Ever seeks out the greatest and the best,<br>And at the rudder places him, e'en though<br>She had been forced to take him from the rabble —<br>She, this Necessity, it was that placed thee<br>In this high office, it was she that gave thee<br>Thy letters patent of inauguration.<br>For, to the uttermost moment that they can.<br>This race still help themselves at cheapest rate<br>With slavish souls, with puppets! At the approach<br>Of extreme peril, when a hollow image<br>Is found a hollow image and no more,<br>Then falls the power into the mighty hands<br>Of [[Nature]], of the spirit giant-born,<br>Who listens only to himself, knows nothing<br>Of stipulations, duties, reverences<br>And, like the emancipated force of [[Fire\|fire]],<br>Unmastered scorches, ere it reaches them,<br>Their fine-spun webs, their artificial policy. |
| WALLENSTEIN | 'Tis true! they saw me always as I am —<br>Always! I did not cheat them in the bargain.<br>I never held it worth my pains to hide<br>The bold all-grasping habit of my [[Soul\|soul]]. |
| COUNTESS | Nay rather — thou hast ever shewn thyself<br>A formidable man, without restraint;<br>Hast exercised the full prerogatives<br>Of thy impetuous [[Nature\|nature]], which had been<br>Once granted to thee. Therefore, Duke, not thou,<br>Who hast still remained consistent with thyself,<br>But they are in the wrong, who fearing thee,<br>Entrusted such a power in hands they feared.<br>For, by the laws of Spirit, in the right<br>Is every individual character<br>That acts in strict consistence with itself.<br>[[Self]]-contradiction is the only wrong.<br>Wert thou another being, then, when thou<br>Eight years ago pursuedst thy march with [[Fire\|fire]]<br>And [[Sword\|sword]], and desolation, through the Circles<br>Of [[Germany]], the universal scourge,<br>Didst mock all ordinances of the empire,<br>The fearful rights of strength [[Alone\|alone]] exertedst,<br>Trampledst to earth each rank, each magistracy,<br>All to extend thy Sultan's domination?<br>Then was the [[Time\|time]] to break thee in, to curb<br>Thy haughty will, to teach thee ordinance.<br>But no! the Emperor felt no touch of [[Conscience\|conscience]],<br>What served him pleased him, and without a murmur<br>He stamped his broad seal on these lawless deeds.<br>What at that [[Time\|time]] was right, because thou didst it<br>For him, to-day is all at once become<br>Opprobrious, foul, because it is directed<br>Against him. — O most flimsy superstition! |
| WALLENSTEIN | I never saw it in this light before.<br>'Tis even so. The Emperor perpetrated<br>Deeds through my arm, deeds most unorderly.<br>And even this prince's mantle, which I wear,<br>I owe to what were services to him,<br>But most high misdemeanours 'gainst the empire. |
| COUNTESS | Then betwixt thee and him (confess it, Friedland!)<br>The point can be no more of right and duty,<br>Only of power and opportunity.<br>That opportunity, lo! it comes yonder,<br>Approaching with swift steeds; then with a swing<br>Throw thyself up into the chariot-seat,<br>Seize with firm hand the reins, ere thy opponent<br>Anticipate thee, and himself make conquest<br>Of the now empty seat. The moment comes —<br>It is already here, when thou must write<br>The absolute total of thy life's vast sum.<br>The constellations stand victorious o'er thee,<br>The planets shoot good fortune in fair junctions,<br>And tell thee, 'Now's the [[Time\|time]]!' The starry courses<br>Hast thou thy life long measured to no purpose?<br>The quadrant and the circle, were they playthings? |
| *(didascalia)* | [Pointing to the different objects in the room. |
| COUNTESS | The zodiacs, the [[rolling]] orbs of heaven,<br>Hast pictured on these walls, and all around thee<br>In dumb, foreboding symbols hast thou placed<br>These seven presiding Lords of Destiny —<br>For toys? Is all this preparation nothing?<br>Is there no marrow in this hollow art,<br>That even to thyself it doth avail<br>Nothing, and has no influence over thee<br>In the great moment of decision? —— |
| WALLENSTEIN | Send Wrangel to me — I will instantly<br>Dispatch three couriers —— |
| ILLO | God in heaven be praised! |
| *(didascalia)* | (hurrying out) |
| WALLENSTEIN | It is his evil genius and mine.<br>Our evil genius! It chastises him<br>Through me, the instrument of his [[Ambition\|ambition]];<br>And I expect no less, than that [[Revenge]]<br>E'en now is whetting for my breast the poniard.<br>Who sows the serpent's teeth, let him not [[Hope\|hope]]<br>To reap a joyous harvest. Every crime<br>Has, in the moment of its perpetration,<br>Its own avenging angel — dark misgiving,<br>An ominous sinking at the inmost heart.<br>He can no longer trust me — Then no longer<br>Can I retreat — so come that which must come. —<br>Still destiny preserves its due relations,<br>The heart within us is its absolute<br>Vicegerent. |
| *(didascalia)* | [To TERTSKY. |
| WALLENSTEIN | Go, conduct you Gustave Wrangel<br>To my state-cabinet. Myself will speak to<br>The couriers. — And dispatch immediately<br>A servant for Octavio Piccolomini. |
| *(didascalia)* | [To the COUNTESS. |
| WALLENSTEIN | No exultation — woman, triumph not!<br>For jealous are the Powers of Destiny.<br>Joy premature, and shouts ere victory,<br>Incroach upon their rights and privileges.<br>We sow the seed, and they the growth determine. |
| *(didascalia)* | [While he is making his exit the curtain drops. |
