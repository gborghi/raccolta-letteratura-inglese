| *(didascalia)* | OCTAVIO and MAX PICCOLOMINI. |
| OCTAVIO | [[Well]] — and what now, son? All will soon be clear;<br>For all, I'm certain, went through that Sesina. |
| MAX | I will procure me light a shorter way.<br>Farewell. |
| OCTAVIO | Where now? — Remain here. |
| MAX | To the Duke. |
| OCTAVIO | What —— |
| MAX | If thou hast believed that I shall act<br>A part in this thy play ——<br>Thou hast miscalculated on me grievously.<br>My way must be straight on. True with the tongue,<br>False with the heart — I may not, cannot be:<br>Nor can I suffer that a man should trust me —<br>As his friend trust me — and then lull my [[Conscience\|conscience]]<br>With such low pleas as these:—'I ask'd him not —<br>He did it all at his own hazard — and<br>My mouth has never lied to him.' — No, no!<br>What a friend takes me for, that I must be.<br>— I'll to the Duke; ere yet this day is ended<br>Will I demand of him that he do save<br>His good name from the world, and with one stride<br>Break through and rend this fine-spun web of yours.<br>He can, he will! — I still am his believer.<br>Yet I'll not pledge myself, but that those letters<br>May furnish you, perchance, with proofs against him.<br>How far may not this Tertsky have proceeded —<br>What may not he himself too have permitted<br>Himself to do, to snare the enemy,<br>The laws of war excusing? Nothing, save<br>His own mouth shall convict him — nothing less!<br>And face to face will I go question him. |
| OCTAVIO | Thou wilt? |
| MAX | I will, as sure as this heart beats. |
| OCTAVIO | I have, indeed, miscalculated on thee.<br>I calculated on a prudent son,<br>Who would have blest the hand beneficent<br>That plucked him back from the abyss — and lo!<br>A fascinated being I discover,<br>Whom his two eyes befool, whom passion wilders,<br>Whom not the broadest light of [[Noon\|noon]] can heal.<br>Go, question him! — Be mad enough, I pray thee.<br>The purpose of thy father, of thy Emperor,<br>Go, give it up free booty: — Force me, drive me<br>To an open breach before the [[Time\|time]]. And now,<br>Now that a miracle of heaven had guarded<br>My secret purpose even to this hour,<br>And laid to [[Sleep\|sleep]] Suspicion's piercing eyes,<br>Let me have lived to see that mine own son,<br>With frantic enterprise, annihilates<br>My toilsome labours and state-policy. |
| MAX | Aye — this state-policy! O how I curse it!<br>You will some [[Time\|time]], with your state-policy,<br>Compel him to the measure: it may happen,<br>Because ye are determined that he is guilty,<br>Guilty ye'll make him. All retreat cut off,<br>You close up every outlet, hem him in<br>Narrower and narrower, till at length ye force him —<br>Yes, ye, — ye force him, in his desperation,<br>To set [[Fire\|fire]] to his [[Prison\|prison]]. Father! Father!<br>That never can end [[Well\|well]] — it cannot — will not!<br>And let it be decided as it may,<br>I see with boding heart the near approach<br>Of an ill-starred unblest catastrophe.<br>For this great Monarch-spirit, if he fall,<br>Will drag a world into the ruin with him.<br>And as a [[Ship\|ship]] (that midway on the ocean<br>Takes [[Fire\|fire]]) at once, and with a thunder-burst<br>Explodes, and with itself shoots out its crew<br>In smoke and ruin betwixt sea and heaven;<br>So will he, falling, draw down in his fall<br>All us, who're fixed and mortised to his fortune.<br>Deem of it what thou wilt; but pardon me,<br>That I must bear me on in my own way.<br>All must remain pure betwixt him and me;<br>And, ere the day-light dawns, it must be known<br>Which I must lose — my father, or my friend. |
