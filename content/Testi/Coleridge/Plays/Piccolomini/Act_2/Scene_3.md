| Chi parla | Battuta |
|---|---|
| *(didascalia)* | The COUNTESS steps out from a Closet.<br>COUNT and COUNTESS TERTSKY. |
| TERTSKY | [[Well]] — is she coming? — I can keep him back<br>No longer. |
| COUNTESS | She will be there instantly.<br>You only send him. |
| TERTSKY | I am not quite certain,<br>I must confess it, Countess, whether or not<br>We are earning the Duke's thanks hereby. You know,<br>No ray has broken from him on this point.<br>You have o'er-ruled me, and yourself know best<br>How far you dare proceed. |
| COUNTESS | I take it on me. |
| *(didascalia)* | [Talking to herself, while she is advancing. |
| COUNTESS | Here's no need of full powers and commissions —<br>My cloudy Duke! we understand each other —<br>And without words. What, could I not unriddle,<br>Wherefore the daughter should be sent for hither,<br>Why first he, and no other, should be chosen<br>To fetch her hither! This sham of betrothing her<br>To a bridegroom, whom no one knows — No! no! ——<br>This may blind others! I see through thee, Brother!<br>But it beseems thee not, to draw a card<br>At such a game. Not yet! — It all remains<br>Mutely delivered up to my finessing ——<br>[[Well]] — thou shalt not have been deceived, Duke Friedland!<br>In her who is thy sister. —— |
| SERVANT | The commanders! |
| TERTSKY | Take care you heat his fancy and<br>affections —<br>Possess him with a reverie, and send him,<br>Absent and dreaming, to the banquet; that<br>He may not boggle at the signature. |
| COUNTESS | Take you care of your guests! — Go, send him hither. |
| TERTSKY | All rests upon his undersigning. |
| COUNTESS | Go to your guests! Go —— |
| ILLO | Where art staying, Tertsky?<br>The [[house]] is full, and all expecting you. |
| TERTSKY | Instantly! Instantly!   [To the COUNTESS.<br>And let him not<br>Stay here too long. It might awake suspicion<br>In the old man —— |
| COUNTESS | A truce with your precautions! |
| *(didascalia)* | [Exeunt TERTSKY and ILLO. |
