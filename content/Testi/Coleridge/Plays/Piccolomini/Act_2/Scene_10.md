| *(didascalia)* | TERTSKY, NEUMANN. |
| *(didascalia)* | Tertsky (beckons to Neumann who is waiting at the side-table, and steps forward with him to the edge of the stage). |
| TERTSKY | Have you the copy with you, Neumann? Give it.<br>It may be changed for the other? |
| NEUMANN | I have copied it<br>Letter by letter, line by line; no eye<br>Would e'er discover other difference,<br>Save only the omission of that clause,<br>According to your Excellency's order. |
| TERTSKY | Right! lay it yonder, and away with this —<br>It has performed its business — to the [[Fire\|fire]] with it — |
| *(didascalia)* | NEUMANN lays the copy on the table and steps back again to the side-table. |
