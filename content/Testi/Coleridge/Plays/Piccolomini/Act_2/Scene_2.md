| Chi parla | Battuta |
|---|---|
| *(didascalia)* | ILLO and TERTSKY. |
| TERTSKY | Now for this evening's business! How intend you<br>To manage with the generals at the banquet? |
| ILLO | Attend! We frame a formal declaration,<br>Wherein we to the Duke consign ourselves<br>Collectively, to be and to remain<br>His both with life and limb, and not to spare<br>The last drop of our [[Blood\|blood]] for him, provided<br>So doing we infringe no oath nor duty,<br>We may be under to the Emperor. — Mark!<br>This reservation we expressly make<br>In a particular clause, and save the [[Conscience\|conscience]].<br>Now hear! This formula so framed and worded<br>Will be presented to them for perusal<br>Before the banquet. No one will find in it<br>Cause of offence or scruple. Hear now further!<br>After the feast, when now the vap'ring wine<br>Opens the heart, and shuts the eyes, we let<br>A counterfeited paper, in the which<br>This one particular clause has been left out,<br>Go round for signatures. |
| TERTSKY | How? think you then<br>That they'll believe themselves bound by an oath,<br>Which we had tricked them into by a juggle? |
| ILLO | We shall have caught and caged them! Let them then<br>Beat their wings bare against the wires, and rave<br>Loud as they may against our treachery,<br>At [[Court\|court]] their signatures will be believed<br>Far more than their most holy affirmations.<br>Traitors they are, and must be; therefore wisely<br>Will make a virtue of necessity. |
| TERTSKY | [[Well]], [[Well\|well]], it shall content me; let but something<br>Be done, let only some decisive blow<br>Set us in motion. |
| ILLO | Besides, 'tis of subordinate importance<br>How, or how far, we may thereby propel<br>The generals. 'Tis enough that we persuade<br>The Duke, that they are his — Let him but act<br>In his determined mood, as if he had them,<br>And he will have them. Where he plunges in,<br>He makes a whirlpool, and all stream down to it. |
| TERTSKY | His policy is such a labyrinth,<br>That many a [[Time\|time]] when I have thought myself<br>Close at his side, he's gone at once, and left me<br>Ignorant of the ground where I was standing.<br>He lends the enemy his ear, permits me<br>To write to them, to Arnheim; to Sesina<br>Himself comes forward blank and undisguised;<br>Talks with us by the hour about his plans,<br>And when I think I have him — off at once ——<br>He has slipped from me, and appears as if<br>He had no scheme, but to retain his place. |
| ILLO | He give up his old plans! I'll tell you, friend!<br>His [[Soul\|soul]] is occupied with nothing else,<br>Even in his [[Sleep\|sleep]] — They are his thoughts, his [[Dreams\|dreams]],<br>That day by day he questions for this purpose<br>The motions of the planets —— |
| TERTSKY | Ay! you know<br>This [[Night\|night]], that is now coming, he with Seni<br>Shuts himself up in the astrological tower<br>To make joint observations — for I hear,<br>It is to be a [[Night\|night]] of weight and crisis;<br>And something great, and of long expectation,<br>Is to make its procession in the heaven. |
| ILLO | Come! be we bold and make dispatch. The work<br>In this next day or two must thrive and grow<br>More than it has for years. And let but only<br>Things first turn up auspicious here below ——<br>Mark what I say — the right [[Stars\|stars]] too will shew themselves.<br>Come, to the generals. All is in the glow,<br>And must be beaten while 'tis malleable. |
| TERTSKY | Do you go thither, Illo. I must stay<br>And wait here for the Countess Tertsky. Know<br>That we too are not idle. Break one string,<br>A second is in readiness. |
| ILLO | Yes! Yes!<br>I saw your Lady smile with such sly meaning.<br>What's in the [[Wind\|wind]]? |
| TERTSKY | A secret. Hush! she comes. |
| *(didascalia)* | [Exit ILLO. |
