| Chi parla | Battuta |
|---|---|
| *(didascalia)* | COUNTESS (returns), THEKLA. |
| COUNTESS | Fie, lady niece! to throw yourself upon him,<br>Like a poor gift to one who cares not for it,<br>And so must be flung after him! For you,<br>Duke Friedland's only [[Child\|child]], I should have thought<br>It had been more beseeming to have shewn yourself<br>More chary of your person. |
| THEKLA | And what mean you? |
| COUNTESS | I mean, niece, that you should not have forgotten<br>Who you are, and who he is. But perchance<br>That never once occurred to you. |
| THEKLA | What then? |
| COUNTESS | That you're the daughter of the Prince-Duke Friedland. |
| THEKLA | [[Well]] — and what farther? |
| COUNTESS | What? a pretty question! |
| THEKLA | He was born that which we have but become.<br>He's of an ancient Lombard family,<br>Son of a reigning princess. |
| COUNTESS | Are you dreaming?<br>Talking in [[Sleep\|sleep]]? An excellent jest, forsooth!<br>We shall no [[Doubt\|doubt]] right courteously entreat him<br>To [[Honour\|honour]] with his hand the richest heiress<br>In Europe. |
| THEKLA | That will not be necessary. |
| COUNTESS | Methinks 'twere [[Well\|well]] though not to run the hazard. |
| THEKLA | His father loves him, Count Octavio<br>Will interpose no difficulty —— |
| COUNTESS | His!<br>His father! his! But yours, niece, what of yours? |
| THEKLA | Why I begin to think you [[Fear\|fear]] his father,<br>So anxiously you hide it from the man!<br>His father, his, I mean. |
| *(didascalia)* | COUNTESS (looks at her). |
| COUNTESS | Niece, you are false. |
| THEKLA | Are you then wounded? O, be friends with me! |
| COUNTESS | You hold your game for won already. Do not<br>Triumph too soon! — |
| THEKLA | Nay now, be friends with me. |
| COUNTESS | It is not yet so far gone. |
| THEKLA | I believe you. |
| COUNTESS | Did you suppose your father had laid out<br>His most important life in toils of war,<br>Denied himself each quiet earthly bliss,<br>Had banished slumber from his tent, devoted<br>His noble head to care, and for this only,<br>To make a happy pair of you? At length<br>To draw you from your convent, and conduct<br>In easy triumph to your arms the man<br>That chanc'd to please your eyes! All this, methinks,<br>He might have purchased at a cheaper rate. |
| THEKLA | That which he did not plant for me might yet<br>Bear me fair fruitage of its own accord.<br>And if my friendly and affectionate fate,<br>Out of his fearful and enormous being,<br>Will but prepare the joys of life for me —<br>Cast thine eye round, bethink thee who thou art.<br>Into no [[house]] of joyance hast thou stepped,<br>For no espousals dost thou find the walls<br>Deck'd out, no guests the nuptial garland wearing.<br>Here is no splendour but of arms. Or think'st thou<br>That all these thousands are here congregated<br>To lead up the long dances at thy wedding?<br>Thou see'st thy father's forehead full of thought,<br>Thy mother's eye in [[Tears\|tears]]: upon the balance<br>Lies the great destiny of all our [[house]].<br>Leave now the puny wish, the girlish feeling,<br>O thrust it far behind thee! Give thou proof,<br>Thou'rt the daughter of the Mighty — his<br>Who where he moves creates the wonderful.<br>Not to herself the woman must belong,<br>Annexed and bound to alien destinies.<br>But she performs the best part, she the wisest,<br>Who can transmute the alien into [[Self\|self]],<br>Meet and disarm necessity by choice;<br>And what must be, take freely to her heart,<br>And bear and foster it with mother's [[Love\|love]]. |
| COUNTESS | Such ever was my lesson in the convent.<br>I had no loves, no wishes, knew myself<br>Only as his — his daughter — his, the Mighty!<br>His fame, the echo of whose blast drove to me<br>From the far distance, wakened in my [[Soul\|soul]]<br>No other thought than this — I am appointed<br>To offer up myself in passiveness to him. |
| THEKLA | That is thy fate. Mould thou thy wishes to it.<br>I and thy mother gave thee the example. |
| COUNTESS | My fate hath shewn me him, to whom behoves it<br>That I should offer up myself. In gladness<br>Him will I follow. |
| THEKLA | Not thy fate hath shewn him!<br>Thy heart, say rather—'twas thy heart, my [[Child\|child]]! |
| COUNTESS | Fate hath no voice but the heart's impulses.<br>I am all his! His Present — his [[Alone\|alone]],<br>Is this new life, which lives in me. He hath<br>A right to his own creature. What was I<br>Ere his fair [[Love\|love]] infused a [[Soul\|soul]] into me? |
| THEKLA | Thou would'st oppose thy father then, should he<br>Have otherwise determined with thy person? |
| *(didascalia)* | [THEKLA remains silent. The COUNTESS continues. |
| COUNTESS | Thou mean'st to force him to thy liking? — [[Child]],<br>His name is Friedland. |
| THEKLA | My name too is Friedland.<br>He shall have found a genuine daughter in me. |
| COUNTESS | What? he has vanquished all impediment,<br>And in the wilful mood of his own daughter<br>Shall a new struggle rise for him? [[Child]]! [[Child\|child]]!<br>As yet thou hast seen thy father's smiles [[Alone\|alone]];<br>The eye of his rage thou hast not seen. Dear [[Child\|child]],<br>I will not frighten thee. To that extreme,<br>I trust, it ne'er shall come. His will is yet<br>Unknown to me: 'tis possible his aims<br>May have the same direction as thy wish.<br>But this can never, never be his will,<br>That thou, the daughter of his haughty fortunes,<br>Should'st e'er demean thee as a [[Love\|love]]-sick maiden;<br>And like some poor cost-nothing, fling thyself<br>Toward the man, who, if that high prize ever<br>Be destined to await him, yet, with sacrifices<br>The highest [[Love\|love]] can bring, must pay for it. |
| *(didascalia)* | [Exit COUNTESS. |
| THEKLA | I thank thee for the hint. It turns<br>My sad presentiment to certainty.<br>And it is so! — Not one friend have we here,<br>Not one true heart! we've nothing but ourselves!<br>O she said rightly — no auspicious signs<br>Beam on this covenant of our affections.<br>This is no theatre, where [[Hope\|hope]] abides.<br>The dull thick noise of war [[Alone\|alone]] stirs here.<br>And [[Love\|love]] himself, as he were armed in steel,<br>Steps forth, and girds him for the strife of [[Death\|death]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | [Music from the banquet-room is heard. |
| THEKLA | There's a dark spirit walking in our [[house]],<br>And swiftly will the Destiny close on us.<br>It drove me hither from my calm asylum,<br>It mocks my [[Soul\|soul]] with charming witchery,<br>It lures me forward in a seraph's shape,<br>I see it near, I see it nearer floating,<br>It draws, it pulls me with a god-like power —<br>And lo! the abyss — and thither am I moving —<br>I have no power within me not to move! |
| *(didascalia)* | [The music from the banquet-room becomes louder. |
| THEKLA | O when a [[house]] is doomed in [[Fire\|fire]] to perish,<br>Many a dark heaven drives his clouds together,<br>Yea, shoots his lightnings down from sunny heights,<br>Flames burst from out the subterraneous chasms,<br>And fiends and angels mingling in their fury,<br>Sling [[Fire\|fire]]-brands at the burning edifice. |
| *(didascalia)* | [Exit THEKLA. |
