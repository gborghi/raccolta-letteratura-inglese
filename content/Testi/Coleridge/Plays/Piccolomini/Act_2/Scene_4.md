| Chi parla | Battuta |
|---|---|
| *(didascalia)* | COUNTESS, MAX PICCOLOMINI. |
| MAX | Aunt Tertsky? may I venture? |
| *(didascalia)* | [Advances to the middle of the stage, and looks around him with uneasiness. |
| MAX | She's not here!<br>Where is she? |
| COUNTESS | Look but somewhat narrowly<br>In yonder corner, lest perhaps she lie<br>Conceal'd behind that screen. |
| MAX | There lie her gloves! |
| *(didascalia)* | [Snatches at them, but the COUNTESS takes them herself. |
| MAX | You unkind Lady! You refuse me this —<br>You make it an amusement to torment me. |
| COUNTESS | And this the thanks you give me for my trouble? |
| MAX | O, if you felt the oppression at my heart!<br>Since we've been here, so to constrain myself —<br>With such poor stealth to hazard words and glances —<br>These, these are not my habits! |
| COUNTESS | You have still<br>Many new habits to acquire, young friend!<br>But on this proof of your obedient temper<br>I must continue to insist; and only<br>On this condition can I play the agent<br>For your concerns. |
| MAX | But wherefore comes she not?<br>Where is she? |
| COUNTESS | Into my hands you must place it<br>Whole and entire. Whom could you find, indeed,<br>More zealously affected to your interest?<br>No [[Soul\|soul]] on earth must know it — not your father.<br>He must not above all. |
| MAX | Alas! what danger?<br>Here is no face on which I might concentre<br>All the enraptured [[Soul\|soul]] stirs up within me.<br>O Lady! tell me. Is all changed around me?<br>Or is it only I?<br>I find myself,<br>As among strangers! Not a trace is left<br>Of all my former wishes, former joys.<br>Where has it vanished to? There was a [[Time\|time]]<br>When even, methought, with such a world as this<br>I was not discontented. Now how flat!<br>How stale! No life, no bloom, no flavour in it!<br>My comrades are intolerable to me.<br>My father — Even to him I can say nothing.<br>My arms, my military duties — O!<br>They are such wearying toys! |
| COUNTESS | But, gentle friend!<br>I must entreat it of your condescension,<br>You would be pleased to sink your eye, and favour<br>With one short glance or two this poor stale world,<br>Where even now much, and of much moment,<br>Is on the eve of its completion. |
| MAX | Something,<br>I can't but know, is going forward round me.<br>I see it gathering, crowding, driving on,<br>In wild uncustomary movements. [[Well]],<br>In due [[Time\|time]], doubtless, it will reach even me.<br>Where think you I have been, dear lady? Nay,<br>No raillery. The turmoil of the camp,<br>The [[spring]]-tide of acquaintance [[rolling]] in,<br>The pointless jest, the empty conversation,<br>Oppress'd and stifled me. I gasped for air —<br>I could not breathe — I was constrain'd to fly,<br>To seek a [[Silence\|silence]] out for my full heart;<br>And a pure spot wherein to feel my happiness.<br>No smiling, Countess! In the church was I.<br>There is a cloister here to the heaven's gate,<br>Thither I went, there found myself [[Alone\|alone]].<br>Over the altar hung a holy mother;<br>A wretched painting 'twas, yet 'twas the friend<br>That I was seeking in this moment. Ah,<br>How oft have I beheld that glorious form<br>In splendour, mid ecstatic worshippers;<br>Yet, still it moved me not! and now at once<br>Was my devotion cloudless as my [[Love\|love]]. |
| COUNTESS | Enjoy your fortune and felicity!<br>Forget the world around you. Meantime, [[Friendship\|friendship]]<br>Shall keep strict vigils for you, anxious, active.<br>Only be manageable when that [[Friendship\|friendship]]<br>Points you the [[Road\|road]] to full accomplishment.<br>How long may it be since you declared your passion? |
| MAX | This morning did I hazard the first word. |
| COUNTESS | This morning the first [[Time\|time]] in twenty days? |
| MAX | 'Twas at that hunting-[[Castle\|castle]], betwixt here<br>And Nepomuck, where you had joined us, and —<br>That was the last relay of the whole [[Journey\|journey]]!<br>In a balcony we were standing mute,<br>And gazing out upon the dreary field:<br>Before us the dragoons were riding onward,<br>The safe-guard which the Duke had sent us — heavy<br>The inquietude of parting lay upon me,<br>And trembling ventured I at length these words:<br>This all reminds me, noble maiden, that<br>To-day I must take leave of my good fortune.<br>A few hours more, and you will find a father,<br>Will see yourself surrounded by new friends,<br>And I henceforth shall be but as a stranger,<br>Lost in the many—'Speak with my aunt Tertsky!'<br>With hurrying voice she interrupted me.<br>She faltered. I beheld a glowing red<br>Possess her beautiful cheeks, and from the ground<br>Raised slowly up her eye met mine — no longer<br>Did I control myself. |
| *(didascalia)* | [The PRINCESS THEKLA appears at the door, and remains standing, observed by the COUNTESS, but not by PICCOLOMINI. |
| MAX | With instant boldness<br>I caught her in my arms, my mouth touched hers;<br>There was a rustling in the room close by;<br>It parted us—'Twas you. What since has happened,<br>You know. |
| COUNTESS | And is it your excess of modesty;<br>Or are you so incurious, that you do not<br>Ask me too of my secret? |
| MAX | Of your secret? |
| COUNTESS | Why, yes! When in the instant after you<br>I stepped into the room, and found my niece there,<br>What she in this first moment of the heart<br>Ta'en with surprise — |
| MAX | [[Well]]? |
