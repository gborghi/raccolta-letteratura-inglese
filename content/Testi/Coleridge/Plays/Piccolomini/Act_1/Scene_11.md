| Chi parla | Battuta |
|---|---|
| *(didascalia)* | ILLO, WALLENSTEIN, TERTSKY. |
| WALLENSTEIN | How stand affairs without? Are they prepared? |
| ILLO | You'll find them in the very mood you wish.<br>They know about the Emperor's requisitions,<br>And are tumultuous. |
| WALLENSTEIN | How hath Isolan<br>Declared himself? |
| ILLO | He's yours, both [[Soul\|soul]] and [[Body\|body]],<br>Since you built up again his Faro-bank. |
| WALLENSTEIN | And which way doth Kolatto bend? Hast thou<br>Made sure of Tiefenbach and Deodate? |
| ILLO | What Piccolomini does, that they do too. |
| WALLENSTEIN | You mean then I may venture somewhat with them? |
| ILLO | — If you are assured of the Piccolomini. |
| WALLENSTEIN | Not more assured of mine own [[Self\|self]]. |
| TERTSKY | And yet<br>I would you trusted not so much to Octavio,<br>The fox! |
| WALLENSTEIN | Thou teachest me to know my man?<br>Sixteen campaigns I have made with that old warrior.<br>Besides, I have his horoscope,<br>We both are born beneath like [[Stars\|stars]] — in short<br>To this belongs its own particular aspect,<br>If therefore thou canst warrant me the rest —— |
| ILLO | There is among them all but this one voice,<br>You must not lay down the command. I hear<br>They mean to send a deputation to you. |
| WALLENSTEIN | If I'm in aught to bind myself to them,<br>They too must bind themselves to me. |
| ILLO | Of course. |
| WALLENSTEIN | Their words of [[Honour\|honour]] they must give, their oaths,<br>Give them in writing to me, promising<br>Devotion to my service unconditional. |
| ILLO | Why not? |
| TERTSKY | Devotion unconditional?<br>The exception of their duties towards Austria<br>They'll always place among the premises.<br>With this reserve —— |
| WALLENSTEIN | All unconditional!<br>No premises, no reserves. |
| ILLO | A thought has struck me.<br>Does not Count Tertsky give us a set banquet<br>This evening? |
| TERTSKY | Yes; and all the Generals<br>Have been invited. |
| ILLO *(to Wallenstein)* | Say, will you here fully<br>Commission me to use my own discretion?<br>I'll gain for you the Generals' words of [[Honour\|honour]],<br>Even as you wish. |
| WALLENSTEIN | Gain me their signatures!<br>How you come by them, that is your concern. |
| ILLO | And if I bring it to you, black on white,<br>That all the leaders who are present here<br>Give themselves up to you, without condition;<br>Say, will you then — then will you shew yourself<br>In earnest, and with some decisive action<br>Make trial of your luck? |
| WALLENSTEIN | The signatures!<br>Gain me the signatures. |
| ILLO | Seize, seize the hour<br>Ere it slips from you. Seldom comes the moment<br>In life, which is indeed [[Sublime\|sublime]] and weighty.<br>To make a great decision possible,<br>O! many things, all transient and all rapid,<br>Must meet at once: and, haply, they thus met<br>May by that confluence be enforced to pause<br>[[Time]] long enough for wisdom, though too short,<br>Far, far too short a [[Time\|time]] for [[Doubt\|doubt]] and scruple!<br>This is that moment. See, our army chieftains,<br>Our best, our noblest, are assembled around you,<br>Their kinglike leader! On your nod they wait.<br>The single threads, which here your prosperous fortune<br>Hath woven together in one potent web<br>Instinct with destiny, O let them not<br>Unravel of themselves. If you permit<br>These chiefs to separate, so unanimous<br>Bring you them not a second [[Time\|time]] together.<br>'Tis the high tide that heaves the stranded [[Ship\|ship]],<br>And every individual's spirit waxes<br>In the great stream of multitudes. Behold<br>They are still here, here still! But soon the war<br>Bursts them once more asunder, and in small<br>Particular anxieties and interests<br>Scatters their spirit, and the sympathy<br>Of each man with the whole. He, who to-day<br>Forgets himself, forced onward with the stream,<br>Will become sober, seeing but himself,<br>Feel only his own weakness, and with [[Speed\|speed]]<br>Will face about, and march on in the old<br>High [[Road\|road]] of duty, the old broad-trodden [[Road\|road]],<br>And seek but to make shelter in good plight. |
| WALLENSTEIN | The [[Time\|time]] is not yet come. |
| TERTSKY | So you say always.<br>But when will it be [[Time\|time]]? |
| WALLENSTEIN | When I shall say it. |
| ILLO | You'll wait upon the [[Stars\|stars]], and on their hours,<br>Till the earthly hour escapes you. O, believe me,<br>In your own bosom are your destiny's [[Stars\|stars]].<br>Confidence in yourself, prompt resolution,<br>This is your [[Venus]]! and the sole malignant,<br>The only one that harmeth you is [[Doubt]]. |
| WALLENSTEIN | Thou speakest as thou understand'st. How oft<br>And many a [[Time\|time]] I've told thee, Jupiter,<br>That lustrous god, was setting at thy birth.<br>Thy visual power subdues no mysteries;<br>Mole-eyed, thou mayest but burrow in the earth,<br>Lead-coloured shine lighted thee into life.<br>The common, the terrestrial, thou mayest see,<br>With serviceable cunning knit together<br>The nearest with the nearest; and therein<br>I trust thee and believe thee! but whate'er<br>Full of mysterious import [[Nature]] weaves,<br>And fashions in the depths — the spirit's ladder,<br>That from this gross and visible world of [[Dust\|dust]]<br>Even to the starry world, with thousand rounds,<br>Builds itself up; on which the unseen powers<br>Move up and down on heavenly ministries —<br>The circles in the circles, that approach<br>The central sun with ever-narrowing orbit —<br>These see the glance [[Alone\|alone]], the unsealed eye,<br>Of Jupiter's glad children born in lustre. |
| *(didascalia)* | [He walks across the chamber, then returns, and standing still, proceeds. |
| WALLENSTEIN | The heavenly constellations make not merely<br>The day and nights, [[Summer\|summer]] and [[spring]], not merely<br>Signify to the husbandman the [[Seasons\|seasons]]<br>Of sowing and of harvest. Human action,<br>That is the seed too of contingencies,<br>Strewed on the dark land of futurity<br>In hopes to reconcile the powers of fate.<br>Whence it behoves us to seek out the seed-[[Time\|time]],<br>To watch the [[Stars\|stars]], select their proper hours,<br>And trace with searching eye the heavenly houses,<br>Whether the enemy of growth and thriving<br>Hide himself not, malignant, in his corner.<br>Therefore permit me my own [[Time\|time]]. Meanwhile<br>Do you your part. As yet I cannot say<br>What I shall do — only, give way I will not.<br>Depose me too they shall not. On these points<br>You may rely. |
| PAGE *(entering)* | My Lords, the Generals. |
| WALLENSTEIN | Let them come in. |
