| Chi parla | Battuta |
|---|---|
| *(didascalia)* | An old Gothic Chamber in the Council [[house\|House]] at Pilsen, decorated with<br>Colours and other War Insignia. |
| *(didascalia)* | ILLO with BUTLER and ISOLANI. |
| ILLO | Ye have come late — but ye are come! The distance,<br>Count Isolan, excuses your delay. |
| ISOLANI | Add this too, that we come not empty-handed.<br>At Donauwert it was reported to us,<br>A Swedish caravan was on its way<br>Transporting a rich cargo of provision,<br>Almost six hundred waggons. This my Croats<br>Plunged down upon and seized, this weighty prize! ——<br>We bring it hither —— |
| ILLO | Just in [[Time\|time]] to banquet<br>The illustrious company assembled here. |
| BUTLER | 'Tis all alive! a stirring scene here! |
| ISOLANI | Ay!<br>The very churches are all full of soldiers.<br>And in the Council-[[house]], too, I observe,<br>You're settled, quite at [[Home\|home]]! [[Well]], [[Well\|well]]! we soldiers<br>Must shift and suit us in what way we can. |
| ILLO | We have the Colonels here of thirty regiments.<br>You'll find Count Tertsky here, and Tiefenbach,<br>Kolatto, Goetz, Maradas, Hinnersam,<br>The Piccolomini, both son and father ——<br>You'll meet with many an unexpected greeting<br>From many an old friend and acquaintance. Only<br>Galas is wanting still, and Altringer. |
| BUTLER | Expect not Galas. |
| ILLO | How so? Do you know —— |
| ISOLANI | Max Piccolomini here? — O bring me to him.<br>I see him yet, ('tis now ten years ago,<br>We were engaged with Mansfeld hard by Dessau)<br>I see the youth, in my mind's eye I see him,<br>Leap his black war-[[Horse\|horse]] from the bridge adown,<br>And t'ward his father, then in extreme peril,<br>Beat up against the strong tide of the Elbe.<br>The down was scarce upon his chin! I hear<br>He has made good the promise of his youth,<br>And the full [[Hero\|hero]] now is finished in him. |
| ILLO | You'll see him yet ere evening. He conducts<br>The Duchess Friedland hither, and the Princess<br>From Carnthen. We expect them here at [[Noon\|noon]]. |
| BUTLER | Both wife and daughter does the Duke call hither?<br>He crowds in visitants from all sides. |
| ISOLANI | Hm!<br>So much the better! I had framed my mind<br>To hear of nought but warlike circumstance,<br>Of marches, and attacks, and batteries:<br>And lo! the Duke provides, that something too<br>Of gentler sort, and lovely, should be present<br>To feast our eyes. |
| ILLO *(aside to Butler)* | And how came you to know<br>That the Count Galas joins us not? |
| BUTLER | Because<br>He importuned me to remain behind. |
| ILLO | And you? — You hold out firmly?<br>Noble Butler! |
| BUTLER | After the obligation which the Duke<br>Had laid so newly on me —— |
| ILLO | I had forgotten<br>A pleasant duty — Major-General,<br>I wish you joy! |
| ISOLANI | What, you mean, of his regiment?<br>I hear, too, that to make the gift still sweeter,<br>The Duke has given him the very same<br>In which he first saw service, and since then,<br>Worked himself, step by step, through each preferment,<br>From the ranks upwards. And verily, it gives<br>A precedent of [[Hope\|hope]], a spur of action<br>To the whole corps, if once in their remembrance<br>An old deserving soldier makes his way. |
| BUTLER | I am perplexed and doubtful, whether or no<br>I dare accept this your congratulation.<br>The Emperor has not yet confirmed the appointment. |
| ISOLANI | Seize it, friend! Seize it! The hand which in that post<br>Placed you, is strong enough to keep you there,<br>Spite of the Emperor and his Ministers! |
| ILLO | Ay, if we would but so consider it! —<br>If we would all of us consider it so!<br>The Emperor gives us nothing; from the Duke<br>Comes all — whate'er we [[Hope\|hope]], whate'er we have. |
| ISOLANI *(to Illo)* | My noble brother! did I tell you how<br>The Duke will satisfy my creditors?<br>Will be himself my banker for the future,<br>Make me once more a creditable man! —<br>And this is now the third [[Time\|time]], think of that!<br>This kingly-minded man has rescued me<br>From absolute ruin, and restored my [[Honour\|honour]]. |
| ILLO | O that his power but kept pace with his wishes!<br>Why, friend! he'd give the whole world to his soldiers.<br>But at Vienna, brother! here's the grievance! —<br>What politic schemes do they not lay to shorten<br>His arm, and, where they can, to clip his pinions.<br>Then these new dainty requisitions! these,<br>Which this same Questenberg brings hither! — |
| BUTLER | Ay,<br>These requisitions of the Emperor, —<br>I too have heard about them; but I [[Hope\|hope]]<br>The Duke will not draw back a single inch! |
| ILLO | Not from his right most surely, unless first<br>— From office! |
| BUTLER | Know you aught then? You alarm me. |
| ISOLANI *(at the same [[Time\|time]] with Butler, and in a hurrying voice)* | We should be ruined, every one of us! |
| ILLO | No more!<br>Yonder I see our worthy friend approaching<br>With the Lieutenant-General, Piccolomini. |
| BUTLER | I [[Fear\|fear]] we shall not go hence as we came. |
