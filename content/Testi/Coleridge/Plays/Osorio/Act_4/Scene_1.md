| *(didascalia)* | SCENE THE FIRST. — A cavern, dark except where a gleam of moonlight is seen on one side of the further end of it, supposed to be cast on it from a cranny [crevice Remorse] in a part of the cavern out of sight. [[Ferdinand|FERDINAND]] [[Alone|alone]], an extinguished torch in his hand. |
| [[Ferdinand]] | Drip! drip! drip! drip! — in such a place as this<br>It has nothing else to do but drip! drip! drip!<br>I wish it had not dripp’d upon my torch.<br>[[Faith]] ‘twas a moving letter — very moving!<br>His life in danger — no place safe but this.<br>‘Twas his turn now to talk of gratitude!<br>And yet — but no! there can’t be such a [[Villain|villain]].<br>It cannot be!<br>Thanks to that little cranny<br>Which lets the moonlight in! I’ll go and sit by it.<br>To peep at a tree, or see a he-goat’s beard,<br>Or hear a cow or two breathe loud in their [[Sleep|sleep]],<br>‘Twere better than this dreary noise of [[Water|water]]-drops! |
| *(didascalia)* | *(He goes out of sight, opposite to the patch of moonlight, [and returns. Remorse] returns after a minute’s elapse in an ecstasy of [[Fear|fear]].)* |
| [[Ferdinand]] | A hellish pit! O God—’tis like my [[Night|night]]-mair!<br>I was just in! — and those damn’d fingers of ice<br>Which clutch’d my hair up! Ha! what’s that? it moved! |
| *(didascalia)* | *([[Ferdinand|FERDINAND]] stands [motionless MS. III erased] staring at another recess in the cavern. In the mean [[Time|time]] OSORIO enters with a torch and hollas to him [halloes to ISIDORE Remorse].)* |
| [[Ferdinand]] | I swear, I saw a something moving there!<br>The moonshine came and went, like a flash of [[Lightning|lightning]].<br>I swear, I saw it move! |
| *(didascalia)* | *(OSORIO goes into the recess, then returns, and with great scorn.)* |
| OSORIO | A jutting clay-stone<br>Drips on the long lank weed that grows beneath;<br>And the weed nods and drips. |
| [[Ferdinand]] | *(forcing a faint laugh.)* A joke to laugh at!<br>It was not that which frighten’d me, my lord! |
| OSORIO | What frighten’d you? |
| [[Ferdinand]] | You see that little cranny?<br>But first permit me, |
| *(didascalia)* | *(Lights his torch at OSORIO’S, and while lighting it.)* |
| [[Ferdinand]] | (A lighted torch in the hand<br>Is no unpleasant object here — one’s breath<br>Floats round the flame, and makes as many colours<br>As the thin clouds that travel near the [[Moon|moon]].)<br>You see that cranny there? |
| OSORIO | [[Well]], what of that? |
| [[Ferdinand]] | I walk’d up to it, meaning to sit there.<br>When I had reach’d it within twenty paces —— |
| *(didascalia)* | *([[Ferdinand|FERDINAND]] starts as if he felt the terror over again.)* |
| [[Ferdinand]] | Merciful Heaven! Do go, my lord! and look. |
| *(didascalia)* | *(OSORIO goes and returns.)* |
| OSORIO | It must have shot some pleasant feelings thro’ you? |
| [[Ferdinand]] | If every atom of a dead man’s flesh<br>Should move, each one with a particular life,<br>Yet all as cold as ever—’twas just so!<br>Or if it drizzled needle-points of [[Frost|frost]]<br>Upon a feverish head made suddenly bald — |
| OSORIO | *(interrupting him.)* Why, [[Ferdinand]]! I blush for thy cowardice.<br>It would have startled any man, I grant thee.<br>But such a panic. |
| [[Ferdinand]] | When a boy, my lord!<br>I could have sat whole hours beside that chasm,<br>Push’d in huge stones and heard them thump and rattle<br>Against its horrid sides; and hung my head<br>Low down, and listen’d till the heavy fragments<br>Sunk, with faint crash, in that still groaning [[Well|well]],<br>Which never thirsty [[Pilgrim|pilgrim]] blest, which never<br>A living thing came near; unless, perchance,<br>Some blind-worm battens on the ropy mould,<br>Close at its edge. |
| OSORIO | Art thou more coward now? |
| [[Ferdinand]] | Call him that fears his fellow-men a coward.<br>I [[Fear|fear]] not man. But this inhuman cavern<br>It were too bad a [[Prison|prison]]-[[house]] for goblins.<br>Besides (you’ll laugh, my lord!) but true it is,<br>My last [[Night|night]]’s [[Sleep|sleep]] was very sorely haunted<br>By what had pass’d between us in the morning.<br>I saw you in a thousand hideous ways,<br>And doz’d and started, doz’d again and started.<br>I do entreat your lordship to believe me,<br>In my last dream —— |
| OSORIO | [[Well]]? |
| [[Ferdinand]] | I was in the act<br>Of falling down that chasm, when Alhadra<br>Waked me. She heard my heart beat! |
| OSORIO | Strange enough!<br>Had you been here before? |
| [[Ferdinand]] | Never, my lord!<br>But my eyes do not see it now more clearly<br>Than in my dream I saw that very chasm. |
| *(didascalia)* | *(OSORIO stands in a deep study — then, after a pause.)* |
| OSORIO | There is no reason why it should be so.<br>And yet it is. |
| [[Ferdinand]] | What is, my lord? |
| OSORIO | Unpleasant<br>To kill a man! |
| [[Ferdinand]] | Except in [[Self|self]]-defence. |
| OSORIO | Why that’s my case: and yet ‘tis still unpleasant.<br>At least I find it so! But you, perhaps,<br>Have stronger nerves? |
| [[Ferdinand]] | Something doth trouble you.<br>How can I serve you? By the life you gave me,<br>By all that makes that life of value to me,<br>My wife, my babes, my [[Honour|honour]], I swear to you,<br>Name it, and I will toil to do the thing,<br>If it be [[Innocent|innocent]]! But this, my lord!<br>Is not a place where you could perpetrate,<br>No, nor propose a wicked thing. The darkness<br>(When ten yards off, we know, ‘tis chearful moonlight)<br>Collects the [[Guilt|guilt]] and crowds it round the heart.<br>It must be [[Innocent|innocent]]. |
| OSORIO | Thyself be judge. |
| *(didascalia)* | *(OSORIO walks round the cavern — then looking round it.)* |
| OSORIO | One of our family knew this place [[Well|well]]. |
| [[Ferdinand]] | Who? when? my lord. |
| OSORIO | What boots it who or when?<br>Hang up the torch. I’ll tell his tale to thee. |
| *(didascalia)* | *(They hang [up] their torches in some shelf of [on some ridge in Remorse] the cavern.)* |
| OSORIO | He was a man different from other men,<br>And he despised them, yet revered himself. |
| [[Ferdinand]] | What? he was mad? |
| OSORIO | All men seem’d mad to him,<br>Their actions noisome folly, and their talk —<br>A goose’s gabble was more musical.<br>[[Nature]] had made him for some other planet,<br>And press’d his [[Soul|soul]] into a human shape<br>By accident or malice. In this world<br>He found no fit companion! |
| [[Ferdinand]] | Ah, poor wretch!<br>Madmen are mostly proud. |
| OSORIO | He walk’d [[Alone|alone]],<br>And phantasies, unsought for, troubled him.<br>Something within would still be shadowing out<br>All possibilities, and with these shadows<br>His mind held dalliance. Once, as so it happen’d,<br>A fancy [[Cross|cross]]’d him wilder than the rest:<br>To this in moody murmur, and low voice,<br>He yielded utterance as some talk in [[Sleep|sleep]].<br>The man who heard him ——<br>Why didst thou look round? |
| [[Ferdinand]] | I have a prattler three years old, my lord!<br>In [[Truth|truth]] he is my darling. As I went<br>From forth my door, he made a moan in [[Sleep|sleep]] —<br>But I am talking idly — pray go on!<br>And what did this man? |
| OSORIO | With his human hand<br>He gave a being and reality<br>To that wild fancy of a possible thing.<br>[[Well]] it was done. *(Then very wildly.)*<br>Why babblest thou of [[Guilt|guilt]]?<br>The deed was done, and it pass’d fairly off.<br>And he, whose tale I tell thee — dost thou listen? |
| [[Ferdinand]] | I would, my lord, you were by my fireside!<br>I’d listen to you with an eager eye,<br>Tho’ you began this cloudy tale at midnight.<br>But I do listen — pray proceed, my lord! |
| OSORIO | Where was I? |
| [[Ferdinand]] | He of whom you tell the tale — |
| OSORIO | Surveying all things with a quiet scorn<br>Tamed himself down to living purposes,<br>The occupations and the semblances<br>Of ordinary men — and such he seem’d.<br>But that some over-ready agent — he —— |
| [[Ferdinand]] | Ah! what of him, my lord? |
| OSORIO | He proved a [[Villain|villain]];<br>Betray’d the mystery to a brother [[Villain|villain]];<br>And they between them hatch’d a damnéd plot<br>To hunt him down to infamy and [[Death|death]]<br>To share the wealth of a most noble family,<br>And stain the [[Honour|honour]] of an [[Orphan|orphan]] lady<br>With barbarous mixture and unnatural [[Union|union]].<br>What did the Velez? I am proud of the name,<br>Since he dared do it. |
| *(didascalia)* | *(OSORIO grasps his [[Sword|sword]] and turns off from [[Ferdinand|FERDINAND]], then, after a pause, returns.)* |
| OSORIO | Our links burn dimly. |
| [[Ferdinand]] | A dark tale darkly finish’d! Nay, my lord!<br>Tell what he did. |
| OSORIO | *(fiercely.)* That which his wisdom prompted.<br>He made the traitor meet him in this cavern,<br>And here he kill’d the traitor. |
| [[Ferdinand]] | No! — the [[Fool|fool]].<br>He had not wit enough to be a traitor.<br>Poor thick-eyed beetle! not to have foreseen<br>That he, who gull’d thee with a whimper’d lie<br>To murder his own brother, would not scruple<br>To murder thee, if e’er his [[Guilt|guilt]] grew jealous<br>And he could steal upon thee in the dark! |
| OSORIO | Thou would’st not then have come, if —— |
| [[Ferdinand]] | O yes, my lord!<br>I would have met him arm’d, and scared the coward! |
| *(didascalia)* | *([[Ferdinand|FERDINAND]] throws off his robe, shows himself armed, and draws his [[Sword|sword]].)* |
| OSORIO | Now this is excellent, and warms the [[Blood|blood]]!<br>My heart was drawing back, drawing me back<br>With womanish pulls of pity. Dusky slave,<br>Now I will kill thee pleasantly, and count it<br>Among my comfortable thoughts hereafter. |
| [[Ferdinand]] | And all my little ones fatherless! Die thou first. |
| *(didascalia)* | *(They fight. OSORIO disarms [[Ferdinand|FERDINAND]], and in disarming him, throws his [[Sword|sword]] up that recess, opposite to which they were standing.)* |
| [[Ferdinand]] | *(springing wildly towards Osorio.)* Still I can strangle thee! |
| OSORIO | Nay, [[Fool|fool]]! stand off.<br>I’ll kill thee — but not so! Go fetch thy [[Sword|sword]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | *([[Ferdinand|FERDINAND]] hurries into the recess with his torch. OSORIO follows him, and in a moment returns [[Alone|alone]].)* |
| OSORIO | Now — this was luck! No bloodstains, no dead [[Body|body]]!<br>His dream, too, is made out. Now for his friend. |
| *(didascalia)* | *(Exit.)* |
| *(didascalia)* | SCENE changes to the [[Court|court]] before the [[Castle]] of VELEZ. |
