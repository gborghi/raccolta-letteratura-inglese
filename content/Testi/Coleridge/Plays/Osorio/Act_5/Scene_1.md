| *(didascalia)* | SCENE THE FIRST. — The Sea Shore. NAOMI and a Moresco. |
| MORESCO | This was no [[Time|time]] for freaks of useless vengeance. |
| NAOMI | True! but Francesco, the Inquisitor,<br>Thou know’st the bloodhound—’twas a strong [[Temptation|temptation]].<br>And when they pass’d within a mile of his [[house]],<br>We could not curb them in. They swore by Mahomet,<br>It were a deed of treachery to their brethren<br>To sail from Spain and leave that man alive. |
| MORESCO | Where is Alhadra? |
| NAOMI | She moved steadily on<br>Unswerving from the path of her resolve.<br>Yet each strange object fix’d her eye: for grief<br>Doth [[Love|love]] to dally with fantastic shapes,<br>And smiling, like a sickly moralist,<br>Gives some resemblance of her own concerns<br>To the straws of chance, and things inanimate.<br>I seek her here; stand thou upon the watch. |
| *(didascalia)* | *(Exit Moresco.)* |
| NAOMI | *(looking wistfully to the distance.)* Stretch’d on the rock!<br>It must be she — Alhadra! |
| *(didascalia)* | *(ALHADRA rises from the rock, and advances slowly, as if musing.)* |
| NAOMI | Once more, [[Well|well]] met! what ponder’st thou so deeply? |
| ALHADRA | I scarce can tell thee! For my many thoughts<br>Troubled me, till with blank and naked mind<br>I only listen’d to the dashing billows.<br>It seems to me, I could have closed my eyes<br>And wak’d without [[A Dream|a dream]] of what has pass’d;<br>So [[Well|well]] it counterfeited quietness,<br>This wearied heart of mine! |
| NAOMI | ‘Tis thus by [[Nature|nature]]<br>Wisely ordain’d, that so excess of sorrow<br>Might bring its own cure with it. |
| ALHADRA | Would to Heaven<br>That it had brought its last and certain cure!<br>That ruin in the wood. |
| NAOMI | It is a place<br>Of ominous fame; but ‘twas the shortest [[Road|road]],<br>Nor could we else have kept clear of the village.<br>Yet some among us, as they scal’d the wall,<br>Mutter’d old rhyming prayers. |
| ALHADRA | On that broad wall<br>I saw a skull; a poppy grew beside it,<br>There was a ghastly solace in the sight! |
| NAOMI | I mark’d it not, and in good [[Truth|truth]] the [[Night|night]]-bird<br>Curdled my [[Blood|blood]], even till it prick’d the heart.<br>Its note comes dreariest in the fall of the year: *(Looking round impatiently.)*<br>Why don’t they come? I will go forth and meet them. |
| *(didascalia)* | *(Exit NAOMI.)* |
| ALHADRA | *([[Alone|alone]].)* The hanging woods, that touch’d by [[Autumn|autumn]] seem’d<br>As they were blossoming hues of [[Fire|fire]] and gold,<br>The hanging woods, most lovely in [[Decay|decay]],<br>The many clouds, the sea, the rock, the sands,<br>Lay in the silent moonshine; and the owl,<br>(Strange! very strange!) the scritch owl only wak’d,<br>Sole voice, sole eye of all that world of [[Beauty|beauty]]!<br>Why such a thing am I! Where are these men?<br>I need the sympathy of human faces<br>To beat away this deep contempt for all things<br>Which quenches my [[Revenge|revenge]]. Oh! — would to Alla<br>[[The Raven|The raven]] and the sea-mew were appointed<br>To bring me food, or rather that my [[Soul|soul]]<br>Could drink in life from the universal air!<br>It were a lot divine in some small skiff,<br>Along some ocean’s boundless [[Solitude|solitude]],<br>To float for ever with a careless course,<br>And think myself the only being alive! *(NAOMI re-enters.)* |
| NAOMI | Thy children —— |
| ALHADRA | Children? Whose children? *(A pause — then fiercely.)*<br>Son of Velez,<br>This hath new-strung my arm! Thou coward [[Tyrant|tyrant]],<br>To stupify a woman’s heart with anguish,<br>Till she forgot even that she was a mother! |
| *(didascalia)* | *(A noise — enter a part of the Morescoes; and from the opposite side of the stage a Moorish Seaman.)* |
| MOORISH SEAMAN | The boat is on the shore, the vessel waits.<br>Your wives and children are already stow’d;<br>I left them prattling of the Barbary coast,<br>Of Mosks, and minarets, and golden crescents.<br>Each had her separate dream; but all were gay,<br>Dancing, in thought, to finger-beaten timbrels! |
| *(didascalia)* | *(Enter MAURICE and the rest of the Morescoes dragging in FRANCESCO.)* |
| FRANCESCO | O spare me, spare me! only spare my life! |
| AN OLD MAN | All hail, Alhadra! O that thou hadst heard him<br>When first we dragg’d him forth! *(Then turning to the band.)*<br>Here! in her presence —— |
| *(didascalia)* | *(He advances with his [[Sword|sword]] as about to kill him. MAURICE leaps in and stands with his drawn [[Sword|sword]] between FRANCESCO and the Morescoes.)* |
| MAURICE | Nay, but ye shall not! |
| OLD MAN | Shall not? Hah? Shall not? |
| MAURICE | What, an unarm’d man?<br>A man that never wore a [[Sword|sword]]? A priest?<br>It is unsoldierly! I say, ye shall not! |
| OLD MAN | *(turning to the bands.)* He bears himself most like an insolent Spaniard! |
| MAURICE | And ye like slaves, that have destroy’d their master,<br>But know not yet what [[Freedom|freedom]] means; how holy<br>And just a thing it is! He’s a fallen foe!<br>Come, come, forgive him! |
| ALL | No, by Mahomet! |
| FRANCESCO | O [[Mercy|mercy]], [[Mercy|mercy]]! talk to them of [[Mercy|mercy]]! |
| OLD MAN | [[Mercy]] to thee! No, no, by Mahomet! |
| MAURICE | Nay, Mahomet taught [[Mercy|mercy]] and [[Forgiveness|forgiveness]].<br>I am sure he did! |
| OLD MAN | Ha! Ha! [[Forgiveness]]! [[Mercy]]! |
| MAURICE | If he did not, he needs it for himself! |
| ALHADRA | Blaspheming [[Fool|fool]]! the law of Mahomet<br>Was given by him, who framed the [[Soul|soul]] of man.<br>This the best proof — it fits the [[Soul|soul]] of man!<br>[[Ambition]], glory, thirst of enterprize,<br>The deep and stubborn purpose of [[Revenge|revenge]],<br>With all the boiling revelries of pleasure —<br>These grow in the heart, yea, intertwine their roots<br>With its minutest fibres! And that Being<br>Who made us, laughs to scorn the lying [[Faith|faith]],<br>Whose puny precepts, like a wall of sand,<br>Would stem the full tide of predestined [[Nature]]! |
| NAOMI | *(who turns toward Francesco with his [[Sword|sword]].)* Speak! |
| ALL | *(to Alhadra.)* Speak! |
| ALHADRA | Is the murderer of your chieftain dead?<br>Now as God liveth, who hath suffer’d him<br>To make my children orphans, none shall die<br>Till I have seen his [[Blood|blood]]!<br>Off with him to the vessel! |
| *(didascalia)* | *(A part of the Morescoes hurry him off.)* |
| ALHADRA | The Tyger, that with unquench’d cruelty,<br>Still thirsts for [[Blood|blood]], leaps on the hunter’s spear<br>With prodigal [[Courage|courage]]. ‘Tis not so with man. |
| MAURICE | It is not so, remember that, my friends!<br>Cowards are cruel, and the cruel cowards. |
| ALHADRA | Scatter yourselves, take each a separate way,<br>And move in [[Silence|silence]] to the [[house]] of Velez. *(Exeunt.)* |
| *(didascalia)* | SCENE. — A Dungeon. |
