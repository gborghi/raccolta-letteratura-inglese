| Chi parla | Battuta |
|---|---|
| *(didascalia)* | The Sea Shore on the Coast of Granada. |
| *(didascalia)* | DON ALVAR, wrapt in a Boat cloak, and ZULIMEZ (a Moresco), both as just landed. |
| ZULIMEZ | No sound, no face of joy to welcome us! |
| ALVAR | My faithful Zulimez, for one brief moment<br>Let me forget my anguish and their crimes.<br>If aught on earth demand an unmix'd feeling,<br>'Tis surely this — after long years of [[Exile\|exile]],<br>To step forth on firm land, and gazing round us,<br>To hail at once our country, and our birth-place.<br>Hail, Spain! Granada, hail! once more I press<br>Thy sands with filial awe, land of my fathers! |
| ZULIMEZ | Then claim your rights in it! O, revered Don Alvar,<br>Yet, yet give up your all too gentle purpose.<br>It is too hazardous! reveal yourself,<br>And let the guilty meet the doom of [[Guilt\|guilt]]! |
| ALVAR | Remember, Zulimez! I am his brother,<br>Injured indeed! O deeply injured! yet<br>Ordonio's brother. |
| ZULIMEZ | Nobly-minded Alvar!<br>This sure but gives his [[Guilt\|guilt]] a blacker dye. |
| ALVAR | The more behoves it I should rouse within him<br>Remorse! that I should save him from himself. |
| ZULIMEZ | Remorse is as the heart in which it grows:<br>If that be gentle, it drops balmy dews<br>Of true repentance; but if proud and gloomy,<br>It is a [[Poison\|poison]]-tree, that pierced to the inmost<br>Weeps only [[Tears\|tears]] of [[Poison\|poison]]! |
| ALVAR | And of a brother,<br>Dare I hold this, unproved? nor make one effort<br>To save him? — Hear me, friend! I have yet to tell thee,<br>That this same life, which he conspired to take,<br>Himself once rescued from the angry flood,<br>And at the imminent hazard of his own.<br>Add too my oath — |
| ZULIMEZ | You have thrice told already<br>The years of absence and of secrecy,<br>To which a forced oath bound you; if in [[Truth\|truth]]<br>A suborned murderer have the power to dictate<br>A binding oath — |
| ALVAR | My long captivity<br>Left me no choice: the very wish too languished<br>With the fond [[Hope\|hope]] that nursed it; the sick babe<br>Drooped at the bosom of its famished mother.<br>But (more than all) Teresa's perfidy;<br>The assassin's strong assurance, when no interest,<br>No motive could have tempted him to falsehood:<br>In the first pangs of his awaken'd [[Conscience\|conscience]],<br>When with abhorrence of his own black purpose<br>The murderous weapon, pointed at my breast,<br>Fell from his palsied hand — |
| ZULIMEZ | Heavy presumption! |
| ALVAR | It weighed not with me — Hark! I will tell thee all;<br>As we passed by, I bade thee mark the base<br>Of yonder cliff — |
| ZULIMEZ | That rocky seat you mean,<br>Shaped by the billows? — |
| ALVAR | There Teresa met me<br>The morning of the day of my departure.<br>We were [[Alone\|alone]]: the purple hue of [[Dawn\|dawn]]<br>Fell from the kindling east aslant upon us,<br>And blending with the blushes on her cheek,<br>Suffused the tear-drops there with rosy light.<br>There seemed a glory round us, and Teresa<br>The angel of the vision!<br>Had'st thou seen<br>How in each motion her most [[Innocent\|innocent]] [[Soul\|soul]]<br>Beamed forth and brightened, thou thyself would'st tell me,<br>[[Guilt]] is a thing impossible in her!<br>She must be [[Innocent\|innocent]]! |
| ZULIMEZ | Proceed, my lord! |
| ALVAR | A portrait which she had procured by stealth,<br>(For even then it seems her heart foreboded<br>Or knew Ordonio's moody rivalry)<br>A portrait of herself with thrilling hand<br>She tied around my neck, conjuring me,<br>With earnest prayers, that I would keep it sacred<br>To my own knowledge: nor did she desist,<br>Till she had won a solemn promise from me,<br>That (save my own) no eye should e'er behold it<br>Till my return. Yet this the assassin knew,<br>Knew that which none but she could have disclosed. |
| ZULIMEZ | A damning proof! |
| ALVAR | My own life wearied me!<br>And but for the imperative voice within,<br>With mine own hand I had thrown off the burthen.<br>That voice, which quelled me, calmed me: and I sought<br>The Belgic states: there joined the better cause;<br>And there too fought as one that courted [[Death\|death]]!<br>Wounded, I fell among the dead and dying,<br>In [[Death\|death]]-like trance: a long [[Imprisonment\|imprisonment]] followed.<br>The fulness of my anguish by degrees<br>Waned to a meditative [[Melancholy\|melancholy]];<br>And still the more I mused, my [[Soul\|soul]] became<br>More doubtful, more perplexed; and still Teresa,<br>[[Night]] after [[Night\|night]], she visited my [[Sleep\|sleep]],<br>Now as a saintly sufferer, wan and tearful,<br>Now as a saint in glory beckoning to me!<br>Yes, still as in contempt of proof and reason,<br>I cherish the fond [[Faith\|faith]] that she is guiltless!<br>Hear then my fix'd resolve: I'll linger here<br>In the disguise of a Moresco chieftain. —<br>The Moorish robes? — |
| ZULIMEZ | All, all are in the sea-[[Cave\|cave]],<br>Some furlong hence. I bade our mariners<br>Secrete the boat there. |
| ALVAR | Above all, the picture<br>Of the assassination — |
| ZULIMEZ | Be assured<br>That it remains uninjured. |
| ALVAR | Thus disguised<br>I will first seek to meet Ordonio's — wife!<br>If possible, [[Alone\|alone]] too. This was her wonted walk,<br>And this the hour; her words, her very looks<br>Will acquit her or convict. |
| ZULIMEZ | Will they not know you? |
| ALVAR | With your aid, friend, I shall unfearingly<br>Trust the disguise; and as to my complexion,<br>My long [[Imprisonment\|imprisonment]], the scanty food,<br>This scar — and toil beneath a burning sun,<br>Have done already half the business for us.<br>Add too my youth, since last we saw each other.<br>Manhood has swoln my chest, and taught my voice<br>A hoarser note — Besides, they think me dead:<br>And what the mind believes impossible,<br>The bodily sense is slow to recognize. |
| ZULIMEZ | 'Tis yours, sir, to command, mine to obey.<br>Now to the [[Cave\|cave]] beneath the vaulted rock,<br>Where having shaped you to a Moorish chieftain,<br>I'll seek our mariners; and in the dusk<br>Transport whate'er we need to the small dell<br>In the Alpujarras — there where Zagri lived. |
| ALVAR | I know it [[Well\|well]]: it is the obscurest haunt<br>Of all the [[Mountains\|mountains]] — |
| *(didascalia)* | Both stand listening. |
| ALVAR | Voices at a distance!<br>Let us away! |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt. |
