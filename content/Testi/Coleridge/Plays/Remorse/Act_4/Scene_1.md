| Chi parla | Battuta |
|---|---|
| *(didascalia)* | A cavern, dark, except where a gleam of moonlight is seen on one side at the further end of it; supposed to be cast on it from a crevice in a part of the cavern out of sight. ISIDORE [[Alone\|alone]], an extinguished torch in his hand. |
| ISIDORE | [[Faith]] 'twas a moving letter — very moving!<br>'His life in danger, no place safe but this!<br>'Twas his turn now to talk of gratitude.'<br>And yet — but no! there can't be such a [[Villain\|villain]].<br>It can not be!<br>Thanks to that little crevice,<br>Which lets the moonlight in! I'll go and sit by it.<br>To peep at a tree, or see a he-goat's beard,<br>Or hear a cow or two breathe loud in their [[Sleep\|sleep]] —<br>Any thing but this crash of [[Water\|water]] drops!<br>These dull abortive sounds that fret the [[Silence\|silence]]<br>With puny thwartings and mock opposition!<br>So beats the [[Death\|death]]-watch to a sick man's ear. |
| *(didascalia)* | He goes out of sight, opposite to the patch of moonlight: and returns. |
| ISIDORE | A hellish pit! The very same I dreamt of!<br>I was just in — and those damn'd fingers of ice<br>Which clutch'd my hair up! Ha! — what's that — it mov'd. |
| *(didascalia)* | ISIDORE stands staring at another recess in the cavern. In the mean [[Time\|time]] ORDONIO enters with a torch, and halloes to ISIDORE. |
| ISIDORE | I swear that I saw something moving there!<br>The moonshine came and went like a flash of [[Lightning\|lightning]] ——<br>I swear, I saw it move. |
| *(didascalia)* | Ordonio goes into the recess, then returns. |
| ORDONIO | A jutting clay stone<br>Drops on the long lank weed, that grows beneath:<br>And the weed nods and drips. |
| ISIDORE | A jest to laugh at!<br>It was not that which scar'd me, good my lord. |
| ORDONIO | What scar'd you, then? |
| ISIDORE | You see that little rift?<br>But first permit me! |
| *(didascalia)* | Lights his torch at ORDONIO'S, and while lighting it. |
| ISIDORE | (A lighted torch in the hand<br>Is no unpleasant object here — one's breath<br>Floats round the flame, and makes as many colours<br>As the thin clouds that travel near the [[Moon\|moon]].)<br>You see that crevice there?<br>My torch extinguished by these [[Water\|water]]-drops,<br>And marking that the moonlight came from thence,<br>I stept in to it, meaning to sit there;<br>But scarcely had I measured twenty paces —<br>My [[Body\|body]] bending forward, yea, o'erbalanced<br>Almost beyond recoil, on the dim brink<br>Of a huge chasm I stept. The shadowy moonshine<br>Filling the void so counterfeited substance,<br>That my foot hung aslant adown the edge.<br>Was it my own [[Fear\|fear]]?<br>[[Fear]] too hath its instincts!<br>(And yet such dens as these are wildly told of,<br>And there are beings that live, yet not for the eye)<br>An arm of [[Frost\|frost]] above and from behind me<br>Pluck'd up and snatched me backward. Merciful Heaven!<br>You smile! alas, even smiles look ghastly here!<br>My lord, I pray you, go yourself and view it. |
| ORDONIO | It must have shot some pleasant feelings through you. |
| ISIDORE | If every atom of a dead man's flesh<br>Should creep, each one with a particular life,<br>Yet all as cold as ever—'twas just so!<br>Or had it drizzled needle-points of [[Frost\|frost]]<br>Upon a feverish head made suddenly bald — |
| ORDONIO | Why, Isidore,<br>I blush for thy cowardice. It might have startled,<br>I grant you, even a brave man for a moment —<br>But such a panic — |
| ISIDORE | When a boy, my lord!<br>I could have sate whole hours beside that chasm,<br>Push'd in huge stones and heard them strike and rattle<br>Against its horrid sides: then hung my head<br>Low down, and listened till the heavy fragments<br>Sank with faint crash in that still groaning [[Well\|well]],<br>Which never thirsty [[Pilgrim\|pilgrim]] blest, which never<br>A living thing came near — unless, perchance,<br>Some blind-worm battens on the ropy mould<br>Close at its edge. |
| ORDONIO | Art thou more coward now? |
| ISIDORE | Call him, that fears his fellow-man, a coward!<br>I [[Fear\|fear]] not man — but this inhuman cavern,<br>It were too bad a [[Prison\|prison]]-[[house]] for goblins.<br>Beside, (you'll smile, my lord) but true it is,<br>My last [[Night\|night]]'s [[Sleep\|sleep]] was very sorely haunted<br>By what had passed between us in the morning.<br>O [[Sleep\|sleep]] of horrors! Now run down and stared at<br>By forms so hideous that they mock remembrance —<br>Now seeing nothing and imagining nothing,<br>But only being afraid — stifled with [[Fear\|fear]]!<br>While every goodly or familiar form<br>Had a strange power of breathing terror round me!<br>I saw you in a thousand fearful shapes;<br>And, I entreat your lordship to believe me,<br>In my last dream —— |
| ORDONIO | [[Well]]? |
| ISIDORE | I was in the act<br>Of falling down that chasm, when Alhadra<br>Wak'd me: she heard my heart beat. |
| ORDONIO | Strange enough!<br>Had you been here before? |
| ISIDORE | Never, my lord!<br>But mine eyes do not see it now more clearly,<br>Than in my dream I saw — that very chasm. |
| ORDONIO | I know not why it should be! yet it is — |
| ISIDORE | What is, my lord? |
| ORDONIO | Abhorrent from our [[Nature\|nature]]<br>To kill a man. — |
| ISIDORE | Except in [[Self\|self]]-defence. |
| ORDONIO | Why that's my case; and yet the [[Soul\|soul]] recoils from it —<br>'Tis so with me at least. But you, perhaps,<br>Have sterner feelings? |
| ISIDORE | Something troubles you.<br>How shall I serve you? By the life you gave me,<br>By all that makes that life of value to me,<br>My wife, my babes, my [[Honour\|honour]], I swear to you,<br>Name it, and I will toil to do the thing,<br>If it be [[Innocent\|innocent]]! But this, my lord!<br>Is not a place where you could perpetrate,<br>No, nor propose a wicked thing. The darkness,<br>When ten strides off we know 'tis cheerful moonlight,<br>Collects the [[Guilt\|guilt]], and crowds it round the heart.<br>It must be [[Innocent\|innocent]]. |
| ORDONIO | Thyself be judge.<br>One of our family knew this place [[Well\|well]]. |
| ISIDORE | Who? when? my lord? |
| ORDONIO | What boots it, who or when?<br>Hang up thy torch — I'll tell his tale to thee. |
| *(didascalia)* | They hang up their torches on some ridge in the cavern. |
| ORDONIO | He was a man different from other men,<br>And he despised them, yet revered himself. |
| ISIDORE | *(aside)* He? He despised? Thou'rt speaking of thyself!<br>I am on my guard, however: no surprise. |
| *(didascalia)* | Then to ORDONIO. |
| ISIDORE | What, he was mad? |
| ORDONIO | All men seemed mad to him!<br>[[Nature]] had made him for some other planet,<br>And pressed his [[Soul\|soul]] into a human shape<br>By accident or malice. In this world<br>He found no fit companion. |
| ISIDORE | *(aside)* Of himself he speaks.<br>Alas! poor wretch!<br>Mad men are mostly proud. |
| ORDONIO | He walked [[Alone\|alone]],<br>And [[Phantom\|phantom]] thoughts unsought-for troubled him.<br>Something within would still be shadowing out<br>All possibilities; and with these shadows<br>His mind held dalliance. Once, as so it happened,<br>A fancy crossed him wilder than the rest:<br>To this in moody murmur and low voice<br>He yielded utterance, as some talk in [[Sleep\|sleep]]:<br>The man who heard him. —<br>Why did'st thou look round? |
| ISIDORE | I have a prattler three years old, my lord!<br>In [[Truth\|truth]] he is my darling. As I went<br>From forth my door, he made a moan in [[Sleep\|sleep]] —<br>But I am talking idly — pray proceed!<br>And what did this man? |
| ORDONIO | With this human hand<br>He gave a substance and reality<br>To that wild fancy of a possible thing. —<br>[[Well]] it was done!<br>Why babblest thou of [[Guilt\|guilt]]?<br>The deed was done, and it passed fairly off.<br>And he whose tale I tell thee — dost thou listen? |
| ISIDORE | I would, my lord, you were by my [[Fire\|fire]]-side,<br>I'd listen to you with an eager eye,<br>Though you began this cloudy tale at midnight,<br>But I do listen — pray proceed, my lord. |
| ORDONIO | Where was I? |
| ISIDORE | He of whom you tell the tale — |
| ORDONIO | Surveying all things with a quiet scorn,<br>Tamed himself down to living purposes,<br>The occupations and the semblances<br>Of ordinary men — and such he seemed!<br>But that same over ready agent — he — |
| ISIDORE | Ah! what of him, my lord? |
| ORDONIO | He proved a traitor,<br>Betrayed the mystery to a brother-traitor,<br>And they between them hatch'd a damnéd plot<br>To hunt him down to infamy and [[Death\|death]].<br>What did the Valdez? I am proud of the name<br>Since he dared do it. — |
| *(didascalia)* | ORDONIO grasps his [[Sword\|sword]], and turns off from ISIDORE, then after a pause returns. |
| ORDONIO | Our links burn dimly. |
| ISIDORE | A dark tale darkly finished! Nay, my lord!<br>Tell what he did. |
| ORDONIO | That which his wisdom prompted —<br>He made the traitor meet him in this cavern,<br>And here he kill'd the traitor. |
| ISIDORE | No! the [[Fool\|fool]]!<br>He had not wit enough to be a traitor.<br>Poor thick-eyed beetle! not to have foreseen<br>That he who gulled thee with a whimpered lie<br>To murder his own brother, would not scruple<br>To murder thee, if e'er his [[Guilt\|guilt]] grew jealous,<br>And he could steal upon thee in the dark! |
| ORDONIO | Thou would'st not then have come, if — |
| ISIDORE | Oh yes, my lord!<br>I would have met him arm'd, and scar'd the coward. |
| *(didascalia)* | ISIDORE throws off his robe; shews himself armed, and draws his [[Sword\|sword]]. |
| ORDONIO | Now this is excellent and warms the [[Blood\|blood]]!<br>My heart was drawing back, drawing me back<br>With weak and womanish scruples. Now my vengeance<br>Beckons me onwards with a warrior's mien,<br>And claims that life, my pity robb'd her of —<br>Now will I kill thee, thankless slave, and count it<br>Among my comfortable thoughts hereafter. |
| ISIDORE | And all my little ones fatherless —<br>Die thou first. |
| *(didascalia)* | They fight, ORDONIO disarms ISIDORE, and in disarming him throws his [[Sword\|sword]] up that recess opposite to which they were standing. ISIDORE hurries into the recess with his torch, ORDONIO follows him; a loud cry of 'Traitor! [[Monster]]!' is heard from the cavern, and in a moment ORDONIO returns [[Alone\|alone]]. |
| ORDONIO | I have hurl'd him down the chasm! treason for treason.<br>He dreamt of it: henceforward let him [[Sleep\|sleep]],<br>A dreamless [[Sleep\|sleep]], from which no wife can wake him.<br>His dream too is made out — Now for his friend. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit ORDONIO. |
