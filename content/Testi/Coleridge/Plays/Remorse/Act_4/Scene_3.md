| Chi parla | Battuta |
|---|---|
| *(didascalia)* | The [[Mountains\|mountains]] by moonlight. ALHADRA [[Alone\|alone]] in a Moorish dress. |
| ALHADRA | Yon hanging woods, that touch'd by [[Autumn\|autumn]] seem<br>As they were blossoming hues of [[Fire\|fire]] and gold<br>The flower-like woods, most lovely in [[Decay\|decay]],<br>The many clouds, the sea, the rock, the sands.<br>Lie in the silent moonshine: and the owl,<br>(Strange! very strange!) the screech-owl only wakes!<br>Sole voice, sole eye of all this world of [[Beauty\|beauty]]!<br>Unless, perhaps, she sing her screeching [[Song\|song]]<br>To a herd of wolves, that skulk athirst for [[Blood\|blood]].<br>Why such a thing am I? — Where are these men?<br>I need the sympathy of human faces,<br>To beat away this deep contempt for all things,<br>Which quenches my [[Revenge\|revenge]]. O! would to Alla,<br>[[The Raven\|The raven]], or the sea-mew, were appointed<br>To bring me food! or rather that my [[Soul\|soul]]<br>Could drink in life from the universal air!<br>It were a lot divine in some small skiff<br>Along some Ocean's boundless [[Solitude\|solitude]],<br>To float for ever with a careless course.<br>And think myself the only being alive!<br>My children! — Isidore's children! — Son of Valdez,<br>This hath new strung mine arm. Thou coward [[Tyrant\|tyrant]]!<br>To stupify a woman's heart with anguish<br>Till she forgot — even that she was a mother! |
| *(didascalia)* | She fixes her eye on the earth. Then drop in one after another, from different parts of the stage, a considerable number of Morescoes, all in Moorish garments and Moorish armour. They form a circle at a distance round ALHADRA, and remain silent till NAOMI enters. |
| NAOMI | Woman! May Alla and the [[Prophet]] bless thee!<br>We have obeyed thy call. Where is our chief?<br>And why didst thou enjoin these Moorish garments? |
| ALHADRA | *(raising her eyes, and looking round on the circle)*<br>Warriors of Mahomet! faithful in the battle!<br>My countrymen! Come ye prepared to work<br>An honourable deed? And would ye work it<br>In the slave's garb? Curse on those Christian robes!<br>They are spell-blasted: and whoever wears them,<br>His arm shrinks wither'd, his heart melts away,<br>And his bones soften. |
| NAOMI | Where is Isidore? |
| ALHADRA | This [[Night\|night]] I went from forth my [[house]], and left<br>His children all asleep: and he was living!<br>And I return'd and found them still asleep,<br>But he had perished —— |
| ALL MORESCOES | Perished? |
| ALHADRA | He had perished!<br>[[Sleep]] on, poor babes! not one of you doth know<br>That he is fatherless — a desolate [[Orphan\|orphan]]!<br>Why should we wake them? Can an infant's arm<br>[[Revenge]] his murder? |
| ONE MORESCO | *(to another)* Did she say his murder? |
| NAOMI | Murder? Not murdered? |
| ALHADRA | Murdered by a Christian! |
| *(didascalia)* | They all at once draw their sabres. |
| ALHADRA | *(to Naomi, who advances from the circle)* Brother of Zagri! fling away thy [[Sword\|sword]];<br>This is thy chieftain's! |
| *(didascalia)* | He steps forward to take it. |
| ALHADRA | Dost thou dare receive it?<br>For I have sworn by Alla and the [[Prophet]],<br>No tear shall dim these eyes, this woman's heart<br>Shall heave no groan, till I have seen that [[Sword\|sword]]<br>Wet with the life-[[Blood\|blood]] of the son of Valdez! |
| *(didascalia)* | A pause. |
| ALHADRA | Ordonio was your chieftain's murderer! |
| NAOMI | He dies, by Alla! |
| ALL | *(kneeling)* By Alla! |
| ALHADRA | This [[Night\|night]] your chieftain armed himself,<br>And hurried from me. But I followed him<br>At distance, till I saw him enter — there! |
| NAOMI | The cavern? |
| ALHADRA | Yes, the mouth of yonder cavern<br>After a while I saw the son of Valdez<br>Rush by with flaring torch; he likewise entered.<br>There was another and a longer pause;<br>And once, methought I heard the clash of swords!<br>And soon the son of Valdez re-appeared:<br>He flung his torch towards the [[Moon\|moon]] in sport,<br>And seemed as he were mirthful! I stood listening,<br>Impatient for the footsteps of my husband! |
| NAOMI | Thou called'st him? |
| ALHADRA | I crept into the cavern —<br>'Twas dark and very silent.<br>What said'st thou?<br>No! no! I did not dare call, Isidore,<br>Lest I should hear no answer! A brief while,<br>Belike, I lost all thought and [[Memory\|memory]]<br>Of that for which I came! After that pause,<br>O Heaven! I heard a groan, and followed it:<br>And yet another groan, which guided me<br>Into a strange recess — and there was light,<br>A hideous light! his torch lay on the ground;<br>Its flame burnt dimly o'er a chasm's brink:<br>I spake; and whilst I spake, a feeble groan<br>Came from that chasm! it was his last! his [[Death\|death]]-groan! |
| NAOMI | Comfort her, Alla! |
| ALHADRA | I stood in unimaginable trance<br>And agony that cannot be remembered,<br>Listening with horrid [[Hope\|hope]] to hear a groan!<br>But I had heard his last: my husband's [[Death\|death]]-groan! |
| NAOMI | Haste! let us onward. |
| ALHADRA | I looked far down the pit —<br>My sight was bounded by a jutting [[Fragment\|fragment]]:<br>And it was stained with [[Blood\|blood]]. Then first I shrieked,<br>My eye-balls burnt, my brain grew hot as [[Fire\|fire]],<br>And all the hanging drops of the wet roof<br>Turned into [[Blood\|blood]] — I saw them turn to [[Blood\|blood]]!<br>And I was leaping wildly down the chasm,<br>When on the farther brink I saw his [[Sword\|sword]],<br>And it said, Vengeance! — Curses on my tongue!<br>The [[Moon\|moon]] hath moved in Heaven, and I am here,<br>And he hath not had vengeance! Isidore!<br>Spirit of Isidore! thy murderer lives!<br>Away! away! |
| ALL | Away! away! |
| *(didascalia)* | She rushes off, all following her. |
