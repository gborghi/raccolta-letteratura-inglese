| *(didascalia)* | The interior [[Court]] of a Saracenic or Gothic [[Castle]], with the Iron Gate of a Dungeon visible. |
| TERESA | Heart-chilling superstition! thou canst glaze<br>Ev'n pity's eye with her own frozen tear.<br>In vain I urge the tortures that await him;<br>Even Selma, reverend guardian of my [[Childhood\|childhood]],<br>My second mother, shuts her heart against me!<br>[[Well]], I have won from her what most imports<br>The present need, this secret of the dungeon<br>Known only to herself. — A Moor! a Sorcerer!<br>No, I have [[Faith\|faith]], that [[Nature]] ne'er permitted<br>Baseness to wear a form so noble. True,<br>I [[Doubt\|doubt]] not that Ordonio had suborned him<br>To act some part in some unholy fraud;<br>As little [[Doubt\|doubt]], that for some unknown purpose<br>He hath baffled his suborner, terror-struck him,<br>And that Ordonio meditates [[Revenge\|revenge]]!<br>But my resolve is fixed! myself will rescue him,<br>And learn if haply he knew aught of Alvar. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter VALDEZ. |
| VALDEZ | Still sad? — and gazing at the massive door<br>Of that fell dungeon which thou ne'er had'st sight of,<br>Save what, perchance, thy infant fancy shap'd it<br>When the [[Nurse\|nurse]] still'd thy cries with unmeant threats.<br>Now by my [[Faith\|faith]], girl! this same wizard haunts thee!<br>A stately man, and eloquent and tender —<br>Who then need [[Wonder\|wonder]] if a lady sighs<br>Even at the thought of what these stern Dominicans — |
| TERESA | The horror of their ghastly punishments<br>Doth so o'ertop the height of all compassion,<br>That I should feel too little for mine enemy,<br>If it were possible I could feel more,<br>Even though the dearest inmates of our household<br>Were doom'd to suffer them. That such things are — |
| VALDEZ | Hush, thoughtless woman! |
| TERESA | Nay, it wakes within me<br>More than a woman's spirit. |
| VALDEZ | No more of this —<br>What if Monviedro or his creatures hear us!<br>I dare not listen to you. |
| TERESA | My honoured lord,<br>These were my Alvar's lessons, and whene'er<br>I bend me o'er his portrait, I repeat them,<br>As if to give a voice to the mute image. |
| VALDEZ | —— We have mourned for Alvar.<br>Of his sad fate there now remains no [[Doubt\|doubt]].<br>Have I no other son? |
| TERESA | Speak not of him!<br>That low imposture! That mysterious picture!<br>If this be [[Madness\|madness]], must I wed a madman?<br>And if not [[Madness\|madness]], there is mystery,<br>And [[Guilt\|guilt]] doth lurk behind it. |
| VALDEZ | Is this [[Well\|well]]? |
| TERESA | Yes, it is [[Truth\|truth]]: saw you his countenance?<br>How rage, remorse, and scorn, and stupid [[Fear\|fear]]<br>Displaced each other with swift interchanges?<br>O that I had indeed the sorcerer's power. ——<br>I would call up before thine eyes the image<br>Of my betrothed Alvar, of thy first-born!<br>His own fair countenance, his kingly forehead,<br>His tender smiles, [[Love\|love]]'s day-[[Dawn\|dawn]] on his lips!<br>That spiritual and almost heavenly light<br>In his commanding eye — his mien heroic,<br>Virtue's own native heraldry! to man<br>Genial, and pleasant to his guardian angel.<br>Whene'er he gladden'd, how the gladness spread<br>Wide round him! and when oft with swelling [[Tears\|tears]],<br>Flash'd through by indignation, he bewail'd<br>The wrongs of Belgium's martyr'd patriots,<br>Oh, what a grief was there — for joy to envy,<br>Or gaze upon enamour'd!<br>O my father!<br>Recall that morning when we knelt together,<br>And thou didst bless our loves! O even now,<br>Even now, my sire! to thy mind's eye present him,<br>As at that moment he [[Rose\|rose]] up before thee,<br>Stately, with beaming look! Place, place beside him<br>Ordonio's dark perturbéd countenance!<br>Then bid me (Oh thou could'st not) bid me turn<br>From him, the joy, the triumph of our kind!<br>To take in exchange that brooding man, who never<br>Lifts up his eye from the earth, unless to scowl. |
| VALDEZ | Ungrateful woman! I have tried to stifle<br>An old man's passion! was it not enough,<br>That thou hast made my son a restless man,<br>Banish'd his health, and half unhing'd his reason;<br>But that thou wilt insult him with suspicion?<br>And toil to blast his [[Honour\|honour]]? I am old,<br>A comfortless old man! |
| TERESA | O grief! to hear<br>Hateful entreaties from a voice we [[Love\|love]]! |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter a Peasant and presents a letter to VALDEZ. |
| VALDEZ | (reading it). 'He dares not venture hither!' Why, what can this mean?<br>'Lest the Familiars of the [[Inquisition]],<br>That watch around my gates, should intercept him;<br>But he conjures me, that without delay<br>I hasten to him — for my own sake entreats me<br>To guard from danger him I hold imprison'd —<br>He will reveal a secret, the joy of which<br>Will even outweigh the sorrow.' — Why what can this be?<br>Perchance it is some Moorish stratagem,<br>To have in me a hostage for his safety.<br>Nay, that they dare not! Ho! collect my servants!<br>I will go thither — let them arm themselves. |
| *(didascalia)* | [Exit VALDEZ. |
| TERESA ([[Alone\|alone]]) | The [[Moon\|moon]] is high in heaven, and all is hush'd.<br>Yet anxious listener! I have seem'd to hear<br>A low dead thunder mutter thro' the [[Night\|night]],<br>As 'twere a giant angry in his [[Sleep\|sleep]].<br>O Alvar! Alvar! that they could return,<br>Those blessed days that imitated heaven,<br>When we two wont to walk at eventide;<br>When we saw nought but [[Beauty\|beauty]]; when we heard<br>The voice of that Almighty One who loved us<br>In every gale that breathed, and wave that murmur'd!<br>O we have listen'd, even till high-wrought pleasure<br>Hath half assumed the countenance of grief,<br>And the deep sigh seemed to heave up a weight<br>Of bliss, that pressed too heavy on the heart. |
| *(didascalia)* | [A pause. |
| TERESA | And this majestic Moor, seems he not one<br>Who oft and long communing with my Alvar<br>Hath drunk in kindred lustre from his presence,<br>And guides me to him with reflected light?<br>What if in yon dark dungeon coward treachery<br>Be groping for him with envenomed poniard —<br>Hence, womanish fears, traitors to [[Love\|love]] and duty —<br>I'll free him. |
| *(didascalia)* | [Exit TERESA. |
