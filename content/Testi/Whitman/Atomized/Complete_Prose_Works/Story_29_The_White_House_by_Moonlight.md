THE WHITE [[house|HOUSE]] BY MOONLIGHT

_February 24th._--A spell of fine soft weather. I wander about a good
deal, sometimes at [[Night|night]] under the [[Moon|moon]]. Tonight took a long look at
the President's [[house]]. The white portico--the palace-like, tall,
round columns, spotless as [[Snow|snow]]--the walls also--the tender and
soft moonlight, flooding the pale marble, and making peculiar faint
languishing shades, not shadows--everywhere a soft transparent
hazy, thin, blue [[Moon|moon]]-lace, hanging in the air--the brilliant and
extra-plentiful clusters of gas, on and around the fa�ade, columns,
portico, &c.--everything so white, so marbly pure and dazzling, yet
soft--the White [[house|House]] of future [[Poems|poems]], and of [[Dreams|dreams]] and dramas, there
in the soft and copious [[Moon|moon]]--the gorgeous front, in the trees, under
the lustrous flooding [[Moon|moon]], full of realty, full of illusion--the forms
of the trees, leafless, silent, in trunk and myriad--angles of branches,
under the [[Stars|stars]] and sky--the White [[house|House]] of the land, and of [[Beauty|beauty]] and
[[Night|night]]--sentries at the gates, and by the portico, silent, pacing there
in blue overcoats--stopping you not at all, but eyeing you with sharp
eyes, whichever way you move.
