| *(didascalia)* | Enter the Bastard With a lightand [[Hubert]] With a [[Pistol|pistol]], severally |
| [[Hubert|HUBERT]] | Who’s there? Speak, ho! Speak quickly, or I shoot. |
| BASTARD | A friend. What art thou? |
| [[Hubert|HUBERT]] | Of the part of England. |
| BASTARD | Whither dost thou go? |
| [[Hubert|HUBERT]] | What’s that to thee?<br>Why may not I demand of thine affairs<br>As [[Well|well]] as thou of mine? |
| BASTARD | [[Hubert]], I think. |
| [[Hubert|HUBERT]] | Thou hast a perfect thought.<br>I will upon all hazards [[Well|well]] believe<br>Thou art my friend that know’st my tongue so [[Well|well]].<br>Who art thou? |
| BASTARD | Who thou wilt. An if thou please,<br>Thou mayst befriend me so much as to think<br>I come one way of the Plantagenets. |
| [[Hubert|HUBERT]] | Unkind remembrance! Thou and eyeless [[Night|night]]<br>Have done me shame. Brave soldier, pardon me<br>That any accent breaking from thy tongue<br>Should ’scape the true acquaintance of mine ear. |
| BASTARD | Come, come, sans compliment. What news abroad? |
| [[Hubert|HUBERT]] | Why, here walk I in the black brow of [[Night|night]]<br>To find you out. |
| BASTARD | Brief, then, and what’s the news? |
| [[Hubert|HUBERT]] | O my sweet sir, news fitting to the [[Night|night]]:<br>Black, fearful, comfortless, and horrible. |
| BASTARD | Show me the very wound of this ill news;<br>I am no woman, I’ll not swoon at it. |
| [[Hubert|HUBERT]] | The [[King]], I [[Fear|fear]], is poisoned by a monk.<br>I left him almost speechless, and broke out<br>To acquaint you with this evil, that you might<br>The better arm you to the sudden [[Time|time]]<br>Than if you had at leisure known of this. |
| BASTARD | How did he take it? Who did taste to him? |
| [[Hubert|HUBERT]] | A monk, I tell you, a resolved [[Villain|villain]],<br>Whose bowels suddenly burst out. The [[King]]<br>Yet speaks, and peradventure may recover. |
| BASTARD | Who didst thou leave to tend his majesty? |
| [[Hubert|HUBERT]] | Why, know you not? The lords are all come back,<br>And brought Prince Henry in their company,<br>At whose request the [[King]] hath pardoned them,<br>And they are all about his majesty. |
| BASTARD | Withhold thine indignation, mighty heaven,<br>And tempt us not to bear above our power.<br>I’ll tell thee, [[Hubert]], half my power this [[Night|night]],<br>Passing these flats, are taken by the tide.<br>These [[Lincoln]] Washes have devoured them;<br>Myself, [[Well|well]] mounted, hardly have escaped.<br>Away before! Conduct me to the [[King]].<br>I [[Doubt|doubt]] he will be dead or ere I come. Exeunt |
