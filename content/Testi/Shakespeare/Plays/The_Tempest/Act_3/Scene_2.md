|---|---|
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Caliban]], Stefano, and [[Trinculo]] |
| STEFANO | (to [[Caliban]]) Tell not me. When the butt is out we will drink [[Water|water]], not a drop before. Therefore bear up and board ’em. Servant [[Monster|monster]], drink to me. |
| [[Trinculo|TRINCULO]] | Servant [[Monster|monster]]? The folly of this island! They say there’s but five upon this isle. We are three of them; if th’other two be brained like us, the state totters. |
| STEFANO | Drink, servant [[Monster|monster]], when I bid thee. Thy eyes are almost set in thy head. |
| [[Trinculo|TRINCULO]] | Where should they be set else? He were a brave [[Monster|monster]] indeed if they were set in his tail. |
| STEFANO | My man-[[Monster|monster]] hath drowned his tongue in sack. For my part, the sea cannot drown me. I swam, ere I could recover the shore, five and thirty leagues, off and on. By this light, thou shalt be my lieutenant, [[Monster|monster]], or my standard. |
| [[Trinculo|TRINCULO]] | Your lieutenant if you list; he’s no standard. |
| STEFANO | We’ll not run, Monsieur [[Monster]]. |
| [[Trinculo|TRINCULO]] | Nor go neither; but you’ll lie like [[dogs]], and yet say nothing neither. |
| STEFANO | [[Moon]]-calf, speak once in thy life, if thou beest a good [[Moon|moon]]-calf. |
| [[Caliban|CALIBAN]] | How does thy [[Honour|honour]]? Let me lick thy shoe.<br>I’ll not serve him; he is not valiant. |
| [[Trinculo|TRINCULO]] | Thou liest, most ignorant [[Monster|monster]]! I am in case to jostle a constable. Why, thou debauched fish, thou, was there ever man a coward that hath drunk so much sack as I today? Wilt thou tell a monstrous lie, being but half a fish and half a [[Monster|monster]]? |
| [[Caliban|CALIBAN]] | (to Stefano) Lo, how he mocks me! Wilt thou let him, my lord? |
| [[Trinculo|TRINCULO]] | ‘Lord’ quoth he? That a [[Monster|monster]] should be such a natural! |
| [[Caliban|CALIBAN]] | (to Stefano) Lo, lo, again! Bite him to [[Death|death]], I prithee. |
| STEFANO | [[Trinculo]], keep a good tongue in your head. If you prove a mutineer, the next tree. The poor [[Monster|monster]]’s my subject, and he shall not suffer indignity. |
| [[Caliban|CALIBAN]] | I thank my noble lord. Wilt thou be pleased<br>To hearken once again to the suit I made to thee? |
| STEFANO | Marry, will I. Kneel and repeat it. I will stand, and so shall [[Trinculo]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Caliban]] kneels. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Ariel]], invisible |
| [[Caliban|CALIBAN]] | As I told thee before, I am subject to a [[Tyrant|tyrant]], a sorcerer, that by his cunning hath cheated me of the island. |
| [[Ariel|ARIEL]] | Thou liest. |
| [[Caliban|CALIBAN]] | (to [[Trinculo]])<br>Thou liest, thou jesting monkey, thou.<br>I would my valiant master would destroy thee.<br>I do not lie. |
| STEFANO | [[Trinculo]], if you trouble him any more in’s tale, by this hand, I will supplant some of your teeth. |
| [[Trinculo|TRINCULO]] | Why, I said nothing. |
| STEFANO | Mum, then, and no more. (To [[Caliban]]) Proceed. |
| [[Caliban|CALIBAN]] | I say by sorcery he got this isle;<br>From me he got it. If thy [[Greatness|greatness]] will<br>[[Revenge]] it on him—for I know thou dar’st,<br>But this thing dare not— |
| STEFANO | That’s most certain. |
| [[Caliban|CALIBAN]] | Thou shalt be lord of it, and I’ll serve thee. |
| STEFANO | How now shall this be compassed? Canst thou bring me to the party? |
| [[Caliban|CALIBAN]] | Yea, yea, my lord. I’ll yield him thee asleep<br>Where thou mayst knock a nail into his head. |
| [[Ariel|ARIEL]] | Thou liest, thou canst not. |
| [[Caliban|CALIBAN]] | What a pied ninny’s thisl (To Tririculo) Thou scurvy<br>patch!<br>(To Stefano) I do beseech thy [[Greatness|greatness]] give him blows,<br>And take his bottle from him. When that’s gone<br>He shall drink naught but brine, for I’ll not show him<br>Where the quick freshes are. |
| STEFANO | [[Trinculo]], run into no further danger. Interrupt the [[Monster|monster]] one word further, and, by this hand, I’ll turn my [[Mercy|mercy]] out o’doors and make a stockfish of thee. |
| [[Trinculo|TRINCULO]] | Why, what did I? I did nothing. I’ll go farther off. |
| STEFANO | Didst thou not say he lied? |
| [[Ariel|ARIEL]] | Thou liest. |
| STEFANO | Do I so? (Striking [[Trinculo]]) Take thou that. As you like this, give me the lie another [[Time|time]]. |
| [[Trinculo|TRINCULO]] | I did not give the lie. Out o‘your wits and hearing too? A pox o’your bottle! This can sack and drinking do. A murrain on your [[Monster|monster]], and the devil take your fingers. |
| [[Caliban|CALIBAN]] | Ha, ha, ha! |
| STEFANO | Now forward with your tale. (To [[Trinculo]]) Prithee, stand further off. |
| [[Caliban|CALIBAN]] | Beat him enough; after a little [[Time|time]]<br>I’ll beat him too. |
| STEFANO | (to [[Trinculo]])<br>Stand farther. (To [[Caliban]]) Come, proceed. |
| [[Caliban|CALIBAN]] | Why, as I told thee, ’tis a custom with him<br>I’th’ afternoon to [[Sleep|sleep]]. There thou mayst brain him,<br>Having first seized his books; or with a log<br>Batter his skull, or paunch him with a stake,<br>Or cut his weasand with thy knife. Remember<br>First to possess his books, for without them<br>He’s but a sot as I am, nor hath not<br>One spirit to command—they all do hate him<br>As rootedly as I. Burn but his books.<br>He has brave utensils, for so he calls them,<br>Which when he has a [[house]] he’ll deck withal.<br>And that most deeply to consider is<br>The [[Beauty|beauty]] of his daughter. He himself<br>Calls her a nonpareil. I never saw a woman<br>But only Sycorax my dam and she,<br>But she as far surpasseth Sycorax<br>As great’st does least. |
| STEFANO | Is it so brave a lass? |
| [[Caliban|CALIBAN]] | Ay, lord. She will become thy bed, I warrant,<br>And bring thee forth brave brood. |
| STEFANO | [[Monster]], I will kill this man. His daughter and I will be [[King|king]] and [[Queen|queen]]—save our graces!—and [[Trinculo]] and thyself shall be viceroys. Dost thou like the plot, [[Trinculo]]? no |
| [[Trinculo|TRINCULO]] | Excellent. |
| STEFANO | Give me thy hand. I am sorry I beat thee. But while thou liv’st, keep a good tongue in thy head. |
| *(didascalia)* | Within this half hour will he be asleep. |
| [[Caliban|CALIBAN]] | Wilt thou destroy him then? |
| STEFANO | Ay, on mine [[Honour|honour]]. |
| [[Ariel|ARIEL]] | (aside) This will I tell my master. |
| [[Caliban|CALIBAN]] | Thou mak’st me merry; I am full of pleasure.<br>Let us be jocund. Will you troll the catch<br>You taught me but while-ere? |
| STEFANO | At thy request, [[Monster|monster]], I will do reason, any reason.—Come on, [[Trinculo]], let us sing.<br>(Sings)<br>Flout ’em and cout ’em, And scout ’em and flout ’em.<br>Thought is free. |
| [[Caliban|CALIBAN]] | That’s not the tune.<br>[[Ariel]] plays the tune on a tabor and pipe |
| STEFANO | What is this same? |
| [[Trinculo|TRINCULO]] | This is the tune of our catch, played by the picture of Nobody. |
| STEFANO | (calls towards [[Ariel]]) If thou beest a man, show thyself in thy likeness. If thou beest a devil, take’t as thou list. |
| [[Trinculo|TRINCULO]] | O, forgive me my sins! |
| STEFANO | He that dies pays all debts. (Calls) I defy thee.—[[Mercy]] upon us! |
| [[Caliban|CALIBAN]] | Art thou afeard? |
| STEFANO | No, [[Monster|monster]], not I. |
| [[Caliban|CALIBAN]] | Be not afeard. The isle is full of noises, |
| *(didascalia)* | Sounds, and sweet airs, that give delight and hurt |
| [[Caliban|CALIBAN]] | not.<br>Sometimes a thousand twangling instruments<br>Will hum about mine ears, and sometime voices<br>That if I then had waked after long [[Sleep|sleep]]<br>Will make me [[Sleep|sleep]] again; and then in dreaming<br>The clouds methought would open and show riches<br>Ready to drop upon me, that when I waked<br>I cried to dream again. |
| STEFANO | This will prove a brave kingdom to me, where I shall have my music for nothing. |
| [[Caliban|CALIBAN]] | When Prospero is destroyed. |
| STEFANO | That shall be by and by. I remember the story. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit [[Ariel]], playing music |
| [[Trinculo|TRINCULO]] | The sound is going away. Let’s follow it, and after do our work. |
| STEFANO | Lead, [[Monster|monster]]; we’ll follow.—I would I could see this taborer. He lays it on. |
| TRICULO | (to [[Caliban]]) Wilt come? I’ll follow Stefano. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt |
