| *(didascalia)* | Enter Ventidius, with Silius and other Roman soldiers, as it were in triumph; the dead [[Body|body]] of Pacorus borne before him |
| VENTIDIUS | Now, darting Parthia, art thou struck; and now<br>Pleased fortune does of Marcus Crassus’ [[Death|death]]<br>Make me revenger. Bear the [[King]]’s son’s [[Body|body]]<br>Before our army. Thy Pacorus, Orodes,<br>Pays this for Marcus Crassus. |
| SILIUS | Noble Ventidius,<br>Whilst yet with Parthian [[Blood|blood]] thy [[Sword|sword]] is warm,<br>The fugitive Parthians follow. Spur through Media,<br>Mesopotamia, and the shelters whither<br>The routed fly. So thy grand captain, Antony,<br>Shall set thee on triumphant chariots and<br>Put garlands on thy head. |
| VENTIDIUS | O Silius, Silius,<br>I have done enough. A lower place, note [[Well|well]],<br>May make too great an act. For learn this, Silius:<br>Better to leave undone than by our deed<br>Acquire too high a fame when him we serve’s away.<br>Caesar and Antony have ever won<br>More in their officer than person. Sossius,<br>One of my place in Syria, his lieutenant,<br>For quick accumulation of renown,<br>Which he achieved by th’ minute, lost his favour.<br>Who does 4’th’ wars more than his captain can<br>Becomes his captain’s captain; and [[Ambition|ambition]],<br>The soldier’s virtue, rather makes choice of loss<br>Than gain which darkens him.<br>I could do more to do Antonius good,<br>But ’twould offend him, and in his offence<br>Should my performance perish. |
| SILIUS | Thou hast, Ventidius, that<br>Without the which a soldier and his [[Sword|sword]]<br>Grants scarce distinction. Thou wilt write to Antony? |
| VENTIDIUS | I’ll humbly signify what in his name,<br>That magical word of war, we have effected;<br>How, with his banners and his [[Well|well]]-paid ranks,<br>The ne‘er-yet-beaten [[Horse|horse]] of Parthia<br>We have jaded out o’th’ field. |
| SILIUS | Where is he now? |
| VENTIDIUS | He purposeth to Athens; whither, with what haste<br>The weight we must convey with’s will permit,<br>We shall appear before him.—On there; pass along. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt |
