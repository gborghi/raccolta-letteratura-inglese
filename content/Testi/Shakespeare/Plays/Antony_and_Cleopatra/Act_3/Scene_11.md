| *(didascalia)* | Enter Antony with Attendants |
| ANTONY | Hark, the land bids me tread no more upon’t,<br>It is ashamed to bear me. Friends, come hither.<br>I am so lated in the world that I<br>Have lost my way for ever. I have a [[Ship|ship]]<br>Laden with gold. Take that; divide it, fly,<br>And make your peace with Caesar. |
| ATTENDANTS | Fly? Not we. |
| ANTONY | I have fled myself, and have instructed cowards<br>To run and show their shoulders. Friends, be gone.<br>I have myself resolved upon a course<br>Which has no need of you. Be gone.<br>My treasure’s in the harbour. Take it. O,<br>I followed that I blush to look upon.<br>My very hairs do mutiny, for the white<br>Reprove the brown for rashness, and they them<br>For [[Fear|fear]] and doting. Friends, be gone. You shall<br>Have letters from me to some friends that will<br>Sweep your way for you. Pray you, look not sad,<br>Nor make replies of loathness. Take the hint<br>Which my [[Despair|despair]] proclaims. Let that be left<br>Which [[Leaves|leaves]] itself. To the seaside straightway!<br>I will possess you of that [[Ship|ship]] and treasure.<br>Leave me, I pray, a little. Pray you now,<br>Nay, do so; for indeed I have lost command.<br>Therefore I pray you; I’ll see you by and by. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt attendants |
| ANTONY | He sits down. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Cleopatra]] led by [[Charmian]], Iras, and Eros |
| EROS | Nay, gentle madam, to him. Comfort him. |
| IRAS | Do, most dear [[Queen]]. |
| [[Charmian|CHARMIAN]] | Do. Why, what else? |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | Let me sit down. O Juno!<br>She sits down |
| ANTONY | No, no, no, no, no. |
| EROS | (to Antony) See you here, sir? |
| ANTONY | O fie, fie, fie! |
| [[Charmian|CHARMIAN]] | Madam. |
| IRAS | Madam. O good Empress! |
| EROS | Sir, sir. |
| ANTONY | Yes, my lord, yes. He at Philippi kept<br>His [[Sword|sword]] e’en like a dancer, while I struck<br>The lean and wrinkled [[Cassius]]; and ’twas I<br>That the mad [[Brutus]] ended. He [[Alone|alone]]<br>Dealt on lieutenantry, and no practice had<br>In the brave squares of war. Yet now—no matter. |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] (rising, to [[Charmian]] and Iras) Ah, stand by. |
| EROS | The [[Queen]], my lord, the [[Queen]]. |
| IRAS | Go to him, madam.<br>Speak to him. He’s unqualitied<br>With very shame. |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | [[Well]] then, sustain me. O! |
| EROS | Most noble sir, arise. The [[Queen]] approaches.<br>Her head’s declined, and [[Death|death]] will seize her but<br>Your comfort makes the rescue. |
| ANTONY | I have offended reputation;<br>A most unnoble swerving. |
| EROS | Sir, the [[Queen]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | ANTONY rising |
| EROS | O, whither hast thou led me, [[Egypt]]? See<br>How I convey my shame out of thine eyes<br>By looking back what I have left behind<br>’Stroyed in dishonour. |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | O, my lord, my lord, Forgive my fearful sails! I little thought<br>You would have followed. |
| ANTONY | [[Egypt]], thou knew’st too [[Well|well]]<br>My heart was to thy rudder tied by th’ strings,<br>And thou shouldst tow me after. O’er my spirit<br>Thy full supremacy thou knew’st, and that<br>Thy beck might from the bidding of the gods<br>Command me. |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | O, my pardon! |
| ANTONY | Now I must<br>To the young man send humble treaties, dodge<br>And palter in the shifts of lowness, who<br>With half the bulk o’th’ world played as I pleased,<br>Making and marring fortunes. You did know<br>How much you were my conqueror, and that<br>My [[Sword|sword]], made weak by my affection, would<br>Obey it on all cause. |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | Pardon, pardon! |
| ANTONY | Fall not a tear, I say. One of them rates<br>All that is won and lost. Give me a kiss.<br>He kisses her<br>Even this repays me. (To an Attendant) We sent our<br>schoolmaster;<br>Is a come back? (To [[Cleopatra]]) [[Love]], I am full of lead.<br>(Calling) Some wine |
| *(didascalia)* | Within there, and our viands! Fortune knows |
| ANTONY | We scorn her most when most she offers blows. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt |
