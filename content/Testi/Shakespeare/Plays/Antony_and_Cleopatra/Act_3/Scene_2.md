| *(didascalia)* | Enter Agrippa at one door, [[Enobarbus]] at another AGRIPPA What, are the brothers parted? |
| [[Enobarbus|ENOBARBUS]] | They have dispatched with [[Pompey]]; he is gone.<br>The other three are sealing. [[Octavia]] weeps<br>To part from Rome, Caesar is sad, and [[Lepidus]]<br>Since [[Pompey]]’s feast, as Menas says, is troubled<br>With the green-sickness. |
| AGRIPPA | ’Tis a noble [[Lepidus]]. |
| [[Enobarbus|ENOBARBUS]] | A very fine one. O, how he loves Caesar! |
| AGRIPPA | Nay, but how dearly he adores [[Mark Antony]]! |
| [[Enobarbus|ENOBARBUS]] | Caesar? Why, he’s the Jupiter of men. |
| AGRIPPA | What’s Antony—the god of Jupiter? |
| [[Enobarbus|ENOBARBUS]] | Spake you of Caesar? How, the nonpareil? |
| AGRIPPA | O Antony, O thou Arabian bird! |
| [[Enobarbus|ENOBARBUS]] | Would you praise Caesar, say ‘Caesar’; go no further. |
| AGRIPPA | Indeed, he plied them both with excellent praises. |
| [[Enobarbus|ENOBARBUS]] | But he loves Caesar best; yet he loves Antony—<br>Hoo! Hearts, tongues, figures, scribes, bards, poets,<br>cannot<br>Think, speak, cast, write, sing, number—hoo!—<br>His [[Love|love]] to Antony. But as for Caesar—<br>Kneel down, kneel down, and [[Wonder|wonder]]. |
| AGRIPPA | Both he loves. |
| [[Enobarbus|ENOBARBUS]] | They are his shards, and he their beetle. |
| *(didascalia)* | Trumpet within |
| [[Enobarbus|ENOBARBUS]] | So,<br>This is to [[Horse|horse]]. Adieu, noble Agrippa. |
| AGRIPPA | Good fortune, worthy soldier, and farewell. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Caesar, Antony, [[Lepidus]], and [[Octavia]] |
| ANTONY | (to Caesar) No further, sir. |
| CAESAR | You take from me a great part of myself.<br>Use me [[Well|well]] in’t. Sister, prove such a wife<br>As my thoughts make thee, and as my farthest bond<br>Shall pass on thy approof. Most noble Antony,<br>Let not the piece of virtue which is set<br>Betwixt us as the cement of our [[Love|love]]<br>To keep it builded, be the ram to batter<br>The fortress of it; for better might we<br>Have loved without this mean if on both parts<br>This be not cherished. |
| ANTONY | Make me not offended<br>In your distrust. |
| CAESAR | I have said. |
| ANTONY | You shall not find,<br>Though you be therein curious, the least cause<br>For what you seem to [[Fear|fear]]. So, the gods keep you,<br>And make the hearts of Romans serve your ends.<br>We will here part. |
| CAESAR | Farewell, my dearest sister, fare thee [[Well|well]].<br>The elements be kind to thee, and make<br>Thy spirits all of comfort. Fare thee [[Well|well]]. |
| [[Octavia|OCTAVIA]] | (weeping) My noble brother! |
| ANTONY | The April’s in her eyes; it is [[Love|love]]’s [[Spring|spring]],<br>And these the showers to bring it on. Be cheerful. |
| [[Octavia|OCTAVIA]] | Sir, look [[Well|well]] to my husband’s [[house]], and— |
| CAESAR | What, [[Octavia]]? |
| [[Octavia|OCTAVIA]] | I’ll tell you in your ear.<br>She whispers to Caesar |
| ANTONY | Her tongue will not obey her heart, nor can<br>Her heart inform her tongue—the swan’-down<br>feather,<br>That stands upon the swell at full of tide,<br>And neither way inclines. |
| [[Enobarbus|ENOBARBUS]] | (aside to Agrippa) Will Caesar weep? |
| AGRIPPA | (aside to [[Enobarbus]]) He has a cloud in’s face. |
| [[Enobarbus|ENOBARBUS]] | (aside to Agrippa)<br>He were the worse for that were he a [[Horse|horse]];<br>So is he, being a man. |
| AGRIPPA | (aside to [[Enobarbus]]) Why, [[Enobarbus]],<br>When Antony found [[Julius Caesar]] dead<br>He cried almost to roaring, and he wept<br>When at Philippi he found [[Brutus]] slain. |
| [[Enobarbus|ENOBARBUS]] | (aside to Agrippa)<br>That year indeed he was troubled with a rheum.<br>What willingly he did confound he wailed,<br>Believe’, till I wept too. |
| CAESAR | No, sweet [[Octavia]],<br>You shall hear from me still. The [[Time|time]] shall not<br>Outgo my thinking on you. |
| ANTONY | Come, sir, come,<br>I’ll wrestle with you in my strength of [[Love|love]].<br>Look, here I have you (embracing Caesar); thus I let<br>you go,<br>And give you to the gods. |
| CAESAR | Adieu, be happy. |
| [[Lepidus|LEPIDUS]] | Let all the number of the [[Stars|stars]] give light<br>To thy fair way. |
| CAESAR | Farewell, farewell.<br>He kisses [[Octavia]] |
| ANTONY | Farewell. |
| *(didascalia)* | Trumpets sound. Exeunt Antony, [[Octavia]], and [[Enobarbus]] at one door, Caesar, [[Lepidus]], and Agrippa at another |
