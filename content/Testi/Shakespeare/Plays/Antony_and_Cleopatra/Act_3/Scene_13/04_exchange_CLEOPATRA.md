| CLEOPATRA | It is my birthday. I had thought to’ve held it poor, but since my lord Is Antony again, I will be Cleopatra. |
| ANTONY | We will yet do well. |
| CLEOPATRA | Call all his noble captains to my lord! |
| ANTONY | Do so. We’ll speak to them, and tonight I’ll force The wine peep through their scars. Come on, my queen, There’s sap in’t yet. The next time I do fight I’ll make death love me, for I will contend Even with his pestilent scythe. Exeunt all but Enobarbus |
| ENOBARBUS | Now he’ll outstare the lightning. To be furious Is to be frighted out of fear, and in that mood The dove will peck the estridge; and I see still A diminution in our captain’s brain Restores his heart. When valour preys on reason, It eats the sword it fights with. I will seek Some way to leave him. Exit |
