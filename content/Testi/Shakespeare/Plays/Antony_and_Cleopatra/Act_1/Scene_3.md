| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Cleopatra]], [[Charmian]], Alexas, and Iras |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | Where is he? |
| [[Charmian|CHARMIAN]] | I did not see him since. |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] to Alexas |
| [[Charmian|CHARMIAN]] | See where he is, who’s with him, what he does.<br>I did not send you. If you find him sad,<br>Say I am dancing; if in mirth, report<br>That I am sudden sick. Quick, and return. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit Alexas |
| [[Charmian|CHARMIAN]] | Madam, methinks, if you did [[Love|love]] him dearly,<br>You do not hold the method to enforce<br>The like from him. |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | What should I do I do not? |
| [[Charmian|CHARMIAN]] | In each thing give him way; [[Cross|cross]] him in nothing. |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | Thou teachest like a [[Fool|fool]], the way to lose him. |
| [[Charmian|CHARMIAN]] | Tempt him not so too far. Iwis, forbear.<br>In [[Time|time]] we hate that which we often [[Fear|fear]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Antony |
| [[Charmian|CHARMIAN]] | But here comes Antony. |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | I am sick and sullen. |
| ANTONY | I am sorry to give breathing to my purpose. |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | Help me away, dear [[Charmian]], I shall fall.<br>It cannot be thus long—the sides of [[Nature|nature]]<br>Will not sustain it. |
| ANTONY | Now, my dearest [[Queen|queen]]. |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | Pray you, stand farther from me. |
| ANTONY | What’s the matter? |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | I know by that same eye there’s some good news.<br>What says the married woman—you may go?<br>Would she had never given you leave to come.<br>Let her not say ’tis I that keep you here.<br>I have no power upon you; hers you are. |
| ANTONY | The gods best know— |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | O, never was there [[Queen|queen]]<br>So mightily betrayed! Yet at the first<br>I saw the treasons planted. |
| ANTONY | [[Cleopatra]]— |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | Why should I think you can be mine and true—<br>Though you in swearing shake the thronèd gods—<br>Who have been false to Fulvia? Riotous [[Madness|madness]],<br>To be entangled with those mouth-made vows<br>Which break themselves in swearing. |
| ANTONY | Most sweet [[Queen|queen]]— |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | Nay, pray you, seek no colour for your going,<br>But bid farewell and go. When you sued staying,<br>Then was the [[Time|time]] for words; no going then.<br>[[Eternity]] was in our lips and eyes,<br>Bliss in our brow’s bent; none our parts so poor<br>But was a race of heaven. They are so still,<br>Or thou, the greatest soldier of the world,<br>Art turned the greatest liar. |
| ANTONY | How now, lady! |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | I would I had thy inches. Thou shouldst know<br>There were a heart in [[Egypt]]. |
| ANTONY | Hear me, [[Queen]].<br>The strong necessity of [[Time|time]] commands<br>Our services a while, but my full heart<br>Remains in use with you. Our [[Italy]]<br>Shines o’er with civil swords. Sextus Pompeius<br>Makes his approaches to the port of Rome.<br>Equality of two domestic powers<br>Breed scrupulous faction. The hated, grown to<br>strength,<br>Are newly grown to [[Love|love]]. The condemned [[Pompey]],<br>Rich in his father’s [[Honour|honour]], creeps apace<br>Into the hearts of such as have not thrived<br>Upon the present state, whose numbers threaten;<br>And quietness, grown sick of rest, would purge<br>By any desperate change. My more particular,<br>And that which most with you should safe my going,<br>Is Fulvia’s [[Death|death]]. |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | Though age from folly could not give me [[Freedom|freedom]],<br>It does from childishness. Can Fulvia die? |
| ANTONY | She’s dead, my [[Queen|queen]].<br>He offers letters<br>Look here, and at thy sovereign leisure read<br>The garboils she awaked. At the last, best,<br>See when and where she died. |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | O most false [[Love|love]]!<br>Where be the sacred vials thou shouldst fill<br>With sorrowful [[Water|water]]? Now I see, I see,<br>In Fulvia’s [[Death|death]] how mine received shall be. |
| ANTONY | Quarrel no more, but be prepared to know<br>The purposes I bear, which are or cease<br>As you shall give th’advice. By the [[Fire|fire]]<br>That quickens Nilus’ slime, I go from hence<br>Thy soldier-servant, making peace or war<br>As thou affects. |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | Cut my lace, [[Charmian]], come.<br>But let it be. I am quickly ill and [[Well|well]];<br>So Antony loves. |
| ANTONY | My precious [[Queen|queen]], forbear, And give true evidence to his [[Love|love]], which stands<br>An honourable trial. |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | So Fulvia told me. I prithee turn aside and weep for her,<br>Then bid adieu to me, and say the [[Tears|tears]]<br>Belong to [[Egypt]]. Good now, play one scene<br>Of excellent dissembling, and let it look<br>Like perfect [[Honour|honour]]. |
| ANTONY | You’ll heat my [[Blood|blood]]. No more. |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | You can do better yet; but this is meetly. |
| ANTONY | Now by my [[Sword|sword]]— |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | And target. Still he mends.<br>But this is not the best. Look, prithee, [[Charmian]],<br>How this Herculean Roman does become<br>The carriage of his chafe. |
| ANTONY | I’ll leave you, lady. |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | Courteous lord, one word.<br>Sir, you and I must part; but that’s not it.<br>Sir, you and I have loved; but there’s not it;<br>That you know [[Well|well]]. Something it is I would—<br>O, my oblivion is a very Antony,<br>And I am all forgotten. |
| ANTONY | But that your royalty Holds idleness your subject, I should take you<br>For idleness itself. |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | ’Tis sweating labour To bear such idleness so near the heart<br>As [[Cleopatra]] this. But sir, forgive me,<br>Since my becomings kill me when they do not<br>Eye [[Well|well]] to you. Your [[Honour|honour]] calls you hence,<br>Therefore be deaf to my unpitied folly,<br>And all the gods go with you. Upon your [[Sword|sword]]<br>Sit laurel victory, and smooth [[success]]<br>Be strewed before your feet. |
| ANTONY | Let us go. Come. Our separation so abides and flies<br>That thou residing here goes yet with me,<br>And I hence fleeting, here remain with thee.<br>Away. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt severally |
