| *(didascalia)* | Enter Caesar, reading a letter, with Agrippa, Maecenas, and his army |
| CAESAR | He calls me boy, and chides as he had power<br>To beat me out of [[Egypt]]. My messenger<br>He hath whipped with rods, dares me to personal<br>combat,<br>Caesar to Antony. Let the old ruffian know<br>I have many other ways to die; meantime,<br>Laugh at his challenge. |
| MAECENAS | Caesar must think,<br>When one so great begins to rage, he’s hunted<br>Even to falling. Give him no breath, but now<br>Make boot of his distraction. Never anger<br>Made good guard for itself. |
| CAESAR | Let our best heads<br>Know that tomorrow the last of many battles<br>We mean to fight. Within our files there are,<br>Of those that served [[Mark Antony]] but late,<br>Enough to fetch him in. See it done,<br>And feast the army. We have store to do’t,<br>And they have earned the waste. Poor Antony! |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt |
