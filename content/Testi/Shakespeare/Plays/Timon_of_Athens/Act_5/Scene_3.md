| *(didascalia)* | Enter two other Senators, with a Messenger |
| *(didascalia)* | THIRD SENATOR |
| *(didascalia)* | Thou hast painfully discovered. Are his files<br>As full as thy report? |
| MESSENGER | I have spoke the least.<br>Besides, his expedition promises<br>Present approach. |
| *(didascalia)* | FOURTH SENATOR |
| MESSENGER | We stand much hazard if they bring not Timon. |
| MESSENGER | I met a courier, one mine ancient friend,<br>Whom, though in general part we were opposed,<br>Yet our old love made a particular force<br>And made us speak like friends. This man was riding<br>From Alcibiades to Timon’s cave<br>With letters of entreaty which imported<br>His fellowship i’th’ cause against your city,<br>In part for his sake moved. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter the other Senators |
| *(didascalia)* | THIRD SENATOR |
| MESSENGER | Here come our brothers. |
| *(didascalia)* | FIRST SENATOR |
| MESSENGER | No talk of Timon; nothing of him expect.<br>The enemy’s drum is heard, and fearful scouring<br>Doth choke the air with dust. In, and prepare.<br>Ours is the fall, I fear, our foe’s the snare. Exeunt |
