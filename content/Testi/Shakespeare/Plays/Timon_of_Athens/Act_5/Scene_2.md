| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Flavius]] and two Senators |
| [[Flavius|FLAVIUS]] | It is in vain that you would speak with [[Timon]],<br>For he is set so only to himself<br>That nothing but himself which looks like man<br>Is friendly with him. |
| FIRST SENATOR | Bring us to his [[Cave|cave]].<br>It is our part and promise to th’ Athenians<br>To speak with [[Timon]]. |
| SECOND SENATOR | At all times alike<br>Men are not still the same. ’Twas [[Time|time]] and griefs<br>That framed him thus. [[Time]] with his fairer hand<br>Offering the fortunes of his former days,<br>The former man may make him. Bring us to him,<br>And chance it as it may. |
| [[Flavius|FLAVIUS]] | Here is his [[Cave|cave]].<br>(Calling) Peace and content be here! Lord [[Timon]],<br>[[Timon]],<br>Look out and speak to friends. Th’Athenians<br>By two of their most reverend senate greet thee.<br>Speak to them, noble [[Timon]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Timon]] out of his [[Cave|cave]] |
| [[Timon|TIMON]] | Thou sun that comforts, burn! Speak and be hanged.<br>For each true word a blister, and each false<br>Be as a cantherizing to the root o’th’ tongue,<br>Consuming it with speaking. |
| FIRST SENATOR | Worthy [[Timon]]— |
| [[Timon|TIMON]] | Of none but such as you, and you of [[Timon]]. |
| FIRST SENATOR | The senators of Athens greet thee, [[Timon]]. |
| [[Timon|TIMON]] | I thank them, and would send them back the plague<br>Could I but catch it for them. |
| FIRST SENATOR | O, forget<br>What we are sorry for, ourselves in thee.<br>The senators with one consent of [[Love|love]]<br>Entreat thee back to Athens, who have thought<br>On special dignities which vacant lie<br>For thy best use and wearing. |
| SECOND SENATOR | They confess<br>Toward thee forgetfulness too general-gross,<br>Which now the public [[Body|body]], which doth seldom<br>Play the recanter, feeling in itself<br>A lack of [[Timon]]’s aid, hath sense withal<br>Of it own fail, restraining aid to [[Timon]];<br>And send forth us to make their sorrowed render,<br>Together with a recompense more fruitful<br>Than their offence can weigh down by the dram;<br>Ay, even such heaps and sums of [[Love|love]] and wealth<br>As shall to thee blot out what wrongs were theirs,<br>And write in thee the figures of their [[Love|love]],<br>Ever to read them thine. |
| [[Timon|TIMON]] | You [[Witch|witch]] me in it,<br>Surprise me to the very brink of [[Tears|tears]].<br>Lend me a [[Fool|fool]]’s heart and a woman’s eyes,<br>And I’ll beweep these comforts, worthy senators. |
| FIRST SENATOR | Therefore so please thee to return with us,<br>And of our Athens, thine and ours, to take<br>The captainship, thou shalt be met with thanks,<br>Allowed with absolute power, and thy good name<br>Live with authority. So soon we shall drive back<br>Of [[Alcibiades]] th’approaches wild,<br>Who, like a boar too savage, doth root up<br>His country’s peace. |
| SECOND SENATOR | And shakes his threat’ning [[Sword|sword]] Against the walls of Athens. |
| FIRST SENATOR | Therefore, [[Timon]]— |
| [[Timon|TIMON]] | [[Well]], sir, I will; therefore I will, sir, thus.<br>If [[Alcibiades]] kill my countrymen,<br>Let [[Alcibiades]] know this of [[Timon]]:<br>That [[Timon]] cares not. But if he sack fair Athens,<br>And take our goodly aged men by th’ beards,<br>Giving our holy virgins to the stain<br>Of contumelious, beastly, mad-brained war,<br>Then let him know, and tell him [[Timon]] speaks it<br>In pity of our aged and our youth,<br>I cannot choose but tell him that I care not;<br>And-let him take’t at worst—for their knives care<br>not<br>While you have throats to answer. For myself,<br>There’s not a whittle in th’ unruly camp<br>But I do prize it at my [[Love|love]] before<br>The reverend’st throat in Athens. So I leave you<br>To the protection of the prosperous gods,<br>As thieves to keepers. |
| [[Flavius|FLAVIUS]] | (to Senators) Stay not; all’s in vain. |
| [[Timon|TIMON]] | Why, I was writing of my epitaph.<br>It will be seen tomorrow. My long sickness<br>Of health and living now begins to mend,<br>And nothing brings me all things. Go; live still.<br>Be [[Alcibiades]] your plague, you his,<br>And last so long enough. |
| FIRST SENATOR | We speak in vain. |
| [[Timon|TIMON]] | But yet I [[Love|love]] my country, and am not<br>One that rejoices in the common wrack<br>As common bruit doth put it. |
| FIRST SENATOR | That’s [[Well|well]] spoke. |
| [[Timon|TIMON]] | Commend me to my loving countrymen— |
| FIRST SENATOR | These words become your lips as they pass through<br>them. |
| SECOND SENATOR | And enter in our ears like great triumphers<br>In their applauding gates. |
| [[Timon|TIMON]] | Commend me to them,<br>And tell them that to ease them of their griefs,<br>Their fears of hostile strokes, their aches, losses,<br>Their pangs of [[Love|love]], with other incident throes<br>That [[Nature|nature]]’s fragile vessel doth sustain<br>In life’s uncertain voyage, I will some kindness do them.<br>I’ll teach them to prevent wild [[Alcibiades]]’ wrath. |
| FIRST SENATOR | (aside)<br>I like this [[Well|well]]; he will return again. |
| [[Timon|TIMON]] | I have a tree which grows here in my close<br>That mine own use invites me to cut down,<br>And shortly must I fell it. Tell my friends,<br>Tell Athens, in the sequence of degree<br>From high to low throughout, that whoso please<br>To stop affliction, let him take his haste,<br>Come hither ere my tree hath felt the axe,<br>And hang himself. I pray you do my greeting. |
| [[Flavius|FLAVIUS]] | (to Senators)<br>Trouble him no further. Thus you still shall find him. |
| [[Timon|TIMON]] | Come not to me again, but say to Athens,<br>[[Timon]] hath made his everlasting mansion<br>Upon the beached verge of the salt flood,<br>Who once a day with his embossed froth<br>The turbulent surge shall cover. Thither come,<br>And let my gravestone be your oracle.<br>lips, let four words go by, and language end.<br>What is amiss, plague and infection mend.<br>Graves only be men’s works, and [[Death|death]] their gain.<br>Sun, hide thy beams. [[Timon]] hath done his reign. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit into his |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Cave|cave]] |
| FIRST SENATOR | His discontents are unremovably<br>Coupled to [[Nature|nature]]. |
| SECOND SENATOR | Our [[Hope|hope]] in him is dead. Let us return,<br>And strain what other means is left unto us<br>In our dear peril. |
| FIRST SENATOR | It requires swift foot. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt |
