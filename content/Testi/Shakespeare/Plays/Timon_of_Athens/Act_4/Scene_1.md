| *(didascalia)* | Enter Timon |
| TIMON | Let me look back upon thee. O thou wall<br>That girdles in those wolves, dive in the earth,<br>And fence not Athens! Matrons, turn incontinent!<br>Obedience fail in children! Slaves and fools,<br>Pluck the grave wrinkled senate from the bench<br>And minister in their steads! To general filths<br>Convert o‘th’ instant, green virginity!<br>Do’t in your parents’ eyes. Bankrupts, hold fast!<br>Rather than render back, out with your knives,<br>And cut your trusters’ throats. Bound servants, steal!<br>Large-handed robbers your grave masters are,<br>And pill by law. Maid, to thy master’s bed!<br>Thy mistress is o’th’ brothel. Son of sixteen,<br>Pluck the lined crutch from thy old limping sire;<br>With it beat out his brains! Piety and fear,<br>Religion to the gods, peace, justice, truth,<br>Domestic awe, night rest, and neighbourhood,<br>Instruction, manners, mysteries, and trades,<br>Degrees, observances, customs, and laws,<br>Decline to your confounding contraries,<br>And let confusion live! Plagues incident to men,<br>Your potent and infectious fevers heap<br>On Athens, ripe for stroke! Thou cold sciatica,<br>Cripple our senators, that their limbs may halt<br>As lamely as their manners! Lust and liberty,<br>Creep in the minds and marrows of our youth,<br>That ‘gainst the stream of virtue they may strive<br>And drown themselves in riot! Itches, blains,<br>Sow all th’Athenian bosoms, and their crop<br>Be general leprosy! Breath infect breath,<br>That their society, as their friendship, may<br>Be merely poison! |
| *(didascalia)* | He tears off his clothes |
| TIMON | Nothing I’ll bear from thee<br>But nakedness, thou detestable town;<br>Take thou that too, with multiplying bans.<br>Timon will to the woods, where he shall find<br>Th‘unkindest beast more kinder than mankind.<br>The gods confound—hear me you good gods all—<br>Th’Athenians, both within and out that wall;<br>And grant, as Timon grows, his hate may grow<br>To the whole race of mankind, high and low.<br>Amen. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit |
