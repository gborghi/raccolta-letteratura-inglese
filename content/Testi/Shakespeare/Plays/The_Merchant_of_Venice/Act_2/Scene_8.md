| *(didascalia)* | Enter Salerio and Solanio |
| SALERIO | Why, man, I saw [[Bassanio]] under sail.<br>With him is Graziano gone along,<br>And in their [[Ship|ship]] I am sure [[Lorenzo]] is not. |
| SOLANIO | The [[Villain|villain]] Jew with outcries raised the Duke,<br>Who went with him to search [[Bassanio]]’s [[Ship|ship]]. |
| SALERIO | He came too late. The [[Ship|ship]] was under sail.<br>But there the Duke was given to understand<br>That in a gondola were seen together<br>[[Lorenzo]] and his amorous [[Jessica]].<br>Besides, [[Antonio]] certified the Duke<br>They were not with [[Bassanio]] in his [[Ship|ship]]. |
| SOLANIO | I never heard a passion so confused,<br>So strange, outrageous, and so variable<br>As the dog Jew did utter in the streets.<br>‘My daughter! O, my ducats! O, my daughter!<br>Fled with a Christian! O, my Christian ducats I<br>Justice! The law! My ducats and my daughter!<br>A sealed bag, two sealèd bags of ducats,<br>Of [[Double|double]] ducats, stol’n from me by my daughter!<br>And jewels, two stones, two rich and precious stones,<br>Stol’n by my daughter! Justice! Find the girl!<br>She hath the stones upon her, and the ducats!’ |
| SALERIO | Why, all the boys in [[Venice]] follow him,<br>Crying, ‘His stones, his daughter, and his ducats!’ |
| SOLANIO | Let good [[Antonio]] look he keep his day,<br>Or he shall pay for this. |
| SALERIO | Marry, [[Well|well]] remembered.<br>I reasoned with a Frenchman yesterday,<br>Who told me in the narrow seas that part<br>The French and English there miscarried<br>A vessel of our country, richly fraught.<br>I thought upon [[Antonio]] when he told me,<br>And wished in [[Silence|silence]] that it were not his. |
| SOLANIO | You were best to tell [[Antonio]] what you hear—<br>Yet do not suddenly, for it may grieve him. |
| SALERIO | A kinder gentleman treads not the earth.<br>I saw [[Bassanio]] and [[Antonio]] part.<br>[[Bassanio]] told him he would make some [[Speed|speed]]<br>Of his return. He answered, ‘Do not so.<br>Slubber not business for my sake, [[Bassanio]],<br>But stay the very riping of the [[Time|time]];<br>And for the Jew’s bond which he hath of me,<br>Let it not enter in your mind of [[Love|love]].<br>Be merry, and employ your chiefest thoughts<br>To courtship and such fair ostents of [[Love|love]]<br>As shall conveniently become you there.’<br>And even there, his eye being big with [[Tears|tears]],<br>Turning his face, he put his hand behind him<br>And, with affection wondrous sensible,<br>He wrung [[Bassanio]]’s hand; and so they parted. |
| SOLANIO | I think he only loves the world for him.<br>I pray thee let us go and find him out,<br>And quicken his embraced heaviness<br>With some delight or other. |
| SALERIO | Do we so. Exeunt |
