| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Pandarus]] and [[Cressida]] |
| [[Pandarus|PANDARUS]] | Is’t possible? No sooner got but lost. The devil take Antenor! The young prince will go mad. A plague upon Antenor! I would they had broke’s neck. |
| [[Cressida|CRESSIDA]] | How now? What’s the matter? Who was here? |
| [[Pandarus|PANDARUS]] | Ah, ah! |
| [[Cressida|CRESSIDA]] | Why sigh you so profoundly? Where’s my lord?<br>Gone? Tell me, sweet uncle, what’s the matter? |
| [[Pandarus|PANDARUS]] | Would I were as deep under the earth as I am above. |
| [[Cressida|CRESSIDA]] | O the gods! What’s the matter? |
| [[Pandarus|PANDARUS]] | Pray thee, get thee in. Would thou hadst ne’er been born. I knew thou wouldst be his [[Death|death]]. O poor gentleman! A plague upon Antenor! |
| [[Cressida|CRESSIDA]] | Good uncle, I beseech you on my knees; I beseech you, what’s the matter? |
| [[Pandarus|PANDARUS]] | Thou must be gone, wench, thou must be gone. Thou art changed for Antenor. Thou must to thy father, and be gone from [[Troilus]]. ‘Twill be his [[Death|death]]. ’Twill be his bane. He cannot bear it. |
| [[Cressida|CRESSIDA]] | O you immortal gods! I will not go. |
| [[Pandarus|PANDARUS]] | Thou must. |
| [[Cressida|CRESSIDA]] | I will not, uncle. I have forgot my father.<br>I know no touch of consanguinity,<br>No kin, no [[Love|love]], no [[Blood|blood]], no [[Soul|soul]], so near me<br>As the sweet [[Troilus]]. O you gods divine,<br>Make Cressid’s name the very [[Crown|crown]] of falsehood<br>If ever she leave [[Troilus]]. [[Time]], force, and [[Death|death]]<br>Do to this [[Body|body]] what extremity you can,<br>But the strong base and building of my [[Love|love]]<br>Is as the very centre of the earth,<br>Drawing all things to it. I’ll go in and weep— |
| [[Pandarus|PANDARUS]] | Do, do. |
| [[Cressida|CRESSIDA]] | Tear my bright hair, and scratch my praised cheeks,<br>Crack my clear voice with sobs, and break my heart<br>With sounding ‘[[Troilus]]’. I will not go from Troy. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt |
