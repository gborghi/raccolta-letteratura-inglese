| *(didascalia)* | Enter Ajax |
| AJAX | Troilus, thou coward Troilus! Show thy head! |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Diomedes |
| DIOMEDES | Troilus, I say! Where’s Troilus? |
| AJAX | What wouldst thou? DIOMEDES I would correct him. |
| AJAX | Were I the general, thou shouldst have my office<br>Ere that correction.—Troitus, I say! What, Troilus! |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Troilus |
| TROILUS | O traitor Diomed! Turn thy false face, thou traitor,<br>And pay the life thou ow’st me for my horse. |
| DIOMEDES | Ha, art thou there? |
| AJAX | I’ll fight with him alone. Stand, Diomed. |
| DIOMEDES | He is my prize; I will not look upon. |
| TROILUS | Come, both you cogging Greeks, have at you both!<br>They fight. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Hector |
| HECTOR | Yea, Troilus? O well fought, my youngest brother! |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit Troilus driving Diomedes and Ajax in |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Achilles behind |
| ACHILLES | Now do I see thee.—Ha! Have at thee, Hector. |
| *(didascalia)* | They fight. Achilles is bested |
| HECTOR | Pause, if thou wilt. |
| ACHILLES | I do disdain thy courtesy, proud Trojan.<br>Be happy that my arms are out of use.<br>My rest and negligence befriends thee now;<br>But thou anon shalt here of me again.<br>Till when, go seek thy fortune. Exit |
| HECTOR | Fare thee well.<br>I would have been much more a fresher man<br>Had I expected thee. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Troilus ain haste |
| HECTOR | How now, my brother? |
| TROILUS | Ajax hath ta‘en Aeneas. Shall it be?<br>No, by the flame of yonder glorious heaven,<br>He shall not carry him. I’ll be ta’en too,<br>Or bring him off. Fate, hear me what I say:<br>I reck not though thou end my life today. Exit |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter one in sumptuous armour |
| HECTOR | Stand, stand, thou Greek! Thou art a goodly mark.<br>No? Wilt thou not? I like thy armour well.<br>I’ll frush it and unlock the rivets all, |
| *(didascalia)* | But I’ll be master of it. Exit one in armour |
| HECTOR | Wilt thou not, beast, abide?<br>Why then, fly on; I’ll hunt thee for thy hide. Exit |
