| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Juliet]] |
| [[Juliet|JULIET]] | The [[Clock|clock]] struck nine when I did send the [[Nurse]].<br>In half an hour she promised to return.<br>Perchance she cannot meet him. That’s not so.<br>O, she is lame! [[Love]]’s heralds should be thoughts,<br>Which ten times faster glides than the sun’s beams<br>Driving back shadows over louring hills.<br>Therefore do nimble-pinioned doves draw [[Love]],<br>And therefore hath the [[Wind|wind]]-swift Cupid wings.<br>Now is the sun upon the highmost [[Hill|hill]]<br>Of this day’s [[Journey|journey]], and from nine till twelve<br>Is three long hours, yet she is not come.<br>Had she affections and warm youthful [[Blood|blood]]<br>She would be as swift in motion as a ball.<br>My words would bandy her to my sweet [[Love|love]],<br>And his to me.<br>But old folks, many feign as they were dead—<br>Unwieldy, slow, heavy, and pale as lead. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter the [[Nurse]] and Peter |
| [[Juliet|JULIET]] | O God, she comes! O honey [[Nurse]], what news?<br>Hast thou met with him ? Send thy man away. |
| [[Nurse|NURSE]] | Peter, stay at the gate. Exit Peter |
| [[Juliet|JULIET]] | Now, good sweet [[Nurse]]—O Lord, why look‘st thou sad ?<br>Though news be sad, yet tell them merrily;<br>If good, thou sham’st the music of sweet news<br>By playing it to me with so sour a face. |
| [[Nurse|NURSE]] | I am a-weary. Give me leave a while.<br>Fie, how my bones ache. What a jaunce have I! |
| [[Juliet|JULIET]] | I would thou hadst my bones and I thy news.<br>Nay, come, I pray thee speak, good, good [[Nurse]], speak. |
| [[Nurse|NURSE]] | Jesu, what haste! Can you not stay a while?<br>Do you not see that I am out of breath? |
| [[Juliet|JULIET]] | How art thou out of breath when thou hast breath<br>To say to me that thou art out of breath?<br>The excuse that thou dost make in this delay<br>Is longer than the tale thou dost excuse.<br>Is thy news good or bad? Answer to that.<br>Say either, and I’ll stay the circumstance.<br>Let me be satisfied: is’t good or bad? |
| [[Nurse|NURSE]] | [[Well]], you have made a simple choice. You know not how to choose a man. [[Romeo]]? No, not he; though his face be better than any man‘s, yet his leg excels all men’s, and for a hand and a foot and a [[Body|body]], though they be not to be talked on, yet they are past compare. He is not the flower of courtesy, but, I’ll warrant him, as gentle as a [[Lamb|lamb]]. Go thy ways, wench. Serve God. What, have you dined at [[Home|home]]? |
| [[Juliet|JULIET]] | No, no. But all this did I know before.<br>What says he of our [[Marriage|marriage]]—what of that? |
| [[Nurse|NURSE]] | Lord, how my head aches! What a head have I!<br>It beats as it would fall in twenty pieces.<br>My back— |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Juliet]] rubs her back |
| [[Nurse|NURSE]] | a’ t’other side—ah, my back, my back!<br>Beshrew your heart for sending me about<br>To catch my [[Death|death]] with jauncing up and down. |
| [[Juliet|JULIET]] | I’[[Faith|faith]], I am sorry that thou art not [[Well|well]].<br>Sweet, sweet, sweet [[Nurse]], tell me, what says my [[Love|love]]? |
| [[Nurse|NURSE]] | Your [[Love|love]] says, like an honest gentleman, and a<br>courteous, and a kind, and a handsome, and, I warrant,<br>a virtuous—where is your mother? |
| [[Juliet|JULIET]] | Where is my mother? Why, she is within.<br>Where should she be? How oddly thou repliest!<br>‘Your [[Love|love]] says like an honest gentleman<br>“Where is your mother?” ’ |
| [[Nurse|NURSE]] | O, God’s Lady dear!<br>Are you so hot? Marry come up, I trow.<br>Is this the poultice for my aching bones?<br>Henceforward do your messages yourself. |
| [[Juliet|JULIET]] | Here’s such a coil ! Come, what says [[Romeo]]? |
| [[Nurse|NURSE]] | Have you got leave to go to shrift today? |
| [[Juliet|JULIET]] | I have. |
| [[Nurse|NURSE]] | Then hie you hence to [[Friar Laurence]]’ cell.<br>There stays a husband to make you a wife.<br>Now comes the wanton [[Blood|blood]] up in your cheeks.<br>They’ll be in scarlet straight at any news.<br>Hie you to church. I must another way,<br>To fetch a ladder by the which your [[Love|love]]<br>Must climb a bird’s nest soon, when it is dark.<br>I am the drudge, and toil in your delight,<br>But you shall bear the burden soon at [[Night|night]].<br>Go, I’ll to dinner. Hie you to the cell. |
| [[Juliet|JULIET]] | Hie to high fortune! Honest [[Nurse]], farewell. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt severally |
