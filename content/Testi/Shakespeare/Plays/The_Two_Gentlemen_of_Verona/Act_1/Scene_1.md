| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Valentine]] and [[Proteus]] |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | Cease to persuade, my loving [[Proteus]].<br>[[Home]]-keeping youth have ever homely wits.<br>Were’t not affection [[Chains|chains]] thy tender days<br>To the sweet glances of thy honoured [[Love|love]],<br>I rather would entreat thy company<br>To see the wonders of the world abroad<br>Than, living dully sluggardized at [[Home|home]],<br>Wear out thy youth with shapeless idleness.<br>But since thou lov’st, [[Love|love]] still, and thrive therein—<br>Even as I would, when I to [[Love|love]] begin. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | Wilt thou be gone? Sweet [[Valentine]], adieu.<br>Think on thy [[Proteus]] when thou haply seest<br>Some rare noteworthy object in thy travel.<br>Wish me partaker in thy happiness<br>When thou dost meet good hap; and in thy danger—<br>If ever danger do environ thee—<br>Commend thy grievance to my holy prayers;<br>For I will be thy beadsman, [[Valentine]]. |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | And on a [[Love|love]]-book pray for my [[success]]? |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | Upon some book I [[Love|love]] I’ll pray for thee. |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | That’s on some shallow story of deep [[Love|love]]—<br>How young Leander crossed the Hellespont. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | That’s a deep story of a deeper [[Love|love]],<br>For he was more than over-shoes in [[Love|love]]. |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | ‘Tis true, for you are over-boots in [[Love|love]],<br>And yet you never swam the Hellespont. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | Over the boots? Nay, give me not the boots. |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | No, I will not; for it boots thee not. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | What? |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | To be in [[Love|love]], where scorn is bought with groans,<br>Coy looks with heart-sore sighs, one fading moment’s<br>mirth<br>With twenty watchful, weary, tedious nights.<br>If haply won, perhaps a hapless gain;<br>If lost, why then a grievous labour won;<br>However, but a folly bought with wit,<br>Or else a wit by folly vanquished. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | So by your circumstance you call me [[Fool|fool]]. |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | So by your circumstance I [[Fear|fear]] you’ll prove. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | ‘Tis [[Love|love]] you cavil at. I am not [[Love|love]]. |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | [[Love]] is your master, for he masters you,<br>And he that is so yoked by a [[Fool|fool]]<br>Methinks should not be chronicled for wise. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | Yet writers say ‘As in the sweetest bud<br>The eating canker dwells, so doting [[Love|love]]<br>Inhabits in the finest wits of all.’ |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | And writers say ‘As the most forward bud<br>Is eaten by the canker ere it blow,<br>Even so by [[Love|love]] the young and tender wit<br>Is turned to folly, blasting in the bud,<br>Losing his verdure even in the prime,<br>And all the fair effects of future hopes.’<br>But wherefore waste I [[Time|time]] to counsel thee<br>That art a votary to fond [[Desire|desire]]?<br>Once more adieu. My father at the [[Road|road]]<br>Expects my coming, there to see me shipped. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | And thither will I bring thee, [[Valentine]]. |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | Sweet [[Proteus]], no. Now let us take our leave.<br>To Milan let me hear from thee by letters<br>Of thy [[success]] in [[Love|love]], and what news else<br>Betideth here in absence of thy friend;<br>And I likewise will visit thee with mine. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | All happiness bechance to thee in Milan. |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | As much to you at [[Home|home]]; and so farewell. Exit |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | He after [[Honour|honour]] hunts, I after [[Love|love]].<br>He [[Leaves|leaves]] his friends to dignify them more,<br>I leave myself, my friends, and all, for [[Love|love]].<br>Thou, [[Julia]], thou hast metamorphosed me,<br>Made me neglect my studies, lose my [[Time|time]],<br>War with good counsel, set the world at naught;<br>Made wit with musing weak, heart sick with thought. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Speed]] |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | Sir [[Proteus]], save you. Saw you my master? |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | But now he parted hence to embark for Milan. |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | Twenty to one, then, he is shipped already,<br>And I have played the sheep in losing him. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | Indeed, a sheep doth very often stray,<br>An if the shepherd be a while away. |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | You conclude that my master is a shepherd, then,<br>and I a sheep? |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | I do. |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | Why then, my horns are his horns, whether I wake or [[Sleep|sleep]]. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | A silly answer, and fitting [[Well|well]] a sheep. |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | This proves me still a sheep. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | True, and thy master a shepherd. |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | Nay, that I can deny by a circumstance. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | It shall go hard but I’ll prove it by another. |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | The shepherd seeks the sheep, and not the sheep the shepherd. But I seek my master, and my master seeks not me. Therefore I am no sheep. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | The sheep for fodder follow the shepherd, the shepherd for food follows not the sheep. Thou for wages followest thy master, thy master for wages follows not thee. Therefore thou art a sheep. |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | Such another proof will make me cry ‘baa’. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | But dost thou hear: gav’st thou my letter to [[Julia]]? |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | Ay, sir. I, a lost mutton, gave your letter to her, a laced mutton, and she, a laced mutton, gave me, a lost mutton, nothing for my labour. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | Here’s too small a pasture for such store of muttons. |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | If the ground be overcharged, you were best stick her. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | Nay, in that you are astray. ‘Twere best pound you. |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | Nay sir, less than a pound shall serve me for carrying your letter. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | You mistake. I mean the pound, a pinfold. |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | From a pound to a pin? Fold it over and over ‘Tis threefold too little for carrying a letter to your [[Lover|lover]]. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | But what said she? |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | (nods, then says) Ay. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | Nod-ay? Why, that’s ‘noddy’. |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | You mistook, sir. I say she did nod, and you ask me if she did nod, and I say ‘Ay’. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | And that set together is ‘noddy’. |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | Now you have taken the pains to set it together, take it for your pains. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | No, no. You shall have it for bearing the letter. |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | [[Well]], I perceive I must be fain to bear with you. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | Why, sir, how do you bear with me? |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | Marry, sir, the letter very orderly, having nothing but the word ‘noddy’ for my pains. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | Beshrew me but you have a quick wit. |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | And yet it cannot overtake your slow purse. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | Come, come, open the matter in brief. What said she? |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | Open your purse, that the money and the matter may be both at once delivered. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | (giving money) [[Well]], sir, here is for your pains. What said she? |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | Truly, sir, I think you’ll hardly win her. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | Why? Couldst thou perceive so much from her? |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | Sir, I could perceive nothing at all from her, no, not so much as a ducat for delivering your letter. And being so hard to me, that brought your mind, I [[Fear|fear]] she’ll prove as hard to you in telling your mind. Give her no token but stones, for she’s as hard as steel. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | What said she? Nothing? |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | No, not so much as ‘Take this for thy pains’. To testify your bounty, I thank you, you have testerned me; in requital whereof, henceforth carry your letters yourself. And so, sir, I’ll commend you to my master. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | Go, go, be gone, to save your [[Ship|ship]] from wreck,<br>Which cannot perish having thee aboard,<br>Being destined to a drier [[Death|death]] on shore.<br>I must go send some better messenger.<br>I [[Fear|fear]] my [[Julia]] would not deign my lines,<br>Receiving them from such a worthless post. Exit |
