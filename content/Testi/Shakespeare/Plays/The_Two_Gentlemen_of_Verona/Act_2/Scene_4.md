| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | I quote it in your jerkin. |

| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | You have said, sir. |
| THORIO | Ay, sir, and done too, for this [[Time|time]]. |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | I know it [[Well|well]], sir, you always end ere you begin. |
| [[Silvia|SILVIA]] | A fine volley of words, gentlemen, and quickly shot off. |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | ‘Tis indeed, madam, we thank the giver. [[Silvia|SILVIA]] Who is that, servant? |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | Yourself, sweet lady, for you gave the [[Fire|fire]]. Sir [[Thurio]] borrows his wit from your ladyship’s looks, and spends what he borrows kindly in your company. |
| [[Thurio|THURIO]] | Sir, if you spend word for word with me, I shall make your wit bankrupt. |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | I know it [[Well|well]], sir. You have an exchequer of words, and, I think, no other treasure to give your followers. For it appears by their bare liveries that they live by your bare words. |
| [[Silvia|SILVIA]] | No more, gentlemen, no more. Here comes my father. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter the Duke |
| DUKE | Now, daughter [[Silvia]], you are hard beset.<br>Sir [[Valentine]], your father is in good health,<br>What say you to a letter from your friends<br>Of much good news? |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | My lord, I will be thankful<br>To any happy messenger from thence. |
| DUKE | Know ye Don [[Antonio]], your countryman? |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | Ay, my good lord, I know the gentleman<br>To be of worth, and worthy estimation,<br>And not without [[Desert|desert]] so [[Well|well]] reputed. |
| DUKE | Hath he not a son? |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | Ay, my good lord, a son that [[Well|well]] deserves<br>The [[Honour|honour]] and regard of such a father. |
| DUKE | You know him [[Well|well]]? |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | I knew him as myself, for from our infancy<br>We have conversed, and spent our hours together.<br>And though myself have been an idle truant,<br>Omitting the sweet benefit of [[Time|time]]<br>To clothe mine age with angel-like perfection,<br>Yet hath Sir [[Proteus]]—for that’s his name—<br>Made use and fair advantage of his days:<br>His years but young, but his experience old;<br>His head unmellowed, but his judgement ripe.<br>And in a word—for far behind his worth<br>Comes all the praises that I now bestow—<br>He is complete, in feature and in mind,<br>With all good grace to grace a gentleman. |
| DUKE | Beshrew me, sir, but if he make this good<br>He is as worthy for an empress’ [[Love|love]]<br>As meet to be an emperor’s counsellor.<br>[[Well]], sir, this gentleman is come to me<br>With commendation from great potentates,<br>And here he means to spend his [[Time|time]] awhile.<br>I think ’tis no unwelcome news to you. |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | Should I have wished a thing it had been he. |
| DUKE | Welcome him then according to his worth.<br>[[Silvia]], I speak to you, and you, Sir [[Thurio]];<br>For [[Valentine]], I need not cite him to it.<br>I will send him hither to you presently. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | This is the gentleman I told your ladyship<br>Had come along with me, but that his mistress<br>Did hold his eyes locked in her crystal looks. |
| [[Silvia|SILVIA]] | Belike that now she hath enfranchised them<br>Upon some other pawn for fealty. |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | Nay, sure, I think she holds them prisoners still. |
| [[Silvia|SILVIA]] | Nay, then he should be blind, and being blind<br>How could he see his way to seek out you? |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | Why, lady, [[Love|love]] hath twenty pair of eyes. |
| [[Thurio|THURIO]] | They say that [[Love|love]] hath not an eye at all. |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | To see such [[lovers]], [[Thurio]], as yourself.<br>Upon a homely object [[Love|love]] can wink. |
| [[Silvia|SILVIA]] | Have done, have done. Here comes the gentleman. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Proteus]] |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | Welcome, dear [[Proteus]]. Mistress, I beseech you<br>Confirm his welcome with some special favour. |
| [[Silvia|SILVIA]] | His worth is warrant for his welcome hither,<br>If this be he you oft have wished to hear from. |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | Mistress, it is. Sweet lady, entertain him<br>To be my fellow-servant to your ladyship. |
| [[Silvia|SILVIA]] | Too low a mistress for so high a servant. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | Not so, sweet lady, but too mean a servant<br>To have a look of such a worthy mistress. |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | Leave off discourse of disability.<br>Sweet lady, entertain him for your servant. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | My duty will I boast of, nothing else. |
| [[Silvia|SILVIA]] | And duty never yet did want his meed.<br>Servant, you are welcome to a worthless mistress. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | I’ll die on him that says so but yourself. |
| [[Silvia|SILVIA]] | That you are welcome? |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | That you are worthless. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter a Servant |
| *(didascalia)* | SERVANT |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | Madam, my lord your father would speak with you. |
| *(didascalia)* | I wait upon his pleasure. Exit the Servant |
| [[Silvia|SILVIA]] | Come, Sir [[Thurio]],<br>Go with me. Once more, new servant, welcome.<br>I’ll leave you to confer of [[Home|home]] affairs.<br>When you have done, we look to hear from you. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | We’ll both attend upon your ladyship. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt [[Silvia]] and [[Thurio]] |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | Now tell me, how do all from whence you came? |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | Your friends are [[Well|well]], and have them much commended. |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | And how do yours? |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | I left them all in health. |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | How does your lady, and how thrives your [[Love|love]]? |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | My tales of [[Love|love]] were wont to weary you.<br>I know you joy not in a [[Love|love]]-discourse. |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | Ay, [[Proteus]], but that life is altered now.<br>I have done penance for contemning [[Love|love]],<br>Whose high imperious thoughts have punished me<br>With bitter fasts, with penitential groans,<br>With nightly [[Tears|tears]] and daily heart-sore sighs.<br>For in [[Revenge|revenge]] of my contempt of [[Love|love]]<br>[[Love]] hath chased [[Sleep|sleep]] from my enthralled eyes,<br>And made them watchers of mine own heart’s sorrow.<br>O gentle [[Proteus]], [[Love|love]]’s a mighty lord,<br>And hath so humbled me as I confess<br>There is no woe to his correction,<br>Nor to his service no such joy on earth.<br>Now, no discourse except it be of [[Love|love]].<br>Now can I break my fast, dine, sup, and [[Sleep|sleep]]<br>Upon the very naked name of [[Love|love]]. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | Enough. I read your fortune in your eye.<br>Was this the idol that you worship so? |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | Even she; and is she not a heavenly saint? |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | No, but she is an earthly paragon. |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | Call her divine. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | I will not flatter her. |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | O flatter me; for [[Love|love]] delights in praises. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | When I was sick you gave me bitter pills,<br>And I must minister the like to you. |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | Then speak the [[Truth|truth]] by her; if not divine,<br>Yet let her be a principality,<br>Sovereign to all the creatures on the earth. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | Except my mistress. |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | Sweet, except not any,<br>Except thou wilt except against my [[Love|love]]. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | Have I not reason to prefer mine own? |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | And I will help thee to prefer her, too.<br>She shall be dignified with this high [[Honour|honour]],<br>To bear my lady’s train, lest the base earth<br>Should from her vesture chance to steal a kiss<br>And, of so great a favour growing proud,<br>Disdain to root the [[Summer|summer]]-swelling flower,<br>And make rough [[Winter|winter]] everlastingly. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | Why, [[Valentine]], what braggartism is this? |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | Pardon me, [[Proteus]], all I can is nothing<br>To her whose worth makes other worthies nothing.<br>She is [[Alone|alone]]. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | Then let her [[Alone|alone]]. |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | Not for the world. Why man, she is mine own,<br>And I as rich in having such a [[Jewel|jewel]]<br>As twenty seas, if all their sand were pearl,<br>The [[Water|water]] nectar, and the rocks pure gold.<br>Forgive me that I do not dream on thee<br>Because thou seest me dote upon my [[Love|love]].<br>My foolish rival, that her father likes<br>Only for his possessions are so huge,<br>Is gone with her along, and I must after;<br>For [[Love|love]], thou know’st, is full of [[Jealousy|jealousy]]. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | But she loves you? |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | Ay, and we are betrothed. Nay more, our [[Marriage|marriage]]<br>hour,<br>With all the cunning manner of our flight,<br>Determined of: how I must climb her [[Window|window]],<br>The ladder made of cords, and all the means<br>Plotted and ‘[[Greed|greed]] on for my happiness.<br>Good [[Proteus]], go with me to my chamber<br>In these affairs to aid me with thy counsel. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | Go on before. I shall enquire you forth.<br>I must unto the [[Road|road]], to disembark<br>Some necessaries that I needs must use,<br>And then I’ll presently attend you. |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | Will you make haste? |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | I will. Exit [[Valentine]]<br>Even as one heat another heat expels,<br>Or as one nail by strength drives out another,<br>So the remembrance of my former [[Love|love]]<br>Is by a newer object quite forgotten.<br>Is it mine eye, or [[Valentine]]’s praise,<br>Her true perfection, or my false transgression<br>That makes me, reasonless, to reason thus?<br>She is fair, and so is [[Julia]] that I [[Love|love]]—<br>That I did [[Love|love]], for now my [[Love|love]] is thawed,<br>Which like a waxen image ‘gainst a [[Fire|fire]]<br>Bears no impression of the thing it was.<br>Methinks my zeal to [[Valentine]] is cold,<br>And that I [[Love|love]] him not as I was wont.<br>O, but I [[Love|love]] his lady too-too much,<br>And that’s the reason I [[Love|love]] him so little.<br>How shall I dote on her with more advice,<br>That thus without advice begin to [[Love|love]] her?<br>‘Tis but her picture I have yet beheld,<br>And that hath dazzled my reason’s light.<br>But when I look on her perfections<br>There is no reason but I shall be blind.<br>If I can check my erring [[Love|love]] I will,<br>If not, to compass her I’ll use my skill. Exit |
