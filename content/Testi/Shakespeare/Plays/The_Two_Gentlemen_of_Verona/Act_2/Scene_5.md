| Chi parla | Battuta |
|---|---|
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Speed]], and Lance with his dog Crab |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | Lance, by mine honesty, welcome to Milan. |
| LANCE | Forswear not thyself, sweet youth, for I am not welcome. I reckon this always, that a man is never undone till he be hanged, nor never welcome to a place till some certain shot be paid and the hostess say ‘Welcome’. |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | Come on, you madcap. I’ll to the alehouse with you presently, where, for one shot of five pence, thou shalt have five thousand welcomes. But sirrah, how did thy master part with Madam [[Julia]]? |
| LANCE | Marry, after they closed in earnest they parted very fairly in jest. |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | But shall she marry him? |
| LANCE | No. |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | How then, shall he marry her? |
| LANCE | No, neither. |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | What, are they broken? |
| LANCE | No, they are both as whole as a fish. |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | Why then, how stands the matter with them? |
| LANCE | Marry, thus: when it stands [[Well|well]] with him it stands [[Well|well]] with her. |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | What an ass art thou! I understand thee not. |
| LANCE | What a block art thou, that thou canst not! My staff understands me. |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | What thou sayst? |
| LANCE | Ay, and what I do too. Look thee, I’ll but lean, and my staff under-stands me. |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | It stands under thee indeed. |
| LANCE | Why, stand-under and under-stand is all one. [[Speed|SPEED]] But tell me true, will’t be a match? |
| LANCE | Ask my dog. If he say ‘Ay’, it will. If he say ‘No’, it will. If he shake his tail and say nothing, it will. |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | The conclusion is, then, that it will. |
| LANCE | Thou shalt never get such a secret from me but by a [[Parable|parable]]. |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | ‘Tis [[Well|well]] that I get it so. But Lance, how sayst thou that my master is become a notable [[Lover|lover]]? |
| LANCE | I never knew him otherwise. |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | Than how? |
| LANCE | A notable lubber, as thou reportest him to be. |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | Why, thou whoreson ass, thou mistak’st me. |
| LANCE | Why, [[Fool|fool]], I meant not thee, I meant thy master. |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | I tell thee my master is become a hot [[Lover|lover]]. |
| LANCE | Why, I tell thee I care not, though he burn himself in [[Love|love]]. If thou wilt, go with me to the alehouse. If not, thou art an Hebrew, a Jew, and not worth the name of a Christian. |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | Why? |
| LANCE | Because thou hast not so much [[Charity|charity]] in thee as to go to the ale with a Christian. Wilt thou go? |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | At thy service. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt |
