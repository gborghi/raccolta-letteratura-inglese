| *(didascalia)* | Enter Sir Eglamour |
| EGLAMOUR | This is the hour that Madam [[Silvia]]<br>Entreated me to call, and know her mind.<br>There’s some great matter she’d employ me in.<br>Madam, madam! |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Silvia]] [above] |
| [[Silvia|SILVIA]] | Who calls? |
| EGLAMOUR | Your servant, and your friend. One that attends your ladyship’s command. |
| [[Silvia|SILVIA]] | Sir Eglamour, a thousand times good morrow! |
| EGLAMOUR | As many, worthy lady, to yourself.<br>According to your ladyship’s impose<br>I am thus early come, to know what service<br>It is your pleasure to command me in. |
| [[Silvia|SILVIA]] | O Eglamour, thou art a gentleman—<br>Think not I flatter, for I swear I do not—<br>Valiant, wise, remorseful, [[Well|well]] accomplished.<br>Thou art not ignorant what dear good will<br>I bear unto the banished [[Valentine]],<br>Nor how my father would enforce me marry<br>Vain [[Thurio]], whom my very [[Soul|soul]] abhors.<br>Thyself hast loved, and I have heard thee say<br>No grief did ever come so near thy heart<br>As when thy lady and thy true [[Love|love]] died,<br>Upon whose [[Grave|grave]] thou vowed’st pure chastity.<br>Sir Eglamour, I would to [[Valentine]],<br>To Mantua, where I hear he makes abode;<br>And for the ways are dangerous to pass<br>I do [[Desire|desire]] thy worthy company,<br>Upon whose [[Faith|faith]] and [[Honour|honour]] I repose.<br>Urge not my father’s anger, Eglamour,<br>But think upon my grief, a lady’s grief,<br>And on the justice of my flying hence<br>To keep me from a most unholy match,<br>Which heaven and fortune still rewards with plagues.<br>I do [[Desire|desire]] thee, even from a heart<br>As full of sorrows as the sea of sands,<br>To bear me company and go with me.<br>If not, to hide what I have said to thee<br>That I may venture to depart [[Alone|alone]]. |
| EGLAMOUR | Madam, I pity much your grievances,<br>Which, since I know they virtuously are placed,<br>I give consent to go along with you,<br>Recking as little what betideth me<br>As much I wish all good befortune you.<br>When will you go? |
| [[Silvia|SILVIA]] | This evening coming. |
| EGLAMOUR | Where shall I meet you? |
| [[Silvia|SILVIA]] | At Friar Patrick’s cell,<br>Where I intend holy confession. |
| EGLAMOUR | I will not fail your ladyship.<br>Good morrow, gentle lady. |
| [[Silvia|SILVIA]] | Good morrow, kind Sir Eglamour. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt |
