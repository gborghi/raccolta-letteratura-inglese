| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Proteus]] |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | Already have I been false to [[Valentine]],<br>And now I must be as unjust to [[Thurio]].<br>Under the colour of commending him<br>I have access my own [[Love|love]] to prefer.<br>But [[Silvia]] is too fair, too true, too holy<br>To be corrupted with my worthless gifts.<br>When I protest true loyalty to her<br>She twits me with my falsehood to my friend.<br>When to her [[Beauty|beauty]] I commend my vows<br>She bids me think how I have been forsworn<br>In breaking [[Faith|faith]] with [[Julia]], whom I loved.<br>And notwithstanding all her sudden quips,<br>The least whereof would quell a [[Lover|lover]]’s [[Hope|hope]],<br>Yet, spaniel-like, the more she spurns my [[Love|love]],<br>The more it grows and fawneth on her still.<br>But here comes [[Thurio]]. Now must we to her [[Window|window]],<br>And give some evening music to her ear. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Thurio]] with Musicians |
| [[Thurio|THURIO]] | How now, Sir [[Proteus]], are you crept before us? |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | Ay, gentle [[Thurio]], for you know that [[Love|love]]<br>Will creep in service where it cannot go. |
| [[Thurio|THURIO]] | Ay, but I [[Hope|hope]], sir, that you [[Love|love]] not here. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | Sir, but I do, or else I would be hence. |
| [[Thurio|THURIO]] | Who, [[Silvia]]? |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | Ay, [[Silvia]]—for your sake. |
| [[Thurio|THURIO]] | I thank you for your own. Now, gentlemen,<br>Let’s tune, and to it lustily awhile. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter the Host, and [[Julia]], dressed as a page-boy. |
| [[Thurio|THURIO]] | They talk apart |
| HOST | Now, my young guest, methinks you’re allycholly. I pray you, why is it? |
| [[Julia|JULIA]] | Marry, mine host, because I cannot be merry. |
| HOST | Come, we’ll have you merry. I’ll bring you where you shall hear music, and see the gentleman that you asked for. |
| [[Julia|JULIA]] | But shall I hear him speak? |
| HOST | Ay, that you shall. |
| [[Julia|JULIA]] | That will be music. |
| HOST | Hark, hark. |
| [[Julia|JULIA]] | Is he among these? |
| HOST | Ay. But peace, let’s hear ’em. |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Song]] |
| HOST | Who is [[Silvia]]? What is she,<br>That all our swains commend her?<br>Holy, fair, and wise is she.<br>The heaven such grace did lend her<br>That she might admired be.<br>Is she kind as she is fair?<br>For [[Beauty|beauty]] lives with kindness.<br>[[Love]] doth to her eyes repair<br>To help him of his blindness,<br>And, being helped, inhabits there.<br>Then to [[Silvia]] let us sing<br>That [[Silvia]] is excelling.<br>She excels each mortal thing<br>Upon the dull earth dwelling.<br>To her let us garlands bring. |
| HOST | How now, are you sadder than you were before? How do you, man? The music likes you not. |
| [[Julia|JULIA]] | You mistake. The musician likes me not. |
| HOST | Why, my pretty youth? |
| [[Julia|JULIA]] | He plays false, father. |
| HOST | How, out of tune on the strings? |
| [[Julia|JULIA]] | Not so, but yet so false that he grieves my very heart-strings. |
| HOST | You have a quick ear. |
| [[Julia|JULIA]] | Ay, I would I were deaf. It makes me have a slow heart. |
| HOST | I perceive you delight not in music. |
| [[Julia|JULIA]] | Not a whit when it jars so. |
| HOST | Hark what fine change is in the music. |
| [[Julia|JULIA]] | Ay, that ‘change’ is the spite. |
| HOST | You would have them always play but one thing? |
| [[Julia|JULIA]] | I would always have one play but one thing. But host, doth this Sir [[Proteus]] that we talk on often resort unto this gentlewoman? |
| HOST | I tell you what Lance his man told me, he loved her out of all nick. |
| [[Julia|JULIA]] | Where is Lance? |
| HOST | Gone to seek his dog, which tomorrow, by his master’s command, he must carry for a present to his lady. |
| [[Julia|JULIA]] | Peace, stand aside. The company parts. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | Sir [[Thurio]], [[Fear|fear]] not you. I will so plead<br>That you shall say my cunning drift excels. |
| [[Thurio|THURIO]] | Where meet we? |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | At Saint Gregory’s [[Well|well]]. |
| [[Thurio|THURIO]] | Farewell. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt [[Thurio]] and the Musicians |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Silvia]], above |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | Madam, good even to your ladyship. |
| [[Silvia|SILVIA]] | I thank you for your music, gentlemen.<br>Who is that that spake? |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | One, lady, if you knew his pure heart’s [[Truth|truth]]<br>You would quickly learn to know him by his voice. |
| [[Silvia|SILVIA]] | Sir [[Proteus]], as I take it. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | Sir [[Proteus]], gentle lady, and your servant. |
| [[Silvia|SILVIA]] | What’s your will? |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | That I may compass yours. |
| [[Silvia|SILVIA]] | You have your wish. My will is even this,<br>That presently you hie you [[Home|home]] to bed.<br>Thou subtle, perjured, false, disloyal man,<br>Think’st thou I am so shallow, so conceitless<br>To be seduced by thy flattery,<br>That hast deceived so many with thy vows?<br>Return, return, and make thy [[Love|love]] amends.<br>For me—by this pale [[Queen|queen]] of [[Night|night]] I swear—<br>I am so far from granting thy request<br>That I despise thee for thy wrongful suit,<br>And by and by intend to chide myself<br>Even for this [[Time|time]] I spend in talking to thee. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | I grant, sweet [[Love|love]], that I did [[Love|love]] a lady,<br>But she is dead. |
| [[Julia|JULIA]] | (aside) ‘Twere false if I should speak it,<br>For I am sure she is not buried. |
| [[Silvia|SILVIA]] | Say that she be, yet [[Valentine]], thy friend,<br>Survives, to whom, thyself art witness,<br>I am betrothed. And art thou not ashamed<br>To wrong him with thy importunacy? |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | I likewise hear that [[Valentine]] is dead. |
| [[Silvia|SILVIA]] | And so suppose am I, for in his [[Grave|grave]],<br>Assure thyself, my [[Love|love]] is buried. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | Sweet lady, let me rake it from the earth. |
| [[Silvia|SILVIA]] | Go to thy lady’s [[Grave|grave]] and call hers thence,<br>Or at the least, in hers sepulchre thine. |
| [[Julia|JULIA]] | (aside) He heard not that. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | Madam, if your heart be so obdurate,<br>Vouchsafe me yet your picture for my [[Love|love]],<br>The picture that is hanging in your chamber.<br>To that I’ll speak, to that I’ll sigh and weep;<br>For since the substance of your perfect [[Self|self]]<br>Is else devoted, I am but a [[Shadow|shadow]],<br>And to your [[Shadow|shadow]] will I make true [[Love|love]]. |
| [[Julia|JULIA]] | (aside)<br>If ’twere a substance, you would sure deceive it<br>And make it but a [[Shadow|shadow]], as I am. |
| [[Silvia|SILVIA]] | I am very loath to be your idol, sir,<br>But since your falsehood shall become you [[Well|well]]<br>To worship shadows and adore false shapes,<br>Send to me in the morning, and I’ll send it.<br>And so, good rest. Exit |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | As wretches have o’ernight,<br>That wait for execution in the morn. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit |
| [[Julia|JULIA]] | Host, will you go? |
| HOST | By my halidom, I was fast asleep. |
| [[Julia|JULIA]] | Pray you, where lies Sir [[Proteus]]? |
| HOST | Marry, at my [[house]]. Trust me, I think ’tis almost day. |
| [[Julia|JULIA]] | Not so; but it hath been the longest [[Night|night]]<br>That e’er I watched, and the most heaviest. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt |
