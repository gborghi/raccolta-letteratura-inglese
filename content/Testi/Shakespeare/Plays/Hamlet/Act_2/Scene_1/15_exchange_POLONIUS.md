| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | This is too long. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | It shall to the barber’s, with your beard. (To First Player) Prithee, say on. He’s for a jig or a tale of bawdry, or he sleeps. Say on, come to Hecuba. |
| FIRST PLAYER | ‘But who, O who had seen the mobbled [[Queen|queen]]’— |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | ‘The mobbled [[Queen|queen]]’? |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | That’s good; ‘mobbled [[Queen|queen]]’ is good. |
| FIRST PLAYER | ‘Run barefoot up and down, threat’ning the flames With bisson rheum; a clout upon that head Where late the diadem stood, and for a robe, About her lank and all o‘er-teemèd loins, A blanket in th’alarm of [[Fear|fear]] caught up— Who this had seen, with tongue in venom steeped, ‘Gainst Fortune’s state would treason have pronounced. But if the gods themselves did see her then, When she saw Pyrrhus make malicious sport In mincing with his [[Sword|sword]] her husband’s limbs, The instant burst of clamour that she made— Unless things mortal move them not at all— Would have made milch the burning eyes of heaven, And passion in the gods.’ |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | Look whe’er he has not turned his colour, and has [[Tears|tears]] in ’s eyes. (To First Player) Prithee, no more. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | (to First Player) ’Tis [[Well|well]]. I’ll have thee speak out the rest soon. (To [[Polonius]]) Good my lord, will you see the players [[Well|well]] bestowed? Do ye hear?—let them be [[Well|well]] used, for they are the abstracts and brief chronicles of the [[Time|time]]. After your [[Death|death]] you were better have a bad epitaph than their ill report while you live. |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | My lord, I will use them according to their [[Desert|desert]]. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | God’s bodykins, man, much better. Use every man after his [[Desert|desert]], and who should scape whipping? Use them after your own [[Honour|honour]] and dignity—the less they deserve, the more merit is in your bounty. Take them in. |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | (to Players) Come, sirs. Exit |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | (to Players) Follow him, friends. We’ll hear a play tomorrow. Dost thou hear me, old friend? Can you play the murder of Gonzago? PLAYERS Ay, my lord. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | We’ll ha’t tomorrow [[Night|night]]. You could for a need study a speech of some dozen or sixteen lines which I would set down and insert in’t, could ye not? PLAYERS Ay, my lord. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Very [[Well|well]]. Follow that lord, and look you mock him not. Exeunt Players My good friends, I’ll leave you till [[Night|night]]. You are welcome to Elsinore. |
