| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Queen]] [[Gertrude]] and [[Horatio]] |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | I will not speak with her. |
| [[Horatio|HORATIO]] | She is importunate,<br>Indeed distraught. Her mood will needs be pitied. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | What would she have? |
| [[Horatio|HORATIO]] | She speaks much of her father, says she hears<br>There’s tricks i’th’ world, and hems, and beats her<br>heart,<br>Spurns enviously at straws, speaks things in [[Doubt|doubt]]<br>That carry but half sense. Her speech is nothing,<br>Yet the unshapèd use of it doth move<br>The hearers to collection. They aim at it,<br>And botch the words up fit to their own thoughts,<br>Which, as her winks and nods and gestures yield them,<br>Indeed would make one think there might be thought,<br>Though nothing sure, yet much unhappily. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | ’Twere good she were spoken with, for she may strew<br>Dangerous conjectures in ill-breeding minds.<br>Let her come in. |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Horatio]] withdraws to admit [[Ophelia]] |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | To my sick [[Soul|soul]], as sin’s true [[Nature|nature]] is,<br>Each toy seems prologue to some great amiss.<br>So full of artless [[Jealousy|jealousy]] is [[Guilt|guilt]],<br>It spills itself in fearing to be spilt. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Ophelia]] mad, her hair down, with a lute |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | Where is the beauteous majesty of [[Denmark]]? |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | How now, [[Ophelia]]? |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | (sings)<br>How should I your true [[Love|love]] know<br>From another one?—<br>By his cockle hat and staff,<br>And his sandal shoon. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | Alas, sweet lady, what imports this [[Song|song]]? |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | Say you? Nay, pray you, mark. (Sings)<br>He is dead and gone, lady,<br>He is dead and gone.<br>At his head a grass-green turf,<br>At his heels a stone. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | Nay, but [[Ophelia]]— |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | Pray you, mark.<br>(Sings)<br>White his shroud as the mountain [[Snow|snow]]— |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[King]] [[Claudius]] |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | Alas, look here, my lord. |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | (sings)<br>Larded with sweet [[Flowers|flowers]],<br>Which bewept to the [[Grave|grave]] did—not—go<br>With true-[[Love|love]] showers. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | How do ye, pretty lady? |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | [[Well]], God’ield you. They say the owl was a baker’s daughter. Lord, we know what we are, but know not what we may be. God be at your table! |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | (to [[Gertrude]]) Conceit upon her father. |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | Pray you, let’s have no words of this, but when they ask you what it means, say you this. (Sings)<br>Tomorrow is Saint [[Valentine]]’s day,<br>All in the morning betime,<br>And I a maid at your [[Window|window]]<br>To be your [[Valentine]].<br>Then up he [[Rose|rose]], and donned his clothes,<br>And dupped the chamber door;<br>Let in the maid, that out a maid<br>Never departed more. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | Pretty [[Ophelia]]— |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | Indeed, la? Without an oath, I’ll make an end on’t.<br>(Sings) By Gis, and by Saint [[Charity]],<br>Alack, and fie for shame!<br>Young men will do’t if they come to‘t,<br>By Cock, they are to blame.<br>Quoth she ‘Before you tumbled me,<br>You promised me to wed.’<br>So would I ‘a’ done, by yonder sun,<br>An thou hadst not come to my bed. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | (to [[Gertrude]]) How long hath she been thus? |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | I [[Hope|hope]] all will be [[Well|well]]. We must be patient. But I cannot choose but weep to think they should lay him i’th’ cold ground. My brother shall know of it. And so I thank you for your good counsel. Come, my coach! Good [[Night|night]], ladies, good [[Night|night]], sweet ladies, good [[Night|night]], good [[Night|night]]. Exit |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | (to [[Horatio]])<br>Follow her close. Give her good watch, I pray you. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit [[Horatio]] |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | O, this is the [[Poison|poison]] of deep grief! It springs<br>All from her father’s [[Death|death]]. O [[Gertrude]], [[Gertrude]],<br>When sorrows come they come not single spies,<br>But in battalions. First, her father slain;<br>Next, your son gone, and he most violent author<br>Of his own just remove; the people muddied,<br>Thick and unwholesome in their thoughts and whispers<br>For good [[Polonius]]’ [[Death|death]]; and we have done but<br>greenly<br>In hugger-mugger to inter him; poor [[Ophelia]]<br>Divided from herself and her fair judgement,<br>Without the which we are pictures or mere beasts;<br>Last, and as much containing as all these,<br>Her brother is in secret come from France,<br>Feeds on this [[Wonder|wonder]], keeps himself in clouds,<br>And wants not buzzers to infect his ear<br>With pestilent speeches of his father’s [[Death|death]];<br>Wherein necessity, of matter beggared,<br>Will nothing stick our persons to arraign<br>In ear and ear. O my dear [[Gertrude]], this,<br>Like to a murd’ring-piece, in many places<br>Gives me superfluous [[Death|death]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | A noise within |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | Alack, what noise is this? |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | Where is my Switzers? Let them guard the door. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter a Messenger |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | What is the matter? |
| MESSENGER | Save yourself, my lord.<br>The ocean, overpeering of his list,<br>Eats not the flats with more impetuous haste<br>Than young [[Laertes]], in a riotous head,<br>O‘erbears your officers. The rabble call him lord,<br>And, as the world were now but to begin,<br>Antiquity forgot, custom not known,<br>The ratifiers and props of every word,<br>They cry ‘Choose we! [[Laertes]] shall be [[King|king]].’<br>Caps, hands, and tongues applaud it to the clouds,<br>‘[[Laertes]] shall be [[King|king]], [[Laertes]] [[King|king]].’ |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | How cheerfully on the false trail they cry! |
| *(didascalia)* | A noise within |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | O, this is counter, you false Danish [[dogs]]! |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | The doors are broke. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Laertes]] with his followers at the door |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | Where is the [[King]]?—Sirs, stand you all without. |
| ALL HIS FOLLOWERS | No, let’s come in. |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | I pray you, give me leave. |
| ALL HIS FOLLOWERS | We will, we will. |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | I thank you. Keep the door. |
| *(didascalia)* | exeunt followers |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | O thou vile [[King|king]],<br>Give me my father. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | Calmly, good [[Laertes]]. |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | That drop of [[Blood|blood]] that’s calm proclaims me bastard,<br>Cries cuckold to my father, brands the harlot<br>Even here between the chaste unsmirchèd brow<br>Of my true mother. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | What is the cause, [[Laertes]],<br>That thy rebellion looks so giant-like?—<br>Let him go, [[Gertrude]]. Do not [[Fear|fear]] our person.<br>There’s such divinity doth hedge a [[King|king]]<br>That treason can but peep to what it would,<br>Acts little of his will.—Tell me, [[Laertes]],<br>Why thou art thus incensed.—Let him go, [[Gertrude]].—<br>Speak, man. |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | Where is my father? |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | Dead. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | (to [[Laertes]])<br>But not by him. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | Let him demand his fill. |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | How came he dead? I’ll not be juggled with.<br>To hell, allegiance! Vows to the blackest devil!<br>[[Conscience]] and grace to the profoundest pit!<br>I dare damnation. To this point I stand,<br>That both the worlds I give to negligence,<br>Let come what comes. Only I’ll be revenged<br>Most throughly for my father. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | Who shall stay you? |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | My will, not all the world;<br>And for my means, I’ll husband them so [[Well|well]]<br>They shall go far with little. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | Good [[Laertes]],<br>If you [[Desire|desire]] to know the certainty<br>Of your dear father’s [[Death|death]], is’t writ in your [[Revenge|revenge]]<br>That, sweepstake, you will draw both friend and foe,<br>Winner and loser? |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | None but his enemies. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | Will you know them then? |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | To his good friends thus wide I’ll ope my arms,<br>And, like the kind life-rend’ring pelican,<br>Repast them with my [[Blood|blood]]. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | Why, now you speak<br>Like a good [[Child|child]] and a true gentleman.<br>That I am guiltless of your father’s [[Death|death]],<br>And am most sensibly in grief for it,<br>It shall as level to your judgement pierce<br>As day does to your eye. |
| *(didascalia)* | A noise within |
| VOICES | (within) Let her come in. |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | How now, what noise is that? |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Ophelia]] as before |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | O heat dry up my brains! [[Tears]] seven times salt<br>Burn out the sense and virtue of mine eye!<br>By heaven, thy [[Madness|madness]] shall be paid by weight<br>Till our scale turns the beam. O [[Rose|rose]] of May,<br>Dear maid, kind sister, sweet [[Ophelia]]!<br>O heavens, is’t possible a young maid’s wits<br>Should be as mortal as an old man’s life?<br>[[Nature]] is fine in [[Love|love]], and where ’tis fine<br>It sends some precious instance of itself<br>After the thing it loves. |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | (sings)<br>They bore him barefaced on the bier,<br>Hey non nony, nony, hey nony,<br>And on his [[Grave|grave]] rained many a tear—<br>Fare you [[Well|well]], my dove. |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | Hadst thou thy wits and didst persuade [[Revenge|revenge]],<br>It could not move thus. |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | You must sing ‘Down, a-down’, and you, ‘Call him a-down-a’. O, how the [[Wheel|wheel]] becomes it! It is the false steward that stole his master’s daughter. [[Laertes|LAERTES]] This nothing’s more than matter. |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | There’s rosemary, that’s for remembrance. Pray, [[Love|love]], remember. And there is pansies; that’s for thoughts. |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | A document in [[Madness|madness]]—thoughts and remembrance fitted. |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | There’s fennel for you, and columbines. There’s rue for you, and here’s some for me. We may call it herb-grace o’ Sundays. O, you must wear your rue with a difference. There’s a daisy. I would give you some violets, but they withered all when my father died. They say a made a good end.<br>(Sings) For bonny sweet Robin is all my joy. |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | Thought and affliction, passion, hell itself<br>She turns to favour and to prettiness. |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | (sings)<br>And will a not come again,<br>And will a not come again?<br>No, no, he is dead,<br>Go to thy [[Death|death]]-bed,<br>He never will come again.<br>His beard as white as [[Snow|snow]],<br>All flaxen was his poll.<br>He is gone, he is gone,<br>And we cast away moan.<br>God ‘a’ [[Mercy|mercy]] on his [[Soul|soul]].<br>And of all Christian souls, I pray God. God b’wi’ ye. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt [[Ophelia]] and [[Gertrude]] |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | Do you see this, O God? |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | [[Laertes]], I must commune with your grief,<br>Or you deny me right. Go but apart,<br>Make choice of whom your wisest friends you will,<br>And they shall hear and judge ’twixt you and me.<br>If by direct or by collateral hand<br>They find us touched, we will our kingdom give,<br>Our [[Crown|crown]], our life, and all that we call ours,<br>To you in satisfaction. But if not,<br>Be you content to lend your patience to us,<br>And we shall jointly labour with your [[Soul|soul]]<br>To give it due content. |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | Let this be so.<br>His means of [[Death|death]], his obscure burial—<br>No trophy, [[Sword|sword]], nor hatchment o‘er his bones,<br>No noble rite nor formal ostentation—<br>Cry to be heard, as ’twere from heaven to earth,<br>That I must call’t in question. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | So you shall;<br>And where th’offence is, let the great axe fall.<br>I pray you go with me. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt |
