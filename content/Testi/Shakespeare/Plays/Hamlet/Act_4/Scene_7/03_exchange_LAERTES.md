| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | Drowned? O, where? |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | There is a willow grows aslant a brook That shows his hoar [[Leaves|leaves]] in the glassy stream. Therewith fantastic garlands did she make Of crow-[[Flowers|flowers]], nettles, daisies, and long purples, That liberal shepherds give a grosser name, But our cold maids do dead men’s fingers call them. There on the pendent boughs her crownet weeds Clamb’ring to hang, an envious sliver broke, When down the weedy trophies and herself Fell in the weeping brook. Her clothes spread wide, And [[Mermaid|mermaid]]-like a while they bore her up; Which [[Time|time]] she chanted snatches of old tunes, As one incapable of her own distress, Or like a creature native and endued Unto that element. But long it could not be Till that her garments, heavy with their drink, Pulled the poor wretch from her melodious lay To muddy [[Death|death]]. |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | Alas, then is she drowned. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | Drowned, drowned. |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | Too much of [[Water|water]] hast thou, poor [[Ophelia]], And therefore I forbid my [[Tears|tears]]. But yet It is our trick; [[Nature|nature]] her custom holds, Let shame say what it will. He weeps When these are gone, The woman will be out. Adieu, my lord. I have a speech of [[Fire|fire]] that fain would blaze, But that this folly douts it. Exit |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | Let’s follow, [[Gertrude]]. How much I had to do to calm his rage! Now [[Fear|fear]] I this will give it start again; Therefore let’s follow. Exeunt |
