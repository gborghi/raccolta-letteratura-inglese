| *(didascalia)* | Enter Prince [[Hamlet]] |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Safely stowed. |
| [[Rosencrantz|ROSENCRANTZ]] | and [[Guildenstern|GUILDENSTERN]] (within)<br>[[Hamlet]], Lord [[Hamlet]]! |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | What noise? Who calls on [[Hamlet]]? |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Rosencrantz]] and [[Guildenstern]] |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | O, here they come. |
| [[Rosencrantz|ROSENCRANTZ]] | What have you done, my lord, with the dead [[Body|body]]? |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Compounded it with [[Dust|dust]], whereto ’tis kin. |
| [[Rosencrantz|ROSENCRANTZ]] | Tell us where ’tis, that we may take it thence<br>And bear it to the chapel. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Do not believe it. |
| [[Rosencrantz|ROSENCRANTZ]] | Believe what? |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | That I can keep your counsel and not mine own. Besides, to be demanded of a sponge—what replication should be made by the son of a [[King|king]]? |
| [[Rosencrantz|ROSENCRANTZ]] | Take you me for a sponge, my lord? |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Ay, sir, that soaks up the [[King]]’s countenance, his rewards, his authorities. But such officers do the [[King]] best service in the end. He keeps them, like an ape an apple in the corner of his jaw, first mouthed to be last swallowed. When he needs what you have gleaned, it is but squeezing you, and, sponge, you shall be dry again. |
| [[Rosencrantz|ROSENCRANTZ]] | I understand you not, my lord. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | I am glad of it. A knavish speech sleeps in a foolish ear. |
| [[Rosencrantz|ROSENCRANTZ]] | My lord, you must tell us where the [[Body|body]] is, and go with us to the [[King]]. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | The [[Body|body]] is with the [[King]], but the [[King]] is not with the [[Body|body]]. The [[King]] is a thing— |
| [[Guildenstern|GUILDENSTERN]] | A thing, my lord? |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Of nothing. Bring me to him. Hide fox, and all after. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit running, pursued by the others |
