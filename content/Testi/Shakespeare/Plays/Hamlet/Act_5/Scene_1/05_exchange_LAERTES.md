| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | The devil take thy [[Soul|soul]]. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Thou pray’st not [[Well|well]]. I prithee take thy fingers from my throat, For though I am not splenative and rash, Yet have I something in me dangerous, Which let thy wiseness [[Fear|fear]]. Away thy hand. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | (to Lords) Pluck them asunder. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | [[Hamlet]], [[Hamlet]]! |
| ALL | THE LORDS Gentlemen! |
| [[Horatio|HORATIO]] | (to [[Hamlet]]) Good my lord, be quiet. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Why, I will fight with him upon this theme Until my eyelids will no longer wag. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | O my son, what theme? |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | I loved [[Ophelia]]. Forty thousand brothers Could not, with all their quantity of [[Love|love]], Make up my sum.—What wilt thou do for her? |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | O, he is mad, [[Laertes]]. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | (to [[Laertes]]) For [[Love|love]] of God, forbear him. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | (to [[Laertes]]) ‘Swounds, show me what thou’lt do. Woot weep, woot fight, woot fast, woot tear thyself, Woot drink up eisel, eat a crocodile? I’ll do’t. Dost thou come here to whine, To outface me with leaping in her [[Grave|grave]]? Be buried quick with her, and so will I. And if thou prate of [[Mountains|mountains]], let them throw Millions of acres on us, till our ground, Singeing his pate against the burning zone, Make Ossa like a wart. Nay, an thou’lt mouth, I’ll rant as [[Well|well]] as thou. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | to [[Laertes]] This is mere [[Madness|madness]], And thus a while the fit will work on him. Anon, as patient as the female dove When that her golden couplets are disclosed, His [[Silence|silence]] will sit drooping. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | (to [[Laertes]]) Hear you, sir, What is the reason that you use me thus? I loved you ever. But it is no matter. Let Hercules himself do what he may, The cat will mew, and dog will have his day. Exit |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | I pray you, good [[Horatio]], wait upon him. Exit [[Horatio]] |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | (To [[Laertes]]).Strengthen your patience in our last [[Night|night]]’s speech. We’ll put the matter to the present push.— Good [[Gertrude]], set some watch over your son.— This [[Grave|grave]] shall have a living monument. An hour of quiet shortly shall we see; Till then, in patience our proceeding be. Exeunt |
