| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Queen]] [[Gertrude]] and [[Polonius]] |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | A will come straight. Look you lay [[Home|home]] to him. Tell him his pranks have been too broad to bear with, And that your grace hath screened and stood between Much heat and him. I’ll [[Silence|silence]] me e’en here. Pray you be round with him. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | (within) Mother, mother, mother! |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | I’ll warr’nt you. [[Fear]] me not. Withdraw; I hear him coming. [[Polonius]] hides behind the arras. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Prince [[Hamlet]] |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Now, mother, what’s the matter? |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | [[Hamlet]], thou hast thy father much offended,. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Mother, you have my father much offended. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | Come, come, you answer with an idle tongue. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Go, go, you question with a wicked tongue. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | Why, how now, [[Hamlet]]? |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | What’s the matter now? |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | Have you forgot me? |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | No, by the rood, not so. You are the [[Queen]], your husband’s brother’s wife. But—would you were not so—you are my mother. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | Nay, then, I’ll set those to you that can speak. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Come, come, and sit you down. You shall not budge. You go not till I set you up a glass Where you may see the inmost part of you. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | What wilt thou do? Thou wilt not murder me? Help, help, ho! |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | (behind the arras) What ho! Help, help, help! |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | How now, a rat? Dead for a ducat, dead. He thrusts his [[Sword|sword]] through the arras |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | O, I am slain! |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | (to [[Hamlet]]) O me, what hast thou done? |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Nay, I know not. Is it the [[King]]? |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | O, what a rash and bloody deed is this! |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | A bloody deed—almost as bad, good-mother, As kill a [[King|king]] and marry with his brother. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | As kill a [[King|king]]? |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Ay, lady, ’twas my word. (To [[Polonius]]) Thou wretched, rash, intruding [[Fool|fool]], farewell. I took thee for thy better. Take thy fortune. Thou find’st to be too busy is some danger.— Leave wringing of your hands. Peace, sit you down, And let me wring your heart; for so I shall If it be made of penetrable stuff, If damned custom have not brassed it so That it is proof and bulwark against sense. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | What have I done, that thou dar’st wag thy tongue In noise so rude against me? |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Such an act That blurs the grace and blush of modesty, Calls virtue hypocrite, takes off the [[Rose|rose]] From the fair forehead of an [[Innocent|innocent]] [[Love|love]] And sets a blister there, makes [[Marriage|marriage]] vows As false as dicers’ oaths—O, such a deed As from the [[Body|body]] of contraction plucks The very [[Soul|soul]], and sweet religion makes A rhapsody of words. Heaven’s face doth glow, Yea, this solidity and compound mass With tristful visage, as against the doom, Is thought-sick at the act. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | Ay me, what act, That roars so loud and thunders in the index? |
