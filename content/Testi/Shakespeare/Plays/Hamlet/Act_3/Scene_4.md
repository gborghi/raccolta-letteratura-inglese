| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | A will come straight. Look you lay [[Home|home]] to him.<br>Tell him his pranks have been too broad to bear with,<br>And that your grace hath screened and stood between<br>Much heat and him. I’ll [[Silence|silence]] me e’en here.<br>Pray you be round with him. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | (within) Mother, mother, mother! |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | I’ll warr’nt you. [[Fear]] me not. Withdraw; I hear him<br>coming.<br>[[Polonius]] hides behind the arras. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Prince [[Hamlet]] |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Now, mother, what’s the matter? |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | [[Hamlet]], thou hast thy father much offended,. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Mother, you have my father much offended. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | Come, come, you answer with an idle tongue. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Go, go, you question with a wicked tongue. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | Why, how now, [[Hamlet]]? |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | What’s the matter now? |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | Have you forgot me? |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | No, by the rood, not so.<br>You are the [[Queen]], your husband’s brother’s wife. But—would you were not so—you are my mother. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | Nay, then, I’ll set those to you that can speak. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Come, come, and sit you down. You shall not budge.<br>You go not till I set you up a glass<br>Where you may see the inmost part of you. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | What wilt thou do? Thou wilt not murder me?<br>Help, help, ho! |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | (behind the arras) What ho! Help, help, help! |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | How now, a rat? Dead for a ducat, dead.<br>He thrusts his [[Sword|sword]] through the arras |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | O, I am slain! |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | (to [[Hamlet]]) O me, what hast thou done? |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Nay, I know not. Is it the [[King]]? |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | O, what a rash and bloody deed is this! |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | A bloody deed—almost as bad, good-mother,<br>As kill a [[King|king]] and marry with his brother. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | As kill a [[King|king]]? |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Ay, lady, ’twas my word.<br>(To [[Polonius]]) Thou wretched, rash, intruding [[Fool|fool]],<br>farewell.<br>I took thee for thy better. Take thy fortune.<br>Thou find’st to be too busy is some danger.—<br>Leave wringing of your hands. Peace, sit you down,<br>And let me wring your heart; for so I shall<br>If it be made of penetrable stuff,<br>If damned custom have not brassed it so<br>That it is proof and bulwark against sense. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | What have I done, that thou dar’st wag thy tongue<br>In noise so rude against me? |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Such an act<br>That blurs the grace and blush of modesty,<br>Calls virtue hypocrite, takes off the [[Rose|rose]]<br>From the fair forehead of an [[Innocent|innocent]] [[Love|love]]<br>And sets a blister there, makes [[Marriage|marriage]] vows<br>As false as dicers’ oaths—O, such a deed<br>As from the [[Body|body]] of contraction plucks<br>The very [[Soul|soul]], and sweet religion makes<br>A rhapsody of words. Heaven’s face doth glow,<br>Yea, this solidity and compound mass<br>With tristful visage, as against the doom,<br>Is thought-sick at the act. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | Ay me, what act,<br>That roars so loud and thunders in the index? |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Look here upon this picture, and on this,<br>The counterfeit presentment of two brothers.<br>See what a grace was seated on this brow—<br>[[Hyperion]]’s curls, the front of Jove himself,<br>An eye like Mars, to threaten or command,<br>A station like the herald Mercury<br>New lighted on a heaven-kissing [[Hill|hill]];<br>A combination and a form indeed<br>Where every god did seem to set his seal<br>To give the world assurance of a man.<br>This was your husband. Look you now what follows.<br>Here is your husband, like a mildewed ear<br>Blasting his wholesome brother. Have you eyes?<br>Could you on this fair mountain leave to feed,<br>And batten on this moor? Ha, have you eyes?<br>You cannot call it [[Love|love]], for at your age<br>The heyday in the [[Blood|blood]] is tame, it’s humble,<br>And waits upon the judgement; and what judgement<br>Would step from this to this? What devil was’t<br>That thus hath cozened you at hood-man blind?<br>O shame, where is thy blush? Rebellious hell,<br>If thou canst mutine in a matron’s bones,<br>To flaming youth let virtue be as wax<br>And melt in her own [[Fire|fire]]. Proclaim no shame<br>When the compulsive ardour gives the charge,<br>Since [[Frost|frost]] itself as actively doth burn,<br>And reason panders will. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | O [[Hamlet]], speak no more!<br>Thou turn’st mine eyes into my very [[Soul|soul]],<br>And there I see such black and grained spots<br>As will not leave their tinct. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Nay, but to live<br>In the rank sweat of an enseamèd bed,<br>Stewed in corruption, honeying and making [[Love|love]]<br>Over the nasty sty— |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | O, speak to me no more!<br>These words like daggers enter in mine ears.<br>No more, sweet [[Hamlet]]. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | A murderer and a [[Villain|villain]],<br>A slave that is not twenti’th part the tithe<br>Of your precedent lord, a vice of kings,<br>A cutpurse of the empire and the rule,<br>That from a shelf the precious diadem stole<br>And put it in his pocket— |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | No more. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | A [[King|king]] of shreds and patches— |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter the [[Ghost]] in his nightgown |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Save me and hover o’er me with your wings,<br>You heavenly guards! (To the [[Ghost]]) What would<br>you, gracious figure? 95 |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | Alas, he’s mad. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | (to the [[Ghost]])<br>Do you not come your tardy son to chide,<br>That, lapsed in [[Time|time]] and passion, lets go by<br>Th’important acting of your dread command?<br>O, say! |
| [[Ghost|GHOST]] | Do not forget. This visitation<br>Is but to whet thy almost blunted purpose.<br>But look, amazement on thy mother sits.<br>O, step between her and her fighting [[Soul|soul]].<br>Conceit in weakest bodies strongest works.<br>Speak to her, [[Hamlet]]. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | How is it with you, lady? |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | Alas, how is’t with you,<br>That you do bend your eye on vacancy,<br>And with th‘incorporal air do hold discourse?<br>Forth at your eyes your spirits wildly peep,<br>And, as the sleeping soldiers in th’alarm,<br>Your bedded hair, like life in excrements,<br>Start up and stand on end. O gentle son,<br>Upon the heat and flame of thy distemper<br>Sprinkle cool patience! Whereon do you look? |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | On him, on him. Look you how pale he glares.<br>His form and cause conjoined, preaching to stones,<br>Would make them capable. (To the [[Ghost]]) Do not look<br>upon me,<br>Lest with this piteous action you [[convert]]<br>My stern effects. Then what I have to do<br>Will want true colour—[[Tears|tears]] perchance for [[Blood|blood]]. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | To whom do you speak this? |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Do you see nothing there? |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | Nothing at all, yet all that is I see. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Nor did you nothing hear? |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | No, nothing but ourselves. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Why, look you there. Look how it steals away.<br>My father, in his habit as he lived.<br>Look where he goes even now out at the portal. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit the [[Ghost]] |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | This is the very coinage of your brain.<br>This bodiless creation ecstasy<br>Is very cunning in. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Ecstasy?<br>My pulse as yours doth temperately keep [[Time|time]],<br>And makes as healthful music. It is not [[Madness|madness]]<br>That I have uttered. Bring me to the test,<br>And I the matter will reword, which [[Madness|madness]]<br>Would gambol from. Mother, for [[Love|love]] of grace<br>Lay not a flattering unction to your [[Soul|soul]]<br>That not your trespass but my [[Madness|madness]] speaks.<br>It will but skin and film the ulcerous place<br>Whilst rank corruption, mining all within,<br>Infects unseen. Confess yourself to heaven;<br>Repent what’s past, avoid what is to come,<br>And do not spread the compost o’er the weeds<br>To make them ranker. Forgive me this my virtue,<br>For in the fatness of these pursy times<br>Virtue itself of vice must pardon beg,<br>Yea, curb and woo for leave to do him good. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | O [[Hamlet]], thou hast cleft my heart in twain! |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | O, throw away the worser part of it,<br>And live the purer with the other half!<br>Good [[Night|night]]—but go not to mine uncle’s bed.<br>Assume a virtue if you have it not.<br>Refrain tonight,<br>And that shall lend a kind of easiness<br>To the next abstinence. Once more, good [[Night|night]];<br>And when you are desirous to be blest,<br>I’ll blessing beg of you. For this same lord,<br>I do repent. But heaven hath pleased it so<br>To punish me with this, and this with me,<br>That I must be their scourge and minister.<br>I will bestow him, and will answer [[Well|well]]<br>The [[Death|death]] I gave him. So, again, good [[Night|night]].<br>I must be cruel only to be kind.<br>Thus bad begins, and worse remains behind. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | What shall I do? |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Not this, by no means, that I bid you do:<br>Let the bloat [[King]] tempt you again to bed,<br>Pinch wanton on your cheek, call you his mouse,<br>And let him for a pair of reechy kisses,<br>Or paddling in your neck with his damned fingers,<br>Make you to ravel all this matter out,<br>That I essentially am not in [[Madness|madness]],<br>But mad in craft. ’Twere good you let him know,<br>For who that’s but a [[Queen|queen]], fair, sober, wise,<br>Would from a paddock, from a bat, a gib,<br>Such dear concernings hide? Who would do so?<br>No, in despite of sense and secrecy,<br>Unpeg the basket on the [[house]]’s top,<br>Let the [[Birds|birds]] fly, and, like the famous ape,<br>To try conclusions in the basket creep,<br>And break your own neck down. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | Be thou assured, if words be made of breath,<br>And breath of life, I have no life to breathe<br>What thou hast said to me. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | I must to England.<br>You know that? |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | Alack, I had forgot.<br>’Tis so concluded on. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | This man shall set me packing.<br>I’ll lug the guts into the neighbour room.<br>Mother, good [[Night|night]] indeed. This counsellor<br>Is now most still, most secret, and most [[Grave|grave]],<br>Who was in life a foolish prating knave.—<br>Come, sir, to draw toward an end with you.—<br>Good [[Night|night]], mother. Exit, tugging in [[Polonius]] |
