| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Laertes]] and [[Ophelia]], his sister |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | My necessaries are inbarqued. Farewell.<br>And, sister, as the winds give benefit<br>And convoy is assistant, do not [[Sleep|sleep]]<br>But let me hear from you. |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | Do you [[Doubt|doubt]] that? |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | For [[Hamlet]] and the trifling of his favour,<br>Hold it a fashion and a toy in [[Blood|blood]],<br>A violet in the youth of primy [[Nature|nature]],<br>Forward not permanent, sweet not lasting,<br>The perfume and suppliance of a minute,<br>No more. |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | No more but so? |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | Think it no more.<br>For [[Nature|nature]] crescent does not grow [[Alone|alone]]<br>In thews and bulk, but as his temple waxes<br>The inward service of the mind and [[Soul|soul]]<br>Grows wide withal. Perhaps he loves you now,<br>And now no soil nor cautel doth besmirch<br>The virtue of his will; but you must [[Fear|fear]],<br>His [[Greatness|greatness]] weighed, his will is not his own,<br>For he himself is subject to his birth.<br>He may not, as unvalued persons do,<br>Carve for himself, for on his choice depends<br>The [[Sanity|sanity]] and health of the whole state;<br>And therefore must his choice be circumscribed<br>Unto the voice and yielding of that [[Body|body]]<br>Whereof he is the head. Then if he says he loves you,<br>It fits your wisdom so far to believe it<br>As he in his peculiar sect and force<br>May give his saying deed, which is no further<br>Than the main voice of [[Denmark]] goes withal.<br>Then weigh what loss your [[Honour|honour]] may sustain<br>If with too credent ear you list his [[songs]],<br>Or lose your heart, or your chaste treasure open<br>To his unmastered importunity.<br>[[Fear]] it, [[Ophelia]], [[Fear|fear]] it, my dear sister,<br>And keep within the rear of your affection,<br>Out of the shot and danger of [[Desire|desire]].<br>The chariest maid is prodigal enough<br>If she unmask her [[Beauty|beauty]] to the [[Moon|moon]].<br>Virtue itself scapes not calumnious strokes.<br>The canker galls the infants of the [[Spring|spring]]<br>Too oft before their buttons be disclosed,<br>And in the morn and liquid dew of youth<br>Contagious blastments are most imminent.<br>Be wary then; best safety lies in [[Fear|fear]];<br>Youth to itself rebels, though none else near. |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | I shall th’effect of this good lesson keep<br>As watchman to my heart; but, good my brother,<br>Do not, as some ungracious pastors do,<br>Show me the steep and thorny way to heaven<br>Whilst like a puffed and reckless libertine<br>Himself the primrose path of dalliance treads<br>And recks not his own rede. |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | O [[Fear|fear]] me not. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Polonius]] |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | I stay too long—but here my father comes.<br>A [[Double|double]] blessing is a [[Double|double]] grace;<br>Occasion smiles upon a second leave. |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | Yet here, [[Laertes]]? Aboard, aboard, for shame!<br>The [[Wind|wind]] sits in the shoulder of your sail,<br>And you are stayed for. There—my blessing with thee,<br>And these few precepts in thy [[Memory|memory]]<br>See thou character. Give thy thoughts no tongue,<br>Nor any unproportioned thought his act.<br>Be thou familiar but by no means vulgar.<br>The friends thou hast, and their adoption tried,<br>Grapple them to thy [[Soul|soul]] with hoops of steel,<br>But do not dull thy palm with entertainment<br>Of each new-hatched unfledged comrade. Beware<br>Of entrance to a quarrel, but being in,<br>Bear’t that th’opposèd may beware of thee.<br>Give every man thine ear but few thy voice.<br>Take each man’s censure, but reserve thy judgement.<br>Costly thy habit as thy purse can buy,<br>But not expressed in fancy; rich not gaudy;<br>For the apparel oft proclaims the man,<br>And they in France of the best rank and station<br>Are of all most select and generous chief in that.<br>Neither a borrower nor a lender be,<br>For loan oft loses both itself and friend,<br>And borrowing dulls the edge of husbandry.<br>This above all—to thine own [[Self|self]] be true,<br>And it must follow, as the [[Night|night]] the day,<br>Thou canst not then be false to any man.<br>Farewell—my blessing season this in thee. |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | Most humbly do I take my leave, my lord. |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | The [[Time|time]] invites you. Go; your servants tend. |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | Farewell, [[Ophelia]], and remember [[Well|well]]<br>What I have said to you. |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | ’Tis in my [[Memory|memory]] locked,<br>And you yourself shall keep the key of it. |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | Farewell. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | What is’t, [[Ophelia]], he hath said to you? |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | So please you, something touching the Lord [[Hamlet]]. |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | Marry, [[Well|well]] bethought.<br>‘Tis told me he hath very oft of late<br>Given private [[Time|time]] to you, and you yourself<br>Have of your audience been most free and bounteous.<br>If it be so—as so ’tis put on me,<br>And that in way of caution—I must tell you<br>You do not understand yourself so clearly<br>As it behoves my daughter and your [[Honour|honour]].<br>What is between you? Give me up the [[Truth|truth]]. |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | He hath, my lord, of late made many tenders<br>Of his affection to me. |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | Affection, pooh! You speak like a green girl<br>Unsifted in such perilous circumstance.<br>Do you believe his ‘tenders’ as you call them? |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | I do not know, my lord, what I should think. |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | Marry, I’ll teach you: think yourself a baby<br>That you have ta’en his tenders for true pay,<br>Which are not sterling. Tender yourself more dearly,<br>Or—not to crack the [[Wind|wind]] of the poor phrase,<br>Running it thus—you’ll tender me a [[Fool|fool]]. |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | My lord, he hath importuned me with [[Love|love]]<br>In honourable fashion— |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | Ay, fashion you may call it. Go to, go to. |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | And hath given countenance to his speech, my lord,<br>With all the vows of heaven. |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | Ay, springes to catch woodcocks. I do know<br>When the [[Blood|blood]] burns how prodigal the [[Soul|soul]]<br>Lends the tongue vows. These blazes, daughter,<br>Giving more light than heat, extinct in both<br>Even in their promise as it is a-making,<br>You must not take for [[Fire|fire]]. From this [[Time|time]], daughter,<br>Be somewhat scanter of your maiden presence.<br>Set your entreatments at a higher rate<br>Than a command to parley. For Lord [[Hamlet]],<br>Believe so much in him, that he is young,<br>And with a larger tether may he walk<br>Than may be given you. In few, [[Ophelia]],<br>Do not believe his vows, for they are brokers,<br>Not of the dye which their investments show,<br>But mere imploratators of unholy suits,<br>Breathing like sanctified and pious bawds<br>The better to beguile. This is for all—<br>I would not, in plain terms, from this [[Time|time]] forth<br>Have you so slander any moment leisure<br>As to give words or talk with the Lord [[Hamlet]].<br>Look to’t, I charge you. Come your ways. |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | I shall obey, my lord. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt |
