|---|---|
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Prince [[Hamlet]], [[Horatio]], and Marcellus |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | The air bites shrewdly, it is very cold. |
| [[Horatio|HORATIO]] | It is a nipping and an eager air. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | What hour now? |
| [[Horatio|HORATIO]] | I think it lacks of twelve. |
| MARCELLUS | No, it is struck. |
| [[Horatio|HORATIO]] | Indeed? I heard it not. Then it draws near the season<br>Wherein the spirit held his wont to walk. |
| *(didascalia)* | A flourish of trumpets, and two pieces of ordnance goes off |
| [[Horatio|HORATIO]] | What does this mean, my lord? |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | The [[King]] doth wake tonight and takes his rouse,<br>Keeps wassail, and the swagg’ring upspring reels,<br>And as he drains his draughts of Rhenish down<br>The kettle-drum and trumpet thus bray out<br>The triumph of his pledge. |
| [[Horatio|HORATIO]] | Is it a custom? |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Ay, marry is’t,<br>And to my mind, though I am native here<br>And to the manner born, it is a custom<br>More honoured in the breach than the observance. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter the [[Ghost]], as before |
| [[Horatio|HORATIO]] | Look, my lord, it comes. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Angels and ministers of grace defend us!<br>Be thou a spirit of health or goblin damned,<br>Bring with thee airs from heaven or blasts from hell,<br>Be thy intents wicked or charitable,<br>Thou com’st in such a questionable shape<br>That I will speak to thee. I’ll call thee [[Hamlet]],<br>[[King]], father, royal Dane. O answer me!<br>Let me not burst in ignorance, but tell<br>Why thy canonized bones, hearsèd in [[Death|death]],<br>Have burst their cerements, why the sepulchre<br>Wherein we saw thee quietly enurned<br>Hath oped his ponderous and marble jaws<br>To cast thee up again. What may this mean,<br>That thou, dead corpse, again in complete steel,<br>Revisitst thus the glimpses of the [[Moon|moon]],<br>Making [[Night|night]] hideous, and we fools of [[Nature|nature]]<br>So horridly to shake our disposition<br>With thoughts beyond the reaches of our souls?<br>Say, why is this? Wherefore? What should we do?<br>The [[Ghost]] beckons [[Hamlet]] |
| [[Horatio|HORATIO]] | It beckons you to go away with it<br>As if it some impartment did [[Desire|desire]]<br>To you [[Alone|alone]]. |
| MARCELLUS | (to [[Hamlet]]) Look with what courteous action<br>It wafts you to a more removed ground.<br>But do not go with it. |
| [[Horatio|HORATIO]] | (to [[Hamlet]])<br>No, by no means. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | It will not speak. Then will I follow it. |
| [[Horatio|HORATIO]] | Do not, my lord. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Why, what should be the [[Fear|fear]]?<br>I do not set my life at a pin’s fee,<br>And for my [[Soul|soul]], what can it do to that,<br>Being a thing immortal as itself?<br>The [[Ghost]] beckons [[Hamlet]]<br>It waves me forth again. I’ll follow it. |
| [[Horatio|HORATIO]] | What if it tempt you toward the flood, my lord,<br>Or to the dreadful summit of the cliff<br>That beetles o’er his base into the sea,<br>And there assume some other horrible form<br>Which might deprive your sovereignty of reason<br>And draw you into [[Madness|madness]]? Think of it.<br>The [[Ghost]] beckons [[Hamlet]] |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | It wafts me still. (To the [[Ghost]]) Go on, I’ll follow thee. |
| MARCELLUS | You shall not go, my lord. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Hold off your hand. |
| [[Horatio|HORATIO]] | Be ruled. You shall not go. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | My fate cries out,<br>And makes each petty artere in this [[Body|body]]<br>As hardy as the Nemean [[Lion|lion]]’s nerve.<br>The [[Ghost]] beckons [[Hamlet]]<br>Still am I called. Unhand me, gentlemen.<br>By heav’n, I’ll make a [[Ghost|ghost]] of him that lets me.<br>I say, away! (To the [[Ghost]]) Go on, I’ll follow thee. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt the [[Ghost]] and [[Hamlet]] |
| [[Horatio|HORATIO]] | He waxes desperate with [[Imagination|imagination]]. |
| MARCELLUS | Let’s follow.’Tis not fit thus to obey him. |
| [[Horatio|HORATIO]] | Have after. To what issue will this come? |
| MARCELLUS | Something is rotten in the state of [[Denmark]]. |
| [[Horatio|HORATIO]] | Heaven will direct it. |
| MARCELLUS | Nay, let’s follow him. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt |
