| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Helen]], the Widow, and [[Diana]], with two attendants |
| [[Helen|HELEN]] | But this exceeding posting day and [[Night|night]]<br>Must wear your spirits low. We cannot help it.<br>But since you have made the days and nights as one<br>To wear your gentle limbs in my affairs,<br>Be bold you do so grow in my requital<br>As nothing can unroot you. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter a Gentleman Austringer |
| [[Helen|HELEN]] | In happy [[Time|time]]!<br>This man may help me to his majesty’s ear,<br>If he would spend his power.—God save you, sir. |
| GENTLEMAN | And you. |
| [[Helen|HELEN]] | Sir, I have seen you in the [[Court|court]] of France. |
| GENTLEMAN | I have been sometimes there. |
| [[Helen|HELEN]] | I do presume, sir, that you are not fall’n<br>From the report that goes upon your goodness,<br>And therefore, goaded with most sharp occasions<br>Which lay nice manners by, I put you to<br>The use of your own virtues, for the which<br>I shall continue thankful. |
| GENTLEMAN | What’s your will? |
| [[Helen|HELEN]] | That it will please you<br>To give this poor petition to the [[King]],<br>And aid me with that store of power you have<br>To come into his presence. |
| GENTLEMAN | The [[King]]’s not here. |
| [[Helen|HELEN]] | Not here, sir? |
| GENTLEMAN | Not indeed.<br>He hence removed last [[Night|night]], and with more haste<br>Than is his use. |
| WIDOW | Lord, how we lose our pains. |
| [[Helen|HELEN]] | All’s [[Well|well]] that ends [[Well|well]] yet,<br>Though [[Time|time]] seem so adverse, and means unfit.—<br>I do beseech you, whither is he gone? |
| GENTLEMAN | Marry, as I take it, to Roussillon,<br>Whither I am going. |
| [[Helen|HELEN]] | I do beseech you, sir,<br>Since you are like to see the [[King]] before me,<br>Commend the paper to his gracious hand,<br>Which I presume shall render you no blame,<br>But rather make you thank your pains for it.<br>I will come after you with what good [[Speed|speed]]<br>Our means will make us means. |
| GENTLEMAN | (taking the paper) This I’ll do for you. |
| [[Helen|HELEN]] | And you shall find yourself to be [[Well|well]] thanked,<br>Whate’er falls more. We must to [[Horse|horse]] again.—<br>Go, go, provide. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt severally |
