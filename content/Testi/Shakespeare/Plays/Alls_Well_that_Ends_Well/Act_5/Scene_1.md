| Chi parla | Battuta |

| *(didascalia)* | Enter Helen, the Widow, and Diana, with two attendants |
| HELEN | But this exceeding posting day and night<br>Must wear your spirits low. We cannot help it.<br>But since you have made the days and nights as one<br>To wear your gentle limbs in my affairs,<br>Be bold you do so grow in my requital<br>As nothing can unroot you. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter a Gentleman Austringer |
| HELEN | In happy time!<br>This man may help me to his majesty’s ear,<br>If he would spend his power.—God save you, sir. |
| GENTLEMAN | And you. |
| HELEN | Sir, I have seen you in the court of France. |
| GENTLEMAN | I have been sometimes there. |
| HELEN | I do presume, sir, that you are not fall’n<br>From the report that goes upon your goodness,<br>And therefore, goaded with most sharp occasions<br>Which lay nice manners by, I put you to<br>The use of your own virtues, for the which<br>I shall continue thankful. |
| GENTLEMAN | What’s your will? |
| HELEN | That it will please you<br>To give this poor petition to the King,<br>And aid me with that store of power you have<br>To come into his presence. |
| GENTLEMAN | The King’s not here. |
| HELEN | Not here, sir? |
| GENTLEMAN | Not indeed.<br>He hence removed last night, and with more haste<br>Than is his use. |
| WIDOW | Lord, how we lose our pains. |
| HELEN | All’s well that ends well yet,<br>Though time seem so adverse, and means unfit.—<br>I do beseech you, whither is he gone? |
| GENTLEMAN | Marry, as I take it, to Roussillon,<br>Whither I am going. |
| HELEN | I do beseech you, sir,<br>Since you are like to see the King before me,<br>Commend the paper to his gracious hand,<br>Which I presume shall render you no blame,<br>But rather make you thank your pains for it.<br>I will come after you with what good speed<br>Our means will make us means. |
| GENTLEMAN | (taking the paper) This I’ll do for you. |
| HELEN | And you shall find yourself to be well thanked,<br>Whate’er falls more. We must to horse again.—<br>Go, go, provide. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt severally |
