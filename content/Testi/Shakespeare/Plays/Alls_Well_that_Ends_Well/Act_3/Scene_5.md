| *(didascalia)* | A tucket afar off. Enter an old Widow, her daughter [[Diana]], and [[Mariana]], with other Florentine citizens |
| WIDOW | Nay, come, for if they do approach the [[City|city]] we shall lose all the sight. |
| [[Diana|DIANA]] | They say the French Count has done most honourable service. |
| WIDOW | It is reported that he has taken their greatest commander, and that with his own hand he slew the Duke’s brother. (Tucket) We have lost our labour; they are gone a contrary way. Hark. You may know by their trumpets. |
| [[Mariana|MARIANA]] | Come, let’s return again, and suffice ourselves with the report of it.—Wett, [[Diana]], take heed of this French earl. The [[Honour|honour]] of a maid is her name, and no legacy is so rich as honesty. |
| WIDOW | (to [[Diana]]) I have told my neighbour how you have been solicited by a gentleman, his companion. |
| [[Mariana|MARIANA]] | I know that knave, hang him! One Paroles. A filthy officer he is in those suggestions for the young earl. Beware of them, [[Diana]]; their promises, enticements, oaths, tokens, and all their engines of lust, are not the things they go under. Many a maid hath been seduced by them; and the misery is, example, that so terrible shows in the wreck of maidenhood, cannot for all that dissuade succession, but that they are limed with the twigs that threatens them. I [[Hope|hope]] I need not to advise you further, but I [[Hope|hope]] your own grace will keep you where you are, though there were no further danger known but the modesty which is so lost. |
| [[Diana|DIANA]] | You shall not need to [[Fear|fear]] me. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Helen]] dressed as a [[Pilgrim|pilgrim]] |
| WIDOW | I [[Hope|hope]] so. Look, here comes a [[Pilgrim|pilgrim]]. I know she will lie at my [[house]]; thither they send one another. I’ll question her.<br>God save you, [[Pilgrim|pilgrim]]. Whither are you bound? |
| [[Helen|HELEN]] | To Saint [[Jaques]] le Grand.<br>Where do the palmers lodge, I do beseech you? |
| WIDOW | At the ’Saint Francis’ here beside the port. |
| [[Helen|HELEN]] | Is this the way? |
| WIDOW | Ay, marry, is’t. |
| *(didascalia)* | Sound of a march, far off |
| WIDOW | Hark you, they come this way. If you will tarry,<br>Holy [[Pilgrim|pilgrim]], but till the troops come by,<br>I will conduct you where you shall be lodged,<br>The rather for I think I know your hostess<br>As ample as myself. |
| [[Helen|HELEN]] | Is it yourself? |
| WIDOW | If you shall please so, [[Pilgrim|pilgrim]]. |
| [[Helen|HELEN]] | I thank you, and will stay upon your leisure. |
| WIDOW | You came, I think, from France? |
| [[Helen|HELEN]] | I did SO. |
| WIDOW | Here you shall see a countryman of yours<br>That has done worthy service. |
| [[Helen|HELEN]] | His name, I pray you? |
| [[Diana|DIANA]] | The Count Roussillon. Know you such a one? |
| [[Helen|HELEN]] | But by the ear, that hears most nobly of him; 50<br>His face I know not. |
| [[Diana|DIANA]] | Whatsome’er he is,<br>He’s bravely taken here. He stole from France,<br>As ’tis reported; for the [[King]] had married him<br>Against his liking. Think you it is so? |
| [[Helen|HELEN]] | Ay, surely, mere the [[Truth|truth]]. I know his lady. |
| [[Diana|DIANA]] | There is a gentleman that serves the Count<br>Reports but coarsely of her. |
| [[Helen|HELEN]] | What’s his name? |
| [[Diana|DIANA]] | A<br>Monsieur Paroles. |
| [[Helen|HELEN]] | O, I believe with him:<br>In argument of praise, or to the worth<br>Of the great Count himself, she is too mean<br>To have her name repeated. All her deserving<br>Is a reserved honesty, and that<br>I have not heard examined. |
| [[Diana|DIANA]] | Alas, poor lady.<br>’Tis a hard bondage to become the wife<br>Of a detesting lord. |
| WIDOW | I warr’nt, good creature, wheresoe’er she is<br>Her heart weighs sadly. This young maid might do her<br>A shrewd turn if she pleased. |
| [[Helen|HELEN]] | How do you mean?<br>Maybe the amorous Count solicits her<br>In the unlawful purpose. |
| WIDOW | He does indeed,<br>And brokes with all that can in such a suit<br>Corrupt the tender [[Honour|honour]] of a maid.<br>But she is armed for him, and keeps her guard<br>In honestest defence. |
| [[Mariana|MARIANA]] | The gods forbid else. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter, with drummer and colours, [[Bertram]], Paroles, and the whole army |
| WIDOW | So, now they come.<br>That is [[Antonio]], the Duke’s eldest son;<br>That, [[Escalus]]. |
| [[Helen|HELEN]] | Which is the Frenchman? |
| [[Diana|DIANA]] | He—<br>That with the plume. ’Tis a most gallant fellow.<br>I would he loved his wife. If he were honester<br>He were much goodlier. Is’t not<br>A handsome gentleman? |
| [[Helen|HELEN]] | I like him [[Well|well]]. |
| [[Diana|DIANA]] | ’Tis pity he is not honest.<br>Yond’s that same knave that leads him to those<br>places.<br>Were I his lady, I would [[Poison|poison]]<br>That vile rascal. |
| [[Helen|HELEN]] | Which is he? |
| [[Diana|DIANA]] | That jackanapes<br>With scarves. Why is he [[Melancholy|melancholy]]? |
| [[Helen|HELEN]] | Perchance he’s hurt i’th’ battle. |
| PAROLES | (aside) Lose our drum? [[Well]]. 90 |
| [[Mariana|MARIANA]] | He’s shrewdly vexed at something.<br>Look, he has spied us. |
| WIDOW | (to Paroles)<br>Marry, hang you! |
| [[Mariana|MARIANA]] | (to Paroles)<br>And your courtesy, for a ring-carrier. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt [[Bertram]], Paroles, and the army |
| WIDOW | The troop is past. Come, [[Pilgrim|pilgrim]], I will bring you<br>Where you shall host. Of enjoined penitents<br>There’s four or five to great Saint [[Jaques]] bound<br>Already at my [[house]]. |
| [[Helen|HELEN]] | I humbly thank you.<br>Please it this matron and this gentle maid<br>To eat with us tonight, the charge and thanking<br>Shall be for me. And to requite you further,<br>I will bestow some precepts of this virgin<br>Worthy the note. |
| WIDOW | and [[Mariana|MARIANA]] We’ll take your offer kindly. Exeunt |
