| *(didascalia)* | Flourish of trumpets. Enter the Duke of Florence and the two Lords [[Dumaine]], with a troop of soldiers |
| DUKE | So that from point to point now have you heard<br>The fundamental reasons of this war,<br>Whose great decision hath much [[Blood|blood]] let forth,<br>And more thirsts after. |
| FIRST LORD [[Dumaine|DUMAINE]] | Holy seems the quarrel<br>Upon your grace’s part; black and fearful<br>On the opposer. |
| DUKE | Therefore we marvel much our cousin France<br>Would in so just a business shut his bosom<br>Against our borrowing prayers. |
| SECOND LORD [[Dumaine|DUMAINE]] | Good my lord,<br>The reasons of our state I cannot yield<br>But like a common and an outward man<br>That the great figure of a council frames<br>By [[Self|self]]-unable motion; therefore dare not<br>Say what I think of it, since I have found<br>Myself in my incertain grounds to fail<br>As often as I guessed. |
| DUKE | Be it his pleasure. |
| FIRST LORD [[Dumaine|DUMAINE]] | But I am sure the younger of our nation,<br>That surfeit on their ease, will day by day<br>Come here for physic. |
| DUKE | Welcome shall they be,<br>And all the honours that can fly from us<br>Shall on them settle. You know your places [[Well|well]];<br>When better fall, for your avails they fell.<br>Tomorrow to the field. |
| *(didascalia)* | Flourish. Exeunt |
