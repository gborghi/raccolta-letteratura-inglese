# All Is True (Henry VIII) — Act IV, Scene 1 — exchange (GRIFFITH) [5]

<nav class="excerpt-nav">
<div class="excerpt-crumb">Shakespeare · <a href="/Works/ALL-IS-TRUE---(HENRY-VIII)-(Shakespeare)">All Is True Henry VIII</a></div>
</nav>


GRIFFITH
Noble madam, Men’s evil manners live in brass, their virtues
We write in water. May it please your highness
To hear me speak his good now?

KATHERINE
Yes, good Griffith,
I were malicious else.
