| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[King]] Henry, [[Queen Margaret]] with her hawk on her fist, Duke Humphrey of [[Gloucester]], [[Cardinal Beaufort]], and the [[Duke of Suffolk]], with falconers hollering |
| [[Queen Margaret|QUEEN MARGARET]] | Believe me, lords, for flying at the brook I saw not better sport these seven years’ day; Yet, by your leave, the [[Wind|wind]] was very high, And, ten to one, old Joan had not gone out. |
| [[King|KING]] HENRY | (to [[Gloucester]]) But what a point, my lord, your falcon made, And what a pitch she flew above the rest! To see how God in all his creatures works! Yea, man and [[Birds|birds]] are fain of climbing high. |
| SUFFOLK | No marvel, an it like your majesty, My Lord Protector’s hawks do tower so [[Well|well]]; They know their master loves to be aloft, And bears his thoughts above his falcon’s pitch. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | My lord, ‘tis but a base ignoble mind That mounts no higher than a bird can soar. |
| [[Cardinal Beaufort|CARDINAL BEAUFORT]] | I thought as much; he would be above the clouds. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Ay, my lord Cardinal, how think you by that? Were it not good your grace could fly to heaven? |
| [[King|KING]] HENRY | The treasury of everlasting joy. |
| [[Cardinal Beaufort|CARDINAL BEAUFORT]] | (to [[Gloucester]]) Thy heaven is on earth; thine eyes and thoughts Beat on a [[Crown|crown]], the treasure of thy heart, Pernicious Protector, dangerous peer, That smooth’st it so with [[King]] and common weal! |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | What, Cardinal? Is your priesthood grown peremptory ? Tantaene animis caelestibus irae? |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Churchmen so hot? Good uncle, hide such malice With some holiness—can you do it? |
| SUFFOLK | No malice, sir, no more than [[Well|well]] becomes So good a quarrel and so bad a peer. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | As who, my lord ? |
| SUFFOLK | Why, as you, my lord— An’t like your lordly Lord’s Protectorship. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Why, Suffolk, England knows thine insolence. |
| [[Queen Margaret|QUEEN MARGARET]] | And thy [[Ambition|ambition]], [[Gloucester]]. |
| [[King|KING]] HENRY | I prithee peace, Good [[Queen]], and whet not on these furious peers—For blessèd are the peacemakers on earth. |
| [[Cardinal Beaufort|CARDINAL BEAUFORT]] | Let me be blessed for the peace I make Against this proud Protector with my [[Sword|sword]]. [[Gloucester]] and [[Cardinal Beaufort]] speak privately to one another |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | [[Faith]], holy uncle, would’t were come to that. |
| [[Cardinal Beaufort|CARDINAL BEAUFORT]] | Marry, when thou dar’st. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Dare? I tell thee, priest, Plantagenets could never brook the dare! |
| [[Cardinal Beaufort|CARDINAL BEAUFORT]] | I am Plantagenet as [[Well|well]] as thou, And son to [[John of Gaunt]]. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | In bastardy. |
| [[Cardinal Beaufort|CARDINAL BEAUFORT]] | I scorn thy words. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Make up no factious numbers for the matter, In thine own person answer thy abuse. |
| [[Cardinal Beaufort|CARDINAL BEAUFORT]] | Ay, where thou dar‘st not peep; an if thou dar’st, This evening on the east side of the grove. |
| [[King|KING]] HENRY | How now, my lords? |
| [[Cardinal Beaufort|CARDINAL BEAUFORT]] | (aloud) Believe me, cousin [[Gloucester]], Had not your man put up the fowl so suddenly, We had had more sport. (Aside to [[Gloucester]]) Come with thy two-hand [[Sword|sword]]. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | (aloud) True, uncle. (Aside to [[Cardinal Beaufort]]) |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Are ye advised? The east side of the grove. |
