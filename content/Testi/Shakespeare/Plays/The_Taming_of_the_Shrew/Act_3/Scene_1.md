| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Lucentio]] with books, as Cambio, [[Hortensio]] with a lute, as Licio, and [[Bianca]] |
| [[Lucentio|LUCENTIO]] | Fiddler, forbear. You grow too forward, sir.<br>Have you so soon forgot the entertainment<br>Her sister Katherine welcomed you withal? |
| [[Hortensio|HORTENSIO]] | But, wrangling pedant, this [[Bianca]] is,<br>The patroness of heavenly harmony.<br>Then give me leave to have prerogative,<br>And when in music we have spent an hour<br>Your lecture shall have leisure for as much. |
| [[Lucentio|LUCENTIO]] | Preposterous ass, that never read so far<br>To know the cause why music was ordained!<br>Was it not to refresh the mind of man<br>After his studies or his usual [[Pain|pain]]?<br>Then give me leave to read [[philosophy]],<br>And while I pause, serve in your harmony. |
| [[Hortensio|HORTENSIO]] | Sirrah, I will not bear these braves of thine. |
| [[Bianca|BIANCA]] | Why, gentlemen, you do me [[Double|double]] wrong<br>To strive for that which resteth in my choice.<br>I am no breeching scholar in the schools.<br>I’ll not be tied to hours nor ‘pointed times,<br>But learn my lessons as I please myself;<br>And to cut off all strife, here sit we down.<br>(To [[Hortensio]]) Take you your instrument, play you the<br>whiles.<br>His lecture will be done ere you have tuned. |
| [[Hortensio|HORTENSIO]] | You’ll leave his lecture when I am in tune? |
| [[Lucentio|LUCENTIO]] | That will be never. Tune your instrument.<br>[[Hortensio]] tunes his lute. [[Lucentio]] opens a book |
| [[Bianca|BIANCA]] | Where left we last? |
| [[Lucentio|LUCENTIO]] | Here, madam.<br>(Reads) ‘Hie ibat Simois, hie est Sigeia tellus, Hie steterat Priami regia celsa senis.’ |
| [[Bianca|BIANCA]] | Construe them. |
| [[Lucentio|LUCENTIO]] | ‘Hie ibat’, as I told you before—‘Simois’, I am [[Lucentio]]—‘hic est’, son unto Vincentio of Pisa-‘Sigeia tellus’, disguised thus to get your [[Love|love]]—‘hic steterat’, and that [[Lucentio]] that comes a-wooing—‘Priami’, is my man [[Tranio]]—‘regia’, bearing my port—‘celsa senis’, that we might beguile the old pantaloon. |
| [[Hortensio|HORTENSIO]] | Madam, my instrument’s in tune. |
| [[Bianca|BIANCA]] | Let’s hear. ([[Hortensio]] plays) O fie, the treble jars. |
| [[Lucentio|LUCENTIO]] | Spit in the hole, man, and tune again.<br>[[Hortensio]] tunes his lute again |
| [[Bianca|BIANCA]] | Now let me see if I can construe it. ‘Hic ibat Simois’, I know you not—‘hic est Sigeia tellus’, I trust you not—‘hic steterat Priami’, take heed he hear us not—‘regia’, presume not—‘celsa senis’, [[Despair|despair]] not. |
| [[Hortensio|HORTENSIO]] | Madam, ’tis now in tune. |
| [[Lucentio|LUCENTIO]] | All but the bass. |
| [[Hortensio|HORTENSIO]] | The bass is right, ’tis the base knave that jars.<br>(Aside) How fiery and forward our pedant is!<br>Now, for my life, the knave doth [[Court|court]] my [[Love|love]].<br>Pedascule, I’ll watch you better yet. |
| [[Bianca|BIANCA]] | (to [[Lucentio]])<br>In [[Time|time]] I may believe; yet, I mistrust. |
| [[Lucentio|LUCENTIO]] | Mistrust it not, for sure Aeacides<br>Was [[Ajax]], called so from his grandfather. |
| [[Bianca|BIANCA]] | I must believe my master, else, I promise you,<br>I should be arguing still upon that [[Doubt|doubt]].<br>But let it rest. Now Licio, to you.<br>Good master, take it not unkindly, pray,<br>That I have been thus pleasant with you both. |
| [[Hortensio|HORTENSIO]] | (to [[Lucentio]])<br>You may go walk and give me leave awhile.<br>My lessons make no music in three parts. |
| [[Lucentio|LUCENTIO]] | Are you so formal, sir? [[Well]], I must wait.<br>(Aside) And watch withal, for but I be deceived<br>Our fine musician groweth amorous. |
| [[Hortensio|HORTENSIO]] | Madam, before you touch the instrument<br>To learn the order of my fingering,<br>I must begin with rudiments of art,<br>To teach you gamut in a briefer sort,<br>More pleasant, pithy, and effectual<br>Than hath been taught by any of my trade;<br>And there it is in writing, fairly drawn.<br>He gives a paper |
| [[Bianca|BIANCA]] | Why, I am past my gamut long ago. |
| [[Hortensio|HORTENSIO]] | Yet read the gamut of [[Hortensio]]. |
| [[Bianca|BIANCA]] | (reads)<br>‘Gam-ut I am, the ground of all accord, A—re—to plead [[Hortensio]]’s passion.<br>B—mi—[[Bianca]], take him for thy lord, C—fa, ut—that loves with all affection.<br>D—so), re—one clef, two notes have I,<br>E—la, mi—show pity, or I die.’<br>Call you this gamut? Tut, I like it not.<br>Old fashions please me best. I am not so nice<br>To change true rules for odd inventions. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter a Messenger |
| MESSENGER | Mistress, your father prays you leave your books<br>And help to dress your sister’s chamber up.<br>You know tomorrow is the wedding day. |
| [[Bianca|BIANCA]] | Farewell, sweet masters both. I must be gone. |
| [[Lucentio|LUCENTIO]] | [[Faith]], mistress, then I have no cause to stay. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt [[Bianca]], Messenger, and [[Lucentio]] |
| [[Hortensio|HORTENSIO]] | But I have cause to pry into this pedant.<br>Methinks he looks as though he were in [[Love|love]].<br>Yet if thy thoughts, [[Bianca]], be so humble<br>To cast thy wand’ring eyes on every stale,<br>Seize thee that list. If once I find thee ranging,<br>[[Hortensio]] will be quit with thee by changing. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit |
