| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Tranio]] as [[Lucentio]], and [[Hortensio]] as Licio |
| [[Tranio|TRANIO]] | Is’t possible, friend Licio, that Mistress [[Bianca]]<br>Doth fancy any other but [[Lucentio]]?<br>I tell you, sir, she bears me fair in hand. |
| [[Hortensio|HORTENSIO]] | Sir, to satisfy you in what I have said,<br>Stand by, and mark the manner of his teaching. 5<br>They stand aside. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Bianca]], and [[Lucentio]] as Cambio |
| [[Lucentio|LUCENTIO]] | Now, mistress, profit you in what you read? |
| [[Bianca|BIANCA]] | What, master, read you? First resolve me that. |
| [[Lucentio|LUCENTIO]] | I read that I profess, The Art to [[Love]]. |
| [[Bianca|BIANCA]] | And may you prove, sir, master of your art. |
| [[Lucentio|LUCENTIO]] | While you, sweet dear, prove mistress of my heart. They stand aside |
| [[Hortensio|HORTENSIO]] | Quick proceeders, marry! Now tell me, I pray,<br>You that durst swear that your mistress [[Bianca]]<br>Loved none in the world so [[Well|well]] as [[Lucentio]]. |
| [[Tranio|TRANIO]] | O despiteful [[Love|love]], unconstant womankind!<br>I tell thee, Licio, this is wonderful. |
| [[Hortensio|HORTENSIO]] | Mistake no more, I am not Licio,<br>Nor a musician as I seem to be,<br>But one that scorn to live in this disguise<br>For such a one as [[Leaves|leaves]] a gentleman<br>And makes a god of such a cullion.<br>Know, sir, that I am called [[Hortensio]]. |
| [[Tranio|TRANIO]] | Signor [[Hortensio]], I have often heard<br>Of your entire affection to [[Bianca]],<br>And since mine eyes are witness of her lightness<br>I will with you, if you be so contented,<br>Forswear [[Bianca]] and her [[Love|love]] for ever. |
| [[Hortensio|HORTENSIO]] | See how they kiss and [[Court|court]]. Signor [[Lucentio]],<br>Here is my hand, and here I firmly vow<br>Never to woo her more, but do forswear her<br>As one unworthy all the former favours<br>That I have fondly flattered her withal. |
| [[Tranio|TRANIO]] | And here I take the like unfeigned oath<br>Never to marry with her, though she would entreat.<br>Fie on her, see how beastly she doth [[Court|court]] him! |
| [[Hortensio|HORTENSIO]] | Would all the world but he had quite forsworn.<br>For me, that I may surely keep mine oath<br>I will be married to a wealthy widow<br>Ere three days pass, which hath as long loved me<br>As I have loved this proud disdainful haggard.<br>And so farewell, Signor [[Lucentio]].<br>Kindness in women, not their beauteous looks,<br>Shall win my [[Love|love]]; and so I take my leave,<br>In resolution as I swore before. Exit |
| [[Tranio|TRANIO]] | Mistress [[Bianca]], bless you with such grace<br>As ‘longeth to a [[Lover|lover]]’s blessed case.<br>Nay, I have ta’en you napping, gentle [[Love|love]],<br>And have forsworn you with [[Hortensio]]. |
| [[Bianca|BIANCA]] | [[Tranio]], you jest. But have you both forsworn me? |
| [[Tranio|TRANIO]] | Mistress, we have. |
| [[Lucentio|LUCENTIO]] | Then we are rid of Licio. |
| [[Tranio|TRANIO]] | I’[[Faith|faith]], he’ll have a lusty widow now,<br>That shall be wooed and wedded in a day. |
| [[Bianca|BIANCA]] | God give him joy. |
| [[Tranio|TRANIO]] | Ay, and he’ll tame her. |
| [[Bianca|BIANCA]] | He says so, [[Tranio]]. |
| [[Tranio|TRANIO]] | [[Faith]], he is gone unto the taming-school. |
| [[Bianca|BIANCA]] | The taming-school—what, is there such a place? |
| [[Tranio|TRANIO]] | Ay, mistress, and Petruccio is the master,<br>That teacheth tricks eleven-and-twenty long<br>To tame a shrew and charm her chattering tongue. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Biondello |
| BIONDELLO | O, master, master, I have watched so long<br>That I am dog-weary, but at last I spied<br>An ancient angel coming down the [[Hill|hill]]<br>Will serve the turn. |
| [[Tranio|TRANIO]] | What is he, Biondello? |
| BIONDELLO | Master, a marcantant or a pedant,<br>I know not what, but formal in apparel,<br>In gait and countenance surely like a father. |
| [[Lucentio|LUCENTIO]] | And what of him, [[Tranio]]? |
| [[Tranio|TRANIO]] | If he be credulous and trust my tale,<br>I’ll make him glad to seem Vincentio<br>And give assurance to [[Baptista]] Minola<br>As if he were the right Vincentio.<br>Take in your [[Love|love]], and then let me [[Alone|alone]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt [[Lucentio]] and [[Bianca]] |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter a Pedant |
| PEDANT | God save you, sir. |
| [[Tranio|TRANIO]] | And you, sir. You are welcome.<br>Travel you farre on, or are you at the farthest? |
| PEDANT | Sir, at the farthest for a week or two,<br>But then up farther and as far as Rome,<br>And so to Tripoli, if God lend me life. |
| [[Tranio|TRANIO]] | What countryman, I pray? |
| PEDANT | Of Mantua. |
| [[Tranio|TRANIO]] | Of Mantua, sir? Marry, God forbid,<br>And come to Padua careless of your life! |
| PEDANT | My life, sir? How, I pray? For that goes hard. |
| [[Tranio|TRANIO]] | ‘Tis [[Death|death]] for anyone in Mantua<br>To come to Padua. Know you not the cause?<br>Your ships are stayed at [[Venice]], and the Duke,<br>For private quarrel ’twixt your Duke and him,<br>Hath published and proclaimed it openly.<br>’Tis marvel, but that you are but newly come,<br>You might have heard it else proclaimed about. |
| PEDANT | Alas, sir, it is worse for me than so,<br>For I have bills for money by exchange<br>From Florence, and must here deliver them. |
| [[Tranio|TRANIO]] | [[Well]], sir, to do you courtesy<br>This will I do, and this I will advise you.<br>First tell me, have you ever been at Pisa? |
| PEDANT | Ay, sir, in Pisa have I often been,<br>Pisa renowned for [[Grave|grave]] citizens. |
| [[Tranio|TRANIO]] | Among them know you one Vincentio? |
| PEDANT | I know him not, but I have heard of him,<br>A merchant of incomparable wealth. |
| [[Tranio|TRANIO]] | He is my father, sir, and sooth to say,<br>In count’nance somewhat doth resemble you. |
| BIONDELLO | (aside) As much as an apple doth an oyster, and all one. |
| [[Tranio|TRANIO]] | To save your life in this extremity<br>This favour will I do you for his sake,<br>And think it not the worst of all your fortunes<br>That you are like to Sir Vincentio.<br>His name and credit shall you undertake,<br>And in my [[house]] you shall be friendly lodged.<br>Look that you take upon you as you should.<br>You understand me, sir? So shall you stay<br>Till you have done your business in the [[City|city]].<br>If this be courtesy, sir, accept of it. |
| PEDANT | O sir, I do, and will repute you ever<br>The patron of my life and liberty. |
| [[Tranio|TRANIO]] | Then go with me to make the matter good.<br>This, by the way, I let you understand—<br>My father is here looked for every day<br>To pass assurance of a dower in [[Marriage|marriage]]<br>’Twixt me and one [[Baptista]]’s daughter here.<br>In all these circumstances I’ll instruct you.<br>Go with me to clothe you as becomes you. Exeunt |
