| Chi parla | Battuta |
|---|---|
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[King]] Edward and the Earl of Derby with soldiers |
| [[King|KING]] EDWARD | Since they refuse our proffered league, my lord,<br>And will not ope their gates and let us in,<br>We will entrench ourselves on every side,<br>That neither victuals nor supply of men<br>May come to succour this accursed town.<br>Famine shall combat where our swords are stopped. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter six poor Frenchmen |
| EARL OF DERBY | The promised aid that made them stand aloof<br>Is now retired and gone another way.<br>It will repent them of their stubborn will.<br>But what are these poor, ragged slaves, my lord? |
| [[King|KING]] EDWARD | Ask what they are. It seems they come from Calais. |
| EARL OF DERBY | (to the poor Frenchmen)<br>You wretched patterns of [[Despair|despair]] and woe—<br>What are you? Living men, or gliding [[Ghosts|ghosts]]<br>Crept from your graves to walk upon the earth? |
| POOR MAN | No [[Ghosts|ghosts]], my lord, but men that breathe a life<br>Far worse than is the quiet [[Sleep|sleep]] of [[Death|death]].<br>We are distressed poor inhabitants<br>That long have been diseased, sick and lame.<br>And now, because we are not fit to serve,<br>The Captain of the town hath thrust us forth<br>That so expense of victuals may be saved. |
| [[King|KING]] EDWARD | A charitable deed, no [[Doubt|doubt]], and worthy praise!<br>But how do you imagine, then, to [[Speed|speed]]?<br>We are your enemies: in such a case<br>We can no less but put ye to the [[Sword|sword]],<br>Since, when we proffered truce, it was refused. |
| POOR MAN | An if your grace no otherwise vouchsafe,<br>As welcome [[Death|death]] is unto us as life. |
| [[King|KING]] EDWARD | Poor seely men, much wronged, and more distressed!<br>Go, Derby, go, and see they be relieved.<br>Command that victuals be appointed them,<br>And give to every one five crowns apiece. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt the Earl of Derby and the six poor Frenchmen |
| [[King|KING]] EDWARD | The [[Lion|lion]] scorns to touch the yielding prey,<br>And Edward’s [[Sword|sword]] must fresh itself in such<br>As wilful stubbornness hath made perverse. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Lord Percy |
| [[King|KING]] EDWARD | Lord Percy, welcome. What’s the news in England? |
| PERCY | The [[Queen]], my lord, commends her to your grace,<br>And from her highness and the lord vicegerent<br>I bring this happy tidings of [[success]]:<br>David of Scotland, lately up in arms,<br>Thinking belike he soonest should prevail—<br>Your highness being absent from the realm—<br>Is by the fruitful service of your peers<br>And painful travail of the [[Queen]] herself—<br>That, big with [[Child|child]], was every day in arms—<br>Vanquished, subdued and taken prisoner. |
| [[King|KING]] EDWARD | Thanks, Percy, for thy news, with all my heart!<br>What was he took him prisoner in the field? |
| PERCY | A squire, my lord. John Copland is his name,<br>Who, since entreated by her majesty,<br>Denies to make surrender of his prize<br>To any but unto your grace [[Alone|alone]]—<br>Whereat the [[Queen]] is grievously displeased. |
| [[King|KING]] EDWARD | [[Well]] then, we’ll have a pursuivant dispatched<br>To summon Copland hither out of hand,<br>And with him he shall bring his prisoner-[[King|king]]. |
| PERCY | The [[Queen]], my lord, herself by this at sea,<br>And purposeth, as soon as [[Wind|wind]] will serve,<br>To land at Calais, and to visit you. |
| [[King|KING]] EDWARD | She shall be welcome, and to wait her coming<br>I’ll pitch my tent near to the sandy shore. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter a Captain of Calais |
| CAPTAIN OF CALAIS | The burgesses of Calais, mighty [[King|king]],<br>Have, by a council, willingly decreed<br>To yield the town and [[Castle|castle]] to your hands<br>Upon condition it will please your grace<br>To grant them benefit of life and goods. |
| [[King|KING]] EDWARD | They will so? Then, belike, they may command,<br>Dispose, elect and govern as they list!<br>No, sirrah. Tell them, since they did refuse<br>Our princely clemency at first proclaimed,<br>They shall not have it now, although they would.<br>I will accept of naught but [[Fire|fire]] and [[Sword|sword]]—<br>Except within these two days, six of them,<br>That are the wealthiest merchants in the town,<br>Come naked all but for their linen shirts,<br>With each a halter hanged about his neck,<br>And, prostrate, yield themselves upon their knees<br>To be afflicted, hanged or what I please.<br>And so you may inform their masterships. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt all but the Captain |
| CAPTAIN OF CALAIS | Why, this it is to trust a broken staff. Had we not been persuaded Jean, our [[King|king]], Would, with his army, have relieved the town, We had not stood upon defiance so. But now ’tis past that no man can recall, 84 And better some do go to wreck than all. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit |
