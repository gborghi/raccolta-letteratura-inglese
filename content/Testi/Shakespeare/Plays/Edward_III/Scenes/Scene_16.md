| Chi parla | Battuta |
|---|---|
| SQUIRE | How fares my lord? |
| AUDLEY | Even as a man may do<br>That dines at such a bloody feast as this. |
| SQUIRE | I hope, my lord, that is no mortal scar. |
| AUDLEY | No matter if it be. The count is cast,<br>And, in the worst, ends but a mortal man.<br>Good friends, convey me to the princely Edward,<br>That, in the crimson bravery of my blood,<br>I may become him with saluting him.<br>I’ll smile and tell him that this open scar<br>Doth end the harvest of his Audley’s war. Exeunt |
