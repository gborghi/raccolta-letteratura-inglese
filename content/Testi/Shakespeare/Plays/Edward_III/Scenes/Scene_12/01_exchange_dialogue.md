| *(didascalia)* | Enter Edward Prince of Wales, Lord Audley and others |
| PRINCE OF WALES | Audley, the arms of death embrace us round And comfort have we none, save that to die We pay sour earnest for a sweeter life. At Crécy field our clouds of warlike smoke Choked up those French mouths and dissevered them, But now their multitudes of millions hide, Masking, as ’twere, the beauteous burning sun, Leaving no hope to us but sullen dark And eyeless terror of all-ending night. |
