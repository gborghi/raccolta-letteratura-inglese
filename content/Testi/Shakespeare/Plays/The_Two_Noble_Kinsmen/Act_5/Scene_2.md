| [[Palamon|PALAMON]] | (to his Knights)<br>Our [[Stars|stars]] must glister with new [[Fire|fire]], or be<br>Today extinct. Our argument is [[Love|love]],<br>Which if the goddess of it grant, she gives<br>Victory too. Then blend your spirits with mine,<br>You whose free nobleness do make my cause<br>Your personal hazard. To the goddess [[Venus]]<br>Commend we our proceeding, and implore<br>Her power unto our party.<br>Here they kneel before the altar,<br>fall on’their faces then on their knees again<br>(Praying to [[Venus]]) Hail, sovereign [[Queen|queen]] of [[Secrets|secrets]],<br>who hast power<br>To call the fiercest [[Tyrant|tyrant]] from his rage<br>And weep unto a girl; that hast the might,<br>Even with an eye-glance, to choke Mars’s drum<br>And turn th‘alarum to whispers; that canst make<br>A cripple flourish with his crutch, and cure him<br>Before [[Apollo]]; that mayst force the [[King|king]]<br>To be his subject’s vassal, and induce<br>Stale gravity to [[Dance|dance]]; the polled bachelor<br>Whose youth, like wanton boys through bonfires,<br>Have skipped thy flame, at seventy thou canst catch<br>And make him to the scorn of his hoarse throat<br>Abuse young lays of [[Love|love]]. What godlike power<br>Hast thou not power upon? To Phoebus thou<br>Add’st flames hotter than his—the heavenly fires<br>Did scorch his mortal son, thine him. The huntress,<br>All moist and cold, some say, began to throw<br>Her bow away and sigh. Take to thy grace<br>Me, thy vowed soldier, who do bear thy yoke<br>As ’twere a wreath of roses, yet is heavier<br>Than lead itself, stings more than nettles.<br>I have never been foul-mouthed against thy law;<br>Ne’er revealed secret, for I knew none; would not,<br>Had I kenned all that were. I never practised<br>Upon man’s wife, nor would the libels read<br>Of liberal wits. I never at great feasts<br>Sought to betray a [[Beauty|beauty]], but have blushed<br>At simp’ring sirs that did. I have been harsh<br>To large confessors, and have hotly asked them<br>If they had mothers—I had one, a woman,<br>And women ’twere they wronged. I knew a man<br>Of eighty winters, this I told them, who<br>A lass of fourteen brided—’twas thy power<br>To put life into [[Dust|dust]]. The aged cramp<br>Had screwed his square foot round,<br>The gout had knit his fingers into knots,<br>Torturing convulsions from his globy eyes<br>Had almost drawn their spheres, that what was life<br>In him seemed torture. This anatomy<br>Had by his young fair fere a boy, and I<br>Believed it was his, for she swore it was,<br>And who would not believe her? Brief—I am<br>To those that prate and have done, no companion;<br>To those that boast and have not, a defier;<br>To those that would and cannot, a rejoicer.<br>Yea, him I do not [[Love|love]] that tells close offices<br>The foulest way, nor names concealments in<br>The boldest language. Such a one I am,<br>And vow that [[Lover|lover]] never yet made sigh<br>Truer than I. O, then, most soft sweet goddess,<br>Give me the victory of this question, which<br>Is true [[Love|love]]’s merit, and bless me with a sign<br>Of thy great pleasure.<br>Here music is heard, doves are seen to flutter. They fall again upon their faces, then on their knees<br>O thou that from eleven to ninety reign’st<br>In mortal bosoms, whose chase is this world<br>And we in herds thy game, I give thee thanks<br>For this fair token, which, being laid unto<br>Mine [[Innocent|innocent]] true heart, arms in assurance<br>My [[Body|body]] to this business. (To his Knights) Let us rise<br>And bow before the goddess.<br>They rise and bow<br>[[Time]] comes on. |
