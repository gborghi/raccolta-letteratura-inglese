| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | [[Arcite]] is gently visaged, yet his eye Is like an engine bent or a sharp weapon In a soft sheath. [[Mercy]] and manly [[Courage|courage]] Are bedfellows in his visage. [[Palamon]] Has a most menacing aspect. His brow Is graved and seems to bury what it frowns on, Yet sometime ’tis not so, but alters to The quality of his thoughts. Long [[Time|time]] his eye Will dwell upon his object. [[Melancholy]] Becomes him nobly—so does [[Arcite]]’s mirth. But [[Palamon]]’s sadness is a kind of mirth, So mingled as if mirth did make him sad And sadness merry. Those darker humours that Stick misbecomingly on others, on them Live in fair dwelling. Cornetts. Trumpets sound as to a charge Hark, how yon spurs to spirit do incite The princes to their proof. [[Arcite]] may win me, And yet may [[Palamon]] wound [[Arcite]] to The spoiling of his figure. O, what pity Enough for such a chance! If I were by I might do hurt, for they would glance their eyes Toward my seat, and in that motion might Omit a ward or forfeit an offence Which craved that very [[Time|time]]. It is much better Cornetts. A great cry and noise within, crying, ‘A [[Palamon]]’ I am not there. O better never born, Than minister to such harm. Enter Servant What is the chance? |
