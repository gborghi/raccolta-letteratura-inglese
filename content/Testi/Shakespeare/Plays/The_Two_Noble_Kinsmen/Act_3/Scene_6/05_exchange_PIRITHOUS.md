| PIRITHOUS | O heaven, What more than man is this! |
| [[Theseus|THESEUS]] | I have sworn. |
| [[Arcite|ARCITE]] | We seek not Thy breath of [[Mercy|mercy]], [[Theseus]]. ‘Tis to me A thing as soon to die as thee to say it, And no more moved. Where this man calls me traitor Let me say thus much—if in [[Love|love]] be treason, In service of so excellent a [[Beauty|beauty]], As I [[Love|love]] most, and in that [[Faith|faith]] will perish, As I have brought my life here to confirm it, As I have served her truest, worthiest, As I dare kill this cousin that denies it, So let me be most traitor and ye please me. For scorning thy edict, Duke, ask that lady Why she is fair, and why her eyes command me Stay here to [[Love|love]] her, and if she say, ‘Traitor’, I am a [[Villain|villain]] fit to lie unburied. |
| [[Palamon|PALAMON]] | Thou shalt have pity of us both, O [[Theseus]], If unto neither thou show [[Mercy|mercy]]. Stop, As thou art just, thy noble ear against us; As thou art valiant, for thy cousin’s [[Soul|soul]], Whose twelve strong labours [[Crown|crown]] his [[Memory|memory]], Let’s die together, at one instant, Duke. Only a little let him fall before me, That I may tell my [[Soul|soul]] he shall not have her. |
| [[Theseus|THESEUS]] | I grant your wish; for to say true, your cousin Has ten times more offended, for I gave him More [[Mercy|mercy]] than you found, sir, your offences Being no more than his. None here speak for ’em, For ere the sun set both shall [[Sleep|sleep]] for ever. |
| [[Hippolyta|HIPPOLYTA]] | (to [[Emilia]]) Alas, the pity! Now or never, sister, Speak, not to be denied. That face of yours Will bear the curses else of after ages For these lost cousins. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | In my face, dear sister, I find no anger to ’em, nor no ruin. The misadventure of their own eyes kill ’em. Yet that I will be woman and have pity, She kneels My knees shall grow to th’ ground, but I’ll get [[Mercy|mercy]]. Help me, dear sister—in a deed so virtuous The powers of all women will be with us. [[Hippolyta]] kneels Most royal brother— |
| [[Hippolyta|HIPPOLYTA]] | Sir, by our tie of [[Marriage|marriage]]— |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | By your own spotless [[Honour|honour]]— |
| [[Hippolyta|HIPPOLYTA]] | By that [[Faith|faith]], That fair hand, and that honest heart you gave me— |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | By that you would have pity in another, By your own virtues infinite— |
| [[Hippolyta|HIPPOLYTA]] | By valour, By all the chaste nights I have ever pleased you— |
| [[Theseus|THESEUS]] | These are strange conjurings. |
| PIRITHOUS | Nay, then, I’ll in too. He kneels By all our [[Friendship|friendship]], sir, by all our dangers, By all you [[Love|love]] most: wars, and this sweet lady— |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | By that you would have trembled to deny A blushing maid— |
| [[Hippolyta|HIPPOLYTA]] | By your own eyes, by strength— In which you swore I went beyond all women, Almost all men—and yet I yielded, [[Theseus]]— |
| PIRITHOUS | To [[Crown|crown]] all this, by your most noble [[Soul|soul]], Which cannot want due [[Mercy|mercy]], I beg first— |
| [[Hippolyta|HIPPOLYTA]] | Next hear my prayers— |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | Last let me entreat, sir— |
| PIRITHOUS | For [[Mercy|mercy]]. |
| [[Hippolyta|HIPPOLYTA]] | [[Mercy]]. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | [[Mercy]] on these princes. |
| [[Theseus|THESEUS]] | Ye make my [[Faith|faith]] reel. Say I felt Compassion to ’em both, how would you place it? They rise |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | Upon their lives—but with their banishments. |
