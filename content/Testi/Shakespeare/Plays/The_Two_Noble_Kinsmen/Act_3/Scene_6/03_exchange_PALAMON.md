| [[Palamon|PALAMON]] | No, no, cousin, I will no more be hidden, nor put off This great adventure to a second trial. I know your cunning and I know your cause— He that faints now, shame take him! Put thyself Upon thy present guard— |
| [[Arcite|ARCITE]] | You are not mad? |
| [[Palamon|PALAMON]] | Or I will make th’advantage of this hour Mine own, and what to come shall threaten me I [[Fear|fear]] less than my fortune. Know, weak cousin, I [[Love|love]] [[Emilia]], and in that I’ll bury Thee and all crosses else. |
