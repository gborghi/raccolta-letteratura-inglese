| *(didascalia)* | Enter the Jailer’s Daughter, with a file |
| JAILER’S DAUGHTER | He has mistook the brake I meant, is gone<br>After his fancy. ’Tis now wellnigh morning.<br>No matter—would it were perpetual [[Night|night]],<br>And darkness lord o’th’ world. Hark, ’tis a wolf!<br>In me hath grief slain [[Fear|fear]], and, but for one thing,<br>I care for nothing—and that’s [[Palamon]].<br>I reck not if the wolves would jaw me, so<br>He had this file. What if I hollered for him?<br>I cannot holler. If I whooped, what then?<br>If he not answered, I should call a wolf<br>And do him but that service. I have heard<br>Strange howls this livelong [[Night|night]]—why may’t not be<br>They have made prey of him? He has no weapons;<br>He cannot run; the jangling of his gyves<br>Might call fell things to listen, who have in them<br>A sense to know a man unarmed, and can<br>Smell where resistance is. I’ll set it down<br>He’s torn to pieces: they howled many together<br>And then they fed on him. So much for that.<br>Be bold to ring the bell. How stand I then?<br>All’s chared when he is gone. No, no, I lie:<br>My father’s to be hanged for his escape,<br>Myself to beg, if I prized life so much<br>As to deny my act—but that I would not,<br>Should I try [[Death|death]] by dozens. I am moped—<br>Food took I none these two days,<br>Sipped some [[Water|water]]. I have not closed mine eyes<br>Save when my lids scoured off their brine. Alas,<br>Dissolve, my life; let not my sense unsettle,<br>Lest I should drown or stab or hang myself.<br>O state of [[Nature|nature]], fail together in me,<br>Since thy best props are warped. So which way now?<br>The best way is the next way to a [[Grave|grave]],<br>Each errant step beside is torment. Lo,<br>The [[Moon|moon]] is down, the crickets chirp, the screech-owl<br>Calls in the [[Dawn|dawn]]. All offices are done<br>Save what I fail in: but the point is this,<br>An end, and that is all. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit |
