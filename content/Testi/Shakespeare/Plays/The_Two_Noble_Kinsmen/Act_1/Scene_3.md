| *(didascalia)* | Enter Pirithous, [[Hippolyta]], and [[Emilia]] |
| PIRITHOUS | No further. |
| [[Hippolyta|HIPPOLYTA]] | Sir, farewell. Repeat my wishes To our great lord, of whose [[success]] I dare not<br>Make any timorous question; yet I wish him<br>Excess and overflow of power, an’t might be,<br>To dure ill-dealing fortune. [[Speed]] to him;<br>Store never hurts good governors. |
| PIRITHOUS | Though I know His ocean needs not my poor drops, yet they<br>Must yield their tribute there. (To [[Emilia]]) My precious<br>maid,<br>Those best affections that the heavens infuse<br>In their best-tempered pieces keep enthroned<br>In your dear heart. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | Thanks, sir. Remember me To our all-royal brother, for whose [[Speed|speed]]<br>The great Bellona I’ll solicit; and<br>Since in our terrene state petitions are not<br>Without gifts understood, I’ll offer to her<br>What I shall be advised she likes. Our hearts<br>Are in his army, in his tent. |
| [[Hippolyta|HIPPOLYTA]] | In’s bosom.<br>We have been soldiers, and we cannot weep<br>When our friends don their helms, or put to sea,<br>Or tell of babes broached on the lance, or women<br>That have sod their infants in—and after eat them—<br>The brine they wept at killing ’em: then if<br>You stay to see of us such spinsters, we<br>Should hold you here forever. |
| PIRITHOUS | Peace be to you As I pursue this war, which shall be then<br>Beyond further requiring. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit Pirithous |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | How his [[Longing|longing]] Follows his friend! Since his depart, his sports,<br>Though craving seriousness and skill, passed slightly<br>His careless execution, where nor gain<br>Made him regard or loss consider, but<br>Playing one business in his hand, another<br>Directing in his head, his mind [[Nurse|nurse]] equal<br>To these so diff’ring twins. Have you observed him<br>Since our great lord departed? |
| [[Hippolyta|HIPPOLYTA]] | With much labour; And I did [[Love|love]] him for’t. They two have cabined<br>In many as dangerous as poor a corner,<br>Peril and want contending; they have skiffed<br>Torrents whose roaring tyranny and power<br>I’th’ least of these was dreadful, and they have<br>Fought out together where [[Death|death]]’s [[Self|self]] was lodged;<br>Yet fate hath brought them off. Their knot of [[Love|love]],<br>Tied, weaved, entangled with so true, so long,<br>And with a finger of so deep a cunning,<br>May be outworn, never undone. I think<br>[[Theseus]] cannot be umpire to himself,<br>Cleaving his [[Conscience|conscience]] into twain and doing<br>Each side like justice, which he loves best. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | Doubtless There is a best, and reason has no manners<br>To say it is not you. I was acquainted<br>Once with a [[Time|time]] when I enjoyed a playfellow;<br>You were at wars when she the [[Grave|grave]] enriched,<br>Who made too proud the bed; took leave o’th’<br>[[Moon|moon]]—<br>Which then looked pale at parting—when our count<br>Was each eleven. |
| [[Hippolyta|HIPPOLYTA]] | ’Twas Flavina. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | Yes.<br>You talk of Pirithous’ and [[Theseus]]’ [[Love|love]]:<br>Theirs has more ground, is more maturely seasoned,<br>More buckled with strong judgement, and their needs<br>The one of th‘other may be said to [[Water|water]]<br>Their intertangled roots of [[Love|love]]; but I<br>And she I sigh and spoke of were things [[Innocent|innocent]],<br>Loved for we did, and like the elements,<br>That know not what, nor why, yet do effect<br>Rare issues by their operance, our souls<br>Did so to one another. What she liked<br>Was then of me approved; what not, condemned—<br>No more arraignment. The flower that I would pluck<br>And put between my breasts—O then but beginning<br>To swell about the blossom—she would long<br>Till she had such another, and commit it<br>To the like [[Innocent|innocent]] cradle, where, phoenix-like,<br>They died in perfume. On my head no toy<br>But was her pattern. Her affections—pretty,<br>Though happily her careless wear—I followed<br>For my most serious decking. Had mine ear<br>Stol’n some new air, or at adventure hummed one,<br>From musical coinage, why, it was a note<br>Whereon her spirits would sojourn—rather dwell on—<br>And sing it in her slumbers. This rehearsal—<br>Which, seely [[innocence]] wots [[Well|well]], comes in<br>Like old emportment’s bastard—has this end:<br>That the true [[Love|love]] ’tween maid and maid may be<br>More than in sex dividual. |
| [[Hippolyta|HIPPOLYTA]] | You’re out of breath, And this high-speeded pace is but to say<br>That you shall never, like the maid Flavina,<br>[[Love]] any that’s called man. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | I am sure I shall not. |
| [[Hippolyta|HIPPOLYTA]] | Now alack, weak sister, I must no more believe thee in this point—<br>Though in’t I know thou dost believe thyself—<br>Than I will trust a sickly appetite<br>That loathes even as it longs. But sure, my sister,<br>If I were ripe for your [[Persuasion|persuasion]], you<br>Have said enough to shake me from the arm<br>Of the all-noble [[Theseus]], for whose fortunes<br>I will now in and kneel, with great assurance<br>That we more than his Pirithous possess<br>The high throne in his heart. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | I am not<br>Against your [[Faith|faith]], yet I continue mine. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt |
