| *(didascalia)* | Enter Robin Goodfellow with a broom |
| ROBIN | Now the hungry [[Lion|lion]] roars,<br>And the wolf behowls the [[Moon|moon]],<br>Whilst the heavy ploughman snores,<br>All with weary task fordone.<br>Now the wasted brands do glow<br>Whilst the screech-owl, screeching loud,<br>Puts the wretch that lies in woe<br>In remembrance of a shroud.<br>Now it is the [[Time|time]] of [[Night|night]]<br>That the graves, all gaping wide,<br>Every one lets forth his sprite<br>In the churchway paths to glide;<br>And we fairies that do run<br>By the triple Hecate’s team<br>From the presence of the sun,<br>Following darkness like [[A Dream|a dream]],<br>Now are frolic. Not a mouse<br>Shall disturb this hallowed [[house]].<br>I am sent with broom before<br>To sweep the [[Dust|dust]] behind the door. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Oberon]] and [[Titania]], [[King]] and [[Queen]] of Fairies, with all their train |
| [[Oberon|OBERON]] | Through the [[house]] give glimmering light.<br>By the dead and drowsy [[Fire|fire]]<br>Every elf and fairy sprite<br>Hop as light as bird from brier,<br>And this ditty after me<br>Sing, and [[Dance|dance]] it trippingly. |
| [[Titania|TITANIA]] | First rehearse your [[Song|song]] by rote,<br>To each word a warbling note.<br>Hand in hand with fairy grace<br>Will we sing and bless this place. |
| *(didascalia)* | The [[Song|song]]. The fairies [[Dance|dance]] |
| [[Oberon|OBERON]] | Now until the break of day<br>Through this [[house]] each fairy stray.<br>To the best bride bed will we,<br>Which by us shall blessèd be,<br>And the issue there create<br>Ever shall be fortunate.<br>So shall all the couples three<br>Ever true in loving be,<br>And the blots of [[Nature|nature]]’s hand<br>Shall not in their issue stand.<br>Never mole, harelip, nor scar,<br>Nor mark prodigious such as are<br>Despised in nativity<br>Shall upon their children be.<br>With this field-dew consecrate<br>Every fairy take his gait<br>And each several chamber bless<br>Through this palace with sweet peace;<br>And the owner of it blessed<br>Ever shall in safety rest.<br>Trip away, make no stay,<br>Meet me all by break of day. Exeunt all but Robin<br><br>Epilogue |
| ROBIN | If we shadows have offended,<br>Think but this, and all is mended:<br>That you have but slumbered here,<br>While these visions did appear;<br>And this weak and idle theme,<br>No more yielding but [[A Dream|a dream]],<br>Gentles, do not reprehend.<br>If you pardon, we will mend.<br>And as I am an honest [[Puck|puck]],<br>If we have unearned luck<br>Now to ’scape the serpent’s tongue,<br>We will make amends ere long,<br>Else the [[Puck|puck]] a liar call.<br>So, good [[Night|night]] unto you all.<br>Give me your hands, if we be friends,<br>And Robin shall restore amends. |
