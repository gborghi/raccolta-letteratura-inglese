| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Titania]], [[Queen]] of Fairies, and [[Bottom]] the clown with the ass-head, and fairies: Peaseblossom, Cobweb, Mote, and Mustardseed |
| [[Titania|TITANIA]] | (to [[Bottom]])<br>Come, sit thee down upon this flow’ry bed,<br>While I thy amiable cheeks do coy,<br>And stick musk-roses in thy sleek smooth head,<br>And kiss thy fair large ears, my gentle joy. |
| [[Bottom|BOTTOM]] | Where’s Peaseblossom? |
| PEASEBLOSSOM | Ready. |
| [[Bottom|BOTTOM]] | Scratch my head, Peaseblossom. Where’s Monsieur Cobweb? |
| COBWEB | Ready. |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Bottom|BOTTOM]] Monsieur Cobweb, good monsieur, get you your weapons in your hand and kill me a red-hipped humble-bee on the top of a thistle; and, good monsieur, bring me the honeybag. Do not fret yourself too much in the action, monsieur; and, good monsieur, have a care the honeybag break not. I would be loath to have you overflowen with a honeybag, signor. Exit Cobweb Where’s Monsieur Mustardseed? |
| MUSTARDSEED | Ready. |
| [[Bottom|BOTTOM]] | Give me your neaf, Monsieur Mustardseed. Pray you, leave your courtesy, good monsieur. |
| MUSTARDSEED | What’s your will? |
| [[Bottom|BOTTOM]] | Nothing, good monsieur, but to help Cavaliery Peaseblossom to scratch. I must to the barber’s, monsieur, for methinks I am marvellous hairy about the face; and I am such a tender ass, if my hair do but tickle me I must scratch. |
| [[Titania|TITANIA]] | What, wilt thou hear some music, my sweet [[Love|love]]? |
| [[Bottom|BOTTOM]] | I have a reasonable good ear in music. Let’s have the tongs and the bones. |
| *(didascalia)* | Rural music |
| [[Titania|TITANIA]] | Or say, sweet [[Love|love]], what thou desir’st to eat. |
| [[Bottom|BOTTOM]] | Truly, a peck of provender. I could munch your good dry oats. Methinks I have a great [[Desire|desire]] to a bottle of hay. Good hay, sweet hay, hath no fellow. |
| [[Titania|TITANIA]] | I have a venturous fairy that shall seek<br>The squirrel’s hoard, and fetch thee off new nuts.<br>[[Bottom]] I had rather have a handful or two of dried peas. But I pray you, let none of your people stir me. I have an exposition of [[Sleep|sleep]] come upon me. |
| [[Titania|TITANIA]] | [[Sleep]] thou, and I will [[Wind|wind]] thee in my arms.<br>Fairies, be gone, and be all ways away. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt Fairies |
| [[Titania|TITANIA]] | So doth the woodbine the sweet honeysuckle<br>Gently entwist; the female ivy so<br>Enrings the barky fingers of the elm.<br>O how I [[Love|love]] thee, how I dote on thee! |
| *(didascalia)* | They [[Sleep|sleep]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Robin Goodfellow and [[Oberon]], meeting |
| [[Oberon|OBERON]] | Welcome, good Robin. Seest thou this sweet sight?<br>Her dotage now I do begin to pity,<br>For meeting her of late behind the wood,<br>Seeking sweet favours for this hateful [[Fool|fool]],<br>I did upbraid her and fall out with her,<br>For she his hairy temples then had rounded<br>With coronet of fresh and fragrant [[Flowers|flowers]],<br>And that same dew which sometime on the buds<br>Was wont to swell like round and [[Orient|orient]] pearls<br>Stood now within the pretty flow’rets’ eyes,<br>Like [[Tears|tears]] that did their own disgrace bewail.<br>When I had at my pleasure taunted her,<br>And she in mild terms begged my patience,<br>I then did ask of her her changeling [[Child|child]],<br>Which straight she gave me, and her fairy sent<br>To bear him to my bower in fairyland.<br>And now I have the boy, I will undo<br>This hateful imperfection of her eyes.<br>And, gentle [[Puck|puck]], take this transformed scalp<br>From off the head of this Athenian swain,<br>That he, awaking when the other do,<br>May all to Athens back again repair,<br>And think no more of this [[Night|night]]’s accidents<br>But as the fierce vexation of [[A Dream|a dream]].<br>But first I will release the Fairy [[Queen]].<br>He drops the juice on [[Titania]]’s eyelids<br>Be as thou wast wont to be,<br>See as thou wast wont to see.<br>Dian’s bud o’er Cupid’s flower<br>Hath such force and blessed power.<br>Now, my [[Titania]], wake you, my sweet [[Queen|queen]]. |
| [[Titania|TITANIA]] | (awaking)<br>My [[Oberon]], what visions have I seen!<br>Methought I was enamoured of an ass. |
| [[Oberon|OBERON]] | There lies your [[Love|love]]. |
| [[Titania|TITANIA]] | How came these things to pass?<br>O, how mine eyes do loathe his visage now! |
| [[Oberon|OBERON]] | [[Silence]] a while.—Robin, take off this head.—[[Titania]], music call, and strike more dead Than common [[Sleep|sleep]] of all these five the sense. |
| *(didascalia)* | Music, ho—music such as charmeth [[Sleep|sleep]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | Still musica |
| ROBIN | (taking the ass-head off [[Bottom]])<br>Now when thou wak’st with thine own [[Fool|fool]]’s eyes peep. |
| *(didascalia)* | Sound music. |
| *(didascalia)* | The music |
| *(didascalia)* | changes |
| [[Oberon|OBERON]] | Come, my [[Queen|queen]], take hands with me,<br>And rock the ground whereon these sleepers be.<br>[[Oberon]] and [[Titania]] [[Dance|dance]]<br><br>Now thou and I are new in amity,<br>And will tomorrow midnight solemnly<br>[[Dance]] in Duke [[Theseus]]’ [[house]], triumphantly,<br>And bless it to all fair prosperity.<br>There shall the pairs of faithful [[lovers]] be<br>Wedded with [[Theseus]], all in jollity. |
| ROBIN | Fairy [[King]], attend and mark.<br>I do hear the morning lark. |
| [[Oberon|OBERON]] | Then, my [[Queen|queen]], in [[Silence|silence]] sad<br>Trip we after nightës shade.<br>We the globe can compass soon,<br>Swifter than the wand’ring [[Moon|moon]]. |
| [[Titania|TITANIA]] | Come, my lord, and in our flight<br>Tell me how it came this [[Night|night]]<br>That I sleeping here was found<br>With these mortals on the ground. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt [[Oberon]], [[Titania]], and Robin. The sleepers lie still [[Wind]] horns within. Enter [[Theseus]] with Egeus, [[Hippolyta]], and all his train |
| [[Theseus|THESEUS]] | Go, one of you, find out the forester,<br>For now our observation is performed;<br>And since we have the vanguard of the day,<br>My [[Love|love]] shall hear the music of my hounds.<br>Uncouple in the western valley; let them go.<br>Dispatch, I say, and find the forester. Exit one<br><br>We will, fair [[Queen]], up to the mountain’s top,<br>And mark the musical confusion<br>Of hounds and echo in conjunction. |
| [[Hippolyta|HIPPOLYTA]] | I was with Hercules and Cadmus once<br>When in a wood of Crete they bayed the bear<br>With hounds of Sparta. Never did I hear<br>Such gallant chiding; for besides the groves,<br>The skies, the fountains, every region near<br>Seemed all one mutual cry. I never heard<br>So musical a discord, such sweet thunder. |
| [[Theseus|THESEUS]] | My hounds are bred out of the Spartan kind,<br>So flewed, so sanded; and their heads are hung<br>With ears that sweep away the morning dew,<br>Crook-kneed, and dewlapped like Thessalian bulls,<br>Slow in pursuit, but matched in mouth like [[Bells|bells]],<br>Each under each. A cry more tuneable<br>Was never holla’d to nor cheered with horn<br>In Crete, in Sparta, nor in Thessaly.<br>Judge when you hear. But soft: what nymphs are<br>these? |
| EGEUS | My lord, this is my daughter here asleep,<br>And this [[Lysander]]; this [[Demetrius]] is;<br>This [[Helena]], old Nedar’s [[Helena]].<br>I [[Wonder|wonder]] of their being here together. |
| [[Theseus|THESEUS]] | No [[Doubt|doubt]] they [[Rose|rose]] up early to observe<br>The rite of May, and, hearing our intent,<br>Came here in grace of our solemnity.<br>But speak, Egeus : is not this the day<br>That [[Hermia]] should give answer of her choice? |
| EGEUS | It is, my lord. |
| [[Theseus|THESEUS]] | Go bid the huntsmen wake them with their horns. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit one |
| *(didascalia)* | Shout within: [[Wind|wind]] horns. The [[lovers]] all start up |
| [[Theseus|THESEUS]] | Good morrow, friends. Saint [[Valentine]] is past.<br>Begin these wood-[[Birds|birds]] but to couple now? |
| [[Lysander|LYSANDER]] | Pardon, my lord.<br>The [[lovers]] kneel |
| [[Theseus|THESEUS]] | I pray you all stand up.<br>The [[lovers]] stand<br>(To [[Demetrius]] and [[Lysander]]) I know you two are rival<br>enemies.<br>How comes this gentle concord in the world,<br>That hatred is so far from [[Jealousy|jealousy]]<br>To [[Sleep|sleep]] by hate, and [[Fear|fear]] no enmity? |
| [[Lysander|LYSANDER]] | My lord, I shall reply amazèdly,<br>Half [[Sleep|sleep]], half waking. But as yet, I swear,<br>I cannot truly say how I came here,<br>But as I think—for truly would I speak,<br>And, now I do bethink me, so it is—<br>I came with [[Hermia]] hither. Our intent<br>Was to be gone from Athens where we might,<br>Without the peril of the Athenian law— |
| EGEUS | (to<br>[[Theseus]])<br>Enough, enough, my lord, you have enough.<br>I beg the law, the law upon his head.—<br>They would have stol’n away, they would, [[Demetrius]],<br>Thereby to have defeated you and me—<br>You of your wife, and me of my consent,<br>Of my consent that she should be your wife. |
| [[Demetrius|DEMETRIUS]] | (to [[Theseus]])<br>My lord, fair [[Helen]] told me of their stealth,<br>Of this their purpose hither to this wood,<br>And I in fury hither followed them,<br>Fair [[Helena]] in fancy following me.<br>But, my good lord, I wot not by what power—<br>But by some power it is—my [[Love|love]] to [[Hermia]],<br>Melted as the [[Snow|snow]], seems to me now<br>As the remembrance of an idle gaud<br>Which in my [[Childhood|childhood]] I did’dote upon,<br>And all the [[Faith|faith]], the virtue of my heart,<br>The object and the pleasure of mine eye<br>Is only [[Helena]]. To her, my lord,<br>Was I betrothed ere I see [[Hermia]].<br>But like in sickness did I loathe this food;<br>But, as in health come to my natural taste,<br>Now I do wish it, [[Love|love]] it, long for it,<br>And will for evermore be true to it. |
| [[Theseus|THESEUS]] | Fair [[lovers]], you are fortunately met.<br>Of this discourse we more will hear anon.—<br>Egeus, I will overbear your will,<br>For in the temple by and by with us<br>These couples shall eternally be knit.—<br>And, for the morning now is something worn,<br>Our purposed hunting shall be set aside.<br>Away with us to Athens. Three and three,<br>We’ll hold a feast in great solemnity.<br>Come, [[Hippolyta]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit Duke [[Theseus]] with [[Hippolyta]], Egeus, and all his train |
| [[Demetrius|DEMETRIUS]] | These things seem small and undistinguishable,<br>Like far-off [[Mountains|mountains]] turned into clouds. |
| [[Hermia|HERMIA]] | Methinks I see these things with parted eye,<br>When everything seems [[Double|double]]. |
| [[Helena|HELENA]] | So methinks,<br>And I have found [[Demetrius]] like a [[Jewel|jewel]],<br>Mine own and not mine own. |
| [[Demetrius|DEMETRIUS]] | It seems to me<br>That yet we [[Sleep|sleep]], we dream. Do not you think<br>The Duke was here and bid us follow him? |
| [[Hermia|HERMIA]] | Yea, and my father. |
| [[Helena|HELENA]] | And [[Hippolyta]]. |
| [[Lysander|LYSANDER]] | And he did bid us follow to the temple. |
| [[Demetrius|DEMETRIUS]] | Why then, we are awake. Let’s follow him,<br>And by the way let us recount our [[Dreams|dreams]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt the [[lovers]] |
| [[Demetrius|DEMETRIUS]] | [[Bottom]] wakes |
| [[Bottom|BOTTOM]] | When my cue comes, call me, and I will answer. My next is ‘most fair Pyramus’. Heigh-ho. Peter Quince? Flute the bellows-mender? Snout the tinker? Starveling? God’s my life! Stolen hence, and left me asleep?—I have had a most rare vision. I have had [[A Dream|a dream]] past the wit of man to say what dream it was. Man is but an ass if he go about t‘expound this dream. Methought I was—there is no man can tell what. Methought I was, and methought I had—but man is but a patched [[Fool|fool]] if he will offer to say what methought I had. The eye of man hath not heard, the ear of man hath not seen, man’s hand is not able to taste, his tongue to conceive, nor his heart to report what my dream was. I will get Peter Quince to write a ballad of this dream. It shall be called ‘[[Bottom]]’s Dream’, because it hath no [[Bottom|bottom]], and I will sing it in the latter end of a play, before the Duke. Peradventure, to make it the more gracious, I shall sing it at her [[Death|death]]. Exit |
