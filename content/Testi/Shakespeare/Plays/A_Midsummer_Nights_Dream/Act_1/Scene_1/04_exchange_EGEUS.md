| EGEUS | With duty and [[Desire|desire]] we follow you. Exeunt all but [[Lysander]] and [[Hermia]] |
| [[Lysander|LYSANDER]] | How now, my [[Love|love]]? Why is your cheek so pate ? How chance the roses there do fade so fast? |
| [[Hermia|HERMIA]] | Belike for want of rain, which I could [[Well|well]] Beteem them from the tempest of my eyes. |
| [[Lysander|LYSANDER]] | Ay me, for aught that I could ever read, Could ever hear by tale or [[history]], The course of true [[Love|love]] never did run smooth, But either it was different in btood— |
| [[Hermia|HERMIA]] | O [[Cross|cross]]!—too high to be enthralled to low. |
| [[Lysander|LYSANDER]] | Or else misgrafted in respect of years— |
| [[Hermia|HERMIA]] | O spite!—too old to be engaged to young. |
| [[Lysander|LYSANDER]] | Or merit stood upon the choice of friends— |
| [[Hermia|HERMIA]] | O hell!—to choose [[Love|love]] by another’s eyes. |
| [[Lysander|LYSANDER]] | Or if there were a sympathy in choice, War, [[Death|death]], or sickness did lay siege to it, Making it momentany as a sound, Swift as a [[Shadow|shadow]], short as any dream, Brief as the [[Lightning|lightning]] in the collied [[Night|night]], That, in a spleen, unfolds both heaven and earth, And, ere a man hath power to say ‘Behold!’ The jaws of darkness do devour it up. So quick bright things come to confusion. |
| [[Hermia|HERMIA]] | If then true [[lovers]] have been ever crossed, It stands as an edict in destiny. Then let us teach our trial patience, Because it is a customary [[Cross|cross]], As due to [[Love|love]] as thoughts, and [[Dreams|dreams]], and sighs, Wishes, and [[Tears|tears]], poor fancy’s followers. |
| [[Lysander|LYSANDER]] | A good [[Persuasion|persuasion]]. Therefore hear me, [[Hermia]]. I have a widow aunt, a dowager Of great revenue, and she hath no [[Child|child]], And she respects me as her only son. From Athens is her [[house]] remote seven leagues. There, gentle [[Hermia]], may I marry thee, And to that place the sharp Athenian law Cannot pursue us. If thou lov’st me then, Steal forth thy father’s [[house]] tomorrow [[Night|night]], And in the wood, a league without the town, Where I did meet thee once with [[Helena]] To do observance to a morn of May, There will I stay for thee. |
| [[Hermia|HERMIA]] | My good [[Lysander]], I swear to thee by Cupid’s strongest bow, By his best arrow with the golden head, By the simplicity of [[Venus]]’ doves, By that which knitteth souls and prospers loves, And by that [[Fire|fire]] which burned the Carthage [[Queen|queen]] When the false Trojan under sail was seen; By all the vows that ever men have broke— In number more than ever women spoke— In that same place thou hast appointed me Tomorrow truly will I meet with thee. |
| [[Lysander|LYSANDER]] | Keep promise, [[Love|love]]. Look, here comes [[Helena]]. Enter [[Helena]] |
| [[Hermia|HERMIA]] | God [[Speed|speed]], fair [[Helena]]. Whither away? |
| [[Helena|HELENA]] | Call you me fair? That ’fair’ again unsay. [[Demetrius]] loves your fair—O happy fair! Your eyes are lodestars, and your tongue’s sweet air More tuneable than lark to shepherd’s ear When wheat is green, when hawthorn buds appear. Sickness is catching. O, were favour so! Your words I catch, fair [[Hermia]]; ere I go, My ear should catch your voice, my eye your eye, My tongue should catch your tongue’s sweet melody. Were the world mine, [[Demetrius]] being bated, The rest I’d give to be to you translated. O, teach me how you look, and with what art You sway the motion of [[Demetrius]]’ heart. |
