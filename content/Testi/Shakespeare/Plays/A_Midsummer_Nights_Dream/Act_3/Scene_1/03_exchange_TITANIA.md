| [[Titania|TITANIA]] | (awaking) What angel wakes me from my flow’ry bed? |
| [[Bottom|BOTTOM]] | (sings) The finch, the sparrow, and the lark, The plainsong cuckoo grey, Whose note full many a man doth mark, And dares not answer ‘Nay’— for indeed, who would set his wit to so foolish a bird? Who would give a bird the lie, though he cry ’Cuckoo’ never so? |
| [[Titania|TITANIA]] | I pray thee, gentle mortal, sing again. Mine ear is much enamoured of thy note; So is mine eye enthralled to thy shape; And thy fair virtue’s force perforce doth move me On the first view to say, to swear, I [[Love|love]] thee. |
| [[Bottom|BOTTOM]] | Methinks, mistress, you should have little reason for that. And yet, to say the [[Truth|truth]], reason and [[Love|love]] keep little company together nowadays—the more the pity that some honest neighbours will not make them friends. Nay, I can gleek upon occasion. |
| [[Titania|TITANIA]] | Thou art as wise as thou art beautiful. |
| [[Bottom|BOTTOM]] | Not so, neither; but if I had wit enough to get out of this wood, I have enough to serve mine own turn. |
| [[Titania|TITANIA]] | Out of this wood do not [[Desire|desire]] to go. Thou shalt remain here, whether thou wilt or no. I am a spirit of no common rate: The [[Summer|summer]] still doth tend upon my state; And I do [[Love|love]] thee. Therefore go with me. I’ll give thee fairies to attend on thee, And they shall fetch thee jewels from the deep, And sing while thou on pressed [[Flowers|flowers]] dost [[Sleep|sleep]]; And I will purge thy mortal grossness so That thou shalt like an airy spirit go. Peaseblossom, Cobweb, Mote, and Mustardseed! Enter four fairies: Peaseblossom, Cobweb, Mote, and Mustardseed |

| [[Titania|TITANIA]] | Be kind and courteous to this gentleman. Hop in his walks, and gambol in his eyes. Feed him with apricots and dewberries, With purple grapes, green figs, and mulberries; The honeybags steal from the humble-[[Bees|bees]], And for [[Night|night]] tapers crop their waxen thighs And light them at the fiery glow-worms’ eyes To have my [[Love|love]] to bed, and to arise; And pluck the wings from painted butterflies To fan the moonbeams from his sleeping eyes. Nod to him, elves, and do him courtesies. |
