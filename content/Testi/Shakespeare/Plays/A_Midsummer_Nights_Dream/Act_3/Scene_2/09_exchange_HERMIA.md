| HERMIA | I am amazed, and know not what to say. Exit Oberon and Robin come forward |
| OBERON | This is thy negligence. Still thou mistak‘st, Or else commit’st thy knaveries wilfully. |
| ROBIN | Believe me, king of shadows, I mistook. Did not you tell me I should know the man By the Athenian garments he had on?— And so far blameless proves my enterprise That I have ’nointed an Athenian’s eyes; And so far am I glad it so did sort As this their jangling I esteem a sport. |
