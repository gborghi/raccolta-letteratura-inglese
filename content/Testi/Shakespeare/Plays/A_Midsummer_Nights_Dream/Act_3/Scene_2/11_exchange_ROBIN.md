| ROBIN | My fairy lord, this must be done with haste, For [[Night|night]]’s swift dragons cut the clouds full fast, And yonder shines Aurora’s harbinger, At whose approach [[Ghosts|ghosts]], wand’ring here and there, Troop [[Home|home]] to churchyards; damned spirits all That in [[Cross|cross]]-ways and floods have burial Already to their wormy beds are gone, For [[Fear|fear]] lest day should look their shames upon. They wilfully themselves exiled from light, And must for aye consort with black-browed [[Night|night]]. |
| [[Oberon|OBERON]] | But we are spirits of another sort. I with the morning’s [[Love|love]] have oft made sport, And like a forester the groves may tread Even till the eastern gate, all fiery red, Opening on Neptune with fair blessed beams Turns into yellow gold his salt green streams. But notwithstanding, haste, make no delay; We may effect this business yet ere day. Exit |
| ROBIN | Up and down, up and down, I will lead them up and down. I am feared in field and town. Goblin, lead them up and down. Here comes one. Enter [[Lysander]] |
| [[Lysander|LYSANDER]] | Where art thou, proud [[Demetrius]]? Speak thou now. |
| ROBIN | shifting place Here, [[Villain|villain]], drawn and ready. Where art thou? |
| [[Lysander|LYSANDER]] | I will be with thee straight. |
| ROBIN | shifting place Follow me then To plainer ground. Exit [[Lysander]] Enter [[Demetrius]] |
| [[Demetrius|DEMETRIUS]] | shifting place [[Lysander]], speak again. Thou runaway, thou coward, art thou fled? Speak! In some bush? Where dost thou hide thy head? |
| ROBIN | shifting place Thou coward, art thou bragging to the [[Stars|stars]], Telling the bushes that thou look’st for wars, And wilt not come? Come, recreant; come, thou [[Child|child]], I’ll whip thee with a rod. He is defiled That draws a [[Sword|sword]] on thee. |
| [[Demetrius|DEMETRIUS]] | shifting place Yea, art thou there? |
| ROBIN | shifting place Follow my voice; we’ll try no manhood here. Exeunt |
| *(didascalia)* | 3.3 Enter [[Lysander]] |
| [[Lysander|LYSANDER]] | He goes before me, and still dares me on; When I come where he calls, then he is gone. The [[Villain|villain]] is much lighter heeled than I; I followed fast, but faster he did fly, That fallen am I in dark uneven way, And here will rest me. He lies down Come, thou gentle day; For if but once thou show me thy grey light, I’ll find [[Demetrius]], and [[Revenge|revenge]] this spite. He sleeps Enter Robin Goodfellow and [[Demetrius]] |
| ROBIN | shifting place Ho, ho, ho, coward, why com’st thou not? |
| [[Demetrius|DEMETRIUS]] | Abide me if thou dar‘st, for [[Well|well]] I wot Thou runn’st before me, shifting every place, And dar’st not stand nor look me in the face. Where art thou now? |
| ROBIN | shifting place Come hither, I am here. |
| [[Demetrius|DEMETRIUS]] | Nay, then thou mock’st me. Thou shalt buy this dear If ever I thy face by daylight see. Now go thy way. Faintness constraineth me To measure out my length on this cold bed. He lies down By day’s approach look to be visited. He sleeps |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Helena]] |
| [[Helena|HELENA]] | O weary [[Night|night]], O long and tedious [[Night|night]], Abate thy hours; shine comforts from the east That I may back to Athens by daylight From these that my poor company detest; And [[Sleep|sleep]], that sometimes shuts up sorrow’s eye, Steal me a while from mine own company. She lies down and sleeps |
