| *(didascalia)* | Enter the [[Duke of Gloucester]], with his Servingmen in blue coats |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | I am come to survey the Tower this day.<br>Since Henry’s [[Death|death]], I [[Fear|fear]] there is conveyance.<br>Where be these warders, that they wait not here? |
| *(didascalia)* | A Servingman knocketh on the gates |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Open the gates: ’tis [[Gloucester]] that calls. |
| *(didascalia)* | FIRST WARDER within the Tower |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Who’s there that knocketh so imperiously? |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]]’S FIRST MAN | It is the noble [[Duke of Gloucester]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | SECOND WARDER within the Tower |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]]’S FIRST MAN | Whoe’er he be, you may not be let in. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]]’S FIRST MAN | Villains, answer you so the Lord Protector? |
| *(didascalia)* | FIRST WARDER within the Tower |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]]’S FIRST MAN | The Lord protect him, so we answer him.<br>We do no otherwise than we are willed. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Who willèd you? Or whose will stands, but mine?<br>There’s none Protector of the realm but I.<br>(To Servingmen) Break up the gates. I’ll be your<br>warrantize.<br><br>Shall I be flouted thus by dunghill grooms?<br>[[Gloucester]]’s men rush at the Tower gates |
| *(didascalia)* | WOODVILLE within the Tower |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | What noise is this? What traitors have we here? |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Lieutenant, is it you whose voice I hear?<br>Open the gates! Here’s [[Gloucester]], that would enter. |
| *(didascalia)* | WOODVILLE within the Tower |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Have patience, noble duke: I may not open.<br>My lord of Winchester forbids.<br>From him I have express commandëment<br>That thou, nor none of thine, shall be let in. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Faint-hearted Woodville! Prizest him fore me?—<br>Arrogant Winchester, that haughty prelate,<br>Whom Henry, our late sovereign, ne’er could brook?<br>Thou art no friend to God or to the [[King]].<br>Open the gates, or I’ll shut thee out shortly. |
| SERVINGMEN | Open the gates unto the Lord Protector,<br>Or we’ll burst them open, if that you come not quickly. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter, to the Lord Protector at the Tower gates, the Bishop of Winchester and his men in tawny coats |
| WINCHESTER | How now, ambitious vizier! What means this? |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Peeled priest, dost thou command me to be shut out? |
| WINCHESTER | I do, thou most usurping proditor,<br>And not ‘Protector’, of the [[King]] or realm. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Stand back, thou manifest conspirator.<br>Thou that contrived’st to murder our dead lord,<br>Thou that giv’st whores indulgences to sin,<br>If thou proceed in this thy insolence— |
| WINCHESTER | Nay, stand thou back! I will not budge a foot.<br>This be Damascus, be thou cursed [[Cain]],<br>To slay thy brother Abel, if thou wilt. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | I will not slay thee, but I’ll drive thee back.<br>Thy purple robes, as a [[Child|child]]’s bearing-cloth,<br>I’ll use to carry thee out of this place. |
| WINCHESTER | Do what thou dar’st, I beard thee to thy face. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | What, am I dared and bearded to my face?<br>Draw, men, for all this privileged place.<br>All draw their swords<br>Blue coats to tawny coats!—Priest, beware your<br>beard.<br>I mean to tug it, and to cuff you soundly.<br>Under my feet I’ll stamp thy bishop’s mitre.<br>In spite of Pope, or dignities of church,<br>Here by the cheeks I’ll drag thee up and down. |
| WINCHESTER | [[Gloucester]], thou wilt answer this before the Pope. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Winchester goose! I cry, ‘A rope, a rope!’<br>(To his Servingmen)<br><br>Now beat them hence. Why do you let them stay?<br>(To Winchester)<br><br>Thee I’ll chase hence, thou wolf in sheep’s array.<br>Out, tawny coats! Out, cloaked hypocrite!<br>Here [[Gloucester]]’s men beat out the Bishop’s men. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter in the hurly-burly the Mayor of [[London]] and |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | his Officers |
| MAYOR | Fie, lords!—that you, being supreme magistrates,<br>Thus contumeliously should break the peace. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Peace, mayor, thou know’st little of my wrongs.<br>Here’s Beaufort—that regards nor God nor [[King|king]]-<br>Hath here distrained the Tower to his use. |
| WINCHESTER | (to Mayor)<br>Here’s [[Gloucester]]—a foe to citizens,<br>One that still motions war, and never peace,<br>O’ercharging your free purses with large fines—<br>That seeks to overthrow religion,<br>Because he is Protector of the realm,<br>And would have armour here out of the Tower<br>To [[Crown|crown]] himself [[King|king]] and suppress the Prince. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | I will not answer thee with words but blows.<br>Here the factions skirmish again |
| MAYOR | Naught rests for me, in this tumultuous strife,<br>But to make open proclamation.<br>Come, officer, as loud as e’er thou canst, cry. |
| OFFICER | All manner of men, assembled here in arms this day against God’s peace and the [[King]]’s, we charge and command you in his highness’ name to repair to your several dwelling places, and not to wear, handle, or use any [[Sword|sword]], weapon, or dagger henceforward, upon [[Pain|pain]] of [[Death|death]].<br>The skirmishes cease |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Bishop, I’ll be no breaker of the law.<br>But we shall meet and break our minds at large. |
| WINCHESTER | [[Gloucester]], we’ll meet to thy cost, be sure.<br>Thy heart-[[Blood|blood]] I will have for this day’s work. |
| MAYOR | I’ll call for clubs, if you will not away.<br>(Aside) This bishop is more haughty than the devil. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Mayor, farewell. Thou dost but what thou mayst. |
| WINCHESTER | Abominable [[Gloucester]], guard thy head,<br>For I intend to have it ere long. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt both factions severally |
| MAYOR | (to Officers)<br>See the coast cleared, and then we will depart.—<br>Good God, these nobles should such stomachs bear!<br>I myself fight not once in forty year. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt |
