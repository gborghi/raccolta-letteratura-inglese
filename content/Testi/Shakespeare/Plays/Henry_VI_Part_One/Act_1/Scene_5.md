| *(didascalia)* | Enter the Master Gunner of Orleans with his Boy |
| MASTER GUNNER | Sirrah, thou know’st how Orléans is besieged,<br>And how the English have the suburbs won. |
| BOY | Father, I know, and oft have shot at them;<br>Howe’er, unfortunate, I missed my aim. |
| MASTER GUNNER | But now thou shalt not. Be thou ruled by me.<br>Chief Master Gunner am I of this town;<br>Something I must do to procure me grace.<br>The Prince’s spials have informed me<br>How the English, in the suburbs close entrenched,<br>Wont, through a secret grate of iron bars<br>In yonder tower, to overpeer the city,<br>And thence discover how with most advantage<br>They may vex us with shot or with assault.<br>To intercept this inconvenience,<br>A piece of ordnance ‘gainst it I have placed,<br>And even these three days have I watched, if I could<br>see them.<br>Now do thou watch, for I can stay no longer.<br>If thou spy’st any, run and bring me word,<br>And thou shalt find me at the governor’s. |
| BOY | Father, I warrant you, take you no care— |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit Master Gunner at one door |
| BOY | I’ll never trouble you, if I may spy them. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit at the other door |
