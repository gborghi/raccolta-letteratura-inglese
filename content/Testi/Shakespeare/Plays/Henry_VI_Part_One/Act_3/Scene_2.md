| *(didascalia)* | Enter Joan la Pucelle, disguised, with four French Soldiers with sacks upon their backs |
| JOAN | These are the city gates, the gates of Rouen,<br>Through which our policy must make a breach.<br>Take heed. Be wary how you place your words.<br>Talk like the vulgar sort of market men<br>That come to gather money for their corn.<br>If we have entrance, as I hope we shall,<br>And that we find the slothful watch but weak,<br>I’ll by a sign give notice to our friends,<br>That Charles the Dauphin may encounter them.<br>A SOLDIER<br>Our sacks shall be a mean to sack the city,<br>And we be lords and rulers over Rouen.<br>Therefore we’ll knock.<br>They knock |
| WATCH | (within)<br>Qui là? |
| JOAN | Paysans, la pauvre gens de France:<br>Poor market folks that come to sell their corn. |
| WATCH | (opening the gates) |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter, go in. The market bell is rung. |
| JOAN | (aside)<br>Now, Rouen, I’ll shake thy bulwarks to the ground. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt |
