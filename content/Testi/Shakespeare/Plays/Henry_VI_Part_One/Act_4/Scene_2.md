| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Lord Talbot]] with a trumpeter and drummer and soldiers before Bordeaux |
| TALBOT | Go to the gates of Bordeaux, trumpeter.<br>Summon their general unto the wall.<br>The trumpeter sounds a parley. Enter French General, aloft<br>English John Talbot, captain, calls you forth,<br>Servant in arms to Harry [[King]] of England;<br>And thus he would: open your [[City|city]] gates,<br>Be humble to us, call my sovereign yours<br>And do him homage as obedient subjects,<br>And I’ll withdraw me and my bloody power.<br>But if you frown upon this proffered peace,<br>You tempt the fury of my three attendants—<br>Lean famine, quartering steel, and climbing [[Fire|fire]]—<br>Who in a moment even with the earth<br>Shall lay your stately and air-braving towers<br>If you forsake the offer of their [[Love|love]]. |
| GENERAL | Thou ominous and fearful owl of [[Death|death]],<br>Our nation’s terror and their bloody scourge,<br>The period of thy tyranny approacheth.<br>On us thou canst not enter but by [[Death|death]],<br>For I protest we are [[Well|well]] fortified<br>And strong enough to issue out and fight.<br>If thou retire, the [[Dauphin]] [[Well|well]] appointed<br>Stands with the snares of war to tangle thee.<br>On either hand thee there are squadrons pitched<br>To wall thee from the liberty of flight,<br>And no way canst thou turn thee for redress<br>But [[Death|death]] doth front thee with apparent spoil,<br>And pale destruction meets thee in the face.<br>Ten thousand French have ta‘en the sacrament<br>To [[Fire|fire]] their dangerous artillery<br>Upon no Christian [[Soul|soul]] but English Talbot.<br>Lo, there thou stand’st, a breathing valiant man<br>Of an invincible unconquered spirit.<br>This is the latest glory of thy praise,<br>That I thy enemy due thee withal,<br>For ere the glass that now begins to run<br>Finish the process of his sandy hour,<br>These eyes that see thee now [[Well|well]] coloured<br>Shall see thee withered, bloody, pale, and dead. |
| *(didascalia)* | Drum afar off |
| GENERAL | Hark, hark, the [[Dauphin]]’s drum, a warning bell,<br>Sings heavy music to thy timorous [[Soul|soul]],<br>And mine shall ring thy dire departure out. Exit |
| TALBOT | He fables not. I hear the enemy.<br>Out, some light horsemen, and peruse their wings. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit one or more |
| TALBOT | O negligent and heedless discipline,<br>How are we parked and bounded in a pale!—<br>A little herd of England’s timorous deer<br>Mazed with a yelping kennel of French curs.<br>If we be English deer, be then in [[Blood|blood]],<br>Not rascal-like to fall down with a pinch,<br>But rather, moody-mad and desperate stags,<br>Turn on the bloody hounds with heads of steel<br>And make the cowards stand aloof at bay.<br>Sell every man his life as dear as mine<br>And they shall find dear deer of us, my friends.<br>God and Saint George, Talbot and England’s right,<br>Prosper our colours in this dangerous fight! Exeunt |
