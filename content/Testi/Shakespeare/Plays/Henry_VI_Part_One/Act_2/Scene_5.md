| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Edmund]] Mortimer, brought in a chair by his Keepers |
| MORTIMER | Kind keepers of my weak decaying age,<br>Let dying Mortimer here rest himself.<br>Even like a man new-haled from the rack,<br>So fare my limbs with long [[Imprisonment|imprisonment]];<br>And these grey locks, the pursuivants of [[Death|death]],<br>Argue the end of [[Edmund]] Mortimer,<br>Nestor-like aged in an age of care.<br>These eyes, like lamps whose wasting oil is spent,<br>Wax dim, as drawing to their exigent;<br>Weak shoulders, overborne with burdening grief,<br>And pithless arms, like to a withered vine<br>That droops his sapless branches to the ground.<br>Yet are these feet—whose strengthless stay is numb,<br>Unable to support this lump of clay—<br>Swift-winged with [[Desire|desire]] to get a [[Grave|grave]],<br>As witting I no other comfort have.<br>But tell me, keeper, will my nephew come? |
| KEEPER | [[Richard]] Plantagenet, my lord, will come.<br>We sent unto the Temple, unto his chamber,<br>And answer was returned that he will come. |
| MORTIMER | Enough. My [[Soul|soul]] shall then be satisfied.<br>Poor gentleman, his wrong doth equal mine.<br>Since Henry Monmouth first began to reign—<br>Before whose glory I was great in arms—<br>This loathsome sequestration have I had;<br>And even since then hath [[Richard]] been obscured,<br>Deprived of [[Honour|honour]] and inheritance.<br>But now the arbitrator of despairs,<br>Just [[Death]], kind umpire of men’s miseries,<br>With sweet enlargement doth dismiss me hence.<br>I would his troubles likewise were expired,<br>That so he might recover what was lost. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Richard]] Plantagenet |
| KEEPER | My lord, your loving nephew now is come. |
| MORTIMER | [[Richard]] Plantagenet, my friend, is he come? |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] PLANTAGENET | Ay, noble uncle, thus ignobly used:<br>Your nephew, late despised [[Richard]], comes. |
| MORTIMER | (to Keepers)<br>Direct mine arms I may embrace his neck<br>And in his bosom spend my latter gasp.<br>O tell me when my lips do touch his cheeks,<br>That I may kindly give one fainting kiss.<br>He embraces [[Richard]]<br>And now declare, sweet stem from York’s great stock,<br>Why didst thou say of late thou wert despised? |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] PLANTAGENET | First lean thine aged back against mine arm,<br>And in that ease I’ll tell thee my dis-ease.<br>This day in argument upon a case<br>Some words there grew ’twixt Somerset and me;<br>Among which terms he used his lavish tongue<br>And did upbraid me with my father’s [[Death|death]];<br>Which obloquy set bars before my tongue,<br>Else with the like I had requited him.<br>Therefore, good uncle, for my father’s sake,<br>In [[Honour|honour]] of a true Plantagenet,<br>And for alliance’ sake, declare the cause<br>My father, Earl of Cambridge, lost his head. |
| MORTIMER | That cause, fair nephew, that imprisoned me,<br>And hath detained me all my flow’ring youth |
| *(didascalia)* | Within a loathsome dungeon, there to pine, |
| MORTIMER | Was cursed instrument of his decease. |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] PLANTAGENET | Discover more at large what cause that was,<br>For I am ignorant and cannot guess. |
| MORTIMER | I will, if that my fading breath permit<br>And [[Death|death]] approach not ere my tale be done.<br>Henry the Fourth, grandfather to this [[King]],<br>Deposed his nephew [[Richard]], Edward’s son,<br>The first begotten and the lawful heir<br>Of Edward [[King|king]], the third of that descent;<br>During whose reign the Percies of the north,<br>Finding his usurpation most unjust,<br>Endeavoured my advancement to the throne.<br>The reason moved these warlike lords to this<br>Was for that—[[Young King|young King]] [[Richard]] thus removed,<br>Leaving no heir begotten of his [[Body|body]]—<br>I was the next by birth and parentage,<br>For by my mother I derived am<br>From Lionel Duke of [[Clarence]], the third son<br>To [[King]] Edward the Third—whereas the [[King]]<br>From [[John of Gaunt]] doth bring his pedigree,<br>Being but fourth of that heroic line.<br>But mark: as in this haughty great attempt<br>They laboured to plant the rightful heir,<br>I lost my liberty, and they their lives.<br>Long after this, when Henry the Fifth,<br>Succeeding his father Bolingbroke, did reign,<br>Thy father, Earl of Cambridge then, derived<br>From famous [[Edmund]] Langley, [[Duke of York]],<br>Marrying my sister that thy mother was,<br>Again, in pity of my hard distress,<br>Levied an army, weening to redeem<br>And have installed me in the diadem;<br>But, as the rest, so fell that noble earl,<br>And was beheaded. Thus the Mortimers,<br>In whom the title rested, were suppressed. |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] PLANTAGENET | Of which, my lord, your [[Honour|honour]] is the last. |
| MORTIMER | True, and thou seest that I no issue have,<br>And that my fainting words do warrant [[Death|death]].<br>Thou art my heir. The rest I wish thee gather—<br>But yet be wary in thy studious care. |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] PLANTAGENET | Thy [[Grave|grave]] admonishments prevail with me.<br>But yet methinks my father’s execution<br>Was nothing less than bloody tyranny. |
| MORTIMER | With [[Silence|silence]], nephew, be thou politic.<br>Strong-fixed is the [[house]] of Lancaster,<br>And like a mountain, not to be removed.<br>But now thy uncle is removing hence,<br>As princes do their courts, when they are cloyed<br>With long continuance in a settled place. |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] PLANTAGENET | O uncle, would some part of my young years<br>Might but redeem the passage of your age. |
| MORTIMER | Thou dost then wrong me, as that slaughterer doth<br>Which giveth many wounds when one will kill.<br>Mourn not, except thou sorrow for my good.<br>Only give order for my funeral.<br>And so farewell, and fair be all thy hopes,<br>And prosperous be thy life in peace and war. Dies |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] PLANTAGENET | And peace, no war, befall thy parting [[Soul|soul]].<br>In [[Prison|prison]] hast thou spent a pilgrimage,<br>And like a hermit overpassed thy days.<br>[[Well]], I will lock his counsel in my breast,<br>And what I do imagine, let that rest.<br>Keepers, convey him hence, and I myself<br>Will see his burial better than his life. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt Keepers with Mortimer’s [[Body|body]] |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] PLANTAGENET | Here dies the dusky torch of Mortimer,<br>Choked with [[Ambition|ambition]] of the meaner sort.<br>And for those wrongs, those bitter injuries,<br>Which Somerset hath offered to my [[house]],<br>I [[Doubt|doubt]] not but with [[Honour|honour]] to redress.<br>And therefore haste I to the Parliament,<br>Either to be restored to my [[Blood|blood]],<br>Or make mine ill th’advantage of my good. Exit |
