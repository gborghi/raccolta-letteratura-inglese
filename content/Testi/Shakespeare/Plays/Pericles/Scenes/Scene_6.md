| *(didascalia)* | Sennet.Enter [[King]] [[Simonides]] and [[Thaisa]], with Lords in attendance, and sit on two thrones |
| [[King|KING]] [[Simonides|SIMONIDES]] | Are the knights ready to begin the triumph? |
| FIRST LORD | They are, my liege,<br>And stay your coming to present themselves. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Simonides|SIMONIDES]] | Return them we are ready; and our daughter,<br>In [[Honour|honour]] of whose birth these triumphs are,<br>Sits here like [[Beauty|beauty]]’s [[Child|child]], whom [[Nature|nature]] gat<br>For men to see and, seeing, [[Wonder|wonder]] at. Exit one |
| [[Thaisa|THAISA]] | It pleaseth you, my father, to express<br>My commendations great, whose merit’s less. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Simonides|SIMONIDES]] | It’s fit it should be so, for princes are<br>A model which heav’n makes like to itself.<br>As jewels lose their glory if neglected,<br>So princes their renown, if not respected.<br>’Tis now your office, daughter, to entertain<br>The labour of each knight in his device. |
| [[Thaisa|THAISA]] | Which, to preserve mine [[Honour|honour]], I’ll perform. |
| *(didascalia)* | Flourish.The first knight passes by |
| [[Thaisa|THAISA]] | richly armed, and his page before him, bearing his device on his shield, delivers it to the Lady [[Thaisa]] |
| [[King|KING]] [[Simonides|SIMONIDES]] | Who is the first that doth prefer himself? |
| [[Thaisa|THAISA]] | A knight of Sparta, my renowned father,<br>And the device he bears upon his shield<br>Is a black Ethiop reaching at the sun.<br>The word, Lux tua vita mihi.<br>She presents it to the [[King]] |
| [[King|KING]] [[Simonides|SIMONIDES]] | He loves you [[Well|well]] that holds his life of you.<br>He returns it to the page, who exits with the first knight. |
| *(didascalia)* | Flourish.The second knight passes by |
| [[King|KING]] [[Simonides|SIMONIDES]] | richly armed, and his page before him, bearing his device on his shield, delivers it to the Lady [[Thaisa]]<br>Who is the second that presents himself? |
| [[Thaisa|THAISA]] | A prince of Macedon, my royal father,<br>And the device he bears upon his shield<br>An armed knight that’s conquered by a lady.<br>The motto thus: Piùe per dolcezza che per forza.<br>she presents it to the [[King]] |
| [[King|KING]] [[Simonides|SIMONIDES]] | You win him more by lenity than force.<br>He returns it to the page, who exits with the second knight. |
| *(didascalia)* | Flourish.The third knight passes by |
| [[King|KING]] [[Simonides|SIMONIDES]] | richly armed, and his page before him, bearing his device on his shield, delivers it to the Lady [[Thaisa]]<br>And what’s the third? |
| [[Thaisa|THAISA]] | The third of Antioch,<br>And his device a wreath of chivalry.<br>The word, Me pompae provexit apex.<br>She presents it to the [[King]] |
| [[King|KING]] [[Simonides|SIMONIDES]] | [[Desire]] of renown he doth devise,<br>The which hath drawn him to this enterprise.<br>He returns it to the page, who exits with the third knight. |
| *(didascalia)* | Flourish.The fourth knight passes by |
| [[King|KING]] [[Simonides|SIMONIDES]] | richly armed, and his page before him, bearing his device on his shield, delivers it to the Lady [[Thaisa]]<br>What is the fourth? |
| [[Thaisa|THAISA]] | A knight of Athens bearing<br>A burning torch that’s turned upside down.<br>The word, Qui me alit me extinguit.<br>She presents it to the [[King]] |
| [[King|KING]] [[Simonides|SIMONIDES]] | Which shows that [[Beauty|beauty]] hath this power and will,<br>Which can as [[Well|well]] inflame as it can kill. |
| *(didascalia)* | He returns it to the page, who exits with the fourth knight. |
| *(didascalia)* | Flourish. The fifth Knight passes by richly armed, and his page before him, bearing his device on his shield, delivers it to the Lady [[Thaisa]] |
| [[King|KING]] [[Simonides|SIMONIDES]] | And who the fifth? |
| [[Thaisa|THAISA]] | The fifth, a prince of Corinth,<br>Presents an hand environèd with clouds,<br>Holding out gold that’s by the [[Touchstone|touchstone]] tried.<br>The motto thus: Sic spectanda fides. |
| *(didascalia)* | She presents it to the [[King]] |
| [[King|KING]] [[Simonides|SIMONIDES]] | So [[Faith|faith]] is to be looked into. |
| *(didascalia)* | He returns it to the page, who exits with the fifth knight. |
| *(didascalia)* | Flourish.l The sixth knight, [[Pericles]], in a rusty armour, who, having neither page to deliver his shield nor shield to deliver, presents his device unto the Lady [[Thaisa]] |
| [[King|KING]] [[Simonides|SIMONIDES]] | And what’s the sixth and last, the which the knight<br>himself<br>With such a graceful courtesy delivereth? |
| [[Thaisa|THAISA]] | He seems to be a stranger, but his present is<br>A withered branch that’s only green at top.<br>The motto, In hac spe vivo. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Simonides|SIMONIDES]] | From the dejected state wherein he is<br>He hopes by you his fortunes yet may flourish. |
| FIRST LORD | He had need mean better than his outward show<br>Can any way speak in his just commend,<br>For by his rusty outside he appears<br>T’have practised more the whipstock than the lance. |
| SECOND LORD | He [[Well|well]] may be a stranger, for he comes<br>Unto an honoured triumph strangely furnished. |
| THIRD LORD | And on set purpose let his armour rust<br>Until this day, to scour it in the [[Dust|dust]]. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Simonides|SIMONIDES]] | Opinion’s but a [[Fool|fool]], that makes us scan<br>The outward habit for the inward man. |
| *(didascalia)* | Cornetts |
| [[King|KING]] [[Simonides|SIMONIDES]] | But stay, the knights are coming. We will withdraw |
| *(didascalia)* | Into the gallery. Exeunt |
| *(didascalia)* | Cornetts and great shouts within, and all cry |
| [[King|KING]] [[Simonides|SIMONIDES]] | ‘The mean knight!’ |
