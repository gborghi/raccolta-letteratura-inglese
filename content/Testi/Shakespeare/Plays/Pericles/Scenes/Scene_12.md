| *(didascalia)* | Enter Lord Cerimon with a poor man and a servant |
| CERIMON | Philemon, ho! |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Philemon |
| PHILEMON | Doth my lord call? |
| CERIMON | Get [[Fire|fire]] and meat for those poor men. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit Philemon |
| CERIMON | ‘T’as been a turbulent and stormy [[Night|night]]. |
| SERVANT | I have seen many, but such a [[Night|night]] as this<br>Till now I ne’er endured. |
| CERIMON | Your master will be dead ere you return.<br>There’s nothing can be ministered in [[Nature|nature]] |
| *(didascalia)* | That can recover him. To poor man Give this to th’ |
| CERIMON | pothecary<br>And tell me how it works. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt poor man and servant |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter two Gentlemen |
| FIRST GENTLEMAN | Good morrow. |
| SECOND GENTLEMAN | Good morrow to your lordship. |
| CERIMON | Gentlemen,<br>Why do you stir so early? |
| FIRST GENTLEMAN | Sir,<br>Our lodgings, standing bleak upon the sea,<br>Shook as the earth did quake.<br>The very principals did seem to rend<br>And all to topple. Pure surprise and [[Fear|fear]]<br>Made me to quit the [[house]]. |
| SECOND GENTLEMAN | That is the cause we trouble you so early;<br>’Tis not our husbandry. |
| CERIMON | O, you say [[Well|well]]. |
| FIRST GENTLEMAN | But I much marvel that your lordship should,<br>Having rich tire about you, at this hour<br>Shake off the golden slumber of repose. ’Tis most<br>strange,<br>[[Nature]] to be so conversant with [[Pain|pain]],<br>Being thereto not compelled. |
| CERIMON | I held it ever<br>Virtue and cunning were endowments greater<br>Than nobleness and riches. Careless heirs<br>May the two latter darken and dispend,<br>But [[Immortality|immortality]] attends the former,<br>Making a man a god. ‘Tis known I ever<br>Have studied physic, through which secret art,<br>By turning o’er authorities, I have,<br>Together with my practice, made familiar<br>To me and to my aid the blest infusions<br>That dwells in vegetives, in metals, stones,<br>And so can speak of the disturbances<br>That [[Nature|nature]] works, and of her cures, which doth<br>give me<br>A more content and cause of true delight<br>Than to be thirsty after tott’ring [[Honour|honour]],<br>Or tie my pleasure up in silken bags<br>To glad the [[Fool|fool]] and [[Death|death]]. |
| SECOND GENTLEMAN | Your [[Honour|honour]] has<br>Through Ephesus poured forth your [[Charity|charity]],<br>And hundreds call themselves your creatures who by<br>you<br>Have been restored. And not [[Alone|alone]] your knowledge,<br>Your personal [[Pain|pain]], but e’en your purse still open<br>Hath built Lord Cerimon such strong renown<br>As [[Time|time]] shall never— |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Philemon and one or two with a chest |
| *(didascalia)* | PHILEMON So, lift there. |
| *(didascalia)* | CERIMON What’s that? PHILEMON Sir, even now |
| SECOND GENTLEMAN | The sea tossed up upon our shore this chest.<br>’Tis off some wreck. |
| CERIMON | Set’t down. Let’s look upon’t. |
| SECOND GENTLEMAN | ’Tis like a coffin, sir. |
| CERIMON | Whate’er it be,<br>’Tis wondrous heavy.—Did the sea cast it up? |
| *(didascalia)* | PHILEMON |
| CERIMON | I never saw so huge a billow, sir,<br>Or a more eager. |
| CERIMON | Wrench it open straight.<br>The others start to work<br>If the sea’s stomach be o‘ercharged with gold<br>’Tis by a good constraint of queasy fortune<br>It belches upon us. |
| SECOND GENTLEMAN | ’Tis so, my lord. |
| CERIMON | How close ’tis caulked and bitumed! |
| *(didascalia)* | They force the lid |
| CERIMON | Soft, it smells<br>Most sweetly in my sense. |
| SECOND GENTLEMAN | A delicate odour. |
| CERIMON | As ever hit my nostril. So, up with it.<br>They take the lid off<br>O you most potent gods! What’s here—a corpse? |
| SECOND GENTLEMAN | Most strange. |
| CERIMON | Shrouded in cloth of state, and crowned,<br>Balmed and entreasured with full bags of spices.<br>A passport, too!<br>He takes a paper from the chest<br>[[Apollo]] perfect me i’th’ characters.<br>‘Here I give to understand,<br>If e’er this coffin drives a-land,<br>I, [[King]] [[Pericles]], have lost<br>This [[Queen|queen]] worth all our mundane cost.<br>Who finds her, give her burying;<br>She was the daughter of a [[King|king]].<br>Besides this treasure for a fee,<br>The gods requite his [[Charity|charity]].’<br>If thou liv’st, [[Pericles]], thou hast a heart<br>That even cracks for woe. This chanced tonight. |
| SECOND GENTLEMAN | Most likely, sir. |
| CERIMON | Nay, certainly tonight,<br>For look how fresh she looks. They were too rash<br>That threw her in the sea. Make a [[Fire|fire]] within. |
| *(didascalia)* | Fetch hither all my boxes in my closet. Exit Philemon |
| CERIMON | [[Death]] may usurp on [[Nature|nature]] many hours,<br>And yet the [[Fire|fire]] of life kindle again<br>The o’erpressed spirits. I have heard<br>Of an Egyptian nine hours dead<br>Who was by good appliances recovered. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Philemon with napkins and [[Fire|fire]] |
| CERIMON | [[Well]] said, [[Well|well]] said, the [[Fire|fire]] and cloths.<br><br>The still and woeful music that we have,<br>Cause it to sound, beseech you. |
| *(didascalia)* | Music |
| CERIMON | The vial once more.<br>How thou stirr‘st, thou block! The music there!<br>I pray you give her air. Gentlemen,<br>This [[Queen|queen]] will live. [[Nature]] awakes, a warmth<br>Breathes out of her. She hath not been entranced<br>Above five hours. See how she ’gins to blow<br>Into life’s flow’r again. |
| FIRST GENTLEMAN | The heavens<br>Through you increase our [[Wonder|wonder]], and set up<br>Your fame for ever. |
| CERIMON | She is alive. Behold,<br>Her eyelids, cases to those heav’nly jewels<br>Which [[Pericles]] hath lost,<br>Begin to part their fringes of bright gold.<br>The diamonds of a most praised [[Water|water]]<br>Doth appear to make the world twice rich.—Live,<br>And make us weep to hear your fate, fair creature,<br>Rare as you seem to be.<br>She moves |
| [[Thaisa|THAISA]] | O dear [[Diana]],<br>Where am I? Where’s my lord? What world is this? |
| SECOND GENTLEMAN | Is not this strange? |
| FIRST GENTLEMAN | Most rare. |
| CERIMON | Hush, gentle neighbours. Lend me your hands. To the next chamber bear her. Get linen. Now this matter must be looked to, For her relapse is mortal. Come, come, And Aesculapius guide us. They carry her away. Exeunt |
