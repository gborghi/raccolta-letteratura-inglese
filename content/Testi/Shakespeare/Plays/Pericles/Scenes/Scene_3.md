| Chi parla | Battuta |
|---|---|
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Thaliart |
| THALIART | So this is Tyre, and this the court. Here must I kill King Pericles, and if I do it and am caught I am like to be hanged abroad, but if I do it not, I am sure to be hanged at home. ‘Tis dangerous. Well, I perceive he was a wise fellow and had good discretion that, being bid to ask what he would of the King, desired he might know none of his secrets. Now do I see he had some reason for’t, for if a king bid a man be a villain, he’s bound by the indenture of his oath to be one. Hush, here comes the lords of Tyre. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Helicanus and Aeschines, with other lords |
| HELICANUS | You shall not need, my fellow peers of Tyre,<br>Further to question of your King’s departure.<br>His sealed commission left in trust with me<br>Does speak sufficiently he’s gone to travel. |
| THALIART | (aside) How? The King gone? |
| HELICANUS | If further yet you will be satisfied<br>Why, as it were unlicensed of your loves,<br>He would depart, I’ll give some light unto you.<br>Being at Antioch— |
| THALIART | (aside)<br>What from Antioch? |
| HELICANUS | Royal Antiochus, on what cause I know not,<br>Took some displeasure at him—at least he judged so—<br>And doubting lest that he had erred or sinned,<br>To show his sorrow he’d correct himself;<br>So puts himself unto the ship-man’s toil,<br>With whom each minute threatens life or death. |
| THALIART | (aside)<br>Well, I perceive I shall not be hanged now,<br>Although I would.<br>But since he’s gone, the King’s ears it must please<br>He scaped the land to perish on the seas.<br>I’ll present myself.—Peace to the lords of Tyre.<br>Lord Thaliart am I, of Antioch. |
| *(didascalia)* | HELICANUS |
| THALIART | Lord Thaliart of Antioch is welcome. |
| THALIART | From King Antiochus I come<br>With message unto princely Pericles,<br>But since my landing I have understood<br>Your lord’s betook himself to unknown travels.<br>Now my message must return from whence it came. |
| HELICANUS | We have no reason to enquire it,<br>Commended to our master, not to us.<br>Yet ere you shall depart, this we desire:<br>As friends to Antioch, we may feast in Tyre. Exeunt |
