| Chi parla | Battuta |
|---|---|
| FIRST GENTLEMAN | Did you ever hear the like? |
| SECOND GENTLEMAN | No, nor never shall do in such a place as this, she being once gone. |
| FIRST GENTLEMAN | But to have divinity preached there—did you ever dream of such a thing? |
| SECOND GENTLEMAN | No, no. Come, I am for no more bawdy houses. Shall ’s go hear the vestals sing? |
| FIRST GENTLEMAN | I’ll do anything now that is virtuous, but I am out of the [[Road|road]] of rutting for ever. Exeunt |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Pander, Bawd, and Boult |
| PANDER | [[Well]], I had rather than twice the worth of her she had ne’er come here. |
| BAWD | Fie, fie upon her, she’s able to freeze the god Priapus and undo the whole of generation. We must either get her ravished or be rid of her. When she should do for clients her fitment and do me the kindness of our profession, she has me her quirks, her reasons, her master reasons, her prayers, her knees, that she would make a puritan of the devil if he should cheapen a kiss of her. |
| BOULT | [[Faith]], I must ravish her, or she’ll disfurnish us of all our cavalleria and make our swearers priests. |
| PANDER | Now, the pox upon her green-sickness for me. BAWD [[Faith]], there’s no way to be rid on’t but by the way to the pox. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Lysimachus, disguised |
| PANDER | Here comes the Lord Lysimachus, disguised. |
| BOULT | We should have both lord and loon if the peevish baggage would but give way to custom. |
| LYSIMACHUS | How now, how a dozen of virginities? BAWD Now, the gods to-bless your [[Honour|honour]]! |
| BOULT | I am glad to see your [[Honour|honour]] in good health. |
| LYSIMACHUS | You may so. ’Tis the better for you that your resorters stand upon sound legs. How now, wholesome iniquity have you, that a man may deal withal and defy the surgeon? |
| BAWD | We have here one, sir, if she woutd—but there never came her like in Mytilene. |
| LYSIMACHUS | If she’d do the deed of darkness, thou wouldst say. |
| *(didascalia)* | BAWD Your [[Honour|honour]] knows what ’tis to say [[Well|well]] enough. LYSIMACHUS [[Well]], call forth, call forth. Exit Pander BOULT For flesh and [[Blood|blood]], sir, [[White and Red|white and red]], you shall see a [[Rose|rose]]. And she were a [[Rose|rose]] indeed, if she had but— |
| LYSIMACHUS | What, prithee? |
| BOULT | O sir, I can be modest. |
| LYSIMACHUS | That dignifies the renown of a bawd no less than it gives a good report to a noble to be chaste. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Pander with [[Marina]] |
| BAWD | Here comes that which grows to the stalk, never plucked yet, I can assure you. Is she not a fair creature? |
| LYSIMACHUS | [[Faith]], she would serve after a long voyage at sea. [[Well]], there’s for you. Leave us. |
| *(didascalia)* | He pays the Bawd |
| BAWD | I beseech your [[Honour|honour]] give me leave: a word, and I’ll have done presently. |
| LYSIMACHUS | I beseech you, do. |
| BAWD | (aside to [[Marina]]) First, I would have you note this is an honourable man. |
| [[Marina|MARINA]] | I [[Desire|desire]] to find him so, that I may honourably know him. |
| BAWD | Next, he’s the governor of this country, and a man whom I am bound to. |
| [[Marina|MARINA]] | If he govern the country you are bound to him indeed, but how honourable he is in that, I know not. |
| BAWD | Pray you, without any more virginal fencing, will you use him kindly? He will line your apron with gold. |
| [[Marina|MARINA]] | What he will do graciously I will thankfully receive. LYSIMACHUS (to Bawd) Ha’ you done? |
| BAWD | My lord, she’s not paced yet. You must take some pains to work her to your manège. (To Boult and Pander) Come, we will leave his [[Honour|honour]] and hers together. Go thy ways. Exeunt Pander, Bawd, and Boult |
| LYSIMACHUS | Fair one, how long have you been at this trade? [[Marina|MARINA]] What trade, sir? |
| LYSIMACHUS | I cannot name it but I shall offend. |
| [[Marina|MARINA]] | I cannot be offended with my trade.<br>Please you to name it. |
| LYSIMACHUS | How long have you been<br>Of this profession? |
| [[Marina|MARINA]] | E’er since I can remember. |
| LYSIMACHUS | Did you go to’t so young? Were you a gamester<br>At five, or seven? |
| [[Marina|MARINA]] | Earlier too, sir,<br>If now I be one. |
| LYSIMACHUS | Why, the [[house]] you dwell in<br>Proclaimeth you a creature of sale. |
| [[Marina|MARINA]] | And do you know this [[house]] to be a place<br>Of such resort and will come into it?<br>I hear say you’re of honourable [[Blood|blood]],<br>And are the governor of this whole province. |
| LYSIMACHUS | What, hath your principal informed you who I am? |
| [[Marina|MARINA]] | Who is my principal? |
| LYSIMACHUS | Why, your herb-woman;<br>She that sets seeds of shame, roots of iniquity. |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Marina]] weeps |
| LYSIMACHUS | O, you’ve heard something of my pow‘r, and so<br>Stand off aloof for a more serious wooing.<br>But I protest to thee,<br>Pretty one, my authority can wink<br>At blemishes, or can on faults look friendly;<br>Or my displeasure punish at my pleasure,<br>From which displeasure, not thy [[Beauty|beauty]] shall<br>Privilege thee, nor my affection, which<br>Hath drawn me here, abate with further ling’ring.<br>Come bring me to some private place. Come, come. |
| [[Marina|MARINA]] | Let not authority, which teaches you<br>To govern others, be the means to make you<br>Misgovern much yourself.<br>If you were born to [[Honour|honour]], show it now;<br>If put upon you, make the judgement good<br>That thought you worthy of it. What reason’s in<br>Your justice, who hath power over all,<br>To undo any? If you take from me<br>Mine [[Honour|honour]], you’re like him that makes a gap<br>Into forbidden ground, whom after<br>Too many enter, and of all their evils<br>Yourself are guilty. My life is yet unspotted;<br>My chastity unstained ev’n in thought.<br>Then if your violence deface this building,<br>The workmanship of heav‘n, you do kill your [[Honour|honour]],<br>Abuse your justice, and impoverish me.<br>My yet good lord, if there be [[Fire|fire]] before me,<br>Must I straight fly and burn myself? Suppose this<br>[[house]]—<br>Which too too many feel such houses are—<br>Should be the doctor’s patrimony, and<br>The surgeon’s feeding; follows it, that I<br>Must needs infect myself to give them maint’nance? |
| LYSIMACHUS | How’s this, how’s this? Some more. Be sage. |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Marina|MARINA]] kneeling For me |
| LYSIMACHUS | That am a maid, though most ungentle fortune<br>Have franked me in this sty, where since I came<br>Diseases have been sold dearer than physic—<br>That the gods would set me free from this unhallowed<br>place,<br>Though they did change me to the meanest bird<br>That flies i’th’ purer air! |
| *(didascalia)* | LYSIMACHUS moved I did not think |
| LYSIMACHUS | Thou couldst have spoke so [[Well|well]], ne’er dreamt thou<br>couldst. |
| *(didascalia)* | He lifts her up with his hands |
| LYSIMACHUS | Though I brought hither a corrupted mind,<br>Thy speech hath altered it, |
| *(didascalia)* | He wipes the wet from her eyes and my foul thoughts |
| LYSIMACHUS | Thy [[Tears|tears]] so [[Well|well]] hath laved that they’re now white.<br>I came here meaning but to pay the price,<br>A piece of gold for thy virginity;<br>Here’s twenty to relieve thine honesty.<br>Persever still in that clear way thou goest,<br>And the gods strengthen thee. |
| [[Marina|MARINA]] | The good gods preserve you! |
| LYSIMACHUS | The very doors and windows savour vilely.<br>Fare thee [[Well|well]]. Thou art a piece of virtue,<br>The best wrought up that ever [[Nature|nature]] made,<br>And I [[Doubt|doubt]] not thy training hath been noble.<br>A curse upon him, die he like a thief,<br>That robs thee of thy [[Honour|honour]]. Hold, here’s more gold.<br>If thou dost hear from me, it shall be for thy good. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Boult standing ready at the door, making his obeisance unto him as Lysimachus should go out |
| BOULT | I beseech your [[Honour|honour]], one piece for me. |
| LYSIMACHUS | Avaunt, thou damned door-keeper!<br>Your [[house]], but for this virgin that doth prop it,<br>Would sink and overwhelm you. Away. Exit |
| BOULT | How’s this? We must take another course with you. If your peevish chastity, which is not worth a breakfast in the cheapest country under the cope, shall undo a whole household, let me be gelded like a spaniel. Come your ways. |
| [[Marina|MARINA]] | Whither would you have me? |
| BOULT | I must have your maidenhead taken off, or the common executioner shall do it. We’ll have no more gentlemen driven away. Come your ways, I say. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Bawd and Pander |
| BAWD | How now, what’s the matter? |
| BOULT | Worse and worse, mistress, she has here spoken holy words to the Lord Lysimachus. |
| BAWD | O, abominable! |
| BOULT | She makes our profession as it were to stink afore the face of the gods. |
| BAWD | Marry hang her up for ever! |
| BOULT | The nobleman would have dealt with her like a nobleman, and she sent him away as cold as a snowball, saying his prayers, too. |
| *(didascalia)* | PANDER Boult, take her away. Use her at thy pleasure. Crack the ice of her virginity, and make the rest malleable. |
| BOULT | An if she were a thornier piece of ground than she is, she shall be ploughed. |
| [[Marina|MARINA]] | Hark, hark, you gods! |
| BAWD | She conjures. Away with her! Would she had never come within my doors.—Marry, hang you!—She’s born to undo us.—Will you not go the way of womenkind? Marry, come up, my dish of chastity with rosemary and bays. Exeunt Bawd and Pander |
| *(didascalia)* | BOULT catching her rashly by the hand Come, mistress, come your way with me. |
| [[Marina|MARINA]] | Whither wilt thou have me? |
| BOULT | To take from you the [[Jewel|jewel]] you hold so dear. |
| [[Marina|MARINA]] | Prithee, tell me one thing first. |
| BOULT | Come, now, your one thing. |
| [[Marina|MARINA]] | What canst thou wish thine enemy to be? |
| BOULT | Why, I could wish him to be my master, or rather my mistress. |
| [[Marina|MARINA]] | Neither of these can be so bad as thou art,<br>Since they do better thee in their command.<br>Thou hold‘st a place the painèd’st fiend of hell<br>Would not in reputation change with thee,<br>Thou damned doorkeeper to ev‘ry coistrel<br>That comes enquiring for his Tib.<br>To th’ choleric fisting of ev’ry rogue<br>Thy ear is liable. Thy food is such<br>As hath been belched on by infected lungs. |
| BOULT | What would you have me do? Go to the wars, would you, where a man may serve seven years for the loss of a leg, and have not money enough in the end to buy him a wooden one? |
| [[Marina|MARINA]] | Do anything but this thou dost. Empty<br>Old receptacles or common sew’rs of filth,<br>Serve by indenture to the public hangman—<br>Any of these are yet better than this.<br>For what thou professest a baboon, could he speak,<br>Would own a name too dear. Here’s gold for thee.<br>If that thy master would make gain by me,<br>Proclaim that I can sing, weave, sew, and [[Dance|dance]],<br>With other virtues which I’ll keep from boast,<br>And I will undertake all these to teach.<br>I [[Doubt|doubt]] not but this populous [[City|city]] will<br>Yield many scholars. |
| BOULT | But can you teach all this you speak of? |
| [[Marina|MARINA]] | Prove that I cannot, take me [[Home|home]] again<br>And prostitute me to the basest groom<br>That doth frequent your [[house]]. |
| BOULT | [[Well]], I will see what I can do for thee. If I can place thee, I will. |
| [[Marina|MARINA]] | But amongst honest women. |
| BOULT | [[Faith]], my acquaintance lies little amongst them; but since my master and mistress hath bought you, there’s no going but by their consent. Therefore I will make them acquainted with your purpose, and I [[Doubt|doubt]] not but I shall find them tractable enough. Come, I’ll do for thee what I can. Come your ways. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt. The sign is removed |
