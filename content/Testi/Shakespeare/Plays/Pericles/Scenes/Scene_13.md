| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Pericles]] at Tarsus, with [[Cleon]] and [[Dionyza]], and Lychorida with a babe |
| [[Pericles|PERICLES]] | Most honoured [[Cleon]], I must needs be gone.<br>My twelve months are expired, and Tyrus stands<br>In a litigious peace. You and your lady<br>Take from my heart all thankfulness. The gods<br>Make up the rest upon you! |
| [[Cleon|CLEON]] | Your strokes of fortune, Though they hurt you mortally, yet glance Full woundingly on us. |
| [[Dionyza|DIONYZA]] | O your sweet [[Queen|queen]]!<br>That the strict fates had pleased you’d brought her<br>hither<br>T’have blessed mine eyes with her! |
| [[Pericles|PERICLES]] | We cannot but obey<br>The pow‘rs above us. Should I rage and roar<br>As doth the sea she lies in, yet the end<br>Must be as ’tis. My gentle babe [[Marina]],<br>Whom for she was born at sea I have named so,<br>Here I charge your [[Charity|charity]] withal, and leave her<br>The infant of your care, beseeching you<br>To give her princely training, that she may be<br>Mannered as she is born. |
| [[Cleon|CLEON]] | [[Fear]] not, my lord, but think<br>Your grace, that fed my country with your corn—<br>For which the people’s pray’rs still fall upon you—<br>Must in your [[Child|child]] be thought on. If neglection<br>Should therein make me vile, the common [[Body|body]]<br>By you relieved would force me to my duty.<br>But if to that my [[Nature|nature]] need a spur,<br>The gods [[Revenge|revenge]] it upon me and mine<br>To th’ end of generation. |
| [[Pericles|PERICLES]] | I believe you.<br>Your [[Honour|honour]] and your goodness teach me to’t<br>Without your vows.—Till she be married, madam,<br>By bright [[Diana]], whom we [[Honour|honour]] all,<br>Unscissored shall this hair of mine remain,<br>Though I show ill in’t. So I take my leave.<br>Good madam, make me blessed in your care<br>In bringing up my [[Child|child]]. |
| [[Dionyza|DIONYZA]] | I have one myself,<br>Who shall not be more dear to my respect<br>Than yours, my lord. |
| [[Pericles|PERICLES]] | Madam, my thanks and prayers. |
| [[Cleon|CLEON]] | We’ll bring your grace e‘en to the edge o’th’ shore,<br>Then give you up to th’ masted Neptune and<br>The gentlest winds of heaven. |
| [[Pericles|PERICLES]] | I will embrace your offer.—Come, dear’st madam.—<br>O, no [[Tears|tears]], Lychorida, no [[Tears|tears]].<br>Look to your little mistress, on whose grace<br>You may depend hereafter.—Come, my lord. Exeunt |
