| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Lincoln]], [George] Betts, [Clown Betts,] Williamson, Sherwin, and other, armed; Doll in a shirt of mail, a headpiece, [[Sword|sword]] and buckler; a crew attending |
| *(didascalia)* | [Original Text (Munday)]<br>[Addition Il (Heywood)] |
| CLOWN BETTS | Come, come, we’ll tickle their turnips, we’ll butter their boxes! Shall strangers rule the roast? Yes, but we’ll baste the roast. Come, come, aflaunt, aflaunt! |
| GEORGE BETTS | Brother, give place, and hear John [[Lincoln]] speak. |
| CLOWN BETTS | Ay, [[Lincoln]], my leader,<br>And Doll, my true breeder,<br>With the rest of our crew<br>Shall ran-tan-tarra-ran.<br>Do all they what they can,<br>Shall we be bobbed, braved?—No!<br>Shall we be held under?—No)<br>We are free born<br>And do take scorn<br>To be used so. |
| DOLL | Peace there, I say! Hear Captain [[Lincoln]] speak.<br>Keep [[Silence|silence]] till we know his mind at large. |
| CLOWN BETTS | [to [[Lincoln]]] Then largely deliver. Speak, bully, and he that presumes to interrupt thee in thy oration, this for him! |
| [[Lincoln|LINCOLN]] | Then gallant bloods, you whose free souls do scorn<br>To bear the enforced wrongs of aliens,<br>Add rage to resolution. [[Fire]] the houses<br>Of these audacious strangers. This is St Martin’s,<br>And yonder dwells Meautis, a wealthy Picardy,<br>At the Green Gate;<br>De Barde, Peter van Hollak, Adrian Martin,<br>With many more outlandish fugitives.<br>Shall these enjoy more privilege than we<br>In our own country? Let’s then become their slaves.<br>Since justice keeps not them in greater awe,<br>We’ll be ourselves rough ministers at law. |
| CLOWN BETTS | Use no more swords,<br>Nor no more words,<br>But [[Fire|fire]] the houses,<br>Brave Captain Courageous,<br>[[Fire]] me their houses. |
| DOLL | Ay, for we may as [[Well|well]] make bonfires on May Day as at Midsummer. We’ll alter the day in the calendar, and set it down in flaming letters. |
| SHERWIN | Stay, no, that would much endanger the whole [[City|city]],<br>Whereto I would not the least prejudice. |
| DOLL | No, nor I neither: so may mine own [[house]] be burned for company. I’ll tell ye what: we’ll drag the strangers into Moorfields, and there bumbaste them till they stink again. |
| CLOWN BETTS | And that’s soon done, for they smell for [[Fear|fear]] already. |
| GEORGE BETTS | Let some of us enter the strangers’ houses,<br>And, if we find them there, then bring them forth. |
| DOLL | But if ye bring them forth ere ye find them, I’ll ne’er allow of that. so |
| CLOWN BETTS | Now Mars for thy [[Honour|honour]],<br>Dutch or French,<br>So it be a wench,<br>I’ll upon her.<br>[Exeunt Sherwin, Clown Betts, and others] |
| [[Lincoln|LINCOLN]] WILLIAMSON | Now, lads, how shall we labour in our safety?<br>I hear the Mayor hath gathered men in arms,<br>And that Sheriff More an hour ago received<br>Some of the Privy Council in at Ludgate.<br>Force now must make our peace, or else we fall.<br>’Twill soon be known we are the principal. |
| DOLL | And what of that? If thou beest afraid, husband, go [[Home|home]] again and hide thy head, for, by the Lord, I’ll have a little sport now we are at it. |
| GEORGE BETTS | Let’s stand upon our swords, and if they come<br>Receive them as they were our enemies. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Sherwin, [Clown Betts,] and the rest |
| CLOWN BETTS | A purchase, a purchase! We have found, we ha’ found— |
| DOLL | What? |
| CLOWN BETTS | Nothing. Not a French Fleming nor a Fleming French to be found, but all fled, in plain English. |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Lincoln|LINCOLN]] to Sherwin |
| CLOWN BETTS | How now, have you found any? |
| SHERWIN | No, not one, they’re all fled. |
| [[Lincoln|LINCOLN]] | Then [[Fire|fire]] the houses, that, the Mayor being busy<br>About the quenching of them, we may scape.<br>Burn down their kennels! Let us straight away,<br>Lest this day prove to us an ill May Day. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt all but Clown |
| CLOWN BETTS | [[Fire]], [[Fire|fire]]! I’ll be the first.<br>If hanging come, ’tis welcome; that’s the worst. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit |
