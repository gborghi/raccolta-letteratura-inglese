| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Benedick]] and Margaret |
| [[Benedick|BENEDICK]] | Pray thee, sweet Mistress Margaret, deserve [[Well|well]] at my hands by helping me to the speech of [[Beatrice]]. |
| MARGARET | Will you then write me a [[Sonnet|sonnet]] in praise of my [[Beauty|beauty]]? |
| [[Benedick|BENEDICK]] | In so high a style, Margaret, that no man living shall come over it, for in most comely [[Truth|truth]], thou deservest it. |
| MARGARET | To have no man come over me—why, shall I always keep below stairs? |
| [[Benedick|BENEDICK]] | Thy wit is as quick as the greyhound’s mouth, it catches. |
| MARGARET | And yours as blunt as the fencer’s foils, which hit but hurt not. |
| [[Benedick|BENEDICK]] | A most manly wit, Margaret, it will not hurt a woman. And so I pray thee call [[Beatrice]]. I give thee the bucklers. |
| MARGARET | Give us the swords. We have bucklers of our own. |
| [[Benedick|BENEDICK]] | If you use them, Margaret, you must put in the pikes with a vice—and they are dangerous weapons for maids. |
| MARGARET | [[Well]], I will call [[Beatrice]] to you, who I think hath legs. Exit |
| [[Benedick|BENEDICK]] | And therefore will come.<br>(Sings)<br>The god of [[Love|love]]<br>That sits above,<br>And knows me, and knows me,<br>How pitiful I deserve—<br>I mean in singing; but in loving, Leander the good<br>swimmer, [[Troilus]] the first employer of panders, and a<br>whole book full of these quondam carpet-mongers<br>whose names yet run smoothly in the even [[Road|road]] of a<br>[[Blank Verse|blank verse]], why they were never so truly turned over<br>and over as my poor [[Self|self]] in [[Love|love]]. Marry, I cannot show<br>it in rhyme. I have tried. I can find out no rhyme to<br>‘lady’ but ‘baby‘, an [[Innocent|innocent]] rhyme; for ‘scorn’ ‘horn’,<br>a hard rhyme; for ‘school’ ‘[[Fool|fool]]’, a babbling rhyme.<br>Very ominous endings. No, I was not born under a<br>rhyming planet, nor I cannot woo in festival terms. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Beatrice]] |
| [[Benedick|BENEDICK]] | Sweet [[Beatrice]], wouldst thou come when I called thee? |
| [[Beatrice|BEATRICE]] | Yea, signor, and depart when you bid me. |
| [[Benedick|BENEDICK]] | O, stay but till then. |
| [[Beatrice|BEATRICE]] | ‘Then’ is spoken. Fare you [[Well|well]] now. And yet ere I go, let me go with that I came for, which is with knowing what hath passed between you and [[Claudio]]. |
| [[Benedick|BENEDICK]] | Only foul words, and thereupon I will kiss thee. |
| [[Beatrice|BEATRICE]] | Foul words is but foul [[Wind|wind]], and foul [[Wind|wind]] is but foul breath, and foul breath is noisome, therefore I will depart unkissed. |
| [[Benedick|BENEDICK]] | Thou hast frighted the word out of his right sense, so forcible is thy wit. But I must tell thee plainly, [[Claudio]] undergoes my challenge, and either I must shortly hear from him or I will subscribe him a coward. And I pray thee now tell me, for which of my bad parts didst thou first fall in [[Love|love]] with me? |
| [[Beatrice|BEATRICE]] | For them all together, which maintain so politic a state of evil that they will not admit any good part to intermingle with them. But for which of my good parts did you first suffer [[Love|love]] for me? |
| [[Benedick|BENEDICK]] | Suffer [[Love|love]]—a good epithet. I do suffer [[Love|love]] indeed, for I [[Love|love]] thee against my will. |
| [[Beatrice|BEATRICE]] | In spite of your heart, I think. Alas, poor heart. If you spite it for my sake I will spite it for yours, for I will never [[Love|love]] that which my friend hates. |
| [[Benedick|BENEDICK]] | Thou and I are too wise to woo peaceably. |
| [[Beatrice|BEATRICE]] | It appears not in this confession. There’s not one wise man among twenty that will praise himself. |
| [[Benedick|BENEDICK]] | An old, an old instance, [[Beatrice]], that lived in the [[Time|time]] of good neighbours. If a man do not erect in this age his own tomb ere he dies, he shall live no longer in monument than the bell rings and the widow weeps. |
| [[Beatrice|BEATRICE]] | And how long is that, think you? |
| [[Benedick|BENEDICK]] | Question—why, an hour in clamour and a quarter in rheum. Therefore is it most expedient for the wise, if Don Worm—his [[Conscience|conscience]]—find no impediment to the contrary, to be the trumpet of his own virtues, as I am to myself. So much for praising myself who, I myself will bear witness, is praiseworthy. And now tell me, how doth your cousin? |
| [[Beatrice|BEATRICE]] | Very ill. |
| [[Benedick|BENEDICK]] | And how do you? |
| [[Beatrice|BEATRICE]] | Very ill too. |
| [[Benedick|BENEDICK]] | Serve God, [[Love|love]] me, and mend. There will I leave you too, for here comes one in haste. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Ursula |
| URSULA | Madam, you must come to your uncle. Yonder’s old coil at [[Home|home]]. It is proved my lady [[Hero]] hath been falsely accused, the Prince and [[Claudio]] mightily abused, and [[Don John]] is the author of all, who is fled and gone. Will you come presently? |
| [[Beatrice|BEATRICE]] | Will you go hear this news, signor? |
| [[Benedick|BENEDICK]] | I will live in thy heart, die in thy lap, and be buried in thy eyes. And moreover, I will go with thee to thy uncle’s. Exeunt |
