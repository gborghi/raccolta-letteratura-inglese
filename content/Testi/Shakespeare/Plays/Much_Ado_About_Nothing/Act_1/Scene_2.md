| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Leonato]] and [[Antonio]], an old man brother to [[Leonato]], severally |
| [[Leonato|LEONATO]] | How now, brother, where is my cousin, your son? Hath he provided this music?<br>[[Antonio|ANTONIO]], He is very busy about it. But brother, I can tell you strange news that you yet dreamt not of. |
| [[Leonato|LEONATO]] | Are they good?<br>[[Antonio|ANTONIO]], As the event stamps them. But they have a good cover, they show [[Well|well]] outward. The Prince and Count [[Claudio]], walking in a thick-pleached alley in mine orchard, were thus much overheard by a man of mine: the Prince discovered to [[Claudio]] that he loved my niece, your daughter, and meant to acknowledge it this [[Night|night]] in a [[Dance|dance]], and if he found her accordant he meant to take the present [[Time|time]] by the top and instantly break with you of it. |
| [[Leonato|LEONATO]] | Hath the fellow any wit that told you this? |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | A good sharp fellow. I will send for him, and question him yourself. |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Leonato|LEONATO]] No, no. We will hold it as [[A Dream|a dream]] till it appear itself. But I will acquaint my daughter withal, that she may be the better prepared for an answer if peradventure this be true. Go you and tell her of it. Enter attendants Cousins, you know what you have to do. O, I cry you [[Mercy|mercy]], friend. Go you with me and I will use your skill.—Good cousin, have a care this busy [[Time|time]]. Exeunt |
