| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Dogberry]] and Verges the constables, and the Sexton, in gowns, and the Watch, with Conrad and Borachio |
| [[Dogberry|DOGBERRY]] | Is our whole dissembly appeared? |
| VERGES | O, a stool and a cushion for the Sexton. |
| *(didascalia)* | SEXTON sits Which be the malefactors? |
| [[Dogberry|DOGBERRY]] | Marry, that am I, and my partner. |
| VERGES | Nay, that’s certain, we have the exhibition to examine. |
| SEXTON | But which are the offenders that are to be examined? Let them come before Master Constable. |
| [[Dogberry|DOGBERRY]] | Yea, marry, let them come before me. What is your name, friend? |
| BORACHIO | Borachio. |
| [[Dogberry|DOGBERRY]] | (to the Sexton) Pray write down ‘Borachio’. (To Conrad) Yours, sirrah? |
| CONRAD | I am a gentleman, sir, and my name is Conrad. |
| [[Dogberry|DOGBERRY]] | Write down ‘Master Gentleman Conrad’.—Masters, do you serve God? |
| CONRAD | and BORACHIO Yea, sir, we [[Hope|hope]]. |
| [[Dogberry|DOGBERRY]] | Write down that they [[Hope|hope]] they serve God. And write ‘God’ first, for God defend but God should go before such villains. Masters, it is proved already that you are little better than false knaves, and it will go near to be thought so shortly. How answer you for yourselves? |
| CONRAD | Marry, sir, we say we are none. |
| [[Dogberry|DOGBERRY]] | A marvellous witty fellow, I assure you, but I will go about with him. Come you hither, sirrah. A word in your ear, sir. I say to you it is thought you are false knaves. |
| BORACHIO | Sir, I say to you we are none. |
| [[Dogberry|DOGBERRY]] | [[Well]], stand aside. Fore God, they are both in a tale. Have you writ down that they are none? |
| SEXTON | Master Constable, you go not the way to examine. You must call forth the watch that are their accusers. |
| [[Dogberry|DOGBERRY]] | Yea, marry, that’s the eftest way. Let the watch come forth. Masters, I charge you in the Prince’s name accuse these men. |
| FIRST WATCHMAN | This man said, sir, that [[Don John]], the Prince’s brother, was a [[Villain|villain]]. |
| [[Dogberry|DOGBERRY]] | Write down [[Prince John]] a [[Villain|villain]]. Why, this is flat perjury, to call a prince’s brother [[Villain|villain]]. |
| BORACHIO | Master Constable. |
| [[Dogberry|DOGBERRY]] | Pray thee, fellow, peace. I do not like thy look, I promise thee. |
| SEXTON | What heard you him say else? |
| SECOND WATCHMAN | Marry, that he had received a thousand ducats of [[Don John]] for accusing the Lady [[Hero]] wrongfully. |
| [[Dogberry|DOGBERRY]] | Flat burglary, as ever was committed. |
| VERGES | Yea, by mass, that it is. |
| SEXTON | What else, fellow? |
| FIRST WATCHMAN | And that Count [[Claudio]] did mean upon his words to disgrace [[Hero]] before the whole assembly, and not marry her. |
| [[Dogberry|DOGBERRY]] | O [[Villain|villain]]! Thou wilt be condemned into everlasting redemption for this. |
| SEXTON | What else? |
| WATCH | This is all. |
| SEXTON | And this is more, masters, than you can deny.<br>[[Prince John]] is this morning secretly stolen away. [[Hero]]<br>was in this manner accused, in this very manner<br>refused, and upon the grief of this suddenly died. Master<br>Constable, let these men be bound and brought to<br>[[Leonato]]’s. I will go before and show him their<br>examination. Exit |
| [[Dogberry|DOGBERRY]] | Come, let them be opinioned. |
| VERGES | Let them be, in the hands— |
| *(didascalia)* | CONRAD Off, coxcomb! |
| [[Dogberry|DOGBERRY]] | God’s my life, where’s the Sexton? Let him write down the Prince’s officer coxcomb. Come, bind them. Thou naughty varlet! |
| CONRAD | Away, you are an ass, you are an ass. |
| [[Dogberry|DOGBERRY]] | Dost thou not suspect my place? Dost thou not suspect my years? O that he were here to write me down an ass! But masters, remember that I am an ass. Though it be not written down, yet forget not that I am an ass. No, thou [[Villain|villain]], thou art full of piety, as shall be proved upon thee by good witness. I am a wise fellow, and which is more, an officer, and which is more, a householder, and which is more, as pretty a piece of flesh as any is in Messina, and one that knows the law, go to, and a rich fellow enough, go to, and a fellow that hath had losses, and one that hath two gowns, and everything handsome about him. Bring him away. O that I had been writ down an ass! Exeunt |
