|---|---|
| BUSHY | More welcome is the stroke of death to me Than Bolingbroke to England. |
| GREEN | My comfort is that heaven will take our souls, And plague injustice with the pains of hell. |
| BOLINGBROKE | My lord Northumberland, see them dispatched. Exit Northumberland, with Bushy and Green, guarded Uncle, you say the Queen is at your house. For God’s sake, fairly let her be intreated. Tell her I send to her my kind commends. Take special care my greetings be delivered. |
| YORK | A gentleman of mine I have dispatched With letters of your love to her at large. |
| BOLINGBROKE | Thanks, gentle uncle.—Come, lords, away, To fight with Glyndwr and his complices. A while to work, and after, holiday. Exeunt |
| *(didascalia)* | 3.2 Flourish. Enter King Richard, the Duke of Aumerle, the Bishop of Carlisle, and soldiers, with drum and colours |
| KING RICHARD | Harlechly Castle call they this at hand? |
| AUMERLE | Yea, my lord. How brooks your grace the air After your late tossing on the breaking seas? |
