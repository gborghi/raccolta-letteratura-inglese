| *(didascalia)* | Enter two Roman Senators, and Tribunes |
| FIRST SENATOR | This is the tenor of the Emperor’s writ:<br>That since the common men are now in action<br>‘Gainst the Pannonians and Dalmatians,<br>And that the legions now in Gallia are<br>Full weak to undertake our wars against<br>The fall’n-off Britons, that we do incite<br>The gentry to this business. He creates<br>[[Lucius]] pro-consul, and to you the tribunes,<br>For this immediate levy, he commends<br>His absolute commission. Long live Caesar!<br>A TRIBUNE<br>Is [[Lucius]] general of the forces? |
| SECOND SENATOR | Ay.<br>A TRIBUNE<br>Remaining now in Gallia? |
| FIRST SENATOR | With those legions<br>Which I have spoke of, whereunto your levy<br>Must be supplyant. The words of your commission<br>Will tie you to the numbers and the [[Time|time]]<br>Of their dispatch.<br>A TRIBUNE<br>We will discharge our duty. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt |
