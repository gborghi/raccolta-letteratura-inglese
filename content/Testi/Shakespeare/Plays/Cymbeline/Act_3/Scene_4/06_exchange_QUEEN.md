| [[Queen|QUEEN]] | Royal Sir, Since the [[Exile|exile]] of [[Posthumus]] most retired Hath her life been, the cure ,whereof, my lord, ’Tis [[Time|time]] must do. Beseech your majesty Forbear sharp speeches to her. She’s a lady So tender of rebukes that words are strokes, And strokes [[Death|death]] to her. Enter a Messenger |
| [[Cymbeline|CYMBELINE]] | Where is she, sir? How Can her contempt be answered? |
| MESSENGER | Please you, sir, Her chambers are all locked, and there’s no answer That will be given to th’ loud’st of noise we make. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] | My lord, when last I went to visit her She prayed me to excuse her keeping close, Whereto constrained by her infirmity, She should that duty leave unpaid to you Which daily she was bound to proffer. This She wished me to make known, but our great [[Court|court]] Made me to blame in [[Memory|memory]]. |
| [[Cymbeline|CYMBELINE]] | Her doors locked? Not seen of late? Grant heavens that which I [[Fear]] prove false. Exit |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] | on, I say, follow the [[King]]. |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | That man of hers, Pisanio, her old servant, I have not seen these two days. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] | Go, look after. Exit [[Cloten]] Pisanio, thou that stand’st so for [[Posthumus]]! He hath a drug of mine. I pray his absence Proceed by swallowing that, for he believes It is a thing most precious. But for her, Where is she gone? Haply [[Despair|despair]] hath seized her, Or, winged with fervour of her [[Love|love]], she’s flown To her desired [[Posthumus]]. Gone she is To [[Death|death]] or to dishonour, and my end Can make good use of either. She being down, I have the placing of the British [[Crown|crown]]. Enter [[Cloten]] How now, my son? |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | ’Tis certain she is fled. Go in and cheer the [[King]]. He rages, none Dare come about him. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] | All the better. May This [[Night|night]] forestall him of the coming day. Exit |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | I [[Love|love]] and hate her. For she’s fair and royal, And that she hath all courtly parts more exquisite Than lady, ladies, woman—from every one The best she hath, and she, of all compounded, Outsells them all—I [[Love|love]] her therefore; but Disdaining me, and throwing favours on The low [[Posthumus]], slanders so her judgement That what’s else rare is choked; and in that point I will conclude to hate her, nay, indeed, To be revenged upon her. For when fools Shall— Enter Pisanio Who is here? What, are you packing, sirrah? Come hither. Ah, you precious pander! [[Villain]], Where is thy lady? In a word, or else Thou art straightway with the fiends. |
| PISANIO | O good my lord! |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | Where is thy lady?—or, by Jupiter, I will not ask again. Close [[Villain|villain]], I’ll have this secret from thy tongue or rip Thy heart to find it. Is she with [[Posthumus]], From whose so many weights of baseness cannot A dram of worth be drawn? |
| PISANIO | Alas, my lord, How can she be with him? When was she missed? He is in Rome. |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | Where is she, sir? Come nearer. No farther halting. Satisfy me [[Home|home]] What is become of her. |
| PISANIO | O my all-worthy lord! |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | All-worthy [[Villain|villain]], Discover where thy mistress is at once, At the next word. No more of ‘worthy lord’. Speak, or thy [[Silence|silence]] on the instant is Thy condemnation and thy [[Death|death]]. |
