| Chi parla | Battuta |
|---|---|
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Cloten]] and the two Lords |
| FIRST LORD | Your lordship is the most patient man in loss, the most coldest that ever turned up ace. |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | It would make any man cold to lose. |
| FIRST LORD | But not every man patient after the noble temper of your lordship. You are most hot and furious when you win. |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | Winning will put any man into [[Courage|courage]]. If I could get this foolish [[Innogen]] I should have gold enough. It’s almost morning, is’t not? |
| FIRST LORD | Day, my lord. |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | I would this music would come. I am advised to give her music o’ mornings; they say it will penetrate. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Musicians |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | Come on, tune. If you can penetrate her with your fingering, so; we’ll try with tongue too. If none will do, let her remain; but I’ll never give o’er. First, a very excellent good-conceited thing; after, a wonderful sweet air with admirable rich words to it; and then let her consider. |
| *(didascalia)* | Music |
| *(didascalia)* | MUSICIAN (sings) |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | Hark, hark, the lark at heaven gate sings,<br>And Phoebus gins arise,<br>His steeds to [[Water|water]] at those springs<br>On chaticed [[Flowers|flowers]] that lies,<br>And winking Mary-buds begin to ope their golden eyes;<br>With everything that pretty is, my lady sweet, arise,<br>Arise, arise! |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | So, get you gone. If this penetrate I will consider your music the better; if it do not, it is a vice in her ears which [[Horse|horse]] hairs and calves’ guts nor the voice of unpaved eunuch to boot can never amend. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt Musicians |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Cymbeline]] and the [[Queen]] |
| SECOND LORD | Here comes the [[King]]. |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | I am glad I was up so late, for that’s the reason I was up so early. He cannot choose but take this service I have done fatherly. Good morrow to your majesty, and to my gracious mother. |
| [[Cymbeline|CYMBELINE]] | Attend you here the door of our stern daughter?<br>Will she not forth? |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | I have assailed her with musics, but she vouchsafes no notice. |
| [[Cymbeline|CYMBELINE]] | The [[Exile|exile]] of her minion is too new.<br>She hath not yet forgot him. Some more [[Time|time]]<br>Must wear the print of his remembrance out,<br>And then she’s yours. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] | (to [[Cloten]]) You are most bound to th’ [[King]],<br>Who lets go by no vantages that may<br>Prefer you to his daughter. Frame yourself<br>To orderly solicits, and be friended<br>With aptness of the season. Make denials<br>Increase your services; so seem as if<br>You were inspired to do those duties which<br>You tender to her; that you in all obey her,<br>Save when command to your dismission tends,<br>And therein you are senseless. |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | Senseless? Not so. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter a Messenger |
| MESSENGER | (to [[Cymbeline]])<br>So like you, sir, ambassadors from Rome;<br>The one is Caius [[Lucius]]. |
| [[Cymbeline|CYMBELINE]] | A worthy fellow,<br>Albeit he comes on angry purpose now:<br>But that’s no fault of his. We must receive him<br>According to the [[Honour|honour]] of his sender,<br>And towards himself, his goodness forespent on us,<br>We must extend our notice. Our dear son,<br>When you have given good morning to your mistress,<br>Attend the [[Queen]] and us. We shall have need<br>T’employ you towards this Roman. Come, our [[Queen|queen]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt all but |
| [[Cymbeline|CYMBELINE]] | [[Cloten]] |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | If she be up, I’ll speak with her; if not,<br>Let her lie still and dream. |
| *(didascalia)* | He knocks |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | By your leave, ho!—<br>I know her women are about her; what<br>If I do line one of their hands? ‘Tis gold<br>Which buys admittance—oft it doth—yea, and makes<br>[[Diana]]’s rangers false themselves, yield up<br>Their deer to th’ stand o’th’ stealer; and ’tis gold<br>Which makes the true man killed and saves the thief,<br>Nay, sometime hangs both thief and true man. What<br>Can it not do and undo? I will make<br>One of her women lawyer to me, for<br>I yet not understand the case myself.—<br>By your leave.<br>Knocks. Enter a Lady |
| LADY | Who’s there that knocks? |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | A gentleman. |
| LADY | No more? |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | Yes, and a gentlewoman’s son. |
| LADY | That’s more |
| *(didascalia)* | Aside Than some whose tailors are as dear as |
| LADY | yours<br>Can justly boast of. (To him) What’s your lordship’s<br>pleasure? |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | Your lady’s person. Is she ready? |
| LADY | Ay. |
| *(didascalia)* | Aside To keep her chamber. |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | There is gold for you.<br>Sell me your good report. |
| LADY | How, my good name?—or to report of you<br>What I shall think is good? |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter |
| LADY | [[Innogen]]<br>The Princess. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | Good morrow, fairest. Sister, your sweet hand. |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | Good morrow, sir. You lay out too much pains<br>For purchasing but trouble. The thanks I give<br>Is telling you that I am poor of thanks,<br>And scarce can spare them. |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | Still I swear I [[Love|love]] you. |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | If you but said so, ’twere as deep with me.<br>If you swear still, your recompense is still<br>That I regard it not. |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | This is no answer. |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | But that you shall not say I yield being silent,<br>I would not speak. I pray you, spare me. [[Faith]],<br>I shall unfold equal discourtesy<br>To your best kindness. One of your great knowing<br>Should learn, being taught, forbearance. |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | To leave you in your [[Madness|madness]], ’twere my sin.<br>I will not. |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | Fools cure not mad folks. |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | Do you call me [[Fool|fool]]? |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | As I am mad, I do.<br>If you’ll be patient, I’ll no more be mad;<br>That cures us both. I am much sorry, sir,<br>You put me to forget a lady’s manners<br>By being so verbal; and learn now for all<br>That I, which know my heart, do here pronounce<br>By th’ very [[Truth|truth]] of it: I care not for you,<br>And am so near the lack of [[Charity|charity]]<br>To accuse myself I hate you, which I had rather<br>You felt than make’t my boast. |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | You sin against<br>Obedience which you owe your father. For<br>The contract you pretend with that base wretch,<br>One bred of alms and fostered with cold dishes,<br>With scraps o‘th’ [[Court|court]], it is no contract, none.<br>And though it be allowed in meaner parties—<br>Yet who than he more mean?—to knit their souls,<br>On whom there is no more dependency<br>But brats and beggary, in [[Self|self]]-figured knot,<br>Yet you are curbed from that enlargement by<br>The consequence o’th’ [[Crown|crown]], and must not foil<br>The precious note of it with a base slave,<br>A hilding for a livery, a squire’s cloth,<br>A pantler—not so eminent. |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | Profane fellow,<br>Wert thou the son of Jupiter, and no more<br>But what thou art besides, thou wert too base<br>To be his groom; thou wert dignified enough,<br>Even to the point of envy, if ’twere made<br>Comparative for your virtues to be styled<br>The under-hangman of his kingdom, and hated<br>For being preferred so [[Well|well]]. |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | The south-fog rot him! |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | He never can meet more mischance than come<br>To be but named of thee. His meanest garment<br>That ever hath but clipped his [[Body|body]] is dearer<br>In my respect than all the hairs above thee,<br>Were they all made such men. How now, Pisanio! |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Pisanio |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | His garment? Now the devil— |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | (to Pisanio)<br>To Dorothy, my woman, hie thee presently. |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | His garment? |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | (to Pisanio) I am sprited with a [[Fool|fool]],<br>Frighted, and angered worse. Go bid my woman<br>Search for a [[Jewel|jewel]] that too casually<br>Hath left mine arm. It was thy master’s. ‘Shrew me<br>If I would lose it for a revenue<br>Of any [[King|king]]’s in Europe! I do think<br>I saw’t this morning; confident I am<br>Last [[Night|night]] ’twas on mine arm; I kissed it.<br>I [[Hope|hope]] it be not gone to tell my lord<br>That I kiss aught but he. |
| PISANIO | ’Twill not be lost. |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | I [[Hope|hope]] so. Go and search. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit Pisanio |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | You have abused me.<br>‘His meanest garment’? |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | Ay, I said so, sir.<br>If you will make’t an action, call witness to’t. |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | I will inform your father. |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | Your mother too.<br>She’s my good lady, and will conceive, I [[Hope|hope]],<br>But the worst of me. So I leave you, sir,<br>To th’ worst of discontent. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | I’ll be revenged.<br>‘His meanest garment’? [[Well]]! Exit |
