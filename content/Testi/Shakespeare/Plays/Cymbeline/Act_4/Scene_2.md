| [[Lucius|LUCIUS]] | When expect you them?<br>A ROMAN CAPTAIN<br>With the next benefit o’th’ [[Wind|wind]]. |
| [[Lucius|LUCIUS]] | This forwardness<br>Makes our hopes fair. Command our present numbers<br>Be mustered; bid the captains look to’t. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit one or more |
| [[Lucius|LUCIUS]] | (To Soothsayer) Now, sir,<br>What have you dreamed of late of this war’s purpose? |
| SOOTHSAYER | Last [[Night|night]] the very gods showed me a vision—<br>I fast, and prayed for their intelligence-thus:<br>I saw Jove’s bird, the Roman eagle, winged<br>From the spongy south to this part of the west,<br>There vanished in the sunbeams; which portends,<br>Unless my sins abuse my divination,<br>[[success|Success]] to th’ Roman host. |
| [[Lucius|LUCIUS]] | Dream often so,<br>And never false.<br>He sees [[Cloten]]’s [[Body|body]]<br>Soft, ho, what trunk is here<br>Without his top? The ruin speaks that sometime<br>It was a worthy building. How, a page?<br>Or dead or sleeping on him? But dead rather,<br>For [[Nature|nature]] doth abhor to make his bed<br>With the defunct, or [[Sleep|sleep]] upon the dead.<br>Let’s see the boy’s face.<br>A ROMAN CAPTAIN<br>He’s alive, my lord. |
| [[Lucius|LUCIUS]] | He’ll then instruct us of this [[Body|body]]. Young one,<br>Inform us of thy fortunes, for it seems<br>They crave to be demanded. Who is this<br>Thou mak’st thy bloody pillow? Or who was he<br>That, otherwise than noble [[Nature|nature]] did,<br>Hath altered that good picture? What’s thy interest<br>In this sad wreck? How came’t? Who is’t?<br>What art thou? |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | I am nothing; or if not,<br>Nothing to be were better. This was my master,<br>A very valiant Briton, and a good,<br>That here by mountaineers lies slain. Alas,<br>There is no more such masters. I may wander<br>From east to occident, cry out for service,<br>Try many, all good; serve truly, never<br>Find such another master. |
| [[Lucius|LUCIUS]] | ’Lack, good youth,<br>Thou mov’st no less with thy complaining than<br>Thy master in bleeding. Say his name, good friend. |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | [[Richard]] du Champ. (Aside) If I do lie and do<br>No harm by it, though the gods hear I [[Hope|hope]]<br>They’ll pardon it. (Aloud) Say you, sir? |
| [[Lucius|LUCIUS]] | Thy name? |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | Fidele, sir. |
| [[Lucius|LUCIUS]] | Thou dost approve thyself the very same.<br>Thy name [[Well|well]] fits thy [[Faith|faith]], thy [[Faith|faith]] thy name.<br>Wilt take thy chance with me? I will not say<br>Thou shalt be so [[Well|well]] mastered, but be sure,<br>No less beloved. The Roman Emperor’s letters<br>Sent by a consul to me should not sooner<br>Than thine own worth prefer thee. Go with me. |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | I’ll follow, sir. But first, an’t please the gods,<br>I’ll hide my master from the flies as deep<br>As these poor pickaxes can dig; and when<br>With wild-wood [[Leaves|leaves]] and weeds I ha’ strewed his<br>[[Grave|grave]]<br>And on it said a century of prayers,<br>Such as I can, twice o’er I’ll weep and sigh,<br>And leaving so his service, follow you,<br>So please you entertain me. |
| [[Lucius|LUCIUS]] | Ay, good youth,<br>And rather father thee than master thee. My friends,<br>The boy hath taught us manly duties. Let us<br>Find out the prettiest daisied plot we can,<br>And make him with our pikes and partisans<br>A [[Grave|grave]]. Come, arm him. Boy, he is preferred<br>By thee to us, and he shall be interred<br>As soldiers can. Be cheerful. Wipe thine eyes.<br>Some falls are means the happier to arise. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt with [[Cloten]]’s [[Body|body]] |
