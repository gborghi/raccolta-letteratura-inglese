| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Cymbeline]], Lords, and Pisanio |
| [[Cymbeline|CYMBELINE]] | Again, and bring me word how ’tis with her. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit one or more |
| [[Cymbeline|CYMBELINE]] | A fever with the absence of her son,<br>A [[Madness|madness]] of which her life’s in danger-heavens,<br>How deeply you at once do touch me! [[Innogen]],<br>The great part of my comfort, gone; my [[Queen|queen]]<br>Upon a desperate bed, and in a [[Time|time]]<br>When fearful wars point at me; her son gone,<br>So needful for this present! It strikes me past<br>The [[Hope|hope]] of comfort. (To Pisanio) But for thee, fellow,<br>Who needs must know of her departure and<br>Dost seem so ignorant, we’ll enforce it from thee<br>By a sharp torture. |
| PISANIO | Sir, my life is yours.<br>I humbly set it at your will. But for my mistress,<br>I nothing know where she remains, why gone,<br>Nor when she purposes return. Beseech your<br>highness,<br>Hold me your loyal servant.<br>A LORD<br>Good my liege,<br>The day that she was missing he was here.<br>I dare be bound he’s true, and shall perform<br>All parts of his subjection loyally. For [[Cloten]],<br>There wants no diligence in seeking him,<br>And will no [[Doubt|doubt]] be found. |
| [[Cymbeline|CYMBELINE]] | The [[Time|time]] is troublesome.<br>(To Pisanio) We’ll slip you for a season, but our [[Jealousy|jealousy]]<br>Does yet depend.<br>A LORD<br>So please your majesty,<br>The Roman legions, all from Gallia drawn,<br>Are landed on your coast with a supply<br>Of Roman gentlemen by the senate sent. |
| [[Cymbeline|CYMBELINE]] | Now for the counsel of my son and [[Queen|queen]]!<br>I am amazed with matter.<br>A LORD<br>Good my liege,<br>Your preparation can affront no less<br>Than what you hear of. Come more, for more you’re<br>ready.<br>The want is but to put those powers in motion<br>That long to move. |
| [[Cymbeline|CYMBELINE]] | I thank you. Let’s withdraw,<br>And meet the [[Time|time]] as it seeks us. We [[Fear|fear]] not<br>What can from [[Italy]] annoy us, but<br>We grieve at chances here. Away. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt [[Cymbeline]] and Lords |
| PISANIO | I heard no letter from my master since<br>I wrote him [[Innogen]] was slain. ‘Tis strange.<br>Nor hear I from my mistress, who did promise<br>To yield me often tidings. Neither know I<br>What is betid to [[Cloten]], but remain<br>Perplexed in all. The heavens still must work.<br>Wherein I am false I am honest; not true, to be true.<br>These present wars shall find I [[Love|love]] my country<br>Even to the note o’th’ [[King]], or I’ll fall in them.<br>All other doubts, by [[Time|time]] let them be cleared:<br>Fortune brings in some boats that are not steered. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit |
