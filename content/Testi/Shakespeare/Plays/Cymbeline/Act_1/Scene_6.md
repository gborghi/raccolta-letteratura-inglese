|---|---|
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Innogen]] |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | A father cruel and a stepdame false,<br>A foolish suitor to a wedded lady<br>That hath her husband banished. O, that husband,<br>My supreme [[Crown|crown]] of grief, and those repeated<br>Vexations of it! Had I been thief-stol‘n,<br>As my two brothers, happy; but most miserable<br>Is the [[Desire|desire]] that’s glorious. Blest be those,<br>How mean soe’er, that have their honest wills,<br>Which [[Seasons|seasons]] comfort. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Pisanio and Giacomo |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | Who may this be? Fie! |
| PISANIO | Madam, a noble gentleman of Rome<br>Comes from my lord with letters. |
| GIACOMO | Change you, madam?<br>The worthy Leonatus is in safety,<br>And greets your highness dearly.<br>He gives her the letters |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | Thanks, good sir.<br>You’re kindly welcome.<br>She reads the letters |
| GIACOMO | (aside)<br>All of her that is out of door most rich!<br>If she be furnished with a mind so rare<br>She is [[Alone|alone]], th’Arabian bird, and I<br>Have lost the wager. Boldness be my friend;<br>Arm me audacity from head to foot,<br>Or, like the Parthian, I shall flying fight;<br>Rather, directly fly. |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | (reads aloud) ’He is one of the noblest note, to whose kindnesses I am most infinitely tied. Reflect upon him accordingly, as you value<br>Your truest<br>Leonatus.’<br>(To Giacomo) So far I read aloud,<br>But even the very middle of my heart<br>Is warmed by th’ rest, and takes it thankfully.<br>You are as welcome, worthy sir, as I<br>Have words to bid you, and shall find it so<br>In all that I can do. |
| GIACOMO | Thanks, fairest lady.<br>What, are men mad? Hath [[Nature|nature]] given them eyes<br>To see this vaulted arch and the rich crop<br>Of sea and land, which can distinguish ‘twixt<br>The fiery orbs above and the twinned stones<br>Upon th’unnumbered beach, and can we not<br>Partition make with spectacles so precious<br>’Twixt fair and foul? |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | What makes your admiration? |
| GIACOMO | It cannot be i‘th’ eye—for apes and monkeys,<br>’Twixt two such shes, would chatter this way and<br>Contemn with mows the other; nor i‘th’ judgement,<br>For idiots in this case of favour would<br>Be wisely definite; nor i’th’ appetite—<br>Sluttery, to such neat excellence opposed,<br>Should make [[Desire|desire]] vomit emptiness,<br>Not so allured to feed. |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | What is the matter, trow? |
| GIACOMO | The cloyed will,<br>That satiate yet unsatisfied [[Desire|desire]], that tub<br>Both filled and running, ravening first the [[Lamb|lamb]],<br>Longs after for the garbage. |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | What, dear sir,<br>Thus raps you? Are you [[Well|well]]? |
| GIACOMO | Thanks, madam, [[Well|well]]. (To Pisanio) Beseech you, sir,<br>[[Desire]] my man’s abode where I did leave him.<br>He’s strange and peevish. |
| PISANIO | I was going, sir,<br>To give him welcome. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | Continues [[Well|well]] my lord?<br>His health, beseech you? |
| GIACOMO | [[Well]], madam. |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | Is he disposed to mirth? I [[Hope|hope]] he is. |
| GIACOMO | Exceeding pleasant, none a stranger there<br>So merry and so gamesome. He is called<br>The Briton Reveller. |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | When he was here<br>He did incline to sadness, and oft-times<br>Not knowing why. |
| GIACOMO | I never saw him sad.<br>There is a Frenchman his companion, one<br>An eminent monsieur that, it seems, much loves<br>A Gallian girl at [[Home|home]]. He furnaces<br>The thick sighs from him, whiles the jolly Briton—<br>Your lord, I mean—laughs from ’s free lungs,<br>cries ‘O,<br>Can my sides hold, to think that man, who knows<br>By [[history]], report or his own proof<br>What woman is, yea, what she cannot choose<br>But must be, will ’s free hours languish<br>For assured bondage?’ |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | Will my lord say so? |
| GIACOMO | Ay, madam, with his eyes in flood with laughter.<br>It is a recreation to be by<br>And hear him mock the Frenchman. But heavens<br>know<br>Some men are much to blame. |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | Not he, I [[Hope|hope]]. |
| GIACOMO | Not he; but yet heaven’s bounty towards him might<br>Be used more thankfully. In himself ’tis much;<br>In you, which I count his, beyond all talents.<br>Whilst I am bound to [[Wonder|wonder]], I am bound<br>To pity too. |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | What do you pity, sir? |
| GIACOMO | Two creatures heartily. |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | Am I one, sir?<br>You look on me; what wreck discern you in me<br>Deserves your pity? |
| GIACOMO | Lamentablel What,<br>To hide me from the radiant sun, and solace<br>I’th’ dungeon by a snuff? |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | I pray you, sir,<br>Deliver with more openness your answers<br>To my demands. Why do you pity me? |
| GIACOMO | That others do—<br>I was about to say enjoy your—but<br>It is an office of the gods to venge it,<br>Not mine to speak on’t. |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | You do seem to know<br>Something of me, or what concerns me. Pray you,<br>Since doubting things go ill often hurts more<br>Than to be sure they do—for certainties<br>Either are past remedies, or, timely knowing,<br>The remedy then born—discover to me<br>What both you spur and stop. |
| GIACOMO | Had I this cheek<br>To bathe my lips upon; this hand whose touch,<br>Whose every touch, would force the feeler’s [[Soul|soul]]<br>To th’oath of loyalty; this object which<br>Takes prisoner the wild motion of mine eye,<br>Firing it only here: should I, damned then,<br>Slaver with lips as common as the stairs<br>That mount the Capitol; join grips with hands<br>Made hard with hourly falsehood—faisehood as<br>With labour; then by-peeping in an eye<br>Base and illustrous as the smoky light<br>That’s fed with stinking tallow—it were fit<br>That all the plagues of hell should at one [[Time|time]]<br>Encounter such revolt. |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | My lord, I [[Fear|fear]],<br>Has forgot Britain. |
| GIACOMO | And himself. Not I<br>Inclined to this intelligence pronounce<br>The beggary of his change, but ’tis your graces<br>That from my mutest [[Conscience|conscience]] to my tongue<br>Charms this report out. |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | Let me hear no more. |
| GIACOMO | O dearest [[Soul|soul]], your cause doth strike my heart<br>With pity that doth make me sick. A lady<br>So fair, and fastened to an empery<br>Would make the great’st [[King|king]] [[Double|double]], to be partnered<br>With tomboys hired with that [[Self|self]] exhibition<br>Which your own coffers yield; with diseased ventures<br>That play with all infirmities for gold<br>Which rottenness can lend to [[Nature|nature]]; such boiled stuff<br>As [[Well|well]] might [[Poison|poison]] [[Poison|poison]]! Be revenged,<br>Or she that bore you was no [[Queen|queen]], and you<br>Recoil from your great stock. |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | Revenged?<br>How should I be revenged? If this be true—<br>As I have such a heart that both mine ears<br>Must not in haste abuse—if it be true,<br>How should I be revenged? |
| GIACOMO | Should he make me<br>Live like [[Diana]]’s priest betwixt cold sheets<br>Whiles he is vaulting variable ramps,<br>In your despite, upon your purse—[[Revenge|revenge]] it.<br>I dedicate myself to your sweet pleasure,<br>More noble than that runagate to your bed,<br>And will continue fast to your affection,<br>Still close as sure. |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | What ho, Pisanio! |
| GIACOMO | Let me my service tender on your lips. |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | Away, I do condemn mine ears that have<br>So long attended thee. If thou wert honourable<br>Thou wouldst have told this tale for virtue, not<br>For such an end thou seek‘st, as base as strange.<br>Thou wrong’st a gentleman who is as far<br>From thy report as thou from [[Honour|honour]], and<br>Solicit’st here a lady that disdains<br>Thee and the devil alike. What ho, Pisanio!<br>The [[King]] my father shall be made acquainted<br>Of thy assault. If he shall think it fit<br>A saucy stranger in his [[Court|court]] to mart<br>As in a Romish stew, and to expound<br>His beastly mind to us, he hath a [[Court|court]]<br>He little cares for, and a daughter who<br>He not respects at all. What ho, Pisanio! |
| GIACOMO | O happy Leonatus! I may say<br>The credit that thy lady hath of thee<br>Deserves thy trust, and thy most perfect goodness<br>Her assured credit. Blessed live you long,<br>A lady to the worthiest sir that ever<br>Country called his; and you his mistress, only<br>For the most worthiest fit. Give me your pardon.<br>I have spoke this to know if your affiance<br>Were deeply rooted, and shall make your lord<br>That which he is new o’er; and he is one<br>The truest mannered, such a holy [[Witch|witch]]<br>That he enchants societies into him;<br>Half all men’s hearts are his. |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | You make amends. |
| GIACOMO | He sits ’mongst men like a descended god.<br>He hath a kind of [[Honour|honour]] sets him off<br>More than a mortal seeming. Be not angry,<br>Most mighty princess, that I have adventured<br>To try your taking of a false report, which hath<br>Honoured with confirmation your great judgement<br>In the election of a sir so rare<br>Which you know cannot err. The [[Love|love]] I bear him<br>Made me to fan you thus, but the gods made you,<br>Unlike all others, chaffless. Pray, your pardon. |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | All’s [[Well|well]], sir. Take my power i’th’ [[Court|court]] for yours. |
| GIACOMO | My humble thanks. I had almost forgot<br>T’entreat your grace but in a small request,<br>And yet of moment too, for it concerns<br>Your lord; myself and other noble friends<br>Are partners in the business. |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | Pray what is’t? |
| GIACOMO | Some dozen Romans of us, and your lord—<br>Best feather of our wing—have mingled sums<br>To buy a present for the Emperor,<br>Which I, the factor for the rest, have done<br>In France. ’Tis plate of rare device, and jewels<br>Of rich and exquisite form; their value’s great,<br>And I am something curious, being strange,<br>To have them in safe stowage. May it please you<br>To take them in protection? |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | Willingly,<br>And pawn mine [[Honour|honour]] for their safety; since<br>My lord hath interest in them, I will keep them<br>In my bedchamber. |
| GIACOMO | They are in a trunk<br>Attended by my men. I will make bold<br>To send them to you, only for this [[Night|night]].<br>I must aboard tomorrow. |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | O, no, no! |
| GIACOMO | Yes, I beseech, or I shall short my word<br>By length’ning my return. From Gallia<br>I crossed the seas on purpose and on promise<br>To see your grace. |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | I thank you for your pains;<br>But not away tomorrow! |
| GIACOMO | O, I must, madam.<br>Therefore I shall beseech you, if you please<br>To greet your lord with writing, do’t tonight.<br>I have outstood my [[Time|time]], which is material<br>To th’ tender of our present. |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | I will write.<br>Send your trunk to me, it shall safe be kept,<br>And truly yielded you. You’re very welcome. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt severally |
