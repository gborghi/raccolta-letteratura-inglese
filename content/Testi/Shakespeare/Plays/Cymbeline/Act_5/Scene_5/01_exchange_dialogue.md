| *(didascalia)* | Enter Posthumus like a poor soldier, and a Briton Lord |
| LORD | Cam’st thou from where they made the stand? |
| POSTHUMUS | I did, Though you, it seems, come from the fliers. |
| LORD | Ay. |
| POSTHUMUS | No blame be to you, sir, for all was lost, But that the heavens fought. The King himself Of his wings destitute, the army broken, And but the backs of Britons seen, all flying Through a strait lane; the enemy full-hearted, Lolling the tongue with slaught‘ring, having work More plentiful than tools to do’t, struck down Some mortally, some slightly touched, some falling Merely through fear, that the strait pass was dammed With dead men hurt behind, and cowards living To die with lengthened shame. |
| LORD | Where was this lane? |
