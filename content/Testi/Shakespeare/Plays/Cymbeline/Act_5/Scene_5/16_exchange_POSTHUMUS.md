| [[Posthumus|POSTHUMUS]] | (coming forward) Ay, so thou dost, Italian fiend! Ay me, most credulous [[Fool|fool]], Egregious murderer, thief, anything That’s due to all the villains past, in being, To come! O, give me cord, or knife, or [[Poison|poison]], Some upright justicer! Thou, [[King]], send out For torturers ingenious. It is I That all th‘abhorrèd things o’th’ earth amend By being worse than they. I am [[Posthumus]], That killed thy daughter—[[Villain|villain]]-like, I lie: That caused a lesser [[Villain|villain]] than myself, A sacrilegious thief, to do’t. The temple Of virtue was she; yea, and she herself. Spit and throw stones, cast mire upon me, set The [[dogs]] o‘th’ street to bay me. Every [[Villain|villain]] Be called [[Posthumus]] Leonatus, and Be ‘[[Villain|villain]]’ less than ’twas! O [[Innogen]]! My [[Queen|queen]], my life, my wife, O [[Innogen]], [[Innogen]], [[Innogen]]! |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | (approaching him) Peace, my lord. Hear, hear. |
| [[Posthumus|POSTHUMUS]] | Shall ’s have a play of this? Thou scornful page, There lie thy part. He strikes her down |
| PISANIO | (coming forward) O gentlemen, help! Mine and your mistress! O my lord [[Posthumus]], You ne’er killed [[Innogen]] till now. Help, help! (To [[Innogen]]) Mine honoured lady. |
| [[Cymbeline|CYMBELINE]] | Does the world go round? |
| [[Posthumus|POSTHUMUS]] | How comes these staggers on me? |
| PISANIO | (to [[Innogen]]) Wake, my mistress. |
| [[Cymbeline|CYMBELINE]] | If this be so, the gods do mean to strike me To [[Death|death]] with mortal joy. |
| PISANIO | (to [[Innogen]]) How fares my mistress? |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | O, get thee from my sight! Thou gav’st me [[Poison|poison]]. Dangerous fellow, hence. Breathe not where princes are. |
| [[Cymbeline|CYMBELINE]] | The tune of [[Innogen]]. |
| PISANIO | Lady, the gods throw stones of sulphur on me if That box I gave you was not thought by me A precious thing. I had it from the [[Queen]]. |
| [[Cymbeline|CYMBELINE]] | New matter still. |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | It poisoned me. |
| CORNELIUS | O gods! I left out one thing which the [[Queen]] confessed (To Pisanio) Which must approve thee honest. ’If Pisanio Have‘, said she, ‘given his mistress that confection Which I gave him for cordial, she is served As I would serve a rat.’ |
| [[Cymbeline|CYMBELINE]] | What’s this, Cornelius? |
| CORNELIUS | The [[Queen]], sir, very oft importuned me To temper poisons for her, still pretending The satisfaction of her knowledge only In killing creatures vile, as cats and [[dogs]] Of no esteem. I, dreading that her purpose Was of more danger, did compound for her A certain stuff which, being ta‘en, would cease The present power of life, but in short [[Time|time]] All offices of [[Nature|nature]] should again Do their due functions. (To [[Innogen]]) Have you ta’en of it? |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | Most like I did, for I was dead. |
| [[Belarius|BELARIUS]] | (aside to [[Guiderius]] and [[Arviragus]]) My boys, There was our error. |
| [[Guiderius|GUIDERIUS]] | This is sure Fidele. |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | (to [[Posthumus]]) Why did you throw your wedded lady from you? Think that you are upon a lock, and now Throw me again. She throws her arms about his neck |
| [[Posthumus|POSTHUMUS]] | Hang there like fruit, my [[Soul|soul]], Till the tree die. |
| [[Cymbeline|CYMBELINE]] | (to [[Innogen]]) How now, my flesh, my [[Child|child]]? What, mak’st thou me a dullard in this act? Wilt thou not speak to me? |
