# Cymbeline — Act V, Scene 5 — exchange (SICILIUS) [9]

<nav class="excerpt-nav">
<div class="excerpt-crumb">Shakespeare · <a href="/Works/CYMBELINE-(Shakespeare)">Cymbeline</a></div>
</nav>


SICILIUS
He came in thunder. His celestial breath
Was sulphurous to smell. The holy eagle
Stooped, as to foot us. His ascension is
More sweet than our blest fields. His royal bird
Preens the immortal wing and claws his beak
As when his god is pleased.

ALL THE GHOSTS Thanks, Jupiter.

SICILIUS
The marble pavement closes, he is entered
His radiant roof. Away, and, to be blest,
Let us with care perform his great behest.
The ghosts vanish
Posthumus awakes
