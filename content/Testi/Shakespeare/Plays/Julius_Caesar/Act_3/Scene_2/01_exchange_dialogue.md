| Chi parla | Battuta |
|---|---|
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Brutus and Cassius, with the Plebeians |
| ALL THE PLEBEIANS | We will be satisfied! Let us be satisfied! |
| BRUTUS | Then follow me, and give me audience, friends. (Aside to Cassius) Cassius, go you into the other street, And part the numbers. (To the Plebeians) Those that will hear me speak, let ’em stay here; Those that will follow Cassius, go with him; And public reasons shall be rendered Of Caesar’s death. Brutus ascends to the pulpit |
| FIRST PLEBEIAN | I will hear Brutus speak. |
| SECOND PLEBEIAN | I will hear Cassius, and compare their reasons When severally we hear them rendered. Exit Cassius, with some Plebeians Enter Brutus abovein the pulpit |
| THIRD PLEBEIAN | The noble Brutus is ascended. Silence. |
