| BRUTUS | They are all welcome. What watchful cares do interpose themselves Betwixt your eyes and night? |
| CASSIUS | Shall I entreat a word? Cassius and Brutus stand aside and whisper |
| DECIUS | Here lies the east. Doth not the day break here? |
| CASCA | No. |
| CINNA | O pardon, sir, it doth; and yon grey lines That fret the clouds are messengers of day. |
| CASCA | You shall confess that you are both deceived. He points his sword |
| *(didascalia)* | Here, as I point my sword, the sun arises, Which is a great way growing on the south, Weighing the youthful season of the year. Some two months hence up higher toward the north He first presents his fire, and the high east Stands, as the Capitol, directly here. He points his sword. Brutus and Cassius join the other conspirators |
| BRUTUS | Give me your hands all over, one by one. He shakes their hands |
| CASSIUS | And let us swear our resolution. |
