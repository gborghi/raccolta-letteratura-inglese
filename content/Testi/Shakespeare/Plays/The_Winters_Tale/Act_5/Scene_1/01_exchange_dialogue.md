| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Leontes]], Cleomenes, Dion, and [[Paulina]] |
| CLEOMENES | (to [[Leontes]]) Sir, you have done enough, and have performed A saint-like sorrow. No fault could you make Which you have not redeemed, indeed, paid down More penitence than done trespass. At the last Do as the heavens have done, forget your evil. With them, forgive yourself. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | Whilst I remember Her and her virtues I cannot forget My blemishes in them, and so still think of The wrong I did myself, which was so much That heirless it hath made my kingdom, and Destroyed the sweet‘st companion that e’er man Bred his hopes out of. True? |
| [[Paulina|PAULINA]] | Too true, my lord. If one by one you wedded all the world, Or from the all that are took something good To make a perfect woman, she you killed Would be unparalleled. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | I think so. Killed? She I killed? I did so. But thou strik’st me Sorely to say I did; it is as bitter Upon thy tongue as in my thought. Now, good now, Say so but seldom. |
| CLEOMENES | Not at all, good lady. You might have spoke a thousand things that would Have done the [[Time|time]] more benefit, and graced Your kindness better. |
| [[Paulina|PAULINA]] | You are one of those Would have him wed again. |
| DION | If you would not so You pity not the state, nor the remembrance Of his most sovereign name, consider little What dangers, by his highness’ fail of issue, May drop upon his kingdom and devour Incertain lookers-on. What were more holy Than to rejoice the former [[Queen|queen]] is [[Well|well]]? What holier, than for royalty’s repair, For present comfort and for future good, To bless the bed of majesty again With a sweet fellow to’t? |
| [[Paulina|PAULINA]] | There is none worthy Respecting her that’s gone. Besides, the gods Will have fulfilled their secret purposes. For has not the divine [[Apollo]] said? Is’t not the tenor of his oracle That [[King]] [[Leontes]] shall not have an heir Till his lost [[Child|child]] be found? Which that it shall Is all as monstrous to our human reason As my Antigonus to break his [[Grave|grave]] And come again to me, who, on my life, Did perish with the infant. ’Tis your counsel My lord should to the heavens be contrary, Oppose against their wills. (To [[Leontes]]) Care not for issue. The [[Crown|crown]] will find an heir. Great Alexander Left his to th’ worthiest, so his successor Was like to be the best. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | Good [[Paulina]], Who hast the [[Memory|memory]] of [[Hermione]], I know, in [[Honour|honour]]—O, that ever I Had squared me to thy counsel! Then even now I might have looked upon my [[Queen|queen]]’s full eyes, Have taken treasure from her lips. |
| [[Paulina|PAULINA]] | And left them More rich for what they yielded. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | Thou speak’st [[Truth|truth]]. No more such wives, therefore no wife. One worse, And better used, would make her sainted spirit Again possess her corpse, and on this stage, Where we offenders mourn, appear [[Soul|soul]]-vexed, And begin, ‘Why to me?’ |
| [[Paulina|PAULINA]] | Had she such power She had just cause. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | She had, and would incense me To murder her I married. |
