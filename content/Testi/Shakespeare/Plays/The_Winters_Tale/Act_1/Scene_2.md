| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Leontes]], [[Hermione]], Mamillius, [[Polixenes]], and [[Camillo]] |
| [[Polixenes|POLIXENES]] | Nine changes of the wat‘ry star hath been<br>The shepherd’s note since we have left our throne<br>Without a burden. [[Time]] as long again<br>Would be filled up, my brother, with our thanks,<br>And yet we should for perpetuity<br>Go hence in debt. And therefore, like a cipher,<br>Yet standing in rich place, I multiply<br>With one ‘We thank you’ many thousands more<br>That go before it. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | Stay your thanks a while,<br>And pay them when you part. |
| [[Polixenes|POLIXENES]] | Sir, that’s tomorrow. I am questioned by my fears of what may chance<br>Or breed upon our absence, that may blow<br>No sneaping winds at [[Home|home]] to make us say<br>‘This is put forth too truly.’ Besides, I have stayed<br>To tire your royalty. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | We are tougher, brother,<br>Than you can put us to’t. |
| [[Polixenes|POLIXENES]] | No longer stay. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | One sennight longer. |
| [[Polixenes|POLIXENES]] | Very sooth, tomorrow. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | We’ll part the [[Time|time]] between’s, then; and in that<br>I’ll no gainsaying. |
| [[Polixenes|POLIXENES]] | Press me not, beseech you, so.<br>There is no tongue that moves, none, none i‘th’ world<br>So soon as yours, could win me. So it should now,<br>Were there necessity in your request, although<br>’Twere needful I denied it. My affairs<br>Do even drag me homeward; which to hinder<br>Were, in your [[Love|love]], a whip to me; my stay<br>To you a charge and trouble. To save both,<br>Farewell, our brother. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | Tongue-tied, our [[Queen|queen]]? Speak you. |
| [[Hermione|HERMIONE]] | I had thought, sir, to have held my peace until<br>You had drawn oaths from him not to stay. You, sir,<br>Charge him too coldly. Tell him you are sure<br>All in Bohemia’s [[Well|well]]. This satisfaction<br>The bygone day proclaimed. Say this to him,<br>He’s beat from his best ward. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | [[Well]] said, [[Hermione]]! |
| [[Hermione|HERMIONE]] | To tell he longs to see his son were strong.<br>But let him say so then, and let him go.<br>But let him swear so and he shall not stay,<br>We’ll thwack him hence with distaffs.<br>(To [[Polixenes]]) Yet of your royal presence I’ll adventure<br>The borrow of a week. When at Bohemia<br>You take my lord, I’ll give him my commission<br>To let him there a month behind the gest<br>Prefixed for’s parting.—Yet, good deed, [[Leontes]],<br>I [[Love|love]] thee not a jar o’th’ [[Clock|clock]] behind<br>What lady she her lord.—You’ll stay? |
| [[Polixenes|POLIXENES]] | No, madam. |
| [[Hermione|HERMIONE]] | Nay, but you will? |
| [[Polixenes|POLIXENES]] | I may not, verily. |
| [[Hermione|HERMIONE]] | Verily?<br>You put me off with limber vows. But I,<br>Though you would seek t‘unsphere the [[Stars|stars]] with<br>oaths,<br>Should yet say ‘Sir, no going.’ Verily<br>You shall not go. A lady’s ‘verily’ ’s<br>As potent as a lord’s. Will you go yet?<br>Force me to keep you as a prisoner,<br>Not like a guest: so you shall pay your fees<br>When you depart, and save your thanks. How say<br>you?<br>My prisoner? or my guest? By your dread ‘verily’,<br>One of them you shall be. |
| [[Polixenes|POLIXENES]] | Your guest then, madam.<br>To be your prisoner should import offending,<br>Which is for me less easy to commit<br>Than you to punish. |
| [[Hermione|HERMIONE]] | Not your jailer then,<br>But your kind hostess. Come, I’ll question you<br>Of my lord’s tricks and yours when you were boys.<br>You were pretty lordings then? |
| [[Polixenes|POLIXENES]] | We were, fair [[Queen]],<br>Two lads that thought there was no more behind<br>But such a day tomorrow as today,<br>And to be boy eternal. |
| [[Hermione|HERMIONE]] | Was not my lord<br>The verier wag o’th’ two? |
| [[Polixenes|POLIXENES]] | We were as twinned lambs that did frisk i‘th’ sun,<br>And bleat the one at th’other. What we changed<br>Was [[innocence]] for [[innocence]]. We knew not<br>The doctrine of ill-doing, nor dreamed<br>That any did. Had we pursued that life,<br>And our weak spirits ne‘er been higher reared<br>With stronger [[Blood|blood]], we should have answered<br>heaven<br>Boldly, ‘Not guilty’, the imposition cleared<br>Hereditary ours. |
| [[Hermione|HERMIONE]] | By this we gather<br>You have tripped since. |
| [[Polixenes|POLIXENES]] | O my most sacred lady,<br>Temptations have since then been born to’s; for<br>In those unfledged days was my wife a girl.<br>Your precious [[Self|self]] had then not crossed the eyes<br>Of my young playfellow. |
| [[Hermione|HERMIONE]] | Grace to boot!<br>Of this make no conclusion, lest you say<br>Your [[Queen|queen]] and I are devils. Yet go on.<br>Th’offences we have made you do we’ll answer,<br>If you first sinned with us, and that with us<br>You did continue fault, and that you slipped not<br>With any but with us. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | Is he won yet? |
| [[Hermione|HERMIONE]] | He’ll stay, my lord. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | At my request he would not.<br>[[Hermione]], my dearest, thou never spok’st<br>To better purpose. |
| [[Hermione|HERMIONE]] | Never? |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | Never but once. |
| [[Hermione|HERMIONE]] | What, have I twice said [[Well|well]]? When was’t before?<br>I prithee tell me. Cram’s with praise, and make’s<br>As fat as tame things. One good deed dying tongueless<br>Slaughters a thousand waiting upon that.<br>Our praises are our wages. You may ride’s<br>With one soft kiss a thousand furlongs ere<br>With spur we heat an acre. But to th’ goal.<br>My last good deed was to entreat his stay.<br>What was my first? It has an elder sister,<br>Or I mistake you. O, would her name were Gracel<br>But once before I spoke to th’ purpose? When?<br>Nay, let me have’t. I long. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | Why, that was when<br>Three crabbed months had soured themselves to [[Death|death]]<br>Ere I could make thee open thy white hand<br>And clap thyself my [[Love|love]]. Then didst thou utter,<br>‘I am yours for ever.’ |
| [[Hermione|HERMIONE]] | ’Tis grace indeed.<br>Why lo you now; I have spoke to th’ purpose twice.<br>The one for ever earned a royal husband;<br>Th’other, for some while a friend. |
| *(didascalia)* | She gives her hand to [[Polixenes]]. |
| [[Hermione|HERMIONE]] | They stand aside |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | (aside)<br>Too hot, too hot:<br>To mingle [[Friendship|friendship]] farre is mingling bloods.<br>I have tremor cordis on me. My heart dances,<br>But not for joy, not joy. This entertainment<br>May a free face put on, derive a liberty<br>From heartiness, from bounty, fertile bosom,<br>And [[Well|well]] become the agent. ’T may, I grant.<br>But to be paddling palms and pinching fingers,<br>As now they are, and making practised smiles<br>As in a looking-glass; and then to sigh, as ‘twere<br>The mort o’th’ deer—O, that is entertainment<br>My bosom likes not, nor my brows.—Mamillius,<br>Art thou my boy? |
| MAMILLIUS | Ay, my good lord. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | I’fecks,<br>Why, that’s my bawcock. What? Hast smutched thy<br>nose?<br>They say it is a copy out of mine. Come, captain,<br>We must be neat—not neat, but cleanly, captain.<br>And yet the steer, the heifer, and the calf<br>Are all called neat.—Still virginalling<br>Upon his palm?—How now, you wanton calf—<br>Art thou my calf? |
| MAMILLIUS | Yes, if you will, my lord. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | Thou want‘st a rough pash and the shoots that I have,<br>To be full like me. Yet they say we are<br>Almost as like as eggs. Women say so,<br>That will say anything. But were they false<br>As o’er-dyed blacks, as [[Wind|wind]], as waters, false<br>As dice are to be wished by one that fixes<br>No bourn ‘twixt his and mine, yet were it true<br>To say this boy were like me. Come, sir page,<br>Look on me with your welkin eye. Sweet [[Villain|villain]],<br>Most dear’st, my collop! Can thy dam—may’t be?—<br>Affection, thy intention stabs the centre.<br>Thou dost make possible things not so held,<br>Communicat‘st with [[Dreams|dreams]]—how can this be?—<br>With what’s unreal thou coactive art,<br>And fellow’st nothing. Then ‘tis very credent<br>Thou mayst co-join with something, and thou dost—<br>And that beyond commission; and I find it—<br>And that to the infection of my brains<br>And hard’ning of my brows. |
| [[Polixenes|POLIXENES]] | What means Sicilia? |
| [[Hermione|HERMIONE]] | He something seems unsettled. |
| [[Polixenes|POLIXENES]] | How, my lord! |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | What cheer? How is’t with you, best brother? |
| [[Hermione|HERMIONE]] | You look<br>As if you held a brow of much distraction.<br>Are you moved, my lord? |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | No, in good earnest.<br>How sometimes [[Nature|nature]] will betray its folly,<br>Its tenderness, and make itself a pastime<br>To harder bosoms! Looking on the lines<br>Of my boy’s face, methoughts I did recoil<br>Twenty-three years, and saw myself unbreeched,<br>In my green velvet coat; my dagger muzzled,<br>Lest it should bite its master, and so prove,<br>As ornament oft does, too dangerous.<br>How like, methought, I then was to this kernel,<br>This squash, this gentleman.—Mine honest friend,<br>Will you take eggs for money? |
| MAMILLIUS | No, my lord, I’ll fight. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | You will? Why, happy man be’s dole!—My brother,<br>Are you so fond of your young prince as we<br>Do seem to be of ours? |
| [[Polixenes|POLIXENES]] | If at [[Home|home]], sir,<br>He’s all my exercise, my mirth, my matter;<br>Now my sworn friend, and then mine enemy;<br>My parasite, my soldier, statesman, all.<br>He makes a July’s day short as December,<br>And with his varying childness cures in me<br>Thoughts that would thick my [[Blood|blood]]. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | So stands this squire<br>Officed with me. We two will walk, my lord,<br>And leave you to your graver steps. [[Hermione]],<br>How thou lov’st us show in our brother’s welcome.<br>Let what is dear in Sicily be cheap.<br>Next to thyself and my young rover, he’s<br>Apparent to my heart. |
| [[Hermione|HERMIONE]] | If you would seek us,<br>We are yours i’th’ [[Garden|garden]]. Shall’s attend you there? |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | To your own bents dispose you. You’ll be found,<br>Be you beneath the sky. (Aside) I am angling now,<br>Though you perceive me not how I give line.<br>Go to, go to!<br>How she holds up the neb, the bill to him,<br>And arms her with the boldness of a wife<br>To her allowing husband! |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt [[Polixenes]] and [[Hermione]] Gone already. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | Inch-thick, knee-deep, o‘er head and ears a forked<br>one!—<br>Go play, boy, play. Thy mother plays, and I<br>Play too; but so disgraced a part, whose issue<br>Will hiss me to my [[Grave|grave]]. Contempt and clamour<br>Will be my knell. Go play, boy, play. There have been,<br>Or I am much deceived, cuckolds ere now,<br>And many a man there is, even at this present,<br>Now, while I speak this, holds his wife by th’arm,<br>That little thinks she has been sluiced in’s absence,<br>And his pond fished by his next neighbour, by<br>Sir Smile, his neighbour. Nay, there’s comfort in‘t,<br>Whiles other men have gates, and those gates opened,<br>As mine, against their will. Should all [[Despair|despair]]<br>That have revolted wives, the tenth of mankind<br>Would hang themselves. Physic for’t there’s none.<br>It is a bawdy planet, that will strike<br>Where ’tis predominant; and ‘tis powerful. Think it:<br>From east, west, north, and south, be it concluded,<br>No barricado for a belly. Know’t,<br>It will let in and out the enemy<br>With bag and baggage. Many thousand on’s<br>Have the disease and feel’t not.—How now, boy? |
| MAMILLIUS | I am like you, they say. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | Why, that’s some comfort.<br>What, [[Camillo]] there! |
| [[Camillo|CAMILLO]] | [coming forward] Ay, my good lord. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | Go play, Mamillius, thou’rt an honest man. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit Mamillius |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | [[Camillo]], this great sir will yet stay longer. |
| [[Camillo|CAMILLO]] | You had much ado to make his anchor hold.<br>When you cast out, it still came [[Home|home]]. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | Didst note it? |
| [[Camillo|CAMILLO]] | He would not stay at your petitions, made<br>His business more material. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | Didst perceive it?<br>(Aside) They’re here with me already, whisp‘ring,<br>rounding,<br>‘Sicilia is a so-forth’. ’Tis far gone<br>When I shall gust it last.—How came’t, [[Camillo]],<br>That he did stay? |
| [[Camillo|CAMILLO]] | At the good [[Queen]]’s entreaty. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | ‘At the [[Queen]]’s’ be’t. ‘Good’ should be pertinent,<br>But so it is, it is not. Was this taken<br>By any understanding pate but thine?<br>For thy conceit is soaking, will draw in<br>More than the common blocks. Not noted, is’t,<br>But of the finer natures? By some severals<br>Of head-piece extraordinary? Lower messes<br>Perchance are to this business purblind? Say. |
| [[Camillo|CAMILLO]] | Business, my lord? I think most understand<br>Bohemia stays here longer. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | Ha? |
| [[Camillo|CAMILLO]] | Stays here longer. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | Ay, but why? |
| [[Camillo|CAMILLO]] | To satisfy your highness, and the entreaties<br>Of our most gracious mistress. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | Satisfy?<br>Th‘entreaties of your mistress? Satisfy?<br>Let that suffice. I have trusted thee, [[Camillo]],<br>With all the near’st things to my heart, as [[Well|well]]<br>My chamber-counsels, wherein, priest-like, thou<br>Hast cleansed my bosom, I from thee departed<br>Thy penitent reformed. But we have been<br>Deceived in thy integrity, deceived<br>In that which seems so. |
| [[Camillo|CAMILLO]] | Be it forbid, my lord. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | To bide upon’t: thou art not honest; or<br>If thou inclin‘st that way, thou art a coward,<br>Which hoxes honesty behind, restraining<br>From course required. Or else thou must be counted<br>A servant grafted in my serious trust<br>And therein negligent, or else a [[Fool|fool]]<br>That seest a game played [[Home|home]], the rich stake drawn,<br>And tak’st it all for jest. |
| [[Camillo|CAMILLO]] | My gracious lord,<br>I may be negligent, foolish, and fearful.<br>In every one of these no man is free,<br>But that his negligence, his folly, [[Fear|fear]],<br>Among the infinite doings of the world<br>Sometime puts forth. In your affairs, my lord,<br>If ever I were wilful-negligent,<br>It was my folly. If industriously<br>I played the [[Fool|fool]], it was my negligence,<br>Not weighing [[Well|well]] the end. If ever fearful<br>To do a thing where I the issue doubted,<br>Whereof the execution did cry out<br>Against the non-performance, ‘twas a [[Fear|fear]]<br>Which oft infects the wisest. These, my lord,<br>Are such allowed infirmities that honesty<br>Is never free of. But beseech your grace<br>Be plainer with me, let me know my trespass<br>By its own visage. If I then deny it,<br>’Tis none of mine. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | Ha’ not you seen, [[Camillo]]—<br>But that’s past [[Doubt|doubt]]; you have, or your eye-glass<br>Is thicker than a cuckold’s horn—or heard—<br>For, to a vision so apparent, rumour<br>Cannot be mute—or thought—for cogitation<br>Resides not in that man that does not think—<br>My wife is slippery? If thou wilt confess—<br>Or else be impudently negative<br>To have nor eyes, nor ears, nor thought—then say<br>My wife’s a hobby-[[Horse|horse]], deserves a name<br>As rank as any flax-wench that puts to<br>Before her troth-plight. Say’t, and justify’t. |
| [[Camillo|CAMILLO]] | I would not be a stander-by to hear<br>My sovereign mistress clouded so without<br>My present vengeance taken. ’Shrew my heart,<br>You never spoke what did become you less<br>Than this, which to reiterate were sin<br>As deep as that, though true. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | Is whispering nothing?<br>Is leaning cheek to cheek? Is meeting noses?<br>Kissing with inside lip? Stopping the career<br>Of laughter with a sigh?—a note infallible<br>Of breaking honesty. Horsing foot on foot?<br>Skulking in corners? Wishing clocks more swift,<br>Hours minutes, [[Noon|noon]] midnight? And all eyes<br>Blind with the pin and web but theirs, theirs only,<br>That would unseen be wicked? Is this nothing?<br>Why then the world and all that’s in’t is nothing,<br>The covering sky is nothing, Bohemia nothing,<br>My wife is nothing, nor nothing have these nothings<br>If this be nothing. |
| [[Camillo|CAMILLO]] | Good my lord, be cured<br>Of this diseased opinion, and betimes,<br>For ’tis most dangerous. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | Say it be, ’tis true. |
| [[Camillo|CAMILLO]] | No, no, my lord. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | It is. You lie, you lie.<br>I say thou liest, [[Camillo]], and I hate thee,<br>Pronounce thee a gross lout, a mindless slave,<br>Or else a hovering temporizer, that<br>Canst with thine eyes at once see good and evil,<br>Inclining to them both. Were my wife’s liver<br>Infected as her life, she would not live<br>The running of one glass. |
| [[Camillo|CAMILLO]] | Who does infect her? |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | Why, he that wears her like her medal, hanging<br>About his neck, Bohemia, who, if I<br>Had servants true about me, that bare eyes<br>To see alike mine [[Honour|honour]] as their profits,<br>Their own particular thrifts, they would do that<br>Which should undo more doing. Ay, and thou<br>His cupbearer, whom I from meaner form<br>Have benched, and reared to worship, who mayst see<br>Plainly as heaven sees earth and earth sees heaven,<br>How I am galled, mightst bespice a cup<br>To give mine enemy a lasting wink,<br>Which draught to me were cordial. |
| [[Camillo|CAMILLO]] | Sir, my lord,<br>I could do this, and that with no rash potion,<br>But with a ling’ring dram, that should not work<br>Maliciously, like [[Poison|poison]]. But I cannot<br>Believe this crack to be in my dread mistress,<br>So sovereignly being honourable.<br>I have loved thee— |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | Make that thy question, and go rot!<br>Dost think I am so muddy, so unsettled,<br>To appoint myself in this vexation?<br>Sully the purity and whiteness of my sheets—<br>Which to preserve is [[Sleep|sleep]], which being spotted<br>Is goads, thorns, nettles, tails of wasps-Give<br>scandal to the [[Blood|blood]] o’th’ prince, my son—<br>Who I do think is mine, and [[Love|love]] as mine—<br>Without ripe moving to’t? Would I do this?<br>Could man so blench? |
| [[Camillo|CAMILLO]] | I must believe you, sir. I do, and will fetch off Bohemia for’t,<br>Provided that when he’s removed your highness<br>Will take again your [[Queen|queen]] as yours at first,<br>Even for your son’s sake, and thereby for sealing<br>The injury of tongues in courts and kingdoms<br>Known and allied to yours. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | Thou dost advise me<br>Even so as I mine own course have set down.<br>I’ll give no blemish to her [[Honour|honour]], none. |
| [[Camillo|CAMILLO]] | My lord, go then, and with a countenance as clear<br>As [[Friendship|friendship]] wears at feasts, keep with Bohemia<br>And with your [[Queen|queen]]. I am his cupbearer.<br>If from me he have wholesome beverage,<br>Account me not your servant. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | This is all. Do‘t, and thou hast the one half of my heart;<br>Do’t not, thou splitt’st thine own. |
| [[Camillo|CAMILLO]] | I’ll do’t, my lord. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | I will seem friendly, as thou hast advised me. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit |
| [[Camillo|CAMILLO]] | O miserable lady. But for me,<br>What case stand I in? I must be the poisoner<br>Of good [[Polixenes]], and my ground to do’t<br>Is the obedience to a master—one<br>Who in rebellion with himself, will have<br>All that are his so too. To do this deed,<br>Promotion follows. If I could find example<br>Of thousands that had struck anointed kings<br>And flourished after, I’d not do’t. But since<br>Nor brass, nor stone, nor parchment bears not one,<br>Let villainy itself forswear’t. I must<br>Forsake the [[Court|court]]. To do’t, or no, is certain<br>To me a break-neck. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Polixenes]] |
| [[Camillo|CAMILLO]] | Happy star reign now!<br>Here comes Bohemia. |
| [[Polixenes|POLIXENES]] | (aside)<br>This is strange. Methinks My favour here begins to warp. Not speak?—<br>Good day, [[Camillo]]. |
| [[Camillo|CAMILLO]] | Hail, most royal sir. |
| [[Polixenes|POLIXENES]] | What is the news i’th’ [[Court|court]]? |
| [[Camillo|CAMILLO]] | None rare, my lord. |
| [[Polixenes|POLIXENES]] | The [[King]] hath on him such a countenance<br>As he had lost some province, and a region<br>Loved as he loves himself. Even now I met him<br>With customary compliment, when he,<br>Wafting his eyes to th’ contrary, and falling<br>A lip of much contempt, speeds from me, and<br>So [[Leaves|leaves]] me to consider what is breeding<br>That changes thus his manners. |
| [[Camillo|CAMILLO]] | I dare not know, my lord. |
| [[Polixenes|POLIXENES]] | How, ‘dare not’? Do not? Do you know, and dare not?<br>Be intelligent to me. ‘Tis thereabouts.<br>For to yourself what you do know you must,<br>And cannot say you ‘dare not’. Good [[Camillo]],<br>Your changed complexions are to me a [[Mirror|mirror]]<br>Which shows me mine changed, too; for I must be<br>A party in this alteration, finding<br>Myself thus altered with’t. |
| [[Camillo|CAMILLO]] | There is a sickness<br>Which puts some of us in distemper, but<br>I cannot name th’ disease, and it is caught<br>Of you that yet are [[Well|well]]. |
| [[Polixenes|POLIXENES]] | How caught of me? Make me not sighted like the basilisk.<br>I have looked on thousands who have sped the better<br>By my regard, but killed none so. [[Camillo]],<br>As you are certainly a gentleman, thereto<br>Clerk-like experienced, which no less adorns<br>Our gentry than our parents’ noble names,<br>In whose [[success]] we are gentle: I beseech you,<br>If you know aught which does behove my knowledge<br>Thereof to be informed, imprison’t not<br>In ignorant concealment. |
| [[Camillo|CAMILLO]] | I may not answer. |
| [[Polixenes|POLIXENES]] | A sickness caught of me, and yet I [[Well|well]]?<br>I must be answered. Dost thou hear, [[Camillo]],<br>I conjure thee, by all the parts of man<br>Which [[Honour|honour]] does acknowledge, whereof the least<br>Is not this suit of mine, that thou declare<br>What incidency thou dost guess of harm<br>Is creeping toward me; how far off, how near,<br>Which way to be prevented, if to be;<br>If not, how best to bear it. |
| [[Camillo|CAMILLO]] | Sir, I will tell you, Since I am charged in [[Honour|honour]], and by him<br>That I think honourable. Therefore mark my counsel,<br>Which must be e’en as swiftly followed as<br>I mean to utter it; or both yourself and me<br>Cry lost, and so good [[Night|night]]! |
| [[Polixenes|POLIXENES]] | On, good [[Camillo]]. |
| [[Camillo|CAMILLO]] | I am appointed him to murder you. |
| [[Polixenes|POLIXENES]] | By whom, [[Camillo]]? |
| [[Camillo|CAMILLO]] | By the [[King]]. |
| [[Polixenes|POLIXENES]] | For what? |
| [[Camillo|CAMILLO]] | He thinks, nay, with all confidence he swears<br>As he had seen‘t, or been an instrument<br>To vice you to’t, that you have touched his [[Queen|queen]]<br>Forbiddenly. |
| [[Polixenes|POLIXENES]] | O, then my best [[Blood|blood]] turn<br>To an infected jelly, and my name<br>Be yoked with his that did betray the Best!<br>Turn then my freshest reputation to<br>A savour that may strike the dullest nostril<br>Where I arrive, and my approach be shunned,<br>Nay hated, too, worse than the great‘st infection<br>That e’er was heard or read. |
| [[Camillo|CAMILLO]] | Swear his thought over By each particular star in heaven, and<br>By all their influences, you may as [[Well|well]]<br>Forbid the sea for to obey the [[Moon|moon]]<br>As or by oath remove or counsel shake<br>The fabric of his folly, whose foundation<br>Is piled upon his [[Faith|faith]], and will continue<br>The standing of his [[Body|body]]. |
| [[Polixenes|POLIXENES]] | How should this grow? |
| [[Camillo|CAMILLO]] | I know not, but I am sure ‘tis safer to<br>Avoid what’s grown than question how ’tis born.<br>If therefore you dare trust my honesty,<br>That lies enclosed in this trunk which you<br>Shall bear along impawned, away tonight!<br>Your followers I will whisper to the business,<br>And will by twos and threes at several posterns<br>Clear them o’th’ [[City|city]]. For myself, I’ll put<br>My fortunes to your service, which are here<br>By this discovery lost. Be not uncertain,<br>For by the [[Honour|honour]] of my parents, I<br>Have uttered [[Truth|truth]]; which if you seek to prove,<br>I dare not stand by; nor shall you be safer<br>Than one condemned by the [[King]]’s own mouth,<br>Thereon his execution sworn. |
| [[Polixenes|POLIXENES]] | I do believe thee, I saw his heart in’s face. Give me thy hand.<br>Be pilot to me, and thy places shall<br>Still neighbour mine. My ships are ready, and<br>My people did expect my hence departure<br>Two days ago. This [[Jealousy|jealousy]]<br>Is for a precious creature. As she’s rare<br>Must it be great; and as his person’s mighty<br>Must it be violent; and as he does conceive<br>He is dishonoured by a man which ever<br>Professed to him, why, his revenges must<br>In that be made more bitter. [[Fear]] o‘ershades me.<br>Good expedition be my friend and comfort<br>The gracious [[Queen]], part of his theme, but nothing<br>Of his ill-ta’en suspicion. Come, [[Camillo]],<br>I will respect thee as a father if<br>Thou bear’st my life off hence. Let us avoid. |
| [[Camillo|CAMILLO]] | It is in mine authority to command<br>The keys of all the posterns. Please your highness<br>To take the urgent hour. Come, sir, away. |
