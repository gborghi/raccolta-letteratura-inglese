| OLD SHEPHERD | I would there were no age between ten and three-and-twenty, or that youth would [[Sleep|sleep]] out the rest; for there is nothing in the between but getting wenches with [[Child|child]], wronging the ancientry, stealing, fighting—hark you now, would any but these boiled-brains of nineteen and two-and-twenty hunt this weather? They have scared away two of my best sheep, which I [[Fear|fear]] the wolf will sooner find than the master. If anywhere I have them, ’tis by the seaside, browsing of ivy. Good luck, an’t be thy will! He sees the babe What have we here? [[Mercy]] on‘s, a bairn! A very pretty bairn. A boy or a [[Child|child]], I [[Wonder|wonder]]? A pretty one, a very pretty one. Sure some scape. Though I am not bookish, yet I can read ‘waiting-gentlewoman’ in the scape. This has been some stair-work, some trunk-work, some behind-door-work. They were warmer that got this than the poor thing is here. I’ll take it up for pity; yet I’ll tarry till my son come. He hallooed but even now. Whoa-ho-hoa! Enter Clown |
