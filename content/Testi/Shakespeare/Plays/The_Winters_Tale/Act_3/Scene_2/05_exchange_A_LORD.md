| LORD | This your request Is altogether just. Therefore bring forth, And in [[Apollo]]’s name, his oracle. [Exeunt certain Officers] |
| [[Hermione|HERMIONE]] | The Emperor of [[Russia]] was my father. O that he were alive, and here beholding His daughter’s trial; that he did but see The flatness of my misery—yet with eyes Of pity, not [[Revenge|revenge]]. [Enter Officers with Cleomenes and Dion] |
| OFFICER | You here shall swear upon this [[Sword|sword]] of justice That you, Cleomenes and Dion, have Been both at Delphos, and from thence have brought This sealed-up oracle, by the hand delivered Of great [[Apollo]]’s priest; and that since then You have not dared to break the holy seal, Nor read the [[Secrets|secrets]] in’t. |
| CLEOMENES | and DION All this we swear. [[Leontes|LEONTES]] Break up the seals, and read. |
| OFFICER | (reads) [[Hermione]] is chaste, [[Polixenes]] blameless, [[Camillo]] a true subject, [[Leontes]] a jealous [[Tyrant|tyrant]], his [[Innocent|innocent]] babe truly begotten, and the [[King]] shall live without an heir if that which is lost be not found. |
| LORDS | Now blessèd be the great [[Apollo]]! |
| [[Hermione|HERMIONE]] | Praised! |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | Hast thou read [[Truth|truth]]? |
| OFFICER | Ay, my lord, even so as it is here set down. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | There is no [[Truth|truth]] at all i’th’ oracle. The sessions shall proceed. This is mere falsehood. Enter a Servant |
| SERVANT | My lord the [[King]]! The [[King]]! |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | What is the business? |
| SERVANT | O sir, I shall be hated to report it. The prince your son, with mere conceit and [[Fear|fear]] Of the [[Queen]]’s [[Speed|speed]], is gone. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | How, ‘gone’? |
| SERVANT | Is dead. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | [[Apollo]]’s angry, and the heavens themselves Do strike at my injustice. [[Hermione]] falls to the ground How now there? |
| [[Paulina|PAULINA]] | This news is mortal to the [[Queen]]. Look down And see what [[Death|death]] is doing. |
