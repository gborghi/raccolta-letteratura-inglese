| EXETER | He’ll make your Paris Louvre shake for it, Were it the mistress court of mighty Europe. And be assured, you’ll find a diff’rence, As we his subjects have in wonder found, Between the promise of his greener days And these he masters now: now he weighs time Even to the utmost grain. That you shall read In your own losses, if he stay in France. |
