| *(didascalia)* | Enter the Earl of [[Kent]] disguised, and First Gentleman |
| [[Kent|KENT]] | Why the [[King of France]] is so suddenly gone back know you no reason? |
| *(didascalia)* | FIRST GENTLEMAN |
| [[Kent|KENT]] | Something he left imperfect in the state<br>Which, since his coming forth, is thought of; which<br>Imports to the kingdom so much [[Fear|fear]] and danger<br>That his personal return was most required<br>And necessary. |
| [[Kent|KENT]] | Who hath he left behind him general? |
| *(didascalia)* | IRST NTLEMAN |
| [[Kent|KENT]] | The Maréchal of France, Monsieur la Far. |
| [[Kent|KENT]] | Did your letters pierce the [[Queen]] to any demonstration of grief? II |
| *(didascalia)* | IRST ENTLEMAN |
| [[Kent|KENT]] | Ay, sir. She took them, read them in my presence,<br>And now and then an ample tear trilled down<br>Her delicate cheek. It seemed she was a [[Queen|queen]]<br>Over her passion who, most [[Rebel|rebel]]-like,<br>Sought to be [[King|king]] o’er her. |
| [[Kent|KENT]] | O, then it moved her. |
| *(didascalia)* | IRSTGENTLEMAN |
| [[Kent|KENT]] | Not to a rage. Patience and sorrow strove<br>Who should express her goodliest. You have seen<br>Sunshine and rain at once; her smiles and [[Tears|tears]]<br>Were like, a better way. Those happy smilets<br>That played on her ripe lip seemed not to know<br>What guests were in her eyes, which parted thence<br>As pearls from diamonds dropped. In brief,<br>Sorrow would be a rarity most beloved<br>If all could so become it. |
| [[Kent|KENT]] | Made she no verbal question? |
| *(didascalia)* | IRST ENTLEMAN |
| [[Kent|KENT]] | [[Faith]], once or twice she heaved the name of ‘father’<br>Pantingly forth as if it pressed her heart,<br>Cried ‘Sisters, sisters, shame of ladies, sisters,<br>[[Kent]], father, sisters, what, i‘th’ [[Storm|storm]], i’th’ [[Night|night]],<br>Let piety not be believed!’ There she shook<br>The holy [[Water|water]] from her heavenly eyes<br>And clamour mastered, then away she started<br>To deal with grief [[Alone|alone]]. |
| [[Kent|KENT]] | It is the [[Stars|stars]],<br>The [[Stars|stars]] above us govern our conditions,<br>Else one [[Self|self]] mate and make could not beget<br>Such different issues. You spoke not with her since? |
| *(didascalia)* | IRST ENTLEMAN No. |
| [[Kent|KENT]] | Was this before the [[King]] returned? |
| *(didascalia)* | IRST ENTLEMAN No, since. |
| [[Kent|KENT]] | [[Well]], sir, the poor distressed Lear’s i’th’ town,<br>Who sometime in his better tune remembers<br>What we are come about, and by no means<br>Will yield to see his daughter. |
| *(didascalia)* | IRST NTLEMAN Why, good sir? |
| [[Kent|KENT]] | A sovereign shame so elbows him: his own unkindness,<br>That stripped her from his benediction, turned her<br>To foreign casualties, gave her dear rights<br>To his dog-hearted daughters—these things sting<br>His mind so venomously that burning shame<br>Detains him from [[Cordelia]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | IRST ENTLEMAN Alack, poor gentleman! |
| [[Kent|KENT]] | Of Albany’s and Cornwall’s powers you heard not? |
| *(didascalia)* | IRST ENTLEMAN ’Tis so; they are afoot. |
| [[Kent|KENT]] | [[Well]], sir, I’ll bring you to our master Lear,<br>And leave you to attend him. Some dear cause<br>Will in concealment wrap me up a while.<br>When I am known aright you shall not grieve<br>Lending me this acquaintance. I pray you go<br>Along with me. Exeunt |
