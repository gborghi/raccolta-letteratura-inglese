| *(didascalia)* | Enter at one door Gonoril and [[Edmund]] the bastard |
| GONORIL | Welcome, my lord. I marvel our mild husband<br>Not met us on the way. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter at another door Oswald the steward |
| GONORIL | Now, where’s your master? |
| OSWALD | Madam, within; but never man so changed.<br>I told him of the army that was landed;<br>He smiled at it. I told him you were coming;<br>His answer was ‘The worse.’ Of [[Gloucester]]’s treachery<br>And of the loyal service of his son<br>When I informed him, then he called me sot,<br>And told me I had turned the wrong side out.<br>What he should most defy seems pleasant to him;<br>What like, offensive. |
| GONORIL | (to [[Edmund]]) Then shall you go no further.<br>It is the cowish terror of his spirit<br>That dares not undertake. He’ll not feel wrongs<br>Which tie him to an answer. Our wishes on the way<br>May prove effects. Back, [[Edmund]], to my brother.<br>Hasten his musters and conduct his powers.<br>I must change arms at [[Home|home]], and give the distaff<br>Into my husband’s hands. This trusty servant<br>Shall pass between us. Ere long you are like to hear,<br>If you dare venture in your own behalf,<br>A mistress’s command. Wear this. Spare speech.<br>Decline your head. This kiss, if it durst speak,<br>Would stretch thy spirits up into the air. |
| *(didascalia)* | she kisses him |
| GONORIL | Conceive, and fare you [[Well|well]]. |
| [[Edmund|EDMUND]] | Yours in the ranks of [[Death|death]]. |
| GONORIL | My most dear [[Gloucester]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit [[Edmund]] |
| GONORIL | To thee a woman’s services are due;<br>My foot usurps my [[Body|body]]. |
| OSWALD | Madam, here comes my lord. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter the Duke of Albany |
| GONORIL | I have been worth the whistling. |
| ALBANY | O Gonoril,<br>You are not worth the [[Dust|dust]] which the rude [[Wind|wind]]<br>Blows in your face. I [[Fear|fear]] your disposition.<br>That [[Nature|nature]] which contemns it origin<br>Cannot be bordered certain in itself.<br>She that herself will sliver and disbranch<br>From her material sap perforce must wither,<br>And come to deadly use. |
| GONORIL | No more. The text is foolish. |
| ALBANY | Wisdom and goodness to the vile seem vile;<br>Filths savour but themselves. What have you done?<br>Tigers, not daughters, what have you performed?<br>A father, and a gracious, aged man,<br>Whose reverence even the head-lugged bear would<br>lick,<br>Most barbarous, most degenerate, have you madded.<br>Could my good-brother suffer you to do it—<br>A man, a prince by him so benefacted?<br>If that the heavens do not their visible spirits<br>Send quickly down to tame these vile offences,<br>It will come,<br>Humanity must perforce prey on itself,<br>Like monsters of the deep. |
| GONORIL | Milk-livered man,<br>That bear‘st a cheek for blows, a head for wrongs;<br>Who hast not in thy brows an eye discerning<br>Thine [[Honour|honour]] from thy [[Suffering|suffering]]; that not know’st<br>Fools do those villains pity who are punished<br>Ere they have done their mischief: where’s thy drum?<br>France spreads his banners in our noiseless land,<br>With plumed helm thy flaxen biggin threats,<br>Whiles thou, a moral [[Fool|fool]], sits still and cries<br>‘Alack, why does he so?’ |
| ALBANY | See thyself, devil.<br>Proper deformity shows not in the fiend<br>So horrid as in woman. |
| GONORIL | O vain [[Fool|fool]]! 60 |
| ALBANY | Thou changed and [[Self|self]]-covered thing, for shame<br>Bemonster not thy feature. Were’t my fitness<br>To let these hands obey my [[Blood|blood]],<br>They are apt enough to dislocate and tear<br>Thy flesh and bones. Howe’er thou art a fiend,<br>A woman’s shape doth shield thee. |
| GONORIL | Marry your manhood, mew— |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Second Gentleman |
| ALBANY | What news? |
| *(didascalia)* | SECOND GENTLEMAN |
| ALBANY | O my good lord, the Duke of Cornwall’s dead,<br>Slain by his servant going to put out<br>The other eye of [[Gloucester]]. |
| ALBANY | [[Gloucester]]’s eyes? |
| *(didascalia)* | FSECOND GENTLEMAN |
| ALBANY | A servant that he bred, thralled with remorse,<br>Opposed against the act, bending his [[Sword|sword]]<br>To his great master, who thereat enraged<br>Flew on him, and amongst them felled him dead,<br>But not without that harmful stroke which since<br>Hath plucked him after. |
| ALBANY | This shows you are above,<br>You justicers, that these our nether crimes<br>So speedily can venge. But O, poor [[Gloucester]]!<br>Lost he his other eye? |
| *(didascalia)* | SECOND GENTLEMAN Both, both, my lord. 80 |
| ALBANY | (To Gonoril) This letter, madam, craves a speedy<br>answer.<br>’Tis from your sister. |
| GONORIL | (aside) One way I like this [[Well|well]];<br>But being widow, and my [[Gloucester]] with her,<br>May all the building on my fancy pluck<br>Upon my hateful life. Another way<br>The news is not so took.—I’ll read and answer. Exit |
| ALBANY | Where was his son when they did take his eyes? |
| *(didascalia)* | ECONDGENTLEMAN |
| ALBANY | Come with my lady hither. |
| ALBANY | He is not here. |
| *(didascalia)* | ECONDGENTLEMAN |
| ALBANY | No, my good lord; I met him back again. |
| ALBANY | Knows he the wickedness? 90 |
| *(didascalia)* | ECONDGENTLEMAN |
| ALBANY | Ay, my good lord; ’twas he informed against him,<br>And quit the [[house]] on purpose that their punishment<br>Might have the freer course. |
| ALBANY | [[Gloucester]], I live<br>To thank thee for the [[Love|love]] thou showed’st the [[King]],<br>And to [[Revenge|revenge]] thy eyes.—Come hither, friend<br>Tell me what more thou knowest. Exeunt |
