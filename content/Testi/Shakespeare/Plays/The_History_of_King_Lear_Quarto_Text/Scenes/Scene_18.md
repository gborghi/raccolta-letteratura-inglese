| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Queen]] [[Cordelia]], a Doctor, and others |
| [[Cordelia|CORDELIA]] | Alack, ’tis he! Why, he was met even now,<br>As mad as the racked sea, singing aloud,<br>Crowned with rank fumitor and furrow-weeds,<br>With burdocks, hemlock, nettles, cuckoo-[[Flowers|flowers]],<br>Darnel, and all the idle weeds that grow<br>In our sustaining corn. The centuries send forth.<br>Search every acre in the high-grown field,<br>And bring him to our eye. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit one or more |
| [[Cordelia|CORDELIA]] | What can man’s wisdom<br>In the restoring his bereaved sense,<br>He that can help him<br>Take all my outward worth. |
| DOCTOR | There is means, madam.<br>Our foster-[[Nurse|nurse]] of [[Nature|nature]] is repose,<br>The which he lacks. That to provoke in him<br>Are many simples operative, whose power<br>Will close the eye of anguish. |
| [[Cordelia|CORDELIA]] | All blest [[Secrets|secrets]],<br>All you unpublished virtues of the earth,<br>[[Spring]] with my [[Tears|tears]], be aidant and remediate<br>In the good man’s distress!—Seek, seek for him,<br>Lest his ungoverned rage dissolve the life<br>That wants the means to lead it. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter a Messenger |
| MESSENGER | News, madam.<br>The British powers are marching hitherward. |
| [[Cordelia|CORDELIA]] | ’Tis known before; our preparation stands<br>In expectation of them.—O dear father,<br>It is thy business that I go about;<br>Therefore great France<br>My mourning and important [[Tears|tears]] hath pitied.<br>No blown [[Ambition|ambition]] doth our arms incite,<br>But [[Love|love]], dear [[Love|love]], and our aged father’s right.<br>Soon may I hear and see him! Exeunt |
