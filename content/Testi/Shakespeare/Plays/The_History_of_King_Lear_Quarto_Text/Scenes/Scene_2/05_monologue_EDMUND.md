| [[Edmund|EDMUND]] | This is the excellent foppery of the world: that when we are sick in fortune—often the surfeit of our own behaviour—we make guilty of our disasters the sun, the [[Moon|moon]], and the [[Stars|stars]], as if we were villains by necessity, fools by heavenly compulsion, knaves, thieves, and treacherers by spherical predominance, drunkards, liars, and adulterers by an enforced obedience of planetary influence, and all that we are evil in by a divine thrusting on. An admirable evasion of whoremaster man, to lay his goatish disposition to the charge of [[Stars|stars]]! My father compounded with my mother under the Dragon’s tail and my nativity was under Ursa Major, so that it follows I am rough and lecherous. Fut! I should have been that I am had the maidenliest star of the firmament twinkled on my bastardy. [[Edgar]] ... Enter [[Edgar]] and on’s cue out he comes, like the catastrophe of the old [[Comedy|comedy]]; mine is villainous [[Melancholy|melancholy]], with a sigh like them of Bedlam.—O, these eclipses do portend these divisions. |
