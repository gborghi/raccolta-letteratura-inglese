| Chi parla | Battuta |
|---|---|
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[King Lear]], the Earl of [[Kent]] disguised, and Lear’s [[Fool]] |
| *(didascalia)* | LEAR to [[Kent]] Go you before to [[Gloucester]] with these letters. Acquaint my daughter no further with anything you know than comes from her demand out of the letter. If your diligence be not speedy, I shall be there before you. |
| [[Kent|KENT]] | I will not [[Sleep|sleep]], my lord, till I have delivered your letter. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit |
| [[Fool|FOOL]] | If a man’s brains were in his heels, were’t not in danger of kibes? |
| LEAR | Ay, boy. |
| [[Fool|FOOL]] | Then, I prithee, be merry: thy wit shall ne’er go slipshod. |
| LEAR | Ha, ha, ha! |
| [[Fool|FOOL]] | Shalt see thy other daughter will use thee kindly, for though she’s as like this as a crab is like an apple, yet I con what I can tell. |
| LEAR | Why, what canst thou tell, my boy? |
| [[Fool|FOOL]] | Why, to keep his eyes on either side ’s nose, that what a man cannot smell out, a may spy into. |
| LEAR | I did her wrong. |
| [[Fool|FOOL]] | Canst tell how an oyster makes his shell? |
| LEAR | No. |
| [[Fool|FOOL]] | Nor I neither; but I can tell why a snail has a [[house]]. |
| LEAR | Why? |
| [[Fool|FOOL]] | Why, to put his head in, not to give it away to his daughter and leave his horns without a case. |
| LEAR | I will forget my [[Nature|nature]]. So kind a father!<br>Be my horses ready? |
| [[Fool|FOOL]] | Thy asses are gone about them. The reason why the seven [[Stars|stars]] are no more than seven is a pretty reason. |
| LEAR | Because they are not eight. |
| [[Fool|FOOL]] | Yes. Thou wouldst make a good [[Fool|fool]]. |
| LEAR | To take’t again perforce—[[Monster|monster]] ingratitude! |
| [[Fool|FOOL]] | If thou wert my [[Fool|fool]], nuncle, I’d have thee beaten for being old before thy [[Time|time]]. |
| LEAR | How’s that? |
| [[Fool|FOOL]] | Thou shouldst not have been old before thou hadst been wise. |
| LEAR | O, let me not be mad, sweet heaven!<br>I would not be mad.<br>Keep me in temper. I would not be mad. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter a Servant |
| LEAR | Are the horses ready? |
| SERVANT | Ready, my lord. |
| LEAR | (to [[Fool]]) Come, boy. Exeunt Lear and Servant |
| [[Fool|FOOL]] | She that is maid now, and laughs at my departure,<br>Shall not be a maid long, except things be cut shorter. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit |
