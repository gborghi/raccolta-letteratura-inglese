| *(didascalia)* | Alarum. The powers of France pass over the stage led by Queen Cordelia with her father in her hand. Then enter Edgar disguised as a peasant, guiding the blind Duke of Gloucester |
| EDGAR | Here, father, take the shadow of this bush<br>For your good host; pray that the right may thrive.<br>If ever I return to you again<br>I’ll bring you comfort. Exit |
| GLOUCESTER | Grace go with you, sir. |
| *(didascalia)* | Alarum and retreat. Enter Edgar |
| EDGAR | Away, old man. Give me thy hand. Away.<br>King Lear hath lost, he and his daughter ta’en.<br>Give me thy hand. Come on. |
| GLOUCESTER | No farther, sir. A man may rot even here. |
| EDGAR | What, in ill thoughts again? Men must endure<br>Their going hence even as their coming hither.<br>Ripeness is all. Come on. Exit Edgar guiding Gloucester |
