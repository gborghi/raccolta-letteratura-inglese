| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Edmund]] the bastard, and Curan, meeting |
| [[Edmund|EDMUND]] | Save thee, Curan. |
| CURAN | And you, sir. I have been with your father, and given him notice that the Duke of Cornwall and his duchess will be here with him tonight. |
| [[Edmund|EDMUND]] | How comes that? |
| CURAN | Nay, I know not. You have heard of the news abroad?—I mean the whispered ones, for there are yet but ear-bussing arguments. |
| [[Edmund|EDMUND]] | Not. I pray you, what are they? |
| CURAN | Have you heard of no likely wars towards twixt the two Dukes of Cornwall and Albany? |
| [[Edmund|EDMUND]] | Not a word. |
| CURAN | You may then in [[Time|time]]. Fare you [[Well|well]], sir. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit |
| [[Edmund|EDMUND]] | The Duke be here tonight! The better, best.<br>This weaves itself perforce into my business. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Edgar]] at a [[Window|window]] above |
| [[Edmund|EDMUND]] | My father hath set guard to take my brother,<br>And I have one thing of a queasy question<br>Which must ask briefness. Wit and fortune help!—<br>Brother, a word. Descend, brother, I say. |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Edgar]] climbs down |
| [[Edmund|EDMUND]] | My father watches. O, fly this place.<br>Intelligence is given where you are hid.<br>You have now the good advantage of the [[Night|night]].<br>Have you not spoken ‘gainst the Duke of Cornwall<br>aught?<br>He’s coming hither now, in the [[Night|night]], i’th’ haste,<br>And [[Regan]] with him. Have you nothing said<br>Upon his party against the Duke of Albany?<br>Advise you— |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | I am sure on’t, not a word. |
| [[Edmund|EDMUND]] | I hear my father coming. Pardon me.<br>In cunning I must draw my [[Sword|sword]] upon you.<br>Seem to defend yourself. Now, quit you [[Well|well]].<br>(Calling) Yield, come before my father. Light here,<br>here!<br>(To [[Edgar]]) Fly, brother, fly! (Calling) Torches, torches!<br>(To [[Edgar]]) So, farewell. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit [[Edgar]] |
| [[Edmund|EDMUND]] | Some [[Blood|blood]] drawn on me would beget opinion<br>Of my more fierce endeavour.<br>He wounds his arm<br>I have seen<br>Drunkards do more than this in sport. (Calling) Father,<br>father!<br>Stop, stop! Ho, help! |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter the [[Duke of Gloucester]] and others |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Now, [[Edmund]], where is the [[Villain|villain]]? |
| [[Edmund|EDMUND]] | Here stood he in the dark, his sharp [[Sword|sword]] out,<br>Warbling of wicked charms, conjuring the [[Moon|moon]]<br>To stand ’s auspicious mistress. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | But where is he? |
| [[Edmund|EDMUND]] | Look, sir, I bleed. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Where is the [[Villain|villain]], [[Edmund]]? |
| [[Edmund|EDMUND]] | Fled this way, sir, when by no means he could— |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Pursue him, go after. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt others |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | By no means what? |
| [[Edmund|EDMUND]] | Persuade me to the murder of your lordship,<br>But that I told him the revengive gods<br>’Gainst parricides did all their thunders bend,<br>Spoke with how manifold and strong a bond<br>The [[Child|child]] was bound to the father. Sir, in fine,<br>Seeing how loathly opposite I stood<br>To his unnatural purpose, with fell motion,<br>With his prepared [[Sword|sword]] he charges [[Home|home]]<br>My unprovided [[Body|body]], lanced mine arm;<br>But when he saw my best alarumed spirits<br>Bold in the quarrel’s rights, roused to the encounter,<br>Or whether ghasted by the noise I made |
| *(didascalia)* | Or   I know not, |
| [[Edmund|EDMUND]] | But suddenly he fled. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Let him fly far,<br>Not in this land shall he remain uncaught,<br>And found, dispatch. The noble Duke my master,<br>My worthy arch and patron, comes tonight.<br><br>By his authority I will proclaim it<br>That he which finds him shall deserve our thanks,<br>Bringing the murderous caitiff to the stake;<br>He that conceals him, [[Death|death]]. |
| [[Edmund|EDMUND]] | When I dissuaded him from his intent<br>And found him pitched to do it, with curst speech<br>I threatened to discover him. He replied,<br>‘Thou unpossessing bastard, dost thou think<br>If I would stand against thee, could the reposure<br>Of any trust, virtue, or worth in thee<br>Make thy words faithed? No, what I should deny—<br>As this I would, ay, though thou didst produce<br>My very character—I’d turn it all<br>To thy suggestion, plot, and damned pretence,<br>And thou must make a dullard of the world<br>If they not thought the profits of my [[Death|death]]<br>Were very pregnant and potential spurs<br>To make thee seek it.’ |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Strong and fastened [[Villain|villain]]!<br>Would he deny his letter? I never got him. |
| *(didascalia)* | Trumpets within |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Hark, the Duke’s trumpets. I know not why he comes.<br>All ports I’ll bar. The [[Villain|villain]] shall not scape.<br>The Duke must grant me that; besides, his picture<br>I will send far and near, that all the kingdom<br>May have note of him—and of my land,<br>Loyal and natural boy, I’ll work the means<br>To make thee capable. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter the Duke |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | of<br>Cornwall and [[Regan]] |
| CORNWALL | How now, my noble friend? Since I came hither,<br>Which I can call but now, I have heard strange news. |
| [[Regan|REGAN]] | If it be true, all vengeance comes too short<br>Which can pursue the offender. How dost, my lord? |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Madam, my old heart is cracked, is cracked. |
| [[Regan|REGAN]] | What, did my father’s godson seek your life?<br>He whom my father named, your [[Edgar]]? |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Ay, lady, lady; shame would have it hid. |
| [[Regan|REGAN]] | Was he not companion with the riotous knights<br>That tend upon my father? |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | I know not, madam. ’Tis too bad, too bad. |
| [[Edmund|EDMUND]] | Yes, madam, he was. |
| [[Regan|REGAN]] | No marvel, then, though he were ill affected.<br>’Tis they have put him on the old man’s [[Death|death]],<br>To have the spoil and waste of his revenues.<br>I have this present evening from my sister<br>Been [[Well|well]] informed of them, and with such cautions<br>That if they come to sojourn at my [[house]]<br>I’ll not be there. |
| CORNWALL | Nor I, assure thee, [[Regan]].<br>[[Edmund]], I heard that you have shown your father<br>A childlike office. |
| [[Edmund|EDMUND]] | ’Twas my duty, sir. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | (to Cornwall)<br>He did betray his practice, and received<br>This hurt you see striving to apprehend him. |
| CORNWALL | Is he pursued? |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Ay, my good lord. |
| CORNWALL | If he be taken, he shall never more<br>Be feared of doing harm. Make your own purpose<br>How in my strength you please. For you, [[Edmund]],<br>Whose virtue and obedience doth this instant<br>So much commend itself, you shall be ours.<br>Natures of such deep trust we shall much need.<br>You we first seize on. |
| [[Edmund|EDMUND]] | I shall serve you truly,<br>However else. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | (to Cornwall) For him I thank your grace. |
| CORNWALL | You know not why we came to visit you— |
| [[Regan|REGAN]] | This out-of-season threat’ning dark-eyed [[Night|night]]-<br>Occasions, noble [[Gloucester]], of some poise,<br>Wherein we must have use of your advice.<br>Our father he hath writ, so hath our sister,<br>Of differences which I least thought it fit<br>To answer from our [[Home|home]]. The several messengers<br>From hence attend dispatch. Our good old friend,<br>Lay comforts to your bosom, and bestow<br>Your needful counsel to our business,<br>Which craves the instant use. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | I serve you, madam.<br>Your graces are right welcome. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt |
