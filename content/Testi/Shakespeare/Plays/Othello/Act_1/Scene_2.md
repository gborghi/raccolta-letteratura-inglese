| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Othello]], [[Iago]], and attendants with torches |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Though in the trade of war I have slain men,<br>Yet do I hold it very stuff o’th’ [[Conscience|conscience]]<br>To do no contrived murder. I lack iniquity,<br>Sometime, to do me service. Nine or ten times<br>I had thought to’ve yerked him here, under the ribs. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | ’Tis better as it is. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Nay, but he prated,<br>And spoke such scurvy and provoking terms<br>Against your [[Honour|honour]]<br>That, with the little godliness I have,<br>I did full hard forbear him. But I pray you, sir,<br>Are you fast married? Be assured of this:<br>That the magnifico is much beloved,<br>And hath in his effect a voice potential<br>As [[Double|double]] as the Duke’s. He will [[divorce]] you,<br>Or put upon you what restraint or grievance<br>The law, with all his might to enforce it on,<br>Will give him cable. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Let him do his spite.<br>My services which I have done the signory<br>Shall out-tongue his complaints. ’Tis yet to know—<br>Which, when I know that boasting is an [[Honour|honour]],<br>I shall promu]gate—I fetch my life and being<br>From men of royal siege, and my demerits<br>May speak unbonneted to as proud a fortune<br>As this that I have reached. For know, [[Iago]],<br>But that I [[Love|love]] the gentle [[Desdemona]]<br>I would not my unhousèd free condition<br>Put into circumscription and confine<br>For the seas’ worth. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Cassio]] and officers, with torches |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | But look, what lights come yond? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Those are the raised father and his friends.<br>You were best go in. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Not I. I must be found.<br>My parts, my title, and my perfect [[Soul|soul]]<br>Shall manifest me rightly. Is it they? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | By Janus, I think no. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | The servants of the Duke, and my lieutenant!<br>The goodness of the [[Night|night]] upon you, friends.<br>What is the news? |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | The Duke does greet you, general,<br>And he requires your haste-post-haste appearance<br>Even on the instant. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | What is the matter, think you? |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | Something from Cyprus, as I may divine;<br>It is a business of some heat. The galleys<br>Have sent a dozen sequent messengers<br>This very [[Night|night]] at one another’s heels,<br>And many of the consuls, raised and met,<br>Are at the Duke’s already. You have been hotly called<br>for,<br>When, being not at your lodging to be found,<br>The senate sent about three several quests<br>To search you out. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | ’Tis [[Well|well]] I am found by you.<br>I will but spend a word here in the [[house]]<br>And go with you. Exit |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | Ensign, what makes he here? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | [[Faith]], he tonight hath boarded a land-carrack.<br>If it prove lawful prize, he’s made for ever. |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | I do not understand. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | He’s married. |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | To who? |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | Brabanzio, [[Roderigo]], and officers, with lights<br><br>and weapons |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Marry, to— |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Othello]] |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | (To [[Othello]]) Come, captain, will you go? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Have with you. |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | Here comes another troop to seek for you. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | It is Brabanzio. General, be advised.<br>He comes to bad intent. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Holla, stand, there! |
| [[Roderigo|RODERIGO]] | (to Brabanzio)<br>Signor, it is the Moor. |
| BRABANZIO | Down with him, thief! |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | (drawing his [[Sword|sword]])<br>You, [[Roderigo]]? Come, sir, I am for you. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Keep up your bright swords, for the dew will rust ’em.<br>(To Brabanzio) Good signor, you shall more command<br>with years<br>Than with your weapons. |
| BRABANZIO | O thou foul thief, where hast thou stowed my<br>daughter?<br>Damned as thou art, thou hast enchanted her,<br>For I’ll refer me to all things of sense,<br>If she in [[Chains|chains]] of [[Magic|magic]] were not bound,<br>Whether a maid so tender, fair, and happy,<br>So opposite to [[Marriage|marriage]] that she shunned<br>The wealthy curled darlings of our nation,<br>Would ever have, t‘incur a general mock,<br>Run from her guardage to the sooty bosom<br>Of such a thing as thou—to [[Fear|fear]], not to delight.<br>Judge me the world if ’tis not gross in sense<br>That thou hast practised on her with foul charms,<br>Abused her delicate youth with drugs or minerals<br>That weakens motion. I’ll have’t disputed on.<br>’Tis probable, and palpable to thinking.<br>I therefore apprehend and do attach thee<br>For an abuser of the world, a practiser<br>Of arts inhibited and out of warrant.<br>(To officers) Lay hold upon him. If he do resist,<br>Subdue him at his peril. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Hold your hands,<br>Both you of my inclining and the rest.<br>Were it my cue to fight, I should have known it<br>Without a prompter. Whither will you that I go<br>To answer this your charge? |
| BRABANZIO | To [[Prison|prison]], till fit [[Time|time]]<br>Of law and course of direct session<br>Call thee to answer. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | What if I do obey?<br>How may the Duke be therewith satisfied,<br>Whose messengers are here about my side<br>Upon some present business of the state<br>To bring me to him? |
| OFFICER | (to Brabanzio) ’Tis true, most worthy signor.<br>The Duke’s in council, and your noble [[Self|self]],<br>I am sure, is sent for. |
| BRABANZIO | How, the Duke in council?<br>In this [[Time|time]] of the [[Night|night]]? Bring him away.<br>Mine’s not an idle cause. The Duke himself,<br>Or any of my brothers of the state,<br>Cannot but feel this wrong as ’twere their own;<br>For if such actions may have passage free,<br>Bondslaves and pagans shall our statesmen be. Exeunt |
