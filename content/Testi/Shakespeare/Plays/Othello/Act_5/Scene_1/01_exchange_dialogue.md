| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Iago]] and [[Roderigo]] |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Here, stand behind this bulk. Straight will he come. Wear thy good rapier bare, and put it [[Home|home]]. Quick, quick, [[Fear|fear]] nothing. I’ll be at thy elbow. It makes us or it mars us. Think on that, And fix most firm thy resolution. |
| [[Roderigo|RODERIGO]] | Be near at hand. I may miscarry in’t. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Here at thy hand. Be bold, and take thy stand. |
| [[Roderigo|RODERIGO]] | (aside) I have no great devotion to the deed, And yet he hath given me satisfying reasons. ’Tis but a man gone. Forth my [[Sword|sword]]—he dies! |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | (aside) I have rubbed this young quat almost to the sense, And he grows angry. Now, whether he kill [[Cassio]] Or [[Cassio]] him, or each do kill the other, Every way makes my gain. live [[Roderigo]], He calls me to a restitution large Of gold and jewels that I bobbed from him As gifts to [[Desdemona]]. It must not be. If [[Cassio]] do remain, He hath a daily [[Beauty|beauty]] in his life That makes me ugly; and besides, the Moor May unfold me to him-there stand I in much peril. No, he must die. But so, I hear him coming. Enter [[Cassio]] |
| [[Roderigo|RODERIGO]] | I know his gait, ’tis he. (Attacking [[Cassio]]) [[Villain]], thou diest. |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | That thrust had been mine enemy indeed, But that my coat is better than thou know’st. I will make proof of thine. He stabs [[Roderigo]], who falls |
| [[Roderigo|RODERIGO]] | O, I am slain! [[Iago]] wounds [[Cassio]] in the leg from behind. Exit [[Iago]] |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | (falling) I am maimed for ever. Help, ho, murder, murder! Enter [[Othello]] [above] |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | The voice of [[Cassio]]. [[Iago]] keeps his word. |
| [[Roderigo|RODERIGO]] | O, [[Villain|villain]] that I am! |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | It is even so. |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | O, help, ho! Light, a surgeon! |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | ’Tis he. O brave [[Iago]], honest and just, That hast such noble sense of thy friend’s wrong— Thou teachest me. Minion, your dear lies dead, And your unblessed fate hies. Strumpet, I come. Forth of my heart those charms, thine eyes, are blotted. Thy bed, lust-stained, shall with lust’s [[Blood|blood]] be spotted. Exit Enter Lodovico and Graziano |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | What ho, no watch, no passage? Murder, murder! |
| GRAZIANO | ’Tis some mischance. The voice is very direful. |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | O, help! |
| LODOVICO | Hark. |
| [[Roderigo|RODERIGO]] | O wretched [[Villain|villain]]! |
| LODOVICO | Two or three groan. ’Tis heavy [[Night|night]]. These may be counterfeits. Let’s think’t unsafe To come into the cry without more help. |
| [[Roderigo|RODERIGO]] | Nobody come? Then shall I bleed to [[Death|death]]. Enter [[Iago]] with a light |
| LODOVICO | Hark. |
| GRAZIANO | Here’s one comes in his shirt, with light and weapons. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Who’s there? Whose noise is this that cries on murder? |
| LODOVICO | We do not know. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Do not you hear a cry? |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | Here, here. For heaven’s sake, help me. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | What’s the matter? |
| GRAZIANO | (to Lodovico) This is [[Othello]]’s ensign, as I take it. |
| LODOVICO | The same indeed, a very valiant fellow. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | (to [[Cassio]]) What are you here that cry so grievously? |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | [[Iago]]—O, I am spoiled, undone by villains. Give me some help. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | O me, lieutenant, what villains have done this? |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | I think that one of them is hereabout And cannot make away. |
