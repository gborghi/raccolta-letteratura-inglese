| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | It is too late. He smothers her |
| DESDEMONAO | Lord, Lord, Lord! [[Emilia]] at the door |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | (within) My lord, my lord, what ho, my lord, my lord! |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | What noise is this? Not dead? Not yet quite dead? I that am cruel am yet merciful. I would not have thee linger in thy [[Pain|pain]]. So, so. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | (within) What ho, my lord, my lord! OTHELLOWho’s there? |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | (within) O, good my lord, I would speak a word with you. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | (calling) Yes.—‘Tis [[Emilia]]. (Calling) By and by!—She’s dead. ’Tis like she comes to speak of [[Cassio]]’s [[Death|death]]. The noise was high. Ha! No more moving. Still as the [[Grave|grave]]. Shall she come in? Were’t good? I think she stirs again. No. What’s best to do? If she come in, she’ll sure speak to my wife. My wife, my wife! What wife? I ha’ no wife. O insupportable, O heavy hour! Methinks it should be now a huge eclipse Of sun and [[Moon|moon]], and that th’affrighted globe Should yawn at alteration. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | (within) I do beseech you That I may speak with you. O, good my lord! |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | I had forgot thee.—O, come in, [[Emilia]].— Soft, by and by. Let me the curtains draw. He closes the bed-curtains Where art thou? He opens the door. Enter [[Emilia]] What’s the matter with thee now? |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | O my good lord, yonder’s foul murder’s done. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | What, now? |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | But now, my lord. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | It is the very error of the [[Moon|moon]], She comes more nearer earth than she was wont, And makes men mad. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | [[Cassio]], my lord, hath killed a young Venetian Called [[Roderigo]]. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | [[Roderigo]] killed And [[Cassio]] killed? |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | No, [[Cassio]] is not killed. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Not [[Cassio]] killed? Then murder’s out of tune, And sweet [[Revenge|revenge]] grows harsh. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | O, falsely, falsely murdered! |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | O Lord, what cry is that? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | That? What? |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | Out and alas, that was my lady’s voice! She opens the bed-curtains Help, help, ho, help ! O lady, speak again! Sweet [[Desdemona]], O sweet mistress, speak! |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | A guiltless [[Death|death]] I die. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | O, who hath done this deed? |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Nobody, I myself. Farewell. Commend me to my kind lord. O, farewell! She dies |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Why, how should she be murdered? |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | Alas, who knows? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | You heard her say herself it was not I. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | She said so, I must needs report the [[Truth|truth]]. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | She’s like a liar gone to burning hell. ’Twas I that killed her. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | O, the more angel she, and you the blacker devil! |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | She turned to folly, and she was a whore. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | Thou dost belie her, and thou art a devil. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | She was false as [[Water|water]]. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | Thou art rash as [[Fire|fire]] To say that she was false. O, she was heavenly true! |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | [[Cassio]] did top her. Ask thy husband else. O, I were damned beneath all depth in hell But that I did proceed upon just grounds To this extremity! Thy husband knew it all. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | My husband? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Thy husband. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | That she was false to wedlock? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Ay, with [[Cassio]]. Had she been true, If heaven would make me such another world Of one entire and perfect chrysolite I’d not have sold her for it. |
