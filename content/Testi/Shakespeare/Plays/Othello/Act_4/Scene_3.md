| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Othello]], [[Desdemona]], Lodovico, [[Emilia]], and attendants |
| LODOVICO | I do beseech you, sir, trouble yourself no further. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | O, pardon me, ’twill do me good to walk. |
| LODOVICO | (to [[Desdemona]])<br>Madam, good [[Night|night]]. I humbly thank your ladyship. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Your [[Honour|honour]] is most welcome. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Will you walk, sir?<br>O, [[Desdemona]]! |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | My lord? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Get you to bed on th’instant. I will be returned forthwith. Dismiss your attendant there. Look’t be done. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | I will, my lord. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt [[Othello]], Lodovico, and attendants |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | How goes it now? He looks gentler than he did. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | He says he will return incontinent.<br>He hath commanded me to go to bed,<br>And bid me to dismiss you. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | Dismiss me? |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | It was his bidding. Therefore, good [[Emilia]],<br>Give me my nightly wearing, and adieu. is<br>We must not now displease him. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | I would you had never seen him. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | So would not I. My [[Love|love]] doth so approve him<br>That even his stubbornness, his checks, his frowns—<br>Prithee unpin me—have grace and favour in them.<br>[[Emilia]] helps [[Desdemona]] to undress |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | I have laid those sheets you bade me on the bed. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | All’s one. Good [[Faith|faith]], how foolish are our minds!<br>If I do die before thee, prithee shroud me<br>In one of these same sheets. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | Come, come, you talk. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | My mother had a maid called Barbary.<br>She was in [[Love|love]], and he she loved proved mad<br>And did forsake her. She had a [[Song|song]] of willow.<br>An old thing ’twas, but it expressed her fortune,<br>And she died singing it. That [[Song|song]] tonight<br>Will not go from my mind. I have much to do<br>But to go hang my head all at one side<br>And sing it, like poor Barbary. Prithee, dispatch. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | Shall I go fetch your nightgown? |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | No. Unpin me here.<br>This Lodovico is a proper man. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | A very handsome man. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | He speaks [[Well|well]]. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | I know a lady in [[Venice]] would have walked barefoot to Palestine for a touch of his nether lip. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | (sings)<br>‘The poor [[Soul|soul]] sat sighing by a sycamore tree,<br>Sing all a green willow.<br>Her hand on her bosom, her head on her knee,<br>Sing willow, willow, willow.<br>The fresh streams ran by her and murmured her<br>moans,<br>Sing willow, willow, willow.<br>Her salt [[Tears|tears]] fell from her and softened the stones,<br>Sing willow’—<br>Lay by these.—<br>‘willow, willow.’<br>Prithee, hie thee. He’ll come anon.<br>‘Sing all a green willow must be my garland.<br>‘Let nobody blame him, his scorn I approve’—<br>Nay, that’s not next. Hark, who is’t that knocks? |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | It’s the [[Wind|wind]]. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | (sings)<br>‘I called my [[Love|love]] false [[Love|love]], but what said he then?<br>Sing willow, willow, willow.<br>If I [[Court|court]] more women, you’ll couch with more men.’<br>So, get thee gone. Good [[Night|night]]. Mine eyes do itch. 56<br>Doth that bode weeping? |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | ’Tis neither here nor there. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | I have heard it said so. O, these men, these men!<br>Dost thou in [[Conscience|conscience]] think—ten me, [[Emilia]]—<br>That there be women do abuse their husbands<br>In such gross kind?<br>EMILIAThere be some such, no question. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Wouldst thou do such a deed for all the world? |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | Why, would not you? |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | No, by this heavenly light. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | Nor I neither, by this heavenly light. I might do’t<br>as [[Well|well]] i’th’ dark. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Wouldst thou do such a deed for all the world? |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | The world’s a huge thing. It is a great price for<br>a small vice. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | In [[Truth|truth]], I think thou wouldst not. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | In [[Truth|truth]], I think I should, and undo’t when I had done. Marry, I would not do such a thing for a joint ring, nor for measures of lawn, nor for gowns, petticoats, nor caps, nor any petty exhibition; but for all the whole world? Ud’s pity, who would not make her husband a cuckold to make him a monarch? I should venture purgatory for’t. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Beshrew me if I would do such a wrong<br>For the whole world. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | Why, the wrong is but a wrong i‘th’ world, and having the world for your labour, ’tis a wrong in your own world, and you might quickly make it right. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | I do not think there is any such woman. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | Yes, a dozen, and as many<br>To th’ vantage as would store the world they played<br>for.<br>But I do think it is their husbands’ faults<br>If wives do fall. Say that they slack their duties,<br>And pour our treasures into foreign laps,<br>Or else break out in peevish jealousies,<br>Throwing restraint upon us; or say they strike us,<br>Or scant our former having in despite:<br>Why, we have galls; and though we have some grace,<br>Yet have we some [[Revenge|revenge]]. Let husbands know<br>Their wives have sense like them. They see, and smell,<br>And have their palates both for sweet and sour,<br>As husbands have. What is it that they do<br>When they change us for others? Is it sport?<br>I think it is. And doth affection breed it?<br>I think it doth. Is’t frailty that thus errs?<br>It is so, too. And have not we affections,<br>Desires for sport, and frailty, as men have?<br>Then let them use us [[Well|well]], else let them know<br>The ills we do, their ills instruct us so. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Good [[Night|night]], good [[Night|night]]. God me such uses send<br>Not to pick bad from bad, but by bad mend! Exeunt |
