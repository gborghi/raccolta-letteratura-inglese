| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | I will chop her into messes. Cuckold me! |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | O, ’tis foul in her. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | With mine officer. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | That’s fouler. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Get me some [[Poison|poison]], [[Iago]], this [[Night|night]]. I’ll not expostulate with her, lest her [[Body|body]] and [[Beauty|beauty]] unprovide my mind again. This [[Night|night]], [[Iago]]. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Do it not with [[Poison|poison]]. Strangle her in her bed, even the bed she hath contaminated. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Good, good, the justice of it pleases, very good. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | And for [[Cassio]], let me be his undertaker. You shall hear more by midnight. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Excellent good. A trumpet What trumpet is that same? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | I warrant, something from [[Venice]]. Enter Lodovico, [[Desdemona]], and attendants ’Tis Lodovico. This comes from the Duke. See, your wife’s with him. |
| LODOVICO | God save the worthy general. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | With all my heart, sir. |
| LODOVICO | (giving [[Othello]] a letter) The Duke and the senators of [[Venice]] greet you. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | I kiss the instrument of their pleasures. He reads the letter |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | And what’s the news, good cousin Lodovico? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | (to Lodovico) I am very glad to see you, signor. Welcome to Cyprus. |
| LODOVICO | I thank you. How does Lieutenant [[Cassio]]? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Lives, sir. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Cousin, there’s fall’n between him and my lord An unkind breach. But you shall make all [[Well|well]]. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Are you sure of that? |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | My lord. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | (reads) ‘This fail you not to do as you will’— |
| LODOVICO | He did not call, he’s busy in the paper. Is there division ’twixt my lord and [[Cassio]]? |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | A most unhappy one. I would do much T’atone them, for the [[Love|love]] I bear to [[Cassio]]. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | [[Fire]] and brimstone! |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | My lord? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Are you wise? |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | What, is he angry? |
| LODOVICO | Maybe the letter moved him, For, as I think, they do command him [[Home|home]], Deputing [[Cassio]] in his [[government]]. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | By my troth, I am glad on’t. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Indeed! |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | My lord? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | (to [[Desdemona]]) I am glad to see you mad. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Why, sweet [[Othello]]! |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Devil! He strikes her |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | I have not deserved this. |
| LODOVICO | My lord, this would not be believed in [[Venice]], Though I should swear I saw’t. ’Tis very much. Make her amends, she weeps. OTHELLOO, devil, devil! If that the earth could teem with woman’s [[Tears|tears]], Each drop she falls would prove a crocodile. Out of my sight! |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | (going) I will not stay to offend you. |
| LODOVICO | Truly, an obedient lady. I do beseech your lordship call her back. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Mistress! |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | (returning) My lord? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | (to Lodovico) What would you with her, sir? |
| LODOVICO | Who, I, my lord? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Ay, you did wish that I would make her turn. Sir; she can turn and turn, and yet go on And turn again, and she can weep, sir, weep, And she’s obedient, as you say, obedient, Very obedient. (To [[Desdemona]]) Proceed you in your [[Tears|tears]]. (To Lodovico) Concerning this, sir—(To [[Desdemona]]) O [[Well|well]] painted passion! (To Lodovico) I am commanded [[Home|home]]. (To [[Desdemona]]) Get you away. I’ll send for you anon. (To Lodovico) Sir, I obey the mandate, And will return to [[Venice]]. (To [[Desdemona]]) Hence, avaunt! Exit [[Desdemona]] (To Lodovico) [[Cassio]] shall have my place, and, sir, tonight I do entreat that we may sup together. You are welcome, sir, to Cyprus. Goats and monkeys! Exit |
