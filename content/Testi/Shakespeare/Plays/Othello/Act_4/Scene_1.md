| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Iago]] and [[Othello]] |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Will you think so? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Think so, [[Iago]]? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | What, to kiss in private? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | An unauthorized kiss. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Or to be naked with her friend in bed<br>An hour or more, not meaning any harm? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Naked in bed, [[Iago]], and not mean harm?<br>It is hypocrisy against the devil.<br>They that mean virtuously and yet do so,<br>The devil their virtue tempts, and they tempt heaven. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | If they do nothing, ’tis a venial slip.<br>But if I give my wife a handkerchief— |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | What then? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Why then, ’tis hers, my lord, and being hers,<br>She may, I think, bestow’t on any man. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | She is protectress of her [[Honour|honour]], too.<br>May she give that? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Her [[Honour|honour]] is an essence that’s not seen.<br>They have it very oft that have it not.<br>But for the handkerchief— |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | By heaven, I would most gladly have forgot it.<br>Thou said‘st—O, it comes o’er my [[Memory|memory]]<br>As doth [[The Raven|the raven]] o’er the infectious [[house]],<br>Boding to all!—he had my handkerchief. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Ay, what of that? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | That’s not so good now. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | What if I had said I had seen him do you wrong,<br>Or heard him say—as knaves be such abroad,<br>Who having by their own importunate suit<br>Or voluntary dotage of some mistress<br>Convinced or supplied them, cannot choose<br>But they must blab— |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Hath he said anything? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | He hath, my lord. But, be you [[Well|well]] assured,<br>No more than he’ll unswear. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | What hath he said? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | [[Faith]], that he did—I know not what he did. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | What, what? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Lie— |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | With her? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | With her, on her, what you will. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Lie with her? Lie on her? We say ‘lie on her’ when they belie her. Lie with her? ’Swounds, that’s fulsome! Handkerchief—confessions—hankerchief. To confess and be hanged for his labour. First to be hanged and then to confess! I tremble at it. [[Nature]] would not invest herself in such shadowing passion without some instruction. It is not words that shakes me thus. Pish! Noses, ears, and lips! Is’t possible? Confess? Handkerchief? O devil!<br>He falls down in a trance |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Work on; my medicine works. Thus credulous fools<br>are caught,<br>And many worthy and chaste dames even thus,<br>All guiltless, meet reproach. What ho, my lord!<br>My lord, I say. [[Othello]]! |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Cassio]] |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | How now, [[Cassio]]? |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | What’s the matter? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | My lord is fall’n into an epilepsy.<br>This is his second fit. He had one yesterday. |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | Rub him about the temples. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | No, forbear.<br>The lethargy must have his quiet course.<br>If not, he foams at mouth, and by and by<br>Breaks out to savage [[Madness|madness]]. Look, he stirs.<br>Do you withdraw yourself a little while,<br>He will recover straight. When he is gone<br>I would on great occasion speak with you. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit [[Cassio]] |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | How is it, general? Have you not hurt your head? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Dost thou mock me? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | I mock you not, by heaven.<br>Would you would bear your fortune like a man. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | A hornèd man’s a [[Monster|monster]] and a beast. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | There’s many a beast then in a populous [[City|city]],<br>And many a civil [[Monster|monster]]. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Did he confess it? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Good sir, be a man.<br>Think every bearded fellow that’s but yoked<br>May draw with you. There’s millions now alive<br>That nightly lie in those unproper beds<br>Which they dare swear peculiar. Your case is better.<br>O, ’tis the spite of hell, the fiend’s arch-mock,<br>To lip a wanton in a secure couch<br>And to suppose her chaste! No, let me know,<br>And knowing what I am, I know what she shall be. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | O, thou art wise, ’tis certain. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Stand you a while apart.<br>Confine yourself but in a patient list.<br>Whilst you were here, o‘erwhelmèd with your grief—<br>A passion most unsuiting such a man—<br>[[Cassio]] came hither. I shifted him away,<br>And laid good ’scuse upon your ecstasy,<br>Bade him anon return and here speak with me,<br>The which he promised. Do but encave yourself,<br>And mark the fleers, the gibes and notable scorns<br>That dwell in every region of his face.<br>For I will make him tell the tale anew,<br>Where, how, how oft, how long ago, and when<br>He hath and is again to cope your wife.<br>I say, but mark his gesture. Marry, patience,<br>Or I shall say you’re all-in-all in spleen,<br>And nothing of a man. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Dost thou hear, [[Iago]]?<br>I will be found most cunning in my patience,<br>But—dost thou hear?—most bloody. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | That’s not amiss,<br>But yet keep [[Time|time]] in all. Will you withdraw?<br>[[Othello]]<br>stands apart<br>Now will I question [[Cassio]] of [[Bianca]],<br>A hussy that by selling her desires<br>Buys herself bread and cloth. It is a creature<br>That dotes on [[Cassio]]—as ’tis the strumpet’s plague<br>To beguile many and be beguiled by one.<br>He, when he hears of her, cannot restrain<br>From the excess of laughter. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Cassio]] |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Here he comes.<br>As he shall smile, [[Othello]] shall go mad;<br>And his unbookish [[Jealousy|jealousy]] must conster<br>Poor [[Cassio]]’s smiles, gestures, and light behaviours<br>Quite in the wrong. How do you now, lieutenant? |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | The worser that you give me the addition<br>Whose want even kills me. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Ply [[Desdemona]] [[Well|well]] and you are sure on’t.<br>Now, if this suit lay in [[Bianca]]’s power,<br>How quickly should you [[Speed|speed]]! |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | (laughing) Alas, poor caitiff! |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | (aside) Look how he laughs already. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | I never knew a woman [[Love|love]] man so. |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | Alas, poor rogue! I think i’[[Faith|faith]] she loves me. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | (aside)<br>Now he denies it faintly, and laughs it out. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Do you hear, [[Cassio]]? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | (aside) Now he importunes him<br>To tell it o’er. Go to, [[Well|well]] said, [[Well|well]] said. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | She gives it out that you shall marry her.<br>Do you intend it? |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | Ha, ha, ha! |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | (aside)<br>Do ye triumph, Roman, do you triumph? |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | I marry! What, a customer? Prithee, bear some [[Charity|charity]] to my wit—do not think it so unwholesome. Ha, ha, ha! |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | (aside) So, so, so, so. They laugh that wins. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | [[Faith]], the cry goes that you marry her. |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | Prithee, say true. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | I am a very [[Villain|villain]] else. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | (aside) Ha’ you scored me? [[Well]]. 125 |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | This is the monkey’s own giving out. She is persuaded I will marry her out of her own [[Love|love]] and flattery, not out of my promise. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | (aside) [[Iago]] beckons me. Now he begins the story. [[Othello]] draws closer |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | She was here even now. She haunts me in every place. I was the other day talking on the sea-bank with certain Venetians, and thither comes the bauble, and falls me thus about my neck. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | (aside) Crying ‘O dear Cassiol’ as it were. His gesture imports it. |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | So hangs and lolls and weeps upon me, so shakes and pulls me—ha, ha, ha! |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | (aside) Now he tells how she plucked him to my chamber. O, I see that nose of yours, but not that dog I shall throw it to! |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | [[Well]], I must leave her company. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Bianca]] |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Before me, look where she comes. |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | ’Tis such another fitchew! Marry, a perfumed one. (To [[Bianca]]) What do you mean by this haunting of me? |
| [[Bianca|BIANCA]] | Let the devil and his dam haunt you. What did you mean by that same handkerchief you gave me even now? I was a fine [[Fool|fool]] to take it. I must take out the whole work—a likely piece of work, that you should find it in your chamber and know not who left it there. This is some minx’s token, and I must take out the work. There, give it your hobby-[[Horse|horse]]. (Giving [[Cassio]] the napkin) Wheresoever you had it, I’ll take out no work on’t. |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | How now, my sweet [[Bianca]], how now, how now? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | (aside)<br>By heaven, that should be my handkerchief. |
| [[Bianca|BIANCA]] | An you’ll come to supper tonight, you may. An you will not, come when you are next prepared for. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | After her, after her. |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | [[Faith]], I must, she’ll rail in the streets else. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Will you sup there? |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | [[Faith]], I intend so. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | [[Well]], I may chance to see you, for I would very fain speak with you. |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | Prithee, come, will you? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Go to, say no more. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit [[Cassio]] |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | How shall I murder him, [[Iago]]? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Did you perceive how he laughed at his vice? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | O [[Iago]]! |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | And did you see the handkerchief? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Was that mine? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Yours, by this hand. And to see how he prizes the foolish woman your wife. She gave it him, and he hath given it his whore. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | I would have him nine years a-killing. A fine woman, a fair woman, a sweet woman. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Nay, you must forget that. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Ay, let her rot and perish, and be damned tonight, for she shall not live. No, my heart is turned to stone; I strike it, and it hurts my hand. O, the world hath not a sweeter creature! She might lie by an emperor’s side, and command him tasks. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Nay, that’s not your way. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Hang her, I do but say what she is—so delicate with her needle, an admirable musician. O, she will sing the savageness out of a bear! Of so high and plenteous wit and invention. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | She’s the worse for all this. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | O, a thousand, a thousand times! And then of so gentle a condition. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Ay, too gentle. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Nay, that’s certain. But yet the pity of it, [[Iago]]. O, [[Iago]], the pity of it, [[Iago]]! |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | If you are so fond over her iniquity, give her patent to offend; for if it touch not you, it comes near nobody. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | I will chop her into messes. Cuckold me! |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | O, ’tis foul in her. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | With mine officer. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | That’s fouler. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Get me some [[Poison|poison]], [[Iago]], this [[Night|night]]. I’ll not expostulate with her, lest her [[Body|body]] and [[Beauty|beauty]] unprovide my mind again. This [[Night|night]], [[Iago]]. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Do it not with [[Poison|poison]]. Strangle her in her bed, even the bed she hath contaminated. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Good, good, the justice of it pleases, very good. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | And for [[Cassio]], let me be his undertaker. You shall hear more by midnight. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Excellent good.<br>A trumpet<br>What trumpet is that same? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | I warrant, something from [[Venice]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Lodovico, [[Desdemona]], and attendants |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | ’Tis Lodovico. This comes from the Duke. See, your wife’s with him. |
| LODOVICO | God save the worthy general. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | With all my heart, sir. |
| LODOVICO | (giving [[Othello]] a letter) The Duke and the senators of [[Venice]] greet you. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | I kiss the instrument of their pleasures.<br>He reads the letter |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | And what’s the news, good cousin Lodovico? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | (to Lodovico) I am very glad to see you, signor. Welcome to Cyprus. |
| LODOVICO | I thank you. How does Lieutenant [[Cassio]]? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Lives, sir. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Cousin, there’s fall’n between him and my lord<br>An unkind breach. But you shall make all [[Well|well]]. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Are you sure of that? |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | My lord. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | (reads) ‘This fail you not to do as you will’— |
| LODOVICO | He did not call, he’s busy in the paper.<br>Is there division ’twixt my lord and [[Cassio]]? |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | A most unhappy one. I would do much<br>T’atone them, for the [[Love|love]] I bear to [[Cassio]]. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | [[Fire]] and brimstone! |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | My lord? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Are you wise? |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | What, is he angry? |
| LODOVICO | Maybe the letter moved him,<br>For, as I think, they do command him [[Home|home]],<br>Deputing [[Cassio]] in his [[government]]. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | By my troth, I am glad on’t. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Indeed! |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | My lord? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | (to [[Desdemona]]) I am glad to see you mad. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Why, sweet [[Othello]]! |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Devil!<br>He strikes her |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | I have not deserved this. |
| LODOVICO | My lord, this would not be believed in [[Venice]],<br>Though I should swear I saw’t. ’Tis very much.<br>Make her amends, she weeps.<br>OTHELLOO, devil, devil!<br>If that the earth could teem with woman’s [[Tears|tears]],<br>Each drop she falls would prove a crocodile.<br>Out of my sight! |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | (going) I will not stay to offend you. |
| LODOVICO | Truly, an obedient lady.<br>I do beseech your lordship call her back. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Mistress! |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | (returning) My lord? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | (to Lodovico) What would you with her, sir? |
| LODOVICO | Who, I, my lord? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Ay, you did wish that I would make her turn.<br>Sir; she can turn and turn, and yet go on<br>And turn again, and she can weep, sir, weep,<br>And she’s obedient, as you say, obedient,<br>Very obedient. (To [[Desdemona]]) Proceed you in your<br>[[Tears|tears]].<br>(To Lodovico) Concerning this, sir—(To [[Desdemona]]) O<br>[[Well|well]] painted passion!<br>(To Lodovico) I am commanded [[Home|home]]. (To [[Desdemona]])<br>Get you away.<br>I’ll send for you anon. (To Lodovico) Sir, I obey the<br>mandate,<br>And will return to [[Venice]]. (To [[Desdemona]]) Hence,<br>avaunt! |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit [[Desdemona]] |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | (To Lodovico) [[Cassio]] shall have my place, and, sir,<br>tonight<br>I do entreat that we may sup together.<br>You are welcome, sir, to Cyprus. Goats and monkeys! |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit |
| LODOVICO | Is this the noble Moor whom our full senate<br>Call all-in-all sufficient? Is this the [[Nature|nature]]<br>Whom passion could not shake, whose solid virtue<br>The shot of accident nor dart of chance<br>Could neither graze nor pierce? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | He is much changed. |
| LODOVICO | Are his wits safe? Is he not light of brain? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | He’s that he is. I may not breathe my censure<br>What he might be. If what he might he is not,<br>I would to heaven he were. |
| LODOVICO | What, strike his wife! |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | [[Faith]], that was not so [[Well|well]]. Yet would I knew<br>That stroke would prove the worst. |
| LODOVICO | Is it his use,<br>Or did the letters work upon his [[Blood|blood]]<br>And new-create his fault?<br>IAGOAlas, alas.<br>It is not honesty in me to speak<br>What I have seen and known. You shall observe him,<br>And his own courses will denote him so<br>That I may save my speech. Do but go after,<br>And mark how he continues. |
| LODOVICO | I am sorry that I am deceived in him. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt |
