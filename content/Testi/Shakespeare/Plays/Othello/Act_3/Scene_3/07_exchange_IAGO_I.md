| [[Iago|IAGO]] | I am sorry to hear this. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | I had been happy if the general camp, Pioneers and all, had tasted her sweet [[Body|body]], So I had nothing known. O, now for ever Farewell the tranquil mind, farewell content, Farewell the plumèd troops and the big wars That makes [[Ambition|ambition]] virtue! O, farewell, Farewell the neighing steed and the shrill trump, The spirit-stirring drum, th‘ear-piercing fife, The royal banner, and all quality, [[Pride]], pomp, and circumstance of glorious war! And O, you mortal engines whose rude throats Th’immortal Jove’s dread clamours counterfeit, Farewell! [[Othello]]’s occupation’s gone. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Is’t possible, my lord? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | taking [[Iago]] by the throat [[Villain]], be sure thou prove my [[Love|love]] a whore. Be sure of it. Give me the ocular proof, Or, by the worth of mine eternal [[Soul|soul]], Thou hadst been better have been born a dog Than answer my waked wrath. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Is’t come to this? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Make me to see’t, or at the least so prove it That the probation bear no hinge nor loop To hang a [[Doubt|doubt]] on, or woe upon thy life. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | My noble lord. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | If thou dost slander her and torture me, Never pray more; abandon all remorse, On horror’s head horrors accumulate, Do deeds to make heaven weep, all earth amazed, For nothing canst thou to damnation add Greater than that. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | O grace, O heaven forgive me! Are you a man? Have you a [[Soul|soul]] or sense? God buy you, take mine office. O wretched [[Fool|fool]], That lov’st to make thine honesty a vice! O monstrous world, take note, take note, O world, To be direct and honest is not safe! I thank you for this profit, and from hence I’ll [[Love|love]] no friend, sith [[Love|love]] breeds such offence. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Nay, stay. Thou shouldst be honest. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | I should be wise, for honesty’s a [[Fool|fool]], And loses that it works for. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | By the world, I think my wife be honest, and think she is not. I think that thou art just, and think thou art not. I’ll have some proof. My name, that was as fresh As Dian’s visage, is now begrimed and black As mine own face. If there be cords, or knives, [[Poison]], or [[Fire|fire]], or suffocating streams, I’ll not endure it. Would I were satisfied! |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | I see, sir, you are eaten up with passion. I do repent me that I put it to you. You would be satisfied? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Would? Nay, and I will. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | And may. But how, how satisfied, my lord? Would you, the supervisor, grossly gape on, Behold her topped? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | [[Death]] and damnation! O! |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | It were a tedious difficulty, I think, To bring them to that prospect. Damn them then If ever mortal eyes do see them bolster More than their own! What then, how then? What shall I say? Where’s satisfaction? It is impossible you should see this, Were they as prime as goats, as hot as monkeys, As salt as wolves in [[Pride|pride]], and fools as gross As ignorance made drunk. But yet I say, If imputation, and strong circumstances Which lead directly to the door of [[Truth|truth]], Will give you satisfaction, you might ha’t. |
