| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Prithee, no more. Let him come when he will. I will deny thee nothing. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Why, this is not a boon. ’Tis as I should entreat you wear your gloves, Or feed on nourishing dishes, or keep you warm, Or sue to you to do a peculiar profit To your own person. Nay, when I have a suit Wherein I mean to touch your [[Love|love]] indeed, It shall be full of poise and difficult weight, And fearful to be granted. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | I will deny thee nothing, Whereon I do beseech thee grant me this: To leave me but a little to myself. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Shall I deny you? No. Farewell, my lord. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Farewell, my [[Desdemona]]. I’ll come to thee straight. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | [[Emilia]], come. (To [[Othello]]) Be as your fancies teach you. Whate’er you be, I am obedient. Exeunt [[Desdemona]] and [[Emilia]] |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Excellent wretch! Perdition catch my [[Soul|soul]] But I do [[Love|love]] thee, and when I [[Love|love]] thee not, Chaos is come again. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | My noble lord. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | What dost thou say, [[Iago]]? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Did [[Michael]] [[Cassio]], when you wooed my lady, Know of your [[Love|love]]? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | He did, from first to last. Why dost thou ask? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | But for a satisfaction of my thought, No further harm. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Why of thy thought, [[Iago]]? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | I did not think he had been acquainted with her. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | O yes, and went between us very oft. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Indeed? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Indeed? Ay, indeed. Discern’st thou aught in that? Is he not honest? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Honest, my lord? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Honest? Ay, honest. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | My lord, for aught I know. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | What dost thou think? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Think, my lord? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | ‘Think, my lord?’ By heaven, thou echo‘st me As if there were some [[Monster|monster]] in thy thought Too hideous to be shown! Thou dost mean something. I heard thee say even now thou liked’st not that, When [[Cassio]] left my wife. What didst not like? And when I told thee he was of my counsel In my whole course of wooing, thou cried‘st ‘Indeed?’ And didst contract and purse thy brow together As if thou then hadst shut up in thy brain Some horrible conceit. If thou dost [[Love|love]] me, Show me thy thought. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | My lord, you know I [[Love|love]] you. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | I think thou dost, And for I know thou‘rt full of [[Love|love]] and honesty, And weigh’st thy words before thou giv’st them breath, Therefore these stops of thine fright me the more; For such things in a false disloyal knave Are tricks of custom, but in a man that’s just, They’re close dilations, working from the heart That passion cannot rule. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | For [[Michael]] [[Cassio]], I dare be sworn I think that he is honest. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | I think so too. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Men should be what they seem, Or those that be not, would they might seem none. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Certain, men should be what they seem. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Why then, I think [[Cassio]]’s an honest man. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Nay, yet there’s more in this. I prithee speak to me as to thy thinkings, As thou dost ruminate, and give thy worst of thoughts The worst of words. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Good my lord, pardon me. Though I am bound to every act of duty, I am not bound to that all slaves are free to. Utter my thoughts? Why, say they are vile and false, As where’s that palace whereinto foul things Sometimes intrude not? Who has that breast so pure But some uncleanly apprehensions Keep leets and law-days, and in sessions sit With meditations lawful? |
