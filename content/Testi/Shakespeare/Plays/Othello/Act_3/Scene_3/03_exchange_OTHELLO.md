| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Thou dost conspire against thy friend, [[Iago]], If thou but think‘st him wronged and mak’st his ear A stranger to thy thoughts. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | I do beseech you, Though I perchance am vicious in my guess— As I confess it is my [[Nature|nature]]’s plague To spy into abuses, and oft my [[Jealousy|jealousy]] Shapes faults that are not—that your wisdom then, From one that so imperfectly conceits, Would take no notice, nor build yourself a trouble Out of his scattering and unsure observance. It were not for your quiet nor your good, Nor for my manhood, honesty, and wisdom, To let you know my thoughts. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | What dost thou mean? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Good name in man and woman, dear my lord, Is the immediate [[Jewel|jewel]] of their souls. Who steals my purse steals trash; ‘tis something, nothing; ’Twas mine, ’tis his, and has been slave to thousands. But he that filches from me my good name Robs me of that which not enriches him And makes me poor indeed. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | By heaven, I’ll know thy thoughts. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | You cannot, if my heart were in your hand; Nor shall not whilst ’tis in my custody. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Ha! |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | O, beware, my lord, of [[Jealousy|jealousy]]. It is the green-eyed [[Monster|monster]] which doth mock The meat it feeds on. That cuckold lives in bliss Who, certain of his fate, loves not his wronger. But O, what damned minutes tells he o’er Who dotes yet doubts, suspects yet fondly loves! |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | O misery! |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Poor and content is rich, and rich enough, But riches fineless is as poor as [[Winter|winter]] To him that ever fears he shall be poor. Good God the souls of all my tribe defend From [[Jealousy|jealousy]]! |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Why, why is this? Think‘st thou I’d make a life of [[Jealousy|jealousy]], To follow still the changes of the [[Moon|moon]] With fresh suspicions? No, to be once in [[Doubt|doubt]] Is once to be resolved. Exchange me for a goat When I shall turn the business of my [[Soul|soul]] To such exsufflicate and blowed surmises Matching thy inference. ’Tis not to make me jealous To say my wife is fair, feeds [[Well|well]], loves company, Is free of speech, sings, plays, and dances [[Well|well]]. Where virtue is, these are more virtuous, Nor from mine own weak merits will I draw The smallest [[Fear|fear]] or [[Doubt|doubt]] of her revolt, For she had eyes and chose me. No, [[Iago]], I’ll see before I [[Doubt|doubt]]; when I [[Doubt|doubt]], prove; And on the proof, there is no more but this: Away at once with [[Love|love]] or [[Jealousy|jealousy]]. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | I am glad of this, for now I shall have reason To show the [[Love|love]] and duty that I bear you With franker spirit. Therefore, as I am bound, Receive it from me. I speak not yet of proof. Look to your wife. Observe her [[Well|well]] with [[Cassio]]. Wear your eyes thus: not jealous, nor secure. I would not have your free and noble [[Nature|nature]] Out of [[Self|self]]-bounty be abused. Look to’t. I know our country disposition [[Well|well]]. In [[Venice]] they do let God see the pranks They dare not show their husbands; their best [[Conscience|conscience]] Is not to leave’t undone, but keep’t unknown. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Dost thou say so? |
