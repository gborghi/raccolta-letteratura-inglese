| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Desdemona]], [[Cassio]], and [[Emilia]] |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Be thou assured, good [[Cassio]], I will do All my abilities in thy behalf. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | Good madam, do. I warrant it grieves my husband As if the cause were his. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | O, that’s an honest fellow. Do not [[Doubt|doubt]], [[Cassio]], But I will have my lord and you again As friendly as you were. |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | Bounteous madam, Whatever shall become of [[Michael]] [[Cassio]] He’s never anything but your true servant. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | I know’t. I thank you. You do [[Love|love]] my lord. You have known him long, and be you [[Well|well]] assured He shall in strangeness stand no farther off Than in a politic distance. |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | Ay, but, lady, That policy may either last so long, Or feed upon such nice and wat’rish diet, Or breed itself so out of circumstance, That, I being absent and my place supplied, My general will forget my [[Love|love]] and service. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Do not [[Doubt|doubt]] that. Before [[Emilia]] here I give thee warrant of thy place. Assure thee, If I do vow a [[Friendship|friendship]] I’ll perform it To the last article. My lord shall never rest. I’ll watch him tame, and talk him out of patience. His bed shall seem a school, his board a shrift. I’ll intermingle everything he does With [[Cassio]]’s suit. Therefore be merry, [[Cassio]], For thy solicitor shall rather die Than give thy cause away. Enter [[Othello]] and [[Iago]] |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | Madam, here comes my lord. |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | Madam, I’ll take my leave. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Why, stay, and hear me speak. |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | Madam, not now. I am very ill at ease, Unfit for mine own purposes. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | [[Well]], do your discretion. Exit [[Cassio]] |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Ha! I like not that. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | What dost thou say? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Nothing, my lord. Or if, I know not what. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Was not that [[Cassio]] parted from my wife? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | [[Cassio]], my lord? No, sure, I cannot think it, That he would steal away so guilty-like Seeing your coming. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | I do believe ’twas he. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | How now, my lord? I have been talking with a suitor here, A man that languishes in your displeasure. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Who is’t you mean? |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Why, your lieutenant, [[Cassio]]; good my lord, If I have any grace or power to move you, His present [[Reconciliation|reconciliation]] take; For if he be not one that truly loves you, That errs in ignorance and not in cunning, I have no judgement in an honest face. I prithee call him back. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Went he hence now? |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Yes, [[Faith|faith]], so humbled That he hath left part of his grief with me To suffer with him. Good [[Love|love]], call him back. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Not now, sweet Desdemon. Some other [[Time|time]]. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | But shall’t be shortly? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | The sooner, sweet, for you. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Shall’t be tonight at supper? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | No, not tonight. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Tomorrow dinner, then? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | I shall not dine at [[Home|home]]. I meet the captains at the citadel. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Why then, tomorrow [[Night|night]], or Tuesday morn, On Tuesday [[Noon|noon]], or [[Night|night]], on Wednesday morn— I prithee name the [[Time|time]], but let it not Exceed three days. In [[Faith|faith]], he’s penitent, And yet his trespass, in our common reason— Save that, they say, the wars must make example Out of her best—is not almost a fault T‘incur a private check. When shall he come? Tell me, [[Othello]]. I [[Wonder|wonder]] in my [[Soul|soul]] What you would ask me that I should deny, Or stand so mamm’ring on? What, [[Michael]] [[Cassio]], That came a-wooing with you, and so many a [[Time|time]] When I have spoke of you dispraisingly Hath ta‘en your part—to have so much to-do To bring him in? By’r Lady, I could do much. |
