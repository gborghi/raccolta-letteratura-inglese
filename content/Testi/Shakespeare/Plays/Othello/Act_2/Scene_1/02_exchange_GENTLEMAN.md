| GENTLEMAN | ’Tis one [[Iago]], ensign to the general. |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | He’s had most favourable and happy [[Speed|speed]]. Tempests themselves, high seas, and howling winds, The guttered rocks and congregated sands, Traitors ensteeped to enclog the guiltless keel, As having sense of [[Beauty|beauty]] do omit Their mortal natures, letting go safely by The divine [[Desdemona]]. |
| MONTANO | What is she? |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | She that I spake of, our great captain’s captain, Left in the conduct of the bold [[Iago]], Whose footing here anticipates our thoughts A sennight’s [[Speed|speed]]. Great Jove, [[Othello]] guard, And swell his sail with thine own powerful breath, That he may bless this bay with his tall [[Ship|ship]], Make [[Love|love]]’s quick pants in [[Desdemona]]’s arms, Give renewed [[Fire|fire]] to our extincted spirits, And bring all Cyprus comfort. Enter [[Desdemona]], [[Iago]], [[Emilia]], and [[Roderigo]] O, behold, The riches of the [[Ship|ship]] is come on shore! You men of Cyprus, let her have your knees. Montano and the Gentlemen make curtsy to [[Desdemona]] Hail to thee, lady, and the grace of heaven Before, behind thee, and on every hand Enwheel thee round! |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | I thank you, valiant [[Cassio]]. What tidings can you tell me of my lord? |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | He is not yet arrived, nor know I aught But that he’s [[Well|well]] and will be shortly here. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | O, but I [[Fear|fear]]—how lost you company? |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | The great contention of the sea and skies Parted our fellowship. |
| VOICES | (within) A sail, a sail! |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | But hark, a sail. A shot |
| *(didascalia)* | A |
| GENTLEMAN | They give their greeting to the citadel. This likewise is a friend. |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | See for the news. Exit Gentleman Good ensign, you are welcome. (Kissing [[Emilia]]) Welcome, mistress. Let it not gall your patience, good [[Iago]], That I extend my manners. ’Tis my breeding That gives me this bold show of courtesy. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Sir, would she give you so much of her lips As of her tongue she oft bestows on me, You would have enough. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Alas, she has no speech! |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | In [[Faith|faith]], too much. I find it still when I ha’ leave to [[Sleep|sleep]]. Marry, before your ladyship, I grant, She puts her tongue a little in her heart, And chides with thinking. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | You ha’ little cause to say so. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Come on, come on. You are pictures out of door, [[Bells]] in your parlours; wildcats in your kitchens, Saints in your injuries; devils being offended, Players in your housewifery, and hussies in your beds. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | O, fie upon thee, slanderer! |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Nay, it is true, or else I am a Turk. You rise to play and go to bed to work. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | You shall not write my praise. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | No, let me not. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | What wouldst write of me, if thou shouldst praise me? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | O, gentle lady, do not put me to’t, For I am nothing if not critical. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Come on, [[Essay|essay]]—there’s one gone to the harbour? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Ay, madam. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | I am not merry, but I do beguile The thing I am by seeming otherwise. Come, how wouldst thou praise me? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | I am about it, but indeed my invention Comes from my pate as birdlime does from frieze— It plucks out brains and all. But my muse labours, And thus she is delivered: If she be fair and wise, fairness and wit, The one’s for use, the other useth it. |
