| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Othello]]’s Herald reading a proclamation |
| HERALD | It is [[Othello]]’s pleasure—our noble and valiant<br>general—that, upon certain tidings now arrived<br>importing the mere perdition of the Turkish fleet, every<br>man put himself into triumph: some to [[Dance|dance]], some to<br>make bonfires, each man to what sport and revels his<br>addiction leads him; for besides these beneficial news,<br>it is the celebration of his nuptial. So much was his<br>pleasure should be proclaimed. All offices are open, and<br>there is full liberty of feasting from this present hour<br>of five till the bell have told eleven. Heaven bless the<br>isle of Cyprus and our noble general, [[Othello]]! Exit |
