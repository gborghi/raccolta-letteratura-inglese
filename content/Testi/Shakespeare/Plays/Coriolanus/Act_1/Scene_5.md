| *(didascalia)* | Enter Roman Soldiers, in retreat, followed by Martius, cursing |
| MARTIUS | All the contagion of the south light on you,<br>You shames of Rome! You herd of—boils and plagues<br>Plaster you o’er, that you may be abhorred<br>Farther than seen, and one infect another<br>Against the [[Wind|wind]] a mile! You souls of geese<br>That bear the shapes of men, how have you run<br>From slaves that apes would beat! Pluto and hell:<br>All hurt behind! Backs red, and faces pale<br>With flight and agued [[Fear|fear]]! Mend and charge [[Home|home]],<br>Or by the fires of heaven I’ll leave the foe<br>And make my wars on you. Look to’t. Come on.<br>If you’ll stand fast, we’ll beat them to their wives,<br>As they us to our trenches. Follow. |
| *(didascalia)* | The Romans come forward towards the walls. Another alarum, and enter the army of the Volsces. Martius beats them back through the gates |
| MARTIUS | So, now the gates are ope. Now prove good seconds.<br>’Tis for the followers fortune widens them,<br>Not for the fliers. Mark me, and do the like.<br>He enters the gates |
| FIRST SOLDIER | Foolhardiness! Not I. |
| SECOND SOLDIER | Nor I. |
| *(didascalia)* | Alarum continues. The gates close, and Martius is shut in |
| FIRST SOLDIER | See, they have shut him in. |
| *(didascalia)* | THIRD SOLDIER To th’ pot, I warrant him. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Lartius |
| LARTIUS | What is become of Martius? |
| *(didascalia)* | FOURTH SOLDIER |
| LARTIUS | Slain, sir, doubtless. |
| FIRST SOLDIER | Following the fliers at the very heels,<br>With them he enters, who upon the sudden<br>Clapped-to their gates. He is himself [[Alone|alone]]<br>To answer all the [[City|city]]. |
| LARTIUS | O noble fellow,<br>Who sensibly outdares his senseless [[Sword|sword]]<br>And, when it bows, stand‘st up! Thou art lost, Martius.<br>A carbuncle entire, as big as thou art,<br>Were not so rich a [[Jewel|jewel]]. Thou wast a soldier<br>Even to Cato’s wish, not fierce and terrible<br>Only in strokes, but with thy grim looks and<br>The thunder-like percussion of thy sounds<br>Thou mad’st thine enemies shake as if the world<br>Were feverous and did tremble. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Martius, bleeding, assaulted by the enemy |
| FIRST SOLDIER | Look, sir. |
| LARTIUS | O, ’tis Martius!<br>Let’s fetch him off, or make remain alike.<br>They fight, and all exeunt into the [[City|city]] |
