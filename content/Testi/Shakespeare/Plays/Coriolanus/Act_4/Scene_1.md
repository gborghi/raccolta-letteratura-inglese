| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Coriolanus]], [[Volumnia]], [[Virgilia]], [[Menenius]], and [[Cominius]], with the young nobility of Rome |
| [[Coriolanus|CORIOLANUS]] | Come, leave your [[Tears|tears]]. A brief farewell. The beast<br>With many heads butts me away. Nay, mother,<br>Where is your ancient [[Courage|courage]]? You were used<br>To say extremities was the trier of spirits,<br>That common chances common men could bear,<br>That when the sea was calm all boats alike<br>Showed mastership in floating; fortune’s blows<br>When most struck [[Home|home]], being gentle wounded craves<br>A noble cunning. You were used to load me<br>With precepts that would make invincible<br>The heart that conned them. |
| [[Virgilia|VIRGILIA]] | O heavens, O heavens! |
| [[Coriolanus|CORIOLANUS]] | Nay, I prithee, woman— |
| [[Volumnia|VOLUMNIA]] | Now the red pestilence strike all trades in Rome,<br>And occupations perish! |
| [[Coriolanus|CORIOLANUS]] | What, what, what?<br>I shall be loved when I am lacked. Nay, mother,<br>Resume that spirit when you were wont to say,<br>If you had been the wife of Hercules<br>Six of his labours you’d have done, and saved<br>Your husband so much sweat. [[Cominius]],<br>Droop not. Adieu. Farewell, my wife, my mother.<br>I’ll do [[Well|well]] yet. Thou old and true [[Menenius]],<br>Thy [[Tears|tears]] are salter than a younger man‘s,<br>And venomous to thine eyes. My sometime general,<br>I have seen thee stern, and thou hast oft beheld<br>Heart-hard’ning spectacles. Tell these sad women<br>‘Tis fond to wail inevitable strokes<br>As ’tis to laugh at ’em. My mother, you wot [[Well|well]]<br>My hazards still have been your solace, and—<br>Believe’t not lightly—though I go [[Alone|alone]],<br>Like to a lonely dragon that his fen<br>Makes feared and talked of more than seen, your son<br>Will or exceed the common or be caught<br>With cautelous baits and practice. |
| [[Volumnia|VOLUMNIA]] | My first son,<br>Whither will thou go? Take good [[Cominius]]<br>With thee a while. Determine on some course<br>More than a wild exposure to each chance<br>That starts i’th’ way before thee. |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Virgilia|VIRGILIA]] O the gods! |
| [[Cominius|COMINIUS]] | I’ll follow thee a month, devise with thee<br>Where thou shalt rest, that thou mayst hear of us<br>And we of thee. So, if the [[Time|time]] thrust forth<br>A cause for thy repeal, we shall not send<br>O‘er the vast world to seek a single man,<br>And lose advantage, which doth ever cool<br>I’th’ absence of the needer. |
| [[Coriolanus|CORIOLANUS]] | Fare ye [[Well|well]].<br>Thou hast years upon thee, and thou art too full<br>Of the wars’ surfeits to go rove with one<br>That’s yet unbruised. Bring me but out at gate.<br>Come, my sweet wife, my dearest mother, and<br>My friends of noble touch. When I am forth,<br>Bid me farewell, and smile. I pray you come.<br>While I remain above the ground you shall<br>Hear from me still, and never of me aught<br>But what is like me formerly. |
| [[Menenius|MENENIUS]] | That’s worthily<br>As any ear can hear. Come, let’s not weep.<br>If I could shake off but one seven years<br>From these old arms and legs, by the good gods,<br>I’d with thee every foot. |
| [[Coriolanus|CORIOLANUS]] | Give me thy hand. Come. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt |
