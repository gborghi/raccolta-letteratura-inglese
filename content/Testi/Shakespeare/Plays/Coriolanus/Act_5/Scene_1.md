| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Menenius]], [[Cominius]], [[Sicinius]] and [[Brutus]], the two tribunes, with others |
| [[Menenius|MENENIUS]] | No, I’ll not go. You hear what he hath said<br>Which was sometime his general, who loved him<br>In a most dear particular. He called me father,<br>But what o’ that? (To the tribunes) Go, you that<br>banished him.<br>A mile before his tent fall down, and knee<br>The way into his [[Mercy|mercy]]. Nay, if he coyed<br>To hear [[Cominius]] speak, I’ll keep at [[Home|home]]. |
| [[Cominius|COMINIUS]] | He would not seem to know me. |
| [[Menenius|MENENIUS]] | (to the tribunes) Do you hear? |
| [[Cominius|COMINIUS]] | Yet one [[Time|time]] he did call me by my name.<br>I urged our old acquaintance and the drops<br>That we have bled together. ‘[[Coriolanus]]’<br>He would not answer to, forbade all names.<br>He was a kind of nothing, titleless,<br>Till he had forged himself a name o’th’ [[Fire|fire]]<br>Of burning Rome. |
| [[Menenius|MENENIUS]] | (to the tribunes)<br>Why, so! You have made good work.<br>A pair of tribunes that have wracked fair Rome<br>To make coals cheap—a noble [[Memory|memory]]! |
| [[Cominius|COMINIUS]] | I minded him how royal ’twas to pardon<br>When it was less expected. He replied<br>It was a bare petition of a state<br>To one whom they had punished. |
| [[Menenius|MENENIUS]] | Very [[Well|well]].<br>Could he say less? |
| [[Cominius|COMINIUS]] | I offered to awaken his regard<br>For’s private friends. His answer to me was<br>He could not stay to pick them in a pile<br>Of noisome, musty chaff. He said ‘twas folly,<br>For one poor grain or two, to leave unburnt<br>And still to nose th’offence. |
| [[Menenius|MENENIUS]] | For one poor grain or two?<br>I am one of those. His mother, wife, his [[Child|child]],<br>And this brave fellow too—we are the grains.<br>(To the tribunes) You are the musty chaff, and you are<br>smelt<br>Above the [[Moon|moon]]. We must be burnt for you. |
| [[Sicinius|SICINIUS]] | Nay, pray be patient. If you refuse your aid<br>In this so never-needed help, yet do not<br>Upbraid’s with our distress. But sure, if you<br>Would be your country’s pleader, your good tongue,<br>More than the instant army we can make,<br>Might stop our countryman. |
| [[Menenius|MENENIUS]] | No, I’ll not meddle. |
| [[Sicinius|SICINIUS]] | Pray you go to him. |
| [[Menenius|MENENIUS]] | What should I do? |
| [[Brutus|BRUTUS]] | Only make trial what your [[Love|love]] can do<br>For Rome towards Martius. |
| [[Menenius|MENENIUS]] | [[Well]], and say that Martius return me,<br>As [[Cominius]] is returned, unheard—what then?<br>But as a discontented friend, grief-shot<br>With his unkindness? Say’t be so? |
| [[Sicinius|SICINIUS]] | Yet your good will<br>Must have that thanks from Rome after the measure<br>As you intended [[Well|well]]. |
| [[Menenius|MENENIUS]] | I’ll undertake’t.<br>I think he’ll hear me. Yet to bite his lip<br>And ’hmh’ at good [[Cominius]] much unhearts me.<br>He was not taken [[Well|well]], he had not dined.<br>The veins unfilled, our [[Blood|blood]] is cold, and then<br>We pout upon the morning, are unapt<br>To give or to forgive; but when we have stuffed<br>These pipes and these conveyances of our [[Blood|blood]]<br>With wine and feeding, we have suppler souls<br>Than in our priest-like fasts. Therefore I’ll watch him<br>Till he be dieted to my request,<br>And then I’ll set upon him. |
| [[Brutus|BRUTUS]] | You know the very [[Road|road]] into his kindness,<br>And cannot lose your way. |
| [[Menenius|MENENIUS]] | Good [[Faith|faith]], I’ll prove him.<br>[[Speed]] how it will, I shall ere long have knowledge<br>Of my [[success]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit |
| [[Cominius|COMINIUS]] | He’ll never hear him. |
| [[Sicinius|SICINIUS]] | Not? |
| [[Cominius|COMINIUS]] | I tell you, he does sit in gold, his eye<br>Red as ‘twould burn Rome, and his injury<br>The jailer to his pity. I kneeled before him;<br>’Twas very faintly he said ‘Rise’, dismissed me<br>Thus with his speechless hand. What he would do<br>He sent in writing after me, what he would not,<br>Bound with an oath to hold to his conditions.<br>So that all [[Hope|hope]] is vain unless his noble mother<br>And his wife, who as I hear mean to solicit him<br>For [[Mercy|mercy]] to his country. Therefore let’s hence,<br>And with our fair entreaties haste them on. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt |
