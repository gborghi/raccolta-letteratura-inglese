| *(didascalia)* | Enter at one door the Duke, Varrius, and lords, at another door [[Angelo]], [[Escalus]], [[Lucio]], citizens, and officers |
| DUKE | (to [[Angelo]]) My very worthy cousin, fairly met. (To [[Escalus]]) Our old and faithful friend, we are glad to see you. |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | and [[Escalus|ESCALUS]] Happy return be to your royal grace. |
| DUKE | Many and hearty thankings to you both. We have made enquiry of you, and we hear Such goodness of your justice that our [[Soul|soul]] Cannot but yield you forth to public thanks, Forerunning more requital. |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | You make my bonds still greater. |
| DUKE | O, your [[Desert|desert]] speaks loud, and I should wrong it To lock it in the wards of covert bosom, When it deserves with characters of brass A forted residence ’gainst the tooth of [[Time|time]] And razure of oblivion. Give me your hand, And let the subject see, to make them know That outward courtesies would fain proclaim Favours that keep within. Come, [[Escalus]], You must walk by us on our other hand, And good supporters are you.  They walk forward.  Enter Friar Peter and [[Isabella]] |
| FRIAR PETER | Now is your [[Time|time]]. Speak loud, and kneel before him. |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | (kneeling) Justice, O royal Duke! Vail your regard Upon a wronged—I would fain have said, a maid. O worthy prince, dishonour not your eye By throwing it on any other object, Till you have heard me in my true complaint, And given me justice, justice, justice, justice! |
| DUKE | Relate your wrongs. In what? By whom? Be brief. Here is Lord [[Angelo]] shall give you justice. Reveal yourself to him. |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | O worthy Duke, You bid me seek redemption of the devil. Hear me yourself, for that which I must speak Must either punish me, not being believed, Or wring redress from you. Hear me, O hear me, hear! |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | My lord, her wits, I [[Fear|fear]] me, are not firm. She hath been a suitor to me for her brother, Cut off by course of justice. |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | standing By course of justice! |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | And she will speak most bitterly and strange. |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | Most strange, but yet most truly, will I speak. That [[Angelo]]’s forsworn, is it not strange? That [[Angelo]]’s a murderer, is’t not strange? That [[Angelo]] is an adulterous thief, An hypocrite, a virgin-violator, Is it not strange, and strange? |
| DUKE | Nay, it is ten times strange! |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | It is not truer he is [[Angelo]] Than this is all as true as it is strange. Nay, it is ten times true, for [[Truth|truth]] is [[Truth|truth]] To th‘end of reck’ning. |
| DUKE | Away with her. Poor [[Soul|soul]], She speaks this in th’infirmity of sense. |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | O prince, I conjure thee, as thou believ‘st There is another comfort than this world, That thou neglect me not with that opinion That I am touched with [[Madness|madness]]. Make not impossible That which but seems unlike. ’Tis not impossible But one, the wicked’st caitiff on the ground, May seem as shy, as [[Grave|grave]], as just, as absolute, As [[Angelo]]; even so may [[Angelo]], In all his dressings, characts, titles, forms, Be an arch-[[Villain|villain]]. Believe it, royal prince, If he be less, he’s nothing; but he’s more, Had I more name for badness. |
