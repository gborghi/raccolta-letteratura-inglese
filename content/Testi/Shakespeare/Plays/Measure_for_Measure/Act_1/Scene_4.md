| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Isabella]], and Francesca, a nun |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | And have you nuns no farther privileges? |
| FRANCESCA | Are not these large enough? |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | Yes, truly. I speak not as desiring more,<br>But rather wishing a more strict restraint<br>Upon the sisterhood, the votarists of Saint Clare. |
| [[Lucio|LUCIO]] | (within)<br>Ho, peace be in this place! |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] to Francesca |
| [[Lucio|LUCIO]] | Who’s that which calls? |
| FRANCESCA | It is a man’s voice. Gentle [[Isabella]].<br>Turn you the key, and know his business of him.<br>You may, I may not; you are yet unsworn.<br>When you have vowed, you must not speak with men<br>But in the presence of the prioress.<br>Then if you speak, you must not show your face;<br>Or if you show your face, you must not speak.<br>[[Lucio]] calls within<br>He calls again. I pray you answer him. |
| *(didascalia)* | She stands asidel |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | Peace and prosperity! Who is’t that calls?<br>She opens the door. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Lucio]] |
| [[Lucio|LUCIO]] | Hail, virgin, if you be—as those cheek-roses<br>Proclaim you are no less. Can you so stead me<br>As bring me to the sight of [[Isabella]],<br>A novice of this place, and the fair sister<br>To her unhappy brother [[Claudio]]? |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | Why her unhappy brother? Let me ask,<br>The rather for I now must make you know<br>I am that [[Isabella]], and his sister. |
| [[Lucio|LUCIO]] | Gentle and fair, your brother kindly greets you.<br>Not to be weary with you, he’s in [[Prison|prison]]. |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | Woe me! For what? |
| [[Lucio|LUCIO]] | For that which, if myself might be his judge,<br>He should receive his punishment in thanks.<br>He hath got his friend with [[Child|child]]. |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | Sir,<br>make me not your story. |
| [[Lucio|LUCIO]] | ‘Tis true. I would not—though ’tis my familiar sin<br>With maids to seem the lapwing, and to jest<br>Tongue far from heart—play with all virgins so.<br>I hold you as a thing enskied and sainted<br>By your renouncement, an immortal spirit,<br>And to be talked with in sincerity<br>As with a saint. |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | You do blaspheme the good in mocking me. |
| [[Lucio|LUCIO]] | Do not believe it. Fewness and [[Truth|truth]], ’tis thus:<br>Your brother and his [[Lover|lover]] have embraced.<br>As those that feed grow full, as blossoming [[Time|time]]<br>That from the seedness the bare fallow brings<br>To teeming foison, even so her plenteous womb<br>Expresseth his full tilth and husbandry. |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | Someone with [[Child|child]] by him? My cousin [[Juliet]]? |
| [[Lucio|LUCIO]] | Is she your cousin? |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | Adoptedly, as schoolmaids change their names<br>By vain though apt affection. |
| [[Lucio|LUCIO]] | She it is. |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | O, let him marry her! |
| [[Lucio|LUCIO]] | This is the point.<br>The Duke is very strangely gone from hence;<br>Bore many gentlemen—mysetf being one—<br>In hand and [[Hope|hope]] of action; but we do learn,<br>By those that know the very nerves of state,<br>His giving out were of an infinite distance<br>From his true-meant design. Upon his place,<br>And with full line of his authority,<br>Governs Lord [[Angelo]]—a man whose [[Blood|blood]]<br>Is very [[Snow|snow]]-broth; one who never feels<br>The wanton stings and motions of the sense,<br>But doth rebate and blunt his natural edge<br>With profits of the mind, study, and fast.<br>He, to give [[Fear|fear]] to use and liberty,<br>Which have for long run by the hideous law<br>As mice by lions, hath picked out an act<br>Under whose heavy sense your brother’s life<br>Falls into forfeit. He arrests him on it,<br>And follows close the rigour of the statute<br>To make him an example. All [[Hope|hope]] is gone,<br>Unless you have the grace by your fair [[prayer]]<br>To soften [[Angelo]]. And that’s my pith<br>Of business ’twixt you and your poor brother. |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | Doth he so seek his life? |
| [[Lucio|LUCIO]] | Has censured him already,<br>And, as I hear, the Provost hath a warrant<br>For’s execution. |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | Alas, what poor<br>Ability’s in me to do him good? |
| [[Lucio|LUCIO]] | Assay the power you have. |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | My power? Alas, I [[Doubt|doubt]]. |
| [[Lucio|LUCIO]] | Our doubts are traitors,<br>And makes us lose the good we oft might win,<br>By fearing to attempt. Go to Lord [[Angelo]];<br>And let him learn to know, when maidens sue,<br>Men give like gods, but when they weep and kneel,<br>All their petitions are as freely theirs<br>As they themselves would owe them. |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | I’ll see what I can do. |
| [[Lucio|LUCIO]] | But speedily. |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | I will about it straight,<br>No longer staying but to give the Mother<br>Notice of my affair. I humbly thank you.<br>Commend me to my brother. Soon at [[Night|night]]<br>I’ll send him certain word of my [[success]]. |
| [[Lucio|LUCIO]] | I take my leave of you. |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | Good sir, adieu. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt [[Isabella]] and Francesca at one door, |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Lucio]] at another door |
