| *(didascalia)* | Enter the Provost and a Servant |
| SERVANT | He’s hearing of a cause; he will come straight.<br>I’ll tell him of you. |
| PROVOST | Pray you do. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit Servant |
| PROVOST | I’ll know<br>His pleasure; maybe he will relent. Alas,<br>He hath but as offended in [[A Dream|a dream]].<br>All sects, all ages, smack of this vice; and he<br>To die for’t! |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter |
| PROVOST | [[Angelo]] |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | Now, what’s the matter, Provost? |
| PROVOST | Is it your will [[Claudio]] shall die tomorrow? |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | Did not I tell thee yea? Hadst thou not order?<br>Why dost thou ask again? |
| PROVOST | Lest I might be too rash.<br>Under your good correction, I have seen<br>When after execution judgement hath<br>Repented o’er his doom. |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | Go to; let that be mine.<br>Do you your office, or give up your place,<br>And you shall [[Well|well]] be spared. |
| PROVOST | I crave your [[Honour|honour]]’s pardon.<br>What shall be done, sir, with the groaning [[Juliet]]?<br>She’s very near her hour. |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | Dispose of her<br>To some more fitter place, and that with [[Speed|speed]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Servant |
| SERVANT | Here is the sister of the man condemned Desires access to you. |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | Hath he a sister? |
| PROVOST | Ay, my good lord; a very virtuous maid,<br>And to be shortly of a sisterhood,<br>If not already. |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | [[Well]], let her be admitted. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit Servant |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | See you the fornicatress be removed.<br>Let her have needful but not lavish means.<br>There shall be order for’t. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Lucio]] and [[Isabella]] |
| PROVOST | God save your [[Honour|honour]]. |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | Stay a little while. (To [[Isabella]]) You’re welcome.<br>What’s your will? |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | I am a woeful suitor to your [[Honour|honour]].<br>Please but your [[Honour|honour]] hear me. |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | [[Well]], what’s your suit? |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | There is a vice that most I do abhor,<br>And most [[Desire|desire]] should meet the blow of justice,<br>For which I would not plead, but that I must;<br>For which I must not plead, but that I am<br>At war ’twixt will and will not. |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | [[Well]], the matter? |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | I have a brother is condemned to die.<br>I do beseech you, let it be his fault,<br>And not my brother. |
| PROVOST | (aside)<br>Heaven give thee moving graces! |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | Condemn the fault, and not the actor of it?<br>Why, every fault’s condemned ere it be done.<br>Mine were the very cipher of a function,<br>To fine the faults whose fine stands in record,<br>And let go by the actor. |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | O just but severe law!<br>I had a brother, then. Heaven keep your [[Honour|honour]]. |
| [[Lucio|LUCIO]] | (aside to [[Isabella]])<br>Give’t not o’er so. To him again; entreat him.<br>Kneel down before him; hang upon his gown.<br>You are too cold. If you should need a pin,<br>You could not with more tame a tongue [[Desire|desire]] it.<br>To him, I say! |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | (to [[Angelo]]) Must he needs die? |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | Maiden, no remedy. |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | Yes, I do think that you might pardon him,<br>And neither heaven nor man grieve at the [[Mercy|mercy]]. |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | I will not do’t. |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | But can you if you would? |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | Look what I will not, that I cannot do. |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | But might you do’t, and do the world no wrong,<br>If so your heart were touched with that remorse<br>As mine is to him? |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | He’s sentenced; ’tis too late. |
| [[Lucio|LUCIO]] | (aside to [[Isabella]]) You are too cold. |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | Too late? Why, no; I that do speak a word<br>May call it again. [[Well]], believe this,<br>No ceremony that to great ones ’longs,<br>Not the [[King|king]]’s [[Crown|crown]], nor the deputed [[Sword|sword]],<br>The marshal’s truncheon, nor the judge’s robe,<br>Become them with one half so good a grace<br>As [[Mercy|mercy]] does.<br>If he had been as you and you as he,<br>You would have slipped like him, but he, like you,<br>Would not have been so stern. |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | Pray you be gone. |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | I would to heaven I had your potency,<br>And you were Isabel! Should it then be thus?<br>No; I would tell what ’twere to be a judge,<br>And what a prisoner. |
| [[Lucio|LUCIO]] | (aside to [[Isabella]]) Ay, touch him; there’s the vein. |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | Your brother is a forfeit of the law,<br>And you but waste your words. |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | Alas, alas!<br>Why, all the souls that were were forfeit once,<br>And He that might the vantage best have took<br>Found out the remedy. How would you be<br>If He which is the top of judgement should<br>But judge you as you are? O, think on that,<br>And [[Mercy|mercy]] then will breathe within your lips,<br>Like man new made. |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | Be you content, fair maid.<br>It is the law, not I, condemn your brother.<br>Were he my kinsman, brother, or my son,<br>It should be thus with him. He must die tomorrow. |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | Tomorrow? O, that’s sudden! Spare him, spare him!<br>He’s not prepared for [[Death|death]]. Even for our kitchens<br>We kill the fowl of season. Shall we serve heaven<br>With less respect than we do minister<br>To our gross selves? Good good my lord, bethink you:<br>Who is it that hath died for this offence?<br>There’s many have committed it. |
| [[Lucio|LUCIO]] | (aside)<br>Ay, [[Well|well]] said. |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | The law hath not been dead, though it hath slept.<br>Those many had not dared to do that evil<br>If the first that did th‘edict infringe<br>Had answered for his deed. Now ’tis awake,<br>Takes note of what is done, and, like a [[Prophet|prophet]],<br>Looks in a glass that shows what future evils,<br>Either raw, or by remissness new conceived<br>And so in progress to be hatched and born,<br>Are now to have no successive degrees,<br>But ere they live, to end. |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | Yet show some pity. |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | I show it most of all when I show justice,<br>For then I pity those I do not know<br>Which a dismissed offence would after gall,<br>And do him right that, answering one foul wrong,<br>Lives not to act another. Be satisfied.<br>Your brother dies tomorrow. Be content. |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | So you must be the first that gives this sentence,<br>And he that suffers. O, it is excellent<br>To have a giant’s strength, but it is tyrannous<br>To use it like a giant. |
| [[Lucio|LUCIO]] | (aside to [[Isabella]]) That’s [[Well|well]] said. |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | Could great men thunder<br>As Jove himself does, Jove would never be quiet,<br>For every pelting petty officer<br>Would use his heaven for thunder, nothing but<br>thunder.<br>Merciful heaven,<br>Thou rather with thy sharp and sulphurous bolt<br>Split’st the unwedgeable and gnarlèd oak<br>Than the soft myrtle. But man, proud man,<br>Dressed in a little brief authority,<br>Most ignorant of what he’s most assured,<br>His glassy essence, like an angry ape<br>Plays such fantastic tricks before high heaven<br>As makes the angels weep, who, with our spleens,<br>Would all themselves laugh mortal. |
| [[Lucio|LUCIO]] | (aside to [[Isabella]])<br>O, to him, to him, wench! He will relent.<br>He’s coming; I perceive’t. |
| PROVOST | (aside)<br>Pray heaven she win him! |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | We cannot weigh our brother with ourself.<br>Great men may jest with saints; ’tis wit in them,<br>But in the less, foul profanation. |
| [[Lucio|LUCIO]] | (aside to [[Isabella]]) Thou’rt i’th’ right, girl. More o’<br>that. |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | That in the captain’s but a choleric word,<br>Which in the soldier is flat blasphemy. |
| [[Lucio|LUCIO]] | (aside to [[Isabella]]) Art advised o’ that? More on’t. |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | Why do you put these sayings upon me? |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | Because authority, though it err like others,<br>Hath yet a kind of medicine in itself<br>That skins the vice o’th’ top. Go to your bosom;<br>Knock there, and ask your heart what it doth know<br>That’s like my brother’s fault. If it confess<br>A natural guiltiness, such as is his,<br>Let it not sound a thought upon your tongue<br>Against my brother’s life. |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | (aside)<br>She speaks, and ’tis such sense<br>That my sense breeds with it. (To [[Isabella]]) Fare you<br>[[Well|well]]. |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | Gentle my lord, turn back. |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | I will bethink me. Come again tomorrow. |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | Hark how I’ll bribe you; good my lord, turn back. |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | How, bribe me? |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | Ay, with such gifts that heaven shall share with you. |
| [[Lucio|LUCIO]] | (aside to [[Isabella]]) You had marred all else. |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | Not with fond shekels of the tested gold,<br>Or stones, whose rate are either rich or poor<br>As fancy values them; but with true prayers,<br>That shall be up at heaven and enter there<br>Ere sunrise, prayers from preserved souls,<br>From fasting maids whose minds are dedicate<br>To nothing temporal. |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | [[Well]], come to me tomorrow |
| [[Lucio|LUCIO]] | (aside to [[Isabella]]) Go to; ’tis [[Well|well]]; away. |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | Heaven keep your [[Honour|honour]] safe. |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | (aside) Amen;<br>For I am that way going to [[Temptation|temptation]],<br>Where [[prayer]] is crossed. |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | At what hour tomorrow<br>Shall I attend your lordship? |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | At any [[Time|time]] fore [[Noon|noon]]. |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | God save your [[Honour|honour]]. |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | (aside)<br>From thee; even from thy virtue. Exeunt [[Isabella]], [[Lucio]], and Provost<br>What’s this? What’s this? Is this her fault or mine?<br>The tempter or the tempted, who sins most, ha?<br>Not she; nor doth she tempt; but it is I<br>That, lying by the violet in the sun,<br>Do, as the carrion does, not as the flower,<br>Corrupt with virtuous season. Can it be<br>That modesty may more betray our sense<br>Than woman’s lightness? Having waste ground enough,<br>Shall we [[Desire|desire]] to raze the sanctuary,<br>And pitch our evils there? O, fie, fie, fie!<br>What dost thou, or what art thou, [[Angelo]]?<br>Dost thou [[Desire|desire]] her foully for those things<br>That make her good? O, let her brother live!<br>Thieves for their robbery have authority,<br>When judges steal themselves. What, do I [[Love|love]] her,<br>That I [[Desire|desire]] to hear her speak again,<br>And feast upon her eyes? What is’t I dream on?<br>O cunning enemy, that, to catch a saint,<br>With saints dost bait thy hook! Most dangerous<br>Is that [[Temptation|temptation]] that doth goad us on<br>To sin in loving virtue. Never could the strumpet,<br>With all her [[Double|double]] vigour—art and [[Nature|nature]]—<br>Once stir my temper; but this virtuous maid<br>Subdues me quite. Ever till now<br>When men were fond, I smiled, and wondered how. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit |
