| Chi parla | Battuta |
|---|---|
| [[Escalus|ESCALUS]] | How know you that? |
| ELBOW | My wife, sir, whom I detest before heaven and your [[Honour|honour]]— |
| [[Escalus|ESCALUS]] | How, thy wife? |
| ELBOW | Ay, sir, whom I thank heaven is an honest woman— |
| [[Escalus|ESCALUS]] | Dost thou detest her therefor? |
| ELBOW | I say, sir, I will detest myself also, as [[Well|well]] as she, that this [[house]], if it be not a bawd’s [[house]], it is pity of her life, for it is a naughty [[house]]. |
| [[Escalus|ESCALUS]] | How dost thou know that, constable? |
| ELBOW | Marry, sir, by my wife, who, if she had been a woman cardinally given, might have been accused in fornication, adultery, and all uncleanliness there. |
| [[Escalus|ESCALUS]] | By the woman’s means? |
| ELBOW | Ay, sir, by Mistress Overdone’s means. But as she spit in his face, so she defied him. |
| [[Pompey|POMPEY]] | (to [[Escalus]]) Sir, if it please your [[Honour|honour]], this is not so. |
| ELBOW | Prove it before these varlets here, thou honourable man, prove it. |
| [[Escalus|ESCALUS]] | (to [[Angelo]]) Do you hear how he misplaces? |
| [[Pompey|POMPEY]] | Sir, she came in great with [[Child|child]], and [[Longing|longing]]-saving your [[Honour|honour]]’s reverence—for stewed prunes. Sir, we had but two in the [[house]], which at that very distant [[Time|time]] stood, as it were, in a fruit dish—a dish of some threepence; your honours have seen such dishes; they are not china dishes, but very good dishes. |
| [[Escalus|ESCALUS]] | Go to, go to, no matter for the dish, sir. |
| [[Pompey|POMPEY]] | No, indeed, sir, not of a pin; you are therein in the right. But to the point. As I say, this Mistress Elbow, being, as I say, with [[Child|child]], and being great-bellied, and [[Longing|longing]], as I said, for prunes; and having but two in the dish, as I said, Master Froth here, this very man, having eaten the rest, as I said, and, as I say, paying for them very honestly; for, as you know, Master Froth, I could not give you threepence again. |
| FROTH | No, indeed. |
| [[Pompey|POMPEY]] | Very [[Well|well]]. You being, then, if you be remembered, cracking the stones of the foresaid prunes— |
| FROTH | Ay, so I did indeed. |
| [[Pompey|POMPEY]] | Why, very [[Well|well]].—I telling you then, if you be remembered, that such a one and such a one were past cure of the thing you wot of, unless they kept very good diet, as I told you— |
| FROTH | All this is true. no |
| [[Pompey|POMPEY]] | Why, very [[Well|well]] then— |
| [[Escalus|ESCALUS]] | Come, you are a tedious [[Fool|fool]]. To the purpose. What was done to Elbow’s wife that he hath cause to complain of? Come me to what was done to her. |
| [[Pompey|POMPEY]] | Sir, your [[Honour|honour]] cannot come to that yet. |
| [[Escalus|ESCALUS]] | No, sir, nor I mean it not. |
| [[Pompey|POMPEY]] | Sir, but you shall come to it, by your [[Honour|honour]]’s leave. And I beseech you, look into Master Froth here, sir, a man of fourscore pound a year, whose father died at Hallowmas—was’t not at Hallowmas, Master Froth? |
| FROTH | All Hallow Eve. |
| [[Pompey|POMPEY]] | Why, very [[Well|well]]. I [[Hope|hope]] here be truths. He, sir, sitting, as I say, in a lower chair, sir—’twas in the Bunch of Grapes, where indeed you have a delight to sit, have you not? |
| FROTH | I have so, because it is an open room, and good for [[Winter|winter]]. |
| [[Pompey|POMPEY]] | Why, very [[Well|well]] then. I [[Hope|hope]] here be truths. |
