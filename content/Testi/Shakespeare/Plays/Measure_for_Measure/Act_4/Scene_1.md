| *(didascalia)* | [[Mariana]] discoveredwith a Boy singing |
| BOY | Take, O take those lips away<br>That so sweetly were forsworn,<br>And those eyes, the break of day<br>Lights that do mislead the morn;<br>But my kisses bring again, bring again,<br>Seals of [[Love|love]], though sealed in vain, sealed in vain. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter the Duke, disguised as a friar |
| [[Mariana|MARIANA]] | Break off thy [[Song|song]], and haste thee quick away.<br>Here comes a man of comfort, whose advice<br>Hath often stilled my brawling discontent. Exit Boy<br>I cry you [[Mercy|mercy]], sir, and [[Well|well]] could wish io<br>You had not found me here so musical.<br>Let me excuse me, and believe me so:<br>My mirth it much displeased, but pleased my woe. |
| DUKE | ’Tis good; though music oft hath such a charm<br>To make bad good, and good provoke to harm.<br>I pray you tell me, hath anybody enquired for me here<br>today? Much upon this [[Time|time]] have I promised here to<br>meet. |
| [[Mariana|MARIANA]] | You have not been enquired after; I have sat here all day. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter |
| [[Mariana|MARIANA]] | [[Isabella]] |
| DUKE | I do constantly believe you; the [[Time|time]] is come even now. I shall crave your forbearance a little. Maybe I will call upon you anon, for some advantage to yourself. |
| [[Mariana|MARIANA]] | I am always bound to you. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit |
| DUKE | Very [[Well|well]] met, and welcome.<br>What is the news from this good deputy? |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | He hath a [[Garden|garden]] circummured with brick,<br>Whose western side is with a vineyard backed;<br>And to that vineyard is a planked gate,<br>That makes his opening with this bigger key.<br>This other doth command a little door<br>Which from the vineyard to the [[Garden|garden]] leads.<br>There have I made my promise<br>Upon the heavy middle of the [[Night|night]]<br>To call upon him. |
| DUKE | But shall you on your knowledge find this way? |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | I have ta‘en a due and wary note upon’t.<br>With whispering and most guilty diligence,<br>In action all of precept, he did show me<br>The way twice o’er. |
| DUKE | Are there no other tokens<br>Between you ’[[Greed|greed]] concerning her observance? |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | No, none, but only a repair i’th’ dark,<br>And that I have possessed him my most stay<br>Can be but brief, for I have made him know<br>I have a servant comes with me along<br>That stays upon me, whose [[Persuasion|persuasion]] is<br>I come about my brother. |
| DUKE | ’Tis [[Well|well]] borne up.<br>I have not yet made known to [[Mariana]]<br>A word of this.—What ho, within! Come forth! |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Mariana]] |
| DUKE | (To [[Mariana]]) I pray you be acquainted with this maid.<br>She comes to do you good. |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | I do [[Desire|desire]] the like. |
| DUKE | (to [[Mariana]])<br>Do you persuade yourself that I respect you? |
| [[Mariana|MARIANA]] | Good friar, I know you do, and so have found it. |
| DUKE | Take then this your companion by the hand,<br>Who hath a story ready for your ear.<br>I shall attend your leisure; but make haste,<br>The vaporous [[Night|night]] approaches. |
| [[Mariana|MARIANA]] | (to [[Isabella]]) Will’t please you walk aside? |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt |
| [[Mariana|MARIANA]] | [[Mariana]]<br>and<br>[[Isabella]] |
| DUKE | O place and [[Greatness|greatness]], millions of false eyes<br>Are stuck upon thee; volumes of report<br>Run with their false and most contrarious [[Quest|quest]]<br>Upon thy doings; thousand escapes of wit<br>Make thee the father of their idle dream,<br>And rack thee in their fancies. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Mariana]] and [[Isabella]] |
| DUKE | Welcome. How agreed? |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | She’ll take the enterprise upon her, father,<br>If you advise it. |
| DUKE | It is not my consent,<br>But my entreaty too. |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | (to [[Mariana]]) Little have you to say<br>When you depart from him but, soft and low,<br>‘Remember now my brother’. |
| [[Mariana|MARIANA]] | [[Fear]] me not. |
| DUKE | Nor, gentle daughter, [[Fear|fear]] you not at all.<br>He is your husband on a pre-contract.<br>To bring you thus together ’tis no sin,<br>Sith that the justice of your title to him<br>Doth flourish the deceit. Come, let us go.<br>Our corn’s to reap, for yet our tilth’s to sow. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt |
