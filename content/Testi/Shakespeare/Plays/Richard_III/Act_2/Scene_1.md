| *(didascalia)* | Flourish. Enter [[King]] Edward, sick, [[Queen Elizabeth]], Lord Marquis Dorset, Lord Rivers, Lord [[Hastings]], Sir William Catesby, the [[Duke of Buckingham]] and Lord Gray |
| [[King|KING]] EDWARD | Why, so! Now have I done a good day’s work.<br>You peers, continue this united league.<br>I every day expect an embassage<br>From my redeemer to redeem me hence,<br>And more in peace my [[Soul|soul]] shall part to heaven<br>Since I have made my friends at peace on earth.<br>[[Hastings]] and Rivers, take each other’s hand.<br>Dissemble not your hatred; swear your [[Love|love]]. |
| RIVERS | By heaven, my [[Soul|soul]] is purged from grudging hate,<br>And with my hand I seal my true heart’s [[Love|love]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | He takes [[Hastings]]’ hand |
| LORD [[Hastings|HASTINGS]] | So thrive I, as I truly swear the like. |
| [[King|KING]] EDWARD | Take heed you dally not before your [[King|king]],<br>Lest he that is the supreme [[King]] of Kings<br>Confound your hidden falsehood, and award<br>Either of you to be the other’s end. |
| LORD [[Hastings|HASTINGS]] | So prosper I, as I swear perfect [[Love|love]]. |
| RIVERS | And I, as I [[Love|love]] [[Hastings]] with my heart. |
| [[King|KING]] EDWARD | (to Elizabeth)<br>Madam, yourself is not exempt from this,<br>Nor your son Dorset;—[[Buckingham]], nor you.<br>You have been factious one against the other.<br>Wife, [[Love|love]] Lord [[Hastings]], let him kiss your hand—<br>And what you do, do it unfeignedly. |
| [[Queen Elizabeth|QUEEN ELIZABETH]] | (giving [[Hastings]] her hand to kiss)<br>There, [[Hastings]]. I will never more remember<br>Our former hatred: so thrive I, and mine. |
| [[King|KING]] EDWARD | Dorset, embrace him. [[Hastings]], [[Love|love]] Lord Marquis. |
| DORSET | This interchange of [[Love|love]], I here protest,<br>Upon my part shall be inviolable. |
| LORD [[Hastings|HASTINGS]] | And so swear I.<br>They embrace |
| [[King|KING]] EDWARD | Now, princely [[Buckingham]], seal thou this league<br>With thy embracements to my wife’s allies,<br>And make me happy in your [[unity]]. |
| [[Buckingham|BUCKINGHAM]] | (to Elizabeth)<br>Whenever [[Buckingham]] doth turn his hate<br>Upon your grace, but with all duteous [[Love|love]]<br>Doth cherish you and yours, God punish me<br>With hate in those where I expect most [[Love|love]].<br>When I have most need to employ a friend,<br>And most assured that he is a friend,<br>Deep, hollow, treacherous, and full of guile<br>Be he unto me. This do I beg of heaven,<br>When I am cold in [[Love|love]] to you or yours.<br>They embrace |
| [[King|KING]] EDWARD | A pleasing cordial, princely [[Buckingham]],<br>Is this thy vow unto my sickly heart.<br>There wanteth now our brother [[Gloucester]] here,<br>To make the blessèd period of this peace. |

| [[King|KING]] EDWARD | [[Gloucester]] |
| [[Buckingham|BUCKINGHAM]] | And in good [[Time|time]],<br>Here comes Sir [[Richard]] Ratcliffe and the Duke. |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Good morrow to my sovereign [[King]] and [[Queen]].—<br>And princely peers, a happy [[Time|time]] of day. |
| [[King|KING]] EDWARD | Happy indeed, as we have spent the day.<br>Brother, we have done deeds of [[Charity|charity]],<br>Made peace of enmity, fair [[Love|love]] of hate,<br>Between these swelling wrong-incensed peers. |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | A blessed labour, my most sovereign lord.<br>Among this princely heap if any here,<br>By false intelligence or wrong surmise,<br>Hold me a foe,<br>If I unwittingly or in my rage<br>Have aught committed that is hardly borne<br>By any in this presence, I [[Desire|desire]]<br>To reconcile me to his friendly peace.<br>‘Tis [[Death|death]] to me to be at enmity.<br>I hate it, and [[Desire|desire]] all good men’s [[Love|love]].—<br>First, madam, I entreat true peace of you,<br>Which I will purchase with my duteous service.—<br>Of you, my noble cousin [[Buckingham]],<br>If ever any grudge were lodged between us.—<br>Of you, Lord Rivers, and Lord Gray of you,<br>That all without [[Desert|desert]] have frowned on me.—<br>Dukes, earls, lords, gentlemen, indeed of all!<br>I do not know that Englishman alive<br>With whom my [[Soul|soul]] is any jot at odds<br>More than the infant that is born tonight.<br>I thank my God for my [[Humility|humility]]. |
| [[Queen Elizabeth|QUEEN ELIZABETH]] | A holy day shall this be kept hereafter.<br>I would to God all strifes were [[Well|well]] compounded.—<br>My sovereign lord, I do beseech your highness<br>To take our brother [[Clarence]] to your grace. |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Why, madam, have I offered [[Love|love]] for this,<br>To be so flouted in this royal presence?<br>Who knows not that the gentle Duke is dead?<br>The others all start<br>You do him injury to scorn his corpse. |
| *(didascalia)* | RIVERS |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Who knows not he is dead? Who knows he is? |
| [[Queen Elizabeth|QUEEN ELIZABETH]] | All-seeing heaven, what a world is this? |
| [[Buckingham|BUCKINGHAM]] | Look I so pale, Lord Dorset, as the rest? |
| DORSET | Ay, my good lord, and no one in the presence<br>But his red colour hath forsook his cheeks. |
| [[King|KING]] EDWARD | Is [[Clarence]] dead? The order was reversed. |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | But he, poor man, by your first order died,<br>And that a winged Mercury did bear;<br>Some tardy cripple bore the countermand,<br>That came too lag to see him buried.<br>God grant that some, less noble and less loyal,<br>Nearer in bloody thoughts, but not in [[Blood|blood]],<br>Deserve not worse than wretched [[Clarence]] did,<br>And yet go current from suspicion. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Lord Stanley Earl of Derby |
| STANLEY | (kneeling)<br>A boon, my sovereign, for my service done. |
| [[King|KING]] EDWARD | I pray thee, peace! My [[Soul|soul]] is full of sorrow. |
| STANLEY | I will not rise, unless your highness hear me. |
| [[King|KING]] EDWARD | Then say at once, what is it thou requests? |
| STANLEY | The forfeit, sovereign, of my servant’s life,<br>Who slew today a riotous gentleman,<br>Lately attendant on the Duke of Norfolk. |
| [[King|KING]] EDWARD | Have I a tongue to doom my brother’s [[Death|death]],<br>And shall that tongue give pardon to a slave?<br>My brother slew no man; his fault was thought;<br>And yet his punishment was bitter [[Death|death]].<br>Who sued to me for him? Who in my wrath<br>Kneeled at my feet, and bid me be advised?<br>Who spoke of brotherhood? Who spoke of [[Love|love]]?<br>Who told me how the poor [[Soul|soul]] did forsake<br>The mighty Warwick and did fight for me?<br>Who told me, in the field at Tewkesbury,<br>When [[Oxford]] had me down, he rescued me,<br>And said, ‘Dear brother, live, and be a [[King|king]]’?<br>Who told me, when we both lay in the field,<br>Frozen almost to [[Death|death]], how he did lap me<br>Even in his garments, and did give himself<br>All thin and naked to the numb-cold [[Night|night]]?<br>All this from my remembrance brutish wrath<br>Sinfully plucked, and not a man of you<br>Had so much grace to put it in my mind.<br>But when your carters or your waiting vassals<br>Have done a drunken slaughter, and defaced<br>The precious image of our dear redeemer,<br>You straight are on your knees for ‘Pardon, pardon!’—<br>And I, unjustly too, must grant it you.<br>But, for my brother, not a man would speak,<br>Nor I, ungracious, speak unto myself<br>For him, poor [[Soul|soul]]. The proudest of you all<br>Have been beholden to him in his life,<br>Yet none of you would once beg for his life.<br>O God, I [[Fear|fear]] thy justice will take hold<br>On me—and you, and mine, and yours, for this.—<br>Come, [[Hastings]], help me to my closet.<br>Ah, poor [[Clarence]]! |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt some with [[King]] and [[Queen]] |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | This is the fruits of rashness. Marked you not<br>How that the guilty kindred of the [[Queen]]<br>Looked pale, when they did hear of [[Clarence]]’ [[Death|death]]?<br>O, they did urge it still unto the [[King]].<br>God will [[Revenge|revenge]] it. Come, lords, will you go<br>To comfort Edward with our company? |
| [[Buckingham|BUCKINGHAM]] | We wait upon your grace. Exeunt |
