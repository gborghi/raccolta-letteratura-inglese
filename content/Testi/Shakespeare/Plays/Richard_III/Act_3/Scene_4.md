| *(didascalia)* | Enter the [[Duke of Buckingham]], Lord Stanley Earl of Derby, Lord [[Hastings]], Bishop of Ely, the Duke of Norfolk, Sir William Catesby, with others at a table |
| LORD [[Hastings|HASTINGS]] | Now, noble peers, the cause why we are met<br>Is to determine of the coronation.<br>In God’s name, speak: when is the royal day? |
| [[Buckingham|BUCKINGHAM]] | Is all things ready for that solemn [[Time|time]]? |
| STANLEY | It is, and wants but nomination. |
| BISHOP OF ELY | Tomorrow, then, I judge a happy day. |
| [[Buckingham|BUCKINGHAM]] | Who knows the Lord Protector’s mind herein?<br>Who is most inward with the noble Duke? |
| BISHOP OF ELY | Your grace, methinks, should soonest know his mind. |
| [[Buckingham|BUCKINGHAM]] | We know each other’s faces. For our hearts,<br>He knows no more of mine than I of yours,<br>Or I of his, my lord, than you of mine.—<br>Lord [[Hastings]], you and he are near in [[Love|love]]. |
| LORD [[Hastings|HASTINGS]] | I thank his grace; I know he loves me [[Well|well]].<br>But for his purpose in the coronation,<br>I have not sounded him, nor he delivered<br>His gracious pleasure any way therein.<br>But you, my honourable lords, may name the [[Time|time]],<br>And in the Duke’s behalf I’ll give my voice,<br>Which I presume he’ll take in gentle part. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Richard]] [[Duke of Gloucester]] |
| BISHOP OF ELY | In happy [[Time|time]], here comes the Duke himself. |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | My noble lords, and cousins all, good morrow.<br>I have been long a sleeper, but I trust<br>My absence doth neglect no great design<br>Which by my presence might have been concluded. |
| [[Buckingham|BUCKINGHAM]] | Had not you come upon your cue, my lord,<br>William Lord [[Hastings]] had pronounced your part—<br>I mean, your voice, for crowning of the [[King]]. |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Than my Lord [[Hastings]] no man might be bolder.<br>His lordship knows me [[Well|well]], and loves me [[Well|well]].—<br>My lord of Ely, when I was last in Holborn<br>I saw good strawberries in your [[Garden|garden]] there.<br>I do beseech you send for some of them. |
| BISHOP OF ELY | Marry, and will, my lord, with all my heart. Exit |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Cousin of [[Buckingham]], a word with you.<br>(Aside) Catesby hath sounded [[Hastings]] in our business,<br>And finds the testy gentleman so hot<br>That he will lose his head ere give consent<br>His ‘master’s [[Child|child]]’—as worshipful he terms it—<br>Shall lose the royalty of England’s throne. |
| [[Buckingham|BUCKINGHAM]] | Withdraw yourself a while; I’ll go with you. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt [[Richard]] and [[Buckingham]] |
| STANLEY | We have not yet set down this day of triumph.<br>Tomorrow, in my judgement, is too sudden,<br>For I myself am not so [[Well|well]] provided<br>As else I would be, were the day prolonged. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Bishop of Ely |
| BISHOP OF ELY | Where is my lord, the [[Duke of Gloucester]]?<br>I have sent for these strawberries. |
| LORD [[Hastings|HASTINGS]] | His grace looks cheerfully and smooth this morning.<br>There’s some conceit or other likes him [[Well|well]],<br>When that he bids good morrow with such spirit.<br>I think there’s never a man in Christendom<br>Can lesser hide his [[Love|love]] or hate than he,<br>For by his face straight shall you know his heart. |
| STANLEY | What of his heart perceive you in his face<br>By any likelihood he showed today? |
| LORD [[Hastings|HASTINGS]] | Marry, that with no man here he is offended—<br>For were he, he had shown it in his looks. |
| STANLEY | I pray God he be not. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Richard]] and [[Buckingham]] |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | I pray you all, tell me what they deserve<br>That do conspire my [[Death|death]] with devilish plots<br>Of damned witchcraft, and that have prevailed<br>Upon my [[Body|body]] with their hellish charms? |
| LORD [[Hastings|HASTINGS]] | The tender [[Love|love]] I bear your grace, my lord,<br>Makes me most forward in this princely presence<br>To doom th’offenders, whatsoe’er they be.<br>I say, my lord, they have deserved [[Death|death]]. |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Then be your eyes the witness of their evil:<br>See how I am bewitched. Behold, mine arm<br>Is like a blasted sapling withered up.<br>And this is Edward’s wife, that monstrous [[Witch|witch]],<br>Consorted with that harlot, strumpet Shore,<br>That by their witchcraft thus have marked me. |
| LORD [[Hastings|HASTINGS]] | If they have done this deed, my noble lord— |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | ‛If’? Thou protector of this damned strumpet,<br>Talk’st thou to me of ‛ifs’ ? Thou art a traitor.—<br>Off with his head. Now, by Saint Paul I swear,<br>I will not dine until I see the same.<br>Some see it done.<br>The rest that [[Love|love]] me, rise and follow me. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt all but Catesby and [[Hastings]] |
| LORD [[Hastings|HASTINGS]] | Woe, woe for England! Not a whit for me,<br>For I, too fond, might have prevented this.<br>Stanley did dream the boar did raze our helms,<br>But I did scorn it and disdain to fly.<br>Three times today my footcloth [[Horse|horse]] did stumble,<br>And started when he looked upon the Tower,<br>As loath to bear me to the slaughterhouse.<br>O now I need the priest that spake to me.<br>I now repent I told the pursuivant,<br>As too triumphing, how mine enemies<br>Today at Pomfret bloodily were butchered,<br>And I myself secure in grace and favour.<br>O Margaret, Margaret! Now thy heavy curse<br>Is lighted on poor [[Hastings]]’ wretched head. |
| *(didascalia)* | CATESBY |
| LORD [[Hastings|HASTINGS]] | Come, come, dispatch: the Duke would be at dinner.<br>Make a short shrift; he longs to see your head. |
| LORD [[Hastings|HASTINGS]] | O momentary grace of mortal men,<br>Which we more hunt for than the grace of God.<br>Who builds his [[Hope|hope]] in th’air of your good looks<br>Lives like a drunken sailor on a mast,<br>Ready with every nod to tumble down<br>Into the fatal bowels of the deep. |
| *(didascalia)* | CATESBY |
| LORD [[Hastings|HASTINGS]] | Come, come, dispatch. ‘Tis bootless to exclaim. |
| LORD [[Hastings|HASTINGS]] | O bloody [[Richard]]! Miserable England!<br>I prophesy the fearful’st [[Time|time]] to thee<br>That ever wretched age hath looked upon.—<br>Come lead me to the block; bear him my head.<br>They smile at me, who shortly shall be dead. Exeunt<br><br>3.5Enter [[Richard]] [[Duke of Gloucester]] and the [[Duke of Buckingham]] in rotten armour, marvellous ill-favoured |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Come, cousin, canst thou quake and change thy colour?<br>Murder thy breath in middle of a word?<br>And then again begin, and stop again,<br>As if thou wert distraught and mad with terror? |
| [[Buckingham|BUCKINGHAM]] | Tut, I can counterfeit the deep tragedian,<br>Tremble and start at wagging of a straw,<br>Speak, and look back, and pry on every side,<br>Intending deep suspicion; ghastly looks<br>Are at my service, like enforced smiles,<br>And both are ready in their offices<br>At any [[Time|time]] to grace my stratagems. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter the [[Lord Mayor]] |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | (aside to [[Buckingham]])<br>Here comes the Mayor. |
| [[Buckingham|BUCKINGHAM]] | (aside to [[Richard]])<br>Let me [[Alone|alone]] to entertain him.—[[Lord Mayor]]— |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Richard|RICHARD]] [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] calling as to one within |
| [[Buckingham|BUCKINGHAM]] | Look to the drawbridge there! |
| [[Buckingham|BUCKINGHAM]] | Hark, a drum! |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Richard|RICHARD]] [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] calling as to one within |
| [[Buckingham|BUCKINGHAM]] | Catesby, o’erlook the walls! |
| [[Buckingham|BUCKINGHAM]] | [[Lord Mayor]], the reason we have sent— |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Look back, defend thee! Here are enemies. |
| [[Buckingham|BUCKINGHAM]] | God and our [[innocence]] defend and guard us. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Sir William Catesby with [[Hastings]]’ head |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | O, O, be quiet! It is Catesby. |
| CATESBY | Here is the head of that ignoble traitor,<br>The dangerous and unsuspected [[Hastings]]. |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | So dear I loved the man that I must weep.<br>I took him for the plainest harmless creature<br>That breathed upon the earth, a Christian,<br>Made him my book wherein my [[Soul|soul]] recorded<br>The [[history]] of all her secret thoughts.<br>So smooth he daubed his vice with show of virtue<br>That, his apparent open [[Guilt|guilt]] omitted—<br>I mean, his conversation with Shore’s wife—<br>He lived from all attainture of suspect. |
| [[Buckingham|BUCKINGHAM]] | The covert’st sheltered traitor that ever lived.<br>(To the Mayor) Would you imagine, or almost believe—<br>Were’t not that, by great preservation,<br>We live to tell it—that the subtle traitor<br>This day had plotted in the Council [[house]]<br>To murder me and my good lord of [[Gloucester]]? |
| MAYOR | Had he done so? |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | What, think you we are Turks or infidels,<br>Or that we would against the form of law<br>Proceed thus rashly in the [[Villain|villain]]’s [[Death|death]]<br>But that the extreme peril of the case,<br>The peace of England, and our persons’ safety,<br>Enforced us to this execution? |
| MAYOR | Now fair befall you, he deserved his [[Death|death]],<br>And your good graces both have [[Well|well]] proceeded,<br>To warn false traitors from the like attempts.<br>I never looked for better at his hands<br>After he once fell in with Mrs Shore. |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Richard|RICHARD]] [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] |
| MAYOR | Yet had not we determined he should die,<br>Until your lordship came to see his end,<br>Which now the loving haste of these our friends—<br>Something against our meanings—have prevented;<br>Because, my lord, we would have had you hear<br>The traitor speak, and timorously confess<br>The manner and the purpose of his treason,<br>That you might [[Well|well]] have signified the same<br>Unto the citizens, who haply may<br>Misconster us in him, and wail his [[Death|death]]. |
| MAYOR | But, my good lord, your graces’ word shall serve<br>As [[Well|well]] as I had seen and heard him speak.<br>And do not [[Doubt|doubt]], right noble princes both,<br>But I’ll acquaint our duteous citizens<br>With all your just proceedings in this cause. |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | And to that end we wished your lordship here,<br>T’avoid the censures of the carping world. |
| [[Buckingham|BUCKINGHAM]] | Which, since you come too late of our intent,<br>Yet witness what you hear we did intend,<br>And so, my good [[Lord Mayor]], we bid farewell. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit Mayor |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Go after; after, cousin [[Buckingham]]!<br>The Mayor towards Guildhall hies him in all post;<br>There, at your meetest vantage of the [[Time|time]],<br>Infer the bastardy of Edward’s children.<br>Tell them how Edward put to [[Death|death]] a citizen<br>Only for saying he would make his son<br>‘Heir to the [[Crown]]’—meaning indeed, his [[house]],<br>Which by the sign thereof was termed so.<br>Moreover, urge his hateful luxury<br>And bestial appetite in change of lust,<br>Which stretched unto their servants, daughters, wives,<br>Even where his raging eye, or savage heart,<br>Without control, listed to make a prey.<br>Nay, for a need, thus far come near my person:<br>Tell them, when that my mother went with [[Child|child]]<br>Of that insatiate Edward, noble York,<br>My princely father, then had wars in France,<br>And by true computation of the [[Time|time]]<br>Found that the issue was not his begot—<br>Which [[Well|well]] appeared in his lineaments,<br>Being nothing like the noble Duke my father.<br>Yet touch this sparingly, as ’twere far off,<br>Because, my lord, you know my mother lives. |
| [[Buckingham|BUCKINGHAM]] | [[Doubt]] not, my lord, I’ll play the orator<br>As if the golden fee for which I plead<br>Were for myself. And so, my lord, adieu.<br>He starts to go |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | If you thrive [[Well|well]], bring them to Baynard’s [[Castle]],<br>Where you shall find me [[Well|well]] accompanied<br>With reverend fathers and [[Well|well]]-learnèd bishops. |
| [[Buckingham|BUCKINGHAM]] | I go, and towards three or four o’[[Clock|clock]]<br>Look for the news that the Guildhall affords. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Now will I in, to take some privy order<br>To draw the brats of [[Clarence]] out of sight,<br>And to give notice that no manner person<br>Have any [[Time|time]] recourse unto the Princes. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt |
