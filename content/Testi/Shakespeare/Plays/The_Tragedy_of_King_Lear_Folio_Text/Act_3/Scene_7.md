| *(didascalia)* | Enter the Duke of Cornwall, Regan, Goneril, Edmond the bastard, and Servants |
| CORNWALL | (to Goneril)<br>Post speedily to my lord your husband.<br>Show him this letter. The army of France is landed.<br>(To Servants) Seek out the traitor Gloucester. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt some |
| REGAN | Hang him instantly. |
| GONERIL | Pluck out his eyes. |
| CORNWALL | Leave him to my displeasure.<br>Edmond, keep you our sister company.<br>The revenges we are bound to take upon your traitorous<br>father are not fit for your beholding. Advise the Duke<br>where you are going, to a most festinate preparation;<br>we are bound to the like. Our posts shall be swift and<br>intelligent betwixt us. (To Goneril) Farewell, dear sister.<br>(To Edmond) Farewell, my lord of Gloucester. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Oswald the steward |
| CORNWALL | How now, where’s the King? |
| OSWALD | My lord of Gloucester hath conveyed him hence.<br>Some five- or six-and-thirty of his knights,<br>Hot questrists after him, met him at gate,<br>Who, with some other of the lord’s dependants,<br>Are gone with him toward Dover, where they boast<br>To have well-armèd friends. |
| CORNWALL | Get horses for your mistress. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit Oswald |
| GONERIL | Farewell, sweet lord, and sister. |
| CORNWALL | Edmond, farewell. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt Goneril and Edmond |
| CORNWALL | (To Servants) Go seek the traitor Gloucester.<br>Pinion him like a thief; bring him before us. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt other Servants |
| CORNWALL | Though well we may not pass upon his life<br>Without the form of justice, yet our power<br>Shall do a curtsy to our wrath, which men<br>May blame but not control. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter the Duke of Gloucester and Servants |
| CORNWALL | Who’s there—the traitor? |
| REGAN | Ingrateful fox, ’tis he. |
| CORNWALL | (to Servants) Bind fast his corky arms. |
| GLOUCESTER | What means your graces? Good my friends, consider<br>You are my guests. Do me no foul play, friends. |
| CORNWALL | (to Servants)<br>Bind him, I say. |
| REGAN | Hard, hard! O filthy traitor! |
| GLOUCESTER | Unmerciful lady as you are, I’m none. |
| CORNWALL | (to Servants)<br>To this chair bind him. (To Gloucester) Villain, thou shalt find—<br>Regan plucks Gloucester’s beard |
| GLOUCESTER | By the kind gods, ’tis most ignobly done,<br>To pluck me by the beard. |
| REGAN | So white, and such a traitor? |
| GLOUCESTER | Naughty lady,<br>These hairs which thou dost ravish from my chin<br>Will quicken and accuse thee. I am your host.<br>With robbers’ hands my hospitable favours<br>You should not ruffle thus. What will you do? |
| CORNWALL | Come, sir, what letters had you late from France? |
| REGAN | Be simple-answered, for we know the truth. |
| CORNWALL | And what confederacy have you with the traitors<br>Late footed in the kingdom? |
| REGAN | To whose hands<br>You have sent the lunatic King. Speak. |
| GLOUCESTER | I have a letter guessingly set down,<br>Which came from one that’s of a neutral heart,<br>And not from one opposed. |
| CORNWALL | Cunning. |
| REGAN | And false. |
| CORNWALL | Where hast thou sent the King? |
| GLOUCESTER | To Dover. |
| REGAN | Wherefore to Dover? Wast thou not charged at peril— |
| CORNWALL | Wherefore to Dover?—Let him answer that. |
| GLOUCESTER | I am tied to th’ stake, and I must stand the course. |
| REGAN | Wherefore to Dover? |
| GLOUCESTER | Because I would not see thy cruel nails<br>Pluck out his poor old eyes, nor thy fierce sister<br>In his anointed flesh stick boarish fangs.<br>The sea, with such a storm as his bare head<br>In hell-black night endured, would have buoyed up<br>And quenched the stellèd fires.<br>Yet, poor old heart, he holp the heavens to rain.<br>If wolves had at thy gate howled that stern time,<br>Thou shouldst have said ‘Good porter, turn the key;<br>All cruels I’ll subscribe.’ But I shall see<br>The winged vengeance overtake such children. |
| CORNWALL | See’t shalt thou never.—Fettows, hold the chair.—<br>Upon these eyes of thine I’ll set my foot. |
| GLOUCESTER | He that will think to live till he be old<br>Give me some help!—O cruel! O you gods! |
| *(didascalia)* | Cornwall pulls out one of Gloucester’s eyes and stamps on it |
| REGAN | (to Cornwall)<br>One side will mock another; th’other, too. |
| CORNWALL | (to Gloucester)<br>If you see vengeance— |
| SERVANT | Hold your hand, my lord.<br>I have served you ever since I was a child,<br>But better service have I never done you<br>Than now to bid you hold. |
| REGAN | How now, you dog! |
| SERVANT | If you did wear a beard upon your chin |
| *(didascalia)* | I’d shake it on this quarrel. To Cornwall What do |
| SERVANT | you mean? |
| CORNWALL | My villein! |
| SERVANT | Nay then, come on, and take the chance of anger.<br>They draw and fight |
| REGAN | (to another Servant)<br>Give me thy sword. A peasant stand up thus! |
| *(didascalia)* | She takes a sword and runs at him behind |
| SERVANT | (to Gloucester)<br>O, I am slain. My lord, you have one eye left<br>To see some mischief on him. |
| *(didascalia)* | Regan stabs him again |
| SERVANT | O!<br>He dies |
| CORNWALL | Lest it see more, prevent it. Out, vile jelly! |
| *(didascalia)* | He pulls out Gloucester’s other eye |
| CORNWALL | Where is thy lustre now? |
| GLOUCESTER | All dark and comfortless. Where’s my son Edmond?<br>Edmond, enkindle all the sparks of nature<br>To quite this horrid act. |
| REGAN | Out, treacherous villain!<br>Thou call’st on him that hates thee. It was he<br>That made the overture of thy treasons to us,<br>Who is too good to pity thee. |
| GLOUCESTER | O, my follies! Then Edgar was abused.<br>Kind gods, forgive me that, and prosper him! |
| REGAN | (to Servants)<br>Go thrust him out at gates, and let him smell<br>His way to Dover. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit one or more with Gloucester |
| REGAN | How is’t, my lord? How look you? |
| CORNWALL | I have received a hurt. Follow me, lady.<br>(To Servants) Turn out that eyeless villain. Throw this<br>slave<br>Upon the dunghill. Regan, I bleed apace.<br>Untimely comes this hurt. Give me your arm. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt with the body |
