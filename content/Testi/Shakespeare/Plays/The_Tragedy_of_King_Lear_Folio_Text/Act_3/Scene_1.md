| *(didascalia)* | [[Storm]] still. Enter the Earl of [[Kent]] disguised and the First Gentleman, severally |
| [[Kent|KENT]] | Who’s there, besides foul weather? |
| *(didascalia)* | FIRST GENTLEMAN |
| [[Kent|KENT]] | One minded like the weather,<br>Most unquietly. |
| [[Kent|KENT]] | I know you. Where’s the [[King]]? |
| *(didascalia)* | FIRST GENTLEMAN |
| [[Kent|KENT]] | Contending with the fretful elements;<br>Bids the [[Wind|wind]] blow the earth into the sea<br>Or swell the curled waters ’bove the main,<br>That things might change or cease. |
| [[Kent|KENT]] | But who is with him? |
| *(didascalia)* | FIRST GENTLEMAN |
| [[Kent|KENT]] | None but the [[Fool]], who labours to outjest<br>His heart-struck injuries. |
| [[Kent|KENT]] | Sir, I do know you,<br>And dare upon the warrant of my note<br>Commend a dear thing to you. There is division,<br>Although as yet the face of it is covered<br>With mutual cunning, ’twixt Albany and Cornwall,<br>Who have—as who have not that their great [[Stars|stars]]<br>Throned and set high—servants, who seem no less,<br>Which are to France the spies and speculations<br>Intelligent of our state. What hath been seen,<br>Either in snuffs and packings of the Dukes,<br>Or the hard rein which both of them hath borne<br>Against the old kind [[King]]; or something deeper,<br>Whereof perchance these are but furnishings— |
| *(didascalia)* | FIRST GENTLEMAN |
| [[Kent|KENT]] | I will talk further with you. |
| [[Kent|KENT]] | No, do not.<br>For confirmation that I am much more<br>Than my out-wall, open this purse, and take<br>What it contains. If you shall see [[Cordelia]]—<br>As [[Fear|fear]] not but you shall—show her this ring<br>And she will tell you who that fellow is<br>That yet you do not know. Fie on this [[Storm|storm]]!<br>I will go seek the [[King]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | FIRST GENTLEMAN |
| [[Kent|KENT]] | Give me your hand. Have you no more to say? |
| [[Kent|KENT]] | Few words, but to effect more than all yet:<br>That when we have found the [[King]]—in which your<br>[[Pain|pain]]<br>That way, I’ll this—he that first lights on him<br>Holla the other. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt severally |
