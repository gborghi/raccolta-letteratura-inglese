| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Edgar]] disguised as a peasant, with a staff, guiding the blind [[Duke of Gloucester]] |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | When shall I come to th’ top of that same [[Hill|hill]]? |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | You do climb up it now. Look how we labour. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Methinks the ground is even. |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | Horrible steep.<br>Hark, do you hear the sea? |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | No, truly. |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | Why, then your other senses grow imperfect<br>By your eyes’ anguish. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | So may it be indeed.<br>Methinks thy voice is altered, and thou speak’st<br>In better phrase and matter than thou didst. |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | You’re much deceived. In nothing am I changed<br>But in my garments. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Methinks you’re better spoken. |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | Come on, sir, here’s the place. Stand still. How fearful<br>And dizzy ‘tis to cast one’s eyes so low!<br>The crows and choughs that wing the midway air<br>Show scarce so gross as beetles. Halfway down<br>Hangs one that gathers samphire, dreadful trade!<br>Methinks he seems no bigger than his head.<br>The fishermen that walk upon the beach<br>Appear like mice, and yon tall anchoring barque<br>Diminished to her cock, her cock a buoy<br>Almost too small for sight. The murmuring surge<br>That on th’unnumbered idle pebble chafes<br>Cannot be heard so high. I’ll look no more,<br>Lest my brain turn and the deficient sight<br>Topple down headlong. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Set me where you stand. |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | Give me your hand. You are now within a foot<br>Of th’extreme verge. For all beneath the [[Moon|moon]]<br>Would I not leap upright. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Let go my hand.<br>Here, friend, ’s another purse; in it a [[Jewel|jewel]]<br>[[Well]] worth a poor man’s taking. Fairies and gods<br>Prosper it with thee! Go thou further off.<br>Bid me farewell, and let me hear thee going. |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | Now fare ye [[Well|well]], good sir.<br>He stands aside |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | With all my heart. |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | (aside)<br>Why I do trifle thus with his [[Despair|despair]]<br>Is done to cure it. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | (keeling) O you mighty gods,<br>This world I do renounce, and in your sights<br>Shake patiently my great affliction off!<br>If I could bear it longer, and not fall<br>To quarrel with your great opposeless wills,<br>My snuff and loathed part of [[Nature|nature]] should<br>Burn itself out. If [[Edgar]] live, O bless him!—<br>Now, fellow, fare thee [[Well|well]]. |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | Gone, sir. Farewell.<br>[[Gloucester]] falls forward<br>(Aside) And yet I know not how conceit may rob<br>The treasury of life, when life itself<br>Yields to the theft. Had he been where he thought,<br>By this had thought been past.—Alive or dead?<br>(To [[Gloucester]]) Ho, you, sir, friend; hear you, sir?<br>Speak.<br>(Aside) Thus might he pass indeed. Yet he revives.<br>(To [[Gloucester]]) What are you, sir? |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Away, and let me die. |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | Hadst thou been aught but gossamer, feathers, air,<br>So many fathom down precipitating<br>Thou‘dst shivered like an egg. But thou dost breathe,<br>Hast heavy substance, bleed’st not, speak’st, art sound.<br>Ten masts a-length make not the altitude<br>Which thou hast perpendicularly fell.<br>Thy life’s a miracle. Speak yet again. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | But have I fall’n, or no? |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | From the dread summit of this chalky bourn.<br>Look up a-height. The shrill-gorged lark so far<br>Cannot be seen or heard. Do but look up. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Alack, I have no eyes.<br>Is wretchedness deprived that benefit<br>To end itself by [[Death|death]]? ’Twas yet some comfort<br>When misery could beguile the [[Tyrant|tyrant]]’s rage<br>And frustrate his proud will. |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | Give me your arm.<br>Up, so. How is’t? Feel you your legs? You stand. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Too [[Well|well]], too [[Well|well]]. |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | This is above all strangeness.<br>Upon the [[Crown|crown]] o’th’ cliff what thing was that<br>Which parted from you? |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | A poor unfortunate beggar. |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | As I stood here below, methoughts his eyes<br>Were two full moons. He had a thousand noses,<br>Horns whelked and waved like the enraged sea.<br>It was some fiend. Therefore, thou happy father,<br>Think that the clearest gods, who make them honours<br>Of men’s impossibilities, have preserved thee. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | I do remember now. Henceforth I’ll bear<br>Affliction till it do cry out itself<br>‘Enough, enough,’ and die. That thing you speak of,<br>I took it for a man. Often ‘twould say<br>‘The fiend, the fiend!’ He led me to that place. |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | Bear free and patient thoughts. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[King Lear]] mad, crowned with weeds and [[Flowers|flowers]] |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | But who comes here?<br>The safer sense will ne’er accommodate<br>His master thus. |
| LEAR | No, they cannot touch me for crying. I am the [[King]] himself. |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | O thou side-piercing sight! |
| LEAR | [[Nature]]’s above art in that respect. There’s your press-money. That fellow handles his bow like a crow-keeper. Draw me a clothier’s yard. Look, look, a mouse! Peace, peace, this piece of toasted [[cheese]] will do’t. There’s my gauntlet. I’ll prove it on a giant. Bring up the brown bills. O, [[Well|well]] flown, bird, i‘th’ clout, i’th’ clout! Whew! Give the word. |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | Sweet marjoram. |
| LEAR | Pass. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | I know that voice. |
| LEAR | Ha! [[Goneril]] with a white beard? They flattered me like a dog, and told me I had the white hairs in my beard ere the black ones were there. To say ‘ay’ and ‘no’ to everything that I said ‘ay’ and ‘no’ to was no good divinity. When the rain came to wet me once, and the [[Wind|wind]] to make me chatter; when the thunder would not peace at my bidding, there I found ‘em, there I smelt ’em out. Go to, they are not men o’ their words. They told me I was everything; ’tis a lie, I am not ague-proof. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | The trick of that voice I do [[Well|well]] remember.<br>Is’t not the [[King]]? |
| LEAR | Ay, every inch a [[King|king]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Gloucester]] kneels |
| LEAR | When I do stare, see how the subject quakes!<br>I pardon that man’s life. What was thy cause?<br>Adultery? Thou shalt not die. Die for adultery!<br>No, the wren goes to‘t, and the small gilded fly<br>Does lecher in my sight. Let copulation thrive,<br>For [[Gloucester]]’s bastard son<br>Was kinder to his father than my daughters<br>Got ’tween the lawful sheets. To‘t, luxury, pell-mell,<br>For I lack soldiers. Behold yon simp’ring dame,<br>Whose face between her forks presages [[Snow|snow]],<br>That minces virtue, and does shake the head<br>To hear of pleasure’s name.<br>The fitchew nor the soilèd [[Horse|horse]] goes to’t<br>With a more riotous appetite. Down from the waist<br>They’re centaurs, though women all above.<br>But to the girdle do the gods inherit;<br>Beneath is all the fiend’s. There’s hell, there’s darkness,<br>there is the sulphurous pit, burning, scalding, stench,<br>consumption. Fie, fie, fie; pah, pah! Give me an ounce<br>of civet, good apothecary, sweeten my [[Imagination|imagination]].<br>There’s money for thee. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | O, let me kiss that hand! |
| LEAR | Let me wipe it first; it smells of [[Mortality|mortality]]. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | O ruined piece of [[Nature|nature]]! This great world<br>Shall so wear out to naught. Dost thou know me? |
| LEAR | I remember thine eyes [[Well|well]] enough. Dost thou squiny at me?<br>No, do thy worst, blind Cupid, I’ll not [[Love|love]].<br>Read thou this challenge. Mark but the penning of it. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Were all thy letters suns, I could not see. |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | (aside)<br>I would not take this from report; it is,<br>And my heart breaks at it. |
| LEAR | (to [[Gloucester]]) Read. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | What—with the case of eyes? |
| LEAR | O ho, are you there with me? No eyes in your head, nor no money in your purse? Your eyes are in a heavy case, your purse in a light; yet you see how this world goes. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | I see it feelingly. |
| LEAR | What, art mad? A man may see how this world goes with no eyes; look with thine ears. See how yon justice rails upon yon simple thief. Hark in thine ear: change places, and handy-dandy, which is the justice, which is the thief? Thou hast seen a farmer’s dog bark at a beggar? |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Ay, sir. |
| LEAR | An the creature run from the cur, there thou mightst behold the great image of authority. A dog’s obeyed in office.<br>Thou rascal beadle, hold thy bloody hand.<br>Why dost thou lash that whore? Strip thy own back.<br>Thou hotly lusts to use her in that kind<br>For which thou whip‘st her. The usurer hangs the<br>cozener.<br>Through tattered clothes great vices do appear;<br>Robes and furred gowns hide all. Plate sin with gold,<br>And the strong lance of justice hurtless breaks;<br>Arm it in rags, a pygmy’s straw does pierce it.<br>None does offend, none, I say none. I’ll able ’em.<br>Take that of me, my friend, who have the power<br>To seal th’accuser’s lips. Get thee glass eyes,<br>And, like a scurvy politician, seem<br>To see the things thou dost not. Now, now, now, now!<br>Pull off my boots. Harder, harder! So. |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | (aside)<br>O, matter and impertinency mixed—170<br>Reason in [[Madness|madness]]! |
| LEAR | If thou wilt weep my fortunes, take my eyes.<br>I know thee [[Well|well]] enough: thy name is [[Gloucester]].<br>Thou must be patient. We came crying hither.<br>Thou know’st the first [[Time|time]] that we smell the air<br>We waul and cry. I will preach to thee. Mark. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Alack, alack the day! |
| *(didascalia)* | LEAR removing his [[Crown|crown]] of weeds |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | When we are born, we cry that we are come<br>To this great stage of fools. This’ a good block.<br>It were a delicate stratagem to shoe 180<br>A troop of [[Horse|horse]] with felt. I’ll put’t in proof,<br>And when I have stol’n upon these son-in-laws,<br>Then kill, kill, kill, kill, kill, kill! |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter two Gentlemen |
| *(didascalia)* | FIRSTGENTLEMAN |
| *(didascalia)* | O, here he is. Lay hand upon him. To LearSir, |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Your most dear daughter— |
| LEAR | No rescue? What, a prisoner? I am even<br>The natural [[Fool|fool]] of fortune. Use me [[Well|well]].<br>You shall have ransom. Let me have surgeons;<br>I am cut to th’ brains. |
| *(didascalia)* | FIRSTGENTLEMAN You shall have anything. |
| LEAR | No seconds? All myself?<br>Why, this would make a man a man of salt,<br>To use his eyes for [[Garden|garden]] [[Water|water]]-pots.<br>I will die bravely, like a smug bridegroom. What,<br>I will be jovial. Come, come, I am a [[King|king]].<br>Masters, know you that? |
| *(didascalia)* | FIRSTGENTLEMAN |
| LEAR | You are a royal one, and we obey you. |
| LEAR | Then there’s life in’t. Come, an you get it, you shall get it by running. Sa, sa, sa, sa! |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit running pursued by a Gentleman |
| *(didascalia)* | FIRSTGENTLEMAN |
| LEAR | A sight most pitiful in the meanest wretch,<br>Past speaking in a [[King|king]]. Thou hast a daughter<br>Who redeems [[Nature|nature]] from the general curse<br>Which twain have brought her to. |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | Hail, gentle sir. |
| *(didascalia)* | FIRSTGENTLEMAN Sir, [[Speed|speed]] you. What’s your will? |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | Do you hear aught, sir, of a battle toward? |
| *(didascalia)* | FIRST+GENTLEMAN |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | Most sure and vulgar, everyone hears that<br>That can distinguish sound. |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | But, by your favour,<br>How near’s the other army? |
| *(didascalia)* | FIRST GENTLEMAN, |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | Near and on speedy foot. The main descry<br>Stands in the hourly thought. |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | I thank you, sir. That’s all. |
| *(didascalia)* | FIRSTGENTLEMAN |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | Though that the [[Queen]] on special cause is here,<br>Her army is moved on. |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | I thank you, sir. Exit Gentleman |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | You ever gentle gods, take my breath from me.<br>Let not my worser spirit tempt me again<br>To die before you please. |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | [[Well]] pray you, father. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Now, good sir, what are you? |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | A most poor man, made tame to fortune’s blows,<br>Who by the art of known and feeling sorrows<br>Am pregnant to good pity. Give me your hand,<br>I’ll lead you to some biding. |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] rising |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | Hearty thanks.<br>The bounty and the benison of heaven<br>To boot and boot. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Oswald the steward |
| OSWALD | A proclaimed prize! Most happy!<br>That eyeless head of thine was first framed flesh<br>To raise my fortunes. Thou old unhappy traitor,<br>Briefly thyself remember. The [[Sword|sword]] is out<br>That must destroy thee. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Now let thy friendly hand<br>Put strength enough to’t. |
| OSWALD | (to [[Edgar]])<br>Wherefore, bold peasant,<br>Durst thou support a published traitor? Hence,<br>Lest that th’infection of his fortune take<br>Like hold on thee. Let go his arm. |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | ‘Chill not let go, sir, without vurther ’cagion. |
| OSWALD | Let go, slave, or thou diest. |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | Good gentleman, go your gate, and let poor volk pass. An ‘chud ha’ been swaggered out of my life, ’twould not ha’ been so long as ‘tis by a vortnight. Nay, come not near th’old man. Keep out, ‘che vor’ ye, or I’s’ try whether your [[Costard|costard]] or my baton be the harder; I’ll be plain with you. |
| OSWALD | Out, dunghill! |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | ’Chill pick your teeth, sir. Come, no matter vor your foins. |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Edgar]] knocks him down |
| OSWALD | Slave, thou hast slain me. [[Villain]], take my purse.<br>If ever thou wilt thrive, bury my [[Body|body]],<br>And give the letters which thou find’st about me<br>To Edmond, Earl of [[Gloucester]]. Seek him out<br>Upon the English party. O untimely [[Death|death]]! [[Death]]!<br>He dies |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | I know thee [[Well|well]]—a serviceable [[Villain|villain]],<br>As duteous to the vices of thy mistress<br>As badness would [[Desire|desire]]. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | What, is he dead? |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | Sit you down, father. Rest you.<br>[[Gloucester]] sits<br>Let’s see these pockets. The letters that he speaks of<br>May be my friends. He’s dead; I am only sorrow<br>He had no other deathsman. Let us see.<br>Leave, gentle wax, and manners; blame us not.<br>To know our enemies’ minds we rip their hearts;<br>Their papers is more lawful.<br>He reads the letter<br>’Let our reciprocal vows be remembered. You have<br>many opportunities to cut him off. If your will want<br>not, [[Time|time]] and place will be fruitfully offered. There is<br>nothing done if he return the conqueror; then am I<br>the prisoner, and his bed my jail, from the loathed<br>warmth whereof, deliver me, and supply the place for<br>your labour.<br>Your—wife, so I would say,—affectionate<br>servant, and for you her own for venture,<br>[[Goneril]].’<br>O indistinguished space of woman’s will—<br>A plot upon her virtuous husband’s life,<br>And the exchange my brother!—Here in the sands<br>Thee I’ll rake up, the post unsanctified<br>Of murderous lechers, and in the mature [[Time|time]]<br>With this ungracious paper strike the sight<br>Of the [[Death|death]]-practised Duke. For him ’tis [[Well|well]]<br>That of thy [[Death|death]] and business I can tell. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit with the [[Body|body]] |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | The [[King]] is mad. How stiff is my vile sense,<br>That I stand up and have ingenious feeling<br>Of my huge sorrows! Better I were distraught,<br>So should my thoughts be severed from my griefs, |
| *(didascalia)* | Drum afar off |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | And woes by wrong imaginations lose<br>The knowledge of themselves. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Edgar]] |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | Give me your hand.<br>Far off methinks I hear the beaten drum.<br>Come, father, I’ll bestow you with a friend. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit [[Edgar]] guiding [[Gloucester]] |
| *(didascalia)* | 4.6 Enter [[Queen]] [[Cordelia]], the Earl of [[Kent]] disguised, and the First Gentleman |
| [[Cordelia|CORDELIA]] | O thou good [[Kent]], how shall I live and work<br>To match thy goodness? My life will be too short,<br>And every measure fail me. |
| [[Kent|KENT]] | To be acknowledged, madam, is o’erpaid.<br>All my reports go with the modest [[Truth|truth]],<br>Nor more, nor clipped, but so. |
| [[Cordelia|CORDELIA]] | Be better suited.<br>These weeds are memories of those worser hours.<br>I prithee put them off. |
| [[Kent|KENT]] | Pardon, dear madam.<br>Yet to be known shortens my made intent.<br>My boon I make it that you know me not<br>Till [[Time|time]] and I think meet. |
| [[Cordelia|CORDELIA]] | Then be’t so, my good lord.—<br>How does the [[King]]? |
| *(didascalia)* | FIRSTGENTLEMAN Madam, sleeps still. |
| [[Cordelia|CORDELIA]] | O you kind gods,<br>Cure this great breach in his abused [[Nature|nature]];<br>Th’untuned and jarring senses O [[Wind|wind]] up<br>Of this [[Child|child]]-changed father! |
| *(didascalia)* | FIRSTGENTLEMAN |
| [[Cordelia|CORDELIA]] | So please your majesty<br>That we may wake the [[King]]? He hath slept long. |
| [[Cordelia|CORDELIA]] | Be governed by your knowledge, and proceed<br>I’th’ sway of your own will. Is he arrayed? |
| *(didascalia)* | FIRSTGENTLEMAN |
| [[Cordelia|CORDELIA]] | Ay, madam. In the heaviness of [[Sleep|sleep]]<br>We put fresh garments on him. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[King Lear]] asleep, in a chair carried by servants |

| [[Cordelia|CORDELIA]] | O my dear father, restoration hang<br>Thy medicine on my lips, and let this kiss<br>Repair those violent harms that my two sisters<br>Have in thy reverence made! |
| [[Kent|KENT]] | Kind and dear princess! |
| [[Cordelia|CORDELIA]] | Had you not been their father, these white flakes<br>Did challenge pity of them. Was this a face<br>To be opposed against the warring winds?<br>Mine enemy’s dog, though he had bit me, should<br>have stood<br>That [[Night|night]] against my [[Fire|fire]]. And wast thou fain, poor<br>father,<br>To hovel thee with swine and rogues forlorn<br>In short and musty straw? Alack, alack,<br>’Tis [[Wonder|wonder]] that thy life and wits at once<br>Had not concluded all! (To the Gentleman) He wakes.<br>Speak to him. |
| *(didascalia)* | FIRSTGENTLEMAN Madam, do you; ’tis fittest. |
| [[Cordelia|CORDELIA]] | (to Lear)<br>How does my royal lord? How fares your majesty? |
| LEAR | You do me wrong to take me out o’th’ [[Grave|grave]].<br>Thou art a [[Soul|soul]] in bliss, but I am bound<br>Upon a [[Wheel|wheel]] of [[Fire|fire]], that mine own [[Tears|tears]]<br>Do scald like molten lead. |
| [[Cordelia|CORDELIA]] | Sir, do you know me? |
| LEAR | You are a spirit, I know. Where did you die? |
| [[Cordelia|CORDELIA]] | (to the Gentleman) Still, still far wide! |
| *(didascalia)* | FIRST GENTLEMAN |
| [[Cordelia|CORDELIA]] | He’s scarce awake. Let him [[Alone|alone]] a while. |
| LEAR | Where have I been? Where am I? Fair daylight?<br>I am mightily abused. I should ev’n die with pity<br>To see another thus. I know not what to say.<br>I will not swear these are my hands. Let’s see:<br>I feel this pin prick. Would I were assured<br>Of my condition. |
| [[Cordelia|CORDELIA]] | (kneeling) O look upon me, sir,<br>And hold your hands in benediction o’er me.<br>You must not kneel. |
| LEAR | Pray do not mock.<br>I am a very foolish, fond old man,<br>Fourscore and upward,<br>Not an hour more nor less; and to deal plainly,<br>I [[Fear|fear]] I am not in my perfect mind.<br>Methinks I should know you, and know this man;<br>Yet I am doubtful, for I am mainly ignorant<br>What place this is; and all the skill I have<br>Remembers not these garments; nor I know not<br>Where I did lodge last [[Night|night]]. Do not laugh at me,<br>For as I am a man, I think this lady<br>To be my [[Child|child]], [[Cordelia]]. |
| [[Cordelia|CORDELIA]] | And so I am, I am. |
| LEAR | Be your [[Tears|tears]] wet? Yes, [[Faith|faith]]. I pray, weep not.<br>If you have [[Poison|poison]] for me, I will drink it.<br>I know you do not [[Love|love]] me; for your sisters<br>Have, as I do remember, done me wrong.<br>You have some cause; they have not. |
| [[Cordelia|CORDELIA]] | No cause, no cause. |
| LEAR | Am I in France? |
| [[Kent|KENT]] | In your own kingdom, sir. |
| LEAR | Do not abuse me. |
| *(didascalia)* | FIRSTGENTLEMAN |
| LEAR | Be comforted, good madam. The great rage<br>You see is killed in him. [[Desire]] him to go in.<br>Trouble him no more till further settling. |
| [[Cordelia|CORDELIA]] | (to Lear) Will’t please your highness walk? |
| LEAR | You must bear with me. Pray you now, forget<br>And forgive. I am old and foolish. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt |

| ALBANY | (to [[Regan]])<br>Our very loving sister, [[Well|well]] bemet.<br>(To Edmond) Sir, this I heard: the [[King]] is come to his<br>daughter,<br>With others whom the rigour of our state<br>Forced to cry out. |
| [[Regan|REGAN]] | Why is this reasoned? |
| [[Goneril|GONERIL]] | Combine together ’gainst the enemy;<br>For these domestic and particular broils<br>Are not the question here. |
| ALBANY | Let’s then determine with th’ensign of war<br>On our proceeding. |
| [[Regan|REGAN]] | Sister, you’ll go with us? |
| [[Goneril|GONERIL]] | No. |
| [[Regan|REGAN]] | ’Tis most convenient. Pray go with us. |
| [[Goneril|GONERIL]] | (aside)<br>O ho, I know the [[Riddle|riddle]]! (To [[Regan]]) I will go. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Edgar]] disguised as a peasant |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | (to Albany)<br>If e’er your grace had speech with man so poor,<br>Hear me one word. |
| ALBANY | (to the others) I’ll overtake you. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt both the armies |
| ALBANY | Speak. |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | Before you fight the battle, ope this letter.<br>If you have victory, let the trumpet sound<br>For him that brought it. Wretched though I seem,<br>I can produce a champion that will prove<br>What is avouchèd there. If you miscarry,<br>Your business of the world hath so an end,<br>And machination ceases. Fortune [[Love|love]] you. |
| ALBANY | Stay till I have read the letter. |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | I was forbid it.<br>When [[Time|time]] shall serve, let but the herald cry,<br>And I’ll appear again. |
| ALBANY | Why, fare thee [[Well|well]].<br>I will o’erlook thy paper. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit [[Edgar]] |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Edmond |
| EDMOND | The enemy’s in view; draw up your powers. |
| *(didascalia)* | He offersAlbany a paper |
| EDMOND | Here is the guess of their true strength and forces<br>By diligent discovery; but your haste<br>Is now urged on you. |
| ALBANY | We will greet the [[Time|time]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit |
| EDMOND | To both these sisters have I sworn my [[Love|love]],<br>Each jealous of the other as the stung<br>Are of the adder. Which of them shall I take?—<br>Both?—one?—or neither? Neither can be enjoyed<br>If both remain alive. To take the widow<br>Exasperates, makes mad, her sister [[Goneril]],<br>And hardly shall I carry out my side,<br>Her husband being alive. Now then, we’ll use<br>His countenance for the battle, which being done,<br>Let her who would be rid of him devise<br>His speedy taking off. As for the [[Mercy|mercy]]<br>Which he intends to Lear and to [[Cordelia]],<br>The battle done, and they within our power,<br>Shall never see his pardon; for my state<br>Stands on me to defend, not to [[debate]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit |
