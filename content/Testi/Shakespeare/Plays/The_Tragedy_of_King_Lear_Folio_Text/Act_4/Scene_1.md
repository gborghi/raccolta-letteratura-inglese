| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Edgar]] as a Bedlam beggar |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | Yet better thus and known to be contemned<br>Than still contemned and flattered. To be worst,<br>The low’st and most dejected thing of fortune,<br>Stands still in esperance, lives not in [[Fear|fear]].<br>The lamentable change is from the best;<br>The worst returns to laughter. Welcome, then,<br>Thou unsubstantial air that I embrace.<br>The wretch that thou hast blown unto the worst<br>Owes nothing to thy blasts. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter the [[Duke of Gloucester]] led by an Old Man |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | But who comes here?<br>My father, parti-eyed? World, world, O world!<br>But that thy strange mutations make us hate thee,<br>Life would not yield to age. |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Edgar]] stands aside |
| OLD MAN | (to [[Gloucester]]) O my good lord,<br>I have been your tenant and your father’s tenant<br>These fourscore years. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Away, get thee away, good friend, be gone.<br>Thy comforts can do me no good at all;<br>Thee they may hurt. |
| OLD MAN | You cannot see your way. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | I have no way, and therefore want no eyes.<br>I stumbled when I saw. Full oft ’tis seen<br>Our means secure us, and our mere defects<br>Prove our commodities. O dear son [[Edgar]],<br>The food of thy abused father’s wrath-<br>Might I but live to see thee in my touch<br>I’d say I had eyes again. |
| OLD MAN | How, now? Who’s there? |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | (aside)<br>O gods! Who is’t can say ‘I am at the worst’?<br>I am worse than e’er I was. |
| OLD MAN | (to [[Gloucester]])<br>’Tis poor mad Tom. |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | (aside)<br>And worse I may be yet. The worst is not<br>So long as we can say ‘This is the worst.’ |
| OLD MAN | (to [[Edgar]]) Fellow, where goest? |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Is it a beggarman? |
| OLD MAN | Madman and beggar too. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | A has some reason, else he could not beg.<br>I’th’ last [[Night|night]]’s [[Storm|storm]] I such a fellow saw,<br>Which made me think a man a worm. My son<br>Came then into my mind, and yet my mind<br>Was then scarce friends with him. I have heard more<br>since.<br>As flies to wanton boys are we to th’ gods;<br>They kill us for their sport. |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | (aside) How should this be?<br>Bad is the trade that must play [[Fool|fool]] to sorrow,<br>Ang’ring itself and others. |
| *(didascalia)* | He comes forward |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | Bless thee, master. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Is that the naked fellow? |
| OLD MAN | Ay, my lord. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Get thee away. If for my sake<br>Thou wilt o‘ertake us hence a mile or twain<br>I’th’ way toward Dover, do it for ancient [[Love|love]],<br>And bring some covering for this naked [[Soul|soul]],<br>Which I’ll entreat to lead me. |
| OLD MAN | Alack, sir, he is mad. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | ’Tis the [[Time|time]]’s plague when madmen lead the blind.<br>Do as I bid thee; or rather do thy pleasure.<br>Above the rest, be gone. |
| OLD MAN | I’ll bring him the best ’parel that I have, so<br>Come on’t what will. Exit |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Sirrah, naked fellow! |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | Poor Tom’s a-cold. (Aside) I cannot daub it further. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Come hither, fellow. |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | (aside)<br>And yet I must.<br>(To [[Gloucester]]) Bless thy sweet eyes, they bleed. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Know’st thou the way to Dover? |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | Both stile and gate, horseway and footpath. Poor Tom hath been scared out of his good wits. Bless thee, goodman’s son, from the foul fiend. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Here, take this purse, thou whom the heavens’<br>plagues<br>Have humbled to all strokes. That I am wretched<br>Makes thee the happier. Heavens deal so still.<br>Let the superfluous and lust-dieted man<br>That slaves your ordinance, that will not see<br>Because he does not feel, feel your power quickly.<br>So distribution should undo excess,<br>And each man have enough. Dost thou know Dover? |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | Ay, master. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | There is a cliff whose high and bending head<br>Looks fearfully in the confined deep.<br>Bring me but to the very brim of it<br>And I’ll repair the misery thou dost bear<br>With something rich about me. From that place<br>I shall no leading need. |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | Give me thy arm.<br>Poor Tom shall lead thee. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit [[Edgar]] guiding [[Gloucester]] |
