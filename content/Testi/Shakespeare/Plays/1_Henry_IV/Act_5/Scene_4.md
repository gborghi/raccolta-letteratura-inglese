| *(didascalia)* | Alarum. Excursions. Enter [[King]] Henry, Prince Harry, wounded, Lord John of Lancaster, and the Earl of Westmorland |
| [[King|KING]] HENRY | I prithee, Harry, withdraw thyself, thou bleed’st too<br>much.<br>Lord John of Lancaster, go you with him. |
| JOHN OF LANCASTER | Not I, my lord, unless I did bleed too. |
| PRINCE HARRY | (to the [[King]])<br>I beseech your majesty, make up,<br>Lest your retirement do amaze your friends. |
| [[King|KING]] HENRY | I will do so. My lord of Westmorland,<br>Lead him to his tent. |
| WESTMORLAND | (to the Prince)<br>Come, my lord, I’ll lead you to your tent. |
| PRINCE HARRY | Lead me, my lord? I do not need your help,<br>And God forbid a shallow scratch should drive<br>The Prince of Wales from such a field as this,<br>Where stained nobility lies trodden on,<br>And rebels’ arms triumph in massacres. |
| JOHN OF LANCASTER | We breathe too long. Come, cousin Westmorland,<br>Our duty this way lies. For God’s sake, come. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt Lancaster and Westmorland |
| PRINCE HARRY | By God, thou hast deceived me, Lancaster;<br>I did not think thee lord of such a spirit.<br>Before I loved thee as a brother, John,<br>But now I do respect thee as my [[Soul|soul]]. |
| [[King|KING]] HENRY | I saw him hold Lord Percy at the point<br>With lustier maintenance than I did look for<br>Of such an ungrown warrior. |
| PRINCE HARRY | O, this boy lends mettle to us all! Exit |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter the Earl of Douglas |
| DOUGLAS | Another [[King|king]]! They grow like Hydra’s heads.<br>I am the Douglas, fatal to all those<br>That wear those colours on them. What art thou<br>That counterfeit’st the person of a [[King|king]]? |
| [[King|KING]] HENRY | The [[King]] himself, who, Douglas, grieves at heart<br>So many of his shadows thou hast met<br>And not the very [[King]]. I have two boys<br>Seek Percy and thyself about the field;<br>But seeing thou fall’st on me so luckily,<br>I will assay thee; and defend thyself. |
| DOUGLAS | I [[Fear|fear]] thou art another counterfeit;<br>And yet, in [[Faith|faith]], thou bear’st thee like a [[King|king]].<br>But mine I am sure thou art, whoe’er thou be,<br>And thus I win thee.<br>They fight. The [[King]] being in danger, enter Prince Harry |
| PRINCE HARRY | Hold up thy head, vile Scot, or thou art like<br>Never to hold it up again. The spirits<br>Of valiant [[Shirley]], Stafford, Blunt, are in my arms.<br>It is the Prince of Wales that threatens thee,<br>Who never promiseth but he means to pay.<br>They fight. Douglas flieth<br>Cheerly, my lord! How fares your grace?<br>Sir Nicholas Gawsey hath for succour sent,<br>And so hath Clifton. I’ll to Clifton straight. |
| [[King|KING]] HENRY | Stay and breathe awhile.<br>Thou hast redeemed thy lost opinion,<br>And showed thou mak’st some tender of my life,<br>In this fair rescue thou hast brought to me. |
| PRINCE HARRY | O God, they did me too much injury<br>That ever said I hearkened for your [[Death|death]].<br>If it were so, I might have let [[Alone|alone]]<br>The insulting hand of Douglas over you,<br>Which would have been as speedy in your end<br>As all the poisonous potions in the world,<br>And saved the treacherous labour of your son. |
| [[King|KING]] HENRY | Make up to Clifton; I’ll to Sir Nicholas Gawsey. Exit |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Hotspur]] |
| [[Hotspur|HOTSPUR]] | If I mistake not, thou art Harry Monmouth. |
| PRINCE HARRY | Thou speak’st as if I would deny my name. |
| [[Hotspur|HOTSPUR]] | My name is Harry Percy. |
| PRINCE HARRY | Why then, I see<br>A very valiant [[Rebel|rebel]] of the name.<br>I am the Prince of Wales; and think not, Percy,<br>To share with me in glory any more.<br>Two [[Stars|stars]] keep not their motion in one sphere,<br>Nor can one England brook a [[Double|double]] reign<br>Of Harry Percy and the Prince of Wales. |
| [[Hotspur|HOTSPUR]] | Nor shall it, Harry, for the hour is come<br>To end the one of us, and would to God<br>Thy name in arms were now as great as mine. |
| PRINCE HARRY | I’ll make it greater ere I part from thee,<br>And all the budding honours on thy crest<br>I’ll crop to make a garland for my head. |
| [[Hotspur|HOTSPUR]] | I can no longer brook thy vanities.<br>They fight. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Sir John Oldcastle |
| SIR JOHN | [[Well]] said, Hal! To it, Hal! Nay, you shall find no boy’s play here, I can tell you. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Douglas. He fighteth with Sir John, who falls down as if he were dead. Exit Douglas. The Prince killeth [[Hotspur]] |
| [[Hotspur|HOTSPUR]] | O Harry, thou hast robbed me of my youth.<br>I better brook the loss of brittle life<br>Than those proud titles thou hast won of me.<br>They wound my thoughts worse than thy [[Sword|sword]] my<br>flesh.<br>But thoughts, the slaves of life, and life, [[Time|time]]’s [[Fool|fool]],<br>And [[Time|time]], that takes survey of all the world,<br>Must have a stop. O, I could prophesy,<br>But that the earthy and cold hand of [[Death|death]]<br>Lies on my tongue. No, Percy, thou art [[Dust|dust]],<br>And food for—He dies |
| PRINCE HARRY | For worms, brave Percy. Fare thee [[Well|well]], great heart.<br>Ill-weaved [[Ambition|ambition]], how much art thou shrunk!<br>When that this [[Body|body]] did contain a spirit,<br>A kingdom for it was too small a bound,<br>But now two paces of the vilest earth<br>Is room enough. This earth that bears thee dead<br>Bears not alive so stout a gentleman.<br>If thou wert sensible of courtesy,<br>I should not make so dear a show of zeal;<br>But let my favours hide thy mangled face,<br>He covers [[Hotspur]]’s face<br>And even in thy behalf I’ll thank myself<br>For doing these fair rites of tenderness.<br>Adieu, and take thy praise with thee to heaven.<br>Thy ignominy [[Sleep|sleep]] with thee in the [[Grave|grave]],<br>But not remembered in thy epitaph.<br>He spieth Sir John on the ground<br>What, old acquaintance! Could not all this flesh<br>Keep in a little life ? Poor Jack, farewell.<br>I could have better spared a better man.<br>O, I should have a heavy miss of thee,<br>If I were much in [[Love|love]] with [[Vanity|vanity]].<br>[[Death]] hath not struck so fat a deer today,<br>Though many dearer in this bloody fray.<br>Embowelled will I see thee by and by.<br>Till then, in [[Blood|blood]] by noble Percy lie. Exit<br>Sir John riseth up |
| SIR JOHN | Embowelled? If thou embowel me today, I’ll give you leave to powder me, and eat me too, tomorrow. ’Sblood, ’twas [[Time|time]] to counterfeit, or that hot termagant Scot had paid me, scot and lot too. Counterfeit? I lie, I am no counterfeit. To die is to be a counterfeit, for he is but the counterfeit of a man who hath not the life of a man. But to counterfeit dying when a man thereby liveth is to be no counterfeit, but the true and perfect image of life indeed. The better part of valour is discretion, in the which better part I have saved my life. Zounds, I am afraid of this gunpowder Percy, though he be dead. How if he should counterfeit too, and rise ? By my [[Faith|faith]], I am afraid he would prove the better counterfeit. Therefore I’ll make him sure; yea, and I’ll swear I killed him. Why may not he rise as [[Well|well]] as I? Nothing confutes me but eyes, and nobody sees me. Therefore, sirrah, (stabbing [[Hotspur]]) with a new wound in your thigh, come you along with me.<br>He takes up [[Hotspur]] on his back. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Prince Harry and Lord John of Lancaster |
| PRINCE HARRY | Come, brother John. Full bravely hast thou fleshed<br>Thy maiden [[Sword|sword]]. |
| JOHN OF LANCASTER | But soft; whom have we here? Did you not tell me this fat man was dead? |
| PRINCE HARRY | I did; I saw him dead, Breathless and bleeding on the ground. (To Sir John) Art thou alive? Or is it fantasy that plays upon our eyesight? I prithee speak; we will not trust our eyes Without our ears. Thou art not what thou seem’st. |
| SIR JOHN | No, that’s certain: I am not a [[Double|double]] man. But if I be not Jack Oldcastle, then am I a jack. There is Percy. If your father will do me any [[Honour|honour]], so; if not, let him kill the next Percy himself. I look to be either earl or duke, I can assure you. |
| PRINCE HARRY | Why, Percy I killed myself, and saw thee dead. |
| SIR JOHN | Didst thou ? Lord, Lord, how this world is given to lying! I grant you I was down and out of breath, and so was he; but we [[Rose|rose]] both at an instant, and fought a long hour by Shrewsbury [[Clock|clock]]. If I may be believed, so; if not, let them that should reward valour bear the sin upon their own heads. I’ll take’t on my [[Death|death]] I gave him this wound in the thigh. If the man were alive and would deny it, zounds, I would make him eat a piece of my [[Sword|sword]]. |
| JOHN OF LANCASTER | This is the strangest tale that e’er I heard. |
| PRINCE HARRY | This is the strangest fellow, brother John.<br>(To Sir John) Come, bring your luggage nobly on your<br>back.<br>For my part, if a lie may do thee grace,<br>I’ll gild it with the happiest terms I have.<br>A retreat is sounded<br>The trumpet sounds retreat; the day is our.<br>Come, brother, let us to the highest of the field<br>To see what friends are living, who are dead. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt the Prince and Lancaster |
| SIR JOHN | I’ll follow, as they say, for reward. He that rewards me, God reward him. If I do grow great, I’ll grow less; for I’ll purge, and leave sack, and live cleanly, as a nobleman should do. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit, bearing [[Hotspur]]’s [[Body|body]] |
