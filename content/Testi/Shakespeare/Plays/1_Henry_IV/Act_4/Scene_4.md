| *(didascalia)* | Enter the Archbishop of York, and Sir Michael |
| ARCHBISHOP | (giving letters)<br>Hie, good Sir Michael, bear this sealed brief<br>With winged haste to the Lord Marshal,<br>This to my cousin Scrope, and all the rest<br>To whom they are directed. If you knew<br>How much they do import, you would make haste. |
| SIR MICHAEL | My good lord,<br>I guess their tenor. |
| ARCHBISHOP | Like enough you do.<br>Tomorrow, good Sir Michael, is a day<br>Wherein the fortune of ten thousand men<br>Must bide the touch; for, sir, at Shrewsbury,<br>As I am truly given to understand,<br>The King with mighty and quick-raised power<br>Meets with Lord Harry. And I fear, Sir Michael,<br>What with the sickness of Northumberland,<br>Whose power was in the first proportion, 15<br>And what with Owain Glyndŵr’s absence thence,<br>Who with them was a rated sinew too,<br>And comes not in, overruled by prophecies,<br>I fear the power of Percy is too weak<br>To wage an instant trial with the King. |
| SIR MICHAEL | Why, my good lord, you need not fear; there is<br>Douglas<br>And Lord Mortimer. |
| ARCHBISHOP | No, Mortimer is not there. |
| SIR MICHAEL | But there is Mordake, Vernon, Lord Harry Percy;<br>And there is my lord of Worcester, and a head<br>Of gallant warriors, noble gentlemen. |
| ARCHBISHOP | And so there is; but yet the King hath drawn<br>The special head of all the land together—<br>The Prince of Wales, Lord John of Lancaster,<br>The noble Westmorland, and warlike Blunt,<br>And many more corrivals, and dear men<br>Of estimation and command in arms. |
| SIR MICHAEL | Doubt not, my lord, they shall be well opposed. |
| ARCHBISHOP | I hope no less, yet needful ’tis to fear;<br>And to prevent the worst, Sir Michael, speed.<br>For if Lord Percy thrive not, ere the King<br>Dismiss his power he means to visit us,<br>For he hath heard of our confederacy,<br>And ’tis but wisdom to make strong against him;<br>Therefore make haste. I must go write again<br>To other friends; and so farewell, Sir Michael. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt |
| *(didascalia)* | severally |
