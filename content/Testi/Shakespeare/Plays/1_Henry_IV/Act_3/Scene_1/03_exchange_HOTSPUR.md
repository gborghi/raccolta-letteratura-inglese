| [[Hotspur|HOTSPUR]] | standíng I think there’s no man speaketh better Welsh. I’ll to dinner. |
| MORTIMER | Peace, cousin Percy, you will make him mad. GLYNDŴR I can call spirits from the vasty deep. |
| [[Hotspur|HOTSPUR]] | Why, so can I, or so can any man; But will they come when you do call for them? GLYNDŴR Why, I can teach you, cousin, to command the devil. |
| [[Hotspur|HOTSPUR]] | And I can teach thee, coz, to shame the devil, By telling [[Truth|truth]]: ‘Tell [[Truth|truth]], and shame the devil’. If thou have power to raise him, bring him hither, And I’ll be sworn I have power to shame him hence. O, while you live, tell [[Truth|truth]] and shame the devil. |
| MORTIMER | Come, come, no more of this unprofitable chat. GLYNDŴR Three times hath [[Henry Bolingbroke]] made head Against my power; thrice from the banks of Wye And sandy-bottomed Severn have I sent him Bootless [[Home|home]], and weather-beaten back. |
| [[Hotspur|HOTSPUR]] | [[Home]] without boots, and in foul weather too! How scapes he agues, in the devil’s name? GLYNDŴR Come, here’s the map. Shall we divide our right, According to our threefold order ta’en? |
