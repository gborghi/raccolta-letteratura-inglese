|---|---|
| NORTHUMBERLAND | (to Worcester) Brother, the [[King]] hath made your nephew mad. |
| WORCESTER | Who struck this heat up after I was gone? |
| [[Hotspur|HOTSPUR]] | He will forsooth have all my prisoners; And when I urged the ransom once again Of my wife’s brother, then his cheek looked pale, And on my face he turned an eye of [[Death|death]], Trembling even at the name of Mortimer. |
| WORCESTER | I cannot blame him: was not he proclaimed By [[Richard]], that dead is, the next of [[Blood|blood]]? |
| NORTHUMBERLAND | He was; I heard the proclamation. And then it was when the unhappy [[King]], Whose wrongs in us God pardon, did set forth Upon his Irish expedition, From whence he, intercepted, did return To be deposed, and shortly murdered. |
| WORCESTER | And for whose [[Death|death]] we in the world’s wide mouth Live scandalized and foully spoken of. |
| [[Hotspur|HOTSPUR]] | But soft, I pray you; did [[King]] [[Richard]] then Proclaim my brother [[Edmund]] Mortimer Heir to the [[Crown|crown]]? |
| NORTHUMBERLAND | He did; myself did hear it. |
