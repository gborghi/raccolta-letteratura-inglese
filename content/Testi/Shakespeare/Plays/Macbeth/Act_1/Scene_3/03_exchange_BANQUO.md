| BANQUO | (to Ross and Angus) Look how our partner’s rapt. MACBETH(aside) If chance will have me king, why, chance may crown me Without my stir. |
| BANQUO | (to Ross and Angus) New honours come upon him, Like our strange garments, cleave not to their mould But with the aid of use. |
| MACBETH | (aside) Come what come may, Time and the hour runs through the roughest day. |
| BANQUO | Worthy Macbeth, we stay upon your leisure. |
| MACBETH | Give me your favour. My dull brain was wrought With things forgotten. (To Ross and Angus) Kind gentlemen, your pains Are registered where every day I turn The leaf to read them. Let us toward the King. (Aside to Banquo) Think upon what hath chanced, and at more time, The interim having weighed it, let us speak Our free hearts each to other. |
| BANQUO | Very gladly. |
| MACBETH | Till then, enough. (To Ross and Angus) Come, friends. Exeunt |
