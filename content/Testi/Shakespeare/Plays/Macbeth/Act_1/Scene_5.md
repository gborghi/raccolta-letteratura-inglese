| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Lady Macbeth]], with a letter |
| [[Lady Macbeth|LADY MACBETH]] | (reading) ‘They met me in the day of [[success]], and I have learned by the perfect’st report they have more in them than mortal knowledge. When I burned in [[Desire|desire]] to question them further, they made themselves air, into which they vanished. Whiles I stood rapt in the [[Wonder|wonder]] of it came missives from the [[King]], who all-hailed me “Thane of Cawdor”, by which title before these weird sisters saluted me, and referred me to the coming on of [[Time|time]] with “Hail, [[King]] that shalt be!” This have I thought good to deliver thee, my dearest partner of [[Greatness|greatness]], that thou mightst not lose the dues of rejoicing by being ignorant of what [[Greatness|greatness]] is promised thee. Lay it to thy heart, and farewell.’<br>Glamis thou art, and Cawdor, and shalt be<br>What thou art promised. Yet do I [[Fear|fear]] thy [[Nature|nature]].<br>It is too full o‘th’ milk of human kindness<br>To catch the nearest way. Thou wouldst be great,<br>Art not without [[Ambition|ambition]], but without<br>The illness should attend it. What thou wouldst highly,<br>That wouldst thou holily; wouldst not play false,<br>And yet wouldst wrongly win. Thou’dst have, great<br>Glamis,<br>That which cries ‘Thus thou must do’ if thou have it,<br>And that which rather thou dost [[Fear|fear]] to do<br>Than wishest should be undone. Hie thee hither,<br>That I may pour my spirits in thine ear<br>And chastise with the valour of my tongue<br>All that impedes thee from the golden round<br>Which fate and metaphysical aid doth seem<br>To have thee crowned withal. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter a Servant |
| [[Lady Macbeth|LADY MACBETH]] | What is your tidings? |
| SERVANT | The [[King]] comes here tonight. |
| [[Lady Macbeth|LADY MACBETH]] | Thou’t mad to say it.<br>Is not thy master with him, who, were’t so,<br>Would have informed for preparation? |
| SERVANT | So please you, it is true. Our thane is coming,<br>One of my fellows had the [[Speed|speed]] of him,<br>Who, almost dead for breath, had scarcely more<br>Than would make up his message. |
| [[Lady Macbeth|LADY MACBETH]] | Give him tending;<br>He brings great news. Exit Servant<br>[[The Raven|The raven]] himself is hoarse<br>That croaks the fatal entrance of [[Duncan]]<br>Under my battlements. Come, you spirits<br>That tend on mortal thoughts, unsex me here,<br>And fill me from the [[Crown|crown]] to the toe top-full<br>Of direst cruelty. Make thick my [[Blood|blood]],<br>Stop up th‘access and passage to remorse,<br>That no compunctious visitings of [[Nature|nature]]<br>Shake my fell purpose, nor keep peace between<br>Th’effect and it. Come to my woman’s breasts,<br>And take my milk for gall, you murd‘ring ministers,<br>Wherever in your sightless substances<br>You wait on [[Nature|nature]]’s mischief. Come, thick [[Night|night]],<br>And pall thee in the dunnest smoke of hell,<br>That my keen knife see not the wound it makes,<br>Nor heaven peep through the blanket of the dark<br>To cry ’Hold, hold!’ |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Macbeth]] |
| [[Lady Macbeth|LADY MACBETH]] | Great Glamis, worthy Cawdor,<br>Greater than both by the all-hail hereafter,<br>Thy letters have transported me beyond<br>This ignorant present, and I feel now<br>The future in the instant. |
| [[Macbeth|MACBETH]] | My dearest [[Love|love]],<br>[[Duncan]] comes here tonight. |
| [[Lady Macbeth|LADY MACBETH]] | And when goes hence? |
| [[Macbeth|MACBETH]] | Tomorrow, as he purposes. |
| [[Lady Macbeth|LADY MACBETH]] | O never<br>Shall sun that morrow see.<br>Your face, my thane, is as a book where men<br>May read strange matters. To beguile the [[Time|time]],<br>Look like the [[Time|time]]; bear welcome in your eye,<br>Your hand, your tongue; look like the [[Innocent|innocent]] flower,<br>But be the serpent under’t. He that’s coming<br>Must be provided for; and you shall put<br>This [[Night|night]]’s great business into my dispatch,<br>Which shall to all our nights and days to come<br>Give solely sovereign sway and masterdom. |
| [[Macbeth|MACBETH]] | We will speak further. |
| [[Lady Macbeth|LADY MACBETH]] | Only look up clear.<br>To alter favour ever is to [[Fear|fear]].<br>Leave all the rest to me. Exeunt |
| *(didascalia)* | 1.6 Hautboys and torches. Enter [[King]] [[Duncan]], [[Malcolm]], Donalbain, [[Banquo]], Lennox, [[Macduff]], Ross, Angus, and attendants |
| [[King|KING]] [[Duncan|DUNCAN]] | This [[Castle|castle]] hath a pleasant seat. The air<br>Nimbly and sweetly recommends itself<br>Unto our gentle senses. |
| [[Banquo|BANQUO]] | This guest of [[Summer|summer]],<br>The temple-haunting martlet, does approve<br>By his loved mansionry that the heavens’ breath<br>Smells wooingly here. No jutty, frieze,<br>Buttress, nor coign of vantage but this bird<br>Hath made his pendant bed and procreant cradle;<br>Where they most breed and haunt I have observed<br>The air is delicate. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Lady Macbeth]] |
| [[King|KING]] [[Duncan|DUNCAN]] | See, see, our honoured hostess!<br>The [[Love|love]] that follows us sometime is our trouble,<br>Which still we thank as [[Love|love]]. Herein I teach you<br>How you shall bid God ’ield us for your pains,<br>And thank us for your trouble. |
| [[Lady Macbeth|LADY MACBETH]] | All our service<br>In every point twice done, and then done [[Double|double]],<br>Were poor and single business to contend<br>Against those honours deep and broad wherewith<br>Your majesty loads our [[house]]. For those of old,<br>And the late dignities heaped up to them,<br>We rest your hermits. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Duncan|DUNCAN]] | Where’s the Thane of Cawdor?<br>We coursed him at the heels, and had a purpose<br>To be his purveyor; but he rides [[Well|well]],<br>And his great [[Love|love]], sharp as his spur, hath holp him<br>To his [[Home|home]] before us. Fair and noble hostess,<br>We are your guest tonight. |
| [[Lady Macbeth|LADY MACBETH]] | Your servants ever<br>Have theirs, themselves, and what is theirs in count<br>To make their audit at your highness’ pleasure,<br>Still to return your own. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Duncan|DUNCAN]] | Give me your hand.<br>Conduct me to mine host. We [[Love|love]] him highly,<br>And shall continue our graces towards him.<br>By your leave, hostess. Exeunt |
