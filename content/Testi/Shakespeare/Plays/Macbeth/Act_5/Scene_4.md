| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Malcolm]], Siward, [[Macduff]], Siward’s Son, Menteith, Caithness, Angus, and soldiers, marching, with a drummer and colours |
| [[Malcolm|MALCOLM]] | Cousins, I [[Hope|hope]] the days are near at hand<br>That chambers will be safe. |
| MENTEITH | We [[Doubt|doubt]] it nothing. |
| SIWARD | What wood is this before us? |
| MENTEITH | The wood of Birnam. |
| [[Malcolm|MALCOLM]] | Let every soldier hew him down a bough<br>And bear’t before him. Thereby shall we [[Shadow|shadow]]<br>The numbers of our host, and make discovery<br>Err in report of us.<br>A SOLDIER<br>It shall be done. |
| SIWARD | We learn no other but the confident [[Tyrant|tyrant]]<br>Keeps still in Dunsinane, and will endure<br>Our setting down before’t. |
| [[Malcolm|MALCOLM]] | ’Tis his main [[Hope|hope]],<br>For where there is advantage to be gone,<br>Both more and less have given him the revolt,<br>And none serve with him but constrained things,<br>Whose hearts are absent too. |
| [[Macduff|MACDUFF]] | Let our just censures<br>Attend the true event, and put we on<br>Industrious soldiership. |
| SIWARD | The [[Time|time]] approaches<br>That will with due decision make us know<br>What we shall say we have, and what we owe.<br>Thoughts speculative their unsure hopes relate,<br>But certain issue strokes must arbitrate;<br>Towards which, advance the war. Exeunt, marching |
