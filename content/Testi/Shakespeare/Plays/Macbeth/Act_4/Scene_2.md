| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Macduff]]’s Wife, her Son, and Ross |
| [[Lady Macduff|LADY MACDUFF]] | What had he done to make him fly the land? |
| ROSS | You must have patience, madam. |
| [[Lady Macduff|LADY MACDUFF]] | He had none.<br>His flight was [[Madness|madness]]. When our actions do not,<br>Our fears do make us traitors.<br>Ross<br>You know not<br>Whether it was his wisdom or his [[Fear|fear]]. |
| [[Lady Macduff|LADY MACDUFF]] | Wisdom—to leave his wife, to leave his babes,<br>His mansion, and his titles in a place<br>From whence himself does fly? He loves us not,<br>He wants the natural touch, for the poor wren,<br>The most diminutive of [[Birds|birds]], will fight,<br>Her young ones in her nest, against the owl.<br>All is the [[Fear|fear]] and nothing is the [[Love|love]];<br>As little is the wisdom, where the flight<br>So runs against all reason.<br>Ross<br>My dearest coz,<br>I pray you school yourself. But for your husband,<br>He is noble, wise, judicious, and best knows<br>The fits o’th’ season. I dare not speak much further,<br>But cruel are the times when we are traitors<br>And do not know ourselves; when we hold rumour<br>From what we [[Fear|fear]], yet know not what we [[Fear|fear]],<br>But float upon a wild and violent sea<br>Each way and none. I take my leave of you;<br>Shall not be long but I’ll be here again.<br>Things at the worst will cease, or else climb upward<br>To what they were before. My pretty cousin,<br>Blessing upon you! |
| [[Lady Macduff|LADY MACDUFF]] | Fathered he is, and yet he’s fatherless. |
| ROSS | I am so much a [[Fool|fool]], should I stay longer<br>It would be my disgrace and your discomfort.<br>I take my leave at once. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit |
| [[Lady Macduff|LADY MACDUFF]] | Sirrah, your father’s dead,<br>And what will you do now? How will you live? |
| [[Macduff|MACDUFF]]’S SON | As [[Birds|birds]] do, mother. |
| [[Lady Macduff|LADY MACDUFF]] | What, with worms and flies? |
| [[Macduff|MACDUFF]]’S SON | With what I get, I mean, and so do they. |
| [[Lady Macduff|LADY MACDUFF]] | Poor bird, thou’dst never [[Fear|fear]] the net nor lime,<br>The pitfall nor the gin. |
| [[Macduff|MACDUFF]]’S SON | Why should I, mother? Poor [[Birds|birds]] they are not set for.<br>My father is not dead, for all your saying. |
| [[Lady Macduff|LADY MACDUFF]] | Yes, he is dead. How wilt thou do for a father? |
| [[Macduff|MACDUFF]]’S SON | Nay, how will you do for a husband? |
| [[Lady Macduff|LADY MACDUFF]] | Why, I can buy me twenty at any market. |
| [[Macduff|MACDUFF]]’S SON | Then you’ll buy ’em to sell again. |
| [[Lady Macduff|LADY MACDUFF]] | Thou speak‘st with all thy wit, and yet, i’[[Faith|faith]], with wit enough for thee. |
| [[Macduff|MACDUFF]]’S SON | Was my father a traitor, mother? |
| [[Lady Macduff|LADY MACDUFF]] | Ay, that he was. |
| [[Macduff|MACDUFF]]’S SON | What is a traitor? |
| [[Lady Macduff|LADY MACDUFF]] | Why, one that swears and lies. |
| [[Macduff|MACDUFF]]’S SON | And be all traitors that do so? |
| [[Lady Macduff|LADY MACDUFF]] | Everyone that does so is a traitor, and must be hanged. |
| [[Macduff|MACDUFF]]’S SON | And must they all be hanged that swear and lie? |
| [[Lady Macduff|LADY MACDUFF]] | Every one. |
| [[Macduff|MACDUFF]]’S SON | Who must hang them? |
| [[Lady Macduff|LADY MACDUFF]] | Why, the honest men. |
| [[Macduff|MACDUFF]]’S SON | Then the liars and swearers are fools, for there are liars and swearers enough to beat the honest men and hang up them. |
| [[Lady Macduff|LADY MACDUFF]] | Now God help thee, poor monkey! But how wilt thou do for a father? |
| [[Macduff|MACDUFF]]’S SON | If he were dead you’d weep for him. If you would not, it were a good sign that I should quickly have a new father. |
| [[Lady Macduff|LADY MACDUFF]] | Poor prattler, how thou talk’st! |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter a Messenger |
| MESSENGER | Bless you, fair dame. I am not to you known,<br>Though in your state of [[Honour|honour]] I am perfect.<br>I [[Doubt|doubt]] some danger does approach you nearly.<br>If you will take a homely man’s advice,<br>Be not found here. Hence with your little ones!<br>To fright you thus methinks I am too savage,<br>To do worse to you were fell cruelty,<br>Which is too nigh your person. Heaven preserve you.<br>I dare abide no longer. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit Messenger |
| [[Lady Macduff|LADY MACDUFF]] | Whither should I fly?<br>I have done no harm. But I remember now<br>I am in this earthly world, where to do harm<br>Is often laudable, to do good sometime<br>Accounted dangerous folly. Why then, alas,<br>Do I put up that womanly defence<br>To say I have done no harm? |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Murderers |
| [[Lady Macduff|LADY MACDUFF]] | What are these faces?<br>A MURDERER Where is your husband? |
| [[Lady Macduff|LADY MACDUFF]] | I [[Hope|hope]] in no place so unsanctified<br>Where such as thou mayst find him.<br>A MURDERER<br>He’s a traitor. |
| [[Macduff|MACDUFF]]’S SON | Thou liest, thou shag-haired [[Villain|villain]].<br>A MURDERER (stabbing him)<br>What, you egg!<br>Young fry of treachery! |
| [[Macduff|MACDUFF]]’S SON | He has killed me, mother.<br>Run away, I pray you. |
| *(didascalia)* | He dies. Exit [[Macduff]]’s Wife crying ‘Murder!’ |
| *(didascalia)* | followed by Murderers with the Son’s [[Body|body]] |
