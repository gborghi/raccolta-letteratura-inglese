| *(didascalia)* | Enter Ross with an Old Man |
| OLD MAN | Threescore and ten I can remember well, |
| *(didascalia)* | Within the volume of which time I have seen |
| OLD MAN | Hours dreadful and things strange, but this sore night<br>Hath trifled former knowings.<br>Ross Ha,<br>good father,<br>Thou seest the heavens, as troubled with man’s act,<br>Threatens his bloody stage. By th’ clock ’tis day,<br>And yet dark night strangles the travelling lamp.<br>Is’t night’s predominance or the day’s shame<br>That darkness does the face of earth entomb<br>When living light should kiss it? |
| OLD MAN | ’Tis unnatural,<br>Even like the deed that’s done. On Tuesday last<br>A falcon, tow’ring in her pride of place,<br>Was by a mousing owl hawked at and killed. |
| ROSS | And Duncan’s horses—a thing most strange and<br>certain—<br>Beauteous and swift, the minions of their race,<br>Turned wild in nature, broke their stalls, flung out,<br>Contending ’gainst obedience, as they would<br>Make war with mankind. |
| OLD MAN | ’Tis said they ate each other. |
| ROSS | They did so, to th’amazement of mine eyes<br>That looked upon’t. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Macduff |
| ROSS | Here comes the good Macduff.<br>How goes the world, sir, now? |
| MACDUFF | Why, see you not? |
| ROSS | Is’t known who did this more than bloody deed? |
| MACDUFF | Those that Macbeth hath slain.<br>Ross<br>Alas the day,<br>What good could they pretend? |
| MACDUFF | They were suborned.<br>Malcolm and Donalbain, the King’s two sons,<br>Are stol’n away and fled, which puts upon them<br>Suspicion of the deed.<br>Ross<br>’Gainst nature still.<br>Thriftless ambition, that will raven up<br>Thine own life’s means! Then ’tis most like<br>The sovereignty will fall upon Macbeth. |
| MACDUFF | He is already named and gone to Scone<br>To be invested.<br>Ross Where is Duncan’s body? |
| MACDUFF | Carried to Colmekill,<br>The sacred storehouse of his predecessors,<br>And guardian of their bones.<br>Ross<br>Will you to Scone? |
| MACDUFF | No, cousin, I’ll to Fife. |
| ROSS | Well, I will thither. |
| MACDUFF | Well, may you see things well done there. Adieu,<br>Lest our old robes sit easier than our new.<br>Ross Farewell, father. |
| OLD MAN | God’s benison go with you, and with those<br>That would make good of bad, and friends of foes. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt severally |
