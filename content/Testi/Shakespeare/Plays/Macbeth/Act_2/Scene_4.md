| *(didascalia)* | Enter Ross with an Old Man |
| OLD MAN | Threescore and ten I can remember [[Well|well]], |
| *(didascalia)* | Within the volume of which [[Time|time]] I have seen |
| OLD MAN | Hours dreadful and things strange, but this sore [[Night|night]]<br>Hath trifled former knowings.<br>Ross Ha,<br>good father,<br>Thou seest the heavens, as troubled with man’s act,<br>Threatens his bloody stage. By th’ [[Clock|clock]] ’tis day,<br>And yet dark [[Night|night]] strangles the travelling lamp.<br>Is’t [[Night|night]]’s predominance or the day’s shame<br>That darkness does the face of earth entomb<br>When living light should kiss it? |
| OLD MAN | ’Tis unnatural,<br>Even like the deed that’s done. On Tuesday last<br>A falcon, tow’ring in her [[Pride|pride]] of place,<br>Was by a mousing owl hawked at and killed. |
| ROSS | And [[Duncan]]’s horses—a thing most strange and<br>certain—<br>Beauteous and swift, the minions of their race,<br>Turned wild in [[Nature|nature]], broke their stalls, flung out,<br>Contending ’gainst obedience, as they would<br>Make war with mankind. |
| OLD MAN | ’Tis said they ate each other. |
| ROSS | They did so, to th’amazement of mine eyes<br>That looked upon’t. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Macduff]] |
| ROSS | Here comes the good [[Macduff]].<br>How goes the world, sir, now? |
| [[Macduff|MACDUFF]] | Why, see you not? |
| ROSS | Is’t known who did this more than bloody deed? |
| [[Macduff|MACDUFF]] | Those that [[Macbeth]] hath slain.<br>Ross<br>Alas the day,<br>What good could they pretend? |
| [[Macduff|MACDUFF]] | They were suborned.<br>[[Malcolm]] and Donalbain, the [[King]]’s two sons,<br>Are stol’n away and fled, which puts upon them<br>Suspicion of the deed.<br>Ross<br>’Gainst [[Nature|nature]] still.<br>Thriftless [[Ambition|ambition]], that will raven up<br>Thine own life’s means! Then ’tis most like<br>The sovereignty will fall upon [[Macbeth]]. |
| [[Macduff|MACDUFF]] | He is already named and gone to Scone<br>To be invested.<br>Ross Where is [[Duncan]]’s [[Body|body]]? |
| [[Macduff|MACDUFF]] | Carried to Colmekill,<br>The sacred storehouse of his predecessors,<br>And guardian of their bones.<br>Ross<br>Will you to Scone? |
| [[Macduff|MACDUFF]] | No, cousin, I’ll to Fife. |
| ROSS | [[Well]], I will thither. |
| [[Macduff|MACDUFF]] | [[Well]], may you see things [[Well|well]] done there. Adieu,<br>Lest our old robes sit easier than our new.<br>Ross Farewell, father. |
| OLD MAN | God’s benison go with you, and with those<br>That would make good of bad, and friends of foes. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt severally |
