| [[King|KING]] HENRY | Thy wish was father, Harry, to that thought. I stay too long by thee, I weary thee. Dost thou so hunger for mine empty chair That thou wilt needs invest thee with my honours Before thy hour be ripe? O foolish youth, Thou seek‘st the [[Greatness|greatness]] that will overwhelm thee! Stay but a little, for my cloud of dignity Is held from falling with so weak a [[Wind|wind]] That it will quickly drop. My day is dim. Thou hast stol’n that which after some few hours Were thine without offence, and at my [[Death|death]] Thou hast sealed up my expectation. Thy life did manifest thou loved’st me not, And thou wilt have me die assured of it. Thou hid’st a thousand daggers in thy thoughts, Whom thou hast whetted on thy stony heart To stab at half an hour of my life. What, canst thou not forbear me half an hour? Then get thee gone and dig my [[Grave|grave]] thyself, And bid the merry [[Bells|bells]] ring to thine ear That thou art crowned, not that I am dead. Let all the [[Tears|tears]] that should bedew my hearse Be drops of balm to sanctify thy head. Only compound me with forgotten [[Dust|dust]]. Give that which gave thee life unto the worms. Pluck down my officers, break my decrees; For now a [[Time|time]] is come to mock at form— Harry the Fifth is crowned. Up, [[Vanity|vanity]]! Down, royal state! All you sage counsellors, hence! And to the English [[Court|court]] assemble now From every region, apes of idleness! Now, neighbour confines, purge you of your scum I Have you a ruffian that will swear, drink, [[Dance|dance]], Revel the [[Night|night]], rob, murder, and commit The oldest sins the newest kind of ways? Be happy; he will trouble you no more. England shall [[Double|double]] gild his treble [[Guilt|guilt]], England shall give him office, [[Honour|honour]], might; For the fifth Harry from curbed licence plucks The muzzle of restraint, and the wild dog 260 Shall flesh his tooth on every [[Innocent|innocent]]. O my poor kingdom, sick with civil blows! When that my care could not withhold thy riots, What wilt thou do when riot is thy care? O, thou wilt be a wilderness again, Peopled with wolves, thy old inhabitants. |
