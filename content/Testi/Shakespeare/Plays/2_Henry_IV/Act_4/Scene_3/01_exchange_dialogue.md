| *(didascalia)* | Enter King Henry in his bed, attended by the Earl of Warwick, Thomas Duke of Clarence, Humphrey Duke of Gloucester, and others |
| KING HENRY | Now, lords, if God doth give successful end To this debate that bleedeth at our doors, We will our youth lead on to higher fields, And draw no swords but what are sanctified. Our navy is addressed, our power collected, Our substitutes in absence well invested, And everything lies level to our wish; Only we want a little personal strength, And pause us till these rebels now afoot Come underneath the yoke of government. 10 |
| WARWICK | Both which we doubt not but your majesty Shall soon enjoy. |
| KING HENRY | Humphrey, my son of Gloucester, Where is the Prince your brother? |
| GLOUCESTER | I think he’s gone to hunt, my lord, at Windsor. |
| KING HENRY | And how accompanied? |
| GLOUCESTER | I do not know, my lord. 15 |
| KING HENRY | Is not his brother Thomas of Clarence with him? |
| GLOUCESTER | No, my good lord, he is in presence here. |
| CLARENCE | What would my lord and father? |
