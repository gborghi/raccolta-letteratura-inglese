| SIR JOHN | Master Shallow, I owe you a thousand pound. |
| SHALLOW | Yea, marry, Sir John; which I beseech you to let me have [[Home|home]] with me. |
| SIR JOHN | That can hardly be,, Master Shallow. Do not you grieve at this. I shall be sent for in private to him. Look you, he must seem thus to the world. [[Fear]] not your advancements. I will be the man yet that shall make you great. |
| SHALLOW | I cannot perceive how, unless you give me your doublet and stuff me out with straw. I beseech you, good Sir John, let me have five hundred of my thousand. |
| SIR JOHN | Sir, I will be as good as my word. This that you heard was but a colour. |
| SHALLOW | A colour I [[Fear|fear]] that you will die in, Sir John. |
| SIR JOHN | [[Fear]] no colours. Go with me to dinner. Come, Lieutenant [[Pistol]]; come, Bardolph. I shall be sent for soon at [[Night|night]]. Enter the [[Lord Chief Justice]] and [[Prince John]], with officers |
| [[Lord Chief Justice|LORD CHIEF JUSTICE]] | (to officers) Go carry Sir John [[Falstaff]] to the Fleet. Take all his company along with him. |
| SIR JOHN | My lord, my lord! |
| [[Lord Chief Justice|LORD CHIEF JUSTICE]] | I cannot now speak. I will hear you soon.— Take them away. |
| [[Pistol|PISTOL]] | Si fortuna me tormenta, spero me contenta. Exeunt all but [[Prince John]] and [[Lord Chief Justice]] |
| [[Prince John|PRINCE JOHN]] | I like this fair proceeding of the [[King]]’s. He hath intent his wonted followers Shall all be very [[Well|well]] provided for, But all are banished till their conversations Appear more wise and modest to the world. |
| [[Lord Chief Justice|LORD CHIEF JUSTICE]] | And so they are. |
| [[Prince John|PRINCE JOHN]] | The [[King]] hath called his parliament, my lord. |
| [[Lord Chief Justice|LORD CHIEF JUSTICE]] | He hath. |
| [[Prince John|PRINCE JOHN]] | I will lay odds that, ere this year expire, We bear our civil swords and native [[Fire|fire]] As far as France. I heard a bird so sing, Whose music, to my thinking, pleased the [[King]]. Come, will you hence? Exeunt |
