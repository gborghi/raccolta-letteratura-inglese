| [[King|KING]] HARRY | I know thee not, old man. Fall to thy prayers. How ill white hairs becomes a [[Fool|fool]] and jester! I have long dreamt of such a kind of man, So surfeit-swelled, so old, and so profane; But being awake, I do despise my dream. Make less thy [[Body|body]] hence, and more thy grace. Leave gormandizing; know the [[Grave|grave]] doth gape For thee thrice wider than for other men. Reply not to me with a [[Fool|fool]]-born jest. Presume not that I am the thing I was, For God doth know, so shall the world perceive, That I have turned away my former [[Self|self]]; So will I those that kept me company. When thou dost hear I am as I have been, Approach me, and thou shalt be as thou wast, The tutor and the feeder of my riots. Till then I banish thee, on [[Pain|pain]] of [[Death|death]], As I have done the rest of my misleaders, Not to come near our person by ten mile. For competence of life I will allow you, That lack of means enforce you not to evils; And as we hear you do [[Reform|reform]] yourselves, We will, according to your strengths and qualities, Give you advancement. (To [[Lord Chief Justice]]) Be it your charge, my lord, To see performed the tenor of our word. (To his train) Set on! Exeunt [[King]] Harry and his train |
