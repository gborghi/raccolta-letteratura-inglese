| *(didascalia)* | Enter the Duke, [[Viola]] as Cesario, Curio, and others |
| [[Orsino|ORSINO]] | Give me some music. Now good morrow, friends.<br>Now good Cesario, but that piece of [[Song|song]],<br>That old and antic [[Song|song]] we heard last [[Night|night]].<br>Methought it did relieve my passion much,<br>More than light airs and recollected terms<br>Of these most brisk and giddy-paced times.<br>Come, but one verse. |
| CURIO | He is not here, so please your lordship, that should sing it. |
| [[Orsino|ORSINO]] | Who was it? |
| CURIO | [[Feste]] the jester, my lord, a [[Fool|fool]] that the lady [[Olivia]]’s father took much delight in. He is about the [[house]]. |
| [[Orsino|ORSINO]] | Seek him out, and play the tune the while. Exit Curio |
| *(didascalia)* | Music plays |
| [[Orsino|ORSINO]] | (To [[Viola]]) Come hither, boy. If ever thou shalt [[Love|love]],<br>In the sweet pangs of it remember me;<br>For such as I am, all true [[lovers]] are,<br>Unstaid and skittish in all motions else<br>Save in the constant image of the creature<br>That is beloved. How dost thou like this tune? |
| [[Viola|VIOLA]] | It gives a very echo to the seat<br>Where [[Love|love]] is throned. |
| [[Orsino|ORSINO]] | Thou dost speak masterly.<br>My life upon’t, young though thou art thine eye<br>Hath stayed upon some favour that it loves.<br>Hath it not, boy? |
| [[Viola|VIOLA]] | A little, by your favour. |
| [[Orsino|ORSINO]] | What kind of woman is’t? |
| [[Viola|VIOLA]] | Of your complexion. |
| [[Orsino|ORSINO]] | She is not worth thee then. What years, i’[[Faith|faith]]? |
| [[Viola|VIOLA]] | About your years, my lord. |
| [[Orsino|ORSINO]] | Too old, by heaven. Let still the woman take<br>An elder than herself. So wears she to him;<br>So sways she level in her husband’s heart.<br>For, boy, however we do praise ourselves,<br>Our fancies are more giddy and unfirm,<br>More [[Longing|longing]], wavering, sooner lost and worn,<br>Than women’s are. |
| [[Viola|VIOLA]] | I think it [[Well|well]], my lord. |
| [[Orsino|ORSINO]] | Then let thy [[Love|love]] be younger than thyself,<br>Or thy affection cannot hold the bent;<br>For women are as roses, whose fair flower<br>Being once displayed, doth fall that very hour. |
| [[Viola|VIOLA]] | And so they are. Alas that they are so:<br>To die even when they to perfection grow. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Curio and [[Feste]] the clown |
| [[Orsino|ORSINO]] | (to [[Feste]])<br>O fellow, come, the [[Song|song]] we had last [[Night|night]].<br>Mark it, Cesario, it is old and plain.<br>The spinsters, and the knitters in the sun,<br>And the free maids that weave their thread with<br>bones,<br>Do use to chant it. It is silly sooth,<br>And dallies with the [[innocence]] of [[Love|love]],<br>Like the old age. |
| [[Feste|FESTE]] | Are you ready, sir? |
| [[Orsino|ORSINO]] | I prithee, sing. |
| *(didascalia)* | Music |
| [[Feste|FESTE]] | (sings)<br>Come away, come away [[Death|death]],<br>And in sad cypress let me be laid.<br>Fie away, fie away breath,<br>I am slain by a fair cruel maid.<br>My shroud of white, stuck all with yew,<br>O prepare it.<br>My part of [[Death|death]] no one so true<br>Did share it.<br>Not a flower, not a flower sweet<br>On my black coffin let there be strewn.<br>Not a friend, not a friend greet<br>My poor corpse, where my bones shall be thrown.<br>A thousand thousand sighs to save,<br>Lay me O where<br>Sad true [[Lover|lover]] never find my [[Grave|grave]],<br>To weep there. |
| DUKE | (giving money) There’s for thy pains. |
| [[Feste|FESTE]] | No pains, sir. I take pleasure in singing, sir. |
| [[Orsino|ORSINO]] | I’ll pay thy pleasure then. |
| [[Feste|FESTE]] | Truly, sir, and pleasure will be paid, one [[Time|time]] or another. |
| [[Orsino|ORSINO]] | Give me now leave to leave thee. |
| [[Feste|FESTE]] | Now the [[Melancholy|melancholy]] god protect thee, and the tailor make thy doublet of changeable taffeta, for thy mind is a very opal. I would have men of such constancy put to sea, that their business might be everything, and their intent everywhere, for that’s it that always makes a good voyage of nothing. Farewell. Exit |
| [[Orsino|ORSINO]] | Let all the rest give place: Exeunt Curio and others<br>Once more, Cesario,<br>Get thee to yon same sovereign cruelty.<br>Tell her my [[Love|love]], more noble than the world,<br>Prizes not quantity of dirty lands.<br>The parts that fortune hath bestowed upon her<br>Tell her I hold as giddily as fortune;<br>But ’tis that miracle and [[Queen|queen]] of gems<br>That [[Nature|nature]] pranks her in attracts my [[Soul|soul]]. |
| [[Viola|VIOLA]] | But if she cannot [[Love|love]] you, sir? |
| [[Orsino|ORSINO]] | I cannot be so answered. |
| [[Viola|VIOLA]] | Sooth, but you must.<br>Say that some lady, as perhaps there is,<br>Hath for your [[Love|love]] as great a pang of heart<br>As you have for [[Olivia]]. You cannot [[Love|love]] her.<br>You tell her so. Must she not then be answered? |
| [[Orsino|ORSINO]] | There is no woman’s sides<br>Can bide the beating of so strong a passion<br>As [[Love|love]] doth give my heart; no woman’s heart<br>So big, to hold so much. They lack retention.<br>Alas, their [[Love|love]] may be called appetite,<br>No motion of the liver, but the palate,<br>That suffer surfeit, cloyment, and revolt.<br>But mine is all as hungry as the sea,<br>And can digest as much. Make no compare<br>Between that [[Love|love]] a woman can bear me<br>And that I owe [[Olivia]]. |
| [[Viola|VIOLA]] | Ay, but I know— |
| [[Orsino|ORSINO]] | What dost thou know? |
| [[Viola|VIOLA]] | Too [[Well|well]] what [[Love|love]] women to men may owe.<br>In [[Faith|faith]], they are as true of heart as we.<br>My father had a daughter loved a man<br>As it might be, perhaps, were I a woman<br>I should your lordship. |
| [[Orsino|ORSINO]] | And what’s her [[history]]? |
| [[Viola|VIOLA]] | A blank, my lord. She never told her [[Love|love]],<br>But let concealment, like a worm i’th’ bud,<br>Feed on her damask cheek. She pined in thought,<br>And with a green and yellow [[Melancholy|melancholy]]<br>She sat like patience on a monument,<br>Smiling at grief. Was not this [[Love|love]] indeed?<br>We men may say more, swear more, but indeed<br>Our shows are more than will; for still we prove<br>Much in our vows, but little in our [[Love|love]]. |
| [[Orsino|ORSINO]] | But died thy sister of her [[Love|love]], my boy? |
| [[Viola|VIOLA]] | I am all the daughters of my father’s [[house]],<br>And all the brothers too; and yet I know not.<br>Sir, shall I to this lady? |
| [[Orsino|ORSINO]] | Ay, that’s the theme,<br>To her in haste. Give her this [[Jewel|jewel]]. Say<br>My [[Love|love]] can give no place, bide no denay. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt severally |
