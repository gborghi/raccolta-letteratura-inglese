| [[King|KING]] | (coming forward) Come, sir, you blush. As his, your case is such. You chide at him, offending twice as much. You do not [[Love|love]] [[Maria]]? [[Longueville]] Did never [[Sonnet|sonnet]] for her sake compile, Nor never lay his wreathed arms athwart His loving bosom to keep down his heart? I have been closely shrouded in this bush, And marked you both, and for you both did blush. I heard your guilty rhymes, observed your fashion, Saw sighs reek from you, noted [[Well|well]] your passion. ‘Ay me!’ says one, ‘O jovel’ the other cries. One, her hairs were gold; crystal the other’s eyes. (To [[Longueville]]) You would for paradise break [[Faith|faith]] and troth, (To [[Dumaine]]) And Jove for your [[Love|love]] would infringe an oath. What will Biron say when that he shall hear [[Faith]] so infringed, which such zeal did swear? How will he scorn, how will he spend his wit! How will he triumph, leap, and laugh at it! For all the wealth that ever I did see I would not have him know so much by me. |
