| BIRON | (aside) Now in thy likeness one more [[Fool|fool]] appear! |
| [[Longueville|LONGUEVILLE]] | Ay me! I am forsworn. |
| BIRON | (aside) Why, he comes in like a perjure, wearing papers. |
| [[King|KING]] | (aside) In [[Love|love]], I [[Hope|hope]]! Sweet fellowship in shame. |
| BIRON | (aside) One drunkard loves another of the name. |
| [[Longueville|LONGUEVILLE]] | Am I the first that have been perjured so? |
| BIRON | (aside) I could put thee in comfort, not by two that I know. Thou makest the triumviry, the corner-cap of society, The shape of [[Love|love]]’s Tyburn, that hangs up simplicity. |
| [[Longueville|LONGUEVILLE]] | I [[Fear|fear]] these stubborn lines lack power to move. O sweet [[Maria]], empress of my [[Love|love]], These numbers will I tear, and write in prose. |
| BIRON | (aside) O, rhymes are guards on wanton Cupid’s hose, Disfigure not his slop. |
| [[Longueville|LONGUEVILLE]] | This same shall go. He reads the [[Sonnet|sonnet]] ‘Did not the heavenly rhetoric of thine eye, ’Gainst whom the world cannot hold argument, Persuade my heart to this false perjury? Vows for thee broke deserve not punishment. A woman I forswore, but I will prove, Thou being a goddess, I forswore not thee. My vow was earthly, thou a heavenly [[Love|love]]. Thy grace being gained cures all disgrace in me. Vows are but breath, and breath a vapour is. Then thou, fair sun, which on my earth dost shine, Exhal‘st this vapour-vow; in thee it is. If broken then, it is no fault of mine. If by me broke, what [[Fool|fool]] is not so wise To lose an oath to win a paradise?’ |
| BIRON | (aside) This is the liver vein, which makes flesh a deity, A green goose a goddess, pure, pure idolatry. God amend us, God amend: we are much out o’th’ way. Enter [[Dumaine]] with a paper |
| [[Longueville|LONGUEVILLE]] | (aside) By whom shall I send this? Company? Stay. He steps aside |
| BIRON | (aside) All hid, all hid—an old infant play. Like a demigod here sit I in the sky, And wretched fools’ [[Secrets|secrets]] heedfully o’er-eye. More sacks to the mill! O heavens, I have my wish. [[Dumaine]] transformed—four woodcocks in a dish! |
| [[Dumaine|DUMAINE]] | O most divine Kate! |
| BIRON | (aside) O most profane coxcomb! |
| [[Dumaine|DUMAINE]] | By heaven, the [[Wonder|wonder]] in a mortal eye! |
| BIRON | (aside) By earth, she is not, corporal; there you lie. |
| [[Dumaine|DUMAINE]] | Her amber hairs for foul hath amber quoted. |
| BIRON | (aside) An amber-coloured raven was [[Well|well]] noted. |
| [[Dumaine|DUMAINE]] | As upright as the cedar. |
| BIRON | (aside) Stoop, I say. Her shoulder is with [[Child|child]]. |
| [[Dumaine|DUMAINE]] | As fair as day. |
| BIRON | (aside) Ay, as some days; but then no sun must shine. |
| [[Dumaine|DUMAINE]] | O that I had my wishl |
| [[Longueville|LONGUEVILLE]] | (aside) And I had mine! |
| [[King|KING]] | (aside) And I mine too, good Lord! |
| BIRON | (aside) Amen, so I had mine. Is not that a good word? |
| [[Dumaine|DUMAINE]] | I would forget her, but a fever she Reigns in my [[Blood|blood]] and will remembered be. |
| BIRON | (aside) A fever in your [[Blood|blood]]—why then, incision Would let her out in saucers—sweet misprision. |
| [[Dumaine|DUMAINE]] | Once more I’ll read the ode that I have writ. |
| BIRON | (aside) Once more I’ll mark how [[Love|love]] can vary wit. [[Dumaine]] reads his [[Sonnet|sonnet]] |
| [[Dumaine|DUMAINE]] | ‘On a day—atack the day— [[Love]], whose month is ever May, Spied a blossom passing fair Playing in the wanton air. Through the velvet [[Leaves|leaves]] the [[Wind|wind]] All unseen can passage find, That the [[Lover|lover]], sick to [[Death|death]], Wished himself the heavens’ breath. “Air”, quoth he, “thy cheeks may blow; Air, would I might triumph so. But, alack, my hand is sworn Ne’er to pluck thee from thy thorn— Vow, alack, for youth unmeet, Youth so apt to pluck a sweet. Do not call it sin in me That I am forsworn for thee, Thou for whom great Jove would swear Juno but an Ethiop were, And deny himself for Jove, Turning mortal for thy [[Love|love]].”’ This will I send, and something else more plain, That shall express my true [[Love|love]]’s fasting [[Pain|pain]]. O, would the [[King]], Biron, and [[Longueville]] Were [[lovers]] too! Ill to example ill Would from my forehead wipe a perjured note, For none offend where all alike do dote. |
