| [[King|KING]] | How [[Well|well]] he’s read, to reason against reading! |
| [[Dumaine|DUMAINE]] | Proceeded [[Well|well]], to stop all good proceeding. |
| [[Longueville|LONGUEVILLE]] | He weeds the corn and still lets grow the weeding. |
| BIRON | The [[Spring|spring]] is near when green geese are a-breeding. |
| [[Dumaine|DUMAINE]] | How follows that? |
| BIRON | Fit in his place and [[Time|time]]. |
| [[Dumaine|DUMAINE]] | In reason nothing. |
| BIRON | Something then in rhyme. |
| [[King|KING]] | Biron is like an envious sneaping [[Frost|frost]], That bites the first-born infants of the [[Spring|spring]]. |
| BIRON | [[Well]], say I am! Why should proud [[Summer|summer]] boast Before the [[Birds|birds]] have any cause to sing? Why should I joy in any abortive birth? At Christmas I no more [[Desire|desire]] a [[Rose|rose]] Than wish a [[Snow|snow]] in May’s new-fangled shows, But like of each thing that in season grows. So you to study, now it is too late, Climb o’er the [[house]] to unlock the little gate. |
| [[King|KING]] | [[Well]], sit you out. Go [[Home|home]], Biron. Adieu. |
| BIRON | No, my good lord, I have sworn to stay with you. And though I have for barbarism spoke more Than for that angel knowledge you can say, Yet confident I’ll keep what I have sworn, And bide the penance of each three years’ day. Give me the paper. Let me read the same, And to the strict’st decrees I’ll write my name. |
| [[King|KING]] | (giving a paper) How [[Well|well]] this yielding rescues thee from shame! |
| BIRON | (reads) ‘Item: that no woman shall come within a mile of my [[Court|court]].’ Hath this been proclaimed? |
| [[Longueville|LONGUEVILLE]] | Four days ago. |
| BIRON | Let’s see the penalty. ‘On [[Pain|pain]] of losing her tongue.’ Who devised this penalty? |
| [[Longueville|LONGUEVILLE]] | Marry, that did I. |
| BIRON | Sweet lord, and why? |
| [[Longueville|LONGUEVILLE]] | To fright them hence with that dread penalty. |
| BIRON | A dangerous law against gentility. ‘Item: if any man be seen to talk with a woman within the term of three years, he shall endure such public shame as the rest of the [[Court|court]] can possible devise.’ This article, my liege, yourself must break; For [[Well|well]] you know here comes in embassy The French [[King]]’s daughter with yourself to speak— A maid of grace and complete majesty— About surrender-up of Aquitaine To her decrepit, sick, and bedrid father. Therefore this article is made in vain, Or vainly comes th’admirèd Princess hither. |
| [[King|KING]] | What say you, lords? Why, this was quite forgot. |
| BIRON | So study evermore is overshot. While it doth study to have what it would, It doth forget to do the thing it should; And when it hath the thing it hunteth most, ’Tis won as towns with [[Fire|fire]]—so won, so lost. |
| [[King|KING]] | We must of force dispense with this decree. She must lie here, on mere necessity. |
| BIRON | Necessity will make us all forsworn Three thousand times within this three years’ space; For every man with his affects is born, Not by might mastered, but by special grace. If I break [[Faith|faith]], this word shall speak for me: I am forsworn on mere necessity. So to the laws at large I write my name, And he that breaks them in the least degree Stands in attainder of eternal shame. He signs Suggestions are to other as to me, But I believe, although I seem so loath, I am the last that will last keep his oath. But is there no quick recreation granted? |
