| MOTE | Come, you transgressing slave. Away! |
| [[Costard|COSTARD]] | Let me not be pent up, sir. I will fast, being loose. |
| MOTE | No, sir. That were fast and loose. Thou shalt to [[Prison|prison]]. |
| [[Costard|COSTARD]] | [[Well]], if ever I do see the merry days of desolation that I have seen, some shall see. |
| MOTE | What shall some see? |
| [[Costard|COSTARD]] | Nay, nothing, Master Mote, but what they look upon. It is not for prisoners to be too silent in their words, and therefore I will say nothing. I thank God I have as little patience as another man, and therefore I can be quiet. Exeunt Mote and [[Costard]] |
