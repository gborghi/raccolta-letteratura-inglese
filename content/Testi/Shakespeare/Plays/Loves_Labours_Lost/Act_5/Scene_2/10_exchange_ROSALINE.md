| [[Rosaline|ROSALINE]] | Sans ‘sans’, I pray you. |
| BIRON | Yet I have a trick Of the old rage. Bear with me, I am sick. I’ll leave it by degrees. Soft, let us see. Write ‘Lord have [[Mercy|mercy]] on us’ on those three. They are infected, in their hearts it lies. They have the plague, and caught it of your eyes. These lords are visited, you are not free; For the Lord’s tokens on you do I see. |
| PRINCESS | No, they are free that gave these tokens to us. |
| BIRON | Our states are forfeit. Seek not to undo us. |
| [[Rosaline|ROSALINE]] | It is not so, for how can this be true, That you stand forfeit, being those that sue? |
| BIRON | Peace, for I will not have to do with you. |
| [[Rosaline|ROSALINE]] | Nor shall not, if I do as I intend. |
| BIRON | (to the lords) Speak for yourselves. My wit is at an end. |
| [[King|KING]] | Teach us, sweet madam, for our rude transgression Some fair excuse. |
| PRINCESS | The fairest is confession. Were not you here but even now disguised? |
| [[King|KING]] | Madam, I was. |
| PRINCESS | And were you [[Well|well]] advised? |
| [[King|KING]] | I was, fair madam. |
| PRINCESS | When you then were here, What did you whisper in your lady’s ear? |
| [[King|KING]] | That more than all the world I did respect her. |
| PRINCESS | When she shall challenge this, you will reject her. |
| [[King|KING]] | Upon mine [[Honour|honour]], no. |
| PRINCESS | Peace, peace,forbear. Your oath once broke, you force not to forswear. |
| [[King|KING]] | Despise me when I break this oath of mine. |
| PRINCESS | I will, and therefore keep it. [[Rosaline]], What did the Russian whisper in your ear? |
| [[Rosaline|ROSALINE]] | Madam, he swore that he did hold me dear As precious eyesight, and did value me Above this world, adding thereto moreover That he would wed me, or else die my [[Lover|lover]]. |
| PRINCESS | God give thee joy of him! The noble lord Most honourably doth uphold his word. |
| [[King|KING]] | What mean you, madam? By my life, my troth, I never swore this lady such an oath. |
| [[Rosaline|ROSALINE]] | By heaven, you did, and to confirm it plain, You gave me this. But take it, sir, again. |
| [[King|KING]] | My [[Faith|faith]] and this the Princess I did give. I knew her by this [[Jewel|jewel]] on her sleeve. |
| PRINCESS | Pardon me, sir, this [[Jewel|jewel]] did she wear, And Lord Biron, I thank him, is my dear. (To Biron) What, will you have me, or your pearl again? |
