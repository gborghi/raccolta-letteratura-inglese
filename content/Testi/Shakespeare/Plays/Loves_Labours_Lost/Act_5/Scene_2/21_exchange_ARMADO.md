| ARMADO | (to the [[King]]) Sweet majesty, vouchsafe me. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] | Was not that [[Hector]]? |
| [[Dumaine|DUMAINE]] | The worthy knight of Troy. |
| ARMADO | I will kiss thy royal finger and take leave. I am a votary, I have vowed to Jaquenetta To hold the plough for her sweet [[Love|love]] three year. But, most esteemed [[Greatness|greatness]], will you hear the dialogue that the two learned men have compiled in praise of the owl and the cuckoo ? It should have followed in the end of our show. |
| [[King|KING]] | Call them forth quickly, we will do so. |
| ARMADO | Holla, approach! Enter Holofernes, Nathaniel, [[Costard]], Mote, Dull, Jaquenetta, and others This side is Hiems, [[Winter|winter]], This Ver, the [[Spring|spring]], the one maintained by the owl, The other by the cuckoo. Ver, begin. |
| [[Spring|SPRING]] | (sings) When daisies pied and violets blue, And lady-smocks, all silver-white, And cuckoo-buds of yellow hue Do paint the meadows with delight, The cuckoo then on every tree Mocks married men, for thus sings he: Cuckoo! Cuckoo, cuckoo—O word of [[Fear|fear]], Unpleasing to a married ear. When shepherds pipe on oaten straws, And merry larks are ploughmen’s clocks; When turtles tread, and rooks and daws, And maidens bleach their [[Summer|summer]] smocks, The cuckoo then on every tree Mocks married men, for thus sings he: Cuckoo! Cuckoo, cuckoo—O word of [[Fear|fear]], Unpleasing to a married ear. |
| [[Winter|WINTER]] | (sings) When icicles hang by the wall, And Dick the shepherd blows his nail, And Tom bears logs into the hall, And milk comes frozen [[Home|home]] in pail; When [[Blood|blood]] is nipped, and ways be foul, Then nightly sings the staring owl: Tu-whit, tu-whoo!—a merry note, While greasy Joan doth keel the pot. When all aloud the [[Wind|wind]] doth blow, And coughing drowns the parson’s saw, And [[Birds|birds]] sit brooding in the [[Snow|snow]], And Marian’s nose looks red and raw; When roasted crabs hiss in the bowl, Then nightly sings the staring owl: Tu-whit, tu-whoo!—a merry note, While greasy Joan doth keel the pot. ARMADO The words of Mercury are harsh after the [[songs]] of [[Apollo]]. You that way, we this way. Exeunt, severally |
