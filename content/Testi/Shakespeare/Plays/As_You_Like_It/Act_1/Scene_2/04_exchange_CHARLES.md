| CHARLES | No, I warrant your grace you shall not entreat him to a second that have so mightily persuaded him from a first. |
| [[Orlando|ORLANDO]] | You mean to mock me after; you should not have mocked me before. But come your ways. |
| [[Rosalind|ROSALIND]] | (to [[Orlando]]) Now Hercules be thy [[Speed|speed]], young man! |
| [[Celia|CELIA]] | I would I were invisible, to catch the strong fellow by the leg. Charles and [[Orlando]] wrestle |
| [[Rosalind|ROSALIND]] | O excellent young man! |
| [[Celia|CELIA]] | If I had a thunderbolt in mine eye, I can tell who should down. [[Orlando]] throws Charles. Shout |
| [[Duke Frederick|DUKE FREDERICK]] | No more, no more. |
| [[Orlando|ORLANDO]] | Yes, I beseech your grace. I am not yet [[Well|well]] breathed. |
| [[Duke Frederick|DUKE FREDERICK]] | How dost thou, Charles? |
| LE BEAU | He cannot speak, my lord. |
| [[Duke Frederick|DUKE FREDERICK]] | Bear him away. Attendants carry Charles off What is thy name, young man? |
| [[Orlando|ORLANDO]] | [[Orlando]], my liege, the youngest son of Sir Rowland de Bois. |
| [[Duke Frederick|DUKE FREDERICK]] | I would thou hadst been son to some man else. The world esteemed thy father honourable, But I did find him still mine enemy. Thou shouldst have better pleased me with this deed Hadst thou descended from another [[house]]. But fare thee [[Well|well]], thou art a gallant youth. I would thou hadst told me of another father. Exeunt [[Duke Frederick]], Le Beau, [[[Touchstone]],] Lords, and attendants |
| [[Celia|CELIA]] | (to [[Rosalind]]) Were I my father, coz, would I do this? |
| [[Orlando|ORLANDO]] | I am more proud to be Sir Rowland’s son, His youngest son, and would not change that calling To be adopted heir to Frederick. |
| [[Rosalind|ROSALIND]] | My father loved Sir Rowland as his [[Soul|soul]], And all the world was of my father’s mind. Had I before known this young man his son I should have given him [[Tears|tears]] unto entreaties Ere he should thus have ventured. |
| [[Celia|CELIA]] | Gentle cousin, Let us go thank him, and encourage him. My father’s rough and envious disposition Sticks me at heart.—Sir, you have [[Well|well]] deserved. If you do keep your promises in [[Love|love]] But justly, as you have exceeded all promise, Your mistress shall be happy. |
| [[Rosalind|ROSALIND]] | (giving him a chain from her neck) Gentleman, Wear this for me—one out of suits with fortune, That could give more but that her hand lacks means. Shall we go, coz? |
| [[Celia|CELIA]] | Ay. Fare you [[Well|well]], fair gentleman. [[Rosalind]] and [[Celia]] turn to go |
| [[Orlando|ORLANDO]] | (aside) Can I not say ‘I thank you’? My better parts Are all thrown down, and that which here stands up Is but a quintain, a mere lifeless block. |
| [[Rosalind|ROSALIND]] | (to [[Celia]]) He calls us back. My [[Pride|pride]] fell with my fortunes, I’ll ask him what he would.—Did you call, sir? Sir, you have wrestled [[Well|well]], and overthrown More than your enemies. |
| [[Celia|CELIA]] | Will you go, coz? |
| [[Rosalind|ROSALIND]] | Have with you. (To [[Orlando]]) Fare you [[Well|well]]. Exeunt [[Rosalind]] and [[Celia]] |
| [[Orlando|ORLANDO]] | What passion hangs these weights upon my tongue? I cannot speak to her, yet she urged conference. Enter Le Beau O poor [[Orlando]]! Thou art overthrown. Or Charles or something weaker masters thee. |
| LE BEAU | Good sir, I do in [[Friendship|friendship]] counsel you To leave this place. Albeit you have deserved High commendation, true applause, and [[Love|love]], Yet such is now the Duke’s condition That he misconsters all that you have done. The Duke is humorous. What he is indeed More suits you to conceive than I to speak of. |
