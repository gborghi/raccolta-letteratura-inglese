| [[Phoebe|PHOEBE]] | Sweet youth, I pray you chide a year together. I had rather hear you chide than this man woo. |
| [[Rosalind|ROSALIND]] | (to [[Phoebe]]) He’s fallen in [[Love|love]] with your foulness, (to [[Silvius]]) and she’ll fall in [[Love|love]] with my anger. If it be so, as fast as she answers thee with frowning looks, I’ll sauce her with bitter words. (To [[Phoebe]]) Why look you so upon me? |
| [[Phoebe|PHOEBE]] | For no ill will I bear you. |
| [[Rosalind|ROSALIND]] | I pray you do not fall in [[Love|love]] with me, For I am falser than vows made in wine. Besides, I like you not. If you will know my [[house]], ’Tis at the tuft of olives, here hard by. (To [[Celia]]) Will you go, sister? (To [[Silvius]]) Shepherd, ply her hard.— Come, sister. (To [[Phoebe]]) Shepherdess, look on him better, And be not proud. Though all the world could see, None could be so abused in sight as he.— Come, to our flock. Exeunt [[Rosalind]], [[Celia]], and Corin |
| [[Phoebe|PHOEBE]] | (aside) Dead shepherd, now I find thy saw of might: ‘Who ever loved that loved not at first sight?’ |
| [[Silvius|SILVIUS]] | Sweet [[Phoebe]]— |
| [[Phoebe|PHOEBE]] | Ha, what sayst thou, [[Silvius]]? |
| [[Silvius|SILVIUS]] | Sweet [[Phoebe]], pity me. |
| [[Phoebe|PHOEBE]] | Why, I am sorry for thee, gentle [[Silvius]]. |
| [[Silvius|SILVIUS]] | Wherever sorrow is, relief would be. If you do sorrow at my grief in [[Love|love]], By giving [[Love|love]] your sorrow and my grief Were both extermined. |
| [[Phoebe|PHOEBE]] | Thou hast my [[Love|love]], is not that neighbourly? |
| [[Silvius|SILVIUS]] | I would have you. |
| [[Phoebe|PHOEBE]] | Why, that were covetousness. [[Silvius]], the [[Time|time]] was that I hated thee; And yet it is not that I bear thee [[Love|love]]. But since that thou canst talk of [[Love|love]] so [[Well|well]], Thy company, which erst was irksome to me, I will endure; and I’ll employ thee, too. But do not look for further recompense Than thine own gladness that thou art employed. |
| [[Silvius|SILVIUS]] | So holy and so perfect is my [[Love|love]], And I in such a [[Poverty|poverty]] of grace, That I shall think it a most plenteous crop To glean the broken ears after the man That the main harvest reaps. Loose now and then A scattered smile, and that I’ll live upon. |
| [[Phoebe|PHOEBE]] | Know’st thou the youth that spoke to me erewhile? |
| [[Silvius|SILVIUS]] | Not very [[Well|well]], but I have met him oft, And he hath bought the cottage and the bounds That the old Carlot once was master of. |
