| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Silvius]] and [[Phoebe]] |
| [[Silvius|SILVIUS]] | Sweet [[Phoebe]], do not scorn me, do not, [[Phoebe]].<br>Say that you [[Love|love]] me not, but say not so<br>In bitterness. The common executioner,<br>Whose heart th’accustomed sight of [[Death|death]] makes<br>hard,<br>Falls not the axe upon the humbled neck<br>But first begs pardon. Will you sterner be<br>Than he that dies and lives by bloody drops? |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Rosalind]] as Ganymede, [[Celia]] as Aliena, and Corin, and stand aside |
| [[Phoebe|PHOEBE]] | (to [[Silvius]])<br>I would not be thy executioner.<br>I fly thee for I would not injure thee.<br>Thou tell‘st me there is murder in mine eye.<br>’Tis pretty, sure, and very probable<br>That eyes, that are the frail’st and softest things,<br>Who shut their coward gates on atomies,<br>Should be called tyrants, butchers, murderers.<br>Now I do frown on thee with all my heart,<br>And if mine eyes can wound, now let them kill thee.<br>Now counterfeit to swoon, why now fall down;<br>Or if thou canst not, O, for shame, for shame,<br>Lie not, to say mine eyes are murderers.<br>Now show the wound mine eye hath made in thee.<br>Scratch thee but with a pin, and there remains<br>Some scar of it. Lean upon a rush,<br>The cicatrice and capable impressure<br>Thy palm some moment keeps. But now mine eyes,<br>Which I have darted at thee, hurt thee not;<br>Nor I am sure there is no force in eyes<br>That can do hurt. |
| [[Silvius|SILVIUS]] | O dear [[Phoebe]],<br>If ever—as that ever may be near—<br>You meet in some fresh cheek the power of fancy,<br>Then shall you know the wounds invisible<br>That [[Love|love]]’s keen arrows make. |
| [[Phoebe|PHOEBE]] | But till that [[Time|time]]<br>Come not thou near me. And when that [[Time|time]] comes,<br>Afflict me with thy mocks, pity me not,<br>As till that [[Time|time]] I shall not pity thee. |
| [[Rosalind|ROSALIND]] | (coming forward)<br>And why, I pray you? Who might be your mother,<br>That you insult, exult, and all at once,<br>Over the wretched? What though you have no<br>[[Beauty|beauty]]—<br>As, by my [[Faith|faith]], I see no more in you<br>Than without candle may go dark to bed—<br>Must you be therefore proud and pitiless?<br>Why, what means this? Why do you look on me?<br>I see no more in you than in the ordinary<br>Of [[Nature|nature]]’s sale-work.—‘Od’s my little life,<br><br>I think she means to tangle my eyes, too.<br>No, [[Faith|faith]], proud mistress, [[Hope|hope]] not after it.<br>’Tis not your inky brows, your black silk hair,<br>Your bugle eyeballs, nor your cheek of cream,<br>That can entame my spirits to your worship.<br>(To [[Silvius]]) You, foolish shepherd, wherefore do you<br>follow her<br>Like foggy south, puffing with [[Wind|wind]] and rain?<br>You are a thousand times a properer man<br>Than she a woman. ‘Tis such fools as you<br>That makes the world full of ill-favoured children.<br>’Tis not her glass but you that flatters her,<br>And out of you she sees herself more proper<br>Than any of her lineaments can show her.<br>(To [[Phoebe]]) But, mistress, know yourself; down on<br>your knees<br>And thank heaven, fasting, for a good man’s [[Love|love]];<br>For I must tell you friendly in your ear,<br>Sell when you can. You are not for all markets.<br>Cry the man [[Mercy|mercy]], [[Love|love]] him, take his offer;<br>Foul is most foul, being foul to be a scoffer.—<br>So, take her to thee, shepherd. Fare you [[Well|well]]. |
| [[Phoebe|PHOEBE]] | Sweet youth, I pray you chide a year together.<br>I had rather hear you chide than this man woo. |
| [[Rosalind|ROSALIND]] | (to [[Phoebe]]) He’s fallen in [[Love|love]] with your foulness, (to [[Silvius]]) and she’ll fall in [[Love|love]] with my anger. If it be so, as fast as she answers thee with frowning looks, I’ll sauce her with bitter words. (To [[Phoebe]]) Why look you so upon me? |
| [[Phoebe|PHOEBE]] | For no ill will I bear you. |
| [[Rosalind|ROSALIND]] | I pray you do not fall in [[Love|love]] with me,<br>For I am falser than vows made in wine.<br>Besides, I like you not. If you will know my [[house]],<br>’Tis at the tuft of olives, here hard by.<br>(To [[Celia]]) Will you go, sister? (To [[Silvius]]) Shepherd,<br>ply her hard.—<br>Come, sister. (To [[Phoebe]]) Shepherdess, look on him<br>better,<br>And be not proud. Though all the world could see,<br>None could be so abused in sight as he.—<br>Come, to our flock. Exeunt [[Rosalind]], [[Celia]], and Corin |
| [[Phoebe|PHOEBE]] | (aside)<br>Dead shepherd, now I find thy saw of might:<br>‘Who ever loved that loved not at first sight?’ |
| [[Silvius|SILVIUS]] | Sweet [[Phoebe]]— |
| [[Phoebe|PHOEBE]] | Ha, what sayst thou, [[Silvius]]? |
| [[Silvius|SILVIUS]] | Sweet [[Phoebe]], pity me. |
| [[Phoebe|PHOEBE]] | Why, I am sorry for thee, gentle [[Silvius]]. |
| [[Silvius|SILVIUS]] | Wherever sorrow is, relief would be.<br>If you do sorrow at my grief in [[Love|love]],<br>By giving [[Love|love]] your sorrow and my grief<br>Were both extermined. |
| [[Phoebe|PHOEBE]] | Thou hast my [[Love|love]], is not that neighbourly? |
| [[Silvius|SILVIUS]] | I would have you. |
| [[Phoebe|PHOEBE]] | Why, that were covetousness.<br>[[Silvius]], the [[Time|time]] was that I hated thee;<br>And yet it is not that I bear thee [[Love|love]].<br>But since that thou canst talk of [[Love|love]] so [[Well|well]],<br>Thy company, which erst was irksome to me,<br>I will endure; and I’ll employ thee, too.<br>But do not look for further recompense<br>Than thine own gladness that thou art employed. |
| [[Silvius|SILVIUS]] | So holy and so perfect is my [[Love|love]],<br>And I in such a [[Poverty|poverty]] of grace,<br>That I shall think it a most plenteous crop<br>To glean the broken ears after the man<br>That the main harvest reaps. Loose now and then<br>A scattered smile, and that I’ll live upon. |
| [[Phoebe|PHOEBE]] | Know’st thou the youth that spoke to me erewhile? |
| [[Silvius|SILVIUS]] | Not very [[Well|well]], but I have met him oft,<br>And he hath bought the cottage and the bounds<br>That the old Carlot once was master of. |
| [[Phoebe|PHOEBE]] | Think not I [[Love|love]] him, though I ask for him.<br>‘Tis but a peevish boy. Yet he talks [[Well|well]].<br>But what care I for words? Yet words do [[Well|well]]<br>When he that speaks them pleases those that hear.<br>It is a pretty youth—not very pretty—<br>But sure he’s proud; and yet his [[Pride|pride]] becomes him.<br>He’ll make a proper man. The best thing in him<br>Is his complexion; and faster than his tongue<br>Did make offence, his eye did heal it up.<br>He is not very tall; yet for his years he’s tall.<br>His leg is but so-so; and yet ’tis [[Well|well]].<br>There was a pretty redness in his lip,<br>A little riper and more lusty-red<br>Than that mixed in his cheek. ’Twas just the<br>difference<br>Betwixt the constant red and mingled damask.<br>There be some women, [[Silvius]], had they marked him<br>In parcels as I did, would have gone near<br>To fall in [[Love|love]] with him; but for my part,<br>I [[Love|love]] him not, nor hate him not. And yet<br>Have I more cause to hate him than to [[Love|love]] him,<br>For what had he to do to chide at me?<br>He said mine eyes were black, and my hair black,<br>And now I am remembered, scorned at me.<br>I marvel why I answered not again.<br>But that’s all one. Omittance is no quittance.<br>I’ll write to him a very taunting letter,<br>And thou shalt bear it. Wilt thou, [[Silvius]]? |
| [[Silvius|SILVIUS]] | [[Phoebe]], with all my heart. |
| [[Phoebe|PHOEBE]] | I’ll write it straight.<br>The matter’s in my head and in my heart.<br>I will be bitter with him, and passing short.<br>Go with me, [[Silvius]]. Exeunt |
