| Chi parla | Battuta |
|---|---|
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Touchstone the clown and Audrey |
| TOUCHSTONE | Tomorrow is the joyful day, Audrey, tomorrow will we be married. |
| AUDREY | I do desire it with all my heart; and I hope it is no dishonest desire to desire to be a woman of the world. Here come two of the banished Duke’s pages. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter two Pages |
| FIRST PAGE | Well met, honest gentleman. |
| TOUCHSTONE | By my troth, well met. Come, sit, sit, and a song. |
| SECOND PAGE | We are for you. Sit i’th’ middle. |
| FIRST PAGE | Shall we clap into’t roundly, without hawking, or spitting, or saying we are hoarse, which are the only prologues to a bad voice? |
| SECOND PAGE | I‘faith, i’faith, and both in a tune, like two gipsies on a horse. |
| BOTH PAGES | (sing)<br>It was a lover and his lass,<br>With a hey, and a ho, and a hey-nonny-no,<br>That o’er the green cornfield did pass<br>In spring-time, the only pretty ring-time,<br>When birds do sing, hey ding-a-ding ding,<br>Sweet lovers love the spring.<br><br>Between the acres of the rye,<br>With a hey, and a ho, and a hey-nonny-no,<br>These pretty country folks would lie,<br>In spring-time, the only pretty ring-time,<br>When birds do sing, hey ding-a-ding ding,<br>Sweet lovers love the spring.<br><br>This carol they began that hour,<br>With a hey, and a ho, and a hey-nonny-no,<br>How that a life was but a flower,<br>In spring-time, the only pretty ring-time,<br>When birds do sing, hey ding-a-ding ding,<br><br>Sweet lovers love the spring.<br>And therefore take the present time,<br>With a hey, and a ho, and a hey-nonny-no,<br>For love is crowned with the prime,<br>In spring time, the only pretty ring-time,<br>When birds do sing, hey ding-a-ding ding,<br>Sweet lovers love the spring. |
| TOUCHSTONE | Truly, young gentlemen, though there was no great matter in the ditty, yet the note was very untunable. |
| FIRST PAGE | You are deceived, sir, we kept time, we lost not our time. |
| TOUCHSTONE | By my troth, yes, I count it but time lost to hear such a foolish song. God b’wi’you, and God mend your voices. Come, Audrey. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt |
| TOUCHSTONE | severally |
