| [[Duke Senior|DUKE SENIOR]] | I think he be transformed into a beast,<br>For I can nowhere find him like a man. |
| FIRST LORD | My lord, he is but even now gone hence.<br>Here was he merry, hearing of a [[Song|song]]. |
| [[Duke Senior|DUKE SENIOR]] | If he, compact of jars, grow musical<br>We shall have shortly discord in the spheres.<br>Go seek him. Tell him I would speak with him. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Jaques]] |
| FIRST LORD | He saves my labour by his own approach. |
| [[Duke Senior|DUKE SENIOR]] | Why, how now, monsieur, what a life is this,<br>That your poor friends must woo your company!<br>What, you look merrily. |
| [[Jaques|JAQUES]] | A [[Fool|fool]], a [[Fool|fool]], I met a [[Fool|fool]] i‘th’ forest,<br>A motley foot—a miserable world!—<br>As I do live by food, I met a [[Fool|fool]],<br>Who laid him down and basked him in the sun,<br>And railed on Lady Fortune in good terms,<br>In good set terms, and yet a motley [[Fool|fool]].<br>‘Good morrow, [[Fool|fool]],’ quoth I. ‘No, sir,’ quoth he,<br>‘Call me not [[Fool|fool]] till heaven hath sent me fortune.’<br>And then he drew a dial from his poke,<br>And looking on it with lack-lustre eye<br>Says very wisely ‘It is ten o‘[[Clock|clock]].’<br>‘Thus we may see’, quoth he, ‘how the world wags.<br>‘Tis but an hour ago since it was nine,<br>And after one hour more ’twill be eleven.<br>And so from hour to hour we ripe and ripe,<br>And then from hour to hour we rot and rot;<br>And thereby hangs a tale.’ When I did hear<br>The motley [[Fool|fool]] thus moral on the [[Time|time]]<br>My lungs began to crow like chanticleer,<br>That fools should be so deep-contemplative,<br>And I did laugh sans intermission<br>An hour by his dial. O noble [[Fool|fool]],<br>A worthy foot—motley’s the only wear. |
| [[Duke Senior|DUKE SENIOR]] | What [[Fool|fool]] is this? 35 |
| [[Jaques|JAQUES]] | O worthy [[Fool|fool]]!—One that hath been a courtier,<br>And says ‘If ladies be but young and fair<br>They have the gift to know it.’ And in his brain,<br>Which is as dry as the remainder biscuit<br>After a voyage, he hath strange places crammed<br>With observation, the which he vents<br>In mangled forms. O that I were a [[Fool|fool]],<br>I am ambitious for a motley coat. |
| [[Duke Senior|DUKE SENIOR]] | Thou shalt have one. |
| [[Jaques|JAQUES]] | It is my only suit,<br>Provided that you weed your better judgements<br>Of all opinion that grows rank in them<br>That I am wise. I must have liberty<br>Withal, as large a charter as the [[Wind|wind]],<br>To blow on whom I please, for so fools have;<br>And they that are most galled with my folly,<br>They most must laugh. And why, sir, must they so?<br>The why is plain as way to parish church:<br>He that a [[Fool|fool]] doth very wisely hit<br>Doth very foolishly, although he smart,<br>Seem aught but senseless of the bob. If not,<br>The wise man’s folly is anatomized<br>Even by the squandering glances of the [[Fool|fool]].<br>Invest me in my motley. Give me leave<br>To speak my mind, and I will through and through<br>Cleanse the foul [[Body|body]] of th’infected world,<br>If they will patiently receive my medicine. |
| [[Duke Senior|DUKE SENIOR]] | Fie on thee, I can tell what thou wouldst do. |
| [[Jaques|JAQUES]] | What, for a counter, would I do but good? |
| [[Duke Senior|DUKE SENIOR]] | Most mischievous foul sin, in chiding sin;<br>For thou thyself hast been a libertine,<br>As sensual as the brutish sting itself,<br>And all th’embossèd sores and headed evils<br>That thou with licence of free foot hast caught<br>Wouldst thou disgorge into the general world. |
| [[Jaques|JAQUES]] | Why, who cries out on [[Pride|pride]]<br>That can therein tax any private party?<br>Doth it not flow as hugely as the sea,<br>Till that the weary very means do ebb?<br>What woman in the [[City|city]] do I name<br>When that I say the [[City|city]]-woman bears<br>The cost of princes on unworthy shoulders?<br>Who can come in and say that I mean her<br>When such a one as she, such is her neighbour?<br>Or what is he of basest function,<br>That says his bravery is not on my cost,<br>Thinking that I mean him, but therein suits<br>His folly to the mettle of my speech?<br>There then, how then, what then, let me see wherein<br>My tongue hath wronged him. If it do him right,<br>Then he hath wronged himself. If he be free,<br>Why then my taxing like a wild goose flies,<br>Unclaimed of any man. But who comes here? |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Orlando]], with [[Sword|sword]] drawn |
| [[Orlando|ORLANDO]] | Forbear, and eat no more! |
| [[Jaques|JAQUES]] | Why, I have eat none yet. |
| [[Orlando|ORLANDO]] | Nor shalt not till necessity be served. |
| [[Jaques|JAQUES]] | Of what kind should this cock come of? |
| [[Duke Senior|DUKE SENIOR]] | Art thou thus boldened, man, by thy distress?<br>Or else a rude despiser of good manners,<br>That in civility thou seem’st so empty? |
| [[Orlando|ORLANDO]] | You touched my vein at first. The thorny point<br>Of bare distress hath ta’en from me the show<br>Of smooth civility. Yet am I inland bred,<br>And know some nurture. But forbear, I say.<br>He dies that touches any of this fruit<br>Till I and my affairs are answered. |
| [[Jaques|JAQUES]] | An you will not be answered with reason, I must die. |
| [[Duke Senior|DUKE SENIOR]] | What would you have? Your gentleness shall force<br>More than your force move us to gentleness. |
| [[Orlando|ORLANDO]] | I almost die for food; and let me have it. |
| [[Duke Senior|DUKE SENIOR]] | Sit down and feed, and welcome to our table. |
| [[Orlando|ORLANDO]] | Speak you so gently? Pardon me, I pray you.<br>I thought that all things had been savage here,<br>And therefore put I on the countenance<br>Of stern commandment. But whate‘er you are<br>That in this [[Desert|desert]] inaccessible,<br>Under the shade of [[Melancholy|melancholy]] boughs,<br>Lose and neglect the creeping hours of [[Time|time]],<br>If ever you have looked on better days,<br>If ever been where [[Bells|bells]] have knolled to church,<br>If ever sat at any good man’s feast,<br>If ever from your eyelids wiped a tear,<br>And know what ’tis to pity, and be pitied,<br>Let gentleness my strong enforcement be.<br>In the which [[Hope|hope]] I blush, and hide my [[Sword|sword]]. |
| [[Duke Senior|DUKE SENIOR]] | True is it that we have seen better days,<br>And have with holy bell been knolled to church,<br>And sat at good men’s feasts, and wiped our eyes<br>Of drops that sacred pity hath engendered.<br>And therefore sit you down in gentleness,<br>And take upon command what help we have<br>That to your wanting may be ministered. |
| [[Orlando|ORLANDO]] | Then but forbear your food a little while<br>Whiles, like a doe, I go to find my fawn<br>And give it food. There is an old poor man<br>Who after me hath many a weary step<br>Limped in pure [[Love|love]]. Till he be first sufficed,<br>Oppressed with two weak evils, age and hunger,<br>I will not touch a bit. |
| [[Duke Senior|DUKE SENIOR]] | Go find him out,<br>And we will nothing waste till you return. |
| [[Orlando|ORLANDO]] | I thank ye; and be blessed for your good comfortl Exit |
| [[Duke Senior|DUKE SENIOR]] | Thou seest we are not all [[Alone|alone]] unhappy.<br>This wide and universal theatre<br>Presents more woeful pageants than the scene<br>Wherein we play in. |
| [[Jaques|JAQUES]] | All the world’s a stage,<br>And all the men and women merely players.<br>They have their exits and their entrances,<br>And one man in his [[Time|time]] plays many parts,<br>His acts being seven ages. At first the infant,<br>Mewling and puking in the [[Nurse|nurse]]’s arms.<br>Then the whining schoolboy with his satchel<br>And shining morning face, creeping like snail<br>Unwillingly to school. And then the [[Lover|lover]],<br>Sighing like furnace, with a woeful ballad<br>Made to his mistress’ eyebrow. Then, a soldier,<br>Full of strange oaths, and bearded like the pard,<br>Jealous in [[Honour|honour]], sudden, and quick in quarrel,<br>Seeking the bubble reputation<br>Even in the cannon’s mouth. And then the justice,<br>In fair round belly with good capon lined,<br>With eyes severe and beard of formal cut,<br>Full of wise saws and modern instances;<br>And so he plays his part. The sixth age shifts<br>Into the lean and slippered pantaloon,<br>With spectacles on nose and pouch on side,<br>His youthful hose, [[Well|well]] saved, a world too wide<br>For his shrunk shank, and his big, manly voice,<br>Turning again toward childish treble, pipes<br>And whistles in his sound. Last scene of all,<br>That ends this strange, eventful [[history]],<br>Is second childishness and mere oblivion,<br>Sans teeth, sans eyes, sans taste, sans everything. |

| [[Orlando|ORLANDO]] | I thank you most for him. |

I will not trouble you<br>As yet to question you about your fortunes.<br>Give us some music, and, good cousin, sing.<br>[amiens] (sings)<br>Blow, blow, thou [[Winter|winter]] [[Wind|wind]],<br>Thou art not so unkind<br>As man’s ingratitude.<br>Thy tooth is not so keen,<br>Because thou art not seen,<br>Although thy breath be rude.<br>Hey-ho, sing hey-ho, unto the green holly.<br>Most [[Friendship|friendship]] is feigning, most loving, mere folly.<br>Then hey-ho, the holly;<br>This life is most jolly.<br><br>Freeze, freeze, thou bitter sky,<br>That dost not bite so nigh<br>As benefits forgot.<br>Though thou the waters warp,<br>Thy sting is not so sharp<br>As friend remembered not.<br>Hey-ho, sing hey-ho, unto the green holly.<br>Most [[Friendship|friendship]] is feigning, most loving, mere folly.<br>Then hey-ho, the holly;<br>This life is most jolly. |
| [[Duke Senior|DUKE SENIOR]] | (to [[Orlando]])<br>If that you were the good Sir Rowland’s son,<br>As you have whispered faithfully you were,<br>And as mine eye doth his effigies witness<br>Most truly limned and living in your face,<br>Be truly welcome hither. I am the Duke<br>That loved your father. The residue of your fortune,<br>Go to my [[Cave|cave]] and tell me. (To Adam) Good old man,<br>Thou art right welcome, as thy master is.—<br>(To Lords) Support him by the arm. (To [[Orlando]]) Give<br>me your hand,<br>And let me all your fortunes understand.
