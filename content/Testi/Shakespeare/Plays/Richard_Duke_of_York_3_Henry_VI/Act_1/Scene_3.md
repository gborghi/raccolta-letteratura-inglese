| *(didascalia)* | Alarums, and then enter the young Earl of Rutland and his Tutor, a chaplain |
| RUTLAND | Ah, whither shall I fly to scape their hands? |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Lord [[Clifford]] with soldiers |
| RUTLAND | Ah, tutor, look where bloody [[Clifford]] comes. |
| [[Clifford|CLIFFORD]] | (to the Tutor)<br>Chaplain, away—thy priesthood saves thy life.<br>As for the brat of this accursed duke,<br>Whose father slew my father—he shall die. |
| TUTOR | And I, my lord, will bear him company. |
| [[Clifford|CLIFFORD]] | Soldiers, away with him. |
| TUTOR | Ah, [[Clifford]], murder not this [[Innocent|innocent]] [[Child|child]]<br>Lest thou be hated both of God and man. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit, guarded |
| *(didascalia)* | Rutland falls to the ground |
| [[Clifford|CLIFFORD]] | How now—is he dead already?<br>Or is it [[Fear|fear]] that makes him close his eyes?<br>I’ll open them. |
| *(didascalia)* | RUTLAND reviving |
| [[Clifford|CLIFFORD]] | So looks the pent-up [[Lion|lion]] o‘er the wretch<br>That trembles under his devouring paws,<br>And so he walks, insulting o’er his prey,<br>And so he comes to rend his limbs asunder.<br>Ah, gentle [[Clifford]], kill me with thy [[Sword|sword]]<br>And not with such a cruel threat’ning look.<br>Sweet [[Clifford]], hear me speak before I die.<br>I am too mean a subject for thy wrath.<br>Be thou revenged on men, and let me live. |
| [[Clifford|CLIFFORD]] | In vain thou speak’st, poor boy. My father’s [[Blood|blood]]<br>Hath stopped the passage where thy words should<br>enter. |
| RUTLAND | Then let my father’s [[Blood|blood]] open it again.<br>He is a man, and, [[Clifford]], cope with him. |
| [[Clifford|CLIFFORD]] | Had I thy brethren here, their lives and thine<br>Were not [[Revenge|revenge]] sufficient for me.<br>No—if I digged up thy forefathers’ graves,<br>And hung their rotten coffins up in [[Chains|chains]],<br>It could not slake mine ire nor ease my heart.<br>The sight of any of the [[house]] of York<br>Is as a fury to torment my [[Soul|soul]].<br>And till I root out their accursed line,<br>And leave not one alive, I live in hell.<br>Therefore— |
| RUTLAND | O, let me pray before I take my [[Death|death]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | Kneeling To thee I pray: sweet [[Clifford]], pity me. |
| [[Clifford|CLIFFORD]] | Such pity as my rapier’s point affords. |
| RUTLAND | I never did thee harm—why wilt thou slay me? |
| [[Clifford|CLIFFORD]] | Thy father hath. |
| RUTLAND | But ’twas ere I was born.<br>Thou hast one son—for his sake pity me,<br>Lest in [[Revenge|revenge]] thereof, sith God is just,<br>He be as miserably slain as I.<br>Ah, let me live in [[Prison|prison]] all my days,<br>And when I give occasion of offence,<br>Then let me die, for now thou hast no cause. |
| [[Clifford|CLIFFORD]] | No cause? Thy father slew my father, therefore die. He stabs him |
| RUTLAND | Dii faciant laudis summa sit ista tuae. He dies |
| [[Clifford|CLIFFORD]] | Plantagenet—I come, Plantagenet!<br>And this thy son’s [[Blood|blood]] cleaving to my blade<br>Shall rust upon my weapon till thy [[Blood|blood]],<br>Congealed with this, do make me wipe off both. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit with Rutland’s [[Body|body]] and soldiers |
