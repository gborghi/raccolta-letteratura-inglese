| *(didascalia)* | Alarum and excursions. Enter [[King]] Edward bringing forth the [[Earl of Warwick]], wounded |
| [[King|KING]] EDWARD | So lie thou there. Die thou, and die our [[Fear|fear]]—<br>For Warwick was a bug that feared us all.<br>Now, Montague, sit fast—I seek for thee<br>That Warwick’s bones may keep thine company. Exit |
| WARWICK | Ah, who is nigh? Come to me, friend or foe,<br>And tell me who is victor, York or Warwick?<br>Why ask I that? My mangled [[Body|body]] shows,<br>My [[Blood|blood]], my want of strength, my sick heart shows,<br>That I must yield my [[Body|body]] to the earth<br>And by my fall the conquest to my foe.<br>Thus yields the cedar to the axe’s edge,<br>Whose arms gave shelter to the princely eagle,<br>Under whose shade the ramping [[Lion|lion]] slept,<br>Whose top-branch over-peered Jove’s spreading tree<br>And kept low shrubs from [[Winter|winter]]’s powerful [[Wind|wind]].<br>These eyes, that now are dimmed with [[Death|death]]’s black<br>veil,<br>Have been as piercing as the midday sun<br>To search the secret treasons of the world.<br>The wrinkles in my brows, now filled with [[Blood|blood]],<br>Were likened oft to kingly sepulchres—<br>For who lived [[King|king]], but I could dig his [[Grave|grave]]?<br>And who durst smile when Warwick bent his brow?<br>Lo now my glory smeared in [[Dust|dust]] and [[Blood|blood]].<br>My parks, my walks, my manors that I had,<br>Even now forsake me, and of all my lands<br>Is nothing left me but my [[Body|body]]’s length,<br>Why, what is pomp, rule, reign, but earth and [[Dust|dust]]?<br>And, live we how we can, yet die we must. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter the Earl of [[Oxford]] and the Duke of Somerset |
| SOMERSET | Ah, Warwick, Warwick—wert thou as we are,<br>We might recover all our loss again.<br>The [[Queen]] from France hath brought a puissant<br>power.<br>Even now we heard the news. Ah, couldst thou fly! |
| WARWICK | Why, then I would not fly. Ah, Montague,<br>If thou be there, sweet brother, take my hand,<br>And with thy lips keep in my [[Soul|soul]] a while.<br>Thou lov’st me not—for, brother, if thou didst,<br>Thy [[Tears|tears]] would wash this cold congealed [[Blood|blood]]<br>That glues my lips and will not let me speak.<br>Come quickly, Montague, or I am dead. |
| SOMERSET | Ah, Warwick—Montague hath breathed his last,<br>And to the latest gasp cried out for Warwick,<br>And said ‘Commend me to my valiant brother.’<br>And more he would have said, and more he spoke,<br>Which sounded like a canon in a vault,<br>That mote not be distinguished; but at last<br>I [[Well|well]] might hear, delivered with a groan,<br>‘O, farewell, Warwick.’ |
| WARWICK | Sweet rest his [[Soul|soul]]. Fly, lords, and save yourselves—<br>For Warwick bids you all farewell, to meet in heaven.<br>He dies |
| [[Oxford|OXFORD]] | Away, away—to meet the [[Queen]]’s great power!<br>Here they bear away Warwick’s [[Body|body]]. Exeunt |
