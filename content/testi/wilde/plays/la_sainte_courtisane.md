| *(didascalia)* | This unfinished play follows [[characters/myrrhina\|Myrrhina]], an Alexandrian noblewoman, who travels to the [[settings/mountains\|mountains]] to tempt [[characters/honorius\|Honorius]], a Christian hermit, away from goodness with her [[concepts/beauty\|beauty]] and wealth. After they talk, he decides to return to sin in Alexandria, while she discovers religion and chooses to remain in the [[motifs/desert\|desert]].<br>Wilde had begun work on the play in 1894, between writing Salomé and The Importance of Being Earnest, but he was unable to complete it before his trial and [[concepts/imprisonment\|imprisonment]]. He considered revisiting the play in 1897 after his release from [[motifs/prison\|prison]], but he then lacked motivation for literary work, although during his [[concepts/imprisonment\|imprisonment]], it was much on his mind and he had described it in a letter to a friend as one among his “beautiful coloured, musical things”. Before his [[concepts/imprisonment\|imprisonment]], the fragments had been entrusted to Mrs. Leverson, who in 1897 went to [[settings/paris\|Paris]] on purpose to restore the manuscript to the author. However, Wilde accidently left the papers in a taxi cab and now only a portion of a first draft survives.<br><br>Wilde, c. 190<br><br>LA SAINTE COURTISANE<br>OR, THE WOMAN COVERED WITH JEWELS<br>The scene represents a corner of a valley in the Thebaid. On the right hand of the stage is a cavern. In front of the cavern stands a great crucifix.<br>On the left<br>[sand dunes].<br>The sky is blue like the inside of a cup of lapis lazuli. The hills are of red sand. Here and there on the hills there are clumps of thorns. |

| FIRST MAN | We worship seven gods. We may not tell their names. It is a very dangerous thing to tell the names of the gods. No one should ever tell the name of his god. Even the priests who praise the gods all day long, and eat of their food with them, do not call them by their right names. |

The pillars of my bed are of cedar and the hangings are of purple. My bed is strewn with purple and the steps are of silver. The hangings are sewn with silver pomegranates and the steps that are of silver are strewn with saffron and with myrrh. My lovers hang garlands round the pillars of my house. At [[motifs/night\|night]] [[motifs/time\|time]] they come with the flute players and the players of the harp. They woo me with apples and on the pavement of my courtyard they write my name in wine.<br>From the uttermost parts of the world my lovers come to me. The kings of the earth come to me and bring me presents.<br>When the Emperor of Byzantium heard of me he left his porphyry chamber and set sail in his galleys. His slaves bare no torches that none might know of his coming. When the [[archetypes/king\|King]] of Cyprus heard of me he sent me ambassadors. The two Kings of Libya who are brothers brought me gifts of amber.<br>I took the minion of Cæsar from Cæsar and made him my playfellow. He came to me at [[motifs/night\|night]] in a litter. He was pale as a narcissus, and his [[concepts/body\|body]] was like honey.<br>The son of the Præfect slew himself in my [[concepts/honour\|honour]], and the Tetrarch of Cilicia scourged himself for my pleasure before my slaves.<br>The [[archetypes/king\|King]] of Hierapolis who is a priest and a robber set carpets for me to walk on.<br>Sometimes I sit in the circus and the gladiators fight beneath me. Sometimes I pass through the gymnasium and watch the young men wrestling or in the race. Their bodies are bright with oil and their brows are wreathed with willow sprays and with myrtle. They stamp their feet on the sand when they wrestle and when they run the sand follows them like a little cloud. He at whom I smile [[motifs/leaves\|leaves]] his companions and follows me to my [[motifs/home\|home]]. At other times I go down to the harbour and watch the merchants unloading their vessels. Those that come from Tyre have cloaks of silk and earrings of emerald. Those that come from Massilia have cloaks of fine wool and earrings of brass. When they see me coming they stand on the prows of their ships and call to me, but I do not answer them. I go to the little taverns where the sailors lie all day long drinking black wine and playing with dice and I sit down with them.<br>I made the Prince my slave, and his slave who was a Tyrian I made my Lord for the space of a [[motifs/moon\|moon]].<br>I put a figured ring on his finger and brought him to my house. I have wonderful things in my house.<br>The [[motifs/dust\|dust]] of the [[motifs/desert\|desert]] lies on your hair and your feet are scratched with thorns and your [[concepts/body\|body]] is scorched by the sun. Come with me, [[characters/honorius\|Honorius]], and I will clothe you in a tunic of silk. I will smear your [[concepts/body\|body]] with myrrh and pour spikenard on your hair. I will clothe you in hyacinth and put honey in your mouth. [[motifs/love\|Love]] — |
