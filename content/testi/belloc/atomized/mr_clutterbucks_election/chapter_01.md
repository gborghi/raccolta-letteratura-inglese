CHAPTER XIV

The novel’s dedicatee, [[G. K. Chesterton]], 1909 — Chesterton (1874-1936) was an English writer, lay theologian and literary and art critic. Belloc collaborated with Chesterton on a number of works, the latter often providing humorous drawings for Belloc’s novels. Due to their association, George Bernard Shaw coined the term ‘Chesterbelloc’.

George Bernard Shaw, Hilaire Belloc and [[G. K. Chesterton]], 1927

Dedication to [[G. K. Chesterton]] – translates (roughly) as ‘a shared attitude towards the State’
