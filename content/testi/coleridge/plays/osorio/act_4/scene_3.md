| *(didascalia)* | ALHADRA [[Alone|alone]] in a Moorish dress, her eyes fixed on the earth. Then drop in one after another, from different parts of the stage, a considerable number of Morescoes, all in their Moorish garments. They form a circle at a distance round ALHADRA. After a pause one of the Morescoes to the man who stands next to him. |
| FIRST MORESCO | The law which forced these Christian dresses on us,<br>‘Twere pleasant to cleave down the wretch who framed it. |
| SECOND | Yet ‘tis not [[Well|well]] to trample on it idly. |
| FIRST | Our country robes are dear. |
| SECOND | And like dear friends,<br>May chance to prove most perilous informers. |
| *(didascalia)* | *(A third Moresco, NAOMI, advances from out the circle.)* |
| NAOMI | Woman! may Alla and the [[Prophet|prophet]] bless thee!<br>We have obey’d thy call. Where is our chief?<br>And why didst thou enjoin the Moorish garments? |
| ALHADRA | *(lifting up [raising Remorse] her eyes, and looking round on the circle.)* Warriors of Mahomet, faithful in the battle,<br>My countrymen! Come ye prepared to work<br>An honourable deed? And would ye work it<br>In the slave’s garb? Curse on those Christian robes!<br>They are spell-blasted; and whoever wears them,<br>His arm shrinks wither’d, his heart melts away,<br>And his bones soften! |
| NAOMI | Where is [[Ferdinand]]? |
| ALHADRA | *(in a deep low voice.)* This [[Night|night]] I went from forth my [[house]], and left<br>His children all asleep; and he was living!<br>And I return’d, and found them still asleep —<br>But he had perish’d. |
| ALL | Perished? |
| ALHADRA | He had perish’d!<br>[[Sleep]] on, poor babes! not one of you doth know<br>That he is fatherless, a desolate [[Orphan|orphan]]!<br>Why should we wake them? Can an infant’s arm<br>[[Revenge]] his murder? |
| ONE TO ANOTHER | Did she say his murder? |
| NAOMI | Murder’d? Not murder’d? |
| ALHADRA | Murder’d by a Christian! |
| *(didascalia)* | *(They all, at once, draw their sabres.)* |
| ALHADRA | *(to Naomi, who on being addressed again advances from the circle.)* Brother of Zagri! fling away thy [[Sword|sword]]:<br>This is thy chieftain’s! *(He steps forward to take it.)*<br>Dost thou dare receive it?<br>For I have sworn by Alia and the [[Prophet|prophet]],<br>No tear shall dim these eyes, this woman’s heart<br>Shall heave no groan, till I have seen that [[Sword|sword]]<br>Wet with the [[Blood|blood]] of all the [[house]] of Velez! |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter MAURICE. |
| ALL | A spy! a spy! *(They seize him.)* |
| MAURICE | Off! off! unhand me, slaves! |
| *(didascalia)* | *(After much struggling he disengages himself and draws his [[Sword|sword]].)* |
| NAOMI | *(to Alhadra.)* Speak! shall we kill him? |
| MAURICE | Yes! ye can kill a man,<br>Some twenty of you! But ye are Spanish slaves!<br>And slaves are always cruel, always cowards. |
| ALHADRA | That man has spoken [[Truth|truth]]. Whence and who art thou? |
| MAURICE | I seek a dear friend, whom for aught I know<br>The son of Velez hath hired one of you<br>To murder! Say, do ye know aught of Albert? |
| ALHADRA | *(starting.)* Albert? — three years ago I heard that name<br>Murmur’d in [[Sleep|sleep]]! High-minded foreigner!<br>Mix thy [[Revenge|revenge]] with mine, and stand among us. |
| *(didascalia)* | *(MAURICE stands among the Morescoes.)* |
| ALHADRA | Was not Osorio my husband’s friend? |
| OLD MAN | He kill’d my son in battle; yet our chieftain<br>Forced me to sheathe my dagger. See — the point<br>Is bright, unrusted with the [[Villain|villain]]’s [[Blood|blood]]! |
| ALHADRA | He is your chieftain’s murderer! |
| NAOMI | He dies by Alla! |
| ALL | *(dropping on one knee.)* By Alla! |
| ALHADRA | This [[Night|night]] a reeking slave came with loud pant,<br>Gave [[Ferdinand]] a letter, and departed,<br>Swift as he came. Pale, with unquiet looks,<br>He read the scroll. |
| MAURICE | Its purport? |
| ALHADRA | Yes, I ask’d it.<br>He answer’d me, ‘Alhadra! thou art worthy<br>A nobler secret; but I have been faithful<br>To this bad man, and faithful I will be.’<br>He said, and arm’d himself, and lit a torch;<br>Then kiss’d his children, each one on its pillow,<br>And hurried from me. But I follow’d him<br>At distance, till I saw him enter there. |
| NAOMI | The cavern? |
| ALHADRA | Yes — the mouth of yonder cavern.<br>After a pause I saw the son of Velez<br>Rush by with flaring torch; he likewise enter’d —<br>There was another and a longer pause —<br>And once, methought, I heard the clash of swords,<br>And soon the son of Velez reappear’d.<br>He flung his torch towards the [[Moon|moon]] in sport,<br>And seem’d as he were mirthful! I stood listening<br>Impatient for the footsteps of my husband! |
| MAURICE | Thou called’st him? |
| ALHADRA | I crept into the cavern:<br>‘Twas dark and very silent. *(Then wildly.)*<br>What said’st thou?<br>No, no! I did not dare call, [[Ferdinand]]!<br>Lest I should hear no answer. A brief while,<br>Belike, I lost all thought and [[Memory|memory]]<br>Of that for which I came! After that pause,<br>O God! I heard a groan! — and follow’d it.<br>And yet another groan — which guided me<br>Into a strange recess — and there was light,<br>A hideous light! his torch lay on the ground —<br>Its flame burnt dimly o’er a chasm’s brink.<br>I spake — and while I spake, a feeble groan<br>Came from that chasm! It was his last! his [[Death|death]] groan! |
| MAURICE | Comfort her, comfort her, Almighty Father! |
| ALHADRA | I stood in unimaginable trance<br>And agony, that cannot be remember’d,<br>Listening with horrid [[Hope|hope]] to hear a groan!<br>But I had heard his last — my husband’s [[Death|death]]-groan! |
| NAOMI | Haste! let us go! |
| ALHADRA | I look’d far down the pit.<br>My sight was bounded by a jutting [[Fragment|fragment]],<br>And it was stain’d with [[Blood|blood]]! Then first I shriek’d!<br>My eyeballs burnt! my brain grew hot as [[Fire|fire]]!<br>And all the hanging drops of the wet roof<br>Turn’d into [[Blood|blood]]. I saw them turn to [[Blood|blood]]!<br>And I was leaping wildly down the chasm<br>When on the further brink I saw his [[Sword|sword]],<br>And it said, Vengeance! Curses on my tongue!<br>The [[Moon|moon]] hath moved in heaven, and I am here,<br>And he hath not had vengeance! [[Ferdinand]]!<br>Spirit of [[Ferdinand]]! thy murderer lives!<br>Away! away! *(She rushes off, all following.)* |
| *(didascalia)* | END OF THE FOURTH ACT |
