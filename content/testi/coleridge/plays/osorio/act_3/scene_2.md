| *(didascalia)* | FRANCESCO and a Spy. |
| FRANCESCO | Yes! yes! I have the key of all their lives.<br>If a man fears me, he is forced to [[Love|love]] me.<br>And if I can, and do not ruin him,<br>He is fast bound to serve and [[Honour|honour]] me! |
| *(didascalia)* | *(ALBERT enters from the [[Castle|castle]], and is crossing the stage.)* |
| SPY | There — there — your Reverence! That is the sorcerer. |
| *(didascalia)* | *(FRANCESCO runs up and rudely catches hold of ALBERT. ALBERT dashes him to the earth. FRANCESCO and the Spy make an uproar, and the servants rush from out the [[Castle|castle]].)* |
| FRANCESCO | Seize, seize and gag him! or the Church curses you! |
| *(didascalia)* | *(The servants seize and gag ALBERT.)* |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter VELEZ and OSORIO. |
| OSORIO | *(aside.)* This is most lucky! |
| FRANCESCO | *(inarticulate with rage.)* See you this, Lord Velez?<br>Good evidence have I of most foul sorcery,<br>And in the name of Holy Church command you<br>To give me up the keys — the keys, my lord!<br>Of that same dungeon-hole beneath your [[Castle|castle]].<br>This imp of hell — but we delay enquiry<br>Till to Granada we have convoy’d him. |
| OSORIO | *(to the Servants.)* Why haste you not? Go, fly and dungeon him!<br>Then bring the keys and give them to his Reverence. |
| *(didascalia)* | *(The Servants hurry off ALBERT. OSORIO goes up to FRANCESCO, and pointing at ALBERT.)* |
| OSORIO | *(with a laugh.)* ‘He that can bring the dead to life again.’ |
| FRANCESCO | What? did you hear it? |
| OSORIO | Yes, and plann’d this scheme<br>To bring conviction on him. Ho! a wizard,<br>Thought I — but where’s the proof! I plann’d this scheme.<br>The scheme has answer’d — we have proof enough. |
| FRANCESCO | My lord, your pious policy astounds me.<br>I trust my honest zeal —— |
| OSORIO | Nay, reverend father!<br>It has but raised my veneration for you.<br>But ‘twould be [[Well|well]] to stop all intertalk<br>Between my servants and this [[Child|child]] of darkness. |
| FRANCESCO | My lord! with [[Speed|speed]] I’ll go, make swift return,<br>And humbly redeliver you the keys. *(Exit FRANCESCO.)* |
| OSORIO | *([[Alone|alone]].)* ‘The stranger, that lives nigh, still picking weeds.’<br>And this was his friend, his crony, his twin-brother!<br>O! I am green, a very simple stripling —<br>The wise men of this world make nothing of me.<br>By Heaven, ‘twas [[Well|well]] contrived! And I, forsooth,<br>I was to cut my throat in [[Honour|honour]] of [[Conscience|conscience]].<br>And this tall wizard — ho! — he was to pass<br>For Albert’s friend! He hath a trick of his manner.<br>He was to tune his voice to honey’d sadness,<br>And win her to a transfer of her [[Love|love]]<br>By lamentable tales of her dear Albert,<br>And his dear Albert! Yea, she would have lov’d him.<br>He, that can sigh out in a woman’s ear<br>Sad recollections of her perish’d [[Lover|lover]],<br>And sob and smile with veering sympathy,<br>And, now and then, as if by accident,<br>Pass his mouth close enough to touch her cheek<br>With timid lip, he takes the [[Lover|lover]]’s place,<br>He takes his place, for certain! Dusky rogue,<br>Were it not sport to whimper with thy mistress,<br>Then steal away and roll upon my [[Grave|grave]],<br>Till thy sides shook with laughter? [[Blood]]! [[Blood|blood]]! [[Blood|blood]]!<br>They want thy [[Blood|blood]]! thy [[Blood|blood]], Osorio! |
| *(didascalia)* | [END OF ACT THE THIRD.] |
