| Chi parla | Battuta |
|---|---|
| *(didascalia)* | ALBERT ([[Alone|alone]]) rises slowly from a bed of reeds. |
| ALBERT | And this place my forefathers made for men!<br>This is the process of our [[Love|love]] and wisdom<br>To each poor brother who offends against us —<br>Most [[Innocent|innocent]], perhaps — and what if guilty?<br>Is this the only cure? Merciful God!<br>Each pore and natural outlet shrivell’d up<br>By ignorance and parching [[Poverty|poverty]],<br>His energies roll back upon his heart,<br>And stagnate and corrupt till changed to [[Poison|poison]],<br>They break out on him like a loathsome plague-spot!<br>Then we call in our pamper’d mountebanks —<br>And this is their best cure! uncomforted<br>And friendless [[Solitude|solitude]], groaning and [[Tears|tears]],<br>And savage faces at the clanking hour<br>Seen thro’ the steaming vapours of his dungeon<br>By the lamp’s dismal twilight! So he lies<br>Circled with evil, till his very [[Soul|soul]]<br>Unmoulds its essence, hopelessly deform’d<br>By sights of ever more deformity!<br>With other ministrations thou, O [[Nature]]!<br>Healest thy wandering and distemper’d [[Child|child]]:<br>Thou pourest on him thy soft influences,<br>Thy sunny hues, fair forms, and breathing sweets,<br>Thy melodies of woods, and winds, and waters,<br>Till he relent, and can no more endure<br>To be a jarring and a dissonant thing<br>Amid this general [[Dance|dance]] and minstrelsy;<br>But bursting into [[Tears|tears]] wins back his way,<br>His angry spirit heal’d and harmoniz’d<br>By the benignant touch of [[Love|love]] and [[Beauty|beauty]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | *(A noise at the dungeon-door. It opens, and OSORIO enters with a goblet in his hand.)* |
| OSORIO | Hail, potent wizard! In my gayer mood<br>I pour’d forth a libation to old Pluto;<br>And as I brimm’d the bowl, I thought of thee! |
| ALBERT | *(in a low voice.)* I have not summon’d up my heart to give<br>That pang, which I must give thee, son of Velez! |
| OSORIO | *(with affected levity.)* Thou hast conspired against my life and [[Honour|honour]],<br>Hast trick’d me foully; yet I hate thee not!<br>Why should I hate thee? This same world of ours —<br>It is a puddle in a [[Storm|storm]] of rain,<br>And we the air-bladders, that course up and down,<br>And joust and tilt in merry tournament,<br>And when one bubble runs foul of another, *(Waving his hand at ALBERT.)*<br>The lesser must needs break! |
| ALBERT | I see thy heart!<br>There is a frightful glitter in thine eye,<br>Which doth betray thee. Crazy-conscienc’d man,<br>This is the gaiety of drunken anguish,<br>Which fain would scoff away the pang of [[Guilt|guilt]],<br>And quell each human feeling! |
| OSORIO | Feeling! feeling!<br>The [[Death|death]] of a man — the breaking of a bubble.<br>‘Tis true, I cannot sob for such misfortunes!<br>But faintness, cold, and hunger — curses on me<br>If willingly I e’er inflicted them!<br>Come, share the beverage — this chill place demands it. *(OSORIO proffers him the goblet.)*<br>[[Friendship]] and wine! |
| ALBERT | Yon insect on the wall,<br>Which moves this way and that its hundred legs,<br>Were it a toy of mere mechanic craft,<br>It were an infinitely curious thing!<br>But it has life, Osorio! life and thought;<br>And by the power of its miraculous will<br>Wields all the complex movements of its frame<br>Unerringly, to pleasurable ends!<br>Saw I that insect on this goblet’s brink,<br>I would remove it with an eager terror. |
| OSORIO | What meanest thou? |
| ALBERT | There’s [[Poison|poison]] in the wine. |
| OSORIO | Thou hast guess’d [[Well|well]]. There’s [[Poison|poison]] in the wine.<br>Shall we throw dice, which of us two shall drink it?<br>For one of us must die! |
| ALBERT | Whom dost thou think me? |
| OSORIO | The accomplice and sworn friend of [[Ferdinand]]. |
| ALBERT | [[Ferdinand]]! [[Ferdinand]]! ‘tis a name I know not. |
| OSORIO | Good! good! that lie! by Heaven! it has restor’d me.<br>Now I am thy master! [[Villain]], thou shalt drink it,<br>Or die a bitterer [[Death|death]]. |
| ALBERT | What strange solution<br>Hast thou found out to satisfy thy fears,<br>And drug them to unnatural [[Sleep|sleep]]? |
| *(didascalia)* | *(ALBERT takes the goblet, and with a sigh throws it on the ground.)* |
| ALBERT | My master! |
| OSORIO | Thou mountebank! |
| ALBERT | Mountebank and [[Villain|villain]]!<br>What then art thou? For shame, put up thy [[Sword|sword]]!<br>What boots a weapon in a wither’d arm?<br>I fix mine eye upon thee, and thou tremblest!<br>I speak — and [[Fear|fear]] and [[Wonder|wonder]] crush thy rage,<br>And turn it to a motionless distraction!<br>Thou blind [[Self|self]]-worshipper! thy [[Pride|pride]], thy cunning,<br>Thy [[Faith|faith]] in universal villainy,<br>Thy shallow sophisms, thy pretended scorn<br>For all thy human brethren — out upon them!<br>What have they done for thee? Have they given thee peace?<br>Cured thee of starting in thy [[Sleep|sleep]]? or made<br>The darkness pleasant, when thou wakest at midnight?<br>Art happy when [[Alone|alone]]? can’st walk by thyself<br>With even step, and quiet cheerfulness?<br>Yet, yet thou mayst be saved. |
| OSORIO | *(stupidly reiterating the word.)* Saved? saved? |
| ALBERT | One pang —<br>Could I call up one pang of true remorse! |
| OSORIO | He told me of the babe, that prattled to him,<br>His fatherless little ones! Remorse! remorse!<br>Where gott’st thou that [[Fool|fool]]’s word? Curse on remorse!<br>Can it give up the dead, or recompact<br>A mangled [[Body|body]] — mangled, dash’d to atoms!<br>Not all the blessings of an host of angels<br>Can blow away a desolate widow’s curse;<br>And tho’ thou spill thy heart’s [[Blood|blood]] for atonement,<br>It will not weigh against an [[Orphan|orphan]]’s tear. |
| ALBERT | *(almost overcome by his feelings.)* But Albert —— |
| OSORIO | Ha! it chokes thee in the throat,<br>Even thee! and yet, I pray thee, speak it out.<br>Still Albert! Albert! Howl it in mine ear!<br>Heap it, like coals of [[Fire|fire]], upon my heart!<br>And shoot it hissing through my brain! |
| ALBERT | Alas —<br>That day, when thou didst leap from off the rock<br>Into the waves, and grasp’d thy sinking brother,<br>And bore him to the strand, then, son of Velez!<br>How sweet and musical the name of Albert!<br>Then, then, Osorio! he was dear to thee,<br>And thou wert dear to him. Heaven only knows<br>How very dear thou wert! Why didst thou hate him?<br>O Heaven! how he would fall upon thy neck,<br>And weep [[Forgiveness|forgiveness]]! |
| OSORIO | Spirit of the dead!<br>Methinks I know thee! Ha! — my brain turns wild<br>At its own [[Dreams|dreams]] — off — off, fantastic [[Shadow|shadow]]! |
| ALBERT | *(seizing his hand.)* I fain would tell thee what I am, but dare not! |
| OSORIO | *(retiring from him.)* Cheat, [[Villain|villain]], traitor! whatsoe’er thou be<br>I [[Fear|fear]] thee, man! |
| *(didascalia)* | *(He starts, and stands in the attitude of listening.)* |
| OSORIO | And is this too my [[Madness|madness]]? |
| ALBERT | It is the step of one that treads in [[Fear|fear]]<br>Seeking to cheat the echo. |
| OSORIO | It approaches —<br>This nook shall hide me. |
| *(didascalia)* | *([[Maria|MARIA]] enters from a plank which slips to and fro.)* |
| [[Maria]] | I have put aside<br>The customs and the terrors of a woman,<br>To work out thy escape. Stranger! begone,<br>And only tell me what thou know’st of Albert. |
| *(didascalia)* | *(ALBERT takes her portrait from his neck, and gives it her with unutterable tenderness.)* |
| ALBERT | [[Maria]]! my [[Maria]]! |
| [[Maria]] | Do not mock me.<br>This is my face — and thou — ha! who art thou?<br>Nay, I will call thee Albert! |
| *(didascalia)* | *(She falls upon his neck. OSORIO leaps out from the nook with frantic wildness, and rushes towards ALBERT with his [[Sword|sword]]. [[Maria|MARIA]] gapes at him, as one helpless with terror, then [[Leaves|leaves]] ALBERT, and flings herself upon OSORIO, arresting his arm.)* |
| [[Maria]] | Madman, stop! |
| ALBERT | *(with majesty and tenderness.)* Does then this thin disguise impenetrably<br>Hide Albert from thee? Toil and painful wounds,<br>And long [[Imprisonment|imprisonment]] in unwholesome dungeons,<br>Have marr’d perhaps all trace and lineament<br>Of what I was! But chiefly, chiefly, brother!<br>My anguish for thy [[Guilt|guilt]]. Spotless [[Maria]],<br>I thought thee guilty too! Osorio, brother!<br>Nay, nay, thou shalt embrace me! |
| OSORIO | *(drawing back and gazing at Albert with a countenance expressive at once of awe and terror.)* Touch me not!<br>Touch not pollution, Albert! — I will die! |
| *(didascalia)* | *(He attempts to fall on his [[Sword|sword]]. ALBERT and [[Maria|MARIA]] struggle with him.)* |
| ALBERT | We will invent some tale to save your [[Honour|honour]].<br>Live, live, Osorio! |
| [[Maria]] | You may yet be happy. |
| OSORIO | *(looking at [[Maria]].)* O horror! Not a thousand years in heaven<br>Could recompose this miserable heart,<br>Or make it capable of one brief joy.<br>Live! live! — why yes! ‘Twere [[Well|well]] to live with you —<br>For is it fit a [[Villain|villain]] should be proud?<br>My brother! I will kneel to you, my brother! |
| *(didascalia)* | *(Throws himself at ALBERT’S feet.)* |
| OSORIO | Forgive me, Albert! — Curse me with [[Forgiveness|forgiveness]]! |
| ALBERT | Call back thy [[Soul|soul]], my brother! and look round thee.<br>Now is the [[Time|time]] for [[Greatness|greatness]]. Think that Heaven —— |
| [[Maria]] | O mark his eye! he hears not what you say. |
| OSORIO | *(pointing at vacancy.)* Yes, mark his eye! there’s fascination in it.<br>Thou said’st thou didst not know him. That is he!<br>He comes upon me! |
| ALBERT | *(lifting his eye to heaven.)* Heal, O heal him, Heaven! |
| OSORIO | Nearer and nearer! And I cannot stir!<br>Will no one hear these stifled groans, and wake me?<br>He would have died to save me, and I kill’d him —<br>A husband and a father! |
| [[Maria]] | Some secret [[Poison|poison]]<br>Drinks up his spirit! |
| OSORIO | *(fiercely recollecting himself.)* Let the eternal Justice<br>Prepare my punishment in the obscure world.<br>I will not bear to live — to live! O agony!<br>And be myself [[Alone|alone]], my own sore torment! |
| *(didascalia)* | *(The doors of the dungeon are burst open with a crash. ALHADRA, MAURICE, and the band of Morescoes enter.)* |
| ALHADRA | *(pointing at Osorio.)* Seize first that man! |
| *(didascalia)* | *(The Moors press round.)* |
| ALBERT | *(rushing in among them.)* Draw thy [[Sword|sword]], Maurice, and defend my brother. |
| *(didascalia)* | *(A scuffle, during which they disarm MAURICE.)* |
| OSORIO | Off, ruffians! I have flung away my [[Sword|sword]].<br>Woman, my life is thine! to thee I give it.<br>Off! he that touches me with his hand of flesh,<br>I’ll rend his limbs asunder! I have strength<br>With this bare arm to scatter you like ashes! |
| ALHADRA | My husband —— |
| OSORIO | Yes! I murder’d him most foully. |
| ALBERT | *(throws himself on the earth.)* O horrible! |
| ALHADRA | Why didst thou leave his children?<br>Demon! thou shouldst have sent thy [[dogs]] of hell<br>To lap their [[Blood|blood]]. Then, then, I might have harden’d<br>My [[Soul|soul]] in misery, and have had comfort.<br>I would have stood far off, quiet tho’ dark,<br>And bade the race of men raise up a mourning<br>For the deep horror of a desolation<br>Too great to be one [[Soul|soul]]’s particular lot!<br>Brother of Zagri! let me lean upon thee. *(Struggling to suppress her anguish.)*<br>The [[Time|time]] is not yet come for woman’s anguish —<br>I have not seen his [[Blood|blood]]. Within an hour<br>Those little ones will crowd around and ask me,<br>Where is our father? *(Looks at OSORIO.)*<br>I shall curse thee then!<br>Wert thou in heaven, my curse would pluck thee thence! |
| [[Maria]] | See — see! he doth repent. I kneel to thee.<br>Be merciful! |
| *(didascalia)* | *([[Maria|MARIA]] kneels to her. ALHADRA regards her face wistfully.)* |
| ALHADRA | Thou art young and [[Innocent|innocent]];<br>‘Twere merciful to kill thee! Yet I will not.<br>And for thy sake none of this [[house]] shall perish,<br>Save only he. |
| [[Maria]] | That aged man, his father! |
| ALHADRA | *(sternly.)* Why had he such a son? |
| *(didascalia)* | *(The Moors press on.)* |
| [[Maria]] | *(still kneeling, and wild with affright.)* Yet spare his life!<br>They must not murder him! |
| ALHADRA | And is it then<br>An enviable lot to waste away<br>With inward wounds, and like the spirit of chaos<br>To wander on disquietly thro’ the earth,<br>Cursing all lovely things? to let him live —<br>It were a deep [[Revenge|revenge]]! |
| *(didascalia)* | *(All the band cry out — No [[Mercy|mercy]]! no [[Mercy|mercy]]!)* |
| *(didascalia)* | *(NAOMI advances with the [[Sword|sword]] towards OSORIO.)* |
| ALHADRA | Nay, bear him forth! Why should this [[Innocent|innocent]] maid<br>Behold the ugliness of [[Death|death]]? |
| OSORIO | *(with great majesty.)* O woman!<br>I have stood silent like a slave before thee,<br>That I might taste the wormwood and the gall,<br>And satiate this [[Self|self]]-accusing spirit<br>With bitterer agonies than [[Death|death]] can give. |
| *(didascalia)* | *(The Moors gather round him in a crowd, and pass off the stage.)* |
| ALHADRA | I thank thee, Heaven! thou hast ordain’d it wisely,<br>That still extremes bring their own cure. That point<br>In misery which makes the oppressed man<br>Regardless of his own life, makes him too<br>Lord of the oppressor’s! Knew I an hundred men<br>Despairing, but not palsied by [[Despair|despair]],<br>This arm should shake the kingdoms of this world;<br>The deep foundations of iniquity<br>Should sink away, earth groaning from beneath them;<br>The strong holds of the cruel men should fall,<br>Their temples and their mountainous towers should fall;<br>Till desolation seem’d a beautiful thing,<br>And all that were and had the spirit of life<br>Sang a new [[Song|song]] to him who had gone forth<br>Conquering and still to conquer! |
| *(didascalia)* | THE END |
