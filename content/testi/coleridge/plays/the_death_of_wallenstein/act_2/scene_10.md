| *(didascalia)* | To these enter TERTSKY. |
| COUNTESS | What meant that cry? What was it? |
| TERTSKY | All is lost! |
| COUNTESS | What! they regarded not his countenance? |
| TERTSKY | 'Twas all in vain. |
| DUCHESS | They shouted Vivat! |
| TERTSKY | To the Emperor. |
| COUNTESS | The traitors! |
| TERTSKY | Nay! he was not once permitted<br>Even to address them. Soon as he began,<br>With deafening noise of warlike instruments<br>They drowned his words. But here he comes. |
