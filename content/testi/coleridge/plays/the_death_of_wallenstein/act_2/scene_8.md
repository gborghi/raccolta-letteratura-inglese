| Chi parla | Battuta |
|---|---|
| *(didascalia)* | To these TERTSKY (returning). |
| TERTSKY | Message and greeting from our faithful regiments.<br>Their ardour may no longer be curbed in.<br>They intreat permission to commence the attack,<br>And if thou would'st but give the word of onset,<br>They could now charge the enemy in rear,<br>Into the [[City\|city]] wedge them, and with ease<br>O'erpower them in the narrow streets. |
| ILLO | O come!<br>Let not their ardour cool. The soldiery<br>Of Butler's corps stand by us faithfully;<br>We are the greater number. Let us charge them,<br>And finish here in Pilsen the revolt. |
| WALLENSTEIN | What? shall this town become a field of slaughter,<br>And brother-killing Discord, [[Fire\|fire]]-eyed,<br>Be let loose through its streets to roam and rage?<br>Shall the decision be delivered over<br>To deaf remorseless Rage, that hears no leader?<br>Here is not room for battle, only for butchery.<br>[[Well]], let it be! I have long thought of it,<br>So let it burst then! |
| *(didascalia)* | [Turns to MAX. |
| WALLENSTEIN | [[Well]], how is it with thee?<br>Wilt thou attempt a heat with me. Away!<br>Thou art free to go. Oppose thyself to me,<br>Front against front, and lead them to the battle;<br>Thou'rt skilled in war, thou hast learned somewhat under me,<br>I need not be ashamed of my opponent,<br>And never had'st thou fairer opportunity<br>To pay me for thy schooling. |
| COUNTESS | Is it then,<br>Can it have come to this? — What! Cousin, Cousin!<br>Have you the heart? |
| MAX | The regiments that are trusted to my care<br>I have pledged my troth to bring away from Pilsen<br>True to the Emperor, and this promise will I<br>Make good, or perish. More than this no duty<br>Requires of me. I will not fight against thee,<br>Unless compelled; for though an enemy,<br>Thy head is holy to me still. |
| *(didascalia)* | [Two reports of cannon. ILLO and TERTSKY hurry to the [[Window\|window]]. |
| WALLENSTEIN | What's that? |
| TERTSKY | He falls. |
| WALLENSTEIN | Falls! Who? |
| ILLO | Tiefenbach's corps<br>Discharged the ordnance. |
| WALLENSTEIN | Upon whom? |
| ILLO | On Neumann,<br>Your messenger. |
| WALLENSTEIN | Ha! [[Death]] and hell! I will — |
| TERTSKY | Expose thyself to their blind frenzy? |
| DUCHESS AND COUNTESS | No!<br>For God's sake, no! |
| ILLO | Not yet, my General! |
| COUNTESS | O, hold him! hold him! |
| WALLENSTEIN | Leave me —— |
| MAX | Do it not<br>Not yet! This rash and bloody deed has thrown them<br>Into a frenzy-fit — allow them [[Time\|time]] —— |
| WALLENSTEIN | Away! too long already have I loitered.<br>They are emboldened to these outrages,<br>Beholding not my face. They shall behold<br>My countenance, shall hear my voice ——<br>Are they not my troops? Am I not their General,<br>And their long-feared commander? Let me see,<br>Whether indeed they do no longer know<br>That countenance, which was their sun in battle!<br>From the balcony (mark!) I shew myself<br>To these rebellious forces, and at once<br>Revolt is mounded, and the high-swoln current<br>Shrinks back into the old bed of obedience. |
| *(didascalia)* | [Exit WALLENSTEIN; ILLO, TERTSKY, and BUTLER follow. |
