| BUTLER | I treasured my good name all my life long;<br>The Duke has cheated me of life's best [[Jewel\|jewel]],<br>So that I blush before this poor weak Gordon!<br>He prizes above all his fealty;<br>His conscious [[Soul\|soul]] accuses him of nothing;<br>In opposition to his own soft heart<br>He subjugates himself to an iron duty.<br>Me in a weaker moment passion warped;<br>I stand beside him, and must feel myself<br>The worst man of the two. What though the world<br>Is ignorant of my purposed treason, yet<br>One man does know it, and can prove it too —<br>High-minded Piccolomini!<br>There lives the man who can dishonour me!<br>This ignominy [[Blood\|blood]] [[Alone\|alone]] can cleanse!<br>Duke Friedland, thou or I — Into my own hands<br>Fortune delivers me — The dearest thing a man has is himself. |
| *(didascalia)* | (The curtain drops.) |
