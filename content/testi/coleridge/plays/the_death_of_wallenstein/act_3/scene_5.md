| *(didascalia)* | To these enter ILLO. |
| ILLO | A courier, Duke! he wishes to speak with thee. |
| TERTSKY | Does he bring confirmation of the victory? |
| WALLENSTEIN | What does he bring? Whence comes he? |
| ILLO | From the Rhinegrave.<br>And what he brings I can announce to you<br>Beforehand. Seven leagues distant are the Swedes;<br>At Neustadt did Max Piccolomini<br>Throw himself on them with the cavalry;<br>A murderous fight took place! o'erpower'd by numbers<br>The Pappenheimers all, with Max their leader,<br>Were left dead on the field. |
| WALLENSTEIN | *(after a pause)* Where is the messenger? Conduct me to him. |
| *(didascalia)* | [WALLENSTEIN is going, when LADY NEUBRUNN rushes into the room. Some servants follow her and run across the stage. |
| NEUBRUNN | Help! Help! |
| ILLO and TERTSKY | *(at the same [[Time\|time]])* What now? |
| NEUBRUNN | The Princess! |
| WALLENSTEIN and TERTSKY | Does she know it? |
| NEUBRUNN | She is dying! |
| *(didascalia)* | [Hurries off the stage, when WALLENSTEIN and TERTSKY follow her. |
