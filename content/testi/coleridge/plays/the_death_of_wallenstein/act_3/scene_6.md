| Chi parla | Battuta |
|---|---|
| *(didascalia)* | BUTLER and GORDON. |
| GORDON | What's this? |
| BUTLER | She has lost the man she lov'd —<br>Young Piccolomini, who fell in the battle. |
| GORDON | Unfortunate Lady! |
| BUTLER | You have heard what Illo<br>Reporteth, that the Swedes are conquerors,<br>And marching hitherward. |
| GORDON | Too [[Well\|well]] I heard it. |
| BUTLER | They are twelve regiments strong, and there are five<br>Close by us to protect the Duke. We have<br>Only my single regiment; and the garrison<br>Is not two hundred strong. |
| GORDON | 'Tis even so. |
| BUTLER | It is not possible with such small force<br>To hold in custody a man like him. |
| GORDON | I grant it. |
| BUTLER | Soon the numbers would disarm us.<br>And liberate him. |
| GORDON | It were to be feared. |
| BUTLER | Know, I am warranty for the event;<br>With my head have I pledged myself for his,<br>Must make my word good, cost it what it will,<br>And if alive we cannot hold him prisoner,<br>Why — [[Death\|death]] makes all things certain! |
| GORDON | Butler! What?<br>Do I understand you? Gracious God! You could — |
| BUTLER | He must not live. |
| GORDON | And you can do the deed! |
| BUTLER | Either you or I. This morning was his last. |
| GORDON | You would assassinate him. |
| BUTLER | 'Tis my purpose. |
| GORDON | Who leans with his whole confidence upon you! |
| BUTLER | Such is his evil destiny! |
| GORDON | Your General!<br>The sacred person of your General! |
| BUTLER | My General he has been. |
| GORDON | That 'tis only<br>A 'has been' washes out no villainy.<br>And without judgment passed? |
| BUTLER | The execution<br>Is here instead of judgment. |
| GORDON | This were murder,<br>Not justice. The most guilty should be heard. |
| BUTLER | His [[Guilt\|guilt]] is clear, the Emperor has passed judgment,<br>And we but execute his will. |
| GORDON | We should not<br>Hurry to realize a bloody sentence.<br>A word may be recalled, a life can never be. |
| BUTLER | Dispatch in service pleases sovereigns. |
| GORDON | No honest man's ambitious to press forward<br>To the hangman's service. |
| BUTLER | And no brave man loses<br>His colour at a daring enterprize. |
| GORDON | A brave man hazards life, but not his [[Conscience\|conscience]]. |
| BUTLER | What then? Shall he go forth anew to kindle<br>The unextinguishable flame of war? |
| GORDON | Seize him, and hold him prisoner — do not kill him. |
| BUTLER | Had not the Emperor's army been defeated,<br>I might have done so. — But 'tis now past by. |
| GORDON | O, wherefore opened I the strong hold to him! |
| BUTLER | His destiny and not the place destroys him. |
| GORDON | Upon these ramparts, as beseemed a soldier,<br>I had fallen, defending the Emperor's citadel! |
| BUTLER | Yes! and a thousand gallant men have perished. |
| GORDON | Doing their duty — that adorns the man!<br>But murder's a black deed, and [[Nature\|nature]] curses it. |
| BUTLER | Here is the manifesto which commands us<br>To gain possession of his person. See —<br>It is addressed to you as [[Well\|well]] as me.<br>Are you content to take the consequences,<br>If through our fault he escape to the enemy? |
| GORDON | I? — Gracious God! |
| BUTLER | Take it on yourself.<br>Let come of it what may, on you I lay it. |
| GORDON | O God in heaven! |
| BUTLER | Can you advise aught else<br>Wherewith to execute the Emperor's purpose?<br>Say if you can. For I [[Desire\|desire]] his fall,<br>Not his destruction. |
| GORDON | Merciful heaven! what must be<br>I see as clear as you. Yet still the heart<br>Within my bosom beats with other feelings! |
| BUTLER | Mine is of harder stuff! Necessity<br>In her rough school hath steeled me. And this Illo<br>And Tertsky likewise, they must not survive him. |
| GORDON | I feel no pang for these. Their own bad hearts<br>Impelled them, not the influence of the [[Stars\|stars]].<br>'Twas they who strewed the seeds of evil passions<br>In his calm breast, and with officious villainy<br>Watered and nursed the pois'nous plants. May they<br>Receive their earnests to the uttermost mite! |
| BUTLER | And their [[Death\|death]] shall precede his!<br>We meant to have taken them alive this evening<br>Amid the merry-making of a feast,<br>And kept them prisoners in the citadels.<br>But this makes shorter work. I go this instant<br>To give the necessary orders. |
