| *(didascalia)* | SCENE — A Room fitted up for astrological Labours, and provided with celestial Charts, with Globes, Telescopes, Quadrants, and other mathematical Instruments. — Seven Colossal Figures, representing the Planets, each with a transparent Star of a different Colour on its Head, stand in a Semi-circle in the Back-ground, so that Mars and [[Saturn]] are nearest the Eye. — The remainder of the Scene, and its Disposition, is given in the Fourth Scene of the Second Act. — There must be a Curtain over the Figures, which may be dropped, and conceal them on Occasions. [In the Fifth Scene of this Act it must be dropped; but in the Seventh Scene, it must be again drawn up wholly or in part.] |
| *(didascalia)* | WALLENSTEIN at a black Table, on which a Speculum Astrologicum is described with [[chalk\|Chalk]]. SENI is taking Observations through a [[Window\|window]]. |
| WALLENSTEIN | All [[Well\|well]] — and now let it be ended, Seni. — Come,<br>The [[Dawn\|dawn]] commences, and Mars rules the hour.<br>We must give o'er the operation. Come,<br>We know enough. |
| SENI | Your Highness must permit me<br>Just to contemplate [[Venus]]. She's now rising:<br>Like as a sun, so shines she in the east. |
| WALLENSTEIN | She is at present in her perigee,<br>And shoots down now her strongest influences.<br>[Contemplating the figure on the table.<br>Auspicious aspect! fateful in conjunction,<br>At length the mighty three corradiate;<br>And the two [[Stars\|stars]] of blessing, Jupiter<br>And [[Venus]], take between them the malignant<br>Slily-malicious Mars, and thus compel<br>Into my service that old mischief-founder;<br>For long he viewed me hostilely, and ever<br>With beam oblique, or perpendicular,<br>Now in the Quartile, now in the Secundan,<br>Shot his red lightnings at my [[Stars\|stars]], disturbing<br>Their blessed influences and sweet aspects.<br>Now they have conquered the old enemy,<br>And bring him in the heavens a prisoner to me. |
| SENI | And in a corner [[house]], your Highness — think of that!<br>That makes each influence of [[Double\|double]] strength. |
| WALLENSTEIN | And sun and [[Moon\|moon]], too, in the Sextile aspect,<br>The soft light with the vehement — so I [[Love\|love]] it.<br>Sol is the heart, Luna the head of heaven,<br>Bold be the plan, fiery the execution. |
| SENI | And both the mighty Lumina by no<br>Maleficus affronted. Lo! Saturnus,<br>Innocuous, powerless, in cadente Domo. |
| WALLENSTEIN | The empire of Saturnus is gone by;<br>Lord of the secret birth of things is he;<br>Within the lap of earth, and in the depths<br>Of the [[Imagination\|imagination]] dominates;<br>And his are all things that eschew the light.<br>The [[Time\|time]] is o'er of brooding and contrivance;<br>For Jupiter, the lustrous, lordeth now,<br>And the dark work, complete of preparation,<br>He draws by force into the realm of light.<br>Now must we hasten on to action, ere<br>The scheme, and most auspicious positure<br>Parts o'er my head, and takes once more its flight;<br>For the heavens [[Journey\|journey]] still, and sojourn not. |
| *(didascalia)* | [There are knocks at the door. |
| WALLENSTEIN | There's some one knocking there. See who it is. |
| TERTSKY | (from without) Open, and let me in. |
| WALLENSTEIN | Aye—'tis Tertsky.<br>What is there of such urgence? We are busy. |
| TERTSKY | (from without) Lay all aside at present, I entreat you.<br>It suffers no delaying. |
| WALLENSTEIN | Open, Seni! |
| *(didascalia)* | [While SENI opens the doors for TERTSKY, WALLENSTEIN draws the curtain over the figures. |
| TERTSKY | (enters) Hast thou already heard it? He is taken.<br>Galas has given him up to the Emperor. |
| *(didascalia)* | [SENI draws off the black table, and exit. |
