| WALLENSTEIN | Wallenstein. Is it possible?<br>Is't so? I can no longer what I would?<br>No longer draw back at my liking? I<br>Must do the deed, because I thought of it,<br>And fed this heart here with [[A Dream\|a dream]]? Because<br>I did not scowl [[Temptation\|temptation]] from my presence,<br>Dallied with thoughts of possible fulfilment,<br>Commenced no movement, left all [[Time\|time]] uncertain,<br>And only kept the [[Road\|road]], the access open?<br>By the great God of Heaven! it was not<br>My serious meaning, it was ne'er resolve.<br>I but amused myself with thinking of it.<br>The free-will tempted me, the power to do<br>Or not to do it. — Was it criminal<br>To make the fancy minister to [[Hope\|hope]],<br>To fill the air with pretty toys of air,<br>And clutch fantastic sceptres moving t'ward me?<br>Was not the will kept free? Beheld I not<br>The [[Road\|road]] of duty close beside me — but<br>One little step, and once more I was in it!<br>Where am I? Whither have I been transported?<br>No [[Road\|road]], no track behind me, but a wall,<br>Impenetrable, insurmountable,<br>Rises obedient to the spells I muttered<br>And meant not — my own doings tower behind me.<br>A punishable man I seem, the [[Guilt\|guilt]],<br>Try what I will, I cannot roll off from me;<br>The equivocal demeanour of my life<br>Bears witness on my prosecutor's party;<br>And even my purest acts from purest motives<br>Suspicion poisons with malicious gloss.<br>Were I that thing, for which I pass, that traitor,<br>A goodly outside I had sure reserved,<br>Had drawn the coverings thick and [[Double\|double]] round me,<br>Been calm and chary of my utterance.<br>But being conscious of the [[innocence]]<br>Of my intent, my uncorrupted will,<br>I gave way to my humours, to my passion:<br>Bold were my words, because my deeds were not.<br>Now every planless measure, chance event,<br>The threat of rage, the vaunt of joy and triumph,<br>And all the May-games of a heart o'erflowing,<br>Will they connect, and weave them all together<br>Into one web of treason; all will be plan,<br>My eye ne'er absent from the far-off mark,<br>Step tracing step, each step a politic progress;<br>And out of all they'll fabricate a charge<br>So specious, that I must myself stand dumb.<br>I am caught in my own net, and only force,<br>Naught but a sudden rent can liberate me.<br>How else! since that the heart's unbiass'd instinct<br>Impelled me to the daring deed, which now<br>Necessity, [[Self\|self]]-preservation, orders.<br>Stern is the On-look of Necessity,<br>Not without shudder many a human hand<br>Grasps the mysterious urn of destiny.<br>My deed was mine, remaining in my bosom,<br>Once suffered to escape from its safe corner<br>Within the heart, its nursery and birthplace,<br>Sent forth into the Foreign, it belongs<br>For ever to those sly malicious powers<br>Whom never art of man conciliated.<br>What is thy enterprize? thy aim? thy object?<br>Hast honestly confessed it to thyself?<br>Power seated on a quiet throne thou'dst shake,<br>Power on an ancient consecrated throne,<br>Strong in possession, founded in old custom;<br>Power by a thousand tough and stringy roots<br>Fixed to the people's pious nursery-[[Faith\|faith]].<br>This, this will be no strife of strength with strength.<br>That feared I not. I brave each combatant,<br>Whom I can look on, fixing eye to eye,<br>Who full himself of [[Courage\|courage]] kindles [[Courage\|courage]]<br>In me too. 'Tis a foe invisible,<br>The which I [[Fear\|fear]] — a fearful enemy,<br>Which in the human heart opposes me,<br>By its coward [[Fear\|fear]] [[Alone\|alone]] made fearful to me.<br>Not that, which full of life, instinct with power,<br>Makes known its present being, that is not<br>The true, the perilously formidable.<br>O no! it is the common, the quite common,<br>The thing of an eternal yesterday,<br>What ever was, and evermore returns,<br>Sterling to-morrow, for to-day 'twas sterling!<br>For of the wholly common is man made,<br>And custom is his [[Nurse\|nurse]]! Woe then to them,<br>Who lay irreverent hands upon his old<br>[[house\|House]] furniture, the dear inheritance<br>From his forefathers. For [[Time\|time]] consecrates;<br>And what is grey with age becomes religion.<br>Be in possession, and thou hast the right,<br>And sacred will the many guard it for thee! |
