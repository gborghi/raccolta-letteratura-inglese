| *(didascalia)* | SCENE — A Chamber in PICCOLOMINI'S Dwelling-[[house\|House]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | OCTAVIO PICCOLOMINI, ISOLANI (entering). |
| ISOLANI | Here am I — [[Well]]! who comes yet of the others? |
| OCTAVIO | But, first, a word with you, Count Isolani. |
| ISOLANI | Will it explode, ha? — Is the Duke about<br>To make the attempt? In me, friend, you may place<br>Full confidence. — Nay, put me to the proof. |
| OCTAVIO | That may happen. |
| ISOLANI | Noble brother, I am<br>Not one of those men who in words are valiant,<br>And when it comes to action skulk away.<br>The Duke has acted towards me as a friend.<br>God knows it is so; and I owe him all ——<br>He may rely on my fidelity. |
| OCTAVIO | That will be seen hereafter. |
| ISOLANI | Be on your guard,<br>All think not as I think; and there are many<br>Who still hold with the [[Court]] — yes, and they say<br>That those stolen signatures bind them to nothing. |
| OCTAVIO | I am rejoiced to hear it. |
| ISOLANI | You rejoice! |
| OCTAVIO | That the Emperor has yet such gallant servants<br>And loving friends. |
| ISOLANI | Nay, jeer not, I entreat you.<br>They are no such worthless fellows, I assure you. |
| OCTAVIO | I am assured already. God forbid<br>That I should jest! — In very serious earnest<br>I am rejoiced to see an honest cause<br>So strong. |
| ISOLANI | The Devil! — what! — why, what means this?<br>Are you not, then —— For what, then, am I here? |
| OCTAVIO | That you may make full declaration, whether<br>You will be called the friend or enemy<br>Of the Emperor. |
| ISOLANI | That declaration, friend,<br>I'll make to him in whom a right is placed<br>To put that question to me. |
| OCTAVIO | Whether, Count,<br>That right is mine, this paper may instruct you. |
| ISOLANI | Why, — why — what! This is the Emperor's hand and seal!<br>[Reads.<br>'Whereas the officers collectively<br>Throughout our army will obey the orders<br>Of the Lieutenant-General Piccolomini<br>As from ourselves.' —— Hem! — Yes! so! — Yes! yes! —<br>I — I give you joy, Lieutenant-General! |
| OCTAVIO | And you submit you to the order? |
| ISOLANI | I ——<br>But you have taken me so by surprise —<br>[[Time]] for reflection one must have —— |
| OCTAVIO | Two minutes. |
| ISOLANI | My God! But then the case is —— |
| OCTAVIO | Plain and simple.<br>You must declare you, whether you determine<br>To act a treason 'gainst your Lord and Sovereign,<br>Or whether you will serve him faithfully. |
| ISOLANI | Treason! — My God! — But who talks then of treason? |
| OCTAVIO | That is the case. The Prince-Duke is a traitor —<br>Means to lead over to the enemy<br>The Emperor's army. — Now, Count! — brief and full —<br>Say, will you break your oath to the Emperor?<br>Sell yourself to the enemy? — Say, will you? |
| ISOLANI | What mean you? I — I break my oath, d'ye say,<br>To his Imperial Majesty?<br>Did I say so? — When, when have I said that? |
| OCTAVIO | You have not said it yet — not yet. This instant<br>I wait to hear, Count, whether you will say it. |
| ISOLANI | Aye! that delights me now, that you yourself<br>Bear witness for me that I never said so. |
| OCTAVIO | And you renounce the Duke then? |
| ISOLANI | If he's planning<br>Treason — why, treason breaks all bonds asunder. |
| OCTAVIO | And are determined, too, to fight against him? |
| ISOLANI | He has done me service — but if he's a [[Villain\|villain]],<br>Perdition seize him! — All scores are rubbed off. |
| OCTAVIO | I am rejoiced that you're so [[Well\|well]] disposed.<br>This [[Night\|night]] break off in the utmost secrecy<br>With all the light-armed troops — it must appear<br>As came the order from the Duke himself.<br>At Frauenberg's the place of rendezvous;<br>There will Count Galas give you further orders. |
| ISOLANI | It shall be done. But you'll remember me<br>With the Emperor — how [[Well\|well]] disposed you found me. |
| OCTAVIO | I will not fail to mention it honourably. |
| *(didascalia)* | [Exit ISOLANI. A Servant enters. |
| OCTAVIO | What, Colonel Butler! — Shew him up. |
| ISOLANI | Forgive me too my bearish ways, old father!<br>Lord God! how should I know, then, what a great<br>Person I had before me. |
| OCTAVIO | No excuses! |
| ISOLANI | I am a merry lad, and if at [[Time\|time]]<br>A rash word might escape me 'gainst the [[Court\|court]]<br>Amidst my wine — You know no harm was meant. |
| *(didascalia)* | [Exit. |
| OCTAVIO | You need not be uneasy on that score.<br>That has succeeded. Fortune favour us<br>With all the others only but as much! |
