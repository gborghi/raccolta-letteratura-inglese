| *(didascalia)* | Enter MAX PICCOLOMINI, and some [[Time\|time]] after COUNT TERTSKY, the others remaining as before. |
| COUNTESS | There comes the Paladin who protected us. |
| WALLENSTEIN | Max! Welcome, ever welcome! Always wert thou<br>The morning star of my best joys! |
| MAX | My General —— |
| WALLENSTEIN | 'Till now it was the Emperor who rewarded thee,<br>I but the instrument. This day thou hast bound<br>The father to thee, Max! the fortunate father,<br>And this debt Friedland's [[Self\|self]] must pay. |
| MAX | My prince!<br>You made no common hurry to transfer it.<br>I come with shame: yea, not without a pang!<br>For scarce have I arrived here, scarce delivered<br>The mother and the daughter to your arms,<br>But there is brought to me from your equerry<br>A splendid richly-plated hunting dress<br>So to remunerate me for my troubles ——<br>Yes, yes, remunerate me! Since a trouble<br>It must be, a mere office, not a favour<br>Which I leapt forward to receive, and which<br>I came already with full heart to thank you for.<br>No! 'twas not so intended, that my business<br>Should be my highest best good fortune! |
| *(didascalia)* | [TERTSKY enters, and delivers letters to the DUKE, which he breaks open hurryingly. |
| COUNTESS | Remunerate your trouble! For his joy<br>He makes you recompense. 'Tis not unfitting<br>For you, Count Piccolomini, to feel<br>So tenderly — my brother it beseems<br>To shew himself for ever great and princely. |
| THEKLA | Then I too must have scruples of his [[Love\|love]]:<br>For his munificent hands did ornament me<br>Ere yet the father's heart had spoken to me. |
| MAX | Yes; 'tis his [[Nature\|nature]] ever to be giving<br>And making happy.<br>How my heart pours out<br>Its all of thanks to him: O! how I seem<br>To utter all things in the dear name Friedland.<br>While I shall live, so long will I remain<br>The captive of this name: in it shall bloom<br>My every fortune, every lovely [[Hope\|hope]].<br>Inextricably as in some [[Magic\|magic]] ring<br>In this name hath my destiny charm-bound me! |
| COUNTESS | My brother wishes us to leave him. Come. |
| WALLENSTEIN | Once more I bid thee welcome to the camp,<br>Thou art the hostess of this [[Court\|court]]. You, Max,<br>Will now again administer your old office,<br>While we perform the sovereign's business here. |
| *(didascalia)* | [MAX PICCOLOMINI offers the DUCHESS his arm, the COUNTESS accompanies the PRINCESS. |
| TERTSKY | Max, we depend on seeing you at the meeting. |
| *(didascalia)* | [He grasps the hand of the DUCHESS with still increasing warmth. |
