|---|---|
| *(didascalia)* | WALLENSTEIN, DUCHESS. |
| WALLENSTEIN | You went then through Vienna, were presented<br>To the [[Queen]] of Hungary? |
| DUCHESS | Yes, and to the Empress too,<br>And by both Majesties were we admitted<br>To kiss the hand. |
| WALLENSTEIN | And how was it received,<br>That I had sent for wife and daughter hither<br>To the camp, in [[Winter\|winter]] [[Time\|time]]? |
| DUCHESS | I did even that<br>Which you commissioned me to do. I told them,<br>You had determined on our daughter's [[Marriage\|marriage]],<br>And wished, ere yet you went into the field,<br>To shew the elected husband his betrothed. |
| WALLENSTEIN | And did they guess the choice which I had made? |
| DUCHESS | They only hoped and wished it may have fallen<br>Upon no foreign nor yet Lutheran noble. |
| WALLENSTEIN | And you — what do you wish, Elizabeth? |
| DUCHESS | Your will, you know, was always mine. |
| WALLENSTEIN | [[Well]], then?<br>And in all else, of what kind and complexion<br>Was your reception at the [[Court\|court]]?<br>Hide nothing from me. How were you received? |
| DUCHESS | O! my dear lord, all is not what it was.<br>A cankerworm, my lord, a cankerworm<br>Has stolen into the bud. |
| WALLENSTEIN | Ay! is it so!<br>What, they were lax? they failed of the old respect? |
| DUCHESS | Not of respect. No Honours were omitted,<br>No outward courtesy; but in the place<br>Of condescending, confidential kindness,<br>Familiar and endearing, there were given me<br>Only these honours and that solemn courtesy.<br>Ah! and the tenderness which was put on,<br>It was the guise of pity, not of favour.<br>No! Albrecht's wife, Duke Albrecht's princely wife,<br>Count Harrach's noble daughter, should not so —<br>Not wholly so should she have been received. |
| WALLENSTEIN | Yes, yes; they have ta'en offence. My latest conduct,<br>They railed at it, no [[Doubt\|doubt]]. |
| DUCHESS | O that they had!<br>I have been long accustomed to defend you,<br>To heal and pacify distempered spirits.<br>No; no one railed at you. They wrapped them up,<br>O Heaven! in such oppressive, solemn [[Silence\|silence]]! —<br>Here is no every-day misunderstanding,<br>No transient pique, no cloud that passes over;<br>Something most luckless, most unhealable,<br>Has taken place. The [[Queen]] of Hungary<br>Used formerly to call me her dear aunt,<br>And ever at departure to embrace me — |
| WALLENSTEIN | Now she omitted it? |
| DUCHESS | She did embrace me,<br>But then first when I had already taken<br>My formal leave, and when the door already<br>Had closed upon me, then did she come out<br>In haste, as she had suddenly bethought herself,<br>And pressed me to her bosom, more with anguish<br>Than tenderness. |
| *(didascalia)* | *(Wallenstein seizes her hand soothingly.)* |
| WALLENSTEIN | Nay, now collect yourself,<br>And what of Eggenberg and Lichtenstein,<br>And of our other friends there? |
| DUCHESS | I saw none. |
| WALLENSTEIN | The Ambassador from Spain, who once was wont<br>To plead so warmly for me? — |
| DUCHESS | Silent, Silent! |
| WALLENSTEIN | These suns then are eclipsed for us. Henceforward<br>Must we roll on, our own [[Fire\|fire]], our own light. |
| DUCHESS | And were it — were it, my dear lord, in that<br>Which moved about the [[Court\|court]] in buzz and whisper,<br>But in the country let itself be heard<br>Aloud — in that which Father Lamormain<br>In sundry hints and —— |
| WALLENSTEIN | Lamormain! what said he? |
| DUCHESS | That you're accused of having daringly<br>O'erstepped the powers entrusted to you, charged<br>With traitorous contempt of the Emperor<br>And his supreme behests. The proud Bavarian,<br>He and the Spaniards stand up your accusers —<br>That there's a [[Storm\|storm]] collecting over you<br>Of far more fearful menace than that former one<br>Which whirled you headlong down at Regensburg.<br>And people talk, said he, of —— Ah! — |
| *(didascalia)* | *(Stifling extreme emotion.)* |
| WALLENSTEIN | Proceed! |
| DUCHESS | I cannot utter it! |
| WALLENSTEIN | Proceed! |
| DUCHESS | They talk —— |
| WALLENSTEIN | [[Well]]! |
| DUCHESS | Of a second —— |
| WALLENSTEIN | Second —— |
| DUCHESS | More disgraceful —— Dismission. |
| *(didascalia)* | *(Wallenstein strides across the chamber in vehement agitation.)* |
| WALLENSTEIN | Talk they?<br>O! they force, they thrust me<br>With violence, against my own will, onward! |
| DUCHESS | O! if there yet be [[Time\|time]], my husband! if<br>By giving way and by submission, this<br>Can be averted — my dear lord, give way!<br>Win down your proud heart to it! Tell that heart<br>It is your sovereign lord, your Emperor<br>Before whom you retreat. O let no longer<br>Low tricking malice blacken your good meaning<br>With abhorred venomous glosses. Stand you up<br>Shielded and helm'd and weapon'd with the [[Truth\|truth]],<br>And drive before you into uttermost shame<br>These slanderous liars! Few firm friends have we —<br>You know it! — The swift growth of our good fortune<br>It hath but set us up, a mark for hatred.<br>What are we, if the sovereign's grace and favour<br>Stand not before us? |
