| Chi parla | Battuta |
|---|---|
| *(didascalia)* | QUESTENBERG, OCTAVIO PICCOLOMINI. |
| QUESTENBERG | Alas, alas! and stands it so?<br>What, friend! and do we let him go away<br>In this delusion — let him go away?<br>Not call him back immediately, not open<br>His eyes upon the spot? |
| OCTAVIO | He has now opened mine,<br>And I see more than pleases me. |
| QUESTENBERG | What is it? |
| OCTAVIO | Curse on this [[Journey\|journey]]! |
| QUESTENBERG | But why so? What is it? |
| OCTAVIO | Come, come along, friend! I must follow up<br>The ominous track immediately. Mine eyes<br>Are opened now, and I must use them. Come! |
| *(didascalia)* | [Draws QUESTENBERG on with him. |
| QUESTENBERG | What now? Where go you then? |
| OCTAVIO | To her herself. |
| QUESTENBERG | To —— |
| OCTAVIO | To the Duke. Come, let us go—'Tis done, 'tis done,<br>I see the net that is thrown over him.<br>O! he returns not to me as he went. |
| QUESTENBERG | Nay, but explain yourself. |
| OCTAVIO | And that I should not<br>Foresee it, not prevent this [[Journey\|journey]]! Wherefore<br>Did I keep it from him? — You were in the right.<br>I should have warned him! Now it is too late. |
| QUESTENBERG | But what's too late? Bethink yourself, my friend,<br>That you are talking absolute riddles to me. |
| OCTAVIO | Come! — to the Duke's. 'Tis close upon the hour<br>Which he appointed you for audience. Come!<br>A curse, a threefold curse, upon this [[Journey\|journey]]! |
| *(didascalia)* | [He leads QUESTENBERG off. |
