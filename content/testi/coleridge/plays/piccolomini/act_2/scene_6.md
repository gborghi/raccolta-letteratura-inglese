| Chi parla | Battuta |
|---|---|
| *(didascalia)* | THEKLA and MAX PICCOLOMINI. |
| *(didascalia)* | Thekla (as soon as the Countess is out of sight, in a quick low voice to Piccolomini). |
| THEKLA | Don't trust them! They are false! |
| MAX | Impossible! |
| THEKLA | Trust no one here but me. I saw at once,<br>They had a purpose. |
| MAX | Purpose! but what purpose?<br>And how can we be instrumental to it? |
| THEKLA | I know no more than you; but yet believe me:<br>There's some design in this! to make us happy,<br>To realize our [[Union\|union]] — trust me, [[Love\|love]]!<br>They but pretend to wish it. |
| MAX | But these Tertskys ——<br>Why use we them at all? Why not your mother?<br>Excellent creature! she deserves from us<br>A full and filial confidence. |
| THEKLA | She doth [[Love\|love]] you,<br>Doth rate you high before all others — but —<br>But such a secret — she would never have<br>The [[Courage\|courage]] to conceal it from my father.<br>For her own peace of mind we must preserve it<br>A secret from her too. |
| MAX | Why any secret?<br>I [[Love\|love]] not [[Secrets\|secrets]]. Mark, what I will do.<br>I'll throw me at your father's feet — let him<br>Decide upon my fortunes! — He is true,<br>He wears no mask — he hates all crooked ways —<br>He is so good, so noble! |
| THEKLA | That are you! |
| *(didascalia)* | (falls on his neck) |
| MAX | You knew him only since this morn; but I<br>Have liv'd ten years already in his presence,<br>And who knows whether in this very moment<br>He is not merely waiting for us both<br>To own our loves, in order to unite us.<br>You are silent! ——<br>You look at me with such a hopelessness!<br>What have you to object against your father? |
| THEKLA | I? Nothing. Only he's so occupied —<br>He has no leisure [[Time\|time]] to think about<br>The happiness of us two. |
| *(didascalia)* | [Taking his hand tenderly.] |
| THEKLA | Follow me!<br>Let us not place too great a [[Faith\|faith]] in men.<br>These Tertskys — we will still be grateful to them<br>For every kindness, but not trust them further<br>Than they deserve; — and in all else rely ——<br>On our own hearts! |
| MAX | O! shall we e'er be happy? |
| THEKLA | Are we not happy now? Art thou not mine?<br>Am I not thine? There lives within my [[Soul\|soul]]<br>A lofty [[Courage\|courage]]—'tis [[Love\|love]] gives it me!<br>I ought to be less open — ought to hide<br>My heart more from thee — so decorum dictates:<br>But where in this place could'st thou seek for [[Truth\|truth]],<br>If in my mouth thou did'st not find it? |
