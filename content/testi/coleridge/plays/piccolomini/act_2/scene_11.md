| Chi parla | Battuta |
|---|---|
| *(didascalia)* | ILLO (comes out from the second chamber), TERTSKY. |
| ILLO | How goes it with young Piccolomini? |
| TERTSKY | All right, I think. He has started no objection. |
| ILLO | He is the only one I [[Fear\|fear]] about —<br>He and his father. Have an eye on both! |
| TERTSKY | How looks it at your table: you forget not<br>To keep them warm and stirring? |
| ILLO | O, quite cordial,<br>They are quite cordial in the scheme. We have them.<br>And 'tis as I predicted too. Already<br>It is the talk, not merely to maintain<br>The Duke in station. 'Since we're once for all<br>Together and unanimous, why not,'<br>Says Montecuculi, 'aye, why not onward,<br>And make conditions with the Emperor<br>There in his own Vienna?' Trust me, Count,<br>Were it not for these said Piccolomini,<br>We might have spared ourselves the cheat. |
| TERTSKY | And Butler? |
| ILLO | How goes it there? Hush! |
