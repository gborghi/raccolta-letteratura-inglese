| *(didascalia)* | A large Saloon lighted up with festal Splendour; in the midst of it, and in the Centre of the Stage, a Table richly set out, at which eight Generals are sitting, among whom are OCTAVIO PICCOLOMINI, TERTSKY, and MARADAS. Right and left of this, but farther back, two other Tables, at each of which six Persons are placed. The Middle Door, which is standing open, gives to the Prospect a Fourth Table, with the same Number of Persons. More forward stands the sideboard. The whole front of the Stage is kept open for the Pages and Servants in waiting. All is in Motion. The Band of Music belonging to Tertsky's Regiment march across the Stage, and draw up round the Tables. Before they are quite off from the Front of the Stage, MAX PICCOLOMINI appears, TERTSKY advances towards him with a Paper, ISOLANI comes up to meet him with a Beaker or Service-cup. |
| *(didascalia)* | TERTSKY, ISOLANI, MAX PICCOLOMINI. |
| ISOLANI | Here brother, what we [[Love\|love]]! Why, where hast been?<br>Off to thy place — quick! Tertsky here has given<br>The mother's holiday wine up to free booty.<br>Here it goes on as at the Heidelberg [[Castle\|castle]].<br>Already hast thou lost the best. They're giving<br>At yonder table ducal crowns in shares;<br>There's Sternberg's lands and chattels are put up,<br>With Egenberg's, Stawata's, Lichtenstein's,<br>And all the great Bohemian feodalities.<br>Be nimble, lad! and something may turn up<br>For thee — who knows? off — to thy place! quick! march! |
| *(didascalia)* | Tiefenbach and Goetz (call out from the second and third tables). |
| TIEFENBACH AND GOETZ | Count Piccolomini! |
| TERTSKY | Stop, ye shall have him in an instant. — Read<br>This oath here, whether as 'tis here set forth,<br>The wording satisfies you. They've all read it,<br>Each in his turn, and each one will subscribe<br>His individual signature. |
| MAX *(reads)* | 'Ingratis servire nefas.' |
| ISOLANI | That sounds to my ears very much like Latin,<br>And being interpreted, pray what may't mean? |
| TERTSKY | No honest man will serve a thankless master. |
| MAX | 'Inasmuch as our supreme Commander, the illustrious<br>Duke of Friedland, in consequence of the manifold affronts and<br>grievances which he has received, had expressed his determination<br>to quit the Emperor, but on our unanimous entreaty has<br>graciously consented to remain still with the army, and not to<br>part from us without our approbation thereof, so we, collectively<br>and each in particular, in the stead of an oath personally taken,<br>do hereby oblige ourselves — likewise by him honourably and<br>faithfully to hold, and in nowise whatsoever from him to<br>part, and to be ready to shed for his interests the last drop of<br>our [[Blood\|blood]], so far, namely, as our oath to the Emperor will permit<br>it. (These last words are repeated by ISOLANI.) In testimony of<br>which we subscribe our names.' |
| TERTSKY | Now! — are you willing to subscribe this paper? |
| ISOLANI | Why should he not? All officers of [[Honour\|honour]]<br>Can do it, aye, must do it. — Pen and ink here! |
| TERTSKY | Nay, let it rest till after meal. |
| ISOLANI *(drawing Max along)* | Come, Max. |
| *(didascalia)* | Both seat themselves at their table. |
