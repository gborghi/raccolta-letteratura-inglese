| BARRERE | The tempest gathers — be it mine to seek<br>A friendly shelter, ere it bursts upon him.<br>But where? and how? I [[Fear\|fear]] the [[Tyrant]]'s [[Soul\|soul]] —<br>Sudden in action, fertile in resource,<br>And rising awful 'mid impending ruins;<br>In splendor gloomy, as the midnight meteor,<br>That fearless thwarts the elemental war.<br>When last in secret conference we met,<br>He scowl'd upon me with suspicious rage,<br>Making his eye the inmate of my bosom.<br>I know he scorns me — and I feel, I hate him —<br>Yet there is in him that which makes me tremble! |
| *(didascalia)* | [Exit. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter TALLIEN and LEGENDRE. |
| TALLIEN | It was Barrere, Legendre! didst thou mark him?<br>Abrupt he turn'd, yet linger'd as he went,<br>And towards us cast a look of doubtful meaning. |
| LEGENDRE | I mark'd him [[Well\|well]]. I met his eye's last glance;<br>It menac'd not so proudly as of yore.<br>Methought he would have spoke — but that he dar'd not —<br>Such agitation darken'd on his brow. |
| TALLIEN | 'Twas all-distrusting [[Guilt\|guilt]] that kept from bursting<br>Th' imprison'd secret struggling in the face:<br>E'en as the sudden breeze upstarting onwards<br>Hurries the thundercloud, that pois'd awhile<br>Hung in mid air, red with its mutinous burthen. |
| LEGENDRE | Perfidious Traitor! — still afraid to bask<br>In the full blaze of power, the rustling serpent<br>Lurks in the thicket of the [[Tyrant]]'s [[Greatness\|greatness]],<br>Ever prepared to sting who shelters him.<br>Each thought, each action in himself converges;<br>And [[Love\|love]] and [[Friendship\|friendship]] on his coward heart<br>Shine like the powerless sun on polar ice;<br>To all attach'd, by turns deserting all,<br>Cunning and dark — a necessary [[Villain\|villain]]! |
| TALLIEN | Yet much depends upon him — [[Well\|well]] you know<br>With plausible harangue 'tis his to paint<br>Defeat like victory — and blind the mob<br>With [[Truth\|truth]]-mix'd falsehood. They led on by him,<br>And wild of head to work their own destruction,<br>Support with uproar what he plans in darkness. |
| LEGENDRE | O what a precious name is Liberty<br>To scare or cheat the simple into slaves!<br>Yes — we must gain him over: by dark hints<br>We'll shew enough to rouse his watchful fears,<br>Till the cold coward blaze a patriot.<br>O Danton! murder'd friend! assist my counsels —<br>Hover around me on sad [[Memory]]'s wings,<br>And pour thy daring vengeance in my heart.<br>Tallien! if but to-morrow's fateful sun<br>Beholds the [[Tyrant]] living — we are dead! |
| TALLIEN | Yet his keen eye that flashes mighty meanings — |
| LEGENDRE | [[Fear]] not — or rather [[Fear\|fear]] th' alternative,<br>And seek for [[Courage\|courage]] e'en in cowardice —<br>But see — hither he comes — let us away!<br>His brother with him, and the bloody Couthon,<br>And high of haughty spirit, young St. Just. |
| *(didascalia)* | [Exeunt. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter ROBESPIERRE, COUTHON, ST. JUST, and ROBESPIERRE JUNIOR. |
| ROBESPIERRE | What? did La Fayette fall before my power?<br>And did I conquer Roland's spotless virtues?<br>The fervent eloquence of Vergniaud's tongue?<br>And Brissot's thoughtful [[Soul\|soul]] unbribed and bold?<br>Did zealot armies haste in vain to save them?<br>What! did th' assassin's dagger aim its point<br>Vain, as [[A Dream\|a dream]] of murder, at my bosom?<br>And shall I dread the soft luxurious Tallien?<br>Th' [[Adonis]] Tallien? banquet-hunting Tallien?<br>Him, whose heart flutters at the dice-box? Him,<br>Who ever on the harlots' downy pillow<br>Resigns his head impure to feverish slumbers! |
| ST. JUST | I cannot [[Fear\|fear]] him — yet we must not scorn him.<br>Was it not Antony that conquer'd [[Brutus]],<br>Th' [[Adonis]], banquet-hunting Antony?<br>The state is not yet purified: and though<br>The stream runs clear, yet at the [[Bottom\|bottom]] lies<br>The thick black sediment of all the factions —<br>It needs no [[Magic\|magic]] hand to stir it up! |
| COUTHON | O we did wrong to spare them — fatal error!<br>Why lived Legendre, when that Danton died?<br>And Collot d'Herbois dangerous in crimes?<br>I've [[Fear\|fear]]'d him, since his iron heart endured<br>To make of Lyons one vast human shambles,<br>Compar'd with which the sun-scorcht wilderness<br>Of Zara were a smiling paradise. |
| ST. JUST | Rightly thou judgest, Couthon! He is one<br>Who flies from silent solitary anguish,<br>Seeking forgetful peace amid the jar<br>Of elements. The howl of maniac uproar<br>Lulls to sad [[Sleep\|sleep]] the [[Memory\|memory]] of himself.<br>A calm is fatal to him — then he feels<br>The dire upboilings of the [[Storm\|storm]] within him.<br>A tiger mad with inward wounds! — I dread<br>The fierce and restless turbulence of [[Guilt\|guilt]]. |
| ROBESPIERRE | Is not the Commune ours? The stern tribunal?<br>Dumas? and Vivier? Fleuriot? and Louvet?<br>And Henriot? We'll denounce an hundred, nor<br>Shall they behold to-morrow's sun roll westward. |
| ROBESPIERRE JUNIOR | Nay — I am sick of [[Blood\|blood]]; my aching heart<br>Reviews the long, long train of hideous horrors<br>That still have gloom'd the rise of the Republic.<br>I should have died before Toulon, when war<br>Became the patriot! |
| ROBESPIERRE | Most unworthy wish!<br>He, whose heart sickens at the [[Blood\|blood]] of traitors,<br>Would be himself a traitor, were he not<br>A coward! 'Tis congenial souls [[Alone\|alone]]<br>Shed [[Tears\|tears]] of sorrow for each other's fate.<br>O thou art brave, my brother! and thine eye<br>Full firmly shines amid the groaning battle —<br>Yet in thine heart the woman-form of pity<br>Asserts too large a share, an ill-timed guest!<br>There is unsoundness in the state — To-morrow<br>Shall see it cleans'd by wholesome massacre! |
| ROBESPIERRE JUNIOR | Beware! already do the sections murmur —<br>'O the great glorious patriot, Robespierre —<br>The [[Tyrant\|tyrant]] guardian of the country's [[Freedom\|freedom]]!' |
| COUTHON | 'Twere folly sure to work great deeds by halves!<br>Much I suspect the darksome fickle heart<br>Of cold Barrere! |
| ROBESPIERRE | I see the [[Villain\|villain]] in him! |
| ROBESPIERRE JUNIOR | If he — if all forsake thee — what remains? |
| ROBESPIERRE | Myself! the steel-strong Rectitude of [[Soul\|soul]]<br>And [[Poverty]] [[Sublime\|sublime]] 'mid circling virtues!<br>The giant Victories my counsels form'd<br>Shall stalk around me with sun-glittering plumes,<br>Bidding the darts of calumny fall pointless. |
| *(didascalia)* | [Exeunt caeteri. Manet COUTHON. |
| COUTHON | So we deceive ourselves! What goodly virtues<br>Bloom on the poisonous branches of [[Ambition\|ambition]]!<br>Still, Robespierre! thou'lt guard thy country's [[Freedom\|freedom]]<br>To despotize in all the patriot's pomp.<br>While [[Conscience]], 'mid the mob's applauding clamours,<br>Sleeps in thine ear, nor whispers — [[Blood\|blood]]-stain'd [[Tyrant\|tyrant]]!<br>Yet what is [[Conscience]]? Superstition's dream,<br>Making such deep impression on our [[Sleep\|sleep]] —<br>That long th' awakened breast retains its horrors!<br>But he returns — and with him comes Barrere. |
| *(didascalia)* | [Exit COUTHON. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter ROBESPIERRE and BARRERE. |
| ROBESPIERRE | There is no danger but in cowardice. —<br>Barrere! we make the danger, when we [[Fear\|fear]] it.<br>We have such force without, as will suspend<br>The cold and trembling treachery of these members. |
| BARRERE | 'Twill be a pause of terror. — |
| ROBESPIERRE | But to whom?<br>Rather the short-lived slumber of the tempest,<br>Gathering its strength anew. The dastard traitors!<br>Moles, that would undermine the rooted oak!<br>A pause! — a moment's pause?—'Tis all their life. |
| BARRERE | Yet much they talk — and plausible their speech.<br>Couthon's decree has given such powers, that — |
| ROBESPIERRE | That what? |
| BARRERE | The [[Freedom\|freedom]] of [[debate]] — |
| ROBESPIERRE | Transparent mask!<br>They wish to clog the wheels of [[government]],<br>Forcing the hand that guides the vast [[Machine\|machine]]<br>To bribe them to their duty — English patriots!<br>Are not the congregated clouds of war<br>Black all around us? In our very vitals<br>Works not the [[King\|king]]-bred [[Poison\|poison]] of rebellion?<br>Say, what shall counteract the selfish plottings<br>Of wretches, cold of heart, nor awed by fears<br>Of him, whose power directs th' eternal justice?<br>Terror? or secret-sapping gold? The first<br>Heavy, but transient as the ills that cause it;<br>And to the virtuous patriot rendered light<br>By the necessities that gave it birth:<br>The other fouls the fount of the republic,<br>Making it flow polluted to all ages:<br>Inoculates the state with a slow venom,<br>That once imbibed, must be continued ever.<br>Myself incorruptible I ne'er could bribe them —<br>Therefore they hate me. |
| BARRERE | Are the sections friendly? |
| ROBESPIERRE | There are who wish my ruin — but I'll make them<br>Blush for the crime in [[Blood\|blood]]! |
| BARRERE | Nay — but I tell thee,<br>Thou art too fond of slaughter — and the right<br>(If right it be) workest by most foul means! |
| ROBESPIERRE | [[Self]]-centering [[Fear]]! how [[Well\|well]] thou canst ape<br>[[Mercy]]!<br>Too fond of slaughter! — matchless hypocrite!<br>Thought Barrere so, when Brissot, Danton died?<br>Thought Barrere so, when through the streaming streets<br>Of [[Paris]] red-eyed Massacre o'erwearied<br>Reel'd heavily, intoxicate with [[Blood\|blood]]?<br>And when (O heavens!) in Lyons' [[Death\|death]]-red square<br>Sick Fancy groan'd o'er putrid hills of slain,<br>Didst thou not fiercely laugh, and bless the day?<br>Why, thou hast been the mouth-piece of all horrors,<br>And, like a [[Blood\|blood]]-hound, crouch'd for murder! Now<br>Aloof thou standest from the tottering pillar,<br>Or, like a frighted [[Child\|child]] behind its mother,<br>Hidest thy pale face in the skirts of — [[Mercy]]! |
| BARRERE | O prodigality of eloquent anger!<br>Why now I see thou'rt weak — thy case is desperate!<br>The cool ferocious Robespierre turn'd scolder! |
| ROBESPIERRE | Who from a bad man's bosom wards the blow<br>Reserves the whetted dagger for his own.<br>Denounced twice — and twice I saved his life! |
| *(didascalia)* | [Exit. |
| BARRERE | The sections will support them — there's the point!<br>No! he can never weather out the [[Storm\|storm]] —<br>Yet he is sudden in [[Revenge\|revenge]] — No more!<br>I must away to Tallien. |
| *(didascalia)* | [Exit. |
| *(didascalia)* | SCENE changes to the [[house]] of ADELAIDE. |
| *(didascalia)* | ADELAIDE enters, speaking to a Servant. |
| ADELAIDE | Didst thou present the letter that I gave thee?<br>Did Tallien answer, he would soon return? |
| SERVANT | He is in the Thuilleries — with him Legendre —<br>In deep discourse they seem'd: as I approach'd<br>He waved his hand as bidding me retire:<br>I did not interrupt him. |
| *(didascalia)* | [Returns the letter. |
| ADELAIDE | Thou didst rightly. |
| *(didascalia)* | [Exit Servant. |
| ADELAIDE | O this new [[Freedom\|freedom]]! at how dear a price<br>We've bought the seeming good! The peaceful virtues<br>And every blandishment of private life,<br>The father's cares, the mother's fond endearment,<br>All sacrificed to liberty's wild riot.<br>The wingéd hours, that scatter'd roses round me,<br>Languid and sad drag their slow course along,<br>And shake big gall-drops from their heavy wings.<br>But I will steal away these anxious thoughts<br>By the soft languishment of warbled airs,<br>If haply melodies may lull the sense<br>Of sorrow for a while. |
| *(didascalia)* | [Soft music. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter TALLIEN. |
| TALLIEN | Music, my [[Love\|love]]? O breathe again that air!<br>Soft [[Nurse\|nurse]] of [[Pain\|pain]], it sooths the weary [[Soul\|soul]]<br>Of care, sweet as the whisper'd breeze of evening<br>That plays around the sick man's throbbing temples. |
| TALLIEN | [[Song\|SONG]]<br>Tell me, on what holy ground<br>May domestic peace be found?<br>Halcyon daughter of the skies,<br>Far on fearful wing she flies,<br>From the pomp of scepter'd state,<br>From the [[Rebel\|rebel]]'s noisy hate.<br>In a cottag'd vale she dwells<br>List'ning to the Sabbath [[Bells\|bells]]!<br>Still around her steps are seen,<br>Spotless honor's meeker mien,<br>[[Love]], the sire of pleasing fears,<br>Sorrow smiling through her [[Tears\|tears]],<br>And conscious of the past employ,<br>[[Memory]], bosom-[[spring]] of joy. |
| TALLIEN | I thank thee, Adelaide! 'twas sweet, though mournful.<br>But why thy brow o'ercast, thy cheek so wan?<br>Thou look'st as a lorn maid beside some stream<br>That sighs away the [[Soul\|soul]] in fond despairing,<br>While sorrow sad, like the dank willow near her,<br>Hangs o'er the troubled fountain of her eye. |
| ADELAIDE | Ah! rather let me ask what mystery lowers<br>On Tallien's darken'd brow. Thou dost me wrong —<br>Thy [[Soul\|soul]] distemper'd, can my heart be tranquil? |
| TALLIEN | Tell me, by whom thy brother's [[Blood\|blood]] was spilt?<br>Asks he not vengeance on these patriot murderers?<br>It has been borne too tamely. Fears and curses<br>Groan on our midnight beds, and e'en our [[Dreams\|dreams]]<br>Threaten the assassin hand of Robespierre.<br>He dies! — nor has the plot escaped his fears. |
| ADELAIDE | Yet — yet — be cautious! much I [[Fear\|fear]] the Commune —<br>The [[Tyrant\|tyrant]]'s creatures, and their fate with his<br>Fast link'd in close indissoluble [[Union\|union]].<br>The pale Convention — |
| TALLIEN | Hate him as they [[Fear\|fear]] him,<br>Impatient of the chain, resolv'd and ready. |
| ADELAIDE | Th' enthusiast mob, confusion's lawless sons — |
| TALLIEN | They are aweary of his stern [[morality]],<br>The fair-mask'd offspring of ferocious [[Pride\|pride]].<br>The sections too support the delegates:<br>All — all is ours! e'en now the vital air<br>Of Liberty, condens'd awhile, is bursting<br>(Force irresistible!) from its compressure —<br>To shatter the arch chemist in the explosion! |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter BILLAUD VARENNES and BOURDON L'OISE. |
| *(didascalia)* | [ADELAIDE retires. |
| BOURDON L'OISE | Tallien! was this a [[Time\|time]] for amorous conference?<br>Henriot, the [[Tyrant\|tyrant]]'s most devoted creature,<br>Marshals the force of [[Paris]]: The fierce Club,<br>With Vivier at their head, in loud acclaim<br>Have sworn to make the guillotine in [[Blood\|blood]]<br>Float on the scaffold. — But who comes here? |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter BARRERE abruptly. |
| BARRERE | Say, are ye friends to [[Freedom\|freedom]]? I am her's!<br>Let us, forgetful of all common feuds,<br>Rally around her shrine! E'en now the [[Tyrant\|tyrant]]<br>Concerts a plan of instant massacre! |
| BILLAUD VARENNES | Away to the Convention! with that voice<br>So oft the herald of glad victory,<br>Rouse their fallen spirits, thunder in their ears<br>The names of [[Tyrant\|tyrant]], plunderer, assassin!<br>The violent workings of my [[Soul\|soul]] within<br>Anticipate the [[Monster\|monster]]'s [[Blood\|blood]]! |
| *(didascalia)* | [Cry from the street of — No [[Tyrant]]! Down with the [[Tyrant]]! |
| TALLIEN | Hear ye that outcry? — If the trembling members<br>Even for a moment hold his fate suspended,<br>I swear by the holy poniard, that stabbed Caesar,<br>This dagger probes his heart! |
| *(didascalia)* | [Exeunt omnes. |
