| *(didascalia)* | A savage wood. At one side a cavern, overhung with ivy. ZAPOLYA and RAAB KIUPRILI discovered: both, but especially the latter, in rude and savage garments. |
| RAAB KIUPRILI | Heard you then aught while I was slumbering? |
| ZAPOLYA | Nothing.<br>Only your face became convulsed. We miserable!<br>Is heaven’s last [[Mercy|mercy]] fled? Is [[Sleep|sleep]] grown treacherous? |
| RAAB KIUPRILI | O for a [[Sleep|sleep]], for [[Sleep|sleep]] itself to rest in!<br>I dream’d I had met with food beneath a tree,<br>And I was seeking you, when all at once<br>My feet became entangled in a net:<br>Still more entangled as in rage I tore it.<br>At length I freed myself, had sight of you,<br>But as I hastened eagerly, again<br>I found my frame encumbered: a huge serpent<br>Twined round my chest, but tightest round my throat. |
| ZAPOLYA | Alas! ‘twas lack of food: for hunger chokes! |
| RAAB KIUPRILI | And now I saw you by a shrivelled [[Child|child]]<br>Strangely pursued. You did not fly, yet neither<br>Touched you the ground, methought, but close above it<br>Did seem to shoot yourself along the air,<br>And as you passed me, turned your face and shrieked. |
| ZAPOLYA | I did in [[Truth|truth]] send forth a feeble shriek,<br>Scarce knowing why. Perhaps the mock’d sense craved<br>To hear the scream, which you but seemed to utter.<br>For your whole face looked like a mask of torture!<br>Yet a [[Child|child]]’s image doth indeed pursue me<br>Shrivelled with toil and penury! |
| RAAB KIUPRILI | Nay! what ails you? |
| ZAPOLYA | A wondrous faintness there comes stealing o’er me.<br>Is it [[Death]]’s lengthening [[Shadow|shadow]], who comes onward,<br>Life’s setting sun behind him? |
| RAAB KIUPRILI | Cheerly! The dusk<br>Will quickly shroud us. Ere the [[Moon|moon]] be up,<br>Trust me I’ll bring thee food! |
| ZAPOLYA | Hunger’s tooth has<br>Gnawn itself blunt. O, I could [[Queen|queen]] it [[Well|well]]<br>O’er my own sorrows as my rightful subjects.<br>But wherefore, O revered Kiuprili! wherefore<br>Did my importunate prayers, my hopes and fancies,<br>Force thee from thy secure though sad retreat?<br>Would that my tongue had then cloven to my mouth!<br>But Heaven is just! With [[Tears|tears]] I conquered thee,<br>And not a tear is left me to repent with!<br>Had’st thou not done already — had’st thou not<br>Suffered — oh, more than e’er man feigned of [[Friendship|friendship]]? |
| RAAB KIUPRILI | Yet be thou comforted! What! had’st thou [[Faith|faith]]<br>When I turned back incredulous? ‘Twas thy light<br>That kindled mine. And shall it now go out,<br>And leave thy [[Soul|soul]] in darkness? Yet look up,<br>And think thou see’st thy sainted lord commissioned<br>And on his way to aid us! Whence those late [[Dreams|dreams]],<br>Which after such long interval of hopeless<br>And silent [[Resignation|resignation]] all at once<br>[[Night]] after [[Night|night]] commanded thy return<br>Hither? and still presented in clear vision<br>This wood as in a scene? this very cavern?<br>Thou darest not [[Doubt|doubt]] that Heaven’s especial hand<br>Worked in those signs. The hour of thy deliverance<br>Is on the stroke: — for misery can not add<br>Grief to thy griefs, or patience to thy sufferance! |
| ZAPOLYA | Can not! Oh, what if thou wert taken from me?<br>Nay, thou said’st [[Well|well]]: for that and [[Death|death]] were one.<br>Life’s grief is at its height indeed; the hard<br>Necessity of this inhuman state<br>Hath made our deeds inhuman as our vestments.<br>Housed in this wild wood, with wild usages,<br>Danger our guest, and famine at our portal —<br>Wolf-like to prowl in the shepherd’s fold by [[Night|night]]!<br>At once for food and safety to affrighten<br>The traveller from his [[Road|road]] —<br>[GLYCINE is heard singing without. |
| RAAB KIUPRILI | Hark! heard you not<br>A distant chaunt? |
| GLYCINE | [[Song|SONG]]<br>By GLYCINE<br>A sunny shaft did I behold,<br>From sky to earth it slanted:<br>And poised therein a bird so bold —<br>Sweet bird, thou wert enchanted!<br>He sank, he [[Rose|rose]], he twinkled, he trolled<br>Within that shaft of sunny mist;<br>His eyes of [[Fire|fire]], his beak of gold,<br>All else of amethyst!<br>And thus he sang: ‘Adieu! adieu!<br>[[Love]]’s [[Dreams|dreams]] prove seldom true.<br>The blossoms, they make no delay:<br>The sparkling dew-drops will not stay.<br>Sweet month of May,<br>We must away;<br>Far, far away!<br>To-day! to-day!’ |
| ZAPOLYA | Sure ‘tis some blest spirit!<br>For since thou slew’st the [[Usurper|usurper]]’s emissary<br>That plunged upon us, a more than mortal [[Fear|fear]]<br>Is as a wall, that wards off the beleaguerer<br>And starves the poor besieged.     [[[Song]] again. |
| RAAB KIUPRILI | It is a maiden’s voice! quick to the [[Cave|cave]]! |
| ZAPOLYA | Hark! her voice falters!   [Exit ZAPOLYA. |
| RAAB KIUPRILI | She must not enter<br>The cavern, else I will remain unseen!<br>[KIUPRILI retires to one side of the stage. GLYCINE enters singing. |
| GLYCINE | A savage place! saints shield me! Bethlen! Bethlen!<br>Not here? — There’s no one here! I’ll sing again!<br>[Sings again.<br>If I do not hear my own voice, I shall fancy<br>Voices in all chance sounds!       [Starts.<br>‘Twas some dry branch<br>Dropt of itself! Oh, he went forth so rashly,<br>Took no food with him — only his arms and boar-spear!<br>What if I leave these cakes, this cruse of wine,<br>Here by this [[Cave|cave]], and seek him with the rest? |
| RAAB KIUPRILI | (unseen). Leave them and flee! |
| GLYCINE | (shrieks, then recovering.)     Where are you? |
| RAAB KIUPRILI | (still unseen.) Leave them! |
| GLYCINE | ‘Tis Glycine!<br>Speak to me, Bethlen! speak in your own voice!<br>All silent! — If this were the war-wolf’s den!<br>‘Twas not his voice! —<br>[GLYCINE [[Leaves|leaves]] the provisions, and exit. KIUPRILI comes forward, seizes them and carries them into the cavern. GLYCINE returns. |
| GLYCINE | Shame! Nothing hurt me!<br>If some fierce beast have gored him, he must needs<br>Speak with a strange voice. Wounds cause thirst and hoarseness!<br>Speak, Bethlen! or but moan. St — St —— No — Bethlen!<br>If I turn back and he should be found dead here,<br>[She creeps nearer and nearer to the cavern.<br>I should go mad! — Again!—’Twas my own heart!<br>Hush, coward heart! better beat loud with [[Fear|fear]],<br>Than break with shame and anguish!<br>[As she approaches to enter the cavern, KIUPRILI stops her. GLYCINE shrieks.<br>Saints protect me! |
| RAAB KIUPRILI | Swear then by all thy hopes, by all thy fears — |
| GLYCINE | Save me! |
| RAAB KIUPRILI | Swear secrecy and [[Silence|silence]]! |
| GLYCINE | I swear! |
| RAAB KIUPRILI | Tell what thou art, and what thou seekest? |
| GLYCINE | Only<br>A harmless [[Orphan|orphan]] youth, to bring him food — |
| RAAB KIUPRILI | Wherefore in this wood? |
| GLYCINE | Alas! it was his purpose — |
| RAAB KIUPRILI | With what intention came he? Would’st thou save him,<br>Hide nothing! |
| GLYCINE | Save him! O forgive his rashness!<br>He is good, and did not know that thou wert human! |
| RAAB KIUPRILI | Human?<br>With what design? |
| GLYCINE | To kill thee, or<br>If that thou wert a spirit, to compel thee<br>By prayers, and with the shedding of his [[Blood|blood]],<br>To make disclosure of his parentage.<br>But most of all — |
| ZAPOLYA | (rushing out from the cavern). Heaven’s blessing on thee!<br>Speak! |
| GLYCINE | Whether his mother live, or perished here! |
| ZAPOLYA | Angel of [[Mercy|mercy]], I was perishing<br>And thou did’st bring me food: and now thou bring’st<br>The sweet, sweet food of [[Hope|hope]] and consolation<br>To a mother’s famished heart! His name, sweet maiden! |
| GLYCINE | E’en till this morning we were wont to name him<br>Bethlen Bathory! |
| ZAPOLYA | Even till this morning?<br>This morning? when my weak [[Faith|faith]] failed me wholly!<br>Pardon, O thou that portion’st out our sufferance,<br>And fill’st again the widow’s empty cruse!<br>Say on! |
| GLYCINE | The false ones charged the valiant youth<br>With treasonous words of Emerick — |
| ZAPOLYA | Ha! my son! |
| GLYCINE | And of Lord Casimir — |
| RAAB KIUPRILI | (aside).    O agony! my son! |
| GLYCINE | But my dear lady — |
| ZAPOLYA and RAAB KIUPRILI | Who? |
| GLYCINE | Lady Sarolta<br>Frowned and discharged these bad men. |
| RAAB KIUPRILI | (to himself).    Righteous Heaven<br>Sent me a daughter once, and I repined<br>That it was not a son. A son was given me.<br>My daughter died, and I scarce shed a tear:<br>And lo! that son became my curse and infamy. |
| ZAPOLYA | (embraces Glycine). Sweet [[Innocent|innocent]]! and you came here to seek him,<br>And bring him food. Alas! thou [[Fear|fear]]’st? |
| GLYCINE | Not much!<br>My own dear lady, when I was a [[Child|child]],<br>Embraced me oft, but her heart never beat so.<br>For I too am an [[Orphan|orphan]], motherless! |
| RAAB KIUPRILI | (to Zapolya). O yet beware, lest [[Hope|hope]]’s brief flash but deepen<br>The after gloom, and make the darkness stormy!<br>In that last conflict, following our escape,<br>The [[Usurper|usurper]]’s cruelty had clogged our flight<br>With many a babe and many a childing mother.<br>This maid herself is one of numberless<br>Planks from the same vast wreck.      [Then to GLYCINE again.<br>[[Well]]! Casimir’s wife — |
| GLYCINE | She is always gracious, and so praised the old man<br>That his heart o’erflowed, and made discovery<br>That in this wood — |
| ZAPOLYA | O speak! |
| GLYCINE | A wounded lady —<br>[ZAPOLYA faints — they both support her. |
| GLYCINE | Is this his mother? |
| RAAB KIUPRILI | She would fain believe it,<br>Weak though the proofs be. [[Hope]] draws towards itself<br>The flame with which it kindles.    [Horn heard without.<br>To the cavern!<br>Quick! quick! |
| GLYCINE | Perchance some huntsmen of the [[King|king]]’s. |
| RAAB KIUPRILI | Emerick? |
| GLYCINE | He came this morning —<br>[They retire to the cavern, bearing ZAPOLYA. Then enter BETHLEN, armed with a boar-spear. |
| BETHLEN | I had a glimpse<br>Of some fierce shape; and but that Fancy often<br>Is [[Nature]]’s intermeddler, and cries halves<br>With the outward sight, I should believe I saw it<br>Bear off some human prey. O my preserver!<br>Bathory! Father! Yes, thou deserv’st that name!<br>Thou did’st not mock me! These are blessed findings!<br>The secret cypher of my destiny       [Looking at his signet.<br>Stands here inscribed: it is the seal of fate!<br>Ha! — Had ever [[Monster|monster]] fitting lair, ‘tis yonder!<br>Thou yawning den, I [[Well|well]] remember thee!<br>Mine eyes deceived me not. Heaven leads me on!<br>Now for a blast, loud as a [[King|king]]’s defiance,<br>To rouse the [[Monster|monster]] couchant o’er his ravine!<br>[Blows the horn — then a pause.<br>Another blast! and with another swell<br>To you, ye charméd watchers of this wood!<br>If haply I have come, the rightful heir<br>Of vengeance: if in me survive the spirits<br>Of those, whose guiltless [[Blood|blood]] flowed streaming here!<br>[Blows again louder.<br>Still silent? Is the [[Monster|monster]] gorged? Heaven shield me!<br>Thou, faithful spear! be both my torch and guide.<br>[As BETHLEN is about to enter, KIUPRILI speaks from the cavern unseen. |
| RAAB KIUPRILI | Withdraw thy foot! Retract thine idle spear,<br>And wait obedient! |
| BETHLEN | Ha! What art thou? speak! |
| RAAB KIUPRILI | (still unseen). Avengers! |
| BETHLEN | By a dying mother’s pangs<br>E’en such am I. Receive me! |
| RAAB KIUPRILI | (still unseen). Wait! Beware!<br>At thy first step, thou treadest upon the light,<br>Thenceforth must darkling flow, and sink in darkness! |
| BETHLEN | Ha! see my boar-spear trembles like a reed! —<br>Oh, [[Fool|fool]]! mine eyes are duped by my own shuddering. —<br>Those piléd thoughts, built up in [[Solitude|solitude]],<br>Year following year, that pressed upon my heart<br>As on the altar of some unknown God,<br>Then, as if touched by [[Fire|fire]] from heaven descending.<br>Blazed up within me at a father’s name —<br>Do they [[Desert|desert]] me now? — at my last trial?<br>Voice of command! and thou, O hidden Light!<br>I have obeyed! Declare ye by what name<br>I dare invoke you! Tell what sacrifice<br>Will make you gracious. |
| RAAB KIUPRILI | (still unseen). Patience! [[Truth]]! Obedience!<br>Be thy whole [[Soul|soul]] transparent! so the Light,<br>Thou seekest, may enshrine itself within thee!<br>Thy name? |
| BETHLEN | Ask rather the poor roaming savage,<br>Whose infancy no holy rite had blest,<br>To him, perchance, rude spoil or ghastly trophy,<br>In chase or battle won, have given a name.<br>I have none — but like a dog have answered<br>To the chance sound which he that fed me, called me. |
| RAAB KIUPRILI | (still unseen). Thy birth-place? |
| BETHLEN | Deluding spirits!<br>Do ye mock me?<br>Question the [[Night]]! Bid Darkness tell its birth-place?<br>Yet hear! Within yon old oak’s hollow trunk,<br>Where the bats cling, have I surveyed my cradle!<br>The mother-falcon hath her nest above it,<br>And in it the wolf litters! —— I invoke you,<br>Tell me, ye secret ones! if ye beheld me<br>As I stood there, like one who having delved<br>For hidden gold hath found a talisman,<br>O tell! what rights, what offices of duty<br>This signet doth command? What [[Rebel|rebel]] spirits<br>Owe homage to its Lord? |
| RAAB KIUPRILI | (still unseen). More, guiltier, mightier,<br>Than thou mayest summon! Wait the destined hour! |
| BETHLEN | O yet again, and with more clamorous [[prayer]],<br>I importune ye! Mock me no more with shadows!<br>This sable mantle — tell, dread voice! did this<br>Enwrap one fatherless! |
| ZAPOLYA | (unseen).    One fatherless! |
| BETHLEN | A sweeter voice! — A voice of [[Love|love]] and pity!<br>Was it the softened echo of mine own?<br>Sad echo! but the [[Hope|hope]] it kill’d was sickly,<br>And ere it died it had been mourned as dead!<br>One other [[Hope|hope]] yet lives within my [[Soul|soul]]:<br>Quick let me ask! — while yet this stifling [[Fear|fear]],<br>This stop of the heart, [[Leaves|leaves]] utterance! — Are — are these<br>The sole remains of her that gave me life?<br>Have I a mother?     [ZAPOLYA rushes out to embrace him.<br>Ha! |
| ZAPOLYA | My son! my son!<br>A wretched — Oh no, no! a blest — a happy mother!<br>[They embrace. KIUPRILI and GLYCINE come forward and the curtain drops. |
