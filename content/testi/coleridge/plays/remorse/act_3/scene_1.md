| Chi parla | Battuta |
|---|---|
| *(didascalia)* | A Hall of Armory, with an Altar at the back of the Stage. Soft Music from an instrument of Glass or Steel. |
| *(didascalia)* | VALDEZ, ORDONIO, and ALVAR in a Sorcerer's robe, are discovered. |
| ORDONIO | This was too [[Melancholy\|melancholy]], Father. |
| VALDEZ | Nay,<br>My Alvar lov'd sad music from a [[Child\|child]].<br>Once he was lost; and after weary search<br>We found him in an open place in the wood.<br>To which spot he had followed a blind boy,<br>Who breath'd into a pipe of sycamore<br>Some strangely moving notes: and these, he said,<br>Were taught him in [[A Dream\|a dream]]. Him we first saw<br>Stretch'd on the broad top of a sunny heath-bank:<br>And lower down poor Alvar, fast asleep,<br>His head upon the blind boy's dog. It pleas'd me<br>To mark how he had fasten'd round the pipe<br>A silver toy his grandam had late given him.<br>Methinks I see him now as he then look'd —<br>Even so! — He had outgrown his infant dress,<br>Yet still he wore it. |
| ALVAR | (aside) My [[Tears\|tears]] must not flow!<br>I must not clasp his knees, and cry, My father! |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter TERESA and Attendants. |
| TERESA | Lord Valdez, you have asked my presence here,<br>And I submit; but (Heaven bear witness for me)<br>My heart approves it not! 'tis mockery. |
| ORDONIO | Believe you then no preternatural influence:<br>Believe you not that spirits throng around us? |
| TERESA | Say rather that I have imagined it<br>A possible thing: and it has sooth'd my [[Soul\|soul]]<br>As other fancies have; but ne'er seduced me<br>To traffic with the black and frenzied [[Hope\|hope]]<br>That the dead hear the voice of [[Witch\|witch]] or wizard. [To ALVAR.]<br>Stranger, I mourn and blush to see you here,<br>On such employment! With far other thoughts<br>I left you. |
| ORDONIO | (aside) Ha! he has been tampering with her? |
| ALVAR | O high-[[Soul\|soul]]'d Maiden! and more dear to me<br>Than suits the stranger's name! —<br>I swear to thee<br>I will uncover all concealéd [[Guilt\|guilt]].<br>[[Doubt]], but decide not! Stand ye from the altar. |
| *(didascalia)* | Here a strain of music is heard from behind the scene. |
| ALVAR | With no irreverent voice or uncouth charm<br>I call up the departed!<br>[[Soul]] of Alvar!<br>Hear our soft suit, and heed my milder spell:<br>So may the gates of Paradise, unbarr'd,<br>Cease thy swift toils! Since haply thou art one<br>Of that innumerable company<br>Who in broad circle, lovelier than the rainbow,<br>Girdle this round earth in a dizzy motion,<br>With noise too vast and constant to be heard:<br>Fitliest unheard! For oh, ye numberless,<br>And rapid travellers! what ear unstunn'd,<br>What sense unmadden'd, might bear up against<br>The rushing of your congregated wings? [Music.]<br>Even now your living [[Wheel\|wheel]] turns o'er my head!<br>Ye, as ye pass, toss high the desart sands,<br>That roar and whiten, like a burst of waters,<br>A sweet appearance, but a dread illusion<br>To the parch'd caravan that roams by [[Night\|night]]!<br>And ye upbuild on the becalmed waves<br>That whirling pillar, which from earth to heaven<br>Stands vast, and moves in blackness! Ye too split<br>The ice mount! and with fragments many and huge<br>Tempest the new-thaw'd sea, whose sudden gulfs<br>Suck in, perchance, some Lapland wizard's skiff!<br>Then round and round the whirlpool's marge ye [[Dance\|dance]],<br>Till from the blue swoln corse the [[Soul\|soul]] toils out,<br>And joins your mighty army. |
| *(didascalia)* | Here behind the scenes a voice sings the three words, 'Hear, Sweet Spirit.' |
| ALVAR | [[Soul]] of Alvar!<br>Hear the mild spell, and tempt no blacker charm!<br>By sighs unquiet, and the sickly pang<br>Of a half-dead, yet still undying [[Hope\|hope]],<br>Pass visible before our mortal sense!<br>So shall the Church's cleansing rites be thine,<br>Her knells and masses that redeem the dead! |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Song\|SONG]] — Behind the Scenes, accompanied by the same Instrument as before. |
| *(didascalia)* | Hear, sweet spirit, hear the spell,<br>Lest a blacker charm compel!<br>So shall the midnight breezes swell<br>With thy deep long-lingering knell.<br>And at evening evermore,<br>In a chapel on the shore,<br>Shall the chaunter, sad and saintly,<br>Yellow tapers burning faintly,<br>Doleful masses chaunt for thee,<br>Miserere Domine!<br>Hark! the cadence dies away<br>On the quiet moonlight sea:<br>The boatmen rest their oars and say,<br>Miserere Domine! [A long pause.] |
| ORDONIO | The [[Innocent\|innocent]] obey nor charm nor spell!<br>My brother is in heaven. Thou sainted spirit,<br>Burst on our sight, a passing visitant!<br>Once more to hear thy voice, once more to see thee,<br>O 'twere a joy to me! |
| ALVAR | A joy to thee!<br>What if thou heard'st him now? What if his spirit<br>Re-enter'd its cold corse, and came upon thee<br>With many a stab from many a murderer's poniard?<br>What if (his stedfast eye still beaming pity<br>And brother's [[Love\|love]]) he turn'd his head aside,<br>Lest he should look at thee, and with one look<br>Hurl thee beyond all power of penitence? |
| VALDEZ | These are unholy fancies! |
| ORDONIO | Yes, my father,<br>He is in Heaven! |
| ALVAR | (still to Ordonio) But what if he had a brother,<br>Who had lived even so, that at his dying hour,<br>The name of Heaven would have convulsed his face,<br>More than the [[Death\|death]]-pang? |
| VALDEZ | Idly prating man!<br>Thou hast guess'd ill: Don Alvar's only brother<br>Stands here before thee — a father's blessing on him!<br>He is most virtuous. |
| ALVAR | (still to Ordonio) What, if his very virtues<br>Had pampered his swoln heart and made him proud?<br>And what if [[Pride\|pride]] had duped him into [[Guilt\|guilt]]?<br>Yet still he stalked a [[Self\|self]]-created god,<br>Not very bold, but exquisitely cunning;<br>And one that at his mother's looking-glass<br>Would force his features to a frowning sternness?<br>Young Lord! I tell thee, that there are such beings —<br>Yea, and it gives fierce merriment to the damn'd,<br>To see these most proud men, that loath mankind,<br>At every stir and buzz of coward [[Conscience\|conscience]],<br>Trick, cant, and lie, most whining hypocrites!<br>Away, away! Now let me hear more music. [Music again.] |
| TERESA | 'Tis strange, I tremble at my own conjectures!<br>But whatsoe'er it mean, I dare no longer<br>Be present at these lawless mysteries,<br>This dark provoking of the hidden Powers!<br>Already I affront — if not high Heaven —<br>Yet Alvar's [[Memory\|memory]]! — Hark! I make appeal<br>Against the unholy rite, and hasten hence<br>To bend before a lawful shrine, and seek<br>That voice which whispers, when the still heart listens,<br>Comfort and faithful [[Hope\|hope]]! Let us retire. |
| ALVAR | (to Teresa) O full of [[Faith\|faith]] and guileless [[Love\|love]], thy Spirit<br>Still prompts thee wisely. Let the pangs of [[Guilt\|guilt]]<br>Surprise the guilty: thou art [[Innocent\|innocent]]! |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt TERESA and Attendant. Music as before. |
| ALVAR | The spell is mutter'd — Come, thou wandering shape,<br>Who own'st no master in a human eye,<br>Whate'er be this man's doom, fair be it, or foul,<br>If he be dead, O come! and bring with thee<br>That which he grasp'd in [[Death\|death]]! But if he live,<br>Some token of his obscure perilous life. |
| *(didascalia)* | The whole Music dashes into a [[Chorus]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Chorus\|CHORUS]]<br>Wandering demons, hear the spell!<br>Lest a blacker charm compel — |
| *(didascalia)* | The incense on the altar takes [[Fire\|fire]] suddenly, and an illuminated picture of ALVAR'S assassination is discovered, and having remained a few seconds is then hidden by ascending flames. |
| ORDONIO | (starting) Duped! duped! duped! — the traitor Isidore! |
| *(didascalia)* | At this instant the doors are forced open, MONVIEDRO and the Familiars of the [[Inquisition]], Servants, &c., enter and fill the stage. |
| MONVIEDRO | First seize the sorcerer! suffer him not to speak!<br>The holy judges of the [[Inquisition]]<br>Shall hear his first words. — Look you pale, Lord Valdez?<br>Plain evidence have we here of most foul sorcery.<br>There is a dungeon underneath this [[Castle\|castle]],<br>And as you [[Hope\|hope]] for mild interpretation,<br>Surrender instantly the keys and charge of it. |
| ORDONIO | (recovering himself as from stupor, to Servants) Why haste you not? Off with him to the dungeon! |
| *(didascalia)* | All rush out in tumult. |
