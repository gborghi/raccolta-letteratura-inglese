|---|---|
| *(didascalia)* | Interior of a Chapel, with painted Windows. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter TERESA. |
| TERESA | When first I entered this pure spot, forebodings<br>Press'd heavy on my heart: but as I knelt,<br>Such calm unwonted bliss possess'd my spirit,<br>A trance so cloudless, that those sounds, hard by,<br>Of trampling uproar fell upon mine ear<br>As alien and unnoticed as the rain-[[Storm\|storm]]<br>Beats on the roof of some fair banquet-room,<br>While sweetest melodies are warbling —— |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter VALDEZ. |
| VALDEZ | Ye pitying saints, forgive a father's blindness,<br>And extricate us from this net of peril! |
| TERESA | Who wakes anew my fears, and speaks of peril? |
| VALDEZ | O best Teresa, wisely wert thou prompted!<br>This was no feat of mortal agency!<br>That picture — Oh, that picture tells me all!<br>With a flash of light it came, in flames it vanished,<br>[[Self]]-kindled, [[Self\|self]]-consum'd: bright as thy life,<br>Sudden and unexpected as thy fate,<br>Alvar! My son! My son! — The Inquisitor — |
| TERESA | Torture me not! But Alvar — Oh of Alvar? |
| VALDEZ | How often would he plead for these Morescoes!<br>The brood accurst! remorseless, coward murderers! |
| TERESA | So? so? — I comprehend you — He is —— |
| VALDEZ | He is no more! |
| TERESA | O sorrow! that a father's voice should say this,<br>A Father's Heart believe it! |
| VALDEZ | A worse sorrow<br>Are fancy's wild hopes to a heart despairing! |
| TERESA | These rays that slant in through those gorgeous windows,<br>From yon bright orb — though coloured as they pass,<br>Are they not light? — Even so that voice, Lord Valdez!<br>Which whispers to my [[Soul\|soul]], though haply varied<br>By many a fancy, many a wishful [[Hope\|hope]],<br>Speaks yet the [[Truth\|truth]]: and Alvar lives for me! |
| VALDEZ | Yes, for three wasting years, thus and no other,<br>He has lived for thee — a spirit for thy spirit!<br>My [[Child\|child]], we must not give religious [[Faith\|faith]]<br>To every voice which makes the heart a listener<br>To its own wish. |
| TERESA | I breath'd to the Unerring<br>Permitted prayers. Must those remain unanswer'd,<br>Yet impious sorcery, that holds no commune<br>Save with the lying spirit, claim belief? |
| VALDEZ | O not to-day, not now for the first [[Time\|time]]<br>Was Alvar lost to thee —<br>Accurst assassins!<br>Disarmed, o'erpowered, despairing of defence,<br>At his bared breast he seem'd to grasp some relique<br>More dear than was his life —— |
| TERESA | O Heavens! my portrait!<br>And he did grasp it in his [[Death\|death]] pang!<br>Off, false demon,<br>That beat'st thy black wings close above my head! |
| *(didascalia)* | ORDONIO enters with the keys of the dungeon in his hand. |
| TERESA | Hush! who comes here? The wizard Moor's employer!<br>Moors were his murderers, you say? Saints shield us<br>From wicked thoughts —— |
| *(didascalia)* | VALDEZ moves towards the back of the stage to meet ORDONIO, and during the concluding lines of TERESA'S speech appears as eagerly conversing with him. |
| TERESA | Is Alvar dead? what then?<br>The nuptial rites and funeral shall be one!<br>Here's no abiding-place for thee, Teresa. —<br>Away! they see me not — Thou seest me, Alvar!<br>To thee I bend my course. — But first one question,<br>One question to Ordonio. — My limbs tremble —<br>There I may sit unmark'd — a moment will restore me. |
| *(didascalia)* | Retires out of sight. |
| ORDONIO | These are the dungeon keys.<br>Monviedro knew not,<br>That I too had received the wizard's message,<br>'He that can bring the dead to life again.'<br>But now he is satisfied, I plann'd this scheme<br>To work a full conviction on the culprit,<br>And he entrusts him wholly to my keeping. |
| VALDEZ | 'Tis [[Well\|well]], my son! But have you yet discovered<br>(Where is Teresa?) what those speeches meant —<br>[[Pride]], and hypocrisy, and [[Guilt\|guilt]], and cunning?<br>Then when the wizard fix'd his eye on you,<br>And you, I know not why, look'd pale and trembled —<br>Why — why, what ails you now? — |
| ORDONIO | Me? what ails me?<br>A pricking of the [[Blood\|blood]] — It might have happen'd<br>At any other [[Time\|time]]. — Why scan you me? |
| VALDEZ | His speech about the corse, and stabs and murderers,<br>Bore reference to the assassins —— |
| ORDONIO | Dup'd! dup'd! dup'd!<br>The traitor, Isidore! |
| *(didascalia)* | A pause, then wildly. |
| ORDONIO | I tell thee, my dear father!<br>I am most glad of this. |
| VALDEZ | True — sorcery<br>Merits its doom; and this perchance may guide us<br>To the discovery of the murderers.<br>I have their statures and their several faces<br>So present to me, that but once to meet them<br>Would be to recognize. |
| ORDONIO | Yes! yes! we recognize them.<br>I was benumb'd, and staggered up and down<br>Through darkness without light — dark — dark — dark!<br>My flesh crept chill, my limbs felt manacled<br>As had a snake coil'd round them! — Now 'tis sunshine,<br>And the [[Blood\|blood]] dances freely through its channels! |
| *(didascalia)* | Then to himself. |
| ORDONIO | This is my virtuous, grateful Isidore! |
| *(didascalia)* | Then mimicking ISIDORE'S manner and voice. |
| ORDONIO | 'A common trick of gratitude, my lord!'<br>Old Gratitude! a dagger would dissect<br>His 'own full heart'—'twere good to see its colour. |
| VALDEZ | These [[Magic\|magic]] sights! O that I ne'er had yielded<br>To your entreaties! Neither had I yielded,<br>But that in spite of your own seeming [[Faith\|faith]]<br>I held it for some [[Innocent\|innocent]] stratagem,<br>Which [[Love\|love]] had prompted, to remove the doubts<br>Of wild Teresa — by fancies quelling fancies! |
| ORDONIO | [[Love]]! [[Love\|love]]! and then we hate! and what? and wherefore?<br>Hatred and [[Love\|love]]! fancies opposed by fancies!<br>What? if one reptile sting another reptile?<br>Where is the crime? The goodly face of [[Nature\|nature]]<br>Hath one disfeaturing stain the less upon it.<br>Are we not all predestined transiency,<br>And cold dishonour? Grant it, that this hand<br>Had given a morsel to the hungry worms<br>Somewhat too early — Where's the crime of this?<br>That this must needs bring on the idiotcy<br>Of moist-eyed penitence—'tis like [[A Dream\|a dream]]! |
| VALDEZ | Wild talk, my son! But thy excess of feeling ——<br>Almost I [[Fear\|fear]] it hath unhinged his brain. |
| ORDONIO | Say, I had laid a [[Body\|body]] in the sun!<br>[[Well]]! in a month there swarm forth from the corse<br>A thousand, nay, ten thousand sentient beings<br>In place of that one man. — Say, I had kill'd him! |
| *(didascalia)* | TERESA stops listening. |
| ORDONIO | Yet who shall tell me, that each one and all<br>Of these ten thousand lives is not as happy,<br>As that one life, which being push'd aside,<br>Made room for these unnumbered —— |
| VALDEZ | O mere [[Madness\|madness]]! |
| *(didascalia)* | TERESA moves hastily forwards, and places herself directly before ORDONIO. |
| ORDONIO | Teresa? or the [[Phantom\|phantom]] of Teresa? |
| TERESA | Alas! the [[Phantom\|phantom]] only, if in [[Truth\|truth]]<br>The substance of her being, her life's life,<br>Have ta'en its flight through Alvar's [[Death\|death]]-wound — |
| *(didascalia)* | A pause. |
| TERESA | Where —<br>(Even coward murder grants the dead a [[Grave\|grave]])<br>O tell me, Valdez! — answer me, Ordonio!<br>Where lies the corse of my betrothéd husband? |
| ORDONIO | There, where Ordonio likewise would fain lie!<br>In the [[Sleep\|sleep]]-compelling earth, in unpierc'd darkness!<br>For while we live —<br>An inward day that never, never sets,<br>Glares round the [[Soul\|soul]], and mocks the closing eyelids!<br>Over his rocky [[Grave\|grave]] the fir-grove sighs<br>A lulling ceaseless [[Dirge\|dirge]]! 'Tis [[Well\|well]] with him. |
| *(didascalia)* | Strides off towards the altar, but returns as VALDEZ is speaking. |
| TERESA | The rock! the fir-grove! Did'st thou hear him say it?<br>Hush! I will ask him! |
| VALDEZ | Urge him not — not now!<br>This we beheld. Nor he nor I know more,<br>Than what the [[Magic\|magic]] imagery revealed.<br>The assassin, who pressed foremost of the three —— |
| ORDONIO | A tender-hearted, scrupulous, grateful [[Villain\|villain]],<br>Whom I will strangle! |
| VALDEZ | While his two companions —— |
| ORDONIO | Dead! dead already! what care we for the dead? |
| VALDEZ | Pity him! soothe him! disenchant his spirit!<br>These [[Supernatural\|supernatural]] shews, this strange disclosure,<br>And this too fond affection, which still broods<br>O'er Alvar's fate, and still burns to avenge it —<br>These, struggling with his hopeless [[Love\|love]] for you,<br>Distemper him, and give reality<br>To the creatures of his fancy. |
| ORDONIO | Is it so?<br>Yes! yes! even like a [[Child\|child]], that too abruptly<br>Roused by a glare of light from deepest [[Sleep\|sleep]]<br>Starts up bewildered and talks idly.<br>Father!<br>What if the Moors that made my brother's [[Grave\|grave]],<br>Even now were digging ours? What if the bolt,<br>Though aim'd, I [[Doubt\|doubt]] not, at the son of Valdez,<br>Yet miss'd its true aim when it fell on Alvar? |
| VALDEZ | Alvar ne'er fought against the Moors, — say rather,<br>He was their advocate; but you had march'd<br>With [[Fire\|fire]] and desolation through their villages. —<br>Yet he by chance was captured. |
| ORDONIO | Unknown, perhaps,<br>Captured, yet as the son of Valdez, murdered.<br>Leave all to me. Nay, whither, gentle lady? |
| VALDEZ | What seek you now? |
| TERESA | A better, surer light<br>To guide me —— |
| VALDEZ | Whither? |
| ORDONIO | Whither? |
| TERESA | To the only place<br>Where life yet dwells for me, and ease of heart.<br>These walls seem threatening to fall in upon me!<br>Detain me not! a dim power drives me hence,<br>And that will be my guide. |
| VALDEZ | To find a [[Lover\|lover]]!<br>Suits that a high-born maiden's modesty?<br>O folly and shame! Tempt not my rage, Teresa! |
| TERESA | Hopeless, I [[Fear\|fear]] no human being's rage.<br>And am I hastening to the arms —— O Heaven!<br>I haste but to the [[Grave\|grave]] of my belov'd! |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit, VALDEZ following after her. |
| ORDONIO | This, then, is my reward! and I must [[Love\|love]] her?<br>Scorn'd! shudder'd at! yet [[Love\|love]] her still? yes! yes!<br>By the deep feelings of [[Revenge\|revenge]] and hate<br>I will still [[Love\|love]] her — woo her — win her too! |
| *(didascalia)* | A pause. |
| ORDONIO | Isidore safe and silent, and the portrait<br>Found on the wizard — he, belike, [[Self\|self]]-[[Poison\|poison]]'d<br>To escape the crueller flames —— My [[Soul\|soul]] shouts triumph!<br>The mine is undermined! [[Blood\|blood]]! [[Blood\|blood]]! [[Blood\|blood]]!<br>They thirst for thy [[Blood\|blood]]! thy [[Blood\|blood]], Ordonio! |
| *(didascalia)* | A pause. |
| ORDONIO | The hunt is up! and in the midnight wood<br>With lights to dazzle and with nets they seek<br>A timid prey: and lo! the tiger's eye<br>Glares in the red flame of his hunter's torch!<br>To Isidore I will dispatch a message,<br>And lure him to the cavern! aye, that cavern!<br>He cannot fail to find it. Thither I'll lure him,<br>Whence he shall never, never more return! |
| *(didascalia)* | Looks through the side [[Window\|window]]. |
| ORDONIO | A rim of the sun lies yet upon the sea,<br>And now 'tis gone! All shall be done to-[[Night\|night]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit. |
