When British [[concepts/freedom|Freedom]] for an happier land  
Spread her broad wings, that flutter’d with affright,  
[[characters/erskine|ERSKINE]]! thy voice she heard, and paus’d her flight  
[[concepts/sublime|Sublime]] of [[concepts/hope|hope]], for dreadless thou didst stand  
(Thy censer glowing with the hallow’d flame)   5  
A hireless Priest before the insulted shrine,  
And at her altar pour the stream divine  
Of unmatch’d eloquence. Therefore thy name  
Her sons shall venerate, and cheer thy breast  
With blessings heaven-ward breath’d. And when the doom  
Of [[concepts/nature|Nature]] bids thee die, beyond the tomb   11  
Thy light shall shine: as sunk beneath the West  
Though the great [[motifs/summer|Summer]] Sun eludes our gaze,  
Still burns wide Heaven with his distended blaze.  
December 1, 1794.

ON READING HIS LATE PROTEST IN THE HOUSE OF LORDS  
‘MORNING CHRONICLE,’ JAN. 31, 1795  
STANHOPE! I hail, with ardent [[forms/hymn|Hymn]], thy name!  
Thou shalt be bless’d and lov’d, when in the [[motifs/dust|dust]]  
Thy corse shall moulder — Patriot pure and just!  
And o’er thy tomb the grateful hand of FAME  
Shall [[motifs/grave|grave]]:—’Here sleeps the Friend of Humankind!’   5  
For thou, untainted by CORRUPTION’S bowl,  
Or foul [[concepts/ambition|AMBITION]], with undaunted [[motifs/soul|soul]]  
Hast spoke the language of a Free-born mind  
Pleading the cause of [[concepts/nature|Nature]]! Still pursue  
Thy path of [[concepts/honour|Honour]]! — To thy Country true,   10  
Still watch th’ expiring flame of Liberty!  
O Patriot! still pursue thy virtuous way,  
As holds his course the splendid Orb of Day,  
Or thro’ the stormy or the tranquil sky!  
ONE OF THE PEOPLE.  
1795.

Not, STANHOPE! with the Patriot’s doubtful name  
I mock thy worth — Friend of the Human Race!  
Since scorning Faction’s low and partial aim  
Aloof thou wendest in thy stately pace,  
Thyself redeeming from that leprous stain,   5  
Nobility: and aye unterrify’d  
Pourest thine Abdiel warnings on the train  
That sit complotting with rebellious [[concepts/pride|pride]]  
‘Gainst Her who from the Almighty’s bosom leapt  
With whirlwind arm, fierce Minister of [[motifs/love|Love]]!   10  
Wherefore, ere Virtue o’er thy tomb hath wept,  
Angels shall lead thee to the Throne above:  
And thou from forth its clouds shalt hear the voice,  
Champion of [[concepts/freedom|Freedom]] and her God! rejoice!  
1795.

O’ertake the heavy sailing Clouds of [[motifs/night|Night]];  
Th’ Horizon kindles with so rich a red,

Th’ impatient Matin-bird, assur’d of Day,  
[[motifs/leaves|Leaves]] his low nest to meet its earliest ray;   10

Seem’d Giant Forms, the Genii of the Land!),  
Thy struggles soon shall wak’ning Britain bless,

Il denaro, ho sentito dire da un saggio,  
si fa le ali e vola via —  
ah, se gli venisse in testa  
di farne invece un paio per me.  
Pubblicato per la prima volta (da un MS.) nel 1893.
