Maid of unboastful charms! whom white-robed [[Truth]]  
Right onward guiding through the maze of youth,  
Forbade the Circe Praise to [[Witch|witch]] thy [[Soul|soul]],  
And dash’d to earth th’ intoxicating bowl:  
Thee meek-eyed Pity, eloquently fair,   5  
Clasp’d to her bosom with a mother’s care;  
And, as she lov’d thy kindred form to trace,  
The slow smile wander’d o’er her pallid face.  
For never yet did mortal voice impart  
Tones more congenial to the sadden’d heart:   10  
Whether, to rouse the sympathetic glow,  
Thou pourest lone Monimia’s tale of woe;  
Or haply clothest with funereal vest  
The bridal loves that wept in [[Juliet]]’s breast.  
O’er our chill limbs the thrilling Terrors creep,   15  
Th’ entrancéd Passions their still vigil keep;  
While the deep sighs, responsive to the [[Song|song]],  
Sound through the [[Silence|silence]] of the trembling throng.  
But purer raptures lighten’d from thy face,  
And spread o’er all thy form an holier grace,   20  
When from the daughter’s breasts the father drew  
The life he gave, and mix’d the big tear’s dew.  
Nor was it thine th’ heroic strain to roll  
With mimic feelings foreign from the [[Soul|soul]]:  
Bright in thy parent’s eye we mark’d the tear;   25  
Methought he said, ‘Thou art no Actress here!  
A semblance of thyself the Grecian dame,  
And Brunton and Euphrasia still the same!’  
O soon to seek the [[City|city]]’s busier scene,  
Pause thee awhile, thou chaste-eyed maid serene,   30  
Till Granta’s sons from all her sacred bowers  
With grateful hand shall weave Pierian [[Flowers|flowers]]  
To twine a fragrant chaplet round thy brow,  
Enchanting ministress of virtuous woe!  
1794.

<span class="atom-split" data-atom="271_the_two_founts" data-title="THE TWO FOUNTS" data-chapter="" data-kind="intro" data-tags="motif/dreams,motif/water,motif/flowers,concept/beauty,concept/grief_and_loss,concept/sin_and_the_fall,concept/friendship,cluster/appearance_vs_reality,cluster/beauty,cluster/eyes_and_sight"></span>


STANZAS ADDRESSED TO A LADY ON HER RECOVERY WITH UNBLEMISHED LOOKS, FROM A SEVERE ATTACK OF [[Pain|PAIN]]  
‘Twas my last waking thought, how it could be  
That thou, sweet friend, such anguish should’st endure;  
When straight from Dreamland came a [[Dwarf]], and he  
Could tell the cause, forsooth, and knew the cure.  
Methought he fronted me with peering look   5  
Fix’d on my heart; and read aloud in game  
The loves and griefs therein, as from a book:  
And uttered praise like one who wished to blame.  
In every heart (quoth he) since Adam’s sin  
Two Founts there are, of [[Suffering]] and of Cheer!   10  
That to let forth, and this to keep within!  
But she, whose aspect I find imaged here,  
Of Pleasure only will to all dispense,  
That Fount [[Alone|alone]] unlock, by no distress  
Choked or turned inward, but still issue thence   15  
Unconquered cheer, persistent loveliness.  
As on the driving cloud the shiny bow,  
That gracious thing made up of [[Tears|tears]] and light,  
Mid the wild rack and rain that slants below  
Stands smiling forth, unmoved and freshly bright;   20  
As though the spirits of all lovely [[Flowers|flowers]],  
Inweaving each its wreath and dewy [[Crown|crown]],  
Or ere they sank to earth in vernal showers,  
Had built a bridge to tempt the angels down.  
Even so, Eliza! on that face of thine,   25  
On that benignant face, whose look [[Alone|alone]]  
(The [[Soul|soul]]’s translucence thro’ her crystal shrine!)  
Has power to soothe all anguish but thine own,  
A [[Beauty|beauty]] hovers still, and ne’er takes wing,  
But with a silent charm compels the stern   30  
And tort’ring Genius of the bitter [[Spring|spring]],  
To shrink aback, and cower upon his urn.  
Who then needs [[Wonder|wonder]], if (no outlet found  
In passion, spleen, or strife) the Fount of [[Pain]]  
O’erflowing beats against its lovely mound,   35  
And in wild flashes shoots from heart to brain?  
[[Sleep]], and the [[Dwarf]] with that unsteady gleam  
On his raised lip, that aped a critic smile,  
Had passed: yet I, my sad thoughts to beguile,  
Lay weaving on the tissue of my dream;   40  
Till audibly at length I cried, as though  
Thou hadst indeed been present to my eyes,  
O sweet, sweet sufferer; if the case be so,  
I pray thee, be less good, less sweet, less wise!  
In every look a barbéd arrow send,   45  
On those soft lips let scorn and anger live!  
Do any thing, rather than thus, sweet friend!  
Hoard for thyself the [[Pain|pain]], thou wilt not give!  
1826.

che tu, dolce amica, dovessi patire tale angoscia;  
quand'ecco dal Paese dei Sogni venne un [[Dwarf|Nano]], e lui  
sapeva dirne la causa, davvero, e conosceva il rimedio.  
Mi parve che mi stesse di fronte con occhiata scrutatrice   5  
fissa sul mio cuore; e leggesse ad alta voce per gioco  
gli amori e i dolori che vi stanno, come da un libro:  
e pronunciasse lode come chi vorrebbe biasimare.  
In ogni cuore (disse) dal peccato d'Adamo  
due Fonti vi sono, di [[Suffering|Sofferenza]] e di Letizia!   10  
quella da lasciar uscire, e questa da tenere dentro!  
Ma colei, il cui aspetto trovo qui raffigurato,  
vuole dispensare a tutti solo Piacere,  
schiudere quella Fonte [[Alone|soltanto]], e da nessuna angustia  
soffocata o rivolta all'interno, ma sempre di là sgorga   15  
letizia invitta, amabilità persistente.  
Come sulla nube incalzante l'arco lucente,  
quella cosa graziosa fatta di [[Tears|lacrime]] e di luce,  
fra lo scompiglio selvaggio e la pioggia che sotto s'inclina  
sta sorridente, immoto e freschissimo splendente;   20  
come se gli spiriti di tutti i [[Flowers|fiori]] amabili,  
intrecciando ciascuno il suo serto e la sua [[Crown|corona]] rugiadosa,  
prima di calare a terra in acquazzoni primaverili,  
avessero costruito un ponte per invogliare gli angeli a scendere.  
Proprio così, Eliza! su quel tuo volto,   25  
su quel volto benigno, il cui sguardo [[Alone|soltanto]]  
(la trasparenza dell'[[Soul|anima]] attraverso il suo sacrario di cristallo!)  
ha potere di lenire ogni angoscia fuorché la tua,  
una [[Beauty|bellezza]] aleggia sempre, e mai prende il volo,  
ma con silenzioso incanto costringe il severo   30  
e torturante Genio dell'amara [[Spring|sorgente]]  
a ritrarsi indietro, e ad accovacciarsi sulla sua urna.  
Chi dunque deve [[Wonder|stupirsi]] se (non trovato sbocco  
in passione, bile o contesa) la Fonte del [[Pain|Dolore]],  
traboccando, batte contro il suo amabile argine,   35  
e in guizzi selvaggi saetta dal cuore al cervello?  
Il [[Sleep|Sonno]], e il [[Dwarf|Nano]] con quel bagliore incerto  
sul labbro sollevato, che scimmiottava un sorriso da critico,  
erano passati: pure io, per ingannare i tristi pensieri,  
giacevo tessendo ancora la trama del mio sogno;   40  
finché alfine gridai forte, come se  
tu fossi davvero presente ai miei occhi:  
o dolce, dolce sofferente; se le cose stanno così,  
ti prego, sii meno buona, meno dolce, meno saggia!  
In ogni sguardo manda una freccia uncinata,   45  
su quelle labbra morbide vivano scherno e ira!  
Fa' qualunque cosa, piuttosto che così, dolce amica!  
Ammassa per te il [[Pain|dolore]] che non vuoi dare!  
1826.
