<span class="atom-split" data-atom="0156_you_love_me_you_are_sure" data-title="156. You love me — you are sure —" data-chapter="" data-kind="intro"></span>


You [[Love|love]] me — you are sure —  
I shall not [[Fear|fear]] mistake —  
I shall not cheated wake —  
Some grinning morn —  
To find the Sunrise left —  
And Orchards — unbereft —  
And Dollie — gone!  

I need not start — you're sure —  
That [[Night|night]] will never be —  
When frightened — [[Home|home]] to Thee I run —  
To find the windows dark —  
And no more Dollie — mark —  
Quite none?  

Be sure you're sure — you know —  
I'll bear it better now —  
If you'll just tell me so —  
Than when — a little dull Balm grown —  
Over this [[Pain|pain]] of mine —  
You sting — again!
