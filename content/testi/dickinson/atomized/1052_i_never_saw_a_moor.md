<span class="atom-split" data-atom="1052_i_never_saw_a_moor" data-title="1052. I never saw a Moor —" data-chapter="" data-kind="intro"></span>


I never saw a Moor--  
I never saw the [[Sea]]--  
Yet know I how the Heather looks  
And what a Billow be.  

I never spoke with [[God]],  
Nor visited in Heaven--  
Yet certain am I of the spot  
As if the Checks were given--
