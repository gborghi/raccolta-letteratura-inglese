<span class="atom-split" data-atom="0035_nobody_knows_this_little_rose" data-title="35. Nobody knows this little Rose —" data-chapter="" data-kind="intro"></span>


Nobody knows this little [[Rose|rose]];  
It might a [[Pilgrim|pilgrim]] be,  
Did I not take it from the ways,  
And lift it up to thee!  

Only a bee will miss it;  
Only a butterfly,  
Hastening from far [[Journey|journey]],  
On it's breast to lie.  

Only a bird will [[Wonder|wonder]];  
Only a breeze will sigh;  
Ah! little [[Rose|rose]], how easy  
For such as thee to die!
