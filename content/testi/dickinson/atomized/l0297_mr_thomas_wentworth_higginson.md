After an interval of [[Silence|silence]] came this [[Letter|letter]], in the same year:—

Must I lose the friend that saved my life without inquiring why? Affection gropes through drifts of awe for his tropic [[Door|door]].

That every bliss we know or guess hourly befall him, is his scholar's [[Prayer|prayer]].
