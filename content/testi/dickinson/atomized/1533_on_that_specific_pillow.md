On that specific Pillow  
Our projects flit away —  
The [[Night]]'s tremendous Morrow  
And whether [[Sleep|sleep]] will stay  
Or usher us — a stranger —  
To situations new  
The effort to comprise it  
Is all the [[Soul|soul]] can do.
