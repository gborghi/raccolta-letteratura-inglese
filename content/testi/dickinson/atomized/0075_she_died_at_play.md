She died at play,  
Gambolled away  
Her lease of spotted hours,  
Then sank as gaily as a Turk  
Upon a Couch of [[Flowers|flowers]].  

Her [[Ghost|ghost]] strolled softly o'er the [[Hill|hill]]  
Yesterday, and Today,  
Her vestments as the silver fleece —  
Her countenance as spray.
