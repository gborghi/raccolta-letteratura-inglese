Oh Future! thou secreted peace  
Or subterranean woe —  
Is there no wandering route of grace  
That leads away from thee —  
No circuit sage of all the course  
Descried by cunning Men  
To balk thee of thy sacred Prey —  
Advancing to thy Den —
