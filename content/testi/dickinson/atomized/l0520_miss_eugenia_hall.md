DEAR “GENIE,”—The lovely flower you sent me is like a little vase of spice, and fills the hall with cinnamon.

You must have skilful hands to make such sweet carnations. Perhaps your doll taught you. I know that dolls are sometimes wise. Robins are my dolls. I am glad you [[Love|love]] the blossoms so [[Well|well]]. I [[Hope|hope]] you [[Love|love]] [[Birds|birds]], too. It is economical. It saves going to Heaven.
