<span class="atom-split" data-atom="1249_the_stars_are_old_that_stood_for_me" data-title="1249. The Stars are old, that stood for me —" data-chapter="" data-kind="intro"></span>


The [[Stars]] are old, that stood for me —  
The West a little worn —  
Yet newer glows the only Gold  
I ever cared to earn —  

Presuming on that lone result  
Her infinite disdain  
But vanquished her with my defeat  
'Twas Victory was slain.
