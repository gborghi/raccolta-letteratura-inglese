<span class="atom-split" data-atom="0010_my_wheel_is_in_the_dark" data-title="10. My wheel is in the dark!" data-chapter="" data-kind="intro"></span>


My [[Wheel]] is in the dark.  
I cannot see a spoke -  
Yet know it's dripping feet  
Go round and round.  

My foot is on the tide -  
An unfrequented [[Road|road]]  
Yet have all roads  
A "Clearing" at the end.  

Some have resigned the Loom -  
Some - in the busy tomb  
Find quaint employ.  
Some with new - stately feet  
Pass royal thro' the gate  
Flinging the problem back, at you and I.
