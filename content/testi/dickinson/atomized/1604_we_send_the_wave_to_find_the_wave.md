We send the Wave to find the Wave —  
An Errand so divine,  
The Messenger enamored too,  
Forgetting to return,  
We make the wise distinction still,  
Soever made in vain,  
The sagest [[Time|time]] to dam the [[Sea|sea]] is when the [[Sea|sea]] is gone —
