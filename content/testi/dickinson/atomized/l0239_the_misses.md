. . . A tone from the old [[Bells|bells]], perhaps, might wake the children.

We send the wave to find the wave, An errand so divine The messenger enamored too, Forgetting to return, We make the sage decision still Soever made in vain, The only [[Time|time]] to dam the [[Sea|sea]] Is when the [[Sea|sea]] is gone.
