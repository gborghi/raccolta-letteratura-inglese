. . . There is that which is called an “awakening” in the church, and I know of no choicer ecstasy than to see Mrs.——roll out in crape every morning, I suppose to intimidate antichrist; at least it would have that effect on me. It reminds me of [[Don Quixote]] demanding the surrender of the [[Wind|wind]]-mill, and of Sir Stephen Toplift, and of Sir Alexander Cockburn.

[[Spring]] is a happiness so beautiful, so unique, so unexpected, that I don't know what to do with my heart. I dare not take it, I dare not leave it—what do you advise?

Life is a spell so exquisite that everything conspires to break, it.

“What do I think of Middlemarch?” What do I think of glory—except that in a few instances this “mortal has already put on [[Immortality|immortality]].”

George Eliot is one. The mysteries of human [[Nature|nature]] surpass the “mysteries of redemption,” for the infinite we only suppose, while we see the finite. . . . I launch Vinnie on Wednesday; it will require the combined efforts of Maggie, [[Providence]] and myself, for whatever advances Vinnie makes in [[Nature|nature]] and art, she has not reduced departure to a science. . . .
