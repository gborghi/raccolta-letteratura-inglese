<span class="atom-split" data-atom="l0118_dr_j_g_holland_and_mrs_holland_friday" data-title="To Dr. J. G. Holland, and Mrs. Holland, Friday" data-chapter="" data-kind="intro"></span>


DEAR FRIENDS,—I write to you. I receive no [[Letter|letter]].

I say “they dignify my trust.” I do not disbelieve. I go again. Cardinals wouldn't do it. Cockneys wouldn't do it, but I can't stop to strut, in a world where [[Bells|bells]] toll. I hear through visitor in town, that “Mrs. Holland is not strong.” The little peacock in me, tells me not to inquire again. Then I remember my tiny friend—how brief she is—how dear she is, and the peacock quite dies away. Now, you need not speak, for perhaps you are weary, and “[[Herod]]” requires all your thought, but if you are [[Well|well]]—let Annie draw me a little picture of an erect flower; if you are ill, she can hang the flower a little on one side!

Then, I shall understand, and you need not stop to write me a [[Letter|letter]]. Perhaps you laugh at me! Perhaps the whole United States are laughing at me too! I can't stop for that! My business is to [[Love|love]]. I found a bird, this morning, down —down—on a little bush at the foot of the [[Garden|garden]], and wherefore sing, I said, since nobody hears?

One sob in the throat, one flutter of bosom—“My business is to sing”—and away she [[Rose|rose]]! How do I know but cherubim, once, themselves, as patient, listened, and applauded her unnoticed [[Hymn|hymn]]?
