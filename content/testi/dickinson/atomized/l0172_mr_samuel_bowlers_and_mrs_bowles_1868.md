<span class="atom-split" data-atom="l0172_mr_samuel_bowlers_and_mrs_bowles_1868" data-title="To Mr. Samuel Bowlers and Mrs. Bowles, 1868?" data-chapter="" data-kind="intro"></span>


I should think you would have few letters, for your own are so noble that they make men afraid. And sweet as your approbation is, it is had in [[Fear|fear]], lest your depth convict us.

You compel us each to remember that when [[Water|water]] ceases to rise, it has commenced falling. That is the law of flood.

The last day that I saw you was the newest and oldest of my life.

[[Resurrection]] can come but once, first, to the same house. Thank you for leading us by it.

Come always, dear friend, but refrain from going. You spoke of not liking to be forgotten. Could you, though you would?

Treason never knew you.
