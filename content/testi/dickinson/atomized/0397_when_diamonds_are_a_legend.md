When Diamonds are a Legend,  
And Diadems — a Tale —  
I Brooch and Earrings for Myself,  
Do sow, and Raise for sale —  

And tho' I'm scarce accounted,  
My Art, a [[Summer]] Day — had Patrons —  
Once — it was a [[Queen]] —  
And once — a Butterfly —
