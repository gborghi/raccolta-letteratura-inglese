THINE —  

How sick — to wait — in any place — but thine —  
I knew last [[Night|night]] — when someone tried to twine —  
Thinking — perhaps — that I looked tired — or [[Alone|alone]] —  
Or breaking — almost — with unspoken [[Pain|pain]] —  

And I turned — ducal —  
That right — was thine —  
One port — suffices — for a Brig — like mine —  

Ours be the tossing — wild though the [[Sea|sea]] —  
Rather than a Mooring — unshared by thee.  
Ours be the Cargo — unladed — here —  
Rather than the "spicy isles —"  
And thou — not there —
