THE last [[Night|night]] that she lived,  
It was a common [[Night|night]],  
Except the dying; this to us  
Made [[Nature|nature]] different.  

We noticed smallest things, —  
Things overlooked before,  
By this great light upon our minds  
Italicized, as 't were.  

That others could exist  
While she must finish quite,  
A [[Jealousy|jealousy]] for her arose  
So nearly infinite.  

We waited while she passed;  
It was a narrow [[Time|time]],  
Too jostled were our souls to speak,  
At length the notice came.  
She mentioned, and forgot;  
Then lightly as a reed  
Bent to the [[Water|water]], shivered scarce,  
Consented, and was dead.  

And we, we placed the hair,  
And drew the head erect;  
And then an awful leisure was,  
Our [[Faith|faith]] to regulate.
