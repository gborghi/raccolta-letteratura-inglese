DEAR FRIEND,—The paper wanders so I cannot write my name on it, so I give you father's portrait instead.

As [[Summer|summer]] into [[Autumn|autumn]] slips And yet we sooner say “The [[Summer|summer]]” than “the [[Autumn|autumn]],” lest We turn the [[Sun|sun]] away,

And almost count it an affront The presence to concede Of one however lovely, not The one that we have loved,—

So we evade the charge of years, One, one attempting shy The circumvention of the shaft Of life's declivity.
