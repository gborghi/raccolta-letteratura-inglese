<span class="atom-split" data-atom="1109_i_fit_for_them" data-title="1109. I fit for them —" data-chapter="" data-kind="intro"></span>


I fit for them —  
I seek the Dark  
Till I am thorough fit.  
The labor is a sober one  
With this sufficient sweet  
That abstinence of mine produce  
A purer food for them, if I succeed,  
If not I had  
The transport of the Aim —
