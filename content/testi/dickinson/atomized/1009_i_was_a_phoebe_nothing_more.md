I was a [[Phoebe]] — nothing more —  
A [[Phoebe]] — nothing less —  
The little note that others dropt  
I fitted into place —  

I dwelt too low that any seek —  
Too shy, that any blame —  
A [[Phoebe]] makes a little print  
Upon the Floors of Fame —
