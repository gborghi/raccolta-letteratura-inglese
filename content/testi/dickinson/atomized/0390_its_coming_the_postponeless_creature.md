It's coming — the postponeless Creature —  
It gains the Block — and now — it gains the [[Door]] —  
Chooses its latch, from all the other fastenings —  
Enters — with a "You know Me — Sir"?  

Simple Salute — and certain Recognition —  
Bold — were it Enemy — Brief — were it friend —  
Dresses each [[house|House]] in Crape, and Icicle —  
And carries one — out of it — to [[God]] —


<span class="qlang-split" data-lang="it"></span>

Sta arrivando — la Creatura indilazionabile —  
Guadagna l'Isolato — e ora — guadagna la [[Door|Porta]] —  
Sceglie il suo saliscendi, fra tutte le altre serrature —  
Entra — con un "Mi conosce — Signore"?  

Semplice Saluto — e certo Riconoscimento —  
Audace — fosse Nemico — Breve — fosse amico —  
Veste ogni [[house|Casa]] di Crespo, e di Ghiacciolo —  
E ne porta uno — fuori — a [[God|Dio]] —
