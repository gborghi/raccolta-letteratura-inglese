<span class="atom-split" data-atom="0024_there_is_a_morn_by_men_unseen" data-title="24. There is a morn by men unseen —" data-chapter="" data-kind="intro"></span>


There is a morn by men unseen -  
Whose maids opon remoter green  
Keep their seraphic May -  
And all day long, with [[Dance|dance]] and game,  
And gambo! I may never name -  
Employ their holiday.  

Here to light measure, move the feet  
Which walk no more [[The Village Street|the village street]] -  
Nor by the wood are found -  
Here are the [[Birds|birds]] that sought the [[Sun|sun]]  
When last year's distaff idle hung  
And [[Summer|summer]]'s brows were bound.  

Ne'er saw I such a wondrous scene -  
Ne'er such a ring on such a green -  
Nor so serene array -  
As if the [[Stars|stars]] some [[Summer|summer]] [[Night|night]]  
Should swing their cups of Chrysolite -  
And revel till the day -  

Like thee to [[Dance|dance]] - like thee to sing -  
People opon that mystic green -  
I ask, each new May morn.  
I wait thy far - fantastic [[Bells|bells]] -  
Announcing me in other dells -  
Unto the different [[Dawn|dawn]]!
