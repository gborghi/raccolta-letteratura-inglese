<span class="atom-split" data-atom="1511_my_country_need_not_change_her_gown" data-title="1511. My country need not change her gown," data-chapter="" data-kind="intro"></span>


My country need not change her gown,  
Her triple suit as sweet  
As when 'twas cut at Lexington,  
And first pronounced "a fit."  

Great Britain disapproves, "the [[Stars|stars]]";  
Disparagement discreet, —  
There's something in their attitude  
That taunts her bayonet.
