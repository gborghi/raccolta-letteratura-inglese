We knew not that we were to live —  
Nor when — we are to die —  
Our ignorance — our cuirass is —  
We wear [[Mortality]]  
As lightly as an Option Gown  
Till asked to take it off —  
By his intrusion, [[God]] is known —  
It is the same with Life —
