<span class="atom-split" data-atom="0339_i_tend_my_flowers_for_thee" data-title="339. I tend my flowers for thee —" data-chapter="" data-kind="intro"></span>


I tend my [[Flowers|flowers]] for thee —  
Bright Absentee!  
My Fuchsia's Coral Seams  
Rip — while the Sower — [[Dreams|dreams]]  

Geraniums — tint — and spot —  
Low Daisies — dot —  
My Cactus — splits her Beard  
To show her throat —  

Carnations — tip their spice —  
And [[Bees]] — pick up —  
A Hyacinth — I hid —  
Puts out a Ruffled Head —  
And odors fall  
From flasks — so small —  
You marvel how they held —  

Globe Roses — break their satin flake —  
Upon my [[Garden]] floor  
Yet — thou — not there —  
I had as lief they bore  
No Crimson — more —  

Thy flower — be gay —  
Her Lord — away!  
It ill becometh me —  
I'll dwell in Calyx — Gray —  
How modestly — alway —  
Thy Daisy —  
Draped for thee!
