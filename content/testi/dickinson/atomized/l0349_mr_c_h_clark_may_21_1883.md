DEAR FRIEND,—We have much [[Fear|fear]], both for your own strength and the health of your brother, having heard nothing since we last asked, many days ago. Will you not, when possible, gives us but a syllable, even a cheering accent, if no more be true? We think of you and your sufferer with intense anxiety, wishing some act or word of ours might be [[Hope|hope]] or help. The humming-[[Birds|birds]] and orioles fly by me as I write, and I long to guide their enchanted feet to your brother's chamber.

Excuse me for knocking. Please also excuse me for staying so long. [[Spring]] is a strange land when our friends are ill.

With my sister's tenderest alarm, as also my own,
