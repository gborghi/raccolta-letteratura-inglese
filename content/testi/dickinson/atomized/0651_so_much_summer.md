So much [[Summer]]  
Me for showing  
Illegitimate —  
Would a Smile's minute bestowing  
Too exorbitant  

To the Lady  
With the Guinea  
Look — if She should know  
Crumb of Mine  
A Robin's Larder  
Would suffice to stow —
