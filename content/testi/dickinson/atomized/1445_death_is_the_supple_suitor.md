[[Death]] is the supple Suitor  
That wins at last —  
It is a stealthy Wooing  
Conducted first  
By pallid innuendoes  
And dim approach  
But brave at last with Bugles  
And a bisected Coach  
It bears away in triumph  
To Troth unknown  
And Kindred as responsive  
As Porcelain.
