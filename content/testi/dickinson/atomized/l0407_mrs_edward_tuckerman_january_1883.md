The presence in life of so sweet an one is of itself fortune—a covert wealth of spirit I shall not disclose.

I have taken all the naughty boys, and Vinnie the navy.

What lovely conceits!

Then the little Smyrna in the dish-—how tiny, how affecting—though the heart in the rear not tiny. Oh, no, vast as the [[Sea|sea]].

To caress its billows is our liquid aim.
