<span class="atom-split" data-atom="0359_i_gained_it_so" data-title="359. I gained it so —" data-chapter="" data-kind="intro"></span>


I gained it so —  
By Climbing slow —  
By Catching at the Twigs that grow  
Between the Bliss — and me —  
It hung so high  
As [[Well|well]] the Sky  
Attempt by Strategy —  

I said I gained it —  
This — was all —  
Look, how I clutch it  
Lest it fall —  
And I a Pauper go —  
Unfitted by an instant's Grace  
For the Contented — Beggar's face  
I wore — an hour ago —
