<span class="atom-split" data-atom="0045_theres_something_quieter_than_sleep" data-title="45. There's something quieter than sleep" data-chapter="" data-kind="intro" data-tags="motif/death,motif/silence,motif/birds,motif/sleep,concept/mortality,concept/grief_and_loss,form/lyric,cluster/death,cluster/silence,cluster/bells"></span>


There's something quieter than [[motifs/sleep|sleep]]  
Within this inner room!  
It wears a sprig upon its breast —  
And will not tell its name.  

Some touch it, and some kiss it —  
Some chafe its idle hand —  
It has a simple gravity  
I do not understand!  

I would not weep if I were they —  
How rude in one to sob!  
Might scare the quiet fairy  
Back to her native wood!  

While simple-hearted neighbors  
Chat of the "Early dead" —  
We — prone to periphrasis  
Remark that [[motifs/birds|Birds]] have fled!




<span class="atom-split" data-atom="0053_taken_from_men_this_morning" data-title="53. Taken from men — this morning —" data-chapter="" data-kind="intro" data-tags="archetype/child,motif/death,concept/immortality,concept/grief_and_loss,form/elegy,histref/christianity,cluster/death,cluster/silence,cluster/bells"></span>


Taken from men — this morning —  
Carried by men today —  
Met by the Gods with banners —  
Who marshalled her away —  

One little maid — from playmates —  
One little mind from school —  
There must be guests in Eden —  
All the rooms are full —  

Far — as the East from Even —  
Dim — as the border star —  
Courtiers quaint, in Kingdoms  
Our departed are.




<span class="atom-split" data-atom="0058_delayed_till_she_had_ceased_to_know" data-title="58. Delayed till she had ceased to know —" data-chapter="" data-kind="intro" data-tags="motif/death,motif/crown,concept/grief_and_loss,concept/paradox,form/elegy,cluster/death,cluster/silence,cluster/bells"></span>


Delayed till she had ceased to know —  
Delayed till in its vest of [[motifs/snow|snow]]  
Her loving bosom lay —  
An hour behind the fleeting breath —  
Later by just an hour than [[motifs/death|Death]] —  
Oh lagging Yesterday!  

Could she have guessed that it would be —  
Could but a crier of the [[concepts/joy|joy]]  
Have climbed the distant [[motifs/hill|hill]] —  
Had not the bliss so slow a pace  
Who knows but this surrendered face  
Were undefeated still?  

Oh if there may departing be  
Any forgot by Victory  
In her imperial round —  
Show them this meek appareled thing  
That could not stop to be a [[archetypes/king|king]] —  
Doubtful if it be crowned!




<span class="atom-split" data-atom="0071_a_throe_upon_the_features" data-title="71. A throe upon the features —" data-chapter="" data-kind="intro" data-tags="topos/memento_mori,motif/death,concept/mortality,concept/grief_and_loss,form/lyric,cluster/death,cluster/silence,cluster/bells"></span>


A throe upon the features —  
A hurry in the breath —  
An ecstasy of parting  
Denominated "[[motifs/death|Death]]" —  

An anguish at the mention  
Which when to patience grown,  
I've known permission given  
To rejoin its own.

Glowing is her Bonnet,  
Glowing is her Cheek,  
Glowing is her Kirtle,  
Yet she cannot speak.

<span class="atom-split" data-atom="0093_went_up_a_year_this_evening" data-title="93. Went up a year this evening!  
I recollect it [[motifs/well|well]]!  
Amid no [[motifs/bells|bells]] nor bravoes  
The bystanders will tell!  
Cheerful — as to the village —  
Tranquil — as to repose —  
Chastened — as to the Chapel  
This humble Tourist [[motifs/rose|rose]]!  
Did not talk of returning!  
Alluded to no [[motifs/time|time]]  
When, were the gales propitious —  
We might look for him!  
Was grateful for the Roses  
In life's diverse bouquet —  
Talked softly of new species  
To pick another day;  
Beguiling thus the [[concepts/wonder|wonder]]  
The wondrous nearer drew —  
Hands bustled at the moorings —  
The [[motifs/crown|crown]] respectful grew —  
Ascended from our vision  
To Countenances new!  
A Difference — A Daisy —  
Is all the rest I knew!

<span class="atom-split" data-atom="0426_it_dont_sound_so_terrible_quite_as_it_did" data-title="426. —  

It don't sound so terrible — quite — as it did —  
I run it over — "Dead", Brain, "Dead."  
Put it in Latin — left of my school —  
Seems it don't shriek so — under rule.  

Turn it, a little — full in the face  
A Trouble looks bitterest —  
Shift it — just —  
Say "When Tomorrow comes this way —  
I shall have waded down one Day."  

I suppose it will interrupt me some  
Till I get accustomed — but then the Tomb  
Like other new Things — shows largest — then —  
And smaller, by Habit —  

It's shrewder then  
Put the Thought in advance — a Year —  
How like "a fit" — then —  
Murder — wear!

DEAR FRIEND,—The only balmless wound is the departed human life we had learned to need.

For that, even [[concepts/immortality|Immortality]] is a slow solace. All other peace has many roots and will [[motifs/spring|spring]] again.

With cheer from one who knows.

After Mrs. Jackson's [[motifs/death|death]], August 12, 1885, Emily wrote of her:—

[[characters/helen|Helen]] of Troy will die, but [[characters/helen|Helen]] of Colorado, never. Dear friend, can you walk, were the last words that I wrote her.

Dear friend, I can fly— Her immortal reply.




<span class="atom-split" data-atom="l0537_mr_c_h_clark" data-title="To Mr. C. H. Clark" data-chapter="" data-kind="intro" data-tags="motif/death,concept/death,concept/immortality,concept/friendship,form/letter,cluster/death,cluster/silence,cluster/bells"></span>


This [[forms/letter|letter]], in her bold, clear, detached hand (to an accustomed eye slightly less firm than usual), is the last one, so far as known, which Emily Dickinson wrote. Afterward, she continued to be delicate, though hardly alarmingly so; but just before the fifteenth of May, 1886, she sent two words to her cousins, freighted with startling import,—

LITLLE COUSINS,—Called back.
