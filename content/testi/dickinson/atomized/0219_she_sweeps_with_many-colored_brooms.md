<span class="atom-split" data-atom="0219_she_sweeps_with_many-colored_brooms" data-title="219. She sweeps with many-colored Brooms —" data-chapter="" data-kind="intro"></span>


She sweeps with many-colored Brooms —  
And [[Leaves|leaves]] the Shreds behind —  
Oh Housewife in the Evening West —  
Come back, and [[Dust|dust]] the Pond!  

You dropped a Purple Ravelling in —  
You dropped an Amber thread —  
And how you've littered all the East  
With duds of Emerald!  

And still, she plies her spotted Brooms,  
And still the Aprons fly,  
Till Brooms fade softly into [[Stars|stars]] —  
And then I come away —
