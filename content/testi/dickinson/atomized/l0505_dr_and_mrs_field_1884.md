Expulsion from Eden grows indistinct in the presence of [[Flowers|flowers]] so blissful, and with no disrespect to Genesis, Paradise remains.

Beaconsfield says “the [[Time|time]] has now come when it must be decided forever, who possesses the great gates to India.”

I think it must be my neighbor.
