<span class="atom-split" data-atom="0320_we_play_at_paste" data-title="320. We play at Paste —" data-chapter="" data-kind="intro"></span>


We play at Paste —  
Till qualified, for Pearl —  
Then, drop the Paste —  
And deem ourself a [[Fool|fool]] —  

The Shapes — though — were similar —  
And our new Hands  
Learned Gem-Tactics —  
Practicing Sands —


<span class="qlang-split" data-lang="it"></span>

Giochiamo a Pasta di vetro —  
Finché degni, di Perla —  
Poi, lasciamo la Pasta —  
E ci stimiamo [[Fool|folli]] —  

Le Forme — però — erano simili —  
E le nostre nuove Mani  
Appresero Tattiche di Gemma —  
Esercitandosi a Sabbie —
