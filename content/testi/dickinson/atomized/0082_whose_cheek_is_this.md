<span class="atom-split" data-atom="0082_whose_cheek_is_this" data-title="82. Whose cheek is this?" data-chapter="" data-kind="intro"></span>


Whose cheek is this?  
What rosy face  
Has lost a blush today?  
I found her — "pleiad" — in the woods  
And bore her safe away.  

Robins, in the [[Tradition|tradition]]  
Did cover such with [[Leaves|leaves]],  
But which the cheek —  
And which the pall  
My scrutiny deceives.
