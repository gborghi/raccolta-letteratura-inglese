<span class="atom-split" data-atom="0017_baffled_for_just_a_day_or_two" data-title="17. Baffled for just a day or two —  
Embarrassed — not afraid —  
Encounter in my [[motifs/garden|garden]]  
An unexpected Maid.

<span class="atom-split" data-atom="0022_all_these_my_banners_be" data-title="22. All these my banners be." data-chapter="" data-kind="intro" data-tags="motif/flowers,motif/spring,motif/the_grave,concept/joy,concept/immortality,form/lyric,setting/a_garden,cluster/flowers,cluster/spring,cluster/garden"></span>


All these my banners be.  
I sow my pageantry  
In May —  
It rises train by train —  
Then sleeps in state again —  
My chancel — all the plain  
Today.  

To lose — if one can find again —  
To miss — if one shall meet —  
The Burglar cannot rob — then —  
The Broker cannot cheat.  
So build the hillocks gaily  
Thou little spade of mine  
Leaving nooks for Daisy  
And for Columbine —  
You and I the secret  
Of the Crocus know —  
Let us chant it softly —  
"There is no more [[motifs/snow|snow]]!"  

To him who keeps an Orchis' heart —  
The swamps are pink with June.

<span class="atom-split" data-atom="0064_some_rainbow_coming_from_the_fair" data-title="64. Some Rainbow — coming from the Fair!  
Some Vision of the World Cashmere —  
I confidently see!  
Or else a Peacock's purple Train  
Feather by feather — on the plain  
Fritters itself away!  

The dreamy Butterflies bestir!  
Lethargic pools resume the whir  
Of last year's sundered tune!  
From some old Fortress on the [[motifs/sun|sun]]  
Baronial [[motifs/bees|Bees]] — march — one by one —  
In murmuring platoon!  

The Robins stand as thick today  
As flakes of [[motifs/snow|snow]] stood yesterday —  
On fence — and Roof — and Twig!  
The Orchis binds her feather on  
For her old [[archetypes/lover|lover]] - Don the [[motifs/sun|Sun]]!  
Revisiting the Bog!  

Without Commander! Countless! Still!  
The Regiments of Wood and [[motifs/hill|Hill]]  
In bright detachment stand!  
Behold! Whose Multitudes are these?  
The children of whose turbaned seas —  
Or what Circassian Land?

Our little [[motifs/garden|garden]] that we lost

So bashful when I spied her,  
So pretty, so ashamed !  
So hidden in her leaflets,  
Lest anybody find ;
