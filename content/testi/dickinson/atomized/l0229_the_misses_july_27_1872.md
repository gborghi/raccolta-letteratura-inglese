Little Irish Maggie went to [[Sleep|sleep]] this morning at six o'[[Clock|clock]], just the [[Time|time]] grandpa rises, and will rest in the grass at Northampton to-morrow. She has had a hard sickness, but her awkward little life is saved and gallant now. Our Maggie is helping her mother put her in the cradle. . . .

Month after this—after that is October, isn't it? That isn't much long. [[Joy]] to enfold our little girls in so close a future. That was a lovely [[Letter|letter]] of F——'s. It put the cat to playing and the kettle to purring, and two or three [[Birds|birds]] in plush teams reined nearer to the [[Window|window]]. . . . You will miss the nasturtiums, but you will meet the chestnuts. You also will miss the south [[Wind|wind]], but I will save the west. . . .

Of course we shall have a telegram that you have left for Nebraska. . . .
