In rags mysterious as these  
The shining Courtiers go —  
Veiling the purple, and the plumes —  
Veiling the ermine so.  

Smiling, as they request an alms —  
At some imposing [[motifs/door|door]]!  
Smiling when we walk barefoot  
Upon their golden floor!




<span class="atom-split" data-atom="0119_talk_with_prudence_to_a_beggar" data-title="119. Talk with prudence to a Beggar" data-chapter="" data-kind="intro" data-tags="topos/the_unattainable_beloved,archetype/outcast_wanderer,motif/money_gold,motif/wine_and_drink,concept/poverty,concept/melancholy,form/epigram,cluster/money_and_gold,cluster/poverty,cluster/imprisonment"></span>


Talk with prudence to a Beggar  
Of "Potose," and the mines!  
Reverently, to the Hungry  
Of your viands, and your wines!  

Cautious, hint to any Captive  
You have passed enfranchised feet!  
Anecdotes of air in Dungeons  
Have sometimes proved deadly sweet!




<span class="atom-split" data-atom="0234_youre_right_the_way_is_narrow" data-title="234. You're right — &quot;the way is narrow&quot; —" data-chapter="" data-kind="intro" data-tags="motif/money_gold,motif/the_grave,concept/history,concept/faith_and_doubt,concept/satire_of_society,form/satire,histref/the_bible,histref/christianity,histref/puritanism,cluster/money_and_gold,cluster/poverty,cluster/imprisonment"></span>


You're right — "the way is narrow" —  
And "difficult the Gate" —  
And "few there be" — Correct again —  
That "enter in — thereat" —  

'Tis Costly — So are purples!  
'Tis just the price of Breath —  
With but the "Discount" of the [[motifs/grave|Grave]] —  
Termed by the Brokers — "[[motifs/death|Death]]"!  

And after that — there's Heaven —  
The Good Man's — "Dividend" —  
And Bad Men — "go to Jail" —  
I guess —




<span class="atom-split" data-atom="0395_reverse_cannot_befall" data-title="395. Reverse cannot befall" data-chapter="" data-kind="intro" data-tags="motif/money_gold,concept/self_and_soul,concept/joy,concept/immortality,concept/paradox,cluster/money_and_gold,cluster/poverty,cluster/imprisonment"></span>


Reverse cannot befall  
That fine Prosperity  
Whose Sources are interior —  
As soon — Adversity  

A Diamond — overtake  
In far — Bolivian Ground —  
Misfortune hath no implement  
Could mar it — if it found —




<span class="atom-split" data-atom="0543_i_fear_a_man_of_frugal_speech" data-title="543. I fear a Man of frugal Speech —" data-chapter="" data-kind="intro" data-tags="motif/silence,motif/money_gold,concept/power_and_corruption,concept/appearance_vs_reality,concept/identity,form/epigram,cluster/money_and_gold,cluster/poverty,cluster/imprisonment"></span>


I [[concepts/fear|fear]] a Man of frugal Speech —  
I [[concepts/fear|fear]] a Silent Man —  
Haranguer — I can overtake —  
Or Babbler — entertain —  

But He who weigheth — While the Rest —  
Expend their furthest pound —  
Of this Man — I am wary —  
I [[concepts/fear|fear]] that He is Grand —




<span class="atom-split" data-atom="0709_publication_is_the_auction" data-title="709. Publication — is the Auction" data-chapter="" data-kind="intro" data-tags="motif/money_gold,motif/letters_writing,concept/the_artist_and_society,concept/immortality_of_art,concept/pride,concept/poverty,form/lyric,histref/christianity,cluster/money_and_gold,cluster/poverty,cluster/imprisonment"></span>


Publication — is the Auction  
Of the Mind of Man —  
[[concepts/poverty|Poverty]] — be justifying  
For so foul a thing  

Possibly — but We — would rather  
From Our Garret go  
White — Unto the White Creator —  
Than invest — Our [[motifs/snow|Snow]] —  

Thought belong to Him who gave it —  
Then — to Him Who bear  
Its Corporeal illustration — Sell  
The Royal Air —  

In the Parcel — Be the Merchant  
Of the Heavenly Grace —  
But reduce no Human Spirit  
To Disgrace of Price —




<span class="atom-split" data-atom="0801_i_play_at_riches_to_appease" data-title="801. I play at Riches — to appease" data-chapter="" data-kind="intro" data-tags="motif/money_gold,concept/poverty,concept/desire,concept/paradox,concept/temptation,form/lyric,cluster/money_and_gold,cluster/poverty,cluster/imprisonment"></span>


I play at Riches — to appease  
The Clamoring for Gold —  
It kept me from a Thief, I think,  
For often, overbold  

With Want, and Opportunity —  
I could have done a Sin  
And been Myself that easy Thing  
An independent Man —  

But often as my lot displays  
Too hungry to be borne  
I deem Myself what I would be —  
And [[forms/novel|novel]] Comforting  

My [[concepts/poverty|Poverty]] and I derive —  
We question if the Man —  
Who own — Esteem the Opulence —  
As We — Who never Can —  

Should ever these exploring Hands  
Chance Sovereign on a Mine —  
Or in the long — uneven term  
To win, become their turn —  

How fitter they will be — for Want —  
Enlightening so [[motifs/well|well]] —  
I know not which, [[motifs/desire|Desire]], or Grant —  
Be wholly beautiful —




<span class="atom-split" data-atom="1093_because_twas_riches_i_could_own" data-title="1093. Because 'twas Riches I could own," data-chapter="" data-kind="intro" data-tags="motif/money_gold,concept/value_and_scarcity,concept/desire,concept/paradox,form/lyric,cluster/money_and_gold,cluster/poverty,cluster/imprisonment"></span>


Because 'twas Riches I could own,  
Myself had earned it — Me,  
I knew the Dollars by their names —  
It feels like [[concepts/poverty|Poverty]]  

An Earldom out of sight to hold,  
An Income in the Air,  
Possession — has a sweeter chink  
Unto a Miser's Ear —




<span class="atom-split" data-atom="1446_his_mind_like_fabrics_of_the_east" data-title="1446. His Mind like Fabrics of the East" data-chapter="" data-kind="intro" data-tags="motif/money_gold,concept/truth,concept/imagination,concept/the_artist_and_society,form/lyric,cluster/money_and_gold,cluster/poverty,cluster/imprisonment"></span>


His Mind like Fabrics of the East  
Displayed to the [[concepts/despair|despair]]  
Of everyone but here and there  
An humble Purchaser —  
For though his price was not of Gold —  
More arduous there is —  
That one should comprehend the worth  
Was all the price there was —




<span class="atom-split" data-atom="1640_take_all_away_from_me_but_leave_me_ecstasy" data-title="1640. Take all away from me, but leave me Ecstasy," data-chapter="" data-kind="intro" data-tags="concept/joy,concept/poverty,concept/wonder_and_gratitude,concept/paradox,form/epigram,cluster/money_and_gold,cluster/poverty,cluster/imprisonment"></span>


ECSTASY,  

Take all away from me, but leave me Ecstasy,  
And I am richer then than all my Fellow Men —  
Ill it becometh me to dwell so wealthily  
When at my very [[motifs/door|Door]] are those possessing more,  
In abject [[concepts/poverty|poverty]] —
