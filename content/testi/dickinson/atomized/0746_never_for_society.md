Never for Society  
He shall seek in vain —  
Who His own acquaintance  
Cultivate — Of Men  
Wiser Men may weary —  
But the Man within  

Never knew Satiety —  
Better entertain  
Than could Border Ballad —  
Or Biscayan [[Hymn]] —  
Neither introduction  
Need You — unto Him —
