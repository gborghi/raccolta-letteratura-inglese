<span class="atom-split" data-atom="1492_and_with_what_body_do_they_come" data-title="1492. &quot;And with what body do they come?&quot; —" data-chapter="" data-kind="intro"></span>


"And with what [[Body|body]] do they come?" —  
Then they do come — Rejoice!  
What [[Door]] — What Hour — Run — run — My [[Soul]]!  
Illuminate the [[house|House]]!  

"[[Body]]!" Then real — a Face and Eyes —  
To know that it is them!  
Paul knew the Man that knew the News —  
He passed through Bethlehem —
