<span class="atom-split" data-atom="0003_sic_transit_gloria_mundi" data-title="3. Sic transit gloria mundi" data-chapter="" data-kind="intro" data-tags="topos/sic_transit_gloria_mundi,topos/memento_mori,topos/carpe_diem,topos/the_sleeper_awakes,concept/mortality,concept/heroism,form/lyric,histref/classical_antiquity,histref/the_bible,cluster/mortality,cluster/grave,cluster/memento_mori"></span>


[[topoi/sic-transit-gloria-mundi|Sic transit gloria mundi]]  
"How doth the busy bee"  
Dum vivamus vivamus  
I stay mine enemy! —  

Oh veni vidi vici!  
Oh caput cap-a-pie!  
And oh "[[topoi/memento-mori|memento mori]]"  
When I am far from thee  

Hurrah for Peter Parley  
Hurrrah for Daniel Boone  
Three cheers sir, for the gentleman  
Who first observed the [[motifs/moon|moon]] —  

Peter put up the sunshine!  
Pattie arrange the [[motifs/stars|stars]]  
Tell Luna, tea is waiting  
And call your brother Mars —  

Put down the apple Adam  
And come away with me  
So shal't thou have a pippin  
From off my Father's [[motifs/tree|tree]]!  

I climb the "[[motifs/hill|Hill]] of Science"  
I "view the Landscape o'er"  
Such transcendental prospect  
I ne'er beheld before! —  

Unto the Legislature  
4  

My country bids me go,  
I'll take my india rubbers  
In case the [[motifs/wind|wind]] should blow.  

During my [[concepts/education|education]]  
It was announced to me  
That gravitation stumbling  
Fell from an apple [[motifs/tree|tree]] —  

The Earth opon it's axis  
Was once supposed to turn  
By way of a gymnastic  
In honor to the [[motifs/sun|sun]] —  

It was the brave [[characters/columbus|Columbus]]  
A sailing o'er the tide  
Who notified the nations  
Of where I would reside  

[[concepts/mortality|Mortality]] is fatal  
Gentility is fine  
Rascality, heroic  
Insolvency, [[concepts/sublime|sublime]]  

Our Fathers being weary  
Laid down on Bunker [[motifs/hill|Hill]]  
And though full many a morn'g  
Yet they are sleeping still  

The trumpet sir, shall wake them  
In streams I see them rise  
Each with a solemn musket  
A marching to the skies!  

A coward will remain, Sir,  
5  

Until the fight is done;  
But an immortal [[archetypes/hero|hero]]  
Will take his hat and run.  

Good bye Sir, I am going  
My country calleth me  
Allow me Sir, at parting  
To wipe my weeping e'e  

In token of our [[concepts/friendship|friendship]]  
Accept this "Bonnie Doon"  
And when the hand that pluck'd it  
Hath passed beyond the [[motifs/moon|moon]]  

The [[motifs/memory|memory]] of my ashes  
Will consolation be  
Then farewell Tuscarora  
And farewell Sir, to thee.




<span class="atom-split" data-atom="0018_the_gentian_weaves_her_fringes" data-title="18. The Gentian weaves her fringes —" data-chapter="" data-kind="intro" data-tags="motif/autumn,motif/bees,motif/flowers,motif/white_and_red,concept/mortality,concept/faith,form/elegy,histref/christianity,cluster/mortality,cluster/grave,cluster/memento_mori"></span>


The Gentian weaves her fringes —  
The Maple's loom is red —  
My departing blossoms  
Obviate parade.  

A brief, but patient illness —  
An hour to prepare,  
And one below this morning  
Is where the angels are —  
It was a short procession,  
The Bobolink was there —  
An aged Bee addressed us —  
And then we knelt in [[motifs/prayer|prayer]] —  
We trust that she was willing —  
We ask that we may be.  
[[motifs/summer|Summer]] — Sister — Seraph!  
Let us go with thee!  

In the name of the Bee —  
And of the Butterfly —  
And of the Breeze — Amen!

<span class="atom-split" data-atom="0051_i_often_passed_the_village" data-title="51. I often passed the village" data-chapter="" data-kind="intro" data-tags="motif/the_grave,motif/childhood,concept/mortality,concept/grief_and_loss,form/lyric,cluster/mortality,cluster/grave,cluster/memento_mori"></span>


I often passed the village  
When going [[motifs/home|home]] from school —  
And wondered what they did there —  
And why it was so still —  

I did not know the year then —  
In which my call would come —  
Earlier, by the Dial,  
Than the rest have gone.  

It's stiller than the sundown.  
It's cooler than the [[motifs/dawn|dawn]] —  
The Daisies dare to come here —  
And [[motifs/birds|birds]] can flutter down —  

So when you are tired —  
Or perplexed — or cold —  
Trust the loving promise  
Underneath the mould,  
Cry "it's I," "take Dollie,"  
And I will enfold!

If I should die,  
And you should live —  
And [[motifs/time|time]] should gurgle on —  
And morn should beam —  
And [[motifs/noon|noon]] should burn —  
As it has usual done —  
If [[motifs/birds|Birds]] should build as early

From enterprise below!

When we with Daisies lie —  
That Commerce will continue —  
And Trades as briskly fly —  
It makes the parting tranquil  
And keeps the [[motifs/soul|soul]] serene —  
That gentlemen so sprightly  
Conduct the pleasing scene!

<span class="atom-split" data-atom="0216_safe_in_their_alabaster_chambers" data-title="216. Safe in their Alabaster Chambers —  
Untouched by Morning  
And untouched by [[motifs/noon|Noon]] —  
[[motifs/sleep|Sleep]] the meek members of the [[concepts/resurrection|Resurrection]] —  
Rafter of satin,  
And Roof of stone.  

Light laughs the breeze

'Twas like a Maelstrom, with a notch,  
That nearer, every Day,  
Kept narrowing its boiling [[motifs/wheel|Wheel]]  
Until the Agony  

Toyed coolly with the final inch  
Of your delirious Hem —  
And you dropt, lost,  
When something broke —  
And let you from a Dream —  

As if a Goblin with a Gauge —  
Kept measuring the Hours —  
Until you felt your Second  
Weigh, helpless, in his Paws —  

And not a Sinew — stirred — could help,  
And sense was setting numb —  
When [[characters/god|God]] — remembered — and the Fiend  
Let go, then, Overcome —
