<span class="atom-split" data-atom="0052_whether_my_bark_went_down_at_sea" data-title="52. Whether my bark went down at sea —" data-chapter="" data-kind="intro"></span>


Whether my bark went down at [[Sea|sea]],  
Whether she met with gales,  
Whether to isles enchanted  
She bent her docile sails ;  

By what mystic mooring  
She is held to-day, —  
This is the errand of the eye  
Out upon the bay.
