Fortitude incarnate  
Here is laid away  
In the swift Partitions  
Of the awful [[Sea]] —  

Babble of the Happy  
Cavil of the Bold  
Hoary the Fruition  
But the [[Sea]] is old  

Edifice of Ocean  
Thy tumultuous Rooms  
Suit me at a venture  
Better than the Tombs
