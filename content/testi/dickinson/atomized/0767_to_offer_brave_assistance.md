<span class="atom-split" data-atom="0767_to_offer_brave_assistance" data-title="767. To offer brave assistance" data-chapter="" data-kind="intro"></span>


To offer brave assistance  
To Lives that stand [[Alone|alone]] —  
When One has failed to stop them —  
Is Human — but Divine  

To lend an Ample Sinew  
Unto a Nameless Man —  
Whose Homely Benediction  
No other — stopped to earn —
