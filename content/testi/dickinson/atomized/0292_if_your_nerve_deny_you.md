If your Nerve, deny you —  
Go above your Nerve —  
He can lean against the [[Grave]],  
If he [[Fear|fear]] to swerve —  

That's a steady posture —  
Never any bend  
Held of those Brass arms —  
Best Giant made —  

If your [[Soul]] seesaw —  
Lift the Flesh [[Door|door]] —  
The Poltroon wants Oxygen —  
Nothing more —
