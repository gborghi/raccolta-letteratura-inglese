DEAR FRIEND,—The Amherst heart is plain and whole and permanent and warm.

In [[Childhood|childhood]] I never sowed a seed unless it was perennial —and that is why my [[Garden|garden]] lasts.

We dare not trust ourselves to know that you indeed have left us.

The fiction is sufficient [[Pain|pain]]. To know you better as you flee, may be our recompense.

I [[Hope|hope]] that you are [[Well|well]], and nothing mars your peace but its divinity—for ecstasy is peril.
