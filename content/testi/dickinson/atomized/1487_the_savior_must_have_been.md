The Savior must have been  
A docile Gentleman —  
To come so far so cold a Day  
For little Fellowmen —  

The [[Road]] to Bethlehem  
Since He and I were Boys  
Was leveled, but for that 'twould be  
A rugged billion [[Miles]] —
