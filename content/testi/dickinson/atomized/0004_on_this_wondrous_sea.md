<span class="atom-split" data-atom="0004_on_this_wondrous_sea" data-title="4. On this wondrous sea" data-chapter="" data-kind="intro"></span>


Write! Comrade, write!  

On this wondrous [[Sea|sea]]  
Sailing silently,  
Ho! Pilot, ho!  
Knowest thou the shore  
Where no breakers roar -  
Where the [[Storm|storm]] is o'er?  

In the peaceful west  
Many the sails at rest -  
The anchors fast -  
Thither I pilot thee -  
Land Ho! [[Eternity]]!  
Ashore at last!
