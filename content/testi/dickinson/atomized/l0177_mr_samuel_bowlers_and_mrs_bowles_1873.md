DEAR FRIEND,—It was so delicious to see you—a peach before the [[Time|time]]—it makes all [[Seasons|seasons]] possible, and zones a caprice.

We, who arraign the Arabian Nights for their understatement, escape the stale sagacity of supposing them sham.

We miss your vivid face, and the besetting accents you bring from your Numidian haunts.

Your coming welds anew that strange trinket of life which each of us wear and none of us own; and the phosphorescence of yours startles us for its permanence.

Please rest the life so many own—for gems abscond.

In your own beautiful words—for the voice is the palace of all of us,—

“Near, but remote.”
