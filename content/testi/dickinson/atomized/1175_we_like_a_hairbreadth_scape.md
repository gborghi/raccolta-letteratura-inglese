We like a Hairbreadth 'scape  
It tingles in the Mind  
Far after Act or Accident  
Like paragraphs of [[Wind]]  

If we had ventured less  
The Breeze were not so fine  
That reaches to our utmost Hair  
Its Tentacles divine.
