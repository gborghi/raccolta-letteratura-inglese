. . . just a word for my children, before the mails shut. L——left a tumbler of sweet-peas on the green room bureau. I am going to leave them there till they make pods and sow themselves in the upper drawer, and then I guess they'll, blossom about Thanksgiving [[Time|time]]. There was a thundershower here Saturday at car-[[Time|time]], and Emily was glad her little ones had gone before the hail and [[Rain|rain]], lest it frighten, them. . . . We wish the visit had just begun instead of ending now; next [[Time|time]] we'll leave “the [[Mountains|mountains]]” out, and tell good Dr. Gregg to recommend the orchards. I defrauded L——of i spool of thread; we will “settle,” however—and F——'s ruff is set high in my book of remembrance. They must be good children and recollect, as they agreed, and grow so strong in health that Emily won't know them when they show again. . . . Such a purple morning—even to the morning-glory that climbs the cherry-[[Tree|tree]]. The cats [[Desire|desire]] [[Love|love]] to F——.
