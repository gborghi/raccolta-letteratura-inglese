<span class="atom-split" data-atom="0409_they_dropped_like_flakes" data-title="409. They dropped like Flakes —" data-chapter="" data-kind="intro"></span>


They dropped like Flakes —  
They dropped like [[Stars]] —  
Like Petals from a [[Rose]] —  
When suddenly across the June  
A [[Wind|wind]] with fingers — goes —  

They perished in the Seamless Grass —  
No eye could find the place —  
But [[God]] can summon every face  
Of his Repealless — List.

Perirono nell'Erba Senza Cuciture —  
Nessun occhio poteva trovare il luogo —  
Ma [[God|Dio]] può convocare ogni volto  
Della sua Irrevocabile — Lista.
