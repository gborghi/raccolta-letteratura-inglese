The robin is a Gabriel In humble circumstances, His dress denotes him socially Of transport's working classes. He has the punctuality Of the [[New England]] farmer— The same oblique integrity, A vista vastly warmer. A small but sturdy residence, A [[Self|self]]-denying household, The guests of perspicacity Are all that [[Cross|cross]] his threshold. As covert as a fugitive, Cajoling consternation By ditties to the enemy. And sylvan punctuation.
