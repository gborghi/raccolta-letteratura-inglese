[[Well]], Austin, dear Austin, you have got back again, codfish and pork and all—all but the slippers, so nicely wrapped to take, yet found when you were gone under the kitchen chair. I [[Hope|hope]] you won't want them. Perhaps you have some more there—I will send them by opportunity, should there be such a thing. Vinnie proposed franking them, but I [[Fear|fear]] they are rather large! What should you think of it? It isn't every day that we have a chance to sponge Congress, . . . but Cæsar is such “an honorable man” that we may all go to the poorhouse for all the American Congress will lift a finger to help us. . . .

The usual rush of callers, and this beleaguered [[Family|family]] as yet in want of [[Time|time]]. I do [[Hope|hope]] [[Immortality|immortality]] will last a little while, but if the A——s should happen to get there first, we shall be driven there. . . .
