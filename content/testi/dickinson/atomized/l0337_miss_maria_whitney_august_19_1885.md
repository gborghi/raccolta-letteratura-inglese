DEAR FRIEND,—May I ask a service so sacred as that you will address and mail a note to the friend of my friend Mrs. Jackson? I do not know Mr. Jackson's address, and [[Desire|desire]] to write him.

That your loved confederate and yourself are in ceaseless peace is my happy [[Faith|faith]].

The sweet-peas you hallowed stand in carmine sheaves. Would that you could plunder them.
