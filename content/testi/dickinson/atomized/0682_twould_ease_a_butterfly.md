'Twould ease — a Butterfly —  
Elate — a Bee —  
Thou'rt neither —  
Neither — thy capacity —  

But, Blossom, were I,  
I would rather be  
Thy moment  
Than a Bee's [[Eternity]] —  

Content of fading  
Is enough for me —  
Fade I unto Divinity —  

And Dying — Lifetime —  
Ample as the Eye —  
Her least attention raise on me —
