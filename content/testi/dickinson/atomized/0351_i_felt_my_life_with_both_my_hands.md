<span class="atom-split" data-atom="0351_i_felt_my_life_with_both_my_hands" data-title="351. I felt my life with both my hands" data-chapter="" data-kind="intro"></span>


I felt my life with both my hands  
To see if it was there —  
I held my spirit to the Glass,  
To prove it possibler —  

I turned my Being round and round  
And paused at every pound  
To ask the Owner's name —  
For [[Doubt|doubt]], that I should know the Sound —  

I judged my features — jarred my hair —  
I pushed my dimples by, and waited —  
If they — twinkled back —  
Conviction might, of me —  

I told myself, "Take [[Courage]], Friend —  
That — was a former [[Time|time]] —  
But we might learn to like the Heaven,  
As [[Well|well]] as our Old [[Home]]!"
