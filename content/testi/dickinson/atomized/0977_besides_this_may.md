<span class="atom-split" data-atom="0977_besides_this_may" data-title="977. Besides this May" data-chapter="" data-kind="intro"></span>


Besides this May  
We know  
There is Another —  
How fair  
Our Speculations of the Foreigner!  

Some know Him whom We knew —  
Sweet [[Wonder]] —  
A [[Nature]] be  
Where Saints, and our plain going Neighbor  
Keep May!
