I trust the Procession of [[Flowers]] was not a premonition.

Of this curious [[Letter|letter]] Colonel Higginson wrote: “Mr. Howells reminds me that Swedenborg somewhere has an image akin to her 'oblique place,' where he symbolizes evil as simply an oblique angle.”
