Me from Myself — to banish —  
Had I Art —  
Impregnable my Fortress  
Unto All Heart —  

But since Myself — assault Me —  
How have I peace  
Except by subjugating  
Consciousness?  

And since We're mutual Monarch  
How this be  
Except by Abdication —  
Me — of Me?
