<span class="atom-split" data-atom="1187_oh_shadow_on_the_grass" data-title="1187. Oh Shadow on the Grass," data-chapter="" data-kind="intro"></span>


Oh [[Shadow]] on the Grass,  
Art thou a Step or not?  
Go make thee fair my Candidate  
My nominated Heart —  
Oh [[Shadow]] on the Grass  
While I delay to guess  
Some other thou wilt consecrate —  
Oh Unelected Face —
