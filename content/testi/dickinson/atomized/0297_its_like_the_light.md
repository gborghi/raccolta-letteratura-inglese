<span class="atom-split" data-atom="0297_its_like_the_light" data-title="297. It's like the Light —" data-chapter="" data-kind="intro"></span>


It's like the Light —  
A fashionless Delight —  
It's like the Bee —  
A dateless — Melody —  

It's like the Woods —  
Private — Like the Breeze —  
Phraseless — yet it stirs  
The proudest Trees —  

It's like the Morning —  
Best — when it's done —  
And the Everlasting Clocks —  
Chime — [[Noon]]!
