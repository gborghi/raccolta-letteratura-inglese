There is an arid Pleasure —  
As different from [[Joy]] —  
As [[Frost]] is different from [[Dew]] —  
Like element — are they —  

Yet one — rejoices [[Flowers]] —  
And one — the [[Flowers]] abhor —  
The finest Honey — curdled —  
Is worthless — to the Bee —
