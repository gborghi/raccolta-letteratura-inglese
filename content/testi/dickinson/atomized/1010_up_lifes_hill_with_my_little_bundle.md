Up Life's [[Hill]] with my little Bundle  
If I prove it steep —  
If a Discouragement withhold me —  
If my newest step  

Older feel than the [[Hope]] that prompted —  
Spotless be from blame  
Heart that proposed as Heart that accepted  
Homelessness, for [[Home]] —
