<span class="atom-split" data-atom="0063_if_pain_for_peace_prepares" data-title="63. If pain for peace prepares" data-chapter="" data-kind="intro"></span>


If [[Pain|pain]] for peace prepares  
Lo, what "Augustan" years  
Our feet await!  

If springs from [[Winter|winter]] rise,  
Can the Anemones  
Be reckoned up?  

If [[Night|night]] stands fast — then [[Noon|noon]]  
To gird us for the [[Sun|sun]],  
What gaze!  

When from a thousand skies  
On our developed eyes  
Noons blaze!
