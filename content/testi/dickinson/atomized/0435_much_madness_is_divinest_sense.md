Much [[Madness|madness]] is divinest sense  
To a discerning eye ;  
Much sense the starkest [[Madness|madness]].  
'T is the majority  
In this, as all, prevails.  
Assent, and you are sane ;  
Demur, — you're straightway dangerous,  
And handled with a chain.
