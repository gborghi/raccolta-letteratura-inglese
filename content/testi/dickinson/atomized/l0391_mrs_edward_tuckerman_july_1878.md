Would it be prudent to subject an apparitional interview to a grosser test?

The [[Bible]] portentously says “that which is spirit is spirit.”

Go not too near a house of [[Rose|rose]], The depredation of a breeze Or inundation of a [[Dew|dew]] Alarm its walls away; Nor try to tie the butterfly, Nor climb the bars of ecstasy, In insecurity to lie Is [[Joy|joy]]'s insuring quality.
