<span class="atom-split" data-atom="0536_the_heart_asks_pleasure_first" data-title="536. The Heart asks Pleasure — first —" data-chapter="" data-kind="intro"></span>


The heart asks pleasure first,  
And then, excuse from [[Pain|pain]] ;  
And then, those little anodynes  
That deaden [[Suffering|suffering]] ;  

And then, to go to [[Sleep|sleep]] ;  
And then, if it should be  
The will of its Inquisitor,  
The [[Liberty|liberty]] to die.
