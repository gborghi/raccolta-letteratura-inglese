Revolution is the Pod  
Systems rattle from  
When the Winds of Will are stirred  
Excellent is Bloom  

But except its Russet Base  
Every [[Summer]] be  
The Entomber of itself,  
So of [[Liberty]] —  

Left inactive on the Stalk  
All its Purple fled  
Revolution shakes it for  
Test if it be dead.
