She dwelleth in the Ground —  
Where Daffodils — abide —  
Her Maker — Her Metropolis —  
The Universe — Her Maid —  

To fetch Her Grace — and Hue —  
And Fairness — and Renown —  
The Firmament's — To Pluck Her —  
And fetch Her Thee — be mine —
