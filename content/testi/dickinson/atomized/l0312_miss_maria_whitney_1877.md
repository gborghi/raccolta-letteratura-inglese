Vinnie and her sister thank Miss Whitney for the delicate kindness, and remember her with peculiar [[Love|love]] these acuter days. . . .

I [[Fear|fear]] we think too lightly of the gift of [[Mortality|mortality]], which, too gigantic to comprehend, certainly cannot be estimated.
