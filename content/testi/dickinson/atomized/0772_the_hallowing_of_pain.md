The hallowing of [[Pain]]  
Like hallowing of Heaven,  
Obtains at a corporeal cost —  
The Summit is not given  

To Him who strives severe  
At middle of the [[Hill]] —  
But He who has achieved the Top —  
All — is the price of All —
