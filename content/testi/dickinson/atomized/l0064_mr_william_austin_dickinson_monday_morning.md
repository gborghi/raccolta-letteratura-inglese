MY DEAR AUSTIN,—. . . The New [[London]] day passed off grandly, so all the people said. It was pretty hot and dusty, but nobody cared for that. Father was, as usual, chief marshal of the day, and went marching around with New [[London]] at his heels like some old Roman general upon a triumph day. Mrs. H. got a capital dinner, and was very much praised. Carriages flew like sparks, hither and thither and yon, and they all said 'twas fine. I 'spose' it was. I sat in Professor Tyler's woods and saw the train move off, and then came [[Home|home]] again for [[Fear|fear]] somebody would see me, or ask me how I did. Dr. Holland was here, and called to see us—was very pleasant indeed, inquired for you, and asked mother if Vinnie and I might come and see them in Springfield. . . . We all send you our [[Love|love]].
