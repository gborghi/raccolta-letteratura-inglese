DEAR FRIEND,—I have thought of you often since the darkness,—though we cannot assist another's [[Night|night]]. I have hoped you were saved. That he has received [[Immortality]] who so often conferred it, invests it with a more sudden charm. . . .

I [[Hope|hope]] you have the power of [[Hope|hope]], and that every bliss we know or guess hourly befalls you.
