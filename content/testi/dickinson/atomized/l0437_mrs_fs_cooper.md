DEAR FRIEND,—Nothing inclusive of a human heart could be “trivial.” That appalling boon makes all things paltry but itself.

To thank you would profane you—there are moments when even gratitude is a desecration.

Go, thy great way! The [[Stars|stars]] thou meetest Are even as thyself. For what are [[Stars|stars]] but asterisks To point a human life?
