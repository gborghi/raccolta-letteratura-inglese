Like her the Saints retire,  
In their Chapeaux of [[Fire|fire]],  
Martial as she!  

Like her the Evenings steal  
Purple and Cochineal  
After the Day!  

"Departed" — both — they say!  
i.e. gathered away,  
Not found,  

Argues the Aster still —  
Reasons the Daffodil  
Profound!
