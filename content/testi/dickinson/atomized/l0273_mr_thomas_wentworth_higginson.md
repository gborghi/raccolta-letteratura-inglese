DEAR FRIEND,—You were so generous to me, that if possible I offended you, I could not too deeply apologize.

To [[Doubt|doubt]] my behavior is a new [[Pain|pain]]. I could be honorable no more, till I asked you about it. I know not what to deem myself—yesterday “your scholar,” but might I be the one you to-[[Night|night]] forgave, 'tis a better honor. Mine is but just the thief's request.
