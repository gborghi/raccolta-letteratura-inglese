DEAR FRIEND,—You have the most triumphant face out of Paradise, probably because you are there constantly, instead of ultimately.

Ourselves we do inter with sweet derision the channel of the [[Dust|dust]]; who once achieves, invalidates the balm of that religion, that doubts as fervently as it believes.
