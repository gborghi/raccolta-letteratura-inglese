As Sleigh [[Bells]] seem in [[Summer|summer]]  
Or [[Bees]], at [[Christmas]] show —  
So fairy — so fictitious  
The individuals do  
Repealed from observation —  
A Party that we knew —  
More distant in an instant  
Than [[Dawn]] in Timbuctoo.
