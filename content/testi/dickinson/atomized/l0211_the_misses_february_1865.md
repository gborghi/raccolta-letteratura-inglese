All that my eyes will let me shall be said for L——, dear little solid gold girl. I am glad to the foot of my heart that you will go to M——. It will make you warm. Touches “from [[Home|home]],” tell Gungl, are better than “sounds.”7

You persuade me to speak of my eyes, which I shunned doing, because I wanted you to rest. I could not bear a single sigh should tarnish your vacation, but, lest through me one bird delay a change of latitude, I will tell you, dear.

The eyes are as with you, sometimes easy, sometimes sad. I think they are not worse, nor do I think them better than when I came [[Home|home]].

The [[Snow|snow]]-light offends them, and the house is bright; notwithstanding, they [[Hope|hope]] some. For the first few weeks I did nothing but comfort my plants, till now their small green cheeks are covered with smiles. I chop the chicken centres when we have roast fowl, frequent now, for the hens contend and the [[Cain]] is slain. . . . Then I make the yellow to the pies, and bang the spice for cake, and knit the soles to the stockings I knit the bodies to last June. They say I am a “help.” Partly because it is true, I suppose, arid the rest applause. Mother and Margaret are so kind, father as gentle as he knows how, and Vinnie good to me, but “cannot see why I don't get [[Well|well]].” This makes me think I am long sick, and this takes the ache to my eyes. I shall try to stay with them a few weeks more before going to Boston, though what it would be to see you and have the doctor's care—that cannot be told. You will not wait for me. Go to M——now. I wish I were there, myself, to start your little feet “lest they seem to come short of it.” I have so much to tell I can tell nothing, except a sand of [[Love|love]]. When I dare I shall ask if I may go, but that will not be now.

Give my [[Love|love]] to my lamp and spoon, and the small lantana. Kindest remembrance for all the house, and write next from M——. Go, little girl, to M——. Life is so fast it will run away, notwithstanding our sweetest whoa.

Already they [[Love|love]] you. Be but the maid you are to me, and they will [[Love|love]] you more.

Carry your heart and your curls, and nothing more but your fingers. Mr. D——will ask for these every candlelight. How I miss ten robins that never flew from the rosewood nest!

DEAR L——,—This is my [[Letter|letter]]—an ill and peevish thing, but when my eyes get [[Well|well]] I'll send you thoughts like daisies, and sentences could hold the [[Bees|bees]]. . . .
