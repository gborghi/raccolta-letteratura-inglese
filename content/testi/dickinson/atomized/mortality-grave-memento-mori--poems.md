[[topoi/sic-transit-gloria-mundi|Sic transit gloria mundi]]  
"How doth the busy bee"  
Dum vivamus vivamus  
I stay mine enemy! —  

Oh veni vidi vici!  
Oh caput cap-a-pie!  
And oh "[[topoi/memento-mori|memento mori]]"  
When I am far from thee  

Hurrah for Peter Parley  
Hurrrah for Daniel Boone  
Three cheers sir, for the gentleman  
Who first observed the [[motifs/moon|moon]] —  

Peter put up the sunshine!  
Pattie arrange the [[motifs/stars|stars]]  
Tell Luna, tea is waiting  
And call your brother Mars —  

Put down the apple Adam  
And come away with me  
So shal't thou have a pippin  
From off my Father's [[motifs/tree|tree]]!  

I climb the "[[motifs/hill|Hill]] of Science"  
I "view the Landscape o'er"  
Such transcendental prospect  
I ne'er beheld before! —  

Unto the Legislature  
4  

My country bids me go,  
I'll take my india rubbers  
In case the [[motifs/wind|wind]] should blow.  

During my [[concepts/education|education]]  
It was announced to me  
That gravitation stumbling  
Fell from an apple [[motifs/tree|tree]] —  

The Earth opon it's axis  
Was once supposed to turn  
By way of a gymnastic  
In honor to the [[motifs/sun|sun]] —  

It was the brave [[characters/columbus|Columbus]]  
A sailing o'er the tide  
Who notified the nations  
Of where I would reside  

[[concepts/mortality|Mortality]] is fatal  
Gentility is fine  
Rascality, heroic  
Insolvency, [[concepts/sublime|sublime]]  

Our Fathers being weary  
Laid down on Bunker [[motifs/hill|Hill]]  
And though full many a morn'g  
Yet they are sleeping still  

The trumpet sir, shall wake them  
In streams I see them rise  
Each with a solemn musket  
A marching to the skies!  

A coward will remain, Sir,  
5  

Until the fight is done;  
But an immortal [[archetypes/hero|hero]]  
Will take his hat and run.  

Good bye Sir, I am going  
My country calleth me  
Allow me Sir, at parting  
To wipe my weeping e'e  

In token of our [[concepts/friendship|friendship]]  
Accept this "Bonnie Doon"  
And when the hand that pluck'd it  
Hath passed beyond the [[motifs/moon|moon]]  

The [[motifs/memory|memory]] of my ashes  
Will consolation be  
Then farewell Tuscarora  
And farewell Sir, to thee.

I often passed the village  
When going [[motifs/home|home]] from school —  
And wondered what they did there —  
And why it was so still —  

I did not know the year then —  
In which my call would come —  
Earlier, by the Dial,  
Than the rest have gone.  

It's stiller than the sundown.  
It's cooler than the [[motifs/dawn|dawn]] —  
The Daisies dare to come here —  
And [[motifs/birds|birds]] can flutter down —  

So when you are tired —  
Or perplexed — or cold —  
Trust the loving promise  
Underneath the mould,  
Cry "it's I," "take Dollie,"  
And I will enfold!
