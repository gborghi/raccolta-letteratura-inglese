. . . I open my [[Window|window]], and it fills the chamber with white dirt. I think [[God]] must be dusting; and the [[Wind|wind]] blows so I expect to read in The Republican “Cautionary signals for Amherst,” or “No ships ventured out from Phoenix Row.” . . . Life is so rotatory that the wilderness falls to each, sometime. It is safe to remember that. . . .
