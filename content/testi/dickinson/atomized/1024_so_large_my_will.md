<span class="atom-split" data-atom="1024_so_large_my_will" data-title="1024. So large my Will" data-chapter="" data-kind="intro"></span>


So large my Will  
The little that I may  
Embarrasses  
Like gentle infamy —  

Affront to Him  
For whom the Whole were small  
Affront to me  
Who know His Meed of all.  

Earth at the best  
Is but a scanty Toy —  
Bought, carried [[Home]]  
To [[Immortality]].  

It looks so small  
We chiefly [[Wonder|wonder]] then  
At our Conceit  
In purchasing.
