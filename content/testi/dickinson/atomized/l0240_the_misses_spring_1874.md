SISTERS—I hear robins a great way off, and wagons a great way off, and rivers a great way off, and all appear to be hurrying somewhere undisclosed to me. Remoteness is the founder of sweetness; could we see all we [[Hope|hope]], or hear the whole we [[Fear|fear]] told tranquil, like another tale, there would be [[Madness|madness]] near. Each of us gives or takes heaven in corporeal person, for each of us has the skill of life. I am pleased by your sweet acquaintance. It is not recorded of any [[Rose|rose]] that it failed of its bee, though obtained in specific instances through scarlet experience. The career of [[Flowers|flowers]] differs from ours only in inaudibleness. I feel more reverence as I grow for these mute creatures whose suspense or transport may surpass my own. Pussy remembered the judgment, and remained with Vinnie. Maggie preferred her [[Home|home]] to “Miggles” and “Oakhurst,” so with a few [[Spring|spring]] touches, [[Nature|nature]] remains unchanged.

The most triumphant bird I ever knew or met, Embarked upon a twig to-day,— And till dominion set I perish to behold So competent a sight— And sang for nothing scrutable But impudent delight. Retired and resumed His transitive estate; To what delicious accident Does finest glory fit!
