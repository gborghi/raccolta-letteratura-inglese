In the dim and early [[Dawn|dawn]] of a fragrant [[Summer|summer]] morning soon after, Emily caused a large cluster of sweet-peas to be gathered from her dewy, old-fashioned [[Garden|garden]], that they might be put on the very first train to Springfield, taking the freshness of [[Summer|summer]] itself to her friends. This note accompanied them:—

[[Dawn]] and [[Dew|dew]] my bearers be.
