Vinnie says your martyrs were fond of [[Flowers|flowers]].

Would these profane their vase?

EMILY

To the Same

Upon a subsequent anniversary

My DEAR MRS. READ,—We have often thought of you to-day, and almost spoken with you, but thought you might like to be alone—if one can be alone with so thronged a Heaven.

E. DICKINSON
