<span class="atom-split" data-atom="0002_there_is_another_sky" data-title="2. There is another sky" data-chapter="" data-kind="intro"></span>


there is another sky  
ever serene and fair,  
and there is another sunshine,  
tho' it be darkness there -  
never mind faded forests, Austin,  
never mind silent fields -  
here is a little forest  
whose leaf is ever green -  
here is a brighter [[Garden|garden]] -  
where not a [[Frost|frost]] has been,  
in its unfading [[Flowers|flowers]]  
I hear the bright bee hum,  
prithee, my Brother,  
into my [[Garden|garden]] come!
