We miss a Kinsman more  
When warranted to see  
Than when withheld of Oceans  
From possibility  

A Furlong than a League  
Inflicts a pricklier [[Pain|pain]],  
Till We, who smiled at Pyrenees —  
Of Parishes, complain.
