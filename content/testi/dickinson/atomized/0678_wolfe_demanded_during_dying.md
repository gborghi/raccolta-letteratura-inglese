Wolfe demanded during dying  
"Which obtain the Day"?  
"General, the British" — "Easy"  
Answered Wolfe "to die"  

Montcalm, his opposing Spirit  
Rendered with a smile  
"Sweet" said he "my own Surrender  
[[Liberty]]'s beguile"
