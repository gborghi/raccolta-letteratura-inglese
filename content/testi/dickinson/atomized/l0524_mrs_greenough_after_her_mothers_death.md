DEAR FRIEND,—I had the luxury of a mother a month longer than you, for my own mother died in November, but the anguish also was granted me to see the first [[Snow|snow]] upon her [[Grave|grave]] the following day, which, dear friend, you were spared.

But Remembrance engulfs me, and I must cease.

I wish I could speak a word of [[Courage|courage]], though that [[Love|love]] has already done. Who could be motherless who has a mother's [[Grave|grave]] within confiding reach?

Let me enclose the tenderness born of bereavement.

To have had a mother—how mighty!
