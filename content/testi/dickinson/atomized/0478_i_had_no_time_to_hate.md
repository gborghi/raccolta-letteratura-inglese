I had no [[Time|time]] to Hate —  
Because  
The [[Grave]] would hinder Me —  
And Life was not so  
Ample I  
Could finish — Enmity —  

Nor had I [[Time|time]] to [[Love]] —  
But since  
Some Industry must be —  
The little Toil of [[Love]] —  
I thought  
Be large enough for Me —
