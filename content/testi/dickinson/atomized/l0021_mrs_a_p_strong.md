Mr. Humphrey, spoken of in the following [[Letter|letter]], is the same friend of whom Emily had already written (page 22); he graduated from Amherst as valedictorian in 1846, being subsequently Principal of the [[Well|well]]-known Amherst Academy, and still later a theological student at Andover, and tutor in Amherst College. His sudden [[Death|death]], November 30, 1850, caused much grief to his many friends, who admired his polished scholarship and lovable personality.
