[[Sic Transit Gloria Mundi|Sic transit gloria mundi]], How doth the busy bee— Dum vivimus vivamus, I stay mine enemy.

Oh, veni, vidi, vici, Oh, caput, cap-a-pie, And oh, [[Memento Mori|memento mori]] When I am far from thee.

Hurrah for Peter Parley, Hurrah for Daniel Boone, Three cheers, sir, for the gentlemen Who first observed the [[Moon|moon]].

Peter put up the sunshine, Pattie arrange the [[Stars|stars]], Tell Luna tea is waiting, And call your brother Mars.

Put down the apple, Adam, And come away with me; So shall thou have a pippin From off my father's [[Tree|tree]].

I climb the [[Hill|hill]] of science I “view the landscape o'er,” Such transcendental prospect I ne'er beheld before.

Unto the Legislature My country bids me go. I'll take my india-rubbers, In case the [[Wind|wind]] should blow.

During my [[Education|education]], It was announced to me That gravitation, stumbling, Fell from an apple-[[Tree|tree]].

The earth upon its axis Was once supposed to turn, By way of a gymnastic In honor to the [[Sun|sun]].

It was the brave [[Columbus]], A-sailing on the tide, Who notified the nations Of where I would reside.

[[Mortality]] is fatal, Gentility is fine, Rascality heroic, Insolvency [[Sublime|sublime]].

Our fathers being weary Lay down on Bunker [[Hill]], And though full many a morning, Yet they are sleeping still.

The trumpet, sir, shall wake them, In dream I see them rise, Each with a solemn musket A-marching to the skies.

A coward will remain, sir, Until the fight is done, But an immortal [[Hero|hero]] Will take his hat and run.

Good-by, sir, I am going— My country calleth me. Allow me, sir, at parting To wipe my weeping e'e.

In token of our [[Friendship|friendship]] Accept this Bonnie Doon, And when the hand that plucked it Has passed beyond the [[Moon|moon]],

The [[Memory|memory]] of my ashes Will consolation be. Then farewell, Tuscarora, And farewell, sir, to thee.
