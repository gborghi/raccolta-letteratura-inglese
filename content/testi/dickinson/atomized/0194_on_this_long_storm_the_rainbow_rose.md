On this long [[Storm|storm]] the rainbow [[Rose|rose]],  
On this late morn the [[Sun|sun]] ;  
The clouds, like listless elephants,  
Horizons straggled down.  

The [[Birds|birds]] [[Rose|rose]] smiling in their nests,  
The gales indeed were done ;  
Alas !  how heedless were the eyes  
On whom the [[Summer|summer]] shone !  

The quiet nonchalance of [[Death|death]]  
No daybreak can bestir ;  
The slow archangel's syllables  
Must awaken her.
