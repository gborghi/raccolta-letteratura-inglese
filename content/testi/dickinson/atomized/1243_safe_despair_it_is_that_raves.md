Safe [[Despair]] it is that raves —  
Agony is frugal.  
Puts itself severe away  
For its own perusal.  

Garrisoned no [[Soul]] can be  
In the Front of Trouble —  
[[Love]] is one, not aggregate —  
Nor is Dying [[Double|double]] —
