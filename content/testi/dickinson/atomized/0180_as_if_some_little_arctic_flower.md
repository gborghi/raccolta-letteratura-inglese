As if some little Arctic flower  
Upon the polar hem —  
Went wandering down the Latitudes  
Until it puzzled came  
To continents of [[Summer|summer]] —  
To firmaments of [[Sun|sun]] —  
To strange, bright crowds of [[Flowers|flowers]] —  
And [[Birds|birds]], of foreign tongue!  
I say, As if this little flower  
To Eden, wandered in —  
What then? Why nothing,  
Only, your inference therefrom!
