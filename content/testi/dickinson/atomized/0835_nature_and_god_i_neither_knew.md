[[Nature]] and [[God]] — I neither knew  
Yet Both so [[Well|well]] knew me  
They startled, like Executors  
Of My [[Identity|identity]].  

Yet Neither told — that I could learn —  
My Secret as secure  
As Herschel's private interest  
Or Mercury's affair —
