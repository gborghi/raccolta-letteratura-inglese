Which will I thank—the perpetrator, the propagator, or the almoner of the delightful bread—or may I compromise and thank them all?

I for the first [[Time|time]] appreciate the exultation of the robin toward a crumb, though he must be a seductive robin, with whom I would share my own.

With the [[Hope|hope]] to requite the loveliness in a future way,
