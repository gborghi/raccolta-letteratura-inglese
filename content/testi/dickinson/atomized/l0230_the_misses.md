. . . J——is coming to put away her black hair on the children's pillow. I hoped she'd come while you were here, to help me with the starch, but [[Satan]]'s ways are not as our ways. I'm straightening all the property, and making things erect and smart, and to-morrow, at twilight, her little heel boots will thump into Amherst. It being [[Summer|summer]] season she will omit the sleigh-bell gown, and that's a palliative. Vinnie is all disgust, and I shall have to smirk for two to make the manners even.
