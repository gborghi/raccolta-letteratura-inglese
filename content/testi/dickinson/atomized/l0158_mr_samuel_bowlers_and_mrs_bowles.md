If she had been the mistletoe, And I had been the [[Rose|rose]], How gay upon your table My velvet life to close! Since I am of the Druid, And she is of the [[Dew|dew]], I'll deck [[Tradition|tradition]]'s buttonhole, And send the [[Rose|rose]] to you.

E.

DEAR MR. BOWLES,—I can't thank you any more. You are thoughtful so many times you grieve me always; now the old words are numb, and there aren't any new ones.

Brooks are useless in freshet [[Time|time]]. When you come to Amherst—please [[God]] it were to-day—I will tell you about the picture—if I can, I will.

Speech is a prank of Parliament, [[Tears]] a trick of the nerve,— But the heart with the heaviest freight on Doesn't always swerve.
