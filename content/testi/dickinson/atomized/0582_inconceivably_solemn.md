Inconceivably solemn!  
Things go gay  
Pierce — by the very Press  
Of Imagery —  

Their far Parades — order on the eye  
With a mute Pomp —  
A pleading Pageantry —  

Flags, are a brave sight —  
But no true Eye  
Ever went by One —  
Steadily —  

Music's triumphant —  
But the fine Ear  
Winces with delight  
Are Drums too near —
