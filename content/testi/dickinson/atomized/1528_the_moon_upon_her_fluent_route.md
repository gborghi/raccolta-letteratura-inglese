The [[Moon]] upon her fluent Route  
Defiant of a [[Road]] —  
The Star's Etruscan Argument  
Substantiate a [[God]] —  

If Aims impel these Astral Ones  
The ones allowed to know  
Know that which makes them as forgot  
As [[Dawn]] forgets them — now —
