The Zeroes — taught us — Phosphorous —  
We learned to like the [[Fire]]  
By playing Glaciers — when a Boy —  
And Tinder — guessed — by power  
Of Opposite — to balance Odd —  
If White — a Red — must be!  
Paralysis — our Primer — dumb —  
Unto Vitality!
