The Dandelion's pallid tube  
Astonishes the Grass,  
And [[Winter]] instantly becomes  
An infinite Alas —  

The tube uplifts a signal Bud  
And then a shouting Flower, —  
The Proclamation of the Suns  
That sepulture is o'er.
