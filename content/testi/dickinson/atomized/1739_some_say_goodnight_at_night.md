Some say goodnight — at [[Night|night]] —  
I say goodnight by day —  
Good-bye — the Going utter me —  
Goodnight, I still reply —  

For parting, that is [[Night|night]],  
And presence, simply [[Dawn|dawn]] —  
Itself, the purple on the height  
Denominated morn.
