Fame is a fickle food  
Upon a shifting plate  
Whose table once a  
Guest but not  
The second [[Time|time]] is set.  

Whose crumbs the crows inspect  
And with ironic caw  
Flap past it to the  
Farmer's Corn —  
Men eat of it and die.
