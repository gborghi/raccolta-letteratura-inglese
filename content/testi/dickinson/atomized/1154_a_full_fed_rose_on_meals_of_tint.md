A full fed [[Rose]] on meals of Tint  
A Dinner for a Bee  
In process of the [[Noon]] became -  
Each bright [[Mortality]]  
The Forfeit is of Creature fair  
Itself, adored before  
Submitting for our unknown sake  
To be esteemed no more —
