<span class="atom-split" data-atom="0041_i_robbed_the_woods" data-title="41. I robbed the Woods —" data-chapter="" data-kind="intro"></span>


I robbed the Woods —  
The trusting Woods.  
The unsuspecting Trees  
Brought out their Burs and mosses  
My fantasy to please.  
I scanned their trinkets curious — I grasped — I bore away —  
What will the solemn Hemlock —  
What will the Oak [[Tree|tree]] say?
