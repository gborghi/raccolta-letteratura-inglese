DEAR FRIEND,—When you wrote you would come in November, it would please me it were November then—but the [[Time|time]] has moved. You went with the coming of the [[Birds|birds]]—they will go with your coming, but to see you is so much sweeter than [[Birds|birds]], I could excuse the [[Spring|spring]].

With the bloom of the flower your friend loved, I have wished for her, but [[God]] cannot discontinue Himself.

Mr. Bowles was not willing to die.

When you have lost a friend, Master, you remember you could not begin again, because there was no world. I have thought of you often since the darkness, though we cannot assist another's [[Night|night]].

I have hoped you were saved.

That those have [[Immortality|immortality]] with whom we talked about it, makes it no more mighty but perhaps more sudden. . . .

How brittle are the piers On which our [[Faith|faith]] doth tread— No bridge below doth totter so, Yet none hath such a crowd,

It is as old as [[God]]— Indeed, 'twas built by Him— He sent His son to test the plank, And he pronounced it firm.

I [[Hope|hope]] you have been [[Well|well]]. I [[Hope|hope]] your rambles have been sweet, and your reveries spacious.

To have seen Stratford on Avon, and the Dresden Madonna, must be almost peace.

And perhaps you have spoken with George Eliot. Will you “tell me about it”? Will you come in November, and will November come, or is this the [[Hope|hope]] that opens and shuts, like the eye of the wax doll?
