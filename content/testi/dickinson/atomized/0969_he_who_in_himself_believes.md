He who in Himself believes —  
Fraud cannot presume —  
[[Faith]] is Constancy's Result —  
And assumes — from [[Home]] —  

Cannot perish, though it fail  
Every second [[Time|time]] —  
But defaced Vicariously —  
For Some Other Shame —
