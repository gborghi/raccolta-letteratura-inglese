As old as Woe —  
How old is that?  
Some eighteen thousand years —  
As old as Bliss  
How old is that  
They are of equal years  

Together chiefest they ard found  
But seldom side by side  
From neither of them tho' he try  
Can Human [[Nature|nature]] hide
