<span class="atom-split" data-atom="0571_must_be_a_woe" data-title="571. Must be a Woe —" data-chapter="" data-kind="intro"></span>


Must be a Woe —  
A loss or so —  
To bend the eye  
Best [[Beauty]]'s way —  

But — once aslant  
It notes Delight  
As difficult  
As Stalactite  

A Common Bliss  
Were had for less —  
The price — is  
Even as the Grace —  

Our lord — thought no  
Extravagance  
To pay — a [[Cross]] —
