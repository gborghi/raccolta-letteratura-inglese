DEAR MR. AND MRS. PASTOR,—Mrs. Holland pleased us and grieved us, by telling us your triumphs.

We want you to conquer, but we want you to conquer here.

“Marathon” is me. Is there nothing but glow in the new horizon?

You see we keep a jealous heart. That is [[Love]]'s. alloy.

Vinnie is full of wrath, and vicious as Saul toward the Holy [[Ghost]], in whatever form. I heard her declaiming the other [[Night|night]], to a foe that called—and sent Maggie to part them.

Vinnie lives on the [[Hope|hope]] that you will return. Is it quite fictitious? You are gone too long.

The red [[Leaves|leaves]] take the green [[Leaves|leaves]]' place, and the landscape yields. We go to [[Sleep|sleep]] with the peach in our hands and wake with the stone, but the stone is the pledge of summers to come.

[[Love]] for each of you, always, and if there are lands longer than “always,” [[Love|love]] also for those.

These are sticks of rowen for your stove. It was chopped by [[Bees|bees]], and butterflies piled it, Saturday afternoons.
