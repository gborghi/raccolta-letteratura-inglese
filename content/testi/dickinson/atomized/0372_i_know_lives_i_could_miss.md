I know lives, I could miss  
Without a Misery —  
Others — whose instant's wanting —  
Would be [[Eternity]] —  

The last — a scanty Number —  
'Twould scarcely fill a Two —  
The first — a Gnat's Horizon  
Could easily outgrow —
