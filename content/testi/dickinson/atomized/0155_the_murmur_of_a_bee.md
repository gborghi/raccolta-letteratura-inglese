The Murmur of a Bee  
A Witchcraft — yieldeth me —  
If any ask me why —  
'Twere easier to die —  
Than tell —  

The Red upon the [[Hill]]  
Taketh away my will —  
If anybody sneer —  
Take care — for [[God]] is here —  
That's all.  

The Breaking of the Day  
Addeth to my Degree —  
If any ask me how —  
[[artist|Artist]] — who drew me so —  
Must tell!
