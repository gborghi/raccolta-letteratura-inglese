DEAR FRIEND,—I cannot make an Indian pipe, but please accept a humming-hird,—

A route of evanescence With a revolving [[Wheel|wheel]]; A resonance of emerald, A rush of cochineal; And every blossom on the bush Adjusts its tumbled head,— The mail from Tunis, probably, An easy morning's ride.

When, after her [[Death|death]], the great mass of Emily Dickinson's verses had been collated and an appropriate drawing for the cover was desired, there seemed a peculiar fitness in this ethereal flower, and the design was cut from the little panel which stood so long in her room.
