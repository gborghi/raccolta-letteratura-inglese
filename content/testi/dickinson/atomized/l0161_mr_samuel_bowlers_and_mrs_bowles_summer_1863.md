DEAR FRIENDS,—I am sorry you came, because you went away.

Hereafter, I will pick no [[Rose|rose]], lest it fade or prick me.

I would like to have you dwell here.

Though it is almost nine o'[[Clock|clock]], the skies are gay and yellow, and there's a purple craft or so, in which a friend could sail. To-[[Night|night]] looks like “[[Jerusalem]]”! . . . I [[Hope|hope]] we may all behave so as to reach [[Jerusalem]].

How are your hearts to-day? Ours are pretty [[Well|well]]. I [[Hope|hope]] your tour was bright, and gladdened Mrs. Bowles. Perhaps the retrospect will call you back some morning.

You shall find us all at the gate if you come in a hundred years, just as we stood that day. If it become of “jasper” previously, you will not object, so that we lean there still, looking after you.

I rode with Austin this morning. He showed me [[Mountains|mountains]] that touched the sky, and brooks that sang like bobolinks. Was he not very kind? I will give them to you, for they are mine, and “all things are mine,” excepting “Cephas and Apollos,” for whom I have no taste. Vinnie's [[Love|love]] brims mine.
