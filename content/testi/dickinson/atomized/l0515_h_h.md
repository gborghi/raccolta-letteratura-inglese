After Mrs. Jackson's [[Death|death]], August 12, 1885, Emily wrote of her:—

[[Helen]] of Troy will die, but [[Helen]] of Colorado, never. Dear friend, can you walk, were the last words that I wrote her.

Dear friend, I can fly— Her immortal reply.
