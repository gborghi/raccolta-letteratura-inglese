[[Dreams]] — are [[Well|well]] — but Waking's better,  
If One wake at morn —  
If One wake at Midnight — better —  
Dreaming — of the [[Dawn]] —  

Sweeter — the Surmising Robins —  
Never gladdened [[Tree]] —  
Than a Solid [[Dawn]] — confronting —  
Leading to no Day —
