The [[Soul]]'s distinct connection  
With [[Immortality|immortality]]  
Is best disclosed by Danger  
Or quick Calamity —  

As [[Lightning]] on a Landscape  
Exhibits Sheets of Place —  
Not yet suspected — but for Flash —  
And Click — and Suddenness.
