<span class="atom-split" data-atom="0487_you_love_the_lord_you_cannot_see" data-title="487. You love the Lord — you cannot see —" data-chapter="" data-kind="intro"></span>


You [[Love|love]] the Lord — you cannot see —  
You write Him — every day —  
A little note — when you awake —  
And further in the Day.  

An Ample [[Letter]] — How you miss —  
And would delight to see —  
But then His [[house|House]] — is but a Step —  
And Mine's — in Heaven — You see.
