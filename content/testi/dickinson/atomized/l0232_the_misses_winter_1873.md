. . . I know I [[Love|love]] my friends—I feel it far in here where neither blue nor black eye goes, and fingers cannot reach. I know 'tis [[Love|love]] for them that sets the blister in my throat, many a [[Time|time]] a day, when winds go sweeter than their wont, or a different cloud puts my brain from [[Home|home]].

I cannot see my [[Soul|soul]], but know 'tis there, Nor ever saw his house nor furniture, Who has invited me with him to dwell; But a confiding guest consult as [[Well|well]], What raiment honor him the most, That I be adequately dressed, For he insures to none Lest men specifical adorn Procuring him perpetual drest By dating it a sudden feast.

[[Love]] for the glad if you know them, for the sad if they know you.
