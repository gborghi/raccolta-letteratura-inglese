. . . Oh that beloved [[Witch|witch]]-hazel which would not reach me till part of the stems were a gentle brown, though one loved stalk as hearty as if just placed in the mail by the woods. It looked like tinsel fringe combined with staider fringes, [[Witch|witch]] and witching too, to my joyful mind.

I never had seen it but once before, and it haunted me like [[Childhood|childhood]]'s Indian pipe, or ecstatic puff-balls, or that mysterious apple that sometimes comes on [[River|river]]-pinks; and is there not a dim suggestion of a dandelion, if her hair were ravelled and she grew on a twig instead of a tube,—though this is timidly submitted. For taking [[Nature]]'s hand to lead her to me, I am softly grateful—was she willing to come? Though her reluctances are sweeter than other ones' avowals.

Trusty as the [[Stars|stars]] Who quit their shining working . Prompt as when I lit them In Genesis' new house, Durable as [[Dawn|dawn]] Whose antiquated blossom Makes a world's suspense Perish and rejoice.

[[Love]] for the cousin sisters, and the lovely alien. . . .
