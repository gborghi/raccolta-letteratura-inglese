DEAR SIR,—Your request to “remain sincerely” mine demands investigation, and if after synopsis of your career all should seem correct, I am tersely yours. I shall try to wear the unmerited honor with becoming volume.

Commend me to your kindred, for whom, although a stranger, I entertain esteem.

I approve the paint—a study of the Soudan, I take it, but the Scripture assures us our hearts are all Dongola.
