Had we our senses  
But perhaps 'tis [[Well|well]] they're not at [[Home]]  
So intimate with [[Madness]]  
He's liable with them  

Had we the eyes without our Head —  
How [[Well|well]] that we are Blind —  
We could not look upon the Earth —  
So utterly unmoved —
