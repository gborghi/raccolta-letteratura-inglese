To Mrs. Holland, on the [[motifs/marriage|marriage]] of her daughter Annie, December 7, 1881.

SWEET SISTER,—We were much relieved to know that the dear event had occurred without overwhelming any loved one, and perhaps it is sweeter and safer so. I feared much for the parting, to you, to whom parting has come so thickly in the last few days. I knew all would be beautiful, and rejoice it was. Few daughters have the [[concepts/immortality|immortality]] of a father for a bridal gift. Could there be one more costly?

As we never have ceased to think of you, we will more tenderly, now. Confide our happiness to Annie, in her happiness. We [[concepts/hope|hope]] the unknown balm may ease the balm withdrawn.

You and Katie, the little sisters, lose her, yet obtain her, for each new width of [[motifs/love|love]] largens all the rest. Mother and Vinnie think and speak. Vinnie hopes to write. Would that mother could, but her poor hand is idle. Shall I return to you your last and sweetest words—“But I [[motifs/love|love]] you all”?

“Da dove vieni?” gli chiesi, perché parlava come un fantasma. “Sono sceso dal mio pulpito e sono salito sul treno,” fu la sua semplice risposta; e quando gli chiesi “quanto tempo fa?”, lui rispose: “vent’anni”, con uno sguardo indecifrabile e malizioso.

Ma quella voce amata è cessata; e per qualcuno che lo ha sentito “rientrare a casa”, parlare con lui è stato dolce. Grazie per ogni singola cosa, e raccontami tutto ciò che desideri dirmi.

Scusate il tono della mia voce. Questo momento è davvero immortale.
