<span class="atom-split" data-atom="0578_the_body_grows_without" data-title="578. The Body grows without —" data-chapter="" data-kind="intro"></span>


The [[Body]] grows without —  
The more convenient way —  
That if the Spirit — like to hide  
Its Temple stands, alway,  

Ajar — secure — inviting —  
It never did betray  
The [[Soul]] that asked its shelter  
In solemn honesty
