Praise it — 'tis dead —  
It cannot glow —  
Warm this inclement Ear  
With the encomium it earned  
Since it was gathered here —  
Invest this alabaster Zest  
In the Delights of [[Dust]] —  
Remitted — since it flitted it  
In recusance august.
