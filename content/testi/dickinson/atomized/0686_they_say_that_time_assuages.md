<span class="atom-split" data-atom="0686_they_say_that_time_assuages" data-title="686. They say that &quot;Time assuages&quot; —" data-chapter="" data-kind="intro"></span>


They say that "[[Time]] assuages" —  
[[Time]] never did assuage —  
An actual [[Suffering|suffering]] strengthens  
As Sinews do, with age —  

[[Time]] is a Test of Trouble —  
But not a Remedy —  
If such it prove, it prove too  
There was no Malady —
