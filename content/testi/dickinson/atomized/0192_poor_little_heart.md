<span class="atom-split" data-atom="0192_poor_little_heart" data-title="192. Poor little Heart!" data-chapter="" data-kind="intro"></span>


Poor little Heart!  
Did they forget thee?  
Then dinna care! Then dinna care!  

Proud little Heart!  
Did they forsake thee?  
Be debonnaire! Be debonnaire!  

Frail little Heart!  
I would not break thee —  
Could'st credit me? Could'st credit me?  

Gay little Heart —  
Like Morning Glory!  
[[Wind]] and [[Sun]] — wilt thee array!
