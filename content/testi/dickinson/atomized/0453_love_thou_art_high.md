<span class="atom-split" data-atom="0453_love_thou_art_high" data-title="453. Love — thou art high —" data-chapter="" data-kind="intro"></span>


[[Love]] — thou art high —  
I cannot climb thee —  
But, were it Two —  
Who knows but we —  
Taking turns — at the Chimborazo —  
Ducal — at last — stand up by thee —  

[[Love]] — thou are deep —  
I cannot [[Cross|cross]] thee —  
But, were there Two  
Instead of One —  
Rower, and Yacht — some sovereign [[Summer]] —  
Who knows — but we'd reach the [[Sun]]?  

[[Love]] — thou are Veiled —  
A few — behold thee —  
Smile — and alter — and prattle — and die —  
Bliss — were an Oddity — without thee —  
Nicknamed by [[God]] —  
[[Eternity]] —
