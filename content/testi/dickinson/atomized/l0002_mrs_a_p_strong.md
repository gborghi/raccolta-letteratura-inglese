<span class="atom-split" data-atom="l0002_mrs_a_p_strong" data-title="To Mrs. A. P. Strong" data-chapter="" data-kind="intro"></span>


Please send me a copy of that Romance you were writing at Amherst. I am in a fever to read it. I expect it will be against my Whig feelings.

After this postscript many others follow, across the top, down the edges, tucked in wherever space will allow. There are also a few lines from each of three girl friends to “dear A.”
