A Counterfeit — a Plated Person —  
I would not be —  
Whatever strata of Iniquity  
My [[Nature]] underlie —  
[[Truth]] is good Health — and Safety, and the Sky.  
How meagre, what an [[Exile]] — is a Lie,  
And Vocal — when we die —
