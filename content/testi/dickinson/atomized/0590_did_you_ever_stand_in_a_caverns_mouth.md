<span class="atom-split" data-atom="0590_did_you_ever_stand_in_a_caverns_mouth" data-title="590. Did you ever stand in a Cavern's Mouth —" data-chapter="" data-kind="intro"></span>


Did you ever stand in a Cavern's Mouth —  
Widths out of the [[Sun]] —  
And look — and shudder, and block your breath —  
And deem to be [[Alone|alone]]  

In such a place, what horror,  
How Goblin it would be —  
And fly, as 'twere pursuing you?  
Then [[Loneliness]] — looks so —  

Did you ever look in a Cannon's face —  
Between whose Yellow eye —  
And yours — the Judgment intervened —  
The Question of "To die" —  

Extemporizing in your ear  
As cool as Satyr's Drums —  
If you remember, and were saved —  
It's liker so — it seems —
