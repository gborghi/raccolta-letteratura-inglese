We never forget Mary.

DEAR MR. BOWLES,—Thank you.

[[Faith]] is a fine invention When gentlemen can see! But microscopes are prudent In an emergency!4

You spoke of the “East.” I have thought about it this [[Winter|winter]].

Don't you think you and I should be shrewder to take the mountain [[Road|road]]?

That bareheaded life, under the grass, worries one like a wasp.

The [[Rose|rose]] is for Mary.
