<span class="atom-split" data-atom="1291_until_the_desert_knows" data-title="1291. Until the Desert knows" data-chapter="" data-kind="intro"></span>


Until the [[Desert]] knows  
That [[Water]] grows  
His Sands suffice  
But let him once suspect  
That Caspian Fact  
Sahara dies  

Utmost is relative —  
Have not or Have  
Adjacent sums  
Enough — the first Abode  
On the familiar [[Road]]  
Galloped in [[Dreams]] —
