<span class="atom-split" data-atom="l0113_dr_j_g_holland_and_mrs_holland_1857" data-title="To Dr. J. G. Holland, and Mrs. Holland, 1857?" data-chapter="" data-kind="intro"></span>


DEAR SISTER,—After you went, a low [[Wind|wind]] warbled through the house like a spacious bird, making it high but lonely. When you had gone the [[Love|love]] came. I supposed it would. The supper of the heart is when the guest has gone.

Shame is so intrinsic in a strong affection we must all experience Adam's reticence. I suppose the street that the [[Lover|lover]] travels is thenceforth divine, incapable of turnpike aims.

That you be with me annuls [[Fear|fear]] and I await Commencement with merry [[Resignation|resignation]]. Smaller than David you clothe me with extreme Goliath.

Friday I tasted life. It was a vast morsel. A circus passed the house—still I feel the red in my mind though the drums are out.

[[The Book|The book]] you mention, I have not met. Thank you for tenderness.

The lawn is full of south and the odors tangle, and I hear to-day for the first the [[River|river]] in the [[Tree|tree]].

You mentioned [[Spring|spring]]'s delaying—I blamed her for the opposite. I would eat evanescence slowly.

Vinnie is deeply afflicted in the [[Death|death]] of her dappled cat, though I convince her it is immortal which assists her some. Mother resumes lettuce, involving my transgression—suggestive of yourself, however, which endears disgrace.

“House” is being “cleaned.” I prefer pestilence. That is more classic and less fell.

Yours was my first arbutus. It was a rosy boast.

I will send you the first [[Witch|witch]] hazel.

A woman died last week, young and in [[Hope|hope]] but a little while—at the end of our [[Garden|garden]]. I thought since of the power of [[Death]], not upon affection, but its mortal signal. It is to us the Nile.

You refer to the unpermitted delight to be with those we [[Love|love]]. I suppose that to be the license not granted of [[God]].

Count not that far that can be had, Though sunset lie between— Nor that adjacent, that beside, Is further than the [[Sun|sun]].

[[Love]] for your embodiment of it.
