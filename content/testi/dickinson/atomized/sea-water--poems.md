Write! Comrade, write!  

On this wondrous [[motifs/sea|sea]]  
Sailing silently,  
Ho! Pilot, ho!  
Knowest thou the shore  
Where no breakers roar -  
Where the [[motifs/storm|storm]] is o'er?  

In the peaceful west  
Many the sails at rest -  
The anchors fast -  
Thither I pilot thee -  
Land Ho! [[concepts/eternity|Eternity]]!  
Ashore at last!
