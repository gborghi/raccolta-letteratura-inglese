A Field of Stubble, lying sere  
Beneath the second [[Sun]] —  
Its Toils to Brindled People thrust —  
Its Triumphs — to the Bin —  
Accosted by a timid Bird  
Irresolute of Alms —  
Is often seen — but seldom felt,  
On our [[New England]] Farms —
