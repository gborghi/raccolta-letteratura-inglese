<span class="atom-split" data-atom="1496_all_that_i_do" data-title="1496. All that I do" data-chapter="" data-kind="intro"></span>


All that I do  
Is in review  
To his enamored mind  
I know his eye  
Where e'er I ply  
Is pushing close behind  
Not any Port  
Nor any flight  
But he doth there preside  
What omnipresence lies in wait  
For her to be a Bride
