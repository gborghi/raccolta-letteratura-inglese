Strong Draughts of Their Refreshing Minds  
To drink — enables Mine  
Through [[Desert]] or the Wilderness  
As bore it Sealed Wine —  

To go elastic — Or as One  
The Camel's trait — attained —  
How powerful the Stimulus  
Of an Hermetic Mind —
