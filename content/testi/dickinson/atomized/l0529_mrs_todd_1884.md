How martial the apology of [[Nature|nature]]! We die, said the deathless of Thermopylæ, in obedience to law.

Not sickness stains the brave, Nor any dart, Nor [[Doubt|doubt]] of scenes to come, But an adjourning heart.

After I had sent her a painting of the gorgeous blossoms of the trumpet vine, which, on first seeing, she had called “the Soudan,” she wrote:—
