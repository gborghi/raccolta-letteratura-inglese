The sweetest Heresy received  
That Man and Woman know —  
Each Other's [[convert|Convert]] —  
Though the [[Faith]] accommodate but Two —  

The Churches are so frequent —  
The Ritual — so small —  
The Grace so unavoidable —  
To fail — is Infidel —
