. . . Did the “[[Stars|stars]] differ” from each other in anything but “glory,” there would be often envy. The competitions of the sky corrodeless ply.

. . . We asked Vinnie to say in the rear of one of her mental products that we had neuralgia, but evidently her theme or her [[Time|time]] did not admit of trifles. . . . I forget no part of that sweet, smarting visit, not even the nettle that stung my [[Rose|rose]].

When [[Macbeth]] asked the physician what could be done for his wife, he made the mighty answer, “That sort must heal itself”; but, sister, that was [[Guilt|guilt]], and [[Love|love]], you know, is [[God]], who certainly “gave the [[Love|love]] to reward the [[Love|love]],” even were there no Browning.

. . . The slips of the last [[Rose|rose]] of [[Summer|summer]] repose in kindred soil with waning [[Bees|bees]] for mates. How softly [[Summer|summer]] shuts, without the creaking of a [[Door|door]], abroad for evermore.

. . . Vinnie has also added a [[Pilgrim|pilgrim]] kitten to her flock, which besides being jet black, is, I think, a lineal descendant of the “beautiful hearse [[Horse|horse]]” recommended to Austin.
