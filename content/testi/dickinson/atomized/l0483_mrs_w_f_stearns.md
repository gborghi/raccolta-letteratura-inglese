One friend writes from [[New York]] of her regret that many of Emily's notes to herself have been destroyed. But she adds: “One little welcome of Emily's to me became a household word; and I can quote it for you, though it should have the setting of her wide, pure margins, and her most dainty penmanship. Here it is:—

“Sweet Mrs. Nellie comes with the robins. Robins have wings. Mrs. Nellie has wings. A society for the prevention of wings would be a benefit to us all.

“It tells its own story, you see, of a flitting visit to the Grove in the [[Spring|spring]], and of her interest in her neighbors and her information as to their interests, though so invisible herself.”
