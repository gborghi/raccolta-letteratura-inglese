DEAR FRIEND,—Accept my timid happiness.

No [[Joy|joy]] can be in vain, but adds to some bright total, whose dwelling is unknown.

The [[Immortality|immortality]] of [[Flowers|flowers]] must enrich our own, and we certainly should resent a redemption that excluded them.

Was not the “breath of fragrance” designed for your cheek solely?

The [[Fear|fear]] that it was crimsons my own, though to divide its Heaven is Heaven's highest half.
