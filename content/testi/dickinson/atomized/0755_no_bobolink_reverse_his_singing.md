No Bobolink — reverse His Singing  
When the only [[Tree]]  
Ever He minded occupying  
By the Farmer be —  

Clove to the Root —  
His Spacious Future —  
Best Horizon — gone —  
Whose Music be His  
Only Anodyne —  
Brave Bobolink —
