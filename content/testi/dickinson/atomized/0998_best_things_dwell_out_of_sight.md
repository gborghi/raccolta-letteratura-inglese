Best Things dwell out of Sight  
The Pearl — the Just — Our Thought.  

Most shun the Public Air  
Legitimate, and Rare —  

The Capsule of the [[Wind]]  
The Capsule of the Mind  

Exhibit here, as doth a Burr —  
Germ's Germ be where?
