Heart, not so heavy as mine  
Wending late [[motifs/home|home]] —  
As it passed my [[motifs/window|window]]  
Whistled itself a tune —  
A careless snatch — a ballad — A ditty of the street —

An Anodyne so sweet —  
It was as if a Bobolink  
Sauntering this way  
Carolled, and paused, and carolled —  
Then bubbled slow away!

Upon a dusty way —  
Set bleeding feet to minuets  
Without the knowing why!  
Tomorrow, [[motifs/night|night]] will come again —  
Perhaps, weary and sore —  
Ah Bugle! By my [[motifs/window|window]]  
I pray you pass once more.

Musicians wrestle everywhere —

Some say that bright Majority  
Of vanished Dames — and Men!  
Some — think it service in the place  
Where we — with late — celestial face —

A Bird came down the Walk —  
He did not know I saw —  
He bit an Angleworm in halves  
And ate the fellow, raw,  

And then he drank a [[motifs/dew|Dew]]

To let a Beetle pass —  

He glanced with rapid eyes  
That hurried all around —  
They looked like frightened Beads, I thought —  
He stirred his Velvet Head  

Like one in danger, Cautious,  
I offered him a Crumb  
And he unrolled his feathers  
And rowed him softer [[motifs/home|home]] —  

Than Oars divide the Ocean,  
Too silver for a seam —  
Or Butterflies, off Banks of [[motifs/noon|Noon]]  
Leap, splashless as they swim.
