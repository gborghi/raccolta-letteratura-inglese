| FORD | In good sadness, sir, I am sorry that for my sake you have suffered all this. My suit then is desperate. You’ll undertake her no more? |
| SIR JOHN | Master Brooke, I will be thrown into Etna as I have been into Thames ere I will leave her thus. Her husband is this morning gone a-birding. I have received from her another embassy of meeting. ’Twixt eight and nine is the hour, Master Brooke. |
| FORD | ’Tis past eight already, sir. |
| SIR JOHN | Is it? I will then address me to my appointment. Come to me at your convenient leisure, and you shall know how I [[Speed|speed]]; and the conclusion shall be crowned with your enjoying her. Adieu. You shall have her, Master Brooke; Master Brooke, you shall cuckold Ford. Exit |
| FORD | Hum! Ha! Is this a vision? Is this [[A Dream|a dream]]? Do I [[Sleep|sleep]]? [[Master Ford]], awake! Awake, [[Master Ford]]! There’s a hole made in your best coat, [[Master Ford]]. This ‘tis to be married! This ’tis to have linen and buck-baskets! [[Well]], I will proclaim myself what I am. I will now take the lecher. He is at my [[house]]. He cannot scape me; ’tis impossible he should. He cannot creep into a halfpenny purse, nor into a pepperbox. But lest the devil that guides him should aid him, I will search impossible places. Though what I am I cannot avoid, yet to be what I would not shall not make me tame. If I have horns to make one mad, let the proverb go with me: I’ll be horn-mad. Exit |
