| *(didascalia)* | Enter Master [[Fenton]] and [[Anne Page]] |
| [[Fenton|FENTON]] | I see I cannot get thy father’s [[Love|love]];<br>Therefore no more turn me to him, sweet Nan. |
| ANNE | Alas, how then? |
| [[Fenton|FENTON]] | Why, thou must be thyself.<br>He doth object I am too great of birth,<br>And that, my state being galled with my expense,<br>I seek to heal it only by his wealth.<br>Besides these, other bars he lays before me—<br>My riots past, my wild societies;<br>And tells me ’tis a thing impossible<br>I should [[Love|love]] thee but as a property. |
| ANNE | Maybe he tells you true. |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Fenton|FENTON]] |
| ANNE | No, heaven so [[Speed|speed]] me in my [[Time|time]] to come!<br>Albeit I will confess thy father’s wealth<br>Was the first motive that I wooed thee, Anne,<br>Yet, wooing thee, I found thee of more value<br>Than stamps in gold or sums in sealèd bags;<br>And ’tis the very riches of thyself<br>That now I aim at. |
| ANNE | Gentle Master [[Fenton]],<br>Yet seek my father’s [[Love|love]], still seek it, sir.<br>If opportunity and humblest suit<br>Cannot attain it, why then— |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Justice Shallow]], Master Slender richly dressedl, and [[Mistress Quickly]] |
| ANNE | Hark you hither.<br>They talk apart |
| SHALLOW | Break their talk, [[Mistress Quickly]]. My kinsman shall speak for himself. |
| SLENDER | I’ll make a shaft or a bolt on’t. ’Slid, ’tis but venturing. |
| SHALLOW | Be not dismayed. |
| SLENDER | No, she shall not dismay me.<br>I care not for that, but that I am afeard. |
| [[Mistress Quickly|MISTRESS QUICKLY]] | (to Anne) Hark ye, Master Slender would speak a word with you. |
| ANNE | I come to him. (To [[Fenton]]) This is my father’s choice.<br>O, what a world of vile ill-favoured faults<br>Looks handsome in three hundred pounds a year I |
| [[Mistress Quickly|MISTRESS QUICKLY]] | And how does good Master [[Fenton]]?<br>Pray you, a word with you.<br>She draws [[Fenton]] aside |
| SHALLOW | She’s coming. To her, coz! O boy, thou hadst a father! |
| SLENDER | I had a father, Mistress Anne; my uncle can tell you good jests of him.—Pray you, uncle, tell Mistress Anne the jest how my father stole two geese out of a pen, good uncle. |
| SHALLOW | Mistress Anne, my cousin loves you. |
| SLENDER | Ay, that I do, as [[Well|well]] as I [[Love|love]] any woman in Gloucestershire. |
| SHALLOW | He will maintain you like a gentlewoman. |
| SLENDER | Ay, by God, that I will, come cut and long-tail, under the degree of a squire. |
| SHALLOW | He will make you a hundred and fifty pounds jointure. |
| ANNE | Good Master Shallow, let him woo for himself. |
| SHALLOW | Marry, I thank you for it, I thank you for that good comfort.—She calls you, coz. I’ll leave you.<br>He stands aside |
| ANNE | Now, Master Slender. |
| SLENDER | Now, good Mistress Anne. |
| ANNE | What is your will? |
| SLENDER | My will? ‘Od’s heartlings, that’s a pretty jest indeed! I ne’er made my will yet, I thank God; I am not such a sickly creature, I give God praise. |
| ANNE | I mean, Master Slender, what would you with me? |
| SLENDER | Truly, for mine own part, I would little or nothing with you. Your father and my uncle hath made motions. If it be my luck, so. If not, happy man be his dole. They can tell you how things go better than I can. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Master Page]] and [[Mistress Page]] |
| SLENDER | You may ask your father: here he comes. |
| PAGE | Now, Master Stender.—[[Love]] him, daughter Anne.—<br>Why, how now? What does Master [[Fenton]] here?<br>You wrong me, sir, thus still to haunt my [[house]].<br>I told you, sir, my daughter is disposed of. |
| [[Fenton|FENTON]] | Nay, [[Master Page]], be not impatient. |
| [[Mistress Page|MISTRESS PAGE]] | Good Master [[Fenton]], come not to my [[Child|child]]. |
| PAGE | She is no match for you. |
| [[Fenton|FENTON]] | Sir, will you hear me? |
| PAGE | No, good Master Penton.—<br>Come, Master Shallow; come, son Slender, in.—<br>Knowing my mind, you wrong me, Master [[Fenton]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt Page, Shallow, and Slender |
| [[Mistress Quickly|MISTRESS QUICKLY]] | (to [[Fenton]]) Speak to [[Mistress Page]]. |
| [[Fenton|FENTON]] | Good [[Mistress Page]], for that I [[Love|love]] your daughter<br>In such a righteous fashion as I do,<br>Perforce against all checks, rebukes, and manners<br>I must advance the colours of my [[Love|love]],<br>And not retire. Let me have your good will. |
| ANNE | Good mother, do not marry me to yon [[Fool|fool]]. |
| [[Mistress Page|MISTRESS PAGE]] | I mean it not; I seek you a better husband. |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Mistress Quickly|MISTRESS QUICKLY]] aside to Anne That’s my master, Master Doctor. |
| ANNE | Alas, I had rather be set quick i’th’ earth<br>And bowled to [[Death|death]] with turnips. |
| [[Mistress Page|MISTRESS PAGE]] | Come, trouble not yourself, good Master [[Fenton]].<br>I will not be your friend nor enemy.<br>My daughter will I question how she loves you,<br>And as I find her, so am I affected.<br>Till then, farewell, sir. She must needs go in.<br>Her father will be angry. |
| [[Fenton|FENTON]] | Farewell, gentle mistress.—Farewell, Nan. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt [[Mistress Page]] and Anne |
| [[Mistress Quickly|MISTRESS QUICKLY]] | This is my doing now. ‘Nay’, said I, ‘will you cast away your [[Child|child]] on a [[Fool|fool]] and a physician? Look on Master [[Fenton]].’ This is my doing. |
| [[Fenton|FENTON]] | I thank thee, (giving her a ring) and I pray thee, once<br>tonight<br>Give my sweet Nan this ring. (Giving money) There’s<br>for thy pains. |
| [[Mistress Quickly|MISTRESS QUICKLY]] | Now heaven send thee good fortune! |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit [[Fenton]] |
| [[Mistress Quickly|MISTRESS QUICKLY]] | A kind heart he hath. A woman would run through [[Fire|fire]] and [[Water|water]] for such a kind heart. But yet I would my master had Mistress Anne; or I would Master Slender had her; or, in sooth, I would Master [[Fenton]] had her. I will do what I can for them all three, for so I have promised, and I’ll be as good as my word—but speciously for Master [[Fenton]]. [[Well]], I must of another errand to Sir John [[Falstaff]] from my two mistresses. What a beast am I to slack it! Exit |
