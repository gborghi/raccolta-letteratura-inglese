| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Mistress Page]], [[Mistress Ford]], and [[Doctor Caius]] |
| [[Mistress Page|MISTRESS PAGE]] | Master Doctor, my daughter is in green. When you see your [[Time|time]], take her by the hand, away with her to the deanery, and dispatch it quickly. Go before into the Park. We two must go together. |
| CAIUS | I know vat I have to do. Adieu. |
| [[Mistress Page|MISTRESS PAGE]] | Fare you [[Well|well]], sir. Exit Caius<br>My husband will not rejoice so much at the abuse of<br>[[Falstaff]] as he will chafe at the doctor’s marrying my<br>daughter. But ’tis no matter. Better a little chiding than<br>a great deal of heartbreak. |
| [[Mistress Ford|MISTRESS FORD]] | Where is Nan now, and her troop of fairies, and the Welsh devil Hugh? |
| [[Mistress Page|MISTRESS PAGE]] | They are all couched in a pit hard by Herne’s Oak, with obscured lights, which, at the very instant of [[Falstaff]]’s and our meeting, they will at once display to the [[Night|night]]. |
| [[Mistress Ford|MISTRESS FORD]] | That cannot choose but amaze him. |
| [[Mistress Page|MISTRESS PAGE]] | If he be not amazed, he will be mocked. If he be amazed, he will every way be mocked. |
| [[Mistress Ford|MISTRESS FORD]] | We’ll betray him finely. |
| [[Mistress Page|MISTRESS PAGE]] | Against such lewdsters and their lechery<br>Those that betray them do no treachery. |
| [[Mistress Ford|MISTRESS FORD]] | The hour draws on. To the Oak, to the<br>Oak I Exeunt |
