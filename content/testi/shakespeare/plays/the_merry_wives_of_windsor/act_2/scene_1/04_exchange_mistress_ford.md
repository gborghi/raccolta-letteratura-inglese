|---|---|
| [[Mistress Ford|MISTRESS FORD]] | Nay, I will consent to act any villainy against him that may not sully the chariness of our honesty. O that my husband saw this letter! It would give eternal food to his [[Jealousy|jealousy]]. Enter [[Master Ford]] with [[Pistol]], and [[Master Page]] with Nim |
| [[Mistress Page|MISTRESS PAGE]] | Why, look where he comes, and my goodman too. He’s as far from [[Jealousy|jealousy]] as I am from giving him cause; and that, I [[Hope|hope]], is an unmeasurable distance. |
| [[Mistress Ford|MISTRESS FORD]] | You are the happier woman. |
| [[Mistress Page|MISTRESS PAGE]] | Let’s consult together against this greasy knight. Come hither. They withdraw. |
| FORD | [[Well]], I [[Hope|hope]] it be not so. |
| [[Pistol|PISTOL]] | [[Hope]] is a curtal dog in some affairs. Sir John affects thy wife. |
| FORD | Why, sir, my wife is not young. |
| [[Pistol|PISTOL]] | He woos both high and low, both rich and poor, Both young and old, one with another, Ford. He loves the gallimaufry, Ford. Perpend. |
| FORD | [[Love]] my wife? |
| [[Pistol|PISTOL]] | With liver burning hot. Prevent, Or go thou like Sir Actaeon, he, With Ringwood at thy heels. O, odious is the name! |
| FORD | What name, sir? |
| [[Pistol|PISTOL]] | The horn, I say. Farewell. Take heed; have open eye; for thieves do foot by [[Night|night]]. Take heed ere [[Summer|summer]] comes, or cuckoo-[[Birds|birds]] do sing.— Away, Sir Corporal Nim!—Believe it, Page; he speaks sense. Exit |
| FORD | (aside) I will be patient. I will find out this. |
| NIM | (to Page) And this is true. I like not the [[Humour|humour]] of lying. He hath wronged me in some humours. I should have borne the humoured letter to her; but I have a [[Sword|sword]], and it shall bite upon my necessity. He loves your wife. There’s the short and the long. My name is Corporal Nim. I speak and I avouch ’tis true. My name is Nim, and [[Falstaff]] loves your wife. Adieu. I [[Love|love]] not the [[Humour|humour]] of bread and [[cheese]]. Adieu. Exit |
| PAGE | (aside) The [[Humour|humour]] of it, quoth a? Here’s a fellow frights English out of his wits. |
| FORD | (aside) I will seek out [[Falstaff]]. |
| PAGE | (aside) I never heard such a drawling, affecting rogue. |
| FORD | (aside) If I do find it—[[Well|well]]. |
| PAGE | (aside) I will not believe such a Cathayan though the priest o’th’ town commended him for a true man. |
| FORD | (aside) ’Twas a good, sensible fellow. [[Well]]. [[Mistress Page]] and [[Mistress Ford]] come forward |
| PAGE | How now, Meg? |
| [[Mistress Page|MISTRESS PAGE]] | Whither go you, George? Hark you. They talk apart |
| [[Mistress Ford|MISTRESS FORD]] | How now, sweet Frank? Why art thou [[Melancholy|melancholy]]? |
| FORD | I [[Melancholy|melancholy]]? I am not [[Melancholy|melancholy]]. Get you [[Home|home]], go. |
| [[Mistress Ford|MISTRESS FORD]] | [[Faith]], thou hast some crotchets in thy head now. Will you go, [[Mistress Page]]? |
| [[Mistress Page|MISTRESS PAGE]] | Have with you.—You’ll come to dinner, George? Enter [[Mistress Quickly]] (Aside to [[Mistress Ford]]) Look who comes yonder. She shall be our messenger to this paltry knight. |
| [[Mistress Ford|MISTRESS FORD]] | (aside to [[Mistress Page]]) Trust me, I thought on her. She’ll fit it. |
| [[Mistress Page|MISTRESS PAGE]] | (to [[Mistress Quickly]]) You are come to see my daughter Anne? |
| [[Mistress Quickly|MISTRESS QUICKLY]] | Ay, forsooth; and I pray how does good Mistress Anne? |
| [[Mistress Page|MISTRESS PAGE]] | Go in with us and see. We have an hour’s talk with you. Exeunt [[Mistress Page]], [[Mistress Ford]], and [[Mistress Quickly]] |
