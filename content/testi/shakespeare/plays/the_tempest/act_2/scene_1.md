| [[Gonzalo|GONZALO]] | (to [[Alonso]])<br>Beseech you, sir, be merry. You have cause,<br>So have we all, of joy; for our escape<br>Is much beyond our loss. Our hint of woe<br>Is common; every day some sailor’s wife,<br>The masters of some merchant, and the merchant,<br>Have just our theme of woe. | [[Alonso|ALONSO]] | Prithee, peace. |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | (to [[Antonio]]) He receives comfort like cold porridge. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | The visitor will not give him o’er so. |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | Look, he’s winding up the watch of his wit. By and by it will strike. |
| [[Gonzalo|GONZALO]] | (to [[Alonso]]) Sir— |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | (to [[Antonio]]) One: tell. |
| [[Gonzalo|GONZALO]] | (to [[Alonso]])<br>When every grief is entertained that’s offered,<br>Comes to th’entertainer— |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | A dollar. |
| [[Gonzalo|GONZALO]] | Dolour comes to him indeed. You have spoken truer than you purposed. |
| SEABASTIAN | You have taken it wiselier than I meant you should. |
| [[Gonzalo|GONZALO]] | (to [[Alonso]]) Therefore my lord— |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | (to [[Sebastian]]) Fie, what a spendthrift is he of his tongue! |
| [[Alonso|ALONSO]] | (to [[Gonzalo]]) I prithee, spare. |
| [[Gonzalo|GONZALO]] | [[Well]], I have done. But yet— |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | (to [[Antonio]]) He will be talking. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | Which of he or Adrian, for a good wager, first begins to crow? |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | The old cock. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | The cockerel. |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | Done. The wager? |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | A laughter. 35 |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | A match! |
| ADRIAN | (to [[Gonzalo]]) Though this island seem to be [[Desert|desert]]— |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] (to [[Sebastian]]) Ha, ha, ha! |
| *(didascalia)* | SEBASTIANSo, you’re paid. |

| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | (to [[Antonio]]) As if it had lungs, and rotten ones. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | Or as ’twere perfumed by a fen. |

| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | (to [[Sebastian]]) True, save means to live. |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | Of that there’s none, or little. |
| [[Gonzalo|GONZALO]] | (to Adrian) How lush and lusty the grass looks! How green! |

| SEABASTIAN | With an eye of green in’t. |

| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | No, he doth but mistake the [[Truth|truth]] totally. |
| [[Gonzalo|GONZALO]] | (to Adrian) But the rarity of it is, which is indeed almost beyond credit— |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | (to [[Antonio]]) As many vouched rarities are. |

| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | ’Twas a sweet [[Marriage|marriage]], and we prosper [[Well|well]] in our return. |

| ADRIAN | (to [[Gonzalo]]) ‘Widow Dido’ said you? You make me study of that: she was of Carthage, not of Tunis. |
| [[Gonzalo|GONZALO]] | This Tunis, sir, was Carthage. |
| ADRIAN | Carthage? |
| [[Gonzalo|GONZALO]] | I assure you, Carthage. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | (to [[Sebastian]]) His word is more than the miraculous harp. |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | He hath raised the wall, and houses too. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | What impossible matter will he make easy next? |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | I think he will carry this island [[Home|home]] in his pocket, and give it his son for an apple. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | And sowing the kernels of it in the sea, bring forth more islands. |
| [[Gonzalo|GONZALO]] | (to Adrian) Ay. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | (to [[Sebastian]]) Why, in good [[Time|time]]. |
| [[Gonzalo|GONZALO]] | (to [[Alonso]]) Sir, we were talking that our garments seem now as fresh as when we were at Tunis, at the [[Marriage|marriage]] of your daughter, who is now [[Queen|queen]]. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | And the rarest that e’er came there. |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | Bate, I beseech you, widow Dido. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | O, widow Dido? Ay, widow Dido. |
| [[Gonzalo|GONZALO]] | (to [[Alonso]]) Is not, sir, my doublet as fresh as the first day I wore it? I mean in a sort. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | (to [[Sebastian]]) That ‘sort’ was [[Well|well]] fished for. |
| [[Gonzalo|GONZALO]] | (to [[Alonso]]) When I wore it at your daughter’s [[Marriage|marriage]]. |
| [[Alonso|ALONSO]] | You cram these words into mine ears against<br>The stomach of my sense. Would I had never<br>Married my daughter there! For, coming thence,<br>My son is lost; and, in my rate, she too,<br>Who is so far from [[Italy]] removed<br>I ne’er again shall see her. O thou mine heir<br>Of Naples and of Milan, what strange fish<br>Hath made his meal on thee? |
| FRANCISCO | Sir, he may live.<br>I saw him beat the surges under him<br>And ride upon their backs. He trod the [[Water|water]],<br>Whose enmity he flung aside, and breasted<br>The surge, most swoll‘n, that met him. His bold head<br>’Bove the contentious waves he kept, and oared<br>Himself with his good arms in lusty stroke<br>To th’ shore, that o’er his wave-worn basis bowed,<br>As stooping to relieve him. I not [[Doubt|doubt]]<br>He came alive to land. |
| [[Alonso|ALONSO]] | No, no; he’s gone. |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | (to [[Alonso]])<br>Sir, you may thank yourself for this great loss,<br>That would not bless our Europe with your daughter,<br>But rather loose her to an African,<br>Where she, at least, is banished from your eye,<br>Who hath cause to wet the grief on’t. |
| [[Alonso|ALONSO]] | Prithee, peace. |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | You were kneeled to and importuned otherwise<br>By all of us, and the fair [[Soul|soul]] herself<br>Weighed between loathness and obedience at<br>Which end o’th’ beam should bow. We have lost your<br>son,<br>I [[Fear|fear]], for ever. Milan and Naples have<br>More widows in them of this business’ making<br>Than we bring men to comfort them. The fault’s your<br>own. |
| [[Alonso|ALONSO]] | So is the dear‘st o’th’ loss. |
| [[Gonzalo|GONZALO]] | My lord [[Sebastian]],<br>The [[Truth|truth]] you speak doth lack some gentleness<br>And [[Time|time]] to speak it in. You rub the sore<br>When you should bring the plaster. |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | (to [[Antonio]]) Very [[Well|well]]. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | And most chirurgeonly. |
| [[Gonzalo|GONZALO]] | (to [[Alonso]])<br>It is foul weather in us all, good sir,<br>When you are cloudy. |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | (to [[Antonio]]) Fowl weather? |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | Very foul. |
| [[Gonzalo|GONZALO]] | (to [[Alonso]])<br>Had I plantation of this isle, my lord— |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | (to Seabastian)<br>He’d sow’t with nettle-seed. |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | Or docks, or mallows. |
| [[Gonzalo|GONZALO]] | And were the [[King|king]] on’t, what would I do? |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | (to [[Antonio]])<br>Scape being drunk, for want of wine. |
| [[Gonzalo|GONZALO]] | I’th’ commonwealth I would by contraries<br>Execute all things. For no kind of traffic<br>Would I admit, no name of magistrate;<br>Letters should not be known; riches, [[Poverty|poverty]],<br>And use of service, none; contract, succession,<br>Bourn, bound of land, tilth, vineyard, none;<br>No use of metal, corn, or wine, or oil;<br>No occupation, all men idle, all;<br>And women too—but [[Innocent|innocent]] and pure;<br>No sovereignty— |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | (to [[Antonio]]) Yet he would be [[King|king]] on’t. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | The latter end of his commonwealth forgets the beginning. |
| [[Gonzalo|GONZALO]] | (to [[Alonso]])<br>All things in common [[Nature|nature]] should produce<br>Without sweat or endeavour. Treason, felony,<br>[[Sword]], pike, knife, gun, or need of any engine,<br>Would I not have; but [[Nature|nature]] should bring forth<br>Of it own kind all foison, all abundance,<br>To feed my [[Innocent|innocent]] people. |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | (to [[Antonio]]) No marrying ’mong his subjects? |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | None, man, all idle: whores and knaves. |
| [[Gonzalo|GONZALO]] | (to [[Alonso]])<br>I would with such perfection govern, sir,<br>T’excel the [[Golden Age]]. |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | Save his majesty! |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | Long live Gonzalol |
| [[Gonzalo|GONZALO]] | (to [[Alonso]]) And—do you mark me, sir? |
| [[Alonso|ALONSO]] | Prithee, no more. Thou dost talk nothing to me. |
| [[Gonzalo|GONZALO]] | I do [[Well|well]] believe your highness, and did it to minister occasion to these gentlemen, who are of such sensible and nimble lungs that they always use to laugh at nothing. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | ’Twas you we laughed at. |
| [[Gonzalo|GONZALO]] | Who, in this kind of merry fooling, am nothing to you. So you may continue, and laugh at nothing still. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | What a blow was there given! |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | An it had not fallen flat-long. |
| [[Gonzalo|GONZALO]] | You are gentlemen of brave mettle. You would lift the [[Moon|moon]] out of her sphere, if she would continue in it five weeks without changing. |

| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | We would so, and then go a-bat-fowling. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | (to [[Gonzalo]]) Nay, good my lord, be not angry. |
| [[Gonzalo|GONZALO]] | No, I warrant you, I will not adventure my discretion so weakly. Will you laugh me asleep? For I am very heavy. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | Go [[Sleep|sleep]], and hear us.<br>[[Gonzalo]],<br>Adrian, and Francisco [[Sleep|sleep]] |
| [[Alonso|ALONSO]] | What, all so soon asleep? I wish mine eyes<br>Would, with themselves, shut up my thoughts.—I find<br>They are inclined to do so. |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | Please you, sir,<br>Do not omit the heavy offer of it.<br>It seldom visits sorrow; when it doth,<br>It is a comforter. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | We two, my lord,<br>Will guard your person while you take your rest,<br>And watch your safety. |
| [[Alonso|ALONSO]] | Thank you. Wondrous heavy. |
| *(didascalia)* | He sleeps. Exit [[Ariel]] |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | What a strange drowsiness possesses them! |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | It is the quality o’th’ climate. |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | Why<br>Doth it not then our eyelids sink? I find<br>Not myself disposed to [[Sleep|sleep]]. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | Nor I; my spirits are nimble.<br>They fell together all, as by consent;<br>They dropped as by a thunderstroke. What might,<br>Worthy [[Sebastian]], O, what might—? No more!—<br>And yet methinks I see it in thy face.<br>What thou shouldst be th’occasion speaks thee, and<br>My strong [[Imagination|imagination]] sees a [[Crown|crown]]<br>Dropping upon thy head. |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | What, art thou waking? |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | Do you not hear me speak? |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | I do, and surely<br>It is a sleepy language, and thou speak’st<br>Out of thy [[Sleep|sleep]]. What is it thou didst say?<br>This is a strange repose, to be asleep<br>With eyes wide open; standing, speaking, moving,<br>And yet so fast asleep. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | Noble [[Sebastian]],<br>Thou letst thy fortune [[Sleep|sleep]], die rather; wink’st<br>Whiles thou art waking. |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | Thou dost snore distinctly;<br>There’s meaning in thy snores. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | I am more serious than my custom. You<br>Must be so too if heed me, which to do<br>Trebles thee o’er. |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | [[Well]], I am standing [[Water|water]]. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | I’ll teach you how to flow. |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | Do so; to ebb<br>Hereditary sloth instructs me. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | O,<br>If you but knew how you the purpose cherish<br>Whiles thus you mock it; how in stripping it<br>You more invest it! Ebbing men, indeed,<br>Most often do so near the [[Bottom|bottom]] run<br>By their own [[Fear|fear]] or sloth. |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | Prithee, say on.<br>The setting of thine eye and cheek proclaim<br>A matter from thee, and a birth, indeed,<br>Which throes thee much to yield. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | Thus, sir.<br>Although this lord of weak remembrance, this,<br>Who shall be of as little [[Memory|memory]]<br>When he is earthed, hath here almost persuaded—<br>For he’s a spirit of [[Persuasion|persuasion]], only<br>Professes to persuade—the [[King]] his son’s alive,<br>’Tis as impossible that he’s undrowned<br>As he that sleeps here swims. |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | I have no [[Hope|hope]]<br>That he’s undrowned. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | O, out of that ‘no [[Hope|hope]]’<br>What great [[Hope|hope]] have you! No [[Hope|hope]] that way is<br>Another way so high a [[Hope|hope]] that even<br>[[Ambition]] cannot pierce a wink beyond,<br>But [[Doubt|doubt]] discovery there. Will you grant with me<br>That [[Ferdinand]] is drowned? |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | He’s gone. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | Then tell me,<br>Who’s the next heir of Naples? |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | Claribel. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | She that is [[Queen]] of Tunis; she that dwells<br>Ten leagues beyond man’s life; she that from Naples<br>Can have no note—unless the sun were post—<br>The man i’th’ [[Moon|moon]]’s too slow—till new-born chins<br>Be rough and razorable; she that from whom<br>We all were sea-swallowed, though some cast again—<br>And by that destiny, to perform an act<br>Whereof what’s past is prologue, what to come<br>In yours and my discharge. |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | What stuff is this? How say you?<br>‘Tis true my brother’s daughter’s [[Queen]] of Tunis;<br>So is she heir of Naples; ’twixt which regions<br>There is some space. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | A space whose every cubit<br>Seems to cry out ‘How shall that Claribel<br>Measure us back to Naples? Keep in Tunis,<br>And let [[Sebastian]] wake.’ Say this were [[Death|death]]<br>That now hath seized them; why, they were no worse<br>Than now they are. There be that can rule Naples<br>As [[Well|well]] as he that sleeps, lords that can prate<br>As amply and unnecessarily<br>As this [[Gonzalo]]; I myself could make<br>A chough of as deep chat. O, that you bore<br>The mind that I do, what a [[Sleep|sleep]] were this<br>For your advancement! Do you understand me? |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | Methinks I do. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | And how does your content<br>Tender your own good fortune? |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | I remember<br>You did supplant your brother Prospero. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | True;<br>And look how [[Well|well]] my garments sit upon me,<br>Much feater than before. My brother’s servants<br>Were then my fellows; now they are my men. |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | But for your [[Conscience|conscience]]. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | Ay, sir, where lies that? If ‘twere a kibe<br>’Twould put me to my slipper; but I feel not<br>This deity in my bosom. Twenty consciences<br>That stand ’twixt me and Milan, candied be they,<br>And melt ere they molest. Here lies your brother,<br>No better than the earth he lies upon<br>If he were that which now he’s like—that’s dead;<br>Whom I with this obedient steel, three inches of it,<br>Can lay to bed for ever; whiles you, doing thus,<br>To the perpetual wink for aye might put<br>This ancient morsel, this Sir Prudence, who<br>Should not upbraid our course. For all the rest,<br>They’ll take suggestion as a cat laps milk;<br>They’ll tell the [[Clock|clock]] to any business that<br>We say befits the hour. |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | Thy case, dear friend,<br>Shall be my precedent. As thou got’st Milan,<br>I’ll come by Naples. Draw thy [[Sword|sword]]. One stroke<br>Shall free thee from the tribute which thou payest,<br>And I the [[King]] shall [[Love|love]] thee. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | Draw together,<br>And when I rear my hand, do you the like<br>To fall it on [[Gonzalo]].<br>They draw |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | O, but one word. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Ariel]], invisible, with music |
| [[Ariel|ARIEL]] | (to [[Gonzalo]])<br>My master through his art foresees the danger<br>That you his friend are in—and sends me forth,<br>For else his project dies, to keep them living.<br>He sings in [[Gonzalo]]’s ear<br>While you here do snoring lie,<br>Open-eyed conspiracy<br>His [[Time|time]] doth take.<br>If of life you keep a care,<br>Shake off slumber, and beware.<br>Awake, awake! |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | (to [[Sebastian]])<br>Then let us both be sudden. |
| [[Gonzalo|GONZALO]] | (awaking)<br>Now good angels<br>Preserve the [[King]]! |
| [[Alonso|ALONSO]] | (awaking)<br>Why, how now? Ho, awake!<br>The others awake<br>(To [[Antonio]] and [[Sebastian]])<br>Why are you drawn?<br>(To [[Gonzalo]]) Wherefore this ghastly looking? |
| [[Gonzalo|GONZALO]] | What’s the matter? |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | Whiles we stood here securing your repose,<br>Even now we heard a hollow burst of bellowing,<br>Like bulls, or rather lions. Did’t not wake you?<br>It struck mine ear most terribly. |
| [[Alonso|ALONSO]] | I heard nothing. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | O, ’twas a din to fright a [[Monster|monster]]’s ear,<br>To make an earthquake! Sure it was the roar<br>Of a whole herd of lions. |
| [[Alonso|ALONSO]] | Heard you this, [[Gonzalo]]? |
| [[Gonzalo|GONZALO]] | Upon mine [[Honour|honour]], sir, I heard a humming,<br>And that a strange one too, which did awake me.<br>I shaked you, sir, and cried. As mine eyes opened<br>I saw their weapons drawn. There was a noise,<br>That’s verily. ’Tis best we stand upon our guard,<br>Or that we quit this place. Let’s draw our weapons. |
| [[Alonso|ALONSO]] | Lead off this ground, and let’s make further search<br>For my poor son. |
| [[Gonzalo|GONZALO]] | Heavens keep him from these beasts!<br>For he is sure i’th’ island. |
| [[Alonso|ALONSO]] | Lead away. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt all but [[Ariel]] |
| [[Ariel|ARIEL]] | Prospero my lord shall know what I have done.<br>So, [[King]], go safely on to seek thy son. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit |
| [[Ariel|ARIEL]] | 2.2 Enter [[Caliban]], wearing a gaberdine, and with a<br>burden of wood |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Caliban|CALIBAN]] throwing down his burden |
| [[Ariel|ARIEL]] | All the infections that the sun sucks up<br>From bogs, fens, flats, on Prosper fall, and make him<br>By inch-meal a disease! |
| *(didascalia)* | A noise of thunder heard |
| [[Ariel|ARIEL]] | His spirits hear me,<br>And yet I needs must curse. But they’ll nor pinch,<br>Fright me with urchin-shows, pitch me i’th’ mire,<br>Nor lead me like a [[Fire|fire]]-brand in the dark<br>Out of my way, unless he bid ’em. But<br>For every trifle are they set upon me;<br>Sometime like apes, that mow and chatter at me<br>And after bite me; then like hedgehogs, which<br>Lie tumbling in my barefoot way and mount<br>Their pricks at my footfall; sometime am I<br>All wound with adders, who with cloven tongues<br>Do hiss me into [[Madness|madness]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Trinculo]] |
| [[Ariel|ARIEL]] | Lo now, lo!<br>Here comes a spirit of his, and to torment me<br>For bringing wood in slowly. I’ll fall flat.<br>Perchance he will not mind me.<br>He lies down |
| [[Trinculo|TRINCULO]] | Here’s neither bush nor shrub to bear off any weather at all, and another [[Storm|storm]] brewing. I hear it sing i‘th’ [[Wind|wind]]. Yon same black cloud, yon huge one, looks like a foul bombard that would shed his liquor. If it should thunder as it did before, I know not where to hide my head. Yon same cloud cannot choose but fall by pailfuls. (Seeing [[Caliban]]) What have we here, a man or a fish? Dead or alive?—A fish, he smells like a fish; a very ancient and fish-like smell; a kind of not-of-the-newest poor-john. A strange fish! Were I in England now, as once I was, and had but this fish painted, not a holiday-[[Fool|fool]] there but would give a piece of silver. There would this [[Monster|monster]] make a man. Any strange beast there makes a man. When they will not give a doit to relieve a lame beggar, they will lay out ten to see a dead Indian. Legged like a man, and his fins like arms! Warm, o’my troth! I do now let loose my opinion, hold it no longer. This is no fish, but an islander that hath lately suffered by a thunderbolt. |
