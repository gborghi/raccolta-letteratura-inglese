| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Alonso]], [[Sebastian]], [[Antonio]], [[Gonzalo]], Adrian, and Francisco |
| [[Gonzalo|GONZALO]] | (to [[Alonso]]) Beseech you, sir, be merry. You have cause, So have we all, of joy; for our escape Is much beyond our loss. Our hint of woe Is common; every day some sailor’s wife, The masters of some merchant, and the merchant, Have just our theme of woe. But for the miracle, I mean our preservation, few in millions Can speak like us. Then wisely, good sir, weigh Our sorrow with our comfort. |
| [[Alonso|ALONSO]] | Prithee, peace. |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | (to [[Antonio]]) He receives comfort like cold porridge. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | The visitor will not give him o’er so. |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | Look, he’s winding up the watch of his wit. By and by it will strike. |
| [[Gonzalo|GONZALO]] | (to [[Alonso]]) Sir— |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | (to [[Antonio]]) One: tell. |
| [[Gonzalo|GONZALO]] | (to [[Alonso]]) When every grief is entertained that’s offered, Comes to th’entertainer— |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | A dollar. |
| [[Gonzalo|GONZALO]] | Dolour comes to him indeed. You have spoken truer than you purposed. |
| SEABASTIAN | You have taken it wiselier than I meant you should. |
| [[Gonzalo|GONZALO]] | (to [[Alonso]]) Therefore my lord— |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | (to [[Sebastian]]) Fie, what a spendthrift is he of his tongue! |
| [[Alonso|ALONSO]] | (to [[Gonzalo]]) I prithee, spare. |
| [[Gonzalo|GONZALO]] | [[Well]], I have done. But yet— |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | (to [[Antonio]]) He will be talking. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | Which of he or Adrian, for a good wager, first begins to crow? |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | The old cock. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | The cockerel. |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | Done. The wager? |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | A laughter. 35 |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | A match! |
| ADRIAN | (to [[Gonzalo]]) Though this island seem to be [[Desert|desert]]— [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] (to [[Sebastian]]) Ha, ha, ha! SEBASTIANSo, you’re paid. |
| ADRIAN | Uninhabitable, and almost inaccessible— |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | (to [[Antonio]]) Yet— |
| ADRIAN | Yet— |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | (to [[Sebastian]]) He could not miss’t. |
| ADRIAN | It must needs be of subtle, tender, and delicate temperance. 45 |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | (to [[Sebastian]]) Temperance was a delicate wench. |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | Ay, and a subtle, as he most learnedly delivered. |
| ADRIAN | (to [[Gonzalo]]) The air breathes upon us here most sweetly. |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | (to [[Antonio]]) As if it had lungs, and rotten ones. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | Or as ’twere perfumed by a fen. |
| [[Gonzalo|GONZALO]] | (to Adrian) Here is everything advantageous to life. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | (to [[Sebastian]]) True, save means to live. |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | Of that there’s none, or little. |
| [[Gonzalo|GONZALO]] | (to Adrian) How lush and lusty the grass looks! How green! |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | The ground indeed is tawny. |
| SEABASTIAN | With an eye of green in’t. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | He misses not much. |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | No, he doth but mistake the [[Truth|truth]] totally. |
| [[Gonzalo|GONZALO]] | (to Adrian) But the rarity of it is, which is indeed almost beyond credit— |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | (to [[Antonio]]) As many vouched rarities are. |
| [[Gonzalo|GONZALO]] | (to Adrian) That our garments being, as they were, drenched in the sea, hold notwithstanding their freshness and glosses, being rather new-dyed than stained with salt [[Water|water]]. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | (to [[Sebastian]]) If but one of his pockets could speak, would it not say he lies? |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | Ay, or very falsely pocket up his report. |
| [[Gonzalo|GONZALO]] | (to Adrian) Methinks our garments are now as fresh as when we put them on first in Afric, at the [[Marriage|marriage]] of the [[King]]’s fair daughter Claribel to the [[King]] of Tunis. |
