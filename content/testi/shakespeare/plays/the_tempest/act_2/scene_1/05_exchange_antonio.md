| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | A space whose every cubit Seems to cry out ‘How shall that Claribel Measure us back to Naples? Keep in Tunis, And let [[Sebastian]] wake.’ Say this were [[Death|death]] That now hath seized them; why, they were no worse Than now they are. There be that can rule Naples As [[Well|well]] as he that sleeps, lords that can prate As amply and unnecessarily As this [[Gonzalo]]; I myself could make A chough of as deep chat. O, that you bore The mind that I do, what a [[Sleep|sleep]] were this For your advancement! Do you understand me? |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | Methinks I do. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | And how does your content Tender your own good fortune? |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | I remember You did supplant your brother Prospero. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | True; And look how [[Well|well]] my garments sit upon me, Much feater than before. My brother’s servants Were then my fellows; now they are my men. |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | But for your [[Conscience|conscience]]. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | Ay, sir, where lies that? If ‘twere a kibe ’Twould put me to my slipper; but I feel not This deity in my bosom. Twenty consciences That stand ’twixt me and Milan, candied be they, And melt ere they molest. Here lies your brother, No better than the earth he lies upon If he were that which now he’s like—that’s dead; Whom I with this obedient steel, three inches of it, Can lay to bed for ever; whiles you, doing thus, To the perpetual wink for aye might put This ancient morsel, this Sir Prudence, who Should not upbraid our course. For all the rest, They’ll take suggestion as a cat laps milk; They’ll tell the [[Clock|clock]] to any business that We say befits the hour. |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | Thy case, dear friend, Shall be my precedent. As thou got’st Milan, I’ll come by Naples. Draw thy [[Sword|sword]]. One stroke Shall free thee from the tribute which thou payest, And I the [[King]] shall [[Love|love]] thee. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | Draw together, And when I rear my hand, do you the like To fall it on [[Gonzalo]]. They draw |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | O, but one word. Enter [[Ariel]], invisible, with music |
| [[Ariel|ARIEL]] | (to [[Gonzalo]]) My master through his art foresees the danger That you his friend are in—and sends me forth, For else his project dies, to keep them living. He sings in [[Gonzalo]]’s ear While you here do snoring lie, Open-eyed conspiracy His [[Time|time]] doth take. If of life you keep a care, Shake off slumber, and beware. Awake, awake! |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | (to [[Sebastian]]) Then let us both be sudden. |
| [[Gonzalo|GONZALO]] | (awaking) Now good angels Preserve the [[King]]! |
| [[Alonso|ALONSO]] | (awaking) Why, how now? Ho, awake! The others awake (To [[Antonio]] and [[Sebastian]]) Why are you drawn? (To [[Gonzalo]]) Wherefore this ghastly looking? |
| [[Gonzalo|GONZALO]] | What’s the matter? |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | Whiles we stood here securing your repose, Even now we heard a hollow burst of bellowing, Like bulls, or rather lions. Did’t not wake you? It struck mine ear most terribly. |
| [[Alonso|ALONSO]] | I heard nothing. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | O, ’twas a din to fright a [[Monster|monster]]’s ear, To make an earthquake! Sure it was the roar Of a whole herd of lions. |
| [[Alonso|ALONSO]] | Heard you this, [[Gonzalo]]? |
