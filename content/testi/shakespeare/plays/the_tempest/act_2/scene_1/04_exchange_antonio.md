| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | We two, my lord, Will guard your person while you take your rest, And watch your safety. |
| [[Alonso|ALONSO]] | Thank you. Wondrous heavy. He sleeps. Exit [[Ariel]] |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | What a strange drowsiness possesses them! |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | It is the quality o’th’ climate. |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | Why Doth it not then our eyelids sink? I find Not myself disposed to [[Sleep|sleep]]. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | Nor I; my spirits are nimble. They fell together all, as by consent; They dropped as by a thunderstroke. What might, Worthy [[Sebastian]], O, what might—? No more!— And yet methinks I see it in thy face. What thou shouldst be th’occasion speaks thee, and My strong [[Imagination|imagination]] sees a [[Crown|crown]] Dropping upon thy head. |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | What, art thou waking? |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | Do you not hear me speak? |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | I do, and surely It is a sleepy language, and thou speak’st Out of thy [[Sleep|sleep]]. What is it thou didst say? This is a strange repose, to be asleep With eyes wide open; standing, speaking, moving, And yet so fast asleep. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | Noble [[Sebastian]], Thou letst thy fortune [[Sleep|sleep]], die rather; wink’st Whiles thou art waking. |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | Thou dost snore distinctly; There’s meaning in thy snores. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | I am more serious than my custom. You Must be so too if heed me, which to do Trebles thee o’er. |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | [[Well]], I am standing [[Water|water]]. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | I’ll teach you how to flow. |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | Do so; to ebb Hereditary sloth instructs me. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | O, If you but knew how you the purpose cherish Whiles thus you mock it; how in stripping it You more invest it! Ebbing men, indeed, Most often do so near the [[Bottom|bottom]] run By their own [[Fear|fear]] or sloth. |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | Prithee, say on. The setting of thine eye and cheek proclaim A matter from thee, and a birth, indeed, Which throes thee much to yield. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | Thus, sir. Although this lord of weak remembrance, this, Who shall be of as little [[Memory|memory]] When he is earthed, hath here almost persuaded— For he’s a spirit of [[Persuasion|persuasion]], only Professes to persuade—the [[King]] his son’s alive, ’Tis as impossible that he’s undrowned As he that sleeps here swims. |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | I have no [[Hope|hope]] That he’s undrowned. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | O, out of that ‘no [[Hope|hope]]’ What great [[Hope|hope]] have you! No [[Hope|hope]] that way is Another way so high a [[Hope|hope]] that even [[Ambition]] cannot pierce a wink beyond, But [[Doubt|doubt]] discovery there. Will you grant with me That [[Ferdinand]] is drowned? |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | He’s gone. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | Then tell me, Who’s the next heir of Naples? |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | Claribel. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | She that is [[Queen]] of Tunis; she that dwells Ten leagues beyond man’s life; she that from Naples Can have no note—unless the sun were post— The man i’th’ [[Moon|moon]]’s too slow—till new-born chins Be rough and razorable; she that from whom We all were sea-swallowed, though some cast again— And by that destiny, to perform an act Whereof what’s past is prologue, what to come In yours and my discharge. |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | What stuff is this? How say you? ‘Tis true my brother’s daughter’s [[Queen]] of Tunis; So is she heir of Naples; ’twixt which regions There is some space. |
