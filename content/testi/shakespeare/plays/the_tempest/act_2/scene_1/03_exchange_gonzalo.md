|---|---|
| [[Gonzalo|GONZALO]] | My lord [[Sebastian]], The [[Truth|truth]] you speak doth lack some gentleness And [[Time|time]] to speak it in. You rub the sore When you should bring the plaster. |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | (to [[Antonio]]) Very [[Well|well]]. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | And most chirurgeonly. |
| [[Gonzalo|GONZALO]] | (to [[Alonso]]) It is foul weather in us all, good sir, When you are cloudy. |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | (to [[Antonio]]) Fowl weather? |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | Very foul. |
| [[Gonzalo|GONZALO]] | (to [[Alonso]]) Had I plantation of this isle, my lord— |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | (to Seabastian) He’d sow’t with nettle-seed. |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | Or docks, or mallows. |
| [[Gonzalo|GONZALO]] | And were the [[King|king]] on’t, what would I do? |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | (to [[Antonio]]) Scape being drunk, for want of wine. |
| [[Gonzalo|GONZALO]] | I’th’ commonwealth I would by contraries Execute all things. For no kind of traffic Would I admit, no name of magistrate; Letters should not be known; riches, [[Poverty|poverty]], And use of service, none; contract, succession, Bourn, bound of land, tilth, vineyard, none; No use of metal, corn, or wine, or oil; No occupation, all men idle, all; And women too—but [[Innocent|innocent]] and pure; No sovereignty— |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | (to [[Antonio]]) Yet he would be [[King|king]] on’t. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | The latter end of his commonwealth forgets the beginning. |
| [[Gonzalo|GONZALO]] | (to [[Alonso]]) All things in common [[Nature|nature]] should produce Without sweat or endeavour. Treason, felony, [[Sword]], pike, knife, gun, or need of any engine, Would I not have; but [[Nature|nature]] should bring forth Of it own kind all foison, all abundance, To feed my [[Innocent|innocent]] people. |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | (to [[Antonio]]) No marrying ’mong his subjects? |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | None, man, all idle: whores and knaves. |
| [[Gonzalo|GONZALO]] | (to [[Alonso]]) I would with such perfection govern, sir, T’excel the [[Golden Age]]. |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | Save his majesty! |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | Long live Gonzalol |
| [[Gonzalo|GONZALO]] | (to [[Alonso]]) And—do you mark me, sir? |
| [[Alonso|ALONSO]] | Prithee, no more. Thou dost talk nothing to me. |
| [[Gonzalo|GONZALO]] | I do [[Well|well]] believe your highness, and did it to minister occasion to these gentlemen, who are of such sensible and nimble lungs that they always use to laugh at nothing. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | ’Twas you we laughed at. |
| [[Gonzalo|GONZALO]] | Who, in this kind of merry fooling, am nothing to you. So you may continue, and laugh at nothing still. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | What a blow was there given! |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | An it had not fallen flat-long. |
| [[Gonzalo|GONZALO]] | You are gentlemen of brave mettle. You would lift the [[Moon|moon]] out of her sphere, if she would continue in it five weeks without changing. Enter [[Ariel]], invisible, playing solemn music |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | We would so, and then go a-bat-fowling. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | (to [[Gonzalo]]) Nay, good my lord, be not angry. |
| [[Gonzalo|GONZALO]] | No, I warrant you, I will not adventure my discretion so weakly. Will you laugh me asleep? For I am very heavy. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | Go [[Sleep|sleep]], and hear us. [[Gonzalo]], Adrian, and Francisco [[Sleep|sleep]] |
| [[Alonso|ALONSO]] | What, all so soon asleep? I wish mine eyes Would, with themselves, shut up my thoughts.—I find They are inclined to do so. |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | Please you, sir, Do not omit the heavy offer of it. It seldom visits sorrow; when it doth, It is a comforter. |
