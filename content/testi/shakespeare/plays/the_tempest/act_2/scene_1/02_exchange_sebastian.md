| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | ’Twas a sweet [[Marriage|marriage]], and we prosper [[Well|well]] in our return. |
| ADRIAN | Tunis was never graced before with such a paragon to their [[Queen|queen]]. |
| [[Gonzalo|GONZALO]] | Not since widow Dido’s [[Time|time]]. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | (to [[Sebastian]]) Widow? A pox o‘that! How came that ‘widow’ in? Widow Dido! |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | What if he had said ‘widower Aeneas’ too? Good Lord, how you take it! |
| ADRIAN | (to [[Gonzalo]]) ‘Widow Dido’ said you? You make me study of that: she was of Carthage, not of Tunis. |
| [[Gonzalo|GONZALO]] | This Tunis, sir, was Carthage. |
| ADRIAN | Carthage? |
| [[Gonzalo|GONZALO]] | I assure you, Carthage. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | (to [[Sebastian]]) His word is more than the miraculous harp. |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | He hath raised the wall, and houses too. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | What impossible matter will he make easy next? |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | I think he will carry this island [[Home|home]] in his pocket, and give it his son for an apple. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | And sowing the kernels of it in the sea, bring forth more islands. |
| [[Gonzalo|GONZALO]] | (to Adrian) Ay. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | (to [[Sebastian]]) Why, in good [[Time|time]]. |
| [[Gonzalo|GONZALO]] | (to [[Alonso]]) Sir, we were talking that our garments seem now as fresh as when we were at Tunis, at the [[Marriage|marriage]] of your daughter, who is now [[Queen|queen]]. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | And the rarest that e’er came there. |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | Bate, I beseech you, widow Dido. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | O, widow Dido? Ay, widow Dido. |
| [[Gonzalo|GONZALO]] | (to [[Alonso]]) Is not, sir, my doublet as fresh as the first day I wore it? I mean in a sort. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | (to [[Sebastian]]) That ‘sort’ was [[Well|well]] fished for. |
| [[Gonzalo|GONZALO]] | (to [[Alonso]]) When I wore it at your daughter’s [[Marriage|marriage]]. |
| [[Alonso|ALONSO]] | You cram these words into mine ears against The stomach of my sense. Would I had never Married my daughter there! For, coming thence, My son is lost; and, in my rate, she too, Who is so far from [[Italy]] removed I ne’er again shall see her. O thou mine heir Of Naples and of Milan, what strange fish Hath made his meal on thee? |
| FRANCISCO | Sir, he may live. I saw him beat the surges under him And ride upon their backs. He trod the [[Water|water]], Whose enmity he flung aside, and breasted The surge, most swoll‘n, that met him. His bold head ’Bove the contentious waves he kept, and oared Himself with his good arms in lusty stroke To th’ shore, that o’er his wave-worn basis bowed, As stooping to relieve him. I not [[Doubt|doubt]] He came alive to land. |
| [[Alonso|ALONSO]] | No, no; he’s gone. |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | (to [[Alonso]]) Sir, you may thank yourself for this great loss, That would not bless our Europe with your daughter, But rather loose her to an African, Where she, at least, is banished from your eye, Who hath cause to wet the grief on’t. |
| [[Alonso|ALONSO]] | Prithee, peace. |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | You were kneeled to and importuned otherwise By all of us, and the fair [[Soul|soul]] herself Weighed between loathness and obedience at Which end o’th’ beam should bow. We have lost your son, I [[Fear|fear]], for ever. Milan and Naples have More widows in them of this business’ making Than we bring men to comfort them. The fault’s your own. |
| [[Alonso|ALONSO]] | So is the dear‘st o’th’ loss. |
