| [[Trinculo|TRINCULO]] | O, forgive me my sins! |
| STEFANO | He that dies pays all debts. (Calls) I defy thee.—[[Mercy]] upon us! |
| [[Caliban|CALIBAN]] | Art thou afeard? |
| STEFANO | No, [[Monster|monster]], not I. |
| [[Caliban|CALIBAN]] | Be not afeard. The isle is full of noises, Sounds, and sweet airs, that give delight and hurt not. Sometimes a thousand twangling instruments Will hum about mine ears, and sometime voices That if I then had waked after long [[Sleep|sleep]] Will make me [[Sleep|sleep]] again; and then in dreaming The clouds methought would open and show riches Ready to drop upon me, that when I waked I cried to dream again. |
| STEFANO | This will prove a brave kingdom to me, where I shall have my music for nothing. |
| [[Caliban|CALIBAN]] | When Prospero is destroyed. |
| STEFANO | That shall be by and by. I remember the story. Exit [[Ariel]], playing music |
| [[Trinculo|TRINCULO]] | The sound is going away. Let’s follow it, and after do our work. |
| STEFANO | Lead, [[Monster|monster]]; we’ll follow.—I would I could see this taborer. He lays it on. |
| TRICULO | (to [[Caliban]]) Wilt come? I’ll follow Stefano. Exeunt |
