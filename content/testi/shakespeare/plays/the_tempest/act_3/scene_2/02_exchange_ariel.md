| [[Ariel|ARIEL]] | Thou liest, thou canst not. |
| [[Caliban|CALIBAN]] | What a pied ninny’s thisl (To Tririculo) Thou scurvy patch! (To Stefano) I do beseech thy [[Greatness|greatness]] give him blows, And take his bottle from him. When that’s gone He shall drink naught but brine, for I’ll not show him Where the quick freshes are. |
| STEFANO | [[Trinculo]], run into no further danger. Interrupt the [[Monster|monster]] one word further, and, by this hand, I’ll turn my [[Mercy|mercy]] out o’doors and make a stockfish of thee. |
| [[Trinculo|TRINCULO]] | Why, what did I? I did nothing. I’ll go farther off. |
| STEFANO | Didst thou not say he lied? |
| [[Ariel|ARIEL]] | Thou liest. |
| STEFANO | Do I so? (Striking [[Trinculo]]) Take thou that. As you like this, give me the lie another [[Time|time]]. |
| [[Trinculo|TRINCULO]] | I did not give the lie. Out o‘your wits and hearing too? A pox o’your bottle! This can sack and drinking do. A murrain on your [[Monster|monster]], and the devil take your fingers. |
| [[Caliban|CALIBAN]] | Ha, ha, ha! |
| STEFANO | Now forward with your tale. (To [[Trinculo]]) Prithee, stand further off. |
| [[Caliban|CALIBAN]] | Beat him enough; after a little [[Time|time]] I’ll beat him too. |
| STEFANO | (to [[Trinculo]]) Stand farther. (To [[Caliban]]) Come, proceed. |
| [[Caliban|CALIBAN]] | Why, as I told thee, ’tis a custom with him I’th’ afternoon to [[Sleep|sleep]]. There thou mayst brain him, Having first seized his books; or with a log Batter his skull, or paunch him with a stake, Or cut his weasand with thy knife. Remember First to possess his books, for without them He’s but a sot as I am, nor hath not One spirit to command—they all do hate him As rootedly as I. Burn but his books. He has brave utensils, for so he calls them, Which when he has a [[house]] he’ll deck withal. And that most deeply to consider is The [[Beauty|beauty]] of his daughter. He himself Calls her a nonpareil. I never saw a woman But only Sycorax my dam and she, But she as far surpasseth Sycorax As great’st does least. |
| STEFANO | Is it so brave a lass? |
| [[Caliban|CALIBAN]] | Ay, lord. She will become thy bed, I warrant, And bring thee forth brave brood. |
| STEFANO | [[Monster]], I will kill this man. His daughter and I will be [[King|king]] and [[Queen|queen]]—save our graces!—and [[Trinculo]] and thyself shall be viceroys. Dost thou like the plot, [[Trinculo]]? no |
| [[Trinculo|TRINCULO]] | Excellent. |
| STEFANO | Give me thy hand. I am sorry I beat thee. But while thou liv’st, keep a good tongue in thy head. |
| [[Caliban|CALIBAN]] | Within this half hour will he be asleep. Wilt thou destroy him then? |
| STEFANO | Ay, on mine [[Honour|honour]]. |
| [[Ariel|ARIEL]] | (aside) This will I tell my master. |
| [[Caliban|CALIBAN]] | Thou mak’st me merry; I am full of pleasure. Let us be jocund. Will you troll the catch You taught me but while-ere? |
| STEFANO | At thy request, [[Monster|monster]], I will do reason, any reason.—Come on, [[Trinculo]], let us sing. (Sings) Flout ’em and cout ’em, And scout ’em and flout ’em. Thought is free. |
| [[Caliban|CALIBAN]] | That’s not the tune. [[Ariel]] plays the tune on a tabor and pipe |
| STEFANO | What is this same? |
| [[Trinculo|TRINCULO]] | This is the tune of our catch, played by the picture of Nobody. |
| STEFANO | (calls towards [[Ariel]]) If thou beest a man, show thyself in thy likeness. If thou beest a devil, take’t as thou list. |
