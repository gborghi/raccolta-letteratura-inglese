| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Caliban]], Stefano, and [[Trinculo]] |
| STEFANO | (to [[Caliban]]) Tell not me. When the butt is out we will drink [[Water|water]], not a drop before. Therefore bear up and board ’em. Servant [[Monster|monster]], drink to me. |
| [[Trinculo|TRINCULO]] | Servant [[Monster|monster]]? The folly of this island! They say there’s but five upon this isle. We are three of them; if th’other two be brained like us, the state totters. |
| STEFANO | Drink, servant [[Monster|monster]], when I bid thee. Thy eyes are almost set in thy head. |
| [[Trinculo|TRINCULO]] | Where should they be set else? He were a brave [[Monster|monster]] indeed if they were set in his tail. |
| STEFANO | My man-[[Monster|monster]] hath drowned his tongue in sack. For my part, the sea cannot drown me. I swam, ere I could recover the shore, five and thirty leagues, off and on. By this light, thou shalt be my lieutenant, [[Monster|monster]], or my standard. |
| [[Trinculo|TRINCULO]] | Your lieutenant if you list; he’s no standard. |
| STEFANO | We’ll not run, Monsieur [[Monster]]. |
| [[Trinculo|TRINCULO]] | Nor go neither; but you’ll lie like [[dogs]], and yet say nothing neither. |
| STEFANO | [[Moon]]-calf, speak once in thy life, if thou beest a good [[Moon|moon]]-calf. |
| [[Caliban|CALIBAN]] | How does thy [[Honour|honour]]? Let me lick thy shoe. I’ll not serve him; he is not valiant. |
| [[Trinculo|TRINCULO]] | Thou liest, most ignorant [[Monster|monster]]! I am in case to jostle a constable. Why, thou debauched fish, thou, was there ever man a coward that hath drunk so much sack as I today? Wilt thou tell a monstrous lie, being but half a fish and half a [[Monster|monster]]? |
| [[Caliban|CALIBAN]] | (to Stefano) Lo, how he mocks me! Wilt thou let him, my lord? |
| [[Trinculo|TRINCULO]] | ‘Lord’ quoth he? That a [[Monster|monster]] should be such a natural! |
| [[Caliban|CALIBAN]] | (to Stefano) Lo, lo, again! Bite him to [[Death|death]], I prithee. |
| STEFANO | [[Trinculo]], keep a good tongue in your head. If you prove a mutineer, the next tree. The poor [[Monster|monster]]’s my subject, and he shall not suffer indignity. |
| [[Caliban|CALIBAN]] | I thank my noble lord. Wilt thou be pleased To hearken once again to the suit I made to thee? |
| STEFANO | Marry, will I. Kneel and repeat it. I will stand, and so shall [[Trinculo]]. [[Caliban]] kneels. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Ariel]], invisible |
| [[Caliban|CALIBAN]] | As I told thee before, I am subject to a [[Tyrant|tyrant]], a sorcerer, that by his cunning hath cheated me of the island. |
| [[Ariel|ARIEL]] | Thou liest. |
| [[Caliban|CALIBAN]] | (to [[Trinculo]]) Thou liest, thou jesting monkey, thou. I would my valiant master would destroy thee. I do not lie. |
| STEFANO | [[Trinculo]], if you trouble him any more in’s tale, by this hand, I will supplant some of your teeth. |
| [[Trinculo|TRINCULO]] | Why, I said nothing. |
| STEFANO | Mum, then, and no more. (To [[Caliban]]) Proceed. |
| [[Caliban|CALIBAN]] | I say by sorcery he got this isle; From me he got it. If thy [[Greatness|greatness]] will [[Revenge]] it on him—for I know thou dar’st, But this thing dare not— |
| STEFANO | That’s most certain. |
| [[Caliban|CALIBAN]] | Thou shalt be lord of it, and I’ll serve thee. |
| STEFANO | How now shall this be compassed? Canst thou bring me to the party? |
| [[Caliban|CALIBAN]] | Yea, yea, my lord. I’ll yield him thee asleep Where thou mayst knock a nail into his head. |
