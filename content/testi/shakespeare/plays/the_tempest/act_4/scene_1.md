| *(didascalia)* | Enter Prospero, [[Ferdinand]], and [[Miranda]] |
| PROSPFRO | (to [[Ferdinand]])<br>If I have too austerely punished you,<br>Your compensation makes amends, for I<br>Have given you here a third of mine own tife—<br>Or that for which I live—who once again<br>I tender to thy hand. All thy vexations<br>Were but my trials of thy [[Love|love]], and thou<br>Hast strangely stood the test. Here, afore heaven,<br>I ratify this my rich gift. O [[Ferdinand]],<br>Do not smile at me that I boast of her,<br>For thou shalt find she will outstrip all praise,<br>And make it halt behind her. |
| [[Ferdinand|FERDINAND]] | I do believe it<br>Against an oracle. |
| PROSPERO | Then, as my gift and thine own acquisition<br>Worthily purchased, take my daughter. But<br>If thou dost break her virgin-knot before<br>All sanctimonious ceremonies may<br>With full and holy rite be ministered,<br>No sweet aspersion shall the heavens let fall<br>To make this contract grow; but barren hate,<br>Sour-eyed disdain, and discord, shall bestrew<br>The [[Union|union]] of your bed with weeds so loathly<br>That you shall hate it both. Therefore take heed,<br>As Hymen’s lamps shall light you. |
| [[Ferdinand|FERDINAND]] | As I [[Hope|hope]]<br>For quiet days, fair issue, and long life<br>With such [[Love|love]] as ’tis now, the murkiest den,<br>The most opportune place, the strong’st suggestion<br>Our worser genius can, shall never melt<br>Mine [[Honour|honour]] into lust to take away<br>The edge of that day’s celebration;<br>When I shall think or Phoebus’ steeds are foundered<br>Or [[Night|night]] kept chained below. |
| PROSPERO | Fairly spoke.<br>Sit, then, and talk with her. She is thine own.<br>[[Ferdinand]] and [[Miranda]] sit and talk together<br>What, [[Ariel]], my industrious servant [[Ariel]]! |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Ariel]] |
| [[Ariel|ARIEL]] | What would my potent master? Here I am. |
| PROSPERO | Thou and thy meaner fellows your last service<br>Did worthily perform, and I must use you<br>In such another trick. Go bring the rabble,<br>O’er whom I give thee power, here to this place.<br>Incite them to quick motion, for I must<br>Bestow upon the eyes of this young couple<br>Some [[Vanity|vanity]] of mine art. It is my promise,<br>And they expect it from me. |
| [[Ariel|ARIEL]] | Presently? |
| PROSPERO | Ay, with a twink. |
| [[Ariel|ARIEL]] | Before you can say ‘Come’ and ‘Go’, And breathe twice, and cry ‘So, so’,<br>Each one tripping on his toe<br>Will be here with mop and mow.<br>Do you [[Love|love]] me, master? No? |
| PROSPERO | Dearly, my delicate [[Ariel]]. Do not approach<br>Till thou dost hear me call. |
| [[Ariel|ARIEL]] | [[Well]]; I conceive. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit |
| PROSPERO | (to [[Ferdinand]])<br>Look thou be true. Do not give dalliance<br>Too much the rein. The strongest oaths are straw<br>To th’ [[Fire|fire]] i’th’ [[Blood|blood]]. Be more abstemious,<br>Or else, good [[Night|night]] your vow. |
| [[Ferdinand|FERDINAND]] | I warrant you, sir,<br>The white cold virgin [[Snow|snow]] upon my heart<br>Abates the ardour of my liver. |
| PROSPERO | [[Well]].—<br>Now come, my [[Ariel]]! Bring a corollary<br>Rather than want a spirit. Appear, and pertly.<br>Soft music<br>(To [[Ferdinand]] and [[Miranda]]) No tongue, all eyes! Be silent. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Iris |
| IRIS | Ceres, most bounteous lady, thy rich leas<br>Of wheat, rye, barley, vetches, oats, and peas;<br>Thy turfy [[Mountains|mountains]] where live nibbling sheep,<br>And flat meads thatched with stover, them to keep;<br>Thy banks with peonied and twillèd brims<br>Which spongy April at thy hest betrims<br>To make cold nymphs chaste crowns; and thy broom-groves,<br>Whose [[Shadow|shadow]] the dismissed bachelor loves,<br>Being lass-lorn; thy pole-clipped vineyard,<br>And thy sea-marge, sterile and rocky-hard,<br>Where thou thyself dost air: the [[Queen]] o’th’ Sky,<br>Whose wat’ry arch and messenger am I,<br>Bids thee leave these, and with her sovereign grace |
| *(didascalia)* | Juno appears in the air |
| IRIS | Here on this grass-plot, in this very place,<br>To come and sport.—Her peacocks fly amain.<br>Approach, rich Ceres, her to entertain. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Ariel]] as Ceres |
| CERES | Hail, many-coloured messenger, that ne’er<br>Dost disobey the wife of Jupiter;<br>Who with thy saffron wings upon my [[Flowers|flowers]]<br>Diffusest honey-drops, refreshing showers,<br>And with each end of thy blue bow dost [[Crown|crown]]<br>My bosky acres and my unshrubbed down,<br>Rich scarf to my proud earth. Why hath thy [[Queen|queen]]<br>Summoned me hither to this short-grassed green? |
| IRIS | A contract of true [[Love|love]] to celebrate,<br>And some donation freely to estate<br>On the blest [[lovers]]. |
| CERES | Tell me, heavenly bow,<br>If [[Venus]] or her son, as thou dost know,<br>Do now attend the [[Queen]]. Since they did plot<br>The means that dusky Dis my daughter got,<br>Her and her blind boy’s scandalled company<br>I have forsworn. |
| IRIS | Of her society<br>Be not afraid. I met her deity<br>Cutting the clouds towards Paphos, and her son<br>Dove-drawn with her. Here thought they to have<br>done<br>Some wanton charm upon this man and maid,<br>Whose vows are that no bed-right shall be paid<br>Till Hymen’s torch be lighted—but in vain.<br>Mars’s hot minion is returned again.<br>Her waspish-headed son has broke his arrows,<br>Swears he will shoot no more, but play with<br>sparrows,<br>And be a boy right out. |
| *(didascalia)* | Music. Juno descends to the stage |
| CERES | Highest [[Queen|queen]] of state,<br>Great Juno, comes; I know her by her gait. |
| JUNO | How does my bounteous sister? Go with me<br>To bless this twain, that they may prosperous be,<br>And honoured in their issue. |
| *(didascalia)* | Ceres joins Juno, and they sing |
| [[Ferdinand|FERDINAND]] | This is a most majestic vision, and<br>Harmonious charmingly. May I be bold<br>To think these spirits? |
| PROSPERO | Spirits, which by mine art<br>I have from their confines called to enact<br>My present fancies. |
| [[Ferdinand|FERDINAND]] | Let me live here ever!<br>So rare a wondered father and a wise<br>Makes this place paradise.<br>Juno and Ceres whisper, and send Iris on employment |
| PROSPERO | Sweet now, [[Silence|silence]].<br>Juno and Ceres whisper seriously.<br>There’s something else to do. Hush, and be mute,<br>Or else our spell is marred. |
| IRIS | You nymphs called naiads of the [[Wind|wind]]’ring brooks,<br>With your sedged crowns and ever-harmless looks,<br>Leave your crisp channels, and on this green land<br>Answer your summons; Juno does command.<br>Come, temperate nymphs, and help to celebrate<br>A contract of true [[Love|love]]. Be not too late. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter certain nymphs |
| IRIS | You sunburned sicklemen, of August weary,<br>Come hither from the furrow and be merry;<br>Make holiday, your rye-straw hats put on,<br>And these fresh nymphs encounter every one<br>In country footing. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter certain reapers, properly habited. They join with the nymphs in a graceful [[Dance|dance]]; towards the end whereof Prospero starts suddenly, and speaks |
| PROSPERO | (aside)<br>I had forgot that foul conspiracy<br>Of the beast [[Caliban]] and his confederates<br>Against my life. The minute of their plot<br>Is almost come. (To the spirits) [[Well]] done! Avoid; no<br>more! |
| *(didascalia)* | To a strange, hollow, and confused noise, the spirits in the pageant heavily vanish. [[Ferdinand]] and [[Miranda]] rise |
| [[Ferdinand|FERDINAND]] | (to [[Miranda]])<br>This is strange. Your father’s in some passion<br>That works him strongly. |
| [[Miranda|MIRANDA]] | Never till this day<br>Saw I him touched with anger so distempered. |
| PROSPERO | You do look, my son, in a moved sort,<br>As if you were dismayed. Be cheerful, sir.<br>Our revels now are ended. These our actors,<br>As I foretold you, were all spirits, and<br>Are melted into air, into thin air;<br>And like the baseless fabric of this vision,<br>The cloud-capped towers, the gorgeous palaces,<br>The solemn temples, the great globe itself,<br>Yea, all which it inherit, shall dissolve;<br>And, like this insubstantial pageant faded,<br>Leave not a rack behind. We are such stuff<br>As [[Dreams|dreams]] are made on, and our little life<br>Is rounded with a [[Sleep|sleep]]. Sir, I am vexed.<br>Bear with my weakness. My old brain is troubled.<br>Be not disturbed with my infirmity.<br>If you be pleased, retire into my cell,<br>And there repose. A turn or two I’ll walk<br>To still my beating mind. |
| [[Ferdinand|FERDINAND]] | and [[Miranda|MIRANDA]]<br>We wish your peace. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt [[Ferdinand]] and [[Miranda]] |
| PROSPERO | Come with a thought! I thank thee, [[Ariel]]. Come! |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Ariel]] |
| [[Ariel|ARIEL]] | Thy thoughts I cleave to. What’s thy pleasure? |
| PROSPERO | Spirit,<br>We must prepare to meet with [[Caliban]]. |
| [[Ariel|ARIEL]] | Ay, my commander. When I presented Ceres<br>I thought to have told thee of it, but I feared<br>Lest I might anger thee. |
| PROSPERO | Say again: where didst thou leave these varlets? |
| [[Ariel|ARIEL]] | I told you, sir, they were red-hot with drinking;<br>So full of valour that they smote the air<br>For breathing in their faces, beat the ground<br>For kissing of their feet; yet always bending<br>Towards their project. Then I beat my tabor,<br>At which like unbacked colts they pricked their ears,<br>Advanced their eyelids, lifted up their noses<br>As they smelt music. So I charmed their ears<br>That calf-like they my lowing followed, through<br>Toothed briars, sharp furzes, pricking gorse, and<br>thorns,<br>Which entered their frail shins. At last I left them<br>I’th’ filthy-mantled pool beyond your cell,<br>There dancing up to th’ chins, that the foul lake<br>O’er-stunk their feet. |
| PROSPERO | This was [[Well|well]] done, my bird.<br>Thy shape invisible retain thou still.<br>The trumpery in my [[house]], go bring it hither<br>For stale to catch these thieves. |
| [[Ariel|ARIEL]] | I go, I go. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit |
| PROSPERO | A devil, a born devil, on whose [[Nature|nature]]<br>Nurture can never stick; on whom my pains,<br>Humanely taken, all, all lost, quite lost,<br>And, as with age his [[Body|body]] uglier grows,<br>So his mind cankers. I will plague them all,<br>Even to roaring. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Ariel]], laden with glistening apparel, etc. |
| PROSPERO | Come, hang them on this lime. |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Ariel]] hangs up the apparel. Exeunt Prospero and |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Ariel]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Caliban]], Stefano, and [[Trinculo]], all wet |
| [[Caliban|CALIBAN]] | Pray you, tread softly, that the blind mole may<br>Not hear a foot fall. We now are near his cell. |
| STEFANO | [[Monster]], your fairy, which you say is a harmless fairy, has done little better than played the Jack with us. |
| [[Trinculo|TRINCULO]] | [[Monster]], I do smell all [[Horse|horse]]-piss, at which my nose is in great indignation. |
| STEFANO | So is mine. Do you hear, [[Monster|monster]]? If I should take a displeasure against you, look you— |
| [[Trinculo|TRINCULO]] | Thou wert but a lost [[Monster|monster]]. |
| [[Caliban|CALIBAN]] | Good my lord, give me thy favour still.<br>Be patient, for the prize I’ll bring thee to<br>Shall hoodwink this mischance. Therefore speak softly.<br>All’s hushed as midnight yet. |
| [[Trinculo|TRINCULO]] | Ay, but to lose our bottles in the pool! |
| STEFANO | There is not only disgrace and dishonour in that, [[Monster|monster]], but an infinite loss. |
| [[Trinculo|TRINCULO]] | That’s more to me than my wetting. Yet this is your harmless fairy, [[Monster|monster]]. |
| STEFANO | I will fetch off my bottle, though I be o’er ears for my labour. |
| [[Caliban|CALIBAN]] | Prithee, my [[King|king]], be quiet. Seest thou here;<br>This is the mouth o’th’ cell. No noise, and enter.<br>Do that good mischief which may make this island<br>Thine own for ever, and I thy [[Caliban]]<br>For aye thy foot-licker. |
| STEFANO | Give me thy hand.<br>I do begin to have bloody thoughts. |
| [[Trinculo|TRINCULO]] | (seeing the apparel) O [[King]] Stefano, O peer! O worthy Stefano, look what a wardrobe here is for thee! |
| [[Caliban|CALIBAN]] | Let it [[Alone|alone]], thou [[Fool|fool]], it is but trash. |
| [[Trinculo|TRINCULO]] | (putting on a gown) O ho, [[Monster|monster]], we know what belongs to a frippery! O [[King]] Stefano! |
| STEFANO | Put off that gown, [[Trinculo]]. By this hand, I’ll have that gown. |
| [[Trinculo|TRINCULO]] | Thy grace shall have it. |
| [[Caliban|CALIBAN]] | The dropsy drown this [[Fool|fool]]! What do you mean<br>To dote thus on such luggage? Let’t [[Alone|alone]],<br>And do the murder first. If he awake,<br>From toe to [[Crown|crown]] he’ll fill our skins with pinches,<br>Make us strange stuff. |
| STEFANO | Be you quiet, [[Monster|monster]].—Mistress lime, is not this my jerkin? Now is the jerkin under the line. Now, jerkin, you are like to lose your hair and prove a bald jerkin.<br>Stefano and [[Trinculo]] take garments |
| [[Trinculo|TRINCULO]] | Do, do! We steal by line and level, an’t like your grace. |
| STEFANO | I thank thee for that jest. Here’s a garment for’t. Wit shall not go unrewarded while I am [[King|king]] of this country. ‘Steal by line and level’ is an excellent pass of pate. There’s another garment for’t. |
| [[Trinculo|TRINCULO]] | [[Monster]], come, put some lime upon your fingers, and away with the rest. |
| [[Caliban|CALIBAN]] | I will have none on’t. We shall lose our [[Time|time]],<br>And all be turned to barnacles, or to apes<br>With foreheads villainous low. |
| STEFANO | [[Monster]], lay to your fingers. Help to bear this away where my hogshead of wine is, or I’ll turn you out of my kingdom. Go to, carry this. |
| [[Trinculo|TRINCULO]] | And this. |
| STEFANO | Ay, and this.<br>They load [[Caliban]] with apparel. A noise of hunters heard. Enter divers spirits in shape of [[dogs]] and hounds, hunting them about; Prospero and [[Ariel]] setting them on |
| PROSPERO | Hey, Mountain, hey! |
| [[Ariel|ARIEL]] | Silver! There it goes, Silver! |
| PROSPERO | Fury, Fury! There, [[Tyrant]], there! Hark, hark! |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt Stefano, [[Trinculo]], and [[Caliban]], pursued by spirits |
| PROSPERO | (To [[Ariel]]) Go, charge my goblins that they grind their<br>joints<br>With dry convulsions, shorten up their sinews<br>With aged cramps, and more pinch-spotted make<br>them<br>Than pard or cat o’mountain.<br>Cries within |
| [[Ariel|ARIEL]] | Hark, they roar! |
| PROSPERO | Let them be hunted soundly. At this hour<br>Lies at my [[Mercy|mercy]] all mine enemies.<br>Shortly shall all my labours end, and thou<br>Shalt have the air at [[Freedom|freedom]]. For a little,<br>Follow, and do me service. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt |
