| *(didascalia)* | Enter Prospero in his [[Magic|magic]] cloak, with a staff, and [[Miranda]] |
| [[Miranda|MIRANDA]] | If by your art, my dearest father, you have<br>Put the wild waters in this roar, allay them.<br>The sky, it seems, would pour down stinking pitch,<br>But that the sea, mounting to th’ welkin’s cheek,<br>Dashes the [[Fire|fire]] out. O, I have suffered<br>With those that I saw suffer! A brave vessel,<br>Who had, no [[Doubt|doubt]], some noble creature in her,<br>Dashed all to pieces! O, the cry did knock<br>Against my very heart! Poor souls, they perished.<br>Had I been any god of power, I would<br>Have sunk the sea within the earth, or ere<br>It should the good [[Ship|ship]] so have swallowed and<br>The fraughting souls within her. |
| PROSPERO | Be collected.<br>No more amazement. Tell your piteous heart<br>There’s no harm done. |
| [[Miranda|MIRANDA]] | O woe the day! |
| PROSPERO | No harm.<br>I have done nothing but in care of thee,<br>Of thee, my dear one, thee, my daughter, who<br>Art ignorant of what thou art, naught knowing<br>Of whence I am, nor that I am more better<br>Than Prospero, master of a full poor cell<br>And thy no greater father. |
| [[Miranda|MIRANDA]] | More to know<br>Did never meddle with my thoughts. |
| PROSPERO | ’Tis [[Time|time]]<br>I should inform thee farther. Lend thy hand,<br>And pluck my [[Magic|magic]] garment from me. |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Miranda]] removes Prospero’s cloak, and he lays it |
| *(didascalia)* | on the ground |
| PROSPERO | So.<br>Lie there, my art.—Wipe thou thine eyes; have comfort.<br>The direful spectacle of the wreck, which touched<br>The very virtue of compassion in thee,<br>I have with such provision in mine art<br>So safely ordered that there is no [[Soul|soul]]—<br>No, not so much perdition as an hair<br>Betid to any creature in the vessel,<br>Which thou heard‘st cry, which thou saw’st sink. Sit<br>down,<br>For thou must now know farther.<br>[[Miranda]] sits |
| [[Miranda|MIRANDA]] | You have often<br>Begun to tell me what I am, but stopped<br>And left me to a bootless [[Inquisition|inquisition]],<br>Concluding ‘Stay; not yet’. |
| PROSPERO | The hour’s now come.<br>The very minute bids thee ope thine ear,<br>Obey, and be attentive. Canst thou remember<br>A [[Time|time]] before we came unto this cell?<br>I do not think thou canst, for then thou wast not<br>Out three years old. |
| [[Miranda|MIRANDA]] | Certainly, sir, I can. |
| PROSPERO | By what? By any other [[house]] or person?<br>Of anything the image tell me that<br>Hath kept with thy remembrance. |
| [[Miranda|MIRANDA]] | ’Tis far off,<br>And rather like [[A Dream|a dream]] than an assurance<br>That my remembrance warrants. Had I not<br>Four or five women once that tended me? |
| PROSPERO | Thou hadst, and more, [[Miranda]]. But how is it<br>That this lives in thy mind? What seest thou else<br>In the dark backward and abyss of [[Time|time]]?<br>If thou rememb‘rest aught ere thou cam’st here,<br>How thou cam’st here thou mayst. |
| [[Miranda|MIRANDA]] | But that I do not. |
| PROSPERO | Twelve year since, [[Miranda]], twelve year since,<br>Thy father was the [[Duke of Milan]], and<br>A prince of power— |
| [[Miranda|MIRANDA]] | Sir, are not you my father? |
| PROSPERO | Thy mother was a piece of virtue, and<br>She said thou wast my daughter; and thy father<br>Was [[Duke of Milan]], and his only heir<br>And princess no worse issued. |
| [[Miranda|MIRANDA]] | O the heavens!<br>What foul play had we that we came from thence?<br>Or blessed was’t we did? |
| PROSPERO | Both, both, my girl.<br>By foul play, as thou sayst, were we heaved thence,<br>But blessedly holp hither. |
| [[Miranda|MIRANDA]] | O, my heart bleeds<br>To think o’th’ teen that I have turned you to,<br>Which is from my remembrance. Please you, farther. |
| PROSPERO | My brother and thy uncle called [[Antonio]]—<br>I pray thee mark me, that a brother should<br>Be so perfidious—he whom next thyself<br>Of all the world I loved, and to him put<br>The manage of my state—as at that [[Time|time]]<br>Through all the signories it was the first,<br>And Prospero the prime duke—being so reputed<br>In dignity, and for the liberal arts<br>Without a parallel—those being all my study,<br>The [[government]] I cast upon my brother,<br>And to my state grew stranger, being transported<br>And rapt in secret studies. Thy false uncle—<br>Dost thou attend me? |
| [[Miranda|MIRANDA]] | Sir, most heedfully. |
| PROSPERO | Being once perfected how to grant suits,<br>How to deny them, who t‘advance and who<br>To trash for over-topping, new created<br>The creatures that were mine, I say—or changed ’em<br>Or else new formed ’em; having both the key<br>Of officer and office, set all hearts i’th’ state<br>To what tune pleased his ear, that now he was<br>The ivy which had hid my princely trunk<br>And sucked my verdure out on’t. Thou attend’st not! |
| [[Miranda|MIRANDA]] | O good sir, I do. |
| PROSPERO | I pray thee mark me.<br>I, thus neglecting worldly ends, all dedicated<br>To closeness and the bettering of my mind<br>With that which but by being so retired<br>O‘er-priced all popular rate, in my false brother<br>Awaked an evil [[Nature|nature]]; and my trust,<br>Like a good parent, did beget of him<br>A falsehood, in its contrary as great<br>As my trust was, which had indeed no limit,<br>A confidence sans bound. He being thus lorded<br>Not only with what my revenue yielded<br>But what my power might else exact, like one<br>Who having into [[Truth|truth]], by telling oft,<br>Made such a sinner of his [[Memory|memory]]<br>To credit his own lie, he did believe<br>He was indeed the Duke. Out o’th’ substitution,<br>And executing th’outward face of royalty<br>With all prerogative, hence his [[Ambition|ambition]] growing—<br>Dost thou hear? |
| [[Miranda|MIRANDA]] | Your tale, sir, would cure deafness. |
| PROSPERO | To have no screen between this part he played<br>And him he played it for, he needs will be<br>Absolute Milan. Me, poor man—my library<br>Was dukedom large enough—of temporal royalties<br>He thinks me now incapable; confederates,<br>So dry he was for sway, wi’th’ [[King]] of Naples<br>To give him annual tribute, do him homage,<br>Subject his coronet to his [[Crown|crown]], and bend<br>The dukedom, yet unbowed—alas,poor Milan—<br>To most ignoble stooping. |
| [[Miranda|MIRANDA]] | O the heavens! |
| PROSPERO | Mark his condition and th’event, then tell me<br>If this might be a brother. |
| [[Miranda|MIRANDA]] | I should sin<br>To think but nobly of my grandmother.<br>Good wombs have borne bad sons. |
| PROSPERO | Now the condition.<br>This [[King]] of Naples, being an enemy<br>To me inveterate, hearkens my brother’s suit;<br>Which was that he, in lieu o‘th’ premises<br>Of homage and I know not how much tribute,<br>Should presently extirpate me and mine<br>Out of the dukedom, and confer fair Milan,<br>With all the honours, on my brother. Whereon,<br>A treacherous army levied, one midnight<br>Fated to th’ purpose did [[Antonio]] open<br>The gates of Milan; and, i’th’ dead of darkness,<br>The ministers for th’ purpose hurried thence<br>Me and thy crying [[Self|self]]. |
| [[Miranda|MIRANDA]] | Alack, for pity!<br>I, not rememb’ring how I cried out then,<br>Will cry it o’er again; it is a hint<br>That wrings mine eyes to’t. |
| *(didascalia)* | PROSPERO sitting |
| [[Miranda|MIRANDA]] | Hear a little further,<br>And then I’ll bring thee to the present business<br>Which now’s upon’s, without the which this story<br>Were most impertinent. |
| [[Miranda|MIRANDA]] | Wherefore did they not<br>That hour destroy us? |
| PROSPERO | [[Well]] demanded, wench;<br>My tale provokes that question. Dear, they durst not,<br>So dear the [[Love|love]] my people bore me; nor set<br>A mark so bloody on the business, but<br>With colours fairer painted their foul ends.<br>In few, they hurried us aboard a barque,<br>Bore us some leagues to sea, where they prepared<br>A rotten carcass of a butt, not rigged,<br>Nor tackle, sail, nor mast—the very rats<br>Instinctively have quit it. There they hoist us,<br>To cry to th’ sea that roared to us, to sigh<br>To th’winds, whose pity, sighing back again,<br>Did us but loving wrong. |
| [[Miranda|MIRANDA]] | Alack, what trouble<br>Was I then to you! |
| PROSPERO | O,a cherubin<br>Thou wast that did preserve me. Thou didst smile,<br>Infused with a fortitude from heaven,<br>When I have decked the sea with drops full salt,<br>Under my burden groaned; which raised in me<br>An undergoing stomach, to bear up<br>Against what should ensue. |
| [[Miranda|MIRANDA]] | How came we ashore? |
| PROSPERO | By [[Providence|providence]] divine.<br>Some food we had, and some fresh [[Water|water]], that<br>A noble Neapolitan, [[Gonzalo]],<br>Out of his [[Charity|charity]]—who being then appointed<br>Master of this design—did give us; with<br>Rich garments, linens, stuffs, and necessaries<br>Which since have steaded much. So, of his gentleness,<br>Knowing I loved my books, he furnished me<br>From mine own library with volumes that<br>I prize above my dukedom. |
| [[Miranda|MIRANDA]] | Would I might<br>But ever see that man! |
| PROSPERO | Now I arise. |
| *(didascalia)* | He stands and puts on his cloak |
| PROSPERO | Sit still, and hear the last of our sea-sorrow.<br>Here in this island we arrived, and here<br>Have I thy schoolmaster made thee more profit<br>Than other princes can, that have more [[Time|time]]<br>For vainer hours and tutors not so careful. |
| [[Miranda|MIRANDA]] | Heavens thank you for’t. And now I pray you, sir—<br>For still ’tis beating in my mind—your reason<br>For raising this sea-[[Storm|storm]]. |
| PROSPERO | Know thus far forth.<br>By accident most strange, bountiful Fortune,<br>Now my dear lady, hath mine enemies<br>Brought to this shore; and by my prescience<br>I find my zenith doth depend upon<br>A most auspicious star, whose influence<br>If now I [[Court|court]] not, but omit, my fortunes<br>Will ever after droop. Here cease more questions.<br>Thou art inclined to [[Sleep|sleep]]; ’tis a good dullness,<br>And give it way. I know thou canst not choose.<br>[[Miranda]] sleeps<br>Come away, servant, come! I am ready now.<br>Approach, my [[Ariel]], come! |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Ariel]] |
| [[Ariel|ARIEL]] | All hail, great master, [[Grave|grave]] sir, hail. I come<br>To answer thy best pleasure. Be’t to fly,<br><br>To swim, to dive into the [[Fire|fire]], to ride<br>On the curled clouds, to thy strong bidding task<br>[[Ariel]] and all his quality. |
| PROSPERO | Hast thou, spirit,<br>Performed to point the tempest that I bade thee? |
| [[Ariel|ARIEL]] | To every article.<br>I boarded the [[King]]’s [[Ship|ship]]. Now on the beak,<br>Now in the waste, the deck, in every cabin,<br>I flamed amazement. Sometime I’d divide,<br>And burn in many places; on the top-mast,<br>The yards, and bowsprit, would I flame distinctly;<br>Then meet and join. Jove’s [[Lightning|lightning]], the precursors<br>O’th’ dreadful thunderclaps, more momentary<br>And sight-outrunning were not. The [[Fire|fire]] and cracks<br>Of sulphurous roaring the most mighty Neptune<br>Seem to besiege, and make his bold waves tremble,<br>Yea, his dread trident shake. |
| PROSPERO | My brave spirit!<br>Who was so firm, so constant, that this coil<br>Would not infect his reason? |
| [[Ariel|ARIEL]] | Not a [[Soul|soul]]<br>But felt a fever of the mad, and played<br>Some tricks of desperation. All but mariners<br>Plunged in the foaming brine and quit the vessel,<br>Then all afire with me. The [[King]]’s son [[Ferdinand]],<br>With hair upstaring—then like reeds, not hair—<br>Was the first man that leaped; cried ‘Hell is empty,<br>And all the devils are here’. |
| PROSPERO | Why, that’s my spirit!<br>But was not this nigh shore? |
| [[Ariel|ARIEL]] | Close by, my master. |
| PROSPERO | But are they, [[Ariel]], safe? |
| [[Ariel|ARIEL]] | Not a hair perished.<br>On their sustaining garments not a blemish,<br>But fresher than before. And, as thou bad’st me,<br>In troops I have dispersed them ’bout the isle.<br>The [[King]]’s son have I landed by himself,<br>Whom I left cooling of the air with sighs<br>In an odd angle of the isle, and sitting,<br>His arms in this sad knot. |
| PROSPERO | Of the [[King]]’s [[Ship|ship]],<br>The mariners, say how thou hast disposed,<br>And all the rest o’th’ fleet. |
| [[Ariel|ARIEL]] | Safely in harbour<br>Is the [[King]]’s [[Ship|ship]], in the deep nook where cnce<br>Thou called‘st me up at midnight to fetch dew<br>From the still-vexed Bermudas, there she’s hid;<br>The mariners all under hatches stowed,<br>Who, with a charm joined to their suffered labour,<br>I have left asleep. And for the rest o’th’ fleet,<br>Which I dispersed, they all have met again,<br>And are upon the Mediterranean float<br>Bound sadly [[Home|home]] for Naples,<br>Supposing that they saw the [[King]]’s [[Ship|ship]] wrecked,<br>And his great person perish. |
| PROSPERO | [[Ariel]], thy charge<br>Exactly is performed; but there’s more work.<br>What is the [[Time|time]] o’th’ day? |
| [[Ariel|ARIEL]] | Past the mid season. |
| PROSPERO | At least two glasses. The [[Time|time]] ’twixt six and now<br>Must by us both be spent most preciously. |
| [[Ariel|ARIEL]] | Is there more toil? Since thou dost give me pains,<br>Let me remember thee what thou hast promised<br>Which is not yet performed me. |
| PROSPERO | How now? Moody?<br>What is’t thou canst demand? |
| [[Ariel|ARIEL]] | My liberty. |
| PROSPERO | Before the [[Time|time]] be out? No more! |
| [[Ariel|ARIEL]] | I prithee,<br>Remember I have done thee worthy service,<br>Told thee no lies, made thee no mistakings, served<br>Without or grudge or grumblings. Thou did promise<br>To bate me a full year. |
| PROSPERO | Dost thou forget<br>From what a torment I did free thee? |
| [[Ariel|ARIEL]] | No. |
| PROSPERO | Thou dost, and think‘st it much to tread the ooze<br>Of the salt deep,<br>To run upon the sharp [[Wind|wind]] of the north,<br>To do me business in the veins o’th’ earth<br>When it is baked with [[Frost|frost]]. |
| [[Ariel|ARIEL]] | I do not, sir. |
| PROSPERO | Thou liest, malignant thing. Hast thou forgot<br>The foul [[Witch|witch]] Sycorax, who with age and envy<br>Was grown into a hoop? Hast thou forgot her? |
| [[Ariel|ARIEL]] | No, sir. |
| PROSPERO | Thou hast. Where was she born? Speak, tell me! |
| [[Ariel|ARIEL]] | Sir, in Algiers. |
| PROSPERO | O, was she so! I must<br>Once in a month recount what thou hast been,<br>Which thou forget‘st. This damned [[Witch|witch]] Sycorax,<br>For mischiefs manifold and sorceries terrible<br>To enter human hearing, from Algiers<br>Thou know’st was banished. For one thing she did<br>They would not take her life. Is not this true? |
| [[Ariel|ARIEL]] | Ay, sir. |
| PROSPERO | This blue-eyed hag was hither brought with [[Child|child]],<br>And here was left by th’ sailors. Thou, my slave,<br>As thou report’st thyself, was then her servant;<br>And for thou wast a spirit too delicate<br>To act her earthy and abhorred commands,<br>Refusing her grand hests, she did confine thee<br>By help of her more potent ministers,<br>And in her most unmitigable rage,<br>Into a cloven pine; within which rift<br>Imprisoned thou didst painfully remain<br>A dozen years, within which space she died<br>And left thee there, where thou didst vent thy groans<br>As fast as mill-wheels strike. Then was this island—<br>Save for the son that she did litter here,<br>A freckled whelp, hag-born—not honoured with<br>A human shape. |
| [[Ariel|ARIEL]] | Yes, [[Caliban]] her son. |
| PROSPERO | Dull thing, I say so: he, that [[Caliban]]<br>Whom now I keep in service. Thou best know’st<br>What torment I did find thee in. Thy groans<br>Did make wolves howl, and penetrate the breasts<br>Of ever-angry bears; it was a torment<br>To lay upon the damned, which Sycorax<br>Could not again undo. It was mine art,<br>When I arrived and heard thee, that made gape<br>The pine and let thee out. |
| [[Ariel|ARIEL]] | I thank thee, master. |
| PROSPERO | If thou more murmur’st, I will rend an oak,<br>And peg thee in his knotty entrails till<br>Thou hast howled away twelve winters. |
| [[Ariel|ARIEL]] | Pardon, master.<br>I will be correspondent to command,<br>And do my spriting gently. |
| PROSPERO | Do so, and after two days<br>I will discharge thee. |
| [[Ariel|ARIEL]] | That’s my noble master!<br>What shall I do? Say what, what shall I do? |
| PROSPERO | Go make thyself like to a nymph o’th’ sea. Be subject<br>To no sight but thine and mine, invisible<br>To every eyeball else. Go take this shape,<br>And hither come in’t. Go; hence with diligence! |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit [[Ariel]] |
| PROSPERO | Awake, dear heart, awake! Thou hast slept [[Well|well]];<br>Awake. |
| [[Miranda|MIRANDA]] | (awaking) The strangeness of your story put<br>Heaviness in me. |
| PROSPERO | Shake it off. Come on;<br>We’ll visit [[Caliban]] my slave, who never<br>Yields us kind answer. |
| [[Miranda|MIRANDA]] | ’Tis a [[Villain|villain]], sir,<br>I do not [[Love|love]] to look on. |
| PROSPERO | But as ’tis,<br>We cannot miss him. He does make our [[Fire|fire]],<br>Fetch in our wood, and serves in offices<br>That profit us.—What ho! Slave, [[Caliban]]!<br>Thou earth, thou, speak! |
| [[Caliban|CALIBAN]] | (within)<br>There’s wood enough within. |
| PROSPERO | Come forth, I say! There’s other business for thee.<br>Come, thou tortoise! When? |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Ariel]], like a [[Water|water]]-nymph |
| PROSPERO | Fine apparition! My quaint [[Ariel]],<br>Hark in thine ear.<br>He whispers |
| [[Ariel|ARIEL]] | My lord, it shall be done. Exit |
| PROSPERO | Thou poisonous slave, got by the devil himself<br>Upon thy wicked dam, come forth! |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Caliban]] |
| [[Caliban|CALIBAN]] | As wicked dew as e’er my mother brushed<br>With raven’s feather from unwholesome fen<br>Drop on you both! A southwest blow on ye,<br>And blister you all o’er! |
| PROSPERO | For this be sure tonight thou shalt have cramps,<br>Side-stitches that shall pen thy breath up. Urchins<br>Shall forth at vast of [[Night|night]], that they may work<br>All exercise on thee. Thou shalt be pinched<br>As thick as honeycomb, each pinch more stinging<br>Than [[Bees|bees]] that made ’em. |
| [[Caliban|CALIBAN]] | I must eat my dinner.<br>This island’s mine, by Sycorax my mother,<br>Which thou tak’st from me. When thou cam’st first,<br>Thou strok’st me and made much of me, wouldst give me<br>[[Water]] with berries in’t, and teach me how<br>To name the bigger light, and how the less,<br>That burn by day and [[Night|night]]; and then I loved thee,<br>And showed thee all the qualities o’th’ isle,<br>The fresh springs, brine-pits, barren place and fertile—<br>Cursed be I that did so! All the charms<br>Of Sycorax, toads, beetles, bats, light on you;<br>For I am all the subjects that you have,<br>Which first was mine own [[King|king]], and here you sty me<br>In this hard rock, whiles you do keep from me<br>The rest o’th’ island. |
| PROSPERO | Thou most lying slave,<br>Whom stripes may move, not kindness! I have used<br>thee,<br>Filth as thou art, with human care, and lodged thee<br>In mine own cell, till thou didst seek to violate<br>The [[Honour|honour]] of my [[Child|child]]. |
| [[Caliban|CALIBAN]] | O ho,O ho! Would’t had been done!<br>Thou didst prevent me; I had peopled else<br>This isle with Calibans. |
| [[Miranda|MIRANDA]] | Abhorred slave,<br>Which any print of goodness wilt not take, Being capable of all ill! I pitied thee, Took pains to make thee speak, taught thee each hour One thing or other. When thou didst not, savage, Know thine own meaning, but wouldst gabble like A thing most brutish, I endowed thy purposes With words that made them known. But thy vile race, Though thou didst learn, had that in’t which good natures Could not abide to be with; therefore wast thou Deservedly confined into this rock, Who hadst deserved more than a [[Prison|prison]]. |
| [[Caliban|CALIBAN]] | You taught me language, and my profit on’t<br>Is I know how to curse. The red plague rid you<br>For learning me your language! |
| PROSPERO | Hag-seed, hence!<br>Fetch us in fuel. And be quick, thou’rt best,<br>To answer other business.—Shrug’st thou, malice?<br>If thou neglect’st or dost unwillingly<br>What I command, I’ll rack thee with old cramps,<br>Fill all thy bones with aches, make thee roar,<br>That beasts shall tremble at thy din. |
| [[Caliban|CALIBAN]] | No, pray thee.<br>(Aside) I must obey. His art is of such power<br>It would control my dam’s god Setebos,<br>And make a vassal of him. |
| PROSPERO | So, slave, hence! |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit [[Caliban]] |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Ariel]] like a [[Water|water]]-nymph,playing and |
| PROSPERO | singing, invisible to [[Ferdinand]], who follows. |
| *(didascalia)* | Prospero and [[Miranda]] stand aside |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Song]] |
| [[Ariel|ARIEL]] | Come unto these yellow sands, And then take hands;<br>Curtsied when you have and kissed—<br>The wild waves whist—<br>Foot it featly here and there,<br>And, sweet sprites, bear<br>The burden. Hark, hark. |
| *(didascalia)* | SPIRITS (dispersedly within) |
| [[Ariel|ARIEL]] | Bow-wow! |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Ariel|ARIEL]] |
| [[Ariel|ARIEL]] | The watch-[[dogs]] bark.<br>FSPIRITS] (within) Bow-wow! |
| [[Ariel|ARIEL]] | Hark, hark, I hear<br>The strain of strutting Chanticleer<br>Cry ‘cock-a-diddle-dow’. |
| [[Ferdinand|FERDINAND]] | Where should this music be? I’th’ air or th’earth?<br>It sounds no more; and sure it waits upon<br>Some god o‘th’ island. Sitting on a bank,<br>Weeping again the [[King]] my father’s wreck,<br>This music crept by me upon the waters,<br>Allaying both their fury and my passion<br>With its sweet air.
