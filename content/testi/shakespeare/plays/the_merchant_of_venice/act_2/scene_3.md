| [[Jessica|JESSICA]] | I am sorry thou wilt leave my father so.<br>Our [[house]] is hell, and thou, a merry devil,<br>Didst rob it of some taste of tediousness.<br>But fare thee [[Well|well]]. There is a ducat for thee.<br>And, Lancelot, soon at supper shalt thou see<br>[[Lorenzo]], who is thy new master’s guest.<br>Give him this letter, do it secretly;<br>And so farewell. | LANCELOT | Adieu. [[Tears]] exhibit my tongue, most beautiful pagan; most sweet Jew; if a Christian do not play the knave and get thee, I am much deceived. But adieu. These foolish drops do something drown my manly spirit. Adieu. |
