| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Nerissa]] and a servitor |
| [[Nerissa|NERISSA]] | Quick, quick, I pray thee, draw the curtain straight.<br>The Prince of Aragon hath ta’en his oath,<br>And comes to his election presently. |
| *(didascalia)* | The servitor draws aside the curtain, revealing the three caskets. Flourish of cornetts. Enter Aragon, his train, and [[Portia]] |
| [[Portia|PORTIA]] | Behold, there stand the caskets, noble Prince.<br>If you choose that wherein I am contained,<br>Straight shall our nuptial rites be solemnized.<br>But if you fail, without more speech, my lord,<br>You must be gone from hence immediately. |
| ARAGON | I am enjoined by oath to observe three things:<br>First, never to unfold to anyone 10<br>Which casket ’twas I chose. Next, if I fail<br>Of the right casket, never in my life<br>To woo a maid in way of [[Marriage|marriage]].<br>Lastly, if I do fail in fortune of my choice,<br>Immediately to leave you and be gone. 15 |
| [[Portia|PORTIA]] | To these injunctions everyone doth swear<br>That comes to hazard for my worthless [[Self|self]]. |
| ARAGON | And so have I addressed me. Fortune now<br>To my heart’s [[Hope|hope]]! Gold, silver, and base lead.<br>He reads the leaden casket<br>‘Who chooseth me must give and hazard all he hath.’<br>You shall look fairer ere I give or hazard.<br>What says the golden chest? Ha, let me see.<br>‘Who chooseth me shall gain what many men [[Desire|desire]].’<br>‘What many men [[Desire|desire]]’—that ‘many’ may be meant<br>By the [[Fool|fool]] multitude, that choose by show,<br>Not learning more than the fond eye doth teach,<br>Which pries not to th’interior but, like the martlet,<br>Builds in the weather on the outward wall<br>Even in the force and [[Road|road]] of casualty.<br>I will not choose what many men [[Desire|desire]],<br>Because I will not jump with common spirits<br>And rank me with the barbarous multitudes.<br>Why then, to thee, thou silver treasure-[[house]].<br>Tell me once more what title thou dost bear.<br>‘Who chooseth me shall get as much as he deserves’—<br>And [[Well|well]] said too, for who shall go about<br>To cozen fortune, and be honourable<br>Without the stamp of merit? Let none presume<br>To wear an undeserved dignity.<br>O, that estates, degrees, and offices<br>Were not derived corruptly, and that clear [[Honour|honour]]<br>Were purchased by the merit of the wearer!<br>How many then should cover that stand bare,<br>How many be commanded that command?<br>How much low peasantry would then be gleaned<br>From the true seed of [[Honour|honour]], and how much [[Honour|honour]]<br>Picked from the chaff and ruin of the times<br>To be new varnished? [[Well]]; but to my choice.<br>‘Who chooseth me shall get as much as he deserves.’<br>I will assume [[Desert|desert]]. Give me a key for this,<br>And instantly unlock my fortunes here. |
| *(didascalia)* | He is given a key. He opens the silver casket |
| [[Portia|PORTIA]] | Too long a pause for that which you find there. |
| ARAGON | What’s here? The portrait of a blinking idiot<br>Presenting me a schedule. I will read it.<br>How much unlike art thou to [[Portia]]!<br>How much unlike my hopes and my deservings!<br>‘Who chooseth me shall have as much as he deserves.’<br>Did I deserve no more than a [[Fool|fool]]’s head?<br>Is that my prize? Are my deserts no better? |
| [[Portia|PORTIA]] | To offend and judge are distinct offices,<br>And of opposed natures. |
| ARAGON | What is here?<br>He reads the schedule<br>‘The [[Fire|fire]] seven times tried this;<br>Seven times tried that judgement is<br>That did never choose amiss.<br>Some there be that shadows kiss;<br>Such have but a [[Shadow|shadow]]’s bliss.<br>There be fools alive, iwis,<br>Silvered o’er; and so was this.<br>Take what wife you will to bed,<br>I will ever be your head.<br>So be gone; you are sped.’<br>Still more [[Fool|fool]] I shall appear<br>By the [[Time|time]] I linger here.<br>With one [[Fool|fool]]’s head I came to woo,<br>But I go away with two.<br>Sweet, adieu. I’ll keep my oath<br>Patiently to bear my wroth. |
| *(didascalia)* | Flourish of cornetts. Exit with his train |
| [[Portia|PORTIA]] | Thus hath the candle singed the moth.<br>O, these deliberate fools! When they do choose<br>They have the wisdom by their wit to lose. 80 |
| [[Nerissa|NERISSA]] | The ancient saying is no heresy:<br>Hanging and wiving goes by destiny. |
| [[Portia|PORTIA]] | Come, draw the curtain, [[Nerissa]].<br>[[Nerissa]] draws the curtain. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter a Messenger |
| MESSENGER | Where is my lady? |
| [[Portia|PORTIA]] | Here. What would my lord? |
| MESSENGER | Madam, there is alighted at your gate<br>A young Venetian, one that comes before<br>To signify th’approaching of his lord,<br>From whom he bringeth sensible regreets,<br>To wit, besides commends and courteous breath,<br>Gifts of rich value. Yet I have not seen 90<br>So likely an ambassador of [[Love|love]].<br>A day in April never came so sweet<br>To show how costly [[Summer|summer]] was at hand<br>As this fore-spurrer comes before his lord. |
| [[Portia|PORTIA]] | No more, I pray thee, I am half afeard<br>Thou wilt say anon he is some kin to thee,<br>Thou spend’st such high-day wit in praising him.<br>Come, come, [[Nerissa]], for I long to see<br>Quick Cupid’s post that comes so mannerly. |
| [[Nerissa|NERISSA]] | [[Bassanio]], Lord [[Love]], if thy will it be! Exeunt |
