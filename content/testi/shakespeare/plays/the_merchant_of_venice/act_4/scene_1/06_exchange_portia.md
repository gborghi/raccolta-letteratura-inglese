| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | Ay, so he says. |
| [[Portia|PORTIA]] | Do you confess the bond? |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | I do. |
| [[Portia|PORTIA]] | Then must the Jew be merciful. |
