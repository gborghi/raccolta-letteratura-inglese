| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Shylock]] the Jew, Solanio, [[Antonio]], and the jailer |
| [[Shylock|SHYLOCK]] | Jailer, look to him. Tell not me of [[Mercy|mercy]].<br>This is the [[Fool|fool]] that lent out money gratis.<br>Jailer, look to him. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | Hear me yet, good [[Shylock]]. |
| [[Shylock|SHYLOCK]] | I’ll have my bond. Speak not against my bond.<br>I have sworn an oath that I will have my bond.<br>Thou called’st me dog before thou hadst a cause,<br>But since I am a dog, beware my fangs.<br>The Duke shall grant me justice. I do [[Wonder|wonder]],<br>Thou naughty jailer, that thou art so fond<br>To come abroad with him at his request. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | I pray thee hear me speak. |
| [[Shylock|SHYLOCK]] | I’ll have my bond. I will not hear thee speak.<br>I’ll have my bond, and therefore speak no more.<br>I’ll not be made a soft and dull-eyed [[Fool|fool]]<br>To shake the head, relent, and sigh, and yield<br>To Christian intercessors. Follow not.<br>I’ll have no speaking. I will have my bond. Exit |
| SOLANIO | It is the most impenetrable cur<br>That ever kept with men. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | Let him [[Alone|alone]].<br>I’ll follow him no more with bootless prayers.<br>He seeks my life. His reason [[Well|well]] I know:<br>I oft delivered from his forfeitures<br>Many that have at times made moan to me.<br>Therefore he hates me. |
| SOLANIO | I am sure the Duke<br>Will never grant this forfeiture to hold. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | The Duke cannot deny the course of law,<br>For the commodity that strangers have<br>With us in [[Venice]], if it be denied,<br>Will much impeach the justice of the state,<br>Since that the trade and profit of the [[City|city]]<br>Consisteth of all nations. Therefore go.<br>These griefs and losses have so bated me<br>That I shall hardly spare a pound of flesh<br>Tomorrow to my bloody creditor.<br>[[Well]], jailer, on. Pray God [[Bassanio]] come<br>To see me pay his debt, and then I care not. Exeunt |
