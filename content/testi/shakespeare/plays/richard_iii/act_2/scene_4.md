| *(didascalia)* | Enter Lord Cardinal, young [[Duke of York]], [[Queen Elizabeth]], and the old Duchess of York |
| *(didascalia)* | CARDINAL |
| *(didascalia)* | Last [[Night|night]], I hear, they lay them at Northampton.<br>At Stony Stratford they do rest tonight.<br>Tomorrow, or next day, they will be here. |
| DUCHESS OF YORK | I long with all my heart to see the Prince.<br>I [[Hope|hope]] he is much grown since last I saw him. |
| [[Queen Elizabeth|QUEEN ELIZABETH]] | But I hear, no. They say my son of York<br>Has almost overta’en him in his growth. |
| YORK | Ay, mother, but I would not have it so. |
| DUCHESS OF YORK | Why, my young cousin, it is good to grow. |
| YORK | Grandam, one [[Night|night]] as we did sit at supper,<br>My uncle Rivers talked how I did grow<br>More than my brother. ‘Ay’, quoth my nuncle<br>[[Gloucester]],<br>‘Small herbs have grace; gross weeds do grow apace’.<br>And since, methinks I would not grow so fast,<br>Because sweet flow’rs are slow, and weeds make<br>haste. |
| DUCHESS OF YORK | Good [[Faith|faith]], good [[Faith|faith]], the saying did not hold<br>In him that did object the same to thee.<br>He was the wretched’st thing when he was young,<br>So long a-growing, and so leisurely,<br>That if his rule were true he should be gracious. |
| *(didascalia)* | CARDINAL |
| DUCHESS OF YORK | Why, so no [[Doubt|doubt]] he is, my gracious madam. |
| DUCHESS OF YORK | I [[Hope|hope]] he is, but yet let mothers [[Doubt|doubt]]. |
| YORK | Now, by my troth, if I had been remembered,<br>I could have given my uncle’s grace a flout<br>To touch his growth, nearer than he touched mine. |
| DUCHESS OF YORK | How, my young York? I pray thee, let me hear it. |
| YORK | Marry, they say my uncle grew so fast<br>That he could gnaw a crust at two hours old.<br>’Twas full two years ere I could get a tooth.<br>Grannam, this would have been a biting jest. |
| DUCHESS OF YORK | I pray thee, pretty York, who told thee this? |
| YORK | Grannam, his [[Nurse|nurse]]. |
| DUCHESS OF YORK | His [[Nurse|nurse]]? Why, she was dead ere thou wast born. |
| YORK | If ’twere not she, I cannot tell who told me. |
| [[Queen Elizabeth|QUEEN ELIZABETH]] | A parlous boy! Go to, you are too shrewd. |
| *(didascalia)* | CARDINAL |
| [[Queen Elizabeth|QUEEN ELIZABETH]] | Good madam, be not angry with the [[Child|child]]. |
| [[Queen Elizabeth|QUEEN ELIZABETH]] | Pitchers have ears. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Marquis Dorset |
| *(didascalia)* | CARDINAL |
| [[Queen Elizabeth|QUEEN ELIZABETH]] | Here comes your son, Lord Dorset.<br>What news, Lord Marquis? |
| *(didascalia)* | DORSET Such news, my lord, |
| [[Queen Elizabeth|QUEEN ELIZABETH]] | As grieves me to report. |
| [[Queen Elizabeth|QUEEN ELIZABETH]] | How doth the Prince? |
| *(didascalia)* | DORSET |
| [[Queen Elizabeth|QUEEN ELIZABETH]] | [[Well]], madam, and in health. |
| DUCHESS OF YORK | What is thy news then? |
| *(didascalia)* | DORSET |
| DUCHESS OF YORK | Lord Rivers and Lord Gray are sent to Pomfret,<br>And with them Thomas Vaughan, prisoners. |
| DUCHESS OF YORK | Who hath committed them? |
| *(didascalia)* | DORSET |
| DUCHESS OF YORK | The mighty dukes,<br>[[Gloucester]] and [[Buckingham]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | CARDINAL |
| DUCHESS OF YORK | For what offence? |
| *(didascalia)* | DORSET |
| DUCHESS OF YORK | The sum of all I can, I have disclosed.<br>Why or for what the nobles were committed<br>Is all unknown to me, my gracious lord. |
| [[Queen Elizabeth|QUEEN ELIZABETH]] | Ay me! I see the ruin of our [[house]].<br>The tiger now hath seized the gentle hind.<br>Insulting tyranny begins to jet<br>Upon the [[Innocent|innocent]] and aweless throne.<br>Welcome destruction, [[Blood|blood]], and massacre!<br>I see, as in a map, the end of all. |
| DUCHESS OF YORK | Accursed and unquiet wrangling days,<br>How many of you have mine eyes beheld?<br>My husband lost his life to get the [[Crown|crown]],<br>And often up and down my sons were tossed,<br>For me to joy and weep their gain and loss.<br>And being seated, and domestic broils<br>Clean overblown, themselves the conquerors<br>Make war upon themselves, brother to brother,<br>[[Blood]] to [[Blood|blood]], [[Self|self]] against [[Self|self]]. O preposterous<br>And frantic outrage, end thy damned spleen,<br>Or let me die, to look on [[Death|death]] no more. |
| [[Queen Elizabeth|QUEEN ELIZABETH]] | (to York)<br>Come, come, my boy, we will to sanctuary.—<br>Madam, farewell. |
| DUCHESS OF YORK | Stay, I will go with you. |
| [[Queen Elizabeth|QUEEN ELIZABETH]] | You have no cause. |
| *(didascalia)* | CARDINAL (to Elizabeth) My gracious lady, go, |
| [[Queen Elizabeth|QUEEN ELIZABETH]] | And thither bear your treasure and your goods.<br>For my part, I’ll resign unto your grace<br>The seal I keep, and so betide to me<br>As [[Well|well]] I tender you and all of yours.<br>Go, I’ll conduct you to the sanctuary. Exeunt |
