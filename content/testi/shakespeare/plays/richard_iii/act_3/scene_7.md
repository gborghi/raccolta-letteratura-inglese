| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Richard]] [[Duke of Gloucester]] at one door and the [[Duke of Buckingham]] at another |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | How now, how now! What say the citizens? |
| [[Buckingham|BUCKINGHAM]] | Now, by the holy mother of our Lord,<br>The citizens are mum, say not a word. |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Touched you the bastardy of Edward’s children? |
| [[Buckingham|BUCKINGHAM]] | I did, with his contract with Lady Lucy,<br>And his contract by deputy in France,<br>Th‘insatiate greediness of his [[Desire|desire]],<br>And his enforcement of the [[City|city]] wives,<br>His tyranny for trifles, his own bastardy—<br>As being got your father then in France,<br>And his resemblance, being not like the Duke.<br>Withal, I did infer your lineaments—<br>Being the right idea of your father<br>Both in your face and nobleness of mind;<br>Laid open all your victories in Scotland,<br>Your discipline in war, wisdom in peace,<br>Your bounty, virtue, fair [[Humility|humility]]—<br>Indeed, left nothing fitting for your purpose<br>Untouched or slightly handled in discourse.<br>And when mine oratory grew toward end,<br>I bid them that did [[Love|love]] their country’s good<br>Cry ‘God save [[Richard]], England’s royal [[King|king]]!’ |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | And did they SO? |
| [[Buckingham|BUCKINGHAM]] | No, so God help me. They spake not a word,<br>But, like dumb statuas or breathing stones,<br>Stared each on other and looked deadly pale—<br>Which, when I saw, I reprehended them,<br>And asked the Mayor, what meant this wilful [[Silence|silence]]?<br>His answer was, the people were not used<br>To be spoke to but by the Recorder.<br>Then he was urged to tell my tale again:<br>‘Thus saith the Duke... thus hath the Duke inferred’—<br>But nothing spoke in warrant from himself.<br>When he had done, some followers of mine own,<br>At lower end of the Hall, hurled up their caps,<br>And some ten voices cried ‘God save [[King]] [[Richard]]!’<br>And thus I took the vantage of those few:<br>‘Thanks, gentle citizens and friends’, quoth I;<br>‘This general applause and cheerful shout<br>Argues your wisdoms and your [[Love|love]] to [[Richard]]’—<br>And even here brake off and came away. |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | What tongueless blocks were they! Would they not speak? |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Buckingham|BUCKINGHAM]] No, by my troth, my lord. |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Will not the Mayor then, and his brethren, come? |
| [[Buckingham|BUCKINGHAM]] | The Mayor is here at hand. Intend some [[Fear|fear]];<br>Be not you spoke with, but by mighty suit;<br>And look you get a [[prayer]] book in your hand,<br>And stand between two churchmen, good my lord,<br>For on that ground I’ll build a holy descant.<br>And be not easily won to our request.<br>Play the maid’s part: still answer ‘nay’—and take it. |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | I go. An if you plead as [[Well|well]] for them<br>As I can say nay to thee for myself,<br>No [[Doubt|doubt]] we’ll bring it to a happy issue.<br>One knocks within |
| [[Buckingham|BUCKINGHAM]] | Go, go, up to the leads! The [[Lord Mayor]] knocks.— |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit [[Richard]] |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter the [[Lord Mayor]], aldermen, and citizens |
| [[Buckingham|BUCKINGHAM]] | Welcome, my lord. I [[Dance|dance]] attendance here.<br>I think the Duke will not be spoke withal. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Catesby |
| [[Buckingham|BUCKINGHAM]] | Now Catesby, what says your lord to my request? |
| CATESBY | He doth entreat your grace, my noble lord,<br>To visit him tomorrow, or next day.<br>He is within with two right reverend fathers,<br>Divinely bent to meditation,<br>And in no worldly suits would he be moved,<br>To draw him from his holy exercise. |
| [[Buckingham|BUCKINGHAM]] | Return, good Catesby, to the gracious Duke.<br>Tell him myself, the Mayor, and aldermen,<br>In deep designs, in matter of great moment,<br>No less importing than our general good,<br>Are come to have some conference with his grace. |
| CATESBY | I’ll signify so much unto him straight. Exit |
| [[Buckingham|BUCKINGHAM]] | Ah ha! My lord, this prince is not an Edward.<br>He is not lolling on a lewd day-bed,<br>But on his knees at meditation;<br>Not dallying with a brace of courtesans,<br>But meditating with two deep divines;<br>Not sleeping to engross his idle [[Body|body]],<br>But praying to enrich his watchful [[Soul|soul]].<br>Happy were England would this virtuous prince<br>Take on his grace the sovereignty thereof.<br>But, sure I [[Fear|fear]], we shall not win him to it. |
| MAYOR | Marry, God defend his grace should say us nay. |
| [[Buckingham|BUCKINGHAM]] | I [[Fear|fear]] he will. Here Catesby comes again. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Catesby |
| [[Buckingham|BUCKINGHAM]] | Now Catesby, what says his grace? |
| CATESBY | He wonders to what end you have assembled<br>Such troops of citizens to come to him,<br>His grace not being warned thereof before.<br>He fears, my lord, you mean no good to him. |
| [[Buckingham|BUCKINGHAM]] | Sorry I am my noble cousin should<br>Suspect me that I mean no good to him.<br>By heaven, we come to him in perfect [[Love|love]],<br>And so once more return and tell his grace. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit Catesby |
| [[Buckingham|BUCKINGHAM]] | When holy and devout religious men<br>Are at their beads, ‘tis much to draw them thence.<br>So sweet is zealous contemplation. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Richard]] aloft, between two bishops. Enter |
| *(didascalia)* | Catesby below |
| MAYOR | See where his grace stands ’tween two clergymen. |
| [[Buckingham|BUCKINGHAM]] | Two props of virtue for a Christian prince,<br>To stay him from the fall of [[Vanity|vanity]];<br>And see, a book of [[prayer]] in his hand—<br>True ornaments to know a holy man.—<br>Famous Plantagenet, most gracious prince,<br>Lend favourable ear to our request,<br>And pardon us the interruption<br>Of thy devotion and right Christian zeal. |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | My lord, there needs no such apology.<br>I do beseech your grace to pardon me,<br>Who, earnest in the service of my God,<br>Deferred the visitation of my friends.<br>But leaving this, what is your grace’s pleasure? |
| [[Buckingham|BUCKINGHAM]] | Even that, I [[Hope|hope]], which pleaseth God above,<br>And all good men of this ungoverned isle. |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | I do suspect I have done some offence<br>That seems disgracious in the [[City|city]]’s eye,<br>And that you come to reprehend my ignorance. |
| [[Buckingham|BUCKINGHAM]] | You have, my lord. Would it might please your grace<br>On our entreaties to amend your fault. |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Else wherefore breathe I in a Christian land? |
| [[Buckingham|BUCKINGHAM]] | Know then, it is your fault that you resign<br>The supreme seat, the throne majestical,<br>The sceptred office of your ancestors,<br>Your state of fortune and your due of birth,<br>The lineal glory of your royal [[house]],<br>To the corruption of a blemished stock,<br>Whiles in the mildness of your sleepy thoughts—<br>Which here we waken to our country’s good—<br>The noble isle doth want her proper limbs:<br>Her face defaced with scars of infamy,<br>Her royal stock graft with ignoble plants<br>And almost shouldered in the swallowing gulf<br>Of dark forgetfulness and deep oblivion,<br>Which to recure we heartily solicit<br>Your gracious [[Self|self]] to take on you the charge<br>And kingly [[government]] of this your land—<br>Not as Protector, steward, substitute,<br>Or lowly factor for another’s gain,<br>But as successively, from [[Blood|blood]] to [[Blood|blood]],<br>Your right of birth, your empery, your own.<br>For this, consorted with the citizens,<br>Your very worshipful and loving friends,<br>And by their vehement instigation,<br>In this just cause come I to move your grace. |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | I cannot tell if to depart in [[Silence|silence]]<br>Or bitterly to speak in your reproof<br>Best fitteth my degree or your condition.<br>Your [[Love|love]] deserves my thanks; but my [[Desert|desert]],<br>Unmeritable, shuns your high request.<br>First, if all obstacles were cut away<br>And that my path were even to the [[Crown|crown]],<br>As the ripe revenue and due of birth,<br>Yet so much is my [[Poverty|poverty]] of spirit,<br>So mighty and so many my defects,<br>That I would rather hide me from my [[Greatness|greatness]]—<br>Being a barque to brook no mighty sea—<br>Than in my [[Greatness|greatness]] covet to be hid,<br>And in the vapour of my glory smothered.<br>But God be thanked, there is no need of me,<br>And much I need to help you, were there need.<br>The royal tree hath left us royal fruit,<br>Which, mellowed by the stealing hours of [[Time|time]],<br>Will [[Well|well]] become the seat of majesty<br>And make, no [[Doubt|doubt]], us happy by his reign.<br>On him I lay that you would lay on me,<br>The right and fortune of his happy [[Stars|stars]],<br>Which God defend that I should wring from him. |
| [[Buckingham|BUCKINGHAM]] | My lord, this argues [[Conscience|conscience]] in your grace,<br>But the respects thereof are nice and trivial,<br>All circumstances [[Well|well]] considered.<br>You say that Edward is your brother’s son;<br>So say we, too—but not by Edward’s wife.<br>For first was he contract to Lady Lucy—<br>Your mother lives a witness to his vow—<br>And afterward, by substitute, betrothed<br>To Bona, sister to the [[King of France]].<br>These both put off, a poor petitioner,<br>A care-crazed mother to a many sons,<br>A [[Beauty|beauty]]-waning and distressed widow<br>Even in the afternoon of her best days,<br>Made prize and purchase of his wanton eye,<br>Seduced the pitch and height of his degree<br>To base declension and loathed bigamy.<br>By her in his unlawful bed he got<br>This Edward, whom our manners call the Prince.<br>More bitterly could I expostulate,<br>Save that for reverence to some alive<br>I give a sparing limit to my tongue.<br>Then, good my lord, take to your royal [[Self|self]]<br>This proffered benefit of dignity—<br>If not to bless us and the land withal,<br>Yet to draw forth your noble ancestry<br>From the corruption of abusing times,<br>Unto a lineal, true-derived course. |
| MAYOR | (to [[Richard]])<br>Do, good my lord; your citizens entreat you. |
| [[Buckingham|BUCKINGHAM]] | (to [[Richard]])<br>Refuse not, mighty lord, this proffered [[Love|love]]. |
| CATFSBY | (to [[Richard]])<br>O make them joyful: grant their lawful suit. |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Alas, why would you heap this care on me?<br>I am unfit for state and majesty.<br>I do beseech you, take it not amiss.<br>I cannot, nor I will not, yield to you. |
| [[Buckingham|BUCKINGHAM]] | If you refuse it-as, in [[Love|love]] and zeal,<br>Loath to depose the [[Child|child]], your brother’s son,<br>As [[Well|well]] we know your tenderness of heart<br>And gentle, kind, effeminate remorse,<br>Which we have noted in you to your kindred,<br>And equally indeed to all estates—<br>Yet know, whe‘er you accept our suit or no,<br>Your brother’s son shall never reign our [[King|king]],<br>But we will plant some other in the throne,<br>To the disgrace and downfall of your [[house]].<br>And in this resolution here we leave you.—<br>Come, citizens. ‘Swounds, I’ll entreat no more. |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | O do not swear, my lord of [[Buckingham]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt [[Buckingham]] and some others |
| CATESBY | Call him again, sweet prince. Accept their suit. |
| *(didascalia)* | ANOTHER |
| CATESBY | If you deny them, all the land will rue it. |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Will you enforce me to a world of cares?<br>Call them again. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit one or more |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | I am not made of stone,<br>But penetrable to your kind entreats,<br>Albeit against my [[Conscience|conscience]] and my [[Soul|soul]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Buckingham]] and the rest |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Cousin of [[Buckingham]], and sage, [[Grave|grave]] men,<br>Since you will buckle fortune on my back,<br>To bear her burden, whe’er I will or no,<br>I must have patience to endure the load.<br>But if black scandal or foul-faced reproach<br>Attend the sequel of your imposition,<br>Your mere enforcement shall acquittance me<br>From all the impure blots and stains thereof;<br>For God doth know, and you may partly see,<br>How far I am from the [[Desire|desire]] of this. |
| MAYOR | God bless your grace! We see it, and will say it. |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | In saying so, you shall but say the [[Truth|truth]]. |
| [[Buckingham|BUCKINGHAM]] | Then I salute you with this royal title:<br>Long live kind [[Richard]], England’s worthy [[King|king]]! |
| *(didascalia)* | ALL BUT [[Richard|RICHARD]] Amen. |
| [[Buckingham|BUCKINGHAM]] | Tomorrow may it please you to be crowned? |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Even when you please, for you will have it so. |
| [[Buckingham|BUCKINGHAM]] | Tomorrow then, we will attend your grace.<br>And so, most joyfully, we take our leave. |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | (to the bishops)<br>Come, let us to our holy work again.—<br>Farewell, my cousin. Farewell, gentle friends. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt [[Richard]] and bishops above, the rest below |
