| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[King]] [[Richard]], Sir [[Richard]] Ratcliffe, Sir William Catesby, and others |
| [[King|KING]] [[Richard|RICHARD]] | What said Northumberland, as touching Richmond? |
| RATCLIFFE | That he was never trained up in arms. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Richard|RICHARD]] | He said the [[Truth|truth]]. And what said Surrey then? |
| RATCLIFFE | He smiled and said, ‘The better for our purpose.’ |
| [[King|KING]] [[Richard|RICHARD]] | He was in the right, and so indeed it is.<br>[[Clock]] strikes<br>Tell the [[Clock|clock]] there. Give me a calendar.<br>Who saw the sun today? |
| *(didascalia)* | A book is brought |
| RATCLIFFE | Not I, my lord. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Richard|RICHARD]] | Then he disdains to shine, for by the book<br>He should have braved the east an hour ago.<br>A black day will it be to somebody.<br>Ratcliffe. |
| RATCLIFFE | My lord? |
| [[King|KING]] [[Richard|RICHARD]] | The sun will not be seen today.<br>The sky doth frown and lour upon our army.<br>I would these dewy [[Tears|tears]] were from the ground.<br>Not shine today—why, what is that to me<br>More than to Richmond? For the selfsame heaven<br>That frowns on me looks sadly upon him. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter the Duke of Norfolk |
| NORFOLK | Arm, arm, my lord! The foe vaunts in the field. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Richard|RICHARD]] | Come, bustle, bustle! Caparison my [[Horse|horse]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Richard]] arms |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit one |
| [[King|KING]] [[Richard|RICHARD]] | Call up Lord Stanley, bid him bring his power.<br>I will lead forth my soldiers to the plain,<br>And thus my battle shall be orderèd.<br>My forward shall be drawn out all in length,<br>Consisting equally of [[Horse|horse]] add foot,<br>Our archers placèd strongly in the midst.<br>John Duke of Norfolk, Thomas [[Earl of Surrey]],<br>Shall have the leading of this multitude.<br>They thus directed, we ourself will follow<br>In the main battle, whose puissance on both sides<br>Shall be [[Well|well]] wingèd with our chiefest [[Horse|horse]].<br>This, and Saint George to boot! What think’st thou, Norfolk? |
| NORFOLK | A good direction, warlike sovereign.<br>He showeth him a paper<br>This paper found I on my tent this morning.<br>(He reads)<br>‘Jackie of Norfolk be not too bold,<br>For Dickon thy master is bought and sold.’ |
| [[King|KING]] [[Richard|RICHARD]] | A thing devisèd by the enemy.—<br>Go, gentlemen, each man unto his charge.<br>Let not our babbling [[Dreams|dreams]] affright our souls.<br>[[Conscience]] is but a word that cowards use,<br>Devised at first to keep the strong in awe.<br>Our strong arms be our [[Conscience|conscience]]; swords, our law.<br>March on, join bravely! Let us to’t, pell mell—<br>If not to heaven, then hand in hand to hell.<br>His oration to his army<br>What shall I say, more than I have inferred?<br>Remember whom you are to cope withal:<br>A sort of vagabonds, rascals and runaways,<br>A scum of Bretons and base lackey peasants,<br>Whom their o‘ercloyèd country vomits forth<br>To desperate ventures and assured destruction.<br>You sleeping safe, they bring to you unrest;<br>You having lands and blessed with beauteous wives,<br>They would distrain the one, distain the other.<br>And who doth lead them, but a paltry fellow?<br>Long kept in Bretagne at our mother’s cost;<br>A milksop; one that never in his life<br>Felt so much cold as over shoes in [[Snow|snow]].<br>Let’s whip these stragglers o’er the seas again,<br>Lash hence these overweening rags of France,<br>These famished beggars, weary of their lives,<br>Who—but for dreaming on this fond exploit—<br>For want of means, poor rats, had hanged themselves.<br>If we be conquered, let men conquer us,<br>And not these bastard Bretons, whom our fathers<br>Have in their own land beaten, bobbed, and thumped,<br>And in record left them the heirs of shame.<br>Shall these enjoy our lands? Lie with our wives?<br>Ravish our daughters? |
| *(didascalia)* | Drum afar off |
| [[King|KING]] [[Richard|RICHARD]] | Hark, I hear their drum.<br>Fight, gentlemen of England! Fight, bold yeomen!<br>Draw, archers, draw your arrows to the head!<br>Spur your proud horses hard, and ride in [[Blood|blood]]!<br>Amaze the welkin with your broken staves! |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter a Messenger |
| [[King|KING]] [[Richard|RICHARD]] | What says Lord Stanley? Will he bring his power? |
| MESSENGER | My lord, he doth deny to come. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Richard|RICHARD]] | Off with young George’s head! |
| NORFOLK | My lord, the enemy is past the marsh.<br>After the battle let George Stanley die. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Richard|RICHARD]] | A thousand hearts are great within my bosom.<br>Advance our standards! Set upon our foes!<br>Our ancient word of [[Courage|courage]], fair Saint George,<br>Inspire us with the spleen of fiery dragons.<br>Upon them! Victory sits on our helms! |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt |
