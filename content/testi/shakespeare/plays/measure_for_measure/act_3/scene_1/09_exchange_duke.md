| DUKE | Still thus and thus; still worse! |
| [[Lucio|LUCIO]] | How doth my dear morsel thy mistress? Procures she still, ha? |
| [[Pompey|POMPEY]] | Troth, sir, she hath eaten up all her beef, and she is herself in the tub. |
| [[Lucio|LUCIO]] | Why, ’tis good, it is the right of it, it must be so. Ever your fresh whore and your powdered bawd; an unshunned consequence, it must be so. Art going to [[Prison|prison]], [[Pompey]]? |
| [[Pompey|POMPEY]] | Yes, [[Faith|faith]], sir. |
| [[Lucio|LUCIO]] | Why ’tis not amiss, [[Pompey]]. Farewell. Go; say I sent thee thither. For debt, [[Pompey]], or how? |
| ELBOW | For being a bawd, for being a bawd. |
| [[Lucio|LUCIO]] | [[Well]] then, imprison him. If [[Imprisonment|imprisonment]] be the due of a bawd, why, ’tis his right. Bawd is he doubtless, and of antiquity too—bawd born. Farewell, good [[Pompey]]. Commend me to the [[Prison|prison]], [[Pompey]]. You will turn good husband now, [[Pompey]]; you will keep the [[house]]. |
| [[Pompey|POMPEY]] | I [[Hope|hope]], sir, your good worship will be my bail? |
| [[Lucio|LUCIO]] | No, indeed, will I not, [[Pompey]]; it is not the wear. I will pray, [[Pompey]], to increase your bondage. If you take it not patiently, why, your mettle is the more. Adieu, trusty [[Pompey]].—Bless you, friar. |
| DUKE | And you. |
| [[Lucio|LUCIO]] | Does Bridget paint still, [[Pompey]], ha? |
| ELBOW | (to [[Pompey]]) Come your ways, sir, come. |
| [[Pompey|POMPEY]] | (to [[Lucio]]) You will not bail me then, sir? |
| [[Lucio|LUCIO]] | Then, [[Pompey]], nor now.—What news abroad, friar, what news? |
| ELBOW | (to [[Pompey]]) Come your ways, sir, come. |
| [[Lucio|LUCIO]] | Go to kennel, [[Pompey]], go. Exeunt Elbow, [[Pompey]], and officers What news, friar, of the Duke? |
| DUKE | I know none. Can you tell me of any? |
| [[Lucio|LUCIO]] | Some say he is with the Emperor of [[Russia]]; other some, he is in Rome. But where is he, think you? |
| DUKE | I know not where; but wheresoever, I wish him [[Well|well]]. |
| [[Lucio|LUCIO]] | It was a mad, fantastical trick of him to steal from the state, and usurp the beggary he was never born to. Lord [[Angelo]] dukes it [[Well|well]] in his absence; he puts transgression to’t. 361 |
| DUKE | He does [[Well|well]] in’t. |
| [[Lucio|LUCIO]] | A little more lenity to lechery would do no harm in him. Something too crabbed that way, friar. |
| DUKE | It is too general a vice, and severity must cure it. |
| [[Lucio|LUCIO]] | Yes, in good sooth, the vice is of a great kindred, it is [[Well|well]] allied. But it is impossible to extirp it quite, friar, till eating and drinking be put down. They say this [[Angelo]] was not made by man and woman, after this downright way of creation. Is it true, think you? |
| DUKE | How should he be made, then? |
| [[Lucio|LUCIO]] | Some report a sea-maid spawned him, some that he was begot between two stockfishes. But it is certain that when he makes [[Water|water]] his urine is congealed ice; that I know to be true. And he is a motion ungenerative; that’s infallible. |
| DUKE | You are pleasant, sir, and speak apace. |
| [[Lucio|LUCIO]] | Why, what a ruthless thing is this in him, for the rebellion of a codpiece to take away the life of a man! Would the Duke that is absent have done this? Ere he would have hanged a man for the getting a hundred bastards, he would have paid for the nursing a thousand. He had some feeling of the sport, he knew the service, and that instructed him to [[Mercy|mercy]]. |
