| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | As much for my poor brother as myself. That is, were I under the terms of [[Death|death]], Th’impression of keen whips I’d wear as rubies, And strip myself to [[Death|death]] as to a bed That [[Longing|longing]] have been sick for, ere I’d yield My [[Body|body]] up to shame. |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | Then must your brother die. |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | And ’twere the cheaper way. Better it were a brother died at once Than that a sister, by redeeming him, Should die for ever. |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | Were not you then as cruel as the sentence That you have slandered so? |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | Ignominy in ransom and free pardon Are of two houses; lawful [[Mercy|mercy]] Is nothing kin to foul redemption. |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | You seemed of late to make the law a [[Tyrant|tyrant]], And rather proved the sliding of your brother A merriment than a vice. |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | O pardon me, my lord. It oft falls out To have what we would have, we speak not what we mean. I something do excuse the thing I hate For his advantage that I dearly [[Love|love]]. |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | We are all frail. |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | Else let my brother die— If not a federy, but only he, Owe and succeed thy weakness. |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | Nay, women are frail too. |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | Ay, as the glasses where they view themselves, Which are as easy broke as they make forms. Women? Help, heaven! Men their creation mar In profiting by them. Nay, call us ten times frail, For we are soft as our complexions are, And credulous to false prints. |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | I think it [[Well|well]], And from this testimony of your own sex, Since I suppose we are made to be no stronger Than faults may shake our frames, let me be bold. I do arrest your words. Be that you are; That is, a woman. If you be more, you’re none. If you be one, as you are [[Well|well]] expressed By all external warrants, show it now, By putting on the destined livery. |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | I have no tongue but one. Gentle my lord, Let me entreat you speak the former language. |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | Plainly conceive, I [[Love|love]] you. |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | My brother did [[Love|love]] [[Juliet]], And you tell me that he shall die for it. |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | He shall not, Isabel, if you give me [[Love|love]]. |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | I know your virtue hath a licence in’t, Which seems a little fouler than it is, To pluck on others. |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | Believe me, on mine [[Honour|honour]], My words express my purpose. |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | Ha, little [[Honour|honour]] to be much believed, And most pernicious purpose! Seeming, seeming! I will proclaim thee, [[Angelo]]; look for’t. Sign me a present pardon for my brother, Or with an outstretched throat I’ll tell the world aloud What man thou art. |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | Who will believe thee, Isabel? My unsoiled name, th‘austereness of my life, My vouch against you, and my place i’th’ state, Will so your accusation overweigh That you shall stifle in your own report, And smell of calumny. I have begun, And now I give my sensual race the rein. Fit thy consent to my sharp appetite. Lay by all nicety and prolixious blushes That banish what they sue for. Redeem thy brother By yielding up thy [[Body|body]] to my will, Or else he must not only die the [[Death|death]], But thy unkindness shall his [[Death|death]] draw out To ling‘ring sufferance. Answer me tomorrow, Or by the affection that now guides me most, I’ll prove a [[Tyrant|tyrant]] to him. As for you, Say what you can, my false o’erweighs your true. Exit |
