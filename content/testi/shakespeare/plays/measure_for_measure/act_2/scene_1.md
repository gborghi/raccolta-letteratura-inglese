| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Angelo]], [[Escalus]], and servants; a Justice |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | We must not make a scarecrow of the law,<br>Setting it up to [[Fear|fear]] the [[Birds|birds]] of prey,<br>And let it keep one shape till custom make it<br>Their perch, and not their terror. |
| [[Escalus|ESCALUS]] | Ay, but yet<br>Let us be keen, and rather cut a little<br>Than fall and bruise to [[Death|death]]. Alas, this gentleman<br>Whom I would save had a most noble father.<br>Let but your [[Honour|honour]] know—<br>Whom I believe to be most strait in virtue—<br>That in the working of your own affections,<br>Had [[Time|time]] cohered with place, or place with wishing,<br>Or that the resolute acting of your [[Blood|blood]]<br>Could have attained th’effect of your own purpose—<br>Whether you had not sometime in your life<br>Erred in this point which now you censure him,<br>And pulled the law upon you. |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | ’Tis one thing to be tempted, [[Escalus]],<br>Another thing to fall. I not deny<br>The jury passing on the prisoner’s life<br>May in the sworn twelve have a thief or two<br>Guiltier than him they try. What knows the law<br>That thieves do pass on thieves? What’s open made to<br>justice,<br>That justice seizes. ’Tis very pregnant:<br>The [[Jewel|jewel]] that we find, we stoop and take’t<br>Because we see it, but what we do not see<br>We tread upon and never think of it.<br>You may not so extenuate his offence<br>For I have had such faults; but rather tell me,<br>When I that censure him do so offend,<br>Let mine own judgement pattern out my [[Death|death]],<br>And nothing come in partial. Sir, he must die. |
| [[Escalus|ESCALUS]] | Be it as your wisdom will. |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | Where is the Provost? |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Provost |
| PROVOST | Here, if it like your [[Honour|honour]]. |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | See that [[Claudio]]<br>Be execute by nine tomorrow morning.<br>Bring him his confessor, let him be prepared,<br>For that’s the utmost of his pilgrimage. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit Provost |
| [[Escalus|ESCALUS]] | [[Well]], heaven forgive him, and forgive us all!<br>Some rise by sin, and some by virtue fall.<br>Some run from brakes of vice, and answer none;<br>And some condemned for a fault [[Alone|alone]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Elbow, Froth, [[Pompey]], and officers |
| ELBOW | Come, bring them away. If these be good people in a commonweal, that do nothing but use their abuses in common houses, I know no law. Bring them away. |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | How now, sir? What’s your name? And what’s the<br>matter? |
| ELBOW | If it please your [[Honour|honour]], I am the poor Duke’s constable, and my name is Elbow. I do lean upon justice, sir; and do bring in here before your good [[Honour|honour]] two notorious benefactors. |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | Benefactors? Welll What benefactors are they? Are they not malefactors? |
| ELBOW | If it please your [[Honour|honour]], I know not [[Well|well]] what they are; but precise villains they are, that I am sure of, and void of all profanation in the world that good Christians ought to have. |
| [[Escalus|ESCALUS]] | (to [[Angelo]]) This comes off [[Well|well]]; here’s a wise officer! |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | Go to, what quality are they of? Elbow is your name? Why dost thou not speak, Elbow? |
| [[Pompey|POMPEY]] | He cannot, sir; he’s out at elbow. |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | What are you, sir? |
| ELBOW | He, sir? A tapster, sir, parcel bawd; one that serves a bad woman whose [[house]], sir, was, as they say, plucked down in the suburbs; and now she professes a hot-[[house]], which I think is a very ill [[house]] too. |
| [[Escalus|ESCALUS]] | How know you that? |
| ELBOW | My wife, sir, whom I detest before heaven and your [[Honour|honour]]— |
| [[Escalus|ESCALUS]] | How, thy wife? |
| ELBOW | Ay, sir, whom I thank heaven is an honest woman— |
| [[Escalus|ESCALUS]] | Dost thou detest her therefor? |
| ELBOW | I say, sir, I will detest myself also, as [[Well|well]] as she, that this [[house]], if it be not a bawd’s [[house]], it is pity of her life, for it is a naughty [[house]]. |
| [[Escalus|ESCALUS]] | How dost thou know that, constable? |
| ELBOW | Marry, sir, by my wife, who, if she had been a woman cardinally given, might have been accused in fornication, adultery, and all uncleanliness there. |
| [[Escalus|ESCALUS]] | By the woman’s means? |
| ELBOW | Ay, sir, by Mistress Overdone’s means. But as she spit in his face, so she defied him. |
| [[Pompey|POMPEY]] | (to [[Escalus]]) Sir, if it please your [[Honour|honour]], this is not so. |
| ELBOW | Prove it before these varlets here, thou honourable man, prove it. |
| [[Escalus|ESCALUS]] | (to [[Angelo]]) Do you hear how he misplaces? |
| [[Pompey|POMPEY]] | Sir, she came in great with [[Child|child]], and [[Longing|longing]]-saving your [[Honour|honour]]’s reverence—for stewed prunes. Sir, we had but two in the [[house]], which at that very distant [[Time|time]] stood, as it were, in a fruit dish—a dish of some threepence; your honours have seen such dishes; they are not china dishes, but very good dishes. |
| [[Escalus|ESCALUS]] | Go to, go to, no matter for the dish, sir. |
| [[Pompey|POMPEY]] | No, indeed, sir, not of a pin; you are therein in the right. But to the point. As I say, this Mistress Elbow, being, as I say, with [[Child|child]], and being great-bellied, and [[Longing|longing]], as I said, for prunes; and having but two in the dish, as I said, Master Froth here, this very man, having eaten the rest, as I said, and, as I say, paying for them very honestly; for, as you know, Master Froth, I could not give you threepence again. |
| FROTH | No, indeed. |
| [[Pompey|POMPEY]] | Very [[Well|well]]. You being, then, if you be remembered, cracking the stones of the foresaid prunes— |
| FROTH | Ay, so I did indeed. |
| [[Pompey|POMPEY]] | Why, very [[Well|well]].—I telling you then, if you be remembered, that such a one and such a one were past cure of the thing you wot of, unless they kept very good diet, as I told you— |
| FROTH | All this is true. no |
| [[Pompey|POMPEY]] | Why, very [[Well|well]] then— |
| [[Escalus|ESCALUS]] | Come, you are a tedious [[Fool|fool]]. To the purpose. What was done to Elbow’s wife that he hath cause to complain of? Come me to what was done to her. |
| [[Pompey|POMPEY]] | Sir, your [[Honour|honour]] cannot come to that yet. |
| [[Escalus|ESCALUS]] | No, sir, nor I mean it not. |
| [[Pompey|POMPEY]] | Sir, but you shall come to it, by your [[Honour|honour]]’s leave. And I beseech you, look into Master Froth here, sir, a man of fourscore pound a year, whose father died at Hallowmas—was’t not at Hallowmas, Master Froth? |
| FROTH | All Hallow Eve. |
| [[Pompey|POMPEY]] | Why, very [[Well|well]]. I [[Hope|hope]] here be truths. He, sir, sitting, as I say, in a lower chair, sir—’twas in the Bunch of Grapes, where indeed you have a delight to sit, have you not? |
| FROTH | I have so, because it is an open room, and good for [[Winter|winter]]. |
| [[Pompey|POMPEY]] | Why, very [[Well|well]] then. I [[Hope|hope]] here be truths. |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | This will last out a [[Night|night]] in [[Russia]],<br>When nights are longest there. (To [[Escalus]]) I’ll take<br>my leave,<br>And leave you to the hearing of the cause,<br>Hoping you’ll find good cause to whip them all. |
| [[Escalus|ESCALUS]] | I think no less. Good morrow to your lordship. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit [[Angelo]] |
| [[Escalus|ESCALUS]] | Now, sir, come on, what was done to Elbow’s wife,<br>once more? |
| [[Pompey|POMPEY]] | Once, sir? There was nothing done to her once. |
| ELBOW | I beseech you, sir, ask him what this man did to my wife. |
| [[Pompey|POMPEY]] | I beseech your [[Honour|honour]], ask me. |
| [[Escalus|ESCALUS]] | [[Well]], sir, what did this gentleman to her? |
| [[Pompey|POMPEY]] | I beseech you, sir, look in this gentleman’s face. Good Master Froth, look upon his [[Honour|honour]]. ’Tis for a good purpose. Doth your [[Honour|honour]] mark his face? |
| [[Escalus|ESCALUS]] | Ay, sir, very [[Well|well]]. |
| [[Pompey|POMPEY]] | Nay, I beseech you, mark it [[Well|well]]. |
| [[Escalus|ESCALUS]] | [[Well]], I do so. |
| [[Pompey|POMPEY]] | Doth your [[Honour|honour]] see any harm in his face? |
| [[Escalus|ESCALUS]] | Why, no. |
| [[Pompey|POMPEY]] | I’ll be supposed upon a book his face is the worst thing about him. Good, then—if his face be the worst thing about him, how could Master Froth do the constable’s wife any harm? I would know that of your [[Honour|honour]]. |
| [[Escalus|ESCALUS]] | He’s in the right, constable; what say you to it? |
| ELBOW | First, an it like you, the [[house]] is a respected [[house]]; next, this is a respected fellow; and his mistress is a respected woman. |
| [[Pompey|POMPEY]] | (to [[Escalus]]) By this hand, sir, his wife is a more respected person than any of us all. |
| ELBOW | Varlet, thou liest; thou liest, wicked varlet. The [[Time|time]] is yet to come that she was ever respected with man, woman, or [[Child|child]]. |
| [[Pompey|POMPEY]] | Sir, she was respected with him before he married with her. |
| [[Escalus|ESCALUS]] | Which is the wiser here, justice or iniquity? (To Elbow) Is this true? |
| ELBOW | (to [[Pompey]]) O thou caitiff, O thou varlet, O thou wicked Hanniball I respected with her before I was married to her? (To [[Escalus]]) If ever I was respected with her, or she with me, let not your worship think me the poor Duke’s officer. (To [[Pompey]]) Prove this, thou wicked Hannibal, or I’ll have mine action of battery on thee. |
| [[Escalus|ESCALUS]] | If he took you a box o’th’ ear you might have your action of slander too. |
| ELBOW | Marry, I thank your good worship for it. What is’t your worship’s pleasure I shall do with this wicked caitiff? |
| [[Escalus|ESCALUS]] | Truly, officer, because he hath some offences in him that thou wouldst discover if thou couldst, let him continue in his courses till thou knowest what they are. |
| ELBOW | Marry, I thank your worship for it.—Thou seest, thou wicked varlet now, what’s come upon thee. Thou art to continue now, thou varlet, thou art to continue. |
| [[Escalus|ESCALUS]] | (to Froth) Where were you born, friend? |
| FROTH | Here in Vienna, sir. |
| [[Escalus|ESCALUS]] | Are you of fourscore pounds a year? |
| FROTH | Yes, an’t please you, sir. |
| [[Escalus|ESCALUS]] | So. (To [[Pompey]]) What trade are you of, sir? |
| [[Pompey|POMPEY]] | A tapster, a poor widow’s tapster. |
| [[Escalus|ESCALUS]] | Your mistress’s name? |
| [[Pompey|POMPEY]] | Mistress Overdone. |
| [[Escalus|ESCALUS]] | Hath she had any more than one husband? |
| [[Pompey|POMPEY]] | Nine, sir—Overdone by the last. |
| [[Escalus|ESCALUS]] | Nine?—Come hither to me, Master Froth. Master Froth, I would not have you acquainted with tapsters. They will draw you, Master Froth, and you will hang them. Get you gone, and let me hear no more of you. |
| FROTH | I thank your worship. For mine own part, I never come into any room in a tap-[[house]] but I am drawn in. |
| [[Escalus|ESCALUS]] | [[Well]], no more of it, Master Froth. Farewell. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit Froth |
| [[Escalus|ESCALUS]] | Come you hither to me, Master Tapster. What’s your<br>name, Master Tapster? |
| [[Pompey|POMPEY]] | [[Pompey]]. |
| [[Escalus|ESCALUS]] | What else? |
| [[Pompey|POMPEY]] | Bum, sir. |
| [[Escalus|ESCALUS]] | Troth, and your bum is the greatest thing about you; so that, in the beastliest sense, you are [[Pompey]] the Great. [[Pompey]], you are partly a bawd, [[Pompey]], howsoever you colour it in being a tapster, are you not? Come, tell me true; it shall be the better for you. |
| [[Pompey|POMPEY]] | Truly, sir, I am a poor fellow that would live. |
| [[Escalus|ESCALUS]] | How would you live, [[Pompey]]? By being a bawd? What do you think of the trade, [[Pompey]]? Is it a lawful trade? |
| [[Pompey|POMPEY]] | If the law would allow it, sir. |
| [[Escalus|ESCALUS]] | But the law will not allow it, [[Pompey]]; nor it shall not be allowed in Vienna. |
| [[Pompey|POMPEY]] | Does your worship mean to geld and spay all the youth of the [[City|city]]? |
| [[Escalus|ESCALUS]] | No, [[Pompey]]. |
| [[Pompey|POMPEY]] | Truly, sir, in my poor opinion they will to’t then. If your worship will take order for the drabs and the knaves, you need not to [[Fear|fear]] the bawds. |
| [[Escalus|ESCALUS]] | There is pretty orders beginning, I can tell you. It is but heading and hanging. |
| [[Pompey|POMPEY]] | If you head and hang all that offend that way but for ten year together, you’ll be glad to give out a commission for more heads. If this law hold in Vienna ten year, I’ll rent the fairest [[house]] in it after threepence a bay. If you live to see this come to pass, say [[Pompey]] told you so. |
| [[Escalus|ESCALUS]] | Thank you, good [[Pompey]]; and in requital of your prophecy, hark you. I advise you, let me not find you before me again upon any complaint whatsoever; no, not for dwelling where you do. If I do, [[Pompey]], I shall beat you to your tent, and prove a shrewd Caesar to you; in plain dealing, [[Pompey]], I shall have you whipped. So for this [[Time|time]], [[Pompey]], fare you [[Well|well]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Pompey|POMPEY]] I thank your worship for your good counsel; aside but I shall follow it as the flesh and fortune shall better determine. Whip me? No, no; let carman whip his jade. The valiant heart’s not whipped out of his trade. Exit |
| [[Escalus|ESCALUS]] | Come hither to me, Master Elbow; come hither, Master Constable. How long have you been in this place of constable? |
| ELBOW | Seven year and a half, sir. |
| [[Escalus|ESCALUS]] | I thought, by the readiness in the office, you had continued in it some [[Time|time]]. You say seven years together? |
| ELBOW | And a half, sir. |
| [[Escalus|ESCALUS]] | Alas, it hath been great pains to you. They do you wrong to put you so oft upon’t. Are there not men in your ward sufficient to serve it? |
| ELBOW | [[Faith]], sir, few of any wit in such matters. As they are chosen, they are glad to choose me for them. I do it for some piece of money, and go through with all. |
| [[Escalus|ESCALUS]] | Look you bring me in the names of some six or seven, the most sufficient of your parish. |
| ELBOW | To your worship’s [[house]], sir? |
| [[Escalus|ESCALUS]] | To my [[house]]. Fare you [[Well|well]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit Elbow with officers |
| [[Escalus|ESCALUS]] | What’s o’[[Clock|clock]], think you? |
| JUSTICE | Eleven, sir. |
| [[Escalus|ESCALUS]] | I pray you [[Home|home]] to dinner with me. |
| JUSTICE | I humbly thank you. |
| [[Escalus|ESCALUS]] | It grieves me for the [[Death|death]] of [[Claudio]],<br>But there’s no remedy. |
| JUSTICE | Lord [[Angelo]] is severe. |
| [[Escalus|ESCALUS]] | It is but needful.<br>[[Mercy]] is not itself that oft looks so.<br>Pardon is still the [[Nurse|nurse]] of second woe.<br>But yet, poor [[Claudio]]! There is no remedy.<br>Come, sir. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt |
