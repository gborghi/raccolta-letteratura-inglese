| [[Lucio|LUCIO]] | (aside to [[Isabella]]) O, to him, to him, wench! He will relent. He’s coming; I perceive’t. |
| PROVOST | (aside) Pray heaven she win him! |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | We cannot weigh our brother with ourself. Great men may jest with saints; ’tis wit in them, But in the less, foul profanation. |
| [[Lucio|LUCIO]] | (aside to [[Isabella]]) Thou’rt i’th’ right, girl. More o’ that. |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | That in the captain’s but a choleric word, Which in the soldier is flat blasphemy. |
| [[Lucio|LUCIO]] | (aside to [[Isabella]]) Art advised o’ that? More on’t. |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | Why do you put these sayings upon me? |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | Because authority, though it err like others, Hath yet a kind of medicine in itself That skins the vice o’th’ top. Go to your bosom; Knock there, and ask your heart what it doth know That’s like my brother’s fault. If it confess A natural guiltiness, such as is his, Let it not sound a thought upon your tongue Against my brother’s life. |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | (aside) She speaks, and ’tis such sense That my sense breeds with it. (To [[Isabella]]) Fare you [[Well|well]]. |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | Gentle my lord, turn back. |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | I will bethink me. Come again tomorrow. |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | Hark how I’ll bribe you; good my lord, turn back. |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | How, bribe me? |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | Ay, with such gifts that heaven shall share with you. |
| [[Lucio|LUCIO]] | (aside to [[Isabella]]) You had marred all else. |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | Not with fond shekels of the tested gold, Or stones, whose rate are either rich or poor As fancy values them; but with true prayers, That shall be up at heaven and enter there Ere sunrise, prayers from preserved souls, From fasting maids whose minds are dedicate To nothing temporal. |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | [[Well]], come to me tomorrow |
| [[Lucio|LUCIO]] | (aside to [[Isabella]]) Go to; ’tis [[Well|well]]; away. |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | Heaven keep your [[Honour|honour]] safe. |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | (aside) Amen; For I am that way going to [[Temptation|temptation]], Where [[prayer]] is crossed. |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | At what hour tomorrow Shall I attend your lordship? |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | At any [[Time|time]] fore [[Noon|noon]]. |
| [[Isabella|ISABELLA]] | God save your [[Honour|honour]]. |
