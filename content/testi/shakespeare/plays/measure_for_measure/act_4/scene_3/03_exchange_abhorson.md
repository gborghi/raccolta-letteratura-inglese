| ABHORSON | Sirrah, bring Barnardine hither. 19 |
| [[Pompey|POMPEY]] | Master Barnardine! You must rise and be hanged, Master Barnardine! |
| ABHORSON | What ho, Barnardine! |
| BARNARDINE | (within) A pox o’ your throats! Who makes that noise there? What are you? |
| [[Pompey|POMPEY]] | Your friends, sir; the hangman. You must be so good, sir, to rise and be put to [[Death|death]]. |
| BARNARDINE | Away, you rogue, away! I am sleepy. |
| ABHORSON | Tell him he must awake, and that quickly too. |
| [[Pompey|POMPEY]] | Pray, Master Barnardine, awake till you are executed, and [[Sleep|sleep]] afterwards. |
| ABHORSON | Go in to him and fetch him out. |
| [[Pompey|POMPEY]] | He is coming, sir, he is coming. I hear his straw rustle. |
| ABHORSON | Is the axe upon the block, sirrah? |
| [[Pompey|POMPEY]] | Very ready, sir. Enter Barnardine |
| BARNARDINE | How now, Abhorson, what’s the news with you? |
| ABHORSON | Truly, sir, I would [[Desire|desire]] you to clap into your prayers, for, look you, the warrant’s come. |
| BARNARDINE | You rogue, I have been drinking all [[Night|night]]. I am not fitted for’t. |
| [[Pompey|POMPEY]] | O, the better, sir; for he that drinks all [[Night|night]], and is hanged betimes in the morning, may [[Sleep|sleep]] the sounder all the next day. Enter the Duke, disguised as a friar |
| ABHORSON | (to Barnardine) Look you, sir, here comes your ghostly father. Do we jest now, think you? |
| DUKE | (to Barnardine) Sir, induced by my [[Charity|charity]], and hearing how hastily you are to depart, I am come to advise you, comfort you, and pray with you. |
| BARNARDINE | Friar, not I. I have been drinking hard all [[Night|night]], and I will have more [[Time|time]] to prepare me, or they shall beat out my brains with billets. I will not consent to die this day, that’s certain. |
| DUKE | O sir, you must; and therefore, I beseech you, Look forward on the [[Journey|journey]] you shall go. |
| BARNARDINE | I swear I will not die today, for any man’s [[Persuasion|persuasion]]. |
| DUKE | But hear you— |
| BARNARDINE | Not a word. If you have anything to say to me, come to my ward, for thence will not I today. Exit |
| DUKE | Unfit to live or die. O gravel heart! After him, fellows; bring him to the block. Exeunt Abhorson and [[Pompey]] Enter Provost |
| PROVOST | Now, sir, how do you find the prisoner? |
| DUKE | A creature unprepared, unmeet for [[Death|death]]; And to transport him in the mind he is Were damnable. |
| PROVOST | Here in the [[Prison|prison]], father, There died this morning of a cruel fever One Ragusine, a most notorious pirate, A man of [[Claudio]]’s years, his beard and head Just of his colour. What if we do omit This reprobate till he were [[Well|well]] inclined, And satisfy the deputy with the visage Of Ragusine, more like to [[Claudio]]? |
| DUKE | O, ’tis an accident that heaven provides. Dispatch it presently; the hour draws on Prefixed by [[Angelo]]. See this be done, And sent according to command, whiles I Persuade this rude wretch willingly to die. |
| PROVOST | This shall be done, good father, presently. |
| PROVOST | But Barnardine must die this afternoon; And how shall we continue [[Claudio]], To save me from the danger that might come If he were known alive? |
