| [[Lucio|LUCIO]] | [[Well]], my lord. |
| DUKE | This is no witness for Lord [[Angelo]]. |
| [[Mariana|MARIANA]] | Now I come to’t, my lord. She that accuses him of fornication In [[Self|self]]-same manner doth accuse my husband, And charges him, my lord, with such a [[Time|time]] When I’ll depose I had him in mine arms With all th’effect of [[Love|love]]. |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | Charges she more than me? |
| [[Mariana|MARIANA]] | Not that I know. |
| DUKE | No? You say your husband. |
| [[Mariana|MARIANA]] | Why just, my lord, and that is [[Angelo]], Who thinks he knows that he ne’er knew my [[Body|body]], But knows, he thinks, that he knows Isabel’s. |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | This is a strange abuse. Let’s see thy face. |
| [[Mariana|MARIANA]] | (unveiling) My husband bids me; now I will unmask. This is that face, thou cruel [[Angelo]], Which once thou swor’st was worth the looking on. This is the hand which, with a vowed contract, Was fast belocked in thine. This is the [[Body|body]] That took away the match from Isabel, And did supply thee at thy [[Garden|garden]]-[[house]] In her imagined person. |
| DUKE | (to [[Angelo]]) Know you this woman? |
| [[Lucio|LUCIO]] | Carnally, she says. |
| DUKE | Sirrah, no more! |
| [[Lucio|LUCIO]] | Enough, my lord. |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | My lord, I must confess I know this woman; And five years since there was some speech of [[Marriage|marriage]] Betwixt myself and her, which was broke off, Partly for that her promised proportions Came short of composition, but in chief For that her reputation was disvalued In levity; since which [[Time|time]] of five years I never spake with her, saw her, nor heard from her, Upon my [[Faith|faith]] and [[Honour|honour]]. |
| [[Mariana|MARIANA]] | kneeling before the Duke Noble prince, As there comes light from heaven, and words from breath, As there is sense in [[Truth|truth]], and [[Truth|truth]] in virtue, I am affianced this man’s wife, as strongly As words could make up vows. And, my good lord, But Tuesday [[Night|night]] last gone, in’s [[Garden|garden]]-[[house]], He knew me as a wife. As this is true, Let me in safety raise me from my knees, Or else forever be confixèd here, A marble monument. |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | I did but smile till now. Now, good my lord, give me the scope of justice. My patience here is touched. I do perceive These poor informal women are no more But instruments of some more mightier member That sets them on. Let me have way, my lord, To find this practice out. |
| DUKE | (standing) Ay, with my heart, And punish them even to your height of pleasure.— Thou foolish friar, and thou pernicious woman Compact with her that’s gone, think‘st thou thy oaths, Though they would swear down each particular saint, Were testimonies against his worth and credit That’s sealed in approbation? You, Lord [[Escalus]], Sit with my cousin; lend him your kind pains To find out this abuse, whence ’tis derived. There is another friar that set them on. Let him be sent for. [[Escalus]] sits |
| FRIAR PETER | Would he were here, my lord, for he indeed Hath set the women on to this complaint. Your Provost knows the place where he abides, And he may fetch him. |
| DUKE | (to one or more) Go, do it instantly. Exit one or more (To [[Angelo]]) And you, my noble and [[Well|well]]-warranted cousin, Whom it concerns to hear this matter forth, Do with your injuries as seems you best In any chastisement. I for a while will leave you, But stir not you till you have [[Well|well]] determined Upon these slanderers. |
