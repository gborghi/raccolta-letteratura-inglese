| *(didascalia)* | Enter the Duke, [[Escalus]], and other lords DUKE [[Escalus]]. |
| [[Escalus|ESCALUS]] | My lord. |
| DUKE | Of [[government]] the properties to unfold<br>Would seem in me t’affect speech and discourse,<br>Since I am put to know that your own science<br>Exceeds in that the lists of all advice<br>My strength can give you. Then no more remains<br>But this: to your sufficiency, as your worth is able,<br>And let them work. The [[Nature|nature]] of our people,<br>Our [[City|city]]’s institutions and the terms<br>For common justice, you’re as pregnant in<br>As art and practice hath enriched any<br>That we remember.<br>He gives [[Escalus]] papers<br>There is our commission,<br>From which we would not have you warp.<br>(To a lord) Call hither,<br>I say bid come before us, [[Angelo]]. Exit lord<br><br>(To [[Escalus]]) What figure of us think you he will<br>bear?—<br>For you must know we have with special [[Soul|soul]]<br>Elected him our absence to supply,<br>Lent him our terror, dressed him with our [[Love|love]],<br>And given his deputation all the organs<br>Of our own power. What think you of it? |
| [[Escalus|ESCALUS]] | If any in Vienna be of worth<br>To undergo such ample grace and [[Honour|honour]],<br>It is Lord [[Angelo]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Angelo]] |
| DUKE | Look where he comes. |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | Always obedient to your grace’s will,<br>I come to know your pleasure. |
| DUKE | [[Angelo]],<br>There is a kind of character in thy life<br>That to th‘observer doth thy [[history]]<br>Fully unfold. Thyself and thy belongings<br>Are not thine own so proper as to waste<br>Thyself upon thy virtues, they on thee.<br>Heaven doth with us as we with torches do,<br>Not light them for themselves; for if our virtues<br>Did not go forth of us, ’twere all alike<br>As if we had them not. Spirits are not finely touched<br>But to fine issues; nor [[Nature|nature]] never lends<br>The smallest scruple of her excellence<br>But, like a thrifty goddess, she determines<br>Herself the glory of a creditor,<br>Both thanks and use. But 1 do bend my speech<br>To one that can my part in him advertise.<br>Hold therefore, [[Angelo]].<br>In our remove be thou at full ourself.<br>[[Mortality]] and [[Mercy|mercy]] in Vienna<br>Live in thy tongue and heart. Old [[Escalus]],<br>Though first in question, is thy secondary.<br>Take thy commission. |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | Now good my lord,<br>Let there be some more test made of my metal<br>Before so noble and so great a figure<br>Be stamped upon it. |
| DUKE | No more evasion.<br>We have with leavened and prepared choice<br>Proceeded to you; therefore take your honours. |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Angelo]] takes his commission |
| DUKE | Our haste from hence is of so quick condition<br>That it prefers itself, and [[Leaves|leaves]] unquestioned<br>Matters of needful value. We shall write to you<br>As [[Time|time]] and our concernings shall importune,<br>How it goes with us; and do look to know<br>What doth befall you here. So fare you [[Well|well]].<br>To th’ hopeful execution do I leave you<br>Of your commissions. |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | Yet give leave, my lord,<br>That we may bring you something on the way. |
| DUKE | My haste may not admit it;<br>Nor need you, on mine [[Honour|honour]], have to do<br>With any scruple. Your scope is as mine own,<br>So to enforce or qualify the laws<br>As to your [[Soul|soul]] seems good. Give me your hand.<br>I’ll privily away. I [[Love|love]] the people,<br>But do not like to stage me to their eyes.<br>Though it do [[Well|well]], I do not relish [[Well|well]]<br>Their loud applause and aves vehement;<br>Nor do I think the man of safe discretion<br>That does affect it. Once more, fare you [[Well|well]]. |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | The heavens give safety to your purposes! |
| [[Escalus|ESCALUS]] | Lead forth and bring you back in happiness! |
| DUKE | I thank you. Fare you [[Well|well]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit |
| [[Escalus|ESCALUS]] | I shall [[Desire|desire]] you, sir, to give me leave<br>To have free speech with you; and it concerns me<br>To look into the [[Bottom|bottom]] of my place.<br>A power I have, but of what strength and [[Nature|nature]]<br>I am not yet instructed. |
| [[Angelo|ANGELO]] | ’Tis so with me. Let us withdraw together,<br>And we may soon our satisfaction have<br>Touching that point. |
| [[Escalus|ESCALUS]] | I’ll wait upon your [[Honour|honour]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt |
