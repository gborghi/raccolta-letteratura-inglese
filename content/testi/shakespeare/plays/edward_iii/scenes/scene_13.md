| *(didascalia)* | Enter Jean [[King of France]] and the [[Dauphin]] |
| [[King of France|KING OF FRANCE]] | A sudden darkness hath defaced the sky,<br>The winds are crept into their caves for [[Fear|fear]],<br>The [[Leaves|leaves]] move not, the world is hushed and still,<br>The [[Birds|birds]] cease singing and the wand’ring brooks<br>Murmur no wonted greeting to their shores.<br>[[Silence]] attends some [[Wonder|wonder]] and expecteth<br>That heaven should pronounce some prophecy.<br>Where or from whom proceeds this [[Silence|silence]], Charles? |
| [[Dauphin|DAUPHIN]] | Our men with open mouths and staring eyes<br>Look on each other as they did attend<br>Each other’s words, and yet no creature speaks.<br>A tongue-tied [[Fear|fear]] hath made a midnight hour,<br>And speeches [[Sleep|sleep]] through all the waking regions. |
| [[King of France|KING OF FRANCE]] | But now the pompous sun in all his [[Pride|pride]]<br>Looked through his golden coach upon the world,<br>And, on a sudden, hath he hid himself,<br>That now the under earth is as a [[Grave|grave]],<br>Dark, deadly, silent and uncomfortable.<br>A clamour of ravens<br>Hark, what a deadly outcry do I hear! |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Prince Philippe |
| [[Dauphin|DAUPHIN]] | Here comes my brother Philippe— |
| [[King of France|KING OF FRANCE]] | All dismayed.<br>(To Philippe) What fearful words are those thy looks presage? |
| PRINCE PHILIPPE | A flight, a flight— |
| [[King of France|KING OF FRANCE]] | Coward, what flight? Thou liest. There needs no flight. |
| PRINCE PHILIPPE | A flight— |
| [[King of France|KING OF FRANCE]] | Awake thy craven powers, and tell on<br>The substance of that very [[Fear|fear]] in deed<br>Which is so ghastly printed in thy face.<br>What is the matter? |
| PRINCE PHILIPPE | A flight of ugly ravens<br>Do croak and hover o’er our soldiers’ heads,<br>And keep in triangles and cornered squares,<br>Right as our forces are embattelèd.<br>With their approach there came this sudden fog<br>Which now hath hid the airy floor of heaven,<br>And made at [[Noon|noon]] a [[Night|night]] unnatural<br>Upon the quaking and dismayed world.<br>In brief, our soldiers have let fall their arms,<br>And stand like metamorphosed images,<br>Bloodless and pale, one gazing on another. |
| *(didascalia)* | [[King of France|KING OF FRANCE]] aside |
| PRINCE PHILIPPE | Ay, now I call to mind the prophecy—<br>But I must give no utterance to a [[Fear|fear]].<br>(To Philippe) Return, and hearten up these yielding souls!<br>Tell them the ravens, seeing them in arms—<br>So many fair against a famished few—<br>Come but to dine upon their handiwork,<br>And prey upon the carrion that they kill.<br>For when we see a [[Horse|horse]] laid down to die—<br>Although not dead—the ravenous [[Birds|birds]]<br>Sit watching the departure of his life.<br>Even so these ravens, for the carcasses<br>Of those poor English that are marked to die,<br>Hover about, and if they cry to us<br>’Tis but for meat that we must kill for them.<br>Away, and comfort up my soldiers,<br>And sound the trumpets and at once dispatch<br>This little business of a silly fraud. Exit Prince Philippe<br>Another noise. Enter the [[Earl of Salisbury]] brought in by a French Captain |
| FRENCH CAPTAIN | Behold, my liege, this knight and forty more,<br>Of whom the better part are slain and fled,<br>With all endeavour sought to break our ranks<br>And make their way to the encompassed Prince.<br>Dispose of him as please your majesty. |
| [[King of France|KING OF FRANCE]] | Go, and the next bough, soldier, that thou seest,<br>Disgrace it with his [[Body|body]] presently,<br>Fore I do hold a tree in France too good<br>To be the gallows of an English thief. |
| [[Earl of Salisbury|EARL OF SALISBURY]] | (to the [[Dauphin]])<br>My lord of Normandy, I have your pass<br>And warrant for my safety through this land. |
| [[Dauphin|DAUPHIN]] | Villiers procured it for thee, did he not? |
| [[Earl of Salisbury|EARL OF SALISBURY]] | He did. |
| [[Dauphin|DAUPHIN]] | And it is current. Thou shalt freely pass. |
| [[King of France|KING OF FRANCE]] | Ay, freely to the gallows to be hanged Without denial or impediment! Away with him. |
| [[Dauphin|DAUPHIN]] | I [[Hope|hope]] your highness will not so disgrace me,<br>And dash the virtue of my seal at arms.<br>He hath my never-broken name to show,<br>Charàctered with this princely hand of mine.<br>And rather let me leave to be a prince<br>Than break the stable verdict of a prince.<br>I do beseech you, let him pass in quiet. |
| [[King of France|KING OF FRANCE]] | Thou and thy word lie both in my command.<br>What canst thou promise that I cannot break?<br>Which of these twain is greater infamy—<br>To disobey thy father or thyself?<br>Thy word, nor no man’s, may exceed his power,<br>Nor that same man doth never break his word<br>That keeps it to the utmost of his power.<br>The breach of [[Faith|faith]] dwells in the [[Soul|soul]]’s consent,<br>Which, if thyself without consent do break,<br>Thou art not charged with the breach of [[Faith|faith]].<br>Go, hang him; for thy licence lies in me,<br>And my constraint stands the excuse for thee. |
| [[Dauphin|DAUPHIN]] | What, am I not a soldier in my word?<br>Then arms, adieu, and let them fight that list.<br>Shall I not give my girdle from my waist<br>But with a guardian I shall be controlled<br>To say I may not give my things away?<br>Upon my [[Soul|soul]], had Edward Prince of Wales<br>Engaged his word, writ down his noble hand,<br>For all your knights to pass his father’s land,<br>The royal [[King]], to grace his warlike son,<br>Would not [[Alone|alone]] safe conduct give to them,<br>But with all bounty feasted them and theirs. |
| [[King of France|KING OF FRANCE]] | Dwell’st thou on precedents? Then be it so.<br>(To Salisbury) Say, Englishman, of what degree thou art. |
| [[Earl of Salisbury|EARL OF SALISBURY]] | An earl in England, though a prisoner here.<br>And those that know me, call me Salisbury. |
| [[King of France|KING OF FRANCE]] | Then, Salisbury, say whither thou art bound. |
| [[Earl of Salisbury|EARL OF SALISBURY]] | To Calais, where my liege, [[King]] Edward, is. |
| [[King of France|KING OF FRANCE]] | To Calais, Salisbury? Then to Calais pack,<br>And bid thy [[King]] prepare a noble [[Grave|grave]]<br>To put his princely son, black Edward, in.<br>And as thou travell’st westward from this place,<br>Some two leagues hence there is a lofty [[Hill|hill]],<br>Whose top seems topless, for the embracing sky<br>Doth hide his high head in her azure bosom,<br>Upon whose tall top, when thy foot attains,<br>Look back upon the humble vale beneath—<br>Humble of late, but now made proud with arms—<br>And thence behold the wretched Prince of Wales<br>Hooped with a band of iron round about.<br>After which sight, to Calais spur amain,<br>And say the Prince was smothered and not slain.<br>And tell thy [[King]], this is not all his ill,<br>For I will greet him ere he thinks I will.<br>Away, be gone. The smoke but of our shot<br>Will choke our foes, though bullets hit them not. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt |
