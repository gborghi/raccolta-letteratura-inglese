| *(didascalia)* | Alarum. Enter and exit a many Frenchmen flying. Chasing after them, enter and exit Edward Prince of Wales running. Then enter Jean [[King of France]] and the Due de Lorraine |
| [[King of France|KING OF FRANCE]] | O Lorraine, say: what mean our men to fly?<br>Our number is far greater than our foes. |
| DUC DE LORRAINE | The garrison of Genoese, my lord,<br>That came from [[Paris]], weary with their march,<br>Grudging to be so suddenly employed,<br>No sooner in the forefront took their place,<br>But straight retiring, so dismayed the rest<br>As likewise they betook themselves to flight,<br>In which, for haste to make a safe escape,<br>More in the clustering throng are pressed to [[Death|death]]<br>Than by the enemy a thousandfold. |
| [[King of France|KING OF FRANCE]] | O, hapless fortune! Let us yet assay<br>If we can counsel some of them to stay. |
