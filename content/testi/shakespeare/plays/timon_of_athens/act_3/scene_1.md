| *(didascalia)* | Enter Flaminius, with a box under his cloak, waiting to speak with [[Lucullus]]. From his master, enters a Servant to him |
| [[Lucullus|LUCULLUS]]’ SERVANT | I have told my lord of you. He is coming down to you. |
| FLAMINIUS | I thank you, sir. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Lucullus]] |
| [[Lucullus|LUCULLUS]]’ SERVANT | Here’s my lord. |
| [[Lucullus|LUCULLUS]] | (aside) One of Lord [[Timon]]’s men? A gift, I warrant. Why, this hits right; I dreamt of a silver basin and ewer tonight.—Ftaminius, honest Flaminius, you are very respectively welcome, sir. (To his Servant) Fill me some wine. Exit Servant And how does that honourable, complete, free-hearted gentleman of Athens, thy very bountiful good lord and master? |
| FLAMINIUS | His health is [[Well|well]], sir. |
| [[Lucullus|LUCULLUS]] | I am right glad that his health is [[Well|well]], sir. And what hast thou there under thy cloak, pretty Flaminius? |
| FLAMINIUS | [[Faith]], nothing but an empty box, sir, which in my lord’s behalf I come to entreat your [[Honour|honour]] to supply, who, having great and instant occasion to use fifty talents, hath sent to your lordship to furnish him, nothing doubting your present assistance therein. |
| [[Lucullus|LUCULLUS]] | La, la, la, la, ‘nothing doubting’ says he? Alas, good lord! A noble gentleman ’tis, if he would not keep so good a [[house]]. Many a [[Time|time]] and often I ha’ dined with him and told him on‘t, and come again to supper to him of purpose to have him spend less; and yet he would embrace no counsel, take no warning by my coming. Every man has his fault, and honesty is his. I ha’ told him on’t, but I could ne’er get him from’t. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Servant, with wine |
| SERVANT | Please your lordship, here is the wine. |
| [[Lucullus|LUCULLUS]] | Flaminius, I have noted thee always wise. (Drinking) Here’s to thee! |
| FLAMINIUS | Your lordship speaks your pleasure. |
| [[Lucullus|LUCULLUS]] | I have observed thee always for a towardly prompt spirit, give thee thy due, and one that knows what belongs to reason; and canst use the [[Time|time]] [[Well|well]] if the [[Time|time]] use thee [[Well|well]]. (Drinking) Good parts in thee! (To his Servant) Get you gone, sirrah. Exit Servant Draw nearer, honest Flaminius. Thy lord’s a bountiful gentleman; but thou art wise, and thou know‘st [[Well|well]] enough, although thou com’st to me, that this is no [[Time|time]] to lend money, especially upon bare [[Friendship|friendship]] without security. (Giving coins) Here’s three solidares for thee. Good boy, wink at me, and say thou saw’st me not. Fare thee [[Well|well]]. |
| FLAMINIUS | Is’t possible the world should so much differ,<br>And we alive that lived?<br>He throws the coins at [[Lucullus]]<br>Fly, damned baseness,<br>To him that worships thee. |
| [[Lucullus|LUCULLUS]] | Ha! Now I see thou art a [[Fool|fool]], and fit for thy master. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit |
| FLAMINIUS | May these add to the number that may scald thee.<br>Let molten coin be thy damnation,<br>Thou disease of a friend, and not himself.<br>Has [[Friendship|friendship]] such a faint and milky heart<br>It turns in less than two nights? O you gods,<br>I feel my master’s passion! This slave<br>Unto this hour has my lord’s meat in him.<br>Why should it thrive and turn to nutriment,<br>When he is turned to [[Poison|poison]]?<br>O, may diseases only work upon’t;<br>And when he’s sick to [[Death|death]], let not that part of [[Nature|nature]]<br>Which my lord paid for be of any power<br>To expel sickness, but prolong his hour. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit |
