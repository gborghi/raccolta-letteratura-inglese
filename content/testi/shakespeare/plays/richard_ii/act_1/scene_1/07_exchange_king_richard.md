| [[King|KING]] [[Richard|RICHARD]] | Wrath-kindled gentlemen, be ruled by me. Let’s purge this choler without letting [[Blood|blood]]. This we prescribe, though no physician: Deep malice makes too deep incision; Forget, forgive, conclude, and be agreed; Our doctors say this is no [[Time|time]] to bleed. Good uncle, let this end where it begun. We’ll calm the Duke of Norfolk, you your son. |
| [[John of Gaunt|JOHN OF GAUNT]] | To be a make-peace shall become my age. Throw down, my son, the Duke of Norfolk’s gage. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Richard|RICHARD]] | And, Norfolk, throw down his. |
| [[John of Gaunt|JOHN OF GAUNT]] | When, Harry, when? Obedience bids I should not bid again. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Richard|RICHARD]] | Norfolk, throw down! We bid; there is no boot. |
| [[Mowbray|MOWBRAY]] | (kneeling) Myself I throw, dread sovereign, at thy foot. My life thou shalt command, but not my shame. The one my duty owes, but my fair name, Despite of [[Death|death]] that lives upon my [[Grave|grave]], To dark dishonour’s use thou shalt not have. I am disgraced, impeached, and baffled here, Pierced to the [[Soul|soul]] with slander’s venomed spear, The which no balm can cure but his heart [[Blood|blood]] Which breathed this [[Poison|poison]]. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Richard|RICHARD]] | Rage must be withstood. Give me his gage. Lions make leopards tame. |
| [[Mowbray|MOWBRAY]] | standing Yea, but not change his spots. Take but my shame, And I resign my gage. My dear dear lord, The purest treasure mortal times afford Is spotless reputation; that away, Men are but gilded loam, or painted clay. A [[Jewel|jewel]] in a ten-times barred-up chest Is a bold spirit in a loyal breast. Mine [[Honour|honour]] is my life. Both grow in one. Take [[Honour|honour]] from me, and my life is done. Then, dear my liege, mine [[Honour|honour]] let me try. In that I live, and for that will I die. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Richard|RICHARD]] | Cousin, throw down your gage. Do you begin. |
| BOLINGBROKE | O God defend my [[Soul|soul]] from such deep sin! Shall I seem crest-fallen in my father’s sight? Or with pale beggar-[[Fear|fear]] impeach my height Before this out-dared dastard? Ere my tongue Shall wound my [[Honour|honour]] with such feeble wrong, Or sound so base a parle, my teeth shall tear The slavish motive of recanting [[Fear|fear]], And spit it bleeding in his high disgrace Where shame doth harbour, even in [[Mowbray]]’s face. Exit [[John of Gaunt]] |
| [[King|KING]] [[Richard|RICHARD]] | We were not born to sue, but to command; Which since we cannot do to make you friends, Be ready, as your lives shall answer it, At Coventry upon Saint Lambert’s day. There shall your swords and lances arbitrate The swelling difference of your settled hate. Since we cannot atone you, we shall see Justice design the victor’s chivalry. Lord Marshal, command our officers-at-arms Be ready to direct these [[Home|home]] alarms. Exeunt |
