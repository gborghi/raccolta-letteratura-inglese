| [[Richard|RICHARD]] | I have been studying how I may compare This [[Prison|prison]] where I live unto the world; And for because the world is populous, And here is not a creature but myself, I cannot do it. Yet I’ll hammer it out. My brain I’ll prove the female to my [[Soul|soul]], My [[Soul|soul]] the father, and these two beget A generation of still-breeding thoughts; And these same thoughts people this little world In humours like the people of this world. For no thought is contented. The better sort, As thoughts of things divine, are intermixed With scruples, and do set the [[Faith|faith]] itself Against the [[Faith|faith]], as thus: ‘Come, little ones’, And then again, ‘It is as hard to come as for a camel To thread the postern of a small needle’s eye.’ Thoughts tending to [[Ambition|ambition]], they do plot Unlikely wonders: how these vain weak nails May tear a passage through the flinty ribs Of this hard world, my ragged [[Prison|prison]] walls; And for they cannot, die in their own [[Pride|pride]]. Thoughts tending to content flatter themselves That they are not the first of fortune’s slaves, Nor shall not be the last—like seely beggars, Who, sitting in the stocks, refuge their shame That many have, and others must, set there; And in this thought they find a kind of ease, Bearing their own misfortunes on the back Of such as have before endured the like. Thus play I in one person many people, And none contented. Sometimes am I [[King|king]]; Then treason makes me wish myself a beggar, And so I am. Then crushing penury Persuades me I was better when a [[King|king]]. Then am I kinged again, and by and by Think that I am unkinged by Bolingbroke, And straight am nothing. But whate’er I be, Nor I, nor any man that but man is, With nothing shall be pleased till he be eased With being nothing. The music plays Music do I hear. Ha, ha; keep [[Time|time]]! How sour sweet music is When [[Time|time]] is broke and no proportion kept. So is it in the music of men’s lives. And here have I the daintiness of ear To check [[Time|time]] broke in a disordered string; But for the concord of my state and [[Time|time]] Had not an ear to hear my true [[Time|time]] broke. I wasted [[Time|time]], and now doth [[Time|time]] waste me, For now hath [[Time|time]] made me his numb‘ring [[Clock|clock]]. My thoughts are minutes, and with sighs they jar Their watches on unto mine eyes, the outward watch Whereto my finger, like a dial’s point, Is pointing still in cleansing them from [[Tears|tears]]. Now, sir, the sounds that tell what hour it is Are clamorous groans that strike upon my heart, Which is the bell. So sighs, and [[Tears|tears]], and groans Show minutes, hours, and times. But my [[Time|time]] Runs posting on in Bolingbroke’s proud joy, While I stand fooling here, his jack of the [[Clock|clock]]. This music mads me. Let it sound no more, For though it have holp madmen to their wits, In me it seems it will make wise men mad. The music ceases Yet blessing on his heart that gives it me, For ’tis a sign of [[Love|love]], and [[Love|love]] to [[Richard]] Is a strange brooch in this all-hating world.
