| *(didascalia)* | Enter the [[Queen]], with her Ladies |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] | This way the [[King]] will come. This is the way<br>To [[Julius Caesar]]’s ill-erected Tower,<br>To whose flint bosom my condemned lord<br>Is doomed a prisoner by proud Bolingbroke.<br>Here let us rest, if this rebellious earth<br>Have any resting for her true [[King|king]]’s [[Queen|queen]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Richard]] and guard |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] | But soft, but see—or rather do not see—<br>My fair [[Rose|rose]] wither. Yet look up, behold,<br>That you in pity may dissolve to dew,<br>And wash him fresh again with true-[[Love|love]] [[Tears|tears]].—<br>Ah, thou the model where old Troy did stand!<br>Thou map of [[Honour|honour]], thou [[King]] [[Richard]]’s tomb,<br>And not [[King]] [[Richard]]! Thou most beauteous inn:<br>Why should hard-favoured grief be lodged in thee,<br>When triumph is become an alehouse guest? |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] | Join not with grief, fair woman, do not so,<br>To make my end too sudden. Learn, good [[Soul|soul]],<br>To think our former state a happy dream,<br>From which awaked, the [[Truth|truth]] of what we are<br>Shows us but this. I am sworn brother, sweet,<br>To grim necessity, and he and I<br>Will keep a league till [[Death|death]]. Hie thee to France,<br>And cloister thee in some religious [[house]].<br>Our holy lives must win a new world’s [[Crown|crown]],<br>Which our profane hours here have stricken down. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] | What, is my [[Richard]] both in shape and mind<br>Transformed and weakenèd? Hath Bolingbroke<br>Deposed thine intellect? Hath he been in thy heart?<br>The [[Lion|lion]] dying thrusteth forth his paw<br>And wounds the earth, if nothing else, with rage<br>To be o’erpowered; and wilt thou, pupil-like,<br>Take the correction, mildly kiss the rod,<br>And fawn on rage with base [[Humility|humility]],<br>Which art a [[Lion|lion]] and the [[King|king]] of beasts ? |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] | A [[King|king]] of beasts indeed! If aught but beasts,<br>I had been still a happy [[King|king]] of men.<br>Good sometimes [[Queen]], prepare thee hence for France.<br>Think I am dead, and that even here thou tak’st,<br>As from my [[Death|death]]-bed, thy last living leave.<br>In [[Winter|winter]]’s tedious nights, sit by the [[Fire|fire]]<br>With good old folks, and let them tell thee tales<br>Of woeful ages long ago betid;<br>And ere thou bid goodnight, to quit their griefs<br>Tell thou the lamentable fall of me,<br>And send the hearers weeping to their beds;<br>Forwhy the senseless brands will sympathize<br>The heavy accent of thy moving tongue,<br>And in compassion weep the [[Fire|fire]] out;<br>And some will mourn in ashes, some coal black,<br>For the deposing of a rightful [[King|king]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter the [[Earl of Northumberland]] |
| NORTHUMBERLAND | My lord, the mind of Bolingbroke is changed.<br>You must to Pomfret, not unto the Tower.<br>And, madam, there is order ta’en for you.<br>With all swift [[Speed|speed]] you must away to France. |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] | Northumberland, thou ladder wherewithal<br>The mounting Bolingbroke ascends my throne,<br>The [[Time|time]] shall not be many hours of age<br>More than it is ere foul sin, gathering head,<br>Shall break into corruption. Thou shalt think,<br>Though he divide the realm and give thee half,<br>It is too little helping him to all.<br>He shall think that thou, which know‘st the way<br>To plant unrightful kings, wilt know again,<br>Being ne’er so little urged another way,<br>To pluck him headlong from the usurped throne.<br>The [[Love|love]] of wicked friends converts to [[Fear|fear]],<br>That [[Fear|fear]] to hate, and hate turns one or both<br>To worthy danger and deserved [[Death|death]]. |
| NORTHUMBERLAND | My [[Guilt|guilt]] be on my head, and there an end.<br>Take leave and part, for you must part forthwith. |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] | Doubly divorced! Bad men, you violate<br>A twofold [[Marriage|marriage]]:‘twixt my [[Crown|crown]] and me,<br>And then betwixt me and my married wife.<br>(To the [[Queen]]) Let me unkiss the oath ’twixt thee and me—<br>And yet not so, for with a kiss ‘twas made.<br>Part us, Northumberland: I towards the north,<br>Where shivering cold and sickness pines the clime;<br>My [[Queen|queen]] to France, from whence set forth in pomp<br>She came adorned hither like sweet May,<br>Sent back like Hallowmas or short’st of day. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] | And must we be divided? Must we part? |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] | Ay, hand from hand, my [[Love|love]], and heart from heart. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] | Banish us both, and send the [[King]] with me. |
| *(didascalia)* | NORTHUMBERLAND |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] | That were some [[Love|love]], but little policy. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] | Then whither he goes, thither let me go. |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] | So two together weeping make one woe.<br>Weep thou for me in France, I for thee here.<br>Better far off than, near, be ne’er the nea’er.<br>Go count thy way with sighs, I mine with groans. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] | So longest way shall have the longest moans. |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] | Twice for one step I’ll groan, the way being short,<br>And piece the way out with a heavy heart.<br>Come, come, in wooing sorrow let’s be brief,<br>Since, wedding it, there is such length in grief.<br>One kiss shall stop our mouths, and dumbly part.<br>Thus give I mine, and thus take I thy heart.<br>They kiss |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] | Give me mine own again. ’Twere no good part<br>To take on me to keep and kill thy heart.<br>They kiss<br>So now I have mine own again, be gone,<br>That I may strive to kill it with a groan. |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] | We make woe wanton with this fond delay.<br>Once more, adieu. The rest let sorrow say. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt FRichard, guarded, and Northumberland at one door, the [[Queen]] and her Ladies at another door |
