| BISHOP OF CARLISLE | My lord, wise men ne’er wail their present woes, But presently prevent the ways to wail. To [[Fear|fear]] the foe, since [[Fear|fear]] oppresseth strength, Gives in your weakness strength unto your foe; And so your follies fight against yourself. [[Fear]], and be slain. No worse can come to fight; And fight and die is [[Death|death]] destroying [[Death|death]], Where fearing dying pays [[Death|death]] servile breath. |
| AUMERLE | My father hath a power. Enquire of him, And learn to make a [[Body|body]] of a limb. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Richard|RICHARD]] | standing Thou chid’st me [[Well|well]]. Proud Bolingbroke, I come To change blows with thee for our day of doom. This ague-fit of [[Fear|fear]] is overblown. An easy task it is to win our own. Say, Scrope, where lies our uncle with his power? Speak sweetly, man, although thy looks be sour. |
| SCROPE | Men judge by the complexion of the sky The state and inclination of the day. So may you by my dull and heavy eye My tongue hath but a heavier tale to say. I play the torturer by small and small To lengthen out the worst that must be spoken. Your uncle York is joined with Bolingbroke, And all your northern castles yielded up, And all your southern gentlemen in arms Upon his faction. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Richard|RICHARD]] | Thou hast said enough. (To Aumerle) Beshrew thee, cousin, which didst lead me forth Of that sweet way I was in to [[Despair|despair]]. What say you now? What comfort have we now? By heaven, I’ll hate him everlastingly That bids me be of comfort any more. Go to Flint [[Castle]]; there I’ll pine away. A [[King|king]], woe’s slave, shall kingly woe obey. That power I have, discharge, and let them go To ear the land that hath some [[Hope|hope]] to grow; For I have none. Let no man speak again To alter this, for counsel is but vain. |
| AUMERLE | My liege, one word. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Richard|RICHARD]] | He does me [[Double|double]] wrong That wounds me with the flatteries of his tongue. Discharge my followers. Let them hence away From [[Richard]]’s [[Night|night]] to Bolingbroke’s fair day. Exeunt |
