| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Antonio]] and Panthino |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | Tell me, Panthino, what sad talk was that<br>Wherewith my brother held you in the cloister? |
| PANTHINO | ‘Twas of his nephew [[Proteus]], your son. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | Why, what of him? |
| PANTHINO | He wondered that your lordship<br>Would suffer him to spend his youth at [[Home|home]]<br>While other men, of slender reputation,<br>Put forth their sons to seek preferment out—<br>Some to the wars, to try their fortune there,<br>Some to discover islands far away,<br>Some to the studious universities.<br>For any or for all these exercises<br>He said that [[Proteus]] your son was meet,<br>And did request me to importune you<br>To let him spend his [[Time|time]] no more at [[Home|home]],<br>Which would be great impeachment to his age<br>In having known no travel in his youth. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | Nor need’st thou much importune me to that<br>Whereon this month I have been hammering.<br>I have considered [[Well|well]] his loss of [[Time|time]],<br>And how he cannot be a perfect man,<br>Not being tried and tutored in the world.<br>Experience is by industry achieved,<br>And perfected by the swift course of [[Time|time]].<br>Then tell me, whither were I best to send him? |
| PANTHINO | I think your lordship is not ignorant<br>How his companion, youthful [[Valentine]],<br>Attends the Emperor in his royal [[Court|court]]. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | I know it [[Well|well]]. |
| PANTHINO | ‘Twere good, I think, your lordship sent him thither.<br>There shall he practise tilts and tournaments,<br>Hear sweet discourse, converse with noblemen,<br>And be in eye of every exercise<br>Worthy his youth and nobleness of birth. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | I like thy counsel. [[Well]] hast thou advised,<br>And that thou mayst perceive how [[Well|well]] I like it,<br>The execution of it shall make known.<br>Even with the speediest expedition<br>I will dispatch him to the Emperor’s [[Court|court]]. |
| PANTHINO | Tomorrow, may it please you, Don Alfonso,<br>With other gentlemen of good esteem,<br>Are journeying to salute the Emperor<br>And to commend their service to his will. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | Good company. With them shall [[Proteus]] go. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Proteus]] with a letter. He does not see [[Antonio]] and Panthino |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | And in good [[Time|time]]. Now will we break with him. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | Sweet [[Love|love]], sweet lines, sweet life!<br>Here is her hand, the agent of her heart.<br>Here is her oath for [[Love|love]], her [[Honour|honour]]’s pawn.<br>O that our fathers would applaud our loves<br>To seal our happiness with their consents.<br>O heavenly [[Julia]]! |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | How now, what letter are you reading there? |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | May’t please your lordship, ‘tis a word or two<br>Of commendations sent from [[Valentine]],<br>Delivered by a friend that came from him. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | Lend me the letter. Let me see what news. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | There is no news, my lord, but that he writes<br>How happily he lives, how [[Well|well]] beloved<br>And daily gracèd by the Emperor,<br>Wishing me with him, partner of his fortune. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | And how stand you affected to his wish? |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | As one relying on your lordship’s will,<br>And not depending on his friendly wish. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | My will is something sorted with his wish.<br>Muse not that I thus suddenly proceed,<br>For what I will, I will, and there an end.<br>I am resolved that thou shalt spend some [[Time|time]]<br>With Valentinus in the Emperor’s [[Court|court]].<br>What maintenance he from his friends receives,<br>Like exhibition thou shalt have from me.<br>Tomorrow be in readiness to go.<br>Excuse it not, for I am peremptory. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | My lord, I cannot be so soon provided.<br>Please you deliberate a day or two. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | Look what thou want’st shall be sent after thee.<br>No more of stay. Tomorrow thou must go.<br>Come on, Panthino. You shall be employed<br>To hasten on his expedition. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt [[Antonio]] and Panthino |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | Thus have I shunned the [[Fire|fire]] for [[Fear|fear]] of burning<br>And drenched me in the sea where I am drowned.<br>I feared to show my father [[Julia]]’s letter<br>Lest he should take exceptions to my [[Love|love]],<br>And with the vantage of mine own excuse<br>Hath he excepted most against my [[Love|love]].<br>O, how this [[spring]] of [[Love|love]] resembleth<br>The uncertain glory of an April day,<br>Which now shows all the [[Beauty|beauty]] of the sun,<br>And by and by a cloud takes all away. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Panthino |
| PANTHINO | Sir [[Proteus]], your father calls for you.<br>He is in haste, therefore I pray you go. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | Why, this it is. My heart accords thereto,<br>And yet a thousand times it answers ‘No’. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt |
