| Chi parla | Battuta |
|---|---|
| LUCETTA | What would your ladyship? |
| [[Julia|JULIA]] | Is’t near dinner-[[Time|time]]? |
| LUCETTA | I would it were, That you might kill your stomach on your meat And not upon your maid. She drops and picks up the letter |
| [[Julia|JULIA]] | What is’t that you Took up so gingerly? |
| LUCETTA | Nothing. |
| [[Julia|JULIA]] | Why didst thou stoop then? |
| LUCETTA | To take a paper up that I let fall. |
| [[Julia|JULIA]] | And is that paper nothing? |
| LUCETTA | Nothing concerning me. |
| [[Julia|JULIA]] | Then let it lie for those that it concerns. |
| LUCETTA | Madam, it will not lie where it concerns, Unless it have a false interpreter. |
| [[Julia|JULIA]] | Some [[Love|love]] of yours hath writ to you in rhyme. |
| LUCETTA | That I might sing it, madam, to a tune, Give me a note. Your ladyship can set. |
| [[Julia|JULIA]] | As little by such toys as may be possible. Best sing it to the tune of ‘Light o’ [[Love|love]]’. |
| LUCETTA | It is too heavy for so light a tune. |
| [[Julia|JULIA]] | Heavy? Belike it hath some burden, then? |
| LUCETTA | Ay, and melodious were it, would you sing it. |
| [[Julia|JULIA]] | And why not you? |
| LUCETTA | I cannot reach so high. |
| [[Julia|JULIA]] | Let’s see your [[Song|song]]. She tries to take the letters How now, minion! |
| LUCETTA | Keep tune there still. So you will sing it out. And yet methinks I do not like this tune. |
| [[Julia|JULIA]] | You do not? |
| LUCETTA | No, madam, ‘tis too sharp. |
| [[Julia|JULIA]] | You, minion, are too saucy. |
| LUCETTA | Nay, now you are too flat, And mar the concord with too harsh a descant. There wanteth but a mean to fill your [[Song|song]]. |
| [[Julia|JULIA]] | The mean is drowned with your unruly bass. |
| LUCETTA | Indeed, I bid the base for [[Proteus]]. |
| [[Julia|JULIA]] | This bauble shall not henceforth trouble me. Here is a coil with protestation. She [[Tears|tears]] the letter and drops the pieces Go, get you gone, and let the papers lie. You would be fing’ring them to anger me. |
| LUCETTA | (aside) She makes it strange, but she would be best pleased To be so angered with another letter. Exit |
