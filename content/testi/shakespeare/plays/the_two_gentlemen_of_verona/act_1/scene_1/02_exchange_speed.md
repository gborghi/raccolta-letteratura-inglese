|---|---|
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | Sir [[Proteus]], save you. Saw you my master? |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | But now he parted hence to embark for Milan. |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | Twenty to one, then, he is shipped already, And I have played the sheep in losing him. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | Indeed, a sheep doth very often stray, An if the shepherd be a while away. |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | You conclude that my master is a shepherd, then, and I a sheep? |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | I do. |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | Why then, my horns are his horns, whether I wake or [[Sleep|sleep]]. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | A silly answer, and fitting [[Well|well]] a sheep. |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | This proves me still a sheep. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | True, and thy master a shepherd. |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | Nay, that I can deny by a circumstance. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | It shall go hard but I’ll prove it by another. |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | The shepherd seeks the sheep, and not the sheep the shepherd. But I seek my master, and my master seeks not me. Therefore I am no sheep. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | The sheep for fodder follow the shepherd, the shepherd for food follows not the sheep. Thou for wages followest thy master, thy master for wages follows not thee. Therefore thou art a sheep. |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | Such another proof will make me cry ‘baa’. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | But dost thou hear: gav’st thou my letter to [[Julia]]? |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | Ay, sir. I, a lost mutton, gave your letter to her, a laced mutton, and she, a laced mutton, gave me, a lost mutton, nothing for my labour. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | Here’s too small a pasture for such store of muttons. |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | If the ground be overcharged, you were best stick her. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | Nay, in that you are astray. ‘Twere best pound you. |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | Nay sir, less than a pound shall serve me for carrying your letter. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | You mistake. I mean the pound, a pinfold. |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | From a pound to a pin? Fold it over and over ‘Tis threefold too little for carrying a letter to your [[Lover|lover]]. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | But what said she? |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | (nods, then says) Ay. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | Nod-ay? Why, that’s ‘noddy’. |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | You mistook, sir. I say she did nod, and you ask me if she did nod, and I say ‘Ay’. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | And that set together is ‘noddy’. |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | Now you have taken the pains to set it together, take it for your pains. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | No, no. You shall have it for bearing the letter. |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | [[Well]], I perceive I must be fain to bear with you. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | Why, sir, how do you bear with me? |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | Marry, sir, the letter very orderly, having nothing but the word ‘noddy’ for my pains. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | Beshrew me but you have a quick wit. |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | And yet it cannot overtake your slow purse. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | Come, come, open the matter in brief. What said she? |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | Open your purse, that the money and the matter may be both at once delivered. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | (giving money) [[Well]], sir, here is for your pains. What said she? |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | Truly, sir, I think you’ll hardly win her. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | Why? Couldst thou perceive so much from her? |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | Sir, I could perceive nothing at all from her, no, not so much as a ducat for delivering your letter. And being so hard to me, that brought your mind, I [[Fear|fear]] she’ll prove as hard to you in telling your mind. Give her no token but stones, for she’s as hard as steel. |
