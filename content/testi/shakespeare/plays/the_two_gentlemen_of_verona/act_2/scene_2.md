| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Proteus]] and [[Julia]] |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | Have patience, gentle [[Julia]]. |
| [[Julia|JULIA]] | I must where is no remedy. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | When possibly I can I will return. |
| [[Julia|JULIA]] | If you turn not, you will return the sooner.<br>She gives him a ring<br>Keep this remembrance for thy [[Julia]]’s sake. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | Why then, we’ll make exchange. Here, take you this.<br>He gives her a ring |
| [[Julia|JULIA]] | And seal the bargain with a holy kiss. |
| *(didascalia)* | They kiss |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | Here is my hand for my true constancy.<br>And when that hour o’erslips me in the day<br>Wherein I sigh not, [[Julia]], for thy sake,<br>The next ensuing hour some foul mischance<br>Torment me for my [[Love|love]]’s forgetfulness.<br>My father stays my coming. Answer not.<br>The tide is now. ([[Julia]] weeps) Nay, not thy tide of [[Tears|tears]],<br>That tide will stay me longer than I should.<br>[[Julia]], farewell. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit [[Julia]] |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | What, gone without a word?<br>Ay, so true [[Love|love]] should do. It cannot speak,<br>For [[Truth|truth]] hath better deeds than words to grace it. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Panthino |
| PANTHINO | Sir [[Proteus]], you are stayed for. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | Go, I come, I come.—<br>Alas, this parting strikes poor [[lovers]] dumb. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt |
