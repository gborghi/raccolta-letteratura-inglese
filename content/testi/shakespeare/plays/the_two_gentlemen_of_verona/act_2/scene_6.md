| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | To leave my [[Julia]] shall I be forsworn;<br>To [[Love|love]] fair [[Silvia]] shall I be forsworn;<br>To wrong my friend I shall be much forsworn.<br>And e‘en that power which gave me first my oath<br>Provokes me to this threefold perjury.<br>[[Love]] bade me swear, and [[Love|love]] bids me forswear.<br>O sweet-suggesting [[Love|love]], if thou hast sinned<br>Teach me, thy tempted subject, to excuse it.<br>At first I did adore a twinkling star,<br>But now I worship a celestial sun.<br>Unheedful vows may heedfully be broken,<br>And he wants wit that wants resolved will<br>To learn his wit t’exchange the bad for better.<br>Fie, fie, unreverent tongue, to call her bad<br>Whose sovereignty so oft thou hast preferred<br>With twenty thousand [[Soul|soul]]-confirming oaths.<br>I cannot leave to [[Love|love]], and yet I do.<br>But there I leave to [[Love|love]] where I should [[Love|love]].<br>[[Julia]] I lose, and [[Valentine]] I lose.<br>If I keep them I needs must lose myself.<br>If I lose them, thus find I by their loss<br>For [[Valentine]], myself, for [[Julia]], [[Silvia]].<br>I to myself am dearer than a friend,<br>For [[Love|love]] is still most precious in itself,<br>And [[Silvia]]—witness heaven that made her fair—<br>Shows [[Julia]] but a swarthy Ethiope.<br>I will forget that [[Julia]] is alive,<br>Rememb’ring that my [[Love|love]] to her is dead,<br>And [[Valentine]] I’ll hold an enemy,<br>Aiming at [[Silvia]] as a sweeter friend.<br>I cannot now prove constant to myself<br>Without some treachery used to [[Valentine]].<br>This [[Night|night]] he meaneth with a corded ladder<br>To climb celestial [[Silvia]]’s chamber-[[Window|window]],<br>Myself in counsel his competitor.<br>Now presently I’ll give her father notice<br>Of their disguising and pretended flight,<br>Who, all enraged, will banish [[Valentine]];<br>For [[Thurio]] he intends shall wed his daughter.<br>But [[Valentine]] being gone, I’ll quickly [[Cross|cross]]<br>By some sly trick blunt [[Thurio]]’s dull proceeding.<br>[[Love]], lend me wings to make my purpose swift,<br>As thou hast lent me wit to plot this drift.
