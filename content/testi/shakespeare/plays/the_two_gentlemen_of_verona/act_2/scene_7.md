| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Julia]] and Lucetta |
| [[Julia|JULIA]] | Counsel, Lucetta. Gentle girl, assist me,<br>And e’en in kind [[Love|love]] I do conjure thee,<br>Who art the table wherein all my thoughts<br>Are visibly charactered and engraved,<br>To lesson me, and tell me some good mean<br>How with my [[Honour|honour]] I may undertake<br>A [[Journey|journey]] to my loving [[Proteus]]. |
| LUCETTA | Alas, the way is wearisome and long. |
| [[Julia|JULIA]] | A true-devoted [[Pilgrim|pilgrim]] is not weary<br>To measure kingdoms with his feeble steps.<br>Much less shall she that hath [[Love|love]]’s wings to fly,<br>And when the flight is made to one so dear,<br>Of such divine perfection as Sir [[Proteus]]. |
| LUCETTA | Better forbear till [[Proteus]] make return. |
| [[Julia|JULIA]] | O, know’st thou not his looks are my [[Soul|soul]]’s food?<br>Pity the dearth that I have pined in<br>By [[Longing|longing]] for that food so long a [[Time|time]].<br>Didst thou but know the inly touch of [[Love|love]]<br>Thou wouldst as soon go kindle [[Fire|fire]] with [[Snow|snow]]<br>As seek to quench the [[Fire|fire]] of [[Love|love]] with words. |
| LUCETTA | I do not seek to quench your [[Love|love]]’s hot [[Fire|fire]],<br>But qualify the [[Fire|fire]]’s extreme rage,<br>Lest it should burn above the bounds of reason. |
| [[Julia|JULIA]] | The more thou damm‘st it up, the more it burns.<br>The current that with gentle murmur glides,<br>Thou know’st, being stopped, impatiently doth rage.<br>But when his fair course is not hindered<br>He makes sweet music with th’enamelled stones,<br>Giving a gentle kiss to every sedge<br>He overtaketh in his pilgrimage.<br>And so by many winding nooks he strays<br>With willing sport to the wild ocean.<br>Then let me go, and hinder not my course.<br>I’ll be as patient as a gentle stream,<br>And make a pastime of each weary step<br>Till the last step have brought me to my [[Love|love]].<br>And there I’ll rest as after much turmoil<br>A blessed [[Soul|soul]] doth in Elysium. |
| LUCETTA | But in what habit will you go along? |
| [[Julia|JULIA]] | Not like a woman, for I would prevent<br>The loose encounters of lascivious men.<br>Gentle Lucetta, fit me with such weeds<br>As may beseem some [[Well|well]]-reputed page. |
| LUCETTA | Why then, your ladyship must cut your hair. |
| [[Julia|JULIA]] | No, girl, I’ll knit it up in silken strings<br>With twenty odd-conceited true-[[Love|love]] knots.<br>To be fantastic may become a youth<br>Of greater [[Time|time]] than I shall show to be. |
| LUCETTA | What fashion, madam, shall I make your breeches? |
| [[Julia|JULIA]] | That fits as [[Well|well]] as ‘Tell me, good my lord,<br>What compass will you wear your farthingale?’<br>Why, e’en what fashion thou best likes, Lucetta. |
| LUCETTA | You must needs have them with a codpiece, madam. |
| [[Julia|JULIA]] | Out, out, Lucetta. That will be ill-favoured. |
| LUCETTA | A round hose, madam, now’s not worth a pin<br>Unless you have a codpiece to stick pins on. |
| [[Julia|JULIA]] | Lucetta, as thou lov‘st me let me have<br>What thou think’st meet and is most mannerly.<br>But tell me, wench, how will the world repute me<br>For undertaking so unstaid a [[Journey|journey]]?<br>I [[Fear|fear]] me it will make me scandalized. |
| LUCETTA | If you think so, then stay at [[Home|home]], and go not. |
| [[Julia|JULIA]] | Nay, that I will not. |
| LUCETTA | Then never dream on infamy, but go.<br>If [[Proteus]] like your [[Journey|journey]] when you come,<br>No matter who’s displeased when you are gone.<br>I [[Fear|fear]] me he will scarce be pleased withal. |
| [[Julia|JULIA]] | That is the least, Lucetta, of my [[Fear|fear]].<br>A thousand oaths, an ocean of his [[Tears|tears]],<br>And instances of infinite of [[Love|love]]<br>Warrant me welcome to my [[Proteus]]. |
| LUCETTA | All these are servants to deceitful men. |
| [[Julia|JULIA]] | Base men, that use them to so base effect.<br>But truer [[Stars|stars]] did govern [[Proteus]]’ birth.<br>His words are bonds, his oaths are oracles,<br>His [[Love|love]] sincere, his thoughts immaculate,<br>His [[Tears|tears]] pure messengers sent from his heart,<br>His heart as far from fraud as heaven from earth. |
| LUCETTA | Pray heaven he prove so when you come to him. |
| [[Julia|JULIA]] | Now, as thou lov’st me, do him not that wrong<br>To bear a hard opinion of his [[Truth|truth]].<br>Only deserve my [[Love|love]] by loving him,<br>And presently go with me to my chamber<br>To take a note of what I stand in need of<br>To furnish me upon my [[Longing|longing]] [[Journey|journey]].<br>All that is mine I leave at thy dispose,<br>My goods, my lands, my reputation;<br>Only in lieu thereof dispatch me hence.<br>Come, answer not, but to it presently.<br>I am impatient of my tarriance. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt |
