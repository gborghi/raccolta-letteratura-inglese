| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | Should I have wished a thing it had been he. |
| DUKE | Welcome him then according to his worth. [[Silvia]], I speak to you, and you, Sir [[Thurio]]; For [[Valentine]], I need not cite him to it. I will send him hither to you presently. Exit |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | This is the gentleman I told your ladyship Had come along with me, but that his mistress Did hold his eyes locked in her crystal looks. |
| [[Silvia|SILVIA]] | Belike that now she hath enfranchised them Upon some other pawn for fealty. |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | Nay, sure, I think she holds them prisoners still. |
| [[Silvia|SILVIA]] | Nay, then he should be blind, and being blind How could he see his way to seek out you? |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | Why, lady, [[Love|love]] hath twenty pair of eyes. |
| [[Thurio|THURIO]] | They say that [[Love|love]] hath not an eye at all. |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | To see such [[lovers]], [[Thurio]], as yourself. Upon a homely object [[Love|love]] can wink. |
| [[Silvia|SILVIA]] | Have done, have done. Here comes the gentleman. Enter [[Proteus]] |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | Welcome, dear [[Proteus]]. Mistress, I beseech you Confirm his welcome with some special favour. |
| [[Silvia|SILVIA]] | His worth is warrant for his welcome hither, If this be he you oft have wished to hear from. |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | Mistress, it is. Sweet lady, entertain him To be my fellow-servant to your ladyship. |
| [[Silvia|SILVIA]] | Too low a mistress for so high a servant. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | Not so, sweet lady, but too mean a servant To have a look of such a worthy mistress. |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | Leave off discourse of disability. Sweet lady, entertain him for your servant. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | My duty will I boast of, nothing else. |
| [[Silvia|SILVIA]] | And duty never yet did want his meed. Servant, you are welcome to a worthless mistress. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | I’ll die on him that says so but yourself. |
| [[Silvia|SILVIA]] | That you are welcome? |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | That you are worthless. Enter a Servant SERVANT Madam, my lord your father would speak with you. |
| [[Silvia|SILVIA]] | I wait upon his pleasure. Exit the Servant Come, Sir [[Thurio]], Go with me. Once more, new servant, welcome. I’ll leave you to confer of [[Home|home]] affairs. When you have done, we look to hear from you. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | We’ll both attend upon your ladyship. Exeunt [[Silvia]] and [[Thurio]] |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | Now tell me, how do all from whence you came? |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | Your friends are [[Well|well]], and have them much commended. |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | And how do yours? |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | I left them all in health. |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | How does your lady, and how thrives your [[Love|love]]? |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | My tales of [[Love|love]] were wont to weary you. I know you joy not in a [[Love|love]]-discourse. |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | Ay, [[Proteus]], but that life is altered now. I have done penance for contemning [[Love|love]], Whose high imperious thoughts have punished me With bitter fasts, with penitential groans, With nightly [[Tears|tears]] and daily heart-sore sighs. For in [[Revenge|revenge]] of my contempt of [[Love|love]] [[Love]] hath chased [[Sleep|sleep]] from my enthralled eyes, And made them watchers of mine own heart’s sorrow. O gentle [[Proteus]], [[Love|love]]’s a mighty lord, And hath so humbled me as I confess There is no woe to his correction, Nor to his service no such joy on earth. Now, no discourse except it be of [[Love|love]]. Now can I break my fast, dine, sup, and [[Sleep|sleep]] Upon the very naked name of [[Love|love]]. |
