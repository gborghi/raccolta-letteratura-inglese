| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | I will. Exit [[Valentine]] Even as one heat another heat expels, Or as one nail by strength drives out another, So the remembrance of my former [[Love|love]] Is by a newer object quite forgotten. Is it mine eye, or [[Valentine]]’s praise, Her true perfection, or my false transgression That makes me, reasonless, to reason thus? She is fair, and so is [[Julia]] that I [[Love|love]]— That I did [[Love|love]], for now my [[Love|love]] is thawed, Which like a waxen image ‘gainst a [[Fire|fire]] Bears no impression of the thing it was. Methinks my zeal to [[Valentine]] is cold, And that I [[Love|love]] him not as I was wont. O, but I [[Love|love]] his lady too-too much, And that’s the reason I [[Love|love]] him so little. How shall I dote on her with more advice, That thus without advice begin to [[Love|love]] her? ‘Tis but her picture I have yet beheld, And that hath dazzled my reason’s light. But when I look on her perfections There is no reason but I shall be blind. If I can check my erring [[Love|love]] I will, If not, to compass her I’ll use my skill. Exit |
