| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | Enough. I read your fortune in your eye. Was this the idol that you worship so? |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | Even she; and is she not a heavenly saint? |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | No, but she is an earthly paragon. |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | Call her divine. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | I will not flatter her. |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | O flatter me; for [[Love|love]] delights in praises. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | When I was sick you gave me bitter pills, And I must minister the like to you. |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | Then speak the [[Truth|truth]] by her; if not divine, Yet let her be a principality, Sovereign to all the creatures on the earth. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | Except my mistress. |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | Sweet, except not any, Except thou wilt except against my [[Love|love]]. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | Have I not reason to prefer mine own? |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | And I will help thee to prefer her, too. She shall be dignified with this high [[Honour|honour]], To bear my lady’s train, lest the base earth Should from her vesture chance to steal a kiss And, of so great a favour growing proud, Disdain to root the [[Summer|summer]]-swelling flower, And make rough [[Winter|winter]] everlastingly. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | Why, [[Valentine]], what braggartism is this? |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | Pardon me, [[Proteus]], all I can is nothing To her whose worth makes other worthies nothing. She is [[Alone|alone]]. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | Then let her [[Alone|alone]]. |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | Not for the world. Why man, she is mine own, And I as rich in having such a [[Jewel|jewel]] As twenty seas, if all their sand were pearl, The [[Water|water]] nectar, and the rocks pure gold. Forgive me that I do not dream on thee Because thou seest me dote upon my [[Love|love]]. My foolish rival, that her father likes Only for his possessions are so huge, Is gone with her along, and I must after; For [[Love|love]], thou know’st, is full of [[Jealousy|jealousy]]. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | But she loves you? |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | Ay, and we are betrothed. Nay more, our [[Marriage|marriage]] hour, With all the cunning manner of our flight, Determined of: how I must climb her [[Window|window]], The ladder made of cords, and all the means Plotted and ‘[[Greed|greed]] on for my happiness. Good [[Proteus]], go with me to my chamber In these affairs to aid me with thy counsel. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | Go on before. I shall enquire you forth. I must unto the [[Road|road]], to disembark Some necessaries that I needs must use, And then I’ll presently attend you. |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | Will you make haste? |
