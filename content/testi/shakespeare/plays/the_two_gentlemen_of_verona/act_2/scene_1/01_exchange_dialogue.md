| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Valentine]] and [[Speed]] |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | (offering [[Valentine]] a glove) Sir, your glove. |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | Not mine. My gloves are on. |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | Why then, this may be yours, for this is but one. |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | Ha, let me see. Ay, give it me, it’s mine— Sweet ornament, that decks a thing divine. Ah, [[Silvia]], [[Silvia]]! |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | Madam [[Silvia]], Madam [[Silvia]]! |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | How now, sirrah? |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | She is not within hearing, sir. |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | Why, sir, who bade you call her? |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | Your worship, sir, or else I mistook. |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | [[Well]], you’ll still be too forward. |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | And yet I was last chidden for being too slow. |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | Go to, sir. Tell me, do you know Madam [[Silvia]]? |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | She that your worship loves? |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | Why, how know you that I am in [[Love|love]]? |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | Marry, by these special marks: first, you have learned, like Sir [[Proteus]], to wreath your arms, like a malcontent; to relish a [[Love|love]]-[[Song|song]], like a robin redbreast; to walk [[Alone|alone]], like one that had the pestilence; to sigh, like a schoolboy that had lost his ABC; to weep, like a young wench that had buried her grandam; to fast, like one that takes diet; to watch, like one that fears robbing; to speak puling, like a beggar at Hallowmas. You were wont, when you laughed, to crow like a cock; when you walked, to walk like one of the lions. When you fasted, it was presently after dinner; when you looked sadly, it was for want of money. And now you are metamorphosed with a mistress, that when I look on you I can hardly think you my master. |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | Are all these things perceived in me? |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | They are all perceived without ye. |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | Without me? They cannot. |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | Without you? Nay, that’s certain, for without you were so simple, none else would. But you are so without these follies that these follies are within you, and shine through you like the [[Water|water]] in an urinal, that not an eye that sees you but is a physician to comment on your malady. |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | But tell me, dost thou know my lady [[Silvia]]? |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | She that you gaze on so as she sits at supper? |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | Hast thou observed that? Even she I mean. |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | Why sir, I know her not. |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | Dost thou know her by my gazing on her, and yet know’st her not? |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | Is she not hard-favoured, sir? |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | Not so fair, boy, as [[Well|well]] favoured. |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | Sir, I know that [[Well|well]] enough. |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | What dost thou know? |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | That she is not so fair as of you [[Well|well]] favoured. |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | I mean that her [[Beauty|beauty]] is exquisite but her favour infinite. |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | That’s because the one is painted and the other out of all count. |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | How painted? And how out of count? |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | Marry, sir, so painted to make her fair that no man counts of her [[Beauty|beauty]]. |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | How esteem’st thou me? I account of her [[Beauty|beauty]]. |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | You never saw her since she was deformed. |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | How long hath she been deformed? |
| [[Speed|SPEED]] | Ever since you loved her. |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | I have loved her ever since I saw her, and still I see her beautiful. |
