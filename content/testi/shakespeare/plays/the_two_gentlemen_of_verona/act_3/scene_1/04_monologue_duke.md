| DUKE | How shall I fashion me to wear a cloak? I pray thee let me feel thy cloak upon me. He lifts [[Valentine]]’s cloak and finds a letter and a rope-ladder What letter is this same? What’s here? ‘To [[Silvia]]’? And here an engine fit for my proceeding. I’ll be so bold to break the seal for once. (Reads) ‘My thoughts do harbour with my [[Silvia]] nightly, And slaves they are to me, that send them flying. O, could their master come and go as lightly, Himself would lodge where, senseless, they are lying. My herald thoughts in thy pure bosom rest them, While I, their [[King|king]], that thither them importune, Do curse the grace that with such grace hath blessed them, Because myself do want my servants’ fortune. I curse myself for they are sent by me, That they should harbour where their lord should be.’ What’s here? ‘[[Silvia]], this [[Night|night]] I will enfranchise thee’? ‘Tis so, and here’s the ladder for the purpose. Why, Phaeton, for thou art Merops’ son Wilt thou aspire to guide the heavenly car, And with thy daring folly burn the world? Wilt thou reach [[Stars|stars]] because they shine on thee? Go, base intruder, over-weening slave, Bestow thy fawning smiles on equal mates, And think my patience, more than thy [[Desert|desert]], Is privilege for thy departure hence. Thank me for this more than for all the favours Which, all too much, I have bestowed on thee. But if thou linger in my territories Longer than swiftest expedition Will give thee [[Time|time]] to leave our royal [[Court|court]], By heaven, my wrath shall far exceed the [[Love|love]] I ever bore my daughter or thyself. Be gone. I will not hear thy vain excuse, But as thou lov’st thy life, make [[Speed|speed]] from hence. Exit |
