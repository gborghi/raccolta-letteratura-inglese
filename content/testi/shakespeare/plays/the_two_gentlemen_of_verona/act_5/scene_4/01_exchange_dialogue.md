| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Valentine]] |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | How use doth breed a habit in a man! This shadowy [[Desert|desert]], unfrequented woods I better brook than flourishing peopled towns. Here can I sit [[Alone|alone]], unseen of any, And to the [[Nightingale|nightingale]]’s complaining notes Tune my distresses and record my woes. O thou that dost inhabit in my breast, Leave not the mansion so long tenantless Lest, growing ruinous, the building fall And leave no [[Memory|memory]] of what it was. Repair me with thy presence, [[Silvia]]. Thou gentle nymph, cherish thy forlorn swain. What hallooing and what stir is this today? These are my mates, that make their wills their law, Have some unhappy passenger in chase. They [[Love|love]] me [[Well|well]], yet I have much to do To keep them from uncivil outrages. Withdraw thee, [[Valentine]]. Who’s this comes here? He stands aside. Enter [[Proteus]], [[Silvia]], and [[Julia]] dressed as a pageboy |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | Madam, this service I have done for you— Though you respect not aught your servant doth— To hazard life, and rescue you from him That would have forced your [[Honour|honour]] and your [[Love|love]]. Vouchsafe me for my meed but one fair look. A smaller boon than this I cannot beg, And less than this I am sure you cannot give. |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | (aside) How like [[A Dream|a dream]] is this I see and hear! [[Love]] lend me patience to forbear awhile. |
| [[Silvia|SILVIA]] | O miserable, unhappy that I am! |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | Unhappy were you, madam, ere I came. But by my coming I have made you happy. |
| [[Silvia|SILVIA]] | By thy approach thou mak’st me most unhappy. |
| [[Julia|JULIA]] | (aside) And me, when he approacheth to your presence. |
| [[Silvia|SILVIA]] | Had I been seized by a hungry [[Lion|lion]] I would have been a breakfast to the beast Rather than have false [[Proteus]] rescue me. O heaven be judge how I [[Love|love]] [[Valentine]], Whose life’s as tender to me as my [[Soul|soul]]. And full as much, for more there cannot be, I do detest false perjured [[Proteus]]. Therefore be gone, solicit me no more. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | What dangerous action, stood it next to [[Death|death]], Would I not undergo for one calm look! O, ’tis the curse in [[Love|love]], and still approved, When women cannot [[Love|love]] where they’re beloved. |
| [[Silvia|SILVIA]] | When [[Proteus]] cannot [[Love|love]] where he’s beloved. Read over [[Julia]]’s heart, thy first, best [[Love|love]], For whose dear sake thou didst then rend thy [[Faith|faith]] Into a thousand oaths, and all those oaths Descended into perjury to [[Love|love]] me. Thou hast no [[Faith|faith]] left now, unless thou’dst two, And that’s far worse than none. Better have none Than plural [[Faith|faith]], which is too much by one, Thou counterfeit to thy true friend. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | In [[Love|love]] Who respects friend? |
| [[Silvia|SILVIA]] | All men but [[Proteus]]. |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | Nay, if the gentle spirit of moving words Can no way change you to a milder form I’ll woo you like a soldier, at arm’s end, And [[Love|love]] you ’gainst the [[Nature|nature]] of [[Love|love]]: force ye. |
| [[Silvia|SILVIA]] | O heaven! |
| [[Proteus|PROTEUS]] | (assailing her) I’ll force thee yield to my [[Desire|desire]]. |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | (coming forward) Ruffian, let go that rude uncivil touch, Thou friend of an ill fashion. |
