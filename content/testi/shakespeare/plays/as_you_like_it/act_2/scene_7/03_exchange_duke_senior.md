| [[Duke Senior|DUKE SENIOR]] | What [[Fool|fool]] is this? 35 |
| [[Jaques|JAQUES]] | O worthy [[Fool|fool]]!—One that hath been a courtier, And says ‘If ladies be but young and fair They have the gift to know it.’ And in his brain, Which is as dry as the remainder biscuit After a voyage, he hath strange places crammed With observation, the which he vents In mangled forms. O that I were a [[Fool|fool]], I am ambitious for a motley coat. |
| [[Duke Senior|DUKE SENIOR]] | Thou shalt have one. |
| [[Jaques|JAQUES]] | It is my only suit, Provided that you weed your better judgements Of all opinion that grows rank in them That I am wise. I must have liberty Withal, as large a charter as the [[Wind|wind]], To blow on whom I please, for so fools have; And they that are most galled with my folly, They most must laugh. And why, sir, must they so? The why is plain as way to parish church: He that a [[Fool|fool]] doth very wisely hit Doth very foolishly, although he smart, Seem aught but senseless of the bob. If not, The wise man’s folly is anatomized Even by the squandering glances of the [[Fool|fool]]. Invest me in my motley. Give me leave To speak my mind, and I will through and through Cleanse the foul [[Body|body]] of th’infected world, If they will patiently receive my medicine. |
| [[Duke Senior|DUKE SENIOR]] | Fie on thee, I can tell what thou wouldst do. |
| [[Jaques|JAQUES]] | What, for a counter, would I do but good? |
| [[Duke Senior|DUKE SENIOR]] | Most mischievous foul sin, in chiding sin; For thou thyself hast been a libertine, As sensual as the brutish sting itself, And all th’embossèd sores and headed evils That thou with licence of free foot hast caught Wouldst thou disgorge into the general world. |
| [[Jaques|JAQUES]] | Why, who cries out on [[Pride|pride]] That can therein tax any private party? Doth it not flow as hugely as the sea, Till that the weary very means do ebb? What woman in the [[City|city]] do I name When that I say the [[City|city]]-woman bears The cost of princes on unworthy shoulders? Who can come in and say that I mean her When such a one as she, such is her neighbour? Or what is he of basest function, That says his bravery is not on my cost, Thinking that I mean him, but therein suits His folly to the mettle of my speech? There then, how then, what then, let me see wherein My tongue hath wronged him. If it do him right, Then he hath wronged himself. If he be free, Why then my taxing like a wild goose flies, Unclaimed of any man. But who comes here? Enter [[Orlando]], with [[Sword|sword]] drawn |
| [[Orlando|ORLANDO]] | Forbear, and eat no more! |
| [[Jaques|JAQUES]] | Why, I have eat none yet. |
| [[Orlando|ORLANDO]] | Nor shalt not till necessity be served. |
| [[Jaques|JAQUES]] | Of what kind should this cock come of? |
| [[Duke Senior|DUKE SENIOR]] | Art thou thus boldened, man, by thy distress? Or else a rude despiser of good manners, That in civility thou seem’st so empty? |
| [[Orlando|ORLANDO]] | You touched my vein at first. The thorny point Of bare distress hath ta’en from me the show Of smooth civility. Yet am I inland bred, And know some nurture. But forbear, I say. He dies that touches any of this fruit Till I and my affairs are answered. |
