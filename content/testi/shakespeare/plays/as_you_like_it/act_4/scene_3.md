| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Rosalind]] as Ganymede and [[Celia]] as Aliena |
| [[Rosalind|ROSALIND]] | How say you now? Is it not past two o’[[Clock|clock]]? And here much [[Orlando]]. |
| [[Celia|CELIA]] | I warrant you, with pure [[Love|love]] and troubled brain he hath ta’en his bow and arrows and is gone forth to [[Sleep|sleep]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Silvius]] |
| [[Celia|CELIA]] | Look who comes here. |
| [[Silvius|SILVIUS]] | (to [[Rosalind]])<br>My errand is to you, fair youth.<br>My gentle [[Phoebe]] did bid me give you this.<br>He offers [[Rosalind]] a letter, which she takes and reads<br>I know not the contents, but as I guess<br>By the stern brow and waspish action<br>Which she did use as she was writing of it,<br>It bears an angry tenor. Pardon me;<br>I am but as a guiltless messenger. |
| [[Rosalind|ROSALIND]] | Patience herself would startle at this letter,<br>And play the swaggerer. Bear this, bear all.<br>She says I am not fair, that I lack manners;<br>She calls me proud, and that she could not [[Love|love]] me<br>Were man as rare as Phoenix. ‘Od’s my will,<br>Her [[Love|love]] is not the hare that I do hunt.<br>Why writes she so to me? [[Well]], shepherd, [[Well|well]],<br>This is a letter of your own device. |
| [[Silvius|SILVIUS]] | No, I protest; I know not the contents.<br>[[Phoebe]] did write it. |
| [[Rosalind|ROSALIND]] | Come, come, you are a [[Fool|fool]],<br>And turned into the extremity of [[Love|love]].<br>I saw her hand. She has a leathern hand,<br>A free-stone coloured hand. I verily did think<br>That her old gloves were on; but ’twas her hands.<br>She has a housewife’s hand—but that’s no matter.<br>I say she never did invent this letter.<br>This is a man’s invention, and his hand. |
| [[Silvius|SILVIUS]] | Sure, it is hers. |
| [[Rosalind|ROSALIND]] | Why, ’tis a boisterous and a cruel style,<br>A style for challengers. Why, she defies me,<br>Like Turk to Christian. Women’s gentle brain<br>Could not drop forth such giant-rude invention,<br>Such Ethiop words, blacker in their effect<br>Than in their countenance. Will you hear the letter? |
| [[Silvius|SILVIUS]] | So please you, for I never heard it yet,<br>Yet heard too much of [[Phoebe]]’s cruelty. |
| [[Rosalind|ROSALIND]] | She Phoebes me. Mark how the [[Tyrant|tyrant]] writes:<br>(reads) ‘Art thou god to shepherd turned,<br>That a maiden’s heart hath burned?’<br>Can a woman rail thus? |
| [[Silvius|SILVIUS]] | Call you this railing? |
| [[Rosalind|ROSALIND]] | (reads)<br>‘Why, thy godhead laid apart,<br>Warr’st thou with a woman’s heart?’<br>Did you ever hear such railing?<br>‘Whiles the eye of man did woo me<br>That could do no vengeance to me.’—<br>Meaning me a beast.<br>‘If the scorn of your bright eyne<br>Have power to raise such [[Love|love]] in mine,<br>Alack, in me what strange effect<br>Would they work in mild aspect?<br>Whiles you chid me I did [[Love|love]];<br>How then might your prayers move?<br>He that brings this [[Love|love]] to thee<br>Little knows this [[Love|love]] in me,<br>And by him seal up thy mind<br>Whether that thy youth and kind<br>Will the faithful offer take<br>Of me, and all that I can make,<br>Or else by him my [[Love|love]] deny,<br>And then I’ll study how to die.’ |
| [[Silvius|SILVIUS]] | Call you this chiding? |
| [[Celia|CELIA]] | Alas, poor shepherd. |
| [[Rosalind|ROSALIND]] | Do you pity him? No, he deserves no pity. (To [[Silvius]]) Wilt thou [[Love|love]] such a woman? What, to make thee an instrument, and play false strains upon thee?—not to be endured. [[Well]], go your way to her—for I see [[Love|love]] hath made thee a tame snake—and say this to her: that if she [[Love|love]] me, I charge her to [[Love|love]] thee. If she will not, I will never have her unless thou entreat for her. If you be a true [[Lover|lover]], hence, and not a word; for here comes more company. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit [[Silvius]] |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Oliver]] |
| [[Oliver|OLIVER]] | Good morrow, fair ones. Pray you, if you know,<br>Where in the purlieus of this forest stands<br>A sheepcote fenced about with olive trees? |
| [[Celia|CELIA]] | West of this place, down in the neighbour [[Bottom|bottom]].<br>The rank of osiers by the murmuring stream<br>Left on your right hand brings you to the place.<br>But at this hour the [[house]] doth keep itself.<br>There’s none within. |
| [[Oliver|OLIVER]] | If that an eye may profit by a tongue,<br>Then should I know you by description.<br>Such garments, and such years. ‘The boy is fair,<br>Of female favour, and bestows himself<br>Like a ripe sister. The woman low<br>And browner than her brother.’ Are not you<br>The owner of the [[house]] I did enquire for? |
| [[Celia|CELIA]] | It is no boast, being asked, to say we are. |
| [[Oliver|OLIVER]] | [[Orlando]] doth commend him to you both,<br>And to that youth he calls his [[Rosalind]]<br>He sends this bloody napkin. Are you he? |
| [[Rosalind|ROSALIND]] | I am. What must we understand by this? |
| [[Oliver|OLIVER]] | Some of my shame, if you will know of me<br>What man I am, and how, and why, and where<br>This handkerchief was stained. |
| [[Celia|CELIA]] | I pray you tell it. |
| [[Oliver|OLIVER]] | When last the young [[Orlando]] parted from you,<br>He left a promise to return again |
| *(didascalia)* | Within an hour, and pacing through the forest, |
| [[Oliver|OLIVER]] | Chewing the food of sweet and bitter fancy,<br>Lo what befell. He threw his eye aside,<br>And mark what object did present itself.<br>Under an old oak, whose boughs were mossed with age<br>And high top bald with dry antiquity,<br>A wretched, ragged man, o‘ergrown with hair,<br>Lay sleeping on his back. About his neck<br>A green and gilded snake had wreathed itself,<br>Who with her head, nimble in threats, approached<br>The opening of his mouth. But suddenly<br>Seeing [[Orlando]], it unlinked itself,<br>And with indented glides did slip away<br>Into a bush, under which bush’s shade<br>A lioness, with udders all drawn dry,<br>Lay couching, head on ground, with catlike watch<br>When that the sleeping man should stir. For ’tis<br>The royal disposition of that beast<br>To prey on nothing that doth seem as dead.<br>This seen, [[Orlando]] did approach the man<br>And found it was his brother, his elder brother. |
| [[Celia|CELIA]] | O, I have heard him speak of that same brother,<br>And he did render him the most unnatural<br>That lived amongst men. |
| [[Oliver|OLIVER]] | And [[Well|well]] he might so do,<br>For [[Well|well]] I know he was unnatural. |
| [[Rosalind|ROSALIND]] | But to [[Orlando]]. Did he leave him there,<br>Food to the sucked and hungry lioness? |
| [[Oliver|OLIVER]] | Twice did he turn his back, and purposed so.<br>But kindness, nobler ever than [[Revenge|revenge]],<br>And [[Nature|nature]], stronger than his just occasion,<br>Made him give battle to the lioness,<br>Who quickly fell before him; in which hurtling<br>From miserable slumber I awaked. |
| [[Celia|CELIA]] | Are you his brother? |
| [[Rosalind|ROSALIND]] | Was’t you he rescued? |
| [[Celia|CELIA]] | Was’t you that did so oft contrive to kill him? |
| [[Oliver|OLIVER]] | ‘Twas I, but ’tis not I. I do not shame<br>To tell you what I was, since my [[conversion]]<br>So sweetly tastes, being the thing I am. |
| [[Rosalind|ROSALIND]] | But for the bloody napkin? |
| [[Oliver|OLIVER]] | By and by.<br>When from the first to last betwixt us two<br>[[Tears]] our recountments had most kindly bathed—<br>As how I came into that [[Desert|desert]] place—<br>I’ brief, he led me to the gentle Duke,<br>Who gave me fresh array, and entertainment,<br>Committing me unto my brother’s [[Love|love]],<br>Who led me instantly unto his [[Cave|cave]],<br>There stripped himself, and here upon his arm<br>The lioness had torn some flesh away,<br>Which all this while had bled. And now he fainted,<br>And cried in fainting upon [[Rosalind]].<br>Brief, I recovered him, bound up his wound,<br>And after some small space, being strong at heart,<br>He sent me hither, stranger as I am,<br>To tell this story, that you might excuse<br>His broken promise, and to give this napkin,<br>Dyed in his [[Blood|blood]], unto the shepherd youth<br>That he in sport doth call his [[Rosalind]].<br>[[Rosalind]] faints |
| [[Celia|CELIA]] | Why, how now, Ganymede, sweet Ganymede! |
| [[Oliver|OLIVER]] | Many will swoon when they do look on [[Blood|blood]]. |
| [[Celia|CELIA]] | There is more in it. Cousin Ganymede! |
| [[Oliver|OLIVER]] | Look, he recovers. |
| [[Rosalind|ROSALIND]] | I would I were at [[Home|home]]. |
| [[Celia|CELIA]] | We’ll lead you thither.<br>(To [[Oliver]]) I pray you, will you take him by the arm? |
| [[Oliver|OLIVER]] | Be of good cheer, youth. You a man? You lack a man’s heart. |
| [[Rosalind|ROSALIND]] | I do so, I confess it. Ah, sirrah, a [[Body|body]] would think this was [[Well|well]] counterfeited. I pray you, tell your brother how [[Well|well]] I counterfeited. Heigh-ho! |
| [[Oliver|OLIVER]] | This was not counterfeit. There is too great testimony in your complexion that it was a passion of earnest. |
| [[Rosalind|ROSALIND]] | Counterfeit, I assure you. |
| [[Oliver|OLIVER]] | [[Well]] then, take a good heart, and counterfeit to be a man. |
| [[Rosalind|ROSALIND]] | So I do; but, i’[[Faith|faith]], I should have been a woman by right. |
| [[Celia|CELIA]] | Come, you look paler and paler. Pray you, draw homewards. Good sir, go with us. |
| [[Oliver|OLIVER]] | That will I, for I must bear answer back<br>How you excuse my brother, [[Rosalind]]. |
| [[Rosalind|ROSALIND]] | I shall devise something. But I pray you commend my counterfeiting to him. Will you go? |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt |
