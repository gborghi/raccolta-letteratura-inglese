| [[Oliver|OLIVER]] | Good morrow, fair ones. Pray you, if you know, Where in the purlieus of this forest stands A sheepcote fenced about with olive trees? |
| [[Celia|CELIA]] | West of this place, down in the neighbour [[Bottom|bottom]]. The rank of osiers by the murmuring stream Left on your right hand brings you to the place. But at this hour the [[house]] doth keep itself. There’s none within. |
| [[Oliver|OLIVER]] | If that an eye may profit by a tongue, Then should I know you by description. Such garments, and such years. ‘The boy is fair, Of female favour, and bestows himself Like a ripe sister. The woman low And browner than her brother.’ Are not you The owner of the [[house]] I did enquire for? |
| [[Celia|CELIA]] | It is no boast, being asked, to say we are. |
| [[Oliver|OLIVER]] | [[Orlando]] doth commend him to you both, And to that youth he calls his [[Rosalind]] He sends this bloody napkin. Are you he? |
| [[Rosalind|ROSALIND]] | I am. What must we understand by this? |
| [[Oliver|OLIVER]] | Some of my shame, if you will know of me What man I am, and how, and why, and where This handkerchief was stained. |
| [[Celia|CELIA]] | I pray you tell it. |
