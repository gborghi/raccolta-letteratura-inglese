| [[Celia|CELIA]] | O, I have heard him speak of that same brother, And he did render him the most unnatural That lived amongst men. |
| [[Oliver|OLIVER]] | And [[Well|well]] he might so do, For [[Well|well]] I know he was unnatural. |
| [[Rosalind|ROSALIND]] | But to [[Orlando]]. Did he leave him there, Food to the sucked and hungry lioness? |
| [[Oliver|OLIVER]] | Twice did he turn his back, and purposed so. But kindness, nobler ever than [[Revenge|revenge]], And [[Nature|nature]], stronger than his just occasion, Made him give battle to the lioness, Who quickly fell before him; in which hurtling From miserable slumber I awaked. |
| [[Celia|CELIA]] | Are you his brother? |
| [[Rosalind|ROSALIND]] | Was’t you he rescued? |
| [[Celia|CELIA]] | Was’t you that did so oft contrive to kill him? |
| [[Oliver|OLIVER]] | ‘Twas I, but ’tis not I. I do not shame To tell you what I was, since my [[conversion]] So sweetly tastes, being the thing I am. |
| [[Rosalind|ROSALIND]] | But for the bloody napkin? |
| [[Oliver|OLIVER]] | By and by. When from the first to last betwixt us two [[Tears]] our recountments had most kindly bathed— As how I came into that [[Desert|desert]] place— |
| [[Oliver|OLIVER]] | I’ brief, he led me to the gentle Duke, Who gave me fresh array, and entertainment, Committing me unto my brother’s [[Love|love]], Who led me instantly unto his [[Cave|cave]], There stripped himself, and here upon his arm The lioness had torn some flesh away, Which all this while had bled. And now he fainted, And cried in fainting upon [[Rosalind]]. Brief, I recovered him, bound up his wound, And after some small space, being strong at heart, He sent me hither, stranger as I am, To tell this story, that you might excuse His broken promise, and to give this napkin, Dyed in his [[Blood|blood]], unto the shepherd youth That he in sport doth call his [[Rosalind]]. [[Rosalind]] faints |
| [[Celia|CELIA]] | Why, how now, Ganymede, sweet Ganymede! |
| [[Oliver|OLIVER]] | Many will swoon when they do look on [[Blood|blood]]. |
| [[Celia|CELIA]] | There is more in it. Cousin Ganymede! |
| [[Oliver|OLIVER]] | Look, he recovers. |
| [[Rosalind|ROSALIND]] | I would I were at [[Home|home]]. |
| [[Celia|CELIA]] | We’ll lead you thither. (To [[Oliver]]) I pray you, will you take him by the arm? |
| [[Oliver|OLIVER]] | Be of good cheer, youth. You a man? You lack a man’s heart. |
| [[Rosalind|ROSALIND]] | I do so, I confess it. Ah, sirrah, a [[Body|body]] would think this was [[Well|well]] counterfeited. I pray you, tell your brother how [[Well|well]] I counterfeited. Heigh-ho! |
| [[Oliver|OLIVER]] | This was not counterfeit. There is too great testimony in your complexion that it was a passion of earnest. |
| [[Rosalind|ROSALIND]] | Counterfeit, I assure you. |
| [[Oliver|OLIVER]] | [[Well]] then, take a good heart, and counterfeit to be a man. |
| [[Rosalind|ROSALIND]] | So I do; but, i’[[Faith|faith]], I should have been a woman by right. |
| [[Celia|CELIA]] | Come, you look paler and paler. Pray you, draw homewards. Good sir, go with us. |
| [[Oliver|OLIVER]] | That will I, for I must bear answer back How you excuse my brother, [[Rosalind]]. |
| [[Rosalind|ROSALIND]] | I shall devise something. But I pray you commend my counterfeiting to him. Will you go? Exeunt |
