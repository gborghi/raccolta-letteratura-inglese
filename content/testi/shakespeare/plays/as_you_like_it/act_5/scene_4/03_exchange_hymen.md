| HYMEN | Peace, ho, I bar confusion. ‘Tis I must make conclusion Of these most strange events. Here’s eight that must take hands To join in Hymen’s bands, If [[Truth|truth]] holds true contents. (To [[Orlando]] and [[Rosalind]]) You and you no [[Cross|cross]] shall part. (To [[Oliver]] and [[Celia]]) You and you are heart in heart. (To [[Phoebe]]) You to his [[Love|love]] must accord, Or have a woman to your lord. (To [[Touchstone]] and Audrey) You and you are sure together As the [[Winter|winter]] to foul weather.— Whiles a wedlock [[Hymn|hymn]] we sing, Feed yourselves with questioning, That reason [[Wonder|wonder]] may diminish How thus we met, and these things finish. [[Song]] Wedding is great Juno’s [[Crown|crown]], O blessèd bond of board and bed. ‘Tis Hymen peoples every town. High wedlock then be honoured. [[Honour]], high [[Honour|honour]] and renown To Hymen, god of every town. |
| [[Duke Senior|DUKE SENIOR]] | (to [[Celia]]) O my dear niece, welcome thou art to me, Even daughter; welcome in no less degree. |
| [[Phoebe|PHOEBE]] | (to [[Silvius]].) I will not eat my word. Now thou art mine, Thy [[Faith|faith]] my fancy to thee doth combine. Enter [[Jaques]] de Bois, the second brother |
| [[Jaques|JAQUES]] DE BOIS | Let me have audience for a word or two. I am the second son of old Sir Rowland, That bring these tidings to this fair assembly. [[Duke Frederick]], hearing how that every day Men of great worth resorted to this forest, Addressed a mighty power, which were on foot, In his own conduct, purposely to take His brother here, and put him to the [[Sword|sword]]. And to the skirts of this wild wood he came Where, meeting with an old religious man, After some question with him was converted Both from his enterprise and from the world, His [[Crown|crown]] bequeathing to his banished brother, And all their lands restored to them again That were with him exiled. This to be true I do engage my life. |
| [[Duke Senior|DUKE SENIOR]] | Welcome, young man. Thou offer’st fairly to thy brothers’ wedding: To one his lands withheld, and to the other A land itself at large, a potent dukedom. First, in this forest let us do those ends That here were [[Well|well]] begun, and [[Well|well]] begot. And after, every of this happy number That have endured shrewd days and nights with us Shall share the good of our returned fortune According to the measure of their states. Meantime, forget this new-fallen dignity And fall into our rustic revelry. Play, music, and you brides and bridegrooms all, With measure heaped in joy to th’ measures fall. |
| [[Jaques|JAQUES]] | Sir, by your patience. (To [[Jaques]] de Bois) If I heard you rightly The Duke hath put on a religious life And thrown into neglect the pompous [[Court|court]]. |
| [[Jaques|JAQUES]] DE BOIS | He hath. |
| [[Jaques|JAQUES]] | To him will I. Out of these convertites There is much matter to be heard and learned. (To the Duke) You to your former [[Honour|honour]] I bequeath; Your patience and your virtue [[Well|well]] deserves it. (To [[Orlando]]) You to a [[Love|love]] that your true [[Faith|faith]] doth merit; (To [[Oliver]]) |
| [[Jaques|JAQUES]] | You to your land, and [[Love|love]], and great allies; (To [[Silvius]]) |
| [[Jaques|JAQUES]] | You to a long and [[Well|well]]-deserved bed; (To [[Touchstone]]) |
| [[Jaques|JAQUES]] | And you to wrangling, for thy loving voyage Is but for two months victualled.—So, to your pleasures; I am for other than for dancing measures. |
