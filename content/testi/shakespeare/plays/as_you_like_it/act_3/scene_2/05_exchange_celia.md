| [[Celia|CELIA]] | You must borrow me Gargantua’s mouth first, ’tis a word too great for any mouth of this age’s size. To say ay and no to these particulars is more than to answer in a catechism. |
| [[Rosalind|ROSALIND]] | But doth he know that I am in this forest, and in man’s apparel? Looks he as freshly as he did the day he wrestled? |
| [[Celia|CELIA]] | It is as easy to count atomies as to resolve the propositions of a [[Lover|lover]]; but take a taste of my finding him, and relish it with good observance. I found him under a tree, like a dropped acorn— |
| [[Rosalind|ROSALIND]] | It may [[Well|well]] be called Jove’s tree when it drops forth such fruit. |
| [[Celia|CELIA]] | Give me audience, good madam. [[Rosalind|ROSALIND]] Proceed. |
| [[Celia|CELIA]] | There lay he, stretched along like a wounded knight— |
| [[Rosalind|ROSALIND]] | Though it be pity to see such a sight, it [[Well|well]] becomes the ground. |
| [[Celia|CELIA]] | Cry ‘holla’ to thy tongue, I prithee: it curvets unseasonably.—He was furnished like a hunter— |
| [[Rosalind|ROSALIND]] | O ominous—he comes to kill my heart. |
| [[Celia|CELIA]] | I would sing my [[Song|song]] without a burden; thou bringest me out of tune. |
| [[Rosalind|ROSALIND]] | Do you not know I am a woman? When I think, I must speak.—Sweet, say on. Enter [[Orlando]] and [[Jaques]] |
| [[Celia|CELIA]] | You bring me out. Soft, comes he not here? |
| [[Rosalind|ROSALIND]] | ’Tis he. Slink by, and note him. [[Rosalind]] and [[Celia]] stand aside |
| [[Jaques|JAQUES]] | (to [[Orlando]]) I thank you for your company, but, good [[Faith|faith]], I had as lief have been myself [[Alone|alone]]. |
| [[Orlando|ORLANDO]] | And so had I. But yet for fashion’ sake, I thank you too for your society. |
| [[Jaques|JAQUES]] | God b’wi’you; let’s meet as little as we can. |
| [[Orlando|ORLANDO]] | I do [[Desire|desire]] we may be better strangers. |
| [[Jaques|JAQUES]] | I pray you mar no more trees with writing [[Love|love]]-[[songs]] in their barks. |
| [[Orlando|ORLANDO]] | I pray you mar no more of my verses with reading them ill-favouredly. |
| [[Jaques|JAQUES]] | [[Rosalind]] is your [[Love|love]]’s name? |
| [[Orlando|ORLANDO]] | Yes, just. |
| [[Jaques|JAQUES]] | I do not like her name. |
| [[Orlando|ORLANDO]] | There was no thought of pleasing you when she was christened. |
| [[Jaques|JAQUES]] | What stature is she of? |
| [[Orlando|ORLANDO]] | Just as high as my heart. |
| [[Jaques|JAQUES]] | You are full of pretty answers. Have you not been acquainted with goldsmiths’ wives, and conned them out of rings? |
| [[Orlando|ORLANDO]] | Not so; but I answer you right painted cloth, from whence you have studied your questions. |
| [[Jaques|JAQUES]] | You have a nimble wit; I think ’twas made of Atalanta’s heels. Will you sit down with me, and we two will rail against our mistress the world, and all our misery? |
| [[Orlando|ORLANDO]] | I will chide no breather in the world but myself, against whom I know most faults. |
| [[Jaques|JAQUES]] | The worst fault you have is to be in [[Love|love]]. |
| [[Orlando|ORLANDO]] | ’Tis a fault I will not change for your best virtue. I am weary of you. |
| [[Jaques|JAQUES]] | By my troth, I was seeking for a [[Fool|fool]] when I found you. |
| [[Orlando|ORLANDO]] | He is drowned in the brook. Look but in, and you shall see him. |
| [[Jaques|JAQUES]] | There I shall see mine own figure. [[Orlando|ORLANDO]] Which I take to be either a [[Fool|fool]] or a cipher. |
| [[Jaques|JAQUES]] | I’ll tarry no longer with you. Farewell, good Signor [[Love]]. |
| [[Orlando|ORLANDO]] | I am glad of your departure. Adieu, good Monsieur [[Melancholy]]. Exit [[Jaques]] |
