| [[Orlando|ORLANDO]] | I am he that is so [[Love|love]]-shaked. I pray you, tell me your remedy. |
| [[Rosalind|ROSALIND]] | There is none of my uncle’s marks upon you. He taught me how to know a man in [[Love|love]], in which cage of rushes I am sure you are not prisoner. |
| [[Orlando|ORLANDO]] | What were his marks? |
| [[Rosalind|ROSALIND]] | A lean cheek, which you have not; a blue eye and sunken, which you have not; an unquestionable spirit, which you have not; a beard neglected, which you have not—but I pardon you for that, for simply your having in beard is a younger brother’s revenue. Then your hose should be ungartered, your bonnet unbanded, your sleeve unbuttoned, your shoe untied, and everything about you demonstrating a careless desolation. But you are no such man. You are rather point-device in your accoutrements, as loving yourself than seeming the [[Lover|lover]] of any other. |
| [[Orlando|ORLANDO]] | Fair youth, I would I could make thee believe I [[Love|love]]. |
| [[Rosalind|ROSALIND]] | Me believe it? You may as soon make her that you [[Love|love]] believe it, which I warrant she is apter to do than to confess she does. That is one of the points in the which women still give the lie to their consciences. But in good sooth, are you he that hangs the verses on the trees wherein [[Rosalind]] is so admired? |
| [[Orlando|ORLANDO]] | I swear to thee, youth, by the white hand of [[Rosalind]], I am that he, that unfortunate he. |
| [[Rosalind|ROSALIND]] | But are you so much in [[Love|love]] as your rhymes speak? |
| [[Orlando|ORLANDO]] | Neither rhyme nor reason can express how much. |
| [[Rosalind|ROSALIND]] | [[Love]] is merely a [[Madness|madness]], and I tell you, deserves as [[Well|well]] a dark [[house]] and a whip as madmen do; and the reason why they are not so punished and cured is that the lunacy is so ordinary that the whippers are in [[Love|love]] too. Yet I profess curing it by counsel. |
| [[Orlando|ORLANDO]] | Did you ever cure any so? |
| [[Rosalind|ROSALIND]] | Yes, one; and in this manner. He was to imagine me his [[Love|love]], his mistress; and I set him every day to woo me. At which [[Time|time]] would I, being but a moonish youth, grieve, be effeminate, changeable, [[Longing|longing]] and liking, proud, fantastical, apish, shallow, inconstant, full of [[Tears|tears]], full of smiles; for every passion something, and for no passion truly anything, as boys and women are for the most part cattle of this colour—would now like him, now loathe him; then entertain him, then forswear him; now weep for him, then spit at him, that I drave my suitor from his mad [[Humour|humour]] of [[Love|love]] to a living [[Humour|humour]] of [[Madness|madness]], which was to forswear the full stream of the world and to live in a nook merely monastic. And thus I cured him, and this way will I take upon me to wash your liver as clean as a sound sheep’s heart, that there shall not be one spot of [[Love|love]] in’t. |
| [[Orlando|ORLANDO]] | I would not be cured, youth. |
| [[Rosalind|ROSALIND]] | I would cure you if you would but call me [[Rosalind]] and come every day to my cot, and woo me. |
| [[Orlando|ORLANDO]] | Now by the [[Faith|faith]] of my [[Love|love]], I will. Tell me where it is. |
| [[Rosalind|ROSALIND]] | Go with me to it, and I’ll show it you. And by the way you shall tell me where in the forest you live. Will you go? |
