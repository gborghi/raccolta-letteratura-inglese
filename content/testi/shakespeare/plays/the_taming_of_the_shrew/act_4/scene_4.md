| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Tranio]] as [[Lucentio]], and the Pedant dressed like Vincentio, booted and bare-headed |
| [[Tranio|TRANIO]] | Sir, this is the [[house]]. Please it you that I call? |
| PEDANT | Ay, what else. And but I be deceived,<br>Signor [[Baptista]] may remember me<br>Near twenty years ago in Genoa— |
| [[Tranio|TRANIO]] | Where we were lodgers at the Pegasus.—<br>Tis [[Well|well]], and hold your own in any case<br>With such austerity as ‘longeth to a father. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Biondello |
| PEDANT | I warrant you. But sir, here comes your boy.<br>’Twere good he were schooled. |
| [[Tranio|TRANIO]] | [[Fear]] you not him. Sirrah Biondello,<br>Now do your duty throughly, I advise you.<br>Imagine ’twere the right Vincentio. |
| BIONDELLO | Tut, [[Fear|fear]] not me. |
| [[Tranio|TRANIO]] | But hast thou done thy errand to [[Baptista]]? |
| BIONDELLO | I told him that your father was at [[Venice]]<br>And that you looked for him this day in Padua. |
| [[Tranio|TRANIO]] | (giving money)<br>Thou’rt a tall fellow. Hold thee that to drink.<br>Here comes [[Baptista]]. Set your countenance, sir. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Baptista]], and [[Lucentio]] as Cambio |
| [[Tranio|TRANIO]] | Signor [[Baptista]], you are happily met.<br>(To the Pedant) Sir, this is the gentleman I told you of.<br>I pray you stand good father to me now.<br>Give me [[Bianca]] for my patrimony. |
| PEDANT | Soft, son. (To [[Baptista]]) Sir, by your leave, having<br>come to Padua<br>To gather in some debts, my son [[Lucentio]]<br>Made me acquainted with a weighty cause<br>Of [[Love|love]] between your daughter and himself,<br>And for the good report I hear of you,<br>And for the [[Love|love]] he beareth to your daughter,<br>And she to him, to stay him not too long<br>I am content in a good father’s care<br>To have him matched, and if you please to like<br>No worse than I, upon some agreement<br>Me shall you find ready and willing<br>With one consent to have her so bestowed,<br>For curious I cannot be with you,<br>Signor [[Baptista]], of whom I hear so [[Well|well]]. |
| [[Baptista|BAPTISTA]] | Sir, pardon me in what I have to say.<br>Your plainness and your shortness please me [[Well|well]].<br>Right true it is your son [[Lucentio]] here<br>Doth [[Love|love]] my daughter, and she loveth him,<br>Or both dissemble deeply their affections.<br>And therefore if you say no more than this,<br>That like a father you will deal with him<br>And pass my daughter a sufficient dower,<br>The match is made, and all is done.<br>Your son shall have my daughter with consent. |
| [[Tranio|TRANIO]] | I thank you, sir. Where then do you know best<br>We be affied, and such assurance ta’en<br>As shall with either part’s agreement stand? |
| [[Baptista|BAPTISTA]] | Not in my [[house]], [[Lucentio]], for you know<br>Pitchers have ears, and I have many servants.<br>Besides, old Gremio is heark’ning still,<br>And happily we might be interrupted. |
| [[Tranio|TRANIO]] | Then at my lodging, an it like you.<br>There doth my father lie, and there this [[Night|night]]<br>We’ll pass the business privately and [[Well|well]].<br>Send for your daughter by your servant here.<br>My boy shall fetch the scrivener presently.<br>The worst is this, that at so slender warning<br>You are like to have a thin and slender pittance. |
| [[Baptista|BAPTISTA]] | It likes me [[Well|well]]. Cambio, hie you [[Home|home]]<br>And bid [[Bianca]] make her ready straight,<br>And if you will, tell what hath happened—<br>[[Lucentio]]’s father is arrived in Padua—<br>And how she’s like to be [[Lucentio]]’s wife.<br>ExitLucentio |
| BIONDELLO | I pray the gods she may with all my heart. |
| [[Tranio|TRANIO]] | Dally not with the gods, but get thee gone. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit Biondello |
| [[Tranio|TRANIO]] | Signor [[Baptista]], shall I lead the way?<br>Welcome. One mess is like to be your cheer.<br>Come, sir, we will better it in Pisa. |
| [[Baptista|BAPTISTA]] | I follow you. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt |
