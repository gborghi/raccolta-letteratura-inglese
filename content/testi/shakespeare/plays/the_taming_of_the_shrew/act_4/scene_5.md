| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Lucentio]] and Biondello |
| BIONDELLO | Cambio. |
| [[Lucentio|LUCENTIO]] | What sayst thou, Biondello? |
| BIONDELLO | You saw my master wink and laugh upon you? |
| [[Lucentio|LUCENTIO]] | Biondello, what of that? |
| BIONDELLO | [[Faith]], nothing, but he’s left me here behind to expound the meaning or moral of his signs and tokens. |
| [[Lucentio|LUCENTIO]] | I pray thee, moralize them. |
| BIONDELLO | Then thus: [[Baptista]] is safe, talking with the deceiving father of a deceitful son. |
| [[Lucentio|LUCENTIO]] | And what of him? |
| BIONDELLO | His daughter is to be brought by you to the supper. |
| [[Lucentio|LUCENTIO]] | And then? |
| BIONDELLO | The old priest at Saint Luke’s church is at your command at all hours. |
| [[Lucentio|LUCENTIO]] | And what of all this? |
| BIONDELLO | I cannot tell, except they are busied about a counterfeit assurance. Take you assurance of her cum privilegio ad imprimendum solum—to th’ church take the priest, clerk, and some sufficient honest witnesses. If this be not that you look for, I have no more to say, But bid [[Bianca]] farewell for ever and a day. |
| [[Lucentio|LUCENTIO]] | Hear’st thou, Biondello? |
| BIONDELLO | I cannot tarry, I knew a wench married in an afternoon as she went to the [[Garden|garden]] for parsley to stuff a rabbit, and so may you, sir, and so adieu, sir. My master hath appointed me to go to Saint Luke’s to bid the priest be ready t’attend against you come with your appendix. Exit |
| [[Lucentio|LUCENTIO]] | I may and will, if she be so contented.<br>She will be pleased, then wherefore should I [[Doubt|doubt]]?<br>Hap what hap may, I’ll roundly go about her.<br>It shall go hard if Cambio go without her. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit |
