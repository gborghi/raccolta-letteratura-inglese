| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | A did comply with his dug before a sucked it. Thus has he—and many more of the same bevy that I know the drossy age dotes on—only got the tune of the [[Time|time]] and outward habit of encounter, a kind of yeasty collection which carries them through and through the most fanned and winnowed opinions; and do but blow them to their trial, the bubbles are out. |
| [[Horatio|HORATIO]] | You will lose this wager, my lord. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | I do not think so. Since he went into France, I have been in continual practice. I shall win at the odds. But thou wouldst not think how all here about my heart—but it is no matter. |
| [[Horatio|HORATIO]] | Nay, good my lord— |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | It is but foolery, but it is such a kind of gain-giving as would perhaps trouble a woman. |
| [[Horatio|HORATIO]] | If your mind dislike anything, obey it. I will forestall their repair hither, and say you are not fit. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Not a whit. We defy augury. There’s a special [[Providence|providence]] in the fall of a sparrow. If it be now, ’tis not to come. If it be not to come, it will be now. If it be not now, yet it will come. The readiness is all. Since no man has aught of what he [[Leaves|leaves]], what is’t to leave betimes? Enter [[King]] [[Claudius]], [[Queen]] [[Gertrude]], [[Laertes]], and |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | lords, with Osric and other attendants with |
| *(didascalia)* | trumpets, drums, cushions, foils, and gauntlets; a |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | table, and flagons of wine on it |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | Come, [[Hamlet]], come, and take this hand from me. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | (to [[Laertes]]) Give me your pardon, sir. I’ve done you wrong; But pardon’t as you are a gentleman. This presence knows, And you must needs have heard, how I am punished With sore distraction. What I have done That might your [[Nature|nature]], [[Honour|honour]], and exception Roughly awake, I here proclaim was [[Madness|madness]]. Was’t [[Hamlet]] wronged [[Laertes]]? Never [[Hamlet]]. If [[Hamlet]] from himself be ta‘en away, And when he’s not himself does wrong [[Laertes]], Then [[Hamlet]] does it not, [[Hamlet]] denies it. Who does it then? His [[Madness|madness]]. If’t be so, [[Hamlet]] is of the faction that is wronged. His [[Madness|madness]] is poor [[Hamlet]]’s enemy. Sir, in this audience Let my disclaiming from a purposed evil Free me so far in your most generous thoughts That I have shot mine arrow o’er the [[house]] And hurt my brother. |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | I am satisfied in [[Nature|nature]], Whose motive in this case should stir me most To my [[Revenge|revenge]]. But in my terms of [[Honour|honour]] I stand aloof, and will no reconcilement Till by some elder masters of known [[Honour|honour]] I have a voice and precedent of peace To keep my name ungored; but till that [[Time|time]] I do receive your offered [[Love|love]] like [[Love|love]], And will not wrong it. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | I do embrace it freely, And will this brothers’ wager frankly play.— (To attendants) Give us the foils. Come on. |
