| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | N. After ‘Sir’ at 5.2.107, Q2 has these lines (in place of F’s ‘you are not ignorant of what excellence [[Laertes]] is at his weapon’): here is newly come to [[Court|court]] [[Laertes]], believe me, an absolute gentleman, full of most excellent differences, of very soft society and great showing. Indeed, to speak feelingly of him, he is the card or calendar of gentry, for you shall find in him the continent of what part a gentleman would see. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Sir, his definement suffers no perdition in you, though I know to divide him inventorially would dizzy th’arithmetic of [[Memory|memory]], and yet but yaw neither in respect of his quick sail. But in the verity of extolment, I take him to be a [[Soul|soul]] of great article, and his infusion of such dearth and rareness as, to make true diction of him, his semblable is his [[Mirror|mirror]], and who else would trace him his umbrage, nothing more. |
