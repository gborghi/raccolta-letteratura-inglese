| Chi parla | Battuta |
|---|---|
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | (to attendants) Come, one for me. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | I’ll be your foil, [[Laertes]]. In mine ignorance Your skill shall, like a star i’th’ darkest [[Night|night]], Stick fiery off indeed. |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | You mock me, sir. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | No, by this hand. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | Give them the foils, young Osric. Cousin [[Hamlet]], You know the wager? |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Very [[Well|well]], my lord. Your grace hath laid the odds o’th’ weaker side. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | I do not [[Fear|fear]] it; I have seen you both. But since he is bettered, we have therefore odds. |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | (taking a foil) This is too heavy; let me see another. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | (taking a foil) This likes me [[Well|well]]. These foils have all a length? |
| OSRIC | Ay, my good lord. [[Hamlet]] and [[Laertes]] prepare to play |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | (to attendants) Set me the stoups of wine upon that table. If [[Hamlet]] give the first or second hit, Or quit in answer of the third exchange, Let all the battlements their ordnance [[Fire|fire]]. The [[King]] shall drink to [[Hamlet]]’s better breath, And in the cup an [[Union|union]] shall he throw Richer than that which four successive kings In [[Denmark]]’s [[Crown|crown]] have worn. Give me the cups, And let the kettle to the trumpet speak, The trumpet to the cannoneer without, The cannons to the heavens, the heaven to earth, |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | ‘Now the [[King]] drinks to [[Hamlet]]’. Trumpets the while he drinks Come, begin. And you, the judges, bear a wary eye. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | (to [[Laertes]]) Come on, sir. |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | Come, my lord. They play |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | One. |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | No. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | (to Osric) Judgement. |
| OSRIC | A hit, a very palpable hit. |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | [[Well]], again. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | Stay. Give me drink. [[Hamlet]], this pearl is thine. Here’s to thy health.— Drum and trumpets sound, and shot goes off Give him the cup. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | I’ll play this bout first. Set it by a while.— Come. They play again Another hit. What say you? |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | A touch, a touch, I do confess. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | Our son shall win. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | He’s fat and scant of breath.— Here, [[Hamlet]], take my napkin. Rub thy brows. The [[Queen]] carouses to thy fortune, [[Hamlet]]. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Good madam. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | [[Gertrude]], do not drink. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | I will, my lord, I pray you pardon me. She drinks, then offers the cup to [[Hamlet]] |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | (aside) It is the poisoned cup; it is too late. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | I dare not drink yet, madam; by and by. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | (to [[Hamlet]]) Come, let me wipe thy face. |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | (aside to [[Claudius]]) My lord, I’ll hit him now. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | (aside to [[Laertes]]) I do not think’t. |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | (aside) And yet ‘tis almost ’gainst my [[Conscience|conscience]]. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Come for the third, [[Laertes]], you but dally. I pray you pass with your best violence. I am afeard you make a wanton of me. |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | Say you so? Come on. They play |
| OSRIC | Nothing neither way. |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | (to [[Hamlet]]) Have at you now! [[Laertes]] wounds [[Hamlet]]. In scuffling, they change |
| *(didascalia)* | rapiers, and [[Hamlet]] wounds [[Laertes]] |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | (to attendants) Part them, they are incensed. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | (to [[Laertes]]) Nay, come again. The [[Queen]] falls down |
