| PLAYER | I [[Hope|hope]] we have reformed that indifferently with us, sir. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | O, [[Reform|reform]] it altogether. And let those that play your clowns speak no more than is set down for them; for there be of them that will themselves laugh to set on some quantity of barren spectators to laugh too, though in the mean [[Time|time]] some necessary question of the play be then to be considered. That’s villainous, and shows a most pitiful [[Ambition|ambition]] in the [[Fool|fool]] that uses it. Go make you ready.
