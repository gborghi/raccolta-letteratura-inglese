| PLAYER [[Queen|QUEEN]] | Nor earth to me give food, nor heaven light, Sport and repose lock from me day and [[Night|night]], Each opposite that blanks the face of joy Meet what I would have [[Well|well]] and it destroy, Both here and hence pursue me lasting strife If, once a widow, ever I be wife. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | If she should break it now! |
| PLAYER [[King|KING]] | (to Player [[Queen]]) ’Tis deeply sworn. Sweet, leave me here a while. My spirits grow dull, and fain I would beguile The tedious day with [[Sleep|sleep]]. |
| PLAYER [[Queen|QUEEN]] | [[Sleep]] rock thy brain, And never come mischance between us twain. Player [[King]] sleeps. Player [[Queen]] exits |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | (to [[Gertrude]]) Madam, how like you this play? |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | The lady protests too much, methinks. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | O, but she’ll keep her word. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | Have you heard the argument? Is there no offence in’t? |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | No, no, they do but jest, [[Poison|poison]] in jest. No offence i’th’ world. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | What do you call the play? |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | The Mousetrap. Marry, how? Tropically. This play is the image of a murder done in Vienna. Gonzago is the Duke’s name, his wife [[Baptista]]. You shall see anon. ’Tis a knavish piece of work; but what o’ that? Your majesty, and we that have free souls, it touches us not. Let the galled jade wince, our withers are unwrung. Enter Player Lucianus This is one Lucianus, nephew to the [[King]]. |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | You are as good as a [[Chorus|chorus]], my lord. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | I could interpret between you and your [[Love|love]] if I could see the puppets dallying. |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | You are keen, my lord, you are keen. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | It would cost you a groaning to take off mine edge. |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | Still better, and worse. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | So you mis-take your husbands. (To Lucianus) Begin, murderer. Pox, leave thy damnable faces and begin. Come: ‘the croaking raven doth bellow for [[Revenge|revenge]]’. |
| PLAYER LUCIANUS | Thoughts black, hands apt, drugs fit, and [[Time|time]] agreeing, Confederate season, else no creature seeing; Thou mixture rank of midnight weeds collected, With Hecate’s ban thrice blasted, thrice infected, Thy natural [[Magic|magic]] and dire property On wholesome life usurp immediately. He pours the [[Poison|poison]] in the Player [[King]]’s ear |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | A poisons him i’th’ [[Garden|garden]] for ’s estate. His name’s Gonzago. The story is extant, and writ in choice Italian. You shall see anon how the murderer gets the [[Love|love]] of Gonzago’s wife. |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | The [[King]] rises. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | What, frighted with false [[Fire|fire]]? |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | (to [[Claudius]]) How fares my lord? POLONIUSGive o’er the play. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | Give me some light. Away. COURTIERS Lights, lights, lights! Exeunt all but [[Hamlet]] and [[Horatio]] |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Why, let the stricken deer go weep, The hart ungallèd play, For some must watch, while some must [[Sleep|sleep]], So runs the world away. Would not this, sir, and a forest of feathers, if the rest of my fortunes turn Turk with me, with two Provençal roses on my razed shoes, get me a fellowship in a cry of players, sir? |
| [[Horatio|HORATIO]] | Half a share. |
