| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[King]] [[Claudius]], [[Queen]] [[Gertrude]], [[Polonius]], [[Ophelia]], [[Rosencrantz]], [[Guildenstern]], and lords |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | (to [[Rosencrantz]] and [[Guildenstern]])<br>And can you by no drift of circumstance<br>Get from him why he puts on this confusion,<br>Grating so harshly all his days of quiet<br>With turbulent and dangerous lunacy? |
| [[Rosencrantz|ROSENCRANTZ]] | He does confess he feels himself distracted,<br>But from what cause a will by no means speak. |
| [[Guildenstern|GUILDENSTERN]] | Nor do we find him forward to be sounded,<br>But with a crafty [[Madness|madness]] keeps aloof<br>When we would bring him on to some confession<br>Of his true state. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | Did he receive you [[Well|well]]? |
| [[Rosencrantz|ROSENCRANTZ]] | Most like a gentleman. |
| [[Guildenstern|GUILDENSTERN]] | But with much forcing of his disposition. |
| [[Rosencrantz|ROSENCRANTZ]] | Niggard of question, but of our demands<br>Most free in his reply. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | Did you assay him<br>To any pastime? |
| [[Rosencrantz|ROSENCRANTZ]] | Madam, it so fell out that certain players<br>We o’er-raught on the way. Of these we told him,<br>And there did seem in him a kind of joy<br>To hear of it. They are about the [[Court|court]],<br>And, as I think, they have already order<br>This [[Night|night]] to play before him. |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | ’Tis most true, And he beseeched me to entreat your majesties<br>To hear and see the matter. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | With all my heart; and it doth much content me<br>To hear him so inclined.—Good gentlemen,<br>Give him a further edge, and drive his purpose on<br>To these delights. |
| [[Rosencrantz|ROSENCRANTZ]] | We shall, my lord. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt [[Rosencrantz]] and [[Guildenstern]] |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | Sweet [[Gertrude]], leave us too,<br>For we have closely sent for [[Hamlet]] hither,<br>That he, as ‘twere by accident, may here<br>Affront [[Ophelia]].<br>Her father and myself, lawful espials,<br>Will so bestow ourselves that, seeing unseen,<br>We may of their encounter frankly judge,<br>And gather by him, as he is behaved,<br>If’t be th’affliction of his [[Love|love]] or no<br>That thus he suffers for. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | I shall obey you.<br>And for your part, [[Ophelia]], I do wish<br>That your good beauties be the happy cause<br>Of [[Hamlet]]’s wildness; so shall I [[Hope|hope]] your virtues<br>Will bring him to his wonted way again,<br>To both your honours. |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | Madam, I wish it may. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit [[Gertrude]] |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | [[Ophelia]], walk you here.—Gracious, so please you,<br>We will bestow ourselves.—Read on this book,<br>That show of such an exercise may colour<br>Your [[Loneliness|loneliness]]. We are oft to blame in this:<br>‘Tis too much proved that with devotion’s visage<br>And pious action we do sugar o’er<br>The devil himself. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | O, ’tis too true.<br>(Aside) How smart a lash that speech doth give my<br>[[Conscience|conscience]].<br>The harlot’s cheek, beautied with plast’ring art,<br>Is not more ugly to the thing that helps it<br>Than is my deed to my most painted word.<br>O heavy burden! |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | I hear him coming. Let’s withdraw, my lord. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt [[Claudius]] and [[Polonius]] |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Prince [[Hamlet]] |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | To be, or not to be; that is the question:<br>Whether ‘tis nobler in the mind to suffer<br>The slings and arrows of outrageous fortune,<br>Or to take arms against a sea of troubles,<br>And, by opposing, end them. To die, to [[Sleep|sleep]]—<br>No more, and by a [[Sleep|sleep]] to say we end<br>The heartache and the thousand natural shocks<br>That flesh is heir to—’tis a consummation<br>Devoutly to be wished. To die, to [[Sleep|sleep]].<br>To [[Sleep|sleep]], perchance to dream. Ay, there’s the rub,<br>For in that [[Sleep|sleep]] of [[Death|death]] what [[Dreams|dreams]] may come<br>When we have shuffled off this mortal coil<br>Must give us pause. There’s the respect<br>That makes calamity of so long life,<br>For who would bear the whips and scorns of [[Time|time]],<br>Th‘oppressor’s wrong, the proud man’s contumely,<br>The pangs of disprized [[Love|love]], the law’s delay,<br>The insolence of office, and the spurns<br>That patient merit of th’unworthy takes,<br>When he himself might his quietus make<br>With a bare bodkin? Who would these fardels bear,<br>To grunt and sweat under a weary life,<br>But that the dread of something after [[Death|death]],<br>The undiscovered country from whose bourn<br>No traveller returns, puzzles the will,<br>And makes us rather bear those ills we have<br>Than fly to others that we know not of?<br>Thus [[Conscience|conscience]] does make cowards of us all,<br>And thus the native hue of resolution<br>Is sicklied o’er with the pale cast of thought,<br>And enterprises of great pith and moment<br>With this regard their currents turn awry,<br>And lose the name of action. Soft you, now,<br>The fair [[Ophelia]]!—Nymph, in thy orisons<br>Be all my sins remembered. |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | Good my lord,<br>How does your [[Honour|honour]] for this many a day? |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | I humbly thank you, [[Well|well]], [[Well|well]], [[Well|well]]. |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | My lord, I have remembrances of yours<br>That I have longed long to redeliver.<br>I pray you now receive them. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | No, no, I never gave you aught. |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | My honoured lord, you know right [[Well|well]] you did,<br>And with them words of so sweet breath composed<br>As made the things more rich. Their perfume lost,<br>Take these again; for to the noble mind<br>Rich gifts wax poor when givers prove unkind.<br>There, my lord. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Ha, ha? Are you honest? |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | My lord. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Are you fair? |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | What means your lordship? |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | That if you be honest and fair, your honesty should admit no discourse to your [[Beauty|beauty]]. |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | Could [[Beauty|beauty]], my lord, have better commerce than with honesty? |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Ay, truly, for the power of [[Beauty|beauty]] will sooner transform honesty from what it is to a bawd than the force of honesty can translate [[Beauty|beauty]] into his likeness. This was sometime a [[Paradox|paradox]], but now the [[Time|time]] gives it proof. I did [[Love|love]] you once. |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | Indeed, my lord, you made me believe so. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | You should not have believed me, for virtue cannot so inoculate our old stock but we shall relish of it. I loved you not. |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | I was the more deceived. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Get thee to a nunnery. Why wouldst thou be a breeder of sinners? I am myself indifferent honest, but yet I could accuse me of such things that it were better my mother had not borne me. I am very proud, revengeful, ambitious, with more offences at my beck than I have thoughts to put them in, [[Imagination|imagination]] to give them shape, or [[Time|time]] to act them in. What should such fellows as I do crawling between heaven and earth? We are arrant knaves, all. Believe none of us. Go thy ways to a nunnery. Where’s your father? |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | At [[Home|home]], my lord. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Let the doors be shut upon him, that he may play the [[Fool|fool]] nowhere but in ’s own [[house]]. Farewell. |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | O help him, you sweet heavens! |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | If thou dost marry, I’ll give thee this plague for thy dowry: be thou as chaste as ice, as pure as [[Snow|snow]], thou shalt not escape calumny. Get thee to a nunnery, go, farewell. Or if thou wilt needs marry, marry a [[Fool|fool]]; for wise men know [[Well|well]] enough what monsters you make of them. To a nunnery, go, and quickly, too. Farewell. |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | O heavenly powers, restore him! |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | I have heard of your paintings, too, [[Well|well]] enough. God hath given you one face, and you make yourselves another. You jig, you amble, and you lisp, and nickname God’s creatures, and make your wantonness your ignorance. Go to, I’ll no more on’t. It hath made me mad. I say we will have no more marriages. Those that are married already—all but one—shall live. The rest shall keep as they are. To a nunnery, go. Exit |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | O what a noble mind is here o‘erthrown!<br>The courtier’s, soldier‘s, scholar’s eye, tongue, [[Sword|sword]],<br>Th’expectancy and [[Rose|rose]] of the fair state,<br>The glass of fashion and the mould of form,<br>Th‘observed of all observers, quite, quite, down!<br>And I, of ladies most deject and wretched,<br>That sucked the honey of his music vows,<br>Now see that noble and most sovereign reason<br>Like sweet [[Bells|bells]] jangled out of tune and harsh;<br>That unmatched form and feature of blown youth<br>Blasted with ecstasy. O woe is me,<br>T’have seen what I have seen, see what I see! |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[King]] [[Claudius]] and [[Polonius]] |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | [[Love]]? His affections do not that way tend,<br>Nor what he spake, though it lacked form a little,<br>Was not like [[Madness|madness]]. There’s something in his [[Soul|soul]]<br>O’er which his [[Melancholy|melancholy]] sits on brood,<br>And I do [[Doubt|doubt]] the hatch and the disclose<br>Will be some danger; which to prevent<br>I have in quick determination<br>Thus set it down: he shall with [[Speed|speed]] to England<br>For the demand of our neglected tribute.<br>Haply the seas and countries different,<br>With variable objects, shall expel<br>This something-settled matter in his heart,<br>Whereon his brains still beating puts him thus<br>From fashion of himself. What think you on’t? |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | It shall do [[Well|well]]. But yet do I believe<br>The origin and commencement of this grief<br>Sprung from neglected [[Love|love]].—How now, [[Ophelia]]?<br>You need not tell us what Lord [[Hamlet]] said;<br>We heard it all.—My lord, do as you please,<br>But, if you hold it fit, after the play<br>Let his [[Queen|queen]] mother all [[Alone|alone]] entreat him<br>To show his griefs. Let her be round with him,<br>And I’ll be placed, so please you, in the ear<br>Of all their conference. If she find him not,<br>To England send him, or confine him where<br>Your wisdom best shall think. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | It shall be so.<br>[[Madness]] in great ones must not unwatched go. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt |
