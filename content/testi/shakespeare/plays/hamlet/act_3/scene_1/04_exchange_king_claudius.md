| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | [[Love]]? His affections do not that way tend, Nor what he spake, though it lacked form a little, Was not like [[Madness|madness]]. There’s something in his [[Soul|soul]] O’er which his [[Melancholy|melancholy]] sits on brood, And I do [[Doubt|doubt]] the hatch and the disclose Will be some danger; which to prevent I have in quick determination Thus set it down: he shall with [[Speed|speed]] to England For the demand of our neglected tribute. Haply the seas and countries different, With variable objects, shall expel This something-settled matter in his heart, Whereon his brains still beating puts him thus From fashion of himself. What think you on’t? |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | It shall do [[Well|well]]. But yet do I believe The origin and commencement of this grief Sprung from neglected [[Love|love]].—How now, [[Ophelia]]? You need not tell us what Lord [[Hamlet]] said; We heard it all.—My lord, do as you please, But, if you hold it fit, after the play Let his [[Queen|queen]] mother all [[Alone|alone]] entreat him To show his griefs. Let her be round with him, And I’ll be placed, so please you, in the ear Of all their conference. If she find him not, To England send him, or confine him where Your wisdom best shall think. |
