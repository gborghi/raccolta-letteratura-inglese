|---|---|
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[King]] [[Claudius]], [[Queen]] [[Gertrude]], [[Polonius]], [[Ophelia]], [[Rosencrantz]], [[Guildenstern]], and lords |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | (to [[Rosencrantz]] and [[Guildenstern]]) And can you by no drift of circumstance Get from him why he puts on this confusion, Grating so harshly all his days of quiet With turbulent and dangerous lunacy? |
| [[Rosencrantz|ROSENCRANTZ]] | He does confess he feels himself distracted, But from what cause a will by no means speak. |
| [[Guildenstern|GUILDENSTERN]] | Nor do we find him forward to be sounded, But with a crafty [[Madness|madness]] keeps aloof When we would bring him on to some confession Of his true state. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | Did he receive you [[Well|well]]? |
| [[Rosencrantz|ROSENCRANTZ]] | Most like a gentleman. |
| [[Guildenstern|GUILDENSTERN]] | But with much forcing of his disposition. |
| [[Rosencrantz|ROSENCRANTZ]] | Niggard of question, but of our demands Most free in his reply. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | Did you assay him To any pastime? |
| [[Rosencrantz|ROSENCRANTZ]] | Madam, it so fell out that certain players We o’er-raught on the way. Of these we told him, And there did seem in him a kind of joy To hear of it. They are about the [[Court|court]], And, as I think, they have already order This [[Night|night]] to play before him. |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | ’Tis most true, And he beseeched me to entreat your majesties To hear and see the matter. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | With all my heart; and it doth much content me To hear him so inclined.—Good gentlemen, Give him a further edge, and drive his purpose on To these delights. |
| [[Rosencrantz|ROSENCRANTZ]] | We shall, my lord. Exeunt [[Rosencrantz]] and [[Guildenstern]] |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | Sweet [[Gertrude]], leave us too, For we have closely sent for [[Hamlet]] hither, That he, as ‘twere by accident, may here Affront [[Ophelia]]. Her father and myself, lawful espials, Will so bestow ourselves that, seeing unseen, We may of their encounter frankly judge, And gather by him, as he is behaved, If’t be th’affliction of his [[Love|love]] or no That thus he suffers for. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | I shall obey you. And for your part, [[Ophelia]], I do wish That your good beauties be the happy cause Of [[Hamlet]]’s wildness; so shall I [[Hope|hope]] your virtues Will bring him to his wonted way again, To both your honours. |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | Madam, I wish it may. Exit [[Gertrude]] |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | [[Ophelia]], walk you here.—Gracious, so please you, We will bestow ourselves.—Read on this book, That show of such an exercise may colour Your [[Loneliness|loneliness]]. We are oft to blame in this: ‘Tis too much proved that with devotion’s visage And pious action we do sugar o’er The devil himself. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | O, ’tis too true. (Aside) How smart a lash that speech doth give my [[Conscience|conscience]]. The harlot’s cheek, beautied with plast’ring art, Is not more ugly to the thing that helps it Than is my deed to my most painted word. O heavy burden! |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | I hear him coming. Let’s withdraw, my lord. Exeunt [[Claudius]] and [[Polonius]] Enter Prince [[Hamlet]] |
