| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | Think it no more. For [[Nature|nature]] crescent does not grow [[Alone|alone]] In thews and bulk, but as his temple waxes The inward service of the mind and [[Soul|soul]] Grows wide withal. Perhaps he loves you now, And now no soil nor cautel doth besmirch The virtue of his will; but you must [[Fear|fear]], His [[Greatness|greatness]] weighed, his will is not his own, For he himself is subject to his birth. He may not, as unvalued persons do, Carve for himself, for on his choice depends The [[Sanity|sanity]] and health of the whole state; And therefore must his choice be circumscribed Unto the voice and yielding of that [[Body|body]] Whereof he is the head. Then if he says he loves you, It fits your wisdom so far to believe it As he in his peculiar sect and force May give his saying deed, which is no further Than the main voice of [[Denmark]] goes withal. Then weigh what loss your [[Honour|honour]] may sustain If with too credent ear you list his [[songs]], Or lose your heart, or your chaste treasure open To his unmastered importunity. [[Fear]] it, [[Ophelia]], [[Fear|fear]] it, my dear sister, And keep within the rear of your affection, Out of the shot and danger of [[Desire|desire]]. The chariest maid is prodigal enough If she unmask her [[Beauty|beauty]] to the [[Moon|moon]]. Virtue itself scapes not calumnious strokes. The canker galls the infants of the [[Spring|spring]] Too oft before their buttons be disclosed, And in the morn and liquid dew of youth Contagious blastments are most imminent. Be wary then; best safety lies in [[Fear|fear]]; Youth to itself rebels, though none else near. |
