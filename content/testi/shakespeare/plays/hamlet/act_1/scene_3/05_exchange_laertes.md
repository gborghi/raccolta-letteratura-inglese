| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | Most humbly do I take my leave, my lord. |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | The [[Time|time]] invites you. Go; your servants tend. |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | Farewell, [[Ophelia]], and remember [[Well|well]] What I have said to you. |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | ’Tis in my [[Memory|memory]] locked, And you yourself shall keep the key of it. |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | Farewell. Exit |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | What is’t, [[Ophelia]], he hath said to you? |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | So please you, something touching the Lord [[Hamlet]]. |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | Marry, [[Well|well]] bethought. ‘Tis told me he hath very oft of late Given private [[Time|time]] to you, and you yourself Have of your audience been most free and bounteous. If it be so—as so ’tis put on me, And that in way of caution—I must tell you You do not understand yourself so clearly As it behoves my daughter and your [[Honour|honour]]. What is between you? Give me up the [[Truth|truth]]. |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | He hath, my lord, of late made many tenders Of his affection to me. |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | Affection, pooh! You speak like a green girl Unsifted in such perilous circumstance. Do you believe his ‘tenders’ as you call them? |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | I do not know, my lord, what I should think. |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | Marry, I’ll teach you: think yourself a baby That you have ta’en his tenders for true pay, Which are not sterling. Tender yourself more dearly, Or—not to crack the [[Wind|wind]] of the poor phrase, Running it thus—you’ll tender me a [[Fool|fool]]. |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | My lord, he hath importuned me with [[Love|love]] In honourable fashion— |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | Ay, fashion you may call it. Go to, go to. |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | And hath given countenance to his speech, my lord, With all the vows of heaven. |
