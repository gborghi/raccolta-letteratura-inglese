| *(didascalia)* | Flourish. Enter [[Claudius]], [[King]] of [[Denmark]], [[Gertrude]] the [[Queen]], members of the Council, such as [[Polonius]], his son [[Laertes]] and daughter [[Ophelia]], Prince [[Hamlet]] dressed in black, with others |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | Though yet of [[Hamlet]] our dear brother’s [[Death|death]]<br>The [[Memory|memory]] be green, and that it us befitted<br>To bear our hearts in grief and our whole kingdom<br>To be contracted in one brow of woe,<br>Yet so far hath discretion fought with [[Nature|nature]]<br>That we with wisest sorrow think on him<br>Together with remembrance of ourselves.<br>Therefore our sometime sister, now our [[Queen|queen]],<br>Th‘imperial jointress of this warlike state,<br>Have we as ’twere with a defeated joy,<br>With one auspicious and one dropping eye,<br>With mirth in funeral and with [[Dirge|dirge]] in [[Marriage|marriage]],<br>In equal scale weighing delight and dole,<br>Taken to wife. Nor have we herein barred<br>Your better wisdoms, which have freely gone<br>With this affair along. For all, our thanks.<br>Now follows that you know young Fortinbras,<br>Holding a weak supposal of our worth,<br>Or thinking by our late dear brother’s [[Death|death]]<br>Our state to be disjoint and out of frame,<br>Co-leaguèd with the dream of his advantage,<br>He hath not failed to pester us with message<br>Importing the surrender of those lands<br>Lost by his father, with all bonds of law,<br>To our most valiant brother. So much for him. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Valtemand and Cornelius |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | Now for ourself, and for this [[Time|time]] of meeting,<br>Thus much the business is: we have here writ<br>To Norway, uncle of young Fortinbras—<br>Who, impotent and bed-rid, scarcely hears<br>Of this his nephew’s purpose—to suppress<br>His further gait herein, in that the levies,<br>The lists, and full proportions are all made<br>Out of his subject; and we here dispatch<br>You, good Cornelius, and you, Valtemand,<br>For bearers of this greeting to old Norway,<br>Giving to you no further personal power<br>To business with the [[King]] more than the scope<br>Of these dilated articles allow.<br>Farewell, and let your haste commend your duty. |
| VALTEMAND | In that and all things will we show our duty. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | We [[Doubt|doubt]] it nothing, heartily farewell. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt Valtemand and Cornelius |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | And now, [[Laertes]], what’s the news with you?<br>You told us of some suit. What is’t, [[Laertes]]?<br>You cannot speak of reason to the Dane<br>And lose your voice. What wouldst thou beg, [[Laertes]],<br>That shall not be my offer, not thy asking?<br>The head is not more native to the heart,<br>The hand more instrumental to the mouth,<br>Than is the throne of [[Denmark]] to thy father.<br>What wouldst thou have, [[Laertes]]? |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | Dread my lord,<br>Your leave and favour to return to France,<br>From whence though willingly I came to [[Denmark]]<br>To show my duty in your coronation,<br>Yet now I must confess, that duty done,<br>My thoughts and wishes bend again towards France<br>And bow them to your gracious leave and pardon. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | Have you your father’s leave? What says [[Polonius]]? |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | He hath, my lord, wrung from me my slow leave<br>By laboursome petition, and at last<br>Upon his will I sealed my hard consent.<br>I do beseech you give him leave to go. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | Take thy fair hour, [[Laertes]]. [[Time]] be thine,<br>And thy best graces spend it at thy will.<br>But now, my cousin [[Hamlet]], and my son— |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | A little more than kin and less than kind. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | How is it that the clouds still hang on you? |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Not so, my lord, I am too much i’th’ sun. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | Good [[Hamlet]], cast thy nightly colour off,<br>And let thine eye look like a friend on [[Denmark]].<br>Do not for ever with thy vailèd lids<br>Seek for thy noble father in the [[Dust|dust]].<br>Thou know‘st ’tis common—all that lives must die,<br>Passing through [[Nature|nature]] to [[Eternity|eternity]]. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Ay, madam, it is common. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | If it be,<br>Why seems it so particular with thee? |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Seems, madam? Nay, it is. I know not ‘seems’.<br>‘Tis not [[Alone|alone]] my inky cloak, good-mother,<br>Nor customary suits of solemn black,<br>Nor windy suspiration of forced breath,<br>No, nor the fruitful [[River|river]] in the eye,<br>Nor the dejected haviour of the visage,<br>Together with all forms, moods, shows of grief<br>That can denote me truly. These indeed ‘seem’,<br>For they are actions that a man might play;<br>But I have that within which passeth show—<br>These but the trappings and the suits of woe. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | ‘Tis sweet and commendable in your [[Nature|nature]], [[Hamlet]],<br>To give these mourning duties to your father;<br>But you must know your father lost a father;<br>That father lost, lost his; and the survivor bound<br>In filial obligation for some term<br>To do obsequious sorrow. But to persever<br>In obstinate condolement is a course<br>Of impious stubbornness, ’tis unmanly grief,<br>It shows a will most incorrect to heaven,<br>A heart unfortified, a mind impatient,<br>An understanding simple and unschooled;<br>For what we know must be, and is as common<br>As any the most vulgar thing to sense,<br>Why should we in our peevish opposition<br>Take it to heart? Fie, ‘tis a fault to heaven,<br>A fault against the dead, a fault to [[Nature|nature]],<br>To reason most absurd, whose common theme<br>Is [[Death|death]] of fathers, and who still hath cried<br>From the first corpse till he that died today,<br>’This must be so’. We pray you throw to earth<br>This unprevailing woe, and think of us<br>As of a father; for let the world take note<br>You are the most immediate to our throne,<br>And with no less nobility of [[Love|love]]<br>Than that which dearest father bears his son<br>Do I impart towards you. For your intent<br>In going back to school in Wittenberg,<br>It is most retrograde to our [[Desire|desire]],<br>And we beseech you bend you to remain<br>Here in the cheer and comfort of our eye,<br>Our chiefest courtier, cousin, and our son. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | Let not thy mother lose her prayers, [[Hamlet]].<br>I pray thee stay with us, go not to Wittenberg. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | I shall in all my best obey you, madam. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | Why, ’tis a loving and a fair reply.<br>Be as ourself in [[Denmark]]. (To [[Gertrude]]) Madam, come.<br>This gentle and unforced accord of [[Hamlet]]<br>Sits smiling to my heart; in grace whereof,<br>No jocund health that [[Denmark]] drinks today<br>But the great cannon to the clouds shall tell,<br>And the [[King]]’s rouse the heavens shall bruit again,<br>Re-speaking earthly thunder. Come, away. |
| *(didascalia)* | Flourish. pmlmmmExeunt all but [[Hamlet]] |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | O that this too too solid flesh would melt,<br>Thaw, and resolve itself into a dew,<br>Or that the Everlasting had not fixed<br>His canon ‘gainst [[Self|self]]-slaughter! O God, O God,<br>How weary, stale, flat, and unprofitable<br>Seem to me all the uses of this world!<br>Fie on’t, ah fie, fie! ’Tis an unweeded [[Garden|garden]]<br>That grows to seed; things rank and gross in [[Nature|nature]]<br>Possess it merely. That it should come to this—<br>But two months dead—nay, not so much, not two—<br>So excellent a [[King|king]], that was to this<br>[[Hyperion]] to a satyr, so loving to my mother<br>That he might not beteem the winds of heaven<br>Visit her face too roughly! Heaven and earth,<br>Must I remember? Why, she would hang on him<br>As if increase of appetite had grown<br>By what it fed on, and yet within a month—<br>Let me not think on’t; frailty, thy name is woman—<br>A little month, or ere those shoes were old<br>With which she followed my poor father’s [[Body|body]],<br>Like Niobe, all [[Tears|tears]], why she, even she—<br>O God, a beast that wants discourse of reason<br>Would have mourned longer!—married with mine<br>uncle,<br>My father’s brother, but no more like my father<br>Than I to Hercules; within a month,<br>Ere yet the salt of most unrighteous [[Tears|tears]]<br>Had left the flushing of her gallèd eyes,<br>She married. O most wicked [[Speed|speed]], to post<br>With such dexterity to incestuous sheets!<br>It is not, nor it cannot come to good.<br>But break, my heart, for I must hold my tongue. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Horatio]], Marcellus, and Barnardo |
| [[Horatio|HORATIO]] | Hail to your lordship. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | I am glad to see you [[Well|well]].<br>[[Horatio]]—or I do forget myself. |
| [[Horatio|HORATIO]] | The same, my lord, and your poor servant ever. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Sir, my good friend; I’ll change that name with you.<br>And what make you from Wittenberg, [[Horatio]]?—<br>Marcellus. |
| MARCELLUS | My good lord. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | I am very glad to see you. (To Barnardo) Good even,<br>sir.—<br>But what in [[Faith|faith]] make you from Wittenberg? |
| [[Horatio|HORATIO]] | A truant disposition, good my lord. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | I would not have your enemy say so,<br>Nor shall you do mine ear that violence<br>To make it truster of your own report<br>Against yourself. I know you are no truant.<br>But what is your affair in Elsinore?<br>We’ll teach you to drink deep ere you depart. |
| [[Horatio|HORATIO]] | My lord, I came to see your father’s funeral. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | I prithee do not mock me, fellow-student;<br>I think it was to see my mother’s wedding. |
| [[Horatio|HORATIO]] | Indeed, my lord, it followed hard upon. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Thrift, thrift, [[Horatio]]. The funeral baked meats<br>Did coldly furnish forth the [[Marriage|marriage]] tables.<br>Would I had met my dearest foe in heaven<br>Ere I had ever seen that day, [[Horatio]].<br>My father—methinks I see my father. |
| [[Horatio|HORATIO]] | O where, my lord? |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | In my mind’s eye, [[Horatio]]. |
| [[Horatio|HORATIO]] | I saw him once. A was a goodly [[King|king]]. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | A was a man. Take him for all in all,<br>I shall not look upon his like again. |
| [[Horatio|HORATIO]] | My lord, I think I saw him yesternight. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Saw? Who? |
| [[Horatio|HORATIO]] | My lord, the [[King]] your father. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | The [[King]] my father? |
| [[Horatio|HORATIO]] | Season your admiration for a while<br>With an attent ear till I may deliver,<br>Upon the witness of these gentlemen,<br>This marvel to you. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | For God’s [[Love|love]] let me hear! |
| [[Horatio|HORATIO]] | Two nights together had these gentlemen,<br>Marcellus and Barnardo, on their watch,<br>In the dead waste and middle of the [[Night|night]],<br>Been thus encountered. A figure like your father,<br>Armed at all points exactly, cap-à-pie,<br>Appears before them, and with solemn march<br>Goes slow and stately by them. Thrice he walked<br>By their oppressed and [[Fear|fear]]-surprised eyes |
| *(didascalia)* | Within his truncheon’s length, whilst they distilled |
| [[Horatio|HORATIO]] | Almost to jelly with the act of [[Fear|fear]]<br>Stand dumb and speak not to him. This to me<br>In dreadful secrecy impart they did,<br>And I with them the third [[Night|night]] kept the watch,<br>Where, as they had delivered, both in [[Time|time]],<br>Form of the thing, each word made true and good,<br>The apparition comes. I knew your father;<br>These hands are not more like. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | But where was this? |
| MARCELLUS | My lord, upon the platform where we watched. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Did you not speak to it? |
| [[Horatio|HORATIO]] | My lord, I did,<br>But answer made it none; yet once methought<br>It lifted up it head and did address<br>Itself to motion like as it would speak,<br>But even then the morning cock crew loud,<br>And at the sound it shrunk in haste away<br>And vanished from our sight. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | ’Tis very strange. |
| [[Horatio|HORATIO]] | As I do live, my honoured lord, ’tis true,<br>And we did think it writ down in our duty<br>To let you know of it. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Indeed, indeed, sirs; but this troubles me.—<br>Hold you the watch tonight? |
| BARNARDO | and MARCELLUS<br>We do, my lord. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Armed, say you? |
| BARNARDO | and MARCELLUS Armed, my lord. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | From top to toe? |
| BARNARDO | and MARCELLUS<br>My lord, from head to foot. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Then saw you not his face. |
| [[Horatio|HORATIO]] | O yes, my lord, he wore his beaver up. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | What looked he? Frowningly? |
| [[Horatio|HORATIO]] | A countenance more<br>In sorrow than in anger. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Pale or red? |
| [[Horatio|HORATIO]] | Nay, very pale. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | And fixed his eyes upon you? |
| [[Horatio|HORATIO]] | Most constantly. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | I would I had been there. |
| [[Horatio|HORATIO]] | It would have much amazed you. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Very like, very like. Stayed it long? |
| [[Horatio|HORATIO]] | While one with moderate haste might tell a hundred. |
| BARNARDO | and MARCELLUS Longer, longer. |
| [[Horatio|HORATIO]] | Not when I saw’t. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | His beard was grizzly, no? |
| [[Horatio|HORATIO]] | It was as I have seen it in his life,<br>A sable silvered. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | I’ll watch tonight. Perchance<br>‘Twill walk again. |
| [[Horatio|HORATIO]] | I warrant you it will. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | If it assume my noble father’s person<br>I’ll speak to it though hell itself should gape<br>And bid me hold my peace. I pray you all,<br>If you have hitherto concealed this sight,<br>Let it be treble in your [[Silence|silence]] still,<br>And whatsoever else shall hap tonight,<br>Give it an understanding but no tongue.<br>I will requite your loves. So fare ye [[Well|well]].<br>Upon the platform ’twixt eleven and twelve<br>I’ll visit you. |
| ALL THREE | Our duty to your [[Honour|honour]]. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Your [[Love|love]], as mine to you. Farewell. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt all but [[Hamlet]] |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | My father’s spirit in arms! All is not [[Well|well]].<br>I [[Doubt|doubt]] some foul play. Would the [[Night|night]] were come.<br>Till then, sit still, my [[Soul|soul]]. Foul deeds will rise,<br>Though all the earth o’erwhelm them, to men’s eyes. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit |
