| BARNARDO | and MARCELLUS We do, my lord. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Armed, say you? |
| BARNARDO | and MARCELLUS Armed, my lord. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | From top to toe? |
| BARNARDO | and MARCELLUS My lord, from head to foot. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Then saw you not his face. |
| [[Horatio|HORATIO]] | O yes, my lord, he wore his beaver up. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | What looked he? Frowningly? |
| [[Horatio|HORATIO]] | A countenance more In sorrow than in anger. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Pale or red? |
| [[Horatio|HORATIO]] | Nay, very pale. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | And fixed his eyes upon you? |
| [[Horatio|HORATIO]] | Most constantly. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | I would I had been there. |
| [[Horatio|HORATIO]] | It would have much amazed you. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Very like, very like. Stayed it long? |
| [[Horatio|HORATIO]] | While one with moderate haste might tell a hundred. |
| BARNARDO | and MARCELLUS Longer, longer. |
| [[Horatio|HORATIO]] | Not when I saw’t. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | His beard was grizzly, no? |
| [[Horatio|HORATIO]] | It was as I have seen it in his life, A sable silvered. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | I’ll watch tonight. Perchance ‘Twill walk again. |
| [[Horatio|HORATIO]] | I warrant you it will. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | If it assume my noble father’s person I’ll speak to it though hell itself should gape And bid me hold my peace. I pray you all, If you have hitherto concealed this sight, Let it be treble in your [[Silence|silence]] still, And whatsoever else shall hap tonight, Give it an understanding but no tongue. I will requite your loves. So fare ye [[Well|well]]. Upon the platform ’twixt eleven and twelve I’ll visit you. |
| ALL THREE | Our duty to your [[Honour|honour]]. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Your [[Love|love]], as mine to you. Farewell. Exeunt all but [[Hamlet]] My father’s spirit in arms! All is not [[Well|well]]. I [[Doubt|doubt]] some foul play. Would the [[Night|night]] were come. Till then, sit still, my [[Soul|soul]]. Foul deeds will rise, Though all the earth o’erwhelm them, to men’s eyes. Exit |
