| MARCELLUS | Is it not like the [[King]]? |
| [[Horatio|HORATIO]] | As thou art to thyself. Such was the very armour he had on When he th‘ambitious Norway combated. So frowned he once when in an angry parley He smote the sledded Polacks on the ice. ’Tis strange. |
| MARCELLUS | Thus twice before, and just at this dead hour, With martial stalk hath he gone by our watch. |
| [[Horatio|HORATIO]] | In what particular thought to work I know not, But in the gross and scope of my opinion This bodes some strange eruption to our state. |
| MARCELLUS | Good now, sit down, and tell me, he that knows, Why this same strict and most observant watch So nightly toils the subject of the land, And why such daily cast of brazen cannon, And foreign mart for implements of war, Why such impress of shipwrights, whose sore task Does not divide the [[Sunday]] from the week: What might be toward that this sweaty haste Doth make the [[Night|night]] joint-labourer with the day, Who is’t that can inform me? |
