| *(didascalia)* | Enter old [[Polonius]] with his man Reynaldo |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | Give him this money and these notes, Reynaldo. |
| REYNALDO | I will, my lord. |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | You shall do marv’lous wisely, good Reynaldo,<br>Before you visit him to make enquire<br>Of his behaviour. |
| REYNALDO | My lord, I did intend it. |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | Marry, [[Well|well]] said, very [[Well|well]] said. Look you, sir,<br>Enquire me first what Danskers are in [[Paris]],<br>And how, and who, what means, and where they keep,<br>What company, at what expense; and finding<br>By this encompassment and drift of question<br>That they do know my son, come you more nearer<br>Than your particular demands will touch it.<br>Take you, as ‘twere, some distant knowledge of him,<br>As thus: ‘I know his father and his friends,<br>And in part him’—do you mark this, Reynaldo? |
| REYNALDO | Ay, very [[Well|well]], my lord. |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | ‘And in part him, but’, you may say, ‘not [[Well|well]],<br>But if’t be he I mean, he’s very wild,<br>Addicted so and so’; and there put on him<br>What forgeries you please—marry, none so rank<br>As may dishonour him, take heed of that—<br>But, sir, such wanton, wild, and usual slips<br>As are companions noted and most known<br>To youth and liberty. |
| REYNALDO | As gaming, my lord? |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | Ay, or drinking, fencing, swearing,<br>Quarrelling, drabbing—you may go so far. |
| REYNALDO | My lord, that would dishonour him. |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | [[Faith]], no, as you may season it in the charge.<br>You must not put another scandal on him,<br>That he is open to incontinency.<br>That’s not my meaning—but breathe his faults so<br>quaintly<br>That they may seem the taints of liberty,<br>The flash and outbreak of a fiery mind,<br>A savageness in unreclaimed [[Blood|blood]],<br>Of general assault. |
| REYNALDO | But, my good lord— |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | Wherefore should you do this? |
| REYNALDO | Ay, my lord.<br>I would know that. |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | Marry, sir, here’s my drift,<br>And I believe it is a fetch of warrant:<br>You laying these slight sullies on my son,<br>As ‘twere a thing a little soiled i’th’ working,<br>Mark you, your party in converse, him you would<br>sound,<br>Having ever seen in the prenominate crimes<br>The youth you breathe of guilty, be assured<br>He closes with you in this consequence:<br>‘Good sir’, or so, or ‘friend’, or ‘gentleman’,<br>According to the phrase and the addition<br>Of man and country. |
| REYNALDO | Very good, my lord. |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | And then, sir, does a this—a does—<br>what was I about to say? By the mass, I was about to<br>say something. Where did I leave? |
| REYNALDO | At ‘closes in the consequence’, at ‘friend,<br>Or so’, and ‘gentleman’. |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | At ‘closes in the consequence’—ay, marry,<br>He closes with you thus: ‘I know the gentleman,<br>I saw him yesterday’—or t‘other day,<br>Or then, or then—’with such and such, and, as you<br>say,<br>There was a gaming, there o‘ertook in ’s rouse,<br>There falling out at tennis’, or perchance<br>‘I saw him enter such a [[house]] of sale’,<br>Videlicet, a brothel, or so forth. See you now,<br>Your bait of falsehood takes this carp of [[Truth|truth]];<br>And thus do we of wisdom and of reach<br>With windlasses and with assays of bias<br>By indirections find directions out.<br>So, by my former lecture and advice,<br>Shall you my son. You have me, have you not? |
| REYNALDO | My lord, I have. |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | God b’wi’ ye. Fare ye [[Well|well]]. |
| REYNALDO | Good my lord. |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | Observe his inclination in yourself. |
| REYNALDO | I shall, my lord. |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | And let him ply his music. |
| REYNALDO | [[Well]], my lord. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Ophelia]] |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | Farewell. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit Reynaldo |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | How now, [[Ophelia]], what’s the matter? |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | Alas, my lord, I have been so affrighted. |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | With what, i’th’ name of God? |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | My lord, as I was sewing in my chamber,<br>Lord [[Hamlet]], with his doublet all unbraced,<br>No hat upon his head, his stockings fouled,<br>Ungartered, and down-gyvèd to his ankle,<br>Pale as his shirt, his knees knocking each other,<br>And with a look so piteous in purport<br>As if he had been loosed out of hell<br>To speak of horrors, he comes before me. |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | Mad for thy [[Love|love]]? |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | My lord, I do not know,<br>But truly I do [[Fear|fear]] it. |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | What said he? |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | He took me by the wrist and held me hard,<br>Then goes he to the length of all his arm,<br>And with his other hand thus o’er his brow<br>He falls to such perusal of my face<br>As a would draw it. Long stayed he so.<br>At last, a little shaking of mine arm,<br>And thrice his head thus waving up and down,<br>He raised a sigh so piteous and profound<br>That it did seem to shatter all his bulk<br>And end his being. That done, he lets me go,<br>And, with his head over his shoulder turned,<br>He seemed to find his way without his eyes,<br>For out o’ doors he went without their help,<br>And to the last bended their light on me. |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | Come, go with me. I will go seek the [[King]].<br>This is the very ecstasy of [[Love|love]],<br>Whose violent property fordoes itself<br>And leads the will to desperate undertakings<br>As oft as any passion under heaven<br>That does afflict our natures. I am sorry—<br>What, have you given him any hard words of late? |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | No, my good lord, but as you did command<br>I did repel his letters and denied<br>His access to me. |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | That hath made him mad.<br>I am sorry that with better [[Speed|speed]] and judgement<br>I had not quoted him. I feared he did but trifle<br>And meant to wreck thee. But beshrew my [[Jealousy|jealousy]]!<br>By heaven, it is as proper to our age<br>To cast beyond ourselves in our opinions<br>As it is common for the younger sort<br>To lack discretion. Come, go we to the [[King]].<br>This must be known, which, being kept close, might<br>move<br>More grief to hide than hate to utter [[Love|love]]. Exeunt |
| *(didascalia)* | 2.2 Flourish. Enter [[King]] [[Claudius]] and [[Queen]] [[Gertrude]], [[Rosencrantz]] and [[Guildenstern]], with others |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | Welcome, dear [[Rosencrantz]] and [[Guildenstern]].<br>Moreover that we much did long to see you,<br>The need we have to use you did provoke<br>Our hasty sending. Something have you heard<br>Of [[Hamlet]]’s transformation—so I call it,<br>Since not th‘exterior nor the inward man<br>Resembles that it was. What it should be,<br>More than his father’s [[Death|death]], that thus hath put him<br>So much from th’understanding of himself,<br>I cannot deem of. I entreat you both<br>That, being of so young days brought up with him,<br>And since so neighboured to his youth and [[Humour|humour]],<br>That you vouchsafe your rest here in our [[Court|court]]<br>Some little [[Time|time]], so by your companies<br>To draw him on to pleasures, and to gather,<br>So much as from occasions you may glean,<br>Whether aught to us unknown afflicts him thus<br>That, opened, lies within our remedy. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | Good gentlemen, he hath much talked of you,<br>And sure I am two men there is not living<br>To whom he more adheres. If it will please you<br>To show us so much gentry and good will<br>As to expend your [[Time|time]] with us a while<br>For the supply and profit of our [[Hope|hope]],<br>Your visitation shall receive such thanks<br>As fits a [[King|king]]’s remembrance. |
| [[Rosencrantz|ROSENCRANTZ]] | Both your majesties<br>Might, by the sovereign power you have of us,<br>Put your dread pleasures more into command<br>Than to entreaty. |
| [[Guildenstern|GUILDENSTERN]] | But we both obey,<br>And here give up ourselves in the full bent<br>To lay our service freely at your feet<br>To be commanded. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | Thanks, [[Rosencrantz]] and gentle [[Guildenstern]]. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | Thanks, [[Guildenstern]] and gentle [[Rosencrantz]].<br>And I beseech you instantly to visit<br>My too-much changed son.—Go, some of ye,<br>And bring the gentlemen where [[Hamlet]] is. |
| [[Guildenstern|GUILDENSTERN]] | Heavens make our presence and our practices<br>Pleasant and helpful to him. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | Ay, amen! |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt [[Rosencrantz]] and [[Guildenstern]] with others Enter [[Polonius]] |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | Th’ambassadors from Norway, my good lord,<br>Are joyfully returned. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | Thou still hast been the father of good news. |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | Have I, my lord? Assure you, my good liege,<br>I hold my duty, as I hold my [[Soul|soul]],<br>Both to my God and to my gracious [[King]].<br>And I do think—or else this brain of mine<br>Hunts not the trail of policy so sure<br>As it hath used to do—that I have found<br>The very cause of [[Hamlet]]’s lunacy. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | O speak of that, that I do long to hear! |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | Give first admittance to th’ambassadors.<br>My news shall be the fruit to that great feast. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | Thyself do grace to them, and bring them in. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit [[Polonius]] |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | He tells me, my sweet [[Queen|queen]], that he hath found<br>The head and source of all your son’s distemper. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | I [[Doubt|doubt]] it is no other but the main—<br>His father’s [[Death|death]] and our o’er-hasty [[Marriage|marriage]]. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | [[Well]], we shall sift him. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Polonius]], Valtemand, and Cornelius Welcome, my good friends. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | Say, Valtemand, what from our brother Norway? |
| VALTEMAND | Most fair return of greetings and desires.<br>Upon our first he sent out to suppress<br>His nephew’s levies, which to him appeared<br>To be a preparation ‘gainst the Polack;<br>But better looked into, he truly found<br>It was against your highness; whereat grieved<br>That so his sickness, age, and impotence<br>Was falsely borne in hand, sends out arrests<br>On Fortinbras, which he, in brief, obeys,<br>Receives rebuke from Norway, and, in fine,<br>Makes vow before his uncle never more<br>To give th’[[Essay|essay]] of arms against your majesty;<br>Whereon old Norway, overcome with joy,<br>Gives him three thousand crowns in annual fee<br>And his commission to employ those soldiers<br>So levied as before, against the Polack,<br>With an entreaty herein further shown,<br>He gives a letter to [[Claudius]]<br><br>That it might please you to give quiet pass<br>Through your dominions for his enterprise<br>On such regards of safety and allowance<br>As therein are set down. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | It likes us [[Well|well]],<br>And at our more considered [[Time|time]] we’ll read,<br>Answer, and think upon this business.<br>Meantime we thank you for your [[Well|well]]-took labour.<br>Go to your rest; at [[Night|night]] we’ll feast together.<br>Most welcome [[Home|home]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt Valtemand and Cornelius |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | This business is very [[Well|well]] ended.<br>My liege, and madam, to expostulate<br>What majesty should be, what duty is,<br>Why day is day, [[Night|night]] [[Night|night]], and [[Time|time]] is [[Time|time]],<br>Were nothing but to waste [[Night|night]], day, and [[Time|time]].<br>Therefore, since brevity is the [[Soul|soul]] of wit,<br>And tediousness the limbs and outward flourishes,<br>I will be brief. Your noble son is mad—<br>‘Mad’ call I it, for to define true [[Madness|madness]],<br>What is’t but to be nothing else but mad?<br>But let that go. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | More matter with less art. |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | Madam, I swear I use no art at all.<br>That he is mad, ‘tis true; ’tis true ‘tis pity,<br>And pity ’tis ‘tis true—a foolish figure,<br>But farewell it, for I will use no art.<br>Mad let us grant him, then; and now remains<br>That we find out the cause of this effect—<br>Or rather say ’the cause of this defect‘,<br>For this effect defective comes by cause.<br>Thus it remains, and the remainder thus.<br>Perpend.<br>I have a daughter—have whilst she is mine—<br>Who in her duty and obedience, mark,<br>Hath given me this. Now gather and surmise.<br>He reads a letter<br><br>’To the celestial and my [[Soul|soul]]’s idol, the most beautified<br>[[Ophelia]]‘—that’s an ill phrase, a vile phrase, ‘beautified’<br>is a vile phrase. But you shall hear—‘these in her<br>excellent white bosom, these’. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | Came this from [[Hamlet]] to her? |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | Good madam, stay a while. I will be faithful.<br>‘[[Doubt]] thou the [[Stars|stars]] are [[Fire|fire]],<br>[[Doubt]] that the sun doth move,<br>[[Doubt]] [[Truth|truth]] to be a liar,<br>But never [[Doubt|doubt]] I [[Love|love]].<br>O dear [[Ophelia]], I am ill at these numbers. I have not<br>art to reckon my groans. But that I [[Love|love]] thee best, O<br>most best, believe it. Adieu.<br>Thine evermore, most dear lady, whilst this<br>[[Machine|machine]] is to him,<br>[[Hamlet]].’<br>This in obedience hath my daughter showed me,<br>And more above hath his solicitings,<br>As they fell out by [[Time|time]], by means, and place,<br>All given to mine ear. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | But how hath she<br>Received his [[Love|love]]? |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | What do you think of me? |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | As of a man faithful and honourable. |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | I would fain prove so. But what might you think,<br>When I had seen this hot [[Love|love]] on the wing,<br>As I perceived it—I must tell you that—<br>Before my daughter told me, what might you,<br>Or my dear majesty your [[Queen|queen]] here, think,<br>If I had played the desk or table-book,<br>Or given my heart a winking mute and dumb,<br>Or looked upon this [[Love|love]] with idle sight—<br>What might you think? No, I went round to work,<br>And my young mistress thus I did bespeak:<br>‘Lord [[Hamlet]] is a prince out of thy star.<br>This must not be’. And then I precepts gave her,<br>That she should lock herself from his resort,<br>Admit no messengers, receive no tokens;<br>Which done, she took the fruits of my advice,<br>And he, repulsèd-a short tale to make—<br>Fell into a sadness, then into a fast,<br>Thence to a watch, thence into a weakness,<br>Thence to a lightness, and, by this declension,<br>Into the [[Madness|madness]] wherein now he raves,<br>And all we wail for. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | (to [[Gertrude]]) Do you think ’tis this? |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | It may be; very likely. |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | Hath there been such a [[Time|time]]—I’d fain know that—<br>That I have positively said ‘ ’Tis so’<br>When it proved otherwise? |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | Not that I know. |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | (touching his head, then his shoulder)<br>Take this from this if this be otherwise.<br>If circumstances lead me I will find<br>Where [[Truth|truth]] is hid, though it were hid indeed |
| *(didascalia)* | Within the centre. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | How may we try it further? |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | You know sometimes he walks four hours together<br>Here in the lobby. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | So he does indeed. |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | At such a [[Time|time]] I’ll loose my daughter to him.<br>(To [[Claudius]]) Be you and I behind an arras then.<br>Mark the encounter. If he [[Love|love]] her not,<br>And be not from his reason fall’n thereon,<br>Let me be no assistant for a state,<br>But keep a farm and carters. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | We will try it. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Prince [[Hamlet]], madly attired, reading on a book |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | But look where sadly the poor wretch comes reading. |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | Away, I do beseech you both, away.<br>I’ll board him presently. O give me leave. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt [[Claudius]] and [[Gertrude]] |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | How does my good Lord [[Hamlet]]? |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | [[Well]], God-‘a’-[[Mercy|mercy]]. |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | Do you know me, my lord? |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Excellent, excellent [[Well|well]]. You’re a fishmonger. |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | Not I, my lord. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Then I would you were so honest a man. |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | Honest, my lord? |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Ay, sir. To be honest, as this world goes, is to be one man picked out of ten thousand. |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | That’s very true, my lord. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | For if the sun breed maggots in a dead dog, being a good kissing carrion—have you a daughter? |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | I have, my lord. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Let her not walk i’th’ sun. Conception is a blessing, but not as your daughter may conceive. Friend, look to’t. |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | (aside) How say you by that? Still harping on my daughter. Yet he knew me not at first—a said I was a fishmonger. A is far gone, far gone, and truly, in my youth I suffered much extremity for [[Love|love]], very near this. I’ll speak to him again.—What do you read, my lord? |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Words, words, words. |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | What is the matter, my lord? |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Between who? |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | I mean the matter you read, my lord. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Slanders, sir; for the satirical slave says here that old men have grey beards, that their faces are wrinkled, their eyes purging thick amber, or plum-tree gum, and that they have a plentiful lack of wit, together with most weak hams. All which, sir, though I most powerfully and potently believe, yet I hold it not honesty to have it thus set down; for you yourself, sir, should be old as I am—if, like a crab, you could go backward. |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | (aside) Though this be [[Madness|madness]], yet there is method in’t.—Will you walk out of the air, my lord? |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Into my [[Grave|grave]]. |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | Indeed, that is out o’th’ air. (Aside) How pregnant sometimes his replies are! A happiness that often [[Madness|madness]] hits on, which reason and [[Sanity|sanity]] could not so prosperously be delivered of. I will leave him, and suddenly contrive the means of meeting between him and my daughter.—My lord, I will take my leave of you. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | You cannot, sir, take from me anything that I will more willingly part withal—except my life, my life, my life. |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | (going) Fare you [[Well|well]], my lord. |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] These tedious old fools! Enter [[Guildenstern]] and [[Rosencrantz]] |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | You go to seek the Lord [[Hamlet]]. There he is. |
| [[Rosencrantz|ROSENCRANTZ]] | God save you, sir. |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Guildenstern|GUILDENSTERN]] to [[Polonius]] Mine honoured lord. Exit [[Polonius]] |
| [[Rosencrantz|ROSENCRANTZ]] | (to [[Hamlet]]) My most dear lord. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | My ex’llent good friends. How dost thou, [[Guildenstern]]? Ah, [[Rosencrantz]]—good lads, how do ye both? |
| [[Rosencrantz|ROSENCRANTZ]] | As the indifferent children of the earth. |
| [[Guildenstern|GUILDENSTERN]] | Happy in that we are not over-happy,<br>On Fortune’s cap we are not the very button. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Nor the soles of her shoe? |
| [[Rosencrantz|ROSENCRANTZ]] | Neither, my lord. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Then you live about her waist, or in the middle of her favour? |
| [[Guildenstern|GUILDENSTERN]] | [[Faith]], her privates we. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | In the secret parts of Fortune? O, most true, she is a strumpet. What’s the news? |
| [[Rosencrantz|ROSENCRANTZ]] | None, my lord, but that the world’s grown honest. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Then is doomsday near. But your news is not true. Let me question more in particular. What have you, my good friends, deserved at the hands of Fortune that she sends you to [[Prison|prison]] hither? |
| [[Guildenstern|GUILDENSTERN]] | [[Prison]], my lord? |
