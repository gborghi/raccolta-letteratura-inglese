| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | Good gentlemen, he hath much talked of you, And sure I am two men there is not living To whom he more adheres. If it will please you To show us so much gentry and good will As to expend your [[Time|time]] with us a while For the supply and profit of our [[Hope|hope]], Your visitation shall receive such thanks As fits a [[King|king]]’s remembrance. |
| [[Rosencrantz|ROSENCRANTZ]] | Both your majesties Might, by the sovereign power you have of us, Put your dread pleasures more into command Than to entreaty. |
| [[Guildenstern|GUILDENSTERN]] | But we both obey, And here give up ourselves in the full bent To lay our service freely at your feet To be commanded. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | Thanks, [[Rosencrantz]] and gentle [[Guildenstern]]. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | Thanks, [[Guildenstern]] and gentle [[Rosencrantz]]. And I beseech you instantly to visit My too-much changed son.—Go, some of ye, And bring the gentlemen where [[Hamlet]] is. |
| [[Guildenstern|GUILDENSTERN]] | Heavens make our presence and our practices Pleasant and helpful to him. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | Ay, amen! Exeunt [[Rosencrantz]] and [[Guildenstern]] with others Enter [[Polonius]] |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | Th’ambassadors from Norway, my good lord, Are joyfully returned. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | Thou still hast been the father of good news. |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | Have I, my lord? Assure you, my good liege, I hold my duty, as I hold my [[Soul|soul]], Both to my God and to my gracious [[King]]. And I do think—or else this brain of mine Hunts not the trail of policy so sure As it hath used to do—that I have found The very cause of [[Hamlet]]’s lunacy. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | O speak of that, that I do long to hear! |
| [[Polonius|POLONIUS]] | Give first admittance to th’ambassadors. My news shall be the fruit to that great feast. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | Thyself do grace to them, and bring them in. Exit [[Polonius]] He tells me, my sweet [[Queen|queen]], that he hath found The head and source of all your son’s distemper. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | I [[Doubt|doubt]] it is no other but the main— His father’s [[Death|death]] and our o’er-hasty [[Marriage|marriage]]. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | [[Well]], we shall sift him. Enter [[Polonius]], Valtemand, and Cornelius Welcome, my good friends. Say, Valtemand, what from our brother Norway? |
