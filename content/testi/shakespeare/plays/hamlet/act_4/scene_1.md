| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | There’s matter in these sighs, these profound heaves;<br>You must translate. ’Tis fit we understand them.<br>Where is your son? |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | Ah, my good lord, what have I seen tonight! |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | What, [[Gertrude]]? How does [[Hamlet]]? |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | Mad as the sea and [[Wind|wind]] when both contend<br>Which is the mightier. In his lawless fit,<br>Behind the arras hearing something stir,<br>He whips his rapier out and cries ‘A rat, a ratl’,<br>And in his brainish apprehension kills<br>The unseen good old man. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | O heavy deed!<br>It had been so with us had we been there.<br>His liberty is full of threats to all—<br>To you yourself, to us, to everyone.<br>Alas, how shall this bloody deed be answered?<br>It will be laid to us, whose [[Providence|providence]]<br>Should have kept short, restrained, and out of haunt<br>This mad young man. But so much was our [[Love|love]],<br>We would not understand what was most fit,<br>But, like the owner of a foul disease,<br>To keep it from divulging, let it feed<br>Even on the pith of life. Where is he gone? |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | To draw apart the [[Body|body]] he hath killed,<br>O’er whom—his very [[Madness|madness]], like some ore<br>Among a mineral of metals base,<br>Shows itself pure—a weeps for what is done. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | O [[Gertrude]], come away!<br>The sun no sooner shall the [[Mountains|mountains]] touch<br>But we will [[Ship|ship]] him hence; and this vile deed<br>We must with all our majesty and skill<br>Both countenance and excuse.—Ho, Guildensternl |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Rosencrantz]] and [[Guildenstern]] |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | Friends both, go join you with some further aid.<br>[[Hamlet]] in [[Madness|madness]] hath [[Polonius]] slain,<br>And from his mother’s closet hath he dragged him.<br>Go seek him out, speak fair, and bring the [[Body|body]]<br>Into the chapel. I pray you haste in this. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt [[Rosencrantz]] and [[Guildenstern]] |
