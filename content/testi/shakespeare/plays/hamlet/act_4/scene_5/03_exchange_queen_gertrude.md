| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | Alack, what noise is this? |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | Where is my Switzers? Let them guard the door. Enter a Messenger What is the matter? |
| MESSENGER | Save yourself, my lord. The ocean, overpeering of his list, Eats not the flats with more impetuous haste Than young [[Laertes]], in a riotous head, O‘erbears your officers. The rabble call him lord, And, as the world were now but to begin, Antiquity forgot, custom not known, The ratifiers and props of every word, They cry ‘Choose we! [[Laertes]] shall be [[King|king]].’ Caps, hands, and tongues applaud it to the clouds, ‘[[Laertes]] shall be [[King|king]], [[Laertes]] [[King|king]].’ |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | How cheerfully on the false trail they cry! A noise within O, this is counter, you false Danish [[dogs]]! |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | The doors are broke. Enter [[Laertes]] with his followers at the door |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | Where is the [[King]]?—Sirs, stand you all without. |
| ALL HIS FOLLOWERS | No, let’s come in. |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | I pray you, give me leave. |
| ALL HIS FOLLOWERS | We will, we will. |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | I thank you. Keep the door. exeunt followers O thou vile [[King|king]], Give me my father. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | Calmly, good [[Laertes]]. |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | That drop of [[Blood|blood]] that’s calm proclaims me bastard, Cries cuckold to my father, brands the harlot Even here between the chaste unsmirchèd brow Of my true mother. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | What is the cause, [[Laertes]], That thy rebellion looks so giant-like?— Let him go, [[Gertrude]]. Do not [[Fear|fear]] our person. There’s such divinity doth hedge a [[King|king]] That treason can but peep to what it would, Acts little of his will.—Tell me, [[Laertes]], Why thou art thus incensed.—Let him go, [[Gertrude]].— Speak, man. |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | Where is my father? |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | Dead. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | (to [[Laertes]]) But not by him. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | Let him demand his fill. |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | How came he dead? I’ll not be juggled with. To hell, allegiance! Vows to the blackest devil! [[Conscience]] and grace to the profoundest pit! I dare damnation. To this point I stand, That both the worlds I give to negligence, Let come what comes. Only I’ll be revenged Most throughly for my father. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | Who shall stay you? |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | My will, not all the world; And for my means, I’ll husband them so [[Well|well]] They shall go far with little. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | Good [[Laertes]], If you [[Desire|desire]] to know the certainty Of your dear father’s [[Death|death]], is’t writ in your [[Revenge|revenge]] That, sweepstake, you will draw both friend and foe, Winner and loser? |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | None but his enemies. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | Will you know them then? |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | To his good friends thus wide I’ll ope my arms, And, like the kind life-rend’ring pelican, Repast them with my [[Blood|blood]]. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | Why, now you speak Like a good [[Child|child]] and a true gentleman. That I am guiltless of your father’s [[Death|death]], And am most sensibly in grief for it, It shall as level to your judgement pierce As day does to your eye. A noise within |
| VOICES | (within) Let her come in. |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | How now, what noise is that? Enter [[Ophelia]] as before O heat dry up my brains! [[Tears]] seven times salt Burn out the sense and virtue of mine eye! By heaven, thy [[Madness|madness]] shall be paid by weight Till our scale turns the beam. O [[Rose|rose]] of May, Dear maid, kind sister, sweet [[Ophelia]]! O heavens, is’t possible a young maid’s wits Should be as mortal as an old man’s life? [[Nature]] is fine in [[Love|love]], and where ’tis fine It sends some precious instance of itself After the thing it loves. |
