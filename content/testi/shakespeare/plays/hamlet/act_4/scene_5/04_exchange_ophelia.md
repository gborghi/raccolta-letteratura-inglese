| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | (sings) They bore him barefaced on the bier, Hey non nony, nony, hey nony, And on his [[Grave|grave]] rained many a tear— Fare you [[Well|well]], my dove. |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | Hadst thou thy wits and didst persuade [[Revenge|revenge]], It could not move thus. |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | You must sing ‘Down, a-down’, and you, ‘Call him a-down-a’. O, how the [[Wheel|wheel]] becomes it! It is the false steward that stole his master’s daughter. [[Laertes|LAERTES]] This nothing’s more than matter. |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | There’s rosemary, that’s for remembrance. Pray, [[Love|love]], remember. And there is pansies; that’s for thoughts. |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | A document in [[Madness|madness]]—thoughts and remembrance fitted. |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | There’s fennel for you, and columbines. There’s rue for you, and here’s some for me. We may call it herb-grace o’ Sundays. O, you must wear your rue with a difference. There’s a daisy. I would give you some violets, but they withered all when my father died. They say a made a good end. (Sings) For bonny sweet Robin is all my joy. |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | Thought and affliction, passion, hell itself She turns to favour and to prettiness. |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | (sings) And will a not come again, And will a not come again? No, no, he is dead, Go to thy [[Death|death]]-bed, He never will come again. His beard as white as [[Snow|snow]], All flaxen was his poll. He is gone, he is gone, And we cast away moan. God ‘a’ [[Mercy|mercy]] on his [[Soul|soul]]. And of all Christian souls, I pray God. God b’wi’ ye. Exeunt [[Ophelia]] and [[Gertrude]] |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | Do you see this, O God? |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | [[Laertes]], I must commune with your grief, Or you deny me right. Go but apart, Make choice of whom your wisest friends you will, And they shall hear and judge ’twixt you and me. If by direct or by collateral hand They find us touched, we will our kingdom give, Our [[Crown|crown]], our life, and all that we call ours, To you in satisfaction. But if not, Be you content to lend your patience to us, And we shall jointly labour with your [[Soul|soul]] To give it due content. |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | Let this be so. His means of [[Death|death]], his obscure burial— No trophy, [[Sword|sword]], nor hatchment o‘er his bones, No noble rite nor formal ostentation— Cry to be heard, as ’twere from heaven to earth, That I must call’t in question. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | So you shall; And where th’offence is, let the great axe fall. I pray you go with me. Exeunt |
