|---|---|
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Queen]] [[Gertrude]] and [[Horatio]] |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | I will not speak with her. |
| [[Horatio|HORATIO]] | She is importunate, Indeed distraught. Her mood will needs be pitied. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | What would she have? |
| [[Horatio|HORATIO]] | She speaks much of her father, says she hears There’s tricks i’th’ world, and hems, and beats her heart, Spurns enviously at straws, speaks things in [[Doubt|doubt]] That carry but half sense. Her speech is nothing, Yet the unshapèd use of it doth move The hearers to collection. They aim at it, And botch the words up fit to their own thoughts, Which, as her winks and nods and gestures yield them, Indeed would make one think there might be thought, Though nothing sure, yet much unhappily. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | ’Twere good she were spoken with, for she may strew Dangerous conjectures in ill-breeding minds. Let her come in. [[Horatio]] withdraws to admit [[Ophelia]] To my sick [[Soul|soul]], as sin’s true [[Nature|nature]] is, Each toy seems prologue to some great amiss. So full of artless [[Jealousy|jealousy]] is [[Guilt|guilt]], It spills itself in fearing to be spilt. Enter [[Ophelia]] mad, her hair down, with a lute |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | Where is the beauteous majesty of [[Denmark]]? |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | How now, [[Ophelia]]? |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | (sings) How should I your true [[Love|love]] know From another one?— By his cockle hat and staff, And his sandal shoon. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | Alas, sweet lady, what imports this [[Song|song]]? |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | Say you? Nay, pray you, mark. (Sings) He is dead and gone, lady, He is dead and gone. At his head a grass-green turf, At his heels a stone. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | Nay, but [[Ophelia]]— |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | Pray you, mark. (Sings) White his shroud as the mountain [[Snow|snow]]— Enter [[King]] [[Claudius]] |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | Alas, look here, my lord. |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | (sings) Larded with sweet [[Flowers|flowers]], Which bewept to the [[Grave|grave]] did—not—go With true-[[Love|love]] showers. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | How do ye, pretty lady? |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | [[Well]], God’ield you. They say the owl was a baker’s daughter. Lord, we know what we are, but know not what we may be. God be at your table! |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | (to [[Gertrude]]) Conceit upon her father. |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | Pray you, let’s have no words of this, but when they ask you what it means, say you this. (Sings) Tomorrow is Saint [[Valentine]]’s day, All in the morning betime, And I a maid at your [[Window|window]] To be your [[Valentine]]. Then up he [[Rose|rose]], and donned his clothes, And dupped the chamber door; Let in the maid, that out a maid Never departed more. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | Pretty [[Ophelia]]— |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | Indeed, la? Without an oath, I’ll make an end on’t. (Sings) By Gis, and by Saint [[Charity]], Alack, and fie for shame! Young men will do’t if they come to‘t, By Cock, they are to blame. Quoth she ‘Before you tumbled me, You promised me to wed.’ So would I ‘a’ done, by yonder sun, An thou hadst not come to my bed. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | (to [[Gertrude]]) How long hath she been thus? |
| [[Ophelia|OPHELIA]] | I [[Hope|hope]] all will be [[Well|well]]. We must be patient. But I cannot choose but weep to think they should lay him i’th’ cold ground. My brother shall know of it. And so I thank you for your good counsel. Come, my coach! Good [[Night|night]], ladies, good [[Night|night]], sweet ladies, good [[Night|night]], good [[Night|night]]. Exit |
