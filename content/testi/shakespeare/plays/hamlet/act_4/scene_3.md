| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[King]] [[Claudius]] |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | I have sent to seek him, and to find the [[Body|body]].<br>How dangerous is it that this man goes loose!<br>Yet must not we put the strong law on him.<br>He’s loved of the distracted multitude,<br>Who like not in their judgement but their eyes,<br>And where ‘tis so, th’offender’s scourge is weighed,<br>But never the offence. To bear all smooth and even,<br>This sudden sending him away must seem<br>Deliberate pause. Diseases desperate grown<br>By desperate appliance are relieved,<br>Or not at all. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Rosencrantz]] |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | How now, what hath befall’n? |
| [[Rosencrantz|ROSENCRANTZ]] | Where the dead [[Body|body]] is bestowed, my lord,<br>We cannot get from him. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | But where is he? |
| [[Rosencrantz|ROSENCRANTZ]] | Without, my lord, guarded to know your pleasure. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | Bring him before us. |
| [[Rosencrantz|ROSENCRANTZ]] | Ho, [[Guildenstern]]! Bring in my lord. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Prince [[Hamlet]] and [[Guildenstern]] |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | Now, [[Hamlet]], where’s [[Polonius]]? |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | At supper. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | At supper? Where? |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Not where he eats, but where a is eaten. A certain convocation of politic worms are e’en at him. Your worm is your only emperor for diet. We fat all creatures else to fat us, and we fat ourselves for maggots. Your fat [[King|king]] and your lean beggar is but variable service—two dishes, but to one table. That’s the end. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | Alas, alas! |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | A man may fish with the worm that hath eat of a [[King|king]], and eat of the fish that hath fed of that worm. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | What dost thou mean by this? |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Nothing but to show you how a [[King|king]] may go a progress through the guts of a beggar. 31 |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | Where is [[Polonius]]? |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | In heaven. Send thither to see. If your messenger find him not there, seek him i’th’ other place yourself. But indeed, if you find him not this month, you shall nose him as you go up the stairs into the lobby. 36 |
| *(didascalia)* | [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] to [[Rosencrantz]] Go seek him there. |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] to [[Rosencrantz]] A will stay till ye come. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit [[Rosencrantz]] |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | [[Hamlet]], this deed of thine, for thine especial safety—<br>Which we do tender as we dearly grieve<br>For that which thou hast done—must send thee hence<br>With fiery quickness. Therefore prepare thyself.<br>The barque is ready, and the [[Wind|wind]] at help,<br>Th’associates tend, and everything is bent<br>For England. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | For England? |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | Ay, [[Hamlet]]. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Good. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | So is it if thou knew’st our purposes. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | I see a cherub that sees them. But come, for<br>England. Farewell, dear mother. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | Thy loving father, [[Hamlet]]. |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | My mother. Father and mother is man and wife, man and wife is one flesh, and so my mother. Come, for England. Exit |
| *(didascalia)* | [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] to [[Guildenstern]] |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | Follow him at foot. Tempt him with [[Speed|speed]] aboard. 56<br>Delay it not. I’ll have him hence tonight.<br>Away, for everything is sealed and done<br>That else leans on th’affair. Pray you, make haste. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit [[Guildenstern]] |
| [[Hamlet|HAMLET]] | And, England, if my [[Love|love]] thou hold‘st at aught—<br>As my great power thereof may give thee sense,<br>Since yet thy cicatrice looks raw and red<br>After the Danish [[Sword|sword]], and thy free awe<br>Pays homage to us—thou mayst not coldly set<br>Our sovereign process, which imports at full,<br>By letters conjuring to that effect,<br>The present [[Death|death]] of [[Hamlet]]. Do it, England,<br>For like the hectic in my [[Blood|blood]] he rages,<br>And thou must cure me. Till I know ’tis done,<br>Howe‘er my haps, my joys were ne’er begun. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit |
