| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[King]] [[Claudius]] and [[Laertes]] |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | Now must your [[Conscience|conscience]] my acquittance seal,<br>And you must put me in your heart for friend,<br>Sith you have heard, and with a knowing ear,<br>That he which hath your noble father slain<br>Pursued my life. |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | It [[Well|well]] appears. But tell me<br>Why you proceeded not against these feats,<br>So crimeful and so capital in [[Nature|nature]],<br>As by your safety, wisdom, all things else,<br>You mainly were stirred up. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | O, for two special reasons,<br>Which may to you perhaps seem much unsinewed,<br>And yet to me they’re strong. The [[Queen]] his mother<br>Lives almost by his looks; and for myself—<br>My virtue or my plague, be it either which—<br>She’s so conjunctive to my life and [[Soul|soul]]<br>That, as the star moves not but in his sphere,<br>I could not but by her. The other motive<br>Why to a public count I might not go<br>Is the great [[Love|love]] the general gender bear him,<br>Who, dipping all his faults in their affection,<br>Would, like the [[spring]] that turneth wood to stone,<br>[[convert|Convert]] his guilts to graces; so that my arrows,<br>Too slightly timbered for so loud a [[Wind|wind]],<br>Would have reverted to my bow again,<br>And not where I had aimed them. |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | And so have I a noble father lost,<br>A sister driven into desp’rate terms,<br>Who has, if praises may go back again,<br>Stood challenger, on mount, of all the age<br>For her perfections. But my [[Revenge|revenge]] will come. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | Break not your sleeps for that. You must not think<br>That we are made of stuff so flat and dull<br>That we can let our beard be shook with danger,<br>And think it pastime. You shortly shall hear more.<br>I loved your father, and we [[Love|love]] ourself.<br>And that, I [[Hope|hope]], will teach you to imagine— |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter a Messenger with letters |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | How now? What news? |
| MESSENGER | Letters, my lord, from [[Hamlet]].<br>This to your majesty; this to the [[Queen]]. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | From [[Hamlet]]? Who brought them? |
| MESSENGER | Sailors, my lord, they say. I saw them not.<br>They were given me by [[Claudio]]. He received them. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | [[Laertes]], you shall hear them.—Leave us. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit Messenger |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | (Reads) ‘High and mighty, you shall know I am set naked on your kingdom. Tomorrow shall I beg leave to see your kingly eyes, when I shall, first asking your pardon, thereunto recount th’occasions of my sudden and more strange return.<br>[[Hamlet]].’<br>What should this mean? Are all the rest come back? Or is it some abuse, and no such thing? |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | Know you the hand? |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | ’Tis [[Hamlet]]’s character.<br>‘Naked’—and in a postscript here he says<br>‘[[Alone]]’. Can you advise me? |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | I’m lost in it, my lord. But let him come.<br>It warms the very sickness in my heart<br>That I shall live and tell him to his teeth,<br>‘Thus diddest thou’. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | If it be so, [[Laertes]]—<br>As how should it be so, how otherwise?—<br>Will you be ruled by me? |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | If so you’ll not o’errule me to a peace. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | To thine own peace. If he be now returned,<br>As checking at his voyage, and that he means<br>No more to undertake it, I will work him<br>To an exploit, now ripe in my device,<br>Under the which he shall not choose but fall;<br>And for his [[Death|death]] no [[Wind|wind]] of blame shall breathe;<br>But even his mother shall uncharge the practice<br>And call it accident. Some two months since<br>Here was a gentleman of Normandy.<br>I’ve seen myself, and served against, the French,<br>And they can [[Well|well]] on horseback; but this gallant<br>Had witchcraft in’t. He grew into his seat,<br>And to such wondrous doing brought his [[Horse|horse]]<br>As had he been incorpsed and demi-natured<br>With the brave beast. So far he passed my thought<br>That I in forgery of shapes and tricks<br>Come short of what he did. |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | A Norman was’t? |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | A Norman. |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | Upon my life, Lamord. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | The very same. |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | I know him [[Well|well]]. He is the brooch indeed,<br>And gem, of all the nation. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | He made confession of you,<br>And gave you such a masterly report<br>For art and exercise in your defence,<br>And for your rapier most especially,<br>That he cried out ‘twould be a sight indeed<br>If one could match you. Sir, this report of his<br>Did [[Hamlet]] so envenom with his envy<br>That he could nothing do but wish and beg<br>Your sudden coming o’er to play with him.<br>Now, out of this— |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | What out of this, my lord? |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | [[Laertes]], was your father dear to you?<br>Or are you like the painting of a sorrow,<br>A face without a heart? |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | Why ask you this? |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | Not that I think you did not [[Love|love]] your father,<br>But that I know [[Love|love]] is begun by [[Time|time]],<br>And that I see, in passages of proof,<br>[[Time]] qualifies the spark and [[Fire|fire]] of it.<br>[[Hamlet]] comes back. What would you undertake<br>To show yourself your father’s son in deed<br>More than in words? |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | To cut his throat i’th’ church. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | No place indeed should murder sanctuarize.<br>[[Revenge]] should have no bounds. But, good [[Laertes]],<br>Will you do this?—keep close within your chamber.<br>[[Hamlet]] returned shall know you are come [[Home|home]].<br>We’ll put on those shall praise your excellence,<br>And set a [[Double|double]] varnish on the fame<br>The Frenchman gave you; bring you, in fine, together,<br>And wager on your heads. He, being remiss,<br>Most generous, and free from all contriving,<br>Will not peruse the foils; so that with ease,<br>Or with a little shuffling, you may choose<br>A [[Sword|sword]] unbated, and, in a pass of practice,<br>Requite him for your father. |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | I will do’t,<br>And for that purpose I’ll anoint my [[Sword|sword]].<br>I bought an unction of a mountebank<br>So mortal that, but dip a knife in it,<br>Where it draws [[Blood|blood]] no cataplasm so rare,<br>Collected from all simples that have virtue<br>Under the [[Moon|moon]], can save the thing from [[Death|death]]<br>That is but scratched withal. I’ll touch my point<br>With this contagion, that if I gall him slightly,<br>It may be [[Death|death]]. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | Let’s further think of this;<br>Weigh what convenience both of [[Time|time]] and means<br>May fit us to our shape. If this should fail,<br>And that our drift look through our bad performance,<br>‘Twere better not essayed. Therefore this project<br>Should have a back or second that might hold<br>If this should blast in proof. Soft, let me see.<br>We’ll make a solemn wager on your cunnings ...<br>I ha’t! When in your motion you are hot and dry—<br>As make your bouts more violent to that end—<br>And that he calls for drink, I’ll have prepared him<br>A chalice for the nonce, whereon but sipping,<br>If he by chance escape your venomed stuck,<br>Our purpose may hold there.— |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Queen]] [[Gertrude]] |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | How now, sweet [[Queen]]? |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | One woe doth tread upon another’s heel,<br>So fast they follow. Your sister’s drowned, [[Laertes]]. |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | Drowned? O, where? |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | There is a willow grows aslant a brook<br>That shows his hoar [[Leaves|leaves]] in the glassy stream.<br>Therewith fantastic garlands did she make<br>Of crow-[[Flowers|flowers]], nettles, daisies, and long purples,<br>That liberal shepherds give a grosser name,<br>But our cold maids do dead men’s fingers call them.<br>There on the pendent boughs her crownet weeds<br>Clamb’ring to hang, an envious sliver broke,<br>When down the weedy trophies and herself<br>Fell in the weeping brook. Her clothes spread wide,<br>And [[Mermaid|mermaid]]-like a while they bore her up;<br>Which [[Time|time]] she chanted snatches of old tunes,<br>As one incapable of her own distress,<br>Or like a creature native and endued<br>Unto that element. But long it could not be<br>Till that her garments, heavy with their drink,<br>Pulled the poor wretch from her melodious lay<br>To muddy [[Death|death]]. |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | Alas, then is she drowned. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Gertrude|GERTRUDE]] | Drowned, drowned. |
| [[Laertes|LAERTES]] | Too much of [[Water|water]] hast thou, poor [[Ophelia]],<br>And therefore I forbid my [[Tears|tears]]. But yet<br>It is our trick; [[Nature|nature]] her custom holds,<br>Let shame say what it will.<br>He weeps<br>When these are gone,<br>The woman will be out. Adieu, my lord.<br>I have a speech of [[Fire|fire]] that fain would blaze,<br>But that this folly douts it. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit |
| [[King|KING]] [[Claudius|CLAUDIUS]] | Let’s follow, [[Gertrude]].<br>How much I had to do to calm his rage!<br>Now [[Fear|fear]] I this will give it start again;<br>Therefore let’s follow. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt |
