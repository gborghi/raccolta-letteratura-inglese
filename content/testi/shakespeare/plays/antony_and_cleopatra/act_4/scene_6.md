| *(didascalia)* | Flourish. Enter Agrippa, Caesar, witih [[Enobarbus]] and Dolabella |
| CAESAR | Go forth, Agrippa, and begin the fight.<br>Our will is Antony be took alive.<br>Make it so known. |
| AGRIPPA | Caesar, I shall. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit |
| CAESAR | The [[Time|time]] of universal peace is near.<br>Prove this a prosp’rous day, the three-nooked world<br>Shall bear the olive freely. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter a Messenger |
| MESSENGER | Antony<br>Is come into the field. |
| CAESAR | Go charge Agrippa<br>Plant those that have revolted in the van,<br>That Antony may seem to spend his fury<br>Upon himself. 10 |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt Messenger at one door, Caesar and Dolabella at another |
| [[Enobarbus|ENOBARBUS]] | Alexas did revolt, and went to Jewry on<br>Affairs of Antony; there did dissuade<br>Great Herod to incline himself to Caesar<br>And leave his master, Antony. For this pains,<br>Caesar hath hanged him. Camidius and the rest 15<br>That fell away have entertainment but<br>No honourable trust. I have done ill,<br>Of which I do accuse myself so sorely<br>That I will joy no more. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter a Soldier of Caesar’s |
| SOLDIER | [[Enobarbus]], Antony<br>Hath after thee sent all thy treasure, with<br>His bounty overplus. The messenger<br>Came on my guard, and at thy tent is now<br>Unloading of his mules. |
| [[Enobarbus|ENOBARBUS]] | I give it you. |
| SOLDIER | Mock not, [[Enobarbus]],<br>I tell you true. Best you safed the bringer<br>Out of the host. I must attend mine office,<br>Or would have done’t myself. Your Emperor<br>Continues still a Jove. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit |
| [[Enobarbus|ENOBARBUS]] | I am [[Alone|alone]] the [[Villain|villain]] of the earth,<br>And feel I am so most. O Antony,<br>Thou mine of bounty, how wouldst thou have paid<br>My better service, when my turpitude<br>Thou dost so [[Crown|crown]] with gold! This blows my heart.<br>If swift thought break it not, a swifter mean<br>Shall outstrike thought; but thought will do‘t, I feel.<br>I fight against thee? No, I will go seek<br>Some ditch wherein to die. The foul’st best fits<br>My latter part of life. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit |
