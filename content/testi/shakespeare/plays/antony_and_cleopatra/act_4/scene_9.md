| *(didascalia)* | Alarum. Enter Antony again in a march; drummers and trumpeters; Scarus, with others |
| ANTONY | We have beat him to his camp. Run one before,<br>And let the [[Queen]] know of our gests. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit a soldier |
| ANTONY | Tomorrow,<br>Before the sun shall see’s, we’ll spill the [[Blood|blood]]<br>That has today escaped. I thank you all,<br>For doughty-handed are you, and have fought<br>Not as you served the cause, but as’t had been<br>Each man’s like mine. You have shown all Hectors. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter the [[City|city]], clip your wives, your friends, |
| ANTONY | Tell them your feats whilst they with joyful [[Tears|tears]]<br>Wash the congealment from your wounds, and kiss<br>The honoured gashes whole. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Cleopatra]] |
| ANTONY | (To Scarus)<br>Give me thy hand.<br>To this great fairy I’ll commend thy acts,<br>Make her thanks bless thee.<br>(To [[Cleopatra]], embracing her) O’thou day o’th’ world,<br>Chain mine armed neck; leap thou, attire and all,<br>Through proof of harness to my heart, and there<br>Ride on the pants triumphing. |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | Lord of lords!<br>O infinite virtue, com’st thou smiling from<br>The world’s great snare uncaught? |
| ANTONY | My [[Nightingale|nightingale]],<br>We have beat them to their beds. What, girl, though<br>grey<br>Do something mingle with our younger brown, yet<br>ha’ we<br>A brain that nourishes our nerves, and can<br>Get goal for goal of youth. Behold this man.<br>Commend unto his lips thy favouring hand;<br>Kiss it, my warrior.<br>Scarus kisses [[Cleopatra]]’s hand<br>He hath fought today<br>As if a god, in hate of mankind, had<br>Destroyed in such a shape. |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | I’ll give thee, friend,<br>An armour all of gold. It was a [[King|king]]’s. |
| ANTONY | He has deserved it, were it carbuncled<br>Like holy Phoebus’ car. Give me thy hand.<br>Through Alexandria make a jolly march.<br>Bear our hacked targets like the men that owe them.<br>Had our great palace the capacity<br>To camp this host, we all would sup together<br>And drink carouses to the next day’s fate,<br>Which promises royal peril. Trumpeters,<br>With brazen din blast you the [[City|city]]’s ear;<br>Make mingle with our rattling taborins,<br>That heaven and earth may strike their sounds<br>together,<br>Applauding our approach. |
| *(didascalia)* | Trumpets sound. Exeunt |
