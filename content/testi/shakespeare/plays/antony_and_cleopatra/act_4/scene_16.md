| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Cleopatra]] and her maids aloft, with [[Charmian]] and Iras |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | O [[Charmian]], I will never go from hence. |
| [[Charmian|CHARMIAN]] | Be comforted, dear madam. |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | No, I will not.<br>All strange and terrible events are welcome,<br>But comforts we despise. Our size of sorrow,<br>Proportioned to our cause, must be as great<br>As that which makes it. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Diomedes below |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | How now? Is he dead? |
| DIOMEDES | His [[Death|death]]’s upon him, but not dead.<br>Look out o’th’ other side your monument.<br>His guard have brought him thither. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter below Antony, borne by the guard |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | O sun, Burn the great sphere thou mov‘st in; darkling stand<br>The varying shore o’th’ world! O Antony, 11<br>Antony, Antony! Help, [[Charmian]],<br>Help, Iras, help, help, friends below!<br>Let’s draw him hither. |
| ANTONY | Peace. Not Caesar’s valour<br>Hath o’erthrown Antony, but Antony’s<br>Hath triumphed on itself. |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | So it should be, That none but Antony should conquer Antony.<br>But woe ’tis so! |
| ANTONY | I am dying, [[Egypt]], dying. Only<br>I here importune [[Death|death]] awhile until<br>Of many thousand kisses the poor last<br>I lay upon thy lips. |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | I dare not, dear, Dear, my lord, pardon. I dare not,<br>Lest I be taken. Nor th’imperious show<br>Of the full-fortuned Caesar ever shall<br>Be brooched with me, if knife, drugs, serpents, have<br>Edge, sting, or operation. I am safe.<br>Your wife, [[Octavia]], with her modest eyes<br>And still conclusion, shall acquire no [[Honour|honour]]<br>Demuring upon me. But come, come, Antony.—<br>Help me, my women.—We must draw thee up.<br>Assist, good friends. |
| ANTONY | O quick, or I am gone! |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | Here’s sport indeed. How heavy weighs my lord!<br>Our strength is all gone into heaviness,<br>That makes the weight. Had I great Juno’s power<br>The strong-winged Mercury should fetch thee up<br>And set thee by Jove’s side. Yet come a little.<br>Wishers were ever fools. O come, come, come!<br>They heave Antony aloft to [[Cleopatra]]<br>And welcome, welcome! Die when thou hast lived,<br>Quicken with kissing. Had my lips that power,<br>Thus would I wear them out.<br>They kiss |
| ALL THE LOOKERS-ON | A heavy sight. |
| ANTONY | I am dying, [[Egypt]], dying.<br>Give me some wine, and let me speak a little. |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | No, let me speak, and let me rail so high<br>That the false hussy Fortune break her [[Wheel|wheel]],<br>Provoked by my offence. |
| ANTONY | One word, sweet [[Queen|queen]].<br>Of Caesar seek your [[Honour|honour]], with your safety. O! |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | They do not go together. |
| ANTONY | Gentle, hear me.<br>None about Caesar trust but Proculeius. |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | My resolution and my hands I’ll trust,<br>None about Caesar. |
| ANTONY | The miserable change now at my end<br>Lament nor sorrow at, but please your thoughts<br>In feeding them with those my former fortunes,<br>Wherein I lived the greatest prince o’th’ world,<br>The noblest; and do now not basely die,<br>Not cowardly put off my helmet to<br>My countryman; a Roman by a Roman<br>Valiantly vanquished. Now my spirit is going;<br>I can no more. |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | Noblest of men, woot die?<br>Hast thou no care of me? Shall I abide<br>In this dull world, which in thy absence is<br>No better than a sty?<br>Antony dies<br>O see, my women,<br>The [[Crown|crown]] o’th’ earth doth melt. My lord!<br>O, withered is the garland of the war.<br>The soldier’s pole is fall’n. Young boys and girls<br>Are level now with men. The odds is gone,<br>And there is nothing left remarkable<br>Beneath the visiting [[Moon|moon]].<br>She falls |
| [[Charmian|CHARMIAN]] | O, quietness, lady! |
| IRAS | She’s dead, too, our sovereign. |
| [[Charmian|CHARMIAN]] | Lady! |
| IRAS | Madam! |
| [[Charmian|CHARMIAN]] | O, madam, madam, madam! |
| IRAS | Royal [[Egypt]], Empress! |
| [[Charmian|CHARMIAN]] | Peace, peace, Iras! |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | (recovering)<br>No more but e’en a woman, and commanded<br>By such poor passion as the maid that milks<br>And does the meanest chores. It were for me<br>To throw my sceptre at the injurious gods,<br>To tell them that this world did equal theirs<br>Till they had stol’n our [[Jewel|jewel]]. All’s but naught.<br>Patience is sottish, and impatience does<br>Become a dog that’s mad. Then is it sin<br>To rush into the secret [[house]] of [[Death|death]]<br>Ere [[Death|death]] dare come to us? How do you, women?<br>What, what, good cheer! Why, how now, [[Charmian]]?<br>My noble girls! Ah, women, women! Look,<br>Our lamp is spent, it’s out. Good sirs, take heart;<br>We’ll bury him, and then what’s brave, what’s noble,<br>Let’s do it after the high Roman fashion,<br>And make [[Death|death]] proud to take us. Come, away.<br>This case of that huge spirit now is cold.<br>Ah, women, women! Come. We have no friend<br>But resolution, and the briefest end. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt, those above bearing off Antony’s [[Body|body]] |
