| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Cleopatra]] and [[Enobarbus]] |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | I will be even with thee, [[Doubt|doubt]] it not. |
| [[Enobarbus|ENOBARBUS]] | But why, why, why? |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | Thou hast forspoke my being in these wars,<br>And sayst it is not fit. |
| [[Enobarbus|ENOBARBUS]] | [[Well]], is it, is it? |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | Is’t not denounced against us? Why should not we<br>Be there in person? |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Enobarbus|ENOBARBUS]] aside [[Well]], I could reply |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | If we should serve with [[Horse|horse]] and mares together,<br>The [[Horse|horse]] were merely lost; the mares would bear<br>A soldier and his [[Horse|horse]]. |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | What is’t you say? |
| [[Enobarbus|ENOBARBUS]] | Your presence needs must puzzle Antony,<br>Take from his heart, take from his brain, from’s [[Time|time]]<br>What should not then be spared. He is already<br>Traduced for levity; and ’tis said in Rome<br>That Photinus, an eunuch, and your maids<br>Manage this war. |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | Sink Rome, and their tongues rot<br>That speak against us! A charge we bear i’th’ war,<br>And as the president of my kingdom will<br>Appear there for a man. Speak not against it.<br>I will not stay behind. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Antony and Camidius |
| [[Enobarbus|ENOBARBUS]] | Nay, I have done.<br>Here comes the Emperor. |
| ANTONY | Is it not strange, Camidius,<br>That from Tarentum and Brundisium<br>He could so quickly cut the Ionian Sea<br>And take in Toryne?—You have heard on’t, sweet? |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | Celerity is never more admired<br>Than by the negligent. |
| ANTONY | A good rebuke,<br>Which might have [[Well|well]] becomed the best of men<br>To taunt at slackness. Camidius, we<br>Will fight with him by sea. |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | By sea—what else? |
| CAMIDIUS | Why will my lord do so? |
| ANTONY | For that he dares us to’t. |
| [[Enobarbus|ENOBARBUS]] | So hath my lord dared him to single fight. |
| CAMIDIUS | Ay, and to wage this battle at Pharsalia,<br>Where Caesar fought with [[Pompey]]. But these offers<br>Which serve not for his vantage, he shakes off,<br>And so should you. |
| [[Enobarbus|ENOBARBUS]] | Your ships are not [[Well|well]] manned, Your mariners are muleters, reapers, people 35<br>Engrossed by swift impress. In Caesar’s fleet<br>Are those that often have ’gainst [[Pompey]] fought.<br>Their ships are yare, yours heavy. No disgrace<br>Shall fall you for refusing him at sea,<br>Being prepared for land. |
| ANTONY | By sea, by sea. |
| [[Enobarbus|ENOBARBUS]] | Most worthy sir, you therein throw away<br>The absolute soldiership you have by land;<br>Distract your army, which doth most consist<br>Of war-marked footmen; leave unexecuted<br>Your own renowned knowledge; quite forgo<br>The way which promises assurance, and<br>Give up yourself merely to chance and hazard<br>From firm security. |
| ANTONY | I’ll fight at sea. |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | I have sixty sails, Caesar none better. |
| ANTONY | Our overplus of shipping will we burn,<br>And with the rest full-manned, from th‘head of<br>Actium<br>Beat th’approaching Caesar. But if we fail,<br>We then can do’t at land. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter a Messenger |
| ANTONY | Thy business? |
| MESSENGER | The news is true, my lord. He is descried. Caesar has taken Toryne. |
| ANTONY | Can he be there in person? ’Tis impossible;<br>Strange that his power should be. Camidius,<br>Our nineteen legions thou shalt hold by land,<br>And our twelve thousand [[Horse|horse]]. We’ll to our [[Ship|ship]].<br>Away, my Thetis! |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter a Soldier |
| ANTONY | How now, worthy soldier? |
| SOLDIER | O noble Emperor, do not fight by sea.<br>Trust not to rotten planks. Do you misdoubt<br>This [[Sword|sword]] and these my wounds? Let th’Egyptians<br>And the Phoenicians go a-ducking; we<br>Have used to conquer standing on the earth,<br>And fighting foot to foot. |
| ANTONY | [[Well]], [[Well|well]]; away! |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt Antony, [[Cleopatra]], and [[Enobarbus]] |
| SOLDIER | By Hercules, I think I am i’th’ right. |
| CAMIDIUS | Soldier, thou art; but his whole action grows<br>Not in the power on’t. So our leader’s led,<br>And we are women’s men. |
| SOLDIER | You keep by land<br>The legions and the [[Horse|horse]] whole, do you not? |
| CAMIDIUS | Marcus Octavius, Marcus Justeius,<br>Publicola and Caelius are for sea,<br>But we keep whole by land. This [[Speed|speed]] of Caesar’s<br>Carries beyond belief. |
| SOLDIER | While he was yet in Rome<br>His power went out in such distractions<br>As beguiled all spies. |
| CAMIDIUS | Who’s his lieutenant, hear you? |
| SOLDIER | They say, one Taurus. |
| CAMIDIUS | [[Well]] I know the man. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter a Messenger |
| MESSENGER | The Emperor calls Camidius. |
| CAMIDIUS | With news the [[Time|time]]’s in labour, and throws forth<br>Each minute some. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt |
