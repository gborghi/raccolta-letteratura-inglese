| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Cleopatra]], [[Charmian]], Iras, and Alexas |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | Where is the fellow? |
| ALEXAS | Half afeard to come. |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | Go to, go to. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter the Messenger as before |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | Come hither, sir. |
| ALEXAS | Good majesty,<br>Herod of Jewry dare not look upon you<br>But when you are [[Well|well]] pleased. |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | That Herod’s head<br>I’ll have; but how, when Antony is gone,<br>Through whom I might command it?<br>(To the Messenger)<br>Come thou near. |
| MESSENGER | Most gracious majesty! |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | Didst thou behold<br>[[Octavia]]? |
| MESSENGER | Ay, dread [[Queen]]. |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | Where? |
| MESSENGER | Madam, in Rome.<br>I looked her in the face, and saw her led<br>Between her brother and [[Mark Antony]]. |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | Is she as tall as me? |
| MESSENGER | She is not, madam. |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | Didst hear her speak? Is she shrill-tongued or low? |
| MESSENGER | Madam, I heard her speak. She is low-voiced. |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | That’s not so good. He cannot like her long. |
| [[Charmian|CHARMIAN]] | Like her? O Isis, ’is impossible! |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | I think so, [[Charmian]]. Dull of tongue, and dwarfish.<br>What majesty is in her gait? Remember<br>If e‘er thou looked’st on majesty. |
| MESSENGER | She creeps.<br>Her motion and her station are as one.<br>She shows a [[Body|body]] rather than a life,<br>A statue than a breather. |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | Is this certain? |
| MESSENGER | Or I have no observance. |
| [[Charmian|CHARMIAN]] | Three in [[Egypt]]<br>Cannot make better note. |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | He’s very knowing,<br>I do perceive’t. There’s nothing in her yet.<br>The fellow has good judgement. |
| [[Charmian|CHARMIAN]] | Excellent. |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | (to the Messenger)<br>Guess at her years, I prithee. |
| MESSENGER | Madam,<br>She was a widow— |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | Widow? [[Charmian]], hark. |
| MESSENGER | And I do think she’s thirty. |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | Bear’st thou her face in mind? Is’t long or round? |
| MESSENGER | Round, even to faultiness. |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | For the most part, too, they are foolish that are so.<br>Her hair—what colour? |
| MESSENGER | Brown, madam; and her forehead<br>As low as she would wish it. |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | (giving money)<br>There’s gold for thee.<br>Thou must not take my former sharpness ill.<br>I will employ thee back again. I find thee<br>Most fit for business. Go, make thee ready.<br>Our letters are prepared. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit Messenger |
| [[Charmian|CHARMIAN]] | A proper man. |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | Indeed he is so. I repent me much<br>That so I harried him. Why, methinks, by him,<br>This creature’s no such thing. |
| [[Charmian|CHARMIAN]] | Nothing, madam. |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | The man hath seen some majesty, and should know. |
| [[Charmian|CHARMIAN]] | Hath he seen majesty? Isis else defend,<br>And serving you so long! |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | I have one thing more to ask him yet, good<br>[[Charmian]].<br>But ’tis no matter. Thou shalt bring him to me<br>Where I will write. All may be [[Well|well]] enough. |
| [[Charmian|CHARMIAN]] | I warrant you, madam. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt |
