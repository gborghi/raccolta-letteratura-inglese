| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Enobarbus]] and Eros, meeting |
| [[Enobarbus|ENOBARBUS]] | How now, friend Eros? |
| EROS | There’s strange news come, sir. |
| [[Enobarbus|ENOBARBUS]] | What, man? |
| EROS | Caesar and [[Lepidus]] have made wars upon [[Pompey]]. [[Enobarbus|ENOBARBUS]] This is old. What is the [[success]]? |
| EROS | Caesar, having made use of him in the wars ’gainst [[Pompey]], presently denied him rivality, would not let him partake in the glory of the action, and, not resting here, accuses him of letters he had formerly wrote to [[Pompey]]; upon his own appeal seizes him; so the poor third is up, till [[Death|death]] enlarge his confine. |
| [[Enobarbus|ENOBARBUS]] | Then, world, thou hast a pair of chops, no more,<br>And throw between them all the food thou hast,<br>They’ll grind the one the other. Where’s Antony? |
| EROS | He’s walking in the [[Garden|garden]], thus, and spurns<br>The rush that lies before him, cries ‘[[Fool]] [[Lepidus]]!’<br>And threats the throat of that his officer<br>That murdered [[Pompey]]. |
| [[Enobarbus|ENOBARBUS]] | Our great navy’s rigged. |
| EROS | For [[Italy]] and Caesar. More, Domitius:<br>My lord desires you presently. My news<br>I might have told hereafter. |
| [[Enobarbus|ENOBARBUS]] | ’Twill be naught.<br>But let it be; bring me to Antony. |
| EROS | Come, sir. Exeunt |
