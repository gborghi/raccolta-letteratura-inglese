| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Demetrius]] and Philo |
| PHILO | Nay, but this dotage of our General’s<br>O‘erflows the measure. Those his goodly eyes,<br>That o’er the files and musters of the war<br>Have glowed like plated Mars, now bend, now turn<br>The office and devotion of their view<br>Upon a tawny front. His captain’s heart,<br>Which in the scuffles of great fights hath burst<br>The buckles on his breast, reneges all temper,<br>And is become the bellows and the fan<br>To cool a gipsy’s lust. |
| *(didascalia)* | Flourish. Enter Antony, [[Cleopatra]], her ladies, the train, with eunuchs fanning her |
| PHILO | Look where they come.<br>Take but good note, and you shall see in him<br>The triple pillar of the world transformed<br>Into a strumpet’s [[Fool|fool]]. Behold and see. |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | (to Antony)<br>If it be [[Love|love]] indeed, tell me how much. |
| ANTONY | There’s beggary in the [[Love|love]] that can be reckoned. |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | I’ll set a bourn how far to be beloved. |
| ANTONY | Then must thou needs find out new heaven, new earth. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter a Messenger |
| MESSENGER | News, my good lord, from Rome. |
| ANTONY | Grates me: the sum. |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | Nay, hear them, Antony.<br>Fulvia perchance is angry; or who knows<br>If the scarce-bearded Caesar have not sent<br>His powerful mandate to you: ‘Do this, or this,<br>Take in that kingdom and enfranchise that.<br>Perform’t, or else we damn thee.’ |
| ANTONY | How, my [[Love|love]]? |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | Perchance? Nay, and most like.<br>You must not stay here longer. Your dismission<br>Is come from Caesar, therefore hear it, Antony.<br>Where’s Fulvia’s process—Caesar’s, I would say—<br>both?<br>Call in the messengers. As I am [[Egypt]]’s [[Queen|queen]],<br>Thou blushest, Antony, and that [[Blood|blood]] of thine<br>Is Caesar’s homager; else so thy cheek pays shame<br>When shrill-tongued Fulvia scolds. The messengers! |
| ANTONY | Let Rome in Tiber melt, and the wide arch<br>Of the ranged empire fall. Here is my space.<br>Kingdoms are clay. Our dungy earth alike<br>Feeds beast as man. The nobleness of life<br>Is to do thus; when such a mutual pair<br>And such a twain can do’t—in which I bind<br>On [[Pain|pain]] of punishment the world to weet—<br>We stand up peerless. |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] aside Excellent falsehood! |
| ANTONY | Why did he marry Fulvia and not [[Love|love]] her?<br>I’ll seem the [[Fool|fool]] I am not. (To Antony) Antony<br>Will be himself. |
| ANTONY | But stirred by [[Cleopatra]].<br>Now, for the [[Love|love]] of [[Love]] and her soft hours<br>Let’s not confound the [[Time|time]] with conference harsh.<br>There’s not a minute of our lives should stretch<br>Without some pleasure now. What sport tonight? |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | Hear the ambassadors. |
| ANTONY | Fie, wrangling [[Queen|queen]],<br>Whom everything becomes—to chide, to laugh,<br>To weep; how every passion fully strives<br>To make itself, in thee, fair and admired!<br>No messenger but thine; and all [[Alone|alone]]<br>Tonight we’ll wander through the streets and note<br>The qualities of people. Come, my [[Queen|queen]].<br>Last [[Night|night]] you did [[Desire|desire]] it. (To the Messenger) Speak<br>not to us. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt Antony and [[Cleopatra]] with the train, and by another door the Messenger |
| [[Demetrius|DEMETRIUS]] | Is Caesar with Antonius prized so slight? |
| PHILO | Sir, sometimes when he is not Antony<br>He comes too short of that great property<br>Which still should go with Antony. |
| [[Demetrius|DEMETRIUS]] | I am full sorry<br>That he approves the common liar who<br>Thus speaks of him at Rome; but I will [[Hope|hope]]<br>Of better deeds tomorrow. Rest you happy. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt |
