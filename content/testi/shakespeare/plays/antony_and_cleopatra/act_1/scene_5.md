| *(didascalia)* | Enter |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Cleopatra]],<br>[[Charmian]], Iras, and Mardian |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | [[Charmian]]! |
| [[Charmian|CHARMIAN]] | Madam? |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | (yawning)<br>Ha, ha. Give me to drink mandragora. |
| [[Charmian|CHARMIAN]] | Why, madam? |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | That I might [[Sleep|sleep]] out this great gap of [[Time|time]]<br>My Antony is away. |
| [[Charmian|CHARMIAN]] | You think of him too much. |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | O, ’tis treason! |
| [[Charmian|CHARMIAN]] | Madam, I trust not so. |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | Thou, eunuch Mardian! |
| MARDIAN | What’s your highness’ pleasure? |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | Not now to hear thee sing. I take no pleasure<br>In aught an eunuch has. ’is [[Well|well]] for thee<br>That, being unseminared, thy freer thoughts<br>May not fly forth of [[Egypt]]. Hast thou affections? |
| MARDIAN | Yes, gracious madam. |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | Indeed? |
| MARDIAN | Not in deed, madam, for I can do nothing<br>But what indeed is honest to be done.<br>Yet have I fierce affections, and think<br>What [[Venus]] did with Mars. |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | O, [[Charmian]], Where think‘st thou he is now? Stands he or sits he?<br>Or does he walk? Or is he on his [[Horse|horse]]?<br>O happy [[Horse|horse]], to bear the weight of Antony!<br>Do bravely, [[Horse|horse]], for wot’st thou whom thou<br>mov’st?—<br>The demi-Atlas of this earth, the arm<br>And burgonet of men. He’s speaking now,<br>Or murmuring ‘Where’s my serpent of old Nile?’—<br>For so he calls me. Now I feed myself<br>With most delicious [[Poison|poison]]. Think on me,<br>That am with Phoebus’ amorous pinches black,<br>And wrinkled deep in [[Time|time]]. Broad-fronted Caesar,<br>When thou wast here above the ground I was<br>A morsel for a monarch, and great [[Pompey]]<br>Would stand and make his eyes grow in my brow.<br>There would he anchor his aspect, and die<br>With looking on his life. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Alexas |
| ALEXAS | Sovereign of [[Egypt]], hail! |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | How much unlike art thou [[Mark Antony]]!<br>Yet, coming from him, that great medicine hath<br>With his tinct gilded thee. How goes it<br>With my brave [[Mark Antony]]? |
| ALEXAS | Last thing he did, dear [[Queen]], He kissed—the last of many doubled kisses—<br>This [[Orient|orient]] pearl. His speech sticks in my heart. |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | Mine ear must pluck it thence. |
| ALEXAS | ‘Good friend,’ quoth he, ‘Say the firm Roman to great [[Egypt]] sends<br>This treasure of an oyster; at whose foot,<br>To mend the petty present, I will piece<br>Her opulent throne with kingdoms. All the East,<br>Say thou, shall call her mistress.’ So he nodded,<br>And soberly did mount an arm-jaunced steed,<br>Who neighed so high that what I would have spoke<br>Was beastly dumbed by him. |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | What, was he sad or merry? |
| ALEXAS | Like to the [[Time|time]] o’th’ year between the extremes<br>Of hot and cold, he was nor sad nor merry. |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | O [[Well|well]] divided disposition! Note him,<br>Note him, good [[Charmian]], ‘tis the man; but note him.<br>He was not sad, for he would shine on those<br>That make their looks by his; he was not merry,<br>Which seemed to tell them his remembrance lay<br>In [[Egypt]] with his joy; but between both.<br>O heavenly mingle! Be’st thou sad or merry,<br>The violence of either thee becomes;<br>So does it no man else. Met’st thou my posts? |
| ALEXAS | Ay, madam, twenty several messengers.<br>Why do you send so thick? |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | Who’s born that day<br>When I forget to send to Antony<br>Shall die a beggar. Ink and paper, [[Charmian]]! I<br>Welcome, my good Alexas. Did I, [[Charmian]],<br>Ever [[Love|love]] Caesar so? |
| [[Charmian|CHARMIAN]] | O, that brave Caesar! |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | Be choked with such another emphasis!<br>Say ‘the brave Antony’. |
| [[Charmian|CHARMIAN]] | The valiant Caesar. |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | By Isis, I will give thee bloody teeth<br>If thou with Caesar paragon again<br>My man of men. |
| [[Charmian|CHARMIAN]] | By your most gracious pardon, I sing but after you. |
| [[Cleopatra|CLEOPATRA]] | My salad days, When I was green in judgement, cold in [[Blood|blood]],<br>To say as I said then. But come, away,<br>Get me ink and paper.<br>He shall have every day a several greeting,<br>Or I’ll unpeople [[Egypt]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt |
