| *(didascalia)* | Enter Octavius reading a letter, [[Lepidus]], and their train |
| CAESAR | You may see, [[Lepidus]], and henceforth know,<br>It is not Caesar’s natural vice to hate<br>Our great competitor. From Alexandria<br>This is the news: he fishes, drinks, and wastes<br>The lamps of [[Night|night]] in revel; is not more manlike<br>Than [[Cleopatra]], nor the [[Queen|queen]] of Ptolemy<br>More womanly than he; hardly gave audience<br>Or vouchsafed to think he had partners. You shall find<br>there<br>A man who is the abstract of all faults<br>That all men follow. |
| [[Lepidus|LEPIDUS]] | I must not think there are<br>Evils enough to darken all his goodness.<br>His faults in him seem as the spots of heaven,<br>More fiery by [[Night|night]]’s blackness; hereditary<br>Rather than purchased; what he cannot change<br>Than what he chooses. |
| CAESAR | You are too indulgent. Let’s grant it is not<br>Amiss to tumble on the bed of Ptolemy,<br>To give a kingdom for a mirth, to sit<br>And keep the turn of tippling with a slave,<br>To reel the streets at [[Noon|noon]], and stand the buffet<br>With knaves that smells of sweat. Say this becomes<br>him—<br>As his composure must be rare indeed<br>Whom these things cannot blemish—yet must Antony<br>No way excuse his foils when we do bear<br>So great weight in his lightness. If he filled<br>His vacancy with his voluptuousness,<br>Full surfeits and the dryness of his bones<br>Call on him for’t. But to confound such [[Time|time]]<br>That drums him from his sport, and speaks as loud<br>As his own state and ours—’tis to be chid<br>As we rate boys who, being mature in knowledge,<br>Pawn their experience to their present pleasure,<br>And so [[Rebel|rebel]] to judgement. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter a Messenger |
| [[Lepidus|LEPIDUS]] | Here’s more news. |
| MESSENGER | Thy biddings have been done, and every hour,<br>Most noble Caesar, shalt thou have report<br>How ’tis abroad. [[Pompey]] is strong at sea,<br>And it appears he is beloved of those<br>That only have feared Caesar. To the ports<br>The discontents repair, and men’s reports<br>Give him much wronged. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit |
| CAESAR | I should have known no less. It hath been taught us from the primal state<br>That he which is was wished until he were,<br>And the ebbed man, ne‘er loved till ne’er worth [[Love|love]],<br>Comes deared by being lacked. This common [[Body|body]],<br>Like to a vagabond [[Flag|flag]] upon the stream,<br>Goes to, and back, lackeying the varying tide,<br>To rot itself with motion. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter a second Messenger |
| SECOND MESSENGER | Caesar, I bring thee word Menecrates and Menas, famous pirates,<br>Makes the sea serve them, which they ear and wound<br>With keels of every kind. Many hot inroads<br>They make in [[Italy]]. The borders maritime<br>Lack [[Blood|blood]] to think on‘t, and flush youth revolt.<br>No vessel can peep forth but ’tis as soon<br>Taken as seen; for [[Pompey]]’s name strikes more<br>Than could his war resisted. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit |
| CAESAR | Antony,<br>Leave thy lascivious wassails. When thou once<br>Was beaten from Modena, where thou slew‘st<br>Hirtius and Pansa, consuls, at thy heel<br>Did famine follow, whom thou fought’st against—<br>Though daintily brought up—with patience more<br>Than savages could suffer. Thou didst drink<br>The stale of horses, and the gilded puddle<br>Which beasts would cough at. Thy palate then did<br>deign<br>The roughest berry on the rudest hedge.<br>Yea, like the stag when [[Snow|snow]] the pasture sheets,<br>The barks of trees thou browsed. On the Alps<br>It is reported thou didst eat strange flesh,<br>Which some did die to look on; and all this—<br>It wounds thine [[Honour|honour]] that I speak it now—<br>Was borne so like a soldier that thy cheek<br>So much as lanked not. |
| [[Lepidus|LEPIDUS]] | ’Tis pity of him. |
| CAESAR | Let his shames quickly<br>Drive him to Rome. ‘Tis [[Time|time]] we twain<br>Did show ourselves i’th’ field; and to that end<br>Assemble we immediate council. [[Pompey]]<br>Thrives in our idleness. |
| [[Lepidus|LEPIDUS]] | Tomorrow, Caesar,<br>I shall be furnished to inform you rightly<br>Both what by sea and land I can be able<br>To front this present [[Time|time]]. |
| CAESAR | Till which encounter<br>It is my business, too. Farewell. |
| [[Lepidus|LEPIDUS]] | Farewell, my lord. What you shall know meantime<br>Of stirs abroad I shall beseech you, sir,<br>To let me be partaker. |
| CAESAR | [[Doubt]] not, sir. I knew it for my bond. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt |
