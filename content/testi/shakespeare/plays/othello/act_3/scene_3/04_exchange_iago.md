| [[Iago|IAGO]] | She did deceive her father, marrying you, And when she seemed to shake and [[Fear|fear]] your looks She loved them most. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | And so she did. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Why, go to, then. She that so young could give out such a seeming, To seel her father’s eyes up close as oak, He thought ’twas witchcraft! But I am much to blame. I humbly do beseech you of your pardon For too much loving you. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | I am bound to thee for ever. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | I see this hath a little dashed your spirits. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Not a jot, not a jot. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | I’[[Faith|faith]], I [[Fear|fear]] it has. I [[Hope|hope]] you will consider what is spoke Comes from my [[Love|love]]. But I do see you’re moved. I am to pray you not to strain my speech To grosser issues, nor to larger reach Than to suspicion. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | I will not. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Should you do so, my lord, My speech should fall into such vile [[success]] Which my thoughts aimed not. [[Cassio]]’s my worthy friend. My lord, I see you’re moved. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | No, not much moved. I do not think but [[Desdemona]]’s honest. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Long live she so, and long live you to think so! |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | And yet how [[Nature|nature]], erring from itself— |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Ay, there’s the point; as, to be bold with you, Not to affect many proposed matches Of her own clime, complexion, and degree, Whereto we see in all things [[Nature|nature]] tends. Foh, one may smell in such a will most rank, Foul disproportions, thoughts unnatural! But pardon me. I do not in position Distinctly speak of her, though I may [[Fear|fear]] Her will, recoiling to her better judgement, May fall to match you with her country forms And happily repent. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Farewell, farewell. If more thou dost perceive, let me know more. Set on thy wife to observe. Leave me, [[Iago]]. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | (going) My lord, I take my leave. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Why did I marry? This honest creature doubtless Sees and knows more, much more, than he unfolds. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | (returning) My lord, I would I might entreat your [[Honour|honour]] To scan this thing no farther. Leave it to [[Time|time]]. Although ’tis fit that [[Cassio]] have his place— For sure he fills it up with great ability— Yet, if you please to hold him off a while, You shall by that perceive him and his means. Note if your lady strain his entertainment With any strong or vehement importunity. Much will be seen in that. In the mean [[Time|time]], Let me be thought too busy in my fears— As worthy cause I have to [[Fear|fear]] I am— And hold her free, I do beseech your [[Honour|honour]]. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | [[Fear]] not my [[government]]. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | I once more take my leave. Exit |
