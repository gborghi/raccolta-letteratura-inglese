| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Desdemona]], [[Emilia]], and the Clown |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Do you know, sirrah, where Lieutenant [[Cassio]] lies? |
| CLOWN | I dare not say he lies anywhere. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Why, man? |
| CLOWN | He’s a soldier, and for me to say a soldier lies, ’tis stabbing. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Go to. Where lodges he? |
| CLOWN | To tell you where he lodges is to tell you where I lie. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Can anything be made of this? |
| CLOWN | I know not where he lodges, and for me to devise a lodging and say he lies here, or he lies there, were to lie in mine own throat. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Can you enquire him out, and be edified by report? |
| CLOWN | I will catechize the world for him; that is, make questions, and by them answer. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Seek him, bid him come hither, tell him I have moved my lord on his behalf, and [[Hope|hope]] all will be [[Well|well]]. |
| CLOWN | To do this is within the compass of man’s wit, and therefore I will attempt the doing it. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Where should I lose the handkerchief, [[Emilia]]? |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | I know not, madam. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Believe me, I had rather have lost my purse<br>Full of crusadoes, and but my noble Moor<br>Is true of mind, and made of no such baseness<br>As jealous creatures are, it were enough<br>To put him to ill thinking. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | Is he not jealous? |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Who, he? I think the sun where he was born<br>Drew all such humours from him. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Othello]] |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | Look where he comes. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | I will not leave him now till [[Cassio]]<br>Be called to him. How is’t with you, my lord? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | [[Well]], my good lady. (Aside) O hardness to dissemble!—<br>How do you, [[Desdemona]]? |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | [[Well]], my good lord. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Give me your hand. This hand is moist, my lady. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | It hath felt no age, nor known no sorrow. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | This argues fruitfulness and liberal heart.<br>Hot, hot and moist—this hand of yours requires<br>A sequester from liberty; fasting, and [[Prayer|prayer]],<br>Much castigation, exercise devout,<br>For here’s a young and sweating devil here<br>That commonly rebels. ’Tis a good hand,<br>A frank one. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | You may indeed say so,<br>For ’twas that hand that gave away my heart. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | A liberal hand. The hearts of old gave hands,<br>But our new heraldry is hands, not hearts. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | I cannot speak of this. Come now, your promise. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | What promise, chuck? |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | I have sent to bid [[Cassio]] come speak with you. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | I have a salt and sorry rheum offends me.<br>Lend me thy handkerchief. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | (offering a handkerchief) Here, my lord. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | That which I gave you. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | I have it not about me. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Not? |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | No, [[Faith|faith]], my lord. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | That’s a fault. That handkerchief<br>Did an Egyptian to my mother give.<br>She was a charmer, and could almost read<br>The thoughts of people. She told her, while she kept it<br>’Twould make her amiable, and subdue my father<br>Entirely to her [[Love|love]]; but if she lost it,<br>Or made a gift of it, my father’s eye<br>Should hold her loathèd, and his spirits should hunt<br>After new fancies. She, dying, gave it me,<br>And bid me, when my fate would have me wived,<br>To give it her. I did so, and take heed on’t.<br>Make it a darling, like your precious eye.<br>To lose’t or give’t away were such perdition<br>As nothing else could match. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Is’t possible? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | ’Tis true. There’s [[Magic|magic]] in the web of it.<br>A sibyl that had numbered in the world<br>The sun to course two hundred compasses<br>In her prophetic fury sewed the work.<br>The worms were hallowed that did breed the silk,<br>And it was dyed in mummy, which the skilful<br>Conserved of maidens’ hearts. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | I’[[Faith|faith]], is’t true? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Most veritable. Therefore look to’t [[Well|well]]. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Then would to God that I had never seen it! |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Ha, wherefore? |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Why do you speak so startingly and rash? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Is’t lost? Is’t gone? Speak, is’t out o’th’ way? |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Heaven bless us! |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Say you? |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | It is not lost, but what an if it were? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | HOW? |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | I say it is not lost. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Fetch’t, let me see’t. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Why, so I can, sir, but I will not now.<br>This is a trick to put me from my suit.<br>Pray you let [[Cassio]] be received again. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Fetch me the handkerchief. My mind misgives. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Come, come, you’ll never meet a more sufficient man. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | The handkerchief. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | I pray, talk me of [[Cassio]]. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | The handkerchief. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | A man that all his [[Time|time]]<br>Hath founded his good fortunes on your [[Love|love]],<br>Shared dangers with you— |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | The handkerchief. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | I’[[Faith|faith]], you are to blame. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | ’Swounds! Exit |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | Is not this man jealous? |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | I ne’er saw this before.<br>Sure there’s some [[Wonder|wonder]] in this handkerchief.<br>I am most unhappy in the loss of it. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | ’Tis not a year or two shows us a man.<br>They are all but stomachs, and we all but food.<br>They eat us hungrily, and when they are full,<br>They belch us. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Iago]] and [[Cassio]] |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | Look you, [[Cassio]] and my husband. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | (to [[Cassio]])<br>There is no other way. ‘Tis she must do’t,<br>And lo, the happiness! Go and importune her. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | How now, good [[Cassio]]? What’s the news with you? |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | Madam, my former suit. I do beseech you<br>That by your virtuous means I may again<br>Exist and be a member of his [[Love|love]]<br>Whom I, with all the office of my heart,<br>Entirely [[Honour|honour]]. I would not be delayed.<br>If my offence be of such mortal kind<br>That nor my service past, nor present sorrows,<br>Nor purposed merit in futurity<br>Can ransom me into his [[Love|love]] again,<br>But to know so must be my benefit.<br>So shall I clothe me in a forced content,<br>And shut myself up in some other course<br>To fortune’s alms. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Alas, thrice-gentle [[Cassio]]!<br>My advocation is not now in tune.<br>My lord is not my lord, nor should I know him<br>Were he in favour as in [[Humour|humour]] altered.<br>So help me every spirit sanctified<br>As I have spoken for you all my best,<br>And stood within the blank of his displeasure<br>For my free speech! You must a while be patient.<br>What I can do I will, and more I will<br>Than for myself I dare. Let that suffice you. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Is my lord angry? |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | He went hence but now,<br>And certainly in strange unquietness. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Can he be angry? I have seen the cannon<br>When it hath blown his ranks into the air,<br>And, like the devil, from his very arm<br>Puffed his own brother; and is he angry?<br>Something of moment then. I will go meet him.<br>There’s matter in’t indeed, if he be angry. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | I prithee do so. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit [[Iago]] |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Something sure of state,<br>Either from [[Venice]] or some unhatched practice<br>Made demonstrable here in Cyprus to him,<br>Hath puddled his clear spirit; and in such cases<br>Men’s natures wrangle with inferior things,<br>Though great ones are their object. ’Tis even so;<br>For let our finger ache and it indues<br>Our other, healthful members even to a sense<br>Of [[Pain|pain]]. Nay, we must think men are not gods,<br>Nor of them look for such observancy<br>As fits the bridal. Beshrew me much, [[Emilia]],<br>I was—unhandsome warrior as I am—<br>Arraigning his unkindness with my [[Soul|soul]];<br>But now I find I had suborned the witness,<br>And he’s indicted falsely. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | Pray heaven it be<br>State matters, as you think, and no conception<br>Nor no jealous toy concerning you. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Alas the day, I never gave him cause. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | But jealous souls will not be answered so.<br>They are not ever jealous for the cause,<br>But jealous for they’re jealous. It is a [[Monster|monster]]<br>Begot upon itself, born on itself. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Heaven keep the [[Monster|monster]] from [[Othello]]’s mind. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | Lady, amen. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | I will go seek him. [[Cassio]], walk here about.<br>If I do find him fit I’ll move your suit,<br>And seek to effect it to my uttermost. |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | I humbly thank your ladyship. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt [[Desdemona]] and [[Emilia]] |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Bianca]] |
| [[Bianca|BIANCA]] | Save you, friend [[Cassio]]. |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | What make you from [[Home|home]]?<br>How is’t with you, my most fair [[Bianca]]?<br>I’[[Faith|faith]], sweet [[Love|love]], I was coming to your [[house]]. |
| [[Bianca|BIANCA]] | And I was going to your lodging, [[Cassio]].<br>What, keep a week away? Seven days and nights,<br>Eightscore-eight hours, and [[lovers]]’ absent hours<br>More tedious than the dial eightscore times!<br>O weary reckoning! |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | Pardon me, [[Bianca]],<br>I have this while with leaden thoughts been pressed,<br>But I shall in a more continuate [[Time|time]]<br>Strike off this score of absence. Sweet [[Bianca]],<br>Take me this work out.<br>He gives her [[Desdemona]]’s napkin |
| [[Bianca|BIANCA]] | O [[Cassio]], whence came this?<br>This is some token from a newer friend.<br>To the felt absence now I feel a cause.<br>Is’t come to this? [[Well]], [[Well|well]]. |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | Go to, woman.<br>Throw your vile guesses in the devil’s teeth,<br>From whence you have them. You are jealous now<br>That this is from some mistress, some remembrance.<br>No, by my [[Faith|faith]], [[Bianca]]. |
| [[Bianca|BIANCA]] | Why, whose is it? |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | I know not, neither. I found it in my chamber.<br>I like the work [[Well|well]]. Ere it be demanded—<br>As like enough it witt—I would have it copied.<br>Take it, and do’t, and leave me for this [[Time|time]]. |
| [[Bianca|BIANCA]] | Leave you? Wherefore? |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | I do attend here on the general,<br>And think it no addition, nor my wish,<br>To have him see me womaned. |
| [[Bianca|BIANCA]] | Why, I pray you? |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | Not that I [[Love|love]] you not. |
| [[Bianca|BIANCA]] | But that you do not [[Love|love]] me.<br>I pray you bring me on the way a little,<br>And say if I shall see you soon at [[Night|night]]. |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | ’Tis but a little way that I can bring you,<br>For I attend here; but I’ll see you soon. |
| [[Bianca|BIANCA]] | ’Tis very good. I must be circumstanced. Exeunt |
