| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Desdemona]], [[Cassio]], and [[Emilia]] |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Be thou assured, good [[Cassio]], I will do<br>All my abilities in thy behalf. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | Good madam, do. I warrant it grieves my husband<br>As if the cause were his. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | O, that’s an honest fellow. Do not [[Doubt|doubt]], [[Cassio]],<br>But I will have my lord and you again<br>As friendly as you were. |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | Bounteous madam,<br>Whatever shall become of [[Michael]] [[Cassio]]<br>He’s never anything but your true servant. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | I know’t. I thank you. You do [[Love|love]] my lord.<br>You have known him long, and be you [[Well|well]] assured<br>He shall in strangeness stand no farther off<br>Than in a politic distance. |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | Ay, but, lady,<br>That policy may either last so long,<br>Or feed upon such nice and wat’rish diet,<br>Or breed itself so out of circumstance,<br>That, I being absent and my place supplied,<br>My general will forget my [[Love|love]] and service. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Do not [[Doubt|doubt]] that. Before [[Emilia]] here<br>I give thee warrant of thy place. Assure thee,<br>If I do vow a [[Friendship|friendship]] I’ll perform it<br>To the last article. My lord shall never rest.<br>I’ll watch him tame, and talk him out of patience.<br>His bed shall seem a school, his board a shrift.<br>I’ll intermingle everything he does<br>With [[Cassio]]’s suit. Therefore be merry, [[Cassio]],<br>For thy solicitor shall rather die<br>Than give thy cause away. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Othello]] and [[Iago]] |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | Madam, here comes my lord. |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | Madam, I’ll take my leave. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Why, stay, and hear me speak. |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | Madam, not now. I am very ill at ease,<br>Unfit for mine own purposes. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | [[Well]], do your discretion. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit [[Cassio]] |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Ha! I like not that. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | What dost thou say? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Nothing, my lord. Or if, I know not what. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Was not that [[Cassio]] parted from my wife? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | [[Cassio]], my lord? No, sure, I cannot think it,<br>That he would steal away so guilty-like<br>Seeing your coming. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | I do believe ’twas he. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | How now, my lord?<br>I have been talking with a suitor here,<br>A man that languishes in your displeasure. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Who is’t you mean? |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Why, your lieutenant, [[Cassio]]; good my lord,<br>If I have any grace or power to move you,<br>His present [[Reconciliation|reconciliation]] take;<br>For if he be not one that truly loves you,<br>That errs in ignorance and not in cunning,<br>I have no judgement in an honest face.<br>I prithee call him back. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Went he hence now? |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Yes, [[Faith|faith]], so humbled<br>That he hath left part of his grief with me<br>To suffer with him. Good [[Love|love]], call him back. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Not now, sweet Desdemon. Some other [[Time|time]]. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | But shall’t be shortly? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | The sooner, sweet, for you. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Shall’t be tonight at supper? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | No, not tonight. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Tomorrow dinner, then? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | I shall not dine at [[Home|home]].<br>I meet the captains at the citadel. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Why then, tomorrow [[Night|night]], or Tuesday morn,<br>On Tuesday [[Noon|noon]], or [[Night|night]], on Wednesday morn—<br>I prithee name the [[Time|time]], but let it not<br>Exceed three days. In [[Faith|faith]], he’s penitent,<br>And yet his trespass, in our common reason—<br>Save that, they say, the wars must make example<br>Out of her best—is not almost a fault<br>T‘incur a private check. When shall he come?<br>Tell me, [[Othello]]. I [[Wonder|wonder]] in my [[Soul|soul]]<br>What you would ask me that I should deny,<br>Or stand so mamm’ring on? What, [[Michael]] [[Cassio]],<br>That came a-wooing with you, and so many a [[Time|time]]<br>When I have spoke of you dispraisingly<br>Hath ta‘en your part—to have so much to-do<br>To bring him in? By’r Lady, I could do much. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Prithee, no more. Let him come when he will.<br>I will deny thee nothing. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Why, this is not a boon.<br>’Tis as I should entreat you wear your gloves,<br>Or feed on nourishing dishes, or keep you warm,<br>Or sue to you to do a peculiar profit<br>To your own person. Nay, when I have a suit<br>Wherein I mean to touch your [[Love|love]] indeed,<br>It shall be full of poise and difficult weight,<br>And fearful to be granted. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | I will deny thee nothing,<br>Whereon I do beseech thee grant me this:<br>To leave me but a little to myself. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Shall I deny you? No. Farewell, my lord. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Farewell, my [[Desdemona]]. I’ll come to thee straight. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | [[Emilia]], come. (To [[Othello]]) Be as your fancies teach you.<br>Whate’er you be, I am obedient. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt [[Desdemona]] and [[Emilia]] |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Excellent wretch! Perdition catch my [[Soul|soul]]<br>But I do [[Love|love]] thee, and when I [[Love|love]] thee not,<br>Chaos is come again. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | My noble lord. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | What dost thou say, [[Iago]]? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Did [[Michael]] [[Cassio]], when you wooed my lady,<br>Know of your [[Love|love]]? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | He did, from first to last. Why dost thou ask? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | But for a satisfaction of my thought,<br>No further harm. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Why of thy thought, [[Iago]]? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | I did not think he had been acquainted with her. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | O yes, and went between us very oft. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Indeed? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Indeed? Ay, indeed. Discern’st thou aught in that?<br>Is he not honest? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Honest, my lord? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Honest? Ay, honest. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | My lord, for aught I know. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | What dost thou think? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Think, my lord? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | ‘Think, my lord?’ By heaven, thou echo‘st me<br>As if there were some [[Monster|monster]] in thy thought<br>Too hideous to be shown! Thou dost mean something.<br>I heard thee say even now thou liked’st not that,<br>When [[Cassio]] left my wife. What didst not like?<br>And when I told thee he was of my counsel<br>In my whole course of wooing, thou cried‘st ‘Indeed?’<br>And didst contract and purse thy brow together<br>As if thou then hadst shut up in thy brain<br>Some horrible conceit. If thou dost [[Love|love]] me,<br>Show me thy thought. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | My lord, you know I [[Love|love]] you. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | I think thou dost,<br>And for I know thou‘rt full of [[Love|love]] and honesty,<br>And weigh’st thy words before thou giv’st them<br>breath,<br>Therefore these stops of thine fright me the more;<br>For such things in a false disloyal knave<br>Are tricks of custom, but in a man that’s just,<br>They’re close dilations, working from the heart<br>That passion cannot rule. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | For [[Michael]] [[Cassio]],<br>I dare be sworn I think that he is honest. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | I think so too. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Men should be what they seem,<br>Or those that be not, would they might seem none. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Certain, men should be what they seem. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Why then, I think [[Cassio]]’s an honest man. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Nay, yet there’s more in this.<br>I prithee speak to me as to thy thinkings,<br>As thou dost ruminate, and give thy worst of thoughts<br>The worst of words. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Good my lord, pardon me.<br>Though I am bound to every act of duty,<br>I am not bound to that all slaves are free to.<br>Utter my thoughts? Why, say they are vile and false,<br>As where’s that palace whereinto foul things<br>Sometimes intrude not? Who has that breast so pure<br>But some uncleanly apprehensions<br>Keep leets and law-days, and in sessions sit<br>With meditations lawful? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Thou dost conspire against thy friend, [[Iago]],<br>If thou but think‘st him wronged and mak’st his ear<br>A stranger to thy thoughts. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | I do beseech you,<br>Though I perchance am vicious in my guess—<br>As I confess it is my [[Nature|nature]]’s plague<br>To spy into abuses, and oft my [[Jealousy|jealousy]]<br>Shapes faults that are not—that your wisdom then,<br>From one that so imperfectly conceits,<br>Would take no notice, nor build yourself a trouble<br>Out of his scattering and unsure observance.<br>It were not for your quiet nor your good,<br>Nor for my manhood, honesty, and wisdom,<br>To let you know my thoughts. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | What dost thou mean? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Good name in man and woman, dear my lord,<br>Is the immediate [[Jewel|jewel]] of their souls.<br>Who steals my purse steals trash; ‘tis something,<br>nothing;<br>’Twas mine, ’tis his, and has been slave to thousands.<br>But he that filches from me my good name<br>Robs me of that which not enriches him<br>And makes me poor indeed. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | By heaven, I’ll know thy thoughts. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | You cannot, if my heart were in your hand;<br>Nor shall not whilst ’tis in my custody. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Ha! |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | O, beware, my lord, of [[Jealousy|jealousy]].<br>It is the green-eyed [[Monster|monster]] which doth mock<br>The meat it feeds on. That cuckold lives in bliss<br>Who, certain of his fate, loves not his wronger.<br>But O, what damned minutes tells he o’er<br>Who dotes yet doubts, suspects yet fondly loves! |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | O misery! |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Poor and content is rich, and rich enough,<br>But riches fineless is as poor as [[Winter|winter]]<br>To him that ever fears he shall be poor.<br>Good God the souls of all my tribe defend<br>From [[Jealousy|jealousy]]! |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Why, why is this?<br>Think‘st thou I’d make a life of [[Jealousy|jealousy]],<br>To follow still the changes of the [[Moon|moon]]<br>With fresh suspicions? No, to be once in [[Doubt|doubt]]<br>Is once to be resolved. Exchange me for a goat<br>When I shall turn the business of my [[Soul|soul]]<br>To such exsufflicate and blowed surmises<br>Matching thy inference. ’Tis not to make me jealous<br>To say my wife is fair, feeds [[Well|well]], loves company,<br>Is free of speech, sings, plays, and dances [[Well|well]].<br>Where virtue is, these are more virtuous,<br>Nor from mine own weak merits will I draw<br>The smallest [[Fear|fear]] or [[Doubt|doubt]] of her revolt,<br>For she had eyes and chose me. No, [[Iago]],<br>I’ll see before I [[Doubt|doubt]]; when I [[Doubt|doubt]], prove;<br>And on the proof, there is no more but this:<br>Away at once with [[Love|love]] or [[Jealousy|jealousy]]. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | I am glad of this, for now I shall have reason<br>To show the [[Love|love]] and duty that I bear you<br>With franker spirit. Therefore, as I am bound,<br>Receive it from me. I speak not yet of proof.<br>Look to your wife. Observe her [[Well|well]] with [[Cassio]].<br>Wear your eyes thus: not jealous, nor secure.<br>I would not have your free and noble [[Nature|nature]]<br>Out of [[Self|self]]-bounty be abused. Look to’t.<br>I know our country disposition [[Well|well]].<br>In [[Venice]] they do let God see the pranks<br>They dare not show their husbands; their best<br>[[Conscience|conscience]]<br>Is not to leave’t undone, but keep’t unknown. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Dost thou say so? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | She did deceive her father, marrying you,<br>And when she seemed to shake and [[Fear|fear]] your looks<br>She loved them most. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | And so she did. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Why, go to, then.<br>She that so young could give out such a seeming,<br>To seel her father’s eyes up close as oak,<br>He thought ’twas witchcraft! But I am much to blame.<br>I humbly do beseech you of your pardon<br>For too much loving you. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | I am bound to thee for ever. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | I see this hath a little dashed your spirits. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Not a jot, not a jot. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | I’[[Faith|faith]], I [[Fear|fear]] it has.<br>I [[Hope|hope]] you will consider what is spoke<br>Comes from my [[Love|love]]. But I do see you’re moved.<br>I am to pray you not to strain my speech<br>To grosser issues, nor to larger reach<br>Than to suspicion. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | I will not. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Should you do so, my lord,<br>My speech should fall into such vile [[success]]<br>Which my thoughts aimed not. [[Cassio]]’s my worthy<br>friend.<br>My lord, I see you’re moved. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | No, not much moved.<br>I do not think but [[Desdemona]]’s honest. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Long live she so, and long live you to think so! |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | And yet how [[Nature|nature]], erring from itself— |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Ay, there’s the point; as, to be bold with you,<br>Not to affect many proposed matches<br>Of her own clime, complexion, and degree,<br>Whereto we see in all things [[Nature|nature]] tends.<br>Foh, one may smell in such a will most rank,<br>Foul disproportions, thoughts unnatural!<br>But pardon me. I do not in position<br>Distinctly speak of her, though I may [[Fear|fear]]<br>Her will, recoiling to her better judgement,<br>May fall to match you with her country forms<br>And happily repent. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Farewell, farewell.<br>If more thou dost perceive, let me know more.<br>Set on thy wife to observe. Leave me, [[Iago]]. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | (going) My lord, I take my leave. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Why did I marry? This honest creature doubtless<br>Sees and knows more, much more, than he unfolds. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | (returning)<br>My lord, I would I might entreat your [[Honour|honour]]<br>To scan this thing no farther. Leave it to [[Time|time]].<br>Although ’tis fit that [[Cassio]] have his place—<br>For sure he fills it up with great ability—<br>Yet, if you please to hold him off a while,<br>You shall by that perceive him and his means.<br>Note if your lady strain his entertainment<br>With any strong or vehement importunity.<br>Much will be seen in that. In the mean [[Time|time]],<br>Let me be thought too busy in my fears—<br>As worthy cause I have to [[Fear|fear]] I am—<br>And hold her free, I do beseech your [[Honour|honour]]. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | [[Fear]] not my [[government]]. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | I once more take my leave. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | This fellow’s of exceeding honesty,<br>And knows all qualities with a learned spirit<br>Of human dealings. If I do prove her haggard,<br>Though that her jesses were my dear heart-strings<br>I’d whistle her off and let her down the [[Wind|wind]]<br>To prey at fortune. Haply for I am black,<br>And have not those soft parts of conversation<br>That chamberers have; or for I am declined<br>Into the vale of years—yet that’s not much—<br>She’s gone. I am abused, and my relief<br>Must be to loathe her. O curse of [[Marriage|marriage]],<br>That we can call these delicate creatures ours<br>And not their appetites! I had rather be a toad<br>And live upon the vapour of a dungeon<br>Than keep a corner in the thing I [[Love|love]]<br>For others’ uses. Yet ‘tis the plague of great ones;<br>Prerogatived are they less than the base.<br>’Tis destiny unshunnable, like [[Death|death]].<br>Even then this forked plague is fated to us<br>When we do quicken. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Desdemona]] and [[Emilia]] |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Look where she comes.<br>If she be false, O then heaven mocks itself!<br>I’ll not believe’t. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | How now, my dear [[Othello]]?<br>Your dinner, and the generous islanders<br>By you invited, do attend your presence. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | I am to blame. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Why do you speak so faintly? Are you not [[Well|well]]? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | I have a [[Pain|pain]] upon my forehead here. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | [[Faith]], that’s with watching. ’will away again.<br>Let me but bind it hard, within this hour<br>It will be [[Well|well]]. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Your napkin is too little.<br>He puts the napkin from him. It drops.<br>Let it [[Alone|alone]]. Come, I’ll go in with you. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | I am very sorry that you are not [[Well|well]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt [[Othello]] and [[Desdemona]] |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | (taking up the napkin)<br>I am glad I have found this napkin.<br>This was her first remembrance from the Moor.<br>My wayward husband hath a hundred times<br>Wooed me to steal it, but she so loves the token—<br>For he conjured her she should ever keep it—<br>That she reserves it evermore about her<br>To kiss and talk to. I’ll ha’ the work ta’en out,<br>And give’t [[Iago]]. What he will do with it,<br>Heaven knows, not I.<br>I nothing, but to please his fantasy. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Iago]] |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | How now, what do you here [[Alone|alone]]? |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | Do not you chide. I have a thing for you. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | You have a thing for me? It is a common thing. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | Ha? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | To have a foolish wife. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | O, is that all? What will you give me now<br>For that same handkerchief? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | What handkerchief? |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | What handkerchief?<br>Why, that the Moor first gave to [[Desdemona]],<br>That which so often you did bid me steal. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Hast stol’n it from her? |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | No, [[Faith|faith]], she let it drop by negligence,<br>And to th‘advantage I, being here, took’t up.<br>Look, here ’tis. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | A good wench! Give it me. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | What will you do with it, that you have been so earnest<br>To have me filch it? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Why, what is that to you?<br>He takes the napkin |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | If it be not for some purpose of import,<br>Give’t me again. Poor lady, she’ll run mad<br>When she shall lack it. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Be not acknown on’t. I have use for it. Go, leave me. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit [[Emilia]] |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | I will in [[Cassio]]’s lodging lose this napkin,<br>And let him find it. Trifles light as air<br>Are to the jealous confirmations strong<br>As proofs of holy writ. This may do something.<br>The Moor already changes with my [[Poison|poison]].<br>Dangerous conceits are in their natures poisons,<br>Which at the first are scarce found to distaste,<br>But, with a little act upon the [[Blood|blood]],<br>Burn like the mines of sulphur. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Othello]] |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | I did say so.<br>Look where he comes. Not poppy nor mandragora<br>Nor all the drowsy syrups of the world<br>Shall ever medicine thee to that sweet [[Sleep|sleep]]<br>Which thou owedst yesterday. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Ha, ha, false to me? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Why, how now, general? No more of that. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Avaunt, be gone. Thou hast set me on the rack.<br>I swear ’tis better to be much abused<br>Than but to know’t a little. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | How now, my lord? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | What sense had I of her stol’n hours of lust?<br>I saw’t not, thought it not; it harmed not me.<br>I slept the next [[Night|night]] [[Well|well]], fed [[Well|well]], was free and<br>merry.<br>I found not [[Cassio]]’s kisses on her lips.<br>He that is robbed, not wanting what is stol’n,<br>Let him not know’t and he’s not robbed at all. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | I am sorry to hear this. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | I had been happy if the general camp,<br>Pioneers and all, had tasted her sweet [[Body|body]],<br>So I had nothing known. O, now for ever<br>Farewell the tranquil mind, farewell content,<br>Farewell the plumèd troops and the big wars<br>That makes [[Ambition|ambition]] virtue! O, farewell,<br>Farewell the neighing steed and the shrill trump,<br>The spirit-stirring drum, th‘ear-piercing fife,<br>The royal banner, and all quality,<br>[[Pride]], pomp, and circumstance of glorious war!<br>And O, you mortal engines whose rude throats<br>Th’immortal Jove’s dread clamours counterfeit,<br>Farewell! [[Othello]]’s occupation’s gone. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Is’t possible, my lord? |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Othello|OTHELLO]] taking [[Iago]] by the throat |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | [[Villain]], be sure thou prove my [[Love|love]] a whore.<br>Be sure of it. Give me the ocular proof,<br>Or, by the worth of mine eternal [[Soul|soul]],<br>Thou hadst been better have been born a dog<br>Than answer my waked wrath. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Is’t come to this? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Make me to see’t, or at the least so prove it<br>That the probation bear no hinge nor loop<br>To hang a [[Doubt|doubt]] on, or woe upon thy life. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | My noble lord. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | If thou dost slander her and torture me,<br>Never pray more; abandon all remorse,<br>On horror’s head horrors accumulate,<br>Do deeds to make heaven weep, all earth amazed,<br>For nothing canst thou to damnation add<br>Greater than that. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | O grace, O heaven forgive me!<br>Are you a man? Have you a [[Soul|soul]] or sense?<br>God buy you, take mine office. O wretched [[Fool|fool]],<br>That lov’st to make thine honesty a vice!<br>O monstrous world, take note, take note, O world,<br>To be direct and honest is not safe!<br>I thank you for this profit, and from hence<br>I’ll [[Love|love]] no friend, sith [[Love|love]] breeds such offence. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Nay, stay. Thou shouldst be honest. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | I should be wise, for honesty’s a [[Fool|fool]],<br>And loses that it works for. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | By the world,<br>I think my wife be honest, and think she is not.<br>I think that thou art just, and think thou art not.<br>I’ll have some proof. My name, that was as fresh<br>As Dian’s visage, is now begrimed and black<br>As mine own face. If there be cords, or knives,<br>[[Poison]], or [[Fire|fire]], or suffocating streams,<br>I’ll not endure it. Would I were satisfied! |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | I see, sir, you are eaten up with passion.<br>I do repent me that I put it to you.<br>You would be satisfied? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Would? Nay, and I will. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | And may. But how, how satisfied, my lord?<br>Would you, the supervisor, grossly gape on,<br>Behold her topped? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | [[Death]] and damnation! O! |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | It were a tedious difficulty, I think,<br>To bring them to that prospect. Damn them then<br>If ever mortal eyes do see them bolster<br>More than their own! What then, how then?<br>What shall I say? Where’s satisfaction?<br>It is impossible you should see this,<br>Were they as prime as goats, as hot as monkeys,<br>As salt as wolves in [[Pride|pride]], and fools as gross<br>As ignorance made drunk. But yet I say,<br>If imputation, and strong circumstances<br>Which lead directly to the door of [[Truth|truth]],<br>Will give you satisfaction, you might ha’t. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Give me a living reason she’s disloyal. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | I do not like the office,<br>But sith I am entered in this cause so far,<br>Pricked to’t by foolish honesty and [[Love|love]],<br>I will go on. I lay with [[Cassio]] lately,<br>And being troubled with a raging tooth,<br>I could not [[Sleep|sleep]]. There are a kind of men<br>So loose of [[Soul|soul]] that in their sleeps<br>Will mutter their affairs. One of this kind is [[Cassio]].<br>In [[Sleep|sleep]] I heard him say ‘Sweet [[Desdemona]],<br>Let us be wary, let us hide our loves’,<br>And then, sir, would he grip and wring my hand,<br>Cry ‘O, sweet creature!’, then kiss me hard,<br>As if he plucked up kisses by the roots,<br>That grew upon my lips, lay his leg o‘er my thigh,<br>And sigh, and kiss, and then cry ‘Cursèd fate,<br>That gave thee to the Moor!’ |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | O, monstrous, monstrous! |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Nay, this was but his dream. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | But this denoted a foregone conclusion. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | ’Tis a shrewd [[Doubt|doubt]], though it be but [[A Dream|a dream]],<br>And this may help to thicken other proofs<br>That do demonstrate thinly. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | I’ll tear her all to pieces. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Nay, yet be wise; yet we see nothing done.<br>She may be honest yet. Tell me but this:<br>Have you not sometimes seen a handkerchief<br>Spotted with strawberries in your wife’s hand? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | I gave her such a one. ’Twas my first gift. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | I know not that, but such a handkerchief—<br>I am sure it was your wife’s—did I today<br>See [[Cassio]] wipe his beard with. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | If it be that— |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | If it be that, or any that was hers,<br>It speaks against her with the other proofs. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | O that the slave had forty thousand lives!<br>One is too poor, too weak for my [[Revenge|revenge]].<br>Now do I see ‘tis true. Look here, [[Iago]].<br>All my fond [[Love|love]] thus do I blow to heaven—’tis gone.<br>Arise, black vengeance, from the hollow hell.<br>Yield up, O [[Love|love]], thy [[Crown|crown]] and hearted throne<br>To tyrannous hate! Swell, bosom, with thy freight,<br>For ’tis of aspics’ tongues. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Yet be content. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | O, [[Blood|blood]], [[Blood|blood]], [[Blood|blood]]! |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Patience, I say. Your mind may change. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Never, [[Iago]]. Like to the Pontic Sea,<br>Whose icy current and compulsive course<br>Ne‘er knows retiring ebb, but keeps due on<br>To the Propontic and the Hellespont,<br>Even so my bloody thoughts with violent pace<br>Shall ne’er look back, ne’er ebb to humble [[Love|love]],<br>Till that a capable and wide [[Revenge|revenge]]<br>[[Swallow]] them up. |
| *(didascalia)* | He kneels |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Now, by yon marble heaven,<br>In the due reverence of a sacred vow<br>I here engage my words. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Do not rise yet.<br>[[Iago]] kneels<br>Witness you ever-burning lights above,<br>You elements that clip us round about,<br>Witness that here [[Iago]] doth give up<br>The execution of his wit, hands, heart<br>To wronged [[Othello]]’s service. Let him command,<br>And to obey shall be in me remorse,<br>What bloody business ever. |
| *(didascalia)* | They rise |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | I greet thy [[Love|love]],<br>Not with vain thanks, but with acceptance bounteous,<br>And will upon the instant put thee to’t. |
| *(didascalia)* | Within these three days let me hear thee say |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | That [[Cassio]]’s not alive. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | My friend is dead.<br>’Tis done at your request; but let her live. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Damn her, lewd minx! O, damn her, damn her!<br>Come, go with me apart. I will withdraw<br>To furnish me with some swift means of [[Death|death]]<br>For the fair devil. Now art thou my lieutenant. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | I am your own for ever. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt |
