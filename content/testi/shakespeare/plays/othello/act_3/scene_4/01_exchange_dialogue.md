| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Desdemona]], [[Emilia]], and the Clown |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Do you know, sirrah, where Lieutenant [[Cassio]] lies? |
| CLOWN | I dare not say he lies anywhere. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Why, man? |
| CLOWN | He’s a soldier, and for me to say a soldier lies, ’tis stabbing. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Go to. Where lodges he? |
| CLOWN | To tell you where he lodges is to tell you where I lie. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Can anything be made of this? |
| CLOWN | I know not where he lodges, and for me to devise a lodging and say he lies here, or he lies there, were to lie in mine own throat. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Can you enquire him out, and be edified by report? |
| CLOWN | I will catechize the world for him; that is, make questions, and by them answer. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Seek him, bid him come hither, tell him I have moved my lord on his behalf, and [[Hope|hope]] all will be [[Well|well]]. |
| CLOWN | To do this is within the compass of man’s wit, and therefore I will attempt the doing it. Exit |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Where should I lose the handkerchief, [[Emilia]]? |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | I know not, madam. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Believe me, I had rather have lost my purse Full of crusadoes, and but my noble Moor Is true of mind, and made of no such baseness As jealous creatures are, it were enough To put him to ill thinking. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | Is he not jealous? |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Who, he? I think the sun where he was born Drew all such humours from him. Enter [[Othello]] |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | Look where he comes. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | I will not leave him now till [[Cassio]] Be called to him. How is’t with you, my lord? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | [[Well]], my good lady. (Aside) O hardness to dissemble!— How do you, [[Desdemona]]? |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | [[Well]], my good lord. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Give me your hand. This hand is moist, my lady. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | It hath felt no age, nor known no sorrow. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | This argues fruitfulness and liberal heart. Hot, hot and moist—this hand of yours requires A sequester from liberty; fasting, and [[Prayer|prayer]], Much castigation, exercise devout, For here’s a young and sweating devil here That commonly rebels. ’Tis a good hand, A frank one. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | You may indeed say so, For ’twas that hand that gave away my heart. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | A liberal hand. The hearts of old gave hands, But our new heraldry is hands, not hearts. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | I cannot speak of this. Come now, your promise. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | What promise, chuck? |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | I have sent to bid [[Cassio]] come speak with you. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | I have a salt and sorry rheum offends me. Lend me thy handkerchief. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | (offering a handkerchief) Here, my lord. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | That which I gave you. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | I have it not about me. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Not? |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | No, [[Faith|faith]], my lord. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | That’s a fault. That handkerchief Did an Egyptian to my mother give. She was a charmer, and could almost read The thoughts of people. She told her, while she kept it ’Twould make her amiable, and subdue my father Entirely to her [[Love|love]]; but if she lost it, Or made a gift of it, my father’s eye Should hold her loathèd, and his spirits should hunt After new fancies. She, dying, gave it me, And bid me, when my fate would have me wived, To give it her. I did so, and take heed on’t. Make it a darling, like your precious eye. To lose’t or give’t away were such perdition As nothing else could match. |
