| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Is’t possible? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | ’Tis true. There’s [[Magic|magic]] in the web of it. A sibyl that had numbered in the world The sun to course two hundred compasses In her prophetic fury sewed the work. The worms were hallowed that did breed the silk, And it was dyed in mummy, which the skilful Conserved of maidens’ hearts. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | I’[[Faith|faith]], is’t true? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Most veritable. Therefore look to’t [[Well|well]]. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Then would to God that I had never seen it! |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Ha, wherefore? |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Why do you speak so startingly and rash? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Is’t lost? Is’t gone? Speak, is’t out o’th’ way? |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Heaven bless us! |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Say you? |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | It is not lost, but what an if it were? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | HOW? |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | I say it is not lost. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Fetch’t, let me see’t. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Why, so I can, sir, but I will not now. This is a trick to put me from my suit. Pray you let [[Cassio]] be received again. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Fetch me the handkerchief. My mind misgives. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Come, come, you’ll never meet a more sufficient man. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | The handkerchief. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | I pray, talk me of [[Cassio]]. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | The handkerchief. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | A man that all his [[Time|time]] Hath founded his good fortunes on your [[Love|love]], Shared dangers with you— |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | The handkerchief. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | I’[[Faith|faith]], you are to blame. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | ’Swounds! Exit |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | Is not this man jealous? |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | I ne’er saw this before. Sure there’s some [[Wonder|wonder]] in this handkerchief. I am most unhappy in the loss of it. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | ’Tis not a year or two shows us a man. They are all but stomachs, and we all but food. They eat us hungrily, and when they are full, They belch us. Enter [[Iago]] and [[Cassio]] Look you, [[Cassio]] and my husband. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | (to [[Cassio]]) There is no other way. ‘Tis she must do’t, And lo, the happiness! Go and importune her. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | How now, good [[Cassio]]? What’s the news with you? |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | Madam, my former suit. I do beseech you That by your virtuous means I may again Exist and be a member of his [[Love|love]] Whom I, with all the office of my heart, Entirely [[Honour|honour]]. I would not be delayed. If my offence be of such mortal kind That nor my service past, nor present sorrows, Nor purposed merit in futurity Can ransom me into his [[Love|love]] again, But to know so must be my benefit. So shall I clothe me in a forced content, And shut myself up in some other course To fortune’s alms. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Alas, thrice-gentle [[Cassio]]! My advocation is not now in tune. My lord is not my lord, nor should I know him Were he in favour as in [[Humour|humour]] altered. So help me every spirit sanctified As I have spoken for you all my best, And stood within the blank of his displeasure For my free speech! You must a while be patient. What I can do I will, and more I will Than for myself I dare. Let that suffice you. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Is my lord angry? |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | He went hence but now, And certainly in strange unquietness. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Can he be angry? I have seen the cannon When it hath blown his ranks into the air, And, like the devil, from his very arm Puffed his own brother; and is he angry? Something of moment then. I will go meet him. There’s matter in’t indeed, if he be angry. |
