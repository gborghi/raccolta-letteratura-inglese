| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | I prithee do so. Exit [[Iago]] Something sure of state, Either from [[Venice]] or some unhatched practice Made demonstrable here in Cyprus to him, Hath puddled his clear spirit; and in such cases Men’s natures wrangle with inferior things, Though great ones are their object. ’Tis even so; For let our finger ache and it indues Our other, healthful members even to a sense Of [[Pain|pain]]. Nay, we must think men are not gods, Nor of them look for such observancy As fits the bridal. Beshrew me much, [[Emilia]], I was—unhandsome warrior as I am— Arraigning his unkindness with my [[Soul|soul]]; But now I find I had suborned the witness, And he’s indicted falsely. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | Pray heaven it be State matters, as you think, and no conception Nor no jealous toy concerning you. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Alas the day, I never gave him cause. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | But jealous souls will not be answered so. They are not ever jealous for the cause, But jealous for they’re jealous. It is a [[Monster|monster]] Begot upon itself, born on itself. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Heaven keep the [[Monster|monster]] from [[Othello]]’s mind. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | Lady, amen. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | I will go seek him. [[Cassio]], walk here about. If I do find him fit I’ll move your suit, And seek to effect it to my uttermost. |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | I humbly thank your ladyship. Exeunt [[Desdemona]] and [[Emilia]] Enter [[Bianca]] |
| [[Bianca|BIANCA]] | Save you, friend [[Cassio]]. |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | What make you from [[Home|home]]? How is’t with you, my most fair [[Bianca]]? I’[[Faith|faith]], sweet [[Love|love]], I was coming to your [[house]]. |
| [[Bianca|BIANCA]] | And I was going to your lodging, [[Cassio]]. What, keep a week away? Seven days and nights, Eightscore-eight hours, and [[lovers]]’ absent hours More tedious than the dial eightscore times! O weary reckoning! |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | Pardon me, [[Bianca]], I have this while with leaden thoughts been pressed, But I shall in a more continuate [[Time|time]] Strike off this score of absence. Sweet [[Bianca]], Take me this work out. He gives her [[Desdemona]]’s napkin |
| [[Bianca|BIANCA]] | O [[Cassio]], whence came this? This is some token from a newer friend. To the felt absence now I feel a cause. Is’t come to this? [[Well]], [[Well|well]]. |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | Go to, woman. Throw your vile guesses in the devil’s teeth, From whence you have them. You are jealous now That this is from some mistress, some remembrance. No, by my [[Faith|faith]], [[Bianca]]. |
| [[Bianca|BIANCA]] | Why, whose is it? |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | I know not, neither. I found it in my chamber. I like the work [[Well|well]]. Ere it be demanded— As like enough it witt—I would have it copied. Take it, and do’t, and leave me for this [[Time|time]]. |
| [[Bianca|BIANCA]] | Leave you? Wherefore? |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | I do attend here on the general, And think it no addition, nor my wish, To have him see me womaned. |
| [[Bianca|BIANCA]] | Why, I pray you? |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | Not that I [[Love|love]] you not. |
| [[Bianca|BIANCA]] | But that you do not [[Love|love]] me. I pray you bring me on the way a little, And say if I shall see you soon at [[Night|night]]. |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | ’Tis but a little way that I can bring you, For I attend here; but I’ll see you soon. |
| [[Bianca|BIANCA]] | ’Tis very good. I must be circumstanced. Exeunt |
