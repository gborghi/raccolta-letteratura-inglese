| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | The heavens forbid But that our loves and comforts should increase Even as our days do grow. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Amen to that, sweet powers! I cannot speak enough of this content. It stops me here, it is too much of joy. And this, (they kiss) and this, the greatest discords be That e’er our hearts shall make. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | (aside) O, you are [[Well|well]] tuned now, But I’ll set down the pegs that make this music, As honest as I am. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Come, let us to the [[Castle|castle]]. News, friends: our wars are done, the Turks are drowned. How does my old acquaintance of this isle?— Honey, you shall be [[Well|well]] desired in Cyprus, I have found great [[Love|love]] amongst them. O my sweet, I prattle out of fashion, and I dote In mine own comforts. I prithee, good [[Iago]], Go to the bay and disembark my coffers. Bring thou the master to the citadel. He is a good one, and his worthiness Does challenge much respect. Come, [[Desdemona]].— Once more, [[Well|well]] met at Cyprus! Exeunt [[Othello]] and [[Desdemona]] with all but lago and [[Roderigo]] |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | (to an attendant as he goes out) Do thou meet me presently at the harbour. (To [[Roderigo]]) Come hither. If thou beest valiant—as they say base men being in [[Love|love]] have then a nobility in their natures more than is native to them—list me. The lieutenant tonight watches on the [[Court|court]] of guard. First, I must tell thee this: [[Desdemona]] is directly in [[Love|love]] with him. |
| RODERICO | With him? Why, ’tis not possible! |
