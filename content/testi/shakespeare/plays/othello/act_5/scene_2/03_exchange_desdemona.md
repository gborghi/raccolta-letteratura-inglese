| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Who’s there? [[Othello]]? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Ay, [[Desdemona]]. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Will you come to bed, my lord? OTHELLOHave you prayed tonight, Desdemon? |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Ay, my lord. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | If you bethink yourself of any crime Unreconciled as yet to heaven and grace, Solicit for it straight. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Alack, my lord, what may you mean by that? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | [[Well]], do it, and be brief. I will walk by. I would not kill thy unprepared spirit. No, heavens forfend! I would not kill thy [[Soul|soul]]. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Talk you of killing? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Ay, I do. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Then heaven Have [[Mercy|mercy]] on me. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Amen, with all my heart. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | If you say so, I [[Hope|hope]] you will not kill me. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Hum! |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | And yet I [[Fear|fear]] you, for you’re fatal then When your eyes roll so. Why I should [[Fear|fear]] I know not, Since guiltiness I know not, but yet I feel I [[Fear|fear]]. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Think on thy sins. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | They are loves I bear to you. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Ay, and for that thou diest. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | That [[Death|death]]’s unnatural that kills for loving. Alas, why gnaw you so your nether lip? Some bloody passion shakes your very frame. These are portents, but yet I [[Hope|hope]], I [[Hope|hope]] They do not point on me. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Peace, and be still. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | I will so. What’s the matter? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | That handkerchief Which I so loved and gave thee, thou gav’st to [[Cassio]]. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | No, by my life and [[Soul|soul]]. Send for the man And ask him. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Sweet [[Soul|soul]], take heed, take heed of perjury. Thou art on thy deathbed. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Ay, but not yet to die. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Presently. Therefore confess thee freely of thy sin, For to deny each article with oath Cannot remove nor choke the strong conception That I do groan withal. Thou art to die. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Then Lord have [[Mercy|mercy]] on me. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | I say amen. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | And have you [[Mercy|mercy]], too. I never did Offend you in my life, never loved [[Cassio]] But with such general warranty of heaven As I might [[Love|love]]. I never gave him token. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | By heaven, I saw my handkerchief in’s hand. O perjured woman! Thou dost stone my heart, And makes me call what I intend to do A murder, which I thought a sacrifice. I saw the handkerchief. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | He found it, then. I never gave it him. Send for him hither. Let him confess a [[Truth|truth]]. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | He hath confessed. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | What, my lord? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | That he hath used thee. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | How, unlawfully? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Ay. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | He will not say so. OTHELLONo, his mouth is stopped. Honest [[Iago]] hath ta’en order for’t. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | O, my [[Fear|fear]] interprets! What, is he dead? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Had all his hairs been lives, my great [[Revenge|revenge]] Had stomach for em all. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | (weeping) Alas, he is betrayed, and I undone. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Out, strumpet! Weep’st thou for him to my face? |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | O, banish me, my lord, but kill me not. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | (attacking her) Down, strumpet. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Kill me tomorrow; let me live tonight. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Nay, an you strive— |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | But half an hour. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Being done, there is no pause. |
