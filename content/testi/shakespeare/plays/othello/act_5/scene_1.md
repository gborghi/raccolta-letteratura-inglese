| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Iago]] and [[Roderigo]] |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Here, stand behind this bulk. Straight will he come.<br>Wear thy good rapier bare, and put it [[Home|home]].<br>Quick, quick, [[Fear|fear]] nothing. I’ll be at thy elbow.<br>It makes us or it mars us. Think on that,<br>And fix most firm thy resolution. |
| [[Roderigo|RODERIGO]] | Be near at hand. I may miscarry in’t. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Here at thy hand. Be bold, and take thy stand. |
| [[Roderigo|RODERIGO]] | (aside)<br>I have no great devotion to the deed,<br>And yet he hath given me satisfying reasons.<br>’Tis but a man gone. Forth my [[Sword|sword]]—he dies! |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | (aside)<br>I have rubbed this young quat almost to the sense,<br>And he grows angry. Now, whether he kill [[Cassio]]<br>Or [[Cassio]] him, or each do kill the other,<br>Every way makes my gain. live [[Roderigo]],<br>He calls me to a restitution large<br>Of gold and jewels that I bobbed from him<br>As gifts to [[Desdemona]].<br>It must not be. If [[Cassio]] do remain,<br>He hath a daily [[Beauty|beauty]] in his life<br>That makes me ugly; and besides, the Moor<br>May unfold me to him-there stand I in much peril.<br>No, he must die. But so, I hear him coming. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Cassio]] |
| [[Roderigo|RODERIGO]] | I know his gait, ’tis he. (Attacking [[Cassio]]) [[Villain]], thou<br>diest. |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | That thrust had been mine enemy indeed,<br>But that my coat is better than thou know’st.<br>I will make proof of thine.<br>He stabs [[Roderigo]], who falls |
| [[Roderigo|RODERIGO]] | O, I am slain!<br>[[Iago]] wounds [[Cassio]] in the leg from behind. Exit [[Iago]] |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | (falling)<br>I am maimed for ever. Help, ho, murder, murder! |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Othello]] [above] |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | The voice of [[Cassio]]. [[Iago]] keeps his word. |
| [[Roderigo|RODERIGO]] | O, [[Villain|villain]] that I am! |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | It is even so. |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | O, help, ho! Light, a surgeon! |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | ’Tis he. O brave [[Iago]], honest and just,<br>That hast such noble sense of thy friend’s wrong—<br>Thou teachest me. Minion, your dear lies dead,<br>And your unblessed fate hies. Strumpet, I come.<br>Forth of my heart those charms, thine eyes, are blotted.<br>Thy bed, lust-stained, shall with lust’s [[Blood|blood]] be spotted. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Lodovico and Graziano |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | What ho, no watch, no passage? Murder, murder! |
| GRAZIANO | ’Tis some mischance. The voice is very direful. |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | O, help! |
| LODOVICO | Hark. |
| [[Roderigo|RODERIGO]] | O wretched [[Villain|villain]]! |
| LODOVICO | Two or three groan. ’Tis heavy [[Night|night]].<br>These may be counterfeits. Let’s think’t unsafe<br>To come into the cry without more help. |
| [[Roderigo|RODERIGO]] | Nobody come? Then shall I bleed to [[Death|death]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Iago]] with a light |
| LODOVICO | Hark. |
| GRAZIANO | Here’s one comes in his shirt, with light and weapons. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Who’s there? Whose noise is this that cries on murder? |
| LODOVICO | We do not know. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Do not you hear a cry? |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | Here, here. For heaven’s sake, help me. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | What’s the matter? |
| GRAZIANO | (to Lodovico)<br>This is [[Othello]]’s ensign, as I take it. |
| LODOVICO | The same indeed, a very valiant fellow. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | (to [[Cassio]])<br>What are you here that cry so grievously? |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | [[Iago]]—O, I am spoiled, undone by villains.<br>Give me some help. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | O me, lieutenant, what villains have done this? |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | I think that one of them is hereabout<br>And cannot make away. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | O treacherous villains!<br>(To Lodovico and Graziano)<br>What are you there? Come in and give some help. |
| [[Roderigo|RODERIGO]] | O, help me there! |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | That’s one of ’em. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | (stabbing [[Roderigo]]) O murderous slave! O [[Villain|villain]]! |
| [[Roderigo|RODERIGO]] | O damned [[Iago]]! O inhuman dog! |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Kill men i’th’ dark? Where be these bloody thieves?<br>How silent is this town! Ho, murder, murder!<br>(To Lodovico and Graziano)<br>What may you be? Are you of good or evil? |
| LODOVICO | As you shall prove us, praise us. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Signor Lodovico. |
| LODOVICO | He, sir. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | I cry you [[Mercy|mercy]]. Here’s [[Cassio]] hurt by villains. |
| GRAZIANO | [[Cassio]]? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | How is’t, brother? |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | My leg is cut in two. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Marry, heaven forbid!<br>Light, gentlemen. I’ll bind it with my shirt. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Bianca]] |
| [[Bianca|BIANCA]] | What is the matter, ho? Who is’t that cried? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Who is’t that cried? |
| [[Bianca|BIANCA]] | O my dear [[Cassio]],<br>My sweet [[Cassio]], O, [[Cassio]], [[Cassio]]! |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | O notable strumpet! [[Cassio]], may you suspect<br>Who they should be that have thus mangled you? 80 |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | No. |
| GRAZIANO | I am sorry to find you thus. I have been to seek you. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Lend me a garter. So. O for a chair,<br>To bear him easily hence! |
| [[Bianca|BIANCA]] | Alas, he faints. O, [[Cassio]], [[Cassio]], [[Cassio]]! |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Gentlemen all, I do suspect this trash<br>To be a party in this injury.<br>Patience a while, good [[Cassio]]. Come, come,<br>Lend me a light. (Going to [[Roderigo]]) Know we this face<br>or no?<br>Alas, my friend, and my dear countryman.<br>[[Roderigo]]? No—yes, sure—O heaven, [[Roderigo]]! |
| GRAZIANO | What, of [[Venice]]? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Even he, sir. Did you know him? |
| GRAZIANO | Know him? Ay. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Signor Graziano, I cry your gentle pardon.<br>These bloody accidents must excuse my manners<br>That so neglected you. |
| GRAZIANO | I am glad to see you. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | How do you, [[Cassio]]? O, a chair, a chair! |
| GRAZIANO | [[Roderigo]]. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | He, he, ’tis he. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter attendants with a chair |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | O, that’s [[Well|well]] said, the chair!<br>Some good man bear him carefully from hence.<br>I’ll fetch the general’s surgeon. (To [[Bianca]]) For you,<br>mistress,<br>Save you your labour. He that lies slain here, [[Cassio]],<br>Was my dear friend. What malice was between you? |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | None in the world, nor do I know the man. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | (to [[Bianca]])<br>What, look you pale? (To attendants) O, bear him out<br>o’th’ air.<br>(To Lodovico and Graziano)<br>Stay you, good gentlemen. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt attendants with [[Cassio]] in the chair [and with [[Roderigo]]’s [[Body|body]]] |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | (To [[Bianca]]) Look you pale, mistress?<br>(To Lodovico and Graziano)<br>Do you perceive the ghastness of her eye?<br>(To [[Bianca]]) Nay, an you stare we shall hear more<br>anon.<br>(To Lodovico and Graziano)<br>Behold her [[Well|well]]; I pray you look upon her.<br>Do you see, gentlemen? Nay, guiltiness<br>Will speak, though tongues were out of use. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Emilia]] |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | Alas, what is the matter? What is the matter,<br>husband? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | [[Cassio]] hath here been set on in the dark<br>By [[Roderigo]] and fellows that are scaped.<br>He’s almost slain, and [[Roderigo]] dead. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | Alas, good gentleman! Alas, good [[Cassio]]! |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | This is the fruits of whoring. Prithee, [[Emilia]],<br>Go know of [[Cassio]] where he supped tonight.<br>(To [[Bianca]]) What, do you shake at that? |
| [[Bianca|BIANCA]] | He supped at my [[house]], but I therefore shake not. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | O, did he so? I charge you go with me. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | (to [[Bianca]]) O, fie upon thee, strumpet! |
| [[Bianca|BIANCA]] | I am no strumpet, but of life as honest<br>As you that thus abuse me. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | As I? Fough, fie upon thee! |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Kind gentlemen, let’s go see poor [[Cassio]] dressed.<br>(To [[Bianca]]) Come, mistress, you must tell’s another tale.<br>[[Emilia]], run you to the citadel<br>And tell my lord and lady what hath happed.<br>Will you go on afore? |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit [[Emilia]] |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | (Aside) This is the [[Night|night]]<br>That either makes me or fordoes me quite. Exeunt |
