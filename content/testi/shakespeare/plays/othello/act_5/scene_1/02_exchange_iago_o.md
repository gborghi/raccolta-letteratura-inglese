| [[Iago|IAGO]] | O treacherous villains! (To Lodovico and Graziano) What are you there? Come in and give some help. |
| [[Roderigo|RODERIGO]] | O, help me there! |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | That’s one of ’em. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | (stabbing [[Roderigo]]) O murderous slave! O [[Villain|villain]]! |
| [[Roderigo|RODERIGO]] | O damned [[Iago]]! O inhuman dog! |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Kill men i’th’ dark? Where be these bloody thieves? How silent is this town! Ho, murder, murder! (To Lodovico and Graziano) What may you be? Are you of good or evil? |
| LODOVICO | As you shall prove us, praise us. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Signor Lodovico. |
| LODOVICO | He, sir. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | I cry you [[Mercy|mercy]]. Here’s [[Cassio]] hurt by villains. |
| GRAZIANO | [[Cassio]]? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | How is’t, brother? |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | My leg is cut in two. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Marry, heaven forbid! Light, gentlemen. I’ll bind it with my shirt. Enter [[Bianca]] |
| [[Bianca|BIANCA]] | What is the matter, ho? Who is’t that cried? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Who is’t that cried? |
| [[Bianca|BIANCA]] | O my dear [[Cassio]], My sweet [[Cassio]], O, [[Cassio]], [[Cassio]]! |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | O notable strumpet! [[Cassio]], may you suspect Who they should be that have thus mangled you? 80 |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | No. |
| GRAZIANO | I am sorry to find you thus. I have been to seek you. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Lend me a garter. So. O for a chair, To bear him easily hence! |
| [[Bianca|BIANCA]] | Alas, he faints. O, [[Cassio]], [[Cassio]], [[Cassio]]! |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Gentlemen all, I do suspect this trash To be a party in this injury. Patience a while, good [[Cassio]]. Come, come, Lend me a light. (Going to [[Roderigo]]) Know we this face or no? Alas, my friend, and my dear countryman. [[Roderigo]]? No—yes, sure—O heaven, [[Roderigo]]! |
| GRAZIANO | What, of [[Venice]]? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Even he, sir. Did you know him? |
| GRAZIANO | Know him? Ay. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Signor Graziano, I cry your gentle pardon. These bloody accidents must excuse my manners That so neglected you. |
| GRAZIANO | I am glad to see you. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | How do you, [[Cassio]]? O, a chair, a chair! |
| GRAZIANO | [[Roderigo]]. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | He, he, ’tis he. Enter attendants with a chair O, that’s [[Well|well]] said, the chair! Some good man bear him carefully from hence. I’ll fetch the general’s surgeon. (To [[Bianca]]) For you, mistress, Save you your labour. He that lies slain here, [[Cassio]], Was my dear friend. What malice was between you? |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | None in the world, nor do I know the man. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | (to [[Bianca]]) What, look you pale? (To attendants) O, bear him out o’th’ air. (To Lodovico and Graziano) Stay you, good gentlemen. Exeunt attendants with [[Cassio]] in the chair [and with [[Roderigo]]’s [[Body|body]]] (To [[Bianca]]) Look you pale, mistress? (To Lodovico and Graziano) Do you perceive the ghastness of her eye? (To [[Bianca]]) Nay, an you stare we shall hear more anon. (To Lodovico and Graziano) Behold her [[Well|well]]; I pray you look upon her. Do you see, gentlemen? Nay, guiltiness Will speak, though tongues were out of use. Enter [[Emilia]] |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | Alas, what is the matter? What is the matter, husband? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | [[Cassio]] hath here been set on in the dark By [[Roderigo]] and fellows that are scaped. He’s almost slain, and [[Roderigo]] dead. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | Alas, good gentleman! Alas, good [[Cassio]]! |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | This is the fruits of whoring. Prithee, [[Emilia]], Go know of [[Cassio]] where he supped tonight. (To [[Bianca]]) What, do you shake at that? |
