| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | I [[Hope|hope]] my noble lord esteems me honest. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | O, ay—as [[Summer|summer]] flies are in the shambles, That quicken even with blowing. O thou weed, Who art so lovely fair, and smell‘st so sweet, That the sense aches at thee—would thou hadst ne’er been born! |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Alas, what ignorant sin have I committed? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Was this fair paper, this most goodly book, Made to write ‘whore’ upon? What committed? Committed? O thou public commoner, I should make very forges of my cheeks, That would to cinders burn up modesty, Did I but speak thy deeds. What committed? Heaven stops the nose at it, and the [[Moon|moon]] winks; The bawdy [[Wind|wind]], that kisses all it meets, Is hushed within the hollow mine of earth And will not hear’t. What committed? |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | By heaven, you do me wrong. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Are not you a strumpet? |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | No, as I am a Christian. If to preserve this vessel for my lord From any other foul unlawful touch Be not to be a strumpet, I am none. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | What, not a whore? |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | No, as I shall be saved. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Is’t possible? |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | O heaven forgive us! |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | I cry you [[Mercy|mercy]] then. I took you for that cunning whore of [[Venice]] That married with [[Othello]]. (Calling) You, mistress, That have the office opposite to Saint Peter And keeps the gate of hell, Enter [[Emilia]] you, you, ay, you. We ha’ done our course. (Giving money) There’s money for your pains. I pray you, turn the key and keep our counsel. Exit |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | Alas, what does this gentleman conceive? How do you, madam? How do you, my good lady? |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | [[Faith]], half asleep. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | Good madam, what’s the matter with my lord? |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | With who? |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | Why, with my lord, madam. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Who is thy lord? |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | He that is yours, sweet lady. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | I ha’ none. Do not talk to me, [[Emilia]]. I cannot weep, nor answers have I none But what should go by [[Water|water]]. Prithee tonight Lay on my bed my wedding sheets, remember. And call thy husband hither. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | Here’s a change indeed. Exit |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | ‘Tis meet I should be used so, very meet. How have I been behaved, that he might stick The small’st opinion on my least misuse? Enter [[Iago]] and [[Emilia]], |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | What is your pleasure, madam? How is’t with you? |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | I cannot tell. Those that do teach young babes Do it with gentle means and easy tasks. He might ha’ chid me so, for, in good [[Faith|faith]], I am a [[Child|child]] to chiding. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | What is the matter, lady? |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | Alas, [[Iago]], my lord hath so bewhored her, Thrown such despite and heavy terms upon her, That true hearts cannot bear it. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Am I that name, [[Iago]]? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | What name, fair lady? |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Such as she said my lord did say I was. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | He called her whore. A beggar in his drink Could not have laid such terms upon his callet. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Why did he so? |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | I do not know. I am sure I am none such. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Do not weep, do not weep. Alas the day! |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | Hath she forsook so many noble matches, Her father and her country and her friends, To be called whore? Would it not make one weep? |
