| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Othello]] and [[Emilia]] |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | You have seen nothing then? |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | Nor ever heard, nor ever did suspect. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Yes, you have seen [[Cassio]] and she together. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | But then I saw no harm, and then I heard<br>Each syllable that breath made up between ’em. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | What, did they never whisper? |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | Never, my lord. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Nor send you out o’th’ way? |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | Never. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | To fetch her fan, her gloves, her mask, nor nothing? |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | Never, my lord. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | That’s strange. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | I durst, my lord, to wager she is honest,<br>Lay down my [[Soul|soul]] at stake. If you think other,<br>Remove your thought; it doth abuse your bosom.<br>If any wretch ha’ put this in your head,<br>Let heaven requite it with the serpent’s curse,<br>For if she be not honest, chaste, and true,<br>There’s no man happy; the purest of their wives<br>Is foul as slander. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Bid her come hither. Go. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit [[Emilia]] |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | She says enough, yet she’s a simple bawd<br>That cannot say as much. This is a subtle whore,<br>A closet lock and key of villainous [[Secrets|secrets]],<br>And yet she’ll kneel and pray—I ha’ seen her do’t. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Desdemona]] and [[Emilia]] |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | My lord, what is your will? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Pray you, chuck, come hither. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | What is your pleasure? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Let me see your eyes.<br>Look in my face. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | What horrible fancy’s this? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | (to [[Emilia]]) Some of your function, mistress.<br>Leave procreants [[Alone|alone]], and shut the door,<br>Cough or cry ‘Hem’ if anybody come.<br>Your mystery, your mystery—nay, dispatch. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit [[Emilia]] |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Upon my knees, what doth your speech import?<br>I understand a fury in your words,<br>But not the words. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Why, what art thou? |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Your wife, my lord, your true and loyal wife. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Come, swear it, damn thyself,<br>Lest, being like one of heaven, the devils themselves<br>Should [[Fear|fear]] to seize thee. Therefore be [[Double|double]]-damned:<br>Swear thou art honest. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Heaven doth truly know it. 40 |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Heaven truly knows that thou art false as hell. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | To whom, my lord? With whom? How am I false? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | (weeping)<br>Ah, Desdemon, away, away, away! |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Alas, the heavy day! Why do you weep?<br>Am I the motive of these [[Tears|tears]], my lord?<br>If haply you my father do suspect<br>An instrument of this your calling back,<br>Lay not your blame on me. If you have lost him,<br>I have lost him too. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Had it pleased God<br>To try me with affliction; had He rained<br>All kind of sores and shames on my bare head,<br>Steeped me in [[Poverty|poverty]] to the very lips,<br>Given to captivity me and my utmost hopes,<br>I should have found in some place of my [[Soul|soul]]<br>A drop of patience. But, alas, to make me<br>The fixed figure for the [[Time|time]] of scorn<br>To point his slow and moving finger at—<br>Yet could I bear that too, [[Well|well]], very [[Well|well]].<br>But there where I have garnered up my heart,<br>Where either I must live or bear no life,<br>The fountain from the which my current runs<br>Or else dries up—to be discarded thence,<br>Or keep it as a cistern for foul toads<br>To knot and gender in! Turn thy complexion there,<br>Patience, thou young and [[Rose|rose]]-lipped cherubin,<br>Ay, here look grim as hell. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | I [[Hope|hope]] my noble lord esteems me honest. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | O, ay—as [[Summer|summer]] flies are in the shambles,<br>That quicken even with blowing. O thou weed,<br>Who art so lovely fair, and smell‘st so sweet,<br>That the sense aches at thee—would thou hadst ne’er<br>been born! |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Alas, what ignorant sin have I committed? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Was this fair paper, this most goodly book,<br>Made to write ‘whore’ upon? What committed?<br>Committed? O thou public commoner,<br>I should make very forges of my cheeks,<br>That would to cinders burn up modesty,<br>Did I but speak thy deeds. What committed?<br>Heaven stops the nose at it, and the [[Moon|moon]] winks;<br>The bawdy [[Wind|wind]], that kisses all it meets,<br>Is hushed within the hollow mine of earth<br>And will not hear’t. What committed? |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | By heaven, you do me wrong. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Are not you a strumpet? |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | No, as I am a Christian.<br>If to preserve this vessel for my lord<br>From any other foul unlawful touch<br>Be not to be a strumpet, I am none. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | What, not a whore? |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | No, as I shall be saved. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Is’t possible? |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | O heaven forgive us! |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | I cry you [[Mercy|mercy]] then.<br>I took you for that cunning whore of [[Venice]]<br>That married with [[Othello]]. (Calling) You, mistress,<br>That have the office opposite to Saint Peter<br>And keeps the gate of hell, |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Emilia]] |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | you, you, ay, you.<br>We ha’ done our course. (Giving money) There’s<br>money for your pains.<br>I pray you, turn the key and keep our counsel. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | Alas, what does this gentleman conceive?<br>How do you, madam? How do you, my good lady? |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | [[Faith]], half asleep. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | Good madam, what’s the matter with my lord? |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | With who? |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | Why, with my lord, madam. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Who is thy lord? |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | He that is yours, sweet lady. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | I ha’ none. Do not talk to me, [[Emilia]].<br>I cannot weep, nor answers have I none<br>But what should go by [[Water|water]]. Prithee tonight<br>Lay on my bed my wedding sheets, remember.<br>And call thy husband hither. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | Here’s a change indeed. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | ‘Tis meet I should be used so, very meet.<br>How have I been behaved, that he might stick<br>The small’st opinion on my least misuse? |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Iago]] and [[Emilia]], |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | What is your pleasure, madam? How is’t with you? |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | I cannot tell. Those that do teach young babes<br>Do it with gentle means and easy tasks.<br>He might ha’ chid me so, for, in good [[Faith|faith]],<br>I am a [[Child|child]] to chiding. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | What is the matter, lady? |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | Alas, [[Iago]], my lord hath so bewhored her,<br>Thrown such despite and heavy terms upon her,<br>That true hearts cannot bear it. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Am I that name, [[Iago]]? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | What name, fair lady? |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Such as she said my lord did say I was. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | He called her whore. A beggar in his drink<br>Could not have laid such terms upon his callet. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Why did he so? |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | I do not know. I am sure I am none such. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Do not weep, do not weep. Alas the day! |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | Hath she forsook so many noble matches,<br>Her father and her country and her friends,<br>To be called whore? Would it not make one weep? |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | It is my wretched fortune. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Beshrew him for’t.<br>How comes this trick upon him? |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | Nay, heaven doth know. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | I will be hanged if some eternal [[Villain|villain]],<br>Some busy and insinuating rogue,<br>Some cogging, cozening slave, to get some office,<br>Have not devised this slander. I will be hanged else. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Fie, there is no such man. It is impossible. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | If any such there be, heaven pardon him. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | A halter pardon him, and hell gnaw his bones!<br>Why should he call her whore? Who keeps her<br>company?<br>What place, what [[Time|time]], what form, what likelihood?<br>The Moor’s abused by some most villainous knave,<br>Some base, notorious knave, some scurvy fellow.<br>O heaven, that such companions thou’dst unfold,<br>And put in every honest hand a whip<br>To lash the rascals naked through the world,<br>Even from the east to th’ west! |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Speak within door. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | O, fie upon them. Some such squire he was<br>That turned your wit the seamy side without,<br>And made you to suspect me with the Moor. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | You are a [[Fool|fool]]. Go to. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | O God, [[Iago]],<br>What shall I do to win my lord again?<br>Good friend, go to him; for by this light of heaven,<br>I know not how I lost him.<br>She kneels<br>Here I kneel.<br>If e‘er my will did trespass ’gainst his [[Love|love]],<br>Either in discourse of thought or actual deed,<br>Or that mine eyes, mine ears, or any sense<br>Delighted them in any other form,<br>Or that I do not yet, and ever did,<br>And ever will—though he do shake me off<br>To beggarly divorcement—[[Love|love]] him dearly,<br>Comfort forswear me. Unkindness may do much,<br>And his unkindness may defeat my life,<br>But never taint my [[Love|love]].<br>[She rises]<br>I cannot say ‘whore’.<br>It does abhor me now I speak the word.<br>To do the act that might the addition earn,<br>Not the world’s mass of [[Vanity|vanity]] could make me. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | I pray you, be content. ’Tis but his [[Humour|humour]].<br>The business of the state does him offence,<br>And he does chide with you. |
| [[Desdemona|DESDEMONA]] | If ’twere no other! |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | It is but so, I warrant. |
| *(didascalia)* | Flourish within |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Hark how these instruments summon you to supper.<br>The messengers of [[Venice]] stays the meat.<br>Go in, and weep not. All things shall be [[Well|well]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt [[Desdemona]] and [[Emilia]] |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Roderigo]] |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | How now, [[Roderigo]]? |
| [[Roderigo|RODERIGO]] | I do not find that thou deal’st justly with me. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | What in the contrary? |
| [[Roderigo|RODERIGO]] | Every day thou daff‘st me with some device, [[Iago]], and rather, as it seems to me now, keep’st from me all conveniency than suppliest me with the least advantage of [[Hope|hope]]. I will indeed no longer endure it, nor am I yet persuaded to put up in peace what already I have foolishly suffered. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Will you hear me, [[Roderigo]]? |
| [[Roderigo|RODERIGO]] | [[Faith]], I have heard too much, for your words and performances are no kin together. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | You charge me most unjustly. |
| [[Roderigo|RODERIGO]] | With naught but [[Truth|truth]]. I have wasted myself out of my means. The jewels you have had from me to deliver [[Desdemona]] would half have corrupted a votarist. You have told me she hath received ’em, and returned me expectations and comforts of sudden respect and acquaintance, but I find none. 195 |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | [[Well]], go to, very [[Well|well]]. |
| [[Roderigo|RODERIGO]] | ‘Very [[Well|well]]’, ‘go to’! I cannot go to, man, nor ’tis not very [[Well|well]]. Nay, I think it is scurvy, and begin to find myself fopped in it. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Very [[Well|well]]. |
| [[Roderigo|RODERIGO]] | I tell you ’tis not very [[Well|well]]. I will make myself known to [[Desdemona]]. If she will return me my jewels, I will give over my suit and repent my unlawful solicitation. If not, assure yourself I will seek satisfaction of you. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | You have said now. |
| [[Roderigo|RODERIGO]] | Ay, and said nothing but what I protest intendment of doing. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Why, now I see there’s mettle in thee, and even from this instant do build on thee a better opinion than ever before. Give me thy hand, [[Roderigo]]. Thou hast taken against me a most just exception, but yet I protest I have dealt most directly in thy affair. |
| [[Roderigo|RODERIGO]] | It hath not appeared. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | I grant, indeed, it hath not appeared, and your suspicion is not without wit and judgement. But, [[Roderigo]], if thou hast that in thee indeed which I have greater reason to believe now than ever—I mean purpose, [[Courage|courage]], and vatour—this [[Night|night]] show it. If thou the next [[Night|night]] following enjoy not [[Desdemona]], take me from this world with treachery, and devise engines for my life. |
| [[Roderigo|RODERIGO]] | [[Well]], what is it? Is it within reason and compass? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Sir, there is especial commission come from [[Venice]] to depute [[Cassio]] in [[Othello]]’s place. |
| [[Roderigo|RODERIGO]] | Is that true? Why then, [[Othello]] and [[Desdemona]] return again to [[Venice]]. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | O no, he goes into Mauritania, and takes away with him the fair [[Desdemona]], unless his abode be lingered here by some accident, wherein none can be so determinate as the removing of [[Cassio]]. |
| [[Roderigo|RODERIGO]] | How do you mean‘removing’ of him? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Why, by making him uncapable of [[Othello]]’s place—knocking out his brains. |
| [[Roderigo|RODERIGO]] | And that you would have me to do. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Ay, if you dare do yourself a profit and a right. He sups tonight with a harlotry, and thither will I go to him. He knows not yet of his honourable fortune. If you will watch his going thence, which I will fashion to fall out between twelve and one, you may take him at your pleasure. I will be near, to second your attempt, and he shall fall between us. Come, stand not amazed at it, but go along with me. I will show you such a necessity in his [[Death|death]] that you shall think yourself bound to put it on him. It is now high supper-[[Time|time]], and the [[Night|night]] grows to waste. About it. |
| [[Roderigo|RODERIGO]] | I will hear further reason for this. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | And you shall be satisfied. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt |
