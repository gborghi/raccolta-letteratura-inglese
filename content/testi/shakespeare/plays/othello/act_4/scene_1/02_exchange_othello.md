| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Did he confess it? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Good sir, be a man. Think every bearded fellow that’s but yoked May draw with you. There’s millions now alive That nightly lie in those unproper beds Which they dare swear peculiar. Your case is better. O, ’tis the spite of hell, the fiend’s arch-mock, To lip a wanton in a secure couch And to suppose her chaste! No, let me know, And knowing what I am, I know what she shall be. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | O, thou art wise, ’tis certain. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Stand you a while apart. Confine yourself but in a patient list. Whilst you were here, o‘erwhelmèd with your grief— A passion most unsuiting such a man— [[Cassio]] came hither. I shifted him away, And laid good ’scuse upon your ecstasy, Bade him anon return and here speak with me, The which he promised. Do but encave yourself, And mark the fleers, the gibes and notable scorns That dwell in every region of his face. For I will make him tell the tale anew, Where, how, how oft, how long ago, and when He hath and is again to cope your wife. I say, but mark his gesture. Marry, patience, Or I shall say you’re all-in-all in spleen, And nothing of a man. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Dost thou hear, [[Iago]]? I will be found most cunning in my patience, But—dost thou hear?—most bloody. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | That’s not amiss, But yet keep [[Time|time]] in all. Will you withdraw? [[Othello]] stands apart Now will I question [[Cassio]] of [[Bianca]], A hussy that by selling her desires Buys herself bread and cloth. It is a creature That dotes on [[Cassio]]—as ’tis the strumpet’s plague To beguile many and be beguiled by one. He, when he hears of her, cannot restrain From the excess of laughter. Enter [[Cassio]] Here he comes. As he shall smile, [[Othello]] shall go mad; And his unbookish [[Jealousy|jealousy]] must conster Poor [[Cassio]]’s smiles, gestures, and light behaviours Quite in the wrong. How do you now, lieutenant? |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | The worser that you give me the addition Whose want even kills me. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Ply [[Desdemona]] [[Well|well]] and you are sure on’t. Now, if this suit lay in [[Bianca]]’s power, How quickly should you [[Speed|speed]]! |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | (laughing) Alas, poor caitiff! |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | (aside) Look how he laughs already. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | I never knew a woman [[Love|love]] man so. |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | Alas, poor rogue! I think i’[[Faith|faith]] she loves me. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | (aside) Now he denies it faintly, and laughs it out. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Do you hear, [[Cassio]]? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | (aside) Now he importunes him To tell it o’er. Go to, [[Well|well]] said, [[Well|well]] said. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | She gives it out that you shall marry her. Do you intend it? |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | Ha, ha, ha! |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | (aside) Do ye triumph, Roman, do you triumph? |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | I marry! What, a customer? Prithee, bear some [[Charity|charity]] to my wit—do not think it so unwholesome. Ha, ha, ha! |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | (aside) So, so, so, so. They laugh that wins. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | [[Faith]], the cry goes that you marry her. |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | Prithee, say true. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | I am a very [[Villain|villain]] else. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | (aside) Ha’ you scored me? [[Well]]. 125 |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | This is the monkey’s own giving out. She is persuaded I will marry her out of her own [[Love|love]] and flattery, not out of my promise. |
