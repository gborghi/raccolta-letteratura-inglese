| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | (aside) [[Iago]] beckons me. Now he begins the story. [[Othello]] draws closer |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | She was here even now. She haunts me in every place. I was the other day talking on the sea-bank with certain Venetians, and thither comes the bauble, and falls me thus about my neck. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | (aside) Crying ‘O dear Cassiol’ as it were. His gesture imports it. |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | So hangs and lolls and weeps upon me, so shakes and pulls me—ha, ha, ha! |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | (aside) Now he tells how she plucked him to my chamber. O, I see that nose of yours, but not that dog I shall throw it to! |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | [[Well]], I must leave her company. Enter [[Bianca]] |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Before me, look where she comes. |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | ’Tis such another fitchew! Marry, a perfumed one. (To [[Bianca]]) What do you mean by this haunting of me? |
| [[Bianca|BIANCA]] | Let the devil and his dam haunt you. What did you mean by that same handkerchief you gave me even now? I was a fine [[Fool|fool]] to take it. I must take out the whole work—a likely piece of work, that you should find it in your chamber and know not who left it there. This is some minx’s token, and I must take out the work. There, give it your hobby-[[Horse|horse]]. (Giving [[Cassio]] the napkin) Wheresoever you had it, I’ll take out no work on’t. |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | How now, my sweet [[Bianca]], how now, how now? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | (aside) By heaven, that should be my handkerchief. |
| [[Bianca|BIANCA]] | An you’ll come to supper tonight, you may. An you will not, come when you are next prepared for. Exit |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | After her, after her. |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | [[Faith]], I must, she’ll rail in the streets else. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Will you sup there? |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | [[Faith]], I intend so. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | [[Well]], I may chance to see you, for I would very fain speak with you. |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | Prithee, come, will you? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Go to, say no more. Exit [[Cassio]] |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | How shall I murder him, [[Iago]]? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Did you perceive how he laughed at his vice? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | O [[Iago]]! |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | And did you see the handkerchief? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Was that mine? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Yours, by this hand. And to see how he prizes the foolish woman your wife. She gave it him, and he hath given it his whore. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | I would have him nine years a-killing. A fine woman, a fair woman, a sweet woman. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Nay, you must forget that. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Ay, let her rot and perish, and be damned tonight, for she shall not live. No, my heart is turned to stone; I strike it, and it hurts my hand. O, the world hath not a sweeter creature! She might lie by an emperor’s side, and command him tasks. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Nay, that’s not your way. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Hang her, I do but say what she is—so delicate with her needle, an admirable musician. O, she will sing the savageness out of a bear! Of so high and plenteous wit and invention. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | She’s the worse for all this. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | O, a thousand, a thousand times! And then of so gentle a condition. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Ay, too gentle. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Nay, that’s certain. But yet the pity of it, [[Iago]]. O, [[Iago]], the pity of it, [[Iago]]! |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | If you are so fond over her iniquity, give her patent to offend; for if it touch not you, it comes near nobody. |
