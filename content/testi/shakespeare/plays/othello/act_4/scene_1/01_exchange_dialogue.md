| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Iago]] and [[Othello]] |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Will you think so? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Think so, [[Iago]]? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | What, to kiss in private? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | An unauthorized kiss. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Or to be naked with her friend in bed An hour or more, not meaning any harm? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Naked in bed, [[Iago]], and not mean harm? It is hypocrisy against the devil. They that mean virtuously and yet do so, The devil their virtue tempts, and they tempt heaven. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | If they do nothing, ’tis a venial slip. But if I give my wife a handkerchief— |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | What then? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Why then, ’tis hers, my lord, and being hers, She may, I think, bestow’t on any man. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | She is protectress of her [[Honour|honour]], too. May she give that? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Her [[Honour|honour]] is an essence that’s not seen. They have it very oft that have it not. But for the handkerchief— |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | By heaven, I would most gladly have forgot it. Thou said‘st—O, it comes o’er my [[Memory|memory]] As doth [[The Raven|the raven]] o’er the infectious [[house]], Boding to all!—he had my handkerchief. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Ay, what of that? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | That’s not so good now. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | What if I had said I had seen him do you wrong, Or heard him say—as knaves be such abroad, Who having by their own importunate suit Or voluntary dotage of some mistress Convinced or supplied them, cannot choose But they must blab— |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Hath he said anything? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | He hath, my lord. But, be you [[Well|well]] assured, No more than he’ll unswear. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | What hath he said? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | [[Faith]], that he did—I know not what he did. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | What, what? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Lie— |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | With her? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | With her, on her, what you will. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Lie with her? Lie on her? We say ‘lie on her’ when they belie her. Lie with her? ’Swounds, that’s fulsome! Handkerchief—confessions—hankerchief. To confess and be hanged for his labour. First to be hanged and then to confess! I tremble at it. [[Nature]] would not invest herself in such shadowing passion without some instruction. It is not words that shakes me thus. Pish! Noses, ears, and lips! Is’t possible? Confess? Handkerchief? O devil! He falls down in a trance |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Work on; my medicine works. Thus credulous fools are caught, And many worthy and chaste dames even thus, All guiltless, meet reproach. What ho, my lord! My lord, I say. [[Othello]]! Enter [[Cassio]] How now, [[Cassio]]? |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | What’s the matter? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | My lord is fall’n into an epilepsy. This is his second fit. He had one yesterday. |
| [[Cassio|CASSIO]] | Rub him about the temples. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | No, forbear. The lethargy must have his quiet course. If not, he foams at mouth, and by and by Breaks out to savage [[Madness|madness]]. Look, he stirs. Do you withdraw yourself a little while, He will recover straight. When he is gone I would on great occasion speak with you. Exit [[Cassio]] How is it, general? Have you not hurt your head? |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | Dost thou mock me? |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | I mock you not, by heaven. Would you would bear your fortune like a man. |
| [[Othello|OTHELLO]] | A hornèd man’s a [[Monster|monster]] and a beast. |
| [[Iago|IAGO]] | There’s many a beast then in a populous [[City|city]], And many a civil [[Monster|monster]]. |
