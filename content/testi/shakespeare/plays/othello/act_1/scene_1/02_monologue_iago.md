| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Despise me If I do not. Three great ones of the [[City|city]], In personal suit to make me his lieutenant, Off-capped to him; and by the [[Faith|faith]] of man I know my price, I am worth no worse a place. But he, as loving his own [[Pride|pride]] and purposes, Evades them with a bombast circumstance Horribly stuffed with epithets of war, Nonsuits my mediators; for ‘Certes,’ says he, ‘I have already chose my officer.’ And what was he? Forsooth, a great arithmetician, One [[Michael]] [[Cassio]], a Florentine, A fellow almost damned in a fair wife, That never set a squadron in the field Nor the division of a battle knows More than a spinster—unless the bookish theoric, Wherein the togaed consuls can propose As masterly as he. Mere prattle without practice Is all his soldiership; but he, sir, had th’election, And I—of whom his eyes had seen the proof At Rhodes, at Cyprus, and on other grounds Christened and heathen—must be beleed and calmed By debitor and creditor. This counter-caster, He in good [[Time|time]] must his lieutenant be, And I—God bless the mark!—his Moorship’s ensign. |
