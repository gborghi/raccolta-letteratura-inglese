| [[Iago|IAGO]] | Thus do I ever make my [[Fool|fool]] my purse— For I mine own gained knowledge should profane If I would [[Time|time]] expend with such a snipe But for my sport and profit. I hate the Moor, And it is thought abroad that ’twixt my sheets He has done my office. I know not if’t be true, But I, for mere suspicion in that kind, Will do as if for surety. He holds me [[Well|well]]: The better shall my purpose work on him. [[Cassio]]’s a proper man. Let me see now, To get his place, and to plume up my will In [[Double|double]] knavery—how, how? Let’s see. After some [[Time|time]] to abuse [[Othello]]’s ears That he is too familiar with his wife; He hath a person and a smooth dispose To be suspected, framed to make women false. The Moor is of a free and open [[Nature|nature]], That thinks men honest that but seem to be so, And will as tenderly be led by th’ nose As asses are. I ha’t. It is ingendered. Hell and [[Night|night]] Must bring this monstrous birth to the world’s light. Exit |
