| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Helen]] and the Widow |
| [[Helen|HELEN]] | If you misdoubt me that I am not she,<br>I know not how I shall assure you further<br>But I shall lose the grounds I work upon. |
| WIDOW | Though my estate be fall’n, I was [[Well|well]] born,<br>Nothing acquainted with these businesses,<br>And would not put my reputation now<br>In any staining act. |
| [[Helen|HELEN]] | Nor would I wish you.<br>First give me trust the Count he is my husband,<br>And what to your sworn counsel I have spoken<br>Is so from word to word, and then you cannot,<br>By the good aid that I of you shall borrow,<br>Err in bestowing it. |
| WIDOW | I should believe you,<br>For you have showed me that which [[Well|well]] approves<br>You’re great in fortune. |
| [[Helen|HELEN]] | Take this purse of gold,<br>And let me buy your friendly help thus far,<br>Which I will over-pay, and pay again<br>When I have found it. The Count he woos your<br>daughter,<br>Lays down his wanton siege before her [[Beauty|beauty]],<br>Resolved to carry her. Let her in fine consent,<br>As we’ll direct her how ‘tis best to bear it.<br>Now his important [[Blood|blood]] will naught deny<br>That she’ll demand. A ring the County wears,<br>That downward hath succeeded in his [[house]]<br>From son to son some four or five descents<br>Since the first father wore it. This ring he holds<br>In most rich choice; yet in his idle [[Fire|fire]]<br>To buy his will it would not seem too dear,<br>Howe’er repented after. |
| WIDOW | Now I see the [[Bottom|bottom]] of your purpose. |
| [[Helen|HELEN]] | You see it lawful then. It is no more<br>But that your daughter ere she seems as won<br>Desires this ring; appoints him an encounter;<br>In fine, delivers me to fill the [[Time|time]],<br>Herself most chastely absent. After,<br>To marry her I’ll add three thousand crowns<br>To what is passed already. |
| WIDOW | I have yielded.<br>Instruct my daughter how she shall persever,<br>That [[Time|time]] and place with this deceit so lawful<br>May prove coherent. Every [[Night|night]] he comes<br>With musics of all sorts, and [[songs]] composed<br>To her unworthiness. It nothing steads us<br>To chide him from our eaves, for he persists<br>As if his life lay on’t. |
| [[Helen|HELEN]] | Why then tonight<br>Let us [[Essay|essay]] our plot, which if it [[Speed|speed]]<br>Is wicked meaning in a lawful deed<br>And lawful meaning in a wicked act,<br>Where both not sin, and yet a sinful fact.<br>But let’s about it. Exeunt |
