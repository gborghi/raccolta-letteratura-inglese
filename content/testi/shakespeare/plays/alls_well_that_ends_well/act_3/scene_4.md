| *(didascalia)* | Enter the Countess and Reynaldo her steward, with a letter |
| COUNTESS | Alas! And would you take the letter of her?<br>Might you not know she would do as she has done,<br>By sending me a letter? Read it again. |
| REYNALDO | (reads the letter)<br>‘I am Saint [[Jaques]]’ [[Pilgrim|pilgrim]], thither gone.<br>Ambitious [[Love|love]] hath so in me offended<br>That barefoot plod I the cold ground upon<br>With sainted vow my faults to have amended.<br>Write, write, that from the bloody course of war<br>My dearest master, your dear son, may hie.<br>Bless him at [[Home|home]] in peace, whilst I from far<br>His name with zealous fervour sanctify.<br>His taken labours bid him me forgive;<br>I, his despiteful Juno, sent him forth<br>From courtly friends, with camping foes to live,<br>Where [[Death|death]] and danger [[dogs]] the heels of worth.<br>He is too good and fair for [[Death|death]] and me;<br>Whom I myself embrace to set him free.’ |
| COUNTESS | Ah, what sharp stings are in her mildest words!<br>Reynaldo, you did never lack advice so much<br>As letting her pass so. Had I spoke with her,<br>I could have [[Well|well]] diverted her intents,<br>Which thus she hath prevented. |
| REYNALDO | Pardon me, madam.<br>If I had given you this at over-[[Night|night]]<br>She might have been o’erta’en—and yet she writes<br>Pursuit would be but vain. |
| COUNTESS | What angel shall<br>Bless this unworthy husband? He cannot thrive<br>Unless her prayers, whom heaven delights to hear<br>And loves to grant, reprieve him from the wrath<br>Of greatest justice. Write, write, Reynaldo,<br>To this unworthy husband of his wife.<br>Let every word weigh heavy of her worth,<br>That he does weigh too light; my greatest grief,<br>Though little he do feel it, set down sharply.<br>Dispatch the most convenient messenger.<br>When haply he shall hear that she is gone,<br>He will return, and [[Hope|hope]] I may that she,<br>Hearing so much, will [[Speed|speed]] her foot again,<br>Led hither by pure [[Love|love]]. Which of them both<br>Is dearest to me I have no skill in sense<br>To make distinction. Provide this messenger.<br>My heart is heavy and mine age is weak;<br>Grief would have [[Tears|tears]], and sorrow bids me speak. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt |
