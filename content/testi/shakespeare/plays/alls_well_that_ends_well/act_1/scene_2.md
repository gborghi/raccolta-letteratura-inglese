| *(didascalia)* | A flourish of cornetts. Enter the [[King of France]] with letters, the two Lords [[Dumaine]], and divers attendants |
| [[King|KING]] | The Florentines and Sienese are by th’ears,<br>Have fought with equal fortune, and continue<br>A braving war. |
| FIRST LORD [[Dumaine|DUMAINE]] | So ’tis reported, sir. |
| [[King|KING]] | Nay, ’tis most credible: we here receive it<br>A certainty vouched from our cousin Austria,<br>With caution that the Florentine will move us<br>For speedy aid-wherein our dearest friend<br>Prejudicates the business, and would seem<br>To have us make denial. |
| FIRST LORD [[Dumaine|DUMAINE]] | His [[Love|love]] and wisdom<br>Approved so to your majesty may plead<br>For amplest credence. |
| [[King|KING]] | He hath armed our answer,<br>And Florence is denied before he comes.<br>Yet for our gentlemen that mean to see<br>The Tuscan service, freely have they leave<br>To stand on either part. |
| SECOND LORD [[Dumaine|DUMAINE]] | It [[Well|well]] may serve<br>A nursery to our gentry, who are sick<br>For breathing and exploit. |
| [[King|KING]] | What’s he comes here? |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Bertram]], Lafeu, and Paroles |
| FIRST LORD [[Dumaine|DUMAINE]] | It is the Count Roussillon, my good lord,<br>Young [[Bertram]]. |
| [[King|KING]] | (to [[Bertram]]) Youth, thou bear’st thy father’s face.<br>Frank [[Nature|nature]], rather curious than in haste,<br>Hath [[Well|well]] composed thee. Thy father’s moral parts<br>Mayst thou inherit, too. Welcome to [[Paris]]. |
| [[Bertram|BERTRAM]] | My thanks and duty are your majesty’s. |
| [[King|KING]] | I would I had that corporal soundness now<br>As when thy father and myself in [[Friendship|friendship]]<br>First tried our soldiership. He did look far<br>Into the service of the [[Time|time]], and was<br>Discipled of the bravest. He lasted long,<br>But on us both did haggish age steal on,<br>And wore us out of act. It much repairs me<br>To talk of your good father. In his youth<br>He had the wit which I can [[Well|well]] observe<br>Today in our young lords, but they may jest<br>Till their own scorn return to them unnoted<br>Ere they can hide their levity in [[Honour|honour]].<br>So like a courtier, contempt nor bitterness<br>Were in his [[Pride|pride]] or sharpness; if they were<br>His equal had awaked them, and his [[Honour|honour]]—<br>[[Clock]] to itself—knew the true minute when<br>Exception bid him speak, and at this [[Time|time]]<br>His tongue obeyed his hand. Who were below him<br>He used as creatures of another place,<br>And bowed his eminent top to their low ranks,<br>Making them proud of his [[Humility|humility]],.<br>In their poor praise he humbled. Such a man<br>Might be a copy to these younger times,<br>Which followed [[Well|well]] would demonstrate them now<br>But goers-backward. |
| [[Bertram|BERTRAM]] | His good remembrance, sir, Lies richer in your thoughts than on his tomb.<br>So in approof lives not his epitaph<br>As in your royal speech. |
| [[King|KING]] | Would I were with him! He would always say—<br>Methinks I hear him now; his plausive words<br>He scattered not in ears, but grafted them<br>To grow there and to bear. ‘Let me not live’—<br>This his good [[Melancholy|melancholy]] oft began<br>On the catastrophe and heel of pastime,<br>When it was out—‘Let me not live’, quoth he,<br>‘After my flame lacks oil, to be the snuff<br>Of younger spirits, whose apprehensive senses<br>All but new things disdain, whose judgements are<br>Mere fathers of their garments, whose constancies<br>Expire before their fashions.’ This he wished.<br>I after him do after him wish too,<br>Since I nor wax nor honey can bring [[Home|home]],<br>I quickly were dissolvèd from my hive<br>To give some labourers room. |
| SECOND LORD [[Dumaine|DUMAINE]] | You’re lovèd, sir.<br>They that least lend it you shall lack you first. |
| [[King|KING]] | I fill a place, I know’t.—How long is’t, Count,<br>Since the physician at your father’s died?<br>He was much famed. |
| [[Bertram|BERTRAM]] | Some six months since, my lord. |
| [[King|KING]] | If he were living I would try him yet.—<br>Lend me an arm.—The rest have worn me out<br>With several applications. [[Nature]] and sickness<br>[[debate|Debate]] it at their leisure. Welcome, Count.<br>My son’s no dearer.<br>[[Bertram|BERTRAM]])<br>Thank your majesty. |
| *(didascalia)* | Flourish. Exeunt |
