| *(didascalia)* | Enter Second Lord [[Dumaine]], with five or six other soldiers, in ambush |
| *(didascalia)* | SECOND LORD [[Dumaine|DUMAINE]] He can come no other way but by this hedge corner. When you sally upon him, speak what terrible language you will. Though you understand it not yourselves, no matter, for we must not seem to understand him, unless some one among us, whom we must produce for an interpreter. 6 |
| INTERPRETER | Good captain, let me be th’interpreter. |
| *(didascalia)* | SECOND LORD [[Dumaine|DUMAINE]] Art not acquainted with him? Knows he not thy voice? |
| INTERPRETER | No, sir, I warrant you. |
| *(didascalia)* | SECOND LORD [[Dumaine|DUMAINE]] But what linsey-woolsey hast thou to speak to us again? |
| INTERPRETER | E’en such as you speak to me. |
| *(didascalia)* | SECOND LORD [[Dumaine|DUMAINE]] He must think us some band of strangers i’th’ adversary’s entertainment. Now he hath a smack of all neighbouring languages, therefore we must every one be a man of his own fancy. Not to know what we speak one to another, so we seem to know, is to know straight our purpose: choughs’ language, gabble enough and good enough. As for you, interpreter, you must seem very politic. But couch, ho! Here he comes, to beguile two hours in a [[Sleep|sleep]], and then to return and swear the lies he forges. |
| *(didascalia)* | They hide. Enter Paroles. [[Clock]] strikes |
| PAROLES | Ten o‘[[Clock|clock]]. Within these three hours ’twill be [[Time|time]] enough to go [[Home|home]]. What shall I say I have done? It must be a very plausive invention that carries it. They begin to smoke me, and disgraces have of late knocked too often at my door. I find my tongue is too foolhardy, but my heart hath the [[Fear|fear]] of Mars before it, and of his creatures, not daring the reports of my tongue. 31 |
| *(didascalia)* | SECOND LORD [[Dumaine|DUMAINE]] (aside) This is the first [[Truth|truth]] that e’er thine own tongue was guilty of. |
| PAROLES | What the devil should move me to undertake the recovery of this drum, being not ignorant of the impossibility, and knowing I had no such purpose? I must give myself some hurts, and say I got them in exploit. Yet slight ones will not carry it. They will say, ‘Came you off with so little?’ And great ones I dare not give. Wherefore, what’s the instance? Tongue, I must put you into a butter-woman’s mouth, and buy myself another of Bajazet’s mute, if you prattle me into these perils. |
| *(didascalia)* | SECOND LORD [[Dumaine|DUMAINE]] (aside) Is it possible he should know what he is, and be that he is? |
| PAROLES | I would the cutting of my garments would serve the turn, or the breaking of my Spanish [[Sword|sword]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | SECOND LORD [[Dumaine|DUMAINE]] (aside) We cannot afford you so. |
| PAROLES | Or the baring of my beard, and to say it was in stratagem. |
| *(didascalia)* | SECOND LORD [[Dumaine|DUMAINE]] (aside) ’Twould not do. |
| PAROLES | Or to drown my clothes, and say I was stripped. |
| *(didascalia)* | SECOND LORD [[Dumaine|DUMAINE]] (aside) Hardly serve. |
| *(didascalia)* | PAROLES Though I swore I leapt from the [[Window|window]] of the citadel? 55 SECOND LORD [[Dumaine|DUMAINE]] (aside) How deep? |
| PAROLES | Thirty fathom. |
| *(didascalia)* | SECOND LORD [[Dumaine|DUMAINE]] (aside) Three great oaths would scarce make that be believed. |
| PAROLES | I would I had any drum of the enemy’s. I would swear I recovered it. |
| *(didascalia)* | SECOND LORD [[Dumaine|DUMAINE]] (aside) You shall hear one anon. |
| *(didascalia)* | PAROLES A drum now of the enemy’s—Alarum within. The ambush rushes forth |
| *(didascalia)* | SECOND LORD [[Dumaine|DUMAINE]] Throca movousus, cargo, cargo, cargo. |
| *(didascalia)* | SOLDIERS (severally) Cargo, cargo, cargo, villianda par corbo, cargo. |
| *(didascalia)* | They seize and blindfold him |
| PAROLES | O ransom, ransom, do not hide mine eyes. |
| INTERPRETER | Boskos thromuldo boskos. |
| PAROLES | I know you are the Moscows regiment,<br>And I shall lose my life for want of language.<br>If there be here German or Dane, Low Dutch,<br>Italian, or French, let him speak to me,<br>I’ll discover that which shall undo the Florentine. |
| INTERPRETER | Boskos vauvado.—<br>I understand thee, and can speak thy tongue.—<br>Kerelybonto.—Sir,<br>Betake thee to thy [[Faith|faith]], for seventeen poniards<br>Are at thy bosom. |
| PAROLES | O! |
| INTERPRETER | O pray, pray, pray!—<br>Manka revania dulche? 80 |
| *(didascalia)* | SECOND LORD [[Dumaine|DUMAINE]] |
| INTERPRETER | Oscorbidulchos volivorco. |
| INTERPRETER | The general is content to spare thee yet,<br>And, hoodwinked as thou art, will lead thee on<br>To gather from thee. Haply thou mayst inform<br>Something to save thy life. |
| PAROLES | O let me live,<br>And all the [[Secrets|secrets]] of our camp I’ll show,<br>Their force, their purposes; nay, I’ll speak that<br>Which you will [[Wonder|wonder]] at. |
| INTERPRETER | But wilt thou faithfully? |
| PAROLES | If I do not, damn me. |
| INTERPRETER | Acordo linta.—<br>Come on, thou art granted space. 90 |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt all but Second Lord [[Dumaine]] and a Soldier |
| INTERPRETER | A short alarum within |
| *(didascalia)* | SECOND LORD [[Dumaine|DUMAINE]] |
| INTERPRETER | Go tell the Count Roussillon and my brother<br>We have caught the woodcock, and will keep him<br>muffled<br>Till we do hear from them. |
| SOLDIER | Captain, I will. |
| *(didascalia)* | SECOND LORD [[Dumaine|DUMAINE]] |
| SOLDIER | A will betray us all unto ourselves.<br>Inform on that. |
| SOLDIER | So I will, sir. |
| *(didascalia)* | SECOND LORD [[Dumaine|DUMAINE]] |
| SOLDIER | Till then I’ll keep him dark and safely locked. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt severally |
