| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Helen]], the Widow, and [[Diana]] |
| [[Helen|HELEN]] | That you may [[Well|well]] perceive I have not wronged you,<br>One of the greatest in the Christian world<br>Shall be my surety; fore whose throne ‘tis needful,<br>Ere I can perfect mine intents, to kneel.<br>[[Time]] was, I did him a desired office<br>Dear almost as his life; which gratitude<br>Through flinty Tartar’s bosom would peep forth<br>And answer ‘Thanks’. I duly am informed<br>His grace is at Marseilles, to which place<br>We have convenient convoy. You must know<br>I am supposed dead. The army breaking,<br>My husband hies him [[Home|home]], where, heaven aiding,<br>And by the leave of my good lord the [[King]],<br>We’ll be before our welcome. |
| WIDOW | Gentle madam,<br>You never had a servant to whose trust<br>Your business was more welcome. |
| [[Helen|HELEN]] | Nor you, mistress,<br>Ever a friend whose thoughts more truly labour<br>To recompense your [[Love|love]]. [[Doubt]] not but heaven<br>Hath brought me up to be your daughter’s dower,<br>As it hath fated her to be my motive<br>And helper to a husband. But O, strange men,<br>That can such sweet use make of what they hate,<br>When saucy trusting of the cozened thoughts<br>Defiles the pitchy [[Night|night]]; so lust doth play<br>With what it loathes, for that which is away.<br>But more of this hereafter. You, [[Diana]],<br>Under my poor instructions yet must suffer<br>Something in my behalf. |
| [[Diana|DIANA]] | Let [[Death|death]] and honesty<br>Go with your impositions, I am yours,<br>Upon your will to suffer. |
| [[Helen|HELEN]] | Yet, I pray you.—<br>But with that word the [[Time|time]] will bring on [[Summer|summer]],<br>When briers shall have [[Leaves|leaves]] as [[Well|well]] as thorns<br>And be as sweet as sharp. We must away,<br>Our wagon is prepared, and [[Time|time]] revives us.<br>All’s [[Well|well]] that ends [[Well|well]]; still the fine’s the [[Crown|crown]].<br>Whate’er the course, the end is the renown. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt |
