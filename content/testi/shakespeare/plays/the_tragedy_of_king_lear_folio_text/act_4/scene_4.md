| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Regan]] and Oswald the steward |
| [[Regan|REGAN]] | But are my brother’s powers set forth? |
| OSWALD | Ay, madam. |
| [[Regan|REGAN]] | Himself in person there? |
| OSWALD | Madam, with much ado.<br>Your sister is the better soldier. |
| [[Regan|REGAN]] | Lord Edmond spake not with your lord at [[Home|home]]? |
| OSWALD | No, madam. |
| [[Regan|REGAN]] | What might import my sister’s letters to him? |
| OSWALD | I know not, lady. |
| [[Regan|REGAN]] | [[Faith]], he is posted hence on serious matter.<br>It was great ignorance, [[Gloucester]]’s eyes being out,<br>To let him live. Where he arrives he moves<br>All hearts against us. Edmond, I think, is gone,<br>In pity of his misery, to dispatch<br>His ‘nighted life, moreover to descry<br>The strength o’th’ enemy. |
| OSWALD | I must needs after, madam, with my letter. |
| [[Regan|REGAN]] | Our troops set forth tomorrow. Stay with us.<br>The ways are dangerous. |
| OSWALD | I may not, madam.<br>My lady charged my duty in this business. |
| [[Regan|REGAN]] | Why should she write to Edmond? Might not you<br>Transport her purposes by word? Belike—<br>Some things—I know not what. I’ll [[Love|love]] thee much:<br>Let me unseal the letter. |
| OSWALD | Madam, I had rather— |
| [[Regan|REGAN]] | I know your lady does not [[Love|love]] her husband.<br>I am sure of that, and at her late being here<br>She gave strange oeillades and most speaking looks<br>To noble Edmond. I know you are of her bosom. |
| OSWALD | I, madam? |
| [[Regan|REGAN]] | I speak in understanding. Y’are, I know’t.<br>Therefore I do advise you take this note.<br>My lord is dead. Edmond and I have talked,<br>And more convenient is he for my hand<br>Than for your lady’s. You may gather more.<br>If you do find him, pray you give him this,<br>And when your mistress hears thus much from you,<br>I pray [[Desire|desire]] her call her wisdom to her.<br>So, fare you [[Well|well]].<br>If you do chance to hear of that blind traitor,<br>Preferment falls on him that cuts him off. |
| OSWALD | Would I could meet him, madam. I should show<br>What party I do follow. |
| [[Regan|REGAN]] | Fare thee [[Well|well]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt severally |
