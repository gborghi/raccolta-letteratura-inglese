| LORD | [[Well]] done, my lord. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | Though I am satisfied, and need no more Than what I know, yet shall the oracle Give rest to th’ minds of others such as he, Whose ignorant credulity will not Come up to th’ [[Truth|truth]]. So have we thought it good From our free person she should be confined, Lest that the treachery of the two fled hence Be left her to perform. Come, follow us. We are to speak in public; for this business Will raise us all. |
| ANTIGONUS | (aside) To laughter, as I take it, If the good [[Truth|truth]] were known. Exeunt |
