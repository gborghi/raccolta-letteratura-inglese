| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Time]], the [[Chorus]] |
| [[Time|TIME]] | I that please some, try all; both joy and terror<br>Of good and bad; that makes and unfolds error,<br>Now take upon me in the name of [[Time]]<br>To use my wings. Impute it not a crime<br>To me or my swift passage that I slide<br>O‘er sixteen years and leave the growth untried<br>Of that wide gap, since it is in my power<br>To o’erthrow law, and in one [[Self|self]]-born hour<br>To plant and o‘erwhelm custom. Let me pass<br>The same I am ere ancient’st order was<br>Or what is now received. I witness to<br>The times that brought them in; so shall I do<br>To th’ freshest things now reigning, and make stale<br>The glistering of this present as my tale<br>Now seems to it. Your patience this allowing,<br>I turn my glass, and give my scene such growing<br>As you had slept between. [[Leontes]] leaving<br>Th‘effects of his fond jealousies, so grieving<br>That he shuts up himself, imagine me,<br>Gentle spectators, that I now may be<br>In fair Bohemia, and remember [[Well|well]]<br>I mentioned a son o’th’ [[King]]‘s, which [[Florizel]]<br>I now name to you; and with [[Speed|speed]] so pace<br>To speak of [[Perdita]], now grown in grace<br>Equal with wond’ring. What of her ensues<br>I list not prophesy, but let [[Time]]’s news<br>Be known when ‘tis brought forth. A shepherd’s<br>daughter<br>And what to her adheres, which follows after,<br>Is th’argument of [[Time]]. Of this allow,<br>If ever you have spent [[Time|time]] worse ere now.<br>If never, yet that [[Time]] himself doth say<br>He wishes earnestly you never may. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit |
