| MAMILLIUS | Yes, if you will, my lord. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | Thou want‘st a rough pash and the shoots that I have, To be full like me. Yet they say we are Almost as like as eggs. Women say so, That will say anything. But were they false As o’er-dyed blacks, as [[Wind|wind]], as waters, false As dice are to be wished by one that fixes No bourn ‘twixt his and mine, yet were it true To say this boy were like me. Come, sir page, Look on me with your welkin eye. Sweet [[Villain|villain]], Most dear’st, my collop! Can thy dam—may’t be?— Affection, thy intention stabs the centre. Thou dost make possible things not so held, Communicat‘st with [[Dreams|dreams]]—how can this be?— With what’s unreal thou coactive art, And fellow’st nothing. Then ‘tis very credent Thou mayst co-join with something, and thou dost— And that beyond commission; and I find it— And that to the infection of my brains And hard’ning of my brows. |
| [[Polixenes|POLIXENES]] | What means Sicilia? |
| [[Hermione|HERMIONE]] | He something seems unsettled. |
| [[Polixenes|POLIXENES]] | How, my lord! |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | What cheer? How is’t with you, best brother? |
| [[Hermione|HERMIONE]] | You look As if you held a brow of much distraction. Are you moved, my lord? |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | No, in good earnest. How sometimes [[Nature|nature]] will betray its folly, Its tenderness, and make itself a pastime To harder bosoms! Looking on the lines Of my boy’s face, methoughts I did recoil Twenty-three years, and saw myself unbreeched, In my green velvet coat; my dagger muzzled, Lest it should bite its master, and so prove, As ornament oft does, too dangerous. How like, methought, I then was to this kernel, This squash, this gentleman.—Mine honest friend, Will you take eggs for money? |
| MAMILLIUS | No, my lord, I’ll fight. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | You will? Why, happy man be’s dole!—My brother, Are you so fond of your young prince as we Do seem to be of ours? |
| [[Polixenes|POLIXENES]] | If at [[Home|home]], sir, He’s all my exercise, my mirth, my matter; Now my sworn friend, and then mine enemy; My parasite, my soldier, statesman, all. He makes a July’s day short as December, And with his varying childness cures in me Thoughts that would thick my [[Blood|blood]]. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | So stands this squire Officed with me. We two will walk, my lord, And leave you to your graver steps. [[Hermione]], How thou lov’st us show in our brother’s welcome. Let what is dear in Sicily be cheap. Next to thyself and my young rover, he’s Apparent to my heart. |
| [[Hermione|HERMIONE]] | If you would seek us, We are yours i’th’ [[Garden|garden]]. Shall’s attend you there? |
