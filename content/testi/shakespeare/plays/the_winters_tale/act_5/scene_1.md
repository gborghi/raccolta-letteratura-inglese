| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Leontes]], Cleomenes, Dion, and [[Paulina]] |
| CLEOMENES | (to [[Leontes]])<br>Sir, you have done enough, and have performed<br>A saint-like sorrow. No fault could you make<br>Which you have not redeemed, indeed, paid down<br>More penitence than done trespass. At the last<br>Do as the heavens have done, forget your evil.<br>With them, forgive yourself. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | Whilst I remember<br>Her and her virtues I cannot forget<br>My blemishes in them, and so still think of<br>The wrong I did myself, which was so much<br>That heirless it hath made my kingdom, and<br>Destroyed the sweet‘st companion that e’er man<br>Bred his hopes out of. True? |
| [[Paulina|PAULINA]] | Too true, my lord.<br>If one by one you wedded all the world,<br>Or from the all that are took something good<br>To make a perfect woman, she you killed<br>Would be unparalleled. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | I think so. Killed?<br>She I killed? I did so. But thou strik’st me<br>Sorely to say I did; it is as bitter<br>Upon thy tongue as in my thought. Now, good now,<br>Say so but seldom. |
| CLEOMENES | Not at all, good lady.<br>You might have spoke a thousand things that would<br>Have done the [[Time|time]] more benefit, and graced<br>Your kindness better. |
| [[Paulina|PAULINA]] | You are one of those<br>Would have him wed again. |
| DION | If you would not so<br>You pity not the state, nor the remembrance<br>Of his most sovereign name, consider little<br>What dangers, by his highness’ fail of issue,<br>May drop upon his kingdom and devour<br>Incertain lookers-on. What were more holy<br>Than to rejoice the former [[Queen|queen]] is [[Well|well]]?<br>What holier, than for royalty’s repair,<br>For present comfort and for future good,<br>To bless the bed of majesty again<br>With a sweet fellow to’t? |
| [[Paulina|PAULINA]] | There is none worthy<br>Respecting her that’s gone. Besides, the gods<br>Will have fulfilled their secret purposes.<br>For has not the divine [[Apollo]] said?<br>Is’t not the tenor of his oracle<br>That [[King]] [[Leontes]] shall not have an heir<br>Till his lost [[Child|child]] be found? Which that it shall<br>Is all as monstrous to our human reason<br>As my Antigonus to break his [[Grave|grave]]<br>And come again to me, who, on my life,<br>Did perish with the infant. ’Tis your counsel<br>My lord should to the heavens be contrary,<br>Oppose against their wills.<br>(To [[Leontes]]) Care not for issue.<br>The [[Crown|crown]] will find an heir. Great Alexander<br>Left his to th’ worthiest, so his successor<br>Was like to be the best. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | Good [[Paulina]],<br>Who hast the [[Memory|memory]] of [[Hermione]],<br>I know, in [[Honour|honour]]—O, that ever I<br>Had squared me to thy counsel! Then even now<br>I might have looked upon my [[Queen|queen]]’s full eyes,<br>Have taken treasure from her lips. |
| [[Paulina|PAULINA]] | And left them<br>More rich for what they yielded. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | Thou speak’st [[Truth|truth]].<br>No more such wives, therefore no wife. One worse,<br>And better used, would make her sainted spirit<br>Again possess her corpse, and on this stage,<br>Where we offenders mourn, appear [[Soul|soul]]-vexed,<br>And begin, ‘Why to me?’ |
| [[Paulina|PAULINA]] | Had she such power<br>She had just cause. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | She had, and would incense me<br>To murder her I married. |
| [[Paulina|PAULINA]] | I should SO.<br>Were I the [[Ghost|ghost]] that walked I’d bid you mark<br>Her eye, and tell me for what dull part in’t<br>You chose her. Then I’d shriek that even your ears<br>Should rift to hear me, and the words that followed<br>Should be, ‘Remember mine’. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | [[Stars]], [[Stars|stars]],<br>And all eyes else, dead coals! [[Fear]] thou no wife.<br>I’ll have no wife, [[Paulina]]. |
| [[Paulina|PAULINA]] | Will you swear<br>Never to marry but by my free leave? |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | Never, [[Paulina]], so be blest my spirit. |
| [[Paulina|PAULINA]] | Then, good my lords, bear witness to his oath. |
| CLEOMENES | You tempt him over-much. |
| [[Paulina|PAULINA]] | Unless another<br>As like [[Hermione]] as is her picture<br>Affront his eye— |
| CLEOMENES | Good madam, I have done. |
| [[Paulina|PAULINA]] | Yet if my lord will marry—if you will, sir;<br>No remedy but you will—give me the office<br>To choose your [[Queen|queen]]. She shall not be so young<br>As was your former, but she shall be such<br>As, walked your first [[Queen|queen]]’s [[Ghost|ghost]], it should take joy<br>To see her in your arms. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | My true [[Paulina]],<br>We shall not marry till thou bidd’st us. |
| [[Paulina|PAULINA]] | That<br>Shall be when your first [[Queen|queen]]’s again in breath.<br>Never till then. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter a Servant |
| SERVANT | One that gives out himself Prince [[Florizel]],<br>Son of [[Polixenes]], with his princess—she<br>The fairest I have yet beheld—desires access<br>To your high presence. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | What with him? He comes not<br>Like to his father’s [[Greatness|greatness]]. His approach,<br>So out of circumstance and sudden, tells us<br>’Tis not a visitation framed, but forced<br>By need and accident. What train? |
| SERVANT | But few,<br>And those but mean. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | His princess, say you, with him? |
| SERVANT | Ay, the most peerless piece of earth, I think,<br>That e’er the sun shone bright on. |
| [[Paulina|PAULINA]] | O, [[Hermione]],<br>As every present [[Time|time]] doth boast itself<br>Above a better, gone, so must thy [[Grave|grave]]<br>Give way to what’s seen now!<br>(To the Servant) Sir, you yourself<br>Have said and writ so; but your writing now<br>Is colder than that theme. She had not been<br>Nor was not to be equalled—thus your verse<br>Flowed with her [[Beauty|beauty]] once. ’Tis shrewdly ebbed<br>To say you have seen a better. |
| SERVANT | Pardon, madam.<br>The one I have almost forgot—your pardon!<br>The other, when she has obtained your eye,<br>Will have your tongue too. This is a creature,<br>Would she begin a sect, might quench the zeal<br>Of all professors else; make proselytes<br>Of who she but bid follow. |
| [[Paulina|PAULINA]] | How? Not women! |
| SERVANT | Women will [[Love|love]] her that she is a woman<br>More worth than any man; men, that she is<br>The rarest of all women. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | Go, Cleomenes.<br>Yourself, assisted with your honoured friends,<br>Bring them to our embracement. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit Cleomenes |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | Still ’tis strange<br>He thus should steal upon us. |
| [[Paulina|PAULINA]] | Had our prince,<br>[[Jewel]] of children, seen this hour, he had paired<br>[[Well]] with this lord. There was not full a month<br>Between their births. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | Prithee no more, cease. Thou know’st<br>He dies to me again when talked of. Sure,<br>When I shall see this gentleman thy speeches<br>Will bring me to consider that which may<br>Unfurnish me of reason. They are come. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Florizel]], [[Perdita]], Cleomenes, and others |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | Your mother was most true to wedlock, Prince,<br>For she did print your royal father off,<br>Conceiving you. Were I but twenty-one,<br>Your father’s image is so hit in you,<br>His very air, that I should call you brother,<br>As I did him, and speak of something wildly<br>By us performed before. Most dearly welcome,<br>And your fair princess—goddess! O, alas,<br>I lost a couple that ’twixt heaven and earth<br>Might thus have stood, begetting [[Wonder|wonder]], as<br>You, gracious couple, do; and then I lost—<br>All mine own folly—the society,<br>Amity too, of your brave father, whom,<br>Though bearing misery, I [[Desire|desire]] my life<br>Once more to look on him. |
| [[Florizel|FLORIZEL]] | By his command<br>Have I here touched Sicilia, and from him<br>Give you all greetings that a [[King|king]] at friend<br>Can send his brother; and but infirmity,<br>Which waits upon worn times, hath something seized<br>His wished ability, he had himself<br>The lands and waters ’twixt your throne and his<br>Measured to look upon you, whom he loves—<br>He bade me say so—more than all the sceptres,<br>And those that bear them, living. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | O, my brother!<br>Good gentleman, the wrongs I have done thee stir<br>Afresh within me, and these thy offices,<br>So rarely kind, are as interpreters<br>Of my behindhand slackness. Welcome hither,<br>As is the [[Spring|spring]] to th‘earth! And hath he too<br>Exposed this paragon to th’ fearful usage—<br>At least ungentle—of the dreadful Neptune<br>To greet a man not worth her pains, much less<br>Th’adventure of her person? |
| [[Florizel|FLORIZEL]] | Good my lord,<br>She came from Libya. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | Where the warlike Smalus,<br>That noble honoured lord, is feared and loved? |
| [[Florizel|FLORIZEL]] | Most royal sir, from thence; from him whose daughter<br>His [[Tears|tears]] proclaimed his, parting with her. Thence,<br>A prosperous south [[Wind|wind]] friendly, we have crossed,<br>To execute the charge my father gave me<br>For visiting your highness. My best train<br>I have from your Sicilian shores dismissed;<br>Who for Bohemia bend, to signify<br>Not only my [[success]] in Libya, sir,<br>But my arrival, and my wife’s, in safety<br>Here where we are. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | The blessed gods<br>Purge all infection from our air whilst you<br>Do climate here! You have a holy father,<br>A graceful gentleman, against whose person,<br>So sacred as it is, I have done sin,<br>For which the heavens, taking angry note,<br>Have left me issueless; and your father’s blessed,<br>As he from heaven merits it, with you,<br>Worthy his goodness. What might I have been,<br>Might I a son and daughter now have looked on,<br>Such goodly things as you? |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter a Lord |
| LORD | Most noble sir,<br>That which I shall report will bear no credit<br>Were not the proof so nigh. Please you, great sir,<br>Bohemia greets you from himself by me;<br>Desires you to attach his son, who has,<br>His dignity and duty both cast off,<br>Fled from his father, from his hopes, and with<br>A shepherd’s daughter. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | Where’s Bohemia? Speak. |
| LORD | Here in your [[City|city]]. I now came from him.<br>I speak amazedly, and it becomes<br>My marvel and my message. To your [[Court|court]]<br>Whiles he was hast’ning—in the chase, it seems,<br>Of this fair couple—meets he on the way<br>The father of this seeming lady and<br>Her brother, having both their country quitted<br>With this young prince. |
| [[Florizel|FLORIZEL]] | [[Camillo]] has betrayed me,<br>Whose [[Honour|honour]] and whose honesty till now<br>Endured all weathers. |
| LORD | Lay’t so to his charge.<br>He’s with the [[King]] your father. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | Who, [[Camillo]]? |
| LORD | [[Camillo]], sir. I spake with him, who now<br>Has these poor men in question. Never saw I<br>Wretches so quake. They kneel, they kiss the earth,<br>Forswear themselves as often as they speak.<br>Bohemia stops his ears, and threatens them<br>With divers deaths in [[Death|death]]. |
| [[Perdita|PERDITA]] | O, my poor father!<br>The heaven sets spies upon us, will not have<br>Our contract celebrated. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | You are married? |
| [[Florizel|FLORIZEL]] | We are not, sir, nor are we like to be.<br>The [[Stars|stars]], I see, will kiss the valleys first.<br>The odds for high and low’s alike. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | My lord,<br>Is this the daughter of a [[King|king]]? |
| [[Florizel|FLORIZEL]] | She is,<br>When once she is my wife. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | That ‘once’, I see, by your good father’s [[Speed|speed]]<br>Will come on very slowly. I am sorry,<br>Most sorry, you have broken from his liking<br>Where you were tied in duty; and as sorry<br>Your choice is not so rich in worth as [[Beauty|beauty]],<br>That you might [[Well|well]] enjoy her. |
| [[Florizel|FLORIZEL]] | (to [[Perdita]])<br>Dear, look up.<br>Though fortune, visible an enemy,<br>Should chase us with my father, power no jot<br>Hath she to change our loves.—Beseech you, sir,<br>Remember since you owed no more to [[Time|time]]<br>Than I do now. With thought of such affections,<br>Step forth mine advocate. At your request<br>My father will grant precious things as trifles. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | Would he do so, I’d beg your precious mistress,<br>Which he counts but a trifle. |
| [[Paulina|PAULINA]] | Sir, my liege,<br>Your eye hath too much youth in’t. Not a month<br>Fore your [[Queen|queen]] died she was more worth such gazes<br>Than what you look on now. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | I thought of her<br>Even in these looks I made.<br>(To [[Florizel]]) But your petition<br>Is yet unanswered. I will to your father.<br>Your [[Honour|honour]] not o’erthrown by your desires,<br>I am friend to them and you. Upon which errand<br>I now go toward him. Therefore follow me,<br>And mark what way I make. Come, good my lord. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt |
