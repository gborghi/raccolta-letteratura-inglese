| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Leontes]], Lords, and Officers |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | This sessions, to our great grief we pronounce,<br>Even pushes ’gainst our heart: the party tried<br>The daughter of a [[King|king]], our wife, and one<br>Of us too much beloved. Let us be cleared<br>Of being tyrannous since we so openly<br>Proceed in justice, which shall have due course<br>Even to the [[Guilt|guilt]] or the purgation.<br>Produce the prisoner. |
| OFFICER | It is his highness’ pleasure<br>That the [[Queen]] appear in person here in [[Court|court]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Hermione]] guarded, with [[Paulina]] and Ladies |
| OFFICER | [[Silence]]. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | Read the indictment. |
| OFFICER | (reads) [[Hermione]], [[Queen|queen]] to the worthy [[Leontes]], [[King]] of Sicilia, thou art here accused and arraigned of high treason in committing adultery with [[Polixenes]], [[King]] of Bohemia, and conspiring with [[Camillo]] to take away the life of our sovereign lord the [[King]], thy royal husband; the pretence whereof being by circumstances partly laid open, thou, [[Hermione]], contrary to the [[Faith|faith]] and allegiance of a true subject, didst counsel and aid them for their better safety to fly away by [[Night|night]]. |
| [[Hermione|HERMIONE]] | Since what I am to say must be but that<br>Which contradicts my accusation, and<br>The testimony on my part no other<br>But what comes from myself, it shall scarce boot me<br>To say ‘Not guilty’. Mine integrity<br>Being counted falsehood shall, as I express it,<br>Be so received. But thus: if powers divine<br>Behold our human actions—as they do—<br>I [[Doubt|doubt]] not then but [[innocence]] shall make<br>False accusation blush, and tyranny<br>Tremble at patience. You, my lord, best know—<br>Who least will seem to do so—my past life<br>Hath been as continent, as chaste, as true<br>As I am now unhappy; which is more<br>Than [[history]] can pattern, though devised<br>And played to take spectators. For behold me,<br>A fellow of the royal bed, which owe<br>A moiety of the throne; a great [[King|king]]’s daughter,<br>The mother to a hopeful prince, here standing<br>To prate and talk for life and [[Honour|honour]], fore<br>Who please to come and hear. For life, I prize it<br>As I weigh grief, which I would spare. For [[Honour|honour]],<br>‘Tis a derivative from me to mine,<br>And only that I stand for. I appeal<br>To your own [[Conscience|conscience]], sir, before [[Polixenes]]<br>Came to your [[Court|court]] how I was in your grace,<br>How merited to be so; since he came,<br>With what encounter so uncurrent I<br>Have strained t’appear thus. If one jot beyond<br>The bound of [[Honour|honour]], or in act or will<br>That way inclining, hardened be the hearts<br>Of all that hear me, and my near‘st of kin<br>Cry ‘Fie’ upon my [[Grave|grave]]. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | I ne’er heard yet<br>That any of these bolder vices wanted<br>Less impudence to gainsay what they did<br>Than to perform it first. |
| [[Hermione|HERMIONE]] | That’s true enough,<br>Though ’tis a saying, sir, not due to me. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | You will not own it. |
| [[Hermione|HERMIONE]] | More than mistress of<br>Which comes to me in name of fault, I must not<br>At all acknowledge. For [[Polixenes]],<br>With whom I am accused, I do confess<br>I loved him as in [[Honour|honour]] he required;<br>With such a kind of [[Love|love]] as might become<br>A lady like me; with a [[Love|love]], even such,<br>So, and no other, as yourself commanded;<br>Which not to have done I think had been in me<br>Both disobedience and ingratitude<br>To you and toward your friend, whose [[Love|love]] had spoke<br>Even since it could speak, from an infant, freely<br>That it was yours. Now for conspiracy,<br>I know not how it tastes, though it be dished<br>For me to try how. All I know of it<br>Is that [[Camillo]] was an honest man;<br>And why he left your [[Court|court]], the gods themselves,<br>Wotting no more than I, are ignorant. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | You knew of his departure, as you know<br>What you have underta’en to do in’s absence. |
| [[Hermione|HERMIONE]] | Sir,<br>You speak a language that I understand not.<br>My life stands in the level of your [[Dreams|dreams]],<br>Which I’ll lay down. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | Your actions are my ‘[[Dreams|dreams]]’.<br>You had a bastard by [[Polixenes]],<br>And I but dreamed it. As you were past all shame—<br>Those of your fact are so—so past all [[Truth|truth]];<br>Which to deny concerns more than avails; for as<br>Thy brat hath been cast out, like to itself,<br>No father owning it—which is indeed<br>More criminal in thee than it—so thou<br>Shalt feel our justice, in whose easiest passage<br>Look for no less than [[Death|death]]. |
| [[Hermione|HERMIONE]] | Sir, spare your threats.<br>The bug which you would fright me with, I seek.<br>To me can life be no commodity.<br>The [[Crown|crown]] and comfort of my life, your favour,<br>I do give lost, for I do feel it gone<br>But know not how it went. My second joy,<br>And first fruits of my [[Body|body]], from his presence<br>I am barred, like one infectious. My third comfort,<br>Starred most unluckily, is from my breast,<br>The [[Innocent|innocent]] milk in it most [[Innocent|innocent]] mouth,<br>Haled out to murder; myself on every post<br>Proclaimed a strumpet, with immodest hatred<br>The childbed privilege denied, which ‘longs<br>To women of all fashion; lastly, hurried<br>Here, to this place, i’th’ open air, before<br>I have got strength of limit. Now, my liege,<br>Tell me what blessings I have here alive,<br>That I should [[Fear|fear]] to die. Therefore proceed.<br>But yet hear this—mistake me not—no life,<br>I prize it not a straw; but for mine [[Honour|honour]],<br>Which I would free: if I shall be condemned<br>Upon surmises, all proofs sleeping else<br>But what your jealousies awake, I tell you<br>’Tis rigour, and not law. Your honours all,<br>I do refer me to the oracle.<br>[[Apollo]] be my judge.<br>A LORD<br>This your request<br>Is altogether just. Therefore bring forth,<br>And in [[Apollo]]’s name, his oracle.<br>[Exeunt certain Officers] |
| [[Hermione|HERMIONE]] | The Emperor of [[Russia]] was my father.<br>O that he were alive, and here beholding<br>His daughter’s trial; that he did but see<br>The flatness of my misery—yet with eyes<br>Of pity, not [[Revenge|revenge]].<br>[Enter Officers with Cleomenes and Dion] |
| OFFICER | You here shall swear upon this [[Sword|sword]] of justice<br>That you, Cleomenes and Dion, have<br>Been both at Delphos, and from thence have brought<br>This sealed-up oracle, by the hand delivered<br>Of great [[Apollo]]’s priest; and that since then<br>You have not dared to break the holy seal,<br>Nor read the [[Secrets|secrets]] in’t. |
| CLEOMENES | and DION All this we swear. [[Leontes|LEONTES]] Break up the seals, and read. |
| OFFICER | (reads) [[Hermione]] is chaste, [[Polixenes]] blameless, [[Camillo]] a true subject, [[Leontes]] a jealous [[Tyrant|tyrant]], his [[Innocent|innocent]] babe truly begotten, and the [[King]] shall live without an heir if that which is lost be not found. |
| LORDS | Now blessèd be the great [[Apollo]]! |
| [[Hermione|HERMIONE]] | Praised! |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | Hast thou read [[Truth|truth]]? |
| OFFICER | Ay, my lord, even so as it is here set down. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | There is no [[Truth|truth]] at all i’th’ oracle.<br>The sessions shall proceed. This is mere falsehood. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter a Servant |
| SERVANT | My lord the [[King]]! The [[King]]! |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | What is the business? |
| SERVANT | O sir, I shall be hated to report it.<br>The prince your son, with mere conceit and [[Fear|fear]]<br>Of the [[Queen]]’s [[Speed|speed]], is gone. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | How, ‘gone’? |
| SERVANT | Is dead. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | [[Apollo]]’s angry, and the heavens themselves<br>Do strike at my injustice.<br>[[Hermione]] falls to the ground<br>How now there? |
| [[Paulina|PAULINA]] | This news is mortal to the [[Queen]]. Look down<br>And see what [[Death|death]] is doing. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | Take her hence.<br>Her heart is but o’ercharged. She will recover.<br>I have too much believed mine own suspicion.<br>Beseech you, tenderly apply to her<br>Some remedies for life. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt [[Paulina]] and Ladies, carrying [[Hermione]] |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | [[Apollo]], pardon<br>My great profaneness ’gainst thine oracle.<br>I’ll reconcile me to [[Polixenes]],<br>New woo my [[Queen|queen]], recall the good [[Camillo]],<br>Whom I proclaim a man of [[Truth|truth]], of [[Mercy|mercy]];<br>For being transported by my jealousies<br>To bloody thoughts and to [[Revenge|revenge]], I chose<br>[[Camillo]] for the minister to [[Poison|poison]]<br>My friend [[Polixenes]], which had been done,<br>But that the good mind of [[Camillo]] tardied<br>My swift command. Though I with [[Death|death]] and with<br>Reward did threaten and encourage him,<br>Not doing it, and being done, he, most humane<br>And filled with [[Honour|honour]], to my kingly guest<br>Unclasped my practice, quit his fortunes here—<br>Which you knew great—and to the certain hazard<br>Of all incertainties himself commended,<br>No richer than his [[Honour|honour]]. How he glisters<br>Through my rust! And how his piety<br>Does my deeds make the blacker! |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Paulina]] |
| [[Paulina|PAULINA]] | Woe the while!<br>O cut my lace, lest my heart, cracking it,<br>Break too.<br>A LORD What fit is this, good lady? |
| [[Paulina|PAULINA]] | (to [[Leontes]])<br>What studied torments, [[Tyrant|tyrant]], hast for me?<br>What wheels, racks, fires? What flaying, boiling<br>In leads or oils? What old or newer torture<br>Must I receive, whose every word deserves<br>To taste of thy most worst? Thy tyranny,<br>Together working with thy jealousies-<br>Fancies too weak for boys, too green and idle<br>For girls of nine—O think what they have done,<br>And then run mad indeed, stark mad, for all<br>Thy bygone fooleries were but spices of it.<br>That thou betrayed‘st [[Polixenes]], ’twas nothing.<br>That did but show thee, of a [[Fool|fool]], inconstant,<br>And damnable ingrateful. Nor was’t much<br>Thou wouldst have poisoned good [[Camillo]]’s [[Honour|honour]]<br>To have him kill a [[King|king]]—poor trespasses,<br>More monstrous standing by, whereof I reckon<br>The casting forth to crows thy baby daughter<br>To be or none or little, though a devil<br>Would have shed [[Water|water]] out of [[Fire|fire]] ere done’t.<br>Nor is’t directly laid to thee the [[Death|death]]<br>Of the young prince, whose honourable thoughts—<br>Thoughts high for one so tender—cleft the heart<br>That could conceive a gross and foolish sire<br>Blemished his gracious dam. This is not, no,<br>Laid to thy answer. But the last—O lords,<br>When I have said, cry woe! The [[Queen]], the [[Queen]],<br>The sweet‘st, dear’st creature’s dead, and vengeance<br>for’t<br>Not dropped down yet.<br>A LORD<br>The higher powers forbid! |
| [[Paulina|PAULINA]] | I say she’s dead. I’ll swear’t. If word nor oath<br>Prevail not, go and see. If you can bring<br>Tincture or lustre in her lip, her eye,<br>Heat outwardly or breath within, I’ll serve you<br>As I would do the gods. But O thou [[Tyrant|tyrant]],<br>Do not repent these things, for they are heavier<br>Than all thy woes can stir. Therefore betake thee<br>To nothing but [[Despair|despair]]. A thousand knees,<br>Ten thousand years together, naked, fasting,<br>Upon a barren mountain, and still [[Winter|winter]]<br>In [[Storm|storm]] perpetual, could not move the gods<br>To look that way thou wert. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | Go on, go on.<br>Thou canst not speak too much. I have deserved<br>All tongues to talk their bitt’rest.<br>A LORD (to [[Paulina]])<br>Say no more.<br>Howe‘er the business goes, you have made fault<br>I’th’ boldness of your speech. |
| [[Paulina|PAULINA]] | I am sorry for’t.<br>All faults I make, when I shall come to know them<br>I do repent. Alas, I have showed too much<br>The rashness of a woman. He is touched<br>To th’ noble heart. What’s gone and what’s past help<br>Should be past grief.<br>(To [[Leontes]]) Do not receive affliction<br>At my petition. I beseech you, rather<br>Let me be punished, that have minded you<br>Of what you should forget. Now, good my liege,<br>Sir, royal sir, forgive a foolish woman.<br>The [[Love|love]] I bore your [[Queen|queen]]—lo, [[Fool|fool]] again!<br>I’ll speak of her no more, nor of your children.<br>I’ll not remember you of my own lord,<br>Who is lost too. Take your patience to you,<br>And I’ll say nothing. |
| [[Leontes|LEONTES]] | Thou didst speak but [[Well|well]]<br>When most the [[Truth|truth]], which I receive much better<br>Than to be pitied of thee. Prithee bring me<br>To the dead bodies of my [[Queen|queen]] and son.<br>One [[Grave|grave]] shall be for both. Upon them shall<br>The causes of their [[Death|death]] appear, unto<br>Our shame perpetual. Once a day I’ll visit<br>The chapel where they lie, and [[Tears|tears]] shed there<br>Shall be my recreation. So long as [[Nature|nature]]<br>Will bear up with this exercise, so long<br>I daily vow to use it. Come, and lead me<br>To these sorrows. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt |
