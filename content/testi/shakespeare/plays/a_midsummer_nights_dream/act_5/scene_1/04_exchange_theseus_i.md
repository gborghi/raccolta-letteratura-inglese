SNOUT (as Wall) In this same interlude it doth befall That I, one Snout by name, present a wall; And such a wall as I would have you think That had in it a crannied hole or chink, Through which the [[lovers]] Pyramus and Thisbe Did whisper often, very secretly. This loam, this roughcast, and this stone doth show That I am that same wall; the [[Truth|truth]] is so. And this the cranny is, right and sinister, Through which the fearful [[lovers]] are to whisper. |
| [[Theseus|THESEUS]] | Would you [[Desire|desire]] lime and hair to speak better? |
| [[Demetrius|DEMETRIUS]] | It is the wittiest partition that ever I heard discourse, my lord. Enter [[Bottom]] as Pyramus |
| [[Theseus|THESEUS]] | Pyramus draws near the wall. [[Silence]]. |
| [[Bottom|BOTTOM]] | (as Pyramus) O grim-looked [[Night|night]], O [[Night|night]] with hue so black, O [[Night|night]] which ever art when day is not; O [[Night|night]], O [[Night|night]], alack, alack, alack, I [[Fear|fear]] my Thisbe’s promise is forgot. And thou, O wall, O sweet O lovely wall, That stand’st between her father’s ground and mine, Thou wall, O wall, O sweet and lovely wall, Show me thy chink, to blink through with mine eyne. Wall shows his chink Thanks, courteous wall. Jove shield thee [[Well|well]] for this. But what see I? No Thisbe do I see. O wicked wall, through whom I see no bliss, Cursed be thy stones for thus deceiving me. |
| [[Theseus|THESEUS]] | The wall methinks, being sensible, should curse again. |
| [[Bottom|BOTTOM]] | (to [[Theseus]]) No, in [[Truth|truth]], sir, he should not. ’Deceiving me’ is Thisbe’s cue. She is to enter now, and I am to spy her through the wall. You shall see, it will fall pat as I told you. Enter Flute as Thisbe Yonder she comes. |

| [[Bottom|BOTTOM]] | (as Pyramus) Think what thou wilt, I am thy [[Lover|lover]]’s grace, And like Lemander am I trusty still. |
| FLUTE | (as Thisbe) And I like [[Helen]], till the fates me kill. |
| [[Bottom|BOTTOM]] | (as Pyramus) Not Shaphalus to Procrus was so true. |
| FLUTE | (as Thisbe) As Shaphalus to Procrus, I to you. |
| [[Bottom|BOTTOM]] | (as Pyramus) O kiss me through the hole of this vile wall. |
| FLUTE | (as Thisbe) I kiss the wall’s hole, not your lips at all. |
| [[Bottom|BOTTOM]] | (as Pyramus) Wilt thou at Ninny’s tomb meet me straightway? |
