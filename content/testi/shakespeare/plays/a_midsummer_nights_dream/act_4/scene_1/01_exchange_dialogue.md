| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Titania]], [[Queen]] of Fairies, and [[Bottom]] the clown with the ass-head, and fairies: Peaseblossom, Cobweb, Mote, and Mustardseed |
| [[Titania|TITANIA]] | (to [[Bottom]]) Come, sit thee down upon this flow’ry bed, While I thy amiable cheeks do coy, And stick musk-roses in thy sleek smooth head, And kiss thy fair large ears, my gentle joy. |
| [[Bottom|BOTTOM]] | Where’s Peaseblossom? |
| PEASEBLOSSOM | Ready. |
| [[Bottom|BOTTOM]] | Scratch my head, Peaseblossom. Where’s Monsieur Cobweb? |
| COBWEB | Ready. |
| [[Bottom|BOTTOM]] | Monsieur Cobweb, good monsieur, get you your weapons in your hand and kill me a red-hipped humble-bee on the top of a thistle; and, good monsieur, bring me the honeybag. Do not fret yourself too much in the action, monsieur; and, good monsieur, have a care the honeybag break not. I would be loath to have you overflowen with a honeybag, signor. Exit Cobweb Where’s Monsieur Mustardseed? |
| MUSTARDSEED | Ready. |
| [[Bottom|BOTTOM]] | Give me your neaf, Monsieur Mustardseed. Pray you, leave your courtesy, good monsieur. |
| MUSTARDSEED | What’s your will? |
| [[Bottom|BOTTOM]] | Nothing, good monsieur, but to help Cavaliery Peaseblossom to scratch. I must to the barber’s, monsieur, for methinks I am marvellous hairy about the face; and I am such a tender ass, if my hair do but tickle me I must scratch. |
| [[Titania|TITANIA]] | What, wilt thou hear some music, my sweet [[Love|love]]? |
| [[Bottom|BOTTOM]] | I have a reasonable good ear in music. Let’s have the tongs and the bones. Rural music |
| [[Titania|TITANIA]] | Or say, sweet [[Love|love]], what thou desir’st to eat. |
| [[Bottom|BOTTOM]] | Truly, a peck of provender. I could munch your good dry oats. Methinks I have a great [[Desire|desire]] to a bottle of hay. Good hay, sweet hay, hath no fellow. |
| [[Titania|TITANIA]] | I have a venturous fairy that shall seek The squirrel’s hoard, and fetch thee off new nuts. [[Bottom]] I had rather have a handful or two of dried peas. But I pray you, let none of your people stir me. I have an exposition of [[Sleep|sleep]] come upon me. |
| [[Titania|TITANIA]] | [[Sleep]] thou, and I will [[Wind|wind]] thee in my arms. Fairies, be gone, and be all ways away. Exeunt Fairies So doth the woodbine the sweet honeysuckle Gently entwist; the female ivy so Enrings the barky fingers of the elm. O how I [[Love|love]] thee, how I dote on thee! They [[Sleep|sleep]]. Enter Robin Goodfellow and [[Oberon]], meeting |
