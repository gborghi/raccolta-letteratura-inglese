| [[Oberon|OBERON]] | Welcome, good Robin. Seest thou this sweet sight? Her dotage now I do begin to pity, For meeting her of late behind the wood, Seeking sweet favours for this hateful [[Fool|fool]], I did upbraid her and fall out with her, For she his hairy temples then had rounded With coronet of fresh and fragrant [[Flowers|flowers]], And that same dew which sometime on the buds Was wont to swell like round and [[Orient|orient]] pearls Stood now within the pretty flow’rets’ eyes, Like [[Tears|tears]] that did their own disgrace bewail. When I had at my pleasure taunted her, And she in mild terms begged my patience, I then did ask of her her changeling [[Child|child]], Which straight she gave me, and her fairy sent To bear him to my bower in fairyland. And now I have the boy, I will undo This hateful imperfection of her eyes. And, gentle [[Puck|puck]], take this transformed scalp From off the head of this Athenian swain, That he, awaking when the other do, May all to Athens back again repair, And think no more of this [[Night|night]]’s accidents But as the fierce vexation of [[A Dream|a dream]]. But first I will release the Fairy [[Queen]]. He drops the juice on [[Titania]]’s eyelids Be as thou wast wont to be, See as thou wast wont to see. Dian’s bud o’er Cupid’s flower Hath such force and blessed power. Now, my [[Titania]], wake you, my sweet [[Queen|queen]]. |
