| [[Oberon|OBERON]] | There lies your [[Love|love]]. |

| [[Oberon|OBERON]] | Sound music. The music changes Come, my [[Queen|queen]], take hands with me, And rock the ground whereon these sleepers be. [[Oberon]] and [[Titania]] [[Dance|dance]] |
| [[Oberon|OBERON]] | Now thou and I are new in amity, And will tomorrow midnight solemnly [[Dance]] in Duke [[Theseus]]’ [[house]], triumphantly, And bless it to all fair prosperity. There shall the pairs of faithful [[lovers]] be Wedded with [[Theseus]], all in jollity. |
| ROBIN | Fairy [[King]], attend and mark. I do hear the morning lark. |
| [[Oberon|OBERON]] | Then, my [[Queen|queen]], in [[Silence|silence]] sad Trip we after nightës shade. We the globe can compass soon, Swifter than the wand’ring [[Moon|moon]]. |
| [[Titania|TITANIA]] | Come, my lord, and in our flight Tell me how it came this [[Night|night]] That I sleeping here was found With these mortals on the ground. Exeunt [[Oberon]], [[Titania]], and Robin. The sleepers lie still [[Wind]] horns within. Enter [[Theseus]] with Egeus, [[Hippolyta]], and all his train |
| [[Theseus|THESEUS]] | Go, one of you, find out the forester, For now our observation is performed; And since we have the vanguard of the day, My [[Love|love]] shall hear the music of my hounds. Uncouple in the western valley; let them go. Dispatch, I say, and find the forester. Exit one |
| [[Theseus|THESEUS]] | We will, fair [[Queen]], up to the mountain’s top, And mark the musical confusion Of hounds and echo in conjunction. |
| [[Hippolyta|HIPPOLYTA]] | I was with Hercules and Cadmus once When in a wood of Crete they bayed the bear With hounds of Sparta. Never did I hear Such gallant chiding; for besides the groves, The skies, the fountains, every region near Seemed all one mutual cry. I never heard So musical a discord, such sweet thunder. |
| [[Theseus|THESEUS]] | My hounds are bred out of the Spartan kind, So flewed, so sanded; and their heads are hung With ears that sweep away the morning dew, Crook-kneed, and dewlapped like Thessalian bulls, Slow in pursuit, but matched in mouth like [[Bells|bells]], Each under each. A cry more tuneable Was never holla’d to nor cheered with horn In Crete, in Sparta, nor in Thessaly. Judge when you hear. But soft: what nymphs are these? |
| EGEUS | My lord, this is my daughter here asleep, And this [[Lysander]]; this [[Demetrius]] is; This [[Helena]], old Nedar’s [[Helena]]. I [[Wonder|wonder]] of their being here together. |
| [[Theseus|THESEUS]] | No [[Doubt|doubt]] they [[Rose|rose]] up early to observe The rite of May, and, hearing our intent, Came here in grace of our solemnity. But speak, Egeus : is not this the day That [[Hermia]] should give answer of her choice? |
| EGEUS | It is, my lord. |
