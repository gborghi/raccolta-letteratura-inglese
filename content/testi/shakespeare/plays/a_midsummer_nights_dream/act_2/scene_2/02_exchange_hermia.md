| [[Hermia|HERMIA]] | With half that wish the wisher’s eyes be pressed. They [[Sleep|sleep]] apart. Enter Robin Goodfellow the [[Puck|puck]] |
| ROBIN | Through the forest have I gone, But Athenian found I none On whose eyes I might approve This flower’s force in stirring [[Love|love]]. [[Night]] and [[Silence|silence]]. Who is here? Weeds of Athens he doth wear. This is he my master said Despised the Athenian maid— And here the maiden, sleeping sound On the dank and dirty ground. Pretty [[Soul|soul]], she durst not lie Near this lack-[[Love|love]], this kill-courtesy. Churl, upon thy eyes I throw All the power this charm doth owe. He drops the juice on [[Lysander]]’s eyelids |
| ROBIN | When thou wak’st, let [[Love|love]] forbid [[Sleep]] his seat on thy eyelid. So, awake when I am gone. For I must now to [[Oberon]]. Exit Enter [[Demetrius]] and [[Helena]], running |
| [[Helena|HELENA]] | Stay, though thou kill me, sweet [[Demetrius]]. |
| [[Demetrius|DEMETRIUS]] | I charge thee hence, and do not haunt me thus. |
| [[Helena|HELENA]] | O, wilt thou darkling leave me? Do not so. |
| [[Demetrius|DEMETRIUS]] | Stay, on thy peril; I [[Alone|alone]] will go. Exit |
| [[Helena|HELENA]] | O, I am out of breath in this fond chase. The more my [[prayer]], the lesser is my grace. Happy is [[Hermia]], wheresoe‘er she lies; For she hath blessed and attractive eyes. How came her eyes so bright? Not with salt [[Tears|tears]]— If so, my eyes are oft’ner washed than hers. No, no; I am as ugly as a bear, 100 For beasts that meet me run away for [[Fear|fear]]. Therefore no marvel though [[Demetrius]] Do, as a [[Monster|monster]], fly my presence thus. What wicked and dissembling glass of mine Made me compare with [[Hermia]]’s sphery eyne! But who is here? [[Lysander]], on the ground? Dead, or asleep? I see no [[Blood|blood]], no wound. [[Lysander]], if you live, good sir, awake. |
| [[Lysander|LYSANDER]] | (awaking) And run through [[Fire|fire]] I will for thy sweet sake. Transparent [[Helena]], [[Nature|nature]] shows art That through thy bosom makes me see thy heart. Where is [[Demetrius]]? O, how fit a word Is that vile name to perish on my [[Sword|sword]]! |
| [[Helena|HELENA]] | Do not say so, [[Lysander]]; say not so. What though he [[Love|love]] your [[Hermia]]? Lord, what though? Yet [[Hermia]] still loves you; then be content. |
| [[Lysander|LYSANDER]] | Content with [[Hermia]]? No, I do repent The tedious minutes I with her have spent. Not [[Hermia]] but [[Helena]] I [[Love|love]]. Who will not change a raven for a dove? The will of man is by his reason swayed, And reason says you are the worthier maid. Things growing are not ripe until their season, So I, being young, till now ripe not to reason. And, touching now the point of human skill, Reason becomes the marshal to my will, And leads me to your eyes, where I o’erlook [[Love]]’s stories written in [[Love|love]]’s richest book. |
| [[Helena|HELENA]] | Wherefore was I to this keen mockery born? When at your hands did I deserve this scorn? Is’t not enough, is’t not enough, young man, That I did never—no, nor never can— Deserve a sweet look from [[Demetrius]]’ eye, But you must flout my insufficiency? Good troth, you do me wrong; good sooth, you do, In such disdainful manner me to woo. But fare you [[Well|well]]. Perforce I must confess I thought you lord of more true gentleness. O, that a lady of one man refused Should of another therefore be abused! Exit |
