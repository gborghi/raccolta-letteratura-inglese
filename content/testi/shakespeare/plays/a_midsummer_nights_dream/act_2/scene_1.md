| *(didascalia)* | Enter a Fairy at one door and Robin Goodfellow, a [[Puck|puck]], at another |
| ROBIN | How now, spirit, whither wander you? |
| FAIRY | Over [[Hill|hill]], over dale,<br>Thorough bush, thorough brier,<br>Over park, over pale,<br>Thorough flood, thorough [[Fire|fire]]:<br>I do wander everywhere<br>Swifter than the moonës sphere,<br>And I serve the Fairy [[Queen]]<br>To dew her orbs upon the green.<br>The cowslips tall her pensioners be.<br>In their gold coats spots you see;<br>Those be rubies, fairy favours;<br>In those freckles live their savours.<br>I must go seek some dewdrops here,<br>And hang a pearl in every cowslip’s ear.<br>Farewell, thou lob of spirits; I’ll be gone.<br>Our [[Queen]] and all her elves come here anon. |
| ROBIN | The [[King]] doth keep his revels here tonight.<br>Take heed the [[Queen]] come not within his sight,<br>For [[Oberon]] is passing fell and wroth 20<br>Because that she, as her attendant, hath<br>A lovely boy stol’n from an Indian [[King|king]].<br>She never had so sweet a changeling;<br>And jealous [[Oberon]] would have the [[Child|child]]<br>Knight of his train, to trace the forests wild.<br>But she perforce withholds the loved boy,<br>Crowns him with [[Flowers|flowers]], and makes him all her joy.<br>And now they never meet in grove, or green,<br>By fountain clear, or spangled starlight sheen,<br>But they do square, that all their elves for [[Fear|fear]]<br>Creep into acorn cups, and hide them there. |
| FAIRY | Either I mistake your shape and making quite<br>Or else you are that shrewd and knavish sprite<br>Called Robin Goodfellow. Are not you he<br>That frights the maidens of the villag‘ry,<br>Skim milk, and sometimes labour in the quern,<br>And bootless make the breathless housewife churn,<br>And sometime make the drink to bear no barm—<br>Mislead [[Night|night]] wanderers, laughing at their harm?<br>Those that ‘hobgoblin’ call you, and ‘sweet [[Puck|puck]]’,<br>You do their work, and they shall have good luck.<br>Are not you he? |
| ROBIN | Thou speak’st aright;<br>I am that merry wanderer of the [[Night|night]].<br>I jest to [[Oberon]], and make him smile<br>When I a fat and bean-fed [[Horse|horse]] beguile,<br>Neighing in likeness of a filly foal;<br>And sometime lurk I in a gossip’s bowl<br>In very likeness of a roasted crab,<br>And when she drinks, against her lips I bob,<br>And on her withered dewlap pour the ale.<br>The wisest aunt telling the saddest tale<br>Sometime for three-foot stool mistaketh me;<br>Then slip I from her bum. Down topples she,<br>And ’tailor’ cries, and falls into a cough,<br>And then the whole choir hold their hips, and laugh,<br>And waxen in their mirth, and sneeze, and swear<br>A merrier hour was never wasted there.— |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Oberon]] the [[King]] of Fairies at one door, with his train, and [[Titania]] the [[Queen]] at another, with hers |
| ROBIN | But make room, fairy: here comes [[Oberon]]. |
| FAIRY | And here my mistress. Would that he were gone. |
| [[Oberon|OBERON]] | I’ll met by moonlight, proud [[Titania]]. |
| [[Titania|TITANIA]] | What, jealous [[Oberon]]?—Fairies, skip hence.<br>I have forsworn his bed and company. |
| [[Oberon|OBERON]] | Tarry, rash wanton. Am not I thy lord? |
| [[Titania|TITANIA]] | Then I must be thy lady; but I know<br>When thou hast stol’n away from fairyland<br>And in the shape of Corin sat all day,<br>Playing on pipes of corn, and versing [[Love|love]]<br>To amorous Phillida. Why art thou here<br>Come from the farthest step of India,<br>But that, forsooth, the bouncing Amazon,<br>Your buskined mistress and your warrior [[Love|love]],<br>To [[Theseus]] must be wedded, and you come<br>To give their bed joy and prosperity? |
| [[Oberon|OBERON]] | How canst thou thus for shame, [[Titania]],<br>Glance at my credit with [[Hippolyta]],<br>Knowing I know thy [[Love|love]] to [[Theseus]]?<br>Didst not thou lead him through the glimmering [[Night|night]]<br>From Perigouna whom he ravished,<br>And make him with fair Aegles break his [[Faith|faith]],<br>With Ariadne and Antiopa? |
| [[Titania|TITANIA]] | These are the forgeries of [[Jealousy|jealousy]],<br>And never since the middle [[Summer|summer]]’s [[Spring|spring]]<br>Met we on [[Hill|hill]], in dale, forest, or mead,<br>By paved fountain or by rushy brook,<br>Or in the beached margin of the sea<br>To [[Dance|dance]] our ringlets to the whistling [[Wind|wind]],<br>But with thy brawls thou hast disturbed our sport.<br>Therefore the winds, piping to us in vain,<br>As in [[Revenge|revenge]] have sucked up from the sea<br>Contagious fogs which, falling in the land,<br>Hath every pelting [[River|river]] made so proud<br>That they have overborne their continents.<br>The ox hath therefore stretched his yoke in vain,<br>The ploughman lost his sweat, and the green corn<br>Hath rotted ere his youth attained a beard.<br>The fold stands empty in the drowned field,<br>And crows are fatted with the murrain flock.<br>The nine men’s morris is filled up with mud,<br>And the quaint mazes in the wanton green<br>For lack of tread are undistinguishable.<br>The human mortals want their [[Winter|winter]] cheer.<br>No [[Night|night]] is now with [[Hymn|hymn]] or [[carol]] blessed.<br>Therefore the [[Moon|moon]], the governess of floods,<br>Pale in her anger washes all the air,<br>That rheumatic diseases do abound;<br>And thorough this distemperature we see<br>The [[Seasons|seasons]] alter: hoary-headed frosts<br>Fall in the fresh lap of the crimson [[Rose|rose]],<br>And on old Hiems’ thin and icy [[Crown|crown]]<br>An odorous chaplet of sweet [[Summer|summer]] buds<br>Is, as in mock’ry, set. The [[Spring|spring]], the [[Summer|summer]],<br>The childing [[Autumn|autumn]], angry [[Winter|winter]] change<br>Their wonted liveries, and the mazèd world<br>By their increase now knows not which is which;<br>And this same progeny of evils comes<br>From our [[debate]], from our dissension.<br>We are their parents and original. |
| [[Oberon|OBERON]] | Do you amend it, then. It lies in you.<br>Why should [[Titania]] [[Cross|cross]] her [[Oberon]]?<br>I do but beg a little changeling boy<br>To be my henchman. |
| [[Titania|TITANIA]] | Set your heart at rest.<br>The fairyland buys not the [[Child|child]] of me.<br>His mother was a vot‘ress of my order,<br>And in the spiced Indian air by [[Night|night]]<br>Full often hath she gossiped by my side,<br>And sat with me on Neptune’s yellow sands,<br>Marking th’embarkèd traders on the flood,<br>When we have laughed to see the sails conceive<br>And grow big-bellied with the wanton [[Wind|wind]],<br>Which she with pretty and with swimming gait<br>Following, her womb then rich with my young squire,<br>Would imitate, and sail upon the land<br>To fetch me trifles, and return again<br>As from a voyage, rich with merchandise.<br>But she, being mortal, of that boy did die;<br>And for her sake do I rear up her boy;<br>And for her sake I will not part with him. |
| [[Oberon|OBERON]] | How long within this wood intend you stay? |
| [[Titania|TITANIA]] | Perchance till after [[Theseus]]’ wedding day.<br>If you will patiently [[Dance|dance]] in our round,<br>And see our moonlight revels, go with us.<br>If not, shun me, and I will spare your haunts. |
| [[Oberon|OBERON]] | Give me that boy and I will go with thee. |
| [[Titania|TITANIA]] | Not for thy fairy kingdom.—Fairies, away.<br>We shall chide downright if I longer stay. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt [[Titania]] and her train |
| [[Oberon|OBERON]] | [[Well]], go thy way. Thou shalt not from this grove<br>Till I torment thee for this injury.—<br>My gentle [[Puck|puck]], come hither. Thou rememb’rest<br>Since once I sat upon a promontory<br>And heard a [[Mermaid|mermaid]] on a dolphin’s back<br>Uttering such dulcet and harmonious breath<br>That the rude sea grew civil at her [[Song|song]]<br>And certain [[Stars|stars]] shot madly from their spheres<br>To hear the sea-maid’s music? |
| ROBIN | I remember. |
| [[Oberon|OBERON]] | That very [[Time|time]] I saw, but thou couldst not,<br>Flying between the cold [[Moon|moon]] and the earth<br>Cupid, all armed. A certain aim he took<br>At a fair vestal thronèd by the west,<br>And loosed his [[Love|love]]-shaft smartly from his bow<br>As it should pierce a hundred thousand hearts.<br>But I might see young Cupid’s fiery shaft<br>Quenched in the chaste beams of the wat‘ry [[Moon|moon]],<br>And the imperial vot’ress passed on,<br>In maiden meditation, fancy-free.<br>Yet marked I where the bolt of Cupid fell. 165<br>It fell upon a little western flower—<br>Before, milk-white; now, purple with [[Love|love]]’s wound—<br>And maidens call it [[Love|love]]-in-idleness.<br>Fetch me that flower; the herb I showed thee once.<br>The juice of it on sleeping eyelids laid<br>Will make or man or woman madly dote<br>Upon the next live creature that it sees.<br>Fetch me this herb, and be thou here again<br>Ere the leviathan can swim a league. |
| ROBIN | I’ll put a girdle round about the earth<br>In forty minutes. Exit |
| [[Oberon|OBERON]] | Having once this juice<br>I’ll watch [[Titania]] when she is asleep,<br>And drop the liquor of it in her eyes.<br>The next thing then she waking looks upon—<br>Be it on [[Lion|lion]], bear, or wolf, or bull,<br>On meddling monkey, or on busy ape—<br>She shall pursue it with the [[Soul|soul]] of [[Love|love]].<br>And ere I take this charm from off her sight—<br>As I can take it with another herb—<br>I’ll make her render up her page to me.<br>But who comes here? I am invisible,<br>And I will overhear their conference. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Demetrius]], [[Helena]] following him |
| [[Demetrius|DEMETRIUS]] | I [[Love|love]] thee not, therefore pursue me not.<br>Where is [[Lysander]], and fair [[Hermia]]?<br>The one I’ll slay, the other slayeth me.<br>Thou told’st me they were stol’n unto this wood,<br>And here am I, and wood within this wood<br>Because I cannot meet my [[Hermia]].<br>Hence, get thee gone, and follow me no more. |
| [[Helena|HELENA]] | You draw me, you hard-hearted adamant,<br>But yet you draw not iron; for my heart<br>Is true as steel. Leave you your power to draw,<br>And I shall have no power to follow you. |
| [[Demetrius|DEMETRIUS]] | Do I entice you? Do I speak you fair?<br>Or rather do I not in plainest [[Truth|truth]]<br>Tell you I do not nor I cannot [[Love|love]] you? |
| [[Helena|HELENA]] | And even for that do I [[Love|love]] you the more.<br>I am your spaniel, and, [[Demetrius]],<br>The more you beat me I will fawn on you.<br>Use me but as your spaniel: spurn me, strike me,<br>Neglect me, lose me; only give me leave,<br>Unworthy as I am, to follow you.<br>What worser place can I beg in your [[Love|love]]—<br>And yet a place of high respect with me—<br>Than to be used as you use your dog? 210 |
| [[Demetrius|DEMETRIUS]] | Tempt not too much the hatred of my spirit;<br>For I am sick when I do look on thee. |
| [[Helena|HELENA]] | And I am sick when I look not on you. |
| [[Demetrius|DEMETRIUS]] | You do impeach your modesty too much,<br>To leave the [[City|city]] and commit yourself 215<br>Into the hands of one that loves you not;<br>To trust the opportunity of [[Night|night]],<br>And the ill counsel of a [[Desert|desert]] place,<br>With the rich worth of your virginity. |
| [[Helena|HELENA]] | Your virtue is my privilege, for that 220<br>It is not [[Night|night]] when I do see your face;<br>Therefore I think I am not in the [[Night|night]],<br>Nor doth this wood lack worlds of company;<br>For you in my respect are all the world.<br>Then how can it be said I am [[Alone|alone]], 225<br>When all the world is here to look on me? |
| [[Demetrius|DEMETRIUS]] | I’ll run from thee, and hide me in the brakes,<br>And leave thee to the [[Mercy|mercy]] of wild beasts. |
| [[Helena|HELENA]] | The wildest hath not such a heart as you.<br>Run when you will. The story shall be changed: 230<br>[[Apollo]] flies, and Daphne holds the chase.<br>The dove pursues the griffin, the mild hind<br>Makes [[Speed|speed]] to catch the tiger: bootless [[Speed|speed]],<br>When cowardice pursues, and valour flies. |
| [[Demetrius|DEMETRIUS]] | I will not stay thy questions. Let me go; 235<br>Or if thou follow me, do not believe<br>But I shall do thee mischief in the wood. |
| [[Helena|HELENA]] | Ay, in the temple, in the town, the field,<br>You do me mischief. Fie, [[Demetrius]],<br>Your wrongs do set a scandal on my sex. 240<br>We cannot fight for [[Love|love]] as men may do;<br>We should be wooed, and were not made to woo.<br>I’ll follow thee, and make a heaven of hell,<br>To die upon the hand I [[Love|love]] so [[Well|well]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit [[Demetrius]], [[Helena]] following him |
| [[Oberon|OBERON]] | Fare thee [[Well|well]], nymph. Ere he do leave this grove<br>Thou shalt fly him, and he shall seek thy [[Love|love]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Robin Goodfellow the [[Puck|puck]] |
| [[Oberon|OBERON]] | Hast thou the flower there? Welcome, wanderer. |
| ROBIN | Ay, there it is. |
| [[Oberon|OBERON]] | I pray thee give it me.<br>I know a bank where the wild thyme blows,<br>Where oxlips and the nodding violet grows, 250<br>Quite overcanopied with luscious woodbine,<br>With sweet musk-roses, and with eglantine.<br>There sleeps [[Titania]] sometime of the [[Night|night]],<br>Lulled in these [[Flowers|flowers]] with dances and delight;<br>And there the snake throws her enamelled skin,<br>Weed wide enough to wrap a fairy in;<br>And with the juice of this I’ll streak her eyes,<br>And make her full of hateful fantasies.<br>Take thou some of it, and seek through this grove.<br>A sweet Athenian lady is in [[Love|love]] 260<br>With a disdainful youth. Anoint his eyes;<br>But do it when the next thing he espies<br>May be the lady. Thou shalt know the man<br>By the Athenian garments he hath on.<br>Effect it with some care, that he may prove<br>More fond on her than she upon her [[Love|love]];<br>And look thou meet me ere the first cock crow. |
| ROBIN | [[Fear]] not, my lord. Your servant shall do so. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt severally |
