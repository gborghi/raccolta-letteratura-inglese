| [[Titania|TITANIA]] | These are the forgeries of [[Jealousy|jealousy]], And never since the middle [[Summer|summer]]’s [[Spring|spring]] Met we on [[Hill|hill]], in dale, forest, or mead, By paved fountain or by rushy brook, Or in the beached margin of the sea To [[Dance|dance]] our ringlets to the whistling [[Wind|wind]], But with thy brawls thou hast disturbed our sport. Therefore the winds, piping to us in vain, As in [[Revenge|revenge]] have sucked up from the sea Contagious fogs which, falling in the land, Hath every pelting [[River|river]] made so proud That they have overborne their continents. The ox hath therefore stretched his yoke in vain, The ploughman lost his sweat, and the green corn Hath rotted ere his youth attained a beard. The fold stands empty in the drowned field, And crows are fatted with the murrain flock. The nine men’s morris is filled up with mud, And the quaint mazes in the wanton green For lack of tread are undistinguishable. The human mortals want their [[Winter|winter]] cheer. No [[Night|night]] is now with [[Hymn|hymn]] or [[carol]] blessed. Therefore the [[Moon|moon]], the governess of floods, Pale in her anger washes all the air, That rheumatic diseases do abound; And thorough this distemperature we see The [[Seasons|seasons]] alter: hoary-headed frosts Fall in the fresh lap of the crimson [[Rose|rose]], And on old Hiems’ thin and icy [[Crown|crown]] An odorous chaplet of sweet [[Summer|summer]] buds Is, as in mock’ry, set. The [[Spring|spring]], the [[Summer|summer]], The childing [[Autumn|autumn]], angry [[Winter|winter]] change Their wonted liveries, and the mazèd world By their increase now knows not which is which; And this same progeny of evils comes From our [[debate]], from our dissension. We are their parents and original. |
