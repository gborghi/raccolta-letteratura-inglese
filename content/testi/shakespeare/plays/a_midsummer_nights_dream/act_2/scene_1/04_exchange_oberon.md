| [[Oberon|OBERON]] | Do you amend it, then. It lies in you. Why should [[Titania]] [[Cross|cross]] her [[Oberon]]? I do but beg a little changeling boy To be my henchman. |
| [[Titania|TITANIA]] | Set your heart at rest. The fairyland buys not the [[Child|child]] of me. His mother was a vot‘ress of my order, And in the spiced Indian air by [[Night|night]] Full often hath she gossiped by my side, And sat with me on Neptune’s yellow sands, Marking th’embarkèd traders on the flood, When we have laughed to see the sails conceive And grow big-bellied with the wanton [[Wind|wind]], Which she with pretty and with swimming gait Following, her womb then rich with my young squire, Would imitate, and sail upon the land To fetch me trifles, and return again As from a voyage, rich with merchandise. But she, being mortal, of that boy did die; And for her sake do I rear up her boy; And for her sake I will not part with him. |
| [[Oberon|OBERON]] | How long within this wood intend you stay? |
| [[Titania|TITANIA]] | Perchance till after [[Theseus]]’ wedding day. If you will patiently [[Dance|dance]] in our round, And see our moonlight revels, go with us. If not, shun me, and I will spare your haunts. |
| [[Oberon|OBERON]] | Give me that boy and I will go with thee. |
| [[Titania|TITANIA]] | Not for thy fairy kingdom.—Fairies, away. We shall chide downright if I longer stay. Exeunt [[Titania]] and her train |
| [[Oberon|OBERON]] | [[Well]], go thy way. Thou shalt not from this grove Till I torment thee for this injury.— My gentle [[Puck|puck]], come hither. Thou rememb’rest Since once I sat upon a promontory And heard a [[Mermaid|mermaid]] on a dolphin’s back Uttering such dulcet and harmonious breath That the rude sea grew civil at her [[Song|song]] And certain [[Stars|stars]] shot madly from their spheres To hear the sea-maid’s music? |
| ROBIN | I remember. |
| [[Oberon|OBERON]] | That very [[Time|time]] I saw, but thou couldst not, Flying between the cold [[Moon|moon]] and the earth Cupid, all armed. A certain aim he took At a fair vestal thronèd by the west, And loosed his [[Love|love]]-shaft smartly from his bow As it should pierce a hundred thousand hearts. But I might see young Cupid’s fiery shaft Quenched in the chaste beams of the wat‘ry [[Moon|moon]], And the imperial vot’ress passed on, In maiden meditation, fancy-free. Yet marked I where the bolt of Cupid fell. 165 It fell upon a little western flower— Before, milk-white; now, purple with [[Love|love]]’s wound— And maidens call it [[Love|love]]-in-idleness. Fetch me that flower; the herb I showed thee once. The juice of it on sleeping eyelids laid Will make or man or woman madly dote Upon the next live creature that it sees. Fetch me this herb, and be thou here again Ere the leviathan can swim a league. |
| ROBIN | I’ll put a girdle round about the earth In forty minutes. Exit |
| [[Oberon|OBERON]] | Having once this juice I’ll watch [[Titania]] when she is asleep, And drop the liquor of it in her eyes. The next thing then she waking looks upon— Be it on [[Lion|lion]], bear, or wolf, or bull, On meddling monkey, or on busy ape— She shall pursue it with the [[Soul|soul]] of [[Love|love]]. And ere I take this charm from off her sight— As I can take it with another herb— I’ll make her render up her page to me. But who comes here? I am invisible, And I will overhear their conference. Enter [[Demetrius]], [[Helena]] following him |
