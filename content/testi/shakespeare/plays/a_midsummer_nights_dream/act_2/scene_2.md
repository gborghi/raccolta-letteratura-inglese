| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Lysander]] and [[Hermia]] |
| [[Lysander|LYSANDER]] | Fair [[Love|love]], you faint with wand’ring in the wood,<br>And, to speak [[Truth|truth]], I have forgot our way.<br>We’ll rest us, [[Hermia]], if you think it good,<br>And tarry for the comfort of the day. |
| [[Hermia|HERMIA]] | Be it so, [[Lysander]]. Find you out a bed;<br>For I upon this bank will rest my head. |
| *(didascalia)* | She lies down |
| [[Lysander|LYSANDER]] | One turf shall serve as pillow for us both;<br>One heart, one bed; two bosoms, and one troth. |
| [[Hermia|HERMIA]] | Nay, good [[Lysander]]; for my sake, my dear,<br>Lie further off yet; do not lie so near. |
| [[Lysander|LYSANDER]] | O, take the sense, sweet, of my [[innocence]]!<br>[[Love]] takes the meaning in [[Love|love]]’s conference—<br>I mean that my heart unto yours is knit,<br>So that but one heart we can make of it.<br>Two bosoms interchainèd with an oath;<br>So, then, two bosoms and a single troth.<br>Then by your side no bed-room me deny;<br>For lying so, [[Hermia]], I do not lie. |
| [[Hermia|HERMIA]] | [[Lysander]] riddles very prettily.<br>Now much beshrew my manners and my [[Pride|pride]]<br>If [[Hermia]] meant to say [[Lysander]] lied.<br>But, gentle friend, for [[Love|love]] and courtesy,<br>Lie further off, in humane modesty.<br>Such separation as may [[Well|well]] be said<br>Becomes a virtuous bachelor and a maid,<br>So far be distant; and good [[Night|night]], sweet friend.<br>Thy [[Love|love]] ne’er alter till thy sweet life end. |
| [[Lysander|LYSANDER]] | Amen, amen, to that fair [[prayer]] say I;<br>And then end life when I end loyalty.<br>Here is my bed; [[Sleep|sleep]] give thee all his rest.<br>He lies down |
| [[Hermia|HERMIA]] | With half that wish the wisher’s eyes be pressed. |
| *(didascalia)* | They [[Sleep|sleep]] apart. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Robin Goodfellow the [[Puck|puck]] |

Exit |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Demetrius]] and [[Helena]], running |
| [[Helena|HELENA]] | Stay, though thou kill me, sweet [[Demetrius]]. |
| [[Demetrius|DEMETRIUS]] | I charge thee hence, and do not haunt me thus. |
| [[Helena|HELENA]] | O, wilt thou darkling leave me? Do not so. |
| [[Demetrius|DEMETRIUS]] | Stay, on thy peril; I [[Alone|alone]] will go. Exit |
| [[Helena|HELENA]] | O, I am out of breath in this fond chase.<br>The more my [[prayer]], the lesser is my grace.<br>Happy is [[Hermia]], wheresoe‘er she lies;<br>For she hath blessed and attractive eyes.<br>How came her eyes so bright? Not with salt [[Tears|tears]]—<br>If so, my eyes are oft’ner washed than hers.<br>No, no; I am as ugly as a bear, 100<br>For beasts that meet me run away for [[Fear|fear]].<br>Therefore no marvel though [[Demetrius]]<br>Do, as a [[Monster|monster]], fly my presence thus.<br>What wicked and dissembling glass of mine<br>Made me compare with [[Hermia]]’s sphery eyne!<br>But who is here? [[Lysander]], on the ground?<br>Dead, or asleep? I see no [[Blood|blood]], no wound.<br>[[Lysander]], if you live, good sir, awake. |
| [[Lysander|LYSANDER]] | (awaking)<br>And run through [[Fire|fire]] I will for thy sweet sake.<br>Transparent [[Helena]], [[Nature|nature]] shows art<br>That through thy bosom makes me see thy heart.<br>Where is [[Demetrius]]? O, how fit a word<br>Is that vile name to perish on my [[Sword|sword]]! |
| [[Helena|HELENA]] | Do not say so, [[Lysander]]; say not so.<br>What though he [[Love|love]] your [[Hermia]]? Lord, what<br>though?<br>Yet [[Hermia]] still loves you; then be content. |
| [[Lysander|LYSANDER]] | Content with [[Hermia]]? No, I do repent<br>The tedious minutes I with her have spent.<br>Not [[Hermia]] but [[Helena]] I [[Love|love]].<br>Who will not change a raven for a dove?<br>The will of man is by his reason swayed,<br>And reason says you are the worthier maid.<br>Things growing are not ripe until their season,<br>So I, being young, till now ripe not to reason.<br>And, touching now the point of human skill,<br>Reason becomes the marshal to my will,<br>And leads me to your eyes, where I o’erlook<br>[[Love]]’s stories written in [[Love|love]]’s richest book. |
| [[Helena|HELENA]] | Wherefore was I to this keen mockery born?<br>When at your hands did I deserve this scorn?<br>Is’t not enough, is’t not enough, young man,<br>That I did never—no, nor never can—<br>Deserve a sweet look from [[Demetrius]]’ eye,<br>But you must flout my insufficiency?<br>Good troth, you do me wrong; good sooth, you do,<br>In such disdainful manner me to woo.<br>But fare you [[Well|well]]. Perforce I must confess<br>I thought you lord of more true gentleness.<br>O, that a lady of one man refused<br>Should of another therefore be abused! Exit |
| [[Lysander|LYSANDER]] | She sees not [[Hermia]]. Exit |
