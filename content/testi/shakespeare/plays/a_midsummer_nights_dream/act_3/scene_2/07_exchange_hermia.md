| [[Hermia|HERMIA]] | I am amazed at your passionate words. I scorn you not. It seems that you scorn me. |
| [[Helena|HELENA]] | Have you not set [[Lysander]], as in scorn, To follow me, and praise my eyes and face? And made your other [[Love|love]], [[Demetrius]]— Who even but now did spurn me with his foot— To call me goddess, nymph, divine, and rare, Precious, celestial? Wherefore speaks he this To her he hates? And wherefore doth [[Lysander]] Deny your [[Love|love]] so rich within his [[Soul|soul]], And tender me, forsooth, affection, But by your setting on, by your consent? What though I be not so in grace as you, So hung upon with [[Love|love]], so fortunate, But miserable most, to [[Love|love]] unloved— This you should pity rather than despise. |
| [[Hermia|HERMIA]] | I understand not what you mean by this. |
| [[Helena|HELENA]] | Ay, do. Persever, counterfeit sad looks, Make mouths upon me when I turn my back, Wink each at other, hold the sweet jest up. This sport [[Well|well]] carried shall be chronicled. If you have any pity, grace, or manners, You would not make me such an argument. But fare ye [[Well|well]]. ’Tis partly my own fault, Which [[Death|death]] or absence soon shall remedy. |
| [[Lysander|LYSANDER]] | Stay, gentle [[Helena]], hear my excuse, My [[Love|love]], my life, my [[Soul|soul]], fair [[Helena]]. |
| [[Helena|HELENA]] | O excellent! |
| [[Hermia|HERMIA]] | (to [[Lysander]]) Sweet, do not scorn her so. |
| [[Demetrius|DEMETRIUS]] | (to [[Lysander]]) If she cannot entreat I can compel. |
| [[Lysander|LYSANDER]] | Thou canst compel no more than she entreat. Thy threats have no more strength than her weak prayers.— [[Helen]], I [[Love|love]] thee; by my life I do. I swear by that which I will lose for thee To prove him false that says I [[Love|love]] thee not. |
| [[Demetrius|DEMETRIUS]] | (to [[Helena]]) I say I [[Love|love]] thee more than he can do. |
| [[Lysander|LYSANDER]] | If thou say so, withdraw, and prove it too. |
| [[Demetrius|DEMETRIUS]] | Quick, come. |
| [[Hermia|HERMIA]] | [[Lysander]], whereto tends all this? She takes him by the arm |
| [[Lysander|LYSANDER]] | Away, you Ethiope. |
| [[Demetrius|DEMETRIUS]] | No, no, sir, yield. Seem to break loose, take on as you would follow, But yet come not. You are a tame man; go. |
| [[Lysander|LYSANDER]] | (to [[Hermia]]) Hang off, thou cat, thou burr; vile thing, let loose, Or I will shake thee from me like a serpent. |
| [[Hermia|HERMIA]] | Why are you grown so rude? What change is this, Sweet [[Love|love]]? |
| [[Lysander|LYSANDER]] | Thy [[Love|love]]? Out, tawny Tartar, out; Out, loathed med’cine; O hated potion, hence. |
| [[Hermia|HERMIA]] | Do you not jest? |
| [[Helena|HELENA]] | Yes, sooth, and so do you. |
| [[Lysander|LYSANDER]] | [[Demetrius]], I will keep my word with thee. |
| [[Demetrius|DEMETRIUS]] | I would I had your bond, for I perceive A weak bond holds you. I’ll not trust your word. |
| [[Lysander|LYSANDER]] | What, should I hurt her, strike her, kill her dead? Although I hate her, I’ll not harm her so. |
| [[Hermia|HERMIA]] | What, can you do me greater harm than hate? Hate me—wherefore O me, what news, my [[Love|love]]? Am not I [[Hermia]]? Are not you [[Lysander]]? I am as fair now as I was erewhile. Since [[Night|night]] you loved me, yet since [[Night|night]] you left me. Why then, you left me—O, the gods forbid— In earnest, shall I say? |
| [[Lysander|LYSANDER]] | Ay, by my life, And never did [[Desire|desire]] to see thee more. Therefore be out of [[Hope|hope]], of question, [[Doubt|doubt]]. Be certain, nothing truer; ’tis no jest That I do hate thee and [[Love|love]] [[Helena]]. |
