| ROBIN | Yet but three? Come one more, Two of both kinds makes up four. Enter [[Hermia]] Here she comes, curst and sad. Cupid is a knavish lad Thus to make poor females mad. |
| [[Hermia|HERMIA]] | Never so weary, never so in woe, Bedabbled with the dew, and torn with briers, I can no further crawl, no further go. My legs can keep no pace with my desires. Here will I rest me till the break of day. She lies down |
| [[Hermia|HERMIA]] | Heavens shield [[Lysander]], if they mean a fray. She sleeps |
| ROBIN | On the ground [[Sleep|sleep]] sound. I’ll apply to your eye, Gentle [[Lover|lover]], remedy. He drops the juice on [[Lysander]]’s eyelids |
| ROBIN | When thou wak‘st thou tak’st True delight in the sight Of thy former lady’s eye, And the country proverb known, That ‘every man should take his own’, In your waking shall be shown. Jack shall have Jill, Naught shall go ill, the man shall have his mare again, and all shall be [[Well|well]]. Exit |
