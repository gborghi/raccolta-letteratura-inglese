| *(didascalia)* | Enter the Duke of Somerset with his army |
| SOMERSET | (to a Captain)<br>It is too late, I cannot send them now.<br>This expedition was by York and Talbot<br>Too rashly plotted. All our general force<br>Might with a sally of the very town<br>Be buckled with. The over-daring Talbot<br>Hath sullied all his gloss of former [[Honour|honour]]<br>By this unheedful, desperate, wild adventure.<br>York set him on to fight and die in shame<br>That, Talbot dead, great York might bear the name. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Lucy |
| CAPTAIN | Here is Sir William Lucy, who with me<br>Set from our o’ermatched forces forth for aid. |
| SOMERSET | How now, Sir William, whither were you sent? |
| LUCY | Whither, my lord? From bought and sold [[Lord Talbot]],<br>Who, ringed about with bold adversity,<br>Cries out for noble York and Somerset<br>To beat assailing [[Death|death]] from his weak legions;<br>And whiles the honourable captain there<br>Drops bloody sweat from his war-wearied limbs<br>And, unadvantaged, ling’ring looks for rescue,<br>You his false hopes, the trust of England’s [[Honour|honour]],<br>Keep off aloof with worthless emulation.<br>Let not your private discord keep away<br>The levied succours that should lend him aid,<br>While he, renowned noble gentleman,<br>Yield up his life unto a world of odds.<br>Orléans the Bastard, Charles, and Burgundy,<br>Alençon, René, compass him about,<br>And Talbot perisheth by your default. |
| SOMERSET | York set him on; York should have sent him aid. |
| LUCY | And York as fast upon your grace exclaims,<br>Swearing that you withhold his levied [[Horse|horse]]<br>Collected for this expedition. |
| SOMERSET | York lies. He might have sent and had the [[Horse|horse]].<br>I owe him little duty and less [[Love|love]],<br>And take foul scorn to fawn on him by sending. |
| LUCY | The fraud of England, not the force of France,<br>Hath now entrapped the noble-minded Talbot.<br>Never to England shall he bear his life,<br>But dies betrayed to fortune by your strife. |
| SOMERSET | Come, go. I will dispatch the horsemen straight. |
| *(didascalia)* | Within six hours they will be at his aid. |
| LUCY | Too late comes rescue. He is ta’en or slain,<br>For fly he could not if he would have fled,<br>And fly would Talbot never, though he might. |
| SOMERSET | If he be dead, brave Talbot, then adieu. |
| LUCY | His fame lives in the world, his shame in you. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt severally |
