| *(didascalia)* | Alarum. Excursions. Enter [[Pistol]], a French soldier, and the Boy |
| [[Pistol|PISTOL]] | Yield, cur. |
| FRENCH SOLDIER | je pense que vous êtes le gentilhomme de bon qualité. |
| [[Pistol|PISTOL]] | Qualité? ‘Calin o custure me!’<br><br>Art thou a gentleman? What is thy name? Discuss. |
| FRENCH SOLDIER | O Seigneur Dieu! |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Pistol|PISTOL]] aside |
| FRENCH SOLDIER | O Seigneur Dew should be a gentleman.—<br>Perpend my words, O Seigneur Dew, and mark:<br>O Seigneur Dew, thou diest, on point of fox,<br>Except, O Seigneur, thou do give to me<br>Egregious ransom. |
| FRENCH SOLDIER | O prenez miséricorde! Ayez pitie de moi! |
| [[Pistol|PISTOL]] | ‘Moy’ shall not serve, I will have forty ‘moys’,<br>Or I will fetch thy rim out at thy throat<br>In drops of crimson [[Blood|blood]]. |
| FRENCH SOLDIER | Est-il impossible d’échapper la force de ton bras? |
| [[Pistol|PISTOL]] | Brass, cur? Thou damned and luxurious mountain<br>goat,<br>Offer’st me brass? |
| FRENCH SOLDIER | O pardonne-moi! |
| [[Pistol|PISTOL]] | Sayst thou me so? Is that a ton of moys?—<br>Come hither boy. Ask me this slave in French<br>What is his name. |
| BOY | Écoutez: comment êtes-vous appelé? |
| FRENCH SOLDIER | Monsieur le Fer. |
| BOY | He says his name is Master Fer. |
| [[Pistol|PISTOL]] | Master Fer? I’ll fer him, and firk him, and ferret him.<br>Discuss the same in French unto him. |
| BOY | I do not know the French for fer and ferret and firk. |
| [[Pistol|PISTOL]] | Bid him prepare, for I will cut his throat. |
| FRENCH SOLDIER | Que dit-il, monsieur? |
| BOY | Il me commande à vous dire que vous faites vous prêt, car ce soldat ici est dispose tout à cette heure de couper votre gorge. 35 |
| [[Pistol|PISTOL]] | Oui, couper la gorge, par ma foi,<br><br>Peasant, unless thou give me crowns, brave crowns;<br>Or mangled shalt thou be by this my [[Sword|sword]]. |
| FRENCH SOLDIER | O je vous supplie, pour l’amour de Dieu, me pardonner. Je suis le gentilhomme de bonne maison. Gardez ma vie, et je vous donnerai deux cents écus. |
| [[Pistol|PISTOL]] | What are his words? |
| BOY | He prays you to save his life. He is a gentleman of a good [[house]], and for his ransom he will give you two hundred crowns. |
| [[Pistol|PISTOL]] | Tell him, my fury shall abate, and I the crowns will take. |
| FRENCH SOLDIER | Petit monsieur, que dit-il? |
| BOY | Encore qu’il est centre son jurement de pardonner aucun prisonnier; neanmoins, pour les ecus que vous lui ci promettez, il est content à vous donner la liberté, le franchisement. |
| FRENCH SOLDIER | (kneeling to [[Pistol]]) Sur mes genoux je vous donne mille remerciements, et je m‘estime heureux que j’ai tombe entre les mains d‘un chevalier, comme je pense, le plus brave, vaillant, et treis-distingué seigneur d’Angleterre. [[Pistol|PISTOL]] Expound unto me, boy. |
| BOY | He gives you upon his knees a thousand thanks, and he esteems himself happy that he hath fallen into the hands of one, as he thinks, the most brave, valorous, and thrice-worthy seigneur of England. |
| [[Pistol|PISTOL]] | As I suck [[Blood|blood]], I will some [[Mercy|mercy]] show.<br>Follow me. |
| BOY | Suivez-vous le grand capitaine. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt [[Pistol]] and French Soldier |
| BOY | I did never know so full a voice issue from so empty a<br>heart. But the saying is true: ‘The empty vessel makes<br>the greatest sound.’ Bardolph and Nim had ten times<br>more valour than this roaring devil i’th’ old play, that<br>everyone may pare his nails with a wooden dagger,<br>and they are both hanged, and so would this be, if he<br>durst steal anything adventurously. I must stay with<br>the lackeys with the luggage of our camp. The French<br>might have a good prey of us, if he knew of it, for there<br>is none to guard it but boys. Exit |
