| ERPINGHAM | My lord, your nobles, jealous of your absence, Seek through your camp to find you. |
| [[King|KING]] HARRY | Good old knight, Collect them all together at my tent. I’ll be before thee. |
| ERPINGHAM | I shall do’t, my lord. Exit |
| [[King|KING]] HARRY | O God of battles, steel my soldiers’ hearts. Possess them not with [[Fear|fear]]. Take from them now The sense of reck‘ning, ere th’opposèd numbers Pluck their hearts from them. Not today, O Lord, O not today, think not upon the fault My father made in compassing the [[Crown|crown]]. I [[Richard]]’s [[Body|body]] have interred new, And on it have bestowed more contrite [[Tears|tears]] Than from it issued forced drops of [[Blood|blood]]. Five hundred poor have I in yearly pay Who twice a day their withered hands hold up Toward heaven to pardon [[Blood|blood]]. And I have built Two chantries, where the sad and solemn priests Sing still for [[Richard]]’s [[Soul|soul]]. More will I do, Though all that I can do is nothing worth, Since that my penitence comes after ill, Imploring pardon. Enter the [[Duke of Gloucester]] |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | My liege. |
| [[King|KING]] HARRY | My brother [[Gloucester]]’s voice? Ay. I know thy errand, I will go with thee. The day, my friends, and all things stay for me. Exeunt |
