| *(didascalia)* | Flourish. Enter [[King]] Charles the Sixth of France, the [[Dauphin]], the Constable, and the Dukes of Berri and Bourbon |
| [[King|KING]] CHARLES | Thus comes the English with full power upon us,<br>And more than carefully it us concerns<br>To answer royally in our defences.<br>Therefore the Dukes of Berri and of Bourbon,<br>Of Brabant and of Orléans shall make forth,<br>And you Prince [[Dauphin]], with all swift dispatch<br>To line and new-repair our towns of war<br>With men of [[Courage|courage]] and with means defendant.<br>For England his approaches makes as fierce<br>As waters to the sucking of a gulf.<br>It fits us then to be as provident<br>As [[Fear|fear]] may teach us, out of late examples<br>Left by the fatal and neglected English<br>Upon our fields. |
| [[Dauphin|DAUPHIN]] | My most redoubted father,<br>It is most meet we arm us ‘gainst the foe,<br>For peace itself should not so dull a kingdom—<br>Though war, nor no known quarrel, were in<br>question—<br>But that defences, musters, preparations<br>Should be maintained, assembled, and collected<br>As were a war in expectation.<br>Therefore, I say, ’tis meet we all go forth<br>To view the sick and feeble parts of France.<br>And let us do it with no show of [[Fear|fear]],<br>No, with no more than if we heard that England<br>Were busied with a Whitsun morris [[Dance|dance]].<br>For, my good liege, she is so idly kinged,<br>Her sceptre so fantastically borne<br>By a vain, giddy, shallow, humorous youth,<br>That [[Fear|fear]] attends her not. |
| CONSTABLE | O peace, Prince [[Dauphin]].<br>You are too much mistaken in this [[King|king]].<br>Question your grace the late ambassadors<br>With what great state he heard their embassy,<br>How [[Well|well]] supplied with aged counsellors,<br>How modest in exception, and withal<br>How terrible in constant resolution,<br>And you shall find his vanities forespent<br>Were but the outside of the Roman [[Brutus]],<br>Covering discretion with a coat of folly,<br>As gardeners do with ordure hide those roots<br>That shall first [[spring]] and be most delicate. |
| [[Dauphin|DAUPHIN]] | [[Well]], ‘tis not so, my Lord High Constable.<br>But though we think it so, it is no matter.<br>In cases of defence ’tis best to weigh<br>The enemy more mighty than he seems.<br>So the proportions of defence are filled—<br>Which, of a weak and niggardly projection,<br>Doth like a miser spoil his coat with scanting<br>A little cloth. |
| [[King|KING]] CHARLES | Think we [[King]] Harry strong.<br>And princes, look you strongly arm to meet him.<br>The kindred of him hath been fleshed upon us,<br>And he is bred out of that bloody strain<br>That haunted us in our familiar paths.<br>Witness our too-much-memorable shame<br>When Crécy battle fatally was struck,<br>And all our princes captived by the hand<br>Of that black name, Edward, Black Prince of Wales,<br>Whiles that his mountant sire, on mountain standing,<br>Up in the air, crowned with the golden sun,<br>Saw his heroical seed and smiled to see him<br>Mangle the work of [[Nature|nature]] and deface<br>The patterns that by God and by French fathers<br>Had twenty years been made. This is a stem<br>Of that victorious stock, and let us [[Fear|fear]]<br>The native mightiness and fate of him. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter a Messenger |
| MESSENGER | Ambassadors from Harry, [[King]] of England,<br>Do crave admittance to your majesty. |
| [[King|KING]] CHARLES | We’ll give them present audience. Go and bring them. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit Messenger |
| [[King|KING]] CHARLES | You see this chase is hotly followed, friends. |
| [[Dauphin|DAUPHIN]] | Turn head and stop pursuit. For coward [[dogs]]<br>Most spend their mouths when what they seem to<br>threaten<br>Runs far before them. Good my sovereign,<br>Take up the English short, and let them know<br>Of what a monarchy you are the head.<br>[[Self]]-[[Love|love]], my liege, is not so vile a sin<br>As [[Self|self]]-neglecting. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter the [[Duke of Exeter]], attended |
| [[King|KING]] CHARLES | From our brother England? |
| EXETER | From him, and thus he greets your majesty:<br>He wills you, in the name of God Almighty,<br>That you divest yourself and lay apart<br>The borrowed glories that by gift of heaven,<br>By law of [[Nature|nature]] and of nations, ‘longs<br>To him and to his heirs, namely the [[Crown|crown]],<br>And all wide-stretched honours that pertain<br>By custom and the ordinance of times<br>Unto the [[Crown|crown]] of France. That you may know<br>’Tis no sinister nor no awkward claim,<br>Picked from the worm-holes of long-vanished days,<br>Nor from the [[Dust|dust]] of old oblivion raked,<br>He sends you this most memorable line,<br>In every branch truly demonstrative,<br>Willing you over-look this pedigree,<br>And when you find him evenly derived<br>From his most famed of famous ancestors,<br>Edward the Third, he bids you then resign<br>Your [[Crown|crown]] and kingdom, indirectly held<br>From him, the native and true challenger. |
| [[King|KING]] CHARLES | Or else what follows? |
| EXETER | Bloody constraint. For if you hide the [[Crown|crown]]<br>Even in your hearts, there will he rake for it.<br>Therefore in fierce tempest is he coming,<br>In thunder and in earthquake, like a Jove,<br>That if requiring fail, he will compel;<br>And bids you, in the bowels of the Lord,<br>Deliver up the [[Crown|crown]], and to take [[Mercy|mercy]]<br>On the poor souls for whom this hungry war<br>Opens his vasty jaws; and on your head<br>Turns he the widows’ [[Tears|tears]], the orphans’ cries,<br>The dead men’s [[Blood|blood]], the pining maidens’ groans,<br>For husbands, fathers, and betrothed [[lovers]]<br>That shall be swallowed in this controversy.<br>This is his claim, his threat’ning, and my message—<br>Unless the [[Dauphin]] be in presence here,<br>To whom expressly I bring greeting too. |
| [[King|KING]] CHARLES | For us, we will consider of this further.<br>Tomorrow shall you bear our full intent<br>Back to our brother England. |
| [[Dauphin|DAUPHIN]] | For the [[Dauphin]],<br>I stand here for him. What to him from England? |
| EXETER | Scorn and defiance, slight regard, contempt;<br>And anything that may not misbecome<br>The mighty sender, doth he prize you at.<br>Thus says my [[King|king]]: an if your father’s highness<br>Do not, in grant of all demands at large,<br>Sweeten the bitter mock you sent his majesty,<br>He’ll call you to so hot an answer for it<br>That caves and womby vaultages of France<br>Shall chide your trespass and return your mock<br>In second accent of his ordinance. |
| [[Dauphin|DAUPHIN]] | Say if my father render fair return<br>It is against my will, for I [[Desire|desire]]<br>Nothing but odds with England. To that end,<br>As matching to his youth and [[Vanity|vanity]],<br>I did present him with the [[Paris]] balls. |
| EXETER | He’ll make your [[Paris]] Louvre shake for it,<br>Were it the mistress [[Court|court]] of mighty Europe.<br>And be assured, you’ll find a diff’rence,<br>As we his subjects have in [[Wonder|wonder]] found,<br>Between the promise of his greener days<br>And these he masters now: now he weighs [[Time|time]]<br>Even to the utmost grain. That you shall read<br>In your own losses, if he stay in France. |
| *(didascalia)* | [[King|KING]] CHARLES rising |
| EXETER | Tomorrow shall you know our mind at full. |
| *(didascalia)* | Flourish |
| EXETER | Dispatch us with all [[Speed|speed]], lest that our [[King|king]]<br>Come here himself to question our delay—<br>For he is footed in this land already. |
| [[King|KING]] CHARLES | You shall be soon dispatched with fair conditions.<br>A [[Night|night]] is but small breath and little pause<br>To answer matters of this consequence. |
| *(didascalia)* | Flourish. Exeunt |
| [[King|KING]] CHARLES | 3.0 Enter [[Chorus]] |
| [[Chorus|CHORUS]] | Thus with imagined wing our swift scene flies<br>In motion of no less celerity<br>Than that of thought. Suppose that you have seen<br>The [[Well|well]]-appointed [[King|king]] at Dover pier<br>Embark his royalty, and his brave fleet<br>With silken streamers the young Phoebus fanning.<br>Play with your fancies, and in them behold<br>Upon the hempen tackle [[Ship|ship]]-boys climbing;<br>Hear the shrill whistle, which doth order give<br>To sounds confused; behold the threaden sails,<br>Borne with th‘invisible and creeping [[Wind|wind]],<br>Draw the huge bottoms through the furrowed sea,<br>Breasting the lofty surge. O do but think<br>You stand upon the rivage and behold<br>A [[City|city]] on th’inconstant billows dancing—<br>For so appears this fleet majestical,<br>Holding due course to Harfleur. Follow, follow!<br>Grapple your minds to sternage of this navy,<br>And leave your England, as dead midnight still,<br>Guarded with grandsires, babies, and old women,<br>Either past or not arrived to pith and puissance.<br>For who is he, whose chin is but enriched<br>With one appearing hair, that will not follow<br>These culled and choice-drawn cavaliers to France?<br>Work, work your thoughts, and therein see a siege.<br>Behold the ordnance on their carriages,<br>With fatal mouths gaping on girded Harfleur.<br>Suppose th’ambassador from the French comes back,<br>Tells Harry that the [[King]] doth offer him<br>Catherine his daughter, and with her, to dowry,<br>Some petty and unprofitable dukedoms.<br>The offer likes not, and the nimble gunner<br>With linstock now the devilish cannon touches, |
| *(didascalia)* | Alarum, and chambers go off |
| [[Chorus|CHORUS]] | And down goes all before them. Still be kind,<br>And eke out our performance with your mind. Exit |
