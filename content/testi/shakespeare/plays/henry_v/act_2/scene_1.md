| *(didascalia)* | Enter Corporal Nim and Lieutenant Bardolph |
| BARDOLPH | [[Well]] met, Corporal Nim. |
| NIM | Good morrow, Lieutenant Bardolph. |
| BARDOLPH | What, are Ensign [[Pistol]] and you friends yet? |
| NIM | For my part, I care not. I say little, but when [[Time|time]] shall serve, there shall be smiles—but that shall be as it may. I dare not fight, but I will wink and hold out mine iron. It is a simple one, but what though? It will toast [[cheese]], and it will endure cold, as another man’s [[Sword|sword]] will—and there’s an end. |
| BARDOLPH | I will bestow a breakfast to make you friends, and we’ll be all three sworn brothers to France. Let’t be so, good Corporal Nim. |
| NIM | [[Faith]], I will live so long as I may, that’s the certain of it, and when I cannot live any longer, I will do as I may. That is my rest, that is the rendezvous of it. |
| BARDOLPH | It is certain, corporal, that he is married to Nell Quickly, and certainly she did you wrong, for you were troth-plight to her. |
| NIM | I cannot tell. Things must be as they may. Men may [[Sleep|sleep]], and they may have their throats about them at that [[Time|time]], and some say knives have edges. It must be as it may. Though Patience be a tired mare, yet she will plod. There must be conclusions. [[Well]], I cannot tell. Enter Ensign [[Pistol]] and Hostess Quickly |
| BARDOLPH | Good morrow, Ensign [[Pistol]]. (To Nim) Here comes Ensign [[Pistol]] and his wife. Good Corporal, be patient here. |
| *(didascalia)* | NIM How now, mine host [[Pistol]]? |
| [[Pistol|PISTOL]] | Base tick, call’st thou me host? Now by Gad’s lugs<br>I swear I scorn the term. Nor shall my Nell keep<br>lodgers. |
| *(didascalia)* | HOSTESS No, by my troth, not long, for we cannot lodge and board a dozen or fourteen gentlewomen that live honestly by the prick of their needles, but it will be thought we keep a bawdy-[[house]] straight. Nim draws his [[Sword|sword]] O [[Well|well]]-a-day, Lady! If he be not hewn now, we shall see wilful adultery and murder committed. 36 [[Pistol]] draws his [[Sword|sword]] |
| BARDOLPH | Good lieutenant, good corporal, offer nothing here. |
| NIM | Pish. |
| [[Pistol|PISTOL]] | Pish for thee, Iceland dog. Thou prick-eared cur of<br>Iceland. |
| HOSTESS | Good Corporal Nim, show thy valour, and put<br>up your [[Sword|sword]].<br>They sheathe their swords |
| NIM | Will you shog off? I would have you solus. |
| [[Pistol|PISTOL]] | ‘Solus’, egregious dog? O viper vile!<br>The solus in thy most marvellous face,<br>The solus in thy teeth, and in thy throat,<br>And in thy hateful lungs, yea in thy maw pardie—<br>And which is worse, within thy nasty mouth.<br>I do retort the solus in thy bowels,<br>For I can take, and [[Pistol]]’s cock is up,<br>And flashing [[Fire|fire]] will follow. |
| NIM | I am not Barbason, you cannot conjure me. I have an [[Humour|humour]] to knock you indifferently [[Well|well]]. If you grow foul with me, [[Pistol]], I will scour you with my rapier, as I may, in fair terms. If you would walk off, I would prick your guts a little, in good terms, as I may, and that’s the [[Humour|humour]] of it. |
| [[Pistol|PISTOL]] | O braggart vile, and damned furious wight!<br>The [[Grave|grave]] doth gape and doting [[Death|death]] is near.<br>Therefore ex-hale.<br>[[Pistol]] and Nim draw their swords |
| BARDOLPH | Hear me, hear me what I say. |
| *(didascalia)* | He draws his [[Sword|sword]] |
| BARDOLPH | He that strikes the first stroke, I’ll run him up to the<br>hilts, as I am a soldier. |
| [[Pistol|PISTOL]] | An oath of mickle might, and fury shall abate. |
| *(didascalia)* | They sheathe their swords |
| [[Pistol|PISTOL]] | (To Nim) Give me thy fist, thy forefoot to me give. 65<br>Thy spirits are most tall. |
| NIM | I will cut thy throat one [[Time|time]] or other, in fair terms, that is the [[Humour|humour]] of it. |
| [[Pistol|PISTOL]] | Couple a gorge,<br>That is the word. I thee defy again.<br>O hound of Crete, think‘st thou my spouse to get?<br>No, to the spital go,<br>And from the powd’ring tub of infamy<br>Fetch forth the lazar kite of Cressid’s kind,<br>[[Doll Tearsheet]] she by name, and her espouse.<br>I have, and I will hold, the quondam Quickly<br>For the only she, and—pauca, there’s enough. Go to. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter the Boy running |
| BOY | Mine host [[Pistol]], you must come to my master, and you, hostess. He is very sick, and would to bed.—Good Bardolph, put thy face between his sheets, and do the office of a warming-pan.—[[Faith]], he’s very ill. |
| BARDOLPH | Away, you rogue! |
| *(didascalia)* | HOSTESS By my troth, he’ll yield the crow a pudding one of these days. The [[King]] has killed his heart. Good husband, come [[Home|home]] presently. Exit with Boy |
| BARDOLPE | Come, shall I make you two friends? We must to France together. Why the devil should we keep knives to cut one another’s throats? |
| [[Pistol|PISTOL]] | Let floods o‘erswell, and fiends for food howl on! |
| NIM | You’ll pay me the eight shillings I won of you at betting? |
| [[Pistol|PISTOL]] | Base is the slave that pays. |
| NIM | That now I will have. That’s the [[Humour|humour]] of it. |
| [[Pistol|PISTOL]] | As manhood shall compound. Push [[Home|home]].<br>[[Pistol]] and Nim draw their swords |
| *(didascalia)* | BARDOLPH drawing his [[Sword|sword]] By this [[Sword|sword]], he that makes the first thrust, I’ll kill him. By this [[Sword|sword]], I will. |
| [[Pistol|PISTOL]] | [[Sword]] is an oath, and oaths must have their course. |
| *(didascalia)* | He sheathes his [[Sword|sword]] |
| BARDOLPH | Corporal Nim, an thou wilt be friends, be friends. An thou wilt not, why then be enemies with me too. Prithee, put up. |
| NIM | I shall have my eight shillings? |
| [[Pistol|PISTOL]] | A noble shalt thou have, and present pay,<br>And liquor likewise will I give to thee,<br>And [[Friendship|friendship]] shall combine, and brotherhood.<br>I’ll live by Nim, and Nim shall live by me.<br>Is not this just? For I shall sutler be<br>Unto the camp, and profits will accrue.<br>Give me thy hand. |
| NIM | I shall have my noble? |
| [[Pistol|PISTOL]] | In cash, most justly paid. |
| *(didascalia)* | NIM [[Well]] then, that’s the [[Humour|humour]] of’t. Nim and Bardolph sheathe their swords. Enter Hostess Quickly |
| *(didascalia)* | HOSTESS As ever you come of women, come in quickly to Sir John. Ah, poor heart, he is so shaked of a burning quotidian-tertian, that it is most lamentable to behold. Sweet men, come to him. Exit |
| NIM | The [[King]] hath run bad humours on the knight, that’s the even of it. |
| [[Pistol|PISTOL]] | Nim, thou hast spoke the right. His heart is fracted and corroborate. |
| NIM | The [[King]] is a good [[King|king]], but it must be as it may. He passes some humours and careers. |
| [[Pistol|PISTOL]] | Let us condole the knight—for, lambkins, we will live. Exeunt |
