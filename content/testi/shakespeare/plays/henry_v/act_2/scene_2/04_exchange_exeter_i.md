| EXETER | I arrest thee of high treason, by the name of [[Richard]], Earl of Cambridge.—I arrest thee of high treason, by the name of Henry, Lord Scrope of Masham.—I arrest thee of high treason, by the name of Thomas Grey, knight, of Northumberland. |
| SCROPE | Our purposes God justly hath discovered, And I repent my fault more than my [[Death|death]], Which I beseech your highness to forgive Although my [[Body|body]] pay the price of it. |
| CAMBRIDGE | For me, the gold of France did not seduce, Although I did admit it as a motive The sooner to effect what I intended. But God be thankèd for prevention, Which heartily in sufferance will rejoice, Beseeching God and you to pardon me. |
| GREY | Never did faithful subject more rejoice At the discovery of most dangerous treason Than I do at this hour joy o’er myself, Prevented from a damned enterprise. My fault, but not my [[Body|body]], pardon, sovereign. |
