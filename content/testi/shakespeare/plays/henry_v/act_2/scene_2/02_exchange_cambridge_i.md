| CAMBRIDGE | I one, my lord. Your highness bade me ask for it today. |
| SCROPE | So did you me, my liege. |
| GREY | And I, my royal sovereign. |
| [[King|KING]] HARRY | Then [[Richard]], Earl of Cambridge, there is yours; There yours, Lord Scrope of Masham, and sir knight, Grey of Northumberland, this same is yours. Read them, and know I know your worthiness.— My lord of Westmorland, and Uncle Exeter, We will aboard tonight.—Why, how now, gentlemen? What see you in those papers, that you lose So much complexion?—Look ye how they change: Their cheeks are paper.—Why, what read you there That have so cowarded and chased your [[Blood|blood]] Out of appearance? |
| CAMBRIDGE | I do confess my fault, And do submit me to your highness’ [[Mercy|mercy]]. |
