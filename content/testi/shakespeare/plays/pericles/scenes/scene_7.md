| *(didascalia)* | [[Simonides]], [[Thaisa]] and their train at one door, and at another door a Marshal conducting [[Pericles]] and the other knights from tilting |
| [[King|KING]] [[Simonides|SIMONIDES]] | (to the knights)<br>To say you’re welcome were superfluous.<br>To place upon the volume of your deeds<br>As in a title page your worth in arms<br>Were more than you expect, or more than’s fit,<br>Since every worth in show commends itself.<br>Prepare for mirth, for mirth becomes a feast.<br>You’re princes, and my guests. |
| [[Thaisa|THAISA]] | (to [[Pericles]]) But you, my knight and guest; To whom this wreath of victory I give, And [[Crown|crown]] you [[King|king]] of this day’s happiness. |
| [[Pericles|PERICLES]] | ’Tis more by fortune, lady, than my merit. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Simonides|SIMONIDES]] | Call it by what you will, the day is yours,<br>And here I [[Hope|hope]] is none that envies it.<br>In framing artists art hath thus decreed,<br>To make some good, but others to exceed.<br>You are her laboured scholar. (To [[Thaisa]]) Come,<br>[[Queen|queen]] o’th’ feast—<br>For, daughter, so you are—here take your place.<br>(To Marshal) Marshal the rest as they deserve their<br>grace. |
| KNIGHTS | We are honoured much by good [[Simonides]]. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Simonides|SIMONIDES]] | Your presence glads our days; [[Honour|honour]] we [[Love|love]],<br>For who hates [[Honour|honour]] hates the gods above. |
| MARSHAL | (to [[Pericles]])<br>Sir, yonder is your place. |
| [[Pericles|PERICLES]] | Some other is more fit. |
| FIRST KNIGHT | Contend not, sir, for we are gentlemen<br>Have neither in our hearts nor outward eyes<br>Envied the great, nor shall the low despise. |
| [[Pericles|PERICLES]] | You are right courteous knights. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Simonides|SIMONIDES]] | Sit, Sir, sit. |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Pericles]] sits directly over against the [[King]] and [[Thaisa]]. The guests feed apace. [[Pericles]] sits still and eats nothing |
| *(didascalia)* | Aside By Jove I [[Wonder|wonder]], that is [[King|king]] of thoughts, These cates distaste me, he but thought upon. |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Thaisa|THAISA]] aside |
| [[King|KING]] [[Simonides|SIMONIDES]] | By Juno, that is [[Queen|queen]] of [[Marriage|marriage]],<br>I am amazed all viands that I eat<br>Do seem unsavoury, wishing him my meat. |
| *(didascalia)* | To the [[King]] Sure he’s a gallant gentleman. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Simonides|SIMONIDES]] | He’s but a country gentleman.<br>He’s done no more than other knights have done.<br>He’s broke a staff or so, so let it pass. |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Thaisa|THAISA]] aside |
| [[King|KING]] [[Simonides|SIMONIDES]] | To me he seems like diamond to glass. |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Pericles|PERICLES]] aside |
| [[King|KING]] [[Simonides|SIMONIDES]] | Yon [[King|king]]’s to me like to my father’s picture,<br>Which tells me in what glory once he was—<br>Had princes sit like [[Stars|stars]] about his throne,<br>And he the sun for them to reverence.<br>None that beheld him but like lesser lights<br>Did vail their crowns to his supremacy;<br>Where now his son’s a glow-worm in the [[Night|night]],<br>The which hath [[Fire|fire]] in darkness, none in light;<br>Whereby I see that [[Time|time]]’s the [[King|king]] of men;<br>He’s both their parent and he is their [[Grave|grave]],<br>And gives them what he will, not what they crave. |
| *(didascalia)* | [[King|KING]] [[Simonides|SIMONIDES]] What, are you merry, knights? THE OTHER KNIGHTS |
| [[King|KING]] [[Simonides|SIMONIDES]] | Who can be other in this royal presence? |
| [[King|KING]] [[Simonides|SIMONIDES]] | Here with a cup that’s stored unto the brim,<br>As you do [[Love|love]], full to your mistress’ lips,<br>We drink this health to you. |
| *(didascalia)* | THE OTHER KNIGHTS We thank your grace. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Simonides|SIMONIDES]] | Yet pause a while. Yon knight doth sit too<br>[[Melancholy|melancholy]],<br>As if the entertainment in our [[Court|court]]<br>Had not a show might countervail his worth.<br>Note it not you, [[Thaisa]]? |
| [[Thaisa|THAISA]] | What is’t to me, my father? |
| [[King|KING]] [[Simonides|SIMONIDES]] | O, attend, my daughter. Princes in this<br>Should live like gods above, who freely give<br>To everyone that come to [[Honour|honour]] them.<br>And princes not so doing are like gnats<br>Which make a sound but, killed, are wondered at.<br>Therefore to make his entertain more sweet,<br>Here bear this standing-bowl of wine to him. |
| [[Thaisa|THAISA]] | Alas, my father, it befits not me<br>Unto a stranger knight to be so bold.<br>He may my proffer take for an offence,<br>Since men take women’s gifts for impudence. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Simonides|SIMONIDES]] | How? Do as I bid you, or you’ll move me else. |
| [[Thaisa|THAISA]] | (aside)<br>Now, by the gods, he could not please me better. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Simonides|SIMONIDES]] | Furthermore, tell him we [[Desire|desire]] to know<br>Of whence he is, his name and parentage. |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Thaisa]] bears the cup to [[Pericles]] |
| [[Thaisa|THAISA]] | The [[King]] my father, sir, has drunk to you,<br>Wishing it so much [[Blood|blood]] unto your life. |
| [[Pericles|PERICLES]] | I thank both him and you, and pledge him freely.<br>He pledges the [[King]] |
| [[Thaisa|THAISA]] | And further he desires to know of you<br>Of whence you are, your name and parentage. |
| [[Pericles|PERICLES]] | A gentleman of Tyre, my name [[Pericles]],<br>My [[Education|education]] been in arts and arms,<br>Who, looking for adventures in the world,<br>Was by the rough unconstant seas bereft<br>Unfortunately both of ships and men,<br>And after shipwreck driven upon this shore. |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Thaisa]] returns to the [[King]] |
| [[Thaisa|THAISA]] | He thanks your grace, names himself [[Pericles]],<br>A gentleman of Tyre, who, seeking adventures,<br>Was solely by misfortune of the seas<br>Bereft of ships and men, cast on this shore. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Simonides|SIMONIDES]] | Now by the gods I pity his mishaps,<br>And will awake him from his [[Melancholy|melancholy]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Simonides]], rising from his state, goes forthwith and embraces [[Pericles]] |
| [[King|KING]] [[Simonides|SIMONIDES]] | Be cheered, for what misfortune hath impaired you of,<br>Fortune by my help can repair to you.<br>My [[Self|self]] and country both shall be your friends,<br>And presently a goodly milk-white steed<br>And golden spurs I first bestow upon you,<br>The prizes due your merit, and ordained<br>For this day’s enterprise. |
| [[Pericles|PERICLES]] | Your kingly courtesy I thankfully accept. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Simonides|SIMONIDES]] | Come, gentlemen, we sit too long on trifles,<br>And waste the [[Time|time]] which looks for other revels.<br>Ev’n in your armours, as you are addressed,<br>Your limbs will [[Well|well]] become a soldier’s [[Dance|dance]].<br>I will not have excuse with saying this,<br>‘Loud music is too harsh for ladies’ heads’,<br>Since they [[Love|love]] men in arms as [[Well|well]] as beds.<br>The knights [[Dance|dance]]<br>So this was [[Well|well]] asked, ’twas so [[Well|well]] performed.<br>Come, here’s a lady that wants breathing too.<br>(To [[Pericles]]) And I have heard, sir, that the knights of<br>Tyre<br>Are excellent in making ladies trip,<br>And that their measures are as excellent. |
| [[Pericles|PERICLES]] | In those that practise them they are, my lord. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Simonides|SIMONIDES]] | O, that’s as much as you would be denied<br>Of your fair courtesy. Unclasp, unclasp.<br>They [[Dance|dance]]<br>Thanks, gentlemen, to all. All have done [[Well|well]],<br>(To [[Pericles]]) But you the best.—Lights, pages, to<br>conduct<br>These knights unto their sev’ral lodgings.—Yours, sir,<br>We have giv’n order should be next our own. |
| [[Pericles|PERICLES]] | I am at your grace’s pleasure. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Simonides|SIMONIDES]] | Princes, it is too late to talk of [[Love|love]],<br>And that’s the mark I know you level at.<br>Therefore each one betake him to his rest;<br>Tomorrow all for speeding do their best. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt severally |
