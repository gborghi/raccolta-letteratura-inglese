| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[King]] [[Simonides]] at one door reading of a letter, the Knights enter at another door and meet him |
| FIRST KNIGHT | Good morrow to the good [[Simonides]]. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Simonides|SIMONIDES]] | Knights, from my daughter this I let you know:<br>That for this twelvemonth she’ll not undertake<br>A married life. Her reason to herself<br>Is only known, which from her none can get. |
| SECOND KNIGHT | May we not have access to her, my lord? |
| [[King|KING]] [[Simonides|SIMONIDES]] | [[Faith]], by no means. It is impossible,<br>She hath so strictly tied her to her chamber.<br>One twelve moons more she’ll wear [[Diana]]’s liv’ry.<br>This by the eye of [[Cynthia]] hath she vowed,<br>And on her virgin [[Honour|honour]] will not break it. |
| THIRD KNIGHT | Loath to bid farewell, we take our [[Leaves|leaves]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt Knights |
| [[King|KING]] [[Simonides|SIMONIDES]] | So, they are [[Well|well]] dispatched. Now to my daughter’s<br>letter.<br>She tells me here she’ll wed the stranger knight,<br>Or never more to view nor day nor light.<br>I like that [[Well|well]]. Nay, how absolute she’s in‘t,<br>Not minding whether I dislike or no!<br>Mistress, ’tis [[Well|well]], I do commend your choice,<br>And will no longer have it be delayed. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Pericles]] |
| [[King|KING]] [[Simonides|SIMONIDES]] | Soft, here he comes. I must dissemble that<br>In show, I have determined on in heart. |
| [[Pericles|PERICLES]] | All fortune to the good [[Simonides]]. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Simonides|SIMONIDES]] | To you as much, sir. I am beholden to you<br>For your sweet music this last [[Night|night]]. My ears,<br>I do protest, were never better fed<br>With such delightful pleasing harmony. |
| [[Pericles|PERICLES]] | It is your grace’s pleasure to commend,<br>Not my [[Desert|desert]]. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Simonides|SIMONIDES]] | Sir, you are music’s master. |
| [[Pericles|PERICLES]] | The worst of all her scholars, my good lord. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Simonides|SIMONIDES]] | Let me ask you one thing. What think you of my daughter? |
| [[Pericles|PERICLES]] | A most virtuous princess. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Simonides|SIMONIDES]] | And fair, too, is she not? |
| [[Pericles|PERICLES]] | As a fair day in [[Summer|summer]]; wondrous fair. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Simonides|SIMONIDES]] | My daughter, sir, thinks very [[Well|well]] of you;<br>So [[Well|well]] indeed that you must be her master<br>And she will be your scholar; therefore look to it. |
| [[Pericles|PERICLES]] | I am unworthy for her schoolmaster. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Simonides|SIMONIDES]] | She thinks not so. Peruse this writing else.<br>He gives the letter to [[Pericles]], who reads |
| [[Pericles|PERICLES]] | (aside)<br>What’s here?—a letter that she loves the knight of Tyre?<br>’Tis the [[King]]’s subtlety to have my life. |
| *(didascalia)* | He prostrates himself at the [[King]]’s feet |
| [[Pericles|PERICLES]] | O, seek not to entrap me, gracious lord,<br>A stranger and distressed gentleman<br>That never aimed so high to [[Love|love]] your daughter,<br>But bent all offices to [[Honour|honour]] her.<br>Never did thought of mine levy offence,<br>Nor never did my actions yet commence<br>A deed might gain her [[Love|love]] or your displeasure. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Simonides|SIMONIDES]] | Thou liest like a traitor. |
| [[Pericles|PERICLES]] | Traitor? |
| [[King|KING]] [[Simonides|SIMONIDES]] | Ay, traitor,<br>That thus disguised art stol’n into my [[Court|court]]<br>With witchcraft of thy actions to bewitch<br>The yielding spirit of my tender [[Child|child]]. 50 |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Pericles|PERICLES]] rising |
| [[King|KING]] [[Simonides|SIMONIDES]] | Who calls me traitor, unless it be the [[King]],<br>Ev’n in his bosom I will write the lie. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Simonides|SIMONIDES]] | (aside)<br>Now, by the gods, I do applaud his [[Courage|courage]]. |
| [[Pericles|PERICLES]] | My actions are as noble as my [[Blood|blood]],<br>That never relished of a base descent. 55<br>I came unto your [[Court|court]] in search of [[Honour|honour]],<br>And not to be a [[Rebel|rebel]] to your state;<br>And he that otherwise accounts of me,<br>This [[Sword|sword]] shall prove he’s [[Honour|honour]]’s enemy. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Simonides|SIMONIDES]] | I shall prove otherwise, since both your practice<br>And her consent therein is evident<br>There, by my daughter’s hand, as she can witness. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Thaisa]] |
| [[Pericles|PERICLES]] | (to [[Thaisa]])<br>Then as you are as virtuous as fair,<br>By what you [[Hope|hope]] of heaven or [[Desire|desire]]<br>By your best wishes here i‘th’ world fulfilled,<br>Resolve your angry father if my tongue<br>Did e’er solicit, or my hand subscribe<br>To any syllable made [[Love|love]] to you. |
| [[Thaisa|THAISA]] | Why, sir, say if you had,<br>Who takes offence at that would make me glad? |
| [[King|KING]] [[Simonides|SIMONIDES]] | How, minion, are you so peremptory?<br>(Aside) I am glad on’t.—Is this a fit match for you?<br>A straggling [[Theseus]], born we know not where,<br>One that hath neither [[Blood|blood]] nor merit<br>For thee to [[Hope|hope]] for, or himself to challenge<br>Of thy perfections e’en the least allowance. |
| [[Thaisa|THAISA]] | (kneeling)<br>Suppose his birth were base, when that his life<br>Shows that he is not so, yet he hath virtue,<br>The very ground of all nobility,<br>Enough to make him noble. I entreat you<br>To remember that I am in [[Love|love]],<br>The power of which [[Love|love]] cannot be confined<br>By th’ power of your will. Most royal father,<br>What with my pen I have in secret written<br>With my tongue now I openly confirm,<br>Which is I have no life but in his [[Love|love]],<br>Nor any being but in joying of his worth. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Simonides|SIMONIDES]] | Equals to equals, good to good is joined.<br>This not being so, the bavin of your mind<br>In rashness kindled must again be quenched,<br>Or purchase our displeasure.—And for you, sir,<br>First learn to know I banish you my [[Court|court]],<br>And yet I scorn our rage should stoop so low.<br>For your [[Ambition|ambition]], sir, I’ll have your life. |
| [[Thaisa|THAISA]] | (to [[Pericles]])<br>For every drop of [[Blood|blood]] he sheds of yours<br>He’ll draw another from his only [[Child|child]]. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Simonides|SIMONIDES]] | I’ll tame you, yea, I’ll bring you in subjection.<br>Will you not having my consent<br>Bestow your [[Love|love]] and your affections<br>Upon a stranger?—(aside) who for aught I know<br>May be, nor can I think the contrary,<br>As great in [[Blood|blood]] as I myself. |
| *(didascalia)* | He catches [[Thaisa]] rashly by the hand |
| [[King|KING]] [[Simonides|SIMONIDES]] | Therefore hear you, mistress: either frame your will to<br>mine— |
| *(didascalia)* | He catches [[Pericles]] rashly by the hand |
| [[King|KING]] [[Simonides|SIMONIDES]] | And you, sir, hear you: either be ruled by me—<br>Or I shall make you |
| *(didascalia)* | He claps their hands together man and wife. 105 |
| [[King|KING]] [[Simonides|SIMONIDES]] | Nay, come, your hands and lips must seal it too,<br>[[Pericles]] and [[Thaisa]] kiss<br>And being joined, I’ll thus your hopes destroy, |
| *(didascalia)* | He parts them |
| [[King|KING]] [[Simonides|SIMONIDES]] | And for your further grief, God give you joy. What, are you pleased? |
| [[Thaisa|THAISA]] | Yes, (to [[Pericles]]) if you [[Love|love]] me, sir. |
| [[Pericles|PERICLES]] | Ev’n as my life my [[Blood|blood]] that fosters it. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Simonides|SIMONIDES]] | What, are you both agreed? |
| [[Pericles|PERICLES]] | and [[Thaisa|THAISA]] Yes, if’t please your majesty. |
| [[King|KING]] [[Simonides|SIMONIDES]] | It pleaseth me so [[Well|well]] that I will see you wed,<br>Then with what haste you can, get you to bed. Exeunt |
