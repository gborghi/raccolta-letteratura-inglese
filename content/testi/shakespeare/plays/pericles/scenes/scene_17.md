| *(didascalia)* | Why, are you foolish? Can it be undone? |
| [[Cleon|CLEON]] | O [[Dionyza]], such a piece of slaughter<br>The sun and [[Moon|moon]] ne’er looked upon. |
| [[Dionyza|DIONYZA]] | I think you’ll turn a [[Child|child]] again. |
| [[Cleon|CLEON]] | Were I chief lord of all this spacious world<br>I’d give it to undo the deed. A lady<br>Much less in [[Blood|blood]] than virtue, yet a princess<br>To equal any single [[Crown|crown]] o‘th’ earth<br>I’th’ justice of compare. O [[Villain|villain]] Leonine,<br>Whom thou hast poisoned too,<br>If thou hadst drunk to him ‘t’ad been a kindness<br>Becoming [[Well|well]] thy fact. What canst thou say<br>When noble [[Pericles]] demands his [[Child|child]]? |
| [[Dionyza|DIONYZA]] | That she is dead. Nurses are not the fates.<br>To foster is not ever to preserve.<br>She died at [[Night|night]]. I’ll say so. Who can [[Cross|cross]] it,<br>Unless you play the pious [[Innocent|innocent]]<br>And, for an honest attribute, cry out<br>‘She died by foul play.’ |
| [[Cleon|CLEON]] | O, go to. [[Well]], [[Well|well]],<br>Of all the faults beneath the heav’ns the gods<br>Do like this worst. |
| [[Dionyza|DIONYZA]] | Be one of those that thinks<br>The petty wrens of Tarsus will fly hence<br>And open this to [[Pericles]]. I do shame<br>To think of what a noble strain you are,<br>And of how cowed a spirit. |
| [[Cleon|CLEON]] | To such proceeding<br>Whoever but his approbation added,<br>Though not his prime consent, he did not flow<br>From honourable sources. |
| [[Dionyza|DIONYZA]] | Be it so, then.<br>Yet none does know but you how she came dead,<br>Nor none can know, Leonine being gone.<br>She did distain my [[Child|child]], and stood between<br>Her and her fortunes. None would look on her,<br>But cast their gazes on [[Marina]]’s face<br>Whilst ours was blurted at, and held a malkin<br>Not worth the [[Time|time]] of day. It pierced me through,<br>And though you call my course unnatural,<br>You not your [[Child|child]] [[Well|well]] loving, yet I find<br>It greets me as an enterprise of kindness<br>Performed to your sole daughter. |
| [[Cleon|CLEON]] | Heavens forgive it. |
| [[Dionyza|DIONYZA]] | And as for [[Pericles]],<br>What should he say? We wept after her hearse,<br>And yet we mourn. Her monument<br>Is almost finished, and her epitaphs<br>In glitt‘ring golden characters express<br>A gen’ral praise to her and care in us,<br>At whose expense ’tis done. |
| [[Cleon|CLEON]] | Thou art like the harpy, Which, to betray, dost, with thine angel face, Seize in thine eagle talons. |
| [[Dionyza|DIONYZA]] | Ye’re like one that superstitiously<br>Do swear to th’ gods that [[Winter|winter]] kills the flies,<br>But yet I know you’ll do as I advise. Exeunt |
