| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Gower]] |
| [[Gower|GOWER]] | Imagine [[Pericles]] arrived at Tyre,<br>Welcomed and settled to his own [[Desire|desire]].<br>His woeful [[Queen|queen]] we leave at Ephesus,<br>Unto [[Diana]] there ’s a votaress.<br>Now to [[Marina]] bend your mind,<br>Whom our fast-growing scene must find<br>At Tarsus, and by [[Cleon]] trained<br>In music, letters; who hath gained<br>Of [[Education|education]] all the grace,<br>Which makes her both the heart and place<br>Of gen‘ral [[Wonder|wonder]]. But, alack,<br>That [[Monster|monster]] envy, oft the wrack<br>Of earned praise, [[Marina]]’s life<br>Seeks to take off by treason’s knife,<br>And in this kind our [[Cleon]] has<br>One daughter, and a full-grown lass<br>E’en ripe for [[Marriage|marriage]]-rite. This maid<br>Hight Philoten, and it is said<br>For certain in our story she<br>Would ever with [[Marina]] be,<br>Be’t when they weaved the sleided silk<br>With fingers long, small, white as milk;<br>Or when she would with sharp nee‘le wound<br>The cambric which she made more sound<br>By hurting it, or when to th’ lute<br>She sung, and made the [[Night|night]] bird mute,<br>That still records with moan; or when<br>She would with rich and constant pen<br>Vail to her mistress Dian. Still<br>This Philoten contends in skill<br>With absolute [[Marina]]; so<br>With dove of Paphos might the crow<br>Vie feathers white. [[Marina]] gets<br>All praises which are paid as debts,<br>And not as given. This so darks<br>In Philoten all graceful marks<br>That [[Cleon]]’s wife with envy rare<br>A present murder does prepare<br>For good [[Marina]], that her daughter<br>Might stand peerless by this slaughter.<br>The sooner her vile thoughts to stead<br>Lychorida, our [[Nurse|nurse]], is dead, |
| *(didascalia)* | A tomb is revealed |
| [[Gower|GOWER]] | And cursed [[Dionyza]] hath<br>The pregnant instrument of wrath<br>Pressed for this blow. Th’[[unborn]] event<br>I do commend to your content,<br>Only I carry winged [[Time]]<br>Post on the lame feet of my rhyme,<br>Which never could I so convey<br>Unless your thoughts went on my way. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Dionyza]] with Leonine |
| [[Gower|GOWER]] | [[Dionyza]] does appear,<br>With Leonine, a murderer. Exit |
| [[Dionyza|DIONYZA]] | Thy oath remember. Thou hast sworn to do’t.<br>‘Tis but a blow, which never shall be known.<br>Thou canst not do a thing i’th’ world so soon<br>To yield thee so much profit. Let not [[Conscience|conscience]],<br>Which is but cold, or fanning [[Love|love]] thy bosom<br>Unflame too nicely, nor let pity, which<br>E’en women have cast off, melt thee; but be<br>A soldier to thy purpose. |
| LEONINE | I will do’t;<br>But yet she is a goodly creature. |
| [[Dionyza|DIONYZA]] | The fitter then the gods should have her. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Marina]] to the tomb with a basket of [[Flowers|flowers]] |
| [[Dionyza|DIONYZA]] | Here she comes, weeping her only [[Nurse|nurse]]’s [[Death|death]].<br>Thou art resolved. |
| LEONINE | I am resolved. |
| [[Marina|MARINA]] | No, I will rob Tellus of her weed<br>To strew thy [[Grave|grave]] with flow’rs. The yellows, blues,<br>The purple violets and marigolds<br>Shall as a carpet hang upon thy tomb<br>While [[Summer|summer]] days doth last. Ay me, poor maid,<br>Born in a tempest when my mother died,<br>This world to me is but a ceaseless [[Storm|storm]]<br>Whirring me from my friends. |
| [[Dionyza|DIONYZA]] | How now, [[Marina]], why do you keep [[Alone|alone]]?<br>How chance my daughter is not with you?<br>Do not consume your [[Blood|blood]] with sorrowing.<br>Have you a [[Nurse|nurse]] of me. Lord, how your favour<br>Is changed with this unprofitable woe!<br>Give me your [[Flowers|flowers]]. Come, o’er the sea margin<br>Walk with Leonine. The air is piercing there,<br>And quick; it sharps the stomach. Come, Leonine,<br>Take her by th’ arm. Walk with her. |
| [[Marina|MARINA]] | No, I pray you,<br>I’ll not bereave you of your servant. |
| [[Dionyza|DIONYZA]] | Come, come,<br>I [[Love|love]] the [[King]] your father and yourself<br>With more than foreign heart. We ev’ry day<br>Expect him here. When he shall come and find<br>Our paragon to all reports thus blasted,<br>He will repent the breadth of his great voyage,<br>Blame both my lord and me, that we have taken<br>No care to your best courses. Go, I pray you,<br>Walk and be cheerful once again; resume<br>That excellent complexion which did steal<br>The eyes of young and old. Care not for me.<br>I can go [[Home|home]] [[Alone|alone]]. |
| [[Marina|MARINA]] | [[Well]], I will go,<br>But truly I have no [[Desire|desire]] to it. |
| [[Dionyza|DIONYZA]] | Nay, I know ’tis good for you. Walk half an hour,<br>Leonine, at the least; remember<br>What I have said. |
| LEONINE | I warr’nt you, madam. |
| [[Dionyza|DIONYZA]] | (to [[Marina]])<br>I’ll leave you, my sweet lady, for a while.<br>Pray you walk softly, do not heat your [[Blood|blood]].<br>What, I must have care of you! |
| [[Marina|MARINA]] | My thanks, sweet madam. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit [[Dionyza]] |
| [[Marina|MARINA]] | Is this [[Wind|wind]] westerly that blows? |
| LEONINE | South-west. |
| [[Marina|MARINA]] | When I was born the [[Wind|wind]] was north. |
| LEONINE | Was’t SO? [[Marina|MARINA]]<br>My father, as [[Nurse|nurse]] says, did never [[Fear|fear]],<br>But cried ’Good seamen’ to the mariners,<br>Galling his kingly hands with haling ropes,<br>And, clasping to the mast, endured a sea<br>That almost burst the deck. |
| LEONINE | When was this? |
| [[Marina|MARINA]] | When I was born.<br>Never was waves nor [[Wind|wind]] more violent.<br>Once from the ladder tackle washes off<br>A canvas-climber. ‘Ha!’ says one, ‘wolt out?’<br>And with a dropping industry they skip<br>From stem to stern. The boatswain whistles, and<br>The master calls and trebles their confusion. |
| LEONINE | Come, say your prayers. |
| [[Marina|MARINA]] | What mean you? |
| LEONINE | If you require a little space for [[Prayer|prayer]]<br>I grant it. Pray, but be not tedious.<br>The gods are quick of ear, and I am sworn<br>To do my work with haste. |
| [[Marina|MARINA]] | Why would you kill me? |
| LEONINE | To satisfy my lady. |
| [[Marina|MARINA]] | Why would she have me killed? Now, as I can remember, by my troth<br>I never did her hurt in all my life.<br>I never spake bad word, nor did ill turn<br>To any living creature. Believe me, la.<br>I never killed a mouse nor hurt a fly.<br>I trod once on a worm against my will,<br>But I wept for it. How have I offended<br>Wherein my [[Death|death]] might yield her any profit<br>Or my life imply her danger? |
| LEONINE | My commission<br>Is not to reason of the deed, but do’t. |
| [[Marina|MARINA]] | You will not do’t for all the world, I [[Hope|hope]].<br>You are [[Well|well]] favoured, and your looks foreshow<br>You have a gentle heart. I saw you lately<br>When you caught hurt in parting two that fought.<br>Good sooth, it showed [[Well|well]] in you. Do so now.<br>Your lady seeks my life. Come you between,<br>And save poor me, the weaker. |
| *(didascalia)* | LEONINE drawing out his [[Sword|sword]] I am sworn, |
| [[Marina|MARINA]] | And will dispatch. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Pirates running |
| FIRST PIRATE | Hold, [[Villain|villain]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | Leonine runs away and hides behind the tomb |
| SECOND PIRATE | A prize, a prize. |
| THIRD PIRATE | Half-part, mates, half-part. Come, let’s have her aboard suddenly. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt Pirates carrying [[Marina]] |
| *(didascalia)* | Leonine steals back |
| LEONINE | These roguing thieves serve the great pirate Valdes.<br>An they have seized [[Marina]], let her go.<br>There’s no [[Hope|hope]] she’ll return. I’ll swear she’s dead<br>And thrown into the sea; but I’ll see further.<br>Perhaps they will but please themselves upon her,<br>Not carry her aboard. If she remain,<br>Whom they have ravished must by me be slain. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit. The tomb is concealed |
