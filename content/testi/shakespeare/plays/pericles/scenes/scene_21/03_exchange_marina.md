| [[Marina|MARINA]] | So indeed I did. |
| [[Pericles|PERICLES]] | Report thy parentage. I think thou said‘st Thou hadst been tossed from wrong to injury, And that thou thought’st thy griefs might equal mine, If both were opened. |
| [[Marina|MARINA]] | Some such thing I said, And said no more but what my circumstance Did warrant me was likely. |
| [[Pericles|PERICLES]] | Tell thy story. If thine considered prove the thousandth part Of my [[Endurance|endurance]], thou art a man, and I Have suffered like a girl. Yet thou dost look Like patience gazing on kings’ graves, and smiling Extremity out of act. What were thy friends? How lost thou them? Thy name, my most kind virgin? Recount, I do beseech thee. Come, sit by me. She sits |
| [[Marina|MARINA]] | My name, sir, is [[Marina]]. |
| [[Pericles|PERICLES]] | O, I am mocked, And thou by some incensed god sent hither To make the world to laugh at me. |
| [[Marina|MARINA]] | Patience, good sir, Or here I’ll cease. |
| [[Pericles|PERICLES]] | Nay, I’ll be patient. Thou little know’st how thou dost startle me To call thyself [[Marina]]. |
| [[Marina|MARINA]] | The name Was given me by one that had some power: My father, and a [[King|king]]. |
| [[Pericles|PERICLES]] | How, a [[King|king]]’s daughter, And called [[Marina]]? |
| [[Marina|MARINA]] | You said you would believe me, But not to be a troubler of your peace I will end here. |
| [[Pericles|PERICLES]] | But are you flesh and [[Blood|blood]]? Have you a working pulse and are no fairy? Motion as [[Well|well]]? Speak on. Where were you born, And wherefore called [[Marina]]? |
| [[Marina|MARINA]] | Called [[Marina]] For I was born at sea. |
| [[Pericles|PERICLES]] | At sea? What mother? |
| [[Marina|MARINA]] | My mother was the daughter of a [[King|king]], Who died when I was born, as my good [[Nurse|nurse]] Lychorida hath oft recounted weeping. |
| [[Pericles|PERICLES]] | O, stop there a little! Aside This is the rarest dream That e’er dulled [[Sleep|sleep]] did mock sad fools withal. This cannot be my daughter, buried. [[Well]]. (To [[Marina]]) Where were you bred? I’ll hear you more to th’ [[Bottom|bottom]] Of your story, and never interrupt you. |
| [[Marina|MARINA]] | You will scarce believe me. ‘Twere best I did give o’er. |
| [[Pericles|PERICLES]] | I will believe you by the syllable Of what you shall deliver. Yet give me leave. How came you in these parts? Where were you bred? |
| [[Marina|MARINA]] | The [[King]] my father did in Tarsus leave me, Till cruel [[Cleon]], with his wicked wife, Did seek to murder me, and wooed a [[Villain|villain]] To attempt the deed; who having drawn to do’t, A crew of pirates came and rescued me. To Mytilene they brought me. But, good sir, What will you of me? Why do you weep? It may be You think me an impostor. No, good [[Faith|faith]], I am the daughter to [[King]] [[Pericles]], If good [[King]] [[Pericles]] be. |
| [[Pericles|PERICLES]] | rising Ho, Helicanus! |
| HELICANUS | (coming forward) Calls my lord? |
| [[Pericles|PERICLES]] | Thou art a [[Grave|grave]] and noble counsellor, Most wise in gen’ral. Tell me if thou canst What this maid is, or what is like to be, That thus hath made me weep. |
| HELICANUS | I know not. But here’s the regent, sir, of Mytilene Speaks nobly of her. |
| LYSIMACHUS | She would never tell Her parentage. Being demanded that, She would sit still and weep. |
| [[Pericles|PERICLES]] | O Helicanus, strike me, honoured sir, Give me a gash, put me to present [[Pain|pain]], Lest this great sea of joys rushing upon me O‘erbear the shores of my [[Mortality|mortality]] And drown me with their sweetness! (To [[Marina]]) O, come hither, [[Marina]] stands Thou that begett’st him that did thee beget, Thou that wast born at sea, buried at Tarsus, And found at sea again!—O Helicanus, Down on thy knees, thank the holy gods as loud As thunder threatens us, this is [[Marina]]! (To [[Marina]]) What was thy mother’s name? Tell me but that, For [[Truth|truth]] can never be confirmed enough, Though doubts did ever [[Sleep|sleep]]. |
