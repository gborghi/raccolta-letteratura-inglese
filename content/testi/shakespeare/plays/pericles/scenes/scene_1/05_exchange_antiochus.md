| [[Antiochus|ANTIOCHUS]] | Prince [[Pericles]], touch not, upon thy life, For that’s an article within our law As dang’rous as the rest. Your [[Time|time]]’s expired. Either expound now, or receive your sentence. |
| [[Pericles|PERICLES]] | Great [[King]], Few [[Love|love]] to hear the sins they [[Love|love]] to act. ‘Twould braid yourself too near for me to tell it. Who has a book of all that monarchs do, He’s more secure to keep it shut than shown, For vice repeated, like the wand’ring [[Wind|wind]], Blows [[Dust|dust]] in others’ eyes to spread itself; And yet the end of all is bought thus dear, The breath is gone, and the sore eyes see clear To stop the air would hurt them. The blind mole casts Copped hills towards heav’n to tell the earth is thronged By man’s oppression, and the poor worm doth die for’t. Kings are earth’s gods; in vice their law’s their will, And if Jove stray, who dares say Jove doth ill? It is enough you know, and it is fit, What being more known grows worse, to smother it. All [[Love|love]] the womb that their first being bred; Then give my tongue like leave to [[Love|love]] my head. |
| [[Antiochus|ANTIOCHUS]] | (aside) Heav’n, that I had thy head! He’s found the meaning. But I will gloze with him.—Young Prince of Tyre, Though by the tenor of our strict edict, Your exposition misinterpreting, We might proceed to cancel of your days, Yet [[Hope|hope]], succeeding from so fair a tree As your fair [[Self|self]], doth tune us otherwise. Forty days longer we do respite you, If by which [[Time|time]] our secret be undone, This [[Mercy|mercy]] shows we’ll joy in such a son. And until then your entertain shall be As doth befit your worth and our degree. Flourish.Exeunt all but [[Pericles]] |
