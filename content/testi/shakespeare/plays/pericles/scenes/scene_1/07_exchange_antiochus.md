| [[Antiochus|ANTIOCHUS]] | He hath found the meaning, for the which we mean To have his head. He must not live To trumpet forth my infamy, nor tell the world [[Antiochus]] doth sin in such a loathèd manner, And therefore instantly this prince must die, For by his fall my [[Honour|honour]] must keep high. Who attends us there? Enter Thaliart |
| THALIART | Doth your highness call? |
| [[Antiochus|ANTIOCHUS]] | Thaliart, you are of our chamber, Thaliart, And to your secrecy our mind partakes Her private actions. For your faithfulness We will advance you, Thaliart. Behold, Here’s [[Poison|poison]], and here’s gold. We hate the Prince of Tyre, and thou must kill him. It fits thee not to ask the reason. Why? Because we bid it. Say, is it done? |
| THALIART | My lord, ’tis done. |
| [[Antiochus|ANTIOCHUS]] | Enough. Enter a Messenger hastily Let your breath cool yourself, telling your haste. |
| MESSENGER | Your majesty, Prince [[Pericles]] is fled. Exit |
| [[Antiochus|ANTIOCHUS]] | (to Thaliart) As thou wilt live, fly after; like an arrow Shot from a [[Well|well]]-experienced archer hits The mark his eye doth level at, so thou Never return unless it be to say ‘Your majesty, Prince [[Pericles]] is dead.’ |
| THALIART | If I can get him in my [[Pistol|pistol]]’s length I’ll make him sure enough. Farewell, your highness. |
| [[Antiochus|ANTIOCHUS]] | Thaliart, adieu. Exit Thaliart Till [[Pericles]] be dead My heart can lend no succour to my head. Exit. The heads are concealed |
