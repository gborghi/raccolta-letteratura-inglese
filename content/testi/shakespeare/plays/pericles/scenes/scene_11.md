| [[Pericles|PERICLES]] | The god of this great vast rebuke these surges<br>Which wash both heav’n and hell; and thou that hast<br>Upon the winds command, bind them in brass,<br>Having called them from the deep. O still<br>Thy deaf‘ning dreadful thunders, gently quench<br>Thy nimble sulph’rous flashes.—O, ho, Lychorida!<br>How does my [[Queen|queen]]?—Thou stormest venomously.<br>Wilt thou spit all thyself The seaman’s whistle<br>Is as a whisper in the ears of [[Death|death]],<br>Unheard.—Lychorida!—Lucina, O!<br>Divinest patroness, and midwife gentle<br>To those that cry by [[Night|night]], convey thy deity<br>Aboard our dancing boat, make swift the pangs<br>Of my [[Queen|queen]]’s travails!—Now, Lychorida. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Lychorida with an infant |
| LYCHORIDA | Here is a thing too young for such a place,<br>Who, if it had conceit, would die, as I<br>Am like to do. Take in your arms this piece<br>Of your dead [[Queen|queen]]. |
| [[Pericles|PERICLES]] | How, how, Lychorida? |
| LYCHORIDA | Patience, good sir, do not assist the [[Storm|storm]].<br>Here’s all that is left living of your [[Queen|queen]],<br>A little daughter. For the sake of it<br>Be manly, and take comfort. |
| [[Pericles|PERICLES]] | O you gods!<br>Why do you make us [[Love|love]] your goodly gifts,<br>And snatch them straight away? We here below<br>Recall not what we give, and therein may<br>Use [[Honour|honour]] with you. |
| LYCHORIDA | Patience, good sir,<br>E’en for this charge. |
| *(didascalia)* | She gives him the infant. [[Pericles]], looking mournfully upon it, shakes his head, and weeps |
| [[Pericles|PERICLES]] | Now mild may be thy life,<br>For a more blust‘rous birth had never babe;<br>Quiet and gentle thy conditions, for<br>Thou art the rudeliest welcome to this world<br>That e’er was prince’s [[Child|child]]; happy what follows.<br>Thou hast as chiding a nativity<br>As [[Fire|fire]], air, [[Water|water]], earth, and heav’n can make<br>To herald thee from th’ womb. Poor inch of [[Nature|nature]],<br>Ev’n at the first thy loss is more than can<br>Thy partage quit with all thou canst find here.<br>Now the good gods throw their best eyes upon’t. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter the Master and a Sailor |
| *(didascalia)* | MASTER What, [[Courage|courage]], sir! God save you. |
| [[Pericles|PERICLES]] | [[Courage]] enough, I do not [[Fear|fear]] the flaw;<br>It hath done to me its worst. Yet for the [[Love|love]]<br>Of this poor infant, this fresh new seafarer,<br>I would it would be quiet. |
| *(didascalia)* | MASTER (calling) Slack the bow-lines, there.—Thou wilt not, wilt thou? Blow, and split thyself. |
| SAILOR | But searoom, an the brine and cloudy billow kiss the [[Moon|moon]], I care not. |
| *(didascalia)* | MASTER (to [[Pericles]]) Sir, your [[Queen|queen]] must overboard. The sea works high, the [[Wind|wind]] is loud, and will not lie till the [[Ship|ship]] be cleared of the dead. |
| [[Pericles|PERICLES]] | That’s but your superstition. |
| *(didascalia)* | MASTER Pardon us, sir; with us at sea it hath been still observed, and we are strong in custom. Therefore briefly yield ’er, for she must overboard straight. |
| [[Pericles|PERICLES]] | As you think meet. Most wretched [[Queen|queen]]! |
| LYCHORIDA | Here she lies, sir. |
| *(didascalia)* | She draws the curtains and discovers the [[Body|body]] of [[Thaisa]] in a bed. [[Pericles]] gives Lychorida the infant |
| [[Pericles|PERICLES]] | (to [[Thaisa]])<br>A terrible childbed hast thou had, my dear,<br>No light, no [[Fire|fire]]. Th‘unfriendly elements<br>Forgot thee utterly, nor have I [[Time|time]]<br>To give thee hallowed to thy [[Grave|grave]], but straight<br>Must cast thee, scarcely coffined, in the ooze,<br>Where, for a monument upon thy bones<br>And aye-remaining lamps, the belching whale<br>And humming [[Water|water]] must o’erwhelm thy corpse,<br>Lying with simple shetts.—O Lychorida,<br>Bid Nestor bring me spices, ink, and paper,<br>My casket and my jewels, and bid Nicander<br>Bring me the satin coffer. Lay the babe<br>Upon the pillow. Hie thee whiles I say<br>A priestly farewell to her. Suddenly, woman. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit Lychorida |
| *(didascalia)* | SAILOR Sir, we have a chest beneath the hatches caulked and bitumed ready. |
| *(didascalia)* | I thank thee. To the Master Mariner, say, what coast is this? |
| *(didascalia)* | MASTER |
| [[Pericles|PERICLES]] | We are near Tarsus. |
| [[Pericles|PERICLES]] | Thither, gentle mariner, |
| *(didascalia)* | Alter thy course from Tyre. When canst thou reach it? MASTER |
| [[Pericles|PERICLES]] | By break of day, if the [[Wind|wind]] cease. |
| [[Pericles|PERICLES]] | Make for Tarsus.<br>There will I visit [[Cleon]], for the babe<br>Cannot hold out to Tyrus. There I’ll leave it<br>At careful nursing. Go thy ways, good mariner.<br>I’ll bring the [[Body|body]] presently. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit Master at one door and Sailor beneath the hatches. Exit [[Pericles]] to [[Thaisa]], closing the curtains |
