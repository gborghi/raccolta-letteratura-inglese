| *(didascalia)* | Alarum; excursions; retreat. Enter [[King John]], [[Queen]] [[Eleanor]], [[Arthur]] Duke of Brittaine, the Bastard, [[Hubert]], lords, with soldiers |
| [[King John|KING JOHN]] | (to [[Queen]] [[Eleanor]])<br>So shall it be; your grace shall stay behind<br>So strongly guarded. (To [[Arthur]]) Cousin, look not sad;<br>Thy grandam loves thee, and thy uncle will<br>As dear be to thee as thy father was. |
| [[Arthur|ARTHUR]] | O, this will make my mother die with grief. 5 |
| [[King John|KING JOHN]] | (to the Bastard)<br>Cousin, away for England! Haste before,<br>And ere our coming, see thou shake the bags<br>Of hoarding abbots. The fat ribs of peace<br>Must by the hungry now be fed upon.<br>Imprisoned angels set at liberty. 10<br>Use our commission in his utmost force. |
| BASTARD | Bell, book, and candle shall not drive me back<br>When gold and silver becks me to come on.<br>I leave your highness.—Grandam, I will pray,<br>If ever I remember to be holy,<br>For your fair safety. So I kiss your hand. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Eleanor|ELEANOR]] | Farewell, gentle cousin. |
| [[King John|KING JOHN]] | Coz, farewell. Exit the Bastard |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Eleanor|ELEANOR]] | Come hither, little kinsman. Hark, a word.<br>She takes [[Arthur]] aside |
| [[King John|KING JOHN]] | Come hither, [[Hubert]].<br>He takes [[Hubert]] aside<br>O my gentle [[Hubert]],<br>We owe thee much. Within this wall of flesh 20<br>There is a [[Soul|soul]] counts thee her creditor,<br>And with advantage means to pay thy [[Love|love]];<br>And, my good friend, thy voluntary oath<br>Lives in this bosom, dearly cherished.<br>Give me thy hand.<br>He takes [[Hubert]]’s hand<br>I had a thing to say, 25<br>But I will fit it with some better tune.<br>By heaven, [[Hubert]], I am almost ashamed<br>To say what good respect I have of thee. |
| [[Hubert|HUBERT]] | I am much bounden to your majesty. |
| [[King John|KING JOHN]] | Good friend, thou hast no cause to say so yet, 30<br>But thou shalt have; and creep [[Time|time]] ne‘er so slow,<br>Yet it shall come for me to do thee good.<br>I had a thing to say—but let it go.<br>The sun is in the heaven, and the proud day,<br>Attended with the pleasures of the world, 35<br>Is all too wanton and too full of gauds<br>To give me audience. If the midnight bell<br>Did with his iron tongue and brazen mouth |
| *(didascalia)* | Sound on into the drowsy race of [[Night|night]]; |
| [[King John|KING JOHN]] | If this same were a churchyard where we stand, 40<br>And thou possessed with a thousand wrongs;<br>Or if that surly spirit, [[Melancholy|melancholy]],<br>Had baked thy [[Blood|blood]] and made it heavy, thick,<br>Which else runs tickling up and down the veins,<br>Making that idiot, laughter, keep men’s eyes 45<br>And strain their cheeks to idle merriment—<br>A passion hateful to my purposes—<br>Or if that thou couldst see me without eyes,<br>Hear me without thine ears, and make reply<br>Without a tongue, using conceit [[Alone|alone]],<br>Without eyes, ears, and harmful sound of words;<br>Then in despite of broad-eyed watchful day<br>I would into thy bosom pour my thoughts.<br>But, ah, I will not. Yet I [[Love|love]] thee [[Well|well]],<br>And by my troth, I think thou lov’st me [[Well|well]]. |
| [[Hubert|HUBERT]] | So [[Well|well]] that what you bid me undertake,<br>Though that my [[Death|death]] were adjunct to my act,<br>By heaven, I would do it. |
| [[King John|KING JOHN]] | Do not I know thou wouldst?<br>Good [[Hubert]], [[Hubert]], [[Hubert]], throw thine eye<br>On yon young boy. I’ll tell thee what, my friend,<br>He is a very serpent in my way,<br>And wheresoe’er this foot of mine doth tread,<br>He lies before me. Dost thou understand me?<br>Thou art his keeper. |
| [[Hubert|HUBERT]] | And I’ll keep him so<br>That he shall not offend your majesty. 65 |
| [[King John|KING JOHN]] | [[Death]]. |
| [[Hubert|HUBERT]] | My lord. |
| [[King John|KING JOHN]] | A [[Grave|grave]]. |
| [[Hubert|HUBERT]] | He shall not live. |
| [[King John|KING JOHN]] | Enough.<br>I could be merry now. [[Hubert]], I [[Love|love]] thee.<br>[[Well]], I’ll not say what I intend for thee.<br>Remember. (To [[Queen]] [[Eleanor]]) Madam, fare you [[Well|well]].<br>I’ll send those powers o’er to your majesty. 70 |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] [[Eleanor|ELEANOR]] | My blessing go with thee. |
| [[King John|KING JOHN]] | (to [[Arthur]]) For England, cousin, go.<br>[[Hubert]] shall be your man, attend on you<br>With all true duty.—On toward Calais, ho! |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt [[Queen]] [[Eleanor]], attended, at one door, the rest at another door |
