| SALISBURY | (to the Bastard)<br>Sir [[Richard]], what think you? You have beheld.<br>Or have you read or heard; or could you think,<br>Or do you almost think, although you see,<br>That you do see? | PEMBROKE | All murders past do stand excused in this,<br>And this, so sole and so unmatchable,<br>Shall give a holiness, a purity,<br>To the yet-unbegotten sin of times,<br>And prove a deadly bloodshed but a jest, 55<br>Exampled by this heinous spectacle. |
