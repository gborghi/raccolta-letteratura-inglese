| *(didascalia)* | Enter from the Phoenix [[Adriana]] and [[Luciana]] |
| [[Adriana|ADRIANA]] | Ah, [[Luciana]], did he tempt thee so?<br>Mightst thou perceive austerely in his eye<br>That he did plead in earnest, yea or no?<br>Looked he or red or pale, or sad or merrily?<br>What observation mad’st thou in this case<br>Of his heart’s meteors tilting in his face? |
| [[Luciana|LUCIANA]] | First he denied you had in him no right. |
| [[Adriana|ADRIANA]] | He meant he did me none, the more my spite. |
| [[Luciana|LUCIANA]] | Then swore he that he was a stranger here. |
| [[Adriana|ADRIANA]] | And true he swore, though yet forsworn he were. |
| [[Luciana|LUCIANA]] | Then pleaded I for you. |
| [[Adriana|ADRIANA]] | And what said he? |
| [[Luciana|LUCIANA]] | That [[Love|love]] I begged for you, he begged of me. |
| [[Adriana|ADRIANA]] | With what [[Persuasion|persuasion]] did he tempt thy [[Love|love]]? |
| [[Luciana|LUCIANA]] | With words that in an honest suit might move.<br>First he did praise my [[Beauty|beauty]], then my speech. |
| [[Adriana|ADRIANA]] | Didst speak him fair? |
| [[Luciana|LUCIANA]] | Have patience, I beseech. |
| [[Adriana|ADRIANA]] | I cannot, nor I will not, hold me still.<br>My tongue, though not my heart, shall have his will.<br>He is deformed, crooked, old, and sere,<br>Ill-faced, worse-bodied, shapeless everywhere,<br>Vicious, ungentle, foolish, blunt, unkind,<br>Stigmatical in making, worse in mind. |
| [[Luciana|LUCIANA]] | Who would be jealous, then, of such a one?<br>No evil lost is wailed when it is gone. |
| [[Adriana|ADRIANA]] | Ah, but I think him better than I say,<br>And yet would herein others’ eyes were worse.<br>Far from her nest the lapwing cries away.<br>My heart prays for him, though my tongue do curse. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Dromio of Syracuse]] running |
| [[Dromio of Syracuse|DROMIO OF SYRACUSE]] | Here, go—the desk, the purse! Sweet now, make haste! |
| [[Luciana|LUCIANA]] | How? Hast thou lost thy breath? |
| DROMIO | or SYRACUSE By running fast. |
| [[Adriana|ADRIANA]] | Where is thy master, Dromio? Is he [[Well|well]]? |
| [[Dromio of Syracuse|DROMIO OF SYRACUSE]] | No, he’s in Tartar limbo, worse than hell.<br>A devil in an everlasting garment hath him,<br>One whose hard heart is buttoned up with steel;<br>A fiend, a fairy, pitiless and rough;<br>A wolf, nay worse, a fellow all in buff;<br>A back-friend, a shoulder-clapper, one that countermands<br>The passages of alleys, creeks, and narrow launds;<br>A hound that runs counter, and yet draws dryfoot [[Well|well]];<br>One that before the Judgement carries poor souls to hell. |
| [[Adriana|ADRIANA]] | Why, man, what is the matter? |
| [[Dromio of Syracuse|DROMIO OF SYRACUSE]] | I do not know the matter, he is ’rested on the case. |
| [[Adriana|ADRIANA]] | What, is he arrested? Tell me at whose suit. |
| [[Dromio of Syracuse|DROMIO OF SYRACUSE]] | I know not at whose suit he is arrested [[Well|well]],<br>But is in a suit of buff which ’rested him, that can I tell.<br>Will you send him, mistress, redemption—the money in his desk? |
| [[Adriana|ADRIANA]] | Go fetch it, sister. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit [[Luciana]] into the Phoenix |
| [[Adriana|ADRIANA]] | This I [[Wonder|wonder]] at,<br>That he unknown to me should be in debt.<br>Tell me, was he arrested on a bond? |
| [[Dromio of Syracuse|DROMIO OF SYRACUSE]] | Not on a bond but on a stronger thing:<br>A chain, a chain—do you not hear it ring? |
| [[Adriana|ADRIANA]] | What, the chain? |
| [[Dromio of Syracuse|DROMIO OF SYRACUSE]] | No, no, the bell. ‘Tis [[Time|time]] that I were gone:<br>It was two ere I left him, and now the [[Clock|clock]] strikes one. |
| [[Adriana|ADRIANA]] | The hours come back! That did I never hear. |
| [[Dromio of Syracuse|DROMIO OF SYRACUSE]] | O yes, if any hour meet a sergeant, a turns back for very [[Fear|fear]]. |
| [[Adriana|ADRIANA]] | As if [[Time|time]] were in debt. How fondly dost thou reason! |
| [[Dromio of Syracuse|DROMIO OF SYRACUSE]] | [[Time]] is a very bankrupt, and owes more than he’s worth to season.<br>Nay, he’s a thief too. Have you not heard men say<br>That [[Time|time]] comes stealing on by [[Night|night]] and day?<br>If a be in debt and theft, and a sergeant in the way,<br>Hath he not reason to turn back an hour in a day? |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Luciana]] from the Phoenix with the money |
| [[Adriana|ADRIANA]] | Go, Dromio, there’s the money. Bear it straight,<br>And bring thy master [[Home|home]] immediately. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit Dromiol |
| [[Adriana|ADRIANA]] | Come, sister, I am pressed down with conceit:<br>Conceit, my comfort and my injury. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt into the Phoenix |
