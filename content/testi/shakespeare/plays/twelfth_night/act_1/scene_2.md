| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Viola]], a Captain, and sailors |
| [[Viola|VIOLA]] | What country, friends, is this? |
| CAPTAIN | This is Illyria, lady. |
| [[Viola|VIOLA]] | And what should I do in Illyria?<br>My brother, he is in Elysium.<br>Perchance he is not drowned. What think you sailors? |
| CAPTAIN | It is perchance that you yourself were saved. |
| [[Viola|VIOLA]] | O my poor brother!—and so perchance may he be. |
| CAPTAIN | True, madam, and to comfort you with chance,<br>Assure yourself, after our [[Ship|ship]] did split,<br>When you and those poor number saved with you<br>Hung on our driving boat, I saw your brother,<br>Most provident in peril, bind himself—<br>[[Courage]] and [[Hope|hope]] both teaching him the practice—<br>To a strong mast that lived upon the sea,<br>Where, like Arion on the dolphin’s back,<br>I saw him hold acquaintance with the waves<br>So long as I could see. |
| [[Viola|VIOLA]] | (giving money)<br>For saying so, there’s gold.<br>Mine own escape unfoldeth to my [[Hope|hope]],<br>Whereto thy speech serves for authority,<br>The like of him. Know’st thou this country? |
| CAPTAIN | Ay, madam, [[Well|well]], for I was bred and born<br>Not three hours’ travel from this very place. |
| [[Viola|VIOLA]] | Who governs here? |
| CAPTAIN | A noble duke, in [[Nature|nature]]<br>As in name. |
| [[Viola|VIOLA]] | What is his name? |
| CAPTAIN | [[Orsino]]. |
| [[Viola|VIOLA]] | [[Orsino]]. I have heard my father name him.<br>He was a bachelor then. |
| CAPTAIN | And so is now, or was so very late,<br>For but a month ago I went from hence,<br>And then ’twas fresh in murmur—as, you know,<br>What great ones do the less will prattle of—<br>That he did seek the [[Love|love]] of fair [[Olivia]]. |
| [[Viola|VIOLA]] | What’s she? |
| CAPTAIN | A virtuous maid, the daughter of a count<br>That died some twelvemonth since, then leaving her<br>In the protection of his son, her brother,<br>Who shortly also died, for whose dear [[Love|love]],<br>They say, she hath abjured the sight<br>And company of men. |
| [[Viola|VIOLA]] | O that I served that lady,<br>And might not be delivered to the world<br>Till I had made mine own occasion mellow,<br>What my estate is. |
| CAPTAIN | That were hard to compass,<br>Because she will admit no kind of suit,<br>No, not the Duke’s. |
| [[Viola|VIOLA]] | There is a fair behaviour in thee, captain,<br>And though that [[Nature|nature]] with a beauteous wall<br>Doth oft close in pollution, yet of thee<br>I will believe thou hast a mind that suits<br>With this thy fair and outward character.<br>I pray thee—and I’ll pay thee bounteously—<br>Conceal me what I am, and be my aid<br>For such disguise as haply shall become<br>The form of my intent. I’ll serve this duke.<br>Thou shalt present me as an eunuch to him.<br>It may be worth thy pains, for I can sing,<br>And speak to him in many sorts of music<br>That will allow me very worth his service.<br>What else may hap, to [[Time|time]] I will commit.<br>Only shape thou thy [[Silence|silence]] to my wit. |
| CAPTAIN | Be you his eunuch, and your mute I’ll be.<br>When my tongue blabs, then let mine eyes not see. |
| [[Viola|VIOLA]] | I thank thee. Lead me on. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt |
