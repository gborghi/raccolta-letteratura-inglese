| *(didascalia)* | Music. Enter [[Orsino]] Duke of Illyria, Curio, and other lords |
| [[Orsino|ORSINO]] | If music be the food of [[Love|love]], play on,<br>Give me excess of it that, surfeiting,<br>The appetite may sicken and so die.<br>That strain again, it had a dying fall.<br>O, it came o‘er my ear like the sweet sound<br>That breathes upon a bank of violets,<br>Stealing and giving odour. Enough, no more,<br>’Tis not so sweet now as it was before. |
| *(didascalia)* | Music ceases |
| [[Orsino|ORSINO]] | O spirit of [[Love|love]], how quick and fresh art thou<br>That, notwithstanding thy capacity<br>Receiveth as the sea, naught enters there,<br>Of what validity and pitch so e’er,<br>But falls into abatement and low price<br>Even in a minute! So full of shapes is fancy<br>That it [[Alone|alone]] is high fantastical. |
| CURIO | Will you go hunt, my lord? |
| [[Orsino|ORSINO]] | What, Curio? |
| CURIO | The hart. |
| [[Orsino|ORSINO]] | Why so I do, the noblest that I have.<br>O, when mine eyes did see [[Olivia]] first<br>Methought she purged the air of pestilence;<br>That instant was I turned into a hart,<br>And my desires, like fell and cruel hounds,<br>E’er since pursue me. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Valentine]] |
| [[Orsino|ORSINO]] | How now, what news from her? |
| [[Valentine|VALENTINE]] | So please my lord, I might not be admitted,<br>But from her handmaid do return this answer:<br>The element itself till seven years’ heat<br>Shall not behold her face at ample view,<br>But like a cloistress she will veiled walk<br>And [[Water|water]] once a day her chamber round<br>With eye-offending brine—all this to season<br>A brother’s dead [[Love|love]], which she would keep fresh<br>And lasting in her sad remembrance. |
| [[Orsino|ORSINO]] | O, she that hath a heart of that fine frame<br>To pay this debt of [[Love|love]] but to a brother,<br>How will she [[Love|love]] when the rich golden shaft<br>Hath killed the flock of all affections else<br>That live in her—when liver, brain, and heart,<br>These sovereign thrones, are all supplied, and filled<br>Her sweet perfections with one [[Self|self]] [[King|king]]!<br>Away before me to sweet beds of [[Flowers|flowers]].<br>[[Love]]-thoughts lie rich when canopied with bowers. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt |
