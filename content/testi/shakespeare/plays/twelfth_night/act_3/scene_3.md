| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Sebastian]] and [[Antonio]] |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | I would not by my will have troubled you,<br>But since you make your pleasure of your pains<br>I will no further chide you. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | I could not stay behind you. My [[Desire|desire]],<br>More sharp than filèd steel, did spur me forth,<br>And not all [[Love|love]] to see you—though so much<br>As might have drawn one to a longer voyage—<br>But [[Jealousy|jealousy]] what might befall your travel,<br>Being skilless in these parts, which to a stranger,<br>Unguided and unfriended, often prove<br>Rough and unhospitable. My willing [[Love|love]]<br>The rather by these arguments of [[Fear|fear]]<br>Set forth in your pursuit. |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | My kind [[Antonio]],<br>I can no other answer make but thanks,<br>And thanks; and ever oft good turns<br>Are shuffled off with such uncurrent pay.<br>But were my worth as is my [[Conscience|conscience]] firm,<br>You should find better dealing. What’s to do?<br>Shall we go see the relics of this town? |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | Tomorrow, sir. Best first go see your lodging. |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | I am not weary, and ’tis long to [[Night|night]].<br>I pray you let us satisfy our eyes<br>With the memorials and the things of fame<br>That do renown this [[City|city]]. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | Would you’d pardon me.<br>I do not without danger walk these streets.<br>Once in a sea-fight ‘gainst the Count his galleys<br>I did some service, of such note indeed<br>That were I ta’en here it would scarce be answered. |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | Belike you slew great number of his people. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | Th’offence is not of such a bloody [[Nature|nature]],<br>Albeit the quality of the [[Time|time]] and quarrel<br>Might [[Well|well]] have given us bloody argument.<br>It might have since been answered in repaying<br>What we took from them, which for traffic’s sake<br>Most of our [[City|city]] did. Only myself stood out,<br>For which if I be latched in this place<br>I shall pay dear. |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | Do not then walk too open. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | It doth not fit me. Hold, sir, here’s my purse.<br>In the south suburbs at the Elephant<br>Is best to lodge. I will bespeak our diet<br>Whiles you beguile the [[Time|time]] and feed your knowledge<br>With viewing of the town. There shall you have me. |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | Why I your purse? |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | Haply your eye shall light upon some toy<br>You have [[Desire|desire]] to purchase; and your store<br>I think is not for idle markets, sir. |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | I’ll be your purse-bearer, and leave you<br>For an hour. |
| [[Antonio|ANTONIO]] | To th’ Elephant. |
| [[Sebastian|SEBASTIAN]] | I do remember. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt severally |
