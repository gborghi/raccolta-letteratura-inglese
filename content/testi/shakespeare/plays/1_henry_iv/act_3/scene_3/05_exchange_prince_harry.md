| PRINCE HARRY | O, my sweet beef, I must still be good angel to thee. The money is paid back again. |
| SIR JOHN | O, I do not like that paying back; ’tis a [[Double|double]] labour. 181 |
| PRINCE HARRY | I am good friends with my father, and may do anything. |
| SIR JOHN | Rob me the exchequer the first thing thou dost, and do it with unwashed hands too. |
| RUSSELL | Do, my lord. |
| PRINCE HARRY | I have procured thee, Jack, a charge of foot. |
| SIR JOHN | I would it had been of [[Horse|horse]] ! Where shall I find one that can steal [[Well|well]]? O, for a fine thief of the age of two-and-twenty or thereabouts! I am heinously unprovided. [[Well]], God be thanked for these rebels-they offend none but the virtuous. I laud them, I praise them. |
| PRINCE HARRY | Russell. |
| RUSSELL | My lord? |
| PRINCE HARRY | (giving letters) Go bear this letter to Lord John of Lancaster, To my brother John; this to my lord of Westmorland. Exit Russell Go, Harvey, to [[Horse|horse]], to [[Horse|horse]], for thou and I Have thirty miles to ride yet ere dinner [[Time|time]]. Exit Harvey Jack, meet me tomorrow in the Temple Hall At two o’[[Clock|clock]] in the afternoon. There shalt thou know thy charge, and there receive Money and order for their furniture. The land is burning, Percy stands on high, And either we or they must lower lie. Exit |
| SIR JOHN | Rare words I Brave world! (Calling) Hostess, my breakfast, come!— O, I could wish this tavern were my drum! Exit |
