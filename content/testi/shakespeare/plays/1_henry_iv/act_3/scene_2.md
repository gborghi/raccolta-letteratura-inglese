| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[King]] Henry, Prince Harry, and lords |
| [[King|KING]] HENRY | Lords, give us leave—the Prince of Wales and I<br>Must have some private conference—but be near at<br>hand,<br>For we shall presently have need of you. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt Lords |
| [[King|KING]] HENRY | I know not whether God will have it so<br>For some displeasing service I have done,<br>That in his secret doom out of my [[Blood|blood]]<br>He’ll breed revengement and a scourge for me,<br>But thou dost in thy passages of life<br>Make me believe that thou art only marked<br>For the hot vengeance and the rod of heaven<br>To punish my mistreadings. Tell me else,<br>Could such inordinate and low desires,<br>Such poor, such bare, such lewd, such mean attempts,<br>Such barren pleasures, rude society,<br>As thou art matched withal and grafted to, 15<br>Accompany the [[Greatness|greatness]] of thy [[Blood|blood]],<br>And hold their level with thy princely heart? |
| PRINCE HARRY | So please your majesty, I would I could<br>Quit all offences with as clear excuse<br>As [[Well|well]] as I am doubtless I can purge<br>Myself of many I am charged withal;<br>Yet such extenuation let me beg<br>As, in reproof of many tales devised—<br>Which oft the ear of [[Greatness|greatness]] needs must hear<br>By smiling pickthanks and base newsmongers—<br>I may, for some things true wherein my youth<br>Hath faulty wandered and irregular,<br>Find pardon on my true submission. |
| [[King|KING]] HENRY | God pardon thee! Yet let me [[Wonder|wonder]], Harry,<br>At thy affections, which do hold a wing<br>Quite from the flight of all thy ancestors.<br>Thy place in Council thou hast rudely lost—<br>Which by thy younger brother is supplied—<br>And art almost an alien to the hearts<br>Of all the [[Court|court]] and princes of my [[Blood|blood]].<br>The [[Hope|hope]] and expectation of thy [[Time|time]]<br>Is ruined, and the [[Soul|soul]] of every man<br>Prophetically do forethink thy fall.<br>Had I so lavish of my presence been,<br>So common-hackneyed in the eyes of men,<br>So stale and cheap to vulgar company,<br>Opinion, that did help me to the [[Crown|crown]],<br>Had still kept loyal to possession,<br>And left me in reputeless banishment,<br>A fellow of no mark nor likelihood.<br>By being seldom seen, I could not stir<br>But, like a comet, I was wondered at,<br>That men would tell their children ‘This is he.’<br>Others would say ‘Where, which is Bolingbroke?’<br>And then I stole all courtesy from heaven,<br>And dressed myself in such [[Humility|humility]]<br>That I did pluck allegiance from men’s hearts,<br>Loud shouts and salutations from their mouths,<br>Even in the presence of the crowned [[King]].<br>Thus did I keep my person fresh and new,<br>My presence like a robe pontifical—<br>Ne‘er seen but wondered at—and so my state,<br>Seldom but sumptuous, showed like a feast,<br>And won by rareness such solemnity.<br>The skipping [[King]], he ambled up and down 60<br>With shallow jesters and rash bavin wits,<br>Soon kindled and soon burnt, carded his state,<br>Mingled his royalty with cap’ring fools,<br>Had his great name profaned with their scorns,<br>And gave his countenance, against his name,<br>To laugh at gibing boys, and stand the push<br>Of every beardless vain comparative;<br>Grew a companion to the common streets,<br>Enfeoffed himself to popularity,<br>That, being daily swallowed by men’s eyes,<br>They surfeited with honey, and began<br>To loathe the taste of sweetness, whereof a little<br>More than a little is by much too much.<br>So when he had occasion to be seen,<br>He was but as the cuckoo is in June,<br>Heard, not regarded, seen but with such eyes<br>As, sick and blunted with community,<br>Afford no extraordinary gaze<br>Such as is bent on sun-like majesty<br>When it shines seldom in admiring eyes,<br>But rather drowsed and hung their eyelids down,<br>Slept in his face, and rendered such aspect<br>As cloudy men use to their adversaries,<br>Being with his presence glutted, gorged, and full.<br>And in that very line, Harry, standest thou; 85<br>For thou hast lost thy princely privilege<br>With vile participation. Not an eye<br>But is a-weary of thy common sight,<br>Save mine, which hath desired to see thee more,<br>Which now doth that I would not have it do—<br>Make blind itself with foolish tenderness.<br>He weeps |
| PRINCE HARRY | I shall hereafter, my thrice-gracious lord,<br>Be more myself. |
| [[King|KING]] HENRY | For all the world,<br>As thou art to this hour was [[Richard]] then,<br>When I from France set foot at Ravenspurgh,<br>And even as I was then is Percy now.<br>Now by my sceptre, and my [[Soul|soul]] to boot,<br>He hath more worthy interest to the state<br>Than thou, the [[Shadow|shadow]] of succession;<br>For, of no right, nor colour like to right,<br>He doth fill fields with harness in the realm,<br>Turns head against the [[Lion|lion]]’s armed jaws,<br>And, being no more in debt to years than thou,<br>Leads ancient lords and reverend bishops on<br>To bloody battles, and to bruising arms. 105<br>What never-dying [[Honour|honour]] hath he got<br>Against renowned Douglas !—whose high deeds,<br>Whose hot incursions and great name in arms,<br>Holds from all soldiers chief majority<br>And military title capital<br>Through all the kingdoms that acknowledge [[Christ]].<br>Thrice hath this [[Hotspur]], Mars in swaddling-clothes,<br>This infant warrior, in his enterprises<br>Discomfited great Douglas; ta‘en him once;<br>Enlarged him; and made a friend of him 115<br>To fill the mouth of deep defiance up,<br>And shake the peace and safety of our throne.<br>And what say you to this ? Percy, Northumberland,<br>The Archbishop’s grace of York, Douglas, Mortimer,<br>Capitulate against us, and are up.<br>But wherefore do I tell these news to thee ?<br>Why, Harry, do I tell thee of my foes,<br>Which art my near’st and dearest enemy ?—<br>Thou that art like enough, through vassal [[Fear|fear]],<br>Base inclination, and the start of spleen,<br>To fight against me under Percy’s pay,<br>To dog his heels, and curtsy at his frowns,<br>To show how much thou art degenerate. |
| PRINCE HARRY | Do not think so; you shall not find it so.<br>And God forgive them that so much have swayed<br>Your majesty’s good thoughts away from me.<br>I will redeem all this on Percy’s head,<br>And in the closing of some glorious day<br>Be bold to tell you that I am your son;<br>When I will wear a garment all of [[Blood|blood]],<br>And stain my favours in a bloody mask,<br>Which, washed away, shall scour my shame with it.<br>And that shall be the day, whene’er it lights,<br>That this same [[Child|child]] of [[Honour|honour]] and renown,<br>This gallant [[Hotspur]], this all-praised knight,<br>And your unthought-of Harry chance to meet.<br>For every [[Honour|honour]] sitting on his helm,<br>Would they were multitudes, and on my head<br>My shames redoubled; for the [[Time|time]] will come<br>That I shall make this northern youth exchange<br>His glorious deeds for my indignities.<br>Percy is but my factor, good my lord,<br>To engross up glorious deeds on my behalf;<br>And I will call him to so strict account<br>That he shall render every glory up, 150<br>Yea, even the slightest worship of his [[Time|time]],<br>Or I will tear the reckoning from his heart.<br>This, in the name of God, I promise here,<br>The which if he be pleased I shall perform,<br>I do beseech your majesty may salve<br>The long-grown wounds of my intemperature;<br>If not, the end of life cancels all bonds,<br>And I will die a hundred thousand deaths<br>Ere break the smallest parcel of this vow. |
| [[King|KING]] HENRY | A hundred thousand rebels die in this.<br>Thou shalt have charge and sovereign trust herein. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Sir Walter Blunt |
| [[King|KING]] HENRY | How now, good Blunt ? Thy looks are full of [[Speed|speed]]. |
| BLUNT | So hath the business that I come to speak of.<br>Lord Mortimer of Scotland hath sent word<br>That Douglas and the English rebels met<br>The eleventh of this month at Shrewsbury.<br>A mighty and a fearful head they are,<br>If promises be kept on every hand,<br>As ever offered foul play in a state. |
| [[King|KING]] HENRY | The Earl of Westmorland set forth today,<br>With him my son Lord John of Lancaster,<br>For this advertisement is five days old.<br>On Wednesday next, Harry, you shall set forward.<br>On [[thursday|Thursday]] we ourselves will march.<br>Our meeting is Bridgnorth, and, Harry, you<br>Shall march through Gloucestershire, by which<br>account,<br>Our business valued, some twelve days hence<br>Our general forces at Bridgnorth shall meet.<br>Our hands are full of business; let’s away.<br>Advantage feeds him fat while men delay. Exeunt |
