| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Hotspur]], reading a letter |
| [[Hotspur|HOTSPUR]] | ‘But for mine own part, my lord, I could be [[Well|well]] contented to be there, in respect of the [[Love|love]] I bear your [[house]].’—He could be contented; why is he not then? In respect of the [[Love|love]] he bears our [[house]] ! He shows in this he loves his own barn better than he loves our [[house]]. Let me see some more.—‘The purpose you undertake is dangerous’—Why, that’s certain: ‘tis dangerous to take a cold, to [[Sleep|sleep]], to drink; but I tell you, my lord [[Fool|fool]], out of this nettle danger we pluck this flower safety.—‘The purpose you undertake is dangerous, the friends you have named uncertain, the [[Time|time]] itself unsorted, and your whole plot too light for the counterpoise of so great an opposition.’—Say you so, say you so? I say unto you again, you are a shallow, cowardly hind, and you lie. What a lack-brain is this! By the Lord, our plot is a good plot as ever was laid, our friends true and constant; a good plot, good friends, and full of expectation; an excellent plot, very good friends. What a frosty-spirited rogue is this! Why, my lord of York commends the plot and the general course of the action. Zounds, an I were now by this rascal, I could brain him with his lady’s fan! Is there not my father, my uncle, and myself? Lord [[Edmund]] Mortimer, my lord of York, and Owain Glyndwr? Is there not besides the Douglas? Have I not all their letters, to meet me in arms by the ninth of the next month? And are they not some of them set forward already? What a pagan rascal is this, an infidel ! Ha, you shall see now, in very sincerity of [[Fear|fear]] and cold heart will he to the [[King]], and lay open all our proceedings! O, I could divide myself and go to buffets for moving such a dish of skim-milk with so honourable an action Hang him ! Let him tell the [[King]] we are prepared; I will set forward tonight. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Lady Percy |
| [[Hotspur|HOTSPUR]] | How now, Kate? I must leave you within these two hours. |
| LADY PERCY | Omy good lord, why are you thus [[Alone|alone]] ?<br>For what offence have I this fortnight been<br>A banished woman from my Harry’s bed?<br>Tell me, sweet lord, what is’t that takes from thee<br>Thy stomach, pleasure, and thy golden [[Sleep|sleep]]?<br>Why dost thou bend thine eyes upon the earth,<br>And start so often when thou sitt‘st [[Alone|alone]]?<br>Why hast thou lost the fresh [[Blood|blood]] in thy cheeks,<br>And given my treasures and my rights of thee<br>To thick-eyed musing and curst [[Melancholy|melancholy]]?<br>In thy faint slumbers I by thee have watched,<br>And heard thee murmur tales of iron wars,<br>Speak terms of manège to thy bounding steed,<br>Cry ‘[[Courage]]! To the field!’ And thou hast talked<br>Of sallies and retires, of trenches, tents,<br>Of palisadoes, frontiers, parapets,<br>Of basilisks, of cannon, culverin,<br>Of prisoners ransomed, and of soldiers slain,<br>And all the currents of a heady fight.<br>Thy spirit within thee hath been so at war,<br>And thus hath so bestirred thee in thy [[Sleep|sleep]],<br>That beads of sweat have stood upon thy brow<br>Like bubbles in a late-disturbed stream;<br>And in thy face strange motions have appeared,<br>Such as we see when men restrain their breath<br>On some great sudden hest. O,what portents are<br>these?<br>Some heavy business hath my lord in hand,<br>And I must know it, else he loves me not. |
| [[Hotspur|HOTSPUR]] | What ho! |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Servant |
| [[Hotspur|HOTSPUR]] | Is Gilliams with the packet gone? |
| SERVANT | He is, my lord, an hour ago. |
| [[Hotspur|HOTSPUR]] | Hath Butler brought those horses from the sheriff? |
| SERVANT | One [[Horse|horse]], my lord, he brought even now. |
| [[Hotspur|HOTSPUR]] | What [[Horse|horse]]? A roan, a crop-ear, is it not? |
| SERVANT | It is, my lord. |
| [[Hotspur|HOTSPUR]] | That roan shall be my throne.<br>[[Well]], I will back him straight.—O,Esperance!—<br>Bid Butler lead him forth into the park. |
| LADY PERCY | But hear you, my lord. |
| [[Hotspur|HOTSPUR]] | What sayst thou, my lady? |
| LADY PERCY | What is it carries you away? |
| [[Hotspur|HOTSPUR]] | Why, my [[Horse|horse]],<br>My [[Love|love]], my [[Horse|horse]]. |
| LADY PERCY | Out, you mad-headed ape!<br>A weasel hath not such a deal of spleen<br>As you are tossed with.<br>In [[Faith|faith]], I’ll know your business, Harry, that I will.<br>I [[Fear|fear]] my brother Mortimer doth stir<br>About his title, and hath sent for you<br>To line his enterprise; but if you go— |
| [[Hotspur|HOTSPUR]] | So far afoot? I shall be weary, [[Love|love]]. |
| LADY PERCY | Come, come, you paraquito, answer me<br>Directly to this question that I ask.<br>In [[Faith|faith]], I’ll break thy little finger, Harry,<br>An if thou wilt not tell me all things true. |
| [[Hotspur|HOTSPUR]] | Away, away, you trifler! [[Love]] ? I [[Love|love]] thee not,<br>I care not for thee, Kate. This is no world<br>To play with maumets and to tilt with lips.<br>We must have bloody noses and cracked crowns,<br>And pass them current, too. God’s me, my [[Horse|horse]]!—<br>What sayst thou, Kate? What wouldst thou have<br>with me? |
| LADY PERCY | Do you not [[Love|love]] me? Do you not indeed?<br>[[Well]], do not, then, for since you [[Love|love]] me not<br>I will not [[Love|love]] myself. Do you not [[Love|love]] me?<br>Nay, tell me if you speak in jest or no. |
| [[Hotspur|HOTSPUR]] | Come, wilt thou see me ride ?<br>And when I am a-horseback, I will swear<br>I [[Love|love]] thee infinitely. But hark you, Kate.<br>I must not have you henceforth question me<br>Whither I go, nor reason whereabout.<br>Whither I must, I must; and, to conclude,<br>This evening must I leave you, gentle Kate.<br>I know you wise, but yet no farther wise<br>Than Harry Percy’s wife; constant you are,<br>But yet a woman; and for secrecy<br>No lady closer, for I [[Well|well]] believe<br>Thou wilt not utter what thou dost not know.<br>And so far will I trust thee, gentle Kate. |
| LADY PERCY | How, so far? |
| [[Hotspur|HOTSPUR]] | Not an inch further. But hark you, Kate,<br>Whither I go, thither shall you go too.<br>Today will I set forth, tomorrow you.<br>Will this content you, Kate? |
| LADY PERCY | It must, of force. Exeunt |
