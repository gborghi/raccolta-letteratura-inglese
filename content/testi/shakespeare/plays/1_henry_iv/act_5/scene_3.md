| *(didascalia)* | [[King]] Henry enters with his power. Alarum, and exeunt to the battle. Then enter the Earl of Douglas, and Sir Walter Blunt, disguised as the [[King]] |
| BLUNT | What is thy name, that in the battle thus<br>Thou crossest me? What [[Honour|honour]] dost thou seek<br>Upon my head? |
| DOUGLAS | Know then my name is Douglas,<br>And I do haunt thee in the battle thus<br>Because some tell me that thou art a [[King|king]]. |
| BLUNT | They tell thee true. |
| DOUGLAS | The Lord of Stafford dear today hath bought<br>Thy likeness, for instead of thee, [[King]] Harry,<br>This [[Sword|sword]] hath ended him. So shall it thee,<br>Unless thou yield thee as my prisoner. |
| BLUNT | I was not born a yielder, thou proud Scot,<br>And thou shalt find a [[King|king]] that will [[Revenge|revenge]]<br>Lord Stafford’s [[Death|death]].<br>They fight. Douglas kills Blunt. Then enter [[Hotspur]] |
| [[Hotspur|HOTSPUR]] | O Douglas, hadst thou fought at Holmedon thus,<br>I never had triumphed upon a Scot. |
| DOUGLAS | All’s done, all’s won: here breathless lies the [[King]]. |
| [[Hotspur|HOTSPUR]] | Where? |
| DOUGLAS | Here. |
| [[Hotspur|HOTSPUR]] | This, Douglas? No, I know this face full [[Well|well]].<br>A gallant knight he was; his name was Blunt—<br>Semblably furnished like the [[King]] himself. |
| DOUGLAS | (to Blunt’s [[Body|body]])<br>A [[Fool|fool]] go with thy [[Soul|soul]], whither it goes !<br>A borrowed title hast thou bought too dear.<br>Why didst thou tell me that thou wert a [[King|king]]? |
| [[Hotspur|HOTSPUR]] | The [[King|king]] hath many marching in his coats. |
| DOUGLAS | Now by my [[Sword|sword]], I will kill all his coats.<br>I’ll murder all his wardrobe, piece by piece,<br>Until I meet the [[King]]. |
| [[Hotspur|HOTSPUR]] | Up and away!<br>Our soldiers stand full fairly for the day. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt, leaving Blunt’s [[Body|body]] |
| *(didascalia)* | Alarum. Enter Sir John Oldcastle |
| SIR JOHN | Though I could scape shot-free at [[London]], I [[Fear|fear]] the shot here. Here’s no scoring but upon the pate.—Soft, who are you?—Sir Walter Blunt. There’s [[Honour|honour]] for you. Here’s no [[Vanity|vanity]]. I am as hot as molten lead, and as heavy too. God keep lead out of me; I need no more weight than mine own bowels. I have led my ragamuffins where they are peppered; there’s not three of my hundred and fifty left alive, and they are for the town’s end, to beg during life. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Prince Harry |
| SIR JOHN | But who comes here? |
| PRINCE HARRY | What, stand’st thou idle here? Lend me thy [[Sword|sword]].<br>Many a noble man lies stark and stiff<br>Under the hoofs of vaunting enemies,<br>Whose deaths as yet are unrevenged. I prithee<br>Lend me thy [[Sword|sword]]. |
| SIR JOHN | O Hal, I prithee give me leave to breathe awhile.<br>Turk Gregory never did such deeds in arms<br>As I have done this day. I have paid Percy,<br>I have made him sure. |
| PRINCE HARRY | He is indeed,<br>And living to kill thee. I prithee<br>Lend me thy [[Sword|sword]]. |
| SIR JOHN | Nay, before God, Hal,<br>If Percy be alive thou gett’st not my [[Sword|sword]];<br>But take my [[Pistol|pistol]] if thou wilt. |
| PRINCE HARRY | Give it me. What, is it in the case? |
| SIR JOHN | Ay, Hal;<br>’Tis hot, ’tis hot. There’s that will sack a [[City|city]].<br>The Prince draws it out, and finds it to be a bottle of sack |
| PRINCE HARRY | What, is it a [[Time|time]] to jest and dally now?<br>He throws the bottle at him. Exit |
| SIR JOHN | [[Well]], if Percy be alive, I’ll pierce him. If he do come in my way, so; if he do not, if I come in his willingly, let him make a carbonado of me. I like not such grinning [[Honour|honour]] as Sir Walter hath. Give me life, which if I can save, so; if not, [[Honour|honour]] comes unlocked for, and there’s an end. Exit [with Blunt’s [[Body|body]]] |
