| WORCESTER | Peace, cousin, say no more. And now I will unclasp a secret book, And to your quick-conceiving discontents I’ll read you matter deep and dangerous, As full of peril and adventurous spirit As to o’erwalk a current roaring loud On the unsteadfast footing of a spear. |
| [[Hotspur|HOTSPUR]] | If he fall in, good [[Night|night]], or sink or swim. Send danger from the east unto the west, So [[Honour|honour]] [[Cross|cross]] it from the north to south; And let them grapple. O, the [[Blood|blood]] more stirs To rouse a [[Lion|lion]] than to start a hare! |
| NORTHUMBERLAND | (to Worcester) [[Imagination]] of some great exploit Drives him beyond the bounds of patience. [[Hotspur|HOTSPUR]] By heaven, methinks it were an easy leap To pluck bright [[Honour|honour]] from the pale-faced [[Moon|moon]], Or dive into the [[Bottom|bottom]] of the deep, Where fathom-line could never touch the ground, And pluck up drowned [[Honour|honour]] by the locks, So he that doth redeem her thence might wear, Without corrival, all her dignities. But out upon this half-faced fellowship! |
| WORCESTER | (to Northumberland) He apprehends a world of figures here, But not the form of what he should attend. (To [[Hotspur]]) Good cousin, give me audience for a while, And list to me. |
| [[Hotspur|HOTSPUR]] | I cry you [[Mercy|mercy]]. |
| WORCESTER | Those same noble Scots That are your prisoners— |
| [[Hotspur|HOTSPUR]] | I’ll keep them all. By God, he shall not have a Scot of them; No, if a scot would save his [[Soul|soul]] he shall not. I’ll keep them, by this hand. |
| WORCESTER | You start away, And lend no ear unto my purposes. Those prisoners you shall keep. |
| [[Hotspur|HOTSPUR]] | Nay, I will; that’s flat. He said he would not ransom Mortimer, Forbade my tongue to speak of Mortimer; But I will find him when he lies asleep, And in his ear I’ll hollo ‘Mortimerl’ Nay, I’ll have a starling shall be taught to speak Nothing but ‘Mortimer’, and give it him To keep his anger still in motion. |
| WORCESTER | Hear you, cousin, a word. |
| [[Hotspur|HOTSPUR]] | All studies here I solemnly defy, Save how to gall and pinch this Bolingbroke. And that same [[Sword|sword]]-and-buckler Prince of Wales— But that I think his father loves him not And would be glad he met with some mischance— I would have him poisoned with a pot of ale. |
| WORCESTER | Farewell, kinsman. I’ll talk to you When you are better tempered to attend. |
| NORTHUMBERLAND | (to [[Hotspur]]) Why, what a wasp-stung and impatient [[Fool|fool]] Art thou to break into this woman’s mood, Tying thine ear to no tongue but thine own I |
| [[Hotspur|HOTSPUR]] | Why, look you, I am whipped and scourged with rods, Nettled and stung with pismires, when I hear Of this vile politician Bolingbroke. In [[Richard]]’s [[Time|time]]—what d‘ye call the place? A plague upon’t, it is in Gloucestershire. ‘Twas where the madcap Duke his uncle kept— His uncle York—where I first bowed my knee Unto this [[King|king]] of smiles, this Bolingbroke. ’Sblood, when you and he came back from Ravenspurgh. |
| NORTHUMBERLAND | At Berkeley [[Castle|castle]]. |
| [[Hotspur|HOTSPUR]] | You say true. Why, what a candy deal of courtesy This fawning greyhound then did proffer me! ‘Look when his infant fortune came to age’, And ‘gentle Harry Percy’, and ‘kind cousin’. O, the devil take such cozeners!—God forgive me. Good uncle, tell your tale; I have done. |
