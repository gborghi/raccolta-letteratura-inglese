| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Capulet]], his Wife, and [[Paris]] |
| [[Capulet|CAPULET]] | Things have fall’n out, sir, so unluckily<br>That we have had no [[Time|time]] to move our daughter.<br>Look you, she loved her kinsman [[Tybalt]] dearly,<br>And so did I. [[Well]], we were born to die.<br>’Tis very late. She’ll not come down tonight.<br>I promise you, but for your company<br>I would have been abed an hour ago. |
| [[Paris|PARIS]] | These times of woe afford no times to woo.<br>Madam, good [[Night|night]]. Commend me to your daughter. |
| [[Capulet|CAPULET]]’S WIFE | I will, and know her mind early tomorrow.<br>Tonight she’s mewed up to her heaviness. |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Paris]] offers to go in, and [[Capulet]] calls him again |
| [[Capulet|CAPULET]] | Sir [[Paris]], I will make a desperate tender<br>Of my [[Child|child]]’s [[Love|love]]. I think she will be ruled<br>In all respects by me. Nay, more, I [[Doubt|doubt]] it not.<br>Wife, go you to her ere you go to bed.<br>Acquaint her here of my son [[Paris]]’ [[Love|love]],<br>And bid her-mark you me?—on Wednesday next—<br>But soft—what day is this? |
| [[Paris|PARIS]] | Monday, my lord. |
| [[Capulet|CAPULET]] | Monday. Ha, ha! [[Well]], Wednesday is too soon.<br>O’ [[thursday|Thursday]] let it be. 0’ [[thursday|Thursday]], tell her,<br>She shall be married to this noble earl.<br>Will you be ready? Do you like this haste?<br>We’ll keep no great ado—a friend or two.<br>For hark you, [[Tybalt]] being slain so late,<br>It may be thought we held him carelessly,<br>Being our kinsman, if we revel much.<br>Therefore we’ll have some half a dozen friends,<br>And there an end. But what say you to [[thursday|Thursday]]? |
| [[Paris|PARIS]] | My lord, I would that [[thursday|Thursday]] were tomorrow. |
| [[Capulet|CAPULET]] | [[Well]], get you gone. O’ [[thursday|Thursday]] be it, then.<br>(To his Wife) Go you to [[Juliet]] ere you go to bed.<br>Prepare her, wife, against this wedding day.—<br>Farewell, my lord.—Light to my chamber, ho!—<br>Afore me, it is so very late that we<br>May call it early by and by. Good [[Night|night]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt [[Capulet]] and his wife at one door, [[Paris]] at another door |
