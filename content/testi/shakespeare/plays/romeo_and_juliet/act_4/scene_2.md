|---|---|
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Capulet]], his Wife, the [[Nurse]], and two Servingmen |
| [[Capulet|CAPULET]] | (giving a Servingman a paper)<br>So many guests invite as here are writ. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit Servingman |
| [[Capulet|CAPULET]] | (To the other Servingman) Sirrah, go hire me twenty<br>cunning cooks. |
| SERVINGMAN | You shall have none ill, sir, for I’ll try if they can lick their fingers. |
| [[Capulet|CAPULET]] | How canst thou try them so? |
| SERVINGMAN | Marry, sir, ’tis an ill cook that cannot lick his own fingers, therefore he that cannot lick his fingers goes not with me. |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Capulet|CAPULET]] Go, be gone. Exit Servingman |
| SERVINGMAN | We shall be much unfurnished for this [[Time|time]].<br>(To the [[Nurse]]) What, is my daughter gone to Friar<br>Laurence? |
| [[Nurse|NURSE]] | Ay, forsooth. |
| [[Capulet|CAPULET]] | [[Well]], he may chance to do some good on her.<br>A peevish, [[Self|self]]-willed harlotry it is. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Juliet]] |
| [[Nurse|NURSE]] | See where she comes from shrift with merry look. |
| [[Capulet|CAPULET]] | (to [[Juliet]])<br>How now, my headstrong, where have you been gadding ? |
| [[Juliet|JULIET]] | Where I have learned me to repent the sin<br>Of disobedient opposition<br>To you and your behests, and am enjoined<br>By holy Laurence to fall prostrate here<br>To beg your pardon. (Kneeling) Pardon, I beseech you.<br>Henceforward I am ever ruled by you. |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Capulet|CAPULET]] to the [[Nurse]] |
| [[Juliet|JULIET]] | Send for the County; go tell him of this.<br>I’ll have this knot knit up tomorrow morning. |
| [[Juliet|JULIET]] | I met the youthful lord at Laurence’ cell,<br>And gave him what becoming [[Love|love]] I might,<br>Not stepping o’er the bounds of modesty. |
| [[Capulet|CAPULET]] | Why, I am glad on’t. This is [[Well|well]]. Stand up.<br>[[Juliet]] rises<br>This is as’t should be. Let me see the County. |
| *(didascalia)* | To [[Nurse]] Ay, marry, go, I say, and fetch him hither. |
| [[Capulet|CAPULET]] | Now, afore God, this reverend holy friar,<br>All our whole [[City|city]] is much bound to him. |
| [[Juliet|JULIET]] | [[Nurse]], will you go with me into my closet<br>To help me sort such needful ornaments<br>As you think fit to furnish me tomorrow? |
| [[Capulet|CAPULET]]’S WIFE | No, not till [[thursday|Thursday]]. There is [[Time|time]] enough. |
| [[Capulet|CAPULET]] | Go, [[Nurse]], go with her. We’ll to church tomorrow. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt [[Juliet]] and [[Nurse]] |
| [[Capulet|CAPULET]]’S WIFE | We shall be short in our provision.<br>’Tis now near [[Night|night]]. |
| [[Capulet|CAPULET]] | Tush, I will stir about,<br>And all things shall be [[Well|well]], I warrant thee, wife.<br>Go thou to [[Juliet]], help to deck up her.<br>I’ll not to bed tonight. Let me [[Alone|alone]].<br>I’ll play the housewife for this once. What, ho!<br>They are all forth. [[Well]], I will walk myself<br>To County [[Paris]] to prepare up him<br>Against tomorrow. My heart is wondrous light,<br>Since this same wayward girl is so reclaimed. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt severally |
