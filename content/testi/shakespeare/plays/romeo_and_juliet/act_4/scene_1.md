| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Friar Laurence]] and [[Paris]] |
| [[Friar Laurence|FRIAR LAURENCE]] | On [[thursday|Thursday]], sir? The [[Time|time]] is very short. |
| [[Paris|PARIS]] | My father [[Capulet]] will have it so,<br>And I am nothing slow to slack his haste. |
| [[Friar Laurence|FRIAR LAURENCE]] | You say you do not know the lady’s mind?<br>Uneven is the course. I like it not. |
| [[Paris|PARIS]] | Immoderately she weeps for [[Tybalt]]’s [[Death|death]],<br>And therefore have I little talked of [[Love|love]],<br>For [[Venus]] smiles not in a [[house]] of [[Tears|tears]].<br>Now, sir, her father counts it dangerous<br>That she do give her sorrow so much sway,<br>And in his wisdom hastes our [[Marriage|marriage]]<br>To stop the inundation of her [[Tears|tears]],<br>Which, too much minded by herself [[Alone|alone]],<br>May be put from her by society.<br>Now do you know the reason of this haste. |
| [[Friar Laurence|FRIAR LAURENCE]] | (aside)<br>I would I knew not why it should be slowed.— |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Juliet]] |
| [[Friar Laurence|FRIAR LAURENCE]] | Look, sir, here comes the lady toward my cell. |
| [[Paris|PARIS]] | Happily met, my lady and my wife. |
| [[Juliet|JULIET]] | That may be, sir, when I may be a wife. |
| [[Paris|PARIS]] | That ’may be’ must be, [[Love|love]], on [[thursday|Thursday]] next. |
| [[Juliet|JULIET]] | What must be shall be. |
| [[Friar Laurence|FRIAR LAURENCE]] | That’s a certain text. |
| [[Paris|PARIS]] | Come you to make confession to this father? |
| [[Juliet|JULIET]] | To answer that, I should confess to you. |
| [[Paris|PARIS]] | Do not deny to him that you [[Love|love]] me. |
| [[Juliet|JULIET]] | I will confess to you that I [[Love|love]] him. |
| [[Paris|PARIS]] | So will ye, I am sure, that you [[Love|love]] me. |
| [[Juliet|JULIET]] | If I do so, it will be of more price,<br>Being spoke behind your back, than to your face. |
| [[Paris|PARIS]] | Poor [[Soul|soul]], thy face is much abused with [[Tears|tears]]. |
| [[Juliet|JULIET]] | The [[Tears|tears]] have got small victory by that,<br>For it was bad enough before their spite. |
| [[Paris|PARIS]] | Thou wrong’st it more than [[Tears|tears]] with that report. |
| [[Juliet|JULIET]] | That is no slander, sir, which is a [[Truth|truth]],<br>And what I spake, I spake it to my face. |
| [[Paris|PARIS]] | Thy face is mine, and thou hast slandered it. |
| [[Juliet|JULIET]] | It may be so, for it is not mine own.—<br>Are you at leisure, holy father, now,<br>Or shall I come to you at evening mass? |
| [[Friar Laurence|FRIAR LAURENCE]] | My leisure serves me, pensive daughter, now.<br>My lord, we must entreat the [[Time|time]] [[Alone|alone]]. |
| [[Paris|PARIS]] | God shield I should disturb devotion!—<br>[[Juliet]], on [[thursday|Thursday]] early will I rouse ye.<br>(Kissing her) Till then, adieu, and keep this holy kiss. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit |
| [[Juliet|JULIET]] | O, shut the door, and when thou hast done so,<br>Come weep with me, past [[Hope|hope]], past cure, past help! |
| [[Friar Laurence|FRIAR LAURENCE]] | O [[Juliet]], I already know thy grief.<br>It strains me past the compass of my wits.<br>I hear thou must, and nothing may prorogue it,<br>On [[thursday|Thursday]] next be married to this County. |
| [[Juliet|JULIET]] | Tell me not, friar, that thou hear’st of this,<br>Unless thou tell me how I may prevent it.<br>If in thy wisdom thou canst give no help,<br>Do thou but call my resolution wise,<br>She draws a knife<br>And with this knife I’ll help it presently.<br>God joined my heart and [[Romeo]]‘s, thou our hands,<br>And ere this hand, by thee to [[Romeo]]’s sealed,<br>Shall be the label to another deed,<br>Or my true heart with treacherous revolt<br>Turn to another, this shall slay them both.<br>Therefore, out of thy long-experienced [[Time|time]],<br>Give me some present counsel; or, behold,<br>’Twixt my extremes and me this bloody knife<br>Shall play the umpire, arbitrating that<br>Which the commission of thy years and art<br>Could to no issue of true [[Honour|honour]] bring.<br>Be not so long to speak. I long to die<br>If what thou speak’st speak not of remedy. |
| [[Friar Laurence|FRIAR LAURENCE]] | Hold, daughter, I do spy a kind of [[Hope|hope]]<br>Which craves as desperate an execution<br>As that is desperate which we would prevent.<br>If, rather than to marry County [[Paris]],<br>Thou hast the strength of will to slay thyself,<br>Then is it likely thou wilt undertake<br>A thing like [[Death|death]] to chide away this shame,<br>That cop‘st with [[Death|death]] himself to scape from it;<br>And, if thou dar’st, I’ll give thee remedy. |
| [[Juliet|JULIET]] | O, bid me leap, rather than marry [[Paris]],<br>From off the battlements of any tower,<br>Or walk in thievish ways, or bid me lurk<br>Where serpents are. Chain me with roaring bears,<br>Or hide me nightly in a charnel [[house]],<br>O’ercovered quite with dead men’s rattling bones,<br>With reeky shanks and yellow chapless skulls;<br>Or bid me go into a new-made [[Grave|grave]]<br>And hide me with a dead man in his tomb—<br>Things that, to hear them told, have made me<br>tremble—<br>And I will do it without [[Fear|fear]] or [[Doubt|doubt]],<br>To live an unstained wife to my sweet [[Love|love]]. |
| [[Friar Laurence|FRIAR LAURENCE]] | Hold, then; go [[Home|home]], be merry, give consent<br>To marry [[Paris]]. Wednesday is tomorrow.<br>Tomorrow [[Night|night]] look that thou lie [[Alone|alone]].<br>Let not the [[Nurse]] lie with thee in thy chamber.<br>Take thou this vial, being then in bed,<br>And this distilling liquor drink thou off,<br>When presently through all thy veins shall run<br>A cold and drowsy [[Humour|humour]]; for no pulse<br>Shall keep his native progress, but surcease.<br>No warmth, no breath shall testify thou livest.<br>The roses in thy lips and cheeks shall fade<br>To wanny ashes, thy eyes’ windows fall<br>Like [[Death|death]] when he shuts up the day of life.<br>Each part, deprived of supple [[government]],<br>Shall, stiff and stark and cold, appear like [[Death|death]];<br>And in this borrowed likeness of shrunk [[Death|death]]<br>Thou shalt continue two-and-forty hours,<br>And then awake as from a pleasant [[Sleep|sleep]].<br>Now, when the bridegroom in the morning comes<br>To rouse thee from thy bed, there art thou dead.<br>Then, as the manner of our country is,<br>In thy best robes, uncovered on the bier<br>Thou shalt be borne to that same ancient vault<br>Where all the kindred of the Capulets lie.<br>In the meantime, against thou shalt awake,<br>Shall [[Romeo]] by my letters know our drift,<br>And hither shall he come, and he and I<br>Will watch thy waking, and that very [[Night|night]]<br>Shall [[Romeo]] bear thee hence to Mantua.<br>And this shall free thee from this present shame,<br>If no inconstant toy nor womanish [[Fear|fear]]<br>Abate thy valour in the acting it. |
| [[Juliet|JULIET]] | Give me, give me O, tell not me of [[Fear|fear]]! |
| [[Friar Laurence|FRIAR LAURENCE]] | (giving her the vial)<br>Hold, get you gone. Be strong and prosperous<br>In this resolve. I’ll send a friar with [[Speed|speed]]<br>To Mantua with my letters to thy lord. |
| [[Juliet|JULIET]] | [[Love]] give me strength, and strength shall help afford.<br>Farewell, dear father. Exeunt [severally] |
