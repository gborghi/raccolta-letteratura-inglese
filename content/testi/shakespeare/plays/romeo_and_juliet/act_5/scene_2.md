| FRIAR JOHN | Holy Franciscan friar, brother, ho! |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Friar Laurence]] at another door |
| [[Friar Laurence|FRIAR LAURENCE]] | This same should be the voice of Friar John.<br>Welcome from Mantua! What says [[Romeo]]?<br>Or if his mind be writ, give me his letter. |
| FRIAR JOHN | Going to find a barefoot brother out—5<br>One of our order—to associate me<br>Here in this [[City|city]] visiting the sick,<br>And finding him, the searchers of the town,<br>Suspecting that we both were in a [[house]]<br>Where the infectious pestilence did reign, 10<br>Sealed up the doors, and would not let us forth,<br>So that my [[Speed|speed]] to Mantua there was stayed. |
| [[Friar Laurence|FRIAR LAURENCE]] | Who bare my letter then to [[Romeo]]? |
| FRIAR JOHN | I could not send it—here it is again—<br>Nor get a messenger to bring it thee,<br>So fearful were they of infection. |
| [[Friar Laurence|FRIAR LAURENCE]] | Unhappy fortuneǃ By my brotherhood,<br>The letter was not nice, but full of charge,<br>Of dear import, and the neglecting it<br>May do much danger. Friar John, go hence.<br>Get me an iron crow, and bring it straight<br>Unto my cell. |
| FRIAR JOHN | Brother, I’ll go and bring it thee. Exit |
| [[Friar Laurence|FRIAR LAURENCE]] | Now must I to the monument [[Alone|alone]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | Within this three hours will fair [[Juliet]] wake. |
| [[Friar Laurence|FRIAR LAURENCE]] | She will beshrew me much that [[Romeo]]<br>Hath had no notice of these accidents.<br>But I will write again to Mantua,<br>And keep her at my cell till [[Romeo]] come.<br>Poor living corpse, closed in a dead man’s tomb! Exit |
