| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Romeo]] |
| [[Romeo|ROMEO]] | If I may trust the flattering [[Truth|truth]] of [[Sleep|sleep]],<br>My [[Dreams|dreams]] presage some joyful news at hand.<br>My bosom’s lord sits lightly in his throne,<br>And all this day an unaccustomed spirit<br>Lifts me above the ground with cheerful thoughts.<br>I dreamt my lady came and found me dead—<br>Strange dream, that gives a dead man leave to<br>think!—<br>And breathed such life with kisses in my lips<br>That I revived and was an emperor.<br>Ah me, how sweet is [[Love|love]] itself possessed<br>When but [[Love|love]]’s shadows are so rich in joy! |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Balthasar, [[Romeo]]’s man, booted |
| [[Romeo|ROMEO]] | News from [[Verona]]! How now, Balthasar?<br>Dost thou not bring me letters from the friar?<br>How doth my lady? Is my father [[Well|well]]?<br>How fares my [[Juliet]]? That I ask again,<br>For nothing can be ill if she be [[Well|well]]. |
| BALTHASAR | Then she is [[Well|well]], and nothing can be ill.<br>Her [[Body|body]] sleeps in Capel’s monument,<br>And her immortal part with angels lives.<br>I saw her laid low in her kindred’s vault,<br>And presently took post to tell it you.<br>O, pardon me for bringing these ill news,<br>Since you did leave it for my office, sir. |
| [[Romeo|ROMEO]] | Is it e’en so? Then I defy you, [[Stars|stars]].<br>Thou knowest my lodging. Get me ink and paper,<br>And hire posthorses. I will hence tonight. |
| BALTHASAR | I do beseech you, sir, have patience.<br>Your looks are pale and wild, and do import<br>Some misadventure. |
| [[Romeo|ROMEO]] | Tush, thou art deceived.<br>Leave me, and do the thing I bid thee do.<br>Hast thou no letters to me from the friar? |
| BALTHASAR | No, my good lord. |
| [[Romeo|ROMEO]] | No matter. Get thee gone,<br>And hire those horses. I’ll be with thee straight. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit Balthasar |
| [[Romeo|ROMEO]] | [[Well]], [[Juliet]], I will lie with thee tonight.<br>Let’s see for means. O mischief, thou art swift<br>To enter in the thoughts of desperate men!<br>I do remember an apothecary,<br>And hereabouts a dwells, which late I noted,<br>In tattered weeds, with overwhelming brows,<br>Culling of simples. Meagre were his looks.<br>Sharp misery had worn him to the bones,<br>And in his needy shop a tortoise hung,<br>An alligator stuffed, and other skins<br>Of ill-shaped fishes; and about his shelves<br>A beggarly account of empty boxes,<br>Green earthen pots, bladders, and musty seeds,<br>Remnants of packthread, and old cakes of roses<br>Were thinly scattered to make up a show.<br>Noting this penury, to myself I said<br>‘An if a man did need a [[Poison|poison]] now,<br>Whose sale is present [[Death|death]] in Mantua,<br>Here lives a caitiff wretch would sell it him.’<br>O, this same thought did but forerun my need,<br>And this same needy man must sell it me.<br>As I remember, this should be the [[house]].<br>Being holiday, the beggar’s shop is shut.<br>What ho, apothecary! |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Apothecary |
| APOTHECARY | Who calls so loud? |
| [[Romeo|ROMEO]] | Come hither, man. I see that thou art poor.<br>He offers money<br>Hold, there is forty ducats. Let me have<br>A dram of [[Poison|poison]]—such soon-speeding gear<br>As will disperse itself through all the veins,<br>That the life-weary taker may fall dead,<br>And that the trunk may be discharged of breath<br>As violently as hasty powder fired<br>Doth hurry from the fatal cannon’s womb. |
| APOTHECARY | Such mortal drugs I have, but Mantua’s law<br>Is [[Death|death]] to any he that utters them. |
| [[Romeo|ROMEO]] | Art thou so bare and full of wretchedness,<br>And [[Fear|fear]]’st to die? Famine is in thy cheeks,<br>Need and oppression starveth in thy eyes,<br>Contempt and beggary hangs upon thy back.<br>The world is not thy friend, nor the world’s law.<br>The world affords no law to make thee rich.<br>Then be not poor, but break it, and take this. |
| APOTHECARY | My [[Poverty|poverty]] but not my will consents. |
| [[Romeo|ROMEO]] | I pay thy [[Poverty|poverty]] and not thy will. |
| APOTHECARY | (handing [[Romeo]] [[Poison|poison]])<br>Put this in any liquid thing you will<br>And drink it off, and if you had the strength<br>Of twenty men it would dispatch you straight. |
| [[Romeo|ROMEO]] | (giving money)<br>There is thy gold—worse [[Poison|poison]] to men’s souls, 80<br>Doing more murder in this loathsome world,<br>Than these poor compounds that thou mayst not sell.<br>I sell thee [[Poison|poison]]; thou hast sold me none.<br>Farewell, buy food, and get thyself in flesh. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit Apothecary |
| [[Romeo|ROMEO]] | Come, cordial and not [[Poison|poison]], go with me 85<br>To [[Juliet]]’s [[Grave|grave]], for there must I use thee. Exit |
