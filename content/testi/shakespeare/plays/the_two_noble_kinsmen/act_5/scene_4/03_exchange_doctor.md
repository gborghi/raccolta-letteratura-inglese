| DOCTOR | I’ll warrant you, within these three or four days I’ll make her right again. Exit the Jailer with the Messenger (To the Wooer) You must not from her, But still preserve her in this way. |
| WOOER | I will. |
| DOCTOR | Let’s get her in. |
| WOOER | (to the Jailer’s Daughter) Come, sweet, we’ll go to dinner, And then we’ll play at cards. |
| JAILER’S DAUGHTER | And shall we kiss too? |
| WOOER | A hundred times. |
| JAILER’S DAUGHTER | And twenty. |
| WOOER | Ay, and twenty. no |
| JAILER’S DAUGHTER | And then we’ll [[Sleep|sleep]] together. |
| DOCTOR | (to the Wooer) Take her offer. |
| WOOER | (to the Jailer’s Daughter) Yes, marry, will we. |
| JAILER’S DAUGHTER | But you shall not hurt me. |
| WOOER | I will not, sweet. |
| JAILER’S DAUGHTER | If you do, [[Love|love]], I’ll cry. Exeunt |
