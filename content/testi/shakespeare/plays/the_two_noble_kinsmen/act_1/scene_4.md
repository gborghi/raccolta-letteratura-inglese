| *(didascalia)* | Cornetts. A battle struck within. Then a retreat. Flourish. Then enter [[Theseus]], victor. The three Queens meet him and fall on their faces before him. Also enter a Herald, and attendants bearing [[Palamon]] and [[Arcite]] on two hearses |
| FIRST [[Queen|QUEEN]] | (to [[Theseus]])<br>To thee no star be dark. |
| SECOND [[Queen|QUEEN]] | (to [[Theseus]]) Both heaven and earth<br>Friend thee for ever. |
| THIRD [[Queen|QUEEN]] | (to [[Theseus]]) All the good that may Be wished upon thy head, I cry ‘Amen’ to’t. |
| [[Theseus|THESEUS]] | Th’impartial gods, who from the mounted heavens<br>View us their mortal herd, behold who err<br>And in their [[Time|time]] chastise. Go and find out<br>The bones of your dead lords and [[Honour|honour]] them<br>With treble ceremony: rather than a gap<br>Should be in their dear rites we would supply’t.<br>But those we will depute which shall invest<br>You in your dignities, and even each thing<br>Our haste does leave imperfect. So adieu,<br>And heaven’s good eyes look on you. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt the Queens |
| [[Theseus|THESEUS]] | What are those? |
| HERALD | Men of great quality, as may be judged<br>By their appointment. Some of Thebes have told’s<br>They are sisters’ children, nephews to the [[King]]. |
| [[Theseus|THESEUS]] | By th’ helm of Mars I saw them in the war,<br>Like to a pair of lions smeared with prey,<br>Make lanes in troops aghast. I fixed my note<br>Constantly on them, for they were a mark<br>Worth a god’s view. What prisoner was’t that told me<br>When I enquired their names? |
| HERALD | Wi’ leave, they’re called [[Arcite]] and [[Palamon]]. |
| [[Theseus|THESEUS]] | ’Tis right: those, those. They are not dead? |
| HERALD | Nor in a state of life. Had they been taken<br>When their last hurts were given, ’twas possible<br>They might have been recovered. Yet they breathe,<br>And have the name of men. |
| [[Theseus|THESEUS]] | Then like men use ‘em. The very lees of such, millions of rates<br>Exceed the wine of others. All our surgeons<br>Convent in their behoof; our richest balms,<br>Rather than niggard, waste. Their lives concern us<br>Much more than Thebes is worth. Rather than have<br>’em<br>Freed of this plight and in their morning state— |
| *(didascalia)* | Sound and at liberty—I would ’em dead; |
| [[Theseus|THESEUS]] | But forty-thousandfold we had rather have ’em<br>Prisoners to us, than [[Death|death]]. Bear ‘em speedily<br>From our kind air, to them unkind, and minister<br>What man to man may do-for our sake, more,<br>Since I have known frights, fury, friends’ behests,<br>[[Love]]’s provocations, zeal, a mistress’ task,<br>[[Desire]] of liberty, a fever, [[Madness|madness]],<br>Hath set a mark which [[Nature|nature]] could not reach to<br>Without some imposition, sickness in will<br>O’er-wrestling strength in reason. For our [[Love|love]]<br>And great [[Apollo]]’s [[Mercy|mercy]], all our best<br>Their best skill tender.—Lead into the [[City|city]]<br>Where, having bound things scattered, we will post<br>To Athens fore our army. |
| *(didascalia)* | Flourish. Exeunt |
