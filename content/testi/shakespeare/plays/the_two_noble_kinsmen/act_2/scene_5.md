| *(didascalia)* | Short flourish of cornetts and shouts within. Enter [[Theseus]], [[Hippolyta]], Pirithous, [[Emilia]], [[Arcite]] disguised, with a garland, and attendants |
| [[Theseus|THESEUS]] | You have done worthily. I have not seen<br>Since Hercules a man of tougher sinews.<br>Whate’er you are, you run the best and wrestle<br>That these times can allow. |
| [[Arcite|ARCITE]] | I am proud to please you. |
| [[Theseus|THESEUS]] | What country bred you? |
| [[Arcite|ARCITE]] | This—but far off, prince. |
| [[Theseus|THESEUS]] | Are you a gentleman? |
| [[Arcite|ARCITE]] | My father said so, And to those gentle uses gave me life. |
| [[Theseus|THESEUS]] | Are you his heir? |
| [[Arcite|ARCITE]] | His youngest, sir. |
| [[Theseus|THESEUS]] | Your father Sure is a happy sire, then. What proves you? |
| [[Arcite|ARCITE]] | A little of all noble qualities.<br>I could have kept a hawk and [[Well|well]] have hollered<br>To a deep cry of [[dogs]]; I dare not praise<br>My feat in horsemanship, yet they that knew me<br>Would say it was my best piece; last and greatest,<br>I would be thought a soldier. |
| [[Theseus|THESEUS]] | You are perfect. |
| PIRITHOIIS | Upon my [[Soul|soul]], a proper man. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | He is so. |
| PIRITHOUS | (to [[Hippolyta]])<br>How do you like him, lady? |
| [[Hippolyta|HIPPOLYTA]] | I admire him. I have not seen so young a man so noble—<br>If he say true—of his sort. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | Believe His mother was a wondrous handsome woman—<br>His face methinks goes that way. |
| [[Hippolyta|HIPPOLYTA]] | But his [[Body|body]] And fiery mind illustrate a brave father. |
| PIRITHOUS | Mark how his virtue, like a hidden sun,<br>Breaks through his baser garments. |
| [[Hippolyta|HIPPOLYTA]] | He’s [[Well|well]] got, sure. |
| [[Theseus|THESEUS]] | (to [[Arcite]])<br>What made you seek this place, sir? |
| [[Arcite|ARCITE]] | Noble [[Theseus]], To purchase name and do my ablest service<br>To such a [[Well|well]]-found [[Wonder|wonder]] as thy worth,<br>For only in thy [[Court|court]] of all the world<br>Dwells fair-eyed [[Honour|honour]]. |
| PIRITHOUS | All his words are worthy. |
| [[Theseus|THESEUS]] | (to [[Arcite]])<br>Sir, we are much indebted to your travel,<br>Nor shall you lose your wish.—Pirithous,<br>Dispose of this fair gentleman. |
| PIRITHOUS | Thanks, [[Theseus]]. (To [[Arcite]]) Whate’er you are, you’re mine, and I shall<br>give you<br>To a most noble service, to this lady,<br>This bright young virgin; pray observe her goodness.<br>You have honoured her fair birthday with your<br>virtues,<br>And as your due you’re hers. Kiss her fair hand, sir. |
| [[Arcite|ARCITE]] | Sir, you’re a noble giver. (To [[Emilia]]) Dearest [[Beauty|beauty]],<br>Thus let me seal my vowed [[Faith|faith]].<br>He kisses her hand<br>When your servant,<br>Your most unworthy creature, but offends you,<br>Command him die, he shall. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | That were too cruel. If you deserve [[Well|well]], sir, I shall soon see’t.<br>You’re mine, and somewhat better than your rank I’ll<br>use you. |
| PIRITHOUS | (to [[Arcite]])<br>I’ll see you furnished, and, because you say<br>You are a horseman, I must needs entreat you<br>This afternoon to ride—but ’tis a rough one. |
| [[Arcite|ARCITE]] | I like him better, prince—I shall not then<br>Freeze in my saddle. |
| [[Theseus|THESEUS]] | (to [[Hippolyta]]) |
| *(didascalia)* | Sweet, you must be ready—And you, [[Emilia]], to Pirithous] and you, friend—and |
| [[Theseus|THESEUS]] | all,<br>Tomorrow by the sun, to do observance<br>To flow’ry May in Dian’s wood. (To [[Arcite]]) Wait [[Well|well]],<br>sir,<br>Upon your mistress.—Emity, I [[Hope|hope]]<br>He shall not go afoot. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | That were a shame, sir, While I have horses. (To [[Arcite]]) Take your choice, and<br>what<br>You want, at any [[Time|time]], let me but know it.<br>If you serve faithfully, I dare assure you,<br>You’ll find a loving mistress. |
| [[Arcite|ARCITE]] | If I do not, Let me find that my father ever hated—<br>Disgrace and blows. |
| [[Theseus|THESEUS]] | Go, lead the way—you have won it. It shall be so: you shall receive all dues<br>Fit for the [[Honour|honour]] you have won. ’Twere wrong else.<br>(To [[Emilia]]) Sister, beshrew my heart, you have a<br>servant<br>That, if I were a woman, would be master.<br>But you are wise. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | I [[Hope|hope]] too wise for that, sir. |
| *(didascalia)* | Flourish. Exeunt |
