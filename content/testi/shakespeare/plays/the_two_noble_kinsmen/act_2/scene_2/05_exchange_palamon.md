| [[Palamon|PALAMON]] | You have made me—I thank you, cousin [[Arcite]]—almost wanton With my captivity. What a misery It is to live abroad, and everywhere! ’Tis like a beast, methinks. I find the [[Court|court]] here; I am sure, a more content; and all those pleasures That woo the wills of men to [[Vanity|vanity]] I see through now, and am sufficient To tell the world ’tis but a gaudy [[Shadow|shadow]], That old [[Time]], as he passes by, takes with him. What had we been, old in the [[Court|court]] of Creon, Where sin is justice, lust and ignorance The virtues of the great ones? Cousin [[Arcite]], Had not the loving gods found this place for us, We had died as they do, ill old men, unwept, And had their epitaphs, the people’s curses. Shall I say more? |
| [[Arcite|ARCITE]] | I would hear you still. |
| [[Palamon|PALAMON]] | Ye shall. Is there record of any two that loved Better than we do, [[Arcite]]? |
| [[Arcite|ARCITE]] | Sure there cannot. |
| [[Palamon|PALAMON]] | I do not think it possible our [[Friendship|friendship]] Should ever leave us. |
| [[Arcite|ARCITE]] | Till our deaths it cannot, Enter [[Emilia]] and her Woman below. [[Palamon]] sees [[Emilia]] and is silent And after [[Death|death]] our spirits shall be led To those that [[Love|love]] eternally. Speak on, sir. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | (to her Woman) This [[Garden|garden]] has a world of pleasure in’t. What flower is this? |
| WOMAN | ’Tis called narcissus, madam. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | That was a fair boy, certain, but a [[Fool|fool]] To [[Love|love]] himself. Were there not maids enough? |
| [[Arcite|ARCITE]] | (to [[Palamon]]) Pray forward. |
| [[Palamon|PALAMON]] | Yes. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | (to her Woman) Or were they all hard-hearted? |
| WOMAN | They could not be to one so fair. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | Thou wouldst not. |
| WOMAN | I think I should not, madam. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | That’s a good wench—But take heed to your kindness, though. |
| WOMAN | Why, madam? |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | Men are mad things. |
| [[Arcite|ARCITE]] | (to [[Palamon]]) Will ye go forward, cousin? |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | (to her Woman) Canst not thou work such [[Flowers|flowers]] in silk, wench? |
| WOMAN | Yes. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | I’ll have a gown full of ’em, and of these. This is a pretty colour—will’t not do Rarely upon a skirt, wench? |
| WOMAN | Dainty, madam. |
| [[Arcite|ARCITE]] | (to [[Palamon]]) Cousin, cousin, how do you, sir? Why, [[Palamon]]! |
| [[Palamon|PALAMON]] | Never till now was I in [[Prison|prison]], [[Arcite]]. |
| [[Arcite|ARCITE]] | Why, what’s the matter, man? |
| [[Palamon|PALAMON]] | Behold and [[Wonder|wonder]]! [[Arcite]] sees [[Emilia]] By heaven, she is a goddess! |
| [[Arcite|ARCITE]] | Ha! |
| [[Palamon|PALAMON]] | Do reverence. She is a goddess, [[Arcite]]. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | (to her Woman) Of all [[Flowers|flowers]] Methinks a [[Rose|rose]] is best. |
| WOMAN | Why, gentle madam? |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | It is the very emblem of a maid— For when the west [[Wind|wind]] courts her gently, How modestly she blows, and paints the sun With her chaste blushes! When the north comes near her, Rude and impatient, then, like chastity, She locks her beauties in her bud again, And [[Leaves|leaves]] him to base briers. |
| WOMAN | Yet, good madam, Sometimes her modesty will blow so far She falls for’t—a maid, If she have any [[Honour|honour]], would be loath To take example by her. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | Thou art wanton. |
| [[Arcite|ARCITE]] | (to [[Palamon]]) She is wondrous fair. |
| [[Palamon|PALAMON]] | She is all the [[Beauty|beauty]] extant. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | (to her Woman) The sun grows high—let’s walk in. Keep these [[Flowers|flowers]]. We’ll see how close art can come near their colours. I am wondrous merry-hearted—I could laugh now. |
