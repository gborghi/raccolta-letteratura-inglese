| [[Palamon|PALAMON]] | I have worn a lighter— But I shall make it serve. |
| [[Arcite|ARCITE]] | I’ll buckle’t close. |
| [[Palamon|PALAMON]] | By any means. |
| [[Arcite|ARCITE]] | You care not for a grand guard? |
| [[Palamon|PALAMON]] | No, no, we’ll use no horses. I perceive You would fain be at that fight. |
| [[Arcite|ARCITE]] | I am indifferent. |
| [[Palamon|PALAMON]] | [[Faith]], so am I. Good cousin, thrust the buckle Through far enough. |
| [[Arcite|ARCITE]] | I warrant you. |
| [[Palamon|PALAMON]] | My casque now. |
| [[Arcite|ARCITE]] | Will you fight bare-armed? |
| [[Palamon|PALAMON]] | We shall be the nimbler. |
| [[Arcite|ARCITE]] | But use your gauntlets, though—those are o’th’ least. Prithee take mine, good cousin. |
| [[Palamon|PALAMON]] | Thank you, [[Arcite]]. How do I look? Am I fall’n much away? |
| [[Arcite|ARCITE]] | [[Faith]], very little—[[Love|love]] has used you kindly. |
| [[Palamon|PALAMON]] | I’ll warrant thee, I’ll strike [[Home|home]]. |
| [[Arcite|ARCITE]] | Do, and spare not— I’ll give you cause, sweet cousin. |
| [[Palamon|PALAMON]] | Now to you, sir. [[Palamon]] arms [[Arcite]] Methinks this armour’s very like that, [[Arcite]], Thou wor’st that day the three kings fell, but lighter. |
| [[Arcite|ARCITE]] | That was a very good one, and that day, I [[Well|well]] remember, you outdid me, cousin. I never saw such valour. When you charged Upon the left wing of the enemy, I spurred hard to come up, and under me I had a right good [[Horse|horse]]. |
| [[Palamon|PALAMON]] | You had indeed— A bright bay, I remember. |
| [[Arcite|ARCITE]] | Yes. But all Was vainly laboured in me—you outwent me, Nor could my wishes reach you. Yet a little I did by [[Imitation|imitation]]. |
| [[Palamon|PALAMON]] | More by virtue— You are modest, cousin. |
| [[Arcite|ARCITE]] | When I saw you charge first, Methought I heard a dreadful clap of thunder Break from the troop. |
| [[Palamon|PALAMON]] | But still before that flew The [[Lightning|lightning]] of your valour. Stay a little, Is not this piece too strait? |
| [[Arcite|ARCITE]] | No, no, ’tis [[Well|well]]. |
| [[Palamon|PALAMON]] | I would have nothing hurt thee but my [[Sword|sword]]— A bruise would be dishonour. |
| [[Arcite|ARCITE]] | Now I am perfect. |
| [[Palamon|PALAMON]] | Stand off, then. |
| [[Arcite|ARCITE]] | Take my [[Sword|sword]]; I hold it better. |
| [[Palamon|PALAMON]] | I thank ye. No, keep it—your life lies on it. Here’s one—if it but hold, I ask no more For all my hopes. My cause and [[Honour|honour]] guard me. |
| [[Arcite|ARCITE]] | And me, my [[Love|love]]. They bow several ways, then advance and stand Is there aught else to say? |
| [[Palamon|PALAMON]] | This only, and no more. Thou art mine aunt’s son, And that [[Blood|blood]] we [[Desire|desire]] to shed is mutual: In me, thine, and in thee, mine. My [[Sword|sword]] Is in my hand, and if thou kill’st me, The gods and I forgive thee. If there be A place prepared for those that [[Sleep|sleep]] in [[Honour|honour]], I wish his weary [[Soul|soul]] that falls may win it. Fight bravely, cousin. Give me thy noble hand. |
| [[Arcite|ARCITE]] | Here, [[Palamon]]. This hand shall never more Come near thee with such [[Friendship|friendship]]. |
| [[Palamon|PALAMON]] | I commend thee. |
| [[Arcite|ARCITE]] | If I fall, curse me, and say I was a coward— For none but such dare die in these just trials. Once more farewell, my cousin. |
| [[Palamon|PALAMON]] | Farewell, [[Arcite]]. Fight. Horns within; they stand |
| [[Arcite|ARCITE]] | Lo, cousin, lo, our folly has undone us. |
| [[Palamon|PALAMON]] | Why? |
| [[Arcite|ARCITE]] | This is the Duke a-hunting, as I told you. If we be found, we are wretched. O, retire, For [[Honour|honour]]’s sake, and safely, presently, Into your bush again. Sir, we shall find Too many hours to die. In, gentle cousin— If you be seen, you perish instantly For breaking [[Prison|prison]], and I, if you reveal me, For my contempt. Then all the world will scorn us, And say we had a noble difference, But base disposers of it. |
