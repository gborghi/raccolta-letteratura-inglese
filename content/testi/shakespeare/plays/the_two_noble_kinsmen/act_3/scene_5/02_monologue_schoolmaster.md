| SCHOOLMASTER | Fie, fie, What tediosity and disinsanity Is here among yet Have my rudiments Been laboured so long with ye, milked unto ye, And, by a figure, even the very plum-broth And marrow of my understanding laid upon ye? And do you still cry ‘where?’ and ‘how?’ and ‘wherefore?’ You most coarse frieze capacities, ye jean judgements, Have I said, ‘thus let be’, and ‘there let be’, And ‘then let be’, and no man understand me? Proh deum, medius fidius-ye are all dunces. Forwhy, here stand I. Here the Duke comes. There are you, Close in the thicket. The Duke appears. I meet him, And unto him I utter learnèd things And many figures. He hears, and nods, and hums, And then cries, ‘Rare!’, and I go forward. At length I fling my cap up—mark there—then do you, As once did Meleager and the boar, Break comely out before him, like true [[lovers]], Cast yourselves in a [[Body|body]] decently, And sweetly, by a figure, trace and turn, boys. |
