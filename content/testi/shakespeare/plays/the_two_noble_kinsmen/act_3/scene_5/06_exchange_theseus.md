| [[Theseus|THESEUS]] | Ay, ay, by any means, dear dominie. |
| PIRITHOUS | Produce. |
| SCHOOLMASTER | (knocks for the [[Dance|dance]]) Intrate filii, come forth and foot it. He flings up his cap. Music. |
| *(didascalia)* | The Schoolmaster ushers in |
| SCHOOLMASTER | May Lord, Servingman, A Country Clown, or Shepherd, An Host, A He-babion, May Lady. |
| SCHOOLMASTER | Chambermaid. Country Wench. |
| SCHOOLMASTER | Hostess. She-babion. |
| SCHOOLMASTER | A He-[[Fool|fool]], The Jailer’s Daughter as |
| *(didascalia)* | She-[[Fool|fool]]. All these persons apparelled to the life, the men issuing out of one door and the wenches from the other. They [[Dance|dance]] a morris Ladies, if we have been merry, And have pleased ye with a derry, And a derry, and a down, Say the schoolmaster’s no clown. Duke, if we have pleased thee too, And have done as good boys should do, Give us but a tree or twain For a maypole, and again, Ere another year run out, We’ll make thee laugh, and all this rout. |
| [[Theseus|THESEUS]] | Take twenty, dominie. (To [[Hippolyta]]) How does my sweetheart? |
| [[Hippolyta|HIPPOLYTA]] | Never so pleased, sir. |
| [[Emilia|EMILIA]] | ’Twas an excellent [[Dance|dance]], And for a preface, I never heard a better. |
| [[Theseus|THESEUS]] | Schoolmaster, I thank you. One see ’em all rewarded. |
| PIRITHOUS | And here’s something to paint your pole withal. He gives them money |
| [[Theseus|THESEUS]] | Now to our sports again. |
| SCHOOLMASTER | May the stag thou hunt’st stand long, And thy [[dogs]] be swift and strong; May they kill him without lets, And the ladies eat his dowsets. Exeunt [[Theseus]] and train. [[Wind]] horns within Come, we are all made. Dii deaeque omnes, Ye have danced rarely, wenches. Exeunt |
