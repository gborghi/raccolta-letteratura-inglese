| SCHOOLMASTER | If you but favour, our country pastime made is. We are a few of those collected here, That ruder tongues distinguish ‘villager’; And to say verity, and not to [[Fable|fable]], We are a merry rout, or else a rabble, Or company, or, by a figure, [[Chorus|chorus]], That fore thy dignity will [[Dance|dance]] a morris. And I, that am the rectifier of all, By title pedagogus, that let fall The birch upon the breeches of the small ones, And humble with a ferula the tall ones, Do here present this [[Machine|machine]], or this frame; And dainty Duke, whose doughty dismal fame From Dis to Daedalus, from post to pillar, Is blown abroad, help me, thy poor [[Well|well]]-willer, And with thy twinkling eyes, look right and straight Upon this mighty ‘Moor’—of mickle weight— ‘Ice’ now comes in, which, being glued together, Makes ‘morris’, and the cause that we came hither. The [[Body|body]] of our sport, of no small study, I first appear, though rude, and raw, and muddy, To speak, before thy noble grace, this tenor At whose great feet I offer up my penner. The next, the Lord of May and Lady bright; The Chambermaid and Servingman, by [[Night|night]] That seek out silent hanging; then mine Host And his fat Spouse, that welcomes, to their cost, The galled traveller, and with a beck’ning Informs the tapster to inflame the reck‘ning; Then the beest-eating Clown; and next, the [[Fool]]; The babion with long tail and eke long tool, Cum multis aliis that make a [[Dance|dance]]— Say ‘ay’, and all shall presently advance. |
