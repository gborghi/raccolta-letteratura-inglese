| BOYET | What would you with the Princess? |
| BIRON | Nothing but peace and gentle visitation. |
| [[Rosaline|ROSALINE]] | What would they, say they? |
| BOYET | Nothing but peace and gentle visitation. |
| [[Rosaline|ROSALINE]] | Why, that they have, and bid them so be gone. |
| BOYET | She says you have it, and you may be gone. |
| [[King|KING]] | Say to her we have measured many miles To tread a measure with her on this grass. |
| BOYET | They say that they have measured many a mile To tread a measure with you on this grass. |
| [[Rosaline|ROSALINE]] | It is not so. Ask them how many inches Is in one mile. If they have measured many, The measure then of one is easily told. |
| BOYET | If to come hither you have measured miles, And many miles, the Princess bids you tell How many inches doth fill up one mile. |
| BIRON | Tell her we measure them by weary steps. |
| BOYET | She hears herself. |
| [[Rosaline|ROSALINE]] | How many weary steps Of many weary miles you have o’ergone Are numbered in the travel of one mile? |
| BIRON | We number nothing that we spend for you. Our duty is so rich, so infinite, That we may do it still without account. Vouchsafe to show the sunshine of your face That we, like savages, may worship it. |
| [[Rosaline|ROSALINE]] | My face is but a [[Moon|moon]], and clouded, too. |
| [[King|KING]] | Blessed are clouds to do as such clouds do. Vouchsafe, bright [[Moon|moon]], and these thy [[Stars|stars]], to shine, Those clouds removed, upon our watery eyne. |
| [[Rosaline|ROSALINE]] | O vain petitioner, beg a greater matter. Thou now requests but moonshine in the [[Water|water]]. |
| [[King|KING]] | Then in our measure do but vouchsafe one change. Thou bid’st me beg; this begging is not strange. |
| [[Rosaline|ROSALINE]] | Play, music, then.  Music plays Nay, you must do it soon. Not yet?—no [[Dance|dance]]! Thus change I like the [[Moon|moon]]. |
| [[King|KING]] | Will you not [[Dance|dance]]? How come you thus estranged? |
| [[Rosaline|ROSALINE]] | You took the [[Moon|moon]] at full, but now she’s changed. |
| [[King|KING]] | Yet still she is the [[Moon|moon]], and I the man.   215 The music plays, vouchsafe some motion to it. |
| [[Rosaline|ROSALINE]] | Our ears vouchsafe it. |
| [[King|KING]] | But your legs should do it. |
| [[Rosaline|ROSALINE]] | Since you are strangers and come here by chance We’ll not be nice. Take hands. We will not [[Dance|dance]]. |
| [[King|KING]] | Why take we hands, then? |
| [[Rosaline|ROSALINE]] | Only to part friends. Curtsy, sweethearts, and so the measure ends. |
| [[King|KING]] | More measure of this measure, be not nice. |
| [[Rosaline|ROSALINE]] | We can afford no more at such a price. |
| [[King|KING]] | Price you yourselves. What buys your company? |
| [[Rosaline|ROSALINE]] | Your absence only. |
| [[King|KING]] | That can never be. |
| [[Rosaline|ROSALINE]] | Then cannot we be bought, and so adieu— Twice to your visor, and half once to you. |
| [[King|KING]] | If you deny to [[Dance|dance]], let’s hold more chat. |
| [[Rosaline|ROSALINE]] | In private, then. |
| [[King|KING]] | I am best pleased with that. The [[King]] and [[Rosaline]] talk apart |
| BIRON | (to the Princess, taking her for [[Rosaline]]) White-handed mistress, one sweet word with thee. |
| PRINCESS | Honey and milk and sugar—there is three. |
| BIRON | Nay then, two treys, an if you grow so nice— Metheglin, wort, and malmsey—[[Well|well]] run, dice! There’s half-a-dozen sweets. |
| PRINCESS | Seventh sweet, adieu. Since you can cog, I’ll play no more with you. |
