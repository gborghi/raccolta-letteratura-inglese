| CATHERINE | What, was your visor made without a tongue? |
| [[Longueville|LONGUEVILLE]] | (taking Catherine for [[Maria]]) I know the reason, lady, why you ask. |
| CATHERINE | O, for your reason ! Quickly, sir, I long. |
| [[Longueville|LONGUEVILLE]] | You have a [[Double|double]] tongue within your mask, And would afford my speechless visor half. |
| CATHERINE | ‘Veal’, quoth the Dutchman. Is not veal a calf? |
| [[Longueville|LONGUEVILLE]] | A calf, fair lady? |
| CATHERINE | No, a fair lord calf. |
| [[Longueville|LONGUEVILLE]] | Let’s part the word. |
| CATHERINE | No, I’ll not be your half. Take all and wean it, it may prove an ox. |
| [[Longueville|LONGUEVILLE]] | Look how you butt yourself in these sharp mocks ! Will you give horns, chaste lady? Do not so. |
| CATHERINE | Then die a calf before your horns do grow. |
| [[Longueville|LONGUEVILLE]] | One word in private with you ere I die. |
| CATHERINE | Bleat softly, then. The butcher hears you cry. [[Longueville]] and Catherine talk apart |
| BOYET | The tongues of mocking wenches are as keen As is the razor’s edge invisible, Cutting a smaller hair than may be seen, Above the sense of sense; so sensible Seemeth their conference. Their conceits have wings Fleeter than arrows, bullets, [[Wind|wind]], thought, swifter things. |
| [[Rosaline|ROSALINE]] | Not one word more, my maids. Break off, break off. |
| BIRON | By heaven, all dry-beaten with pure scoff! |
| [[King|KING]] | Farewell, mad wenches, you have simple wits. Exeunt the [[King]], lords, and blackamoors The ladies unmask |
| PRINCESS | Twenty adieus, my frozen Muscovites. Are these the breed of wits so wondered at? |
| BOYET | Tapers they are, with your sweet breaths puffed out. |
| [[Rosaline|ROSALINE]] | [[Well]]-liking wits they have; gross, gross; fat, fat. |
| PRINCESS | O [[Poverty|poverty]] in wit, kingly-poor flout ! Will they not, think you, hang themselves tonight, Or ever but in visors show their faces? This pert Biron was out of count’nance quite. |
| [[Rosaline|ROSALINE]] | Ah, they were all in lamentable cases. The [[King]] was weeping-ripe for a good word. |
| PRINCESS | Biron did swear himself out of all suit. |
| [[Maria|MARIA]] | [[Dumaine]] was at my service, and his [[Sword|sword]]. ‘Non point,’ quoth I. My servant straight was mute. |
| CATHERINE | Lord [[Longueville]] said I came o’er his heart, And trow you what he called me? |
| PRINCESS | ‘Qualm’, perhaps. |
| CATHERINE | Yes, in good [[Faith|faith]]. |
| PRINCESS | Go, sickness as thou art. |
| [[Rosaline|ROSALINE]] | [[Well]], better wits have worn plain statute-caps. But will you hear? The [[King]] is my [[Love|love]] sworn. |
| PRINCESS | And quick Biron hath plighted [[Faith|faith]] to me. |
| CATHERINE | And [[Longueville]] was for my service born. |
| [[Maria|MARIA]] | [[Dumaine]] is mine, as sure as bark on tree. |
| BOYET | Madam, and pretty mistresses, give ear. Immediately they will again be here In their own shapes, for it can never be They will digest this harsh indignity. |
