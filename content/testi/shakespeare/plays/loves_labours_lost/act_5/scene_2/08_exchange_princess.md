| PRINCESS | You nickname virtue. ‘Vice’ you should have spoke, For virtue’s office never breaks men’s troth. Now by my maiden [[Honour|honour]], yet as pure As the unsullied lily, I protest, A world of torments though I should endure, I would not yield to be your [[house]]’s guest, So much I hate a breaking cause to be Of heavenly oaths, vowed with integrity. |
| [[King|KING]] | O, you have lived in desolation here, Unseen, unvisited, much to our shame. |
| PRINCESS | Not so, my lord. It is not so, I swear. We have had pastimes here, and pleasant game. A mess of Russians left us but of late. |
| [[King|KING]] | How, madam? Russians? |
| PRINCESS | Ay, in [[Truth|truth]], my lord. Trim gallants, full of courtship and of state. |
| [[Rosaline|ROSALINE]] | Madam, speak true.—It is not so, my lord. My lady, to the manner of the days, In courtesy gives undeserving praise. We four indeed confronted were with four In Russian habit. Here they stayed an hour, And talked apace, and in that hour, my lord, They did not bless us with one happy word. I dare not call them fools, but this I think: When they are thirsty, fools would fain have drink. |
| BIRON | This jest is dry to me. Gentle sweet, Your wits makes wise things foolish. When we greet, With eyes’ best seeing, heaven’s fiery eye, By light we lose light. Your capacity Is of that [[Nature|nature]] that to your huge store Wise things seem foolish, and rich things but poor. |
| [[Rosaline|ROSALINE]] | This proves you wise and rich, for in my eye— |
| BIRON | I am a [[Fool|fool]], and full of [[Poverty|poverty]]. |
| [[Rosaline|ROSALINE]] | But that you take what doth to you belong It were a fault to snatch words from my tongue. |
| BIRON | O, I am yours, and all that I possess. |
| [[Rosaline|ROSALINE]] | All the [[Fool|fool]] mine! |
| BIRON | I cannot give you less. |
| [[Rosaline|ROSALINE]] | Which of the visors was it that you wore? |
| BIRON | Where? When? What visor? Why demand you this? |
| [[Rosaline|ROSALINE]] | There, then, that visor, that superfluous case, That hid the worse and showed the better face. |
| [[King|KING]] | (aside to the lords) We were descried. They’ll mock us now, downright. |
| [[Dumaine|DUMAINE]] | (aside to the [[King]]) Let us confess, and turn it to a jest. |
| PRINCESS | Amazed, my lord? Why looks your highness sad? |
| [[Rosaline|ROSALINE]] | Help, hold his brows, he’ll swoon. Why look you pale? Seasick, I think, coming from Muscovy. |
