| *(didascalia)* | Enter the [[Princess of France]] with three attending ladies-[[Maria]], Catherine, and [[Rosaline]]-and three lords, one named Boyet |
| BOYET | Now, madam, summon up your dearest spirits.<br>Consider who the [[King]] your father sends,<br>To whom he sends, and what’s his embassy:<br>Yourself, held precious in the world’s esteem,<br>To parley with the sole inheritor<br>Of all perfections that a man may owe,<br>Matchless Navarre; the plea of no less weight<br>Than Aquitaine, a dowry for a [[Queen|queen]].<br>Be now as prodigal of all dear grace<br>As [[Nature|nature]] was in making graces dear<br>When she did starve the general world beside<br>And prodigally gave them all to you. |
| PRINCESS | Good Lord Boyet, my [[Beauty|beauty]], though but mean,<br>Needs not the painted flourish of your praise.<br>[[Beauty]] is bought by judgement of the eye,<br>Not uttered by base sale of chapmen’s tongues.<br>I am less proud to hear you tell my worth<br>Than you much willing to be counted wise<br>In spending your wit in the praise of mine.<br>But now to task the tasker: good Boyet,<br>You are not ignorant all-telling fame<br>Doth noise abroad Navarre hath made a vow<br>Till painful study shall outwear three years<br>No woman may approach his silent [[Court|court]].<br>Therefore to’s seemeth it a needful course,<br>Before we enter his forbidden gates,<br>To know his pleasure; and in that behalf,<br>Bold of your worthiness, we single you<br>As our best-moving fair solicitor.<br>Tell him the daughter of the [[King of France]]<br>On serious business, craving quick dispatch,<br>Importunes personal conference with his grace.<br>Haste, signify so much while we attend,<br>Like humble-visaged suitors, his high will. |
| BOYET | Proud of employment, willingly I go. |
| PRINCESS | All [[Pride|pride]] is willing [[Pride|pride]], and yours is so. Exit Boyet<br>Who are the votaries, my loving lords,<br>That are vow-fellows with this virtuous duke?<br>A LORD<br>Lord [[Longueville]] is one. |
| PRINCESS | Know you the man? |
| [[Maria|MARIA]] | I know him, madam. At a [[Marriage|marriage]] feast<br>Between Lord Perigord and the beauteous heir<br>Of [[Jaques]] Fauconbridge solemnized<br>In Normandy saw I this [[Longueville]].<br>A man of sovereign parts he is esteemed,<br>[[Well]] fitted in arts, glorious in arms.<br>Nothing becomes him ill that he would [[Well|well]].<br>The only soil of his fair virtue’s gloss-<br>If virtue’s gloss will stain with any soil-<br>Is a sharp wit matched with too blunt a will,<br>Whose edge hath power to cut, whose will still wills<br>It should none spare that come within his power. |
| PRINCESS | Some merry mocking lord, belike-is’t so? |
| [[Maria|MARIA]] | They say so most that most his humours know. |
| PRINCESS | Such short-lived wits do wither as they grow.<br>Who are the rest? |
| CATHERINE | The young [[Dumaine]], a [[Well|well]]-accomplished youth,<br>Of all that virtue [[Love|love]] for virtue loved.<br>Most power to do most harm, least knowing ill,<br>For he hath wit to make an ill shape good,<br>And shape to win grace, though he had no wit.<br>I saw him at the Duke Alençon’s once,<br>And much too little of that good I saw<br>Is my report to his great worthiness. |
| [[Rosaline|ROSALINE]] | Another of these students at that [[Time|time]]<br>Was there with him, if I have heard a [[Truth|truth]].<br>Biron they call him, but a merrier man, |
| *(didascalia)* | Within the limit of becoming mirth, |
| [[Rosaline|ROSALINE]] | I never spent an hour’s talk withal.<br>His eye begets occasion for his wit,<br>For every object that the one doth catch<br>The other turns to a mirth-moving jest,<br>Which his fair tongue, conceit’s expositor,<br>Delivers in such apt and gracious words<br>That aged ears play truant at his tales,<br>And younger hearings are quite ravished,<br>So sweet and voluble is his discourse. |
| PRINCESS | God bless my ladies, are they all in [[Love|love]],<br>That every one her own hath garnished<br>With such bedecking ornaments of praise?<br>A LORD<br>Here comes Boyet. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Boyet |
| PRINCESS | Now, what admittance, lord? |
| BOYET | Navarre had notice of your fair approach,<br>And he and his competitors in oath<br>Were all addressed to meet you, gentle lady,<br>Before I came. Marry, thus much I have learnt:<br>He rather means to lodge you in the field,<br>Like one that comes here to besiege his [[Court|court]],<br>Than seek a dispensation for his oath<br>To let you enter his unpeopled [[house]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Navarre, [[Longueville]], [[Dumaine]], and Biron |
| BOYET | Here comes Navarre. |
| [[King|KING]] | Fair Princess, welcome to the [[Court|court]] of Navarre. |
| PRINCESS | ‘Fair’ I give you back again, and welcome I have not yet. The roof of this [[Court|court]] is too high to be yours, and welcome to the wide fields too base to be mine. |
| [[King|KING]] | You shall be welcome, madam, to my [[Court|court]]. |
| PRINCESS | I will be welcome, then. Conduct me thither. |
| [[King|KING]] | Hear me, dear lady. I have sworn an oath— |
| PRINCESS | Our Lady help my lord He’ll be forsworn. |
| [[King|KING]] | Not for the world, fair madam, by my will. |
| PRINCESS | Why, will shall break it—will and nothing else. |
| [[King|KING]] | Your ladyship is ignorant what it is. |
| PRINCESS | Were my lord so his ignorance were wise,<br>Where now his knowledge must prove ignorance.<br>I hear your grace hath sworn out housekeeping.<br>’Tis deadly sin to keep that oath, my lord,<br>And sin to break it.<br>But pardon me, I am too sudden-bold.<br>To teach a teacher ill beseemeth me.<br>Vouchsafe to read the purpose of my coming,<br>And suddenly resolve me in my suit.<br>She gives him a paper |
| [[King|KING]] | Madam, I will, if suddenly I may. |
| PRINCESS | You will the sooner that I were away,<br>For you’ll prove perjured if you make me stay.<br>Navarre reads the paper |
| BIRON | (to [[Rosaline]])<br>Did not I [[Dance|dance]] with you in Brabant once? |
| *(didascalia)* | FROSALINE |
| BIRON | Did not I [[Dance|dance]] with you in Brabant once? |
| BIRON | I know you did. |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Rosaline|ROSALINE]] |
| BIRON | How needless was it then<br>To ask the question! |
| BIRON | You must not be so quick. |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Rosaline|ROSALINE]] |
| BIRON | ‘Tis ’long of you, that spur me with such questions. |
| BIRON | Your wit’s too hot, it speeds too fast, ’twill tire. |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Rosaline|ROSALINE]] |
| BIRON | Not till it leave the rider in the mire. |
| BIRON | What [[Time|time]] o’ day? |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Rosaline|ROSALINE]] |
| BIRON | The hour that fools should ask. |
| BIRON | Now fair befall your mask. |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Rosaline|ROSALINE]] |
| BIRON | Fair fall the face it covers. |
| BIRON | And send you many [[lovers]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Rosaline|ROSALINE]] |
| BIRON | Amen, so you be none. |
| BIRON | Nay, then will I be gone. |
| [[King|KING]] | (to the Princess)<br>Madam, your father here doth intimate<br>The payment of a hundred thousand crowns,<br>Being but the one-half of an entire sum<br>Disbursed by my father in his wars.<br>But say that he or we—as neither have—<br>Received that sum, yet there remains unpaid<br>A hundred thousand more, in surety of the which<br>One part of Aquitaine is bound to us,<br>Although not valued to the money’s worth.<br>If then the [[King]] your father will restore<br>But that one half which is unsatisfied,<br>We will give up our right in Aquitaine<br>And hold fair [[Friendship|friendship]] with his majesty.<br>But that, it seems, he little purposeth,<br>For here he doth demand to have repaid<br>A hundred thousand crowns, and not demands,<br>On payment of a hundred thousand crowns,<br>To have his title live in Aquitaine,<br>Which we much rather had depart withal,<br>And have the money by our father lent,<br>Than Aquitaine, so gelded as it is.<br>Dear Princess, were not his requests so far<br>From reason’s yielding, your fair [[Self|self]] should make<br>A yielding ’gainst some reason in my breast,<br>And go [[Well|well]] satisfied to France again. |
| PRINCESS | You do the [[King]] my father too much wrong,<br>And wrong the reputation of your name,<br>In so unseeming to confess receipt<br>Of that which hath so faithfully been paid. |
| [[King|KING]] | I do protest I never heard of it,<br>And if you prove it I’ll repay it back<br>Or yield up Aquitaine. |
| PRINCESS | We arrest your word.<br>Boyet, you can produce acquittances<br>For such a sum from special officers<br>Of Charles, his father. |
| [[King|KING]] | Satisfy me so. |
| BOYET | So please your grace, the packet is not come<br>Where that and other specialties are bound.<br>Tomorrow you shall have a sight of them. |
| [[King|KING]] | It shall suffice me, at which interview<br>All liberal reason I will yield unto.<br>Meantime receive such welcome at my hand<br>As [[Honour|honour]], without breach of [[Honour|honour]], may<br>Make tender of to thy true worthiness.<br>You may not come, fair princess, within my gates,<br>But here without you shall be so received<br>As you shall deem yourself lodged in my heart,<br>Though so denied fair harbour in my [[house]].<br>Your own good thoughts excuse me, and farewell.<br>Tomorrow shall we visit you again. |
| PRINCESS | Sweet health and fair desires consort your grace. |
| [[King|KING]] | Thy own wish wish I thee in every place. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit with [[Longueville]] and [[Dumaine]] |
| BIRON | (to [[Rosaline]]) Lady, I will commend you to mine own heart. |
| [[Rosaline|ROSALINE]] | Pray you, do my commendations. I would be glad to see it. |
| BIRON | I would you heard it groan. |
| [[Rosaline|ROSALINE]] | Is the [[Fool|fool]] sick? |
| BIRON | Sick at the heart. |
| [[Rosaline|ROSALINE]] | Alack, let it [[Blood|blood]]. |
| BIRON | Would that do it good? |
| [[Rosaline|ROSALINE]] | My physic says ‘Ay’. |
| BIRON | Will you prick’t with your eye? |
| [[Rosaline|ROSALINE]] | Non point, with my knife. |
| BIRON | Now God save thy life. |
| [[Rosaline|ROSALINE]] | And yours, from long living. |
| BIRON | I cannot stay thanksgiving. Exit |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Dumaine]] |
| [[Dumaine|DUMAINE]] | (to Boyet)<br>Sir, I pray you a word. What lady is that same? |
| BOYET | The heir of Alençon, Catherine her name. |
| [[Dumaine|DUMAINE]] | A gallant lady. Monsieur, fare you [[Well|well]]. Exit |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Longueville]] |
| [[Longueville|LONGUEVILLE]] | (to Boyet)<br>I beseech you a word, what is she in the white? |
| BOYET | A woman sometimes, an you saw her in the light. |
| [[Longueville|LONGUEVILLE]] | Perchance light in the light. I [[Desire|desire]] her name. |
| BOYET | She hath but one for herself; to [[Desire|desire]] that were a shame. |
| [[Longueville|LONGUEVILLE]] | Pray you, sir, whose daughter? |
| BOYET | Her mother’s, I have heard. |
| [[Longueville|LONGUEVILLE]] | God’s blessing on your beard ! |
| BOYET | Good sir, be not offended.<br>She is an heir of Fauconbridge. |
| [[Longueville|LONGUEVILLE]] | Nay, my choler is ended.<br>She is a most sweet lady. |
| BOYET | Not unlike, sir. That may be. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit [[Longueville]] |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Biron |
| BIRON | What’s her name in the cap? |
| BOYET | [[Rosaline]], by good hap. |
| BIRON | Is she wedded or no? |
| BOYET | To her will, sir, or so. |
| BIRON | O, you are welcome, sir. Adieu. |
| BOYET | Farewell to me, sir, and welcome to you. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit Biron |
| [[Maria|MARIA]] | That last is Biron, the merry madcap lord.<br>Not a word with him but a jest. |
| BOYET | And every jest but a word. |
| PRINCESS | It was [[Well|well]] done of you to take him at his word. |
| BOYET | I was as willing to grapple as he was to board. |
| *(didascalia)* | CATHERINE |
| BOYET | Two hot sheeps, marry. |
| BOYET | And wherefore not ships?<br>No sheep, sweet [[Lamb|lamb]], unless we feed on your lips. |
| *(didascalia)* | CATHERINE |
| BOYET | You sheep and I pasture—shall that finish the jest? |
| BOYET | So you grant pasture for me. |
| *(didascalia)* | CATHERINE |
| BOYET | Not so, gentle beast.<br>My lips are no common, though several they be. |
| BOYET | Belonging to whom? |
| *(didascalia)* | CATHERINE |
| BOYET | To my fortunes and me. |
| PRINCESS | Good wits will be jangling; but, gentles, agree.<br>This civil war of wits were much better used<br>On Navarre and his bookmen, for here ’tis abused. |
| BOYET | If my observation, which very seldom lies,<br>By the heart’s still rhetoric disclosed with eyes,<br>Deceive me not now, Navarre is infected. |
| PRINCESS | With what? |
| BOYET | With that which we [[lovers]] entitle ‘affected’. |
| PRINCESS | Your reason? |
| BOYET | Why, all his behaviours did make their retire<br>To the [[Court|court]] of his eye, peeping thorough [[Desire|desire]].<br>His heart like an agate with your print impressed,<br>Proud with his form, in his eye [[Pride|pride]] expressed.<br>His tongue, all impatient to speak and not see,<br>Did stumble with haste in his eyesight to be.<br>All senses to that sense did make their repair,<br>To feel only looking on fairest of fair.<br>Methought all his senses were locked in his eye,<br>As jewels in crystal, for some prince to buy,<br>Who, tendering their own worth from where they were glassed,<br>Did point you to buy them along as you passed.<br>His face’s own margin did quote such amazes<br>That all eyes saw his eyes enchanted with gazes.<br>I’ll give you Aquitaine and all that is his<br>An you give him for my sake but one loving kiss. |
| PRINCESS | Come, to our pavilion. Boyet is disposed. |
| BOYET | But to speak that in words which his eye hath disclosed.<br>I only have made a mouth of his eye<br>By adding a tongue, which I know will not lie. |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Rosaline|ROSALINE]] |
| BOYET | Thou art an old [[Love|love]]-monger, and speak’st skilfully. |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Maria|MARIA]] |
| BOYET | He is Cupid’s grandfather, and learns news of him. |
| *(didascalia)* | CATHERINE |
| BOYET | Then was [[Venus]] like her mother, for her father is but grim. |
| BOYET | Do you hear, my mad wenches? |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Maria|MARIA]] |
| BOYET | No. |
| BOYET | What then, do you see? |
| *(didascalia)* | CATHERINE |
| BOYET | Ay—our way to be gone. |
| BOYET | You are too hard for me. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt |
