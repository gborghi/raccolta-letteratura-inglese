| *(didascalia)* | With the drummer playing and trumpeter sounding, enter the [[Earl of Warwick]], the Duke of Somerset, and the rest bringing [[King]] Edward out in his gown, sitting in a chair. [[Richard]] [[Duke of Gloucester]] and Lord [[Hastings]] flies over the stage |
| SOMERSET | What are they that fly there? |
| WARWICK | [[Richard]] and [[Hastings]]—let them go. Here is the Duke. |
| [[King|KING]] EDWARD | ‘The Duke’! Why, Warwick, when we parted,<br>Thou calledst me [[King|king]]. |
| WARWICK | Ay, but the case is altered.<br>When you disgraced me in my embassade,<br>Then I degraded you from being [[King|king]],<br>And come now to create you [[Duke of York]].<br>Alas, how should you govern any kingdom<br>That know not how to use ambassadors,<br>Nor how to be contented with one wife,<br>Nor how to use your brothers brotherly,<br>Nor how to study for the people’s welfare,<br>Nor how to shroud yourself from enemies? |
| [[King|KING]] EDWARD | (seeing George)<br>Yea, brother of [[Clarence]], art thou here too?<br>Nay, then, I see that Edward needs must down.<br>Yet, Warwick, in despite of all mischance,<br>Of thee thyself and all thy complices,<br>Edward will always bear himself as [[King|king]].<br>Though fortune’s malice overthrow my state,<br>My mind exceeds the compass of her [[Wheel|wheel]]. |
| WARWICK | Then, for his mind, be Edward England’s [[King|king]].<br>Warwick takes off Edward’s [[Crown|crown]]<br>But Henry now shall wear the English [[Crown|crown]],<br>And be true [[King|king]] indeed, thou but the [[Shadow|shadow]].<br>My lord of Somerset, at my request,<br>See that, forthwith, Duke Edward be conveyed<br>Unto my brother, Archbishop of York.<br>When I have fought with Pembroke and his fellows,<br>I’ll follow you, and tell what answer<br>Louis and the Lady Bona send to him.<br>Now for a while farewell, good [[Duke of York]].<br>They begin to lead Edward out forcibly |
| [[King|KING]] EDWARD | What fates impose, that men must needs abide.<br>It boots not to resist both [[Wind|wind]] and tide. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt some with Edward |
| [[Oxford|OXFORD]] | What now remains, my lords, for us to do<br>But march to [[London]] with our soldiers? |
| WARWICK | Ay, that’s the first thing that we have to do—<br>To free [[King]] Henry from [[Imprisonment|imprisonment]]<br>And see him seated in the regal throne. Exeunt |
