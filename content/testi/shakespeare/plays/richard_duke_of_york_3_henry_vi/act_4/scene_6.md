| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Richard]] [[Duke of Gloucester]], Lord [[Hastings]], and Sir William Stanley, with soldiers |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] OF [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Now my lord [[Hastings]] and Sir William Stanley,<br>Leave off to [[Wonder|wonder]] why I drew you hither<br>Into this chiefest thicket of the park.<br>Thus stands the case: you know our [[King]], my brother,<br>Is prisoner to the Bishop here, at whose hands<br>He hath good usage and great liberty,<br>And, often but attended with weak guard,<br>Comes hunting this way to disport himself.<br>I have advertised him by secret means<br>That if about this hour he make this way<br>Under the colour of his usual game,<br>He shall here find his friends with [[Horse|horse]] and men<br>To set him free from his captivity. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[King]] Edward and a Huntsman with him |
| HUNTSMAN | This way, my lord—for this way lies the game. |
| [[King|KING]] EDWARD | Nay, this way, man—see where the huntsmen stand.<br>Now, brother of [[Gloucester]], Lord [[Hastings]], and the<br>rest,<br>Stand you thus close to steal the Bishop’s deer? |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] OF [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Brother, the [[Time|time]] and case requireth haste.<br>Your [[Horse|horse]] stands ready at the park corner. |
| [[King|KING]] EDWARD | But whither shall we then? |
| [[Hastings|HASTINGS]] | To Lynn, my lord,<br>And shipped from thence to Flanders. |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Richard|RICHARD]] OF [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] aside |
| [[Hastings|HASTINGS]] | [[Well]] guessed, believe me—for that was my meaning. |
| [[King|KING]] EDWARD | Stanley, I will requite thy forwardness. |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] OF [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | But wherefore stay we? ’Tis no [[Time|time]] to talk. |
| [[King|KING]] EDWARD | Huntsman, what sayst thou? Wilt thou go along? |
| HUNTSMAN | Better do so than tarry and be hanged. |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] OF [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Come then, away—let’s have no more ado. |
| [[King|KING]] EDWARD | Bishop, farewell—shield thee from Warwick’s frown,<br>And pray that I may repossess the [[Crown|crown]]. Exeunt |
