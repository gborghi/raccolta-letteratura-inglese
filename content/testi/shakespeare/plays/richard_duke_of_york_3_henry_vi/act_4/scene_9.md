| *(didascalia)* | Flourish. Enter [[King]] Henry, the [[Earl of Warwick]], the Marquis of Montague, George Duke of [[Clarence]], and the Earl of [[Oxford]] |
| WARWICK | What counsel, lords? Edward from Belgia,<br>With hasty Germans and blunt Hollanders,<br>Hath passed in safety through the narrow seas,<br>And with his troops doth march amain to [[London]],<br>And many giddy people flock to him. |
| [[King|KING]] HENRY | Let’s levy men and beat him back again. |
| GEORGE OF [[Clarence|CLARENCE]] | A little [[Fire|fire]] is quickly trodden out,<br>Which, being suffered, rivers cannot quench. |
| WARWICK | In Warwickshire I have true-hearted friends,<br>Not mutinous in peace, yet bold in war.<br>Those will I muster up. And thou, son [[Clarence]],<br>Shalt stir in Suffolk, Norfolk, and in [[Kent]],<br>The knights and gentlemen to come with thee.<br>Thou, brother Montague, in [[Buckingham]],<br>Northampton, and in Leicestershire shalt find<br>Men [[Well|well]] inclined to hear what thou command’st.<br>And thou, brave [[Oxford]], wondrous [[Well|well]] beloved<br>In Oxfordshire, shalt muster up thy friends.<br>My sovereign, with the loving citizens,<br>Like to his island girt in with the ocean,<br>Or modest Dian circled with her nymphs,<br>Shall rest in [[London]] till we come to him.<br>Fair lords, take leave and stand not to reply.<br>Farewell, my sovereign. |
| [[King|KING]] HENRY | Farewell, my [[Hector]], and my Troy’s true [[Hope|hope]]. |
| GEORGE OF [[Clarence|CLARENCE]] | In sign of [[Truth|truth]], I kiss your highness’ hand.<br>He kisses [[King]] Henry’s hand |
| [[King|KING]] HENRY | [[Well]]-minded [[Clarence]], be thou fortunate. |
| MONTAGUE | Comfort, my lord, and so I take my leave. |
| *(didascalia)* | He kisses [[King]] Henry’s hand |
| [[Oxford|OXFORD]] | And thus I seal my [[Truth|truth]] and bid adieu. |
| *(didascalia)* | He kisses [[King]] Henry’s hand |
| [[King|KING]] HENRY | Sweet [[Oxford]], and my loving Montague,<br>And all at once, once more a happy farewell. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit |
| WARWICK | Farewell, sweet lords—let’s meet at Coventry. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt severally! |
| *(didascalia)* | 4.10 Erenter [[King]] Henry and the [[Duke of Exeter]] |
| [[King|KING]] HENRY | Here at the palace will I rest a while.<br>Cousin of Exeter, what thinks your lordship?<br>Methinks the power that Edward hath in field<br>Should not be able to encounter mine. |
| EXETER | The [[Doubt|doubt]] is that he will seduce the rest. |
| [[King|KING]] HENRY | That’s not my [[Fear|fear]]. My meed hath got me fame.<br>I have not stopped mine ears to their demands,<br>Nor posted off their suits with slow delays.<br>My pity hath been balm to heal their wounds,<br>My mildness hath allayed their swelling griefs,<br>My [[Mercy|mercy]] dried their [[Water|water]]-flowing [[Tears|tears]].<br>I have not been desirous of their wealth,<br>Nor much oppressed them with great subsidies,<br>Nor forward of [[Revenge|revenge]], though they much erred.<br>Then why should they [[Love|love]] Edward more than me?<br>No, Exeter, these graces challenge grace;<br>And when the [[Lion|lion]] fawns upon the [[Lamb|lamb]],<br>The [[Lamb|lamb]] will never cease to follow him. |
| *(didascalia)* | Shout within ‘A Lancaster’, ‘A York’ |
| EXETER | Hark, hark, my ford—what shouts are these? |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[King]] Edward and [[Richard]] [[Duke of Gloucester]], with soldiers |
| [[King|KING]] EDWARD | Seize on the shame-faced Henry—bear him hence,<br>And once again proclaim us [[King]] of England.<br>You are the fount that makes small brooks to flow.<br>Now stops thy [[Spring|spring]]—my sea shall suck them dry,<br>And swell so much the higher by their ebb.<br>Hence with him to the Tower—tet him not speak. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt some with [[King]] Henry and Exeter |
| [[King|KING]] EDWARD | And lords, towards Coventry bend we our course,<br>Where peremptory Warwick now remains.<br>The sun shines hot, and, if we use delay,<br>Cold biting [[Winter|winter]] mars our hoped-for hay. |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] OF [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Away betimes, before his forces join,<br>And take the great-grown traitor unawares.<br>Brave warriors, march amain towards Coventry. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt |
