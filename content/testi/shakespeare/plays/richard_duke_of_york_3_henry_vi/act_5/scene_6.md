| *(didascalia)* | Enter on the walls [[King]] Henry the Sixth, reading a book, [[Richard]] [[Duke of Gloucester]], and the Lieutenant of the Tower |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] OF [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Good day, my lord. What, at your book so hard? |
| [[King|KING]] HENRY | Ay, my good ford—‘my lord’, I should say, rather.<br>‘Tis sin to flatter; ‘good’ was little better.<br>‘Good [[Gloucester]]’ and ‘good devil’ were alike,<br>And both preposterous—therefore not ‘good lord’. |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] OF [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | (to the Lieutenant)<br>Sirrah, leave us to ourselves. We must confer. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit Lieutenant |
| [[King|KING]] HENRY | So flies the reckless shepherd from the wolf;<br>So first the harmless sheep doth yield his fleece,<br>And next his throat unto the butcher’s knife.<br>What scene of [[Death|death]] hath Roscius now to act? |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] OF [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Suspicion always haunts the guilty mind;<br>The thief doth [[Fear|fear]] each bush an officer. |
| [[King|KING]] HENRY | The bird that hath been limèd in a bush<br>With trembling wings misdoubteth every bush.<br>And I, the hapless male to one sweet bird,<br>Have now the fatal object in my eye<br>Where my poor young was limed, was caught and<br>killed. |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] OF [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Why, what a peevish [[Fool|fool]] was that of Crete,<br>That taught his son the office of a fowl!<br>And yet, for all his wings, the [[Fool|fool]] was drowned. |
| [[King|KING]] HENRY | I, Daedalus; my poor boy, Icarus;<br>Thy father, Minos, that denied our course;<br>The sun that seared the wings of my sweet boy,<br>Thy brother Edward; and thyself, the sea,<br>Whose envious gulf did [[Swallow|swallow]] up his life.<br>Ah, kill me with thy weapon, not with words!<br>My breast can better brook thy dagger’s point<br>Than can my ears that tragic [[history]].<br>But wherefore dost thou come? Is’t for my life? |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] OF [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Think’st thou I am an executioner? |
| [[King|KING]] HENRY | A persecutor I am sure thou art;<br>If murdering innocents be executing,<br>Why, then thou art an executioner. |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] OF [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Thy son I killed for his presumption. |
| [[King|KING]] HENRY | Hadst thou been killed when first thou didst presume,<br>Thou hadst not lived to kill a son of mine.<br>And thus I prophesy: that many a thousand<br>Which now mistrust no parcel of my [[Fear|fear]],<br>And many an old man’s sigh, and many a widow‘s,<br>And many an [[Orphan|orphan]]’s [[Water|water]]-standing eye—<br>Men for their sons’, wives for their husbands‘,<br>Orphans for their parents’ timeless [[Death|death]]—<br>Shall rue the hour that ever thou wast born.<br>The owl shrieked at thy birth—an evil sign;<br>The [[Night|night]]-crow cried, aboding luckless [[Time|time]];<br>[[dogs|Dogs]] howled, and hideous tempests shook down trees;<br>[[The Raven|The raven]] rooked her on the chimney’s top;<br>And chatt’ring pies in dismal discords sung.<br>Thy mother felt more than a mother’s [[Pain|pain]],<br>And yet brought forth less than a mother’s [[Hope|hope]]—<br>To wit, an indigested and deformed lump,<br>Not like the fruit of such a goodly tree.<br>Teeth hadst thou in thy head when thou wast born,<br>To signify thou cam‘st to bite the world;<br>And if the rest be true which I have heard<br>Thou cam’st— |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] | I’ll hear no more. Die, [[Prophet|prophet]], in thy speech,<br>He stabs him<br>For this, amongst the rest, was I ordained. |
| [[King|KING]] HENRY | Ay, and for much more slaughter after this.<br>O, God forgive my sins, and pardon thee. He dies |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] OF [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | What—will the aspiring [[Blood|blood]] of Lancaster<br>Sink in the ground? I thought it would have mounted.<br>See how my [[Sword|sword]] weeps for the poor [[King]]’s [[Death|death]].<br>O, may such purple [[Tears|tears]] be alway shed<br>From those that wish the downfall of our [[house]]!<br>If any spark of life be yet remaining,<br>Down, down to hell, and say I sent thee thither—<br>He stabs him again<br>I that have neither pity, [[Love|love]], nor [[Fear|fear]].<br>Indeed, ‘tis true that Henry told me of,<br>For I have often heard my mother say<br>I came into the world with my legs forward.<br>Had I not reason, think ye, to make haste,<br>And seek their ruin that usurped our right?<br>The midwife wondered and the women cried<br>‘O, Jesus bless us, he is born with teeth !—<br>And so I was, which plainly signified<br>That I should snarl and bite and play the dog.<br>Then, since the heavens have shaped my [[Body|body]] so,<br>Let hell make crooked my mind to answer it.<br>I had no father, I am like no father;<br>I have no brother, I am like no brother;<br>And this word, ‘[[Love|love]]’, which greybeards call divine,<br>Be resident in men like one another<br>And not in me—I am myself [[Alone|alone]].<br>[[Clarence]], beware; thou kept’st me from the light—<br>But I will sort a pitchy day for thee.<br>For I will buzz abroad such prophecies<br>That Edward shall be fearful of his life,<br>And then, to purge his [[Fear|fear]], I’ll be thy [[Death|death]].<br>Henry and his son are gone; thou, [[Clarence]], art next;<br>And by one and one I will dispatch the rest,<br>Counting myself but bad till I be best.<br>I’ll throw thy [[Body|body]] in another room<br>And triumph, Henry, in thy day of doom. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit with the [[Body|body]] |
