| *(didascalia)* | Flourish. Enter [[King]] Edward, [[Richard]] [[Duke of Gloucester]], and George Duke of [[Clarence]] with [[Queen Margaret]], the Earl of [[Oxford]], and the Duke of Somerset, guarded |
| [[King|KING]] EDWARD | Now here a period of tumultuous broils.<br>Away with [[Oxford]] to Hames [[Castle]] straight;<br>For Somerset, off with his guilty head.<br>Go bear them hence—I will not hear them speak. |
| [[Oxford|OXFORD]] | For my part, I’ll not trouble thee with words. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit, guarded |
| SOMERSET | Nor I, but stoop with patience to my fortune. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit, guarded |
| [[Queen Margaret|QUEEN MARGARET]] | So part we sadly in this troublous world<br>To meet with joy in sweet Jerusalem. |
| [[King|KING]] EDWARD | Is proclamation made that who finds Edward<br>Shall have a high reward and he his life? |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] OF [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | It is, and lo where youthful Edward comes. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Prince Edward, guarded |
| [[King|KING]] EDWARD | Bring forth the gallant—let us hear him speak.<br>What, can so young a thorn begin to prick?<br>Edward, what satisfaction canst thou make<br>For bearing arms, for stirring up my subjects,<br>And all the trouble thou hast turned me to? |
| PRINCE EDWARD | Speak like a subject, proud ambitious York.<br>Suppose that I am now my father’s mouth—<br>Resign thy chair, and where I stand, kneel thou,<br>Whilst I propose the [[Self|self]]-same words to thee,<br>Which, traitor, thou wouldst have me answer to. |
| [[Queen Margaret|QUEEN MARGARET]] | Ah, that thy father had been so resolved. |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] OF [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | That you might still have worn the petticoat<br>And ne’er have stolen the breech from Lancaster. |
| PRINCE EDWARD | Let Aesop [[Fable|fable]] in a [[Winter|winter]]’s [[Night|night]]—<br>His currish riddles sorts not with this place. |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] OF [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | By heaven, brat, I’ll plague ye for that word. |
| [[Queen Margaret|QUEEN MARGARET]] | Ay, thou wast born to be a plague to men. |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] OF [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | For God’s sake take away this captive scold. |
| PRINCE EDWARD | Nay, take away this scolding crookback rather. |
| [[King|KING]] EDWARD | Peace, wilful boy, or I will charm your tongue. |
| GEORGE OF [[Clarence|CLARENCE]] | (to Prince Edward)<br>Untutored lad, thou art too malapert. |
| PRINCE EDWARD | I know my duty—you are all undutiful.<br>Lascivious Edward, and thou, perjured George,<br>And thou, misshapen Dick—I tell ye all<br>I am your better, traitors as ye are,<br>And thou usurp’st my father’s right and mine. |
| [[King|KING]] EDWARD | Take that, the likeness of this railer here.<br>[[King]] Edward stabs Prince Edward |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] OF [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Sprawl’st thou? Take that, to end thy agony.<br>[[Richard]] stabs Prince Edward |
| GEORGE OF [[Clarence|CLARENCE]] | And there’s for twitting me with perjury. |
| *(didascalia)* | George stabs Prince Edward, who dies |
| [[Queen Margaret|QUEEN MARGARET]] | O, kill me too! |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] OF [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Marry, and shall.<br>He offers to kill her |
| [[King|KING]] EDWARD | Hold, [[Richard]], hold—for we have done too much. |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] OF [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Why should she live to fill the world with words?<br>[[Queen Margaret]] faints |
| [[King|KING]] EDWARD | What—doth she swoon? Use means for her recovery. |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] OF [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | (aside to George)<br>[[Clarence]], excuse me to the [[King]] my brother.<br>I’ll hence to [[London]] on a serious matter.<br>Ere ye come there, be sure to hear some news. |
| GEORGE OF [[Clarence|CLARENCE]] | (aside to [[Richard]]) What? What? |
| [[Richard|RICHARD]] OF [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | (aside to George)<br>The Tower, the Tower. Exit |
| [[Queen Margaret|QUEEN MARGARET]] | O Ned, sweet Ned—speak to thy mother, boy.<br>Canst thou not speak? O traitors, murderers!<br>They that stabbed Caesar shed no [[Blood|blood]] at all,<br>Did not offend, nor were not worthy blame,<br>If this foul deed were by to equal it.<br>He was a man—this, in respect, a [[Child|child]];<br>And men ne’er spend their fury on a [[Child|child]].<br>What’s worse than murderer that I may name it?<br>No, no, my heart will burst an if I speak;<br>And I will speak that so my heart may burst.<br>Butchers and villains! Bloody cannibals!<br>How sweet a plant have you untimely cropped!<br>You have no children, butchers; if you had,<br>The thought of them would have stirred up remorse.<br>But if you ever chance to have a [[Child|child]],<br>Look in his youth to have him so cut off<br>As, deathsmen, you have rid this sweet young Prince! |
| [[King|KING]] EDWARD | Away with her—go, bear her hence perforce. |
| [[Queen Margaret|QUEEN MARGARET]] | Nay, never bear me hence—dispatch me here.<br>Here sheathe thy [[Sword|sword]]—I’ll pardon thee my [[Death|death]].<br>What? Wilt thou not? Then, [[Clarence]], do it thou. |
| GEORGE OF [[Clarence|CLARENCE]] | By heaven, I will not do thee so much ease. |
| [[Queen Margaret|QUEEN MARGARET]] | Good [[Clarence]], do; sweet [[Clarence]], do thou do it. |
| GEORGE OF [[Clarence|CLARENCE]] | Didst thou not hear me swear I would not do it? |
| [[Queen Margaret|QUEEN MARGARET]] | Ay, but thou usest to forswear thyself.<br>‘Twas sin before, but now ’tis [[Charity|charity]].<br>What, wilt thou not? Where is that devil’s butcher,<br>Hard-favoured [[Richard]]? [[Richard]], where art thou?<br>Thou art not here. Murder is thy atms-deed—<br>Petitioners for [[Blood|blood]] thou ne‘er putt’st back. |
| [[King|KING]] EDWARD | Away, I say—t charge ye, bear her hence. |
| [[Queen Margaret|QUEEN MARGARET]] | So come to you and yours as to this Prince! |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit, guarded |
| [[King|KING]] EDWARD | Where’s [[Richard]] gone? |
| *(didascalia)* | To [[London]] all in post—aside and as I guess, |
| GEORGE OF [[Clarence|CLARENCE]] | To make a bloody supper in the Tower. |
| [[King|KING]] EDWARD | He’s sudden if a thing comes in his head.<br>Now march we hence. Discharge the common sort<br>With pay and thanks, and let’s away to [[London]],<br>And see our gentle [[Queen]] how [[Well|well]] she fares.<br>By this I [[Hope|hope]] she hath a son for me. Exeunt |
