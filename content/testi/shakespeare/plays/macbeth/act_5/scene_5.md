| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Macbeth]], Seyton, and soldiers, with a drummer and colours |
| [[Macbeth|MACBETH]] | Hang out our banners on the outward walls.<br>The cry is still ‘They come.’ Our [[Castle|castle]]’s strength<br>Will laugh a siege to scorn. Here let them lie<br>Till famine and the ague eat them up.<br>Were they not forced with those that should be ours<br>We might have met them dareful, beard to beard,<br>And beat them backward [[Home|home]].<br>A cry within of women<br>What is that noise? |
| SEYTON | It is the cry of women, my good lord.<br>[Exit] |
| [[Macbeth|MACBETH]] | I have almost forgot the taste of fears.<br>The [[Time|time]] has been my senses would have cooled<br>To hear a [[Night|night]]-shriek, and my fell of hair<br>Would at a dismal treatise rouse and stir<br>As life were in’t. I have supped full with horrors.<br>Direness, familiar to my slaughterous thoughts,<br>Cannot once start me. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Seyton |
| [[Macbeth|MACBETH]] | Wherefore was that cry? |
| SEYTON | The [[Queen]], my lord, is dead. |
| [[Macbeth|MACBETH]] | She should have died hereafter.<br>There would have been a [[Time|time]] for such a word.<br>Tomorrow, and tomorrow, and tomorrow<br>Creeps in this petty pace from day to day<br>To the last syllable of recorded [[Time|time]],<br>And all our yesterdays have lighted fools<br>The way to dusty [[Death|death]]. Out, out, brief candle.<br>Life’s but a walking [[Shadow|shadow]], a poor player<br>That struts and frets his hour upon the stage,<br>And then is heard no more. It is a tale<br>Told by an idiot, full of sound and fury,<br>Signifying nothing. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter a Messenger |
| [[Macbeth|MACBETH]] | Thou com’st to use<br>Thy tongue: thy story quickly. |
| MESSENGER | Gracious my lord,<br>I should report that which I say I saw,<br>But know not how to do’t. |
| [[Macbeth|MACBETH]] | [[Well]], say, sir. |
| MESSENGER | As I did stand my watch upon the [[Hill|hill]]<br>I looked toward Birnam, and anon methought<br>The wood began to move. |
| [[Macbeth|MACBETH]] | Liar and slave! |
| MESSENGER | Let me endure your wrath if’t be not so. |
| *(didascalia)* | Within this three mile may you see it coming. |
| MESSENGER | I say, a moving grove. |
| [[Macbeth|MACBETH]] | If thou speak’st false<br>Upon the next tree shall thou hang alive<br>Till famine cling thee. If thy speech be sooth,<br>I care not if thou dost for me as much.<br>I pall in resolution, and begin<br>To [[Doubt|doubt]] th‘equivocation of the fiend,<br>That lies like [[Truth|truth]]. ’[[Fear]] not till Birnam Wood<br>Do come to Dunsinane‘—and now a wood<br>Comes toward Dunsinane. Arm, arm, and out.<br>If this which he avouches does appear<br>There is nor flying hence nor tarrying here.<br>I ’gin to be aweary of the sun,<br>And wish th‘estate o’th’ world were now undone.<br>Ring the alarum bell. Alarums Blow [[Wind|wind]], come wrack,<br>At least we’ll die with harness on our back. Exeunt |
