| [[Lady Macbeth|LADY MACBETH]] | That which hath made them drunk hath made me bold.<br>What hath quenched them hath given me [[Fire|fire]]. Hark,<br>peace!—<br>It was the owl that shrieked, the fatal bellman<br>Which gives the stern’st good-[[Night|night]]. He is about it.<br>The doors are open, and the surfeited grooms<br>Do mock their charge with snores. I have drugged<br>their possets<br>That [[Death|death]] and [[Nature|nature]] do contend about them<br>Whether they live or die. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Macbeth]] [above] |
| [[Macbeth|MACBETH]] | Who’s there?<br>What ho? Exit |
| [[Lady Macbeth|LADY MACBETH]] | Alack, I am afraid they have awaked,<br>And ‘tis not done. Th’attempt and not the deed<br>Confounds us. Hark!—I laid their daggers ready;<br>He could not miss ’em. Had he not resembled<br>My father as he slept, I had done’t.<br>[Enter [[Macbeth]] below]<br>My husband! |
| [[Macbeth|MACBETH]] | I have done the deed. Didst thou not hear a noise? |
| [[Lady Macbeth|LADY MACBETH]] | I heard the owl scream and the crickets cry.<br>Did not you speak? |
| [[Macbeth|MACBETH]] | When? |
| [[Lady Macbeth|LADY MACBETH]] | Now. |
| [[Macbeth|MACBETH]] | As I descended? |
| [[Lady Macbeth|LADY MACBETH]] | Ay. |
| [[Macbeth|MACBETH]] | Hark!—Who lies i’th’ second chamber? |
| [[Lady Macbeth|LADY MACBETH]] | Donalbain. |
| [[Macbeth|MACBETH]] | (looking at his hands) This is a sorry sight. |
| [[Lady Macbeth|LADY MACBETH]] | A foolish thought, to say a sorry sight. |
| [[Macbeth|MACBETH]] | There’s one did laugh in’s [[Sleep|sleep]], and one cried ‘Murder!’<br>That they did wake each other. I stood and heard<br>them.<br>But they did say their prayers and addressed them<br>Again to [[Sleep|sleep]]. |
| [[Lady Macbeth|LADY MACBETH]] | There are two lodged together. |
| [[Macbeth|MACBETH]] | One cried ‘God bless us’ and ‘Amen’ the other,<br>As they had seen me with these hangman’s hands.<br>List‘ning their [[Fear|fear]] I could not say ‘Amen’<br>When they did say ‘God bless us.’ |
| [[Lady Macbeth|LADY MACBETH]] | Consider it not so deeply. |
| [[Macbeth|MACBETH]] | But wherefore could not I pronounce ‘Amen’?<br>I had most need of blessing, and ‘Amen’<br>Stuck in my throat. |
| [[Lady Macbeth|LADY MACBETH]] | These deeds must not be thought<br>After these ways. So, it will make us mad. |
| [[Macbeth|MACBETH]] | Methought I heard a voice cry ‘[[Sleep]] no more,<br>[[Macbeth]] does murder [[Sleep|sleep]]’—the [[Innocent|innocent]] [[Sleep|sleep]],<br>[[Sleep]] that knits up the ravelled sleave of care,<br>The [[Death|death]] of each day’s life, sore labour’s bath,<br>Balm of hurt minds, great [[Nature|nature]]’s second course,<br>Chief nourisher in life’s feast— |
| [[Lady Macbeth|LADY MACBETH]] | What do you mean? |
| [[Macbeth|MACBETH]] | Still it cried ‘[[Sleep]] no more’ to all the [[house]],<br>‘Glamis hath murdered [[Sleep|sleep]], and therefore Cawdor<br>Shall [[Sleep|sleep]] no more, [[Macbeth]] shall [[Sleep|sleep]] no more.’ |
| [[Lady Macbeth|LADY MACBETH]] | Who was it that thus cried? Why, worthy thane,<br>You do unbend your noble strength to think<br>So brain-sickly of things. Go get some [[Water|water]]<br>And wash this filthy witness from your hand.<br>Why did you bring these daggers from the place?<br>They must lie there. Go, carry them, and smear<br>The sleepy grooms with [[Blood|blood]]. |
| [[Macbeth|MACBETH]] | I’ll go no more.<br>I am afraid to think what I have done,<br>Look on’t again I dare not. |
| [[Lady Macbeth|LADY MACBETH]] | Infirm of purpose!<br>Give me the daggers. The sleeping and the dead<br>Are but as pictures. ’Tis the eye of [[Childhood|childhood]]<br>That fears a painted devil. If he do bleed<br>I’ll gild the faces of the grooms withal,<br>For it must seem their [[Guilt|guilt]]. Exit<br>Knock within |
| [[Macbeth|MACBETH]] | Whence is that knocking?—<br>How is’t with me when every noise appals me?<br>What hands are here! Ha, they pluck out mine eyes.<br>Will all great Neptune’s ocean wash this [[Blood|blood]]<br>Clean from my hand? No, this my hand will rather<br>The multitudinous seas incarnadine,<br>Making the green one red. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Lady Macbeth]] |
| [[Lady Macbeth|LADY MACBETH]] | My hands are of your colour, but I shame<br>To wear a heart so white.<br>Knock within<br><br>I hear a knocking<br>At the south entry. Retire we to our chamber.<br>A little [[Water|water]] clears us of this deed.<br>How easy is it then! Your constancy<br>Hath left you unattended.<br>Knock within<br><br>Hark, more knocking.<br>Get on your nightgown, lest occasion call us<br>And show us to be watchers. Be not lost<br>So poorly in your thoughts. |
| [[Macbeth|MACBETH]] | To know my deed ’twere best not know myself.<br>Knock within<br><br>Wake [[Duncan]] with thy knocking. I would thou<br>couldst. Exeunt |
