| *(didascalia)* | Enter Lennox and another Lord |
| LENNOX | My former speeches have but hit your thoughts,<br>Which can interpret farther. Only I say<br>Things have been strangely borne. The gracious<br>[[Duncan]]<br>Was pitied of [[Macbeth]]: marry, he was dead;<br>And the right valiant [[Banquo]] walked too late,<br>Whom you may say, if’t please you, Fleance killed,<br>For Fleance fled: men must not walk too late.<br>Who cannot want the thought how monstrous<br>It was for [[Malcolm]] and for Donalbain<br>To kill their gracious father? Damned fact,<br>How it did grieve [[Macbeth]]! Did he not straight<br>In pious rage the two delinquents tear,<br>That were the slaves of drink, and thralls of [[Sleep|sleep]]?<br>Was not that nobly done? Ay, and wisely too,<br>For ‘twould have angered any heart alive<br>To hear the men deny’t. So that I say<br>He has borne all things [[Well|well]], and I do think<br>That had he [[Duncan]]’s sons under his key—<br>As, an’t please heaven, he shall not—they should find<br>What ’twere to kill a father. So should Fleance.<br>But peace, for from broad words, and ’cause he failed<br>His presence at the [[Tyrant|tyrant]]’s feast, I hear<br>[[Macduff]] lives in disgrace. Sir, can you tell<br>Where he bestows himself? |
| LORD | The son of [[Duncan]]<br>From whom this [[Tyrant|tyrant]] holds the due of birth<br>Lives in the English [[Court|court]], and is received<br>Of the most pious Edward with such grace<br>That the malevolence of fortune nothing<br>Takes from his high respect. Thither [[Macduff]]<br>Is gone to pray the holy [[King]] upon his aid<br>To wake Northumberland and warlike Siward,<br>That by the help of these—with Him above<br>To ratify the work—we may again<br>Give to our tables meat, [[Sleep|sleep]] to our nights,<br>Free from our feasts and banquets bloody knives,<br>Do faithful homage, and receive free honours,<br>All which we pine for now. And this report<br>Hath so exasperate their [[King|king]] that he<br>Prepares for some attempt of war. |
| LENNOX | Sent he to [[Macduff]]? |
| LORD | He did, and with an absolute ‘Sir, not I,’<br>The cloudy messenger turns me his back<br>And hums, as who should say ‘You’ll rue the [[Time|time]]<br>That clogs me with this answer.’ |
| LENNOX | And that [[Well|well]] might<br>Advise him to a caution t’hold what,distance<br>His wisdom can provide. Some holy angel<br>Fly to the [[Court|court]] of England and unfold<br>His message ere he come, that a swift blessing<br>May soon return to this our [[Suffering|suffering]] country<br>Under a hand accursed. |
| LORD | I’ll send my prayers with him. Exeunt |
