| Chi parla | Battuta |
|---|---|
| *(didascalia)* | Enter three Murderers |
| FIRST MURDERER | (to Third Murderer)<br>But who did bid thee join with us? |
| THIRD MURDERER | [[Macbeth]]. |
| SECOND MURDERER | (to First Murderer)<br>He needs not our mistrust, since he delivers<br>Our offices and what we have to do<br>To the direction just. |
| FIRST MURDERER | (to Third Murderer) Then stand with us.<br>The west yet glimmers with some streaks of day.<br>Now spurs the lated traveller apace<br>To gain the timely inn, and near approaches<br>The subject of our watch. |
| THIRD MURDERER | Hark, I hear horses. |
| [[Banquo|BANQUO]] | (within)<br>Give us a light there, ho! |
| SECOND MURDERER | Then ’tis he. The rest<br>That are within the note of expectation<br>Already are i’th’ [[Court|court]]. |
| FIRST MURDERER | His horses go about. |
| THIRD MURDERER | Almost a mile; but he does usually,<br>So all men do, from hence to th’ palace gate<br>Make it their walk. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Banquo]] and Fleance with a torch |
| SECOND MURDERER | (aside) A light, a light. |
| THIRD MURDERER | (aside)<br>’Tis he. |
| FIRST MURDERER | (aside) Stand to’t. |
| [[Banquo|BANQUO]] | It will be rain tonight. |
| FIRST MURDERER | Let it come down.<br>First Murderer strikes out the torch. The others attack [[Banquo]] |
| [[Banquo|BANQUO]] | O, treachery! Fly, good Fleance, fly, fly, fly!<br>Thou mayst [[Revenge|revenge]].—O slave! He dies. Exit Fleance |
| THIRD MURDERER | Who did strike out the light? |
| FIRST MURDERER | Was’t not the way? |
| THIRD MURDERER | There’s but one down. The son is fled. |
| SECOND MURDERER | We have lost best half of our affair. |
| FIRST MURDERER | [[Well]], let’s away and say how much is done. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt with [[Banquo]]’s [[Body|body]] |
