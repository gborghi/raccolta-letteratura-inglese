| Chi parla | Battuta |
|---|---|
| *(didascalia)* | Alarum, as in battle. Enter Martius, bloody, and [[Aufidius]], at several doors |
| MARTIUS | I’ll fight with none but thee, for I do hate thee<br>Worse than a promise-breaker. |
| [[Aufidius|AUFIDIUS]] | We hate alike.<br>Not Afric owns a serpent I abhor<br>More than thy fame and envy. Fix thy foot. |
| MARTIUS | Let the first budger die the other’s slave,<br>And the gods doom him after. |
| [[Aufidius|AUFIDIUS]] | If I fly, Martius,<br>Holla me like a hare. |
| MARTIUS | Within these three hours, Tullus,<br>[[Alone]] I fought in your Corioles’ walls,<br>And made what work I pleased. ’Tis not my [[Blood|blood]]<br>Wherein thou seest me masked. For thy [[Revenge|revenge]],<br>Wrench up thy power to th’ highest. |
| [[Aufidius|AUFIDIUS]] | Wert thou the [[Hector]]<br>That was the whip of your bragged progeny,<br>Thou shouldst not scape me here. |
| *(didascalia)* | Here they fight, and certain Volsces come in the aid of [[Aufidius]]. Martius fights till the Volsces be driven in breathless, Martius following |
| [[Aufidius|AUFIDIUS]] | Officious and not valiant, you have shamed me<br>In your condemned seconds. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit |
