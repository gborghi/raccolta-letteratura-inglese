| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Cominius]], as it were in retire, with soldiers |
| [[Cominius|COMINIUS]] | Breathe you, my friends. [[Well]] fought. We are come off<br>Like Romans, neither foolish in our stands<br>Nor cowardly in retire. Believe me, sirs,<br>We shall be charged again. Whiles we have struck,<br>By interims and conveying gusts we have heard<br>The charges of our friends. The Roman gods<br>Lead their successes as we wish our own,<br>That both our powers, with smiling fronts<br>encount’ring,<br>May give you thankful sacrifice! |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter a Messenger |
| [[Cominius|COMINIUS]] | Thy news? |
| MESSENGER | The citizens of Corioles have issued,<br>And given to Lartius and to Martius battle.<br>I saw our party to their trenches driven,<br>And then I came away. |
| [[Cominius|COMINIUS]] | Though thou speak‘st [[Truth|truth]], Methinks thou speak’st not [[Well|well]]. How long is’t since? |
| MESSENGER | Above an hour, my lord. |
| [[Cominius|COMINIUS]] | ’Tis not a mile; briefly we heard their drums.<br>How couldst thou in a mile confound an hour,<br>And bring thy news so late? |
| MESSENGER | Spies of the Volsces<br>Held me in chase, that I was forced to [[Wheel|wheel]]<br>Three or four miles about; else had I, sir, |
| *(didascalia)* | Half an hour since brought my report. Exit |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Martius, bloody |
| [[Cominius|COMINIUS]] | Who’s yonder,<br>That does appear as he were flayed? O gods!<br>He has the stamp of Martius, and I have<br>Before-[[Time|time]] seen him thus. |
| MARTIUS | Come I too late? |
| [[Cominius|COMINIUS]] | The shepherd knows not thunder from a tabor<br>More than I know the sound of Martius’ tongue<br>From every meaner man. |
| MARTIUS | Come I too late? |
| [[Cominius|COMINIUS]] | Ay, if you come not in the [[Blood|blood]] of others,<br>But mantled in your own. |
| MARTIUS | O, let me clip ye<br>In arms as sound as when I wooed, in heart<br>As merry as when our nuptial day was done,<br>And tapers burnt to bedward! |
| *(didascalia)* | They embrace |
| [[Cominius|COMINIUS]] | Flower of warriors! How is’t with Titus Lartius? |
| MARTIUS | As with a man busied about decrees,<br>Condemning some to [[Death|death]] and some to [[Exile|exile]],<br>Ransoming him or pitying, threat‘ning th’other;<br>Holding Corioles in the name of Rome<br>Even like a fawning greyhound in the leash,<br>To let him slip at will. |
| [[Cominius|COMINIUS]] | Where is that slave<br>Which told me they had beat you to your trenches?<br>Where is he? Call him hither. |
| MARTIUS | Let him [[Alone|alone]].<br>He did inform the [[Truth|truth]]. But for our gentlemen,<br>The common file—a plague—tribunes for them?—<br>The mouse ne’er shunned the cat as they did budge<br>From rascals worse than they. |
| [[Cominius|COMINIUS]] | But how prevailed you? |
| MARTIUS | Will the [[Time|time]] serve to tell? I do not think.<br>Where is the enemy? Are you lords o’th’ field?<br>If not, why cease you till you are so? |
| [[Cominius|COMINIUS]] | Martius, we have at disadvantage fought,<br>And did retire to win our purpose. |
| MARTIUS | How lies their battle? Know you on which side<br>They have placed their men of trust? |
| [[Cominius|COMINIUS]] | As I guess, Martius,<br>Their bands i‘th’ vanguard are the Antiates,<br>Of their best trust; o’er them [[Aufidius]],<br>Their very heart of [[Hope|hope]]. |
| MARTIUS | I do beseech you<br>By all the battles wherein we have fought,<br>By th’ [[Blood|blood]] we have shed together, by th’ vows we<br>have made<br>To endure friends, that you directly set me<br>Against [[Aufidius]] and his Antiates,<br>And that you not delay the present, but,<br>Filling the air with swords advanced and darts,<br>We prove this very hour. |
| [[Cominius|COMINIUS]] | Though I could wish<br>You were conducted to a gentle bath<br>And balms applied to you, yet dare I never<br>Deny your asking. Take your choice of those<br>That best can aid your action. |
| MARTIUS | Those are they<br>That most are willing. If any such be here—<br>As it were sin to [[Doubt|doubt]]—that [[Love|love]] this painting<br>Wherein you see me smeared; if any [[Fear|fear]]<br>Lesser his person than an ill report;<br>If any think brave [[Death|death]] outweighs bad life,<br>And that his country’s dearer than himself,<br>Let him [[Alone|alone]], or so many so minded,<br>He waves his [[Sword|sword]]<br>Wave thus to express his disposition,<br>And follow Martius. |
| *(didascalia)* | They all shout and wave their swords, then some take him up in their arms and they cast up their caps |
| MARTIUS | O’ me [[Alone|alone]], make you a [[Sword|sword]] of me?<br>If these shows be not outward, which of you<br>But is four Volsces? None of you but is<br>Able to bear against the great [[Aufidius]]<br>A shield as hard as his. A certain number—<br>Though thanks to all—must I select from all.<br>The rest shall bear the business in some other fight<br>As cause will be obeyed. Please you to march,<br>And I shall quickly draw out my command,<br>Which men are best inclined. |
| [[Cominius|COMINIUS]] | March on, my fellows.<br>Make good this ostentation, and you shall<br>Divide in all with us. Exeunt marching |
