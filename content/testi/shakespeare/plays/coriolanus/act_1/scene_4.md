|---|---|
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Martius, Lartius with a drummer, a trumpeter, and colours, with captains and Soldiers carrying scaling ladders, as before the [[City|city]] Corioles; to them a Messenger |
| MARTIUS | Yonder comes news. A wager they have met. |
| LARTIUS | My [[Horse|horse]] to yours, no. |
| MARTIUS | ’Tis done. |
| LARTIUS | Agreed. |
| MARTIUS | (to the Messenger)<br>Say, has our general met the enemy? |
| MESSENGER | They lie in view, but have not spoke as yet. |
| LARTIUS | So, the good [[Horse|horse]] is mine. |
| MARTIUS | I’ll buy him of you. |
| LARTIUS | No, I’ll nor sell nor give him. Lend you him I will,<br>For half a hundred years.<br>(To the trumpeter) Summon the town. |
| MARTIUS | (to the Messenger)<br>How far off lie these armies? |
| MESSENGER | Within this mile and half. |
| MARTIUS | Then shall we hear their ’larum, and they ours.<br>Now Mars, I prithee, make us quick in work,<br>That we with smoking swords may march from hence<br>To help our fielded friends.<br>(To the trumpeter) Come, blow thy blast.<br>They sound a parley. Enter two Senators, with others, on the walls of Corioles<br>(To the Senators) Tullus [[Aufidius]], is he within your walls? |
| FIRST SENATOR | No, nor a man that fears you less than he:<br>That’s lesser than a little. |
| *(didascalia)* | Drum afar off |
| *(didascalia)* | To the Volscians Hark, our drums |
| FIRST SENATOR | Are bringing forth our youth. We’ll break our walls<br>Rather than they shall pound us up. Our gates,<br>Which yet seem shut, we have but pinned with rushes.<br>They’ll open of themselves. |
| *(didascalia)* | Alarum far off |
| FIRST SENATOR | (To the Romans) Hark you, far off<br>There is [[Aufidius]]. List what work he makes<br>Amongst your cloven army. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt Volscians from the walls |
| MARTIUS | O, they are at it! |
| LARTIUS | Their noise be our instruction. Ladders, ho! |
| *(didascalia)* | They prepare to assault the walls. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter the army of the Volsces from the gates |
| MARTIUS | They [[Fear|fear]] us not, but issue forth their [[City|city]].<br>Now put your shields before your hearts, and fight<br>With hearts more proof than shields. Advance, brave<br>Titus.<br>They do disdain us much beyond our thoughts,<br>Which makes me sweat with wrath. Come on, my<br>fellows.<br>He that retires, I’ll take him for a Volsce,<br>And he shall feel mine edge. |
| *(didascalia)* | Alarum. The Romans are beat back and exeunt to their trenches, the Volsces following |
