| *(didascalia)* | Enter the two tribunes, [[Sicinius]] and [[Brutus]], with the Aedile |
| [[Sicinius|SICINIUS]] | (to the Aedile)<br>Bid them all [[Home|home]]. He’s gone, and we’ll no further.<br>The nobility are vexed, whom we see have sided<br>In his behalf. |
| [[Brutus|BRUTUS]] | Now we have shown our power,<br>Let us seem humbler after it is done<br>Than when it was a-doing. |
| [[Sicinius|SICINIUS]] | (to the Aedile) Bid them [[Home|home]].<br>Say their great enemy is gone, and they<br>Stand in their ancient strength. |
| [[Brutus|BRUTUS]] | Dismiss them [[Home|home]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit Aedile |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Volumnia]], [[Virgilia]], weeping, and [[Menenius]] |
| [[Brutus|BRUTUS]] | Here comes his mother. |
| [[Sicinius|SICINIUS]] | Let’s not meet her. |
| [[Brutus|BRUTUS]] | Why? |
| [[Sicinius|SICINIUS]] | They say she’s mad. |
| [[Brutus|BRUTUS]] | They have ta’en note of us. Keep on your way. |
| [[Volumnia|VOLUMNIA]] | O, you’re [[Well|well]] met! Th‘hoarded plague o’th’ gods<br>Requite your [[Love|love]]! |
| [[Menenius|MENENIUS]] | Peace, peace, be not so loud. |
| [[Volumnia|VOLUMNIA]] | (to the tribunes)<br>If that I could for weeping, you should hear—<br>Nay, and you shall hear some. Will you be gone? |
| [[Virgilia|VIRGILIA]] | (to the tribunes)<br>You shall stay, too. I would I had the power<br>To say so to my husband. |
| [[Sicinius|SICINIUS]] | (to [[Volumnia]]) Are you mankind? |
| [[Volumnia|VOLUMNIA]] | Ay, [[Fool|fool]]. Is that a shame? Note but this, [[Fool|fool]]:<br>Was not a man my father? Hadst thou foxship<br>To banish him that struck more blows for Rome<br>Than thou hast spoken words? |
| [[Sicinius|SICINIUS]] | O blessed heavens! |
| [[Volumnia|VOLUMNIA]] | More noble blows than ever thou wise words,<br>And for Rome’s good. I’ll tell thee what—yet go.<br>Nay, but thou shalt stay too. I would my son<br>Were in Arabia, and thy tribe before him,<br>His good [[Sword|sword]] in his hand. |
| [[Sicinius|SICINIUS]] | What then? |
| [[Virgilia|VIRGILIA]] | What then?<br>He’d make an end of thy posterity. |
| [[Volumnia|VOLUMNIA]] | Bastards and all.<br>Good man, the wounds that he does bear for Romeǃ [[Menenius|MENENIUS]] Come, come, peace. |
| [[Sicinius|SICINIUS]] | I would he had continued to his country<br>As he began, and not unknit himself<br>The noble knot he made. |
| [[Brutus|BRUTUS]] | I would he had. |
| [[Volumnia|VOLUMNIA]] | ‘I would he had’! ’Twas you incensed the rabble—<br>Cats that can judge as fitly of his worth<br>As I can of those mysteries which heaven<br>Will not have earth to know. |
| [[Brutus|BRUTUS]] | (to [[Sicinius]]) Pray, let’s go. |
| [[Volumnia|VOLUMNIA]] | Now pray, sir, get you gone.<br>You have done a brave deed. Ere you go, hear this:<br>As far as doth the Capitol exceed<br>The meanest [[house]] in Rome, so far my son—<br>This lady’s husband here, this, do you see?—<br>Whom you have banished does exceed you all. |
| [[Brutus|BRUTUS]] | [[Well]], [[Well|well]], we’ll leave you. |
| [[Sicinius|SICINIUS]] | Why stay we to be baited<br>With one that wants her wits? |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt tribunes |
| [[Volumnia|VOLUMNIA]] | Take my prayers with you.<br>I would the gods had nothing else to do<br>But to confirm my curses. Could I meet ’em<br>But once a day, it would unclog my heart<br>Of what lies heavy to’t. |
| [[Menenius|MENENIUS]] | You have told them [[Home|home]]<br>And, by my troth, you have cause. You’ll sup with me? |
| [[Volumnia|VOLUMNIA]] | Anger’s my meat, I sup upon myself,<br>And so shall starve with feeding.<br>(To [[Virgilia]]) Come, let’s go.<br>Leave this faint puling and lament as I do,<br>In anger, Juno-like. Come, come, come. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt [[Volumnia]] and [[Virgilia]] |
| [[Menenius|MENENIUS]] | Fie, fie, fie. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit |
