| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Edmund]], [[Regan]], and their powers |
| [[Edmund|EDMUND]] | Know of the Duke if his last purpose hold,<br>Or whether since he is advised by aught<br>To change the course. He’s full of abdication<br>And [[Self|self]]-reproving. Bring his constant pleasure. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit one or more |
| [[Regan|REGAN]] | Our sister’s man is certainly miscarried. |
| [[Edmund|EDMUND]] | ’Tis to be doubted, madam. |
| [[Regan|REGAN]] | Now, sweet lord,<br>You know the goodness I intend upon you.<br>Tell me but truly—but then speak the [[Truth|truth]]-<br>Do you not [[Love|love]] my sister? |
| [[Edmund|EDMUND]] | Ay: honoured [[Love|love]]. |
| [[Regan|REGAN]] | But have you never found my brother’s way<br>To the forfended place? |
| [[Edmund|EDMUND]] | That thought abuses you. |
| [[Regan|REGAN]] | I am doubtful<br>That you have been conjunct and bosomed with her,<br>As far as we call hers. |
| [[Edmund|EDMUND]] | No, by mine [[Honour|honour]], madam. |
| [[Regan|REGAN]] | I never shall endure her. Dear my lord,<br>Be not familiar with her. |
| [[Edmund|EDMUND]] | [[Fear]] me not.<br>She and the Duke her husband— |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter the Duke of Albany and Gonoril with troops |
| GONORIL | (aside)<br>I had rather lose the battle than that sister<br>Should loosen him and me. |
| ALBANY | (to [[Regan]])<br>Our very loving sister, [[Well|well]] bemet,<br>For this I hear: the [[King]] is come to his daughter,<br>With others whom the rigour of our state<br>Forced to cry out. Where I could not be honest<br>I never yet was valiant. For this business,<br>It touches us as France invades our land;<br>Yet bold’s the [[King]], with others whom I [[Fear|fear]].<br>Most just and heavy causes make oppose. |
| [[Edmund|EDMUND]] | Sir, you speak nobly. |
| [[Regan|REGAN]] | Why is this reasoned? 30 |
| GONORIL | Combine together ’gainst the enemy;<br>For these domestic poor particulars<br>Are not to question here. |
| ALBANY | Let us then determine with the ensign of war<br>On our proceedings. |
| [[Edmund|EDMUND]] | I shall attend you<br>Presently at your tent. [Exit with his powers] |
| [[Regan|REGAN]] | Sister, you’ll go with us? |
| GONORIL | No. |
| [[Regan|REGAN]] | ’Tis most convenient. Pray you go with us. |
| GONORIL | [aside]<br>O ho, I know the [[Riddle|riddle]]! (To [[Regan]]) I will go. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Edgar]] disguised as a peasant |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | (to Albany)<br>If e’er your grace had speech with man so poor, 40<br>Hear me one word. |
| ALBANY | (to the others) I’ll overtake you. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt all but Albany and [[Edgar]] |
| ALBANY | Speak. |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | Before you fight the battle, ope this letter.<br>If you have victory, let the trumpet sound<br>For him that brought it. Wretched though I seem,<br>I can produce a champion that will prove<br>What is avouchèd there. If you miscarry,<br>Your business of the world hath so an end.<br>Fortune [[Love|love]] you— |
| ALBANY | Stay till I have read the letter. |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | I was forbid it. 50<br>When [[Time|time]] shall serve, let but the herald cry,<br>And I’ll appear again. |
| ALBANY | Why, fare thee [[Well|well]].<br>I will o’erlook the paper. Exit [[Edgar]] |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Edmund]] |
| [[Edmund|EDMUND]] | The enemy’s in view; draw up your powers. 55<br>He [offers] Albany a paper<br>Here is the guess of their great strength and forces<br>By diligent discovery; but your haste<br>Is now urged on you. |
| ALBANY | We will greet the [[Time|time]]. Exit |
| [[Edmund|EDMUND]] | To both these sisters have I sworn my [[Love|love]],<br>Each jealous of the other as the stung<br>Are of the adder. Which of them shall I take?—<br>Both?—one?—or neither? Neither can be enjoyed<br>If both remain alive. To take the widow<br>Exasperates, makes mad, her sister Gonoril,<br>And hardly shall I carry out my side,<br>Her husband being alive. Now then, we’ll use<br>His countenance for the battle, which being done,<br>Let her that would be rid of him devise<br>His speedy taking off. As for his [[Mercy|mercy]]<br>Which he intends to Lear and to [[Cordelia]],<br>The battle done, and they within our power,<br>Shall never see his pardon; for my state<br>Stands on me to defend, not to [[debate]]. Exit |
