| LEAR | And my poor [[Fool|fool]] is hanged. No, no life. Why should a dog, a [[Horse|horse]], a rat have life, And thou no breath at all? O, thou wilt come no more. Never, never, never.—Pray you, undo This button. Thank you, sir. O, O, O, O! |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | He faints. (To Lear) My lord, my lord! |
| LEAR | Break, heart, I prithee break. |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | Look up, my lord. |
| [[Kent|KENT]] | Vex not his [[Ghost|ghost]]. O, let him pass. He hates him That would upon the rack of this tough world Stretch him out longer. [Lear dies] |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | O, he is gone indeed. |
| [[Kent|KENT]] | The [[Wonder|wonder]] is he hath endured so long. He but usurped his life. |
| ALBANY | (to attendants) Bear them from hence. Our present business Is to general woe. (To [[Kent]] and [[Edgar]]) Friends of my [[Soul|soul]], you twain Rule in this kingdom, and the gored state sustain. |
| [[Kent|KENT]] | I have a [[Journey|journey]], sir, shortly to go: My master calls, and I must not say no. |
| ALBANY | The weight of this sad [[Time|time]] we must obey, Speak what we feel, not what we ought to say. The oldest have borne most. We that are young Shall never see so much, nor live so long. Exeunt carrying the bodies |
