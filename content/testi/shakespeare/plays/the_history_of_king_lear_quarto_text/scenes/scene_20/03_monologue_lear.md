| LEAR | Ay, every inch a [[King|king]]. [[Gloucester]] kneels When I do stare, see how the subject quakes! I pardon that man’s life. What was thy cause? Adultery? Thou shalt not die for adultery. No, the wren goes to‘t, and the small gilded fly Does lecher in my sight. Let copulation thrive, for [[Gloucester]]’s bastard son Was kinder to his father than my daughters Got ’tween the lawful sheets. To‘t, luxury, pell-mell, For I lack soldiers. Behold yon simp’ring dame, Whose face between her forks presageth [[Snow|snow]], That minces virtue, and does shake the head To hear of pleasure’s name: The fitchew nor the soiled [[Horse|horse]] goes to’t With a more riotous appetite. Down from the waist They’re centaurs, though women all above. But to the girdle do the gods inherit; Beneath is all the fiend’s. There’s hell, there’s darkness, There’s the sulphury pit, burning, scalding, Stench, consummation. Fie, fie, fie; pah, pah! Give me an ounce of civet, good apothecary, To sweeten my [[Imagination|imagination]]. There’s money for thee. |
