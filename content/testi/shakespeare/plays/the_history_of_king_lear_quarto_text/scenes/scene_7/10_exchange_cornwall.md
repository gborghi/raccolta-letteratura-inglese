|---|---|
| CORNWALL | Let us withdraw. ’Twill be a [[Storm|storm]]. |
| [[Regan|REGAN]] | This [[house]] is little. The old man and his people Cannot be [[Well|well]] bestowed. |
| GONORIL | ’Tis his own blame; Hath put himself from rest, and must needs taste his folly. |
| [[Regan|REGAN]] | For his particular I’ll receive him gladly, But not one follower. |
| CORNWALL | So am I purposed. Where is my lord of [[Gloucester]]? |
| [[Regan|REGAN]] | Followed the old man forth. Enter the [[Duke of Gloucester]] He is returned. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | The [[King]] is in high rage, and will I know not whither. |
| [[Regan|REGAN]] | ’Tis good to give him way. He leads himself. |
| GONORIL | (to [[Gloucester]]) My lord, entreat him by no means to stay. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Alack, the [[Night|night]] comes on, and the bleak winds Do sorely rustle. For many miles about There’s not a bush. |
| [[Regan|REGAN]] | O sir, to wilful men The injuries that they themselves procure Must be their schoolmasters. Shut up your doors. He is attended with a desperate train, And what they may incense him to, being apt To have his ear abused, wisdom bids [[Fear|fear]]. |
| CORNWALL | Shut up your doors, my lord. ‘Tis a wild [[Night|night]]. My [[Regan]] counsels [[Well|well]]. Come out o’th’ [[Storm|storm]]. Exeunt |
