| *(didascalia)* | Enter the Duke of Cornwall and [[Regan]], and Gonoril and [[Edmund]] the bastard, and Servants |
| CORNWALL | (to Gonoril)<br>Post speedily to my lord your husband.<br>Show him this letter. The army of France is landed.<br>(To Servants) Seek out the [[Villain|villain]] [[Gloucester]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt some |
| [[Regan|REGAN]] | Hang him instantly. |
| GONORIL | Pluck out his eyes. |
| CORNWALL | Leave him to my displeasure.—<br>[[Edmund]], keep you our sister company.<br>The revenges we are bound to take upon your traitorous<br>father are not fit for your beholding. Advise the Duke<br>where you are going, to a most festinate preparation;<br>we are bound to the like. Our posts shall be swift, and<br>intelligence betwixt us.—<br>Farewell, dear sister. Farewell, my lord of [[Gloucester]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Oswald the steward |
| CORNWALL | How now, where’s the [[King]]? |
| OSWALD | My lord of [[Gloucester]] hath conveyed him hence.<br>Some five- or six-and-thirty of his knights,<br>Hot questants after him, met him at gate,<br>Who, with some other of the lord’s dependants,<br>Are gone with him towards Dover, where they boast<br>To have [[Well|well]]-armed friends. |
| CORNWALL | Get horses for your mistress. Exit Oswald |
| GONORIL | Farewell, sweet lord, and sister. |
| CORNWALL | [[Edmund]], farewell. Exeunt Gonoril and [[Edmund]]<br>(To Servants) Go seek the traitor [[Gloucester]].<br>Pinion him like a thief; bring him before us. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt other Servants |
| CORNWALL | Though we may not pass upon his life<br>Without the form of justice, yet our power<br>Shall do a curtsy to our wrath, which men<br>May blame but not control. Who’s there—the traitor? |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter the [[Duke of Gloucester]] brought in by two or |
| CORNWALL | three |
| [[Regan|REGAN]] | Ingrateful fox, ’tis he. |
| CORNWALL | (to Servants) Bind fast his corky arms. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | What means your graces? Good my friends, consider<br>You are my guests. Do me no foul play, friends. |
| CORNWALL | (to Servants)<br>Bind him, I say— |
| [[Regan|REGAN]] | Hard, hard! O filthy traitor! |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Unmerciful lady as you are, I am true. |
| CORNWALL | (to Servants)<br>To this chair bind him. (To [[Gloucester]]) [[Villain]], thou<br>shalt find—<br>[[Regan]] plucks [[Gloucester]]’s beard |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | By the kind gods, ’tis most ignobly done,<br>To pluck me by the beard. |
| [[Regan|REGAN]] | So white, and such a traitor! |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Naughty lady,<br>These hairs which thou dost ravish from my chin<br>Will quicken and accuse thee. I am your host.<br>With robbers’ hands my hospitable favours<br>You should not ruffle thus. What will you do? |
| CORNWALL | Come, sir, what letters had you late from France? |
| [[Regan|REGAN]] | Be simple, answerer, for we know the [[Truth|truth]]. |
| CORNWALL | And what confederacy have you with the traitors<br>Late footed in the kingdom? |
| [[Regan|REGAN]] | To whose hands<br>You have sent the lunatic [[King]]. Speak. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | I have a letter guessingly set down,<br>Which came from one that’s of a neutral heart,<br>And not from one opposed. |
| CORNWALL | Cunning. |
| [[Regan|REGAN]] | And false. |
| CORNWALL | Where hast thou sent the [[King]]? |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | To Dover. |
| [[Regan|REGAN]] | Wherefore to Dover? Wast thou not charged at peril— |
| CORNWALL | Wherefore to Dover? Let him first answer that. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | I am tied to th’ stake, and I must stand the course. |
| [[Regan|REGAN]] | Wherefore to Dover, sir? |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Because I would not see thy cruel nails<br>Pluck out his poor old eyes, nor thy fierce sister<br>In his anointed flesh rash boarish fangs.<br>The sea, with such a [[Storm|storm]] as his bowed head<br>In hell-black [[Night|night]] endured, would have buoyed up<br>And quenched the stellèd fires. Yet, poor old heart,<br>He holped the heavens to rage.<br>If wolves had at thy gate howled that dern [[Time|time]],<br>Thou shouldst have said ‘Good porter, turn the key;<br>All cruels I’ll subscribe.’ But I shall see<br>The winged vengeance overtake such children. |
| CORNWALL | See’t shalt thou never.—Fellows, hold the chair.—<br>Upon those eyes of thine I’ll set my foot. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | He that will think to live till he be old<br>Give me some help!—O cruel! O ye gods! |
| *(didascalia)* | Cornwall pulls out one of [[Gloucester]]’s eyes and |
| *(didascalia)* | stamps on it |
| [[Regan|REGAN]] | (to Cornwall)<br>One side will mock another; t’other, too. |
| CORNWALL | (to [[Gloucester]])<br>If you see vengeance— |
| SERVANT | Hold your hand, my lord.<br>I have served you ever since I was a [[Child|child]],<br>But better service have I never done you<br>Than now to bid you hold. |
| [[Regan|REGAN]] | How now, you dog! |
| SERVANT | If you did wear a beard upon your chin |
| *(didascalia)* | I’d shake it on this quarrel. To Cornwall What do |
| SERVANT | you mean? |
| CORNWALL | My villein! |
| SERVANT | Why then, come on, and take the chance of anger.<br>They draw and fight |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Regan|REGAN]] to another Servant |
| SERVANT | Give me thy [[Sword|sword]]. A peasant stand up thus!<br>She takes a [[Sword|sword]] and runs at him behind |
| SERVANT | (to [[Gloucester]])<br>O, I am slain, my lord! Yet have you one eye left<br>To see some mischief on him. |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Regan]] stabs him again |
| SERVANT | O! He dies |
| CORNWALL | Lest it see more, prevent it. Out, vile jelly! |
| *(didascalia)* | He pulls out [[Gloucester]]’s other eye |
| CORNWALL | Where is thy lustre now? |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | All dark and comfortless. Where’s my son [[Edmund]]?<br>[[Edmund]], enkindle all the sparks of [[Nature|nature]]<br>To quite this horrid act. |
| [[Regan|REGAN]] | Out, [[Villain|villain]]!<br>Thou call’st on him that hates thee. It was he<br>That made the overture of thy treasons to us,<br>Who is too good to pity thee. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | O, my follies! Then [[Edgar]] was abused.<br>Kind gods, forgive me that, and prosper him! |
| [[Regan|REGAN]] | (to Servants)<br>Go thrust him out at gates, and let him smell<br>His way to Dover. (To Cornwall) How is’t, my lord?<br>How look you? |
| CORNWALL | I have received a hurt. Follow me, lady.<br>(To Servants) Turn out that eyeless [[Villain|villain]]. Throw this<br>slave<br>Upon the dunghill. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit one or more with [[Gloucester]] |
| *(didascalia)* | and the [[Body|body]] |
| CORNWALL | [[Regan]], I bleed apace.<br>Untimely comes this hurt. Give me your arm. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt Cornwall and [[Regan]] |
| SECOND SERVANT | I’ll never care what wickedness I do<br>If this man come to good. |
| THIRD SERVANT | If she live long<br>And in the end meet the old course of [[Death|death]],<br>Women will all turn monsters. |
| SECOND SERVANT | Let’s follow the old Earl and get the bedlam<br>To lead him where he would. His roguish [[Madness|madness]]<br>Allows itself to anything. |
| THIRD SERVANT | Go thou. I’ll fetch some flax and whites of eggs<br>To apply to his bleeding face. Now heaven help him! |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt severally |
