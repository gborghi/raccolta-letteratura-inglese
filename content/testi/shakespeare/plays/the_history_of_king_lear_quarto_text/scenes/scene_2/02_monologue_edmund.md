| [[Edmund|EDMUND]] | Thou, [[Nature|nature]], art my goddess. To thy law My services are bound. Wherefore should I Stand in the plague of custom and permit The curiosity of nations to deprive me For that I am some twelve or fourteen moonshines Lag of a brother? Why ‘bastard’? Wherefore ‘base’, When my dimensions are as [[Well|well]] compact, My mind as generous, and my shape as true As honest madam’s issue? Why brand they us with ‘base, base bastardy’, Who in the lusty stealth of [[Nature|nature]] take More composition and fierce quality Than doth within a stale, dull-eyed bed go To the creating a whole tribe of fops Got ’tween a [[Sleep|sleep]] and wake? [[Well]] then, Legitimate [[Edgar]], I must have your land. Our father’s [[Love|love]] is to the bastard [[Edmund]] As to the legitimate. [[Well]], my legitimate, if This letter [[Speed|speed]] and my invention thrive, [[Edmund]] the base shall to th’ legitimate. I grow, I prosper. Now gods, stand up for bastards! Enter the [[Duke of Gloucester]]. [[Edmund]] reads a letter |
