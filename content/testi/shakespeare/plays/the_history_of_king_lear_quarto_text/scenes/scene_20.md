| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Edgar]] disguised as a peasant, with a staff, guiding the blind [[Duke of Gloucester]] |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | When shall we come to th’ top of that same [[Hill|hill]]? |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | You do climb up it now. Look how we labour. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Methinks the ground is even. |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | Horrible steep.<br>Hark, do you hear the sea? |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | No, truly. |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | Why, then your other senses grow imperfect<br>By your eyes’ anguish. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | So may it be indeed.<br>Methinks thy voice is altered, and thou speak’st<br>With better phrase and matter than thou didst. |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | You’re much deceived. In nothing am I changed<br>But in my garments. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Methinks you’re better spoken. |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | Come on, sir, here’s the place. Stand still. How fearful<br>And dizzy ’tis to cast one’s eyes so low!<br>The crows and choughs that wing the midway air<br>Show scarce so gross as beetles. Halfway down<br>Hangs one that gathers samphire, dreadful trade!<br>Methinks he seems no bigger than his head.<br>The fishermen that walk upon the beach<br>Appear like mice, and yon tall anchoring barque<br>Diminished to her cock, her cock a buoy<br>Almost too small for sight. The murmuring surge<br>That on the unnumbered idle pebble chafes<br>Cannot be heard, it’s so high. I’ll look no more,<br>Lest my brain turn and the deficient sight<br>Topple down headlong. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Set me where you stand. |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | Give me your hand. You are now within a foot<br>Of th’extreme verge. For all beneath the [[Moon|moon]]<br>Would I not leap upright. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Let go my hand.<br>Here, friend, ’s another purse; in it a [[Jewel|jewel]]<br>[[Well]] worth a poor man’s taking. Fairies and gods<br>Prosper it with thee! Go thou farther off.<br>Bid me farewell, and let me hear thee going. |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | Now fare you [[Well|well]], good sir.<br>He stands aside |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | With all my heart. |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | (aside)<br>Why I do trifle thus with his [[Despair|despair]]<br>Is done to cure it. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | O you mighty gods,<br>He kneels<br>This world I do renounce, and in your sights<br>Shake patiently my great affliction off!<br>If I could bear it longer, and not fall<br>To quarrel with your great opposeless wills,<br>My snuff and loathed part of [[Nature|nature]] should<br>Burn itself out. If [[Edgar]] live, O bless him!—<br>Now, fellow, fare thee [[Well|well]]. |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | Gone, sir. Farewell.<br>[[Gloucester]] falls forward<br>(Aside) And yet I know not how conceit may rob<br>The treasury of life, when life itself<br>Yields to the theft. Had he been where he thought,<br>By this had thought been past.—Ative or dead?<br>(To [[Gloucester]]) Ho you, sir; hear you, sir? Speak.<br>(Aside) Thus might he pass indeed. Yet he revives.<br>(To [[Gloucester]]) What are you, sir? |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Away, and let me die. |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | Hadst thou been aught but goss‘mer, feathers, air,<br>So many fathom down precipitating<br>Thou hadst shivered like an egg. But thou dost breathe,<br>Hast heavy substance, bleed’st not, speak’st, art sound.<br>Ten masts a-length make not the altitude<br>Which thou hast perpendicularly fell.<br>Thy life’s a miracle. Speak yet again. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | But have I fallen, or no? |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | From the dread summit of this chalky bourn.<br>Look up a-height. The shrill-gorged lark so far<br>Cannot be seen or heard. Do but look up. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Alack, I have no eyes.<br>Is wretchedness deprived that benefit<br>To end itself by [[Death|death]]? ’Twas yet some comfort<br>When misery could beguile the [[Tyrant|tyrant]]’s rage<br>And frustrate his proud will. |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | Give me your arm.<br>Up. So, how now? Feel you your legs? You stand. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Too [[Well|well]], too [[Well|well]]. |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | This is above all strangeness.<br>Upon the [[Crown|crown]] of the cliff what thing was that<br>Which parted from you? |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | A poor unfortunate beggar. |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | As I stood here below, methoughts his eyes<br>Were two full moons. A had a thousand noses,<br>Horns whelked and waved like the enridged sea.<br>It was some fiend. Therefore, thou happy father,<br>Think that the clearest gods, who made their honours<br>Of men’s impossibilities, have preserved thee. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | I do remember now. Henceforth I’ll bear<br>Affliction till it do cry out itself<br>‘Enough, enough,’ and die. That thing you speak of,<br>I took it for a man. Often would it say<br>‘The fiend, the fiend!’ He led me to that place. |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | Bear free and patient thoughts. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[King Lear]] mad, crowned with weeds and |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Flowers|flowers]] |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | But who comes here?<br>The safer sense will ne’er accommodate<br>His master thus. |
| LEAR | No, they cannot touch me for coining. I am the [[King]] himself. |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | O thou side-piercing sight! |
| LEAR | [[Nature]] is above art in that respect. There’s your press-money. That fellow handles his bow like a crow-keeper. Draw me a clothier’s yard. Look, look, a mouse! Peace, peace, this toasted [[cheese]] will do it. There’s my gauntlet. I’ll prove it on a giant. Bring up the brown bills. O, [[Well|well]] flown, bird, in the air. Hal Give the word. |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | Sweet marjoram. |
| LEAR | Pass. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | I know that voice. |
| LEAR | Ha, Gonorill Ha, [[Regan]]! They flattered me like a dog, and told me I had white hairs in my beard ere the black ones were there. To say ‘ay’ and ‘no’ to everything I said ‘ay’ and ‘no’ to was no good divinity. When the rain came to wet me once, and the [[Wind|wind]] to make me chatter, when the thunder would not peace at my bidding, there I found them, there I smelt them out. Go to, they are not men of their words. They told me I was everything; ’tis a lie, I am not ague-proof. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | The trick of that voice I do [[Well|well]] remember.<br>Is’t not the [[King]]? |
| LEAR | Ay, every inch a [[King|king]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Gloucester]] kneels |
| LEAR | When I do stare, see how the subject quakes!<br>I pardon that man’s life. What was thy cause?<br>Adultery? Thou shalt not die for adultery.<br>No, the wren goes to‘t, and the small gilded fly<br>Does lecher in my sight.<br>Let copulation thrive, for [[Gloucester]]’s bastard son<br>Was kinder to his father than my daughters<br>Got ’tween the lawful sheets. To‘t, luxury, pell-mell,<br>For I lack soldiers. Behold yon simp’ring dame,<br>Whose face between her forks presageth [[Snow|snow]],<br>That minces virtue, and does shake the head<br>To hear of pleasure’s name:<br>The fitchew nor the soiled [[Horse|horse]] goes to’t<br>With a more riotous appetite. Down from the waist<br>They’re centaurs, though women all above.<br>But to the girdle do the gods inherit;<br>Beneath is all the fiend’s. There’s hell, there’s<br>darkness,<br>There’s the sulphury pit, burning, scalding,<br>Stench, consummation. Fie, fie, fie; pah, pah!<br>Give me an ounce of civet, good apothecary,<br>To sweeten my [[Imagination|imagination]].<br>There’s money for thee. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | O, let me kiss that hand! |
| LEAR | Here, wipe it first; it smells of [[Mortality|mortality]]. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | O ruined piece of [[Nature|nature]]! This great world<br>Shall so wear out to naught. Do you know me? |
| LEAR | I remember thy eyes [[Well|well]] enough. Dost thou squiny on me?<br>No, do thy worst, blind Cupid, I’ll not [[Love|love]].<br>Read thou that challenge. Mark the penning of’t. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Were all the letters suns, I could not see one. |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | (aside)<br>I would not take this from report; it is,<br>And my heart breaks at it. |
| LEAR | (to [[Gloucester]]) Read. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | What—with the case of eyes? |
| LEAR | O ho, are you there with me? No eyes in your head, nor no money in your purse? Your eyes are in a heavy case, your purse in a light; yet you see how this world goes. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | I see it feelingly. |
| LEAR | What, art mad? A man may see how the world goes with no eyes; look with thy ears. See how yon justice rails upon yon simple thief. Hark in thy ear: handy-dandy, which is the thief, which is the justice? Thou hast seen a farmer’s dog bark at a beggar? |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Ay, sir. |
| LEAR | An the creature run from the cur, there thou mightst behold the great image of authority. A dog’s obeyed in office.<br>Thou rascal beadle, hold thy bloody hand.<br>Why dost thou lash that whore? Strip thine own back.<br>Thy [[Blood|blood]] as hotly lusts to use her in that kind<br>For which thou whip’st her. The usurer hangs the<br>cozener.<br>Through tattered rags small vices do appear;<br>Robes and furred gowns hides all. Get thee glass eyes,<br>And, like a scurvy politician, seem<br>To see the things thou dost not. No [[Tears|tears]], now.<br>Pull off my boots. Harder, harder! So. |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | (aside)<br>O, matter and impertinency mixed—<br>Reason in [[Madness|madness]]! |
| LEAR | If thou wilt weep my fortune, take my eyes.<br>I know thee [[Well|well]] enough: thy name is [[Gloucester]].<br>Thou must be patient. We came crying hither.<br>Thou know’st the first [[Time|time]] that we smell the air<br>We wail and cry. I will preach to thee. Mark me. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Alack, alack, the day! |
| *(didascalia)* | LEAR removing his [[Crown|crown]] of weeds |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | When we are born, we cry that we are come<br>To this great stage of fools. This’ a good block.<br>It were a delicate stratagem to shoe<br>A troop of [[Horse|horse]] with felt; and when I have stole<br>upon<br>These son-in-laws, then kill, kill, kill, kill, kill, kill! |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter three Gentlemen |
| *(didascalia)* | FIRST GENTLEMAN |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | O, here he is. Lay hands upon him, sirs.<br>(To Lear) Your most dear— |
| LEAR | No rescue? What, a prisoner? I am e’en<br>The natural [[Fool|fool]] of fortune. Use me [[Well|well]].<br>You shall have ransom. Let me have a surgeon;<br>I am cut to the brains. |
| *(didascalia)* | FIRST GENTLEMAN You shall have anything. |
| LEAR | No seconds? All myself?<br>Why, this would make a man a man of salt,<br>To use his eyes for [[Garden|garden]] [[Water|water]]-pots,<br>Ay, and laying [[Autumn|autumn]]’s [[Dust|dust]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | FIRST GENTLEMAN Good sir— |
| LEAR | I will die bravely, like a bridegroom.<br>What, I will be jovial. Come, come,<br>I am a [[King|king]], my masters, know you that? |
| *(didascalia)* | FIRST GENTLEMAN |
| LEAR | You are a royal one, and we obey you. |
| LEAR | Then there’s life in’t. Nay, an you get it, you shall get it with running. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit running, pursued by two Gentlemen |
| *(didascalia)* | FIRST GENTLEMAN |
| LEAR | A sight most pitiful in the meanest wretch,<br>Past speaking in a [[King|king]]. Thou hast one daughter<br>Who redeems [[Nature|nature]] from the general curse<br>Which twain hath brought her to. |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | Hail, gentle sir. |
| *(didascalia)* | FIRST GENTLEMAN Sir, [[Speed|speed]] you. What’s your will? |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | Do you hear aught of a battle toward? |
| *(didascalia)* | FIRST GENTLEMAN |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | Most sure and vulgar, everyone hears that<br>That can distinguish sense. |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | But, by your favour,<br>How near’s the other army? |
| *(didascalia)* | FIRST GENTLEMAN |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | Near and on speedy foot, the main; descriers<br>Stands on the hourly thoughts. |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | I thank you, sir. That’s all. |
| *(didascalia)* | FIRST GENTLEMAN |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | Though that the [[Queen]] on special cause is here,<br>Her army is moved on. |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | I thank you, sir. Exit Gentleman |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | You ever gentle gods, take my breath from me.<br>Let not my worser spirit tempt me again<br>To die before you please. |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | [[Well]] pray you, father. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Now, good sir, what are you? |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | A most poor man, made lame by fortune’s blows,<br>Who by the art of known and feeling sorrows<br>Am pregnant to good pity. Give me your hand,<br>I’ll lead you to some biding. |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] rising Hearty thanks. |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | The bounty and the benison of heaven<br>To send thee boot to boot. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Oswald the steward |
| OSWALD | A proclaimed prize! Most happy!<br>That eyeless head of thine was first framed flesh<br>To raise my fortunes. Thou most unhappy traitor,<br>Briefly thyself remember. The [[Sword|sword]] is out<br>That must destroy thee. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Now let thy friendly hand<br>Put strength enough to’t. |
| OSWALD | (to [[Edgar]]) Wherefore, bold peasant,<br>Durst thou support a published traitor? Hence,<br>Lest the infection of his fortune take<br>Like hold on thee. Let go his arm. |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | ‘Chill not let go, sir, without ’cagion. |
| OSWALD | Let go, slave, or thou diest. |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | Good gentleman, go your gate. Let poor volk pass. An ‘chud have been swaggered out of my life, it would not have been so long by a vortnight. Nay, come not near the old man. Keep out, ’che vor’ ye, or I’ll try whether your [[Costard|costard]] or my baton be the harder; I’ll be plain with you. |
| OSWALD | Out, dunghill!<br>They fight |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | ’Chill pick your teeth, sir. Come, no matter for your foins. |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Edgar]] knocks him down |
| OSWALD | Slave, thou hast slain me. [[Villain]], take my purse.<br>If ever thou wilt thrive, bury my [[Body|body]],<br>And give the letters which thou find’st about me<br>To [[Edmund]], Earl of [[Gloucester]]. Seek him out<br>Upon the British party. O untimely [[Death|death]]! [[Death]]!<br>He dies |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | I know thee [[Well|well]]—a serviceable [[Villain|villain]],<br>As duteous to the vices of thy mistress<br>As badness would [[Desire|desire]]. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | What, is he dead? |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | Sit you down, father. Rest you.<br>[[Gloucester]] sits<br>Let’s see his pockets. These letters that he speaks of<br>May be my friends. He’s dead; I am only sorrow<br>He had no other deathsman. Let us see.<br>Leave, gentle wax; and manners, blame us not.<br>To know our enemies’ minds we’d rip their hearts;<br>Their papers is more lawful.<br>He reads a letter<br>‘Let your reciprocal vows be remembered. You have<br>many opportunities to cut him off. If your will want<br>not, [[Time|time]] and place will be fruitfully offered. There is<br>nothing done if he return the conqueror; then am I<br>the prisoner, and his bed my jail, from the loathed<br>warmth whereof, deliver me, and supply the place for<br>your labour.<br>Your—wife, so I would say—your affectionate<br>servant, and for you her own for venture,<br>Gonoril.’<br>O indistinguished space of woman’s wit—<br>A plot upon her virtuous husband’s life,<br>And the exchange my brother!—Here in the sands<br>Thee I’ll rake up, the post unsanctified<br>Of murderous lechers, and in the mature [[Time|time]]<br>With this ungracious paper strike the sight<br>Of the [[Death|death]]-practised Duke. For him ’tis [[Well|well]]<br>That of thy [[Death|death]] and business I can tell.<br>[Exit with the [[Body|body]]] |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | The [[King]] is mad. How stiff is my vile sense,<br>That I stand up and have ingenious feeling<br>Of my huge sorrows! Better I were distraught;<br>So should my thoughts be fenced from my griefs,<br>And woes by wrong imaginations lose<br>The knowledge of themselves.<br>A drum afar off. [Enter [[Edgar]]] |
| [[Edgar|EDGAR]] | Give me your hand.<br>Far off methinks I hear the beaten drum.<br>Come, father, I’ll bestow you with a friend. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit [[Edgar]] guiding [[Gloucester]] |
