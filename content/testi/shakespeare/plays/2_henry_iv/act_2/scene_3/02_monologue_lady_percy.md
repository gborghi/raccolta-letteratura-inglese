| LADY PERCY | O yet, for God’s sake, go not to these wars! The [[Time|time]] was, father, that you broke your word When you were more endeared to it than now— When your own Percy, when my heart’s dear Harry, Threw many a northward look to see his father Bring up his powers; but he did long in vain. Who then persuaded you to stay at [[Home|home]]? There were two honours lost, yours and your son’s. For yours, the God of heaven brighten it! For his, it stuck upon him as the sun In the grey vault of heaven, and by his light Did all the chivalry of England move To do brave acts. He was indeed the glass Wherein the noble youth did dress themselves. He had no legs that practised not his gait; And speaking thick, which [[Nature|nature]] made his blemish, Became the accents of the valiant; For those that could speak low and tardily Would turn their own perfection to abuse To seem like him. So that in speech, in gait, In diet, in affections of delight, In military rules, humours of [[Blood|blood]], He was the mark and glass, copy and book, That fashioned others. And him—O wondrous him! O miracle of men!—him did you leave, Second to none, unseconded by you, To look upon the hideous god of war In disadvantage, to abide a field Where nothing but the sound of [[Hotspur]]’s name Did seem defensible; so you left him. Never, O never do his [[Ghost|ghost]] the wrong To hold your [[Honour|honour]] more precise and nice With others than with him. Let them [[Alone|alone]]. The Marshal and the Archbishop are strong. Had my sweet Harry had but half their numbers, Today might I, hanging on [[Hotspur]]’s neck, Have talked of Monmouth’s [[Grave|grave]]. |
