| *(didascalia)* | Enter the [[Earl of Northumberland]], Lady Northumberland, and Lady Percy |
| NORTHUMBERLAND | I pray thee, loving wife and gentle daughter,<br>Give even way unto my rough affairs.<br>Put not you on the visage of the times<br>And be like them to Percy troublesome. |
| LADY NORTHUMBERLAND | I have given over; I will speak no more.<br>Do what you will; your wisdom be your guide. |
| NORTHUMBERLAND | Alas, sweet wife, my [[Honour|honour]] is at pawn,<br>And, but my going, nothing can redeem it. |
| LADY PERCY | O yet, for God’s sake, go not to these wars!<br>The [[Time|time]] was, father, that you broke your word<br>When you were more endeared to it than now—<br>When your own Percy, when my heart’s dear Harry,<br>Threw many a northward look to see his father<br>Bring up his powers; but he did long in vain.<br>Who then persuaded you to stay at [[Home|home]]?<br>There were two honours lost, yours and your son’s.<br>For yours, the God of heaven brighten it!<br>For his, it stuck upon him as the sun<br>In the grey vault of heaven, and by his light<br>Did all the chivalry of England move<br>To do brave acts. He was indeed the glass<br>Wherein the noble youth did dress themselves.<br>He had no legs that practised not his gait;<br>And speaking thick, which [[Nature|nature]] made his blemish,<br>Became the accents of the valiant;<br>For those that could speak low and tardily<br>Would turn their own perfection to abuse<br>To seem like him. So that in speech, in gait,<br>In diet, in affections of delight,<br>In military rules, humours of [[Blood|blood]],<br>He was the mark and glass, copy and book,<br>That fashioned others. And him—O wondrous him!<br>O miracle of men!—him did you leave,<br>Second to none, unseconded by you,<br>To look upon the hideous god of war<br>In disadvantage, to abide a field<br>Where nothing but the sound of [[Hotspur]]’s name<br>Did seem defensible; so you left him.<br>Never, O never do his [[Ghost|ghost]] the wrong<br>To hold your [[Honour|honour]] more precise and nice<br>With others than with him. Let them [[Alone|alone]].<br>The Marshal and the Archbishop are strong.<br>Had my sweet Harry had but half their numbers,<br>Today might I, hanging on [[Hotspur]]’s neck,<br>Have talked of Monmouth’s [[Grave|grave]]. |
| NORTHUMBERLAND | Beshrew your heart,<br>Fair daughter, you do draw my spirits from me<br>With new lamenting ancient oversights.<br>But I must go and meet with danger there,<br>Or it will seek me in another place,<br>And find me worse provided. |
| LADY NORTHUMBERLAND | O fly to Scotland,<br>Till that the nobles and the armed commons<br>Have of their puissance made a little taste. |
| LADY PERCY | If they get ground and vantage of the [[King]],<br>Then join you with them like a rib of steel,<br>To make strength stronger; but, for all our loves,<br>First let them try themselves. So did your son.<br>He was so suffered. So came I a widow,<br>And never shall have length of life enough<br>To rain upon remembrance with mine eyes,<br>That it may grow and sprout as high as heaven<br>For recordation to my noble husband. |
| NORTHUMBERLAND | Come, come, go in with me. ’Tis with my mind<br>As with the tide swelled up unto his height,<br>That makes a still stand, running neither way.<br>Fain would I go to meet the Archbishop,<br>But many thousand reasons hold me back.<br>I will resolve for Scotland. There am I<br>Till [[Time|time]] and vantage crave my company. Exeunt |
