| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Mistress Quickly]] (the hostess of a tavern), and an officer, Fang followed at a distance by another officer, Snare |
| [[Mistress Quickly|MISTRESS QUICKLY]] | Master Fang, have you entered the action ? |
| FANG | It is entered. |
| [[Mistress Quickly|MISTRESS QUICKLY]] | Where’s your yeoman? Is’t a lusty yeoman? Will a stand to’t? |
| FANG | Sirrah!—Where’s Snare? |
| [[Mistress Quickly|MISTRESS QUICKLY]] | O Lord, ay, good Master Snare. |
| SNARE | coming forward Here, here. |
| FANG | Snare, we must arrest Sir John [[Falstaff]]. |
| [[Mistress Quickly|MISTRESS QUICKLY]] | Yea, good Master Snare, I have entered him and all. |
| SNARE | It may chance cost some of us our lives, for he will stab. |
| [[Mistress Quickly|MISTRESS QUICKLY]] | Alas the day, take heed of him; he stabbed me in mine own [[house]], most beastly, in good [[Faith|faith]]. A cares not what mischief he does; if his weapon be out, he will foin like any devil, he will spare neither man, woman, nor [[Child|child]]. |
| FANG | If I can close with him, I care not for his thrust. [[Mistress Quickly|MISTRESS QUICKLY]] No, nor I neither. I’ll be at your elbow. FANG An I but fist him once, an a come but within my vice— |
