| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[King]] Henry, [[Queen Margaret]] with her hawk on her fist, Duke Humphrey of [[Gloucester]], [[Cardinal Beaufort]], and the [[Duke of Suffolk]], with falconers hollering |
| [[Queen Margaret|QUEEN MARGARET]] | Believe me, lords, for flying at the brook<br>I saw not better sport these seven years’ day;<br>Yet, by your leave, the [[Wind|wind]] was very high,<br>And, ten to one, old Joan had not gone out. |
| [[King|KING]] HENRY | (to [[Gloucester]])<br>But what a point, my lord, your falcon made,<br>And what a pitch she flew above the rest!<br>To see how God in all his creatures works!<br>Yea, man and [[Birds|birds]] are fain of climbing high. |
| SUFFOLK | No marvel, an it like your majesty,<br>My Lord Protector’s hawks do tower so [[Well|well]];<br>They know their master loves to be aloft,<br>And bears his thoughts above his falcon’s pitch. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | My lord, ‘tis but a base ignoble mind<br>That mounts no higher than a bird can soar. |
| [[Cardinal Beaufort|CARDINAL BEAUFORT]] | I thought as much; he would be above the clouds. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Ay, my lord Cardinal, how think you by that?<br>Were it not good your grace could fly to heaven? |
| [[King|KING]] HENRY | The treasury of everlasting joy. |
| [[Cardinal Beaufort|CARDINAL BEAUFORT]] | (to [[Gloucester]])<br>Thy heaven is on earth; thine eyes and thoughts<br>Beat on a [[Crown|crown]], the treasure of thy heart,<br>Pernicious Protector, dangerous peer,<br>That smooth’st it so with [[King]] and common weal! |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | What, Cardinal? Is your priesthood grown<br>peremptory ?<br>Tantaene animis caelestibus irae?<br><br>Churchmen so hot? Good uncle, hide such malice<br>With some holiness—can you do it? |
| SUFFOLK | No malice, sir, no more than [[Well|well]] becomes<br>So good a quarrel and so bad a peer. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | As who, my lord ? |
| SUFFOLK | Why, as you, my lord—<br>An’t like your lordly Lord’s Protectorship. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Why, Suffolk, England knows thine insolence. |
| [[Queen Margaret|QUEEN MARGARET]] | And thy [[Ambition|ambition]], [[Gloucester]]. |
| [[King|KING]] HENRY | I prithee peace, Good [[Queen]], and whet not on these furious peers—For blessèd are the peacemakers on earth. |
| [[Cardinal Beaufort|CARDINAL BEAUFORT]] | Let me be blessed for the peace I make<br>Against this proud Protector with my [[Sword|sword]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Gloucester]] and [[Cardinal Beaufort]] speak privately to one another |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | [[Faith]], holy uncle, would’t were come to that. |
| [[Cardinal Beaufort|CARDINAL BEAUFORT]] | Marry, when thou dar’st. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Dare? I tell thee, priest,<br>Plantagenets could never brook the dare! |
| [[Cardinal Beaufort|CARDINAL BEAUFORT]] | I am Plantagenet as [[Well|well]] as thou,<br>And son to [[John of Gaunt]]. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | In bastardy. |
| [[Cardinal Beaufort|CARDINAL BEAUFORT]] | I scorn thy words. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Make up no factious numbers for the matter,<br>In thine own person answer thy abuse. |
| [[Cardinal Beaufort|CARDINAL BEAUFORT]] | Ay, where thou dar‘st not peep; an if thou dar’st,<br>This evening on the east side of the grove. |
| [[King|KING]] HENRY | How now, my lords? |
| [[Cardinal Beaufort|CARDINAL BEAUFORT]] | (aloud)<br>Believe me, cousin [[Gloucester]],<br>Had not your man put up the fowl so suddenly,<br>We had had more sport. (Aside to [[Gloucester]]) Come<br>with thy two-hand [[Sword|sword]]. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | (aloud) True, uncle.<br>(Aside to [[Cardinal Beaufort]])<br><br>Are ye advised? The east side of the grove. |
| [[Cardinal Beaufort|CARDINAL BEAUFORT]] | (aside to [[Gloucester]])<br>I am with you. |
| [[King|KING]] HENRY | Why, how now, uncle [[Gloucester]]? |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Talking of hawking, nothing else, my lord.<br>(Aside to the Cardinal)<br>Now, by God’s mother, priest, I’ll shave your [[Crown|crown]]<br>for this,<br>Or all my fence shall fail. |
| [[Cardinal Beaufort|CARDINAL BEAUFORT]] | (aside to [[Gloucester]])<br>Medice, teipsum—<br>Protector, see to’t [[Well|well]]; protect yourself. |
| [[King|KING]] HENRY | The winds grow high; so do your stomachs, lords.<br>How irksome is this music to my heart !<br>When such strings jar, what [[Hope|hope]] of harmony?<br>I pray, my lords, let me compound this strife. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter one crying ‘a miracle’ |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | What means this noise?<br>Fellow, what miracle dost thou proclaim? |
| ONE | A miracle, a miracle! |
| SUFFOLK | Come to the [[King]]—tell him what miracle. |
| ONE | (to [[King]] Henry)<br>Forsooth, a blind man at Saint Alban’s shrine |
| *(didascalia)* | Within this half-hour hath received his sight— |
| ONE | A man that ne’er saw in his life before. |
| [[King|KING]] HENRY | Now God be praised, that to believing souls<br>Gives light in darkness, comfort in [[Despair|despair]]! |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter the Mayor and aldermen of Saint Albans, with music, bearing the man, Simpcox, between two in a chair. Enter Simpcox’s Wife and other townsmeni with them |
| [[Cardinal Beaufort|CARDINAL BEAUFORT]] | Here comes the townsmen on procession<br>To present your highness with the man. |
| *(didascalia)* | The townsmen kneel |
| [[King|KING]] HENRY | Great is his comfort in this earthly vale,<br>Although by sight his sin be multiplied. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | (to the townsmen)<br>Stand by, my masters, bring him near the [[King]].<br>His highness’ pleasure is to talk with him. |
| *(didascalia)* | They  rise and  bear Simpcox before the [[King]] |
| [[King|KING]] HENRY | (to Simpcox)<br>Good fellow, tell us here the circumstance,<br>That we for thee may glorify the Lord.<br>What, hast thou been long blind and now restored? |
| SIMPCOX | Born blind, an’t please your grace. |
| SIMPCOX’S WIFE | Ay, indeed, was he. |
| SUFFOLK | What woman is this? |
| SIMPCOX’S WIFE | His wife, an’t like your worship. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Hadst thou been his mother<br>Thou couldst have better told. |
| [[King|KING]] HENRY | (to Simpcox) Where wert thou born? |
| SIMPCOX | At Berwick, in the north, an’t like your grace. |
| [[King|KING]] HENRY | Poor [[Soul|soul]], God’s goodness hath been great to thee.<br>Let never day nor [[Night|night]] unhallowed pass,<br>But still remember what the Lord hath done. |
| [[Queen Margaret|QUEEN MARGARET]] | (to Simpcox)<br>Tell me, good fellow, cam’st thou here by chance,<br>Or of devotion to this holy shrine? |
| SIMPCOX | God knows, of pure devotion, being called<br>A hundred times and oftener, in my [[Sleep|sleep]],<br>By good Saint Alban, who said, ‘Simon, come;<br>Come offer at my shrine and I will help thee.’ |
| SIMPCOX’S WIFE | Most true, forsooth, and many [[Time|time]] and oft<br>Myself have heard a voice to call him so. |
| [[Cardinal Beaufort|CARDINAL BEAUFORT]] | (to Simpcox)<br>What, art thou lame ? |
| SIMPCOX | Ay, God almighty help me. |
| SUFFOLK | How cam’st thou so? |
| SIMPCOX | A fall off of a tree. |
| SIMPCOX’S WIFE | (to Suffolk)<br>A plum tree, master. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | How long hast thou been blind? |
| SIMPCOX | O, born so, master. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | What, and wouldst climb a tree? SIMPCOX<br>But that in all my life, when I was a youth. |
| SIMPCOX’S WIFE | (to [[Gloucester]])<br>Too true—and bought his climbing very dear. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | (to Simpcox)<br>Mass, thou loved’st plums [[Well|well]] that wouldst venture so. |
| SIMPCOX | Alas, good master, my wife desired some damsons,<br>And made me climb with danger of my life. |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] aside |
| SIMPCOX | A subtle knave, but yet it shall not serve.<br>(To Simpcox) Let me see thine eyes: wink now, now<br>open them.<br>In my opinion yet thou seest not [[Well|well]]. |
| SIMPCOX | Yes, master, clear as day, I thank God and Saint Alban. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Sayst thou me so? (Pointing) What colour is this cloak of? |
| SIMPCOX | Red, master; red as [[Blood|blood]]. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Why, that’s [[Well|well]] said.<br>(Pointing) And his cloak? |
| SIMPCOX | Why, that’s green. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | (pointing) And what colour’s<br>His hose? |
| SIMPCOX | Yellow, master; yellow as gold. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | And what colour’s my gown? |
| SIMPCOX | Black, sir; coal-black, as jet. |
| [[King|KING]] HENRY | Why, then, thou know’st what colour jet is of? |
| UFFOLK | And yet I think jet did he never see. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | But cloaks and gowns before this day, a many. |
| SIMPCOX’S WIFE | Never before this day in all his life. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Tell me, sirrah, what’s my name? |
| SIMPCOX | Alas, master, I know not. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | (pointing) What’s his name? |
| SIMPCOX | I know not. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | (pointing) Nor his? |
| SIMPCOX | No, truly, sir. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | (pointing) Nor his name? |
| SIMPCOX | No indeed, master. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | What’s thine own name? |
| SIMPCOX | Simon Simpcox, an it please you, master. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Then, Simon, sit thou there the lying’st knave<br>In Christendom. If thou hadst been born blind<br>Thou mightst as [[Well|well]] have known our names as thus<br>To name the several colours we do wear.<br>Sight may distinguish colours, but suddenly<br>To nominate them all—it is impossible.<br>Saint Alban here hath done a miracle.<br>Would you not think his cunning to be great<br>That could restore this cripple to his legs again? |
| SIMPCOX | O master, that you could! |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | (to the Mayor and aldermen)<br>My masters of Saint Albans, have you not<br>Beadles in your town, and things called whips? |
| MAYOR | We have, my lord, an if it please your grace. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Then send for one presently. |
| MAYOR | (to a townsman)<br>Sirrah, go fetch the beadle hither straight. Exit one |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Bring me a stool.<br>A stool is brought<br>(To Simpcox) Now, sirrah, if you mean<br>To save yourself from whipping, leap me o’er<br>This stool and run away. |
| SIMPCOX | Alas, master,<br>I am not able even to stand [[Alone|alone]].<br>You go about to torture me in vain. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter a Beadle with whips |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | [[Well]], sirrah, we must have you find your legs.<br>(To the Beadle) Whip him till he leap over that same<br>stool. |
| BEADLE | I will, my lord.<br>(To Simpcox) Come on, sirrah, off with your doublet quickly. |
| SIMPCOX | Alas, master, what shall I do ? I am not able to stand. |
| *(didascalia)* | After the Beadle hath hit him once, he leaps over the stool and runs away. Some of the townsmen follow and cry, ‘A miracle ! A miracle !’ |
| [[King|KING]] HENRY | O God, seest thou this and bear’st so long? |
| [[Queen Margaret|QUEEN MARGARET]] | It made me laugh to see the [[Villain|villain]] run! |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] to the Beadlel |
| [[Queen Margaret|QUEEN MARGARET]] | Follow the knave, and take this drab away. |
| SIMPCOX’S WIFE | Alas, sir, we did it for pure need. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit the Beadle with the Wife |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] to the Mayor |
| SIMPCOX’S WIFE | Let them be whipped through every market-town 160<br>Till they come to Berwick, from whence they came. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt the Mayor and any remaining townsmen |
| [[Cardinal Beaufort|CARDINAL BEAUFORT]] | Duke Humphrey has done a miracle today. |
| SUFFOLK | True: made the lame to leap and fly away. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | But you have done more miracles than I—<br>You made, in a day, my lord, whole towns to fly. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter the [[Duke of Buckingham]] |
| [[King|KING]] HENRY | What tidings with our cousin [[Buckingham]]? |
| [[Buckingham|BUCKINGHAM]] | Such as my heart doth tremble to unfold.<br>A sort of naughty persons, lewdly bent,<br>Under the countenance and confederacy<br>Of Lady [[Eleanor]], the Protector’s wife,<br>The ringleader and head of all this rout,<br>Have practised dangerously against your state,<br>Dealing with witches and with conjurors,<br>Whom we have apprehended in the fact,<br>Raising up wicked spirits from under ground,<br>Demanding of [[King]] Henry’s life and [[Death|death]]<br>And other of your highness’ Privy Council.<br>And here’s the answer the devil did make to them.<br>[[Buckingham]] gives [[King]] Henry the writings |
| *(didascalia)* | [[King|KING]] HENRY (reads) |
| [[Buckingham|BUCKINGHAM]] | ‘First of the [[King]]: what shall of him become?<br>The Duke yet lives that Henry shall depose,<br>But him outlive and die a violent [[Death|death]].’<br>God’s will be done in all. [[Well]], to the rest.<br>(Reads) ‘Tell me what fate awaits the [[Duke of Suffolk]]?<br>By [[Water|water]] shall he die, and take his end.’ |
| SUFFOLK | [aside]<br>By [[Water|water]] must the [[Duke of Suffolk]] die?<br>It must be so, or else the devil doth lie. |
| [[King|KING]] HENRY | (reads)<br>‘What shall betide the Duke of Somerset?<br>Let him shun castles. Safer shall he be<br>Upon the sandy plains than where castles mounted<br>stand.’ |
| [[Cardinal Beaufort|CARDINAL BEAUFORT]] | (to [[Gloucester]])<br>And so, my Lord Protector, by this means<br>Your lady is forthcoming yet at [[London]].<br>(Aside to [[Gloucester]])<br>This news, I think, hath turned your weapon’s edge.<br>᾽“Tis like, my lord, you will not keep your hour. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Ambitious churchman, leave to afflict my heart.<br>Sorrow and grief have vanquished all my powers,<br>And, vanquished as I am, I yield to thee<br>Or to the meanest groom. |
| [[King|KING]] HENRY | O God, what mischiefs work the wicked ones,<br>Heaping confusion on their own heads thereby! |
| [[Queen Margaret|QUEEN MARGARET]] | [[Gloucester]], see here the tainture of thy nest,<br>And look thyself be faultless, thou wert best. |
| [[Gloucester|GLOUCESTER]] | Madam, for myself, to heaven I do appeal,<br>How I have loved my [[King]] and common weal;<br>And for my wife, I know not how it stands.<br>Sorry I am to hear what I have heard.<br>Noble she is, but if she have forgot<br>[[Honour]] and virtue and conversed with such<br>As, like to pitch, defile nobility,<br>I banish her my bed and company,<br>And give her as a prey to law and shame<br>That hath dishonoured [[Gloucester]]’s honest name. |
| [[King|KING]] HENRY | [[Well]], for this [[Night|night]] we will repose us here;<br>Tomorrow toward [[London]] back again,<br>To look into this business thoroughly,<br>And call these foul offenders to their answers,<br>And poise the cause in justice’ equal scales,<br>Whose beam stands sure, whose rightful cause<br>prevails. Flourish. Exeunt |
