| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[King]] Henry and the Earls of Salisbury and Warwick. Then the curtains be drawn revealing [[Cardinal Beaufort]] in his bed raving and staring as if he were mad |
| [[King|KING]] HENRY | (to [[Cardinal Beaufort]])<br>How fares my lord? Speak, Beaufort, to thy sovereign. |
| [[Cardinal Beaufort|CARDINAL BEAUFORT]] | If thou beest [[Death|death]], I’ll give thee England’s treasure<br>Enough to purchase such another island,<br>So thou wilt let me live and feel no [[Pain|pain]]. |
| [[King|KING]] HENRY | Ah, what a sign it is of evil life<br>Where [[Death|death]]’s approach is seen so terrible. |
| WARWICK | Beaufort, it is thy sovereign speaks to thee. |
| [[Cardinal Beaufort|CARDINAL BEAUFORT]] | Bring me unto my trial when you will.<br>Died he not in his bed ? Where should he die?<br>Can I make men live whe’er they will or no?<br>O, torture me no more—I will confess.<br>Alive again? Then show me where he is.<br>I’ll give a thousand pound to look upon him.<br>He hath no eyes! The [[Dust|dust]] hath blinded them.<br>Comb down his hair—look, look: it stands upright,<br>Like lime twigs set to catch my winged [[Soul|soul]].<br>Give me some drink, and bid the apothecary<br>Bring the strong [[Poison|poison]] that I bought of him. |
| [[King|KING]] HENRY | O Thou eternal mover of the heavens,<br>Look with a gentle eye upon this wretch.<br>O, beat away the busy meddling fiend<br>That lays strong siege unto this wretch’s [[Soul|soul]],<br>And from his bosom purge this black [[Despair|despair]]. |
| WARWICK | See how the pangs of [[Death|death]] do make him grin. |
| SALISBURY | Disturb him not; let him pass peaceably. |
| [[King|KING]] HENRY | Peace to his [[Soul|soul]], if God’s good pleasure be.<br>Lord Card‘nal, if thou think’st on heaven’s bliss,<br>Hold up thy hand, make signal of thy [[Hope|hope]].<br>[[Cardinal Beaufort]] dies<br>He dies and makes no sign. O God, forgive him. |
| WARWICK | So bad a [[Death|death]] argues a monstrous life. |
| [[King|KING]] HENRY | Forbear to judge, for we are sinners all.<br>Close up his eyes and draw the curtain close,<br>And let us all to meditation. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt, drawing the curtains. The bed is |
