| [[Cardinal Beaufort|CARDINAL BEAUFORT]] | A breach that craves a quick expedient stop! What counsel give you in this weighty cause? |
| YORK | That Somerset be sent as regent thither. ’Tis meet that lucky ruler be employed— Witness the fortune he hath had in France. |
| SOMERSET | If York, with all his far-fet policy, Had been the regent there instead of me, He never would have stayed in France so long. |
| YORK | No, not to lose it all as thou hast done. I rather would have lost my life betimes Than bring a burden of dishonour [[Home|home]] By staying there so long till all were lost. Show me one scar charactered on thy skin. Men’s flesh preserved so whole do seldom win. |
| [[Queen Margaret|QUEEN MARGARET]] | Nay, then, this spark will prove a raging [[Fire|fire]] If [[Wind|wind]] and fuel be brought to feed it with. No more, good York; sweet Somerset, be still. Thy fortune, York, hadst thou been regent there, Might happily have proved far worse than his. |
| YORK | What, worse than naught? Nay, then a shame take all! |
| SOMERSET | And, in the number, thee that wishest shame. |
| [[Cardinal Beaufort|CARDINAL BEAUFORT]] | My lord of York, try what your fortune is. Th’uncivil kerns of Ireland are in arms And temper clay with [[Blood|blood]] of Englishmen. To Ireland will you lead a band of men Collected choicely, from each county some, And try your hap against the Irishmen? |
| YORK | I will, my lord, so please his majesty. |
| SUFFOLK | Why, our authority is his consent, And what we do establish he confirms. Then, noble York, take thou this task in hand. |
| YORK | I am content. Provide me soldiers, lords, Whiles I take order for mine own affairs. |
| SUFFOLK | A charge, Lord York, that I will see performed. But now return we to the false Duke Humphrey. |
| [[Cardinal Beaufort|CARDINAL BEAUFORT]] | No more of him—for I will deal with him That henceforth he shall trouble us no more. And so, break off; the day is almost spent. Lord Suffolk, you and I must talk of that event. |
| YORK | My lord of Suffolk, within fourteen days At Bristol I expect my soldiers; For there I’ll [[Ship|ship]] them all for Ireland. |
| SUFFOLK | I’ll see it truly done, my lord of York. Exeunt all but York |
