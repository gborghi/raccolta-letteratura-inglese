| [[Queen Margaret|QUEEN MARGARET]] | Away. Though parting be a fretful corrosive, It is applied to a deathful wound. To France, sweet Suffolk. Let me hear from thee. For wheresoe’er thou art in this world’s Globe I’ll have an Iris that shall find thee out. |
| SUFFOLK | I go. |
| [[Queen Margaret|QUEEN MARGARET]] | And take my heart with thee. She kisseth him |
| SUFFOLK | A [[Jewel|jewel]], locked into the woefull’st cask That ever did contain a thing of worth. Even as a splitted barque, so sunder we—This way fall I to [[Death|death]]. |
| [[Queen Margaret|QUEEN MARGARET]] | This way for me. Exeunt severally |
