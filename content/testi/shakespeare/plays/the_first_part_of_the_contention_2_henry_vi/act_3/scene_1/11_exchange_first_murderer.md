| FIRST MURDERER | (to the Second Murderer) Run to my lord of Suffolk—let him know We have dispatched the Duke as he commanded. |
| SECOND MURDERER | O that it were to do! What have we done? Didst ever hear a man so penitent? Enter the [[Duke of Suffolk]] |
| FIRST MURDERER | Here comes my lord. |
| SUFFOLK | Now, sirs, have you dispatched this thing? |
| FIRST MURDERER | Ay, my good lord, he’s dead. |
| SUFFOLK | Why, that’s [[Well|well]] said. Go, get you to my [[house]]. I will reward you for this venturous deed. |
| SUFFOLK | The [[King]] and all the peers are here at hand. Have you laid fair the bed? Is all things [[Well|well]], According as I gave directions? |
| FIRST MURDERER | ’Tis, my good lord. |
| SUFFOLK | Then draw the curtains close; away, be gone! Exeunt the Murderers, drawing the curtains as they leave Sound trumpets, then enter [[King]] Henry and [[Queen]] |
| SUFFOLK | Margaret, [[Cardinal Beaufort]], the Duke of Somerset, |
| SUFFOLK | and attendants |
| [[King|KING]] HENRY | to Suffolk Go call our uncle to our presence straight. Say we intend to try his grace today If he be guilty, as ’tis published. |
| SUFFOLK | I’ll call him presently, my noble lord. Exit |
| [[King|KING]] HENRY | Lords, take your places; and, I pray you all, Proceed no straiter ’gainst our uncle [[Gloucester]] Than from true evidence, of good esteem, He be approved in practice culpable. |
| [[Queen Margaret|QUEEN MARGARET]] | God forbid any malice should prevail That faultless may condemn a noble man! Pray God he may acquit him of suspicion! |
| [[King|KING]] HENRY | I thank thee, Meg. These words content me much. Enter Suffolk How now? Why look’st thou pale? Why tremblest thou? Where is our uncle? What’s the matter, Suffolk? |
| SUFFOLK | Dead in his bed, my lord—[[Gloucester]] is dead. |
| [[Queen Margaret|QUEEN MARGARET]] | Marry, God forfend! |
| [[Cardinal Beaufort|CARDINAL BEAUFORT]] | God’s secret judgement. I did dream tonight The Duke was dumb and could not speak a word. [[King]] Henry falls to the ground |
| [[Queen Margaret|QUEEN MARGARET]] | How fares my lord? Help, lords—the [[King]] is dead! |
| SOMERSET | Rear up his [[Body|body]]; wring him by the nose. |
| [[Queen Margaret|QUEEN MARGARET]] | Run, go, help, help! O Henry, ope thine eyes! |
| SUFFOLK | He doth revive again. Madam, be patient. |
| [[King|KING]] HENRY | O heavenly God! |
| [[Queen Margaret|QUEEN MARGARET]] | How fares my gracious lord? |
| SUFFOLK | Comfort, my sovereign; gracious Henry, comfort. |
| [[King|KING]] HENRY | What, doth my lord of Suffolk comfort me? Came he right now to sing a raven’s note Whose dismal tune bereft my vital powers; And thinks he that the chirping of a wren, By crying comfort from a hollow breast Can chase away the first-conceived sound? Hide not thy [[Poison|poison]] with such sugared words. He begins to rise. Suffolk offers to assist him Lay not thy hands on me—forbear, I say! Their touch affrights me as a serpent’s sting. Thou baleful messenger, out of my sight! Upon thy eyeballs murderous tyranny Sits in grim majesty to fright the world. Look not upon me, for thine eyes are wounding— Yet do not go away. Come, basilisk, And kill the [[Innocent|innocent]] gazer with thy sight. For in the shade of [[Death|death]] I shall find joy; In life, but [[Double|double]] [[Death|death]], now [[Gloucester]]’s dead. |
