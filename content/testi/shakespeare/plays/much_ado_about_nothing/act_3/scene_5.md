| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Leonato]], and |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Dogberry]] the constable, and Verges the headborough |
| [[Leonato|LEONATO]] | What would you with me, honest neighbour? |
| [[Dogberry|DOGBERRY]] | Marry, sir, I would have some confidence with you that decerns you nearly. |
| [[Leonato|LEONATO]] | Brief I pray you, for you see it is a busy [[Time|time]] with me. |
| [[Dogberry|DOGBERRY]] | Marry, this it is, sir. |
| VERGES | Yes, in [[Truth|truth]] it is, sir. |
| [[Leonato|LEONATO]] | What is it, my good friends? |
| [[Dogberry|DOGBERRY]] | Goodman Verges, sir, speaks a little off the matter—an old man, sir, and his wits are not so blunt as, God help, I would [[Desire|desire]] they were. But in [[Faith|faith]], honest as the skin between his brows. |
| VERGES | Yes, I thank God, I am as honest as any man living that is an old man and no honester than I. |
| [[Dogberry|DOGBERRY]] | Comparisons are odorous. Palabras, neighbour Verges. |
| [[Leonato|LEONATO]] | Neighbours, you are tedious. |
| [[Dogberry|DOGBERRY]] | It pleases your worship to say so, but we are the poor Duke’s officers. But truly, for mine own part, if I were as tedious as a [[King|king]] I could find in my heart to bestow it all of your worship. |
| [[Leonato|LEONATO]] | All thy tediousness on me, ah? |
| [[Dogberry|DOGBERRY]] | Yea, an ’twere a thousand pound more than ’tis, for I hear as good exclamation on your worship as of any man in the [[City|city]], and though I be but a poor man, I am glad to hear it. |
| VERGES | And so am I. |
| [[Leonato|LEONATO]] | I would fain know what you have to say. |
| VERGES | Marry, sir, our watch tonight, excepting your worship’s presence, ha’ ta’en a couple of as arrant knaves as any in Messina. |
| [[Dogberry|DOGBERRY]] | A good old man, sir. He will be talking. As they say, when the age is in, the wit is out. God help us, it is a world to see. [[Well]] said, i‘[[Faith|faith]], neighbour Verges. [[Well]], God’s a good man. An two men ride of a [[Horse|horse]], one must ride behind. An honest [[Soul|soul]], i’[[Faith|faith]], sir, by my troth he is, as ever broke bread. But, God is to be worshipped, all men are not alike, alas, good neighbour. |
| [[Leonato|LEONATO]] | Indeed, neighbour, he comes too short of you. |
| [[Dogberry|DOGBERRY]] | Gifts that God gives! |
| [[Leonato|LEONATO]] | I must leave you. |
| [[Dogberry|DOGBERRY]] | One word, sir. Our watch, sir, have indeed comprehended two auspicious persons, and we would have them this morning examined before your worship. |
| [[Leonato|LEONATO]] | Take their examination yourself, and bring it me. I am now in great haste, as it may appear unto you. |
| [[Dogberry|DOGBERRY]] | It shall be suffigance. |
| [[Leonato|LEONATO]] | Drink some wine ere you go. Fare you [[Well|well]]. Enter a Messenger |
| MESSENGER | My lord, they stay for you to give your daughter to her husband. |
| [[Leonato|LEONATO]] | I’ll wait upon them, I am ready. Exeunt [[Leonato]] and Messenger |
| [[Dogberry|DOGBERRY]] | Go, good partner, go get you to Francis Seacoal, bid him bring his pen and inkhorn to the jail. We are now to examination these men. |
| VERGES | And we must do it wisely. |
| [[Dogberry|DOGBERRY]] | We will spare for no wit, I warrant you. Here’s that shall drive some of them to a non-com. Only get the learned writer to set down our excommunication, and meet me at the jail. Exeunt |
