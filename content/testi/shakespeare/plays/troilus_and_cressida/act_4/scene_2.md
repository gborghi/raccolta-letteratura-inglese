| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Troilus]] and [[Cressida]] |
| [[Troilus|TROILUS]] | Dear, trouble not yourself. The morn is cold. |
| [[Cressida|CRESSIDA]] | Then, sweet my lord, I’ll call mine uncle down.<br>He shall unbolt the gates. |
| [[Troilus|TROILUS]] | Trouble him not.<br>To bed, to bed! [[Sleep]] lull those pretty eyes<br>And give as soft attachment to thy senses<br>As to infants empty of all thought. |
| [[Cressida|CRESSIDA]] | Good morrow, then. |
| [[Troilus|TROILUS]] | I prithee now, to bed. |
| [[Cressida|CRESSIDA]] | Are you aweary of me? |
| [[Troilus|TROILUS]] | O [[Cressida]]! But that the busy day,<br>Waked by the lark, hath roused the ribald crows,<br>And dreaming [[Night|night]] will hide our joys no longer,<br>I would not from thee. |
| [[Cressida|CRESSIDA]] | [[Night]] hath been too brief. |
| [[Troilus|TROILUS]] | Beshrew the [[Witch|witch]]! With venomous wights she stays<br>As hideously as hell, but flies the grasps of [[Love|love]]<br>With wings more momentary-swift than thought.<br>You will catch cold and curse me. |
| [[Cressida|CRESSIDA]] | Prithee, tarry. You men will never tarry.<br>O foolish Cressid! I might have still held off,<br>And then you would have tarried.—Hark, there’s one<br>up. |
| *(didascalia)* | She veils herself |
| [[Pandarus|PANDARUS]] | (within) What’s all the doors open here? |
| [[Troilus|TROILUS]] | It is your uncle. |
| [[Cressida|CRESSIDA]] | A pestilence on him! Now will he be mocking.<br>I shall have such a life. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Pandarus]] |
| [[Pandarus|PANDARUS]] | How now, how now, how go maidenheads? (To [[Cressida]]) Here, you, maid! Where’s my cousin Cressid? |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Cressida|CRESSIDA]] unveiling |
| [[Pandarus|PANDARUS]] | Go hang yourself. You naughty, mocking uncle!<br>You bring me to do—and then you flout me too. |
| [[Pandarus|PANDARUS]] | To do what? To do what?—Let her say what.—What have I brought you to do? |
| [[Cressida|CRESSIDA]] | Come, come, beshrew your heart. You’ll ne’er be<br>good,<br>Nor suffer others. |
| PINDARUS | Ha ha! Alas, poor wretch. Ah, poor capocchia, hast not slept tonight? Would he not—a naughty man—let it [[Sleep|sleep]]? A bugbear take him. |
| [[Cressida|CRESSIDA]] | (to [[Troilus]])<br>Did not I tell you? Would he were knocked i’th’ head. |
| *(didascalia)* | One knocks within |
| [[Cressida|CRESSIDA]] | Who’s that at door?—Good uncle, go and see.—<br>My lord, come you again into my chamber.<br>You smile and mock me, as if I meant naughtily. |
| [[Troilus|TROILUS]] | Ha ha! |
| [[Cressida|CRESSIDA]] | Come, you are deceived, I think of no such thing.<br>One knocks within<br><br>How earnestly they knock! Pray you come in.<br>I would not for half Troy have you seen here. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt [[Troilus]] and [[Cressida]] |
| [[Pandarus|PANDARUS]] | Who’s there? What’s the matter? Will you beat down the door? |
| *(didascalia)* | He opens the door. Enter Aeneas |
| [[Pandarus|PANDARUS]] | How now, what’s the matter? |
| AENEAS | Good morrow, lord, good morrow. |
| [[Pandarus|PANDARUS]] | Who’s there? My Lord Aeneas? By my troth,<br>I knew you not. What news with you so early? |
| AENEAS | Is not Prince [[Troilus]] here? |
| [[Pandarus|PANDARUS]] | Here? What should he do here? |
| AENEAS | Come, he is here, my lord. Do not deny him.<br>It doth import him much to speak with me. |
| [[Pandarus|PANDARUS]] | Is he here, say you? It’s more than I know, I’ll be sworn. For my own part, I came in late. What should he do here? |
| AENEAS | Whoa! Nay, then. Come, come, you’ll do him wrong<br>Ere you are ware. You’ll be so true to him<br>To be false to him. Do not you know of him, |
| *(didascalia)* | But yet go fetch him hither. Go. Exit [[Pandarus]] |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Troilus]] |
| [[Troilus|TROILUS]] | How now, what’s the matter? |
| AENEAS | My lord, I scarce have leisure to salute you,<br>My matter is so rash. There is at hand<br>[[Paris]] your brother and Deiphobus,<br>The Grecian Diomed, and our Antenor<br>Delivered to us—and for him forthwith,<br>Ere the first sacrifice, within this hour,<br>We must give up to Diomedes’ hand<br>The Lady [[Cressida]]. |
| [[Troilus|TROILUS]] | Is it so concluded? |
| AENEAS | By Priam and the general state of Troy.<br>They are at hand, and ready to effect it. |
| [[Troilus|TROILUS]] | How my achievements mock me.<br>I will go meet them—and, my Lord Aeneas,<br>We met by chance: you did not find me here. |
| AENEAS | Good, good, my lord: the secrecies of [[Nature|nature]]<br>Have not more gift in taciturnity. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt |
