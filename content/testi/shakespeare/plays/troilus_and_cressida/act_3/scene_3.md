| *(didascalia)* | Flourish. Enter [[Ulysses]], Diomedes, Nestor, [[Agamemnon]], Menelaus, [[Ajax]], and Calchas |
| CALCHAS | Now, princes, for the service I have done you,<br>Th‘advantage of the [[Time|time]] prompts me aloud<br>To call for recompense. Appear it to your mind<br>That through the sight I bear in things to come<br>I have abandoned Troy, left my profession,<br>Incurred a traitor’s name, exposed myself<br>From certain and possessed conveniences<br>To doubtful fortunes, sequest’ring from me all<br>That [[Time|time]], acquaintance, custom, and condition<br>Made tame and most familiar to my [[Nature|nature]],<br>And here to do you service am become<br>As new into the world, strange, unacquainted.<br>I do beseech you, as in way of taste,<br>To give me now a little benefit<br>Out of those many registered in promise<br>Which you say live to come in my behalf. |
| [[Agamemnon|AGAMEMNON]] | What wouldst thou of us, Trojan? Make demand. |
| CALCHAS | You have a Trojan prisoner called Antenor,<br>Yesterday took. Troy holds him very dear.<br>Oft have you—often have you thanks therefor—<br>Desired my Cressid in right great exchange,<br>Whom Troy hath still denied. But this Antenor<br>I know is such a wrest in their affairs<br>That their negotiations all must slack,<br>Wanting his manage, and they will almost<br>Give us a prince of [[Blood|blood]], a son of Priam,<br>In change of him. Let him be sent, great princes,<br>And he shall buy my daughter, and her presence<br>Shall quite strike off all service I have done<br>In most accepted [[Pain|pain]]. |
| [[Agamemnon|AGAMEMNON]] | Let Diomedes bear him,<br>And bring us Cressid hither; Calchas shall have<br>What he requests of us. Good Diomed,<br>Furnish you fairly for this interchange;<br>Withal bring word if [[Hector]] will tomorrow<br>Be answered in his challenge. [[Ajax]] is ready. |
| DIOMEDES | This shall I undertake, and ’tis a burden<br>Which I am proud to bear. Exit with Calchas |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Achilles]] and Patroclus in their tent |
| [[Ulysses|ULYSSES]] | [[Achilles]] stands i‘th’ entrance of his tent.<br>Please it our general pass strangely by him,<br>As if he were forgot; and, princes all,<br>Lay negligent and loose regard upon him.<br>I will come last. ’Tis like he’ll question me<br>Why such unplausive eyes are bent, why turned on<br>him.<br>If so, I have derision medicinable<br>To use between your strangeness and his [[Pride|pride]],<br>Which his own will shall have [[Desire|desire]] to drink.<br>It may do good. [[Pride]] hath no other glass<br>To show itself but [[Pride|pride]]; for supple knees<br>Feed arrogance and are the proud man’s fees. |
| [[Agamemnon|AGAMEMNON]] | We’ll execute your purpose and put on<br>A form of strangeness as we pass along.<br>So do each lord, and either greet him not<br>Or else disdainfully, which shall shake him more<br>Than if not looked on. I will lead the way.<br>They pass by the tent, in turn |
| [[Achilles|ACHILLES]] | What, comes the general to speak with me?<br>You know my mind: I’ll fight no more ’gainst Troy. |
| [[Agamemnon|AGAMEMNON]] | (to Nestor)<br>What says [[Achilles]]? Would he aught with us? |
| NESTOR | (to [[Achilles]])<br>Would you, my lord, aught with the general? |
| [[Achilles|ACHILLES]] | No. |
| NESTOR | (to [[Agamemnon]])<br>Nothing, my lord. |
| [[Agamemnon|AGAMEMNON]] | The better. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt [[Agamemnon]] and Nestor |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Achilles|ACHILLES]] to Menelaus Good day, good day. |
| MENELAUS | How do you? How do you? |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit |
| [[Achilles|ACHILLES]] | (to Patroclus)<br>What, does the cuckold scorn me? |
| [[Ajax|AJAX]] | How now, Patroclus? |
| [[Achilles|ACHILLES]] | Good morrow, [[Ajax]]. |
| [[Ajax|AJAX]] | Ha? |
| [[Achilles|ACHILLES]] | Good morrow. |
| [[Ajax|AJAX]] | Ay, and good next day too. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit |
| [[Achilles|ACHILLES]] | (to Patroclus)<br>What mean these fellows? Know they not [[Achilles]]? |
| PATROCLUS | They pass by strangely. They were used to bend,<br>To send their smiles before them to [[Achilles]],<br>To come as humbly as they use to creep<br>To holy altars. |
| [[Achilles|ACHILLES]] | What, am I poor of late?<br>‘Tis certain, [[Greatness|greatness]] once fall’n out with fortune<br>Must fall out with men too. What the declined is<br>He shall as soon read in the eyes of others<br>As feel in his own fall; for men, like butterflies,<br>Show not their mealy wings but to the [[Summer|summer]],<br>And not a man, for being simply man,<br>Hath any [[Honour|honour]], but [[Honour|honour]] for those honours<br>That are without him—as place, riches, and favour:<br>Prizes of accident as oft as merit;<br>Which, when they fall, as being slippery standers—<br>The [[Love|love]] that leaned on them, as slippery too—<br>Doth one pluck down another, and together<br>Die in the fall. But ’tis not so with me.<br>Fortune and I are friends. I do enjoy<br>At ample point all that I did possess,<br>Save these men’s looks—who do methinks find out<br>Something not worth in me such rich beholding<br>As they have often given. Here is [[Ulysses]];<br>I’ll interrupt his reading. How now, [[Ulysses]]? |
| [[Ulysses|ULYSSES]] | Now, great Thetis’ son. |
| [[Achilles|ACHILLES]] | What are you reading? |
| [[Ulysses|ULYSSES]] | A strange fellow here<br>Writes me that man, how dearly ever parted,<br>How much in having, or without or in,<br>Cannot make boast to have that which he hath,<br>Nor feels not what he owes, but by reflection—<br>As when his virtues, shining upon others,<br>Heat them, and they retort that heat again<br>To the first givers. |
| [[Achilles|ACHILLES]] | This is not strange, [[Ulysses]].<br>The [[Beauty|beauty]] that is borne here in the face<br>The bearer knows not, but commends itself<br>To others’ eyes. Nor doth the eye itself,<br>That most pure spirit of sense, behold itself,<br>Not going from itself; but eye to eye opposed<br>Salutes each other with each other’s form.<br>For speculation turns not to itself<br>Till it hath travelled and is mirrored there<br>Where it may see itself. This is not strange at all. |
| [[Ulysses|ULYSSES]] | I do not strain at the position—<br>It is familiar—but at the author’s drift;<br>Who in his circumstance expressly proves<br>That no man is the lord of anything,<br>Though in and of him there be much consisting,<br>Till he communicate his parts to others.<br>Nor doth he of himself know them for aught<br>Till he behold them formed in th‘applause<br>Where they’re extended—who, like an arch, reverb’rate<br>The voice again; or, like a gate of steel<br>Fronting the sun, receives and renders back<br>His figure and his heat. I was much rapt in this,<br>And apprehended here immediately<br>The unknown [[Ajax]].<br>Heavens, what a man is there! A very [[Horse|horse]],<br>That has he knows not what. [[Nature]], what things<br>there are,<br>Most abject in regard and dear in use.<br>What things again, most dear in the esteem<br>And poor in worth. Now shall we see tomorrow<br>An act that very chance doth throw upon him.<br>[[Ajax]] renowned? O heavens, what some men do,<br>While some men leave to do.<br>How some men creep in skittish Fortune’s hall<br>Whiles others play the idiots in her eyes;<br>How one man eats into another’s [[Pride|pride]]<br>While [[Pride|pride]] is fasting in his wantonness.<br>To see these Grecian lords! Why, even already<br>They clap the lubber [[Ajax]] on the shoulder,<br>As if his foot were on brave [[Hector]]’s breast<br>And great Troy shrinking. |
| [[Achilles|ACHILLES]] | I do believe it,<br>For they passed by me as misers do by beggars,<br>Neither gave to me good word nor look.<br>What, are my deeds forgot? |
| [[Ulysses|ULYSSES]] | [[Time]] hath, my lord,<br>A wallet at his back, wherein he puts<br>Alms for oblivion, a great-sized [[Monster|monster]]<br>Of ingratitudes. Those scraps are good deeds past,<br>Which are devoured as fast as they are made,<br>Forgot as soon as done. Perseverance, dear my lord,<br>Keeps [[Honour|honour]] bright. To have done is to hang<br>Quite out of fashion, like a rusty mail<br>In monumental mock‘ry. Take the instant way,<br>For [[Honour|honour]] travels in a strait so narrow,<br>Where one but goes abreast. Keep then the path,<br>For emulation hath a thousand sons<br>That one by one pursue: if you give way,<br>Or hedge aside from the direct forthright,<br>Like to an entered tide they all rush by<br>And leave you hindmost;<br>Or, like a gallant [[Horse|horse]] fall’n in first rank,<br>Lie there for pavement to the abject rear,<br>O’errun and trampled on. Then what they do in<br>present,<br>Though less than yours in past, must o‘ertop yours.<br>For [[Time]] is like a fashionable host,<br>That slightly shakes his parting guest by th’ hand<br>And, with his arms outstretched as he would fly,<br>Grasps in the comer. Welcome ever smiles,<br>And Farewell goes out sighing. O let not virtue seek<br>Remuneration for the thing it was;<br>For [[Beauty|beauty]], wit,<br>High birth, vigour of bone, [[Desert|desert]] in service,<br>[[Love]], [[Friendship|friendship]], [[Charity|charity]], are subjects all<br>To envious and calumniating [[Time|time]].<br>One touch of [[Nature|nature]] makes the whole world kin—<br>That all with one consent praise new-born gauds,<br>Though they are made and moulded of things past,<br>And give to [[Dust|dust]] that is a little gilt<br>More laud than gilt o’er-dusted.<br>The present eye praises the present object.<br>Then marvel not, thou great and complete man,<br>That all the Greeks begin to worship [[Ajax]],<br>Since things in motion sooner catch the eye<br>Than what not stirs. The cry went once on thee,<br>And still it might, and yet it may again,<br>If thou wouldst not entomb thyself alive<br>And case thy reputation in thy tent,<br>Whose glorious deeds but in these fields of late<br>Made emulous missions ’mongst the gods themselves,<br>And drove great Mars to faction. |
| [[Achilles|ACHILLES]] | Of this my privacy<br>I have strong reasons. |
| [[Ulysses|ULYSSES]] | But ’gainst your privacy<br>The reasons are more potent and heroical.<br>’Tis known, [[Achilles]], that you are in [[Love|love]]<br>With one of Priam’s daughters. |
| [[Achilles|ACHILLES]] | Ha? Known? |
| [[Ulysses|ULYSSES]] | Is that a [[Wonder|wonder]]?<br>The [[Providence|providence]] that’s in a watchful state<br>Knows almost every grain of Pluto’s gold,<br>Finds [[Bottom|bottom]] in th’uncomprehensive deeps,<br>Keeps place with aught, and almost like the gods<br>Do infant thoughts unveil in their dumb cradles.<br>There is a mystery, with whom relation<br>Durst never meddle, in the [[Soul|soul]] of state,<br>Which hath an operation more divine<br>Than breath or pen can give expressure to.<br>All the commerce that you have had with Troy<br>As perfectly is ours as yours, my lord;<br>And better would it fit [[Achilles]] much<br>To throw down [[Hector]] than Polyxena.<br>But it must grieve young Pyrrhus now at [[Home|home]],<br>When fame shall in his island sound her trump<br>And all the Greekish girls shall tripping sing,<br>’Great [[Hector]]’s sister did [[Achilles]] win,<br>But our great [[Ajax]] bravely beat down him’.<br>Farewell, my lord. I as your [[Lover|lover]] speak.<br>The [[Fool|fool]] slides o’er the ice that you should break. Exit |
| PATROCLUS | To this effect, [[Achilles]], have I moved you.<br>A woman impudent and mannish grown<br>Is not more loathed than an effeminate man<br>In [[Time|time]] of action. I stand condemned for this.<br>They think my little stomach to the war<br>And your great [[Love|love]] to me restrains you thus.<br>Sweet, rouse yourself, and the weak wanton Cupid<br>Shall from your neck unloose his amorous fold<br>And like a dew-drop from the [[Lion|lion]]’s mane<br>Be shook to air. |
| [[Achilles|ACHILLES]] | Shall [[Ajax]] fight with [[Hector]]? |
| PATROCLUS | Ay, and perhaps receive much [[Honour|honour]] by him. |
| [[Achilles|ACHILLES]] | I see my reputation is at stake.<br>My fame is shrewdly gored. |
| PATROCLUS | O then beware:<br>Those wounds heal ill that men do give themselves.<br>Omission to do what is necessary<br>Seals a commission to a blank of danger,<br>And danger like an ague subtly taints<br>Even then when we sit idly in the sun. |
| [[Achilles|ACHILLES]] | Go call [[Thersites]] hither, sweet Patroclus.<br>I’ll send the [[Fool|fool]] to [[Ajax]], and [[Desire|desire]] him<br>T’invite the Trojan lords after the combat<br>To see us here unarmed. I have a woman’s [[Longing|longing]],<br>An appetite that I am sick withal,<br>To see great [[Hector]] in his weeds of peace, |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Thersites]] |
| [[Achilles|ACHILLES]] | To talk with him and to behold his visage<br>Even to my full of view.—A labour saved. |
| [[Thersites|THERSITES]] | A [[Wonder|wonder]]! |
| [[Achilles|ACHILLES]] | What? |
| [[Thersites|THERSITES]] | [[Ajax]] goes up and down the field, as asking for himself. |
| [[Achilles|ACHILLES]] | How so? |
| [[Thersites|THERSITES]] | He must fight singly tomorrow with [[Hector]], and is so prophetically proud of an heroical cudgelling that he raves in saying nothing. |
| [[Achilles|ACHILLES]] | How can that be? |
| [[Thersites|THERSITES]] | Why, a stalks up and down like a peacock—a stride and a stand; ruminates like an hostess that hath no arithmetic but her brain to set down her reckoning; bites his lip with a politic regard, as who should say ‘There were wit in this head, an’t would out’—and so there is; but it lies as coldly in him as [[Fire|fire]] in a flint, which will not show without knocking. The man’s undone for ever, for if [[Hector]] break not his neck i‘th’ combat he’ll break’t himself in vainglory. He knows not me. I said, ‘Good morrow, [[Ajax]]’, and he replies, ‘Thanks, [[Agamemnon]]’. What think you of this man that takes me for the General? He’s grown a very land-fish, languageless, a [[Monster|monster]]. A plague of opinion! A man may wear it on both sides like a leather jerkin. |
| [[Achilles|ACHILLES]] | Thou must be my ambassador to him, [[Thersites]]. |
| [[Thersites|THERSITES]] | Who, I? Why, he’ll answer nobody. He professes not answering. Speaking is for beggars. He wears his tongue in’s arms. I will put on his presence. Let Patroclus make demands to me. You shall see the pageant of [[Ajax]]. |
| [[Achilles|ACHILLES]] | To him, Patroclus. Tell him I humbly [[Desire|desire]] the valiant [[Ajax]] to invite the most valorous [[Hector]] to come unarmed to my tent, and to procure safe-conduct for his person of the magnanimous and most illustrious six-or-seven-times-honoured captain-general of the Grecian army, [[Agamemnon]]; et cetera. Do this. |
| PATROCLUS | (to [[Thersites]]) Jove bless great [[Ajax]]! |
| [[Thersites|THERSITES]] | H’m. |
| PATROCLUS | I come from the worthy [[Achilles]]— |
| [[Thersites|THERSITES]] | Ha? |
| PATROCLUS | Who most humbly desires you to invite [[Hector]] to his tent— |
| [[Thersites|THERSITES]] | H’m! |
| PATROCLUS | And to procure safe-conduct from [[Agamemnon]]. |
| [[Thersites|THERSITES]] | [[Agamemnon]]? |
| PATROCLUS | Ay, my lord. |
| [[Thersites|THERSITES]] | Ha! |
| PATROCLUS | What say you to’t? |
| [[Thersites|THERSITES]] | God b’wi’ you, with all my heart. |
| PATROCLUS | Your answer, sir? |
| [[Thersites|THERSITES]] | If tomorrow be a fair day, by eleven o’[[Clock|clock]] it will go one way or other. Howsoever, he shall pay for me ere he has me. |
| PATROCLUS | Your answer, sir? |
| [[Thersites|THERSITES]] | Fare ye [[Well|well]], with all my heart. |
| [[Achilles|ACHILLES]] | Why, but he is not in this tune, is he? |
| [[Thersites|THERSITES]] | No, but he’s out o’ tune thus. What music will be in him when [[Hector]] has knocked out his brains, I know not. But I am feared none, unless the fiddler [[Apollo]] get his sinews to make catlings on. |
| [[Achilles|ACHILLES]] | Come, thou shalt bear a letter to him straight. |
| [[Thersites|THERSITES]] | Let me carry another to his [[Horse|horse]], for that’s the more capable creature. |
| [[Achilles|ACHILLES]] | My mind is troubled like a fountain stirred,<br>And I myself see not the [[Bottom|bottom]] of it. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit with Patroclus |
| [[Thersites|THERSITES]] | Would the fountain of your mind were clear again, that I might [[Water|water]] an ass at it. I had rather be a tick in a sheep than such a valiant ignorance. Exit |
