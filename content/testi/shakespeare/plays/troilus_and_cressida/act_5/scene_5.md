|---|---|
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Diomedes and Servants |
| DIOMEDES | Go, go, my servant, take thou [[Troilus]]’ [[Horse|horse]].<br>Present the fair steed to my Lady Cressid.<br>Fellow, commend my service to her [[Beauty|beauty]].<br>Tell her I have chastised the amorous Trojan,<br>And am her knight by proof. |
| SERVANT | I go, my lord. Exit |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Agamemnon]] |
| [[Agamemnon|AGAMEMNON]] | Renew, renew! The fierce Polydamas<br>Hath beat down Menon; bastard Margareton<br>Hath Doreus prisoner,<br>And stands colossus-wise waving his beam<br>Upon the pashèd corpses of the kings<br>Epistropus and Cedius; [[Polixenes]] is slain,<br>Amphimacus and Thoas deadly hurt,<br>Patroclus ta’en or slain, and Palamedes<br>Sore hurt and bruised; the dreadful sagittary<br>Appals our numbers. Haste we, Diomed,<br>To reinforcement, or we perish all. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Nestor |
| *(didascalia)* | with Patroclus’ [[Body|body]] |
| NESTOR | Go, bear Patroclus’ [[Body|body]] to [[Achilles]],<br>And bid the snail-paced [[Ajax]] arm for shame. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit one or more with the [[Body|body]] |
| NESTOR | There is a thousand Hectors in the field.<br>Now here he fights on Galathe his [[Horse|horse]],<br>And there lacks work; anon he’s there afoot,<br>And there they fly or die, like scalèd schools<br>Before the belching whale. Then is he yonder,<br>And there the strawy Greeks, ripe for his edge,<br>Fall down before him like the mower’s swath.<br>Here, there, and everywhere he [[Leaves|leaves]] and takes,<br>Dexterity so obeying appetite<br>That what he will he does, and does so much<br>That proof is called impossibility. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Ulysses]] |
| [[Ulysses|ULYSSES]] | O [[Courage|courage]], [[Courage|courage]], princes! Great [[Achilles]]<br>Is arming, weeping, cursing, vowing vengeance.<br>Patroclus’ wounds have roused his drowsy [[Blood|blood]],<br>Together with his mangled Myrmidons,<br>That noseless, handless, hacked and chipped come to<br>him<br>Crying on [[Hector]]. [[Ajax]] hath lost a friend<br>And foams at mouth, and he is armed and at it,<br>Roaring for Troitus—who hath done today<br>Mad and fantastic execution,<br>Engaging and redeeming of himself<br>With such a careless force and forceless care<br>As if that luck, in very spite of cunning,<br>Bade him win all. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Ajax]] |
| [[Ajax|AJAX]] | [[Troilus]], thou coward Troitus! Exit |
| *(didascalia)* | DIOMEDES Ay, there, there! Exit |
| NESTOR | So, so, we draw together. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Achilles]] |
| [[Achilles|ACHILLES]] | Where is this [[Hector]]?<br>Come, come, thou brave boy-queller, show thy face.<br>Know what it is to meet [[Achilles]] angry.<br>[[Hector]]! Where’s [[Hector]]? I will none but [[Hector]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt |
