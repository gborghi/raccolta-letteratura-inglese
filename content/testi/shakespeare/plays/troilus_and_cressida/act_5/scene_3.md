| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Hector]] armed, and Andromache |
| ANDROMACHE | When was my lord so much ungently tempered<br>To stop his ears against admonishment?<br>Unarm, unarm, and do not fight today. |
| [[Hector|HECTOR]] | You train me to offend you. Get you in.<br>By all the everlasting gods, I’ll go. |
| ANDROMACHE | My [[Dreams|dreams]] will sure prove ominous to the day. |
| [[Hector|HECTOR]] | No more, I say. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Cassandra |
| CASSANDRA | Where is my brother [[Hector]]? |
| ANDROMACHE | Here, sister, armed and bloody in intent.<br>Consort with me in loud and dear petition,<br>Pursue we him on knees—for I have dreamed<br>Of bloody turbulence, and this whole [[Night|night]]<br>Hath nothing been but shapes and forms of slaughter. |
| CASSANDRA | O ’tis true. |
| [[Hector|HECTOR]] | Ho! Bid my trumpet sound. |
| CASSANDRA | No notes of sally, for the heavens, sweet brother. |
| [[Hector|HECTOR]] | Begone, I say. The gods have heard me swear. |
| CASSANDRA | The gods are deaf to hot and peevish vows.<br>They are polluted off’rings, more abhorred<br>Than spotted livers in the sacrifice. |
| ANDROMACHE | (to [[Hector]])<br>O, be persuaded. Do not count it holy<br>To hurt by being just. It is as lawful,<br>For we would give much, to use violent thefts,<br>And rob in the behalf of [[Charity|charity]]. |
| CASSANDRA | It is the purpose that makes strong the vow,<br>But vows to every purpose must not hold.<br>Unarm, sweet [[Hector]]. |
| [[Hector|HECTOR]] | Hold you still, I say.<br>Mine [[Honour|honour]] keeps the weather of my fate.<br>Life every man holds dear, but the dear man<br>Holds [[Honour|honour]] far more precious-dear than life. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Troilus]], armed |
| [[Hector|HECTOR]] | How now, young man, mean’st thou to fight today? |
| *(didascalia)* | ANDROMACHE aside |
| [[Hector|HECTOR]] | Cassandra, call my father to persuade. Exit Cassandra |
| [[Hector|HECTOR]] | No, [[Faith|faith]], young [[Troilus]]. Doff thy harness, youth.<br>I am today i’th’ vein of chivalry.<br>Let grow thy sinews till their knots be strong,<br>And tempt not yet the brushes of the war.<br>Unarm thee, go—and [[Doubt|doubt]] thou not, brave boy,<br>I’ll stand today for thee and me and Troy. |
| [[Troilus|TROILUS]] | Brother, you have a vice of [[Mercy|mercy]] in you,<br>Which better fits a [[Lion|lion]] than a man. |
| [[Hector|HECTOR]] | What vice is that? Good [[Troilus]], chide me for it. |
| [[Troilus|TROILUS]] | When many times the captive Grecian falls<br>Even in the fan and [[Wind|wind]] of your fair [[Sword|sword]],<br>You bid them rise and live. |
| [[Hector|HECTOR]] | O ’tis fair play. |
| [[Troilus|TROILUS]] | [[Fool]]’s play, by heaven, [[Hector]]. |
| [[Hector|HECTOR]] | How now! How now! |
| [[Troilus|TROILUS]] | For th’ [[Love|love]] of all the gods,<br>Let’s leave the hermit pity with our mother<br>And, when we have our armours buckled on,<br>The venomed vengeance ride upon our swords,<br>Spur them to ruthful work, rein them from ruth. |
| [[Hector|HECTOR]] | Fie, savage, fie! |
| [[Troilus|TROILUS]] | [[Hector]], then ’tis wars. |
| [[Hector|HECTOR]] | [[Troilus]], I would not have you fight today. |
| [[Troilus|TROILUS]] | Who should withhold me?<br>Not fate, obedience, nor the hand of Mars<br>Beck‘ning with fiery truncheon my retire,<br>Not Priamus and Hecuba on knees,<br>Their eyes o’er-gallèd with recourse of [[Tears|tears]],<br>Nor you, my brother, with your true [[Sword|sword]] drawn<br>Opposed to hinder me, should stop my way<br>But by my ruin. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Priam and Cassandra |
| CASSANDRA | Lay hold upon him, Priam, hold him fast.<br>He is thy crutch: now if thou loose thy stay,<br>Thou on him leaning and all Troy on thee,<br>Fall all together. |
| PRIAM | Come, [[Hector]], come. Go back.<br>Thy wife hath dreamt, thy mother hath had visions,<br>Cassandra doth foresee, and I myself<br>Am like a [[Prophet|prophet]] suddenly enrapt<br>To tell thee that this day is ominous.<br>Therefore come back. |
| [[Hector|HECTOR]] | Aeneas is afield,<br>And I do stand engaged to many Greeks,<br>Even in the [[Faith|faith]] of valour, to appear<br>This morning to them. |
| PRIAM | Ay, but thou shalt not go. |
| *(didascalia)* | [[Hector|HECTOR]] kneeling I must not break my [[Faith|faith]]. |
| PRIAM | You know me dutiful; therefore, dear sire,<br>Let me not shame respect, but give me leave<br>To take that course, by your consent and voice,<br>Which you do here forbid me, royal Priam. |
| CASSANDRA | O Priam, yield not to him. |
| ANDROMACHE | Do not, dear father. |
| [[Hector|HECTOR]] | Andromache, I am offended with you.<br>Upon the [[Love|love]] you bear me, get you in. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit Andromache |
| [[Troilus|TROILUS]] | This foolish, dreaming, superstitious girl<br>Makes all these bodements. |
| CASSANDRA | O farewell, dear [[Hector]].<br>Look how thou diest; look how thy eye turns pale;<br>Look how thy wounds do bleed at many vents.<br>Hark how Troy roars, how Hecuba cries out,<br>How poor Andromache shrills her dolours forth.<br>Behold: distraction, frenzy, and amazement<br>Like witless antics one another meet,<br>And all cry ‘[[Hector]], [[Hector]]’s dead, O [[Hector]]!’ |
| [[Troilus|TROILUS]] | Away, away! |
| CASSANDRA | Farewell. Yet soft: [[Hector]], I take my leave.<br>Thou dost thyself and all our Troy deceive. Exit |
| [[Hector|HECTOR]] | (to Priam)<br>You are amazed, my liege, at her exclaim.<br>Go in and cheer the town. We’ll forth and fight,<br>Do deeds of praise, and tell you them at [[Night|night]]. |
| PRIAM | Farewell. The gods with safety stand about thee. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt Priam and [[Hector]] severally. Alarum |
| [[Troilus|TROILUS]] | They are at it, hark! Proud Diomed, believe<br>I come to lose my arm or win my sleeve. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Pandarus]] |
| [[Pandarus|PANDARUS]] | Do you hear, my lord, do you hear? |
| [[Troilus|TROILUS]] | What now? |
| [[Pandarus|PANDARUS]] | Here’s a letter come from yon poor girl. |
| [[Troilus|TROILUS]] | Let me read.<br>[[Troilus]] reads the letter |
| [[Pandarus|PANDARUS]] | A whoreson phthisic, a whoreson rascally phthisic so troubles me, and the foolish fortune of this girl, and what one thing, what another, that I shall leave you one o’ these days. And I have a rheum in mine eyes too, and such an ache in my bones that unless a man were cursed I cannot tell what to think on’t.—What says she there? no |
| [[Troilus|TROILUS]] | (tearing the letter)<br>Words, words, mere words, no matter from the heart.<br>Th’effect doth operate another way.<br>Go, [[Wind|wind]], to [[Wind|wind]]: there turn and change together.<br>My [[Love|love]] with words and errors still she feeds,<br>But edifies another with her deeds. |
| [[Pandarus|PANDARUS]] | Why, but hear you— |
| [[Troilus|TROILUS]] | Hence, broker-lackey! Ignomy and shame<br>Pursue thy life, and live aye with thy name. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt severally |
