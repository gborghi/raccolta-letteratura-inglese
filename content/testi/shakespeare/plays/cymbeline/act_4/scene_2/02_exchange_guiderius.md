| [[Guiderius|GUIDERIUS]] | I do note That grief and patience, rooted in him both, Mingle their spurs together. |
| [[Arviragus|ARVIRAGUS]] | Grow patience, And let the stinking elder, grief, untwine His perishing root with the increasing vine. |
| RELARIUS | It is great morning. Come away. Who’s there? Enter [[Cloten]] in [[Posthumus]]’ suit |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | I cannot find those runagates. That [[Villain|villain]] Hath mocked me. I am faint. |
| [[Belarius|BELARIUS]] | (aside to [[Arviragus]] and [[Guiderius]]) ‘Those runagates’? Means he not us? I partly know him; ‘tis [[Cloten]], the son o’th’ [[Queen]]. I [[Fear|fear]] some ambush. I saw him not these many years, and yet I know ’tis he. We are held as outlaws. Hence! |
| [[Guiderius|GUIDERIUS]] | (aside to [[Arviragus]] and [[Belarius]]) He is but one. You and my brother search What companies are near. Pray you, away. Let me [[Alone|alone]] with him. Exeunt [[Arviragus]] and [[Belarius]] |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | Soft, what are you That fly me thus? Some [[Villain|villain]] mountaineers? I have heard of such. What slave art thou? |
| [[Guiderius|GUIDERIUS]] | A thing More slavish did I ne’er than answering A slave without a knock. |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | Thou art a robber, A law-breaker, a [[Villain|villain]]. Yield thee, thief. |
| [[Guiderius|GUIDERIUS]] | To who? To thee? What art thou? Have not I An arm as big as thine, a heart as big? Thy words, I grant, are bigger, for I wear not My dagger in my mouth. Say what thou art, Why I should yield to thee. |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | Thou [[Villain|villain]] base, Know’st me not by my clothes? |
| [[Guiderius|GUIDERIUS]] | No, nor thy tailor, rascal, Who is thy grandfather. He made those clothes, Which, as it seems, make thee. |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | Thou precious varlet, My tailor made them not. |
| [[Guiderius|GUIDERIUS]] | Hence, then, and thank The man that gave them thee. Thou art some [[Fool|fool]]. I am loath to beat thee. |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | Thou injurious thief, Hear but my name and tremble. GUlDERIUS What’s thy name? |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | [[Cloten]], thou [[Villain|villain]]. |
| [[Guiderius|GUIDERIUS]] | [[Cloten]], thou [[Double|double]] [[Villain|villain]], be thy name, I cannot tremble at it. Were it toad or adder, spider, ’Twould move me sooner. |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | To thy further [[Fear|fear]], Nay, to thy mere confusion, thou shalt know I am son to th’ [[Queen]]. |
| [[Guiderius|GUIDERIUS]] | I am sorry for’t, not seeming So worthy as thy birth. |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | Art not afeard? |
| [[Guiderius|GUIDERIUS]] | Those that I reverence, those I [[Fear|fear]], the wise. At fools I laugh, not [[Fear|fear]] them. |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | Die the [[Death|death]]. When I have slain thee with my proper hand I’ll follow those that even now fled hence, And on the gates of Lud’s town set your heads. Yield, rustic mountaineer. Fight and exeunt Enter [[Belarius]] and [[Arviragus]] |
| [[Belarius|BELARIUS]] | No company’s abroad? |
| [[Arviragus|ARVIRAGUS]] | None in the world. You did mistake him, sure. |
| [[Belarius|BELARIUS]] | I cannot tell. Long is it since I saw him, But [[Time|time]] hath nothing blurred those lines of favour Which then he wore. The snatches in his voice And burst of speaking were as his. I am absolute ’Twas very [[Cloten]]. |
| [[Arviragus|ARVIRAGUS]] | In this place we left them. I wish my brother make good [[Time|time]] with him, You say he is so fell. |
| [[Belarius|BELARIUS]] | Being scarce made up, I mean to man, he had not apprehension Of roaring terrors; for defect of judgement Is oft the cause of [[Fear|fear]]. Enter [[Guiderius]] with [[Cloten]]’s head But see, thy brother. |
