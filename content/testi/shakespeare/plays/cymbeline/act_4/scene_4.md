|---|---|
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Belarius]], [[Guiderius]], and [[Arviragus]] |
| [[Guiderius|GUIDERIUS]] | The noise is round about us. |
| [[Belarius|BELARIUS]] | Let us from it. |
| [[Arviragus|ARVIRAGUS]] | What pleasure, sir, find we in life to lock it<br>From action and adventure? |
| [[Guiderius|GUIDERIUS]] | Nay, what [[Hope|hope]]<br>Have we in hiding us? This way the Romans<br>Must or for Britains slay us, or receive us<br>For barbarous and unnatural revolts<br>During their use, and slay us after. |
| [[Belarius|BELARIUS]] | Sons,<br>We’ll higher to the [[Mountains|mountains]]; there secure us.<br>To the [[King]]’s party there’s no going. Newness<br>Of [[Cloten]]’s [[Death|death]]-we being not known, not mustered<br>Among the bands—may drive us to a render<br>Where we have lived, and so extort from ’s that<br>Which we have done, whose answer would be [[Death|death]]<br>Drawn on with torture. |
| [[Guiderius|GUIDERIUS]] | This is, sir, a [[Doubt|doubt]]<br>In such a [[Time|time]] nothing becoming you<br>Nor satisfying us. |
| [[Arviragus|ARVIRAGUS]] | It is not likely<br>That when they hear the Roman horses neigh,<br>Behold their quartered files, have both their eyes<br>And ears so cloyed importantly as now,<br>That they will waste their [[Time|time]] upon our note,<br>To know from whence we are. |
| [[Belarius|BELARIUS]] | O, I am known<br>Of many in the army. Many years,<br>Though [[Cloten]] then but young, you see, not wore him<br>From my remembrance. And besides, the [[King]]<br>Hath not deserved my service nor your loves,<br>Who find in my [[Exile|exile]] the want of breeding,<br>The certainty of this hard life; aye hopeless<br>To have the courtesy your cradle promised,<br>But to be still hot [[Summer|summer]]’s tanlings, and<br>The shrinking slaves of [[Winter|winter]]. |
| [[Guiderius|GUIDERIUS]] | Than be so,<br>Better to cease to be. Pray, sir, to th‘army.<br>I and my brother are not known; yourself<br>So out of thought, and thereto so o’ergrown,<br>Cannot be questioned. |
| [[Arviragus|ARVIRAGUS]] | By this sun that shines,<br>I’ll thither. What thing is’t that I never<br>Did see man die, scarce ever looked on [[Blood|blood]]<br>But that of coward hares, hot goats, and venison,<br>Never bestrid a [[Horse|horse]] save one that had<br>A rider like myself, who ne’er wore rowel<br>Nor iron on his heel! I am ashamed<br>To look upon the holy sun, to have<br>The benefit of his blest beams, remaining<br>So long a poor unknown. |
| [[Guiderius|GUIDERIUS]] | By heavens, I’ll go.<br>If you will bless me, sir, and give me leave,<br>I’ll take the better care; but if you will not,<br>The hazard therefore due fall on me by<br>The hands of Romans. |
| [[Arviragus|ARVIRAGUS]] | So say I, amen. |
| [[Belarius|BELARIUS]] | No reason I, since of your lives you set<br>So slight a valuation, should reserve<br>My cracked one to more care. Have with you, boys!<br>If in your country wars you chance to die,<br>That is my bed, too, lads, and there I’ll lie.<br>Lead, lead. (Aside) The [[Time|time]] seems long. Their [[Blood|blood]]<br>thinks scorn<br>Till it fly out and show them princes born. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt |
