| *(didascalia)* | Enter Pisanio, and [[Innogen]] in a riding-suit |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | Thou told‘st me when we came from [[Horse|horse]] the place<br>Was near at hand. Ne’er longed my mother so<br>To see me first as I have now. Pisanio, man,<br>Where is [[Posthumus]]? What is in thy mind<br>That makes thee stare thus? Wherefore breaks that<br>sigh<br>From th’inward of thee? One but painted thus<br>Would be interpreted a thing perplexed<br>Beyond [[Self|self]]-explication. Put thyself<br>Into a haviour of less [[Fear|fear]], ere wildness<br>Vanquish my staider senses. What’s the matter?<br>Pisanio gives her a letter<br>Why tender‘st thou that paper to me with<br>A look untender? If’t be [[Summer|summer]] news,<br>Smile to’t before; if winterly, thou need’st<br>But keep that count’nance still. My husband’s hand?<br>That drug-damned [[Italy]] hath out-craftied him,<br>And he’s at some hard point. Speak, man. Thy tongue<br>May take off some extremity which to read<br>Would be even mortal to me. |
| PISANIO | Please you read,<br>And you shall find me, wretched man, a thing<br>The most disdained of fortune. |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | (reads) ‘Thy mistress, Pisanio, hath played the strumpet in my bed, the testimonies whereof lies bleeding in me. I speak not out of weak surmises but from proof as strong as my grief and as certain as I expect my [[Revenge|revenge]]. That part thou, Pisanio, must act for me, if thy [[Faith|faith]] be not tainted with the breach of hers. Let thine own hands take away her life. I shall give thee opportunity at Milford Haven. She hath my letter for the purpose, where if thou [[Fear|fear]] to strike and to make me certain it is done, thou art the pander to her dishonour and equally to me disloyal.’ |
| PISANIO | (aside)<br>What shall I need to draw my [[Sword|sword]]? The paper<br>Hath cut her throat already. No, ’tis slander,<br>Whose edge is sharper than the [[Sword|sword]], whose tongue<br>Outvenoms all the worms of Nile, whose breath<br>Rides on the posting winds and doth belie<br>All corners of the world. Kings, queens, and states,<br>Maids, matrons, nay, the [[Secrets|secrets]] of the [[Grave|grave]]<br>This viperous slander enters. (To [[Innogen]]) What cheer,<br>madam? |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | False to his bed? What is it to be false?<br>To lie in watch there and to think on him?<br>To weep ’twixt [[Clock|clock]] and [[Clock|clock]]? If [[Sleep|sleep]] charge [[Nature|nature]],<br>To break it with a fearful dream of him<br>And cry myself awake? That’s false to ’s bed, is it? |
| PISANIO | Alas, good lady. |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | I false? Thy [[Conscience|conscience]] witness, Giacomo,<br>Thou didst accuse him of incontinency.<br>Thou then lookedst like a [[Villain|villain]]; now, methinks,<br>Thy favour’s good enough. Some jay of [[Italy]],<br>Whose mother was her painting, hath betrayed him.<br>Poor I am stale, a garment out of fashion,<br>And for I am richer than to hang by th’ walls<br>I must be ripped. To pieces with me! O,<br>Men’s vows are women’s traitors. All good seeming,<br>By thy revolt, O husband, shall be thought<br>Put on for villainy; not born where’t grows,<br>But worn a bait for ladies. |
| PISANIO | Good madam, hear me. |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | True honest men being heard like false Aeneas<br>Were in his [[Time|time]] thought false, and Sinon’s weeping<br>Did scandal many a holy tear, took pity<br>From most true wretchedness. So thou, [[Posthumus]],<br>Wilt lay the leaven on all proper men.<br>Goodly and gallant shall be false and perjured<br>From thy great fail. (To Pisanio) Come, fellow, be thou<br>honest,<br>Do thou thy master’s bidding. When thou seest<br>him,<br>A little witness my obedience. Look,<br>I draw the [[Sword|sword]] myself. Take it, and hit<br>The [[Innocent|innocent]] mansion of my [[Love|love]], my heart.<br>[[Fear]] not, ‘tis empty of all things but grief.<br>Thy master is not there, who was indeed<br>The riches of it. Do his bidding; strike.<br>Thou mayst be valiant in a better cause,<br>But now thou seem’st a coward. |
| PISANIO | Hence, vile instrument,<br>Thou shalt not damn my hand! |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | Why, I must die,<br>And if I do not by thy hand thou art<br>No servant of thy master’s. Against [[Self|self]]-slaughter<br>There is a prohibition so divine<br>That cravens my weak hand. Come, here’s my heart.<br>Something’s afore’t. Soft, soft, we’ll no defence;<br>Obedient as the scabbard. What is here?<br>She takes letters from her bosom<br>The scriptures of the loyal Leonatus,<br>All turned to heresy? Away, away,<br>Corrupters of my [[Faith|faith]], you shall no more<br>Be stomachers to my heart. Thus may poor fools<br>Believe false teachers. Though those that are betrayed<br>Do feel the treason sharply, yet the traitor<br>Stands in worse case of woe. And thou, [[Posthumus]],<br>That didst set up my disobedience ’gainst the [[King]]<br>My father, and make me put into contempt the suits<br>Of princely fellows, shalt hereafter find<br>It is no act of common passage but<br>A strain of rareness; and I grieve myself<br>To think, when thou shalt be disedged by her<br>That now thou tirest on, how thy [[Memory|memory]]<br>Will then be panged by me. (To Pisanio) Prithee,<br>dispatch.<br>The [[Lamb|lamb]] entreats the butcher. Where’s thy knife?<br>Thou art too slow to do thy master’s bidding<br>When I [[Desire|desire]] it too. |
| PISANIO | O gracious lady,<br>Since I received command to do this business<br>I have not slept one wink. |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | Do’t, and to bed, then. |
| PISANIO | I’ll wake mine eyeballs out first. |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | Wherefore then<br>Didst undertake it? Why hast thou abused<br>So many miles with a pretence?—this place,<br>Mine action, and thine own? Our horses’ labour,<br>The [[Time|time]] inviting thee? The perturbed [[Court|court]],<br>For my being absent, whereunto I never<br>Purpose return? Why hast thou gone so far<br>To be unbent when thou hast ta‘en thy stand,<br>Th’elected deer before thee? |
| PISANIO | But to win [[Time|time]]<br>To lose so bad employment, in the which<br>I have considered of a course. Good lady,<br>Hear me with patience. |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | Talk thy tongue weary. Speak.<br>I have heard I am a strumpet, and mine ear,<br>Therein false struck, can take no greater wound,<br>Nor tent to [[Bottom|bottom]] that. But speak. |
| PISANIO | Then, madam,<br>I thought you would not back again. |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | Most like,<br>Bringing me here to kill me. |
| PISANIO | Not so, neither.<br>But if I were as wise as honest, then<br>My purpose would prove [[Well|well]]. It cannot be<br>But that my master is abused. Some [[Villain|villain]],<br>Ay, and singular in his art, hath done you both<br>This cursed injury. |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | Some Roman courtesan. |
| PISANIO | No, on my life.<br>I’ll give but notice you are dead, and send him<br>Some bloody sign of it, for ’tis commanded<br>I should do so. You shall be missed at [[Court|court]],<br>And that will [[Well|well]] confirm it. |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | Why, good fellow,<br>What shall I do the while, where bide, how live,<br>Or in my life what comfort when I am<br>Dead to my husband? |
| PISANIO | If you’ll back to th’ [[Court|court]]— |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | No [[Court|court]], no father, nor no more ado<br>With that harsh, churlish, noble, simple nothing,<br>That [[Cloten]], whose [[Love|love]] suit hath been to me<br>As fearful as a siege. |
| PISANIO | If not at [[Court|court]],<br>Then not in Britain must you bide. |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | Where then?<br>Hath Britain all the sun that shines? Day, [[Night|night]],<br>Are they not but in Britain? I‘th’ world’s volume<br>Our Britain seems as of it but not in’t,<br>In a great pool a swan’s nest. Prithee, think<br>There’s livers out of Britain. |
| PISANIO | I am most glad<br>You think of other place. Th‘ambassador,<br>[[Lucius]] the Roman, comes to Milford Haven<br>Tomorrow. Now if you could wear a mind<br>Dark as your fortune is, and but disguise<br>That which t’appear itself must not yet be<br>But by [[Self|self]]-danger, you should tread a course<br>Pretty and full of view; yea, haply near<br>The residence of [[Posthumus]]; so nigh, at least,<br>That though his actions were not visible, yet<br>Report should render him hourly to your ear<br>As truly as he moves. |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | O, for such means,<br>Though peril to my modesty, not [[Death|death]] on’t,<br>I would adventure. |
| PISANIO | [[Well]] then, here’s the point:<br>You must forget to be a woman; change<br>Command into obedience, [[Fear|fear]] and niceness—<br>The handmaids of all women, or more truly<br>Woman it pretty [[Self|self]]—into a waggish [[Courage|courage]],<br>Ready in gibes, quick-answered, saucy and<br>As quarrelous as the weasel. Nay, you must<br>Forget that rarest treasure of your cheek,<br>Exposing it—but O, the harder heart!—<br>Alack, no remedy—to the greedy touch<br>Of common-kissing Titan, and forget<br>Your laboursome and dainty trims wherein<br>You made great Juno angry. |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | Nay, be brief.<br>I see into thy end, and am almost<br>A man already. |
| PISANIO | First, make yourself but like one.<br>Forethinking this, I have already fit—<br>’Tis in my cloak-bag—doublet, hat, hose, all<br>That answer to them. Would you in their serving,<br><br>And with what [[Imitation|imitation]] you can borrow<br>From youth of such a season, fore noble [[Lucius]]<br>Present yourself, [[Desire|desire]] his service, tell him<br>Wherein you’re happy—which will make him know<br>If that his head have ear in music—doubtless<br>With joy he will embrace you, for he’s honourable,<br>And, doubling that, most holy. Your means abroad—<br>You have me, rich, and I will never fail<br>Beginning nor supplyment. |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | Thou art all the comfort<br>The gods will diet me with. Prithee away.<br>There’s more to be considered, but we’ll even<br>All that good [[Time|time]] will give us. This attempt<br>I am soldier to, and will abide it with<br>A prince’s [[Courage|courage]]. Away, I prithee. |
| PISANIO | [[Well]], madam, we must take a short farewell<br>Lest, being missed, I be suspected of<br>Your carriage from the [[Court|court]]. My noble mistress,<br>Here is a box. I had it from the [[Queen]].<br>What’s in’t is precious. If you are sick at sea<br>Or stomach-qualmed at land, a dram of this<br>Will drive away distemper. To some shade,<br>And fit you to your manhood. May the gods<br>Direct you to the best. |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | Amen. I thank thee. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt severally |
| *(didascalia)* | 3.5 Flourish. Enter [[Cymbeline]], the [[Queen]], [[Cloten]], [[Lucius]], and lords |
| [[Cymbeline|CYMBELINE]] | (to [[Lucius]])<br>Thus far, and so farewell. |
| [[Lucius|LUCIUS]] | Thanks, royal sir.<br>My emperor hath wrote I must from hence;<br>And am right sorry that I must report ye<br>My master’s enemy. |
| [[Cymbeline|CYMBELINE]] | Our subjects, sir,<br>Will not endure his yoke, and for ourself<br>To show less sovereignty than they must needs<br>Appear unkinglike. |
| [[Lucius|LUCIUS]] | So, sir, I [[Desire|desire]] of you<br>A conduct over land to Milford Haven. |
| *(didascalia)* | (To the [[Queen]]) Madam, all joy befall your grace, to [[Cloten]] and you. |
| [[Cymbeline|CYMBELINE]] | My lords, you are appointed for that office.<br>The due of [[Honour|honour]] in no point omit.<br>So farewell, noble [[Lucius]]. |
| [[Lucius|LUCIUS]] | Your hand, my lord. |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | Receive it friendly, but from this [[Time|time]] forth<br>I wear it as your enemy. |
| [[Lucius|LUCIUS]] | Sir, the event<br>Is yet to name the winner. Fare you [[Well|well]]. |
| [[Cymbeline|CYMBELINE]] | Leave not the worthy [[Lucius]], good my lords,<br>Till he have crossed the Severn. Happiness. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt [[Lucius]] and lords |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] | He goes hence frowning, but it honours us<br>That we have given him cause. |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | ’Tis all the better.<br>Your valiant Britons have their wishes in it. |
| [[Cymbeline|CYMBELINE]] | [[Lucius]] hath wrote already to the Emperor<br>How it goes here. It fits us therefore ripely<br>Our chariots and our horsemen be in readiness.<br>The powers that he already hath in Gallia<br>Will soon be drawn to head, from whence he moves<br>His war for Britain. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] | ’Tis not sleepy business,<br>But must be looked to speedily and strongly. |
| [[Cymbeline|CYMBELINE]] | Our expectation that it would be thus<br>Hath made us forward. But, my gentle [[Queen|queen]],<br>Where is our daughter? She hath not appeared<br>Before the Roman, nor to us hath tendered<br>The duty of the day. She looks us like<br>A thing more made of malice than of duty.<br>We have noted it. Call her before us, for<br>We have been too slight in sufferance. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit one or more |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] | Royal Sir,<br>Since the [[Exile|exile]] of [[Posthumus]] most retired<br>Hath her life been, the cure ,whereof, my lord,<br>’Tis [[Time|time]] must do. Beseech your majesty<br>Forbear sharp speeches to her. She’s a lady<br>So tender of rebukes that words are strokes,<br>And strokes [[Death|death]] to her. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter a Messenger |
| [[Cymbeline|CYMBELINE]] | Where is she, sir? How<br>Can her contempt be answered? |
| MESSENGER | Please you, sir,<br>Her chambers are all locked, and there’s no answer<br>That will be given to th’ loud’st of noise we make. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] | My lord, when last I went to visit her<br>She prayed me to excuse her keeping close,<br>Whereto constrained by her infirmity,<br>She should that duty leave unpaid to you<br>Which daily she was bound to proffer. This<br>She wished me to make known, but our great<br>[[Court|court]]<br>Made me to blame in [[Memory|memory]]. |
| [[Cymbeline|CYMBELINE]] | Her doors locked?<br>Not seen of late? Grant heavens that which I<br>[[Fear]] prove false. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] | on, I say, follow the [[King]]. |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | That man of hers, Pisanio, her old servant,<br>I have not seen these two days. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] | Go, look after. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit [[Cloten]] |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] | Pisanio, thou that stand’st so for [[Posthumus]]!<br>He hath a drug of mine. I pray his absence<br>Proceed by swallowing that, for he believes<br>It is a thing most precious. But for her,<br>Where is she gone? Haply [[Despair|despair]] hath seized her,<br>Or, winged with fervour of her [[Love|love]], she’s flown<br>To her desired [[Posthumus]]. Gone she is<br>To [[Death|death]] or to dishonour, and my end<br>Can make good use of either. She being down,<br>I have the placing of the British [[Crown|crown]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Cloten]] |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] | How now, my son? |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | ’Tis certain she is fled.<br>Go in and cheer the [[King]]. He rages, none<br>Dare come about him. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] | All the better. May<br>This [[Night|night]] forestall him of the coming day. Exit |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | I [[Love|love]] and hate her. For she’s fair and royal,<br>And that she hath all courtly parts more exquisite<br>Than lady, ladies, woman—from every one<br>The best she hath, and she, of all compounded,<br>Outsells them all—I [[Love|love]] her therefore; but<br>Disdaining me, and throwing favours on<br>The low [[Posthumus]], slanders so her judgement<br>That what’s else rare is choked; and in that point<br>I will conclude to hate her, nay, indeed,<br>To be revenged upon her. For when fools<br>Shall— |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Pisanio |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | Who is here? What, are you packing, sirrah?<br>Come hither. Ah, you precious pander! [[Villain]],<br>Where is thy lady? In a word, or else<br>Thou art straightway with the fiends. |
| PISANIO | O good my lord! |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | Where is thy lady?—or, by Jupiter,<br>I will not ask again. Close [[Villain|villain]],<br>I’ll have this secret from thy tongue or rip<br>Thy heart to find it. Is she with [[Posthumus]],<br>From whose so many weights of baseness cannot<br>A dram of worth be drawn? |
| PISANIO | Alas, my lord,<br>How can she be with him? When was she missed?<br>He is in Rome. |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | Where is she, sir? Come nearer.<br>No farther halting. Satisfy me [[Home|home]]<br>What is become of her. |
| PISANIO | O my all-worthy lord! |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | All-worthy [[Villain|villain]],<br>Discover where thy mistress is at once,<br>At the next word. No more of ‘worthy lord’.<br>Speak, or thy [[Silence|silence]] on the instant is<br>Thy condemnation and thy [[Death|death]]. |
| PISANIO | Then, sir,<br>This paper is the [[history]] of my knowledge<br>Touching her flight.<br>He gives [[Cloten]] a letter |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | Let’s see’t. I will pursue her<br>Even to Augustus’ throne. |
| *(didascalia)* | PISANIO aside |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | Or this or perish.<br>She’s far enough, and what he learns by this<br>May prove his travel, not her danger. |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | Hum! |
| PISANIO | (aside)<br>I’ll write to my lord she’s dead. O [[Innogen]],<br>Safe mayst thou wander, safe return again! |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | Sirrah, is this letter true? |
| PISANIO | Sir, as I think. |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | It is [[Posthumus]]’ hand; I know’t. Sirrah, if thou wouldst not be a [[Villain|villain]] but do me true service, undergo those employments wherein I should have cause to use thee with a serious industry—that is, what villainy soe’er I bid thee do, to perform it directly and truly—I would think thee an honest man. Thou shouldst neither want my means for thy relief nor my voice for thy preferment. |
| PISANIO | [[Well]], my good lord. |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | Wilt thou serve me? For since patiently and constantly thou hast stuck to the bare fortune of that beggar [[Posthumus]], thou canst not in the course of gratitude but be a diligent follower of mine. Wilt thou serve me? 121 |
| PISANIO | Sir, I will. |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | Give me thy hand. Here’s my purse. Hast any of thy late master’s garments in thy possession? |
| PISANIO | I have, my lord, at my lodging the same suit he wore when he took leave of my lady and mistress. |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | The first service thou dost me, fetch that suit hither. Let it be thy first service. Go. |
| PISANIO | I shall, my lord. Exit |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | Meet thee at Milford Haven! I forgot to ask him one thing; I’ll remember’t anon. Even there, thou [[Villain|villain]] [[Posthumus]], will I kill thee. I would these garments were come. She said upon a [[Time|time]]—the bitterness of it I now belch from my heart—that she held the very garment of [[Posthumus]] in more respect than my noble and natural person, together with the adornment of my qualities. With that suit upon my back will I ravish her—first kill him, and in her eyes; there shall she see my valour, which will then be a torment to her contempt. He on the ground, my speech of insultment ended on his dead [[Body|body]], and when my lust hath dined—which, as I say, to vex her I will execute in the clothes that she so praised—to the [[Court|court]] I’ll knock her back, foot her [[Home|home]] again. She hath despised me rejoicingly, and I’ll be merry in my [[Revenge|revenge]]. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Pisanio with [[Posthumus]]’ suit |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | Be those the garments? |
| PISANIO | Ay, my noble lord. |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | How long is’t since she went to Milford Haven? |
| PISANIO | She can scarce be there yet. |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | Bring this apparel to my chamber. That is the second thing that I have commanded thee. The third is that thou wilt be a voluntary mute to my design. Be but duteous, and true preferment shall tender itself to thee. My [[Revenge|revenge]] is now at Milford. Would I had wings to follow it. Come, and be true. Exit |
| PISANIO | Thou bidd’st me to my loss, for true to thee<br>Were to prove false, which I will never be<br>To him that is most true. To Milford go,<br>And find not her whom thou pursuest. Flow, flow,<br>You heavenly blessings, on her. This [[Fool|fool]]’s [[Speed|speed]]<br>Be crossed with slowness; labour be his meed. Exit |
