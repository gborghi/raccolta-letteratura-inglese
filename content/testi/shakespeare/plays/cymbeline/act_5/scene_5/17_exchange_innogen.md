| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | (kneeling) Your blessing, sir. |
| [[Belarius|BELARIUS]] | (aside to [[Guiderius]] and [[Arviragus]]) Though you did [[Love|love]] this youth, I blame ye not. You had a motive for’t. |
| [[Cymbeline|CYMBELINE]] | My [[Tears|tears]] that fall Prove holy [[Water|water]] on thee! He raises her [[Innogen]], Thy mother’s dead. |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | I am sorry for’t, my lord. |
| [[Cymbeline|CYMBELINE]] | O, she was naught, and ’long of her it was That we meet here so strangely. But her son Is gone, we know not how nor where. |
| PISANIO | My lord, Now [[Fear|fear]] is from me I’ll speak [[Truth|truth]]. Lord [[Cloten]], Upon my lady’s missing, came to me With his [[Sword|sword]] drawn, foamed at the mouth, and swore If I discovered not which way she was gone It was my instant [[Death|death]]. By accident I had a feigned letter of my master’s Then in my pocket, which directed him To seek her on the [[Mountains|mountains]] near to Milford, Where in a frenzy, in my master’s garments, Which he enforced from me, away he posts With unchaste purpose, and with oath to violate My lady’s [[Honour|honour]]. What became of him I further know not. |
| [[Guiderius|GUIDERIUS]] | Let me end the story. I slew him there. |
| [[Cymbeline|CYMBELINE]] | Marry, the gods forfend! I would not thy good deeds should from my lips Pluck a hard sentence. Prithee, valiant youth, Deny’t again. |
| [[Guiderius|GUIDERIUS]] | I have spoke it, and I did it. |
| [[Cymbeline|CYMBELINE]] | He was a prince. |
| [[Guiderius|GUIDERIUS]] | A most incivil one. The wrongs he did me Were nothing prince-like, for he did provoke me With language that would make me spurn the sea If it could so roar to me. I cut off ’s head, And am right glad he is not standing here To tell this tale of mine. |
| [[Cymbeline|CYMBELINE]] | I am sorrow for thee. By thine own tongue thou art condemned, and must Endure our law. Thou’rt dead. |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | That headless man I thought had been my lord. |
| [[Cymbeline|CYMBELINE]] | (to soldiers) Bind the offender, And take him from our presence. |
| [[Belarius|BELARIUS]] | Stay, sir [[King]]. This boy is better than the man he slew, As [[Well|well]] descended as thyself, and hath More of thee merited than a band of Clotens Had ever scar for. Let his arms [[Alone|alone]]; They were not born for bondage. |
| [[Cymbeline|CYMBELINE]] | Why, old soldier, Wilt thou undo the worth thou art unpaid for By tasting of our wrath? How of descent As good as we? |
| [[Arviragus|ARVIRAGUS]] | In that he spake too far. |
| [[Cymbeline|CYMBELINE]] | to [[Belarius]] And thou shalt die for’t. |
| [[Belarius|BELARIUS]] | We will die all three But I will prove that two on ’s are as good As I have given out him. My sons, I must For mine own part unfold a dangerous speech, Though haply [[Well|well]] for you. |
| [[Arviragus|ARVIRAGUS]] | Your danger’s ours. |
| [[Guiderius|GUIDERIUS]] | And our good his. |
| [[Belarius|BELARIUS]] | Have at it then. By leave, Thou hadst, great [[King]], a subject who Was called [[Belarius]]. |
| [[Cymbeline|CYMBELINE]] | What of him? He is A banished traitor. |
| [[Belarius|BELARIUS]] | He it is that hath Assumed this age. Indeed, a banished man; I know not how a traitor. |
| [[Cymbeline|CYMBELINE]] | (to soldiers) Take him hence. The whole world shall not save him. |
| [[Belarius|BELARIUS]] | Not too hot. First pay me for the nursing of thy sons, And let it be confiscate all so soon As I have received it. |
