| LORD | This was strange chance: A narrow lane, an old man, and two boys. |
| [[Posthumus|POSTHUMUS]] | Nay, do not [[Wonder|wonder]] at it. Yet you are made Rather to [[Wonder|wonder]] at the things you hear Than to work any. Will you rhyme upon‘t, And vent it for a mock’ry? Here is one: ‘Two boys, an old man twice a boy, a lane, Preserved the Britons, was the Romans’ bane.’ |
| LORD | Nay, be not angry, sir. |
| [[Posthumus|POSTHUMUS]] | ’Lack, to what end? Who dares not stand his foe, I’ll be his friend, For if he’ll do as he is made to do, I know he’ll quickly fly my [[Friendship|friendship]] too. You have put me into rhyme. |
| LORD | Farewell; you’re angry. Exit |
