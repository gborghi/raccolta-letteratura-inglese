| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | The south-fog rot him! |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | He never can meet more mischance than come To be but named of thee. His meanest garment That ever hath but clipped his [[Body|body]] is dearer In my respect than all the hairs above thee, Were they all made such men. How now, Pisanio! Enter Pisanio |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | His garment? Now the devil— |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | (to Pisanio) To Dorothy, my woman, hie thee presently. |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | His garment? |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | (to Pisanio) I am sprited with a [[Fool|fool]], Frighted, and angered worse. Go bid my woman Search for a [[Jewel|jewel]] that too casually Hath left mine arm. It was thy master’s. ‘Shrew me If I would lose it for a revenue Of any [[King|king]]’s in Europe! I do think I saw’t this morning; confident I am Last [[Night|night]] ’twas on mine arm; I kissed it. I [[Hope|hope]] it be not gone to tell my lord That I kiss aught but he. |
| PISANIO | ’Twill not be lost. |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | I [[Hope|hope]] so. Go and search. Exit Pisanio |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | You have abused me. ‘His meanest garment’? |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | Ay, I said so, sir. If you will make’t an action, call witness to’t. |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | I will inform your father. |
| [[Innogen|INNOGEN]] | Your mother too. She’s my good lady, and will conceive, I [[Hope|hope]], But the worst of me. So I leave you, sir, To th’ worst of discontent. Exit |
| [[Cloten|CLOTEN]] | I’ll be revenged. ‘His meanest garment’? [[Well]]! Exit |
