| GIACOMO | Sir, my circumstances, Being so near the [[Truth|truth]] as I will make them, Must first induce you to believe; whose strength I will confirm with oath, which I [[Doubt|doubt]] not You’ll give me leave to spare when you shall find You need it not. |
| [[Posthumus|POSTHUMUS]] | Proceed. |
| GIACOMO | First, her bedchamber— Where I confess I slept not, but profess Had that was [[Well|well]] worth watching—it was hanged With tapestry of silk and silver; the story Proud [[Cleopatra]] when she met her Roman, And Cydnus swelled above the banks, or for The press of boats or [[Pride|pride]]: a piece of work So bravely done, so rich, that it did strive In workmanship and value; which I wondered Could be so rarely and exactly wrought, Such the true life on’t was. |
| [[Posthumus|POSTHUMUS]] | This is true, And this you might have heard of here, by me Or by some other. |
| GIACOMO | More particulars Must justify my knowledge. |
| [[Posthumus|POSTHUMUS]] | So they must, Or do your [[Honour|honour]] injury. |
| GIACOMO | The chimney Is south the chamber, and the chimney-piece Chaste Dian bathing. Never saw I figures So likely to report themselves; the cutter Was as another [[Nature|nature]]; dumb, outwent her, Motion and breath left out. |
| [[Posthumus|POSTHUMUS]] | This is a thing Which you might from relation likewise reap, Being, as it is, much spoke of. |
| GIACOMO | The roof o’th’ chamber With golden cherubins is fretted. Her andirons— I had forgot them—were two winking Cupids Of silver, each on one foot standing, nicely Depending on their brands. |
| [[Posthumus|POSTHUMUS]] | This is her [[Honour|honour]]! Let it be granted you have seen all this—and praise Be given to your remembrance—the description Of what is in her chamber nothing saves The wager you have laid. |
| GIACOMO | Then, if you can Be pale, I beg but leave to air this [[Jewel|jewel]]. See! He shows the bracelet And now ’tis up again; it must be married To that your diamond. I’ll keep them. |
| [[Posthumus|POSTHUMUS]] | Jove! Once more let me behold it. Is it that Which I left with her? |
| GIACOMO | Sir, I thank her, that. She stripped it from her arm. I see her yet. Her pretty action did outsell her gift, And yet enriched it too. She gave it me, And said she prized it once. |
| [[Posthumus|POSTHUMUS]] | Maybe she plucked it off To send it me. |
| GIACOMO | She writes so to you, doth she? |
| [[Posthumus|POSTHUMUS]] | O, no, no, no—’tis true! Here, take this too. He gives Giacomo his ring It is a basilisk unto mine eye, Kills me to look on’t. Let there be no [[Honour|honour]] Where there is [[Beauty|beauty]], [[Truth|truth]] where semblance, [[Love|love]] Where there’s another man. The vows of women Of no more bondage be to where they are made Than they are to their virtues, which is nothing! O, above measure false! |
| PILASIO | Have patience, sir, And take your ring again; ’tis not yet won. It may be probable she lost it, or Who knows if one her woman, being corrupted, Hath stol’n it from her? |
| [[Posthumus|POSTHUMUS]] | Very true, And so I [[Hope|hope]] he came by’t. Back my ring. He takes his ring again Render to me some corporal sign about her More evident than this; for this was stol’n. |
