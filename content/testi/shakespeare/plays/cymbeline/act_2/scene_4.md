| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Posthumus]] and Filario |
| [[Posthumus|POSTHUMUS]] | [[Fear]] it not, sir. I would I were so sure<br>To win the [[King]] as I am bold her [[Honour|honour]]<br>Will remain hers. |
| FILARIO | What means do you make to him? |
| [[Posthumus|POSTHUMUS]] | Not any; but abide the change of [[Time|time]],<br>Quake in the present [[Winter|winter]]’s state, and wish<br>That warmer days would come. In these seared hopes<br>I barely gratify your [[Love|love]]; they failing,<br>I must die much your debtor. |
| FILARIO | Your very goodness and your company<br>O‘erpays all I can do. By this, your [[King|king]]<br>Hath heard of great Augustus. Caius [[Lucius]]<br>Will do ’s commission throughly. And I think<br>He’ll grant the tribute, send th’arrearages,<br>Ere look upon our Romans, whose remembrance<br>Is yet fresh in their grief. |
| [[Posthumus|POSTHUMUS]] | I do believe,<br>Statist though I am none, nor like to be,<br>That this will prove a war, and you shall hear<br>The legions now in Gallia sooner landed<br>In our not-fearing Britain than have tidings<br>Of any penny tribute paid. Our countrymen<br>Are men more ordered than when [[Julius Caesar]]<br>Smiled at their lack of skill but found their [[Courage|courage]]<br>Worthy his frowning at. Their discipline,<br>Now wing-led with their [[Courage|courage]], will make known<br>To their approvers they are people such<br>That mend upon the world. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Giacomo |
| FILARIO | See, Giacomo. |
| [[Posthumus|POSTHUMUS]] | (to Giacomo)<br>The swiftest harts have posted you by land,<br>And winds of all the corners kissed your sails<br>To make your vessel nimble. |
| FILARIO | (to Giacomo)<br>Welcome, sir. |
| [[Posthumus|POSTHUMUS]] | (to Giacomo)<br>I [[Hope|hope]] the briefness of your answer made<br>The speediness of your return. |
| GIACOMO | Your lady is<br>One of the fair’st that I have looked upon— |
| [[Posthumus|POSTHUMUS]] | And therewithal the best, or let her [[Beauty|beauty]]<br>Look through a casement to allure false hearts,<br>And be false with them. |
| GIACOMO | Here are letters for you. |
| [[Posthumus|POSTHUMUS]] | Their tenor good, I trust. |
| GIACOMO | ’Tis very like.<br>[[Posthumus]] reads the letters |
| *(didascalia)* | FILARIO |
| GIACOMO | Was Caius [[Lucius]] in the Briton [[Court|court]]<br>When you were there? |
| GIACOMO | He was expected then,<br>But not approached. |
| [[Posthumus|POSTHUMUS]] | All is [[Well|well]] yet.<br>Sparkles this stone as it was wont, or is’t not<br>Too dull for your good wearing? |
| GIACOMO | If I had lost it<br>I should have lost the worth of it in gold.<br>I’ll make a [[Journey|journey]] twice as far t’enjoy<br>A second [[Night|night]] of such sweet shortness which<br>Was mine in Britain; for the ring is won. |
| POSTHUMOUS | The stone’s too hard to come by. |
| GIACOMO | Not a whit,<br>Your lady being so easy. |
| [[Posthumus|POSTHUMUS]] | Make not, sir,<br>Your loss your sport. I [[Hope|hope]] you know that we<br>Must not continue friends. |
| GIACOMO | Good sir, we must,<br>If you keep covenant. Had I not brought<br>The knowledge of your mistress [[Home|home]] I grant<br>We were to question farther, but I now<br>Profess myself the winner of her [[Honour|honour]],<br>Together with your ring, and not the wronger<br>Of her or you, having proceeded but<br>By both your wills. |
| [[Posthumus|POSTHUMUS]] | If you can make’t apparent<br>That you have tasted her in bed, my hand<br>And ring is yours. If not, the foul opinion<br>You had of her pure [[Honour|honour]] gains or loses<br>Your [[Sword|sword]] or mine, or masterless [[Leaves|leaves]] both<br>To who shall find them. |
| GIACOMO | Sir, my circumstances,<br>Being so near the [[Truth|truth]] as I will make them,<br>Must first induce you to believe; whose strength<br>I will confirm with oath, which I [[Doubt|doubt]] not<br>You’ll give me leave to spare when you shall find<br>You need it not. |
| [[Posthumus|POSTHUMUS]] | Proceed. |
| GIACOMO | First, her bedchamber—<br>Where I confess I slept not, but profess<br>Had that was [[Well|well]] worth watching—it was hanged<br>With tapestry of silk and silver; the story<br>Proud [[Cleopatra]] when she met her Roman,<br>And Cydnus swelled above the banks, or for<br>The press of boats or [[Pride|pride]]: a piece of work<br>So bravely done, so rich, that it did strive<br>In workmanship and value; which I wondered<br>Could be so rarely and exactly wrought,<br>Such the true life on’t was. |
| [[Posthumus|POSTHUMUS]] | This is true,<br>And this you might have heard of here, by me<br>Or by some other. |
| GIACOMO | More particulars<br>Must justify my knowledge. |
| [[Posthumus|POSTHUMUS]] | So they must,<br>Or do your [[Honour|honour]] injury. |
| GIACOMO | The chimney<br>Is south the chamber, and the chimney-piece<br>Chaste Dian bathing. Never saw I figures<br>So likely to report themselves; the cutter<br>Was as another [[Nature|nature]]; dumb, outwent her,<br>Motion and breath left out. |
| [[Posthumus|POSTHUMUS]] | This is a thing<br>Which you might from relation likewise reap,<br>Being, as it is, much spoke of. |
| GIACOMO | The roof o’th’ chamber<br>With golden cherubins is fretted. Her andirons—<br>I had forgot them—were two winking Cupids<br>Of silver, each on one foot standing, nicely<br>Depending on their brands. |
| [[Posthumus|POSTHUMUS]] | This is her [[Honour|honour]]!<br>Let it be granted you have seen all this—and praise<br>Be given to your remembrance—the description<br>Of what is in her chamber nothing saves<br>The wager you have laid. |
| GIACOMO | Then, if you can<br>Be pale, I beg but leave to air this [[Jewel|jewel]]. See!<br>He shows the bracelet<br>And now ’tis up again; it must be married<br>To that your diamond. I’ll keep them. |
| [[Posthumus|POSTHUMUS]] | Jove!<br>Once more let me behold it. Is it that<br>Which I left with her? |
| GIACOMO | Sir, I thank her, that.<br>She stripped it from her arm. I see her yet.<br>Her pretty action did outsell her gift,<br>And yet enriched it too. She gave it me,<br>And said she prized it once. |
| [[Posthumus|POSTHUMUS]] | Maybe she plucked it off<br>To send it me. |
| GIACOMO | She writes so to you, doth she? |
| [[Posthumus|POSTHUMUS]] | O, no, no, no—’tis true! Here, take this too.<br>He gives Giacomo his ring<br>It is a basilisk unto mine eye,<br>Kills me to look on’t. Let there be no [[Honour|honour]]<br>Where there is [[Beauty|beauty]], [[Truth|truth]] where semblance, [[Love|love]]<br>Where there’s another man. The vows of women<br>Of no more bondage be to where they are made<br>Than they are to their virtues, which is nothing!<br>O, above measure false! |
| PILASIO | Have patience, sir,<br>And take your ring again; ’tis not yet won.<br>It may be probable she lost it, or<br>Who knows if one her woman, being corrupted,<br>Hath stol’n it from her? |
| [[Posthumus|POSTHUMUS]] | Very true,<br>And so I [[Hope|hope]] he came by’t. Back my ring.<br>He takes his ring again<br>Render to me some corporal sign about her<br>More evident than this; for this was stol’n. |
| GIACOMO | By Jupiter, I had it from her arm. |
| [[Posthumus|POSTHUMUS]] | Hark you, he swears, by Jupiter he swears.<br>‘Tis true, nay, keep the ring, ’tis true. I am sure<br>She would not lose it. Her attendants are<br>All sworn and honourable. They induced to steal it?<br>And by a stranger? No, he hath enjoyed her.<br>The cognizance of her incontinency<br>Is this. She hath bought the name of whore thus<br>dearly.<br>He gives Giacomo his ring<br>There, take thy hire, and all the fiends of hell<br>Divide themselves between you! |
| FILARIO | Sir, be patient.<br>This is not strong enough to be believed<br>Of one persuaded [[Well|well]] of. |
| [[Posthumus|POSTHUMUS]] | Never talk on’t.<br>She hath been colted by him. |
| GIACOMO | If you seek<br>For further satisfying, under her breast—<br>Worthy the pressing—lies a mole, right proud<br>Of that most delicate lodging. By my life,<br>I kissed it, and it gave me present hunger<br>To feed again, though full. You do remember<br>This stain upon her? |
| [[Posthumus|POSTHUMUS]] | Ay, and it doth confirm<br>Another stain as big as hell can hold,<br>Were there no more but it. |
| GIACOMO | Will you hear more? |
| [[Posthumus|POSTHUMUS]] | Spare your arithmetic, never count the turns.<br>Once, and a million! |
| GIACOMO | I’ll be sworn. |
| [[Posthumus|POSTHUMUS]] | No swearing.<br>If you will swear you have not done‘t, you lie,<br>And I will kill thee if thou dost deny<br>Thou’st made me cuckold.<br>GlACOMO<br>I’ll deny nothing. |
| [[Posthumus|POSTHUMUS]] | O that I had her here to tear her limb-meal!<br>I will go there and do’t i’th’ [[Court|court]], before<br>Her father. I’ll do something. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit |
| FILARIO | Quite besides<br>The [[government]] of patience! You have won.<br>Let’s follow and pervert the present wrath<br>He hath against himself. |
| GIACOMO | With all my heart. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt |
