| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Queen]], Ladies, and Cornelius, a doctor |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] | Whiles yet the dew’s on ground, gather those [[Flowers|flowers]].<br>Make haste. Who has the note of them?<br>A LADY<br>I, madam. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] | Dispatch. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt Ladies |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] | Now, Master Doctor, have you brought those drugs? |
| CORNELIUS | Pleaseth your highness, ay. Here they are, madam.<br>He gives her a box<br>But I beseech your grace, without offence—<br>My [[Conscience|conscience]] bids me ask—wherefore you have<br>Commanded of me these most poisonous compounds,<br>Which are the movers of a languishing [[Death|death]],<br>But though slow, deadly. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] | I [[Wonder|wonder]], doctor,<br>Thou ask‘st me such a question. Have I not been<br>Thy pupil long? Hast thou not learned me how<br>To make perfumes, distil, preserve—yea, so<br>That our great [[King]] himself doth woo me oft<br>For my confections? Having thus far proceeded,<br>Unless thou think’st me devilish, is’t not meet<br>That I did amplify my judgement in<br>Other conclusions? I will try the forces<br>Of these thy compounds on such creatures as<br>We count not worth the hanging, but none human,<br>To try the vigour of them, and apply<br>Allayments to their act, and by them gather<br>Their several virtues and effects. |
| CORNELIUS | Your highness<br>Shall from this practice but make hard your heart.<br>Besides, the seeing these effects will be<br>Both noisome and infectious. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] | O, content thee. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Pisanio |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] | (Aside) Here comes a flattering rascal; upon him<br>Will I first work. He’s factor for his master,<br>And enemy to my son. (Aloud) How now, Pisanio?—<br>Doctor, your service for this [[Time|time]] is ended.<br>Take your own way. |
| CORNELIUS | (aside)<br>I do suspect you, madam.<br>But you shall do no harm. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] | (to Pisanio)<br>Hark thee, a word. |
| CORNELIUS | (aside)<br>I do not like her. She doth think she has<br>Strange ling’ring poisons. I do know her spirit,<br>And will not trust one of her malice with<br>A drug of such damned [[Nature|nature]]. Those she has<br>Will stupefy and dull the sense a while,<br>Which first, perchance, she’ll prove on cats and [[dogs]],<br>Then afterward up higher; but there is<br>No danger in what show of [[Death|death]] it makes<br>More than the locking up the spirits a [[Time|time]],<br>To be more fresh, reviving. She is fooled<br>With a most false effect, and I the truer<br>So to be false with her. |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] | No further service, doctor,<br>Until I send for thee. |
| CORNELIUS | I humbly take my leave. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] | (to Pisanio)<br>Weeps she still, sayst thou? Dost thou think in [[Time|time]]<br>She will not quench, and let instructions enter<br>Where folly now possesses? Do thou work.<br>When thou shalt bring me word she loves my son<br>I’ll tell thee on the instant thou art then<br>As great as is thy master—greater, for<br>His fortunes all lie speechless, and his name<br>Is at last gasp. Return he cannot, nor<br>Continue where he is. To shift his being<br>Is to exchange one misery with another,<br>And every day that comes comes to [[Decay|decay]]<br>A day’s work in him. What shalt thou expect<br>To be depender on a thing that leans,<br>Who cannot be new built nor has no friends<br>So much as but to prop him? |
| *(didascalia)* | She drops her box. He takes it up |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] | Thou tak’st up<br>Thou know‘st not what; but take it for thy labour.<br>It is a thing I made which hath the [[King]]<br>Five times redeemed from [[Death|death]]. I do not know<br>What is more cordial. Nay, I prithee take it.<br>It is an earnest of a farther good<br>That I mean to thee. Tell thy mistress how<br>The case stands with her; do’t as from thyself.<br>Think what a chance thou changest on, but think<br>Thou hast thy mistress still; to boot, my son,<br>Who shall take notice of thee. I’ll move the [[King]]<br>To any shape of thy preferment, such<br>As thou’lt [[Desire|desire]]; and then myself, I chiefly,<br>That set thee on to this [[Desert|desert]], am bound<br>To load thy merit richly. Call my women.<br>Think on my words. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit Pisanio |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] | A sly and constant knave,<br>Not to be shaked; the agent for his master,<br>And the remembrancer of her to hold<br>The hand-fast to her lord. I have given him that<br>Which, if he take, shall quite unpeople her<br>Of liegers for her sweet, and which she after,<br>Except she bend her [[Humour|humour]], shall be assured<br>To taste of too. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter Pisanio and Ladies |
| [[Queen|QUEEN]] | So, so; [[Well|well]] done, [[Well|well]] done.<br>The violets, cowslips, and the primroses<br>Bear to my closet. Fare thee [[Well|well]], Pisanio.<br>Think on my words, Pisanio. |
| PISANIO | And shall do. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt [[Queen]] and Ladies |
| PISANIO | But when to my good lord I prove untrue,<br>I’ll choke mysetf—there’s all I’ll do for you. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exit |
