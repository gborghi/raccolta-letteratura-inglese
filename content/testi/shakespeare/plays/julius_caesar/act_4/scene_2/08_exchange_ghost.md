| [[Ghost|GHOST]] | Thy evil spirit, [[Brutus]]. |
| [[Brutus|BRUTUS]] | Why com’st thou? |
| [[Ghost|GHOST]] | To tell thee thou shalt see me at Philippi. |
| [[Brutus|BRUTUS]] | [[Well]]; then I shall see thee again? |
| [[Ghost|GHOST]] | Ay, at Philippi. |
| [[Brutus|BRUTUS]] | Why, I will see thee at Philippi then. Exit [[Ghost]] |
| [[Brutus|BRUTUS]] | Now I have taken heart, thou vanishest. Ill spirit, I would hold more talk with thee.— Boy, [[Lucius]], Varrus, [[Claudio]], sirs, awake! [[Claudio]]! |
| [[Lucius|LUCIUS]] | The strings, my lord, are false. |
| [[Brutus|BRUTUS]] | He thinks he still is at his instrument.— [[Lucius]], awake! |
| [[Lucius|LUCIUS]] | My lord. |
| [[Brutus|BRUTUS]] | Didst thou dream, [[Lucius]], that thou so cried’st out? |
| [[Lucius|LUCIUS]] | My lord, I do not know that I did cry. |
| [[Brutus|BRUTUS]] | Yes, that thou didst. Didst thou see anything? |
| [[Lucius|LUCIUS]] | Nothing, my lord. |
| [[Brutus|BRUTUS]] | [[Sleep]] again, [[Lucius]].—Sirrah [[Claudio]]! (To Varrus) Fellow, Thou, awake! |
| VARRUS | My lord. |
| [[Claudio|CLAUDIO]] | My lord. |
| [[Brutus|BRUTUS]] | Why did you so cry out, sirs, in your [[Sleep|sleep]]? |
| BOTH | Did we, my lord? |
| [[Brutus|BRUTUS]] | Ay. Saw you anything? |
| VARRUS | No, my lord, I saw nothing. |
| [[Claudio|CLAUDIO]] | Nor I, my lord. |
| [[Brutus|BRUTUS]] | Go and commend me to my brother [[Cassius]]. Bid him set on his powers betimes before, And we will follow. |
| BOTH | It shall be done, my lord. Exeunt Varrus and [[Claudio]] at one door, [[Brutus]] and [[Lucius]] at another door |
