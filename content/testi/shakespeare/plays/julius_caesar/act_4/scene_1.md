| *(didascalia)* | Enter Antony with papers, Octavius, and [[Lepidus]] |
| ANTONY | These many, then, shall die; their names are pricked. |
| OCTAVIUS | (to [[Lepidus]])<br>Your brother too must die. Consent you, [[Lepidus]]? |
| [[Lepidus|LEPIDUS]] | I do consent. |
| OCTAVIUS | Prick him down, Antony. |
| [[Lepidus|LEPIDUS]] | Upon condition Publius shall not live,<br>Who is your sister’s son, [[Mark Antony]]. |
| ANTONY | He shall not live. Look, with a spot I damn him.<br>But [[Lepidus]], go you to Caesar’s [[house]];<br>Fetch the will hither, and we shall determine<br>How to cut off some charge in legacies. |
| [[Lepidus|LEPIDUS]] | What, shall I find you here? |
| OCTAVIUS | Or here or at the Capitol. Exit [[Lepidus]] |
| ANTONY | This is a slight, unmeritable man,<br>Meet to be sent on errands. Is it fit,<br>The three-fold world divided, he should stand<br>One of the three to share it? |
| OCTAVIUS | So you thought him,<br>And took his voice who should be pricked to die<br>In our black sentence and proscription. |
| ANTONY | Octavius, I have seen more days than you,<br>And though we lay these honours on this man<br>To ease ourselves of divers sland’rous loads,<br>He shall but bear them as the ass bears gold,<br>To groan and sweat under the business,<br>Either led or driven as we point the way;<br>And having brought our treasure where we will,<br>Then take we down his load, and turn him off,<br>Like to the empty ass, to shake his ears<br>And graze in commons. |
| OCTAVIUS | You may do your will;<br>But he’s a tried and valiant soldier. |
| ANTONY | So is my [[Horse|horse]], Octavius, and for that<br>I do appoint him store of provender.<br>It is a creature that I teach to fight,<br>To [[Wind|wind]], to stop, to run directly on,<br>His corporal motion governed by my spirit;<br>And in some taste is [[Lepidus]] but so.<br>He must be taught, and trained, and bid go forth—<br>A barren-spirited fellow, one that feeds<br>On objects, arts, and imitations,<br>Which, out of use and staled by other men,<br>Begin his fashion. Do not talk of him<br>But as a property. And now, Octavius,<br>Listen great things. [[Brutus]] and [[Cassius]]<br>Are levying powers. We must straight make head.<br>Therefore let our alliance be combined,<br>Our best friends made, our meinies stretched,<br>And let us presently go sit in council,<br>How covert matters may be best disclosed,<br>And open perils surest answered. |
| OCTAVIUS | Let us do so, for we are at the stake<br>And bayed about with many enemies;<br>And some that smile have in their hearts, I [[Fear|fear]],<br>Millions of mischiefs. Exeunt |
