| *(didascalia)* | Enter [[Portia]] and [[Lucius]] |
| [[Portia|PORTIA]] | I prithee, boy, run to the Senate [[house|House]].<br>Stay not to answer me, but get thee gone.—<br>Why dost thou stay? |
| [[Lucius|LUCIUS]] | To know my errand, madam. |
| [[Portia|PORTIA]] | I would have had thee there and here again<br>Ere I can tell thee what thou shouldst do there.<br>(Aside) O constancy, be strong upon my side;<br>Set a huge mountain ’tween my heart and tongue.<br>I have a man’s mind, but a woman’s might.<br>How hard it is for women to keep counsel!<br>(To [[Lucius]]) Art thou here yet? |
| [[Lucius|LUCIUS]] | Madam, what should I do?<br>Run to the Capitol, and nothing else?<br>And so return to you, and nothing else? |
| [[Portia|PORTIA]] | Yes, bring me word, boy, if thy lord look [[Well|well]],<br>For he went sickly forth; and take good note<br>What Caesar doth, what suitors press to him.<br>Hark, boy, what noise is that? |
| [[Lucius|LUCIUS]] | I hear none, madam. |
| [[Portia|PORTIA]] | Prithee, listen [[Well|well]].<br>I heard a bustling rumour, like a fray,<br>And the [[Wind|wind]] brings it from the Capitol. |
| [[Lucius|LUCIUS]] | Sooth, madam, I hear nothing. |
| *(didascalia)* | Enter the Soothsayer |
| [[Portia|PORTIA]] | Come hither, fellow. Which way hast thou been? |
| SOOTHSAYER | At mine own [[house]], good lady. |
| [[Portia|PORTIA]] | What is’t o’[[Clock|clock]]? |
| SOOTHSAYER | About the ninth hour, lady. |
| [[Portia|PORTIA]] | Is Caesar yet gone to the Capitol? |
| SOOTHSAYER | Madam, not yet. I go to take my stand<br>To see him pass on to the Capitol. |
| [[Portia|PORTIA]] | Thou hast some suit to Caesar, hast thou not? |
| SOOTHSAYER | That I have, lady. If it will please Caesar<br>To be so good to Caesar as to hear me,<br>I shall beseech him to befriend himself. |
| [[Portia|PORTIA]] | Why, know’st thou any harms intended towards him? |
| SOOTHSAYER | None that I know will be; much that I [[Fear|fear]] may chance.<br>Good morrow to you. |
| *(didascalia)* | He moves away Here the street is narrow. |
| SOOTHSAYER | The throng that follows Caesar at the heels,<br>Of senators, of praetors, common suitors,<br>Will crowd a feeble man almost to [[Death|death]].<br>I’ll get me to a place more void, and there<br>Speak to great Caesar as he comes along. Exit |
| [[Portia|PORTIA]] | (aside)<br>I must go in. Ay me! How weak a thing<br>The heart of woman is! O [[Brutus]],<br>The heavens [[Speed|speed]] thee in thine enterprise!—<br>Sure the boy heard me. (To [[Lucius]]) [[Brutus]] hath a suit<br>That Caesar will not grant. (Aside) O, I grow faint!<br>(To [[Lucius]]) Run, [[Lucius]], and commend me to my lord.<br>Say I am merry. Come to me again,<br>And bring me word what he doth say to thee. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt severally |
