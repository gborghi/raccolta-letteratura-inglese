| [[Lucius|LUCIUS]] | The taper burneth in your closet, sir. Searching the [[Window|window]] for a flint, I found This paper, thus sealed up, and I am sure It did not lie there when I went to bed. He gives him the letter |
| [[Brutus|BRUTUS]] | Get you to bed again; it is not day. Is not tomorrow, boy, the ides of March? |
| [[Lucius|LUCIUS]] | I know not, sir. |
| [[Brutus|BRUTUS]] | Look in the calendar and bring me word. |
| [[Lucius|LUCIUS]] | I will, sir. Exit |
| [[Brutus|BRUTUS]] | The exhalations whizzing in the air Give so much light that I may read by them. He opens the letter and reads |
| [[Brutus|BRUTUS]] | ‘[[Brutus]], thou [[Sleep|sleep]]’st. Awake, and see thyself. Shall Rome, et cetera? Speak, strike, redress.‘— ‘[[Brutus]], thou [[Sleep|sleep]]‘st. Awake.’ Such instigations have been often dropped Where I have took them up. ‘Shall Rome, et cetera?’ Thus must I piece it out: Shall Rome stand under one man’s awe? What, Rome? My ancestors did from the streets of Rome The [[Tarquin]] drive when he was called a [[King|king]]. ‘Speak, strike, redress.’ Am I entreated To speak and strike? O Rome, I make thee promise, If the redress will follow, thou receivest Thy full petition at the hand of [[Brutus]]. Enter [[Lucius]] |
| [[Lucius|LUCIUS]] | Sir, March is wasted fifteen days. Knock within |
| [[Brutus|BRUTUS]] | ‘Tis good. Go to the gate; somebody knocks. Exit [[Lucius]] Since [[Cassius]] first did whet me against Caesar I have not slept. Between the acting of a dreadful thing And the first motion, all the interim is Like a phantasma or a hideous dream. The genius and the mortal instruments Are then in counsel, and the state of man, Like to a little kingdom, suffers then The [[Nature|nature]] of an insurrection. Enter [[Lucius]] |
| [[Lucius|LUCIUS]] | Sir, ’tis your brother [[Cassius]] at the door, Who doth [[Desire|desire]] to see you. |
| [[Brutus|BRUTUS]] | Is he [[Alone|alone]]? |
| [[Lucius|LUCIUS]] | No, sir, there are more with him. |
| [[Brutus|BRUTUS]] | Do you know them? |
| [[Lucius|LUCIUS]] | No, sir; their hats are plucked about their ears, And half their faces buried in their cloaks, That by no means I may discover them By any mark of favour. |
| [[Brutus|BRUTUS]] | Let ’em enter. Exit [[Lucius]] They are the faction. O conspiracy, Sham‘st thou to show thy dang’rous brow by [[Night|night]], When evils are most free? O then by day Where wilt thou find a cavern dark enough To mask thy monstrous visage? Seek none, conspiracy. Hide it in smiles and affability; For if thou put thy native semblance on, Not Erebus itself were dim enough To hide thee from prevention. Enter the conspirators, muffled: [[Cassius]], [[Casca]], Decius, Cinna, Metellus, and Trebonius |
| [[Cassius|CASSIUS]] | I think we are too bold upon your rest. Good morrow, [[Brutus]]. Do we trouble you? |
| [[Brutus|BRUTUS]] | I have been up this hour, awake all [[Night|night]]. Know I these men that come along with you? |
| [[Cassius|CASSIUS]] | Yes, every man of them; and no man here But honours you; and every one doth wish You had but that opinion of yourself Which every noble Roman bears of you. This is Trebonius. |
| [[Brutus|BRUTUS]] | He is welcome hither. |
| [[Cassius|CASSIUS]] | This, Decius [[Brutus]]. |
| [[Brutus|BRUTUS]] | He is welcome too. |
| [[Cassius|CASSIUS]] | This, [[Casca]]; Cinna, this; and this, Metellus Cimber. |
