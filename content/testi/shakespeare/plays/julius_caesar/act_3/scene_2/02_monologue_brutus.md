| [[Brutus|BRUTUS]] | Be patient till the last. Romans, countrymen, and [[lovers]], hear me for my cause, and be silent that you may hear. Believe me for mine [[Honour|honour]], and have respect to mine [[Honour|honour]], that you may believe. Censure me in your wisdom, and awake your senses, that you may the better judge. If there be any in this assembly, any dear friend of Caesar’s, to him I say that [[Brutus]]’ [[Love|love]] to Caesar was no less than his. If then that friend demand why [[Brutus]] [[Rose|rose]] against Caesar, this is my answer: not that I loved Caesar less, but that I loved Rome more. Had you rather Caesar were living, and die all slaves, than that Caesar were dead, to live all free men? As Caesar loved me, I weep for him. As he was fortunate, I rejoice at it. As he was valiant, I [[Honour|honour]] him. But as he was ambitious, I slew him. There is [[Tears|tears]] for his [[Love|love]], joy for his fortune, [[Honour|honour]] for his valour, and [[Death|death]] for his [[Ambition|ambition]]. Who is here so base that would be a bondman? If any, speak, for him have I offended. Who is here so rude that would not be a Roman? If any, speak, for him have I offended. Who is here so vile that will not [[Love|love]] his country? If any, speak, for him have I offended. I pause for a reply. |
