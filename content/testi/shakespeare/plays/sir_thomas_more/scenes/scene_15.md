| *(didascalia)* | Enter Butler, Brewer, Porter, and [[Horse]]-keeper, several ways |
| BUTLER | Robin Brewer, how now, man? What cheer, what cheer? |
| BREWER | [[Faith]], Ned Butler, sick of thy disease, and these our other fellows here, Ralph [[Horse]]-keeper and Giles Porter: sad, sad. They say my lord goes to his trial today. |
| [[Horse|HORSE]]-KEEPER | To it, man? Why, he is now at it. God send him [[Well|well]] to [[Speed|speed]]! |
| PORTER | Amen. Even as I wish to mine own [[Soul|soul]], so [[Speed|speed]] it with my honourable lord and master [[Sir Thomas More]]! |
| BUTLER | I cannot tell—I have nothing to do with matters above my capacity—but, as God judge me, if I might speak my mind, I think there lives not a more harmless gentleman in the universal world. |
| BREWER | Nor a wiser, nor a merrier, nor an honester. Go to, I’ll put that in upon mine own knowledge. |
| PORTER | Nay, an ye bate him his due of his housekeeping, hang ye all! Ye have many lord chancellors comes in debt at the year’s end, and for very housekeeping! |
| [[Horse|HORSE]]-KEEPER | [[Well]], he was too good a lord for us, and therefore, I [[Fear|fear]], God himself will take him. But I’ll be hanged if ever I have such another service. |
| BREWER | Soft, man, we are not discharged yet. My lord may come [[Home|home]] again, and all will be [[Well|well]]. |
| BUTLER | I much mistrust it. When they go to ’raigning once, there’s ever foul weather for a great while after. Enter Gough and Catesby, with a paper<br>But soft, here comes Master Gough and Master Catesby.<br>Now we shall hear more. |
| [[Horse|HORSE]]-KEEPER | Before God, they are very sad. I [[Doubt|doubt]] my lord is condemned. |
| PORTER | God bless his [[Soul|soul]], and a fig then for all worldly condemnation! |
| GOUGH | [[Well]] said, Giles Porter, I commend thee for it.<br>’Twas spoken like a [[Well|well]]-affected servant<br>Of him that was a kind lord to us all. |
| CATESBY | Which now no more he shall be, for, dear fellows,<br>Now we are masterless. Though he may live<br>So long as please the [[King]], but law hath made him<br>A dead man to the world, and given the axe his head,<br>But his sweet [[Soul|soul]] to live among the saints. |
| GOUGH | Let us entreat ye to go call together<br>The rest of your sad fellows—by the roll<br>You’re just seven score—and tell them what ye hear<br>A virtuous, honourable lord hath done<br>Even for the meanest follower that he had.<br>This writing found my lady in his study<br>This instant morning, wherein is set down<br>Each servant’s name, according to his place<br>And office in the [[house]]. On every man<br>He frankly hath bestown twenty nobles,<br>The best and worst together, all alike,<br>Which Master Catesby hereforth will pay ye. |
| CATESBY | Take it as it is meant, a kind remembrance<br>Of a far kinder lord, with whose sad fall<br>He gives up [[house]], and farewell to us all.<br>Thus the fair spreading oak falls not [[Alone|alone]],<br>But all the neighbour plants and under-trees<br>Are crushed down with his weight. No more of this.<br>Come and receive your due, and after go<br>Fellow-like hence, co-partners of one woe. |
| *(didascalia)* | Exeunt |
