       1  
  Starting from fish-shape Paumanok where I was born,  
  [[Well]]-begotten, and rais’d by a perfect mother,  
  After roaming many lands, [[Lover|lover]] of populous pavements,  
  Dweller in Mannahatta my [[City|city]], or on southern savannas,  
  Or a soldier camp’d or carrying my knapsack and gun, or a miner  
      in California,  
  Or rude in my [[Home|home]] in Dakota’s woods, my diet meat, my drink from  
      the [[spring]],  
  Or withdrawn to muse and meditate in some deep recess,  
  Far from the clank of crowds intervals passing rapt and happy,  
  Aware of the fresh free giver the flowing Missouri, aware of  
      mighty Niagara,  
  Aware of the buffalo herds grazing the plains, the hirsute and  
      strong-breasted bull,  
  Of earth, rocks, Fifth-month [[Flowers|flowers]] experienced, [[Stars|stars]], rain, [[Snow|snow]],  
      my amaze,  
  Having studied the mocking-bird’s tones and the flight of the  
      mountain-hawk,  
  And heard at [[Dawn|dawn]] the unrivall’d one, the hermit thrush from the  
      swamp-cedars,  
  Solitary, singing in the West, I strike up for a New World.
