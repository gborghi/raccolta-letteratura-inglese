       6  
  The [[Soul|soul]],  
  Forever and forever--longer than soil is brown and solid--longer  
      than [[Water|water]] ebbs and flows.  
  I will make the [[Poems|poems]] of materials, for I think they are to be the  
      most spiritual [[Poems|poems]],  
  And I will make the [[Poems|poems]] of my [[Body|body]] and of [[Mortality|mortality]],  
  For I think I shall then supply myself with the [[Poems|poems]] of my [[Soul|soul]] and  
      of [[Immortality|immortality]].  

  I will make a [[Song|song]] for these States that no one State may under any  
      circumstances be subjected to another State,  
  And I will make a [[Song|song]] that there shall be comity by day and by  
      [[Night|night]] between all the States, and between any two of them,  
  And I will make a [[Song|song]] for the ears of the President, full of  
      weapons with menacing points,  
  And behind the weapons countless dissatisfied faces;  
  And a [[Song|song]] make I of the One form’d out of all,  
  The fang’d and glittering One whose head is over all,  
  Resolute warlike One including and over all,  
  (However high the head of any else that head is over all.)  

  I will acknowledge contemporary lands,  
  I will trail the whole geography of the globe and salute courteously  
      every [[City|city]] large and small,  
  And employments! I will put in my [[Poems|poems]] that with you is [[Heroism|heroism]]  
      upon land and sea,  
  And I will report all [[Heroism|heroism]] from an American point of view.  

  I will sing the [[Song|song]] of companionship,  
  I will show what [[Alone|alone]] must finally compact these,  
  I believe these are to found their own ideal of manly [[Love|love]],  
      indicating it in me,  
  I will therefore let flame from me the burning fires that were  
      threatening to consume me,  
  I will lift what has too long kept down those smouldering fires,  
  I will give them complete abandonment,  
  I will write the evangel-poem of comrades and of [[Love|love]],  
  For who but I should understand [[Love|love]] with all its sorrow and joy?  
  And who but I should be the poet of comrades?
