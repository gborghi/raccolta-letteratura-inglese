<span class="atom-split" data-atom="107_on_the_beach_at_night" data-title="On the Beach at Night" data-chapter="" data-kind="intro" data-tags="motif/stars,motif/night,motif/childhood,motif/the_sea,concept/immortality,concept/mortality,form/free_verse,cluster/stars,cluster/night,cluster/military_camp"></span>


_Cluster: SEA-DRIFT_  


On the beach at [[motifs/night|night]],  
Stands a [[archetypes/child|child]] with her father,  
Watching the east, the [[motifs/autumn|autumn]] sky.  

Up through the darkness,  
While ravening clouds, the burial clouds, in black masses spreading,  
Lower sullen and fast athwart and down the sky,  
Amid a transparent clear belt of ether yet left in the east,  
Ascends large and calm the lord-star Jupiter,  
And nigh at hand, only a very little above,  
Swim the delicate sisters the Pleiades.  

From the beach the [[archetypes/child|child]] holding the hand of her father,  
Those burial-clouds that lower victorious soon to devour all,  
Watching, silently weeps.  

Weep not, [[archetypes/child|child]],  
Weep not, my darling,  
With these kisses let me remove your [[motifs/tears|tears]],  
The ravening clouds shall not long be victorious,  
They shall not long possess the sky, they devour the [[motifs/stars|stars]] only in  
apparition,  
Jupiter shall emerge, be patient, watch again another [[motifs/night|night]], the  
Pleiades shall emerge,  
They are immortal, all those [[motifs/stars|stars]] both silvery and golden shall  
shine out again,  
The great [[motifs/stars|stars]] and the little ones shall shine out again, they endure,  
The vast immortal suns and the long-enduring pensive moons shall  
again shine.  

Then dearest [[archetypes/child|child]] mournest thou only for jupiter?  
Considerest thou alone the burial of the [[motifs/stars|stars]]?  

Something there is,  
(With my lips soothing thee, adding I whisper,  
I give thee the first suggestion, the problem and indirection,)  
Something there is more immortal even than the [[motifs/stars|stars]],  
(Many the burials, many the days and nights, passing away,)  
Something that shall endure longer even than lustrous Jupiter  
Longer than sun or any revolving satellite,  
Or the radiant sisters the Pleiades.

<span class="atom-split" data-atom="109_on_the_beach_at_night_alone" data-title="On the Beach at Night Alone" data-chapter="" data-kind="intro" data-tags="motif/the_sea,motif/stars,motif/night,concept/self_and_soul,concept/the_sublime,concept/immortality,form/free_verse,cluster/stars,cluster/night,cluster/military_camp"></span>


_Cluster: SEA-DRIFT_  


On the beach at [[motifs/night|night]] alone,  
As the old mother sways her to and fro singing her husky song,  
As I watch the bright [[motifs/stars|stars]] shining, I think a thought of the clef  
of the universes and of the future.  

A vast similitude interlocks all,  
All spheres, grown, ungrown, small, large, suns, moons, planets,  
All distances of place however wide,  
All distances of [[motifs/time|time]], all inanimate forms,  
All souls, all living bodies though they be ever so different, or in  
different worlds,  
All gaseous, watery, vegetable, mineral processes, the fishes, the brutes,  
All nations, colors, barbarisms, civilizations, languages,  
All identities that have existed or may exist on this globe, or any globe,  
All lives and deaths, all of the past, present, future,  
This vast similitude spans them, and always has spann’d,  
And shall forever span them and compactly hold and enclose them.

When I heard the learn’d astronomer,  
When the proofs, the figures, were ranged in columns before me,  
When I was shown the charts and diagrams, to add, divide, and measure them,  
When I sitting heard the astronomer where he lectured with much

Till rising and gliding out I wander’d off by myself,  
In the mystical moist [[motifs/night|night]]-air, and from [[motifs/time|time]] to [[motifs/time|time]],  
Look’d up in perfect [[motifs/silence|silence]] at the [[motifs/stars|stars]].

moderate [[motifs/night|night]]-[[motifs/wind|wind]],

<span class="atom-split" data-atom="232_the_sleepers" data-title="The Sleepers" data-chapter="" data-kind="intro" data-tags="topos/night_journey,archetype/great_mother,motif/sleep,motif/dreams,motif/night,motif/death,motif/the_sea,concept/self_and_soul,concept/mortality,concept/identity,form/free_verse,cluster/stars,cluster/night,cluster/military_camp"></span>



1  
I wander all [[motifs/night|night]] in my vision,  
Stepping with light feet, swiftly and noiselessly stepping and stopping,  
Bending with open eyes over the shut eyes of sleepers,  
Wandering and confused, lost to myself, ill-assorted, contradictory,  
Pausing, gazing, bending, and stopping.  

How solemn they look there, stretch’d and still,  
How quiet they breathe, the little children in their cradles.  

The wretched features of ennuyes, the white features of corpses, the  
livid faces of drunkards, the sick-gray faces of onanists,  
The gash’d bodies on battle-fields, the insane in their  
strong-door’d rooms, the sacred idiots, the new-born emerging  
from gates, and the dying emerging from gates,  
The [[motifs/night|night]] pervades them and infolds them.  

The married couple [[motifs/sleep|sleep]] calmly in their bed, he with his palm on  
the hip of the wife, and she with her palm on the hip of the husband,  
The sisters [[motifs/sleep|sleep]] lovingly side by side in their bed,  
The men [[motifs/sleep|sleep]] lovingly side by side in theirs,  
And the mother sleeps with her little [[archetypes/child|child]] carefully wrapt.  

The blind [[motifs/sleep|sleep]], and the deaf and dumb [[motifs/sleep|sleep]],  
The prisoner sleeps [[motifs/well|well]] in the [[motifs/prison|prison]], the runaway son sleeps,  
The murderer that is to be hung next day, how does he [[motifs/sleep|sleep]]?  
And the murder’d person, how does he [[motifs/sleep|sleep]]?  

The female that loves unrequited sleeps,  
And the male that loves unrequited sleeps,  
The head of the money-maker that plotted all day sleeps,  
And the enraged and treacherous dispositions, all, all [[motifs/sleep|sleep]].  

I stand in the dark with drooping eyes by the worst-[[concepts/suffering|suffering]] and  
the most restless,  
I pass my hands soothingly to and fro a few inches from them,  
The restless sink in their beds, they fitfully [[motifs/sleep|sleep]].  

Now I pierce the darkness, new beings appear,  
The earth recedes from me into the [[motifs/night|night]],  
I saw that it was beautiful, and I see that what is not the earth is  
beautiful.  

I go from bedside to bedside, I [[motifs/sleep|sleep]] close with the other sleepers  
each in turn,  
I dream in my dream all the [[motifs/dreams|dreams]] of the other dreamers,  
And I become the other dreamers.  

I am a [[motifs/dance|dance]]--play up there! the fit is whirling me fast!  

I am the ever-laughing--it is new [[motifs/moon|moon]] and twilight,  
I see the hiding of douceurs, I see nimble [[motifs/ghosts|ghosts]] whichever way look,  
Cache and cache again deep in the ground and sea, and where it is  
neither ground nor sea.  

[[motifs/well|Well]] do they do their jobs those journeymen divine,  
Only from me can they hide nothing, and would not if they could,  
I reckon I am their boss and they make me a pet besides,  
And surround me and lead me and run ahead when I walk,  
To lift their cunning covers to signify me with stretch’d arms, and  
resume the way;  
Onward we move, a gay gang of blackguards! with mirth-shouting  
music and wild-flapping pennants of joy!  

I am the actor, the actress, the voter, the politician,  
The emigrant and the [[concepts/exile|exile]], the criminal that stood in the box,
