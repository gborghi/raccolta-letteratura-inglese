> [!abstract] 
> An ars-poetica lyric arguing the truest drama is ordinary life enacted in the human heart, needing no Shakespeare or recorded theatre; invokes Hamlet and Romeo. On art, the theatrum mundi and the common day.

## Testo integrale / Full text

DAY  

Drama's Vitallest Expression is the Common Day  
That arise and set about Us —  
Other Tragedy  

Perish in the Recitation —  
This — the best enact  
When the Audience is scattered  
And the Boxes shut —  

"Hamlet" to Himself were Hamlet —  
Had not Shakespeare wrote —  
Though the "Romeo" left no Record  
Of his Juliet,  

It were infinite enacted  
In the Human Heart —  
Only Theatre recorded  
Owner cannot shut —
