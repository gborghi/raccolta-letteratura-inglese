*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Self-Knowledge · Immortality · Self-Reliance]]

> [!abstract] 
> A compressed lyric of one who returned with a tinge of the other world, half of dust and half of day, like a sod espousing a violet allied chiefly to the skies. Treats immortality, the soul and the union of earthly and heavenly.

## Testo integrale / Full text

To this World she returned.  
But with a tinge of that —  
A Compound manner,  
As a Sod  
Espoused a Violet,  
That chiefer to the Skies  
Than to himself, allied,  
Dwelt hesitating, half of Dust,  
And half of Day, the Bride.
