## Testo integrale / Full text

After an interval of silence came this letter, in the same year:—

Must I lose the friend that saved my life without inquiring why? Affection gropes through drifts of awe for his tropic door.

That every bliss we know or guess hourly befall him, is his scholar's prayer.
