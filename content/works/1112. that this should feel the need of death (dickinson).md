> An epigram on the irony and audacity that the small, no less than the great, must die — mortality, paradox and the universality of death.

## Testo integrale / Full text

That this should feel the need of Death  
The same as those that lived  
Is such a Feat of Irony  
As never was — achieved —  

Not satisfied to ape the Great  
In his simplicity  
The small must die, as well as He —  
Oh the Audacity —
