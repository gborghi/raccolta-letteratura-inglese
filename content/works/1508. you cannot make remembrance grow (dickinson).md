*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Memory and Time · Memory · Nostalgia]]

> [!abstract] 
> An aphoristic lyric using horticultural imagery to argue that genuine memory is rooted, indestructible (shod with adamant), and cannot be artificially cultivated or cut down.

## Testo integrale / Full text

You cannot make Remembrance grow  
When it has lost its Root —  
The tightening the Soil around  
And setting it upright  
Deceives perhaps the Universe  
But not retrieves the Plant —  
Real Memory, like Cedar Feet  
Is shod with Adamant —  
Nor can you cut Remembrance down  
When it shall once have grown —  
Its Iron Buds will sprout anew  
However overthrown —
