*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Essay · Critique of Progress · Common Man]]

> [!abstract] 
> A political-botanical conceit: revolution is the pod from which systems rattle, and shaking liberty tests whether it is still alive or dead on the stalk.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Revolution is the Pod  
Systems rattle from  
When the Winds of Will are stirred  
Excellent is Bloom  

But except its Russet Base  
Every Summer be  
The Entomber of itself,  
So of Liberty —  

Left inactive on the Stalk  
All its Purple fled  
Revolution shakes it for  
Test if it be dead.
