*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Melancholy · Suffering · Pain and Suffering]]

> [!abstract] 
> A man buried under fallen timbers is dug out only to die, the rescue ironically becoming death, named the grace of death. Themes of mortality, suffering, futility, and death as deliverance.

## Testo integrale / Full text

In falling Timbers buried —  
There breathed a Man —  
Outside — the spades — were plying —  
The Lungs — within —  

Could He — know — they sought Him —  
Could They — know — He breathed —  
Horrid Sand Partition —  
Neither — could be heard —  

Never slacked the Diggers —  
But when Spades had done —  
Oh, Reward of Anguish,  
It was dying — Then —  

Many Things — are fruitless —  
'Tis a Baffling Earth —  
But there is no Gratitude  
Like the Grace — of Death —
