> [!abstract] 
> A lyric questioning whether art (the bird's song, the rose's bloom) merits reward when no audience comes — a meditation on the artist, merit, neglect, and self-justifying beauty.

## Testo integrale / Full text

The Bird must sing to earn the Crumb  
What merit have the Tune  
No Breakfast if it guaranty  

The Rose content may bloom  
To gain renown of Lady's Drawer  
But if the Lady come  
But once a Century, the Rose  
Superfluous become —
