> [!abstract] 
> Begging for life is penury in chief; the speaker grants a smuggled reprieve lest gratitude revive the snake — mercy, power over a life and lurking sin.

## Connections

**Motifs:** [[Serpent Snake]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

It came his turn to beg —  
The begging for the life  
Is different from another Alms  
'Tis Penury in Chief —  

I scanned his narrow realm  
I gave him leave to live  
Lest Gratitude revive the snake  
Though smuggled his reprieve
