## Testo integrale / Full text

March is the Month of Expectation.  
The things we do not know —  
The Persons of prognostication  
Are coming now —  
We try to show becoming firmness —  
But pompous Joy  
Betrays us, as his first Betrothal  
Betrays a Boy.
