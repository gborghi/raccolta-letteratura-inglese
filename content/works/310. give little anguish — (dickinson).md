## Testo integrale / Full text

Give little Anguish —  
Lives will fret —  
Give Avalanches —  
And they'll slant —  
Straighten — look cautious for their Breath —  
But make no syllable — like Death —  
Who only shows the Marble Disc —  
Sublimer sort — than Speech —
