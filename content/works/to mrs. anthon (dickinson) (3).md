## Testo integrale / Full text

Following are letters written to her sister, Miss Lavinia Dickinson, while Emily was receiving treatment for her eyes in Boston. She was there for this purpose twice,—during the summer of 1864, and again in 1865, usually writing of these years as “when I was sick so long,” which has given many persons the idea of an invalidism she never had.
