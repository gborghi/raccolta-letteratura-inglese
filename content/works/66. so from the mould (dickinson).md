## Testo integrale / Full text

So from the mould  
Scarlet and Gold  
Many a Bulb will rise —  
Hidden away, cunningly, From sagacious eyes.  

So from Cocoon  
Many a Worm  
Leap so Highland gay,  
Peasants like me,  
Peasants like Thee  
Gaze perplexedly!
