> [!abstract] 
> A lyric on sunset and sunrise witnessed only by the earth, the speaker, and a nameless bird — solitary witnesses to the sun's majesty and crown.

## Testo integrale / Full text

The Sun went down — no Man looked on —  
The Earth and I, alone,  
Were present at the Majesty —  
He triumphed, and went on —  

The Sun went up — no Man looked on —  
The Earth and I and One  
A nameless Bird — a Stranger  
Were Witness for the Crown —
