*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Lyric · Romantic Love · Marriage and Fidelity]]

> [!abstract] 
> A compact lyric arguing that constancy is bedrock: only when astronomer, explorer, and sailor abandon their fixed objects will 'treason' become thinkable, an emblem of unbreakable faith and fidelity.

## Testo integrale / Full text

When the Astronomer stops seeking  
For his Pleiad's Face —  
When the lone British Lady  
Forsakes the Arctic Race  

When to his Covenant Needle  
The Sailor doubting turns —  
It will be amply early  
To ask what treason means.
