*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Mortality · Grave · Memento Mori]]

> [!abstract] 
> A lyric on the 'Compound Vision' of the dying, who through an open tomb see clearly what time obscured — the finite furnished with the infinite; a meditation on death, time, and revelation.

## Testo integrale / Full text

TIME —  

The Admirations — and Contempts — of time —  
Show justest — through an Open Tomb —  
The Dying — as it were a Height  
Reorganizes Estimate  
And what We saw not  
We distinguish clear —  
And mostly — see not  
What We saw before —  

'Tis Compound Vision —  
Light — enabling Light —  
The Finite — furnished  
With the Infinite —  
Convex — and Concave Witness —  
Back — toward Time —  
And forward —  
Toward the God of Him —
