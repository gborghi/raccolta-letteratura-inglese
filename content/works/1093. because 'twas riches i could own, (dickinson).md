> A lyric on possession and value: earned wealth feels like poverty, while an unattainable, intangible income holds a miser's sweeter charm — wealth, paradox and desire.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Because 'twas Riches I could own,  
Myself had earned it — Me,  
I knew the Dollars by their names —  
It feels like Poverty  

An Earldom out of sight to hold,  
An Income in the Air,  
Possession — has a sweeter chink  
Unto a Miser's Ear —
