> [!abstract] 
> A lyric of speculation on another May beyond this one, a heavenly spring where saints and the plain neighbor keep eternal season; themes of immortality, faith, and wonder.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Besides this May  
We know  
There is Another —  
How fair  
Our Speculations of the Foreigner!  

Some know Him whom We knew —  
Sweet Wonder —  
A Nature be  
Where Saints, and our plain going Neighbor  
Keep May!
