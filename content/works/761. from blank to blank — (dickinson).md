## Testo integrale / Full text

From Blank to Blank —  
A Threadless Way  
I pushed Mechanic feet —  
To stop — or perish — or advance —  
Alike indifferent —  

If end I gained  
It ends beyond  
Indefinite disclosed —  
I shut my eyes — and groped as well  
'Twas lighter — to be Blind —
