*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Time · Tempus Fugit · Clock]]

> [!abstract] 
> A short lyric on the accumulating weight of time and memory after loss; the speaker begs the absent beloved's face to efface the mountain of years and catch her before she falls.

## Testo integrale / Full text

The Pile of Years is not so high  
As when you came before  
But it is rising every Day  
From recollection's Floor  
And while by standing on my Heart  
I still can reach the top  
Efface the mountain with your face  
And catch me ere I drop
