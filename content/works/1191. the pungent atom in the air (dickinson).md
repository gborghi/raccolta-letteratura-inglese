## Testo integrale / Full text

The pungent atom in the Air  
Admits of no debate —  
All that is named of Summer Days  
Relinquished our Estate —  

For what Department of Delight  
As positive are we  
As Limit of Dominion  
Or Dams — of Ecstasy —
