> [!abstract] 
> A compressed lyric on each soul's allotted portion of night and morning, bliss and scorn; stars and mists give way at last to day, an emblem of suffering resolving into light.

## Testo integrale / Full text

OUR share of night to bear,  
Our share of morning,  
Our blank in bliss to fill,  
Our blank in scorning.  

Here a star, and there a star,  
Some lose their way.  
Here a mist, and there a mist,  
Afterwards—day!
