## Testo integrale / Full text

MR. COWAN graduated at Amherst College in 1866, and “Peter” was Emily Dickinson's especial appellation for this favorite cousin. The first letter was written upon the occasion of his marriage to Miss Margaret Elizabeth Rhea.
