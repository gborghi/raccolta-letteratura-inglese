*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Rose · Desire · Value and Scarcity]]

> [!abstract] 
> A brief lyric distinguishing garlands and laurels for queens and heroes from the humble rose Nature ordains in charity and equity; humility, nature and remembrance.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Garlands for Queens, may be -  
Laurels - for rare degree  
Of soul or sword -  
Ah - but remembering me -  
Ah - but remembering thee -  
Nature in chivalry -  
Nature in charity -  
Nature in equity -  
The Rose ordained!
