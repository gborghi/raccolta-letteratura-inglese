> A lyric on the soul's nocturnal reach toward infinity within a short life, likened to kept children turning to the sea; transience, soul, infinity.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Contained in this short Life  
Are magical extents  
The soul returning soft at night  
To steal securer thence  

As Children strictest kept  
Turn soonest to the sea  
Whose nameless Fathoms slink away  
Beside infinity
