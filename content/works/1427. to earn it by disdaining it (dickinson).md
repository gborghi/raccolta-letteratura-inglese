## Testo integrale / Full text

To earn it by disdaining it  
Is Fame's consummate Fee —  
He loves what spurns him —  
Look behind — He is pursuing thee.  

So let us gather — every Day —  
The Aggregate of  
Life's Bouquet  
Be Honor and not shame —
