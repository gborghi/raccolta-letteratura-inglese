## Testo integrale / Full text

DEAR FRIEND,—Accept my timid happiness.

No joy can be in vain, but adds to some bright total, whose dwelling is unknown.

The immortality of flowers must enrich our own, and we certainly should resent a redemption that excluded them.

Was not the “breath of fragrance” designed for your cheek solely?

The fear that it was crimsons my own, though to divide its Heaven is Heaven's highest half.
