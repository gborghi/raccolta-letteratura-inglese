## Testo integrale / Full text

DEAR FRIEND:—

To try to speak, and miss the way, And ask it of the tears, Is gratitude's sweet poverty, The tatters that he wears. A better coat, if he possessed, Would help him to conceal, Not subjugate, the mutineer Whose title is “the soul.”
