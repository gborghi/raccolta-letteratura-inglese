*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Self-Knowledge · Immortality · Self-Reliance]]

> [!abstract] 
> A lyric meditating on the precarious double inheritance of body and soul as property entailed upon an unsuspecting heir, with God as a frontier — identity, the self and soul, immortality.

**Themes & Concepts:** [[Self-Knowledge]], [[Identity]], [[Immortality]], [[Faith]], [[Mortality]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

I am afraid to own a Body —  
I am afraid to own a Soul —  
Profound — precarious Property —  
Possession, not optional —  

Double Estate — entailed at pleasure  
Upon an unsuspecting Heir —  
Duke in a moment of Deathlessness  
And God, for a Frontier.
