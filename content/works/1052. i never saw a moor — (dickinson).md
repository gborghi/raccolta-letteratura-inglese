> [!abstract] 
> A famous lyric of faith without sight: never having seen moor, sea, God, or Heaven, the speaker is yet certain of them as if given the checks.

## Testo integrale / Full text

I never saw a Moor--  
I never saw the Sea--  
Yet know I how the Heather looks  
And what a Billow be.  

I never spoke with God,  
Nor visited in Heaven--  
Yet certain am I of the spot  
As if the Checks were given--
