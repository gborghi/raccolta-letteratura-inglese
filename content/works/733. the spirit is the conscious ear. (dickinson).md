## Testo integrale / Full text

The Spirit is the Conscious Ear.  
We actually Hear  
When We inspect — that's audible —  
That is admitted — Here —  

For other Services — as Sound —  
There hangs a smaller Ear  
Outside the Castle — that Contain —  
The other — only — Hear —
