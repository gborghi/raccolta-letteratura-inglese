*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[White and Red · Riddle · Secrecy and Concealment]]

> [!abstract] 
> A lyric of riddling definitions personifying grief as mouse, thief, juggler and gourmand, emphasizing its secrecy and refusal to confess even under torture. Explores the concealment and silent endurance of inward suffering.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Grief is a Mouse —  
And chooses Wainscot in the Breast  
For His Shy House —  
And baffles quest —  

Grief is a Thief — quick startled —  
Pricks His Ear — report to hear  
Of that Vast Dark —  
That swept His Being — back —  

Grief is a Juggler — boldest at the Play —  
Lest if He flinch — the eye that way  
Pounce on His Bruises — One — say — or Three —  
Grief is a Gourmand — spare His luxury —  

Best Grief is Tongueless — before He'll tell —  
Burn Him in the Public Square —  
His Ashes — will  
Possibly — if they refuse — How then know —  
Since a Rack couldn't coax a syllable — now.
