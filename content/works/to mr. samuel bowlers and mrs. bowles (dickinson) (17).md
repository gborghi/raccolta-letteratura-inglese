## Testo integrale / Full text

The following lines, sent with flowers, have almost as quaint and “seventeenth century” a flavor as the now famous quatrain beginning,—

“A death-blow is a life-blow to some.” If recollecting were forgetting Then I remember not. And if forgetting, recollecting, How near I had forgot! And if to miss were merry, And if to mourn were gay, How very blithe the fingers That gathered this, to-day!
