> [!abstract] 
> A lyric of being trapped between sea and abyss, retreating up and down as the undermining feet meet — peril at the edge instructs toward the divine; a meditation on terror, the sublime, and revelation.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Escaping backward to perceive  
The Sea upon our place —  
Escaping forward, to confront  
His glittering Embrace —  

Retreating up, a Billow's height  
Retreating blinded down  
Our undermining feet to meet  
Instructs to the Divine.
