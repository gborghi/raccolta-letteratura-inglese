## Testo integrale / Full text

DEAR FRIEND,—You were so generous to me, that if possible I offended you, I could not too deeply apologize.

To doubt my behavior is a new pain. I could be honorable no more, till I asked you about it. I know not what to deem myself—yesterday “your scholar,” but might I be the one you to-night forgave, 'tis a better honor. Mine is but just the thief's request.
