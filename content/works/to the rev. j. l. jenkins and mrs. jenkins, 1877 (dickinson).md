## Testo integrale / Full text

DEAR FRIEND,—It was pathetic to see your voice, instead of hearing it, for it had grown sweetly familiar in the house, as a bird's.

Father left us in June—you leave us in May. I am glad there will be no April till another year. . . .

Sorrow is unsafe, when it is real sorrow—I am glad so many are counterfeits—guileless because they believe themselves.

Kiss “Diddie” and Mac for us—precious refugees, with love for our brother, whom with yourself, we follow the peculiar distance, “even unto the end.”

Perhaps it is “the end” now—I think the bell thought so, because it bade us all good-by when you stood in the door.

You concealed that you heard it. Thank you.
