> [!abstract] 
> A lyric on the deprivation of light: only one who saw sunrise can grieve its loss, the emigrant of light afflicted, blindness that beheld and blest yet cannot find its eye. Themes of loss, vision, and grief.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Who saw no Sunrise cannot say  
The Countenance 'twould be.  
Who guess at seeing, guess at loss  
Of the Ability.  

The Emigrant of Light, it is  
Afflicted for the Day.  
The Blindness that beheld and blest —  
And could not find its Eye.
