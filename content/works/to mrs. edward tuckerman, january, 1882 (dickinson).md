## Testo integrale / Full text

DEAR FRIEND,—The gray afternoon—the sweet knock, and the ebbing voice of the boys are a pictorial memory; and then the little bins and the purple kernels—'twas like the larder of a doll.

To the inditing heart we wish no sigh had come.

Sweet pirate of the heart, Not pirate of the sea, What wrecketh thee? Some spice's mutiny— Some attar's perfidy? Confide in me.
