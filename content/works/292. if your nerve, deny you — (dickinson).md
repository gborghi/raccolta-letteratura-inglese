*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Self-Knowledge · Immortality · Self-Reliance]]

> [!abstract] 
> Lyric of stoic courage urging the soul to lean against the grave when nerve fails; fortitude in the face of death and despair.

## Testo integrale / Full text

If your Nerve, deny you —  
Go above your Nerve —  
He can lean against the Grave,  
If he fear to swerve —  

That's a steady posture —  
Never any bend  
Held of those Brass arms —  
Best Giant made —  

If your Soul seesaw —  
Lift the Flesh door —  
The Poltroon wants Oxygen —  
Nothing more —
