*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Self-Knowledge · Immortality · Self-Reliance]]

> [!abstract] 
> The soul like a balloon longs for ascension/release, indignant at the dust that bound it; the spirit's yearning for immortality.

## Connections

**Motifs:** [[Dust]], [[Birds]], [[Music and Song]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

As from the earth the light Balloon  
Asks nothing but release —  
Ascension that for which it was,  
Its soaring Residence.  
The spirit looks upon the Dust  
That fastened it so long  
With indignation,  
As a Bird  
Defrauded of its song.
