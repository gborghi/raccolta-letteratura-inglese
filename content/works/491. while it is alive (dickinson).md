*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Self-Knowledge · Immortality · Self-Reliance]]

> [!abstract] 
> Love as inseparable union sharing one air, blood, and sacrament; love outlasting death, fellow of the resurrection raising the dust to live.

## Connections

**Motifs:** [[Love]], [[Death]], [[Dust]]
**Themes & Concepts:** [[Immortality]], [[Grace and Redemption]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

While it is alive  
Until Death touches it  
While it and I lap one Air  
Dwell in one Blood  
Under one Sacrament  
Show me Division can split or pare —  

Love is like Life — merely longer  
Love is like Death, during the Grave  
Love is the Fellow of the Resurrection  
Scooping up the Dust and chanting "Live"!
