## Testo integrale / Full text

If she had been the mistletoe, And I had been the rose, How gay upon your table My velvet life to close! Since I am of the Druid, And she is of the dew, I'll deck tradition's buttonhole, And send the rose to you.

E.

DEAR MR. BOWLES,—I can't thank you any more. You are thoughtful so many times you grieve me always; now the old words are numb, and there aren't any new ones.

Brooks are useless in freshet time. When you come to Amherst—please God it were to-day—I will tell you about the picture—if I can, I will.

Speech is a prank of Parliament, Tears a trick of the nerve,— But the heart with the heaviest freight on Doesn't always swerve.
