*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Crown · Pride · Fortune's Wheel]]

> [!abstract] 
> An aphoristic lyric on inward sovereignty: who holds court within himself sees every man a king, and poverty of monarchy is interior. Treats the self, kingship as metaphor and self-sufficiency.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Who Court obtain within Himself  
Sees every Man a King —  
And Poverty of Monarchy  
Is an interior thing —  

No Man depose  
Whom Fate Ordain —  
And Who can add a Crown  
To Him who doth continual  
Conspire against His Own
