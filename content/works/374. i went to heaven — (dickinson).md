## Testo integrale / Full text

I went to Heaven —  
'Twas a small Town —  
Lit — with a Ruby —  
Lathed — with Down —  

Stiller — than the fields  
At the full Dew —  
Beautiful — as Pictures —  
No Man drew.  
People — like the Moth —  
Of Mechlin — frames —  
Duties — of Gossamer —  
And Eider — names —  
Almost — contented —  
I — could be —  
'Mong such unique  
Society —
