> A famous epigrammatic lyric defining presentiment as the long shadow warning grass that darkness is about to fall. Themes: foreboding, transience, intuition of death, nature as omen.

## Testo integrale / Full text

THE LAWN —  

Presentiment — is that long Shadow — on the Lawn —  
Indicative that Suns go down —  

The Notice to the startled Grass  
That Darkness — is about to pass —
