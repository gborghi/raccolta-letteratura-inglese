> [!abstract] 
> A storm lyric dramatizing thunder, lightning, and a galloping gale, ending in the safe refuge of tombs beyond nature's vengeance; storm, the grave, nature.

## Testo integrale / Full text

The Clouds their Backs together laid  
The North begun to push  
The Forests galloped till they fell  
The Lightning played like mice  

The Thunder crumbled like a stuff  
How good to be in Tombs  
Where Nature's Temper cannot reach  
Nor vengance ever comes
