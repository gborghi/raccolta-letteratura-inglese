> A jubilant lyric of permanent possession, no more vacation, faultless as the rotation of seasons and sun, each dawn first upon God's purple programme. Joins joy, love, dawn and the constancy of grace through seasonal imagery.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Always Mine!  
No more Vacation!  
Term of Light this Day begun!  
Failless as the fair rotation  
Of the Seasons and the Sun.  

Old the Grace, but new the Subjects —  
Old, indeed, the East,  
Yet upon His Purple Programme  
Every Dawn, is first.
