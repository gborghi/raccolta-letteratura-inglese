*by Conan Doyle*  ·  **Cluster:** [[clusters/detective-fiction-·-ratiocination-detection-·-crime-and-punishment|Detective Fiction · Ratiocination Detection · Crime and Punishment]]

> [!abstract] 
> A stage play co-written with William Gillette, combining elements of several Holmes stories into an original plot. Holmes is drawn into the schemes of the criminal Larrabee gang, who hold captive a young woman, Alice Faulkner, possessing compromising letters, while Holmes's old nemesis Professor Moriarty plots his destruction.
