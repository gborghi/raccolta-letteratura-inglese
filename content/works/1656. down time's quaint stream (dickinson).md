> [!abstract] 
> Life as a forced, oarless voyage down Time's stream toward a secret port, no surety from wind or tide; the journey of life toward death.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Down Time's quaint stream  
Without an oar  
We are enforced to sail  
Our Port a secret  
Our Perchance a Gale  
What Skipper would  
Incur the Risk  
What Buccaneer would ride  
Without a surety from the Wind  
Or schedule of the Tide —
