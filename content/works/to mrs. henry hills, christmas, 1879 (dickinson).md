## Testo integrale / Full text

DEAR FRIEND,—I think Heaven will not be as good as earth, unless it bring with it that sweet power to remember, which is the staple of Heaven here.

How can we thank each other when omnipotent?

You, who endear our mortal Christmas, will perhaps assure us.
