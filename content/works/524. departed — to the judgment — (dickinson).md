## Testo integrale / Full text

Departed to the judgment,  
A mighty afternoon ;  
Great clouds like ushers leaning,  
Creation looking on.  

The flesh surrendered, cancelled,  
The bodiless begun ;  
Two worlds, like audiences, disperse  
And leave the soul alone.
