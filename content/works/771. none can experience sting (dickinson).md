*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Paradox · Food and Feasting · Aphorism]]

> [!abstract] 
> A gnomic lyric on definition by contrast: only those who have known bounty can know want, and poverty requires the memory of wealth. Themes: paradox, privation, knowledge through opposites.

## Connections

**Themes & Concepts:** [[Paradox]], [[Poverty]], [[Suffering]], [[Truth]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

None can experience stint  
Who Bounty — have not known —  
The fact of Famine — could not be  
Except for Fact of Corn —  

Want — is a meagre Art  
Acquired by Reverse —  
The Poverty that was not Wealth —  
Cannot be Indigence.
