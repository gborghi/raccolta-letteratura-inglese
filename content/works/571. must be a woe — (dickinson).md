## Testo integrale / Full text

Must be a Woe —  
A loss or so —  
To bend the eye  
Best Beauty's way —  

But — once aslant  
It notes Delight  
As difficult  
As Stalactite  

A Common Bliss  
Were had for less —  
The price — is  
Even as the Grace —  

Our lord — thought no  
Extravagance  
To pay — a Cross —
