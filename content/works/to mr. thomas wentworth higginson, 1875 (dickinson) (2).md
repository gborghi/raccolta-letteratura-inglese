## Testo integrale / Full text

DEAR FRIEND,—I am sorry your brother is dead. I fear he was dear to you. I should be glad to know you were painlessly grieved.

Of Heaven above the firmest proof We fundamental know— Except for its marauding hand It had been heaven below.
