## Testo integrale / Full text

Alone, I cannot be —  
For Hosts — do visit me —  
Recordless Company —  
Who baffle Key —  

They have no Robes, nor Names —  
No Almanacs — nor Climes —  
But general Homes  
Like Gnomes —  

Their Coming, may be known  
By Couriers within —  
Their going — is not —  
For they've never gone —
