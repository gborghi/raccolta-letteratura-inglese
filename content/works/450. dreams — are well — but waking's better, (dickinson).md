*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Dreams · Moon]]

> [!abstract] 
> Weighs dream against waking, surmised dawn against a solid dawn that leads to no day; a meditation on hope, illusion and disappointment.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Dreams — are well — but Waking's better,  
If One wake at morn —  
If One wake at Midnight — better —  
Dreaming — of the Dawn —  

Sweeter — the Surmising Robins —  
Never gladdened Tree —  
Than a Solid Dawn — confronting —  
Leading to no Day —
