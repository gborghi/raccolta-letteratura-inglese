## Testo integrale / Full text

Would it be prudent to subject an apparitional interview to a grosser test?

The Bible portentously says “that which is spirit is spirit.”

Go not too near a house of rose, The depredation of a breeze Or inundation of a dew Alarm its walls away; Nor try to tie the butterfly, Nor climb the bars of ecstasy, In insecurity to lie Is joy's insuring quality.
