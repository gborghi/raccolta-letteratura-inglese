## Testo integrale / Full text

To be forgot by thee  
Surpasses Memory  
Of other minds  
The Heart cannot forget  
Unless it contemplate  
What it declines  
I was regarded then  
Raised from oblivion  
A single time  
To be remembered what —  
Worthy to be forgot  
Is my renown
