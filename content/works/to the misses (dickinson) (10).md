## Testo integrale / Full text

. . . A tone from the old bells, perhaps, might wake the children.

We send the wave to find the wave, An errand so divine The messenger enamored too, Forgetting to return, We make the sage decision still Soever made in vain, The only time to dam the sea Is when the sea is gone.
