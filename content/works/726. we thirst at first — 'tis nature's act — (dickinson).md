> [!abstract] 
> An epigrammatic lyric: natural thirst in life and the dying's last call for water intimate a finer thirst whose only supply is the 'Great Water in the West,' immortality; on mortality and immortality.

## Testo integrale / Full text

We thirst at first — 'tis Nature's Act —  
And later — when we die —  
A little Water supplicate —  
Of fingers going by —  

It intimates the finer want —  
Whose adequate supply  
Is that Great Water in the West —  
Termed Immortality —
