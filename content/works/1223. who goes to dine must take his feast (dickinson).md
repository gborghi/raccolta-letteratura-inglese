## Testo integrale / Full text

Who goes to dine must take his Feast  
Or find the Banquet mean —  
The Table is not laid without  
Till it is laid within.  

For Pattern is the Mind bestowed  
That imitating her  
Our most ignoble Services  
Exhibit worthier.
