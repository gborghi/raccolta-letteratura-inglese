> [!abstract] 
> Eliot's drawing-room comedy after Euripides' Ion, turning on mistaken parentage and disputed identity: Sir Claude, Lady Elizabeth and the clerk Colby discover who is whose lost child, while Colby seeks a true vocation over an inherited one.

| EGGERSON | True. |

| EGGERSON | And so I hope. A most suitable arrangement.<br><br><br>But will you tell me this: if it comes to the point<br>At which Lady Elizabeth wants to adopt him —<br>An admirable solution — then what follows?<br>Will you let her know, then, that Mr. Simpkins<br>Is actually your son? |
