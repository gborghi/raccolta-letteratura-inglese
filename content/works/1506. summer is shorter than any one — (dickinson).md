## Testo integrale / Full text

Summer is shorter than any one —  
Life is shorter than Summer —  
Seventy Years is spent as quick  
As an only Dollar —  

Sorrow — now — is polite — and stays —  
See how well we spurn him —  
Equally to abhor Delight —  
Equally retain him —
