*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Transience · Decay · Carpe Diem]]

> [!abstract] 
> A carpe-diem lyric begging a sumptuous moment to slow, contrasting famine and abundance like a condemned man savoring morning before the gallows — seizing the moment, joy and death.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Oh Sumptuous moment  
Slower go  
That I may gloat on thee —  
'Twill never be the same to starve  
Now I abundance see —  

Which was to famish, then or now —  
The difference of Day  
Ask him unto the Gallows led —  
With morning in the sky —
