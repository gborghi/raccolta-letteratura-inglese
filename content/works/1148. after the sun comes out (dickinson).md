## Testo integrale / Full text

After the Sun comes out  
How it alters the World —  
Waggons like messengers hurry about  
Yesterday is old —  

All men meet as if  
Each foreclosed a news —  
Fresh as a Cargo from Batize  
Nature's qualities —
