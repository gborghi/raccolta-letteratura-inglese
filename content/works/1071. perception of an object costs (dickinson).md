*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Imagination · Fragment · Heroic Couplet]]

> [!abstract] 
> An epistemological lyric: perceiving an object costs its loss, perception itself a gain that idealizes and then upbraids a perfectness set far off.

## Connections

**Themes & Concepts:** [[Perception and Consciousness]], [[Imagination]], [[Paradox]], [[Appearance vs Reality]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

Perception of an object costs  
Precise the Object's loss —  
Perception in itself a Gain  
Replying to its Price —  

The Object Absolute — is nought —  
Perception sets it fair  
And then upbraids a Perfectness  
That situates so far —
