> [!abstract] 
> A sunset rendered as theatrical footlights applauded by the universe, with God distinguished as chief of the crowd. Themes of nature as spectacle, the sublime, and wonder.

## Connections

**Themes & Concepts:** [[Sublime]], [[Wonder and Gratitude]], [[Natural Order]], [[Imagination]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

Like Mighty Foot Lights — burned the Red  
At Bases of the Trees —  
The far Theatricals of Day  
Exhibiting — to These —  

'Twas Universe — that did applaud —  
While Chiefest — of the Crowd —  
Enabled by his Royal Dress —  
Myself distinguished God —
