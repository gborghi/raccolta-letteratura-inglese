*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Mortality · Grave · Memento Mori]]

> [!abstract] 
> A lyric on the drowning man measuring unconsciousness between sea and spar, weighing the terror of failing within a chance against never having had one — a meditation on death, suspense, and risk.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Each Second is the last  
Perhaps, recalls the Man  
Just measuring unconsciousness  
The Sea and Spar between.  

To fail within a Chance —  
How terribler a thing  
Than perish from the Chance's list  
Before the Perishing!
