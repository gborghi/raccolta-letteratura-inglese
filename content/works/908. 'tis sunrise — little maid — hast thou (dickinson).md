> [!abstract] 
> A lyric addressing a 'little Maid' through sunrise, noon, and night who has chosen to die instead of labor — a tender elegy on death, lost industry, and the wish to have dissuaded her.

## Testo integrale / Full text

'Tis Sunrise — Little Maid — Hast Thou  
No Station in the Day?  
'Twas not thy wont, to hinder so —  
Retrieve thine industry —  

'Tis Noon — My little Maid —  
Alas — and art thou sleeping yet?  
The Lily — waiting to be Wed —  
The Bee — Hast thou forgot?  

My little Maid — 'Tis Night — Alas  
That Night should be to thee  
Instead of Morning — Had'st thou broached  
Thy little Plan to Die —  
Dissuade thee, if I could not, Sweet,  
I might have aided — thee —
