> [!abstract] 
> A flower lyric celebrating a blossom prized by bees, butterflies and birds, enamelled by noon, for whom the universe seems made. Treats nature, beauty and flowers as a self-sufficing little world.

## Testo integrale / Full text

This Bauble was preferred of Bees —  
By Butterflies admired  
At Heavenly — Hopeless Distances —  
Was justified of Bird —  

Did Noon — enamel — in Herself  
Was Summer to a Score  
Who only knew of Universe —  
It had created Her.
