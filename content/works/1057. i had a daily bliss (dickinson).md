*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Joy · Animals · Comic Verse Song]]

> [!abstract] 
> A lyric on the paradox of value: a bliss half-ignored grows as it is pursued and only when lost beyond reach is learned to be estimated.

## Connections

**Themes & Concepts:** [[Joy]], [[Paradox]], [[Grief and Loss]], [[Nostalgia]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

I had a daily Bliss  
I half indifferent viewed  
Till sudden I perceived it stir —  
It grew as I pursued  

Till when around a Height  
It wasted from my sight  
Increased beyond my utmost scope  
I learned to estimate.
