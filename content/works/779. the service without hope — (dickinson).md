> [!abstract] 
> A gnomic lyric praising the tenderest service as that done without hope of reward or goal, a diligence that knows no 'Until.' Themes: selfless devotion, hope, disinterested labor, paradox.

## Testo integrale / Full text

The Service without Hope —  
Is tenderest, I think —  
Because 'tis unsustained  
By stint — Rewarded Work —  

Has impetus of Gain —  
And impetus of Goal —  
There is no Diligence like that  
That knows not an Until —
