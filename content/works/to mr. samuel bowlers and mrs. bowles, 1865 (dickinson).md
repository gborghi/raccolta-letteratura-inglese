## Testo integrale / Full text

DEAR FRIEND,—Vinnie accidentally mentions that you hesitated between the Theophilus and the Junius.

Would you confer so sweet a favor as to accept that too, when you come again?

I went to the room as soon as you left, to confirm your presence, recalling the Psalmist's sonnet to God beginning

I have no life but this— To lead it here, Nor any death but lest Dispelled from there. Nor tie to earths to come, Nor action new, Except through this extent— The love of you.

It is strange that the most intangible thing is the most adhesive.
