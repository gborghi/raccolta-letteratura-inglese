## Testo integrale / Full text

Vinnie and her sister thank Miss Whitney for the delicate kindness, and remember her with peculiar love these acuter days. . . .

I fear we think too lightly of the gift of mortality, which, too gigantic to comprehend, certainly cannot be estimated.
