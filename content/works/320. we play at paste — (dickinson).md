## Testo integrale / Full text

We play at Paste —  
Till qualified, for Pearl —  
Then, drop the Paste —  
And deem ourself a fool —  

The Shapes — though — were similar —  
And our new Hands  
Learned Gem-Tactics —  
Practicing Sands —
