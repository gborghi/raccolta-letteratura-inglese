*by Belloc*  ·  **Cluster:** [[clusters/history-·-catholicism-·-europe|History · Catholicism · Europe]]

> [!abstract] 
> The opening volume of Belloc's popular military history of the Great War, tracing the deep political causes (the German drive for supremacy, the Prussian 'Frederician Tradition', the Hapsburg position) and contrasting the opposed forces. It narrates the first operations of 1914 up to early September, using simplified maps and clear outlines rather than exhaustive detail.
