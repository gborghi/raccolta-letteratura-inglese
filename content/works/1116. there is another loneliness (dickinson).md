## Testo integrale / Full text

There is another Loneliness  
That many die without —  
Not want of friend occasions it  
Or circumstances of Lot  

But nature, sometimes, sometimes thought  
And whoso it befall  
Is richer than could be revealed  
By mortal numeral —
