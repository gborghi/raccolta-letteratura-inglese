*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Melancholy · Suffering · Pain and Suffering]]

> [!abstract] 
> A lyric of pain that procures nearness to the immense and boundless; affliction locates the soul in illocality, beyond contentment's quiet suburb. Themes of suffering and the sublime.

## Testo integrale / Full text

A nearness to Tremendousness —  
An Agony procures —  
Affliction ranges Boundlessness —  
Vicinity to Laws  

Contentment's quiet Suburb —  
Affliction cannot stay  
In Acres — Its Location  
Is Illocality —
