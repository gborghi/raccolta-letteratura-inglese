*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Dreams · Moon]]

> [!abstract] 
> A cosmological lyric in which moon and stars (the Star's Etruscan Argument) substantiate a God, yet the knowing astral ones are forgotten as Dawn forgets them.

**Motifs:** [[Moon]], [[Stars]], [[Dawn]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

The Moon upon her fluent Route  
Defiant of a Road —  
The Star's Etruscan Argument  
Substantiate a God —  

If Aims impel these Astral Ones  
The ones allowed to know  
Know that which makes them as forgot  
As Dawn forgets them — now —
