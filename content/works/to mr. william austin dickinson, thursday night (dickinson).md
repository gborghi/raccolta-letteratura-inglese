## Testo integrale / Full text

DEAR AUSTIN,—. . . I have read Ellen Middleton. I needn't tell you I like it, nor need I tell you more, for you know already.

I thank you more and more for all the pleasures you give me—I can give you nothing, Austin, but a warm and grateful heart that is yours now and always. Love from all.
