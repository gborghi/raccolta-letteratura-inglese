## Testo integrale / Full text

The Road to Paradise is plain,  
And holds scarce one.  
Not that it is not firm  
But we presume  
A Dimpled Road  
Is more preferred.  
The Belles of Paradise are few —  
Not me — nor you —  
But unsuspected things —  
Mines have no Wings.
