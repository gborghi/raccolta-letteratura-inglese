> [!abstract] 
> Life too short and the grave too near for hatred; the little toil of love chosen instead — time, death, and the priority of love over enmity.

## Connections

## Testo integrale / Full text

I had no time to Hate —  
Because  
The Grave would hinder Me —  
And Life was not so  
Ample I  
Could finish — Enmity —  

Nor had I time to Love —  
But since  
Some Industry must be —  
The little Toil of Love —  
I thought  
Be large enough for Me —
