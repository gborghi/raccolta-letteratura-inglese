## Testo integrale / Full text

FROM THE many notes sent me by Emily Dickinson during the last four years of her life, it was a difficult matter to select those best fitted for publication. But the first one available was probably this response to a small panel which I had painted for her,—a group of Indian pipes, those weird white flowers of shade and silence:—
