## Testo integrale / Full text

Rehearsal to Ourselves  
Of a Withdrawn Delight —  
Affords a Bliss like Murder —  
Omnipotent — Acute —  

We will not drop the Dirk —  
Because We love the Wound  
The Dirk Commemorate — Itself  
Remind Us that we died.
