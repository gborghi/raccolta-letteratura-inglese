## Testo integrale / Full text

He is alive, this morning —  
He is alive — and awake —  
Birds are resuming for Him —  
Blossoms — dress for His Sake.  
Bees — to their Loaves of Honey  
Add an Amber Crumb  
Him — to regale — Me — Only —  
Motion, and am dumb.
