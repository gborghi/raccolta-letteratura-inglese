## Testo integrale / Full text

. . . He never spoke of himself, and encroachment I know would have slain him. . . . He was a dusk gem, born of troubled waters, astray in any crest below. Heaven might give him peace, it could not give him grandeur, for that he carried with himself to whatever scene. . . .

Obtaining but his own extent In whatsoever realm, 'Twas Christ's own personal expanse That bore him from the tomb.
