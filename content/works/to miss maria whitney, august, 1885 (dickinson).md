## Testo integrale / Full text

DEAR FRIEND,—I did not know.

God bless you indeed!

Extend to that small hand my own “right hand of fellowship,” and guide the woman of your heart softly to my own.

I give “his angels charge”—well-remembered angels, whose absence only dims our eyes. The magnanimity I asked, you how freely gave!

If ever of any act of mine you should be in need, let me reply with the laureate, “Speak that I live to hear.”
