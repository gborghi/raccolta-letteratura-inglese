*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Mortality · Grave · Memento Mori]]

> [!abstract] 
> A lyric on renunciation and death likened to sunset, where surrendering life makes lesser losses trivial; a meditation on grief, transience, and the diminishment of regret.

## Testo integrale / Full text

When One has given up One's life  
The parting with the rest  
Feels easy, as when Day lets go  
Entirely the West  

The Peaks, that lingered last  
Remain in Her regret  
As scarcely as the Iodine  
Upon the Cataract.
