## Testo integrale / Full text

DEAR FRIENDS,—The seraphic shame generosity causes is perhaps its most heavenly result.

To make even Heaven more heavenly, is within the aim of us all.

I was much touched by the little fence dividing the devotions, though devotion should always wear a fence, to pre-empt its claim.

Why the full heart is speechless, is one of the great wherefore.
