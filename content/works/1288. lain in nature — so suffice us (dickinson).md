## Testo integrale / Full text

Lain in Nature — so suffice us  
The enchantless Pod  
When we advertise existence  
For the missing Seed —  

Maddest Heart that God created  
Cannot move a sod  
Pasted by the simple summer  
On the Longed for Dead
