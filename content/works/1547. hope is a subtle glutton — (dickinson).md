*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Paradox · Food and Feasting · Aphorism]]

> [!abstract] 
> An epigrammatic lyric personifying Hope as a glutton who feeds on the Fair yet practices abstinence, dining alone at a Halcyon Table where nothing is diminished.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Hope is a subtle Glutton —  
He feeds upon the Fair —  
And yet — inspected closely  
What Abstinence is there —  

His is the Halcyon Table —  
That never seats but One —  
And whatsoever is consumed  
The same amount remain —
