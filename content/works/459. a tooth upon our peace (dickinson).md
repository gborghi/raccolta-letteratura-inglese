## Testo integrale / Full text

A Tooth upon Our Peace  
The Peace cannot deface —  
Then Wherefore be the Tooth?  
To vitalize the Grace —  

The Heaven hath a Hell —  
Itself to signalize —  
And every sign before the Place  
Is Gilt with Sacrifice —
