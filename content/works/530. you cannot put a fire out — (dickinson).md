*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Sun · Light and Dark · Fire]]

> [!abstract] 
> An aphorism on the irrepressible nature of vital forces — fire and flood — that cannot be contained or hidden away.

## Connections

**Motifs:** [[Fire]], [[Desire]], [[Water]], [[Wind]]
**Themes & Concepts:** [[Natural Order]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

You cannot put a Fire out —  
A Thing that can ignite  
Can go, itself, without a Fan —  
Upon the slowest Night —  

You cannot fold a Flood —  
And put it in a Drawer —  
Because the Winds would find it out —  
And tell your Cedar Floor —
