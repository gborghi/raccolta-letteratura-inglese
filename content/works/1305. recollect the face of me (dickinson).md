## Testo integrale / Full text

Recollect the Face of me  
When in thy Felicity,  
Due in Paradise today  
Guest of mine assuredly —  

Other Courtesies have been —  
Other Courtesy may be —  
We commend ourselves to thee  
Paragon of Chivalry.
