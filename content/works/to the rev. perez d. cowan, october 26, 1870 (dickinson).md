## Testo integrale / Full text

DEAR PETER,—It is indeed sweet news. I am proud of your happiness. To Peter, and Peter's, let me give both hands. Delight has no competitor, so it is always most.

“Maggie” is a warm name.

I shall like to take it.

Home is the definition of God.
