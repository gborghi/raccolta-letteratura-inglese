## Testo integrale / Full text

DEAR FRIEND,—The thoughtfulness was picturesque and the glimpse delightful. The residence of Vinnie's friends could but be fair to me.

And will you, in exchange, accept a view of my house, which Nature painted white without consulting me? But Nature is “old-fashioned,” perhaps a Puritan.
