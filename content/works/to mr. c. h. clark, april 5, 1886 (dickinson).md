## Testo integrale / Full text

DEAR FRIEND,—Are you living and well, and your father in peace, and the home in——Street without effacing change? I received, your very kind message, I think in November, since which I have been very ill, and begin to roam in my room a little, an hour at a time.

Do you, as time steals on, know anything of the W——whom Mr.——so loved, and of whom he said with a smile, “Should he find a gold watch in the street he would not pick it up, so unsullied was he”? . . .

My sister gives her faithful remembrance to yourself and your father, the brother so cherished never once forgot.

You will recall the flower sacred to your brother.

No sloth has memory.
