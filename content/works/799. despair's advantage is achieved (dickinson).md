*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Paradox · Food and Feasting · Aphorism]]

> [!abstract] 
> An aphoristic lyric arguing that the worth of suffering, despair and death is known only by tasting them oneself. Treats despair, affliction and the epistemology of pain.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Despair's advantage is achieved  
By suffering — Despair —  
To be assisted of Reverse  
One must Reverse have bore —  

The Worthiness of Suffering like  
The Worthiness of Death  
Is ascertained by tasting —  

As can no other Mouth  

Of Savors — make us conscious —  
As did ourselves partake —  
Affliction feels impalpable  
Until Ourselves are struck —
