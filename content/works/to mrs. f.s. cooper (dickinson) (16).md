## Testo integrale / Full text

DEAR FRIEND,—It distressed us that you were pained.

Are you easier now?

You have sheltered our tears too often that yours should fall unsolaced.

Give us half the thorn—then it will tear you less. To divulge itself is sorrow's right, never its presumption.
