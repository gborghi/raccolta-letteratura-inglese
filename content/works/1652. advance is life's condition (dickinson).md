*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Mortality · Grave · Memento Mori]]

> [!abstract] 
> The grave is only a relay, not a terminus; an unlit tunnel of continued existence preferable to non-existence. Mortality and immortality.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Advance is Life's condition  
The Grave but a Relay  
Supposed to be a terminus  
That makes it hated so —  

The Tunnel is not lighted  
Existence with a wall  
Is better we consider  
Than not exist at all —
