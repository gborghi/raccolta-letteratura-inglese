## Testo integrale / Full text

Answer July —  
Where is the Bee —  
Where is the Blush —  
Where is the Hay?  

Ah, said July —  
Where is the Seed —  
Where is the Bud —  
Where is the May —  
Answer Thee — Me —  

Nay — said the May —  
Show me the Snow —  
Show me the Bells —  
Show me the Jay!  

Quibbled the Jay —  
Where be the Maize —  
Where be the Haze —  
Where be the Bur?  
Here — said the Year —
