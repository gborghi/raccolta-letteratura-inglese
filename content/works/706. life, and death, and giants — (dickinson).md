*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Death · Silence · Bells]]

> [!abstract] 
> A terse lyric contrasting great abstractions (Life, Death, Giants) that stay silent with small noisy things (a fife, a beetle) that proclaim themselves; on greatness, fame and accidental noise.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Life, and Death, and Giants —  
Such as These — are still —  
Minor — Apparatus — Hopper of the Mill —  
Beetle at the Candle —  
Or a Fife's Fame —  
Maintain — by Accident that they proclaim —
