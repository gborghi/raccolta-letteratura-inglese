> [!abstract] 
> Fame as fickle food on a shifting plate that crows scorn; 'Men eat of it and die' — satire of glory's transience. Sic transit gloria.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Fame is a fickle food  
Upon a shifting plate  
Whose table once a  
Guest but not  
The second time is set.  

Whose crumbs the crows inspect  
And with ironic caw  
Flap past it to the  
Farmer's Corn —  
Men eat of it and die.
