> A reflective lyric on Thanksgiving Day, dissecting the holiday as a reflex of memory and registering loss — the sharp subtraction of those once present at the table. Joins memory, grief and absence within a New England festival.

## Testo integrale / Full text

One Day is there of the Series  
Termed Thanksgiving Day.  
Celebrated part at Table  
Part in Memory.  

Neither Patriarch nor Pussy  
I dissect the Play  
Seems it to my Hooded thinking  
Reflex Holiday.  

Had there been no sharp Subtraction  
From the early Sum —  
Not an Acre or a Caption  
Where was once a Room —  

Not a Mention, whose small Pebble  
Wrinkled any Sea,  
Unto Such, were such Assembly  
'Twere Thanksgiving Day.
