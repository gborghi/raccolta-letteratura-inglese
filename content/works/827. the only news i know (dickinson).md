> [!abstract] 
> A hymn-like lyric declaring that the only news, shows and company the speaker knows are immortality, eternity and God along the street of existence. Joins faith, immortality and the eternal as the whole of experience.

## Testo integrale / Full text

The Only News I know  
Is Bulletins all Day  
From Immortality.  

The Only Shows I see —  
Tomorrow and Today —  
Perchance Eternity —  

The Only One I meet  
Is God — The Only Street —  
Existence — This traversed  

If Other News there be —  
Or Admirable Show —  
I'll tell it You —
