## Testo integrale / Full text

You left me — Sire — two Legacies —  
A Legacy of Love  
A Heavenly Father would suffice  
Had He the offer of —  

You left me Boundaries of Pain —  
Capacious as the Sea —  
Between Eternity and Time —  
Your Consciousness — and Me —
