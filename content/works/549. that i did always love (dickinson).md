## Testo integrale / Full text

That I did always love  
I bring thee Proof  
That till I loved  
I never lived — Enough —  

That I shall love alway —  
I argue thee  
That love is life —  
And life hath Immortality —  

This — dost thou doubt — Sweet —  
Then have I  
Nothing to show  
But Calvary —
