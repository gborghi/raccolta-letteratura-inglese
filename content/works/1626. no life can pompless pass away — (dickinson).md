## Testo integrale / Full text

No Life can pompless pass away —  
The lowliest career  
To the same Pageant wends its way  
As that exalted here —  

How cordial is the mystery!  
The hospitable Pall  
A "this way" beckons spaciously —  
A Miracle for all!
