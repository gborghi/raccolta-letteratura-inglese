> [!abstract] 
> A gnomic lyric contrasting happiness, which lets the least lift a ton, with misery, whose cargo of self is too infinite for slow consciousness to bear. Themes: joy and misery, the weight of suffering, paradox.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Such is the Force of Happiness —  
The Least — can lift a Ton  
Assisted by its stimulus —  

Who Misery — sustain —  
No Sinew can afford —  
The Cargo of Themselves —  
Too infinite for Consciousness'  
Slow capabilities.
