## Testo integrale / Full text

Come show thy Durham Breast  
To her who loves thee best,  
Delicious Robin —  
And if it be not me  
At least within my Tree  
Do the avowing —  
Thy Nuptial so minute  
Perhaps is more astute  
Than vaster suing —  
For so to soar away  
Is our propensity  
The Day ensuing —
