> A found flower (a fallen 'pleiad') likened to a dead face covered by robins; nature, death and the indistinct line between cheek and pall.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Whose cheek is this?  
What rosy face  
Has lost a blush today?  
I found her — "pleiad" — in the woods  
And bore her safe away.  

Robins, in the tradition  
Did cover such with leaves,  
But which the cheek —  
And which the pall  
My scrutiny deceives.
