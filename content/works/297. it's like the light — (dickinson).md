## Testo integrale / Full text

It's like the Light —  
A fashionless Delight —  
It's like the Bee —  
A dateless — Melody —  

It's like the Woods —  
Private — Like the Breeze —  
Phraseless — yet it stirs  
The proudest Trees —  

It's like the Morning —  
Best — when it's done —  
And the Everlasting Clocks —  
Chime — Noon!
