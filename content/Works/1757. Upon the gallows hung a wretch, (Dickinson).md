# 1757. Upon the gallows hung a wretch,

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Death · Silence · Bells]]

> [!abstract] 
> An executed outcast, condemned by law, mourned by his mother; the grim ballad of justice and a mother's love.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Upon the gallows hung a wretch, 
Too sullied for the hell 
To which the law entitled him. 
As nature's curtain fell 
The one who bore him tottered in , — 
For this was woman's son. 
"'Twere all I had," she stricken gasped — 
Oh, what a livid boon!
