# 1159. Great Streets of silence led away

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Death · Silence · Bells]]

> [!abstract] 
> A visionary lyric of a placeless realm of silence beyond time, law, and universe; death, eternity, silence, and the suspension of clocks and epochs.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Great Streets of silence led away 
To Neighborhoods of Pause — 
Here was no Notice — no Dissent 
No Universe — no laws — 
 
By Clocks, 'twas Morning, and for Night 
The Bells at Distance called — 
But Epoch had no basis here 
For Period exhaled.
