*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Mortality · Grave · Memento Mori]]

> [!abstract] 
> A deathbed lyric: as we die honors taste dry and flags vex, but a friend's small ministering kindness (dew, a fan) cools like rain; on mortality, thirst, consolation and tender service.

## Testo integrale / Full text

The World — feels Dusty  
When We stop to Die —  
We want the Dew — then —  
Honors — taste dry —  

Flags — vex a Dying face —  
But the least Fan  
Stirred by a friend's Hand —  
Cools — like the Rain —  

Mine be the Ministry  
When thy Thirst comes —  
And Hybla Balms —  
Dews of Thessaly, to fetch —
