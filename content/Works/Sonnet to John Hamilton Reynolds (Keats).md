# Sonnet to John Hamilton Reynolds

*by Keats*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Transience · Time · Tempus Fugit]]

> [!abstract] 
> A sonnet to his friend Reynolds wishing each week were an age so as to crowd long life with joys and annihilate time; friendship, time and the desire to bind eternal joy.
