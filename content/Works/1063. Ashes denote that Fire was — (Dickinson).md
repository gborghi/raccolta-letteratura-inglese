# 1063. Ashes denote that Fire was —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Mortality · Grave · Memento Mori]]

> [!abstract] 
> An emblematic lyric reading ashes as evidence of a departed fire, reverencing the gray pile for the vanished creature that once burned there.

## Connections

**Topoi:** [[Memento Mori]]
**Motifs:** [[Fire]], [[Dust]]
**Themes & Concepts:** [[Mortality]], [[Memory and Time]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

Ashes denote that Fire was — 
Revere the Grayest Pile 
For the Departed Creature's sake 
That hovered there awhile — 
 
Fire exists the first in light 
And then consolidates 
Only the Chemist can disclose 
Into what Carbonates.
