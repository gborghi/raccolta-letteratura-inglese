# EPITAPHIUM TESTAMENTARIUM

*by Coleridge*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Death · Mortality · Immortality]]

> [!abstract] 
> A self-epitaph giving his sins to Death and the rest to Christ, with a coda on how the heart reveals love it would conceal and absence of love it would show. Themes: mortality, redemption, the concealed heart.
