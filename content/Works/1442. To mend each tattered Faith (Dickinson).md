# 1442. To mend each tattered Faith

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Faith · Providence · Divine Love]]

> [!abstract] 
> Short lyric on repairing faith with an invisible, air-threaded needle; themes of faith, doubt and spiritual mending.

## Connections

**Themes & Concepts:** [[Faith]], [[Faith and Doubt]], [[Grace and Redemption]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

To mend each tattered Faith 
There is a needle fair 
Though no appearance indicate — 
'Tis threaded in the Air — 
 
And though it do not wear 
As if it never Tore 
'Tis very comfortable indeed 
And spacious as before —
