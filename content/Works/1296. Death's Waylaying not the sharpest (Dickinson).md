# 1296. Death's Waylaying not the sharpest

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Death · Silence · Bells]]

> [!abstract] 
> Death is not Time's sharpest theft; Silence is a sorer robber who supplants the balm of life's consummate cluster.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Death's Waylaying not the sharpest 
Of the thefts of Time — 
There Marauds a sorer Robber, 
Silence — is his name — 
No Assault, nor any Menace 
Doth betoken him. 
But from Life's consummate Cluster — 
He supplants the Balm.
