# 626. Only God — detect the Sorrow —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Faith · Providence · Divine Love]]

> [!abstract] 
> A terse lyric: only the Trinity perceives and keeps secret the speaker's sorrow. Themes: grief, divine confidence, the soul. Form: lyric.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Only God — detect the Sorrow — 
Only God — 
The Jehovahs — are no Babblers — 
Unto God — 
God the Son — Confide it — 
Still secure — 
God the Spirit's Honor — 
Just as sure —
