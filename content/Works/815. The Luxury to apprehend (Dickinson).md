*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Rose · Desire · Value and Scarcity]]

> [!abstract] 
> A love lyric figuring the beloved's countenance as banquet and sumptuous food, the consciousness of 'Thee' the single crumb that feeds plainer days. Treats romantic love, desire and longing through imagery of feasting.

## Testo integrale / Full text

The Luxury to apprehend  
The Luxury 'twould be  
To look at Thee a single time  
An Epicure of Me  

In whatsoever Presence makes  
Till for a further Food  
I scarcely recollect to starve  
So first am I supplied —  

The Luxury to meditate  
The Luxury it was  
To banguet on thy Countenance  
A Sumptuousness bestows  

On plainer Days, whose Table far  
As Certainty can see  
Is laden with a single Crumb  
The Consciousness of Thee.
