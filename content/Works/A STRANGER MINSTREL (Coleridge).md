# A STRANGER MINSTREL

*by Coleridge*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Wonder and Gratitude · The Sublime · Sun]]

> [!abstract] 
> A lyric written to the dying Mrs. Robinson, in which the poet on Skiddaw wishes she were present; the personified mountain converses with him, praising her free, immortal poetic spirit.
