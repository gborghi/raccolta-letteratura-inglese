# 846. Twice had Summer her fair Verdure

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Seasons · Winter · Autumn]]

> [!abstract] 
> An elegiac lyric marking two years of summers, winters and autumns to reproach Nature for having no berry for her wandering bird. Joins seasons, nature, grief and the passage of time.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Twice had Summer her fair Verdure 
Proffered to the Plain — 
Twice a Winter's silver Fracture 
On the Rivers been — 
 
Two full Autumns for the Squirrel 
Bounteous prepared — 
Nature, Had'st thou not a Berry 
For thy wandering Bird?
