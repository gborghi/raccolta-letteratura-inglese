# 846. Twice had Summer her fair Verdure

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Seasons · Winter · Spring]]

> [!abstract] 
> An elegiac lyric marking two years of summers, winters and autumns to reproach Nature for having no berry for her wandering bird. Joins seasons, nature, grief and the passage of time.
