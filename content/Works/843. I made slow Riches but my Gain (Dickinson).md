# 843. I made slow Riches but my Gain

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Maturation and Growth · Childhood · Innocence and Experience]]

> [!abstract] 
> An aphoristic lyric on slow, steady riches that increase nightly like the sun, the imperceptible gain inferred less by growing than by the sum it had grown. Treats patience, growth and accumulation through economic metaphor.
