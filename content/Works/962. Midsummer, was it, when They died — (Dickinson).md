# 962. Midsummer, was it, when They died —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Grief and Loss · Elegy · Clothing and Costume]]

> [!abstract] 
> An elegy figuring the dead as a perfected summer harvest, ripened to fullness before the flail of death; merges seasons, fruition, and burial.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Midsummer, was it, when They died — 
A full, and perfect time — 
The Summer closed upon itself 
In Consummated Bloom — 
 
The Corn, her furthest kernel filled 
Before the coming Flail — 
When These — leaned unto Perfectness — 
Through Haze of Burial —
