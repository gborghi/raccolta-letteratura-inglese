# 1256. Not any higher stands the Grave

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Mortality · Grave · Memento Mori]]

> [!abstract] 
> The grave levels hero and man, beggar and queen; death as the great democrat, memento mori and the equality of mortality.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Not any higher stands the Grave 
For Heroes than for Men — 
Not any nearer for the Child 
Than numb Three Score and Ten — 
 
This latest Leisure equal lulls 
The Beggar and his Queen 
Propitiate this Democrat 
A Summer's Afternoon —
