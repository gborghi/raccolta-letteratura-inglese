# white-horse2

*by Chesterton*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Swords and Weapons · The Battlefield · Horse]]

> [!abstract] 
> Chesterton's long narrative ballad-epic on King Alfred's stand against the heathen Danes at Ethandune, framed as legend rather than history; it celebrates Christian civilization, hope against odds, and the last stand of the West.
