# 990. Not all die early, dying young —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Mortality · Grave · Memento Mori]]

> [!abstract] 
> A meditative lyric on the maturity of fate, consummated in a night or in years; it is the act not the period that dies. Themes of mortality, time, and fate.

## Connections

**Motifs:** [[Time]], [[Death]]
**Themes & Concepts:** [[Mortality]], [[Fate and Free Will]], [[Paradox]]
**Forms & Genres:** [[Lyric]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

Not all die early, dying young — 
Maturity of Fate 
Is consummated equally 
In Ages, or a Night — 
 
A Hoary Boy, I've known to drop 
Whole statured — by the side 
Of Junior of Fourscore — 'twas Act 
Not Period — that died.
