# 1657. Eden is that old-fashioned House

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Bible · Sin and the Fall · Fall from Innocence]]

> [!abstract] 
> Paradise is the ordinary home we inhabit unawares until we leave it irrecoverably; nostalgia, the fall from innocence, paradise lost.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Eden is that old-fashioned House 
We dwell in every day 
Without suspecting our abode 
Until we drive away. 
 
How fair on looking back, the Day 
We sauntered from the Door — 
Unconscious our returning, 
But discover it no more.
