# 467. We do not play on Graves —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Maturation and Growth · Childhood · Innocence and Experience]]

> [!abstract] 
> A child's perspective on graves and mourners, avoiding the slanted ground where grief might crush their play; childhood facing death.

## Testo integrale / Full text

We do not play on Graves — 
Because there isn't Room — 
Besides — it isn't even — it slants 
And People come — 
 
And put a Flower on it — 
And hang their faces so — 
We're fearing that their Hearts will drop — 
And crush our pretty play — 
 
And so we move as far 
As Enemies — away — 
Just looking round to see how far 
It is — Occasionally —
