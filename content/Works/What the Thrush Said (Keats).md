# What the Thrush Said

*by Keats*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Seasons · Winter · Spring]]

> [!abstract] 
> An unrhymed sonnet-letter spoken as the thrush's counsel, urging trust in instinctive song over anxious pursuit of knowledge, set against winter passing into spring. Central to it are negative capability, the seasons, and the value of receptive idleness.
