# 856. There is a finished feeling

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Mortality · Grave · Memento Mori]]

> [!abstract] 
> A graveyard lyric on the strange completed feeling experienced at graves, where death's 'bold Exhibition' clarifies what we are and lets us infer the eternal — mortality and immortality conjoined.

## Testo integrale / Full text

There is a finished feeling 
Experienced at Graves — 
A leisure of the Future — 
A Wilderness of Size. 
 
By Death's bold Exhibition 
Preciser what we are 
And the Eternal function 
Enabled to infer.
