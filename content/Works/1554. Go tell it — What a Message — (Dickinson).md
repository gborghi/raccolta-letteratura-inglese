# 1554. "Go tell it" — What a Message —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Heroism · Hero · Last Stand]]

> [!abstract] 
> A lyric on dutiful obedience unto death, invoking the Spartans at Thermopylae who gave their dying kiss to Law rather than to longing or endearment.

## Testo integrale / Full text

"Go tell it" — What a Message — 
To whom — is specified — 
Not murmur — not endearment — 
But simply — we — obeyed — 
Obeyed — a Lure — a Longing? 
Oh Nature — none of this — 
To Law — said sweet Thermopylae 
I give my dying Kiss —
