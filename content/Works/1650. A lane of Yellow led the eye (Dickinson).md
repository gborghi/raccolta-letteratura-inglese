# 1650. A lane of Yellow led the eye

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Nature · Book of Nature · Natural Order]]

> [!abstract] 
> A painterly sunset of yellow and purple wood whose hidden inhabitants and the truth of the scene remain impossible to know; nature and the unknowable.

## Testo integrale / Full text

A lane of Yellow led the eye 
Unto a Purple Wood 
Whose soft inhabitants to be 
Surpasses solitude 
If Bird the silence contradict 
Or flower presume to show 
In that low summer of the West 
Impossible to know —
