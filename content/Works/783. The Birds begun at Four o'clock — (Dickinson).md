# 783. The Birds begun at Four o'clock —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Wonder and Gratitude · The Sublime · Sun]]

> [!abstract] 
> A dawn lyric on the immense flood of birdsong at four o'clock, an independent ecstasy of Deity and Men that ends by six, the miracle forgotten as fulfilled. Themes: dawn, birdsong, nature's ecstasy, the sacred ordinary.
