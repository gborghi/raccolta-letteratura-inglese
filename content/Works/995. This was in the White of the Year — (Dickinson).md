# 995. This was in the White of the Year —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Memory and Time · Memory · Nostalgia]]

> [!abstract] 
> A lyric contrasting the white (winter) and green (summer) of the year and the difficulty of imagining absent seasons; retrospection becomes half of prospect. Themes of seasons, memory, and time.

**Motifs:** [[Seasons]], [[Winter]], [[Flowers]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

This was in the White of the Year — 
That — was in the Green — 
Drifts were as difficult then to think 
As Daisies now to be seen — 
 
Looking back is best that is left 
Or if it be — before — 
Retrospection is Prospect's half, 
Sometimes, almost more.
