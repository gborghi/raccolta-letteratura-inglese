# Bright star! would I were steadfast as thou art

*by Keats*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Death · Mortality · Immortality]]

> [!abstract] 
> A sonnet longing for the steadfastness of a star, but redirected to eternal repose on the beloved's breast; love, constancy, mortality and the wish to live forever or swoon to death.
