# A Farewell to Arms

*by Hemingway*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Nationalism and Patriotism · The First World War · Flags and Banners]]

> [!abstract] 
> Hemingway's 1929 war novel: Frederic Henry, an American ambulance officer on the Italian front in WWI, deserts after the retreat from Caporetto and flees with Catherine Barkley to Switzerland, where she dies in childbirth — love and disillusionment set against the futility of war.
