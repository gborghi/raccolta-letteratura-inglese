*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Grief and Loss · Melancholy · Elegy]]

> [!abstract] 
> An elegy in which the sun is superfluous once excellence is dead, faith just saving the speaker from despair as stars drop anonymous from an abundant sky. Themes of grief, faith, and immortality of fame.
