# 999. Superfluous were the Sun

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Grief and Loss · Elegy · Clothing and Costume]]

> [!abstract] 
> An elegy in which the sun is superfluous once excellence is dead, faith just saving the speaker from despair as stars drop anonymous from an abundant sky. Themes of grief, faith, and immortality of fame.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Superfluous were the Sun 
When Excellence be dead 
He were superfluous every Day 
For every Day be said 
 
That syllable whose Faith 
Just saves it from Despair 
And whose "I'll meet You" hesitates 
If Love inquire "Where"? 
 
Upon His dateless Fame 
Our Periods may lie 
As Stars that drop anonymous 
From an abundant sky.
