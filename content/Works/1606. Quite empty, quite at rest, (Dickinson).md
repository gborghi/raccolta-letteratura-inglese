# 1606. Quite empty, quite at rest,

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Birds · Music and Song · Nightingale]]

> [!abstract] 
> A robin, nest emptied of lost young, sets out homeless on faith for 'rumored Springs'; bereavement and resilient hope in nature.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Quite empty, quite at rest, 
The Robin locks her Nest, and tries her Wings. 
She does not know a Route 
But puts her Craft about 
For rumored Springs — 
She does not ask for Noon — 
She does not ask for Boon, 
Crumbless and homeless, of but one request — 
The Birds she lost —
