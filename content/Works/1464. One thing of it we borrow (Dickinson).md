# 1464. One thing of it we borrow

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Memory and Time · Memory · Nostalgia]]

> [!abstract] 
> Experience borrowed and returned, its sweetness paid for by sorrow; the coveted power to forget and the avarice of memory.

## Connections

**Motifs:** [[Memory]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

One thing of it we borrow 
And promise to return — 
The Booty and the Sorrow 
Its Sweetness to have known — 
One thing of it we covet — 
The power to forget — 
The Anguish of the Avarice 
Defrays the Dross of it —
