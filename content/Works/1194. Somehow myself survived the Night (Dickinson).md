# 1194. Somehow myself survived the Night

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Stars · Night · Military Camp]]

> [!abstract] 
> A lyric on barely surviving a night of anguish, entering day as one commuted yet 'dated with the Dead'; suffering, survival, death, night and dawn.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Somehow myself survived the Night 
And entered with the Day — 
That it be saved the Saved suffice 
Without the Formula. 
 
Henceforth I take my living place 
As one commuted led — 
A Candidate for Morning Chance 
But dated with the Dead.
