# 121. As Watchers hang upon the East,

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Faith and Doubt · Doubt · Allegory]]

> [!abstract] 
> Heaven figured through watchers awaiting dawn, beggars at a fancied feast, and brooks in deserts; deferred longing for bliss whose reality is uncertain ('Heaven to us, if true').
