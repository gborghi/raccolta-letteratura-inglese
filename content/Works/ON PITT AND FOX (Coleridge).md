# ON PITT AND FOX

*by Coleridge*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Satire of Society · Epigram · Satire]]

> [!abstract] 
> Political epigrams on the deaths of statesmen Pitt and Fox, sardonically praising the dead, plus an adage that of the dead one says only good; satire of politics and the convention of speaking well of the dead.
