> [!abstract] 
> A meditative lyric on the days we squander, stranded when a function, friend, or nature dies, letting go of time without reckoning; time, loss, transience.

## Testo integrale / Full text

The Days that we can spare  
Are those a Function die  
Or Friend or Nature — stranded then  
In our Economy  

Our Estimates a Scheme —  
Our Ultimates a Sham —  
We let go all of Time without  
Arithmetic of him —
