> [!abstract] 
> A lyric of irreplaceable loss: the speaker's one unbuyable treasure went out the unshut door, leaving her alone and longing only to look it in the eye. Treats grief, loss, longing and the priceless.

## Testo integrale / Full text

I cannot buy it — 'tis not sold —  
There is no other in the World —  
Mine was the only one  

I was so happy I forgot  
To shut the Door And it went out  
And I am all alone —  

If I could find it Anywhere  
I would not mind the journey there  
Though it took all my store  

But just to look it in the Eye —  
"Did'st thou?" "Thou did'st not mean," to say,  
Then, turn my Face away.
