# 997. Crumbling is not an instant's Act

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Transience · Decay · Carpe Diem]]

> [!abstract] 
> A lyric anatomizing ruin as a slow, organized decay of the soul, beginning with a cobweb and rust; the devil's consecutive work. Themes of decay, sin, and mutability.

## Connections

**Motifs:** [[Decay]], [[Mutability]], [[Dust]]
**Themes & Concepts:** [[Self-Knowledge]], [[Transience]]
**Forms & Genres:** [[Lyric]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

Crumbling is not an instant's Act 
A fundamental pause 
Dilapidation's processes 
Are organized Decays. 
 
'Tis first a Cobweb on the Soul 
A Cuticle of Dust 
A Borer in the Axis 
An Elemental Rust — 
 
Ruin is formal — Devil's work 
Consecutive and slow — 
Fail in an instant, no man did 
Slipping — is Crash's law.
