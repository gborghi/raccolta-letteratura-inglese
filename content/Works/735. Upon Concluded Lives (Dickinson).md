# 735. Upon Concluded Lives

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Death · Mortality · Immortality]]

> [!abstract] 
> A lyric on how nothing is colder to the dying than life's sweet calculations, with bells and palls making a lacerating tune both 'Coronal and Funeral'; on mortality and the contrast of life's sweetness against death.
