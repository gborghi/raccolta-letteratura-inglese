# 453. Love — thou art high —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Lyric · Romantic Love · Marriage and Fidelity]]

> [!abstract] 
> Love addressed as a high mountain, deep sea, and veiled mystery reaching toward eternity; love as a divine, near-unattainable sublime.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Love — thou art high — 
I cannot climb thee — 
But, were it Two — 
Who knows but we — 
Taking turns — at the Chimborazo — 
Ducal — at last — stand up by thee — 
 
Love — thou are deep — 
I cannot cross thee — 
But, were there Two 
Instead of One — 
Rower, and Yacht — some sovereign Summer — 
Who knows — but we'd reach the Sun? 
 
Love — thou are Veiled — 
A few — behold thee — 
Smile — and alter — and prattle — and die — 
Bliss — were an Oddity — without thee — 
Nicknamed by God — 
Eternity —
