# 731. "I want" — it pleaded — All its life —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Mortality · Grave · Memento Mori]]

> [!abstract] 
> A deathbed lyric on a soul that pleaded 'I want' all its life, its last steadfast sigh placed like a 'Please' toward eternity; on desire, death and longing.

## Connections

**Motifs:** [[Death]]
**Themes & Concepts:** [[Desire (2)]], [[Mortality]], [[Grief and Loss]], [[Immortality]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

"I want" — it pleaded — All its life — 
I want — was chief it said 
When Skill entreated it — the last — 
And when so newly dead — 
 
I could not deem it late — to hear 
That single — steadfast sigh — 
The lips had placed as with a "Please" 
Toward Eternity —
