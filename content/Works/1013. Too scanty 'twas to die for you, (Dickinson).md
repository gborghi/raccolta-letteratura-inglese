# 1013. Too scanty 'twas to die for you,

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Romantic Love · The Lover · Unrequited Frustrated Love]]

> [!abstract] 
> A love lyric arguing that to live for the beloved is costlier than to die, since living includes dying multifold without the respite of death; merges love, sacrifice, and mortality.
