# 1147. After a hundred years

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Mortality · Grave · Memento Mori]]

> [!abstract] 
> A graveyard lyric on oblivion: after a century a grave's agony is forgotten, weeds and strangers obscure it; transience, memory, the forgotten dead.

## Testo integrale / Full text

After a hundred years 
Nobody knows the Place 
Agony that enacted there 
Motionless as Peace 
 
Weeds triumphant ranged 
Strangers strolled and spelled 
At the lone Orthography 
Of the Elder Dead 
 
Winds of Summer Fields 
Recollect the way — 
Instinct picking up the Key 
Dropped by memory —
