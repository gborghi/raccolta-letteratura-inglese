*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Melancholy · Suffering · Pain and Suffering]]

> [!abstract] 
> An epigrammatic lyric arguing suspense is crueler than death because it never concludes, dying and reviving endlessly; meditates on mortality, immortality and psychic anguish.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Suspense — is Hostiler than Death —  
Death — tho'soever Broad,  
Is Just Death, and cannot increase —  
Suspense — does not conclude —  

But perishes — to live anew —  
But just anew to die —  
Annihilation — plated fresh  
With Immortality —
