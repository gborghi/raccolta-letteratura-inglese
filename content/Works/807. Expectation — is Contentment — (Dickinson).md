# 807. Expectation — is Contentment —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Paradox · Food and Feasting · Aphorism]]

> [!abstract] 
> An aphoristic lyric preferring expectation over satiety, since secure fortune is austere and danger deepens the sum of pleasure. Treats desire, anticipation and the paradox of fulfilment.

## Connections

**Themes & Concepts:** [[Desire (2)]], [[Paradox]], [[Patience and Waiting]], [[Joy]]
**Forms & Genres:** [[Aphorism]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

Expectation — is Contentment — 
Gain — Satiety — 
But Satiety — Conviction 
Of Necessity 
 
Of an Austere trait in Pleasure — 
Good, without alarm 
Is a too established Fortune — 
Danger — deepens Sum —
