# 1646. Why should we hurry — why indeed?

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Self-Knowledge · Immortality · Self-Reliance]]

> [!abstract] 
> No escape from the molesting inference of immortality; uncertainty about life's begun labors besets sight in the 'mighty night.'

## Testo integrale / Full text

Why should we hurry — why indeed? 
When every way we fly 
We are molested equally 
By immortality. 
No respite from the inference 
That this which is begun, 
Though where its labors lie 
A bland uncertainty 
Besets the sight 
This mighty night —
