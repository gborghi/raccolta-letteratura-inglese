# THE TWO FOUNTS

*by Coleridge*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Sonnet · Beauty · Shakespearean Sonnet]]

> [!abstract] 
> Stanzas to a suffering lady whose beauty dispenses only cheer; a dream-dwarf explains the two inner founts of Suffering and Cheer since Adam's sin. Themes: suffering, beauty, the fall, compassion.
