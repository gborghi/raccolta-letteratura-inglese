# The Sun Also Rises

*by Hemingway*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Disillusionment · Odysseus Ulysses · Grace Under Pressure Stoic Endurance]]

> [!abstract] 
> Hemingway's 1926 modernist novel of the post-WWI 'Lost Generation': expatriate Americans and Britons drinking through Paris and the Pamplona bullfight fiesta, narrated by the war-wounded, impotent Jake Barnes, whose hopeless love for Brett Ashley dramatizes disillusionment, aimlessness, and spiritual emptiness rendered in terse, understated prose.
