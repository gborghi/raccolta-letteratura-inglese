# 742. Four Trees — upon a solitary Acre —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Nature · Book of Nature · Natural Order]]

> [!abstract] 
> A stark lyric on four trees standing without apparent design or order, their deed to general nature unknown; a meditation on nature, purpose, randomness and the inscrutability of the natural order.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Four Trees — upon a solitary Acre — 
Without Design 
Or Order, or Apparent Action — 
Maintain — 
 
The Sun — upon a Morning meets them — 
The Wind — 
No nearer Neighbor — have they — 
But God — 
 
The Acre gives them — Place — 
They — Him — Attention of Passer by — 
Of Shadow, or of Squirrel, haply — 
Or Boy — 
 
What Deed is Theirs unto the General Nature — 
What Plan 
They severally — retard — or further — 
Unknown —
