# 1195. What we see we know somewhat

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Bible · Sin and the Fall · Fall from Innocence]]

> [!abstract] 
> An epistemological lyric on the partial reach of knowledge and surmise, voting for locked lands and the doubtful dividend patented by Adam; knowledge, the fall.

## Testo integrale / Full text

What we see we know somewhat 
Be it but a little — 
What we don't surmise we do 
Though it shows so fickle 
 
I shall vote for Lands with Locks 
Granted I can pick 'em — 
Transport's doubtful Dividend 
Patented by Adam.
