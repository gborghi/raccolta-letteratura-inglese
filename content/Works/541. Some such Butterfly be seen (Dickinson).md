# 541. Some such Butterfly be seen

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Transience · Decay · Carpe Diem]]

> [!abstract] 
> Brief noon-licensed beauty of butterfly and spice, transient and seizable only for a moment, like stars turned foreign by morning.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Some such Butterfly be seen 
On Brazilian Pampas — 
Just at noon — no later — Sweet — 
Then — the License closes — 
 
Some such Spice — express and pass — 
Subject to Your Plucking — 
As the Stars — You knew last Night — 
Foreigners — This Morning —
