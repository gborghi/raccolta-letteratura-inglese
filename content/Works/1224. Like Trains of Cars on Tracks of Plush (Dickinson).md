# 1224. Like Trains of Cars on Tracks of Plush

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Sonnet · Shakespearean Sonnet · Jealousy]]

> [!abstract] 
> A bee assaulting flowers rendered as chivalric, victorious knight; nature, love as war, and playful conquest of blooms.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Like Trains of Cars on Tracks of Plush 
I hear the level Bee — 
A Jar across the Flowers goes 
Their Velvet Masonry — 
 
Withstands until the sweet Assault 
Their Chivalry consumes — 
While He, victorious tilts away 
To vanquish other Blooms.
