# 717. The Beggar Lad — dies early —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Money and Gold · Marriage and Fidelity · Social Injustice]]

> [!abstract] 
> A lyric of social protest and pity: a beggar boy dies of cold and hunger ignored by the 'Cruel — smiling — bowing World,' then is received among the redeemed; on poverty, social injustice, death and grace.
