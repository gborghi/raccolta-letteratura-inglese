# 1171. On the World you colored

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Sun · Light and Dark · Fire]]

> [!abstract] 
> A grief lyric in which a beloved's wrinkled finger shoves away the rosy dawn, plunging the world's day into midnight's awful pattern; loss, light and dark.

## Testo integrale / Full text

On the World you colored 
Morning painted rose — 
Idle his Vermillion 
Aimless crept the Glows 
Over Realms of Orchards 
I the Day before 
Conquered with the Robin — 
Misery - how fair 
Till your wrinkled Finger 
Shoved the sun away 
Midnight's awful Pattern 
In the Goods of Day —
