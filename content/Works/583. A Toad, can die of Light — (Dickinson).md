# 583. A Toad, can die of Light —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Mortality · Grave · Memento Mori]]

> [!abstract] 
> A leveling epigram: death is the common right of toad and man, earl and midge, so why swagger? Life alone differs in worth. Themes of mortality, the leveling of death, and humility.

## Connections

**Themes & Concepts:** [[Mortality]], [[Humility]], [[Paradox]], [[Self-Knowledge]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

A Toad, can die of Light — 
Death is the Common Right 
Of Toads and Men — 
Of Earl and Midge 
The privilege — 
Why swagger, then? 
The Gnat's supremacy is large as Thine — 
 
Life — is a different Thing — 
So measure Wine — 
Naked of Flask — Naked of Cask — 
Bare Rhine — 
Which Ruby's mine?
