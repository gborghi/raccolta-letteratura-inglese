# ON MY JOYFUL DEPARTURE FROM THE SAME CITY

*by Coleridge*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Satire of Society · Epigram · Satire]]

> [!abstract] 
> A comic epigram companion-piece, joyfully leaving the 'body-and-soul-stinking town of Cologne', wine and a church the only redeeming things. Themes: satire, the city, comedy.

## Connections

**Motifs:** [[City]], [[Wine and Drink]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

As I am a Rhymer,
And now at least a merry one,
Mr. Mum’s Rudesheimer
And the church of St. Geryon
Are the two things alone   5
That deserve to be known
In the body-and-soul-stinking town of Cologne.
1828.
