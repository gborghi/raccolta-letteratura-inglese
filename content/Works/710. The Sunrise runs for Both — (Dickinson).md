> A lyric in which sunrise, noon, night and stars equally serve two separated lovers across hemispheres; cosmic nature imagery as a figure for love, distance and union.

## Testo integrale / Full text

The Sunrise runs for Both —  
The East — Her Purple Troth  
Keeps with the Hill —  
The Noon unwinds Her Blue  
Till One Breadth cover Two —  
Remotest — still —  

Nor does the Night forget  
A Lamp for Each — to set —  
Wicks wide away —  
The North — Her blazing Sign  
Erects in Iodine —  
Till Both — can see —  

The Midnight's Dusky Arms  
Clasp Hemispheres, and Homes  
And so  
Upon Her Bosom — One —  
And One upon Her Hem —  
Both lie —
