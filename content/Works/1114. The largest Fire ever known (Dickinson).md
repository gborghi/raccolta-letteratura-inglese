# 1114. The largest Fire ever known

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Sun · Light and Dark · Fire]]

> [!abstract] 
> A lyric figuring the daily sunset as the largest fire, consuming an Occidental Town rebuilt and burned each morning — the sun, cyclical recurrence and transience.

## Connections

**Topoi:** [[Eternal Return]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

The largest Fire ever known 
Occurs each Afternoon — 
Discovered is without surprise 
Proceeds without concern — 
Consumes and no report to men 
An Occidental Town, 
Rebuilt another morning 
To be again burned down.
