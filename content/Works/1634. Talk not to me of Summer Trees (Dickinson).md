# 1634. Talk not to me of Summer Trees

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Imagination · Fragment · Heroic Couplet]]

> [!abstract] 
> The mind's inner foliage as a tabernacle for incorporeal birds; imagination and the spiritual realms beyond physical nature.

## Connections

**Topoi:** [[Book of Nature]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

Talk not to me of Summer Trees 
The foliage of the mind 
A Tabernacle is for Birds 
Of no corporeal kind 
And winds do go that way at noon 
To their Ethereal Homes 
Whose Bugles call the least of us 
To undepicted Realms
