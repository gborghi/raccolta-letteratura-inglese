# 94. Angels, in the early morning

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Lyric · Flowers · Stars]]

> [!abstract] 
> A short lyric in which angels are seen gathering dew and flowers at dawn and noon; nature is read as a sacrament tended by heavenly figures, with flowers parching by day.
