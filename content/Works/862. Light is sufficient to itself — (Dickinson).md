# 862. Light is sufficient to itself —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Sun · Light and Dark · Fire]]

> [!abstract] 
> A lyric on light's self-sufficiency and impartial generosity — its glow as large to a squirrel in the Himalaya as to us; a meditation on nature, grace, and disinterested abundance.

## Connections

**Motifs:** [[Light and Dark]], [[Window]], [[Animals]]
**Themes & Concepts:** [[Self-Reliance]], [[Grace and Redemption]]
**Forms & Genres:** [[Lyric]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

Light is sufficient to itself — 
If Others want to see 
It can be had on Window Panes 
Some Hours in the Day. 
 
But not for Compensation — 
It holds as large a Glow 
To Squirrel in the Himmaleh 
Precisely, as to you.
