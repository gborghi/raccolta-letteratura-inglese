# 896. Of Silken Speech and Specious Shoe

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Satire of Society · Epigram · Satire]]

> [!abstract] 
> A satiric lyric portraying the bee as a smooth-tongued traitor whose courtship is fickle, pronouncing continual divorce — a meditation on inconstancy, deception, and faithless love.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Of Silken Speech and Specious Shoe 
A Traitor is the Bee 
His service to the newest Grace 
Present continually 
 
His Suit a chance 
His Troth a Term 
Protracted as the Breeze 
Continual Ban propoundeth He 
Continual Divorce.
