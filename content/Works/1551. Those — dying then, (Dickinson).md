# 1551. Those — dying then,

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Faith and Doubt · Doubt · Allegory]]

> [!abstract] 
> A lyric on the loss of faith: the dying once knew their destination, but now God's Right Hand is amputated; better an ignis fatuus than no illumination.
