# 1551. Those — dying then,

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Christianity · Faith and Doubt · Doubt]]

> [!abstract] 
> A lyric on the loss of faith: the dying once knew their destination, but now God's Right Hand is amputated; better an ignis fatuus than no illumination.

## Connections

**Motifs:** [[Death]]
**Themes & Concepts:** [[Faith and Doubt]], [[Spiritual Emptiness]], [[Doubt]], [[Disillusionment]]
**Forms & Genres:** [[Epigram]], [[Lyric]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

Those — dying then, 
Knew where they went — 
They went to God's Right Hand — 
That Hand is amputated now 
And God cannot be found — 
 
The abdication of Belief 
Makes the Behavior small — 
Better an ignis fatuus 
Than no illume at all —
