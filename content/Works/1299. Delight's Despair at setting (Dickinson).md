# 1299. Delight's Despair at setting

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Melancholy · Suffering · Pain and Suffering]]

> [!abstract] 
> Delight at its setting despairs because it is less than the longing that craved it; the enchantment of anticipation over fruition.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Delight's Despair at setting 
Is that Delight is less 
Than the sufficing Longing 
That so impoverish. 
 
Enchantment's Perihelion 
Mistaken oft has been 
For the Authentic orbit 
Of its Anterior Sun.
