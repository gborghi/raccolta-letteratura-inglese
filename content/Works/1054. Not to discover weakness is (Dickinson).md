# 1054. Not to discover weakness is

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Paradox · Food and Feasting · Aphorism]]

> [!abstract] 
> An aphoristic lyric on strength as the artifice of concealing weakness, impregnability resting on faith and pyramidal nerve like a hidden clockwork.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Not to discover weakness is 
The Artifice of strength — 
Impregnability inheres 
As much through Consciousness 
 
Of faith of others in itself 
As Pyramidal Nerve 
Behind the most unconscious clock 
What skilful Pointers move —
