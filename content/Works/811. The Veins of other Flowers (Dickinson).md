# 811. The Veins of other Flowers

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Lyric · Flowers · Stars]]

> [!abstract] 
> A brief nature lyric on Nature's silent, syllableless creativity, conjugating flowers' veins into 'Branch' and 'Jugular' while she abides and we pass. Treats nature, mortality, transience and the inexpressible.
