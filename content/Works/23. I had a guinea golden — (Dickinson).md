# 23. I had a guinea golden —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Lyric · Flowers · Stars]]

> [!abstract] 
> A reproachful lyric on three lost treasures — a gold guinea, a robin, a Pleiad star — as figures for a missing friend; loss, fidelity and the wish for the traitor's remorse.
