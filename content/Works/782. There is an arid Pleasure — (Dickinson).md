# 782. There is an arid Pleasure —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Joy · Animals · Comic Verse Song]]

> [!abstract] 
> A lyric distinguishing arid pleasure from true joy as frost from dew, one nourishing flowers, the other abhorred. Themes: joy versus its counterfeit, paradox, nature as analogy.

## Testo integrale / Full text

There is an arid Pleasure — 
As different from Joy — 
As Frost is different from Dew — 
Like element — are they — 
 
Yet one — rejoices Flowers — 
And one — the Flowers abhor — 
The finest Honey — curdled — 
Is worthless — to the Bee —
