# 790. Nature — the Gentlest Mother is,

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Nature · The Book of Nature · The Natural Order]]

> [!abstract] 
> A pastoral lyric personifying Nature as the gentlest mother tending all her children, squirrel, bird, cricket, and flower, then hushing them to sleep with golden lamps. Themes: nature as great mother, providence, tenderness, the natural order.
