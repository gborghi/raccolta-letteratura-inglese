# 797. By my Window have I for Scenery

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Nature · The Book of Nature · The Natural Order]]

> [!abstract] 
> A contemplative lyric on the pine tree seen from her window as an emblem of the divine and immortality, asking whether the dumb can define the divine. Joins nature, faith, immortality and the inexpressibility of the sublime.
