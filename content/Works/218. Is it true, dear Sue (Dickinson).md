## Testo integrale / Full text

Is it true, dear Sue?  
Are there two?  
I shouldn't like to come  
For fear of joggling Him!  
If I could shut him up  
In a Coffee Cup,  
Or tie him to a pin  
Till I got in —  
Or make him fast  
To "Toby's" fist —  
Hist! Whist! I'd come!
