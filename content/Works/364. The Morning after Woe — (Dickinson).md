*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Wonder and Gratitude · The Sublime · Sun]]

> [!abstract] 
> A lyric on nature's cruel jubilance the morning after sorrow, its birdsong falling like litanies of lead on the grieving; on grief, dawn, and nature's indifference.
