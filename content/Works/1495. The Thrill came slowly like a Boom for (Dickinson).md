# 1495. The Thrill came slowly like a Boom for

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Joy · Animals · Comic Verse Song]]

> [!abstract] 
> A long-delayed thrill swelling like a flood into ravished holiness; rapture, ecstasy and the slow growth of joy out of desolation.

## Connections

**Motifs:** [[Water]]
**Themes & Concepts:** [[Joy]], [[Wonder and Gratitude]], [[Melancholy]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

The Thrill came slowly like a Boom for 
Centuries delayed 
It's fitness growing like the Flood 
In sumptuous solitude — 
The desolation only missed 
While Rapture changed it's Dress 
And stood arrayed before the Change 
In ravished Holiness —
