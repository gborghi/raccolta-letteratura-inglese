# MARK AKENSIDE

*by Coleridge*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Grief and Loss · Melancholy · Elegy]]

> [!abstract] 
> A blank-verse inscription after Akenside: the traveller is bidden to mourn at the grave of Edmund the learned shepherd, who died of ill-requited love, while faithless Matilda is avenged by Heaven; rural elegy on love, death and pride.
