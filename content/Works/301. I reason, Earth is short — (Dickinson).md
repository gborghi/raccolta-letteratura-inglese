> Skeptical syllogistic lyric weighing earth's brevity, inevitable decay, and an uncertain heaven, each undercut by 'but what of that?'; faith and doubt.

## Testo integrale / Full text

I reason, earth is short,  
And anguish absolute,  
And many hurt ;  
But what of that ?  

I reason, we could die :  
The best vitality  
Cannot excel decay ;  
But what of that ?  

I reason that in heaven  
Somehow, it will be even,  
Some new equation given ;  
But what of that ?
