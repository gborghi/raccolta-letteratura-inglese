# 850. I sing to use the Waiting

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Maturation and Growth · Childhood · Innocence and Experience]]

> [!abstract] 
> A quiet lyric of one who sings to use the waiting, bonnet tied and door shut, until His best step approaches and they journey to the Day. Joins faith, hope, music and song against the dark, and waiting for the beloved or the divine.
