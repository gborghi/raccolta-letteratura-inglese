## Testo integrale / Full text

My Heart ran so to thee  
It would not wait for me  
And I affronted grew  
And drew away  

For whatsoe'er my pace  
He first achieve they Face  
How general a Grace  
Allotted two —  

Not in malignity  
Mentioned I this to thee —  
Had he obliquity  
Soonest to share  
But for the Greed of him —  
Boasting my Premium —  
Basking in Bethleem  
Ere I be there —
