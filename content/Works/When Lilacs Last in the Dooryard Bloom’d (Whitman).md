# When Lilacs Last in the Dooryard Bloom’d

*by Whitman*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Grief and Loss · Melancholy · Elegy]]

> [!abstract] 
> Whitman's great pastoral elegy for the assassinated Lincoln, built on the trinity of lilac, fallen western star, and hermit thrush, culminating in a hymn of reconciliation to 'lovely and soothing death.'
