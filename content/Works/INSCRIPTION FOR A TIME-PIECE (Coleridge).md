# INSCRIPTION FOR A TIME-PIECE

*by Coleridge*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Transience · Time · Tempus Fugit]]

> [!abstract] 
> A meditative inscription on fleeting hours, each parting hour yielding a ghost to dwell within as an 'eternal Now', with attached squibs on Tom Hill and on tears as the eye's language; time, transience, and silence.
