## Testo integrale / Full text

There are two Ripenings — one — of sight —  
Whose forces Spheric wind  
Until the Velvet product  
Drop spicy to the ground —  
A homelier maturing —  
A process in the Bur —  
That teeth of Frosts alone disclose  
In far October Air.
