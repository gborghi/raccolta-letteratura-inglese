# 713. Fame of Myself, to justify,

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Crown · Pride · Fortune's Wheel]]

> [!abstract] 
> An epigrammatic lyric on self-sufficiency in fame: self-justification makes all other praise superfluous, while lacking one's own approval renders any supreme name a 'futile Diadem'; on identity, integrity and fame.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Fame of Myself, to justify, 
All other Plaudit be 
Superfluous — An Incense 
Beyond Necessity — 
 
Fame of Myself to lack — Although 
My Name be else Supreme — 
This were an Honor honorless — 
A futile Diadem —
