# A Prophecy: to George Keats in America

*by Keats*  ·  **Cluster:** [[The Poet as Prophet · The Great Mother · The Prophet]]

> [!abstract] 
> A midnight lullaby-prophecy over his brother's unborn child in America, foretelling it will be a poet who grasps the flaming lyre unharmed. It joins poetic vocation, childhood, night and the supernatural.
