# 1070. To undertake is to achieve

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Paradox · Food and Feasting · Aphorism]]

> [!abstract] 
> An aphoristic lyric on resolve: to undertake with fortitude against obstacle is itself achievement, revering departed standards and few true criterion sources.

## Connections

**Themes & Concepts:** [[Heroism]], [[Paradox]], [[Faith]]
**Forms & Genres:** [[Aphorism]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

To undertake is to achieve 
Be Undertaking blent 
With fortitude of obstacle 
And toward encouragement 
 
That fine Suspicion, Natures must 
Permitted to revere 
Departed Standards and the few 
Criterion Sources here
