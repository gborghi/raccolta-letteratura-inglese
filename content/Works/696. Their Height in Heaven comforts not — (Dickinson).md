# 696. Their Height in Heaven comforts not —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Christianity · Faith and Doubt · Doubt]]

> [!abstract] 
> A lyric of doubt preferring finite earthly evidence to heaven's uncertain glory, ending 'I don't know.' Central themes: doubt, faith and doubt, the finite, uncertainty.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Their Height in Heaven comforts not — 
Their Glory — nought to me — 
'Twas best imperfect — as it was — 
I'm finite — I can't see — 
 
The House of Supposition — 
The Glimmering Frontier that 
Skirts the Acres of Perhaps — 
To Me — shows insecure — 
 
The Wealth I had — contented me — 
If 'twas a meaner size — 
Then I had counted it until 
It pleased my narrow Eyes — 
 
Better than larger values — 
That show however true — 
This timid life of Evidence 
Keeps pleading — "I don't know."
