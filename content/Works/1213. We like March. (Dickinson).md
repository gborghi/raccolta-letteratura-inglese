# 1213. We like March.

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Flowers · Spring · Garden]]

> [!abstract] 
> March personified as a bold new arrival heralding spring, sun and bluebirds; seasonal renewal and joy in nature.

## Testo integrale / Full text

We like March. 
His Shoes are Purple — 
He is new and high — 
Makes he Mud for Dog and Peddler. 
Makes he Forests dry. 
Knows the Adder Tongue his coming 
And presents her Spot — 
Stands the Sun so close and mighty 
That our Minds are hot. 
 
News is he of all the others — 
Bold it were to die 
With the Blue Birds exercising 
On his British Sky.

We like March — his shoes are Purple. 
He is new and high — 
Makes he Mud for Dog and Peddler — 
Makes he Forests Dry — 
Knows the Adder's Tongue his coming 
And begets her spot — 
Stands the Sun so close and mighty — 
That our Minds are hot. 
News is he of all the others — 
Bold it were to die 
With the Blue Birds buccaneering 
On his British sky —
