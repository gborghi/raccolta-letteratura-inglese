*by Coleridge*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Grief and Loss · Melancholy · Elegy]]

> [!abstract] 
> A translated Hebrew dirge and hymn for the funeral of King George III, mourning a just king as a fallen day-star and praying for the royal line. Themes: grief and loss, kingship, faith, providence.
