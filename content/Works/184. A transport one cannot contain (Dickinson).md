## Testo integrale / Full text

A transport one cannot contain  
May yet a transport be —  
Though God forbid it lift the lid —  
Unto its Ecstasy!  

A Diagram — of Rapture!  
A sixpence at a Show —  
With Holy Ghosts in Cages!  
The Universe would go!
