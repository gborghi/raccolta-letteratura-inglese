# 1533. On that specific Pillow

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Sleep · Sleeper Awakes · Resurrection]]

> [!abstract] 
> A lyric on the threshold of sleep and death, where projects flit away and the soul can do no more than try to comprise the night's tremendous morrow.

## Connections

**Topoi:** [[Sleeper Awakes]]
**Motifs:** [[Sleep]], [[Night]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

On that specific Pillow 
Our projects flit away — 
The Night's tremendous Morrow 
And whether sleep will stay 
Or usher us — a stranger — 
To situations new 
The effort to comprise it 
Is all the soul can do.
