## Testo integrale / Full text

His oriental heresies  
Exhilarate the Bee,  
And filling all the Earth and Air  
With gay apostasy  

Fatigued at last, a Clover plain  
Allures his jaded eye  
That lowly Breast where Butterflies  
Have felt it meet to die —
