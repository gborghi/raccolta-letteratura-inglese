# Song: The stranger lighted from his steed

*by Keats*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Night · Fire · Comic Verse Song]]

> [!abstract] 
> A ballad-like song of a stranger who kisses a lady's hand, lips and maid before riding off, closing with a rapt vow of adoration over a sleeping beloved. Its matter is desire, kissing, and amorous worship.
