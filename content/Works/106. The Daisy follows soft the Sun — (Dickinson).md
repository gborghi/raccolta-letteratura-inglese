*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Romantic Love · The Lover · Unrequited Frustrated Love]]

> [!abstract] 
> An allegory of devotion: the daisy follows the sun as the soul/lover follows the beloved or the divine, stealing nearer at the parting west; love as religious longing.
