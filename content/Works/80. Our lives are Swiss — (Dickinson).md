# 80. Our lives are Swiss —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Rose · Desire · Value and Scarcity]]

> [!abstract] 
> Life as cool Switzerland glimpsing forbidden Italy beyond the intervening Alps; longing for a sublime beyond barred by death/limit.

## Connections

**Themes & Concepts:** [[Sublime]], [[Desire (2)]], [[Immortality]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

Our lives are Swiss — 
So still — so Cool — 
Till some odd afternoon 
The Alps neglect their Curtains 
And we look farther on! 
 
Italy stands the other side! 
While like a guard between — 
The solemn Alps — 
The siren Alps 
Forever intervene!
