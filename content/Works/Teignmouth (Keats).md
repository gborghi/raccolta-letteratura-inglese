*by Keats*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Nature vs Civilization · The Garden · Pastoral Retreat]]

> [!abstract] 
> A doggerel song praising the rural Devon landscape of Teignmouth with its brooks, woods and maidens, preferring it to dark literary London. It is a pastoral retreat poem rich in nature and flowers.
