# 1031. Fate slew Him, but He did not drop —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Heroism · Hero · Last Stand]]

> [!abstract] 
> A heroic lyric in which a man withstands all that personified Fate inflicts, his unmoved endurance forcing Fate to acknowledge him a Man.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Fate slew Him, but He did not drop — 
She felled — He did not fall — 
Impaled Him on Her fiercest stakes — 
He neutralized them all — 
 
She stung Him — sapped His firm Advance — 
But when Her Worst was done 
And He — unmoved regarded Her — 
Acknowledged Him a Man.
