# 1452. Your thoughts don't have words every day

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Wonder and Gratitude · Sublime · Storm]]

> [!abstract] 
> Rare, wordless thoughts likened to communion wine — sacramental and infrequent; the inexpressible value of inspiration.

## Connections

**Topoi:** [[Inexpressibility]]
**Motifs:** [[Bread and Wine Eucharist]]
**Themes & Concepts:** [[Imagination]], [[Transience]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

Your thoughts don't have words every day 
They come a single time 
Like signal esoteric sips 
Of the communion Wine 
Which while you taste so native seems 
So easy so to be 
You cannot comprehend its price 
Nor its infrequency
