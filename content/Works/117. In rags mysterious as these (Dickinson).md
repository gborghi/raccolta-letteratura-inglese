# 117. In rags mysterious as these

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Money and Gold · Poverty · Imprisonment]]

> [!abstract] 
> A lyric on hidden royalty: shining courtiers (perhaps flowers or souls) veil their purple and ermine in rags and beg for alms; appearance versus reality, humility cloaking glory.

## Connections

**Motifs:** [[Clothing and Costume]], [[Money and Gold]]

**Forms & Genres:** [[Lyric]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

In rags mysterious as these 
The shining Courtiers go — 
Veiling the purple, and the plumes — 
Veiling the ermine so. 
 
Smiling, as they request an alms — 
At some imposing door! 
Smiling when we walk barefoot 
Upon their golden floor!
