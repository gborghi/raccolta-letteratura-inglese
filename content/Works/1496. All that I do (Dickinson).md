# 1496. All that I do

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Lyric · Romantic Love · Marriage and Fidelity]]

> [!abstract] 
> All the speaker does reviewed by an enamored omnipresent eye awaiting her as a bride; love, surveillance and the bride motif, devotional or amorous.

## Testo integrale / Full text

All that I do 
Is in review 
To his enamored mind 
I know his eye 
Where e'er I ply 
Is pushing close behind 
Not any Port 
Nor any flight 
But he doth there preside 
What omnipresence lies in wait 
For her to be a Bride
