# 728. Let Us play Yesterday —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Maturation and Growth · Childhood · Innocence and Experience]]

> [!abstract] 
> A lyric recalling childhood schooldays and the dawn of consciousness through images of egg, shell, bird and manacles; a prayer to the 'God of the Manacle' not to take liberty; on innocence, growth, freedom and bondage.
