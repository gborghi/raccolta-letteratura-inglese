# 543. I fear a Man of frugal Speech —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Money and Gold · Poverty · Imprisonment]]

> [!abstract] 
> The silent, weighing man is feared as potentially Grand, while the talkative are easily managed; silence as power.

## Connections

**Motifs:** [[Silence]], [[Money and Gold]]
**Themes & Concepts:** [[Power and Corruption]], [[Appearance vs Reality]], [[Identity]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

I fear a Man of frugal Speech — 
I fear a Silent Man — 
Haranguer — I can overtake — 
Or Babbler — entertain — 
 
But He who weigheth — While the Rest — 
Expend their furthest pound — 
Of this Man — I am wary — 
I fear that He is Grand —
