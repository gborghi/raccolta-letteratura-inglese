# 806. A Plated Life — diversified

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Paradox · Food and Feasting · Aphorism]]

> [!abstract] 
> A metaphysical epigram in which gold-and-silver pain proves the precious ore within, a value and power that proclaim themselves even as annihilation piles chaos upon them. Treats suffering, endurance and inner worth.

## Testo integrale / Full text

A Plated Life — diversified 
With Gold and Silver Pain 
To prove the presence of the Ore 
In Particles — 'tis when 
 
A Value struggle — it exist — 
A Power — will proclaim 
Although Annihilation pile 
Whole Chaoses on Him —
