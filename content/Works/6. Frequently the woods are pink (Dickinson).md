# 6. Frequently the woods are pink

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Nature · Book of Nature · Natural Order]]

> [!abstract] 
> A brief riddling lyric on the seasonal turning of the woods and the wonder of the earth's rotation through the twelve months.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Frequently the woods are pink — 
Frequently are brown. 
Frequently the hills undress 
Behind my native town. 
Oft a head is crested 
I was wont to see — 
And as oft a cranny 
Where it used to be — 
And the Earth — they tell me — 
On its axis turned! 
Wonderful Rotation! 
By but twelve performed!
