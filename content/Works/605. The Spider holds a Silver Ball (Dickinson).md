# 605. The Spider holds a Silver Ball

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Imagination · Fragment · Heroic Couplet]]

> [!abstract] 
> The spider as silent artist spinning continents of light in an hour, only to be swept away and his boundaries forgotten. Themes of the artist's labor, transience, and unrewarded creation.

## Connections

**Themes & Concepts:** [[Creativity and Artistic Labor]], [[Artist and Society]], [[Transience]], [[Imagination]], [[Mortality]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

The Spider holds a Silver Ball 
In unperceived Hands — 
And dancing softly to Himself 
His Yarn of Pearl — unwinds — 
 
He plies from Nought to Nought — 
In unsubstantial Trade — 
Supplants our Tapestries with His — 
In half the period — 
 
An Hour to rear supreme 
His Continents of Light — 
Then dangle from the Housewife's Broom — 
His Boundaries — forgot —
