*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Mortality · Grave · Memento Mori]]

> [!abstract] 
> A graveyard epigram noting that quiet dust was once laughter, frocks and curls, a summer's nimble mansion that ceased like bloom and bees. A memento-mori meditation on mortality, dust and transience.

## Testo integrale / Full text

This quiet Dust was Gentleman and Ladies  
And Lads and Girls —  
Was laughter and ability and Sighing  
And Frocks and Curls.  

This Passive Place a Summer's nimble mansion  
Where Bloom and Bees  
Exists an Oriental Circuit  
Then cease, like these —
