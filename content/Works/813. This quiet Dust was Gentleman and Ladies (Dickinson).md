# 813. This quiet Dust was Gentleman and Ladies

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Death · Mortality · Immortality]]

> [!abstract] 
> A graveyard epigram noting that quiet dust was once laughter, frocks and curls, a summer's nimble mansion that ceased like bloom and bees. A memento-mori meditation on mortality, dust and transience.
