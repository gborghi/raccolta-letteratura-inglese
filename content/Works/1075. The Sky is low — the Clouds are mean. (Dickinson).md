# 1075. The Sky is low — the Clouds are mean.

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Nature · Book of Nature · Natural Order]]

> [!abstract] 
> A wintry weather lyric personifying a complaining wind and a hesitant snowflake, finding Nature, like us, sometimes caught without her diadem.

## Connections

**Motifs:** [[Winter]], [[Wind]], [[Nature]], [[Crown]]
**Forms & Genres:** [[Lyric]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

The Sky is low — the Clouds are mean. 
A Travelling Flake of Snow 
Across a Barn or through a Rut 
Debates if it will go — 
 
A Narrow Wind complains all Day 
How some one treated him 
Nature, like Us is sometimes caught 
Without her Diadem.
