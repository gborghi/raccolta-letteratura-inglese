# Epistle to John Hamilton Reynolds

*by Keats*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Imagination · Creativity and Artistic Labor · The One Life (Pantheism)]]

> [!abstract] 
> A verse epistle moving from chaotic dream-imagery and a Claude-like enchanted castle to a dark vision of nature's 'eternal fierce destruction' seen too far into the sea. It probes imagination, dreams, melancholy, and the cruelty of the natural order.
