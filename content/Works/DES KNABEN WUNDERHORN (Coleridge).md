# DES KNABEN WUNDERHORN

*by Coleridge*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Dreams · Sleep · Resurrection]]

> [!abstract] 
> German folk-song ('Wenn ich ein Vöglein wär'): the lover wishes to be a bird to fly to the beloved, is with her only in dream, and wakes alone; a folk lyric of longing, separation and dreams.
