## Testo integrale / Full text

To be alive — is Power —  
Existence — in itself —  
Without a further function —  
Omnipotence — Enough —  

To be alive — and Will!  
'Tis able as a God —  
The Maker — of Ourselves — be what —  
Such being Finitude!
