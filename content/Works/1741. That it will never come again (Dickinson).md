# 1741. That it will never come again

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Transience · Decay · Carpe Diem]]

> [!abstract] 
> Transience itself makes life sweet; the paradox that finitude rather than belief instigates desire.

## Connections

**Topoi:** [[Carpe Diem]], [[Tempus Fugit]]
**Themes & Concepts:** [[Transience]], [[Paradox]], [[Mortality]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

That it will never come again 
Is what makes life so sweet. 
Believing what we don't believe 
Does not exhilarate. 
 
That if it be, it be at best 
An ablative estate — 
This instigates an appetite 
Precisely opposite.
