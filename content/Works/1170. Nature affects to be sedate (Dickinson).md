# 1170. Nature affects to be sedate

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Nature · Book of Nature · Natural Order]]

> [!abstract] 
> A lyric on nature's deceptive sedateness masking necromancy and conjuring trades, the grand citizen turned juggler; nature, appearance vs reality, wonder.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Nature affects to be sedate 
Upon occasion, grand 
But let our observation shut 
Her practices extend 
 
To Necromancy and the Trades 
Remote to understand 
Behold our spacious Citizen 
Unto a Juggler turned —
