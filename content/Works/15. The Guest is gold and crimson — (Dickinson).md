# 15. The Guest is gold and crimson —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[White and Red · Riddle · Secrecy and Concealment]]

> [!abstract] 
> A riddle-lyric personifying the autumn sunset (or a migrating season) as a richly robed guest stopping at every door; nature, color and transience.

## Testo integrale / Full text

The Guest is gold and crimson — 
An Opal guest and gray — 
Of Ermine is his doublet — 
His Capuchin gay — 
 
He reaches town at nightfall — 
He stops at every door — 
Who looks for him at morning 
I pray him too — explore 
The Lark's pure territory — 
Or the Lapwing's shore!
