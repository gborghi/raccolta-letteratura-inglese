# 1392. Hope is a strange invention —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Satire of Society · Epigram · Satire]]

> [!abstract] 
> Hope defined as a tireless 'electric' invention of the heart; abstract definition poem on inner spiritual resource.

## Connections

**Themes & Concepts:** [[Imagination]], [[Self-Knowledge]], [[Joy]], [[Faith]]
**Forms & Genres:** [[Epigram]], [[Lyric]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

Hope is a strange invention — 
A Patent of the Heart — 
In unremitting action 
Yet never wearing out — 
 
Of this electric Adjunct 
Not anything is known 
But its unique momentum 
Embellish all we own —
