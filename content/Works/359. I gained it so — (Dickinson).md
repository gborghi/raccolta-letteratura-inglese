# 359. I gained it so —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Allegory · Quest · Pilgrim]]

> [!abstract] 
> A lyric of a hard-climbed bliss clutched in fear of loss, the speaker dreading return to the contented beggar's poverty; on desire, grace, and the quest.

## Testo integrale / Full text

I gained it so — 
By Climbing slow — 
By Catching at the Twigs that grow 
Between the Bliss — and me — 
It hung so high 
As well the Sky 
Attempt by Strategy — 
 
I said I gained it — 
This — was all — 
Look, how I clutch it 
Lest it fall — 
And I a Pauper go — 
Unfitted by an instant's Grace 
For the Contented — Beggar's face 
I wore — an hour ago —
