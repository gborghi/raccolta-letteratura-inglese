# 1021. Far from Love the Heavenly Father

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Faith · Providence · Divine Love]]

> [!abstract] 
> A lyric on divine providence: God leads the elect child through briar and the dragon's claw rather than mild meadows, framing suffering as predestined guidance toward heaven.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Far from Love the Heavenly Father 
Leads the Chosen Child, 
Oftener through Realm of Briar 
Than the Meadow mild. 
 
Oftener by the Claw of Dragon 
Than the Hand of Friend 
Guides the Little One predestined 
To the Native Land.
