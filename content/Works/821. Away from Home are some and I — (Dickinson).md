# 821. Away from Home are some and I —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Alienation · Outsider · Exile]]

> [!abstract] 
> A lyric on being an emigrant away from home in a metropolis of homes, the foreign sky hard to acquire as children's feet retire while their faces remain. Treats exile, home, alienation and childhood.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Away from Home are some and I — 
An Emigrant to be 
In a Metropolis of Homes 
Is easy, possibly — 
 
The Habit of a Foreign Sky 
We — difficult — acquire 
As Children, who remain in Face 
The more their Feet retire.
