# 776. The Color of a Queen, is this —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Sun · Light and Dark · Fire]]

> [!abstract] 
> A lyric cataloguing a royal color glimpsed in sunset, amber, beryl, and aurora, where nature keeps a rank for witchcraft and iodine. Themes: beauty, color, nature's regal splendor, wonder.

## Testo integrale / Full text

The Color of a Queen, is this — 
The Color of a Sun 
At setting — this and Amber — 
Beryl — and this, at Noon — 
 
And when at night — Auroran widths 
Fling suddenly on men — 
'Tis this — and Witchcraft — nature keeps 
A Rank — for Iodine —
