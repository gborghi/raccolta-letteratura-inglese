# 904. Had I not This, or This, I said,

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Ship · Endurance]]

> [!abstract] 
> A lyric dialogue with the self on need and deprivation, concluding that hunger persists though the food is gone and that dwelling on loss enfeebles advance — a meditation on want, loss, and endurance.

## Connections

**Motifs:** [[Food and Feasting]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

Had I not This, or This, I said, 
Appealing to Myself, 
In moment of prosperity — 
Inadequate — were Life — 
 
"Thou hast not Me, nor Me" — it said, 
In Moment of Reverse — 
"And yet Thou art industrious — 
No need — hadst Thou — of us"? 
 
My need — was all I had — I said — 
The need did not reduce — 
Because the food — exterminate — 
The hunger — does not cease — 
 
But diligence — is sharper — 
Proportioned to the Chance — 
To feed upon the Retrograde — 
Enfeebles — the Advance —
