# 469. The Red — Blaze — is the Morning —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Sun · Light and Dark · Fire]]

> [!abstract] 
> The colors of a day's arc — red morning, violet noon, yellow falling day — and the unconsumed silver territory of evening sparks; time and light.

## Connections

**Topoi:** [[Tempus Fugit]]
**Motifs:** [[Light and Dark]], [[Fire]], [[Time]], [[Sun]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

The Red — Blaze — is the Morning — 
The Violet — is Noon — 
The Yellow — Day — is falling — 
And after that — is none — 
 
But Miles of Sparks — at Evening — 
Reveal the Width that burned — 
The Territory Argent — that 
Never yet — consumed —
