# 1589. Cosmopolities without a plea

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Birds · Music and Song · Nightingale]]

> [!abstract] 
> Wandering birds as world-citizens bearing 'compliments of Paradise'; their roving theology is 'Knock and it shall be opened.'

## Testo integrale / Full text

Cosmopolites without a plea 
Alight in every Land 
The compliments of Paradise 
From those within my Hand 
 
Their dappled Journey to themselves 
A compensation fair 
Knock and it shall be opened 
Is their Theology
