# 1280. The harm of Years is on him —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Transience · Decay · Carpe Diem]]

> [!abstract] 
> Time deposes a man like a fading fashion; the glory of decay is a lesser pageant than the least vitality.

## Connections

## Testo integrale / Full text

The harm of Years is on him — 
The infamy of Time — 
Depose him like a Fashion 
And give Dominion room. 
 
Forget his Morning Forces — 
The Glory of Decay 
Is a minuter Pageant 
Than least Vitality.
