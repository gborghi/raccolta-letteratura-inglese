# Men Without Women

*by Hemingway*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Disillusionment · Odysseus Ulysses · Grace Under Pressure Stoic Endurance]]

> [!abstract] 
> A 1927 collection of fourteen short stories (The Undefeated, The Killers, Hills Like White Elephants, Fifty Grand, Now I Lay Me, etc.) exploring masculine codes, grace under pressure, violence, war trauma and the strained or absent relations between men and women, told in Hemingway's spare iceberg prose.
