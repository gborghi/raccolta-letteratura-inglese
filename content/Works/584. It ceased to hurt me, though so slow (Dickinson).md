# 584. It ceased to hurt me, though so slow

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Melancholy · Suffering · Pain and Suffering]]

> [!abstract] 
> A retrospective on grief's imperceptible fading into something almost peace, recognized only by looking back. Themes of grief, pain, healing, and memory.

## Testo integrale / Full text

It ceased to hurt me, though so slow 
I could not feel the Anguish go — 
But only knew by looking back — 
That something — had benumbed the Track — 
 
Nor when it altered, I could say, 
For I had worn it, every day, 
As constant as the Childish frock — 
I hung upon the Peg, at night. 
 
But not the Grief — that nestled close 
As needles — ladies softly press 
To Cushions Cheeks — 
To keep their place — 
 
Nor what consoled it, I could trace — 
Except, whereas 'twas Wilderness — 
It's better — almost Peace —
