# 1663. His mind of man, a secret makes

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Self-Knowledge · Immortality · Self-Reliance]]

> [!abstract] 
> Each mind carries an impregnable circumference, a secret unknowable even to the neighborly other; the inviolable solitude of the self.

## Connections

**Motifs:** [[Silence]]
**Themes & Concepts:** [[Identity]], [[Self-Knowledge]], [[Alienation]], [[Appearance vs Reality]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

His mind of man, a secret makes 
I meet him with a start 
He carries a circumference 
In which I have no part — 
 
Or even if I deem I do 
He otherwise may know 
Impregnable to inquest 
However neighborly —
