# 362. It struck me — every Day —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Wonder and Gratitude · Sublime · Storm]]

> [!abstract] 
> A lyric of recurring, lightning-like anguish that nature has lost the date of and left burning in the sky; on suffering, storm imagery, and unending pain.

## Connections

**Motifs:** [[Storm]], [[Fire]], [[Dreams]], [[Time]], [[Nature]]
**Themes & Concepts:** [[Grief and Loss]], [[Melancholy]]
**Forms & Genres:** [[Lyric]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

It struck me — every Day — 
The Lightning was as new 
As if the Cloud that instant slit 
And let the Fire through — 
 
It burned Me — in the Night — 
It Blistered to My Dream — 
It sickened fresh upon my sight — 
With every Morn that came — 
 
I though that Storm — was brief — 
The Maddest — quickest by — 
But Nature lost the Date of This — 
And left it in the Sky —
