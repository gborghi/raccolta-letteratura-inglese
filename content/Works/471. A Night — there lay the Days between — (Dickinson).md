# 471. A Night — there lay the Days between —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Stars · Night · Military Camp]]

> [!abstract] 
> A long night that must be watched away grain by grain, dividing identical days; suffering, time, and the slow vigil of darkness.

## Connections

**Topoi:** [[Night Journey Vision]]
**Motifs:** [[Night]], [[Time]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

A Night — there lay the Days between — 
The Day that was Before — 
And Day that was Behind — were one — 
And now — 'twas Night — was here — 
 
Slow — Night — that must be watched away — 
As Grains upon a shore — 
Too imperceptible to note — 
Till it be night — no more —
