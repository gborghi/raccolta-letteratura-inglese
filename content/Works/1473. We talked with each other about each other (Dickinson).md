# 1473. We talked with each other about each other

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Time · Tempus Fugit · Clock]]

> [!abstract] 
> Wordless communion before the hoofs of the clock, time offering arks of reprieve; intimacy, mortality and the pressure of time.

## Connections

**Motifs:** [[Clock]], [[Time]], [[Silence]]
**Themes & Concepts:** [[Mercy]]
**Historical References:** [[Bible]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

We talked with each other about each other 
Though neither of us spoke — 
We were listening to the seconds' Races 
And the Hoofs of the Clock — 
Pausing in Front of our Palsied Faces 
Time compassion took — 
Arks of Reprieve he offered to us — 
Ararats — we took —
