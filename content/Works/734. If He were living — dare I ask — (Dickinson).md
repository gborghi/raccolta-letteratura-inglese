*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Death · Mortality · Immortality]]

> [!abstract] 
> A lyric dramatizing the dread of asking whether an absent person is dead, circling cautiously around graves until the blunt 'Buried!' lands; on death, fear, grief and suspense.
