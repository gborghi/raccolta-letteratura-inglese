# FRANCE: AN ODE

*by Coleridge*  ·  **Cluster:** [[The French Revolution · Liberty Political Freedom · Pindaric Ode]]

> [!abstract] 
> A recantation ode tracing the poet's hope in the French Revolution, his disillusion at France's invasion of Switzerland, and his relocation of true liberty to nature and the individual soul; themes of liberty, disillusionment and the sublime.
