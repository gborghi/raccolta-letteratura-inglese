# Great Expectations

*by Dickens*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Money and Gold · Marriage and Fidelity · Social Injustice]]

> [!abstract] 
> A first-person bildungsroman in which the orphan Pip rises from village forge to gentlemanly 'expectations' funded by a secret convict benefactor; central themes of guilt and conscience, class and snobbery, unrequited love, and the gulf between appearance and moral reality.
