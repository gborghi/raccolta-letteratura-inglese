*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Death · Silence · Bells]]

> [!abstract] 
> Frost personified as a nocturnal visitor whose caress kills the flowers it touches, annihilating them as if they never were.

## Connections

**Motifs:** [[Winter]], [[Death]], [[Flowers]], [[Night]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

A Visitor in Marl —  
Who influences Flowers —  
Till they are orderly as Busts —  
And Elegant — as Glass —  

Who visits in the Night —  
And just before the Sun —  
Concludes his glistening interview —  
Caresses — and is gone —  

But whom his fingers touched —  
And where his feet have run —  
And whatsoever Mouth be kissed —  
Is as it had not been —
