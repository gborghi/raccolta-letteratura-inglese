# 1062. He scanned it — staggered —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Terror and Horror · Madness · Madness]]

> [!abstract] 
> A stark narrative of a man's mental collapse and suicide: groping for God and himself, he caresses a trigger and wanders out of life.

## Connections

**Motifs:** [[Death]], [[Swords and Weapons]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

He scanned it — staggered — 
Dropped the Loop 
To Past or Period — 
Caught helpless at a sense as if 
His Mind were going blind — 
 
Groped up, to see if God was there — 
Groped backward at Himself 
Caressed a Trigger absently 
And wandered out of Life.
