# 1649. A Cap of Lead across the sky

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Wonder and Gratitude · Sublime · Storm]]

> [!abstract] 
> A leaden, face-hiding storm-sky chills noon into a well, combining the charms of winter and hell; nature's sublime menace and God withdrawn.

## Connections

**Motifs:** [[Storm]], [[Winter]], [[Light and Dark]]
**Themes & Concepts:** [[Sublime]], [[Terror and Horror]], [[Doubt]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

A Cap of Lead across the sky 
Was tight and surly drawn 
We could not find the mighty Face 
The Figure was withdrawn — 
 
A Chill came up as from a shaft 
Our noon became a well 
A Thunder storm combines the charms 
Of Winter and of Hell.
