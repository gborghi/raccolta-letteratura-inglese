*by Coleridge*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Lust Sexual Desire · Desire · Sin and the Fall]]

> [!abstract] 
> Marino's Italian sonnet: two guilty lovers, condemned to death and to hell, find their eternal fire in each other's heart and eyes; a Baroque sonnet of guilty love, sin, damnation and fire (with an appended German love-fragment).
