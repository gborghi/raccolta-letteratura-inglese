*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[White and Red · Riddle · Secrecy and Concealment]]

> [!abstract] 
> A riddling lyric asserting the indivisibility and indestructibility of essential things (air, light, flame, steam) — a near-aphoristic statement of the soul's or love's immortality against human impotence.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Banish Air from Air —  
Divide Light if you dare —  
They'll meet  
While Cubes in a Drop  
Or Pellets of Shape  
Fit  
Films cannot annul  
Odors return whole  
Force Flame  
And with a Blonde push  
Over your impotence  
Flits Steam.
