# 1009. I was a Phoebe — nothing more —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Letters and Writing · Immortality Through Verse · Immortality of Art Fame]]

> [!abstract] 
> A self-deprecating lyric in which the speaker identifies as a humble phoebe-bird, dwelling low and shy yet leaving a small print upon the floors of fame; themes of humility, identity, and modest art.

## Testo integrale / Full text

I was a Phoebe — nothing more — 
A Phoebe — nothing less — 
The little note that others dropt 
I fitted into place — 
 
I dwelt too low that any seek — 
Too shy, that any blame — 
A Phoebe makes a little print 
Upon the Floors of Fame —
