# 466. 'Tis little I — could care for Pearls —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Sea · Water · Sea]]

> [!abstract] 
> The speaker disdains gems and gold, owning sea, mines, and a continual diadem; inner/spiritual wealth surpassing material riches.

## Connections

**Motifs:** [[Money and Gold]], [[Sea]]
**Themes & Concepts:** [[Self-Knowledge]], [[Joy]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

'Tis little I — could care for Pearls — 
Who own the ample sea — 
Or Brooches — when the Emperor — 
With Rubies — pelteth me — 
 
Or Gold — who am the Prince of Mines — 
Or Diamonds — when have I 
A Diadem to fit a Dome — 
Continual upon me —
