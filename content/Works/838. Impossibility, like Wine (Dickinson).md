*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Paradox · Food and Feasting · Aphorism]]

> [!abstract] 
> An aphoristic lyric arguing that impossibility, like wine, exhilarates while possibility is flavorless; a chance's faintest tincture lends enchantment as surely as doom. Treats desire, paradox and the allure of the unattainable.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Impossibility, like Wine  
Exhilarates the Man  
Who tastes it; Possibility  
Is flavorless — Combine  

A Chance's faintest Tincture  
And in the former Dram  
Enchantment makes ingredient  
As certainly as Doom —
