# Hast Never Come to Thee an Hour

*by Whitman*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Vanitas · Contemptus Mundi · Vanity of Worldly Things]]

> [!abstract] 
> A brief lyric questioning whether a sudden divine gleam has revealed the nothingness of worldly business, wealth and fashion; a vanitas-tinged meditation.

## Testo integrale / Full text

_Cluster: BY THE ROADSIDE_


  Hast never come to thee an hour,
  A sudden gleam divine, precipitating, bursting all these bubbles,
      fashions, wealth?
  These eager business aims--books, politics, art, amours,
  To utter nothingness?
