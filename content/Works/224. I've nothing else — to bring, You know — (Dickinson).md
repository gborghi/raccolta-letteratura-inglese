# 224. I've nothing else — to bring, You know —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Lyric · Romantic Love · Marriage and Fidelity]]

> [!abstract] 
> A tender lyric on humble repeated gifts, likened to night's faithful fetching of stars that guide one home; love and constancy.

## Connections

**Motifs:** [[Stars]], [[Night]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

I've nothing else — to bring, You know — 
So I keep bringing These — 
Just as the Night keeps fetching Stars 
To our familiar eyes — 
 
Maybe, we shouldn't mind them — 
Unless they didn't come — 
Then — maybe, it would puzzle us 
To find our way Home —
