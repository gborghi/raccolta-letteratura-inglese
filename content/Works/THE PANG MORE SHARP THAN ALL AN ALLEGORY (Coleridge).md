# THE PANG MORE SHARP THAN ALL: AN ALLEGORY

*by Coleridge*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Faith and Doubt · Doubt · Allegory]]

> [!abstract] 
> An allegory of lost Hope figured as a vanished faery boy, leaving the playmates Esteem and Kindness, the sharpest pang being Kindness counterfeiting absent Love. Themes: grief and loss, hope, love, the imagination.
