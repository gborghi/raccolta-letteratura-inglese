> [!abstract] 
> A lyric on love fastened like a rivet at a forge; flowers and letters as the small tokens by which love is welded fast against labor and weariness.

## Testo integrale / Full text

By a flower — By a letter —  
By a nimble love —  
If I weld the Rivet faster —  
Final fast — above —  

Never mind my breathless Anvil!  
Never mind Repose!  
Never mind the sooty faces  
Tugging at the Forge!
