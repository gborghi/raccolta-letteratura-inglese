# 835. Nature and God — I neither knew

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Faith and Doubt · Doubt · Allegory]]

> [!abstract] 
> An epigram in which Nature and God, unknown to the speaker, yet know her like executors of her identity, keeping her secret as secure as Herschel's or Mercury's affairs. Joins faith, nature, identity and the divine gaze.
