## Testo integrale / Full text

Until the Desert knows  
That Water grows  
His Sands suffice  
But let him once suspect  
That Caspian Fact  
Sahara dies  

Utmost is relative —  
Have not or Have  
Adjacent sums  
Enough — the first Abode  
On the familiar Road  
Galloped in Dreams —
