# 382. For Death — or rather

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Death · Silence · Bells]]

> [!abstract] 
> Death weighed as a transaction buying room, escape and a name, its gifts incommensurable with life's because the rates lie here.

## Connections

**Motifs:** [[Death]], [[Money and Gold]]
**Themes & Concepts:** [[Mortality]], [[Immortality]], [[Paradox]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

For Death — or rather 
For the Things 'twould buy — 
This — put away 
Life's Opportunity — 
 
The Things that Death will buy 
Are Room — 
Escape from Circumstances — 
And a Name — 
 
With Gifts of Life 
How Death's Gifts may compare — 
We know not — 
For the Rates — lie Here —
