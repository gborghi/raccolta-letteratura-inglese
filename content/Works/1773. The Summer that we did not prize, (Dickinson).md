# 1773. The Summer that we did not prize,

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Transience · Decay · Carpe Diem]]

> [!abstract] 
> The unprized summer instructs by departing; belated recognition chases trains already out of sight.

## Testo integrale / Full text

The Summer that we did not prize, 
Her treasures were so easy 
Instructs us by departing now 
And recognition lazy — 
 
Bestirs itself — puts on its Coat, 
And scans with fatal promptness 
For Trains that moment out of sight, 
Unconscious of his smartness.
