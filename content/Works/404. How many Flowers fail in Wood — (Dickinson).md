# 404. How many Flowers fail in Wood —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Flowers · Spring · Garden]]

> [!abstract] 
> Unseen flowers perish unconscious of their own beauty, an emblem of unwitnessed worth and obscure fading.

## Connections

**Motifs:** [[Flowers]], [[Nature]]
**Themes & Concepts:** [[Beauty]], [[Mortality]], [[Transience]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

How many Flowers fail in Wood — 
Or perish from the Hill — 
Without the privilege to know 
That they are Beautiful — 
 
How many cast a nameless Pod 
Upon the nearest Breeze — 
Unconscious of the Scarlet Freight — 
It bear to Other Eyes —
