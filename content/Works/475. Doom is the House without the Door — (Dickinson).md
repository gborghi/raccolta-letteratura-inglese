# 475. Doom is the House without the Door —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Death · Silence · Bells]]

> [!abstract] 
> Doom as a doorless house entered from the sun with the ladder thrown away — irreversible fate, with the free outside world only dreamed of.

## Connections

**Motifs:** [[Death]], [[Nature]]
**Themes & Concepts:** [[Fate and Free Will]], [[Mortality]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

Doom is the House without the Door — 
'Tis entered from the Sun — 
And then the Ladder's thrown away, 
Because Escape — is done — 
 
'Tis varied by the Dream 
Of what they do outside — 
Where Squirrels play — and Berries die — 
And Hemlocks — bow — to God —
