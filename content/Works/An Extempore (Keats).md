# An Extempore

*by Keats*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Money and Gold · Marriage and Fidelity · Social Injustice]]

> [!abstract] 
> An extempore comic narrative of a wilful princess and her transformed servants (dwarf, ape, fool) at an empty faery court, with a sly moral on beauty and folly. It is a playful fairy-tale satire of vanity and transformation in an enchanted wood.
