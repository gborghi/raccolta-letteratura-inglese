# 335. 'Tis not that Dying hurts us so —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Death · Silence · Bells]]

> [!abstract] 
> A lyric arguing that living hurts more than dying, figuring the dying as migrating birds and the living as those who stay to suffer winter.

## Connections

**Motifs:** [[Death]], [[Birds]], [[Winter]], [[Seasons]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

'Tis not that Dying hurts us so — 
'Tis Living — hurts us more — 
But Dying — is a different way — 
A Kind behind the Door — 
 
The Southern Custom — of the Bird — 
That ere the Frosts are due — 
Accepts a better Latitude — 
We — are the Birds — that stay. 
 
The Shiverers round Farmers' doors — 
For whose reluctant Crumb — 
We stipulate — till pitying Snows 
Persuade our Feathers Home.
