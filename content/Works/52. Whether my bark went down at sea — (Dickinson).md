## Testo integrale / Full text

Whether my bark went down at sea,  
Whether she met with gales,  
Whether to isles enchanted  
She bent her docile sails ;  

By what mystic mooring  
She is held to-day, —  
This is the errand of the eye  
Out upon the bay.
