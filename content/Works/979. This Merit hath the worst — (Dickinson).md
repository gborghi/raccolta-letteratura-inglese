# 979. This Merit hath the worst —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Melancholy · Suffering · Pain and Suffering]]

> [!abstract] 
> An aphoristic lyric on the strange relief of the worst, after fate's furthest stone, when the maimed deer no longer attracts the hound; themes of suffering, fate, and endurance.

## Testo integrale / Full text

This Merit hath the worst — 
It cannot be again — 
When Fate hath taunted last 
And thrown Her furthest Stone — 
 
The Maimed may pause, and breathe, 
And glance securely round — 
The Deer attracts no further 
Than it resists — the Hound —
