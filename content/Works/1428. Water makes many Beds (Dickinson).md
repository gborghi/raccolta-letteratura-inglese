# 1428. Water makes many Beds

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Mortality · Grave · Memento Mori]]

> [!abstract] 
> Water as a fathomless bed/grave for the drowned; death by water, the sea, the grave, terror of the boundless.

**Motifs:** [[Water]], [[Death]], [[Grave]], [[Sea]], [[Sleep]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

Water makes many Beds 
For those averse to sleep — 
Its awful chamber open stands — 
Its Curtains blandly sweep — 
Abhorrent is the Rest 
In undulating Rooms 
Whose Amplitude no end invades — 
Whose Axis never comes.
