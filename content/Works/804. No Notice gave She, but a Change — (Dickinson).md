# 804. No Notice gave She, but a Change —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Dreams · Sleep · Resurrection]]

> [!abstract] 
> An elegy on a woman who died quietly, planted like a seed in fitted ground toward an everlasting spring, declining the mourners' imploring invitation. Joins death, grief, resurrection-hope and seasonal renewal.
