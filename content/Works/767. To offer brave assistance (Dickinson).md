# 767. To offer brave assistance

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Paradox · Truth · Aphorism]]

> [!abstract] 
> A gnomic lyric distinguishing human from divine charity: to aid the solitary and nameless, whose benediction none stopped to earn, is divine. Themes: charity, humility, the common man, the divine in compassion.
