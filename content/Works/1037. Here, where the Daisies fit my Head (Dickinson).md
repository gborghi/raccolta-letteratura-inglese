# 1037. Here, where the Daisies fit my Head

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Mortality · Grave · Memento Mori]]

> [!abstract] 
> A grave-bed lyric in which the speaker, lying among daisies, entrusts a flower to the earth and imagines union beyond death as a single bloom.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Here, where the Daisies fit my Head 
'Tis easiest to lie 
And every Grass that plays outside 
Is sorry, some, for me. 
 
Where I am not afraid to go 
I may confide my Flower — 
Who was not Enemy of Me 
Will gentle be, to Her. 
 
Nor separate, Herself and Me 
By Distances become — 
A single Bloom we constitute 
Departed, or at Home —
