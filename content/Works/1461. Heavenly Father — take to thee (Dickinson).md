# 1461. "Heavenly Father" — take to thee

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Christianity · Faith and Doubt · Doubt]]

> [!abstract] 
> Ironic apology to God for the sin he himself fashioned, charging the Creator with duplicity; doubt, the fall and theological paradox.

## Testo integrale / Full text

"Heavenly Father" — take to thee 
The supreme iniquity 
Fashioned by thy candid Hand 
In a moment contraband — 
Though to trust us — seems to us 
More respectful — "We are Dust" — 
We apologize to thee 
For thine own Duplicity —
