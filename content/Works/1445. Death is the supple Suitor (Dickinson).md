# 1445. Death is the supple Suitor

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Death · Silence · Bells]]

> [!abstract] 
> Personifies Death as a stealthy wooing suitor who wins at last and bears the soul away to an unknown troth; mortality as courtship.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Death is the supple Suitor 
That wins at last — 
It is a stealthy Wooing 
Conducted first 
By pallid innuendoes 
And dim approach 
But brave at last with Bugles 
And a bisected Coach 
It bears away in triumph 
To Troth unknown 
And Kindred as responsive 
As Porcelain.
