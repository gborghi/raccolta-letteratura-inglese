# THE HOUR-GLASS

*by Coleridge*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Transience · Time · Tempus Fugit]]

> [!abstract] 
> Coleridge's free version of Amaltei's Latin epigram (via Jonson): the sands of the hour-glass were once a swain who loved in vain, so lovers' ashes find no rest; epigram on time, death and unrequited love.
