# 817. Given in Marriage unto Thee

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Faith · Providence · Divine Love]]

> [!abstract] 
> A hymn-like lyric on mystical marriage to the Trinity, whose ring alone conquers mortality while earthly betrothals decay. Joins divine love, faith, marriage and immortality.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Given in Marriage unto Thee 
Oh thou Celestial Host — 
Bride of the Father and the Son 
Bride of the Holy Ghost. 
 
Other Betrothal shall dissolve — 
Wedlock of Will, decay — 
Only the Keeper of this Ring 
Conquer Mortality —
