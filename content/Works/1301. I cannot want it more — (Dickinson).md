# 1301. I cannot want it more —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Greek Mythology · Lust Sexual Desire · Desire]]

> [!abstract] 
> Human nature's fullest force spent on a longing that is nothing to one who easily owns; the gulf between yearning and possession.

## Testo integrale / Full text

I cannot want it more — 
I cannot want it less — 
My Human Nature's fullest force 
Expends itself on this. 
 
And yet it nothing is 
To him who easy owns — 
Is Worth itself or Distance 
He fathoms who obtains.
