## Testo integrale / Full text

US public domain covers works published **through 1929** (95-year rule).
Everything Hemingway published in **1930 or later remains under copyright**.

### Note on "In Our Time"
The task requested **In Our Time (1925)** — the expanded New York (Boni &
Liveright) edition that interleaves the vignettes with full short stories
("Indian Camp", "Big Two-Hearted River", etc.). Project Gutenberg only hosts the
earlier **1924 Paris *in our time*** (ID 61085), the slim chapbook of 18
prose vignettes (the "chapter" interchapters) **without** the full stories. That
1924 edition is what was downloaded and saved as `In_Our_Time_1924.md`. The
1925 expanded *In Our Time* is **not** separately available on Gutenberg; several
of its stories do appear within the 1924 vignettes and within other collections.

## Requested but UNAVAILABLE
- **The Torrents of Spring (1926)** — would be US public domain by date, but
  **not hosted on Project Gutenberg** (a `torrents of spring` search returns only
  Turgenev's unrelated novel). Recorded UNAVAILABLE; not fabricated.

## Excluded — still under US copyright (1930+)
Death in the Afternoon (1932), Winner Take Nothing (1933), Green Hills of Africa
(1935), To Have and Have Not (1937), For Whom the Bell Tolls (1940), Across the
River and Into the Trees (1950), The Old Man and the Sea (1952), and all
posthumous works (A Moveable Feast 1964, Islands in the Stream 1970, The Garden
of Eden 1986, etc.).
