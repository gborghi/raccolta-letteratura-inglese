# 762. The Whole of it came not at once —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Grief and Loss · Melancholy · Elegy]]

> [!abstract] 
> A lyric on protracted suffering as 'murder by degrees,' likened to a cat toying with a mouse; quick death preferred to dying half and rallying. Themes: suffering, mortality, cruelty of prolonged pain.
