# 754. My Life had stood — a Loaded Gun —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Death · Mortality · Immortality]]

> [!abstract] 
> Dickinson's enigmatic masterpiece: the speaker as a loaded gun claimed and wielded by a Master/Owner, exploring agency, power, lethal force, and the paradox of power to kill without power to die. Themes: power, identity, self and soul, the riddle of mortality and service.
