# 1257. Dominion lasts until obtained —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Unrequited Frustrated Love · Unattainable Beloved · Petrarchan Sonnet]]

> [!abstract] 
> Power and possession last only until gained, while the ungrasped belongs eternally; the unattainable and transience of dominion.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Dominion lasts until obtained — 
Possession just as long — 
But these — endowing as they flit 
Eternally belong. 
 
How everlasting are the Lips 
Known only to the Dew — 
These are the Brides of permanence 
Supplanting me and you.
