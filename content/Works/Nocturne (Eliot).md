*by Eliot*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Night · Fire · Comic Verse Song]]

> [!abstract] 
> Ironic Laforguean sonnet staging Romeo and Juliet under a 'bored but courteous moon'; the poet-puppeteer has Romeo murdered for an effective bloody climax, mocking romantic love.
