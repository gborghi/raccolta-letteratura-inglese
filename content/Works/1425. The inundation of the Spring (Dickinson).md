# 1425. The inundation of the Spring

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Self-Knowledge · Immortality · Self-Reliance]]

> [!abstract] 
> Spring's flood enlarges and estranges then acclimates the soul; spring, water/flood, the soul, transformation, faith.

## Testo integrale / Full text

The inundation of the Spring 
Enlarges every soul — 
It sweeps the tenement away 
But leaves the Water whole — 
 
In which the soul at first estranged — 
Seeks faintly for its shore 
But acclimated — pines no more 
For that Peninsula —
