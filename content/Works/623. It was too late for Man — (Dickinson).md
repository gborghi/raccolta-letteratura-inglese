# 623. It was too late for Man —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Faith · Providence · Divine Love]]

> [!abstract] 
> When human help fails, prayer and God remain; heaven is most welcome when earth cannot be had. Themes: faith, providence, prayer. Form: lyric/hymn.

## Testo integrale / Full text

It was too late for man, 
But early yet for God ; 
Creation impotent to help, 
But prayer remained our side. 
 
How excellent the heaven, 
When earth cannot be had ; 
How hospitable, then, the face 
Of our old neighbor, God !
