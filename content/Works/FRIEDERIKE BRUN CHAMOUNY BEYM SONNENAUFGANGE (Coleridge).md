# FRIEDERIKE BRUN: CHAMOUNY BEYM SONNENAUFGANGE

*by Coleridge*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Wonder and Gratitude · The Sublime · Sun]]

> [!abstract] 
> Brun's German ode (Coleridge's source for 'Hymn before Sunrise'): the awed beholder of Mont Blanc asks who set its pillars and torrents, and all nature thunders 'Jehovah'; a sublime nature-hymn of wonder and the presence of God.
