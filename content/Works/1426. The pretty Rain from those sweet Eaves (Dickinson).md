# 1426. The pretty Rain from those sweet Eaves

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Appearance vs Reality · Beauty · Eyes and Sight]]

> [!abstract] 
> Tears ('Rain from sweet Eaves') as an involuntary crowning of feeling; emotion, eyes, innocence overcome by fervor.

## Testo integrale / Full text

The pretty Rain from those sweet Eaves 
Her unintending Eyes — 
Took her own Heart, including ours, 
By innocent Surprise — 
 
The wrestle in her simple Throat 
To hold the feeling down 
That vanquished her — defeated Feat — 
Was Fervor's sudden Crown —
