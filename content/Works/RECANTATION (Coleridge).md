*by Coleridge*  ·  **Cluster:** [[The World Upside Down · Madness · Madness]]

> [!abstract] 
> A satirical ballad-fable in which a mob drives an inoffensive ox to madness, allegorizing political panic, mob hysteria and the abuse of the 'mad' label against reformers.
