*by Coleridge*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Transience · Time · Tempus Fugit]]

> [!abstract] 
> A penitential lyric lamenting hours wasted in sloth and idle revelry, wishing for time recovered to pursue learning, and concluding that experience and wisdom come only with years.
