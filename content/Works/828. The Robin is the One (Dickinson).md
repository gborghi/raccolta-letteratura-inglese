# 828. The Robin is the One

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Birds · Music and Song · Tears]]

> [!abstract] 
> A bird lyric celebrating the robin who heralds March and April and submits, speechless from her nest, that home, certainty and sanctity are best. Joins birds, spring, nature and domestic devotion.
