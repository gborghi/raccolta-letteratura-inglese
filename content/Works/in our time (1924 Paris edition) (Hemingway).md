# in our time (1924 Paris edition)

*by Hemingway*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Disillusionment · Odysseus Ulysses · Grace Under Pressure Stoic Endurance]]

> [!abstract] 
> A sequence of eighteen terse prose vignettes (war on the Italian/Greek fronts, the Greco-Turkish evacuation, bullfighting, executions and a hanging) in stripped, imagistic modern style; central themes are war's brutality and aftermath, violent death, disillusionment and stoic endurance.
