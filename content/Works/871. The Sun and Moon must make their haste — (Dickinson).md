*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Faith · Providence · Divine Love]]

> [!abstract] 
> A hymnlike lyric in which the Lord's burning glory eclipses sun, moon, and stars, yet He kindles a single candle 'entirely for Thee' — a meditation on divine love, providence, and consolation.

## Testo integrale / Full text

The Sun and Moon must make their haste —  
The Stars express around  
For in the Zones of Paradise  
The Lord alone is burned —  

His Eye, it is the East and West —  
The North and South when He  
Do concentrate His Countenance  
Like Glow Worms, flee away —  

Oh Poor and Far —  
Oh Hindred Eye  
That hunted for the Day —  
The Lord a Candle entertains  
Entirely for Thee —
