*by Keats*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Satire of Society · Epigram · Satire]]

> [!abstract] 
> A refrain song cataloguing comic mishaps and human absurdities, each capped by 'Huzza for folly O!'. It is a festive satire of human folly built on the figure of the fool.
