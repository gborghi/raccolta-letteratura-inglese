# 196. We don't cry — Tim and I,

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Child · Orphan]]

> [!abstract] 
> A lyric in a child's voice on hidden grief, prayer, and a wish to die simultaneously with a beloved companion.

## Testo integrale / Full text

We don't cry — Tim and I, 
We are far too grand — 
But we bolt the door tight 
To prevent a friend — 
 
Then we hide our brave face 
Deep in our hand — 
Not to cry — Tim and I — 
We are far too grand — 
 
Nor to dream — he and me — 
Do we condescend — 
We just shut our brown eye 
To see to the end — 
 
Tim — see Cottages — 
But, Oh, so high! 
Then — we shake — Tim and I — 
And lest I — cry — 
 
Tim — reads a little Hymn — 
And we both pray — 
Please, Sir, I and Tim — 
Always lost the way! 
 
We must die — by and by — 
Clergymen say — 
Tim — shall — if I — do — 
I — too — if he — 
 
How shall we arrange it — 
205

Tim — was — so — shy? 
Take us simultaneous — Lord — 
I — "Tim" — and Me!
