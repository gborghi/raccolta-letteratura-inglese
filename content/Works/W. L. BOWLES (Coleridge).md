# W. L. BOWLES

*by Coleridge*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Faith · The Bible · Providence]]

> [!abstract] 
> A meditative lyric (1793) in the manner of Bowles, mourning misspent youth and longing that all who silently mourn might hear joy, accepting man's mixed lot of pleasure, toil and pain by God's will.
