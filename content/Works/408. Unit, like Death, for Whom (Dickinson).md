## Testo integrale / Full text

Unit, like Death, for Whom?  
True, like the Tomb,  
Who tells no secret  
Told to Him —  
The Grave is strict —  
Tickets admit  
Just two — the Bearer —  
And the Borne —  
And seat — just One —  
The Living — tell —  
The Dying — but a Syllable —  
The Coy Dead — None —  
No Chatter — here — no tea —  
So Babbler, and Bohea — stay there —  
But Gravity — and Expectation — and Fear —  
A tremor just, that All's not sure.
