*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Death · Mortality · Immortality]]

> [!abstract] 
> A lyric on death's nearness to every heart, whose 'white inhabitant' the soul must emigrate at any moment, losing its neighbor — a meditation on mortality, the soul, and the imminence of death.
