*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Imagination · Fragment · Heroic Couplet]]

> [!abstract] 
> A declaration of imaginative freedom: shut up in prose like a girl in a closet, the mind, like a caged bird or a star, abolishes captivity at will. Themes of imagination, female confinement, and artistic liberty.

## Testo integrale / Full text

They shut me up in Prose —  
As when a little Girl  
They put me in the Closet —  
Because they liked me "still" —  

Still! Could themself have peeped —  
And seen my Brain — go round —  
They might as wise have lodged a Bird  
For Treason — in the Pound —  

Himself has but to will  
And easy as a Star  
Abolish his Captivity —  
And laugh — No more have I —
