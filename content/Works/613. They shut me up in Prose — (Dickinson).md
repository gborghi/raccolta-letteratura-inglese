*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Imagination · Creativity and Artistic Labor · The One Life (Pantheism)]]

> [!abstract] 
> A declaration of imaginative freedom: shut up in prose like a girl in a closet, the mind, like a caged bird or a star, abolishes captivity at will. Themes of imagination, female confinement, and artistic liberty.
