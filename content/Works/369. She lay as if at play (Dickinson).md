# 369. She lay as if at play

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Sleep · Sleeper Awakes · Resurrection]]

> [!abstract] 
> A dead child is depicted as if merely sleeping mid-play, death softened into deep light slumber.

## Connections

## Testo integrale / Full text

She lay as if at play 
Her life had leaped away — 
Intending to return — 
But not so soon — 
 
Her merry Arms, half dropt — 
As if for lull of sport — 
An instant had forgot — 
The Trick to start — 
 
Her dancing Eyes — ajar — 
As if their Owner were 
Still sparkling through 
For fun — at you — 
 
Her Morning at the door — 
Devising, I am sure — 
To force her sleep — 
So light — so deep —
