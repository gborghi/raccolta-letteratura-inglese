# 602. Of Brussels — it was not —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Nature · Book of Nature · Natural Order]]

> [!abstract] 
> A riddle naming autumn's fallen leaves as a carpet the winds bought of the woods, with the wind as upholsterer of pines and pond. Themes of nature, the seasons, and humble beauty.

## Connections

**Themes & Concepts:** [[Natural Order]], [[Wonder and Gratitude]], [[Transience]], [[Imagination]], [[Humility]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

Of Brussels — it was not — 
Of Kidderminster? Nay — 
The Winds did buy it of the Woods — 
They — sold it unto me 
 
It was a gentle price — 
The poorest — could afford — 
It was within the frugal purse 
Of Beggar — or of Bird — 
 
Of small and spicy Yards — 
In hue — a mellow Dun — 
Of Sunshine — and of Sere — Composed — 
But, principally — of Sun — 
 
The Wind — unrolled it fast — 
And spread it on the Ground — 
Upholsterer of the Pines — is He — 
Upholsterer — of the Pond —
