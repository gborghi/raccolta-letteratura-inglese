# 532. I tried to think a lonelier Thing

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Supernatural · Uncanny · Double]]

> [!abstract] 
> Seeks comfort by imagining a doppelganger of equal loneliness, a mutual pity across the partition of separate cells of horror.

## Connections

**Topoi:** [[Double]]
**Archetypes:** [[Double Doppelganger]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

I tried to think a lonelier Thing 
Than any I had seen — 
Some Polar Expiation — An Omen in the Bone 
Of Death's tremendous nearness — 
 
I probed Retrieveless things 
My Duplicate — to borrow — 
A Haggard Comfort springs 
 
From the belief that Somewhere — 
Within the Clutch of Thought — 
There dwells one other Creature 
Of Heavenly Love — forgot — 
 
I plucked at our Partition 
As One should pry the Walls — 
Between Himself — and Horror's Twin — 
Within Opposing Cells — 
 
I almost strove to clasp his Hand, 
Such Luxury — it grew — 
That as Myself — could pity Him — 
Perhaps he — pitied me —
