## Testo integrale / Full text

September's Baccalaureate 
A combination is 
Of Crickets — Crows — and Retrospects 
And a dissembling Breeze 
 
That hints without assuming — 
An Innuendo sear 
That makes the Heart put up its Fun 
And turn Philosopher.
