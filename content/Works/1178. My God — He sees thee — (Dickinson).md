> [!abstract] 
> A hymn-like lyric urging the sun to shine its best before God, who outraces it; faith, the sun, divine omniscience, and praise of creation.

## Testo integrale / Full text

My God — He sees thee —  
Shine thy best —  
Fling up thy Balls of Gold  
Till every Cubit play with thee  
And every Crescent hold —  
Elate the Acre at his feet —  
Upon his Atom swim —  
Oh Sun — but just a Second's right  
In thy long Race with him!
