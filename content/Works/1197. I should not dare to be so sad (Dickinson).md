# 1197. I should not dare to be so sad

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Grief and Loss · Elegy · Clothing and Costume]]

> [!abstract] 
> A lyric on grief's paradox: a load is impossible only once set down, and the withdrawal of the superhuman support leaves us to perish; grief, suffering, soul.

## Connections

**Themes & Concepts:** [[Grief and Loss]], [[Melancholy]], [[Paradox]], [[Self-Knowledge]], [[Faith and Doubt]]
**Forms & Genres:** [[Lyric]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

I should not dare to be so sad 
So many Years again — 
A Load is first impossible 
When we have put it down — 
 
The Superhuman then withdraws 
And we who never saw 
The Giant at the other side 
Begin to perish now.
