## Testo integrale / Full text

A Sparrow took a Slice of Twig  
And thought it very nice  
I think, because his empty Plate  
Was handed Nature twice —  

Invigorated, waded  
In all the deepest Sky  
Until his little Figure  
Was forfeited away —
