# 573. The Test of Love — is Death —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Martyrdom · Martyr Saint · Cross]]

> [!abstract] 
> Death is the test of love, as Christ 'so loved'; the Cross's request asks acceptance of love's most, overlooking the dust.

## Testo integrale / Full text

The Test of Love — is Death — 
Our Lord — "so loved" — it saith — 
What Largest Lover — hath 
Another — doth — 
 
If smaller Patience — be — 
Through less Infinity — 
If Bravo, sometimes swerve — 
Through fainter Nerve — 
 
Accept its Most — 
And overlook — the Dust — 
Last — Least — 
The Cross' — Request —
