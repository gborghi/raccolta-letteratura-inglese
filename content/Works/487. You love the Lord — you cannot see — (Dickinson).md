# 487. You love the Lord — you cannot see —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Faith · Providence · Divine Love]]

> [!abstract] 
> Contrasts loving an unseen Lord through daily notes/letters with the speaker's own beloved whose house is in Heaven; faith, love, and longing.

## Connections

**Topoi:** [[Love as Religion]]
**Motifs:** [[Letters and Writing]]
**Themes & Concepts:** [[Divine Love]], [[Faith]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

You love the Lord — you cannot see — 
You write Him — every day — 
A little note — when you awake — 
And further in the Day. 
 
An Ample Letter — How you miss — 
And would delight to see — 
But then His House — is but a Step — 
And Mine's — in Heaven — You see.
