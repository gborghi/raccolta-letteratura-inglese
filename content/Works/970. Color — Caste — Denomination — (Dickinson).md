# 970. Color — Caste — Denomination —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Death · Silence · Bells]]

> [!abstract] 
> A lyric on death's democratic leveling that rubs away the brands of color, caste, and creed, transforming chrysalis to equal butterfly; themes of mortality and equality.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Color — Caste — Denomination — 
These — are Time's Affair — 
Death's diviner Classifying 
Does not know they are — 
 
As in sleep — All Hue forgotten — 
Tenets — put behind — 
Death's large — Democratic fingers 
Rub away the Brand — 
 
If Circassian — He is careless — 
If He put away 
Chrysalis of Blonde — or Umber — 
Equal Butterfly — 
 
They emerge from His Obscuring — 
What Death — knows so well — 
Our minuter intuitions — 
Deem unplausible —
