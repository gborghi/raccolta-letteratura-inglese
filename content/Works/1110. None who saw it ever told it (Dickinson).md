# 1110. None who saw it ever told it

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Rose · Desire · Value and Scarcity]]

> [!abstract] 
> A riddling lyric on a hidden treasure as secret as death, whose value depends on rarity — the inexpressible, secrecy and the relation of value to scarcity.

## Testo integrale / Full text

None who saw it ever told it 
'Tis as hid as Death 
Had for that specific treasure 
A departing breath — 
Surfaces may be invested 
Did the Diamond grow 
General as the Dandelion 
Would you serve it so?
