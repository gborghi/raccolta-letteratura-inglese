*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Dreams · Sleep · Resurrection]]

> [!abstract] 
> A graveside lyric in which the speaker tends a beloved's grave, sowing seed and birds' nests so that resurrection-like spring will mark the way; death as sleep and seasonal renewal.
