# 788. Joy to have merited the Pain —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Romantic Love · The Lover · Unrequited Frustrated Love]]

> [!abstract] 
> A lyric of reunion and devotion: joy to have earned pain in compassing Paradise, and to look again with old eyes upon a beloved face whose haunting will last to eternity. Themes: love, suffering rewarded, memory, immortality of devotion.
