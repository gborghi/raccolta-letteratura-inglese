# 149. She went as quiet as the Dew

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Grief and Loss · Elegy · Clothing and Costume]]

> [!abstract] 
> A brief elegy comparing a woman's quiet, irrecoverable death to dew and a fallen star; death, grief and the difficulty of belief.

## Connections

**Motifs:** [[Death]], [[Flowers]], [[Stars]], [[Nature]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

She went as quiet as the dew 
From a familiar flower. 
Not like the dew did she return 
At the accustomed hour ! 
 
She dropt as softly as a star 
From out my summer's eve ; 
Less skilful than Leverrier 
It's sorer to believe!
