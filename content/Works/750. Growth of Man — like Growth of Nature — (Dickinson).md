# 750. Growth of Man — like Growth of Nature —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Nature · Book of Nature · Natural Order]]

> [!abstract] 
> A meditative lyric on inward self-development: like nature, the soul must achieve its ideal through solitary effort, patience, and belief, unaided by any audience. Themes: identity, self-reliance, growth.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Growth of Man — like Growth of Nature — 
Gravitates within — 
Atmosphere, and Sun endorse it — 
Bit it stir — alone — 
 
Each — its difficult Ideal 
Must achieve — Itself — 
Through the solitary prowess 
Of a Silent Life — 
 
Effort — is the sole condition — 
Patience of Itself — 
Patience of opposing forces — 
And intact Belief — 
 
Looking on — is the Department 
Of its Audience — 
But Transaction — is assisted 
By no Countenance —
