# 352. Perhaps I asked too large —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Imagination · Fragment · Heroic Couplet]]

> [!abstract] 
> An epigrammatic lyric claiming a cosmic largeness of desire — skies and firmaments easy to hold, small bundles hard; on imagination and aspiration.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Perhaps I asked too large — 
I take — no less than skies — 
For Earths, grow thick as 
Berries, in my native town — 
 
My Basket holds — just — Firmaments — 
Those — dangle easy — on my arm, 
But smaller bundles — Cram.
