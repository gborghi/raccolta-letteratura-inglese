# The Death of Saint Narcissus

*by Eliot*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Martyrdom · The Martyr Saint · The Cross]]

> [!abstract] 
> Visionary free-verse poem of a self-absorbed ascetic who, transformed through tree, fish and girl, becomes a dancer before God and dies pierced by burning arrows; self-love, martyrdom, mortification of the flesh, and the gray rock of The Waste Land.
