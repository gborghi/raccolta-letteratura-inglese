*by Coleridge*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Satire of Society · Epigram · Satire]]

> [!abstract] 
> A cluster of satirical epigrams (several adapted from Wernicke) attacking a treacherous friend, the spread of scandal and lies, and a sneering miser; themes of betrayed friendship, falsehood, and avarice.
