## Testo integrale / Full text

It knew no lapse, nor Diminuation —  
But large — serene —  
Burned on — until through Dissolution —  
It failed from Men —  

I could not deem these Planetary forces  
Annulled —  
But suffered an Exchange of Territory —  
Or World —
