# 907. Till Death — is narrow Loving —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Romantic Love · The Lover · Unrequited Frustrated Love]]

> [!abstract] 
> A lyric arguing that love bounded by death is narrow, while true love so resembles the lost beloved that the lover abdicates self — a meditation on love, grief, self-abnegation, and immortality.
