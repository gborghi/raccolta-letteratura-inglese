# 46. I keep my pledge.

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Death · Mortality · Immortality]]

> [!abstract] 
> A vow-lyric: spared by death, the speaker renews her pledge with a rose, sworn by bee, daisy and bobolink that blossom and soul will come again; faith, fidelity and renewal.
