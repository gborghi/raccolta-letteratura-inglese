> [!abstract] 
> A vow-lyric: spared by death, the speaker renews her pledge with a rose, sworn by bee, daisy and bobolink that blossom and soul will come again; faith, fidelity and renewal.

## Testo integrale / Full text

I keep my pledge.  
I was not called —  
Death did not notice me.  
I bring my Rose.  
I plight again,  
By every sainted Bee —  
By Daisy called from hillside —  
by Bobolink from lane.  
Blossom and I —  
Her oath, and mine —  
Will surely come again.
