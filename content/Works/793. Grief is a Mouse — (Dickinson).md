# 793. Grief is a Mouse —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Joy · Animals · Riddle]]

> [!abstract] 
> A lyric of riddling definitions personifying grief as mouse, thief, juggler and gourmand, emphasizing its secrecy and refusal to confess even under torture. Explores the concealment and silent endurance of inward suffering.
