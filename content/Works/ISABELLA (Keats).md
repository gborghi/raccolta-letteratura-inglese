# ISABELLA

*by Keats*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Ballad Ballade · Narrative Poem · The Last Stand]]

> [!abstract] 
> Narrative poem from Boccaccio in ottava rima: Isabella's brothers murder her lover Lorenzo for money; she keeps his severed head in a pot of basil until she dies of grief; love, murder, grief and the cruelty of commerce.
