# 97. The rainbow never tells me

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Nature · Book of Nature · Natural Order]]

> [!abstract] 
> A lyric setting nature's wordless evidence (rainbow, flowers, birds) above classical philosophy and Cato; the book of nature outpersuades reasoned argument.

## Testo integrale / Full text

The rainbow never tells me 
That gust and storm are by, 
Yet is she more convincing 
Than Philosophy. 
 
My flowers turn from Forums — 
Yet eloquent declare 
What Cato couldn't prove me 
Except the birds were here!
