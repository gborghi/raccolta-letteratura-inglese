# 1279. The Way to know the Bobolink

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Birds · Music and Song · Nightingale]]

> [!abstract] 
> The bobolink as joyous, impudent, seditious sorcerer of the meadow whose departure nullifies the scene; the bird-poet of nature.

## Testo integrale / Full text

The Way to know the Bobolink 
From every other Bird 
Precisely as the Joy of him — 
Obliged to be inferred. 
 
Of impudent Habiliment 
Attired to defy, 
Impertinence subordinate 
At times to Majesty. 
 
Of Sentiments seditious 
Amenable to Law — 
As Heresies of Transport 
Or Puck's Apostacy. 
 
Extrinsic to Attention 
Too intimate with Joy — 
He compliments existence 
Until allured away 
 
By Seasons or his Children — 
Adult and urgent grown — 
Or unforeseen aggrandizement 
Or, happily, Renown — 
 
By Contrast certifying 
The Bird of Birds is gone — 
How nullified the Meadow — 
Her Sorcerer withdrawn!
