# Ode on Indolence

*by Keats*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Greek Mythology · Classical Antiquity · Ode]]

> [!abstract] 
> An ode in which three urn-like figures, Love, Ambition and Poesy, drift past the indolent speaker who bids them farewell to keep his blissful idleness. It treats indolence, the artist's vocation, ambition, and the Grecian urn.
