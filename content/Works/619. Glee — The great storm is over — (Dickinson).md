# 619. Glee — The great storm is over —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Death · Silence · Bells]]

> [!abstract] 
> A ballad-like lyric of a shipwreck: four saved, forty drowned; ringing and tolling, then silence as children ask after the lost. Themes: death, grief, the sea. Form: ballad/lyric.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Glee ! the great storm is over ! 
Four have recovered the land ; 
Forty gone down together 
Into the boiling sand. 
 
Ring, for the scant salvation ! 
Toll, for the bonnie souls, — 
Neighbor and friend and bridegroom, 
Spinning upon the shoals ! 
 
How they will tell the shipwreck 
When winter shakes the door, 
Till the children ask, "But the forty ? 
Did they come back no more ?" 
 
Then a silence suffuses the story, 
And a softness the teller's eye ; 
And the children no further question, 
And only the waves reply.
