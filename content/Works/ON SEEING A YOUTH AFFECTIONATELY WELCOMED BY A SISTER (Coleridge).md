# ON SEEING A YOUTH AFFECTIONATELY WELCOMED BY A SISTER

*by Coleridge*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Grief and Loss · Melancholy · Elegy]]

> [!abstract] 
> A sonnet of mourning recalling the poet's dead sister's gentle virtues, prompted by the sight of another youth's sisterly welcome, finding bitter comfort that for her the grave is peace.
