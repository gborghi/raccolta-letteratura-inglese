# DEJECTION: AN ODE

*by Coleridge*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Nature · The Book of Nature · The Natural Order]]

> [!abstract] 
> Major Romantic ode lamenting the loss of joy and the failure of the imaginative shaping spirit; nature lives only as the soul gives. Storm, Aeolian lute, and inward fountains figure spiritual desolation.
