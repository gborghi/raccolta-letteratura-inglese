# 985. The Missing All — prevented Me

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Spiritual Emptiness · Waste Land (Sterility) · Greek Tragedy]]

> [!abstract] 
> A lyric in which an absolute loss (the Missing All) renders cosmic catastrophe trivial, the speaker unmoved even by a world's departure; explores grief, loss, and numb despair.

## Connections

**Motifs:** [[Sun]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

The Missing All — prevented Me 
From missing minor Things. 
If nothing larger than a World's 
Departure from a Hinge — 
Or Sun's extinction, be observed — 
'Twas not so large that I 
Could lift my Forehead from my work 
For Curiosity.
