# 1122. 'Tis my first night beneath the Sun

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Sun · Light and Dark · Fire]]

> [!abstract] 
> A riddling lyric on distance and expectation beneath the sun, whose delights are confided to those who visit from afar — distance, longing and nature.

## Connections

**Motifs:** [[Sun]], [[Wind]]
**Themes & Concepts:** [[Unrequited Frustrated Love]], [[Exile]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

'Tis my first night beneath the Sun 
If I should spend it here — 
Above him is too low a height 
For his Barometer 
Who Airs of expectation breathes 
And takes the Wind at prime — 
But Distance his Delights confides 
To those who visit him —
