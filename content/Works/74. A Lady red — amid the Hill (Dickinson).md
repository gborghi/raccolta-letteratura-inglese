# 74. A Lady red — amid the Hill

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Flowers · Spring · Garden]]

> [!abstract] 
> Red and white flowers sleeping in the hills as figures of seasonal resurrection; nature's quiet annual rebirth treated with playful awe.

## Testo integrale / Full text

A Lady red — amid the Hill 
Her annual secret keeps! 
A Lady white, within the Field 
In placid Lily sleeps! 
 
The tidy Breezes, with their Brooms — 
Sweep vale — and hill — and tree! 
Prithee, My pretty Housewives! 
Who may expected be? 
 
The Neighbors do not yet suspect! 
The Woods exchange a smile! 
Orchard, and Buttercup, and Bird — 
In such a little while! 
 
And yet, how still the Landscape stands! 
How nonchalant the Hedge! 
As if the "Resurrection" 
Were nothing very strange!
