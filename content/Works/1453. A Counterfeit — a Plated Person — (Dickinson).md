# 1453. A Counterfeit — a Plated Person —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Appearance vs Reality · Beauty · Eyes and Sight]]

> [!abstract] 
> Refuses to be a counterfeit, plated person; truth as health and safety, the lie as meagre exile — integrity versus deception.

## Connections

**Themes & Concepts:** [[Truth]], [[Deception]], [[Identity]], [[Mortality]]
**Forms & Genres:** [[Epigram]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

A Counterfeit — a Plated Person — 
I would not be — 
Whatever strata of Iniquity 
My Nature underlie — 
Truth is good Health — and Safety, and the Sky. 
How meagre, what an Exile — is a Lie, 
And Vocal — when we die —
