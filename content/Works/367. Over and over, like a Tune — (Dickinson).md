## Testo integrale / Full text

Over and over, like a Tune —  
The Recollection plays —  
Drums off the Phantom Battlements  
Cornets of Paradise —  

Snatches, from Baptized Generations —  
Cadences too grand  
But for the Justified Processions  
At the Lord's Right hand.
