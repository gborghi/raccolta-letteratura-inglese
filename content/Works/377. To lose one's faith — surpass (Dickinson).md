*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Faith · Providence · Divine Love]]

> [!abstract] 
> Lost faith is irreplaceable unlike a depleted estate; annihilating one clause beggars one's being.

## Connections

**Motifs:** [[Money and Gold]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

To lose one's faith — surpass  
The loss of an Estate —  
Because Estates can be  
Replenished — faith cannot —  

Inherited with Life —  
Belief — but once — can be —  
Annihilate a single clause —  
And Being's — Beggary —
