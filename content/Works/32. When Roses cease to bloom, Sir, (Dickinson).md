# 32. When Roses cease to bloom, Sir,

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Death · Mortality · Immortality]]

> [!abstract] 
> A lyric anticipating the speaker's own death (the gathering hand lying idle in Auburn cemetery) once summer's flowers and bees are gone; mortality and transience.
