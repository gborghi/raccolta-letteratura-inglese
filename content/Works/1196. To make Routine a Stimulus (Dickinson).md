## Testo integrale / Full text

To make Routine a Stimulus  
Remember it can cease —  
Capacity to Terminate  
Is a Specific Grace —  
Of Retrospect the Arrow  
That power to repair  
Departed with the Torment  
Become, alas, more fair —
