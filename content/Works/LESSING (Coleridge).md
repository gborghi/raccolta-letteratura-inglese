# LESSING

*by Coleridge*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Romantic Love · The Lover · Unrequited Frustrated Love]]

> [!abstract] 
> Lessing's light lyric 'Die Namen': the poet asks his love by what poetic name to call her, and she answers that names are mere sounds, asking only to be called 'his'; a witty epigram-song on love and naming.
