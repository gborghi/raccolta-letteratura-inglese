*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Vanitas · Contemptus Mundi · Vanity of Worldly Things]]

> [!abstract] 
> An emblematic lyric: the fully blossomed rose, food for a bee, becomes an image of bright mortality and the forfeit of beauty; transience and the rose.

## Testo integrale / Full text

A full fed Rose on meals of Tint  
A Dinner for a Bee  
In process of the Noon became -  
Each bright Mortality  
The Forfeit is of Creature fair  
Itself, adored before  
Submitting for our unknown sake  
To be esteemed no more —
