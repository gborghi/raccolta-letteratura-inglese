# Three Stories & Ten Poems

*by Hemingway*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Disillusionment · Odysseus Ulysses · Grace Under Pressure Stoic Endurance]]

> [!abstract] 
> Hemingway's 1923 debut: three terse, plainspoken short stories (sexual disillusionment in rural Michigan, a failed fishing outing and strained marriage in the Italian Alps, a boy's love for his crooked jockey father who dies on the track) plus ten poems dominated by war, soldiers' deaths, the sea, suicide and lost youth. Central themes: disillusionment, loss of innocence, mortality and the cost of war, rendered in his early stripped-down style.
