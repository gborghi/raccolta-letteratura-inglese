# 134. Perhaps you'd like to buy a flower,

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Flowers · Spring · Garden]]

> [!abstract] 
> A playful lyric refusing to sell a flower but offering to lend it until spring's daffodils and bees return; flowers, seasons, and the small economy of nature's gifts.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Perhaps you'd like to buy a flower ? 
But I could never sell. 
If you would like to borrow 
Until the daffodil 
 
Unties her yellow bonnet 
Beneath the village door, 
Until the bees, from clover rows 
Their hock and sherry draw, 
 
Why, I will lend until just then, 
But not an hour more!
