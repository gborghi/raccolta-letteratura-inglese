# 780. The Truth — is stirless —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Artist and Society · Truth · Regency Georgian England]]

> [!abstract] 
> A lyric on Truth as an unmoving force that stands without bone or prop even as cedars swerve and mountains lean, holding boldly up all who trust her. Themes: truth, constancy, faith, the immovable.

## Testo integrale / Full text

The Truth — is stirless — 
Other force — may be presumed to move — 
This — then — is best for confidence — 
When oldest Cedars swerve — 
 
And Oaks untwist their fists — 
And Mountains — feeble — lean — 
How excellent a Body, that 
Stands without a Bone — 
 
How vigorous a Force 
That holds without a Prop — 
Truth stays Herself — and every man 
That trusts Her — boldly up —
