*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Mortality · Grave · Memento Mori]]

> [!abstract] 
> A compressed lyric contrasting Life and Death as differing plans, the foot upon the earth straining toward achievement, the foot upon the grave toward conclusion. Themes of mortality and the soul.

## Connections

**Motifs:** [[Death]], [[Grave]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

As plan for Noon and plan for Night  
So differ Life and Death  
In positive Prospective —  
The Foot upon the Earth  

At Distance, and Achievement, strains,  
The Foot upon the Grave  
Makes effort at conclusion  
Assisted faint of Love.
