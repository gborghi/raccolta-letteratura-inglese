# 562. Conjecturing a Climate

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Seasons · Winter · Autumn]]

> [!abstract] 
> Imagining a warmer climate sharpens winter; fancy turns to a fictitious country to palliate cold that cannot be erased.

## Connections

**Motifs:** [[Winter]], [[Seasons]], [[Sun]]
**Themes & Concepts:** [[Melancholy]], [[Imagination]], [[Nostalgia]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

Conjecturing a Climate 
Of unsuspended Suns — 
Adds poignancy to Winter — 
The Shivering Fancy turns 
 
To a fictitious Country 
To palliate a Cold — 
Not obviated of Degree — 
Nor erased — of Latitude —
