# 1259. A Wind that rose

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Nature · Book of Nature · Natural Order]]

> [!abstract] 
> An invisible inner wind beyond nature, a lone delight like separation's swell restored to the invisible; wind as spirit and the sublime.

## Testo integrale / Full text

A Wind that rose 
Though not a Leaf 
In any Forest stirred 
But with itself did cold engage 
Beyond the Realm of Bird — 
A Wind that woke a lone Delight 
Like Separation's Swell 
Restored in Arctic Confidence 
To the Invisible —
