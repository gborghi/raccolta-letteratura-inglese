# 624. Forever — is composed of Nows —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Time · Tempus Fugit · Clock]]

> [!abstract] 
> A philosophical lyric arguing eternity is made of present moments; time and infinity collapsed into the now. Themes: time, immortality, eternity. Form: lyric.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Forever — is composed of Nows — 
'Tis not a different time — 
Except for Infiniteness — 
And Latitude of Home — 
 
From this — experienced Here — 
Remove the Dates — to These — 
Let Months dissolve in further Months — 
And Years — exhale in Years — 
 
Without Debate — or Pause — 
Or Celebrated Days — 
No different Our Years would be 
From Anno Domini's —
