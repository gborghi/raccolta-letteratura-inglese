*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Imagination · Fragment · Heroic Couplet]]

> [!abstract] 
> Hope builds a sill-less house of pure pinnacle; an abstract emblem of hope as supreme, groundless edifice.

## Connections

**Themes & Concepts:** [[Imagination]], [[Faith]], [[Paradox]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

The way Hope builds his House  
It is not with a sill —  
Nor Rafter — has that Edifice  
But only Pinnacle —  

Abode in as supreme  
This superficies  
As if it were of Ledges smit  
Or mortised with the Laws —
