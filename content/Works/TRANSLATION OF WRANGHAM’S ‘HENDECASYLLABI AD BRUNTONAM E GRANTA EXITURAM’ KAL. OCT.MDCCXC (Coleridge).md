*by Coleridge*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Sonnet · Beauty · Shakespearean Sonnet]]

> [!abstract] 
> Tribute to the actress Miss Brunton, praising her truth, pity and tragic art (Monimia, Juliet, Euphrasia); art, beauty, theatrical sorrow and Pierian garlands.
