# HAMLET

*by Shakespeare*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Power and Corruption · The Crown · Pride]]

> [!abstract] 
> Shakespeare's great revenge tragedy: the Danish prince, charged by his father's ghost to avenge a murder by his usurping uncle, confronts mortality, madness, deception and the gap between appearance and reality in soliloquy-driven blank verse.
