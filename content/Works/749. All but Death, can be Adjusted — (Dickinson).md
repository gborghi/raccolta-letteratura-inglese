# 749. All but Death, can be Adjusted —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Transience · Time · Tempus Fugit]]

> [!abstract] 
> A compact lyric arguing that everything in life and history is mutable and repairable except death, which alone is exempt from change. Themes: mortality, transience, the unique finality of death.
