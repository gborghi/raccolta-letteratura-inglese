*by Coleridge*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Faith and Doubt · Doubt · Allegory]]

> [!abstract] 
> A defence of Berengar, who recanted under fear of torture, urging that we judge the man within his dark age and not scorn the timid dawn of truth. Themes: faith and doubt, conscience, martyrdom, progress of truth.
