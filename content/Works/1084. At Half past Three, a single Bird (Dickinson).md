# 1084. At Half past Three, a single Bird

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Birds · Music and Song · Nightingale]]

> [!abstract] 
> A timed lyric tracing a bird's dawn song from a cautious term to full silver principle to vanished presence, leaving only circumference between.

## Connections

**Motifs:** [[Birds]], [[Music and Song]], [[Time]], [[Dawn]]
**Themes & Concepts:** [[Transience]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

At Half past Three, a single Bird 
Unto a silent Sky 
Propounded but a single term 
Of cautious melody. 
 
At Half past Four, Experiment 
Had subjugated test 
And lo, Her silver Principle 
Supplanted all the rest. 
 
At Half past Seven, Element 
Nor Implement, be seen — 
And Place was where the Presence was 
Circumference between.
