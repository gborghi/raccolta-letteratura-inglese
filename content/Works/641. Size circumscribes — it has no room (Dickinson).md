## Testo integrale / Full text

Size circumscribes — it has no room  
For petty furniture —  
The Giant tolerates no Gnat  
For Ease of Gianture —  

Repudiates it, all the more —  
Because intrinsic size  
Ignores the possibility  
Of Calumnies — or Flies.
