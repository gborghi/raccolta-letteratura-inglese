# 399. A House upon the Height —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Supernatural · Uncanny · Double]]

> [!abstract] 
> An unreachable house on the height, untouched by wagon or death, holds an empty pane and a fate only conjecture and God know.

## Connections

**Motifs:** [[Supernatural]], [[Death]], [[Window]]
**Themes & Concepts:** [[Uncanny]], [[Terror and Horror]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

A House upon the Height — 
That Wagon never reached — 
No Dead, were ever carried down — 
No Peddler's Cart — approached — 
 
Whose Chimney never smoked — 
Whose Windows — Night and Morn — 
Caught Sunrise first — and Sunset — last — 
Then — held an Empty Pane — 
 
Whose fate — Conjecture knew — 
No other neighbor — did — 
And what it was — we never lisped — 
Because He — never told —
