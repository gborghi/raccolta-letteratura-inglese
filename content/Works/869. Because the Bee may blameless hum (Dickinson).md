# 869. Because the Bee may blameless hum

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Lyric · Romantic Love · Marriage and Fidelity]]

> [!abstract] 
> A lyric of devotion in which the speaker would become bee, flower, or robin to worship the beloved freely — a meditation on love as worship and on nature as the language of adoration.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Because the Bee may blameless hum 
For Thee a Bee do I become 
List even unto Me. 
 
Because the Flowers unafraid 
May lift a look on thine, a Maid 
Alway a Flower would be. 
 
Nor Robins, Robins need not hide 
When Thou upon their Crypts intrude 
So Wings bestow on Me 
Or Petals, or a Dower of Buzz 
That Bee to ride, or Flower of Furze 
I that way worship Thee.
