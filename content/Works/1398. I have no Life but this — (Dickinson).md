# 1398. I have no Life but this —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Lyric · Romantic Love · Marriage and Fidelity]]

> [!abstract] 
> A love lyric collapsing life, death, and the afterlife into the beloved's realm; love elevated to religion.

## Testo integrale / Full text

I have no Life but this — 
To lead it here — 
Nor any Death — but lest 
Dispelled from there — 
 
Nor tie to Earths to come — 
Nor Action new — 
Except through this extent — 
The Realm of you —
