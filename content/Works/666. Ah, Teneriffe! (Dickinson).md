# 666. Ah, Teneriffe!

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Wonder and Gratitude · Sublime · Storm]]

> [!abstract] 
> An apostrophe to the unreachable mountain Teneriffe, clad in ice and steel, indifferent to sunset's homage. Central themes: the sublime, nature's grandeur, awe, the unattainable.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Ah, Teneriffe! 
Retreating Mountain! 
Purples of Ages — pause for you — 
Sunset — reviews her Sapphire Regiment — 
Day — drops you her Red Adieu! 
 
Still — Clad in your Mail of ices — 
Thigh of Granite — and thew — of Steel — 
Heedless — alike — of pomp — or parting 
 
Ah, Teneriffe! 
I'm kneeling — still —
