# SELF-KNOWLEDGE

*by Coleridge*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Self-Knowledge · Immortality · Self-Reliance]]

> [!abstract] 
> A meditative poem on the Delphic maxim 'know thyself', concluding that man is an unknowable fluxion and should rather strive to know God.
