# WORK WITHOUT HOPE

*by Coleridge*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Seasons · Winter · Spring]]

> [!abstract] 
> A sonnet contrasting busy springtime Nature with the poet, 'the sole unbusy thing', concluding that work without hope draws nectar in a sieve. Themes: despair, creative sterility, transience, melancholy.
