# 842. Good to hide, and hear 'em hunt!

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[White and Red · Riddle · Secrecy and Concealment]]

> [!abstract] 
> A playful epigram on the pleasures of hiding and being found, fox fitting hound, of knowing and telling to the rare, not-too-dull ear. Treats secrecy, concealment and the desire to be understood.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Good to hide, and hear 'em hunt! 
Better, to be found, 
If one care to, that is, 
The Fox fits the Hound — 
 
Good to know, and not tell, 
Best, to know and tell, 
Can one find the rare Ear 
Not too dull —
