*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Alienation · Exile · Fog and Smoke]]

> [!abstract] 
> A lyric on a woman renouncing the 'Playthings' of her life for the honorable work of wife; whatever amplitude she lost lies unmentioned like pearl in the sea. On marriage, gender, sacrifice and the hidden self.
