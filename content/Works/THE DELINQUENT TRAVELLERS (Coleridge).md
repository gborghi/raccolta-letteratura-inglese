*by Coleridge*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Satire of Society · Epigram · Satire]]

> [!abstract] 
> A satirical verse on the post-war fashion for restless travel and tourism, mock-celebrating transported convicts as the truest 'delinquent travellers'. Themes: satire of society, the journey, social criticism.
