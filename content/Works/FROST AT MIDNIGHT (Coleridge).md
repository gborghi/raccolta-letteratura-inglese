# FROST AT MIDNIGHT

*by Coleridge*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Maturation and Growth · Childhood · Innocence and Experience]]

> [!abstract] 
> A meditative blank-verse conversation poem at the fireside in which the poet, watching his sleeping infant, moves from memory to a prayer that the child be reared by Nature as God's eternal language; themes of childhood, memory, nature and faith.
