# 1492. "And with what body do they come?" —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Bible · Sin and the Fall · Fall from Innocence]]

> [!abstract] 
> An ecstatic riff on Paul's resurrection question, the soul rejoicing that the dead come with a real face and eyes; faith, immortality and the body's return.

## Connections

**Themes & Concepts:** [[Immortality]], [[Faith]], [[Joy]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

"And with what body do they come?" — 
Then they do come — Rejoice! 
What Door — What Hour — Run — run — My Soul! 
Illuminate the House! 
 
"Body!" Then real — a Face and Eyes — 
To know that it is them! 
Paul knew the Man that knew the News — 
He passed through Bethlehem —
