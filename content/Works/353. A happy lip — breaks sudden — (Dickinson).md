# 353. A happy lip — breaks sudden —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Short Story · Concealment Hidden Identity · Justice Right and Wrong]]

> [!abstract] 
> A lyric distinguishing spontaneous joy from a patient, gilded merriment that masks pain from unqualified observers; on appearance versus reality and concealed grief.

## Connections

**Motifs:** [[Concealment Hidden Identity]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

A happy lip — breaks sudden — 
It doesn't state you how 
It contemplated — smiling — 
Just consummated — now — 
But this one, wears its merriment 
So patient — like a pain — 
Fresh gilded — to elude the eyes 
Unqualified, to scan —
