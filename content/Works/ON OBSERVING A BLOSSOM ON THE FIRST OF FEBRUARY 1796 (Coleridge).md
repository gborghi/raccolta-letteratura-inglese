*by Coleridge*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Lyric · Flowers · Stars]]

> [!abstract] 
> A lyric on a too-early blossom doomed by winter, likened to a consumptive girl, to Chatterton, and to Poland's killed hope; themes of transience, mortality, and the fragility of hope.
