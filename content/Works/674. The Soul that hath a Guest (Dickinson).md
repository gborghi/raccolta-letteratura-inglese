# 674. The Soul that hath a Guest

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Self-Knowledge · Immortality · Self-Reliance]]

> [!abstract] 
> A brief lyric: a soul hosting a divine guest needs no outward society. Central themes: the self and soul, divine presence, inwardness.

## Testo integrale / Full text

The Soul that hath a Guest 
Doth seldom go abroad — 
Diviner Crowd at Home — 
Obliterate the need — 
 
And Courtesy forbid 
A Host's departure when 
Upon Himself be visiting 
The Emperor of Men —
