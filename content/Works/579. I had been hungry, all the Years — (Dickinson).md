*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Romantic Love · The Lover · Unrequited Frustrated Love]]

> [!abstract] 
> An allegory of long deprivation: when long-craved plenty finally arrives it sickens and dissatisfies, for hunger belongs to those outside the window. Themes of desire, deprivation, and the loss of longing in fulfillment.
