*by Coleridge*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Parody · Heroic Couplet · Mock Epic]]

> [!abstract] 
> A mock-elegy lamenting a ruined tea-kettle with funereal pomp, parodying the elegiac monody and praising tea over wine, before Hope consoles with a new kettle.
