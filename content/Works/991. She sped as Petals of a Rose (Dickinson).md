# 991. She sped as Petals of a Rose

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Grief and Loss · Elegy · Clothing and Costume]]

> [!abstract] 
> An elegy figuring a woman's death as rose-petals scattered by the wind, a frail aristocrat of time leaving a slight default on nature but mountains of grief in the heart.

## Testo integrale / Full text

She sped as Petals of a Rose 
Offended by the Wind — 
A frail Aristocrat of Time 
Indemnity to find — 
Leaving on nature — a Default 
As Cricket or as Bee — 
But Andes in the Bosoms where 
She had begun to lie —
