# On Death

*by Keats*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Dreams · Sleep · Resurrection]]

> [!abstract] 
> A short meditative lyric likening death to sleep and life to a dream, questioning why men cling to a life of woe; its central matter is mortality and the transience of earthly pleasure.
