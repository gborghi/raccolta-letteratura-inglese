# JEUX D’ESPRIT: MY GODMOTHER’S BEARD

*by Coleridge*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Satire of Society · Epigram · Satire]]

> [!abstract] 
> A mythological jeu d'esprit: chaste Pallas grants Mrs. Mundy a beard to repel suitors, with the catch that kisses are barred only until she shaves; comic use of Greek myth to mock female vanity and chastity.
