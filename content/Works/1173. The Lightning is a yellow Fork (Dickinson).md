# 1173. The Lightning is a yellow Fork

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Sun · Light and Dark · Fire]]

> [!abstract] 
> An emblematic lyric figuring lightning as cutlery dropped from celestial tables, revealing the dark mansions of the divine; nature, the sublime, wonder.

## Connections

**Topoi:** [[Book of Nature]]
**Motifs:** [[Fire]], [[Storm]], [[Nature]], [[Light and Dark]]
**Themes & Concepts:** [[Sublime]], [[Wonder and Gratitude]]
**Forms & Genres:** [[Lyric]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

The Lightning is a yellow Fork 
From Tables in the sky 
By inadvertent fingers dropt 
The awful Cutlery 
 
Of mansions never quite disclosed 
And never quite concealed 
The Apparatus of the Dark 
To ignorance revealed.
