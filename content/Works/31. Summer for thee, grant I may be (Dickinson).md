## Testo integrale / Full text

Summer for thee, grant I may be  
When Summer days are flown!  
Thy music still, when Whippowil  
And Oriole - are done!  

For thee to bloom, I'll skip the tomb  
And row my blossoms o'er!  
Pray gather me -  
   Anemone -  
Thy flower - forevermore!
