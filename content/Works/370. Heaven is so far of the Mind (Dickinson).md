# 370. Heaven is so far of the Mind

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Faith · Providence · Divine Love]]

> [!abstract] 
> Heaven is internalized as a function of mind and capacity, near or infinitely far according to desire.

## Connections

**Themes & Concepts:** [[Heaven and the Afterlife]], [[Imagination]], [[Self-Knowledge]], [[Faith]], [[Paradox]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

Heaven is so far of the Mind 
That were the Mind dissolved — 
The Site — of it — by Architect 
Could not again be proved — 
 
'Tis vast — as our Capacity — 
As fair — as our idea — 
To Him of adequate desire 
No further 'tis, than Here —
