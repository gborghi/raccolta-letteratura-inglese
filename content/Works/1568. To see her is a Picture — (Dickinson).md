# 1568. To see her is a Picture —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Friendship · Tears]]

> [!abstract] 
> A lyric of devoted love and friendship: to see, hear and know her is picture, tune and innocent intemperance, while to lack her is affliction and to own her sunlit warmth.

## Connections

**Motifs:** [[Music and Song]], [[Sun]]
**Themes & Concepts:** [[Friendship]], [[Romantic Love]], [[Beauty]]
**Forms & Genres:** [[Lyric]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

To see her is a Picture — 
To hear her is a Tune — 
To know her an Intemperance 
As innocent as June — 
To know her not — Affliction — 
To own her for a Friend 
A warmth as near as if the Sun 
Were shining in your Hand.
