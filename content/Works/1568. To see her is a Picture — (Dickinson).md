# 1568. To see her is a Picture —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Lyric · Flowers · Stars]]

> [!abstract] 
> A lyric of devoted love and friendship: to see, hear and know her is picture, tune and innocent intemperance, while to lack her is affliction and to own her sunlit warmth.
