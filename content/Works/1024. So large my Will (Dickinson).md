*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Vanitas · Contemptus Mundi · Vanity of Worldly Things]]

> [!abstract] 
> A meditation on the smallness of earthly gifts against eternity: human will is large but its offerings are a scanty toy, an affront to the God who possesses all.

## Testo integrale / Full text

So large my Will  
The little that I may  
Embarrasses  
Like gentle infamy —  

Affront to Him  
For whom the Whole were small  
Affront to me  
Who know His Meed of all.  

Earth at the best  
Is but a scanty Toy —  
Bought, carried Home  
To Immortality.  

It looks so small  
We chiefly wonder then  
At our Conceit  
In purchasing.
