# 1226. The Popular Heart is a Cannon first —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Wine and Drink · Ballad Ballade · Road]]

> [!abstract] 
> Satire of mob enthusiasm: cannon, drum, bells and rum giving way to jail; critique of popular glory and society.

## Testo integrale / Full text

The Popular Heart is a Cannon first — 
Subsequent a Drum — 
Bells for an Auxiliary 
And an Afterward of Rum — 
 
Not a Tomorrow to know its name 
Nor a Past to stare — 
Ditches for Realms and a Trip to Jail 
For a Souvenir —
