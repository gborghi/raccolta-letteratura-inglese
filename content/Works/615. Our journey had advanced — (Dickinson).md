# 615. Our journey had advanced —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Self-Knowledge · Immortality · Self-Reliance]]

> [!abstract] 
> Life's journey reaches the fork into Eternity, with the Forest of the Dead between, no retreat, and God at every gate. Themes of mortality, the soul's journey, and the threshold of eternity.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Our journey had advanced — 
Our feet were almost come 
To that odd Fork in Being's Road — 
Eternity — by Term — 
 
Our pace took sudden awe — 
Our feet — reluctant — led — 
Before — were Cities — but Between — 
The Forest of the Dead — 
 
Retreat — was out of Hope — 
Behind — a Sealed Route — 
Eternity's White Flag — Before — 
And God — at every Gate —
