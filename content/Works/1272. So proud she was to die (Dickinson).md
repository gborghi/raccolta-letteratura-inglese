*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Mortality · Grave · Memento Mori]]

> [!abstract] 
> A woman dies so proudly and satisfied that her survivors' anguish almost turns to jealousy of her heavenward going.

## Testo integrale / Full text

So proud she was to die  
It made us all ashamed  
That what we cherished, so unknown  
To her desire seemed —  
So satisfied to go  
Where none of us should be  
Immediately — that Anguish stooped  
Almost to Jealousy —
