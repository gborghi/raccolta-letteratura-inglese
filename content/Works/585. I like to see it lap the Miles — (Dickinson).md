# 585. I like to see it lap the Miles —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Wonder and Gratitude · Sublime · Storm]]

> [!abstract] 
> A riddling personification of the railway train as a docile yet omnipotent animal lapping miles and neighing like Boanerges. Themes of nature versus the machine, wonder, and the natural order.

## Connections

**Themes & Concepts:** [[Wonder and Gratitude]], [[Natural Order]], [[Imagination]], [[Nature vs Civilization]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

I like to see it lap the Miles — 
And lick the Valleys up — 
And stop to feed itself at Tanks — 
And then — prodigious step 
 
Around a Pile of Mountains — 
And supercilious peer 
In Shanties — by the sides of Roads — 
And then a Quarry pare 
 
To fit its Ribs 
And crawl between 
Complaining all the while 
In horrid — hooting stanza — 
Then chase itself down Hill — 
 
And neigh like Boanerges — 
Then — punctual as a Star 
Stop — docile and omnipotent 
At its own stable door —
