# 805. This Bauble was preferred of Bees —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Lyric · Flowers · Stars]]

> [!abstract] 
> A flower lyric celebrating a blossom prized by bees, butterflies and birds, enamelled by noon, for whom the universe seems made. Treats nature, beauty and flowers as a self-sufficing little world.
