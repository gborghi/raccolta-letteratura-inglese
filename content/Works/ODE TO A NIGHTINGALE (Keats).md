# ODE TO A NIGHTINGALE

*by Keats*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Grief and Loss · Melancholy · Elegy]]

> [!abstract] 
> Great ode in which the bird's deathless song draws the poet toward easeful death and the imagination, then back to his sole self; mortality, transience, the immortality of art and the longing to escape human suffering.
