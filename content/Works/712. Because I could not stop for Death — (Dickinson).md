# 712. Because I could not stop for Death —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Death · Mortality · Immortality]]

> [!abstract] 
> Dickinson's most famous lyric: Death personified as a courteous suitor drives the speaker by carriage past school, fields and setting sun to the grave and on toward eternity; central meditation on mortality, immortality and time.
