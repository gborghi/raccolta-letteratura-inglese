# 1530. A Pang is more conspicuous in Spring

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Grief and Loss · Elegy · Clothing and Costume]]

> [!abstract] 
> A lyric on grief sharpened by spring's renewal; the contrast of singing things makes loss conspicuous, and even Resurrection had to wait till they moved a Stone.

## Testo integrale / Full text

A Pang is more conspicuous in Spring 
In contrast with the things that sing 
Not Birds entirely — but Minds — 
Minute Effulgencies and Winds — 
When what they sung for is undone 
Who cares about a Blue Bird's Tune — 
Why, Resurrection had to wait 
Till they had moved a Stone —
