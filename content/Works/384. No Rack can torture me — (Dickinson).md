# 384. No Rack can torture me —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Self-Knowledge · Immortality · Self-Reliance]]

> [!abstract] 
> The soul's liberty defies all bodily torture; the only true captivity is consciousness of being one's own enemy.

## Connections

**Motifs:** [[Birds]], [[Swords and Weapons]]
**Themes & Concepts:** [[Self-Knowledge]], [[Pain and Suffering]], [[Immortality]], [[Paradox]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

No Rack can torture me — 
My Soul — at Liberty — 
Behind this mortal Bone 
There knits a bolder One — 
 
You cannot prick with saw — 
Nor pierce with Scimitar — 
Two Bodies — therefore be — 
Bind One — The Other fly — 
 
The Eagle of his Nest 
No easier divest — 
And gain the Sky 
Than mayest Thou — 
 
Except Thyself may be 
Thine Enemy — 
Captivity is Consciousness — 
So's Liberty.
