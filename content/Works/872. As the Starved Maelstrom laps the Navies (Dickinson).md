# 872. As the Starved Maelstrom laps the Navies

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Greek Mythology · Lust Sexual Desire · Desire]]

> [!abstract] 
> A lyric piling images of devouring hunger — maelstrom, vulture, blood-tasting tiger — to figure the speaker's own 'finer Famine' of desire; a meditation on insatiable longing and appetite.

## Testo integrale / Full text

As the Starved Maelstrom laps the Navies 
As the Vulture teased 
Forces the Broods in lonely Valleys 
As the Tiger eased 
 
By but a Crumb of Blood, fasts Scarlet 
Till he meet a Man 
Dainty adorned with Veins and Tissues 
And partakes — his Tongue 
 
Cooled by the Morsel for a moment 
Grows a fiercer thing 
Till he esteem his Dates and Cocoa 
A Nutrition mean 
 
I, of a finer Famine 
Deem my Supper dry 
For but a Berry of Domingo 
And a Torrid Eye —
