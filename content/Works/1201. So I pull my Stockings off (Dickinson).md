## Testo integrale / Full text

So I pull my Stockings off 
Wading in the Water 
For the Disobedience' Sake 
Boy that lived for "or'ter" 
 
Went to Heaven perhaps at Death 
And perhaps he didn't 
Moses wasn't fairly used — 
Ananias wasn't —
