# 829. Ample make this Bed —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Dreams · Sleep · Resurrection]]

> [!abstract] 
> A solemn elegiac lyric figuring the grave as a bed made with awe, where the dead wait undisturbed till Judgment breaks. Joins death, the grave and resurrection-hope as sleep awaiting the Last Day.
