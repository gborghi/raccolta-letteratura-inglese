# 1087. We miss a Kinsman more

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Alienation · Outsider · Exile]]

> [!abstract] 
> A lyric on the paradox of nearness and absence: a kinsman is missed more when expected and close than when oceans away, exploring distance, separation and longing.

## Connections

**Motifs:** [[Sea]]
**Themes & Concepts:** [[Exile]], [[Paradox]], [[Grief and Loss]], [[Unrequited Frustrated Love]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

We miss a Kinsman more 
When warranted to see 
Than when withheld of Oceans 
From possibility 
 
A Furlong than a League 
Inflicts a pricklier pain, 
Till We, who smiled at Pyrenees — 
Of Parishes, complain.
