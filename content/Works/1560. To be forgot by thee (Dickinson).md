# 1560. To be forgot by thee

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Romantic Love · The Lover · Unrequited Frustrated Love]]

> [!abstract] 
> A lyric of unrequited love and memory: to be forgotten by the beloved surpasses all other minds' remembrance; being worthy to be forgot is the speaker's renown.
