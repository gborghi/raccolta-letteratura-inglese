# THE EOLIAN HARP

*by Coleridge*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Imagination · Creativity and Artistic Labor · The One Life (Pantheism)]]

> [!abstract] 
> A major conversation poem at Clevedon: the wind-harp prompts the pantheistic 'one Life' vision of nature as organic harps swept by an 'intellectual breeze', checked by pious humility; themes of imagination, the One Life, faith, love, and nature.
