# 1175. We like a Hairbreadth 'scape

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Nature · Book of Nature · Natural Order]]

> [!abstract] 
> A reflective lyric on the lingering thrill of a narrow escape, the breeze of risk reaching divinely to our utmost hair; risk, wind, the value of daring.

## Connections

**Motifs:** [[Wind]]
**Themes & Concepts:** [[Fate and Free Will]], [[Joy]], [[Sublime]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

We like a Hairbreadth 'scape 
It tingles in the Mind 
Far after Act or Accident 
Like paragraphs of Wind 
 
If we had ventured less 
The Breeze were not so fine 
That reaches to our utmost Hair 
Its Tentacles divine.
