*by Coleridge*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Wonder and Gratitude · The Sublime · Sun]]

> Stolberg's hexameter 'Hymn to the Earth': the Earth as mother and nurse of countless children, bride of heaven, whose first blush was the first dawn and whose embrace bred teeming life; a sublime pantheistic nature-hymn.
