# 651. So much Summer

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Lyric · Romantic Love · Marriage and Fidelity]]

> [!abstract] 
> The speaker's small but ample 'summer' set against the rich Lady with the guinea; a robin's larder of contentment. Themes: nature, humility, contentment vs wealth. Form: lyric.

## Testo integrale / Full text

So much Summer 
Me for showing 
Illegitimate — 
Would a Smile's minute bestowing 
Too exorbitant 
 
To the Lady 
With the Guinea 
Look — if She should know 
Crumb of Mine 
A Robin's Larder 
Would suffice to stow —
