# 1413. Sweet Skepticism of the Heart —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Christianity · Faith and Doubt · Doubt]]

> [!abstract] 
> Doubt cherished because certainty would be sere; faith and doubt, truth deferred, the thrill of suspended knowing.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Sweet Skepticism of the Heart — 
That knows — and does not know — 
And tosses like a Fleet of Balm — 
Affronted by the snow — 
Invites and then retards the Truth 
Lest Certainty be sere 
Compared with the delicious throe 
Of transport thrilled with Fear —
