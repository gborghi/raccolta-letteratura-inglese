# 347. When Night is almost done —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Sun · Light and Dark · Fire]]

> [!abstract] 
> A short dawn lyric on the near approach of sunrise dissolving night's terror into trivial memory; on hope, light and dark, and overcome fear.

## Connections

**Motifs:** [[Dawn]], [[Night]], [[Light and Dark]]
**Themes & Concepts:** [[Joy]], [[Grace and Redemption]]
**Forms & Genres:** [[Lyric]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

When Night is almost done — 
And Sunrise grows so near 
That we can touch the Spaces — 
It's time to smooth the Hair — 
 
And get the Dimples ready — 
And wonder we could care 
For that old — faded Midnight — 
That frightened — but an Hour —
