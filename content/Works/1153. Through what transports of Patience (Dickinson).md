# 1153. Through what transports of Patience

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Grief and Loss · Elegy · Clothing and Costume]]

> [!abstract] 
> A grief lyric on the bleak endurance of living without a lost beloved, envying the dead their privilege of dying; loss, suffering, longing for death.

## Testo integrale / Full text

Through what transports of Patience 
I reached the stolid Bliss 
To breathe my Blank without thee 
Attest me this and this — 
By that bleak exultation 
I won as near as this 
Thy privilege of dying 
Abbreviate me this —
