# 769. One and One — are One —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Self-Knowledge · Immortality · Self-Reliance]]

> [!abstract] 
> An epigrammatic lyric rejecting school arithmetic for the soul's stark minor choosing among Life, Death, or the Everlasting. Themes: mortality, immortality, the soul's ultimate alternatives, paradox.

## Testo integrale / Full text

One and One — are One — 
Two — be finished using — 
Well enough for Schools — 
But for Minor Choosing — 
 
Life — just — or Death — 
Or the Everlasting — 
More — would be too vast 
For the Soul's Comprising —
