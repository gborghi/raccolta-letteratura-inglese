# 812. A Light exists in Spring

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Seasons · Winter · Spring]]

> [!abstract] 
> A celebrated lyric on a special light of early spring that science cannot overtake but human nature feels, whose passing is a quality of loss, like trade encroaching on a sacrament. Joins seasons, spring, transience and the sacred in nature.
