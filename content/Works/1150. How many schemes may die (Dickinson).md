# 1150. How many schemes may die

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Lyric · Romantic Love · Marriage and Fidelity]]

> [!abstract] 
> A lyric on the unseen contingencies of fate: how plans, escapes, and loves turn on accident; chance, fate, and the unknown course of a day.

## Testo integrale / Full text

How many schemes may die 
In one short Afternoon 
Entirely unknown 
To those they most concern — 
The man that was not lost 
Because by accident 
He varied by a Ribbon's width 
From his accustomed route — 
The Love that would not try 
Because beside the Door 
It must be competitions 
Some unsuspecting Horse was tied 
Surveying his Despair
