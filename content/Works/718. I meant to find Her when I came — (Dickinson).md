# 718. I meant to find Her when I came —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Death · Silence · Bells]]

> [!abstract] 
> An elegiac lyric: the speaker came to find the beloved but Death had the same design and won; left to wander in restless grief and memory. On death, loss and bereavement.

## Testo integrale / Full text

I meant to find Her when I came — 
Death — had the same design — 
But the Success — was His — it seems — 
And the Surrender — Mine — 
 
I meant to tell Her how I longed 
For just this single time — 
But Death had told Her so the first — 
And she had past, with Him — 
 
To wander — now — is my Repose — 
To rest — To rest would be 
A privilege of Hurricane 
To Memory — and Me.
