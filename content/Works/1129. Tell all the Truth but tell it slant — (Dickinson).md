*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Paradox · Truth · Aphorism]]

> [!abstract] 
> An ars-poetica epigram counselling indirection: truth too bright must dazzle gradually like lightning explained, or it blinds — truth, the inexpressible and the art of telling.
