# 1759. Which misses most,

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Grief and Loss · Elegy · Clothing and Costume]]

> [!abstract] 
> A meditation on grief weighing the loss of the tending hand and the blessing lip now gone to sleep.

## Connections

**Motifs:** [[Death]], [[Sleep]]
**Themes & Concepts:** [[Grief and Loss]], [[Melancholy]]
**Forms & Genres:** [[Lyric]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

Which misses most, 
The hand that tends, 
Or heart so gently borne, 
'Tis twice as heavy as it was 
Because the hand is gone? 
 
Which blesses most, 
The lip that can, 
Or that that went to sleep 
 
With "if I could" endeavoring 
Without the strength to shape?
