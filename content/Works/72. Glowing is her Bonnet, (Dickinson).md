# 72. Glowing is her Bonnet,

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Death · Silence · Bells]]

> [!abstract] 
> A dead woman figured as a glowing flower/daisy vanishing unrecorded, mourned only by sunrise and rill; death, nature and quiet elegy.

## Connections

**Motifs:** [[Death]], [[Flowers]], [[Nature]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

Glowing is her Bonnet, 
Glowing is her Cheek, 
Glowing is her Kirtle, 
Yet she cannot speak. 
 
Better as the Daisy 
From the Summer hill 
Vanish unrecorded 
Save by tearful rill — 
 
Save by loving sunrise 
Looking for her face. 
Save by feet unnumbered 
Pausing at the place.
