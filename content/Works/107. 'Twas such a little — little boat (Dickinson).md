# 107. 'Twas such a little — little boat

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Sea · Water · Sea]]

> [!abstract] 
> A miniature allegory of a little boat swallowed by a greedy sea — the fragile soul or life lost on the vast deep; transience and the indifferent grandeur of the sea.

## Connections

**Motifs:** [[Sea]], [[Water]], [[Journey]]

## Testo integrale / Full text

'Twas such a little — little boat 
That toddled down the bay! 
'Twas such a gallant — gallant sea 
That beckoned it away! 
 
'Twas such a greedy, greedy wave 
That licked it from the Coast — 
Nor ever guessed the stately sails 
My little craft was lost!
