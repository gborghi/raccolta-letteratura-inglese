# 831. Dying! To be afraid of thee

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Death · Mortality · Immortality]]

> [!abstract] 
> A martial lyric in which death's artillery is feared only for the friend or beloved it exposes, two armies — Love and Certainty — fighting sternly in a dying eye. Joins death, love, grief and fear through battle imagery.
