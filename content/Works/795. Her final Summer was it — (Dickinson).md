# 795. Her final Summer was it —

*by Dickinson*  ·  **Cluster:** [[Grief and Loss · Melancholy · Elegy]]

> [!abstract] 
> An elegy on a woman whose last summer was unrecognized until death made her hurried 'finishing' plain, marked by her Carrara (marble) guide post. Treats mortality, the blindness of the living and seasonal transience.
