# English Literature — A Knowledge Graph

A connected reading of the English canon, published as a free website:

### 🔗 https://gborghi.github.io/raccolta-letteratura-inglese

Not a plain archive of texts. Every work is broken into readable units and woven to every other work through the **ideas they share** — themes, archetypes, motifs, forms, settings, historical references and recurring characters. Open a work to follow its connections; open a concept to see every work that carries it.

## What's inside

- **2,857 works** by **13 authors** — Austen, Brontë, Chesterton, Coleridge, Dickens, Dickinson, Eliot, Keats, Poe, Sayers, Shakespeare, Whitman, Wilde.
- **14,940 reading units** — each long work is split into chapters, scenes, poems or prose sections, so you read (and link to) the exact passage, not a wall of text.
- A shared concept vocabulary that binds it all together:
  - **178 themes / concepts** (e.g. *alienation*, *mortality*, *faith*)
  - **712 characters**
  - **127 motifs** · **43 topoi** · **66 literary forms** · **42 historical references**
  - **96 thematic clusters** grouping works by the constellations of theme and form they share.

## How to use it

- **Browse works** — a sortable table of everything, by author and title.
- **Follow a concept** — every note lists the works that share it, so a theme becomes a path across authors and centuries.
- **Search by theme** — find works through the ideas in them, not just keywords.
- **Author & cluster wheels** — spin the radial emblems on the home page to jump into a body of work or a thematic family.
- **Interactive graph & full-text search** — the whole corpus as a navigable network.
- **Related chapters** — within a work, each unit surfaces the passages most connected to it.

## Bilingual (in progress)

Works are being given **Italian translations** alongside the English, with a per-page language toggle — literary translations done chapter by chapter (Sayers complete; Chesterton underway). The site interface is in Italian (*opere*, *cerca*, *naviga*) for Italian readers of English literature.

## How it's built

The content is an [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md) vault: Markdown notes linked with `[[wikilinks]]`, where concept notes back-reference the works and the interlinks are generated from those references. The site is rendered and hosted for free on GitHub Pages using [Quartz](https://quartz.jzhao.xyz/) (v5). Pushing to `main` rebuilds and deploys the site.
